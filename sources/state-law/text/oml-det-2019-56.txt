RE: Open Meeting Law Complaints
Dear Mr. Hegarty:
This office received two complaints from Marguerite Lee, Second Assistant Register 
of Deeds in Norfolk County, on November 20, 2018 and one complaint from Richard 
Kennedy, First Assistant Register of Deeds on November 23, 2018, alleging that the Norfolk 
County Commission (the “Commission”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 
18-25. The complaints were originally filed with the Commission on or about September 21 
and September 25, 2018, and you responded to the complaints, on behalf of the Commission, 
in separate letters dated October 3, 2018. All three complaints allege that the Commission 
failed to review Open Meeting Law complaints prior to sending its responses.1
We resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 CMR 
29.07(2)(a), after reviewing the original complaints, the Commission’s responses to the 
complaint, and the complaints filed with our office requesting further review. We find that 
the Commission violated the Open Meeting Law in the manner alleged.
The complaints allege that the Commission failed to review Open Meeting Law 
complaints prior to sending its responses. When an individual files an Open Meeting Law 
complaint with a public body, the chair “shall disseminate copies of the complaint to the

For the reasons stated above, we order the Board’s immediate and future compliance 
with the Open Meeting Law, and we caution that similar future violations may be considered 
evidence of intent to violate the law. We now consider the complaint addressed by this 
determination to be resolved. This determination does not address any other complaints that 
may be pending with the Commission or with our office. Please feel free to contact our office
