RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Linde; 
This office received a complaint from Jeanne Holmes, dated December 15, 2015, alleging 
that the Brockton Board of Library Trustees (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. 
c. 30A, §§ 18-25. Specifically, the complaint alleges that the Board held a meeting on 
September 28, 2015 that was not properly noticed. The complaint was originally filed with the 
Board on October 12, 2015. The Board responded by letter dated October 19, 2015. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged. A 
public body must post notice of every meeting at least 48 hours in advance of the meeting, not 
including Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays. G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b). The notice for a local 
public body must be posted in or on the building in which the municipal clerk's office is located, 
and must be conspicuously visible to the public at all hours. G.L. c. 30A, § 20(c). As an 
alternative notice posting method, a municipality may choose to post notices to its website. 940 
CMR 29.03(2)(b)a. The City of Brockton notified our office on December 24, 2014 that it was 
adopting its website, www.brockton.ma.us, as its alternative notice posting location. 
The notice for the Board's September 28, 2015 meeting was posted in the City Clerk's 
Office on September 23, 2015, but was not posted to the City's website. Although the notice 
was publicly posted at least 48 hours before the meeting, it was not posted to the City's official 
===PAGE 2===
posting location, the municipal website. Accordingly, we find that the Board violated the Open 
Meeting Law. See OML 2014-153; OML 2013-55; OML 2011-32.1 
Although the Board does not directly acknowledge its violation in its response to the 
complaint, the Board pledges to post all future meetings notices to the City website and to 
consider scheduling an Open Meeting Law training for its members. In addition to these actions, 
we order the Board's immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and caution 
the Board that a similar future violation of the law may be considered evidence of an intentional 
violation. Because the Board took no votes or actions during its September 28, 2015 meeting, 
we do not order that votes from the meeting be rescinded, as requested by the complainant. 
We now consider this matter closed. Please be advised that this letter does not resolve 
any other complaints that may be pending with this office or with the Board. Please feel free to 
contact our office at 617-963-2540 if you have any questions regarding this letter.
