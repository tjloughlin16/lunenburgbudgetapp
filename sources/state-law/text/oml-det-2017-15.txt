RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Chair Dunn: 
This office received a complaint from Heather Meninger, dated October 27, 2016, 
alleging that the Groveland Board of Selectmen (the '"Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, 
G.L. c. 3OA, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on September 12, 
2016, and the Board responded by letter dated September 22, 2016. In her complaint, Ms. 
Meninger alleges that the Board denied an individual the option of recording an open meeting. 
Following our review, we resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law, 
The complaint concerns the Board's August 15, 2016 meeting. During this meeting, the 
Board anticipated holding an executive session pursuant to G.L. c. 30A, § 21(a)(1) to discuss 
complaints or charges against the chair of the Conservation Commission. The Conservation 
Commission chair was present, and he requested that the discussion be held in open session. The 
Board complied with this request. The Conservation Commission chair did not state that he 
wished to have the open session discussion recorded, nor did the Board offer him that option. 
The complaint alleges that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law by refusing the 
Conservation Commission chair his right to have the meeting recorded. While individuals have 
a right to record open session meetings after notifying the chair of an intent to record, see G.L. c. 
30A, § 20(f), no such request was made during the August 15, 2016 meeting. Additionally, there 
is no requirement in the law that the Board make an affirmative offer to an individual to record a 
meeting. We therefore find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Board. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any questions.
