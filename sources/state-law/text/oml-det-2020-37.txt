RE: Open  Meeting Law Complaint
Dear Mr. McAuliffe:
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins, on April 16, 2019, alleging that 
the Swansea Recreation Commission (the “Commission”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. 
c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Commission on March 14, 2019, 
and you responded on behalf of the Commission by letter dated April 1, 2019. In his complaint, 
Mr. Higgins alleges that the Commission failed to list the names of candidates who the 
Commission anticipated interviewing during its March 5, 2019, meeting.
We appreciate the patience of the parties while we reviewed this complaint. We resolve 
this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the 
Commission violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged. A public body must post notice of every 
meeting at least 48 hours in advance, not including Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays. G.L. 
c. 30A, § 20(b). Notices must include “a listing of topics that the chair reasonably anticipates 
will be discussed at the meeting.” Id. The list of topics must have sufficient specificity to 
reasonably advise the public of the issues to be discussed at the meeting. 940 CMR 29.03(1 )(b).
Here, the Commission’s notice included an item, “Swansea Rec Coordinator/ 
interviews.” The Commission does not argue that the names of the applicants were 
unanticipated. Rather the Commission asserts that the position was only part-time and of limited 
interest to the public. We find these arguments unavailing. We find that the notice should have 
included the identities of all anticipated interviewees. See OML 2016-109; OML 2012-90.
We order the Commission’s immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting 
Law, and caution the Commission that a determination by our office of a similar violation in the 
future may be considered evidence of intent to violate the Open Meeting Law.
