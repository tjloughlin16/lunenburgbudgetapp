 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Attorney King: 
This Office received a complaint from George Harris on December 9, 2020, alleging that 
the Wayland High School Council (the “Council”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, 
§§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Council on October 21.1 On November 10, 
counsel for the Council requested an extension of time to respond. On November 11, our office 
granted the Council an extension until December 2. As the Council had not formally responded 
to the complaint at the time it was filed with our office, we emailed with you on several 
occasions in December 2020 to investigate the complaint.2 In his complaint, Mr. Harris alleges 
that the Council failed to post meeting notices in Wayland’s official notice posting location and 
failed to create and approve meeting minutes in a timely manner for meetings which took place 
between July 1, 2018, and June 30, 2019. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). This complaint is nearly identical to one filed by Mr. Harris concerning 
notices and minutes for the Council’s meetings during the 2019-2020 school year. We resolved 
that complaint in OML 2020-162, finding that the Council violated the Open Meeting Law by

failing to post notices and create and approve minutes for meetings held during the 2019-2020

In response to the two complaints, we understand that the Council is in the midst of an 
extensive effort to create and approve all missing meeting minutes. We commend the Council for 
undertaking these remedial actions and order the Council, to the best of its ability, to finish 
creating meeting minutes for all meetings that took place during the 2018-2019 school year 
within 90 days of receipt of this determination.  
 We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Council. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any questions.
