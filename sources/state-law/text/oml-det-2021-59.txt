 RE:        Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Attorney Feodoroff: 
 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on April 9, 2021, alleging that the 
Easton Select Board (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.  The 
complaint was originally filed with the Board on February 26, 2021, and you responded, on 
behalf of the Board, by letter dated March 9, 2021.  The complaint alleges that the Board 
violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to announce the name of each member who 
participated remotely in the Board’s February 22, 2021, meeting. 
 
We resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), 
after reviewing the original complaint, the Board’s response to the complaint, the complaint filed 
with our office requesting further review, and a video recording of the Board’s meeting held on 
February 22, 2021.1  For substantially the same reasons stated in our recent determinations OML 
2020-168 and OML 2021-44, copies of which are enclosed, we find that the Board did not 
violate the Open Meeting Law as alleged.     
 
 
1 A video recording of the Board’s February 22, 2021, meeting is available at 
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xiVc9SC-7vE.
