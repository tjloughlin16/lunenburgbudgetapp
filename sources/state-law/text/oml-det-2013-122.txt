RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Doyle: 
This office received a complaint from David MacLean, dated February 7, 2013, 
alleging that the Franklin Board of Assessors (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on or 
about December 21, 2012, and the Board responded to the original complaint by letter 
dated January 10, 2013. 
Following our review, we find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting 
Law's requirements regarding the timely creation and approval of minutes. In reaching 
this determination, we reviewed the December 21, 2012 complaint filed with the Board; 
the Board's January 10, 2013 response; and the February 7, 2013 complaint filed with our 
office, requesting further review. 
The Open Meeting Law requires that the "[mjinutes of all open sessions shall be 
created and approved in a timely manner." G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c). The Law does not 
provide a definition of "timely manner," but we recommend that minutes be approved at 
the next meeting, whenever possible. OML 2012-91. According to the complainant, the 
minutes from the Board's December 6, 2012 and December 13, 2012 meetings had not 
been approved as of the Board's next meeting on December 20, 2012, despite the fact that 
the Board met on a weekly basis. While the Board's general practice is to approve meeting 
minutes at its next meeting, these particular sets of minutes were approved at the Board's 
December 27, 2012 meeting. We are satisfied that the Board approved these minutes in a 
timely manner, and we note that the Board has nevertheless expressed a commitment to 
improving the process of drafting its meeting minutes going forward. 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. 
This determination does not address any other complaints which may be pending with our
