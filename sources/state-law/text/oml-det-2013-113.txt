RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Frank: 
This office received a complaint filed by Patrick Higgins, dated May 30, 2013 alleging 
that the Swansea Board of Selectmen (the "Board") and Board member M. Scott Ventura 
violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with 
the Board on or about April 23, 2013, and you responded on behalf of the Board to the complaint 
by letter dated May 7, 2013. In his complaint, Mr. Higgins alleges that on April 21, 2013, the 
Board engaged in a deliberation outside of a properly posted meeting through email. 
After reviewing the complaint and the Board's response, we have decided to resolve this 
complaint by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.017(2)(a). The facts in this case 
are similar to those underlying our decision in OML 2013-27. While here it is a close question, 
and the complainant is not without blame, we likewise find that, while attempting to respond to a 
question posed in the complainant's email, Mr. Ventura individually violated the Open Meeting 
Law by sending an email sharing his opinion on a matter of public business within the Board's 
jurisdiction to a quorum of the Board. However, we do not find that the violation was 
intentional, and we acknowledge that Mr. Ventura was attempting to cut off potentially improper 
email communication when he sent the email at issue. We order immediate and future 
compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and order the Board to disclose the content of Mr. 
Ventura's April 21, 2013 email during a properly posted open meeting. 
Emails sent to a quorum of a public body inviting a response have the potential to result 
in an inadvertent or intentional reply to all recipients containing a public body members' opinion 
on the matter being discussed. Because of this risk, we continue to recommend that public 
bodies avoid communicating over email at all except for distributing meeting agendas, 
scheduling meetings and distributing documents created by non-members to be discussed at 
meetings, provided that no opinions are expressed. OML 2013-29; OML 2013-4.
