RE: Open Meeting Law Complaints
Dear Assistant Superintendent Luff:
This office received two nearly identical complaints from Ronald Alexander on July 11, 
2019, alleging that the Natick School Committee (the “Committee”) violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. Mr. Alexander numbered these complaints 2019-3 and 2019-4. 
These complaints were originally filed with the Committee on June 3, 2019, and you responded 
on behalf of the Committee by email dated June 5, 2019.
Following our review, we resolve these complaints by informal action in accordance 
with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a) and find that there was no violation of the Open Meeting Law. 
Shortly after filing these Open Meeting Law complaints, Mr. Alexander filed additional, 
substantially identical complaints, numbered 2019-10 and 2019-11. We resolved those 
complaints in a separate determination, OML 2019-126, and found that the Committee did not 
violate the Open Meeting Law. For the same reasons explained therein, we find that the 
Committee did not violate the Open Meeting Law as alleged in complaints 2019-3 and 2019-4.
We now consider the complaints addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Committee. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any 
questions.
