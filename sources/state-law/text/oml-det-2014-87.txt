RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Bragan, 
This office received a complaint filed by Ms. Deborah Skauen-Hinchliffe, dated June 20, 
2014, alleging that the Harvard Town Hall Building Committee (the "Committee") violated the 
Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. Specifically, the complaint alleges that Committee 
Chair Peter Jackson sent emails to a quorum of the Committee discussing matters within the 
Committee's jurisdiction. The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on or about 
April 9, 2014, and the Chair responded by letter dated May 9, 2014.' 
We have decided to resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a). Following our review, we find that the Committee Chair violated the Open 
Meeting Law by sending two emails concerning matters within the Committee's jurisdiction to a 
quorum of the Committee. See OML 2012-67; OML 2012-15; OML 2012-8; OML 2011-37." 
The emails, dated January 24, 2014 and March 31, 2014, were sent by Chair Jackson and 
contained his opinion regarding business before the Committee as well as a memorandum to the 
Board of Selectmen that the Chair had drafted. While we find that these emails constituted 
improper deliberation outside of an open meeting, because no Committee members responded to 
either message we find this was an individual violation of the law. See OML 2012-67; OML
