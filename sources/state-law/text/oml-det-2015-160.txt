RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Waugh: 
This office received a complaint from Ronald Beaty, dated September 26, alleging that 
the Dennis-Yarmouth Regional School Committee (the "Committee") violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 3OA, §§ 18-25.1 The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on 
September 8, and the Committee responded by letter dated September 23. In his complaint, Mr. 
Beaty alleges that the Committee failed to post notice for a meeting at least 48 hours in advance. 
Following our review, we find that the Committee did not violate the Open Meeting Law. 
In reaching a determination, we reviewed the original complaint; the Committee's response; and 
the request for further review filed with our office. We also reviewed documentation included 
with these filings. Finally, we spoke with the Committee's attorney by phone on October 16.2 
FACTS 
We find the facts as follows. The Committee planned a meeting for Tuesday, September 
8, the day after Labor Day, to begin at 6:30 P.M. On September 3, the preceding Thursday, the 
School Superintendent's administrative assistant sent copies of the meeting notice by e-mail to 
the town clerks in Dennis and Yarmouth. At 4:25 P.M. on September 3, the office assistant in 
Dennis responded by e-mail stating, "The meeting has been posted in Dennis." The meeting 
notice was marked as posted in Yarmouth at 4:59 P.M. on September 3. The Committee posted 
notice of the meeting on its website on Friday, September 4 at 12:21 P.M.

DISCUSSION 
The Open Meeting Law requires that, except in an emergency, a public body "shall post 
notice of every meeting at least 48 hours prior to such meeting excluding Saturdays, Sundays and 
legal holidays." G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b). For meetings of a local public body, notice must be filed 
with the municipal clerk and posted in a manner conspicuously visible to the public at all hours 
in or on the municipal building in which the clerk's office is located. G.L. c. 30A, § 20(c). 
Regional school committees must post notice 48 hours in advance of a meeting in each city or 
town within the district in the manner prescribed for local public bodies. See G.L. c. 3OA, § 
20(c); 940 CMR 29.03(2); OML 2014-139. However, regional school committees may choose 
an alternative posting method, such as posting to a website. 940 CMR 29.03(4)(b). 
Here, the Committee has not adopted its website as an alternative posting location, and 
the Committee is therefore required to post notice in each town that composes the district. The 
town clerks in both Dennis and Yarmouth posted notice of the meeting on Thursday, September 
3. This was more than 48 hours in advance, excluding Saturday, Sunday, and the legal holiday 
on Monday. We therefore find that the Committee complied with its notice posting obligations 
under the Open Meeting Law. See G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b); 940 CMR 29.03(2). 
While not raised in the original complaint, it appears that the Committee delegated the 
duty of responding to this Open Meeting Law complaint to its legal counsel without first 
discussing the complaint during a meeting. The Open Meeting Law requires that, within 14 
business days after receiving an Open Meeting Law complaint, a public body shall review the 
complaint's allegations; take remedial action, if appropriate; and send to the Attorney General's 
Office a copy of the complaint and a description of any remedial action taken. G.L. c. 30A, § 
23(b); 940 CMR 29.05(5). The body must convene a meeting in order to review the complaint 
before sending a response or authorizing its legal counsel to send a response. See OML 2012-
90; OML 2011 -6. We take this opportunity to remind the Committee of its obligations under the 
Open Meeting Law. 
For the reasons stated above, we find that the Committee did not violate the Open 
Meeting Law. We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. 
This determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or 
the Committee. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any 
questions.
