This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on January 15, 2016, alleging 
that the Swansea Library Building Planning Committee (the Committee) violated the Open 
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Committee 
on or about December 4, 2015, and you responded, on behalf of the Committee, to the 
complaint by letter dated January 12, 2016.1 In his complaint, Mr. Higgins alleges that the 
revised notice for the Committee's December 3, 2015 meeting failed to include the date and 
time that the original notice was posted. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance 
with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Committee did not violate the Open Meeting 
Law. The complaint alleges that the amended notice for a Committee meeting held on 
December 3, 2015, posted that same day at 3:28 P.M., did not include the date and time that 
the original notice was posted. The notice was originally posted on November 25, 2015. 
Upon prompting by the complainant, the Committee amended the original notice on the day 
of the meeting by spelling out two acronyms that it had listed on the original notice. As the 
Committee conceded, this amended notice did not include the requisite date and time that the 
original notice was posted, however no new topics were added. See 940 CMR 29.03(l)(b); 
OML 2015-43 ("If a meeting notice is revised, the revised notice must conspicuously record 
on or with the notice both the date and time the original notice was posted as well as the date 
and time the last revision was posted.") Because no new topics were added to the revised 
notice, we decline to find a violation for the Committee's attempt to clarify existing topics on
