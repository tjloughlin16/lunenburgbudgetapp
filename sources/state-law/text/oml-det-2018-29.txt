RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Troy: 
This office received a complaint from Gary Maloney, dated January 22, 2018, alleging 
that the Bourne Board of Health (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 
18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on November 20, 2017, and, after an 
extension of time was granted, the Board responded by letter dated December 13, 2017. In his 
complaint, Mr. Maloney alleges that the Board failed to post notice of a meeting. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and we find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged in 
the complaint. The Town of Bourne has adopted its municipal website for notice posting 
purposes, see 940 CMR 29.03(2)(b), and notices are therefore considered posted for Open 
Meeting Law purposes when they appear on that website. The complaint concerns a Board 
meeting held on October 25, 2017. Notice of this meeting was posted to the bulletin board 
outside the town clerk's office, but notice was not posted to the municipal website. The Board 
does not dispute this fact. Accordingly, we find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law 
as alleged. See G.L. c. 3OA, 20(b); OML 2017-134.1 The Board stated to our office that the 
error was inadvertent and that efforts will be made to ensure that notices are properly posted in 
the future. We commend the Board for these efforts in response to this complaint. 
We order the Board's immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and 
we caution that similar future violations may be considered evidence of intent to violate the law. 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Board. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any questions.
