RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Rice: 
This office received an Open Meeting Law complaint from Richard Langone, on 
behalf of the Swan Place Committee, dated November 9, 2012, alleging that the Arlington 
Board of Selectmen (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. 
Specifically, the complaint alleges that the Board, or its Parking Subcommittee, met in 
violation of the Law to approve a plan to remove resident permit parking signs from Swan 
Place in Arlington and replace them with one-hour parking signs. The complaint was 
originally filed with the Board on or about September 20, 2012. The Board responded by 
letter dated October 3, 2012. 
We reviewed the September 20, 2012 complaint filed with the Board; the Board's 
October 3, 2012 response; and the November 9, 2012 complaint filed with our office. We 
also reviewed the notice and minutes of the Board's August 27, 2012 meeting, and reviewed 
a letter from you, dated December 13, 2012, which was sent to this office in response to our 
request for documents and information. 
Following our review, we have decided to resolve this complaint by informal action, 
in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the Board's Parking Subcommittee 
violated the Law by failing to post notice for its June 19, 2012 and August 20, 2012 
meetings. However, because the Board acknowledged the violation and took appropriate 
remedial action, we decline to nullify the Subcommittee's actions as requested in the 
complaint. In response to the complaint, the Board vowed to "ensure that all future Sub-

Committee meetings are posted and conducted in accordance with the Open Meeting Law." 
Furthermore, the Town replaced the resident parking signs at issue while the Board and the 
Subcommittee continued discussion of the matter. We therefore order immediate and future 
compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and caution that future similar violations may be 
considered evidence of an intentional violation of the Law. 
We now consider this matter closed. Please contact our office at (617) 962-2540 if 
you have any questions regarding this letter.
