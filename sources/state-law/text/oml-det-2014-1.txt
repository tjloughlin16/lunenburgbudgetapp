RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint - Boxford Zoning Board of Appeals 
Dear Attorney Reich: 
This office received a complaint from Boxford Town Clerk Robin Phelan, dated June 17, 
2013, alleging violations of the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25, by the Boxford 
Zoning Board of Appeals (the "Board"). Specifically, the complaint alleges that the Board has 
failed to comply with the Open Meeting Law's meeting minutes requirements. The complaint 
was originally filed with the Board on or about June 3, 2013. The Board responded by letter 
dated June 14, 2013. 
Following our review, we have decided to resolve this complaint by informal action, in 
accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law 
by failing to approve meeting minutes in a timely manner. However, the Board took appropriate 
remedial action in response to the complaint by reviewing and approving all of the meeting 
minutes at issue. 
The Law requires that "[m]inutes of all open sessions shall be created and approved in a 
timely manner." G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c). The Open Meeting Law does not define "timely manner," 
however we recommend that minutes be approved at the next meeting, whenever possible. See 
OML 2013-31; OML 2012-91.1 The complaint identified six sets of minutes that were approved 
by the Board four to nine months after the meetings they record. Additionally, the complaint 
identified seven sets of minutes from meetings held during 2012 and early 2013 that were never 
approved. In its June 14, 2013 response to the complaint, the Board committed to timely
