RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Feeley: 
This office reviewed a complaint filed by John P. Gagliardi (the complainant) alleging 
that the Billerica Board of Selectmen (the Board) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. BOA, 
§§ 18-25. The complaint specifically alleges that an item on the Board's posted agenda for its 
April 17, 2014 meeting was insufficiently specific. The complaint was originally filed with the 
Board on April 30, 2014. You responded to the complaint, on behalf of the Board, by letter 
dated May 7, 2014.1 A request for further review dated May 21, 2014 was filed with this office. 
In reaching a determination, we reviewed the April 30, 2014 complaint filed with the 
Board; the Board's May 7, 2014 response; and the May 21, 2014 request for further review filed 
with our office. We also reviewed the agenda posted for the Board's April 17, 2014 meeting as 
well as posted agendas for the Board's April 8, 2013 and August 26, 2013 meetings. 
Additionally, we reviewed the minutes for the Board's April 17, 2014 meeting. Finally, we 
spoke via telephone with counsel for the Board in June 2014. Following our review, we have 
decided to resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). 
We find that the agenda item "Spring 2014 Town Meeting Warrant Articles" was sufficiently 
specific. 
The agenda for the Board's April 17, 2014 meeting listed one topic for discussion: 
"Spring 2014 Town Meeting Warrant Articles." At the meeting, the Board reviewed the entire 
Town Meeting warrant and deliberated on multiple articles. The complainant asserts that the 
Board should have given the public notice that it contemplated removing a particular article, 
which had been submitted by the Building Commissioner, from the Town Meeting warrant. 
We have previously stated that when a public body is reviewing a warrant in its entirety, 
and not specific warrant articles, that an agenda item such as the one at issue here is sufficiently
