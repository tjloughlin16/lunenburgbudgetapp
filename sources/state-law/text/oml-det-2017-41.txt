RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Mead: 
This office received a complaint from Stephen Morgan on February 6, 2017, alleging 
that the Ashland Open Space & Recreation Committee (the "Committee") violated the Open 
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.1 The complaint was originally filed with the Committee 
on or about December 29, and you responded, on behalf of the Committee, by letter dated 
January 11, 2017. In his complaint, Mr. Morgan alleges that, during its October 19 meeting, 
the Committee discussed a topic that was not listed on the notice.2 Mr. Morgan also alleges 
that the Committee has failed to file meeting minutes since July 12. 
After reviewing the complaint, the Committee's response, and the request for further 
review filed with our office, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the Committee did not violate the Open Meeting Law. 
The Open Meeting Law requires public bodies to "create and maintain accurate minutes of all 
meetings, including executive sessions," G.L. c, BOA, § 22(a). These meeting minutes must 
be reviewed and approved in a timely manner. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c). Although the Open 
Meeting Law does not define "timely manner," we recommend that minutes of a meeting be 
approved at the next meeting, whenever possible. See OML 2014-15; OML 2012-91.3 The

Law does not require that minutes be filed or published to a website, however.4 See G.L. c. 
30A, § 22; OML 2017-25; OML 2016-17. 
The Committee approved all of the minutes at issue within two months. We find this 
to be an appropriate amount of time for approving minutes. See OML 2016-157; OML 2016-
62. Those minutes have since been posted to the Committee's website. In the meantime, we 
note that anyone can request a copy of open session minutes from the Committee, which must 
be provided if they exist, whether in draft or final form, within 10 days. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c). 
For the reasons stated above, we find that the Committee did not violate the Open 
Meeting Law. We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be 
resolved. This determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with 
our office or the Committee. Please feel free to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you 
have any questions regarding this letter.
