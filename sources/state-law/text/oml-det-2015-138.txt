RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Ms. Metcalf: 
This office received a complaint from Robert and Anne Krasnecky, dated July 10, 
alleging that the Ware Board of Health (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
3OA, §§ 18-25.1 The complaint alleges that the Board failed to meet to review an April 10 Open 
Meeting Law complaint prior to issuing its response. The present complaint was filed with the 
Board on June 5.2 The Board responded by letter dated June 18. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged. The 
Krasneckys filed an Open Meeting Law complaint with the Board on April 10. The Director of 
Public Health, Judy Metcalf, responded to that complaint by letter dated May 5.3 A public body 
must, within 14 business days of receipt of an Open Meeting Law complaint, meet to review the 
Open Meeting Law complaint, send a copy of the complaint to the Attorney General's Office, 
and notify the Attorney General's Office of any remedial action taken. G.L. c. 30A, § 23(b); 940 
CMR 29.05(5), (6). Here, the Board failed to follow the requirements of both the Open Meeting 
Law and the Attorney General's regulations. We note that the Board has committed to following 
proper procedures, and indeed met during its June 17 meeting to review the present complaint.
