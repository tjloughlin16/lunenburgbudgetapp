 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Hoort: 
This office received a complaint from Judith Ahern on October 15, 2019, alleging that the 
Wellfleet Select Board (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. 
The complaint was originally filed with the Board on August 19, and you responded on behalf of 
the Board by letter dated August 27.1 In her complaint, Ms. Ahern alleges that in response to 
multiple public records requests, the Board should have released executive session meeting 
minutes regarding a property at 3 Kendrick Avenue in the town of Wellfleet.2 For the reasons 
discussed below, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 CMR 
29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law as alleged.  
 On several occasions, the Board met in executive session under  Purpose 6 to consider 
purchasing property at 3 Kendrick Avenue. The complainant contests the ongoing confidentiality 
of these executive session meeting minutes. Under the Open Meeting Law, executive session 
minutes may be withheld from disclosure to the public “as long as publication may defeat the 
lawful purposes of the executive session, but no longer.” G.L. c. 30A, § 22(f). When the purpose 
for a valid executive session has been served, the minutes and any documents or exhibits used at 
the session must be disclosed, unless the attorney-client privilege or an exemption to the Public 
Records Law applies. Id. The burden of justifying continued nondisclosure of executive session

Here, we find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law because, at the time 
of Ms. Ahern’s July 19 public records request and continuing into the spring of 2020, the Board 
was still considering purchasing property at 3 Kendrick Avenue and therefore the lawful purpose 
for secrecy remained. Accordingly, the Board was not required to release the executive session 
minutes at the time of Ms. Ahern’s request. We understand that on May 12, 2020, the Board 
determined that it was no longer considering purchasing property at 3 Kenrick Avenue and 
therefore, the purpose for maintaining the secrecy of these executive session minutes had 
concluded. The Board has since released the minutes to the public.  
 We now consider the complaint addressed by this  determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Board. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any questions.
