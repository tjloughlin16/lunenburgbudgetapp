RE; Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Carter: 
This office received a complaint from Suzanne Corbett, dated January 24, 2014, alleging 
that the Ashfield Select Board (the Board) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§18-
25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on or about December 1, 2013, and the 
Board responded to the original complaint by letter dated January 13, 2014.1 In her complaint, 
Ms. Corbett alleges that the minutes from the Board's meetings held on September 23, 2013; 
September 30, 2013; October 7, 2013; and November 18, 2013 are inaccurate. Additionally, Ms. 
Corbett alleges that the October 7, 2013 meeting minutes failed to list a particular document used 
at that meeting. 
The Open Meeting Law states that "prior to the filing of a complaint with the attorney 
general, the complainant shall file a written complaint with the public body, setting forth the 
circumstances which constitute the alleged violation; provided, however, that such complaint 
shall be filed within 30 days of the date of the alleged violation." G.L. c. 30A, § 23(b). If the 
alleged violation could not reasonably have been discovered at the time it occurred, the 
complaint must be filed with the public body within 30 days of when it reasonably could have 
been discovered. 940 CMR 29.05(3). The September 23, 2013 meeting minutes were approved 
on September 30, 2013; the September 30, 2013 meeting minutes were approved on October 7, 
2013; and the October 7, 2013 meeting minutes were approved on October 21, 2013. Because 
the complaint was filed more than 30 days after each of these sets of minutes was approved, we 
find the allegations concerning these meeting minutes untimely and decline to review them. See 
1-13-14 Declination.

With respect to the Board's November 18, 2013 meeting, at the time this complaint was 
filed those minutes had not been approved.2 Because these minutes remained in draft form at the 
time of the complaint, we find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law by creating 
insufficiently detailed minutes. OML 2014-7. 
We now consider this matter closed. Please contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you 
have any questions regarding this letter.
