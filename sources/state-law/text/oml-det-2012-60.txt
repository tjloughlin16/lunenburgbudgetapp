RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Paicos, 
This office received a complaint from Mr. Paul Mortenson, dated March 13, 2012, 
alleging that the Foxborough Board of Selectmen (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, 
G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. Specifically, the complaint alleges that the Board should have released 
the executive session minutes of meetings held "for the purpose of discussing the 'purchase, 
exchange, lease, or value of real property' in connection with negotiations with the Kraft 
Organization" concerning a wastewater treatment facility. The complaint was filed with the 
Board on or about November 30, 2011. The Board responded by a letter from Town Manager 
Kevin Paicos I  dated April 2, 2012. 
After reviewing the complaint and the Board's response, and speaking with the 
complainant by telephone on July 2, 2012, we have decided to resolve this complaint by 
informal action  in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). In its April 2, 2012 response to the 
complaint, the Board provided copies of all of the executive session minutes concerning the 
Kraft Organization's agreement to build a wastewater treatment facility for Foxborough. Only 
the minutes of the Board's January 11, 2011 executive session remained undisclosed. The Board 
voted to publicly release those minutes at its May 22, 2012 meeting. 
The Open Meeting Law requires that when the purpose for which a valid executive 
session was held has been served, the public body shall disclose the minutes unless the attorney- 
client privilege or one or more of the exemptions under the Public Records Law applies to 
withhold the minutes from disclosure. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(f). Here, the Board took action to 
publicly release the executive session minutes requested by Mr. Mortenson after he filed his

Open Meeting Law complaint. Although the Board initially maintained that the executive 
session purpose had not yet expired, the Board reviewed the minutes after receiving the 
complaint and decided to publicly release the minutes requested by the complainant. We spoke 
with Mr. Mortenson by telephone on July 2, 2012, and he confirmed that the Board released the 
minutes he was seeking, and that he was satisfied with the Board's action to address his 
complaint. Therefore, we find that the Board's action to publicly release the executive session 
minutes was appropriate to resolve the complaint. 
We now consider this matter closed. If you have any questions regarding this 
determination, or believe any of the facts presented to be inaccurate, please do not hesitate to 
contact me.
