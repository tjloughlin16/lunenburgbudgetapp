RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Chairman Rucho: 
This office received a complaint from Mr. John Schlichte, dated June 18, 2018, alleging 
that the West Boylston Public Access TV PEG Board (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on March 21, 
2018, and the Board responded by letter dated April 6, 2018. In his complaint, Mr. Schlichte 
alleges that the Board failed to create accurate minutes of a meeting conducted on March 20, 
2018. 
Following our review, we resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a). Although we find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged in 
the complaint, we find that the Board took appropriate remedial action in response to the 
complaint and we order no further action. 
The facts are not in dispute. The Board met on December 19, 2017. On March 20, 2018, 
the Board approved the minutes of its December 19, 2017. The minutes that were approved 
during this meeting were sparse and omitted several discussion topics. Upon receipt of the 
present complaint, each Board member reviewed a video recording of the meeting at issue and 
agreed that the minutes did not accurately reflect the December meeting. On March 29, 2018, 
the Board approved amended minutes that provide further detail and include the previously-
omitted discussions. We find that the Board's original failure to create accurate minutes of the 
December 12, 2017 meeting violated the Open Meeting Law. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a). However, 
because the Board amended the minutes during the March 29, 2018 meeting, we find that the 
Board took sufficient remedial action in response to the complaint and we order no additional 
relief. See G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a); OML 2015-118 ("The goal of this office in enforcing the Open 
Meeting Law is achieving compliance with the law. Where a body acknowledges it failed to fully 
comply, therefore, and takes steps to insure compliance going forward, we will generally find 
that to be sufficient remedial action in response to a complaint.")
