Dear Attorney Howayeck: 
 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on February 28, 2024, alleging that 
the Fall River City Council Committee on Ordinances and Legislation (the “Committee”) 
violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.  The complaint was originally filed with 
the Committee on or about February 15, 2024, and you responded to the complaint, on behalf of 
the Committee, by letter dated February 28, 2024.  The complaint alleges that the Committee 
failed to properly post notice for a meeting held on February 15, 2024. 
 
Following our review, we find that the Committee did not violate the Open Meeting Law 
in the way alleged.  In reaching this determination, we reviewed the original complaint, the 
Committee’s response to the complaint, the complaint filed with our office requesting further 
review, and the original and revised notices, as well as a video recording, of the Committee 
meeting held on February 15, 2024.

FACTS 
 
On Tuesday, February 6, 2024, at 2:11 P.M., the Committee posted notice of a meeting to 
be held on Tuesday, February 13, 2024, at 5:00 P.M.  Due to inclement weather, the Committee 
cancelled this meeting and updated the notice to indicate that the meeting was cancelled. 
 
On Monday, February 12, 2024, at 2:43 P.M., the Committee rescheduled the cancelled 
meeting by posting notice of a meeting to be held on February 15, 2024, at 5:00 P.M.  The notice 
listed the meeting as occurring on Tuesday, February 15 rather than Thursday, February 15.  The 
notice listed four topics, all four of which were to be discussed by the Committee on February 
13, 2024.  On February 14, 2024, at 12:34 P.M., the Committee revised the notice to change the 
day of the meeting from Tuesday, February 15 to Thursday, February 15.  The Committee did 
not amend the date of the meeting or revise any of the four topics that were listed on the original 
notice.   
 
The February 15, 2024, meeting was held as planned.  During the meeting, the 
Committee approved one set of meeting minutes and tabled discussion of an ordinance requiring 
property owners to keep premises free of litter and weeds.  The Committee then heard from the 
Assistant City Planner regarding the adoption of a new ordinance which would impose fees for 
wine and spirit pouring permits.  The Committee unanimously voted to refer the matter to 
corporation counsel for the establishment of a new ordinance to impose fees for wine and spirit 
pouring permits.   
 
DISCUSSION 
 
The Open Meeting Law was enacted “to eliminate much of the secrecy surrounding 
deliberation and decisions on which public policy is based.”  Ghiglione v. School Committee of 
Southbridge, 376 Mass. 70, 72 (1978).  The Open Meeting Law requires that “a public body shall 
post notice of every meeting at least 48 hours prior to such meeting, excluding Saturdays, 
Sundays and legal holidays” and that every notice include “the date, time and place of such 
meeting and a listing of topics that the chair reasonably anticipates will be discussed at the 
meeting.”  G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b).     
 
Here, the Committee posted notice for its February 15, 2024, meeting 48 hours in 
advance, and included the correct date, time, and place in the meeting notice.  However, the 
notice listed the wrong day of the meeting referring to the meeting as taking place on Tuesday, 
February 15 rather than Thursday, February 15.  The Committee explains that the inconsistency 
with the day of the meeting was a clerical error.  Because the Open Meeting Law requires that a 
notice include the date of a meeting, rather than the day, and the correct date, as well as time and 
place, were included in the meeting notice here, we do not find a violation of the Open Meeting 
Law.  See OML 2013-46 (finding no violation where the notice included the correct meeting date 
but an agenda that was posted with the notice listed the incorrect date of the meeting).2  We 
recognize that it may be unlikely that any member of the public was genuinely confused by the 
error where the Committee meeting that was originally to be held on Tuesday, February 13,
