RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Mead; 
This office received a complaint from Stephen Morgan, dated December 6, 2016, 
alleging that the Ashland Affordable Housing Trust (the "Trust") violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Trust on November 20, 
2016, and the Trust responded by letter dated November 29, 2016. In his complaint, Mr. Morgan 
alleges that the Trust violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to post notice of a meeting.1 
After reviewing the original complaint, the Trust's response, the request for further 
review filed with our office, and the minutes of Trust meetings held on September 20 and 
November 17, 2016, we resolve this matter by informal action in accordance with 940 CMR 
29.07(2)(2). Although we find that the Trust violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged in the 
complaint, we also find that the Trust took appropriate remedial action in response to this 
violation and order no further action. 
At issue is a Trust meeting held on September 20, 2016. Due to an oversight, the Trust 
did not post notice of this meeting. During the meeting, the Trust voted to appropriate funds for 
a project. Following this meeting, the Trust learned that notice of the meeting had not been
