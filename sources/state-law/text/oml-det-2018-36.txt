RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Riley: 
This office received a complaint from Lauren Walsh, dated December 8, 2017, alleging 
that the Seekonk Veterans Memorial Park Committee (the "Committee") violated the Open 
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on 
September 26, 2017, and, after an extension of time was granted, the Committee responded by 
letter dated October 30, 2017. In her complaint, Ms. Walsh alleges that the Committee did not 
create and approve minutes for a number of meetings. 
Following our review, we find that the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law by 
failing to create and approve minutes of certain meetings. In reaching a determination, we 
reviewed the original complaint, the Committee's response, and the request for further review 
filed with our office. We spoke with the complainant by telephone on December 19, 2017. We 
reviewed supplementary e-mails from the Committee's legal counsel dated February 20 and 27 
and March 7, 2018.1 
We find the facts as follows. Relevant to this complaint, the Committee held meetings in 
2016 on January 12; February 16; March 1,15, and 29; May 10; June 28; July 26; August 30; 
September 27; and October 18, The Committee held meetings in 2017 on April 27, July 20, and 
August 10. The Committee posted notice of meetings on the following dates, but no meetings 
were held on those dates: January 26; March 8; April 5, 12, 13, and 26; and May 24, 2016. 
On September 10, 2017, the complainant made a request to the Town of Seekonk's 
records access officer for the minutes of all Committee meetings between January 2016 and the 
date of the request. After relaying this request to the Committee, the records access officer

responded on September 12, 2017, providing the minutes from June 2016 through August 2017, 
but providing the minutes of no meetings prior to June 2016. The complainant reviewed the 
minutes that were provided and identified dates from early in 2016 where meeting notices had 
been posted yet no minutes were provided to her. The present complaint followed. 
The Open Meeting Law requires that a public body "create and maintain accurate 
minutes of all meetings, including executive sessions, setting forth the date, time and place, 
the members present or absent, a summary of the discussions on each subject, a list of 
documents and other exhibits used at the meeting, the decisions made and the actions taken at 
each meeting, including the record of all votes." G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a). Minutes of open session 
meetings must be created in a timely manner. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c). The minutes of an open 
session, if they exist and whether approved or in draft form, must be made available within 10 
days of a request. Id 
Here, the complaint alleges that the Committee failed to create and maintain minutes of 
certain meetings, or, if the minutes do exist, that the Committee failed to provide them to her. 
Indeed, the Committee held meetings on at least six dates during the period of the complainant's 
request for which minutes were not provided to the complainant. After consulting with the 
Committee's legal counsel, it appears that the Committee did not create and approve minutes for 
the meetings held on January 12; February 16; March 1,15, and 29; and May 10, 2016. This 
failure violated the Open Meeting Law. See OML 2015-149.2 Because several scheduled 
meetings were cancelled during the relevant time period, the Committee was not required to 
create minutes on those dates. Id. 
For the reasons stated above, we find that the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law. 
We order the Committee's immediate and future compliance, and we caution that similar future 
violations may be considered evidence of intent to violate the law. Further, we order the 
Committee, within thirty (30) days from receipt of this letter, to create and approve minutes of 
the six meetings addressed in this letter, to the best of its ability. Upon completion, the 
Committee must notify our office of compliance in writing, pursuant to 940 CMR 29.07(4). 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Committee. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any 
questions.
