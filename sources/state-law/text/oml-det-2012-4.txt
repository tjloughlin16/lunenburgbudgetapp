Dear Mr. Filson: 
Our office received your complaint, dated August 8, 2011, alleging violations of the 
Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25, by the Massachusetts Department of Correction ("the 
Department"). The complaint was originally filed with the Department on April 12, 2011. The 
complaint alleges that the Department violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to provide you 
with minutes from meetings of the following Department committees: (1) the Religious 
Committee; (2) the Religious Services Review Committee; and finally (3) the Inmate Property 
Committee. The Department responded to your complaint on July 15, 2011, stating that only 
two such committees exist — the Inmate Property Committee and the Religious Services Review 
Committee — and that neither is subject to the Open Meeting Law. 
We find that the Department did not violate the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18- 
25, because the Department and its committees are exempt from the Law pursuant to the 
Department's enabling legislation. That statute, G.L. c. 30A, § 1A, states that "the department of 
correction shall be subject to sections one through eight, inclusive and shall not otherwise be 
subject to this chapter, notwithstanding the exclusion of said department from the definition of 
the word 'agency' in section one." 
We now consider this matter resolved. Please feel free to contact our office if you have 
any questions regarding the matter.
