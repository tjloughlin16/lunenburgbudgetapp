RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Baldassini; 
This office received a complaint from Michael Alfano, dated December 4, 2013, alleging 
that the Pembroke Zoning Board of Appeals (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. 
c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on or about October 28, 
2013. The complaint specifically alleges that the minutes of the Board's September 23, 2013 
meeting arc inaccurate. The Board failed respond to the complaint in writing. 
Following our review, we have decided to resolve this complaint by informal action, in 
accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the minutes of the Board's September 23, 
2013 meeting were not accurate because the minutes included statements that were incorrectly 
attributed to people who did not attend the meeting. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a). After speaking with 
our office, the Board reviewed the matter and amended the meeting minutes during its January 
27, 2014 meeting. It also amended the record of its proceedings to include an accurate summary 
of the discussion that occurred and to properly attribute statements made during the September 
23, 2013 meeting. We find that this was appropriate remedial action in response to the 
Additionally, we find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to 
respond to Mr. Alfano's complaint. We remind the Board that unless our office grants an 
extension, a public body must respond within 14 business days of receipt of a complaint, send a 
copy of the complaint to our office and notify us of any remedial action taken. See G.L. c. 30A, 
§ 23(b); 940 CMR 29.05(5). We order the Board's immediate and future compliance with the 
Open Meeting Law, and caution that future similar violations may be considered evidence of 
intent to violate the law.
