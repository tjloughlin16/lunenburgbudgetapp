RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Ms. Conner, 
This office received a complaint filed by Bruce Stetson, dated June 17, alleging that the 
Hinsdale Select Board (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.' 
Specifically, the complaint alleges that the Board failed to respond to Mr. Stetson's April 17 
written request for three sets of executive session minutes. The complaint was originally filed 
with the Board on or about May 2, and you responded by letter dated May 21. 
We have decided to resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a). Following our review, we find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law 
by failing to respond to Mr. Stetson's request for executive session minutes within 10 days of the 
request, and by failing to review the requested executive session minutes at the Board's next 
meeting, or within 30 days of the request. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(g)(2); OML 2014-30; OML 2013-
99.2 After this complaint was filed, we contacted the Board and the Board reviewed Mr. 
Stetson's request and voted during its July 14 meeting to release the executive session minutes 
he requested to the public. We find this was appropriate remedial action, and order no additional 
relief. 
We remind the Board that it must respond to a request for executive session minutes 
within 10 days. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(g)(2). If it has not previously reviewed the requested minutes 
to determine whether continued confidentiality is required, it must perform that review and
