RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Bresnahan, 
This office received a complaint filed by Douglas Babineau, dated August 4, alleging that 
the North Middlesex Regional School Committee (the "Committee") violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.1 Specifically, the complaint alleges that the notice for the 
Committee's May 27 meeting failed to list a topic the Committee anticipated discussing with 
sufficient specificity. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on or about June 10, 
and the Committee responded by letter dated June 25. 
We have decided to resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a). Following our review, we find that the Committee did not violate the Open 
Meeting Law. The notice for the Committee's May 27 meeting included a topic under the 
category of "Old Business" that read "High School Bell Schedule - Discussion/Vote." Below 
this header were three paragraphs proposing a schedule for the Committee's consideration. 
During the meeting, the Committee voted to adopt a different version of the proposal contained 
in the meeting notice. 
The notice was sufficiently specific because it clearly identified the topic that the 
Committee anticipated discussing. See G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b); 940 CMR 29.03(l)(b); OML 2012-
34.2 The notice also indicated that the Committee might vote on the topic. The notice need not
