Re: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Elsbach, 
This office received a complaint from David West, dated August 12, 2013, alleging that 
the Sheffield Zoning Board of Appeals (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
30A, §§ 18-25. Specifically, the complaint alleges that you refused to recognize Mr. West and 
allow him to address the Board during its May 15, 2013 meeting. The complaint was originally 
filed with the Board on May 23, 2013. The Board responded to that complaint by letter dated 
June 4, 2013. 
After reviewing the complaint and the Board's response, we have decided to resolve this 
complaint by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the 
Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. As we explained in a prior determination 
resolving a complaint filed by Mr. West against another public body, the Open Meeting Law 
does not require that a public body allow public participation during an open meeting. See OML 
2012 - 54.1 The Law states that, "[n]o person shall address a meeting of a public body without 
permission of the chair, and all persons shall, at the request of the chair, be silent." G.L. c. 30A, 
§ 20(f). Members of the public have the right to attend any open session meeting of a public 
body, however they do not have the right to address the body without the permission of the chair.
