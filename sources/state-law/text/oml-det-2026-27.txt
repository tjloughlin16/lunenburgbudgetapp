 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Chair Erkkila: 
 
This office received a complaint from Dave Holdcraft on September 22, 2025, alleging 
that the Brookfield Planning Board (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, 
§§ 18-25.  The complaint was originally filed with the Board on or about July 2, and you 
responded on behalf of the Board by letter dated July 23.
1  The complaint alleges that the Board 
approved minutes of a meeting held on April 9 that were not sufficiently detailed.   
 
The April 9 meeting minutes were the subject of a prior determination, OML 2025-209.
2  
For the reasons set forth in OML 2025-209, incorporated herein by reference, we find that 
minutes of the April 9 meeting were sufficiently detailed. 
 
The complaint alleges an additional inaccuracy in the April 9 meeting minutes that was 
not addressed in OML 2025-209, to wit, that the minutes inaccurately reflect that a hearing was 
continued at 7:00 p.m. but the meeting was adjourned at 9:16 p.m.  The minutes do not state that 
the hearing was continued at 7:00 p.m.  Rather, they state that the Board “agreed to continue the 
public hearing on May 7, 2025, at 7:00 PM in the banquet room of the Town Hall.”  Although 
the minutes might have been clearer if they stated that the public hearing was continued to May 7 
at 7:00 p.m., the minutes as worded substantially complied with the accuracy requirement.   
 
Finally, we decline to review the complaint’s allegation that the Board failed to vote to 
open or continue the public hearing.  The Open Meeting Law does not govern the procedural 
requirements for conducting a public hearing.  See OML Declination 7-14-23 (Belmont Planning
