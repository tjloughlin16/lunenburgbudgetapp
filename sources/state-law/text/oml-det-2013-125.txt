RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Ms. Sabourin: 
This office received two Open Meeting Law complaints from Terry Kinnas, dated 
February 7, 2013, alleging that the Pittsfield Reid Middle School's School Council Community 
Outreach Subcommittee (the "Subcommittee") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 
18-25. Specifically, the complaints allege that the Subcommittee met on November 21, 2012 
and December 3, 2012 without providing 48 hours' notice. The complaints were filed with the 
Subcommittee on or about December 11, 2012. The Subcommittee did not respond to the 
complaints. 
Following our review, we find that the Subcommittee violated the Open Meeting Law by 
failing to provide 48 hours' notice for its November 21, 2012 and December 3, 2012 meetings, 
and by failing to respond to a properly filed Open Meeting Law complaint. In reaching this 
determination, we reviewed the December 11, 2012 and February 7, 2013 complaints and the 
notices for the Subcommittee's November 21, 2012 and December 3, 2012 meetings. 
The Subcommittee submitted to the Pittsfield City Clerk a notice, dated November 19, 
2012, for a November 21, 2012 meeting. Although the notice was dated November 19, 2012, the 
Clerk stamped the notice as received on November 20, 2012 at 8:40AM. The Subcommittee 
submitted to the Clerk a notice, dated November 29, 2012, for its December 3, 2012 meeting. 
The Clerk stamped that notice as received November 30, 2012 at 10:46 AM. 
The Open Meeting Law requires that "[e]xcept in an emergency, in addition to any notice 
otherwise required by law, a public body shall post notice of every meeting at least 48 hours

prior to such meeting, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays." G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b). 
For meetings of a local public body, notice shall be filed with the municipal clerk and posted in a 
manner conspicuously visible to the public at all hours in or on the municipal building in which 
the clerk's office is located. G.L. c. 30A, § 20(c). The 48 hours is measured from when the 
notice is actually posted, not when it is submitted to the municipal clerk. Here, the 
Subcommittee's notice for its November 21, 2012 meeting was not posted until November 20, 
2012. This posting did not meet the 48 hours requirement. The Subcommittee's notice for its 
Monday, December 3, 2012 meeting was not posted until Friday, November 30, 2012. This 
posting also did not meet the 48 hours requirement, which excludes Saturdays and Sundays. 
G.L. c. 3OA, § 20(b). 
The Subcommittee also violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to respond to Mr. 
Kinnas' two December 11, 2012 complaints. A public body shall, within 14 business days of 
receipt of an Open Meeting Law complaint, review the complaint's allegations; take remedial 
action, if appropriate; and send to the Attorney General a copy of the complaint and a description 
of any remedial action taken. G.L. c. 30A, §23(b); 940 CMR 29.05(5). The public body shall 
simultaneously notify the complainant that it has sent such materials to the Attorney General and 
shall provide the complainant with a copy of the description of any remedial action taken. Id. 
Our office sent the Subcommittee a letter on February 25, 2013 enclosing a copy of the 
complaints and requesting that the Subcommittee respond by March 15, 2013. The 
Subcommittee failed to respond. We remind the Committee that it must respond to Open 
Meeting Law complaints. 
We order the Subcommittee's immediate and future compliance with Open Meeting Law, 
and caution the Subcommittee that similar future violations may be considered evidence of an 
intentional violation of the law. Additionally, are order the Subcommittee members to review 
the Attorney General's Open Meeting Law Guide, available at www.mass.gov/ago/openmeeting. 
and certify in writing to our office within in thirty (30) days that the members have complied. 
We now consider the complaints addressed by this determination to be resolved. Please feel free 
to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions or believe any facts in this 
letter to be inaccurate. 
cc: Terry Kinnas
