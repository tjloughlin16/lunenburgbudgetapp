RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney O'Donnell; 
This office received a complaint from Robert Belbin dated July 24, 2013, alleging that 
the Carver Redevelopment Authority (the "Authority") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
3OA, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on or about June 1, 2013, and 
Chair Will Sinclair responded on July 15, 2013.1 You then responded, on behalf of the Board, to 
the original complaint by letter dated July 24, 2013. In his complaint, Mr. Belbin alleges that the 
Authority engaged in a deliberation outside of a properly posted meeting through email. 
After reviewing the complaint and the Board's response, we have decided to resolve this 
complaint by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.017(2)(a). While it is a close 
question,2 we find that a member of the Authority, Mr. Abatiello, individually violated the Open 
Meeting Law by sending an email on May 29, 2013, sharing his opinion on a matter of public 
business within the Board's jurisdiction with a quorum of the Board. OML 2013-27; OML 
2012-67.3 However, we do not find that the violation was intentional, and we commend Mr. 
Abatiello for acknowledging his mistake during an open meeting following the filing of this 
complaint.4 We order immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and order 
the Authority to disclose the May 29, 2013 email exchange during a properly posted open 
meeting.

Emails sent to a quorum of a public body inviting a response have the potential to result 
in an inadvertent or unintentional reply to all recipients containing a public body member's 
opinion on the matter being discussed. Because of this risk, we continue to recommend that 
public bodies avoid communicating over email at all except for distributing meeting agendas, 
scheduling meetings and distributing documents created by non-members to be discussed at 
meetings, provided that no opinions are expressed. OML 2013-113; OML 2013-29; OML 2013-
4.
