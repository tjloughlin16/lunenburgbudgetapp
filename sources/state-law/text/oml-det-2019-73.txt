RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint
Dear Attorney Macy:
This office received a complaint from Collin Dias on January 24, 2019, alleging that the 
Fall River Zoning Board of Appeals (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, 
§§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on December 17, 2018, and you 
responded to the complaint, on behalf of the Board by letter dated January 23, 2019.1 In his 
complaint, Mr. Dias alleges that prior to the meeting on December 13, 2018, members of the 
Board discussed granting permits to City Councilor Steve Camara with Mayor Correia and 
Councilor Camara.
We resolve this complaint by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), 
after reviewing the original complaint, the Board’s response to the complaint, and the complaint 
filed with our office requesting further review. Additionally, we viewed the video of the Board’s 
meeting on December 13, 2018.
On December 13, 2018, the Board met to discuss nine variances and special permits, 
including a special permit for Mr. Steven A. Camara. The complaint alleges that members of the 
Board communicated with Mr. Camara and Mayor Correia prior to the Board’s meeting 
regarding Mr. Camara’s special permit. Although Board Chairman Assad admits to speaking to 
Mr. Camara “on a separate manner” prior to the December 5, 2018, meeting, the Board denies 
that a quorum of Board members spoke with Mr. Camara at any time outside of a public 
meeting. The complainant does not provide any additional information to support the assertion 
of improper deliberation outside of a noticed meeting, such as when such deliberation allegedly
