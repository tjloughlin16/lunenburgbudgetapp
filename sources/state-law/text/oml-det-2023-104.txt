 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Attorney Ahearn: 
 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on February 2, 2023, alleging that 
the Westwood Select Board (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 
18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on January 19, 2023, and you 
responded to the complaint, on behalf of the Board, by letter dated February 2, 2023. The 
complaint alleges that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to include sufficient 
specificity on the notice for the Board meeting held on December 19, 2022.
 1 
 
We resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), 
after reviewing the original complaint, the Board’s response to the complaint, the complaint filed 
with our office requesting further review, and the notice, minutes, and video recording of the 
Board meeting held on December 19.
2 We find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting 
Law in the way alleged. 
 
The complaint alleges that the notice for the Board’s December 19 meeting lacked 
sufficient specificity because it did not list the businesses being considered for license renewals.  
Here, the notice did not include a topic for license renewals and the Board did not discuss license
