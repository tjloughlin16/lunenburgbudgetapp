Re: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Frank: 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins, dated April 7, alleging that the 
Swansea Board of Selectmen (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-
25.1 The complaint was originally filed with the Board on March 15. The Board responded by 
letter dated April 3. The complaint alleges that the Board held a meeting on March 14 without 
posting notice at least 48 hours prior to the meeting. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law by posting notice 
for its March 14 meeting fewer than 48 hours before the meeting. G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b). The 
Open Meeting Law requires that notice of every meeting of a public body be posted at least 48 
hours prior to the meeting. G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b). Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays are 
excluded by law from the calculation of the 48 hour notice period. Id. Here, the Board posted 
notice for its Saturday, March 14 meeting on Thursday, March 12. Because Saturdays are not 
included in the 48 hours required to notice a meeting, this notice was not timely posted. See id.; 
OML 2014-150.2 
In response to the complaint, the Board acknowledged the violation and explained that it 
was mistaken in its calculation of the 48 hours, believing it could include Saturday. The Board 
writes that it now understands that Saturday is not included in the 48 hours required to notice a 
meeting, and that in the future, any Saturday meetings will be noticed no later than the previous 
Wednesday. We find this to be a satisfactory response to the complaint and order no additional 
remedial action.
