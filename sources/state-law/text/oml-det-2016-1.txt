RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Thomas: 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins, dated December 16, 2015, 
alleging that the Fall River Board of Assessors (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, 
G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. Specifically, the complaint alleges that the Board failed to take formal 
votes on motions made during its September 17, 2015 meeting. The complaint was originally 
filed with the Board on October 2, 2015. The Board responded through counsel by letter dated 
November 17, 2015.1 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. The 
complaint alleges that the Board failed to take formal votes on motions made during its 
September 17, 2015 meeting. However, with the exception of certain actions such as entering 
into executive session, the Open Meeting Law does not require a public body to take votes, only 
that it record in the minutes all votes and actions taken. See G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a); see also OML 
2015-26 (noting that a public body had approved meeting minutes without a formal vote).2 
Because the Law does not require the Board to take such votes, it did not violate the Open 
Meeting Law by failing to do so during its September 17, 2015 meeting. Further, as this decision 
concerns only compliance with the Open Meeting Law, we take no position as to whether any 
other law or the Board's own policies required the Board to act differently than it did on
