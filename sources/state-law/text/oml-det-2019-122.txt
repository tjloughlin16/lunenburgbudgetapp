RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint
Dear Ms. Schreiner-Oldham:
This office received a complaint from Valerie Frias, on July 10, 2019, alleging that the 
Brookline School Committee (the “Committee”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, 
§§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on May 31, 2019, and you 
responded on behalf of the Committee by letter dated June 28, 2019. In her complaint, Ms. Frias 
alleges that a Committee member deliberated via social media about matters that will come 
before the Committee.
We resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), 
and find that the Committee did not violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged. The Open 
Meeting Law does not restrict a public body member’s right to make comments to the general 
public.1 Where, as here, a member of a public body posts on a town-wide social media forum in 
a manner that is directed to the general public and no other member of the public body responds, 
we do not find a violation of the Open Meeting Law. See OML 2017-111 (Brookline Board of 
Selectmen); OML 2018-145 (Provincetown Board of Selectmen); OML 2018-153 (West 
Bridgewater Board of Assessors).2
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Committee. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any 
questions.
