RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Riley: 
This office received a complaint from Ronald Beaty on November 30, alleging that the 
Plymouth Board of Selectmen (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 
18-25.1 Specifically, the complaint alleges that the notices for the Board's September 29, 
October 6, and October 13 meetings failed to include sufficiently detailed executive session 
topics. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on October 15. The Board responded 
by letter dated November 16. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged. The 
notices for the three meetings identified in the complaint each stated that the Board would enter 
into executive session pursuant to G.L. c. 30A, § 21(a)(3), but failed to identify the subject of the 
executive session. See OML 2015-63; OML 2014-145; OML 2013-97.2 In response to the 
complaint, the Board acknowledged this violation, and recognized that it should have included 
the names of the bargaining units that were the subject of its discussions, because doing so would 
not have compromised its bargaining positions. The Board identified the bargaining units 
discussed during the three executive sessions as the Office and Professional Employees 
International Union, International Association of Firefighters, and the Department of Public 
Works. Where, as occurred here, a public body acknowledges its violation in response to a 
complaint and commits to future compliance, we strongly encourage complainants to reconsider 
whether a full investigation by this office remains necessary.

We order the Board's immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and 
caution the Board that a similar future violation may be considered evidence of an intentional 
violation of the law. We now consider this matter closed. Please be advised that this letter does 
not resolve any other complaints that may be pending with this office or with the Board. Please 
feel free to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions regarding this letter.
