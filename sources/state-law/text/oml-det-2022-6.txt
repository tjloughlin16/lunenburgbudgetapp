 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Mr. Kelliher: 
 
This office received a complaint from Walter Craven on October 14, 2021, alleging that 
the Ludlow School Committee (the “Committee”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, 
§§ 18-25.1  The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on August 18, and you 
responded, on behalf of the Committee, by letter dated September 7.  The complaint alleges that 
the Committee failed to properly post notice of the meetings that were held on July 20 and 
August 17.    
 
We resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), 
after reviewing the original complaint, the Committee’s response to the complaint, the complaint 
filed with our office requesting further review, and the notices of the Committee meetings held 
on July 20 and August 17.  In addition, we communicated with the complainant on October 22 
and October 29.  We find that the Committee did not violate the Open Meeting Law in the ways 
alleged. 
 
A public body must post notice of every meeting at least 48 hours in advance, not 
including Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays.  G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b).  The notice must be 
printed in a legible, easily understandable format and must contain the date, time and place of the 
meeting, as well as a listing of topics that the chair reasonably anticipates will be discussed.  
G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b).  The list of topics shall have “sufficient specificity to reasonably advise the 
public of the issues to be discussed at the meeting.”  940 CMR 29.03(l)(b).  We generally

For local public bodies, notice shall be filed with the municipal clerk and posted in a 
manner conspicuously visible to the public at all hours in or on the municipal building in which 
the clerk’s office is located.  G.L. c. 30A, § 20(c).  A municipality may designate a website as an 
alternative notice posting location for all public bodies within the municipality.  940 CMR 
29.03(2)(b).  The Town of Ludlow has not adopted its website as the official method of posting 
notice.  Rather, the official method of posting notice for all Ludlow public bodies, including the 
Committee, is a binder located at the ground level outer entrance of Town Hall.  It is our 
understanding that notices often are also posted to the town website, 
http://ludlow.homeip.net:81/Clerk.htm, to the Committee’s website, 
https://www.ludlowps.org/school-committee/meeting-schedule, and to an indoor bulletin board 
located next to the Town Clerk’s office.  Although public bodies are welcome to post notice of 
their meetings in additional locations if they choose, a municipality has one official notice 
posting location for purposes of complying with the Open Meeting Law.  See 940 CMR 
29.03(2)(b)(3) & (c); OML 2021-94; OML 2020-20.  
 
Here, the Town Clerk posted notices to the binder outside Town Hall on Thursday, July 
15 at 11:20 A.M. for a Committee meeting to be held on Tuesday, July 20 at 6:00 P.M., and on 
Friday, August 13 at 1:40 P.M. for a Committee meeting to be held on Tuesday, August 17 at 
6:00 P.M.  We find that the notices were properly posted in compliance with the Law where the 
Town Clerk posted the notices for the Committee’s July 20 and August 17 meetings more than 
48 hours in advance in the official notice posting location.  There is no requirement that a 
meeting notice also be posted to either the Town or Committee website.   
 
For the reasons stated above, we find that the Committee did not violate the Open 
Meeting Law.  We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved.  
This determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or 
the Committee.  Please feel free to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any 
questions regarding this letter.
