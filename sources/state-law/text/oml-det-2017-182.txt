RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Weilgus: 
This office received a complaint from Ronald Beaty on October 31 alleging that the Cape 
Cod Commission's Executive Committee (the "Committee") violated the Open Meeting Law, 
G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.1 The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on October 12, 
and you responded, on behalf of the Committee, by letter dated October 31. The complaint 
alleges that the notice for the Committee's October 12 meeting was not printed in a legible, 
easily understandable format. 
After reviewing the original complaint, the Committee's response to the complaint, the 
complaint filed with our office requesting further review, the notice of the Committee's October 
12 meeting, and speaking with Mr. Beaty by telephone on November 28, we resolve this 
complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the 
Committee did not violate the Open Meeting Law, 
On October 5, the Commission posted notice for a meeting to be held on October 12. 
The notice was type-written, posted in three locations, and included the date, time and place of 
the meeting, as well as a list of topics. One of the three locations where the notice was posted 
was on the Barnstable County Commissioners' exterior bulletin board located at Barnstable 
Superior Court; this bulletin board is the official method of notice posting for the Commission. 
Handwritten in the upper right-hand corner of the notice was the following notation; "Posted 
10/5 @ 10:15 A.M." The complaint alleges that the handwritten date was not legible.

A public body must post notice of every meeting at least 48 hours in advance, not 
including Saturdays, Sundays, and legal holidays. G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b). The notice must be 
printed in a legible, easily understandable format and must contain the date, time and place of the 
meeting, as well as a listing of topics that the chair reasonably anticipates will be discussed. Id. 
The date and time that the notice is posted must be conspicuously recorded on or with the notice 
itself 940 CMR 29.03(l)(b). We find that the Committee complied with the requirement to 
indicate the date and time of posting. Although the number five contained in the date of "10/5" 
may have been difficult to read, the date was sufficiently legible. Therefore, we find that any 
potential ambiguity over the date did not rise to the level of an Open Meeting Law violation. See 
OML 2017-60.2 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Committee. Please feel free to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions 
regarding this letter.
