Project Description
Districtwide
Safety Window Film Project cost:  $160,000
Project description:
Installation of safety film for 
• inside of all exterior doors that have glass panels in them
• inside of all accessible large windows on the first floor 
Application of the film will:
• deter intrusion from the exterior by creating a barrier that prevents plate glass from immediately 
shattering, allowing immediate access. (Typically it would take an intruder approximately 90 – 
120 seconds to completely “kick in” any piece of glass that is treated with the film, allowing time 
for police response.)
• decrease solar glare into the rooms decreasing the heat load in the building/decreasing cooling 
costs 
• 
The “tint” on the film is barely noticeable and does not impact line of sight from the exterior.

Project Description
Lunenburg Primary School (in conjunction with THES)
Access & Security Upgrades Project cost:  $83,500
Project description:
Installation of: 
• newly created vestibule to enhance security
• upgraded door access controls and alarm keypad (to match LMHS).
• allow access cards to be programmed to arm and disarm the system eliminating the 
issue of having to remember 4 & 6 digit pass codes; one monitoring system 
districtwide
• allow easier management  of access with employment status changes
• additional security cameras at ALL exterior doors and other areas inside the building as well 
as building mounted cameras for the playground area; providing greater security video 
coverage with increased video quality
• additional motion detectors
All three (3) school building will be controlled by one system 
• accessible at each school- eliminating the need for different access cards for key 
districtwide personnel
• providing LPD quick access to any building in emergency situations

Project Description
Lunenburg Middle-High School
Athletic Complex Feasibility Study   Project cost:  $50,000.00
Project description:
Engage a Civil Engineering organization in order to address the following:
• Drainage problems on and around the perimeter of the turf field
• Drainage issues leading to the failure of the small block retaining wall & walkway 
accessibility
• Drainage issues leading to failure of the Bin-Block retaining wall supporting the 
press box
• Possible expansion of the snack shack, restrooms and storage capacity
The feasibility study will:
• determine whether or not the project is viable
• prioritize what site remediations need to be made in order to complete the project
