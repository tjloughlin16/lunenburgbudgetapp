Assessing Department OverviewThe Assessing Department is responsible for accurately determining the value of all Real and Personal Property located within the Town of Lunenburg for the purpose of taxation. Assessors are obliged by the Massachusetts Department of Revenue (DOR) to assess all property at its full and fair market value as of January 1st of the calendar year for the Fiscal Year which runs from July 1st to June 30th of the following year.The updated property assessments are submitted to the DOR each fiscal year for their review and approval before Lunenburg's tax rate can be set by the town’s Select Board.  The Assessors are responsible for the maintenance of all Real and Personal property data records.
Functions of the Assessor’s Office:•Abatements on Real Estate, Personal Property and Motor Vehicle Excise Tax•Chapter Land Classification•Real Estate Tax Exemptions•Real Estate Tax Deferrals•Personal Property•3ABC/Charitable Organizations•Income and Expense Forms•Request for Abutters Lists•Perform Cyclical Inspections

Department OverviewKey ServicesThe Town Clerk's Office serves as the central information point for local residents and citizens. The Town Clerk is responsible for all elections, census, and voter registration, as well as overseeing the polling place and the conduct of all elections and elected-related activity, and records all actions of the Town Meeting. The Town Clerk records and certifies all official actions of the Town, including town meeting legislation and appropriations. The Town Clerk's Office administers the oaths of office and maintains appointments and resignations of all Town Officials.The Town Clerk's Office issues licenses and permits, including marriage licenses, business licenses and renewals, dog licenses, and requests for vital records.
