Department OverviewKey Services•Building Maintenance•Grounds Maintenance •Facilities Capital Planning•Facilities Budgeting•Parks Maintenance•Oversee Recreation Dept.•Oversee The Town Beach.
The Facilities Department is responsible for the upkeep and maintenance of our eleven town buildings (Town Hall, Ritter Memorial, Library, Teen Center, Eagle House/ Adult Activity Center, Public Safety Building, DPW Building, Town Beach House, TC Passios and Brooks House, grounds (town commons), parks (Marshall Park, McNally Field, Fitzgerald Field, Wallis Park, and Veterans Park), and town beach.
===PAGE 3===
Organizational StructureStaffing OverviewThe Facilities and Parks Department is run by the Facilities Director and two dedicated full time staff members, the Facilities Superintendent and a Facilities Technician. Facilities administrative duties are covered by a shared Administrative Assistant with DPW. There is also a part time Recreation Director @ 19hrs per week who oversees recreation programming and the beach staff. The Recreation Director also handles parks billing.
