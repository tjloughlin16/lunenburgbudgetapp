Time Definitions
•
 Dispatch Time 
–
 The time required to 
process an emergency call from receipt 
to notification of units
•
 Turnout Time 
–
 The time from 
notification to wheels turning on 
the 
apparatus
•
 Response Time 
–
 Wheels turning to 
arrival at scene
Source: NFPA 1710
