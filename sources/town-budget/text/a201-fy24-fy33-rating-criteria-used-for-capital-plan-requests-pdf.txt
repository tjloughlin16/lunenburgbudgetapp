CAPITAL PLANNING COMMITTEE RATING CRITERIA 
 MAXIMUM 73 POINTS 
I. EFFECTIVENESS OF GOVERNMENT (Max 38 Pts): 
A. State, Federal Mandate  
Does this request address a current or foreseeable state or federal regulatory or legal 
mandate such as Protection Agency (EPA) directives, the Americans with Disabilities Act or 
other County, State and Federal laws? (Yes=10 Pts, No=0 Pts)  
 
B. Useful Life of Requested Asset (Years)  
Identify the estimated useful life of the facility, vehicle, equipment, etc. (20 Years or more=5 
Pts, 10-19 Years=4 Pts, 6-9 Years=3 Pts, 5 Years or less=2 Pts, N/A=0 Pts)  
 
C. Age of Asset to be Replaced 
Identify whether asset is Over Reasonable Life, At Reasonable Life or Under Reasonable Life 
or N/A. (Over Reasonable Life=8 Pts, At Reasonable Life=5 Pts, Under Reasonable Life=3 Pts, 
N/A=0 Pts)  
 
D. Increases Effectiveness or Efficiency  
Does the request increase effectiveness or efficiencies of the department? (Yes=5 Pts, No=0 
Pts)  
 
E. Reduces Potential Liabilities/Threats to Operations  
Does the request reduce potential liabilities or threats to operations? (Yes=5 Pts, No=0 Pts)  
 
F. Adds or Expands Customer Service  
Does the request add or expand customer service? (Yes=5 Pts, No=0 Pts)  
 
II. EFFECT OF CAPITAL INVESTMENT ON OPERATING COSTS AND REVENUES (Max 20 Pts): 
A. Anticipated Change in Operating Costs  
(Cost Decrease=5 Pts, No Impact=3 Pts, Cost Increase=0 Pts)  
 
B. Anticipated Changes in Revenues  
(Request will increase revenues=5 Pts, Request will have no impact on revenues=3 Pts, 
Request will decrease revenues=0 Pts)  
 
C. Grant Funding  
Is grant funding available for this request? (Yes=10 Pts, No=0 Pts)  
 
III. COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION (Max 10 Pts): 
A. Economic Growth -Community Contribution  
Does the request contribute to economic growth? (Yes=1 Pt, No=0 Pts)

B. Cultural or Recreational Opportunities -Community Contribution  
Does the request contribute to cultural or recreational opportunities? (Yes=1 Pt, No=0 
Pts)  
 
C. Education -Community Contribution 
Does the request contribute to education? (Yes=2 Pts, No=0 Pts)  
 
D. Public Safety-Community Contribution  
Does the request contribute to public safety? (Yes=2 Pts, No=0 Pts)  
 
E. Aesthetics, Historic Preservation-Community Contribution  
Does the request contribute to the aesthetics a town building or property? Does the 
request contribute the historic preservation of the building or property? (Yes=1 Pt, No=0 
Pts)  
 
F. Environmental Sustainability-Community Contribution  
Does the request contribute to environmental sustainability? Does the request help 
create a beautiful and clean community? Does the request specifically promote the 
responsible use of resources? (Yes=1 Pt, No=0 Pts)  
 
G. Public Health-Community Contribution  
Does the request contribute to the health and/or wellbeing of the community? (Yes=2 
Pts, No=0 Pts)  
 
IV. PLANNING (Max 5 Pts): 
Preliminary Planning Requirement    
Is this request for necessary preliminary planning (i.e. landscape design, architectural work, 
engineering, feasibility study) that is required for a future project? Is this request for a long-
term strategic plan? (Yes=5 Pts, No=0 Pts)
