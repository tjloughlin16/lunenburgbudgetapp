What are State Formula Grant Funds?
Formula grants are funding programs that you do not compete for, even though you must submit an application, and meet 
other specified requirements. They ensure that designated recipients will receive funds and are usually administered and 
managed by State Administering Agencies.
• COA Formula Grant funds are designated for Massachusetts cities & towns to be used for programs and services 
through the Councils on Aging for residents age 60+.
• The amount that each municipality receives is driven by the Federal Census every 10 years.
• The dollar amount given is determined by the Governor each year.  It can be increased or decreased year to year 
depending on the needs in the state.
• 2010 Federal Census showed 2103 Lunenburg residents age 60+ in Lunenburg
• 2020 Federal Census numbers have not been officially released
• FY22 dollar amount for Lunenburg was $12 per elder x 2103 = $25,236  (2010 Federal Census)
• FY23 dollar amount for Lunenburg was $12 per elder x 3231 = $38,772  (Umass Donahue Institute)
• If 2020 Federal Census count is released in time for FY24 FG funding, it is predicted to be a lower amount due to the 
fact that not all people return census counts.
