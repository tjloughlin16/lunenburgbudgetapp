Certification for Public LibrariesThe Massachusetts Board of Library Commissioners sets standards for public library service in Massachusetts. Meeting these requirements allows a Library to be certified to receive State Aid to Libraries funding ($34,000+ in FY26). The benefits of certification include participation in the statewide resources sharing networks, which means access to 59 million items across the Commonwealth; certification is part of the eligibility requirements for MBLC grants (~$13,000 grant received in 2023).To be certified the municipality and the library must: •meet its Municipal Appropriation Requirement•meet Minimum Standards of Free Public Library Service•submit annual reports and forms to show compliance

This “level services” Library budget reflects staffing changes that occurred during FY26. Filling the position left by our retired Children’s Librarian of ten years, and two other new staff members significantly decreases salary expense.Library materials costs continues to rise, especially digital resources for which there is increasing demand. Similarly, programming expenses are increasing and the demand for this core service is on the rise.Our Library is uniquely advantaged with its Friends of the LPL. Whereas most towns have single- or double-digit membership, our Friends have more than 300 active members. Their generosity and financial support reflect the value they place in our Library. As the town administration has committed greater investment in the library, they Friends have also committed greater amounts of support (FY26 is a record high).
