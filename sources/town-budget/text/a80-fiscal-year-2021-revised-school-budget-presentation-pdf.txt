===PAGE 4===
COVID-19 creates new challenges: 
Under remote learning ensuring:  
 our students are fed 
 our students can equitably access remote learning: internet access, computers for each student   
 training for teachers in instructional technology 
 quality teaching in a virtual classroom 
 delivery of special education services 
 behavioral health supports for students, families & staff 
Opening schools with physical distancing: 
 Transporting students 
 Serving lunch 
 Classrooms with desks 6 feet apart 
 Passing between classes 
 Recess 
 Masks? Temperature checks for staff and students? 
Lingering impacts: 
 Will staff and students feel that it is safe to return?
