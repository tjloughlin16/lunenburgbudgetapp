ARTICLE 32
NOTICE OF TOWN MEEETING 
This article proposes an amendment to the bylaws to change the 
method by which Town Meeting Warrants are provided to residents.
The Warrant Booklet is currently mailed to every household in Town.  
This mailing has a substantial cost ($15,000 in calendar 2025).  
The amendment would allow notice of Town Meeting, in the form of a letter or 
postcard, to be mailed to each household at a much lower cost.  
The printing of the booklet also causes time constraints for Boards, Committees, 
and staff.  The mailer must be to the Post Office 14 days prior to Town Meeting and 
requires a minimum of 5 business days to print.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE?
NOTICE BY MAIL
A postcard or letter  will be mailed to 
every household in which a registered 
voter resides at least 14 days before 
Town Meeting. 
OPT-IN FOR PRINTED COPY
Residents can choose to receive a printed 
copy of the warrant by adding their name to an 
opt-in list at the Select Board’s office.
FULL WARRANT ACCESS
The full warrant will be available:
• Online at www.lunenburgma.gov
• At Town Hall and other Town buildings
• At current posting locations
• For those who opt-in- by mail
