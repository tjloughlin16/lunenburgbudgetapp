Grants and RevenueOverview of Funding SourcesGift FundDonations made to the COA Gift Fund allow us to acquire new furnishings, program supplies, updated equipment, and host special events. These funds enhance and complement the services and programs already provided by the Council on Aging (COA).Supporters GroupThe Eagle House Supporters generously donate $10,000 annually to the Lunenburg Council on Aging, to be used at the discretion of the Director. This year, the funds will help cover the costs of the Coffee Station. Coffee, cups, sweeteners, stirrers, lids, and cream total approximately $3,500 per year. The remaining funds will be used to provide entertainers for special events and support our monthly Memory Café.State Aid – Formula GrantFormula Grant award amounts are calculated using the payment rate established for Fiscal Year 2025, based on the 2020 Census. The current rate is $15 per person aged 60 and over. According to the Lunenburg census, there are 3,120 seniors in the community. One of the primary ways the Formula Grant is utilized is to help cover the Meal Program’s shortfall.Meal Program Revolving AccountFunds collected from meal fees are used to purchase groceries for the Meal Program.
