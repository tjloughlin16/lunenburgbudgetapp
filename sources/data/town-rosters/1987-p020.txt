   2| @09 During the calendar year 1987, the Fire Department responded to 545 calls for
   3| @04 assistance. This was an increase of 38 calls from the previous year. The calls were
   4| @04 classified as follows: Structural - 50; Motor Vehicle - 15; Motor Vehicle Accident - 8;
   5| @04 Investigations - 26; Trains - 2; Storm Standby - 2; Service - 42; Medical - 3; Hazardous
   6| @04 Materials 31; Smoke Detector Activation - 3; Box Alarms - 15; Mutual Aid - 28; Illegal
   7| @04 Burning - 5; Rubbish - 11; Brush and Grass - 16; Telephonic - 6; Outside Electrical - 10;
   8| @04 Good Intent - 2; Reset Box - 3; Water/Ice Rescue - 3; Ambulance - 264.
   9| @09 The Fire Department placed an order for a new Rescue/Ambulance this year with
  10| @04 delivery expected in February of 1988. This new vehicle will replace our present 11-year
  11| @04 old ambulance. Even with the replacement of the ambulance, we are still behind in
  12| @04 replacing apparatus, as we have trucks that are over 20 years old.
  13| @09 As you can see from the number of calls, we are a very busy department. As the Town
  14| @04 rapidly grows, we can only expect to be even busier. We must make every effort to keep
  15| @04 fire protection ahead of this growth. We have to make sure that we have reliable
  16| @04 apparatus and adequate manpower to protect the Town. Tax dollars that are spent on public
  17| @04 safety are wisely spent. We ask for and appreciate the support that the Townspeople give
  18| @04 to the Fire Department.
  19| @09 The most critical problems that we now face are: 1. Having enough money budgeted
  20| @04 for operating the Fire Department. 2. Replacement of apparatus on a timely basis and the
  21| @04 purchase of a ladder truck. 3. The construction of a fire station that will adequately
  22| @04 house our apparatus and give us the needed room to operate. These are areas that have to
  23| @04 be met if we are going to continue to offer adequate fire protection to a growing
  24| @04 community.
  25| @09 I would like to take this opportunity to thank all Town Officials for their help and
  26| @04 assistance during the past year. Also I want to thank all our firefighters and ambulance
  27| @04 personnel for their dedication and work to make this Department what it is today.
