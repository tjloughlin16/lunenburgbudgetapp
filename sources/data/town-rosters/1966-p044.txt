   4| @06 As technological conditions change, civil defense functions and procedures change. Civil defense is planning for

   8| @06 government, local, state and national.

  10| @06 of these devices is very great. We must learn to understand and live with them and accept as fact that there can

  17| @06 active fallout if they are not protected. Studies made by the Department of Defense both for military and civilian

  19| @11 Materials giving complete details about radioactivity are available from your local Civil Defense Department.
