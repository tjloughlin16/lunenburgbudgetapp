   3| @08 well as fires. This trend coupled with the planned development in the community over the next several years' highlights the need

   5| @08 increased funding for vehicle maintenance and supplies. The age of the fleet and the increase in responses makes it critical that

   7| @08 also experience an increase in the demand for inspections and fire prevention services. Inspections conducted by the

   9| @08 other emergency and non-emergency functions.
  10| @08 The Town's first ladder truck was placed into service on February 2, 2006. This vehicle is a refurbished 1988 E-One truck with a
  11| @08 110-foot ladder. The ladder is also equipped with more than 200 feet of ground ladders and special tools and equipment. The
  12| @08 ladder provides the department with a long needed tool that will make operations both safer and more efficient. Firefighters
  13| @08 spent more than 1000 man-hours of time training to use this new and complex truck. This training, coordinated by Captain
  14| @08 Patrick Sullivan, covered driving, ladder operation and aerial ladder tactics. This training was in addition to the regular training
  15| @08 that firefighters and EMT's must complete annually.
  16| @08 I would like to thank all of the departments who have worked together to make Lunenburg a safer place to live and work. The

  18| @08 should be proud of.
