   1| @12 A program for upgrading our street lighting, adopted in 1965, and carried out in conjunction with the Police
   2| @08 Department, will be completed in 1970. All incandescent lighting will have been replaced with the more modern
   3| @08 mercury vapor light. This new lighting has helped reduce accidents in some accident-prone areas, and has served
   4| @08 as a deterent to crime in others. The need for a similar program with regard to the erection and maintenance of
   5| @08 traffic signs and devices, adhering to the guidelines provided by the Massachusetts Department of Public Works, is
   6| @08 recognized by the Chief, and it is recommended that a long-range program in this area be adopted. In conjunction
   7| @08 with this program, it is again recommended that a traffic consultant and engineer be engaged to assist in the
   8| @08 correction of the traffic hazards and congestion in Lunenburg Center.
   9| @13 Again, it is recommended that twenty-four hour coverage be instituted to provide better police protection.
  10| @08 With the present population and the ever-increasing demands for police services, it is not possible in the opinion of
  11| @08 the Chief, to extend this coverage without the addition of more full-time Funds necessary to cover this expansion
  12| @08 would be more than justified, and it is hoped that this can be accomplished in the very near future.
  13| @08 Fire Department
  14| @11 "To The Rescue"
  15| @13 During 1969 the Fire Department answered 215 calls as follows: Rescue Calls 56; Service Calls 34; Brush Fires 36;
  16| @08 Car Fires 27; Town Dump 17; Building 15; Accidents 10; Oil Burners 6; Mutual Aid 4; Chimney 2; Washing Machines 2;
  17| @09 Electrical 2; False Alarms 2; Cooking Fires 1; Junk Yard 1. Of these calls, 102 were alarms. A total of 1440
  18| @08 burning permits were issued. Our brush fires were kept to a minimum this year with the cooperation of most residents
  19| @08 who obtained burning permits and burned only when conditions were favorable. The average rainfall was also a factor.
  20| @13 Our Rescue Unit was put to service on January 1, 1969, responded 56 times and transported 67 patients. There
  21| @08 were almost twice as many runs as estimated. Both Squad A and Squad B are doing an outstanding job. All members
  22| @08 hold an American Red Cross Advanced First Aid Certificate and attend an annual refresher course. Two members
  23| @08 attended a four-day course on transportation of sick and injured held in Boston in 1968, two more members attended
  24| @09 in 1969 and two more will attend in 1970. This course is put on by the American Academy of Orthopedic Surgeons.
  25| @09 Twelve members attended the Meadowood Fire School in New Hampshire this Fall. Courses taken were Ventilation,
  26| @09 Breathing Apparatus, L. P. Gas Firefighting, Pump Operation and Structural Fires. Two members also attended
  27| @09 four-day course in Topsfield, Massachusetts, sponsored by Civil Defense.
  28| @13 It is hoped that an addition to the Fire Station will be forthcoming in 1971. We are in need of space for storage,
  29| @09 facilities for the care of hose, a wash-bay, compressor, generator, and floor space for future equipment. A request
  30| @09 has been made to the Board of Selectmen for some full-time help to man our communication center. We are still
  31| @09 filling the job, around the clock, 365 days a year, with part-time help.
