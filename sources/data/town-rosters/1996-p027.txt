   1| @38 FIRE DEPARTMENT
   2| @19 The Lunenburg Fire Department experienced a very busy year for 1996. The increased
   3| @19 in our run total are indicative of a growing community, with a population that is requiring
   4| @19 more and more of our services. During calendar year 1996, we responded to 400
   5| @19 rescue\ambulance calls. This is an increase of 22 calls from the previous year. We
   6| @19 responded to 413 fire related incidents. These increased by 90 calls from the year
   7| @19 before. In addition to the fire and rescue\ambulance calls, our personnel conducted 350
   8| @06 inspections mandated by various Fire Prevention and Building Codes.
   9| @06 The makeup of our fire department consists of 3 full time firefighters, including the Chief. The full time
  10| @06 personnel cover calls during the day, along with any of our paid\call firefighters that are available. At
  11| @06 night and on weekends our call force handles calls. Whenever needed, we can call on our neighbors
  12| @06 through the Mid State Mutual Aid System to assist us when our resources are used up. The present
  13| @06 system is providing excellent fire & EMS service at this time, although we do have occasional times when
  14| @06 we are shorthanded. As the community grows and more and more demands are placed on our
  15| @06 personnel, we may be forced to hire more full time people, but I don't' see that happening in the very
  16| @06 near future.
  17| @06 We are still in desperate need of more adequate facilities to carry out our mission. Not only do we need
  18| @06 more space for storage of apparatus, but we must address space needs for living quarters,
  19| @06 decontamination rooms, material storage, training areas, maintenance areas, office space, parking
  20| @06 areas, apparatus cleaning and apparatus maintenance areas, etc. The building that we operate from
  21| @06 now is 20 to 90 years old, depending on which addition you refer to and the site that it sits on is totally
  22| @06 inadequate with regards to parking and safety. We need an adequate facility that is not only functional
  23| @06 but is also safe for our people to work out of and is also safe for the people that have business with us.
  24| @06 This year, we were very excited to see the implementation of E-911 in our community. Our dispatchers
  25| @06 are fully trained and certified E-911 telecommunicators. This service to the townspeople is the very best
  26| @06 means of identifying and pinpointing where and when the 911 calls are coming from, and enables our
  27| @06 dispatchers to get the proper emergency response out to you as quickly as possible. We have been very
  28| @06 busy educating the school children, senior citizens, business groups and citizens in our community on
  29| @06 how E-911 works. If we can answer any questions you may have relative to the operation of E-911,
  30| @06 please call us on our general business line 582-4155. A special thanks to all our emergency service
  31| @06 dispatchers for their dedication and training and also for the services they provide to us on a daily basis.
  32| @06 Your firefighters and EMT's are totally committed and dedicated to providing you with the best service.
  33| @06 They expend a tremendous amount of time and effort to train and keep their skills at the highest possible
  34| @06 level so that they will be ready to answer your call for help when you need it. I am very proud to be Chief
  35| @06 of such a dedicated group of people. I thank them for their service.
