   2| @08 The year 2005 will be remembered as when we moved into Lunenburg's new Public Safety Complex. We are immensely thankful
   3| @08 to the townspeople for this facility. In addition, thanks go out to my fellow Building Committee members who proved to be an
   4| @08 excellent team under the direction of Police Chief Dan Bourgeois as chairman and members Aime DeNault, David Pajari, Patrick
   5| @08 Slattery, Marion Benson, Selectmen Joe Bilotta, and Acting CAFO Laura Williams. I would like to recognize Bill Gustus, past
   6| @08 CAFO, who was responsible for getting the project started.
   7| @08 We have adapted well to our new surroundings, though we are still living out of boxes and will be for a few more months. We
   8| @08 reap daily the benefits of thoughtful design, efficient use of space and state-of-the-art equipment that places firefighter safety
   9| @08 first. Our dedicated firefighter/EMT's are very appreciative of being able to clean and restock the ambulances and fire apparatus
  10| @08 inside during this cold and snowy winter. There are many features of the new facility that allow the department to better perform
  11| @08 the work of fire protection, safety and suppression, hence ultimately providing better service to the town. If you were not able to
  12| @08 make it to the open house and would like a tour please give us a call and we will schedule a time for you to see the facility.
  13| @08 This year we have not seen a significant increase in incident responses. We are still watchful of the daytime responses when
  14| @08 most of our call personnel are out of town at their full time jobs. We have recently recruited some new members and hope to
  15| @08 have them trained and available as call Firefighter/EMT's by the time you read this report. Our commitment to training is still
  16| @08 unwavering, so that we are prepared for any situation that arises in order to protect both the rescuer and the citizens alike.
  17| @08 I would like to thank all the departments who together have worked to make Lunenburg a safe community. I especially want to
  18| @08 thank the officers and members of this department for their dedication and commitment to the citizens of the Town of
  19| @08 Lunenburg. I would like to recognize our Assistant Chief, Kenneth Paton, for his service to our country in Iraq serving with the
  20| @08 Army Corps of Engineers as we eagerly await his return home to his family, and his fire department family.
  21| @08 The Fire Department responded to 1100 calls for assistance and performed 358 inspections. The following is a summary of
