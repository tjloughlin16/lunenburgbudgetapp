  30| @11 In 1988 this Department spent much of its time complying with the federally-mandated
  31| @06 "Superfund Amendment and Reauthorization Act (SARA) According to this Act, each city
  32| @06 and town in the Commonwealth was charged with planning for the identification of, and
  33| @06 response to, any hazardous materials incident in its jurisdiction. This included
  34| @06 identifying known sources of potentially hazardous materials, transportation routes,
  35| @06 planning for emergency response, evacuation, shelter identification, and control and
  36| @06 clean-up. Our plan was submitted to the State Civil Defense Office and subsequently
  37| @06 approved. It has now become part of our Comprehensive Emergency Management Plan.
  38| @11 This year also saw the completion of a plan to procure a supply of cots and blankets
  39| @06 to be used should sheltering of citizens be necessary during any disasters. Current plans
  40| @06 to upgrade the Emergency Operations Center in the High School are proceeding. Fire Chief
  41| @06 Carrier has installed the Town's back-up radio transmitter which replaced the older "parts
  42| @06 not available" radio that had been our emergency transmitter.
  43| @12 It is also our intention to be able to use the Emergency Operations Center as a
  44| @06 dispatch center should the dispatch area at the Police/Fire Station become inoperable.
  45| @06 This will entail the installation of extension phone lines from the current dispatch
  46| @06 center and the installation of additional radio and telephone equipment.
  47| @11 The improvements made in our Comprehensive Emergency Management Plan and subsequent
  48| @06 improvements in our communications system will allow Lunenburg to be better prepared to
  49| @06 assist those in need during natural and man-made disasters.
