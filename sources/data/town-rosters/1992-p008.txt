  18| @09 The Fire Department responded to the following calls in 1992: Service - 12; Mutual Aid - 21; Hazardous
  19| @09 Materials - 23; Structure - 35; Motor Vehicle - 16; Alarm Activation - 63; Motor Vehicle Accidents - 13;
  20| @09 Investigations - 15; Rubbish - 2; Brush/Grass - 18; Medical - 2; Ice Rescue - 3; Outside Electrical - 7;
  21| @09 Good Intent - 2; Illegal Burning - 2; Miscellaneous - 4; Rescue/Ambulance - 317.
  22| @09 The Fire Department is quickly getting to the point where we are not going to be able to continue to meet
  23| @09 the needs of the public if we don't invest some money in capital items. These items include a larger and
  24| @09 more modern fire station to adequately house our present and future apparatus and give our workers a
  25| @09 safer environment to work in. The conditions at our station now are totally inadequate in every aspect.
  26| @08 The station is also unsafe to work in due to a lack of ventilation of diesel fumes. This affects the
  27| @08 health of firefighters police officers, dispatchers and office personnel. The lack of an adequate sewage
  28| @08 disposal system is another concern, as we can't decontaminate protective clothing and personnel the way
  29| @08 we should. Even if we had the septic facilities, we don't have the room for decontamination areas and
  30| @08 washers and dryers. These are problems that didn't exist when the station was originally built, but they
  31| @08 must be addressed as soon as possible.
  32| @08 Another major item we must address is the purchase of a ladder truck. New construction and the size of
  33| @08 buildings has made it almost foolhardy to place a firefighter on a roof without a ladder or platform to
  34| @08 stand on. The cost of a ladder truck or platform cannot be compared to the loss of one life. Both the
  35| @08 station replacement and the ladder truck needs have been outlined in my annual reports every year since
  36| @08 1985. I think we have to seriously take a look at these two items this year.
  37| @08 The recent fire at MacMillans cost us our 1961 reserve pumper. Major damage was done to the engine and
  38| @08 the cost to repair or replace the engine is more than the truck is worth. This is another problem that
  39| @08 needs to be addressed.
  40| @07 Our budget is extremely small to run a 48 man, 24 hours per day department on. It has been mostly level
  41| @08 funded for the last 3-4 years and before that, we saw only minimal increases. These actions have
  42| @07 seriously eroded our capabilities to supply you with adequate services. It's only because the people in
  43| @07 this department are truly dedicated to your protection and -well-being that we have been able to function
  44| @07 at the level we have been. We cannot expect the present circumstances to remain the same and still
  45| @07 expect to receive the same service. Something has to give eventually, or someone is going to end up
  46| @07 getting hurt. There are also numerous other problems that we are starting to see develop, due to lack of
  47| @07 adequate funding of the budget.
  48| @07 I would like to thank the officers and men and women of my department for their dedication to their
  49| @07 duties as firefighters and EMT's. Their efforts make Lunenburg a safer place to live.
