   2| @06 During calendar year 1989, the Fire Department responded to 575 incidents. The calls
   3| @06 are classified as follows: Helicopter Landing - 1; Sprinkler Alarms - 6; Structure 50;
   4| @06 Motor Vehicle - 14; Motor Vehicle Accidents - 12; Investigations - 34; Explosions - 1;
   5| @06 Service - 18; Medical - 6; Hazardous Materials - 11; Box Alarms - 17; Mutual Aid -
   6| @06 18; Illegal Burning - 13; Rubbish - 3; Brush and Grass - 25; Telephonic - 10; Outside
   7| @06 Electrical - 21; Good Intent - 2; Water/Ice Rescue - 8; Ambulance - 305.
   8| @06 The Fire Department continues it's struggle in trying to find enough resources and
   9| @06 money to operate and provide the basic fire protection and medical assistance that our
  10| @06 Townspeople demand of us. It is getting very difficult to survive in this climate of
  11| @06 economic uncertainty. Fire protection and ambulance service are two basic services
  12| @06 that the public demands. Unfortunately, they cost money. When the Town turns a
  13| @06 deaf ear to our requests for budgetary money or apparatus replacement, we all suffer.
  14| @06 We protect your lives and property, but we can't do it without the proper apparatus or
  15| @06 a realistic budget to adequately fund our operation.
  16| @06 For four years I have stressed the need to replace Engine 1. I will ask again in
  17| @06 1990, as this truck has some very serious problems. We have had to downgrade the
  18| @06 duties of this truck and are not using it to its full capacity. In addition, we have
  19| @06 two other trucks that have major problems that have to be addressed either through
  20| @06 major repairs or replacements. Our space needs and the need for a ladder truck are
  21| @06 still severely hampering our operation, as I have stated for the past two years. We
  22| @06 can only put these problems off for SO long before something drastic happens. We
  23| @06 always seem to wait for something catastrophic to happen before we realize that we
  24| @06 should have taken care of the problem beforehand.
  25| @06 The officers and members of the Fire Department ask for the support of our Town
  26| @06 officials and the public in our endeavor to provide this community with adequate fire
  27| @06 and medical services. I wish to express my sincere thanks to our firefighters and
  28| @06 EMTs for their dedicated service throughout the year.
