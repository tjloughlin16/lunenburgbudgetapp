  33| OFFICE OF INSTRUCTION
  34| Overview
  35| The Lunenburg Public Schools, with its history of educational excellence, continues to develop
  36| an instructional program to prepare students for the 21st century. In order to achieve our
  37| Mission, a comprehensive, integrated set of local initiatives continues to be implemented.
  38| Based upon a philosophy of continuous improvement, our goal is to strengthen our instructional
  39| program in order to increase student learning outcomes.
  40| The Office of Instruction, working in conjunction with the school faculties, administrators,
  41| School Committee, and community members, provides support and guidance in a variety of
  42| areas to achieve this goal. Activities are focused on curriculum development (standards),
  43| assessment and evaluation (measures), and school improvement. Embedded within this overall
  44| framework are additional activities designed to (a) support parent and community involvement,
  45| (b) address the individual needs of students, (c) support a positive school climate, (d) provide
  46| staff development, and (e) guide the allocation of the districts' fiscal and other resources.
