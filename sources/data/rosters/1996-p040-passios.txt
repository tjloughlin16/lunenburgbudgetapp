   1| The Adopt a Child Program continues to reinforce to our children that there are children less fortunate in
   2| the world. We support Cody, a needy child from North Carolina, through a monthly financial
   3| commitment, personal letters, and small gifts on special occasions. We are hopeful that through this
   4| program our children will develop into adults with an understanding and concern for the world.
   5| New to the Passios Elementary School this year is the Apple Valley Alliance. This professional
   6| development organization was formed by the Department of Education to support teachers in their
   7| pursuit of professional development. Our opening kick-off event was highlighted by Dr. Robert
   8| Antonucci, State Commissioner of Education, who spoke about the future of education in Massachusetts.
   9| Last, but not least, there are several new practices that have been initiated this year to stimulate interest
  10| and involvement among children at the Elementary School. All children come together in a group
  11| meeting at the beginning and end of each week. Such items as acknowledgment for behaviors/projects
  12| well done, both on a class and individual basis, and explanations and reminders of upcoming events are
  13| covered in the weekly group meetings.
  14| "Lunch with the Principal" was initiated this fall. Beginning with the third grade children, Mrs.
  15| Daukantas has scheduled a different class each Friday for lunch. Mrs. Daukantas brings dessert (Much
  16| to the children's amazement, she can cook!).
