  22|                                                                      LUNENBURG HIGH SCHOOL
  23|             Lunenburg High School and its faculty continue to focus on rigorous curriculum, high standards and opportunities for
  24|             students, both academic and extracurricular, so that every student feels supported and included in our community. LHS
  25|             faculty is student centered in their teaching practices, and strive to learn and to change in order to best serve all of our
  26|             2024 School Year
  27|             Lunenburg High School returned this August with a renewed philosophy of support for our school community. We have
  28|             assembled an outstanding, talented faculty with a shared vison of supporting students academically, socially and emo-
  29|             tionally as they tackle a rigorous and challenging curriculum. With our renewed sense of support, students are able to
  30|             acquire the skills necessary to earn a high school diploma as well as be successful in post-secondary education as well as
  31|             in the work force. One pillar of support that has proven to be successful for students is the LHS Intervention Teacher.
  32|             This teaching position provides direct instruction to students who were identified by their teachers as to needing addi-
  33|             tional support to be successful in the classroom. Students report to the Interventionist during their study skills block and
  34|             receive assistance to the specific class or classes they are struggling in. The data show the students participating in the
  35|            intervention classroom show substantial improvement in the completion of classwork and in their overall grades.
