  36| ROBERT A. JARVIS
  37| Lunenburg High School continues to provide a first-rate, quality education
  38| for all its students. The Lunenburg School Committee has made a commitment to
  39| offer a comprehensive educational program in order to meet the needs of all of
  40| our students. The comprehensive educational program at Lunenburg High School
  41| includes: Advanced Placement courses for the academically gifted; college
  42| preparatory courses for students who has post-secondary educational plans; art
  43| and music enrichment courses for all students; Industrial Technology courses
  44| in graphic arts, automotive, materials processing, woodworking, photography
  45| and mechanical drawing; and a Business Education curriculum for students
  46| interested in secretarial/clerical and/or college business programs.
