  13|             Since May of 2020, when the United States Department of Agriculture's (USDA) Food and Nutrition Service
  14|             (FNS) approved a request by the Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE) to allow school
  15|             food authorities flexibility while continuing to feed children for free. Therefore, we can ensure all students
  16|             have access to the nutrition they need during the school day. Our program like many others in the United
  17|             States continues to struggle with the supply chain breakdown. Our orders are late or shorted sometimes while
  18|             the numbers for breakfast and lunch are growing.
  19|             Lunenburg School Lunch Program is participating in Harvest of the Month. Massachusetts Harvest of the
  20|             Month program promotes a different Massachusetts-produced food each month in cafeterias and meal sites
  21|             across the state. The goal is to encourage healthy food choices by increasing students' exposure to seasonal
  22|             foods while supporting local farmers. Harvest of the Month is a great opportunity to celebrate local foods,
  23|             experiment with new recipes and lead the way in making farm-fresh foods available to students and staff.
  24|             It also provides wonderful opportunities for school food and nutrition staff, educators, school administrators,
  25|             family members and students to collaborate and celebrate a commitment to serving locally grown foods within
  26|             the community.
