  23|            students' social and emotional growth. These programs ensure that each child becomes a self-motivated,
  24|            creative, lifelong learner in a safe, stimulating environment that encourages diversity and open honest
  25|            communication. The dedicated and accomplished staff of professionals within the school embraces the
  26|            philosophy of educating the whole child academically, socially, and emotionally. This philosophy is a means by

  29|             Lunenburg Middle School is a co-located middle - high school serving grades six through eight. Our effective,
  30|            student centered middle school embraces interdisciplinary teams of teachers that enable teachers to work with
  31|            smaller groups of students. Smaller groups of students allow teachers to learn and engage students in their
  32|            preferred learning style, making them more confident and successful.
  33|            Our effective middle school model is student centered and provides opportunities to explore a variety of
  34|            different interests and talents through special areas and enrichment classes and extracurricular activities. One of
  35|            our goals is for students to discover something they are passionate about and want to continue exploring as they
  36|            move through high school and beyond. We will continue to seek out opportunities for students that foster
  37|            critical thinking and problem-solving skills that prepare them for high school and beyond or where their passion
  38|            may lead them to.
