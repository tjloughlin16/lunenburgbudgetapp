   1| THOMAS C. PASSIOS ELEMENTARY SCHOOL
   2| Christine L. Bonci, Principal
   3| The 2006 School year began with the reconfiguration of the Thomas C. Passios Elementary to Grades 3, 4 and 5 under the
   4| leadership of newly hired Principal Mrs. Christine L. Boncl. Nearly 375 students and 50 staff members embarked on a journey
   5| that would forever be remembered in the history books. The school mascot, the Penguin, was selected in a school-wide
   6| election. The Penguin symbolizes the Power of Team where members of the school community support and rely on each other.
   7| It is our vision that we all work together to achieve the goals of the school as well as the district.
   8| This year continues to be challenging and exciting for students, faculty and parents. We have been able to keep class size in
   9| grades three and four to 23 or fewer; however, we had an unexpected increase in registrations during the month of August in
  10| Grade five bringing our student/teacher ratio to 25:1 or 26:1 in some classes. We constantly strive to find better ways to meet
  11| the collective, as well as the individual needs of our students. Several teachers participated in staff development programs
  12| throughout the summer and well into the school year. Topics include Results, Co-teaching in Instructional Teams,
  13| Understanding by Design, Literacy (Six-Traits in particular), and the Academic/Social/Emotional Curriculum. Components of all
  14| these programs have been or will be implemented.
  15| For the first time in the history of the school our students can participate in Technology classes. We have set up a 24-station lab
  16| with keyboarding, internet research and project-based learning as the focus of instruction. As we further develop this area, we
  17| will see more of an integration of technology with other core academic areas.
  18| Learning has gone off-campus for our students this year. The opening of the new Lunenburg Public Library has allowed us to
  19| bring our students to the library for projects, research, borrow a wide variety of materials and storytelling. We are thrilled to take
  20| advantage of this opportunity.
  21| Thomas C. Passios has launched its first website as a link to the Town of Lunenburg site. Members of the community can easily
  22| access the site to keep abreast of school happenings. There is a Principal's Page, News and Announcements, Calendar, Lunch
  23| Menus, Teacher pages and a whole lot more.
  24| There are many "firsts" here at the elementary school. The first ever all school Turkey Trot and Lip Sync Concert, Grades 4 and
  25| 5 Holiday Concert, Spelling Bee, and Spring Concert. We hope to plan more of these events as we refine the vision of the
  26| school.
  27| Our School Improvement Plan for this year is focused on creating the vision and guiding beliefs for the elementary school. We
  28| are also focused on raising student achievement by reaching "all" students, expanding Technology and defining the culture and
  29| climate of our school. All students will be tested in both MCAS English Language Arts and Mathematics this year and Grade 5
  30| will participate in the History and Science, Technology and Engineering assessments.
  31| Our school council meets regularly to discuss issues and develop the annual School Improvement Plan. Meeting dates are in
  32| our weekly News and Announcement letter that Is sent home each Thursday and posted to our website. Parents and community
  33| members are welcome to attend.
