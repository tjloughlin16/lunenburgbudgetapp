   1| Mrs. Marjorie Karabatsos came back to the Turkey Hill Middle School last year
   2| with our new fifth grade student population. She served as a fifth grade teacher
   3| here in 1971 through 1978. The remainder of her seventeen years were spent in the
   4| classrooms of the T. C. Passios Elementary School. Mrs. Karabatsos was a driving
   5| force behind the Lunenburg Public Schools Art Festival and her love of Shakes-
   6| pearian productions led many of her students into the performance of his works on
   7| stage.
   8| These four individuals and their collective one hundred plus years of experi-
   9| ence and dedication to the students of Lunenburg will surely be missed. We wish
  10| them best wishes for a long, fulfilling and happy retired life.
