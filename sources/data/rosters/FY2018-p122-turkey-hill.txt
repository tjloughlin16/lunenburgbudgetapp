  11|                                                                 Turkey Hill Elementary School
  12|                       Turkey Hill Elementary is now in its third year as a 3-5 building. There are currently 372 students,
  13|                       with 121 third graders, 115 fourth graders, and 136 fifth graders. We employ 24.5 teachers, 100%
  14|                       of whom are licensed in their assignment area. Our student/teacher ratio is 15.2 to 1, including the
  15|                       special area and special education teachers, not as an average homeroom size. Our MCAS data
  16|                       from the spring of 2018 indicates we are meeting or exceeding expectations above state averages
  17|                       in the following areas: Grade 3 Reading at 59%, Grade 4 Math at 51%, Grade 5 Math at 47% and
  18|                       Grade 5 Science at 50%. There are teaching teams of 2 at each grade level as well as self-contained
  19|                       classrooms in grades 3 and 4. Special area classes include art, music, PE, library, technology,
  20|                       computer skills, and for 5'h graders, developmental guidance.
  21|                       Teachers are engaging in a variety of professional development trainings. To address physical and
  22|                       mental health concerns there's been training in Trauma, Mindfulness, Epilepsy, and Executive
  23|                       Functioning. To improve literacy, teachers have taken classes in Calkins Units of Study, Reader's
  24|                       Workshop, and Project Read. Math teachers have participated in several hours of preparation in
  25|                       the Everyday Math platform as well as IXL math. A full day of professional development in
  26|                       November was dedicated to computer literacy and software offerings.
  27|                       We offered many after school activities for students in the winter and spring last year. Over 100
  28|                       students signed up for cooking, student council, crafts, paper making, board games, kickball and
  29|                       garden art. We had the Girls on the Run program both in the spring and fall with between 15-25
  30|                       participants and a chess program taught by a chess master which includes about a dozen students.
  31|                       $6100 was raised during our annual Penny Wars event to support funding for a variety of field
  32|                       trips and in-house performances. 5' graders spent a week in Beckett, MA enjoying the Nature's
  33|                       Classroom program. The Bridges program introduced students to several senior citizen volunteers
  34|                       who came in to share their life experiences and learn about the younger generation. The six week
  35|                       program culminated in a walking field trip to the Eagle House to celebrate the bonds that had been
  36|                       created and allow students to tour the senior center. In June they attended a Worcester
  37|                       Braveheart's baseball game at Hanover Park and took in the activities for Baseball in Education
  38|                       Day. In the fall, grade 5 teachers instituted regular walking field trips to the public library every
  39|                       month so students could become familiar with one of Lunenburg's best public resources.
