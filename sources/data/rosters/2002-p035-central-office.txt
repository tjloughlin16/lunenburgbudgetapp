  14| SUPERINTENDENT'S MESSAGE
  15| Loxi Jo Calmes, Acting Superintendent
  16| By action of the School Committee on November 6, 2002 the Lunenburg Public Schools adopted a five-year strategic plan that
  17| will guide the operation of the schools. While always important, in times of fiscal emergencies like the one the Commonwealth
  18| and by extension the Town and the District are experiencing, the strategic plan provides a validated format for decision making
  19| and resource allocation. The group that developed the plan represented a cross section of the community, which Included
  20| students, parents, faculty, administrators, a member of the school committee and community leaders. Serving on this
  21| committee were: Christie Letarte, Keith Hochstein, Susan Cavaioli, Carol Higgins, Anna Keegan, Mary Ann Weisman, Chad
  22| Adams, Milree Keeling, Elaine Murphy, Roger St. Jean, Cynthia Daukantas, Colleen Stillman, Michael Barney and Loxl Jo Calmes.
  23| Our Vision: The Lunenburg Public Schools prides itself on excellence. We provide a child-centered environment that challenges
  24| all learners to achieve their highest potential. This is accomplished through a partnership with a supportive community.
  25| Our Mission: We prepare our students for life-long learning and responsible community membership.
  26| Our Guiding Beliefs-We Belleve:
  27| Education is a community partnership.
  28| Learning is reflective, creative, ongoing and takes place in all environments.
  29| A successful school actively engages students and staff.
  30| All students have talents and can excel.
  31| Professional growth is fundamental to instructional excellence.
  32| All students are capable of learning and are entitled to instructional excellence.
  33| Staff and students are entitled to a safe, healthy and positive learning environment.
  34| An atmosphere of mutual respect is essential for learning.
  35| Our Goals and Objectives:
  36| 1. To foster the talents, skills, and potential of all students.
  37| 1.1. To expose all students to a variety of opportunities to explore individual interests and talents.
  38| 1.2. To develop a process and structure for implementing transition plans for each student.
  39| 2. To ensure that staff and programs are conducive to instructional excellence.
  40| 2.1. To develop and Implement a process of data-drive decision-making.
  41| 2.2. To create a professional development program based on data and need.
  42| 2.3. To identify staffing needs and make recommendations for hiring staff.
  43| 3. To use technology as an integral component of teaching, learning, and management.
  44| 3.1. To develop a district technology plan to address equipment and staffing needs.
  45| 3.2. To provide professional development to raise the level of appropriate technological use in the instructional program
  46| and to Improve communication.
  47| 4. To provide facilities that meet staff, program, and community needs.
  48| 4.1. To complete a needs assessment for building transitions related to construction of the new primary school.
  49| 4.2. To develop and implement a centralized process for building use.
  50| 4.3. To work in partnership with the community to promote present and future capital projects.
