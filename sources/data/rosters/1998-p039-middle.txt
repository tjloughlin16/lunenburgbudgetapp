   1| Academic standards and expectations are a high priority at THMS. The entire staff strives hard
   2| to provide numerous opportunities for all students to learn. A positive response from students
   3| and families is expected and encouraged. The staff recognizes the challenge of helping all
   4| students reach their academic potential. The four basic curricular areas in grades five through
   5| eight are; English language arts, math, science and social studies. All eighth grade students
   6| have the opportunity to study French, Spanish or Latin. Special area subjects (art, music,
   7| family and consumer science, technology education, health, guidance, library skills, world
   8| languages, and physical education) offer a unique element to the middle school curriculum.
   9| New curriculum has been written and has been implemented in all subjects except history social
  10| science and foreign languages. These two areas are currently under study and new curriculum
  11| guides will be written and implemented for the 1999-2000 school year. All subjects will then be
  12| in compliance with the frameworks from the Massachusetts Department of Education. All
  13| students in grades four, eight and ten will be required to take the MCAS tests. These tests will
  14| be based on the frameworks.
  15| THMS was fortunate to receive a twelve thousand dollar grant from the Department of Education
  16| for after school activities. This grant has enabled us to offer free programs for all students.
  17| Students are able to extend their school day and participate in floor hockey, chorus,
  18| environmental club, drama, homework help, weightlifting, cross-country running and spring
  19| track. We are also fortunate to have a late bus provide transportation two days per week. This
  20| helps working families and provides the students with the opportunity to extend their school
  21| day.
  22| The School Advisory Council developed their third School Improvement Plan. Council members
  23| Diane Joe, Maryellen McKenna, Becky Stillman, Kris Devaney, Sharon McCullah, Noreen Berry,
  24| Kerry McCarthy, Kathy Patry and Mary Cringan are proud of their accomplishments and believe
  25| that THMS is a fine educational institution. The goal of the school improvement plan is to
  26| extend writing requirements across all curricular areas. A complete copy of the school
  27| improvement plan is available in the school office.
