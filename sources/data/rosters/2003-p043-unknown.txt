   1| The other half of our school mission is to provide an environment that is enjoyable and safe for our students. This is
   2| accomplished through a variety of activities. The student government programs do a great deal. Our 5th/6th grade group runs
   3| activities such as pumpkin painting, ice cream socials and video with pizza parties. They also run field trips to The Nutcracker,
   4| Roll-on America, snow tubing, and Galactic Bowling. Our 7th/8th grade group runs all the dances, homeroom spirit contests,
   5| dress up days for students and raising money for charity. Another new initiative this year was the school store that the 7th and
   6| 8th grade student government started. It is an initiative that students organize and operate; they make many of the decisions
   7| related to purchasing, pricing, promoting and sales. What a great way to learn about business and responsibility. The school
   8| continues to provide students the chance to join our Community Service Organization. This Is another terrific opportunity for
   9| students. They plan and organize charitable events in which they raise money for the less fortunate. Again a wonderful chance
  10| for students to learn about the joy of helping others. Our student run yearbook, the ski/snowboarding program, track programs
  11| and many other activities provide a variety of offerings for involvement on the part of our students.
  12| It Is the hope of the administration and faculty that the citizens of Lunenburg are pleased with how THMS continues to service
  13| students academically and provide for them personally.
