  10|                                                                       Turkey Hill Elementary School
  11|                                                                                Town Report 2025
  12|            Turkey Hill Elementary School is an elementary school serving students in third grade through fifth grade. As
  13|            of Feb 2026, Turkey Hill Elementary School serves 355 enrolled students. There are five sections of grade 3,
  14|            grade 4, and grade 5. Grade 3 has 111 students. Grade 4 has 136 students and Grade 5 has 108 students.
  15|            Our unified arts areas include physical education, art, music, library, and STEM. We are also served by a full
  16|            time assistant principal, full time nurse, a psychologist, a full time adjustment counselor/social worker, and 21
  17|            full time teachers. Other support personnel who work across the district include an ELA coach (through
  18|            December), a Math Specialist, a Director of Teaching and Learning, two EL teachers, a Speech and Language
  19|            Pathologist, OT, and PT. In addition, we have a half-time interventionist targeted support for small groups of
  20|            students.
  21|            Staff continually work on the development of the whole child by supporting students' development and growth
  22|            in the areas of social emotional learning, literacy, math, social studies and science. We are able to meet student
  23|            needs with the support of the district curriculum of teaching and learning and the two coaches which enables
  24|            teachers to have the opportunity to meet at grade levels to develop and provide evidence based instruction,
  25|            common learning experiences, and student data analysis and share effective teaching strategies to support
  26|            student academic growth. Students continue to have benchmark assessments three times throughout the school
  27|            year (beginning, middle and end of year) that are used to help us celebrate students' academic growth and
  28|            effort. The data provided by these assessments also helps refine our focus for interventions. We began the
  29|           implementation of the OpenSci Ed curriculum with initial training and a trial unit. The implementation will
  30|            continue next school year.
  31|            This fall our fifth grade students had their annual trip to Nature's Classroom. It was a successful week with good
  32|            weather, community building, and the creation of fond memories of a unique time together, thanks to the
  33|            combined efforts of our entire fifth grade staff.
  34|            We held an open house in September. It was a well-attended event and for many parents their first opportunity
  35|            to be physically present in our building. It was accompanied by a book fair facilitated by our librarian, Mrs.
  36|            McNamara. Parent conferences in January offered both in person and virtual options and appointments were
  37|            fully booked within a few days.
  38|            We held our annual Penny Wars fundraiser, it was a huge success and the money raised will be put toward the
  39|            purchase and installation of a shade structure in our playing field. As always, our PTO assisted in the weeklong
  40|            collection. We could not be successful without their efforts. We were able to bring back a fifth grade band and
  41|            all students will be performing in an upcoming spring concert under the direction of our music teacher, Mr.
  42|            Anthony Balester. Turkey Hill also held its annual Turkey Trot in November. Led by Mr. Derek Guimond, the
  43|            Turkey Trot was a wonderful experience.
