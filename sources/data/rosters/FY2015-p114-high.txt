   3|                         COMMUNITY BUILDING
   4|                         National Honor Society successfully supported the "Giftmart" drive during the holidays, helping to provide joy to
   5|                         needy children and families in conjunction with local agencies. The World Hunger Task Force will be raising
   6|                         money through Penny Wars this spring. The money will be donated toward the fight against hunger. Also, this
   7|                         winter, one of our eighth grade students participated in Project 351. She spent the day in Boston with hundreds of
   8|                         eighth grade students, giving back to the community.
   9|                         In October, we brought Survive the Drive, to LHS. Survive the Drive highlighted the dangers that our students
  10|                         (and society face) with distracted driving. There was a big emphasis on the dangers of texting while driving. The
  11|                         presentation was made possible by a donation from North End Subaru. A crashed car was located in the back
  12|                         parking lot of the school. The car was placed at the school by Quality Towing and Recovery. SADD provided
  13|                         the students with information about distracted driving during the course of the week and had students and staff
  14|                         pledge to not text and drive. During an Advisory period, SADD showed the short video, The Last Text.
  15|                         We have started an Advisory program at LHs. Une goal of Advisory is to have students have at least one adult
  16|                         who will get to know him/her over the course of four years. Students were randomly distributed into small grade-
  17|                         level groups who they will stay with during the four year period. Another goal of Advisory is to develop
  18|                         relationships with students of different backgrounds and social groups so that students can get to know each other
  19|                         and develop an appreciation of the diversity that we do have in Lunenburg.
