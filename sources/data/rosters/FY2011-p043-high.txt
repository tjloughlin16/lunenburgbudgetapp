  11|                 NEASC ACCREDITATION
  12|                  On December 1, 2011, LHS submitted a Special Progress Report to the New England Association of Schools and
  13|                  Colleges (NEASC) with an emphasis on the standards of Curriculum and Community Resources for Learning. In the
  14|                  area of curriculum, all LHS content areas identified the primary and secondary academic, social, and civic expectations
  15|                 for which they would be responsible to ensure that each student at LHS would have regular opportunities to practice
  16|                 and achieve these expectations. Additionally, a curriculum template was developed for all curricula which included the
  17|                 following; units of study with enduring understandings, essential questions, concepts and skill, the school's learning
  18|                 expectations, and instructional strategies that include the use of school-wide analytic and course specific rubrics.
