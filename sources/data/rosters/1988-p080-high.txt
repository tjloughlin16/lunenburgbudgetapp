  24| Enrollment Update
  25| On October 1, 1988, Lunenburg High School had four hundred and thirty-six (436)
  26| students. As enrollments at the secondary school level continue to decline it
  27| becomes increasingly difficult to provide a quality, comprehensive education for
  28| our students. Nationally, the U.S. faces the dilemma for the first time in its
  29| history, where a succeeding generation will not be as well-off as the preceding
  30| generation educationally, financially, etc. With that in mind, we are hopeful that
  31| regionalization with the town of Shirley will take place SO that we will be able to
  32| continue to provide a quality educational program for all students.
