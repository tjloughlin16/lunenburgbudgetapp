   1| This process as well as our subject area task forces continued our focus to promote consistency and standardization among
   2| teachers. Curriculum mapping is the name of this effort, and the goal is to ensure that all children receive an educational
   3| program that has the same high quality standards. This is an ongoing process. Each year teachers make adjustments in their
   4| program to match the Standards of the Massachusetts Frameworks. This year THMS met the performance targets in all areas as
   5| established by the Department of Education. As our school moves into Cycle 3 of this program, our goal will always be to meet
   6| or exceed target goals.
   7| Turkey Hill Middle School took a big step forward in another area. For the first time our students had access to a computer lab.
   8| Twenty-five computers were installed in the library, and all were connected to the Internet. This was long overdue. Technology
   9| education remains a high priority for all students, and we cannot allow this school to lag behind. With the addition of this lab, all
  10| teachers can take an entire class to the library to do research, to create Power Point presentations, to design science labs and
  11| do a variety of other activities. The effort to improve the physical plant continued. A community school partnership again used
  12| funds ralsed by the school to remodel an area of the school; this year we chose to renovate the front office counter, doors and
  13| trim. There is now a brand new look to this important focal point of the school. Two years ago the cafeteria was renovated, last
  14| year the front courtyard was re-landscaped and now the front office is revitalized. It is all the result of terrific school-community
  15| partnerships, and it was all done no cost to the town!
  16| THMS continues to improve our ability to meet the needs of all students. This year the Guidance Team created a system which
  17| increases the level of communication among teachers. Designated as the Student Data Sharing System, the system ensures that
  18| information about a student is shared with all of the student's teachers. If a student is having an academic or social problem, the
  19| system begins to accumulate information and Initiates a sharing system so that all teachers are aware of the problem. Lastly we
  20| have Initiated a new tradition here at THMS. Our eighth grade class will travel to Washington DC in June. This class trip serves
  21| many functions. It reinforces lessons learned in social studies about the history and geography of Washington; it provides
  22| students first hand examples of lessons learned in civics; and it gives our graduating class a chance to make friendships, to
  23| have memorable experiences and to Increase the positive feelings they have about their middle school experience.
