  10|                   In October 2018, approximately 450 district eighth graders participated in the annual "Tour Day"
  11|                   event. Students toured our twenty-one vocational-technical areas and learned about the school's
  12|                   challenging academic offerings and exciting athletic and extracurricular programs.
  13|                   Career Awareness Night offered interested students the opportunity to return in the evening with their
  14|                   family members to further explore the facilities and speak with staff members.
  15|                   The Vocational Interest Program (V.I.P.) offers area seventh and eighth grade students the chance to
  16|                   visit Monty Tech after school and participate in hands
  17|                   on learning experiences across a variety of vocational-technical areas. The program continued to
  18|                   attract a large number of students during the 2018-2019 school year, serving approximately 700 area
  19|                   students.
  20|                   Class of 2019 Awards
  21|                   Members of the Class of 2019 were awarded approximately $200,000 in scholarships, tools, and
  22|                   equipment. The Monty Tech Foundation generously provided $36,000 in scholarships to graduating
  23|                   seniors, ranging in amounts of $500 to $2,000. The Foundation also awarded $3,250 to the Practical
  24|                   Nursing graduates. Once again, local and state organizations, as well as generous individuals,
  25|                   continue to recognize the ability and potential of Monty Tech graduates in the form of financial
  26|                   donations. The School Committee, administration, faculty, and graduates are grateful for this support.
  27|                   Articulation Agreements with local colleges also play an important role in helping reduce the cost of
  28|                   higher education. Qualified Monty Tech students are eligible to receive college credits through a
  29|                   number of articulation agreements with public and private colleges across the country.
  30|                   Approximately 38% of the graduating class of 2019 reported plans to enroll at a 4-ear college/
  31|                   university upon graduation. By earning college credits while still in high school, these students will
  32|                   save both time and money as they pursue advanced educational programs.
