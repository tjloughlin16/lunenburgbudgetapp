   2| Our Vision: The Lunenburg Public Schools prides itself on excellence. We provide a child-centered environment that

   5| Our Mission: We prepare our students for life-long learning and responsible community membership.

  10| All students have talents and can excel.
  11| Professional growth is fundamental to instructional excellence.
