   1| Turkey Hill Middle School Principal's Report
   2| (Mary E. Cringan)
   3| Turkey Hill Middle School focuses on an educational process that
   4| recognizes and provides for the uniqueness of the individual. This is
   5| accompanied by a program that is intellectually challenging and
   6| recognizes the students academic, emotional, physical, aesthetic, and
   7| social needs. In a time of transition between elementary and high
   8| school, our students will be encouraged and guided to discover not
   9| only self-worth and potential, but sensitivity toward human
  10| relationships, respect for the abilities, values, and attitudes of
  11| others, and a sense of responsibility to the entire community.
  12| Turkey Hill Middle School is home to 484 students in grades five
  13| through eight. Our curriculum in grades five, six and seven include
  14| reading, english, math, science and social studies. In grade eight,
  15| all students take a foreign language of their choice; Spanish, French
  16| or Latin and english, science, geography and either algebra,
  17| pre-algebra or general math. Our unified arts program consists of
  18| home economics, industrial arts, instrumental music, art, health and
  19| physical education.
  20| Our students continue to score well above the State average on the
  21| Massachusetts Educational Assessment Test and the Massachusetts Basic
  22| Skills Test.
  23| Suspensions and truancies are extremely low. We look at each
  24| student as an individual when dealing with discipline and we have very
  25| close contact with parents and social agencies when needed.
  26| Our Special Education percentage is 13%. We integrate and
  27| mainstream as many students as we can. Our Special Education teachers
  28| consult, team teach and support our regular education teachers.
