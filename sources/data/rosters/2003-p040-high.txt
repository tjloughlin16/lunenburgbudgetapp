   1| HIGH SCHOOL
   2| Principal Michael Barney
   3| Our community can continue to be proud of the accomplishments and hard work of our students throughout their school days in
   4| Lunenburg. With the MCAS assessment still drawing headlines across the state, we are proud to say that all of our seniors have
   5| passed the MCAS and proven competency and in fact our school was rated high performing in both Mathematics and English in
   6| our Cycle II rating by the state.
   7| Our summer began with the first annual Lunenburg High School Alumni Golf Tournament to benefit the Athletic Department. We
   8| also had a group of volunteers including coaches, teachers and students renovate the boys locker room, creating a new team
   9| room and locker area. We are also grateful to the Lunenburg Track and Field Association and the volunteers behind them who
  10| will complete the beautiful new track and field for our community and our students this year. Your perseverance, vision and
  11| dedication to our young people are remarkable.
  12| Students In our new Senior Transition Experience Program (STEP) have completed their first year and delivered their Internship
  13| presentations. We're very pleased with the success of more than fifty seniors who participated in Internships in various career
  14| areas, ranging from teaching to radio broadcasting to a 3-month leadership experience in Baja. The STEP program is quickly
  15| becoming a model for other schools because of the innovative way that our community is working together to provide better
  16| learning opportunities for students. We're grateful to all of the community partners for their support of this important new
  17| program.
  18| We're very proud of Mrs. Karyn Giuliani and the members of our Students Against Destructive Decisions (SADD) chapter who
  19| were awarded a grant from the Mass. Governor's Highway Safety Bureau, the Executive Office of Public Safety, and the Office of
  20| Justice Programs. The grant funds will be used to provide awareness programs about underage drinking, impaired driving, and
  21| seat belt use. Thank you to Mrs. Gluliani for her initiative and foresight in seeking out this grant to address issues of critical
  22| importance to our students.
  23| The LHS athletic tradition continued with kudos on several fronts. Our football team was recognized by Central Massachusetts
  24| with the Sportsmanship Award for 2003. Two of our freshmen girls cross country runners qualified for the state meet. Our Boys'
  25| winter track team brought home the league championship this season. The boys and girls basketball teams and hockey team
  26| advanced to tournament play. Beyond their winning records, we're particularly proud of the determination, maturity, and
  27| sportsmanship that all of our young men and women showed while they wore the blue and white of LHS. I'm certain the spring
  28| season will hold many more Individual and team successes for our student athletes.
  29| Once again, our music and art programs flourished, providing creative outlets for hundreds of our students. We again had many
  30| of our students recognized on the district and state level in band, chorus and in the Boston Globe Scholastic Art Awards. New
  31| extra-curricular organizations emerged this year as well, including a video club, broadcasting events on cable channel 8 as well
  32| as capturing major events on film for the archives. Our new mock trial team competed with some seasoned veterans in the area,
  33| defeating powerhouse St. Johns in a big trial, and our woodturning club "turned out some beautiful pieces.
  34| We also continued other great traditions at the high school, including the 38th annual Competitive Class Plays and Freshman
  35| Speech Competition as well as our Multicultural Festival, when our community comes together for a celebration of music, the
  36| arts, and the variety of cultures represented in our local area. The variety of food alone was worthy of the visit to LHS on that
  37| night.
  38| We're proud of our students' accomplishments and thankful for the dedication of our teachers and the support of our community
  39| because that's what makes this good news possible.
