  12| During the year, Mrs. Lahti and Miss Schultz are on duty at the opening of school daily to
  13| check on children who have been absent due to illness for a period of three days or longer and
  14| also to see students who wish to see us for other reasons,
  15| Sabin Oral Polio Vaccine was administered in the schools as follows: TYPE I was offered in
  16| January to students beyond Grade 2; 743 doses were given. TYPE II was offered in March; this was
  17| the first time this type had been offered so all students were included; 1565 doses were given.
  18| TYPE 111 was offered in May, again to students beyond Grade 2; 1265 doses were given. This pro-
  19| gram is completed and in the future children who enter school should have received this protection
  20| during infancy either at clinics conducted by the Board of Health or at their physicians.
  21| Booster shots for diphtheria and tetanus were offered to students in Grades 6 and 11, and
  22| smallpox vaccinations were offered to students in Grade 11. 193 diphtheria tetanus boosters were
  23| given and 65 smallpox vaccinations were done.
  24| The dental fluorine program was started in November and is'still in progress. Miss June
  25| Duteau, Dental Hygienist for the Nashoba Associated Boards of Health is in charge again this year.
  26| Students in Grades 2, 5, and 8 participate in this program.
  27| The P-TA sponsored a dental education program during Dental Health Week in February. Talks
  28| and demonstrations were given in Grades I, 3, and 4, by Mrs. Clarence May, Dental Hygienist.
  29| The annual vision and hearing tests have been completed and notices of defects sent home
  30| along with a form to be completed by the doctor at the time he makes the examination. Many of
  31| these forms have already come back indicating that the children are receiving care. Financial
  32| assistance has been obtained in several instances to help defray the cost of glasses.
  33| Physical examinations are made routinely involving Grades I, 4, 8 and 10 by Dr. DeLeo, School
  34| Physician. All students who participate in competitive sports have annual physical examinations.
  35| In closing, I wish to express my appreciation to the volunteer workers, especially the mem-
  36| bers of the Future Nurses Club and the P-TA groups who assisted at the polio clinics and also to
  37| the administration, the teaching staff and the school personnel for their continuing cooperation.
  38| SIGNE E. SCHULTZ, School Nurse
