   1| HIGH SCHOOL
   2| Lunenburg High School is pleased to announce that all members of the Class of 2003 passed the MCAS in both English
   3| Language Arts and Mathematics. We are proud of all our students and faculty for meeting the challenge in the first year of state
   4| competency determination for the issuance of a diploma. LHS also posted a performance rating of "High" in both English
   5| Language Arts and Mathematics this year from the Massachusetts Department of Education, showing an improvement rating of
   6| "Above Target" in both areas for Cycle II.
   7| It has been an exciting year for many other reasons. We have resurrected both the Multicultural Festival and the School Musical
   8| ("Grease") this year, as well as carrying on traditions such as Homecoming and the 38th annual Competitive Class Plays. Our
   9| students have traveled both domestically and abroad, to Florida, France and Italy. Our athletic teams have performed admirably,
  10| with our football team sharing the league title, girl's cross country going 7-1, our boys track team winning the league and District
  11| E championship, our boy's basketball team being named as the sportsmanship award winners, our hockey team achieving
  12| league co-champ status, the girl's soccer teams qualifying for district play as well as winning the league sportsmanship award,
  13| and our girl's basketball team winning the Mid-Wachusett C championship as well as the Mount Wachusett Invitational
  14| Basketball Tournament.
  15| Our music students participated in the Massachusetts Instrumental and Choral Conductors Association Festival for high school
  16| ensembles, as well as Band Day at UMASS. Our construction class completed the restoration of the spire for the town hall. We
  17| instituted a new applied graphic design course in the art department. As you can tell, our students are always doing something
  18| new and exciting at LHS, and are fine representatives of our school and community.
  19| We are looking forward to the implementation of a revised schedule next year to allow for the creation of the STEP program
  20| (Senior Transitional Experience Program), a community based elective program for seniors involving an internship, community
  21| service or college experience. We look forward to the development of this program as a way to meet the needs of all our
  22| students and to foster our district's mission of preparing our students for life-long learning and responsible community
  23| membership.
  24| As always, we thank the community of Lunenburg for its continued enthusiastic support. We welcome your Involvement In our
  25| school community through event attendance, volunteering and supporting our students in any way you can. This positive
  26| partnership helps us continue to provide an excellent education for our greatest resource, our youth.
