  19|                                                                         Superintendent's Message
  20|                       I am pleased to report another engaging and productive year of teaching and learning in
  21|                       the Lunenburg Public Schools. Typically I would review our state assessment results in
  22|                       this report, but our District did not receive an accountability rating this year since
  23|                       students in grades 3-8 participated in 2017 Next Generation MCAS tests. Students
  24|                       received individual results and the high school received the highest accountability
  25|                       ranking. The next-generation MCAS is a reformatted test, and as such the scores are not
  26|                       comparable to the prior tests. The next-generation MCAS emphasizes readiness for
  27|                       higher-level work at the next grade level and has different performance categories.
  28|                       To support the students our faculty engaged in focused professional development and
  29|                       curriculum renewal to ensure alignment with the new rigorous State standards and
  30|                       assessments consistent with the new strategic plan. After a year-long development effort
  31|                       involving the entire community, in the fall of 2017 the School Committee adopted a new
  32|                       mission, vision and strategic plan, based upon the core values identified in the planning
  33|                        process.
