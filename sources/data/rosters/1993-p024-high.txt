   1| School officials are reminded that all valid recommendations should have been completed or be in the final
   2| stages of implementation when the school submits its Five-Year Progress Report. The Commission
   3| requests that you report any substantive change which may occur in the school no later than sixty (60)
   4| days following its occurrence. The Commission will review the schools accreditation when it considers the
   5| Five-Year Progress Report.
   6| The 1992-93 school year was a challenging one for Lunenburg High School, but by any standard of
   7| measure, including the rigorous criteria employed by the New England Association of Schools and
   8| Colleges, it was a very successful and productive one. The pattern of increasing student enrollment first
   9| noted in 1991 has continued. LHS' 1993 pupil population of almost 500 should grow by as much as 10%
  10| next year. Many benefits such as a broadened, deepened curriculum, improved student class schedules
  11| and key staffing additions have been the result.
  12| While much surely remains to be done, significant deficiencies in our educational facilities (e.g. library,
  13| language and science labs, etc.) and physical plant remain as yet unaddressed, we are confident that with
  14| the continued support and good will of this community we will be able to overcome these challenges.
  15| Let us for the moment at least reflect with some pride and satisfaction on what we have thus far
  16| accomplished. Lunenburg High School remains committed to providing its students with every opportunity
  17| to learn and grow as well as to contributing to the overall quality of life in the community.
