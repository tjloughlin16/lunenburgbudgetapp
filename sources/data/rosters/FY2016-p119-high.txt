   1|                         In December, we brought the SHINE Initiative, to LHS. Three members of the SHINE Initiative gave
   2|                         presentations to students in all grade levels. The presentations were about 45 minutes long. The SHINE Ini-
   3|                         tiative focuses on the mental health and wellness of children and young adults. They raise awareness of
   4|                         mental illness and help to defuse and debunk some of the misconceptions and myths that have fueled stigma
   5|                          for generations. In January, we hosted a community presentation.
   6|                         We started an Advisory program two years ago. One goal of Advisory is to have students have at least one
   7|                         adult who will get to know him/her over the course of four years. Students were randomly distributed into
   8|                         small grade-level groups. They will stay with the same group of students, as well. Another goal of Adviso-
   9|                         ry is to develop relationships with students of different backgrounds and social groups so that students can
  10|                         get to know each other and develop an appreciation of the diversity that we do have in Lunenburg. Some
  11|                         Advisories will be facilitating some community service learning projects.
