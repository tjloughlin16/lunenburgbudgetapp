Flat Hill Road Bridge - $350,000
Capital funds are required to completely 
fund the replacement of the Flat Hill Road 
over Catacoonamug Brook. 
In April 2024, the Town awarded a 
construction contract to Kenefick 
Corporation for the replacement of the 
bridge. The contract specified a 
Completion Date of May 31, 2025. 
Unfortunately, Kenefick Corporation failed 
to perform any of the required work within 
the agreed timeframe. The Town was 
unable to reach a satisfactory resolution 
with Kenefick

===PAGE 13===
Flat Hill Road Bridge - $350,000
The Town has since re-advertised the 
project and an IFB is pending.  
Based upon the prior bid opening, it is 
anticipated that $1,200,000 will be need 
for construction and $50,000 for 
engineering.
The project is presently funded through a 
$600,000 MassDOT Small Bridge Grant 
and $300,000 FY22 Capital appropriation 
for a  total of $900,000. 
These funds will bridge that gap for a 
total project cost estimated at $1,250,000.

Police Cruiser - $68,627
A new Explorer Interceptor/GAS vehicle to replace the 
2019 Chevrolet Tahoe. With 78,320 in mileage. This Tahoe 
is the last of the Tahoe's and has reached its end of life. 
Why not a hybrid?  This vehicle is a gas powered vehicle 
as opposed to a hybrid. Between July-September 2025 the 
Department experienced three hybrid failures.  The 
failures were related to the batteries which require an 
expensive ($20K repair) if not under warranty.   The gas 
vehicles can also be repaired in-house.
The goal is to blend the fleet with a mix of gas and hybrid 
vehicles to extend the overall lifespan of the fleet. 


===PAGE 15===
Engine 2 - 4x4 Brush Truck - $335,000*
Replacement of 37 year old chassis/15 year old 
body. 7 years past planned life expectancy; has 
been postponed more than once
Existing vehicle is increasingly mechanically 
unreliable and has limited firefighting capability. 
New unit would carry double the water (300 
gallons), a larger pump and more space to safely 
store equipment. As the last couple years have 
shown, brush fire are becoming a major concern 
again.
Safer; Current unit has no modern safety features 
like airbags and antilock brakes; Less maintenance 
on new vehicle; More capability (Larger pump & 
water tank; More storage area for equipment and 
protective clothing
*Funded from Vehicle Use Special Purpose Stabilization Fund

Primary School Entry and Driveway Design - $25,000
Current parking capacity is not sufficient for school day 
and weekend event this study will provide solutions.
Currently emergency vehicles do not have sufficient access 
to the school should an event occur during arrival, 
dismissal or a whole school event. 
Contracting a designer to review the feasibility of the following 
study areas: 
• Massachusetts Avenue entry expansion options 
• Current parking capacity during the school day/weekend 
events 
• Expansion of the driveway and driveway parking to the 
school facility 
• Evaluate emergency access Police, Fire and EMT vehicles 
with various capacity levels 
• Review any environmental limitations related to wetlands

Public Safety Roof Repair - $494,500
This request is for funding to replace the roof of the public safety building in its entirety. The roof is 
original to the building and over 20 years old.
There have been several leaks on the asphalt shingle section and the shingles are showing signs of 
serious deterioration.  The rubber roofing over the fire garage is also showing its age and is in the 
early stages of roof failure.
This project would remove all existing roofing and replace the shingled areas with new 25 year 
architectural shingles with 100% ice and water shield coverage. The rubber roof over the garage will 
be replaced with a white TPO roof which will help keep the building cooler in the summer months.

Public Safety Boiler Upgrades - $115,000
This project is for replacement / upgrades to 
the 2 current oil-fired boilers and pumps. 
Current system is 20 years old and original to 
the building. 
The current system has experienced constant 
maintenance issues and affect staff that work 
in the building 24/7. 
Working heat and hot water is essential to this 
building as the state requires prisoners to have 
heat and hot water as well as the Fire/EMS 
side needing hot water for decontamination 
purposes.

Public Safety Building Controls - $35,000
Replacement of the head-end control system 
for building’s HVAC controls, boilers, chiller, 
unit heaters and air handlers.
The current building controls system is original 
to the building - 20 years old. The current 
system has constant maintenance issues, parts 
are hard to find and the current system uses 
proprietary parts.
Upgrading the controls will improve energy 
efficiency in the building while also improving 
employee comfort and reducing service calls.


===PAGE 23===
Front End Loader with Snow Plow - $259,000*
This would replace the current back up loader 
which is quickly approaching the point of not 
being cost effective to make the necessary repairs 
to be safe and reliable.  In FY 2027 this loader will 
be 16 years old or 1 year past its planned life of 15 
years.  Ideally, the DPW should have two large 
loaders.  
A second loader was rented for 2.4 months last 
winter as this is the most critical time to have 2 
loaders.  The rental cost was $6,712/month. 
If a loader is rented for the 15 year life span of 
a loader the cost would be $402,720 or 
$123,720 more than the cost of a new loader.
*Funded from Vehicle Use Special Purpose Stabilization Fund

Adult Activity Center Network Upgrade - $20,405
The Adult Activity Center requires a network 
modernization to replace unstandardized, end-of-life 
(EOL) hardware that currently poses an operational risk. 
The current network infrastructure consists of ad-hoc 
equipment that is inconsistent with Town standards. 
This hardware has reached 'end-of-life' status, leaving 
it ineligible for critical security patches and updates. 
Upgrading this infrastructure will ensure compatibility 
with the rest of the Town’s network while significantly 
strengthening the building's security posture
Replacing EOL hardware ensures the building receives 
critical security patches, protecting the Town’s network 
from modern cyber threats. Upgrading to a single, 
town-wide platform standardizes management and 
ensures seamless connectivity across all municipal 
locations.
