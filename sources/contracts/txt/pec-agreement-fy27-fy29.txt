Memorandum of Agreement 
Between 
Town of Lunenburg 
And 
Lunenburg Public Employee Committee 
Effective July 1, 2026 – June 30, 2029 
 
 
Pursuant to M.G.L. Chapter 32B, Section 19, this Memorandum of Agreements amends the 
current in-force health insurance agreement as follows: 
 
The Town of Lunenburg (“Town”) and the Lunenburg Public Employee Committee (“PEC”) 
agree that the Town shall provide the following health plans (refer to Attachment A for benefit 
brochures): 
 
1. PPO Benchmark 4, HMO Benchmark 4 and HMO Blue Select Benchmark 4 provided by 
MIIA.  The plan shall include, but not be limited to, in-network cost-sharing of: 
 
Deductible:    $500 per individual/$1,000 per family 
PCP Copayments   $20 
Specialist Copayments  $45 
Emergency Room Copayment $100 after deductible 
Inpatient Hospital Copayment Tier 1 - $275 after deductible 
     Tier 2- $1,500 after deductible 
Hospital Outpatient Surgery  $150 after deductible for low cost hospitals 
     $250 after deductible for high cost hospitals 
High Tech Imaging (MRI, CT, PET) $100 after deductible 
 
Retail Prescriptions   $10/30/65 
Mail-Order Prescriptions  $25/75/165 
 
2. The Town will offer Access Blue NE Saver as an alternative.  The plan shall include, but 
not be limited to, cost-sharing of: 
 
Deductible:    $2,000 per individual/$4,000 per family 
Primary Care OV:   $20 after deductible 
Specialist OV:    $35 after deductible 
Emergency Room:   $150 after deductible 
Hospital Admission:   $500 after deductible 
Hospital Outpatient Surgery:  $250 after deductible 
High Tech Imaging (MRI, CT, PET) $125 after deductible 
Retail Prescriptions:   $10/$25/$50 
Mail-Order Prescriptions:  $20/$50/$100 
 
===PAGE 2===
For any employee who enrolls in the HDHP, the Town will contribute the following 
annual amount to an HSA: 
 
Individual - $1,000 
Family - $2,000  
 
3. Managed Blue for Seniors, with PDP, provided by MIIA.  The plan prescription drug 
copayments shall be as follows: 
 
Retail Prescriptions   $10/25/50 
Mail-Order Prescriptions  $20/50/110 
 
4. Medex, with PDP, provided by MIIA.  The plan prescription drug copayments shall be as 
follows: 
Retail Prescriptions   $10/25/50 
Mail-Order Prescriptions  $20/50/110 
 
5. Medicare Part B reimbursement:  The Town and the PEC agree that, upon the effective 
date of this agreement, the Town shall reimburse enrolled subscribers (retirees and their 
spouse) $96 per month toward the cost of their Medicare Part B premium.  Medicare 
enrolled subscribers shall be entitled to reimbursement by the Town on a semi-annual 
basis upon the submission of appropriate documentation from the Social Security 
Administration. 
 
6. The Town will continue to offer the health insurance opt out plan as detailed in 
Attachment B, for the duration of this agreement. 
 
The Town and the PEC agree that the Town shall contribute 75% of the health insurance 
premium and the employee/Non-Medicare retiree shall contribute 25% of the health insurance 
premium for all active plans. The Town and the PEC agree that the Town shall contribute 50% of 
the health insurance premium and the retiree shall contribute 50% of the health insurance 
premium for Medex. The Town and the PEC agree that the Town shall contribute 75% of the 
health insurance premium and the retiree shall contribute 25% of the health insurance premium 
for Managed Blue for Seniors.  
 
The Town and the PEC agree that all the above provisions will be in effect from July 1, 2026 
through June 30, 2029.  However, if the Town anticipates that the plans will be affected by any 
Federal changes under the Affordable Care Act, both parties agree to open up this agreement.
