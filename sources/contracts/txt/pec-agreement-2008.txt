B. Medicare Part-B
The Town agrees that, upon the effective date of this agreement, it shall be
responsible for 75% of the Medicare Part-B premium incurred by subscribers.
enrolled in Medicare Part-B. The reimbursement shall be based on the non-
income adjusted rate set by Medicare. Medicare Eligible subscribers shall be
entitled to reimbursement by the town on a semi-annual basis upon the
submission of appropriate documentation from the Social Security
Administration.

1. The provisions of this Agreement shall supersede any language to the contrary in any
collective bargaining Agreement.

3. The duration of this Agreement shall be from July 1, 2008 through June 30, 2012 on all

4. The duration of this Agreement as set forth herein shall continue thereafter until the new

provision.

such times as is necessary to implement this Agreement.

The retiree group shall have a ten percent vote. The remaining ninety percent vote shall be
divided as follows: each collective bargaining unit represented on the PEC shall have a
weighted vote equal the proportion which the number of employees eligible for health
insurance under this chapter employed in the bargaining unit he/she represents bears to the
total number of employees eligible for health insurance in all bargaining units of the
governmental unit. Any agreement with the public authority must be approved by seventy
percent of the votes cast by the representatives on the public employee committee.
Future unions that organize under M.G.L. c. 150E shall be allowed to become voting
members at the next scheduled meeting of the PEC. Voting percentages shall be adjusted
consistent with Section 19.
E. Meetings of the Committee
The PEC shall meet as necessary to maintain the health benefits of its members. Either
he PEC or the Town may call a meeting of the PEC, provide that the Town or PEC shall
give 30 calendar days notice for the initial meeting between the two parties and shall give no
less than 7 calendar days notice of any subsequent meeting between the PEC and the Town.
This provision shall be waived only if both parties agree to a date other than previously
stated
It is agreed that one member of the committee from each bargaining unit shall be allowed
time off, not to exceed two hours for each meeting, and that notification of time off be
provided to the department head in writing no less than (72) hours before said meeting, for
PEC meetings in addition to their allocated vacation, personal and union or contractual leave
time.
F. Dispute Resolution
Any dispute arising over the interpretation or application of this Agreement may be
submitted to binding arbitration under the labor arbitration provisions of the American
Arbitration Association upon request of the PEC or the Town. Any request by the PEC for
arbitration must be approved by 70% of votes cast by the representatives of the PEC.
If the parties are unable to reach agreement on a successor Agreement, either party
may submit the outstanding issue(s) to mediation as stated in Chapter 150E of the
M.G.L. or to the American Arbitration Association for their mediation services.
G. Eligibility
The parties agree that benefit eligibility for (a) active employees and their dependents,
and survivors and for (b) retired employees and their dependents and survivors shall be
governed by the provisions of M.G.L., ch.32B.
