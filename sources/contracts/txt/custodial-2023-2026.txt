AGREEMENT 
This agreement is entered into this I st day of July 2026 between the School Committee 
of the Town of Lunenburg, Massachusetts, hereinafter referred to as the "Committee", and 
Lunenburg Custodial Union AFSCME, Local 503, State Council 93 American Federation of 
State County and Municipal Employees AFL-CIO hereinafter referred to as the "Union". 
ARTICLE I: 
INTENT AND PURPOSE 
The general intent and purpose of this agreement is in the mutual interest of the School 
District and the Union; to provide for the operation of our school buildings under methods 
which will further to the fullest extent possible, the safety, welfare and health of the school 
children of Lunenburg under conditions which will insure economy of operation, quality and 
quantity of performance, cleanliness of the buildings; and protection of the property. 
By the consummation of the Agreement the parties seek to continue and promote 
harmonious relations and mutual cooperation between the District and the Union to formulate 
work rules to govern the relationship; to insure the uninterrupted operation of the facilities; to 
set fo11h the agreement of the pat1ies with respect to rates of pay, hours of work, and conditions 
of employment under which members represented by the Union perform their duties; to provide 
for an orderly and equitable adjustment of all grievances as herein defined, all with the goal of 
improving the educational system in the Town of Lunenburg. In seeking to achieve these 
goals, the parties acknowledge that the District has and must retain complete authority over the 
policies and administration of the schools, which it exercises under law, except, as expressly 
modified by a specific provision of this Agreement.

ARTICLE II: 
RECOGNITION 
Section 1: 
The District recognizes the Union as the sole and exclusive bargaining agent for the 
purposes of establishing salaries, wages, hours, and other conditions of employment for all 
employees of the Lunenburg Public Schools as certified by the Massachusetts Labor Relations 
Commission in Case No. C.R.-692: for all custodians in the School Department of Lunenburg 
excluding: 
I. Supervisor/Foreman of Buildings and Grounds.
2. Temporary, intermittent or emergency custodians.
3. All other employees of the Lunenburg Public Schools.
Section 2: 
The District will not aid, promote or finance any labor group or organization which 
purports to engage in collective bargaining or make any agreement with any such group or 
individual for the purpose of undermining the Union or changing any condition contained in 
this agreement. 
ARTICLE III: 
ST ABILITY OF AGREEMENT 
This agreement is a complete agreement between the parties covering all mandatory 
subjects of discussion. The parties agree that the relations between them shall be governed by 
the terms of this agreement only. No prior agreements or understandings, oral or written, shall 
be controlling or in any way affect the relations between the parties unless otherwise provided 
in this agreement or unless and until such agreements or understandings have been reduced to 
writing and duly executed by both parties subsequent to the date of this agreement. 
All matters not dealt with herein shall be treated as having been brought up and 
disposed of. No change or modification of this agreement shall be binding on either the District 
or the Union unless reduced to writing and executed by the respective duly authorized 
representatives.

ARTICLE IV: 
NO DISCRIMINATION 
Section 1: 
There shall be no discrimination, interference, restraint, or coercion by the District, or 
the Union or their respective agents against any employee because of membership or non­
membership in the Union. 
Section 2: 
The Union agrees to represent all employees covered in this unit on all matters without 
regard to membership or participation in or association with the activities of the Union and to 
continue to admit said persons to membership except for the payment of periodic dues or 
initiation fees regularly required by the Union. There shall be no discrimination by either party 
against any employee because of race creed, color, sex, or age.

ARTICLE VI: 
GRIEVANCE AND ARBITRATION PROCEDURES 
Section 1: 
For the purposes of this agreement, a grievance shall be defined as a complaint between 
the District and the Union and/or any custodian involving only an alleged specific and direct 
violation of express language of a specific provision of this agreement. The first six (6) months 
of employment shall be a probationary period during which there shall be no right to grieve the 
discharge or discipline of an employee. 
Section 2: 
A grievance must be presented within ten ( 10) working days of the time of the 
occurrence of the alleged contract violation and must be processed in accordance with the steps, 
time limits, and conditions set forth below in this a11icle. A working day shall be defined as a 
regular work day for custodians, other than a weekend or holiday (as defined in this agreement). 
Steps: 
( 1) The custodian, with a steward, may take up the grievance or dispute in writing to the
Director of Facilities and Grounds within ten (10) working days of the occurrence giving rise to 
the grievance. The Director of Facilities and Grounds shall meet, attempt to adjust the matter 
and respond in writing within ten (10) working days. If the grievance is not satisfactorily settled 
at this step, (2) it may be presented in writing to the Superintendent of Schools within ten (10) 
working days after the Director of Facilities and Grounds' response is due. The Superintendent 
shall respond in writing within ten (10) working days. If the matter is not satisfactorily settled 
at this this step it may be, (3) appealed in writing within ten (10) working days after receipt of 
the written answer of the Superintendent by the custodian or the Union, to the Committee. The 
Committee, or its designated representative, and the custodian, and if the Union or custodian so 
elects, counsel and/or an authorized representative of the Union shall meet to discuss the 
grievance as promptly as possible, normally within fourteen (14) working days, at a time 
designated by the Chairman of the School Committee. The School Committee, or its 
designated representative, shall elect whether this discussion shall take place during working 
hours or not. The School Committee will give its written answer to the grievance within ten 
(10) working days following the conclusion of the meeting. The School Committee may

Section 3: 
A grievance not initiated within the time specified shall be deemed waived. Failure of

of performance. 
ARTICLE VII: 
ARBITRATION 
Section 1: 
In case of the either party may submit the grievance for arbitration, in which 
event the School Committee and the Union agree to submit the grievance to the American 
Arbitration Association for disposition in accordance with the applicable rules of the said 
American Arbitration Association; or to select some other mutually acceptable arbitration 
board. In order to be arbitrable, the demand for arbitration must be filed with the AAA 
within 30 days of the last step grievance  response.

Section 2: 
Each party shall bear the expense of its representatives, paiticipants, witnesses for the 
preparation and representation of its own case. The fees and expenses (if any) of the arbitrator 
shall be shared equally by the parties provided that the obligation of the Committee to pay shall 
be limited to the obligation, which the Committee can legally undertake in that connection. 
In no event shall any present or future member of the Committee have any personal 
obligation for any payment under any provision of this Agreement. If either patty desires a 
verbatim record of the proceedings; it may cause such a record to be made, providing it pays for 
the record and makes copies available without charge to the other patty and the arbitrator. 
Section 3: 
 The arbitrator shall have no power to add to, subtract from, or modify any of the 
terms of this Agreement. The patties are agreed that no restrictions are intended on the rights 
and power of the Committee except those specifically and directly set forth in express language 
in specific provisions of this Agreement. The arbitrator shall arrive at his decision solely upon 
the facts, evidence and contentions as presented by the parties during the arbitration 
proceedings. 
Section 4: 
The arbitrator thereunder shall be without power to alter, amend, add to or detract from 
the language of this Agreement, or to hold expert hearings. The decision of the arbitrator shall 
be final and binding upon the patties. The arbitration award may be appealed to Superior Court 
in accordance with the procedures set forth in M.G.L. Chapter I 50C, on the grounds that it fails 
to draw its essence from the agreement, conflicts with state or federal law, or intrudes upon core 
managerial rights as defined in this agreement or by law.

ARTICLE VIII: 
CONTINUITY OF EMPLOYMENT 
During the term of this Agreement or any extension or renewal hereof, the Union or its 
agents shall not cause, sponsor, or assist, and no employee covered by this Agreement shall 
cause or participate in any strike; work stoppage, and concerted absence or other illegal 
activities directed against the Lunenburg School System. If the Union disclaims such activities 
in writing to the District and advises the individuals concerned that the activity is illegal and in 
violation of contract, and instructs them to cease such activity, it shall not be liable in any way 
therefor. 
Employees who participate in any such activity may be disciplined or discharged as the 
District in its judgment deems proper without recourse to arbitration and said discipline shall be 
final and binding on the parties affected thereby and not subject to arbitration, provided 
however, that an issue of fact as to whether an individual has engaged in such activities may be 
made the subject of the grievance and arbitration procedure. 
In connection with any negotiations held for a successor agreement, said negotiations 
shall be conducted without threats of strikes; or any other public pressure by either pat1y until 
mediation, fact finding and any other statutory impasse procedures have been exhausted. 
ARTICLE IX:

Whenever a position covered by this Agreement becomes vacant, notice of the vacancy 
shall be posted on the Union bulletin board and/or publicized electronically and/or distributed

be posted for seven (7) calendar days during which interested employees may apply in writing. 
Under normal conditions the award of the position will be made within forty-five ( 45) calendar 
days. For the purposes of this Agreement, "vacancy" shall be defined as a position that is 
funded in the budget that the District has decided to fill.

Section I: 
The workweek for payroll purposes shall begin on Sunday at 12:0 I a.m. and continue 
for seven (7) consecutive days. The normal workday for all employees shall consist of five (5) 
eight (8) hour days. The regular hours for each day shall be consecutive, except for meal period 
of which is twenty (20) minute paid duty free meal period and one scheduled 15 minute paid 
rest period. It is understood employees are required to remain in the building during their meal 
period and may be called back to work in an emergency situation; however, no employee shall 
lose the remaining time on their meal period. The normal workweek is fotty ( 40) hours per 
week, Monday through Friday, with two (2) consecutive days off on Saturday and Sunday. 
Section 2:

includes a paid twenty minute lunch period.

bargaining unit who uses no sick leave from January I st through June 30th of any fiscal year will 
be granted an additional vacation day. Bargaining unit members must use vacation granted 
pursuant to this section within twelve months of the date on which the vacation was granted. 
ARTICLE XV: 
JURY PAY 
All persons permanently employed, whether full-time or part-time, called for jury duty 
who miss normally scheduled working time required by the court shall be paid an amount equal 
to the difference between the regular straight time compensation paid for that time and the 
amount paid by the court, excluding allowance for travel; and this will be certified by the 
Superintendent upon presentation of the check for moneys received for jury duty. As a 
condition of this, the person may be required to submit evidence of such service.

ARTICLE XXI: 
RIGHTS OF THE DISTRICT 
Section 1: 
In recognition of the fact that the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts vest 
responsibility to the Lunenburg School District for the quality of education in; and the efficient 
and economical operation of the Lunenburg Public Schools; it is herein agreed that except as 
specifically and directly modified by express language in a specific provision of this agreement; 
the District retains all rights and power that it has or may hereafter be granted by law in 
managing the public schools and directing the working force and may exercise the same at its 
discretion without any such exercise being made the subject of a grievance except as expressly 
provided herein. 
Said rights and power include but are in no way to be construed as limited to: the 
establishment of rules and regulations; the right to determine the extent to which work will be 
performed by members of the bargaining unit; the right to hire, fire, suspend, or in any other 
manner discipline for just cause; to classify, promote, demote, transfer permanently or 
temporarily; to determine hours for and the number of employees required at any location; to 
determine the qualification and competence of; to evaluate the performance of; to assign any 
added, lessened or differed work or responsibility to; to set standards and requirements 
application to and make determination of the steps and the eligibility for any in-step wage 
increase for; to make any pay deduction because of absence or failure to perform work by any 
employee covered by this agreement and to introduce new or change existing operational 
methods. 
The above rights are reserved exclusively and solely as prerogatives of the District 
subject only to such limitations as are expressly provided for in this Agreement.

A Safety Committee consisting of two (2) Union appointed representatives and two (2) 
District appointed representatives will meet no more than twice a year at the request of either 
the Union or the District to discuss matters of safe working conditions.

Each employee will receive a $455.00 payment for clothing allowance (less applicable 
taxes) with the first paycheck of August, in a separate check.

In order to maintain the safest, most efficient and effective workplace, all staff should be 
trained to the latest standards of cleaning and maintenance. In order to achieve this, the District 
agrees to provide up to twelve ( 12) professional development hours to all custodial and 
maintenance staff.

(a) The Employer shall not suspend, demote, or discharge any non-probationary employee
without just cause.

(b) If the Employer has reason to reprimand an Employee, it shall be done privately.
(c) Any employee who receives a written reprimand has the right to submit a written

employee's personnel file.
( d) In case of discharge, or a non-probationary employee, the employee affected may

(e) When an employee is discharged by the Employer, the Union shall be promptly

(f) All copies of warning notices shall be forwarded to the Union office upon request.
Section 7 : Layoff and Recall 
(a) When the Employer determines it necessary to layoff employees due to lack of
work or reduce work hours, the Employer agrees to meet with the Union in advance
of any layoff in order to discuss the provisions of this Article
(b) Employees shall be given fourteen (14) calendar days' notice, whenever feasible in
cases of any layoff.
(c) In the event it becomes necessary to layoff employees, the principal of seniority
shall control within classification. The least senior employee in the job
classification affected by the layoff shall be the first laid off. Such employee due to
be laid off shall have the right to bump other employees in the lower classification
having less seniority.
(d) Laid off employees shall be given preference in reemployment for up to one (I) year
the employee was laid off. In the event of recall, employees shall be recalled in the
reverse order of the layoff (that is, the last person laid off will be the person
recalled).
(e) For the purposes of recall notification, the Employee shall notify the employee by a
reliable, documented means at the last known address at the time of layoff that has
been supplied by the employee. Employees must notify the Employer within five
(5) working days of the date the message was received of their intent to repo11 to
work after notification. Employees will receive only one recall notification; failure

period expires one year from the layoff date. Employees shall report to work within 
three (3) working days after indicating their willingness to be reinstated.

procedure.

When classes are canceled and the district offices are closed but the unit 
members must report to work for snow removal activities, the parties agree that no other work

shoveling, snowplowing, plowing, salting, repair of equipment and other related work. Upon 
completion of the snow removal work, custodial and maintenance workers will be released 
from their duties and will be paid straight time compensation for the balance of the regular

ARTICLE XXIII: 
PERFORMANCE EVALUATION 
Section 1: 
Performance evaluation of an employee shall be made annually by the supervisor on or 
before May I st with the exception of a probationary employee who shall be evaluated at the
completion of the first six (6) months of service, and annually thereafter. The Director of 
Facilities and Grounds may evaluate all Bargaining Unit Members. Head Custodians will 
evaluate all custodians assigned to their building, and may, upon the direction of the Director of 
Facilities and Grounds, evaluate any unit member who has worked in their building during the 
school year. The building principal may attach comments to the evaluation of any custodian 
who has been assigned to work in his/her building during the evaluation year. Such evaluation 
will be recorded in writing on the form attached hereto as Appendix B and shall be made on the 
basis of the following criteria: 
A. Quality and quantity of work;
B. Work habits;
C. Work attitudes;
D. Working relationships with others;
E. Supervisory ability (if employee supervises others)
In the event that an employee is evaluated by more than one evaluator, the Director of Facilities 
and Grounds will be responsible for preparing the final evaluation with consideration to the 
input from other evaluators. 
Section 2: 
Each employee shall receive a written copy of his/her evaluation and shall be entitled to 
discuss the evaluation with the Director of Facilities and Grounds, any building principal who 
has attached comments, and, if requested, with the Superintendent of Schools. The Employee 
may submit a written statement, which will be attached to his/her evaluation within two weeks 
after he/she receives a copy of the evaluation.
