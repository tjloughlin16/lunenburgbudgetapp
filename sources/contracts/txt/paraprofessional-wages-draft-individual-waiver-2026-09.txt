INDIVIDUAL ACKNOWLEDGEMENT 
OF PAYMENT AND WAIVER OF RIGHTS
BETWEEN
THE LUNENBURG PUBLIC SCHOOLS	 (“District” or “Employer”)
AND
[_________________________], (“the Employee”) 
WHEREAS, the District employs the Employee as a paraprofessional; and
WHEREAS, the Employee’s terms and conditions of employment are governed by a collective bargaining agreement (“CBA”) between the District and the Lunenburg Paraprofessional Union AFSCME Council 93, Local 503 (“the Union”); and
WHEREAS, the District and Union recently uncovered that paraprofessionals received incorrectly calculated wage payments during the 2025-2026 school year and a certain period of the 2026-2027 school year; and
NOW THEREFORE, the parties agree, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt of which is acknowledged as follows:
	•	Consideration: In consideration for the waiver of rights in this Agreement, the District shall provide the Employee with one (1) additional personal day to use during the 2026-2027 school year and a lump sum payment reflecting retroactive payment for the 2026-2027 school year to be issued no later than <DATE>.

	•	Waiver of Rights Pursuant to the Massachusetts Wage Act: The Employee is voluntarily and knowingly waiving any and all rights and claims that the Employee, their executors, heirs, administrators, assigns and anyone else claiming by, through or under them, may have pursuant to the Massachusetts Wage Act, M.G.L. c. 149, §§ 148 and 150 related to compensation received during the 2025-2026 school year and the 2026-2027 school year (through the date of the execution of this Agreement.)
