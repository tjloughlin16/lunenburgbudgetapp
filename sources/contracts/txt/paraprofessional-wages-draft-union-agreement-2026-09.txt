AGREEMENT Between and Among  Lunenburg Public Schools (“District” or “Employer”) and 
And
Lunenburg Paraprofessional Union AFSCME Council 93, Local 503 (“Union”) Collectively referred to as (“the Parties”) September 2026
WHEREAS, the District employs paraprofessionals; and

WHEREAS, the District and Union are parties to a collective bargaining agreement (“CBA”) that establishes the terms and conditions of employment for paraprofessionals; and 

WHEREAS, the District and Union recently uncovered that paraprofessionals received incorrectly calculated wage payments during the 2025-2026 school year and a certain period of the 2026-2027 school year; and 

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to resolve issues related to the paraprofessionals’  underpayment of longevity compensation.

NOW THEREFORE, the Parties, for good and valuable consideration, agree as follows:

	•	Introductory “Whereas” Clauses: The introductory “Whereas” clauses above are incorporated into the Parties’ Agreement.  

	•	Consideration: In consideration for the waiver of rights in this Agreement, the District shall provide affected paraprofessionals who sign an individual waiver with one (1) additional personal day to use during the 2026-2027 school year and a lump sum payment reflecting retroactive payment for the 2026-2027 school year to be issued no later than <DATE>. 
	•	Union Waiver of Rights: The Union agrees not to grieve, appeal, or otherwise challenge the provisions of this Agreement via the Parties’ collective bargaining agreement, through the contractual grievance, through the Department of Labor Relations, or in any other forum.  
	•	Precedent: The Parties agree that this Agreement shall not be used to demonstrate a practice or precedent in any other matter.
