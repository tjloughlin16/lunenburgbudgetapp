2. Article VIII.-Sick Leave
I. Addition under Section 8-1 as follows:
Accruals for all paraprofessionals including personal time will now be calculated in days rather than hours as calculated
in previous agreements. Paraprofessionals may use their sick and personal time by the hour if needed and deductions will
be based on a 6.5 hour day.
