Joslin Property  
The Collaborative secured 143 Joslin Street property over the summer for $1.25 million.  
As part of the financing evaluation for the construction loan of Joslin the Collaborative’s initial 
investment in the Electric Ave property has now been appraised at 4 times the value of the initial 
purchase. OPM and architect are underway, and we anticipate that demo and breaking 
ground should occur in November of this year.   
The additional property next door will be on the market within the next 6-8 months as part of 
the initial Unique Acquisition to supply the program with the necessary playground green space 
for students and program space within the 1-acre plot. This will require the Board to revote when 
the non-APR property becomes available.   
DESE will be doing an initial site visit of the Joslin property at the end of September.   
 
PPA: Power Purchase Agreement  
The Collaborative has been researching solar options to defer some of the cost of electricity via  
UNITIL for the Fitchburg site; in discussions with Progeneration Solar Co. who anticipate the cost 
savings of 10% on electric ity cost and at no cost to the C ollaborative on the  installation and 
excavation (e.g., new roofing installed, solar carports installed with excavation of  hill in the back 
of the property which will expand parking needs).  
The Collaborative has secured Mirick, O'Connell, DeMallie & Lougee, LLP to review and edit the 
contract as they have a specialty in commercial properties and solar contracts. Currently, we 
are awaiting the solar companies’ data confirming the data on the projected energy output and 
savings.   
Annual Audit   
Annual Audit is underway by Bruce Norling CPA, PC and associates.  
PLAT Program  
The Collaborative has re-joined the Autism Higher Education Foundation and their launched 
PLAT program (Paralegal Asst. Training Program). The Collaborative will be supporting the 
Fitchburg District Court and soon the Ayer District Court to supply students the opportunity 
for vocational opportunities within the court system.  Currently, there are two (2) students that 
will be participating. 
Contract Services   
The Collaborative has brought on board a TVI and are back to supplying service contracts.  We 
have secured one of our members' needed areas of a full time SLP and BCBA’s and continue to 
support our member districts and their needs in the areas of AAC, SLP-CF supervision, OT, PT, 
and BCBA. 
 
Trexo  
The Collaborative has signed a lease to own for the Trexo robotic gait trainer and has been 
able to fundraise about $10,000 (cost approximately $30,000).  The Collaborative is only 1 of 
2 providers that have this device in MA; Spaulding Rehab being the second.
