About the MA DESE Family-School Partnership Initiative (FSPI)
▪ Opportunity for districts to use the updated Massachusetts Family, School, and 
Community Partnership Fundamentals (Version 2.0) to reflect on family-school 
partnership and receive training, coaching, and strategic planning support to 
strengthen district systems for working collaboratively with families/caregivers.
▪Our District Team worked with coaches/facilitators from School & Main Institute 
(SMI)

The program ran from November 2022 through June 2023.  During this 
time our District Team participated in:
● Facilitated Team Meetings
● Independent Team Meetings
● Cross District Sessions
● District Dialogues
The District Team assisted the SMI Facilitators in setting up Focus 
Groups to gather input.

District Team Priority 1: Relationship building with & among all stakeholders
Connection to the:
FSP Framework - Fundamental 1: Building Positive Relationships
Strategy - Cultivating Culture & Climate (CC&C.1): Foster a climate of safety, caring and 
inclusivity for all in our district through secure facilities and safety training, evidence-based social 
emotional and mental health curricula, and building strong staff-student relationships, student 
peer relationships, staff peer relationships, administrator-student/staff relationships, 
school-family relationships.

District Team Priority 3:Strengthen school culture to create 
capacity for stronger partnerships
Connection to the: 
FSP Framework - Fundamental 5: Building Capacity of the Staff
Strategy - Transforming Teaching & Learning (TT&L.1): Establish effective teams to lead the 
essential work of teaching and learning, including development of high-quality, rigorous, relevant and 
enriching curricula, with a priority focus on literacy and social-emotional learning; identification and 
communication of expectations for demonstration of high-quality teaching and learning 
competencies; and implementation of effective instructional practices, interventions, 
communication, and professional development.
