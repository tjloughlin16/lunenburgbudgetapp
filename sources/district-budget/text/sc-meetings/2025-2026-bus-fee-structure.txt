Payment Guidelines 
Grades K-6 :  0-1.99 miles are charged 
Grades K-6 :  2 or more miles no charge 
Grades 7-12 :  all students charged 
Fee Structure 
$180 Families with 1 student 
$50 Families with 1 student that qualify for a reduced fee (through free/reduced application 
which will be verified) 
$270 Families with 2 or more students 
$75 Families with 2 or more students that qualify for a reduced fee (through free/reduced 
application which will be verified) 
Free - Families that have submitted a Free/Reduced Application and qualify which will be 
verified 
Free & Reduced Meal Information 
Any family that believes they may qualify for a free or reduced fee has to complete and submit 
the application AND the sharing information form found in the links below.  Families that do not 
submit the sharing form checking the third box on the form for Transportation fees, will not be 
considered for a free or reduced fee.
