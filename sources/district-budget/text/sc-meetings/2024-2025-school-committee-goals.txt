Goal 1: Financial Sustainability and Efficiency 
Objective: 
Ensure the long-term financial stability of 
the district by developing a budget that balances sustainability, competitive 
compensation, and efficient resource management. Collaboration with key 
stakeholders will be prioritized to maximize resources and efficiency. 
Action Steps:     
● Work collaboratively with staff, unions through collective 
● bargaining, administrators, parents, students, and community members, as well 
● as town partners including the Select Board, Finance Committee, and Town 
● Manager, to create a fiscally responsible budget that supports sustainability 
○ and long-term planning. 
● Collaborate with the Budget Task Force Committee to establish and 
○ communicate long-term financial goals for the district. 
● Prioritize competitive salaries to attract and retain talented 
○ staff members. 
● Maintain transparency by engaging in open discussions with 
○ stakeholders to balance educational needs with fiscal realities. 
● Review and revise fees, including but not limited to facility 
rentals, student activities, and athletics. 
Benchmarks: 
● Passage of a budget that reflects sustainability and long-term 
○ planning goals. 
● Regular updates and reports on the district’s financial status and 
progress toward improving efficiency and cost-effectiveness.

Goal 2: Facilities and Capital Planning 
Objective: 
Develop a comprehensive plan that focuses on 
the maintenance as well as future needs of school buildings and athletic fields, ensuring 
safety and sustainability through capital planning, budgeting, policy development, and potential 
long-term project proposals that may involve long-term borrowing. 
Action Steps:        
● Develop a long-term facilities plan that addresses the district’s 
● needs, with special attention given to school administration offices, Turkey Hill School 
and the turf field.    
● Collaborate with town officials and the community to ensure that 
● planned projects and upgrades are well-documented and presented at public forums, such 
as town meetings. 
● Establish a long-term plan for all district buildings, outlining 
● timelines and budget estimates for repairs, upgrades, and sustainability improvements. 
Benchmarks: 
● Completion and public presentation of a detailed long-term facilities plan. 
● Public documentation and acknowledgment of facility projects and upgrades through 
official town records or meetings. 
 
Goal 3: Transparent, Efficient, and Effective Communication 
 Objective: 
 To improve communication by reviewing and revising policies and/or protocols that enhance 
transparency, efficiency, and effectiveness between the school committee, superintendent, and 
the public. 
===PAGE 3===
Action Steps: 
●  Policy and Protocol Review: Workshop and revise communication policies and/or 
protocols, focusing on increasing efficiency, reducing misinformation, and fostering 
transparency. 
Benchmarks: 
● Successful implementation of the new communication policies and/or protocols.
