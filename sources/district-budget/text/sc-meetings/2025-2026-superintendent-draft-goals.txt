 Lunenburg Public Schools – 
 Superintendent Evaluation Goals 
 2025–2026 School Year 
 Goal 1 – Budget Development & Fiscal Leadership 
 District Improvement Goal 
 Aligned Standards & Elements: 
 ●  Standard II: Management & Operations  – II-E-1:Fiscal  Systems 
 ●  Standard III: Family & Community Engagement  – III-C-1:  Culturally Proficient 
 Communication; 
 ●  Standard IV: Professional Culture  – IV-C-1: Communication  Skills; 
 Goal Statement: 
 By June 2026, lead a transparent, inclusive, and fiscally responsible budget development 
 process that addresses the district’s financial constraints while advancing Lunenburg’s 
 educational priorities. This process will result in three distinct FY27 budget scenarios: a 
 level-services budget, a 2.5% (or town allocation) increase budget, and a needs-based 
 budget—and will include active collaboration with municipal leadership to explore sustainable 
 funding options, including an operational override if needed. 
 Key Actions & Benchmarks: 
 ●  Present three complete budget scenarios to the School Committee by January 2026. 
 ●  Hold at least five joint meetings with the Town Manager, Finance Committee, and Select 
 Board by January 2026. 
 ●  Conduct two or more public budget forums and maintain a transparent online budget 
 portal. 
 ●  Collaborate with municipal leadership on override planning, communications, and 
 advocacy if necessary.

 Goal 2 – Improving Instruction Through High-Quality 
 Observations & Feedback 
 Student Learning Goal 
 Aligned Standards & Elements: 
 ●  Standard I: Instructional Leadership  —I-D-3 Observations  and Feedback, I-D-4 
 Performance Ratings 
 ●  Standard IV: Professional Culture  – IV-A-1: Commitment  to High Standards; IV-D-1: 
 Continuous Learning of Staff 
 Goal Statement: 
 By June 2026, ensure that all school administrators consistently conduct timely, high-quality 
 classroom observations and provide specific, actionable feedback that drives instructional 
 improvement and student achievement. At least 85% of educators will affirm that feedback is 
 clear, actionable, and supports their professional growth. Student achievement in targeted 
 classrooms will increase by at least 10 percentage points on district benchmark assessments 
 from fall to spring. 
 Key Actions & Benchmarks: 
 ●  Deliver administrator professional development on observation and feedback best 
 practices by November 2025. 
 ●  Monitor observation logs quarterly to ensure timelines and quality standards are met. 
 ●  Review a sample of written feedback for clarity, specificity, and instructional alignment.

 ●  Teacher survey results show at least 85% positive responses regarding feedback. 
 ●  Targeted classrooms demonstrate 10 percentage point or greater growth on benchmark

 Goal 3 – Superintendent Entry Plan & Community 
 Understanding 
 Professional Practice Goal 
 Aligned Standards & Elements: 
 ●  Standard I: Instructional Leadership  – I-E-1: Data-Informed  Decision Making 
 ●  Standard III: Family & Community Engagement  – III-A-1:Family  Engagement; III-C-1:

 ●  Standard IV: Professional Culture  – IV-C-1: Communication  Skills; IV-E-1: Shared

 Goal Statement: 
 By March 2026, complete a comprehensive Superintendent Entry Report to establish strong 
 relationships with stakeholders, assess district culture and performance, and identify priorities 
 for strategic planning. 
 Key Actions & Benchmarks: 
 ●  Conduct at least 40 stakeholder interviews across diverse groups by February 2025. 
 ●  Visit all district schools and departments by November 2025. 
 ●  Host or attend a minimum of five community engagement events by March 2025.

 Goal 4 – Building a Culture of Belonging Through Aloha 
 Leadership 
 District Improvement Goal 
 Aligned Standards & Elements: 
 ●  Standard III: Family & Community Engagement  – III-A-1:  Family Engagement; III-C-1: 
 Culturally Proficient Communication; 
 ●  Standard IV: Professional Culture  – IV-B-1: Policies  and Practices; IV-C-1: 
 Communication Skills; IV-E-1: Shared Vision Development 
 Goal Statement: 
 By June 2026, foster a districtwide “One ʻOhana” culture (Welcoming all, lifting each other, 
 learning with purpose and leading with heart) that prioritizes belonging, respect, and shared 
 responsibility, embedding the values of aloha (compassion, peace, respect) and makana (gifts 
 of time, care, and recognition) into the daily life of the district. 
 Key Actions & Benchmarks: 
 ●  Launch “One ʻOhana” initiatives across all schools by September 2025. 
 ●  Integrate inclusive, culturally responsive practices into events, communications, and 
 recognition programs.

 ●  Collect annual feedback from staff and families on belonging, respect, and collaboration. 
 ●  Publicly celebrate and share examples of staff and student actions that reflect district 
 values. 
 Success Criteria: 
 ●  Increased positive responses in staff and family surveys regarding belonging and 
 respect. 
 ●  Clear evidence of “One ʻOhana” values in school culture, communications, and 
 traditions. 
 ●  Staff and community narratives reflect a shared commitment to kindness, empathy, and 
 service.

 teaching and learning the central focus of schooling

 school and district goals and improve organizational performance, educator effectiveness, and 
 student learning.

 Standard 4:  Professional Culture:  Promotes success  for all students by nurturing and 
 sustaining a school culture of reflective practice, high expectations, and continuous learning for 
 staff. 
 Indicators: 
 IV-A-Commitment to high standards:  Fosters a shared  commitment to high standards of 
 teaching and learning with high expectations for achievement for all, including: 
 1.  Mission and core values  : Develops, promotes, and  secures staff commitment to core values 
 that guide the development of a succinct, results-oriented mission statement and ongoing 
 decision-making. 
 2.  Meetings:  Plans and leads well-run and engaging  meetings that have clear purpose, focus 
 on matters of consequence, and engage participants in a thoughtful and productive series of 
 conversations and deliberations about important school matters.
