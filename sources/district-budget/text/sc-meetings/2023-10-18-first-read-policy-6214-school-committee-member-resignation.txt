SCHOOL COMMITTEE MEMBER RESIGNATION 6214 
 
The resignation of any school committee member must be submitted to the Town Clerk, who in 
turn shall enter it into the official records of the town.  The resignation is effective as dated by the 
member in writing  and submitted to the Town Clerk.
