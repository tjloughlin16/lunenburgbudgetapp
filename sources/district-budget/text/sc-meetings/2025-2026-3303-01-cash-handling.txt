FISCAL MANAGEMENT AND NON-INSTRUCTIONAL OPERATIONS 3000 
 
ACCOUNTS  3300 
 
CASH HANDLING  3303.01 
 
 
 
Lunenburg School District shall be a cashless environment by the end of the 2025-2026 school 
year.  With all deliberate speed, the Superintendent, her subordinates and designees, shall 
make all necessary arrangements to convert the district to all cashless transactions.  This shall 
include all purchases made by District employees and all gates for all District events including 
sports events.  
 
Gates for events shall use a cashless payment system and all funds shall be deposited into a 
single District account.  Any District employee engaging in transactions of any kind using cash 
without prior approval of the Superintendent or her designee shall be subject to discipline up to 
and including termination and repayment of all costs arising out of and/or related to the cash 
transaction.
