Continue to Follow DESE/DPH guidance
Masks will continue to be REQUIRED in some 
situations
● School health offices & medical waiting rooms are subject to 
the mask requirements 
● Continue with mask requirement in Health Office per 
CDC/Mass DPH guidance, until CDC/DPH lift the 
requirement (No SC Policy needed)

===PAGE 5===
Continue to Follow DESE/DPH guidance
Strongly Recommend:
● After a 5-day isolation and/or quarantine period, wear a mask around 
others for an additional 5 days, except when eating, drinking, or outdoors.
● Individuals who experience COVID-19 symptoms should stay home and 
obtain testing. 
○ If they receive a negative result and their symptoms improve 
(including remaining fever-free for a 24-hour period without 
fever-reducing medication), they can return to school but should also 
wear a mask until their symptoms fully resolve.
● Unvaccinated individuals should continue to wear masks in school 
settings. 
 

===PAGE 6===
Recommendation to CONTINUE
● Use of “Masks Optional” (not “no longer required”)
● Supporting choice (of staff and students to mask/not 
mask) 
● Cleaning/Disinfecting (with ‘sign-off’ measures)
● Hand Hygiene (encourage good hand hygiene, provide 
hand sanitizer) 

===PAGE 7===
Recommendation to CONTINUE
● Ventilation (encourage windows to classrooms and buses 
to be opened)
● Added PPE for Special Areas (TLC, Achieve, PT/OT, 
Health Offices -for staff who may need to work closely with 
students who choose to no longer mask)
● Stay home when sick (strongly emphasize)

===PAGE 8===
Recommendation to LIFT
● Mandatory classroom seats in rows (maintain a seating 
plan and keep seating as stable as possible)
● Mandatory no group work (no restrictions)
● Mandatory cafe seating distance (provide options for 
students who wish to continue distancing)
● ACE carpool restrictions (Mask optional, continue with daily 
symptom screening)
● Mandatory no food in classrooms (return to pre-COVID 
Policy and Procedure)
