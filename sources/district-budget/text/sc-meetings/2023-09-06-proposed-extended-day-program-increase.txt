SUBJECT:   PROPOSED RATE INCREASES FOR  
  EXTENDED DAY AND EARLY LEARNING PROGRAMS 
 
The memorandum outlines a proposed increased rates for the Extended Day Program, the 
district’s before and after school program, and the Early Learning Center, daily preschool 
offering.  Currently both programs are operated from the Passios Elementary School, 1025 
Massachusetts Avenue.  The Extended Day Program is open from 7:00 am to 9:00 am and then 
3:30 pm to 5:30 pm.  The Early Learning Center operates from 8:30 am to 2:30 pm.   
 
History 
A review of costs have not occurred since the Spring of 2019 for 2019/2020 school year; those 
changes included: 
 Eliminated “as needed” registrations 
 Establish an enrollment cap on students 
 Close program during December break 
 Hire a Nurse for the after school program 
 Set schedule for registrations 
 Full week schedules will have first priority 
 Eliminate pay for only services rendered, parents will pay for set schedule, (even if not used). 
 
Operating Revenues and Expenses 
Program revenues are strictly generated from “user fees” or tuition costs from families.  
Revenues have fluctuated over the last few years due to the pandemic and restricted enrollment.

Reason for Proposed Rate Changes 
Program revenues do not meet the program expenses.  The local budget has been able to support 
the health insurance costs to keep the program from deficit.  The revenue challenge is around 
enrollment – a higher enrollment in the extended day program creates revenues to support fixed 
costs, including Early Learning Center.  With an anticipation to a “return to normal” operations, 
a programmatic review was conducted to keep the program self-sustaining.  Fee increase should 
be considered to close the gap on the annual fixed programmatic costs.

Proposed changes and adjustments will be considered next year.  There is consideration that 
universal preschool programming will be mandated for School Year 2022/2023.  This action 
would dissolve the district’s Early Learning Center. 
 
Financial Impact on Families 
The financial impact will vary from family to family, depending on their need and family size.  
A family who sends their Primary School child to the morning OR afternoon session every day 
and currently pays $1,800 per year will see their rate increased by a maximum of $360.00 per 
year.  A family that sends to child to the morning AND afterschool program every day and 
currently pays $3,600 per year will see their rate increased a maximum $720.00 per year.
