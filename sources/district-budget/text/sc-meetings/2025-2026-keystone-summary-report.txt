Annual Audit Summary 
Key financial highlights from the 2025 audit of the Keystone Educational Collaborative underscore 
the organization's strategic investments and fiscal responsibility: 
 
Total assets were approximately $10.8 million, supporting operations and capital investments. 
 
A significant Other Post-Employment Benefits (OPEB) liability of $5.2 million was recognized, 
reflecting a proactive commitment to employee health benefits and workforce stability. 
 
The Collaborative secured a loan totaling over $6 million for the acquisition and renovation of 
property at 143 Joslin Street, enhancing physical resources for students. 
 
Tuition and service fee revenues exceeded budget by $418,864, demonstrating strong operational 
revenue performance. 
 
Total expenditures were slightly over budget by about $59,000, an improvement over the prior year’s 
$517,210 overspending, reflecting tighter expense control. 
 
The net position decreased to $3.23 million (to a negative net position), primarily due to OPEB 
implementation, increased long-term liabilities, and capital investments, reflecting a capital-intensive 
growth phase rather than operational weakness. 
 
Previous years showed modest net position gains, illustrating overall financial stability even amidst 
capital expansion. 
 
As the Collaborative enters its 50th year, the financial approach focuses on securing facility 
ownership and investing in long-term employee benefits, building equity and sustainability. While this 
strategy results in short-term negative net position due to property debt and OPEB obligations, it 
positions the Collaborative for sustainable growth by building capital assets valued at over $9 million. 
Careful revenue and expense management support continued growth in student services, stable 
employment, and enhanced physical resources for member districts and students. Facility ownership 
provides a stable foundation for future leaders, avoiding disruptions from lease renewals or 
relocations, thus safeguarding the collaborative’s longevity and resilience as an educational partner. 
 
This balanced, capital-intensive strategy supports Keystone Educational Collaborative’s mission and 
ensures a solid financial foundation for future growth and service excellence. 
 
Annual Report Summary 
Keystone Educational Collaborative’s 2024– 2025 annual report highlights the organization’s strong 
support for its mission and 12 member districts through expanded, high- quality special education 
programs aligned with district needs. Eleven of the twelve me mber districts utilized Collaborative 
programs, and notably, about 88% of students enrolled in the Collaborative are from its member 
districts, showing close alignment with member priorities and student requirements. 
===PAGE 5===
Financially, the Collaborative saw about a 17% increase in primary revenue over the prior year, 
primarily from student programs and contract services, which constitute over 97% of revenue. This 
growth funds ongoing investments in staffing and infrastructur e, including the purchase and 
renovation of a second building to expand capacity and reduce lease costs, while operational 
expenses remained below projections. 
The Collaborative’s programs demonstrate significant cost -effectiveness for member districts. 
Tuition rates for the three core programs —KEY, Keystone Elementary/Middle, and Keystone 
Academy—offer substantial savings compared to private special education op tions within a one-hour 
radius, with estimated annual savings per student of approximately $43,810 for KEY, $17,029 for the 
Elementary/Middle program, and $16,090 for the Academy, translating to an overall estimated cost 
savings of around $76,929 per stude nt annually for districts choosing these programs. 
Staffing remains heavily focused on direct student support, with 93% of positions involving daily 
student contact . Approximately 32% of employees have over five years of tenure, supporting 
program stability. The Collaborative has also expanded vocational and therapeutic roles in response 
to member district needs. 
Member district survey feedback reflects high satisfaction with the Collaborative’s quality, 
responsiveness, and effectiveness. The organization’s nearly 50 -year legacy, combined with ongoing 
program innovation and community partnerships, positions it well to support students and families 
effectively into the future. 
This summary reflects data and insights from the 2024 -2025 Keystone Educational Collaborative 
annual report, illustrating its mission fulfillment, financial strength, member district satisfaction, and 
substantial cost-saving benefits for special education programming.
