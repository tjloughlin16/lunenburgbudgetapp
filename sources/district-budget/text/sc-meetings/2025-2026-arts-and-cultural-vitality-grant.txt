 TO:  Dr. Jodi Fortuna, Superintendent 
 FROM:  Dr. Deborah E. Bookis, Director of Teaching and Learning 
 RE:  Arts and Cultural Vitality Grant (Year 3) 
 DATE:  September, 2025 
 We are requesting permission from the School Committee to apply for the Arts and Cultural Vitality 
 Grant. Please see the information below. 
 Arts and Cultural Vitality Grant (Year 3) 
 Anticipated Amount $7,225 
 This will be year three for the Performing and Visual Arts Department and we fully expect to be one of a 
 handful of Districts to receive the grant for the third year. The overall purpose of this competitive grant is to 
 provide funding support for schools to conduct an arts program review using  DESE's Arts & Cultural Vitality 
 (ACV) Index  . The grant supports stipends and the necessary  materials for the members of school review teams. 
 Last year, the District was awarded $7,225 to engage in planning around their  FY24 ACV review.  This year, 
 the  grant will support stipends for 7 educators and the necessary materials to engage in implementation and 
 monitoring of their ACV action planning in FY24. Participating schools also receive guidance about using the 
 ACV Index tools and resources and support from a DESE Arts specialist. The District already completed Tracks 
 1 and 2 below: 
 ●  Implement an arts program review. (Track 1) 
 ●  Engage in action planning around their FY24 ACV review. (Track 2) The  ACV Action Plan  was shared 
 with you at your June 18, 2025 School Committee meeting. 
 ●  Engage in implementation and monitoring of their ACV action planning in FY24. (Track 3)
