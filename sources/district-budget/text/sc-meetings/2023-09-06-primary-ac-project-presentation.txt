Lunenburg Primary School
AC Project
Update and Recommendation March 15, 2023
===PAGE 2===
BACKGROUND
During the 2021-2022 school year, two hundred and thirty eight thousand dollars 
($238,000.00) was approved under the 2023 Capital Plan to install air conditioning 
improvements at the Lunenburg Primary School, 1401 Massachusetts Avenue. 
The original budgeted amount was $290,000, and then reduced to $238,000. The 
initial assumption for the air conditioning upgrade was that the existing rooftop air 
handling units could be utilized for this purpose. It was later determined that it was 
not possible.

===PAGE 3===
BACKGROUND
A subsequent assessment and project pricing for a complete air conditioning 
upgrade/replacement was conducted by VAV International. This consultant 
provided recommendations and complete project pricing; a total estimated project 
was $ 7,984,035. This was not viable.

===PAGE 4===
PROPOSED SOLUTION
Alternative solutions were reviewed and it was determined that the best alternative plan 
would be installing ductless split systems for each classroom. These “systems” consists 
of one (1) 48,000 BTU Dual Zone condenser with Heat Pumps and two (2) 24,000 BTU 
wall or ceiling mounted heads for distribution. Each system will provide coverage for two 
(2) classrooms.
Currently there are seven (7) rooms on the second floor and eight (8) room on the first 
floor that are air conditioned by permanently installed AC units as opposed to removable 
window mounted units.
We are looking to provide air conditioning and supplemental heat in twenty two (22) 
classrooms and an additional two (2) offices.

===PAGE 5===
PROJECT PRICING
The district has been working with various vendors on estimated projects costs, 
here is summary:
PHASE 1 – FY23 Capital Plan Funding $238,000.00
It is estimated that the district will be able to provide cooling and supplemental 
heating in 20 classrooms. This includes all thirteen (13) classrooms on the 2nd 
floor as well as four (4) classrooms on the first floor.

===PAGE 6===
TIMELINE
Due to the disruptive nature of the work, the installation of these systems can only 
be completed during the summer break. Unless there are supply chain challenges, 
we are quite confident that PHASE 1 can be completed during the 2023 summer 
break.

===PAGE 7===
RECOMMENDATION
It is recommended that this solution moves forward. The project intent remains the 
same – managing classroom air comfort, the execution is different than originally 
presented. 
If supported by the Committee, we will move this proposal forward to the Town 
Manager.
