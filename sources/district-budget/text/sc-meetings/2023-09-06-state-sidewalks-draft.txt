        August 31,2022 
 
Dear Senator Cronin, Representative Kushmerek, and 
Representative Sena, 
 
The Lunenburg School Committee respectfully requests that 
should funds become available for sidewalks and crosswalks 
that Massachusetts Avenue (Rte 2A) be considered for such 
funding. 
 
Students that live east of our Middle High School property have 
no safe way of walking on Mass. Ave.  Students and parents 
who may wish to walk to our Primary School also encounter no 
crosswalks or sidewalks.  
 
Our town officials cannot authorize this work so we are asking 
you to help to find funding to keep our students and our 
citizens safe. 
 
Sincerely, 
 
Lunenburg School Committee
