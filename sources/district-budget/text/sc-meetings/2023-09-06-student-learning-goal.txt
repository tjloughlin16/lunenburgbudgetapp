Student Learning Goal
The 2021-2022 iReady benchmark assessments in math and literacy for grades K-8 indicate that our students’ academic performancedoes not reflect the typical distribution of students in a model of tiered instruction  (80% Tier 1; 15% Tier 2; 5% Tier 3); therefore, ourgoal is to achieve typical distribution by June 2024 as measured by the end-of-year iReady benchmark assessment.
