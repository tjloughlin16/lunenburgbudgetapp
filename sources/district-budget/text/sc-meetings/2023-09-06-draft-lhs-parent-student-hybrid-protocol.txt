Safety
 
●
All PPE protocols and requirements must be adhered to.
 
●
Masks/face coverings will be required for all staff and students in all
 
grades.  These face coverings should be worn at all times (outside of
 
mask breaks.)
 
 
●
Staff must wear a mask/face covering and maintain 6 feet of social
 
distancing between themselves and other staff and students.
 
Teacher movement within classrooms is allowed but should attempt
 
to limit long term close proximity (<3’).
 
●
There will be signs at entrances to the building reminding students
 
of the safety protocols that are in place to keep everyone safe. There
 
will be stickers placed in the hallways that indicate six feet of space in
 
between students in order to assist with distancing.
 
●
Hand sanitizer will be available at each building entrance and staff
 
will be monitoring each entrance upon student arrival and
 
departure.. Students will use hand sanitizer upon entrance into the
 
building.
 
●
Lockers will not be used by students in order to minimize passing
 
time.
 
●
Multi stall Bathrooms will only be allowed to have two students at a
 
time in the bathroom.

Classrooms
 
●
Students will be assigned to a cohort in the high school.  The cohorts
 
will be done based on the high school schedule. These will be Cohort
 
A and Cohort B. Cohorts should generally have no more than 13
 
students.
 
●
Classrooms will have been disinfected before student arrival.
 
Students will have an assigned seat depending on which cohort they
 
are assigned to in order to keep up to six feet of space between
 
student and teacher. Cohort A and Cohort B will have different
 
student desk assignments. Example:  25 desks in a classroom Cohort
 
A is assigned to 13 desks 6 feet apart, Cohort B is assigned to a
 
separate 13 desks six feet apart.
 
●
When  students change classes they will sit at desks that were not
 
occupied by the prior class..  This allows for transitioning students to
 
always be seated at a disinfected desk.  Upon exiting a classroom,
 
students are dismissed by seat, use hand sanitizer, the teacher sprays
 
the row that was occupied by the cohort, row by row. The next cohort
 
comes in and sits at desks that were not occupied by students of the
 
last class.
 
●
No more than two educators should be assigned to a classroom
 
cohort. If an additional educator is needed it will need administrative
 
approval.
 
●
Monitor classroom environment and immediately report to
 
administration if a student violates the mask or social distancing
 
guidelines.
 
●
Bins will be provided in the hallways to drop off micro fibers provided
 
for classroom cleaning and returned to the classroom.

●
Physical education classes will have further guidelines in order to
 
keep students and teachers safe. Gym classes will happen outside
 
when weather allows.  Students will need to be 10 feet from each
 
other if participating in a strenuous activity. Equipment will be
 
disinfected in between cohorts of students. Classes may happen
 
inside with appropriate spacing and activity level for indoor gym.

Band and Chorus classes will be following the DESE guidelines and

●
Art classes will be following DESE guidelines and adapting their class

Arrival &
 
Dismissal
 
Bus
 
●
When students arrive on the bus, they will be directed by an adult to
 
exit their bus two buses at a time.  For example, bus 1 and bus 10 will
 
exit the bus and enter the building. This staggered exit of the bus
 
keeps large amounts of students from forming.  Face coverings must
 
be worn at all times.
 
●
Once off the bus, students will be directed to enter the building
 
through three separate entrances. Students with 1st period classes on
 
the first floor, which includes gym, art, health and music  will enter
 
through the main entrance on the high school side.. Students with 1st
 
period classes on the second floor will enter the building through the
 
high school staff entrance (located at the end of the high school wing
 
next to the student parent drop off loop).
 
 
Dismissal
 
●
Bus students will be dismissed first, by classes in a staggered fashion
 
to allow for social distancing. After bus students are di​
smissed,
 
student drivers, walkers and pickups will be dismissed in a staggered
 
fashion.
 
 
Pick Up & Drop
 
●
Parent drop will continue in the drop off circle adjacent to the bus
 
circle with students from different families remaining 6 ft. apart..
 
After buses leave the bus circle, an administrator will signal that it is
 
ok for parents to enter the circle to drop off students.
 
Teaching &
 
Learning
 
●
The full scope and sequence of the curriculum will continue to be
 
taught in all subjects.  Social-emotional learning components will
 
also be embedded.
 
===PAGE 4===
●
Hybrid and remote instruction will be rich and rigorous in order for
 
students to engage in the curriculum.  New instruction/material
 
must be included each day.
 
●
Work/activities/assignments will be graded traditionally and grades
 
should be recorded in PowerSchool.
 
●
Teachers will be responsible to plan in-person, hybrid, and remote
 
lessons as needed. Best practices from remote and in-person
 
teaching and learning will be combined to create a blended
 
experience for students.
 
 
●
It is strongly encouraged that work/activities/assignments be created
 
in concert with teachers’ shared common
 
classes/department/subject area colleagues in order to create
 
common learning experiences for students and enhance unity and
 
support among teachers.
 
 
●
All educators will use Google Classroom as the platform through
 
which  to teach and connect with students.  Google Classrooms must
 
be created prior to the first day of school and students must be
 
invited to the Classrooms by the first day of school.
 
●
Administrators should be invited to be teaching members of Google
 
Classrooms in order for them to be able to visit Classrooms remotely,
 
provide support, and help maintain consistency across grade
 
levels/departments.
 
●
Google Meets with each class are required according to the building
 
schedule on Monday and applicable cohort days.
 
●
All teachers are required to schedule Google meet office hours for
 
remote cohort Tuesday-Friday students. The Google Meet time the
 
teacher may provide instruction, guide students with remote
 
learning experiences and assist students in problem solving while
 
remote learning. Teachers will provide times of hybrid Google Meets
 
to administration in order to establish a schedule for students and
 
families.  These meets should be scheduled during common
 
planning prep time. Alternate times will be considered by the
 
administration.
 
●
Students should have their video turned on during all Meets.
 
●
Recorded teacher videos (which do not include students or student
 
participation) may be created and posted as a form of instruction.
 
●
Attendance must be taken each day for both in-person and remote
 
cohorts.
 
●
Links to apps/platforms and subscription-based programs can be
 
used, however they must be district approved and linked through
 
Google Classroom.
 
 
●
Assignments/instruction for the remote cohorts must be posted prior
 
to the start of the school day.
 
●
Communication with parents and students is part of a teachers
 
professional responsibility. Teachers will respond to parents and
 
students in a timely manner, within 2 school days of receiving
 
communication.

Lunch
 
●
Staff members must remain at least 6 feet apart when they are
 
eating lunch.
 
●
Students will be eating lunch in the cafeteria. We will utilize the
 
cafeteria to 25% of its occupancy (70 students). Tables have been
 
marked where students may sit six feet apart and facing in the same
 
direction. Masks will be removed and placed on a napkin, the outside
 
facing down, until finished eating when the mask is placed back on.
 
●
We will utilize outdoor seating and outdoor grassy areas to sit and
 
eat a bagged lunch when weather permits us to do so. Students will
 
be allowed to enter the recess yard after they finish eating. Students
 
may take a mask break as long as they adhere to the 6 feet of
 
distance from other students and staff.
 
===PAGE 6===
●
Transitions from lunch will mostly happen outside the school
 
building, weather permitting, with staff supervision back to academic
 
or special area classes.
 
●
Seats and tables will be disinfected by staff in between lunch periods.
 
●
During the hybrid model, teachers should wear clothing that enables
 
them to feel comfortable, while maintaining a professional culture.
 
Hospital pants or scrubs will be allowed.
