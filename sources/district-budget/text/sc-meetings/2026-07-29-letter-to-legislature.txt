Senate President Karen SpilkaSpeaker Ron MarianoChairman Michael RodriguesChairman Aaron Michlewitz

Re: H5586 An Act Making Appropriations for Fiscal Year 2027 to Supplement Certain Existing Appropriations and for Certain Other Activities and Projects 

Dear President Spilka, Speaker Mariano, Chair Rodrigues, and Chair Michlewitz:
On behalf of the Lunenburg School Committee, we respectfully and urgently request your support and swift passage of H.5586, An Act Making Appropriations for Fiscal Year 2027 to Supplement Certain Existing Appropriations and for Certain Other Activities and Projects. 

Specifically, we urge you to swiftly approve the Per Pupil increase proposed by Governor Healey and maintain current legislative language that sends these funds directly to school districts with local expenditure decisions. As the nation's leader in public education, we owe it to our students, educators, and communities to invest in their future.

For Lunenburg Public Schools, these funds would provide essential relief to protect baseline services and support our students' immediate needs. Our district has cut staff positions for the last three years and is on the brink of disaster.  Our class sizes from 1 to 5th grade have ballooned to almost 30 students. 

With the start of the 2026–2027 school year just weeks away, districts are making final staffing and programming decisions. Immediate action on your part to pass H.5586 would allow hundreds of districts to put these funds to work right away. 

Please help move Massachusetts' public schools from a state of survival back to one of strength and excellence.  Thank you for your consideration and your continued support of public education.

Sincerely,


Laura Brzozinski, Chair
Lunenburg School Committee
Lunenburg, MA
