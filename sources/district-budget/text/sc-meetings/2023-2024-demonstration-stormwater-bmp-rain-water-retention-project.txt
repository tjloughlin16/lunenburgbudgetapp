• Stormwater is runoff from rainfall and snow melt and represents 
the single largest source of water quality impairments in the 
Commonwealth’s rivers, lakes, ponds, and marine waters. 
• BMP – Best Management Practice
• BMP’s are devices and actions that improve or prevent the 
pollution of urban runoff and stormwater.
• BMP’s are designed so that post-development peak discharge rates 
do not exceed pre-development peak discharge rates.
• BMP’s are designed to remove 80% of the average annual post-
construction load of Total Suspended Solids (TSS).
