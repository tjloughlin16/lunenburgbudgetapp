===PAGE 6===
Mission
LEAF will be a citizen-led non-profit catalyst that enhances academic
excellence in Lunenburg schools by connecting corporate and private
sector funds as grants to educators seeking to implement innovative
learning opportunities for the K-12 students of our town.
Grants will support creative curricular development beyond the scope of
the typical school budget. Creative ideas reflect alternative approaches to
typical instruction and incorporate tools or methodologies not currently
available in Lunenburg classrooms.

===PAGE 7===
What Could Be Funded
Innovative, academically related projects outside the scope of current
curricular implementation that align with the foundation’s mission
Enrichment opportunities that are above and beyond regular, remedial or
required study
Single-teacher/classroom initiatives to larger scale, multi-teacher projects that
reach multiple grade levels/schools
