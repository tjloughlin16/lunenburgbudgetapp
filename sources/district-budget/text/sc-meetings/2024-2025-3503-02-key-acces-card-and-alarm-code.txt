KEY/ACCESS CARD AND ALARM CODE       3503.02 
 
Keys, access cards, and alarm codes will be issued to school employees for Lunenburg School 
facilities on an as needed basis. These items are issued to individuals directly for their sole use 
and are not to be transferred to another individual. Keys and access cards shall be returned to 
the building key custodian  Director of Facilities when no longer needed or at the request of the 
Superintendent or their designee.  
 
Alarm codes are issued by the Director of Facilities and will be removed from the alarm 
system when an individual no longer has access to a school facility. 
 
 Master keys are to be issued by the Director of Facilities to School Administrators and 
Custodial/Maintenance Staff and will be returned to the Director when no longer needed. Sign 
out logs on keys shall be maintained by all key custodians and shall be checked by the 
Director.  
 
 
 
 
Approved by the School Committee: 6-20-18
