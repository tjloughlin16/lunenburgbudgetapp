June 13. 2022 
School Lunch Price increase for school year 2022 - 2023 
 
 
 
 On June 30 2022 the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA) pandemic school meal waivers 
that allowed our lunch program to serve meals to students at no charge will end. This change means 
that school year 2022 -2023 Lunenburg School Food Service will return to normal operational 
procedures. The USDA Food and Nutrition service released the new Paid Lunch Equity Tool this tool is 
part of the yearly renewal packet for the Nation School Lunch Program (NSLP) it helps districts access 
lunch prices for compliancy with NSLP regulations. Consequently Lunenburg school meal prices are not 
compliant, we are 31 cents below required meal price. 
Suggested Price Increase- $0.50 across the district setting our price at $3.50 slightly above required price 
to push out future price increases another 2 to 3 years. 
 We should also consider increasing the adult meal prices $1.00 making it $3.00 for and adult breakfast 
$5.00 for an adult lunch. 
 
 
Respectfully Submitted, 
 
Nadine Lorenzen 
Food Service Director
