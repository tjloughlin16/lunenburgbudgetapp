 Below is a brief summary of our School Improvement Plan to date: 
 School Improvement Plan (2024-2026) Summary – Year 2 
 Building Strategic Goal #1: To promote a deeper learning  of the HMH literacy curriculum and  increase 
 the number of  students to the pathway to proﬁciency in literacy through implementation of  MTSS, 
 speciﬁcally students who have an IEP and scored 1-2  grade levels below on the fall benchmark iReady 
 assessment and signiﬁcantly below (red) on DIBELS. for the 2025-2026 we are looking at DIBELS for all 
 three grade levels, iRead was cut due to budget limitations. 
 In Year 2 of our plan, we continue to use DIBELS 8th Edition to assess early literacy skills in 
 place of iReady. Beginning-of-year benchmark data show that across Kindergarten through 
 Grade 2, students fall into three primary groups: those meeting or exceeding grade-level 
 benchmarks, those needing strategic support, and those requiring intensive foundational 
 instruction. Data from DIBELS guide our  Deep Data Dives, where teams analyze results, form 
 intervention groups, and plan next instructional steps. To support literacy growth, we are 
 providing professional development on HMH v3, structured literacy, small-group instruction, and 
 progress monitoring, along with literacy coaching and targeted PD for PreK and special 
 education teachers. These coordinated efforts aim to close early literacy gaps and strengthen 
 Tier I instruction across classrooms. 
 Building Strategic Goal #2:  To promote a deeper learning of the HMH math curriculum and  increase 
 the number of  students to the pathway to proﬁciency in math through implementation of  MTSS , 
 speciﬁcally students who have anIEP and/or scored 1-2 more grade levels below on fall benchmark 
 iReady and well below basic or basic below on the Forefront Universal Screeners for Number Sense. 
 For math, we are using the Universal Screener for Number Sense (USNS) to assess students’ 
 foundational math skills and identify specific areas for intervention. Data from USNS guide our 
 MTSS Deep Data Dives, where teams analyze results, form intervention groups, and plan next 
 instructional steps.  Our data shows that incoming K–2 students are well-prepared, indicating 
 strong mathematical instruction and routines at those levels, while a dip in readiness among 
 incoming third graders highlights the need for more consistent instructional practices across 
 grade levels. This year, professional development and monthly staff meetings will continue to 
 focus on building coherence in math instruction, deepening teachers’ understanding of number 
 sense, and using USNS data to inform instructional adjustments and intervention planning. 
 Building Strategic Goal #3:  Improve students, staﬀ  and families' sense of belonging by strengthening 
 school culture and climate, implementing a social/emotional curriculum and learning about inclusive 
 and culturally responsive instruction.

 We continue to strengthen the sense of belonging among students, staff, and families through 
 the implementation of a social-emotional learning curriculum (trails to wellness), professional 
 learning focused on inclusive and culturally responsive instruction, and ongoing collaboration 
 with community partners to enhance safety and well-being. Three members of the leadership 
 team became ALICE trainers last year, and this year, we are engaging staff in scenario-based 
 safety training while maintaining strong partnerships with the fire and police departments. We 
 also use regular staff and family surveys to gather feedback that informs ongoing improvement 
 efforts in climate, culture, and communication.
