Goal 3: Transparent, Efficient, and Effective Communication 
Objective: 
To improve communication by reviewing and revising policies and/or protocols that enhance 
transparency, efficiency, and effectiveness between the school committee, superintendent, and the 
public. 
Action Steps: 
Policy and Protocol Review: Workshop and revise communication policies and/or protocols, focusing on 
increasing efficiency, reducing misinformation, and fostering transparency. 
Benchmarks: 
Successful implementation of the new communication policies and/or protocols.
