SUBJECT:  Lunenburg Public Schools, Scoreboard Replacement 
 
To: All Bidders 
 
The Lunenburg Public Schools (LPS) hereby invites the submission of bids from 
responsive and responsible vendors to replace and provide a new football scoreboard in 
Lunenburg, MA, as described in the Invitation For Bids (IFB) packet.  The scope of work 
includes removal of the existing All American Scoreboard and the installation of new scoreboard 
per specifications. 
 
IFQ’s will be available beginning Wednesday, May 04, 2022; vendors are asked to email 
mcassidy@lunenburgonline.com to obtain a copy.  Bids will be accepted by mail or in person to 
the Lunenburg Public Schools, 1025 Massachusetts Avenue, Business Office, Lunenburg, MA 
until 11:00 a.m. local time on Thursday, May 19, 2022.  Please return your bid using the format 
on the specifications.  Installation work must be completed no later than June 30, 2022.  
 
To schedule a visit to the site please contact me by e-mail at 
mcassidy@lunenburgonline.com or call me. If you have questions on this bid, please call me at 
(978) 582-4100.
