Transforming 
Teaching & 
Learning
OBJECTIVE: To modernize teaching and learning by establishing effective teams of educational leaders to accomplish the essential 
work for transforming practice, and hiring and developing excellent educators who are continually learning and adapting practice to 
meet the needs of all students.
Priority 1: Establish effective teams to lead the essential 
work of teaching and learning, including development of 
high-quality, rigorous, relevant
and enriching curricula, with a priority focus on 
literacy and social-emotional learning; 
identification and communication of expectations for
demonstration of high-quality teaching and learning 
competencies; and implementation of effective 
instructional practices, interventions,
communication, and professional development.
Priority 2: Establish a districtwide rigorous 
and multi-phased hiring process to recruit, 
select, develop and retain highly skilled, 
student-centered teachers and 
administrators, and other personnel with 
the capacity to contribute to the forward 
progress of the organization.
===PAGE 8===
Cultivating 
Climate & 
Culture
OBJECTIVE: To create a culture that values data use and continuous learning in a climate that is safe, caring, and inclusive and 
reflective of our core values.
Priority 1: Foster a climate of safety, caring and 
inclusivity for all in our district through secure 
facilities and safety training, evidence-based social 
emotional and mental health curricula, and 
building strong staff-student relationships, student 
peer relationships, staff peer relationships, 
administrator-student/staff relationships, school-family 
relationships.
Priority 2: Foster a culture of learning and data 
use where students, faculty, staff and administrators 
analyze, synthesize, and apply knowledge and skill to 
work collaboratively to solve problems, think critically, 
and adjust practice; learn collaboratively from each other 
and from mistakes; engage deeply in reflective practice; 
and justify allocation of resources needed to ensure a 
high-quality interactive deeper learning 
experience with appropriate levels of support 
and challenge for all students.

===PAGE 10===
Communication 
& Operations
OBJECTIVE: To improve operational efficiencies, including communication within the district and with the community, in order to 
decrease time spent on management and increase time spent on instructional leadership. 
Priority 1: Improve operational efficiencies, 
including communication within the district between 
administration and teams of faculty and staff; with 
families and with the community, in order to improve 
shared responsibilities to meet student needs, decrease 
time spent on clerical tasks and management, and 
increase time spent on innovation and instructional 
leadership, including allocation of resources and 
reorganization of roles and responsibilities.

Equity, Access, 
Achievement & 
Opportunity 
for ALL 
Students 
OBJECTIVE: To ensure all students are provided equal access to a quality education, providing them the appropriate support and 
challenge so that they can become skilled life-long learners.
Priority 2: Evaluate the current status of equity 
across the district, including continuity and 
consistency of practice and process, and outline 
appropriate actions necessary to improve equity, 
access, and opportunity for all students so that 
they may realize their fullest potential.
===PAGE 12===
What is Equity?
The term “equity” refers to fairness 
and justice and is distinguished from 
equality: Whereas equality means 
providing the same to all, equity 
means recognizing that we do not all 
start from the same place and must 
acknowledge and make adjustments 
to imbalances. The process is 
ongoing, requiring us to identify and 
overcome intentional and 
unintentional barriers arising from bias 
or systemic structures.

===PAGE 14===
The Mission of the Lunenburg Public Schools is to provide all students with the 
skills, confidence, and passion for life-long learning so that they may find 
their own paths to successful careers, active citizenship, and rewarding 
lives.
Vision:The Lunenburg Public Schools is committed to its values of 
attending to the personal well-being of its students, providing 
quality instruction at all levels, and promoting life-long 
learning for all.
