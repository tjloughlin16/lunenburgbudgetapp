Goal 2: (District Improvement) Student-Centered Budget Development In 
order to ensure that resources are maximized to achieve highest impact on teaching 
and learning, and meeting the needs of all students; the District Leadership Team will 
work collaboratively from October 2020 through April 2021 to identify and evaluate 
district needs; present a prioritized list of resources to meet needs; and determine 
which items on the prioritized needs list will be incorporated, with justifications and 
data, in the Superintendent’s Recommended FY22 Budget.

Goal 3: Student Learning: Ensuring Student Access to Learning Given that we 
are uncertain about the academic, social and emotional impact of Spring 2020 remote 
learning on our students, and in order to (1) engage all students in learning, (2) meet 
their academic, social and emotional needs, and (3) demonstrate they are performing 
on grade-level/developmentally appropriately and making progress in closing any 
learning gaps; our goal is to utilize instructional and assessment tools to (1) create 
accessible lessons/services; (2) establish baseline data where needed, and (3) use data 
to make adjustments to practice; (4) use data to demonstrate appropriate student 
performance and/or growth, regardless of learning model (hybrid or remote), through 
the 2020-21 school-year.

Goal 4: Professional Practice Goal: Providing Engaging and Effective 
Instruction  In order to provide engaging and effective instruction in a hybrid or 
remote learning environment, we will (1) engage in professional development and 
trainings to learn how to utilize new tools and strategies; (2) work collaboratively 
with colleagues in the planning and preparation of instructional materials; (3) 
implement new teaching tools and strategies; and (4) review data and feedback to 
make adjustments to practice.  We will monitor progress on this goal using student 
participation and growth/performance data through the 2020-21 school-year.
