SUBJECT: Capital Improvement Project Update 
 
Below you will find a status update on the Capital Projects that have been approved and funded. 
LMHS Turf Field Feasibility Study (Phase 1) – This Capital Approved project for $50,000 will allow 
Lunenburg Public Schools to engage with a Civil Engineering Study to outline the drainage 
problems on and around the perimeter of the existing turf field.  This will also include drainage 
issues leading to the failure of the small block retaining wall and walkway as well as the Bin-block 
retaining wall supporting the press box.  This project has gone out to bid and I have Request for 
Written Quotes to be back by February 9, 2024.  The estimated start date will be February 26, 
2024 and estimated completion date of July 31, 2024.  At this time I am currently awaiting bid 
responses. 
2/9/24 – Update – My February 9th noon deadline has passed and I have received one quote from 
Collins Engineers Inc. for $49,990.00.  The drainage study will be approached as four distinct areas 
as follows:  
1. The Upper Area which includes the new High/Middle School and associated drive. 
2. The Retaining Walls Area, which includes the concrete Bin-Block retaining wall, and the 
CMU block wall adjacent to the track. 
3. The Playing Field Area, including the track. 
4. The Lower Grass Open Area, located east of the track. 
I will be working on the evaluating the next steps in the process. 
3/6/24 – Update – I am drafting an award letter for Collins Engineers, Inc and I will be submitting 
a Requisition for the $49,990.00. 
3/22/24 – Signed Contract with Collins Engineers and will they will be assembling a team to work 
on the project and will set the first meeting soon. 
4/3/24 – I had the kick off meeting with Collins Engineers to start reviewing the drainage issues at 
the LMHS Turf Field.

5/22/24 – Update – Collins Engineers was on-site 4/28 for a Site Inspection & Schematic Sketch.  
There was a Drone deployed over the Turf Field and GPS was used to identify the drainage ports 
and elevations.  By using the the GPS coordinates they are able to estimate the pitch of the pipes.  
During the last rain storm that we had, I was able to capture the water accumulation on and 
around the Turf Field which was very helpful in there analysis.  We have 5/29/24 as the tentative 
date to have our collaboration Meetings to discuss the findings.

perimeter of the school.  I am currently working on the Invitation to Bid documents and will be 
sent out to bid soon.
