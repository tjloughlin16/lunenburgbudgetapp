DESE Diversification Strategy
◦ Provide tangible financial support and incentives to recruit and retain teachers of color;
◦ Proactively advertise the benefits of becoming educators (including the financial incentives) to 
prospective teachers of color
◦ Improve the experience students and teachers of color are having in our state’s K-12 schools.
===PAGE 6===
MTA Diversification Strategy
◦ Improve the experience students and teachers of color are having in our state’s K-12 schools.
◦ Support legislation, specifically “An Act relative to educator diversity”, that will help grow, recruit and 
retain educators of color and educators from varied backgrounds to better reflect our students, who 
themselves represent ever more diverse backgrounds.
◦ Create spaces and opportunities for educators to connect with colleagues both inside and outside of the 
school setting
◦ Develop a climate and culture that is supportive for students and educators, particularly educators who 
are joining us from outside of the district and/or from diverse backgrounds

===PAGE 10===
Staff Retention Strategy
Current model: one mentor in each building and one special education mentor 
districtwide, Ratio 1:9
Pilot a new model of teacher mentorship
• Teachers/licensed professionals in first year will have a 1:1 mentor ( in some cases 1:2 ) 
• Teachers/licensed professionals in 2 and 3 attend facilitated sessions throughout the year; this is designed to build 
a Professional Learning Community (PLC) where protocols (consultancy, looking at student work, tuning, etc) will 
be used to facilitate reflective practice.
• Experienced teachers new to our district will participate in orientation and will be provided a list of staff available 
for specific types of support as they acclimate to our school community.
• Mentor training will be provided for new mentors
