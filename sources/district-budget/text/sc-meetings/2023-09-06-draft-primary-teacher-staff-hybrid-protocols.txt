●
All PPE protocols and requirements must be adhered to.
 
●

●
Staff must wear a mask/face covering and maintain 6 feet of social

●

will be replenished as needed to mitigate risk.

be stickers placed in the hallways that indicate six feet of space in
 
between students in order to assist with distancing.
 
●
Hand sanitizer will be available at each building entrance, and staff
 
will be monitoring each entrance upon student arrival and departure.
 
Students will use hand sanitizer upon entrance into the building.
 
●
Extra masks will be available for distribution at each entryway if
 
needed.
 
●
Cubbies will not be used by students. Items will be stored in the
 
classroom or on designated tables outside of class in defined spaces.
 
 
●
Classroom teachers will teach and include in their daily schedule
 
regular hand washing and sanitizing routines. This includes before
 
and after bathroom use. Before leaving the classroom and upon

Mask Breaks
 
●
Teachers will utilize at least two mask breaks a day.
 
 
●
Individual student mask breaks can take place outside the classroom
 
in the cubby hallways or on 6 foot markers in hallways.
 
●
In addition to these mask breaks, homerooms attending special area
 
classes are going to transition outside the building, six feet apart, for
 
an additional two to four mask breaks a day. This will be written into
 
classroom schedules.
 
●
Students are not allowed to take mask breaks in the classroom.
 
 
Water Breaks/Snack Breaks
 
●
Water breaks can be taken during scheduled mask breaks or can be
 
requested by a student on an individual basis in the hallway, on a six
 
foot dot away from peers.
 
●
Snack breaks will be built into classroom schedules, using outdoor
 
spaces as weather permits, or indoor spaces that are marked off at 6
 
foot distances in the cafeteria. Grade levels will coordinate the
 
scheduling in the cafeteria.
 
Classrooms
 
●
Students will be assigned to a cohort of learners.
 
 
●
The first cohort will be the traditional classroom. During the hybrid
 
model operation students will be further split into cohorts to keep
 
numbers of students in classrooms small. These will be Cohort A
 
and Cohort B. Cohorts should generally have no more than 14
 
students depending on the overall class size. Grade 2 has the
 
highest enrollment. In order to accommodate newly identified
 
high needs remote and 2 day students returning to school or
 
increasing attendance we need to increase the cap size by 1.
 
Careful consideration will be  given to the overall physical
 
dimensions of the classroom and the current population of the
 
classroom.
 
 
●
Classrooms will have been disinfected before student arrival. Students
 
will have an assigned seat depending on which cohort they are
 
assigned to in order to keep up to 6’ feet of space between student
 
and teacher.
 
 
●
If a student requires a decreased staff proximity, PPE is available and
 
will be replenished as needed to mitigate risk.
 
 
●
Student transitions will be kept to a minimum with the exceptions of
 
recess, mask breaks, lunch, and PE and special services as needed

●
Due to safety and protocol monitoring concerns, these student
 
transitions will use a decreased physical distancing of 3 feet as the
 
situation dictates.
 
●
Teachers will actively monitor the classroom environment and
 
immediately report to administration if a student violates the mask or
 
social distancing guidelines.
 
●
Bins will be provided in the hallways to drop off micro fibers provided
 
for classroom cleaning, cleaned and returned to the classroom.
 
 
●
Special area teachers, art, music, tech/library, will travel to classrooms.
 
●

Physical education classes will have further guidelines in order to

disinfected in between cohorts of students. Classes may happen
 
inside with appropriate spacing and activity level for indoor gym.

Bus
 
●
When students arrive on the bus, they will be directed by an adult to
 
exit their bus two buses at a time.  For example, bus 1 and bus 10 will
 
exit the bus and enter the building. This staggered exit of the bus
 
keeps large amounts of students from forming.  Face coverings must
 
be worn at all times.
 
●
Once off the bus, students will be directed to enter the building
 
through three separate entrances.
 
 
●
Second grade drop offs and buses will enter through the bus loop
 
entrance and proceed up the bus loop stairwell to their classrooms.
 
 
●
Second grade staff will be in hallways monitoring student transition to
 
ensure physical distancing and mask compliance.
 
●
First grade will walk through Pre -School  Playground to the Library
 
door and then proceed up the library stairs to their classrooms.
 
●
During inclement weather First grade will enter through the bus loop
 
entrance and keep left down the  Pre- School hallway to the library
 
door.
 
●
 First grade staff will be positioned along the route to the first grade
 
classes to ensure physical distancing and mask compliance.
 
●
Kindergarten children will enter through the bus loop entrance and
 
move to the right of the Pre-School  hallway to their classrooms.
 
Kindergarten staff will be positioned along the route to ensure
 
physical distancing and mask compliance.
 
●
Drop offs will queue up in the drop- off loop.

Dismissal
 
●
Students being picked up will be called down to the cafeteria.
 
They will use the lunch seating and will be monitored by a staff
 
member with a radio.
 
 
●
Parents will form a pick- up line with placards of the student's last
 
name clearly visible. The staff on pick up duty will radio the
 
students names in the same order as the vehicles (wordage)
 
●
Students will leave the cafeteria at 6 ft intervals and proceed to
 
their vehicles. Once all the cars are loaded we will move the line
 
up. And repeat the process.
 
●
Parents will need to stay in their vehicles.

●
The full scope and sequence of the curriculum will continue to be
 
taught in all subjects.  Social-emotional learning components will also
 
be embedded.
