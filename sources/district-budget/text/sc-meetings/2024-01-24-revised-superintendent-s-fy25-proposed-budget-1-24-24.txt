Proposal for reducing transportation costs 
Move Extended Day AM and PM out of TCP
THES would be the location for AM Extended Day
● parents drop students (K-5) at THES in the morning● when THES school day begins, Extended Day students in grades 3-5 walk to class and students in grades K-2 board the buses after all THES students get off the buses  ● buses depart THES and take students to Primary School for drop off
Primary School would be the location for PM Extended Day
● buses arrive at THES to pick up students at dismissal● Extended Day students grades 3-5 board the bus● buses depart THES after all bus students board● buses go to Primary School to pick up students (K-2) for delivery back home● Upon arrival at Primary, Extended Day students from THES get off the bus before Primary School students board to be delivered home● parents will pick up students (K-5) at Primary School in the afternoon
still staff one location 
open seats on the bus 
We have asked Dee Bus to see if they can meet the ridership needs with 10 buses.
