Cell Phone policy feedback  
 
After discussing the topic on whether the development of School Committee Policy would be beneficial 
with both LMS and LHS School Advisory councils as well as the LHS Department head leadership, the 
feedback received was pretty consistent. 
 
1. All agree cellphones are distracting to the educational environment in the classroom.  
 
2. All agree that classrooms should be sacred in terms of having no cell phones being used. 
 
 
3. All agreed that procedures developed in individual school handbooks is the best way to address 
cell phone use. It allows parents, teachers and administrators to develop consistent protocols 
that are specific to the developmental needs of the students they serve.  
 
4. Cell phone procedures that are developed and listed in the handbooks are reviewed yearly and 
revisions can be made based upon the needs of the school community.  
 
5. Zero tolerance cell phone policies may affect students with medical needs who require their cell 
phones. It also may affect safety apps (safer watch) that the district have allowed students to 
download on their cell phones and utilize during the school day. Many athletic teams at the high 
school communicate practice or schedule changes via text message through a sports app.  
 
 
6. If school committee decided to write policy about cellphones, all groups agreed that “keeping 
classroom instruction sacred” would be a pillar of what the policy should focus on.
