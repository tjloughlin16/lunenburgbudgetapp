System Monitoring (Accountability)
MCAS (Massachusetts Comprehensive Assessment System)
The Massachusetts accountability system is designed to measure how a school or 
district is doing and what kind of support it may need and  to provide information to the 
community.
DESE strongly encourages districts to:
• Focus on using the results for improvement purposes at the local level
• Connect to local initiatives – use the opportunity to discuss the bigger picture and 
the importance of knowing what the data tell us
Caution should be exercised when making comparisons across Districts and across 
schools within the same district

Universal Screening
Purpose: take stock of students’ achievements or assets and areas where students 
may require support that varies in terms of intensity and duration.
How Use:
• Universal screening starts the discussion about students who may need different 
and/or additional instruction.
• Low scores on the universal screener can be considered a red flag, indicating that 
it would be beneficial to look more closely at the student's skills and progress.

MCAS Student Growth Percentile (SGP)
                          
• SGP in ELA continues to be in the typical range (40-60) for all grade bands over the past 4 
years.
• SGP in mathematics continues to be in the typical range (40-60) for grade 10 over the past

• In grades 3-5, the mean growth has risen from 33 (low growth) to 56 (high typical growth).

Areas for Improvement Next Steps Overview
• Advanced coursework completion at LHS
• Chronic absenteeism at LHS
• ELA Achievement grades 3-8 for all students
• Focus on ELA foundational skills in grades K-2
• Closing achievement gaps in mathematics and ELA for students with disabilities
• Courses: Dyscalculia and Wilson
• Special Educators joining grade level meetings with and Coaches 
• Progress Monitoring student groups with Building  and District Leaders
• Individual and small group mathematics challenge activities
• Explore DESE STE resources and new STE programs that complement the new grades 5 
and 8 STE MCAS performance tasks
• Continue to monitor achievement of ELLs.
