Implicit Bias MIAA Requirement
 On October 27, 2021, the MIAA Board of Directors unanimously 
approved the following proposals for immediate 
implementation:
 1) NFHS Implicit Bias Course and the MIAA DEI Pledge
 In order to gain a better understanding of actions, language and 
behavior which can be interpreted as discriminatory in nature 
and to conﬁrm their commitment to diversity, equity and 
inclusion, all student athletes, coaches and athletic directors 
must take the National Federation of State High School 
Associations (NFHS) free online course entitled Implicit Bias and 
take the MIAA Diversity, Equity and Inclusion Pledge. School 
administrators (coaches and athletic directors) must take the 
course and pledge every school year, prior to the start of the 
academic year. Student-athletes must take the course and 
pledge every year as well, and they must do so prior to the start 
of their athletic season.
 MIAA Pledge
 Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Pledge
 During the winter season this pledge was signed off by the 
athletes and turned into the athletic ofﬁce. 
 For the spring season and moving forward, as part of the athletic 
registration the athletes and parents verify that they have taken 
the NFHS Free Implicit Bias course and have read and agree to 
the MIAA Pledge. 
 NFHS Course
 Implicit biases are the automatic reactions we have toward other 
people based on our past learning and expectations. Studies 
show that Implicit Bias affects the experiences of students in 
school athletic and activity programs. The NFHS has partnered 
with Project Implicit to bring you this course, Implicit Bias. 
Studies support that there is a relationship between implicit bias 
and real-world behavior, which highlights the importance of 
being aware of and managing your bias. This course deﬁnes and 
illustrates examples of Implicit Bias and provides research on 
how it impacts our society. (NFHS Course Descrption)

Athlete Tryout Evaluation Sample
 Each sport being different by nature, sport speciﬁc or program speciﬁc assessment may vary 
by each team.
 General skills to be evaluated: Running (duration), stamina, speed, agility
 Sport speciﬁc skills i.e. ball/stick handling, use of non dominant hand/foot, catching/receiving 
 In game communication
 Positioning / Understanding of the game
 Rubric: 1) does not show skill, does not try. Not game ready. 2)still developing skills, basic level, 
skill needs to be practiced to be game ready. 3) Developing/moderate level of skill, practice 
needed, average and game ready. 4) Above average skill is, game ready now. 5) Exemplary level 
of skill, not only game ready now, but is able to model skills for other players.
