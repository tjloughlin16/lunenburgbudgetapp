Safety
 
●
All PPE protocols and requirements must be adhered to.
 
●
Masks/face coverings will be required for all staff and students in all
 
grades.  These face coverings should be worn at all times (outside of
 
mask breaks.)
 
 
●
Staff must wear a mask/face covering and maintain 6 feet of social
 
distancing between themselves and other staff and students.
 
●
There will be signs at entrances to the building reminding students of
 
the safety protocols that are in place to keep everyone safe. There will
 
be stickers placed in the hallways that indicate six feet of space in
 
between students in order to assist with distancing.
 
●
Hand sanitizer will be available at each building entrance, and staff will
 
be monitoring each entrance upon student arrival and departure..
 
Students will use hand sanitizer upon entrance into the building.
 
●
Lockers will not be used by students in order to minimize passing
 
time.
 
Mask Breaks
 
●
If a student is attending class and suddenly is in need of an individual
 
mask break, they will be allowed to enter the hallway, stand on one of
 
the blue “six feet apart” stickers, remove their mask for a quick mask
 
break and then return to class with the mask back on the nose and
 
mouth. No mask breaks are allowed in the classroom.
 
●
Students are not allowed to take mask breaks in the classroom.

Water Breaks
 
●
Water breaks can be taken during scheduled mask breaks or can be
 
requested by a student on an individual basis in the hallway, on a six
 
foot dot away from peers.
 
Classrooms
 
●
Students will be assigned to a cohort in the high school.  The cohorts
 
will be done based on the high school schedule. These will be Cohort A
 
and Cohort B. Cohorts should generally have no more than 13
 
students.
 
●
Classrooms will have been disinfected before student arrival. Students
 
will have an assigned seat depending on which cohort they are
 
assigned to in order to keep up to six feet of space between student
 
and teacher. Cohort A and Cohort B will have different student desk
 
assignments. Example:  25 desks in a classroom Cohort A is assigned to
 
12 desks 6 feet apart, Cohort B is assigned to a separate 12 desks six
 
feet apart.
 
●
When  students change classes they will sit at desks that were not
 
occupied by the prior class..  This allows for transitioning students to
 
always be seated at a disinfected desk.  Upon exiting a classroom,
 
students are dismissed by row, use hand sanitizer, the teacher sprays
 
the row that was occupied by the cohort, row by row. The next cohort
 
comes in and sits at desks that were not occupied by students of the
 
last class.
 
●
No more than two educators should be assigned to a classroom
 
cohort.
 
●
Monitor classroom environment and immediately report to
 
administration if a student violates the mask or social distancing
 
guidelines.
 
●
Bins will be provided in the hallways to drop off micro fibers provided
 
for classroom cleaning and returned to the classroom.
 
 
●
Physical education classes will have further guidelines in order to keep
 
students and teachers safe. Gym classes will happen outside when
 
weather allows, students will need to be 10 feet from each other if
 
participating in a strenuous activity. Equipment will be disinfected in
 
between cohorts of students. Classes may happen inside with
 
appropriate spacing and activity level for indoor gym.
 
 
●
Band and Chorus classes will be following the DESE guidelines and
 
adapting their class protocols to follow those guidelines.
 
 
Arrival &
 
Dismissal
 
Bus
 
●
When students arrive on the bus, they will be directed by an adult to
 
exit their bus two buses at a time.  For example, bus 1 and bus 10 will
 
===PAGE 3===
exit the bus and enter the building. This staggered exit of the bus
 
keeps large amounts of students from forming.  Face coverings must
 
be worn at all times.
 
●
Once off the bus, students will be directed to enter the building
 
through three separate entrances. Students with 1st period classes on
 
the first floor, which includes gym, art, tech and music  will enter
 
through the main entrance on the high school side.. Students with 1st
 
period classes on the top floor will enter the building through the high
 
school staff entrance (located at the end of the high school wing next
 
to the student parent drop off loop).
 
 
Dismissal
 
●
Bus students will be dismissed first, by classes in a staggered fashion
 
to allow for social distancing. After bus students are dismissed, student
 
drivers, walkers and pickups will be dismissed in a staggered fashion.
 
 
Pick Up & Drop
 
●
Parent drop will continue in the drop off circle adjacent to the bus
 
circle with students from different families remaining 6 ft. apart.. After
 
buses leave the bus circle, an administrator will signal that it is ok for
 
parents to enter the circle to drop off students.
 
Teaching &
 
Learning
 
●
The full scope and sequence of the curriculum will continue to be
 
taught in all subjects.  Social-emotional learning components will also
 
be embedded.
 
●
Hybrid instruction will be rich and rigorous in order for students to
 
engage in the curriculum.  New instruction must be included each
 
day.
 
●
Work/activities/assignments will be graded traditionally, and grades
 
should be recorded in PowerSchool.
 
●
Teachers will be responsible to plan in-person and livestreaming
 
lessons. Best practices for live-streaming remote students and
 
in-person teaching will be used.
 
 
●
It is strongly encouraged that work/activities/assignments be created
 
in concert with teachers’ shared common classes/department/subject
 
area colleagues in order to create common learning experiences for
 
students and enhance unity and support among teachers.
 
 
●
All educators will use Google Classroom as the platform through
 
which  to teach and connect with students.  Google Classrooms must
 
be created prior to the first day of school and students must be invited
 
to the Classrooms by the first day of school.
 
●
Administrators should be invited to be teaching members of Google
 
Classrooms in order for them to be able to visit Classrooms remotely,

Lunch
 
●
Staff members must remain at least 6 feet apart when they are eating
 
lunch.
 
●
Students will be eating lunch in the cafeteria. We will utilize the
 
cafeteria to 25% of its occupancy (70 students). Tables have been
 
marked where students may sit six feet apart and facing in the same
 
direction. We will have homeroom assigned seating. Masks will be
 
removed and placed on a napkin, the outside facing down, until
 
finished eating when the mask is placed back on.
 
●
We will utilize outdoor seating and outdoor grassy areas to sit and eat
 
a bagged lunch when weather permits us to do so. Students will be
 
allowed to enter the recess yard after they finish eating. Students may
 
take a mask break as long as they adhere to the 6 feet of distance from
 
other students and staff.
 
●
Transitions from lunch will mostly happen outside the school building,
 
weather permitting, with staff supervision back to academic or special
 
area classes.
 
●
Seats and tables will be disinfected by staff in between lunch periods.
 
●
During the hybrid model, teachers should wear clothing that enables
 
them to feel comfortable, while maintaining a professional culture.
 
Hospital pants or scrubs will be allowed.
