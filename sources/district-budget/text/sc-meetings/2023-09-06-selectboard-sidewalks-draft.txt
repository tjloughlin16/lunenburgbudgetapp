        August 31, 2022 
 
Dear Lunenburg Selectboard, 
 
The Lunenburg School Committee respectfully requests that 
some of the town’s ARPA funds be used for sidewalks.  
 
Many students live near the Turkey Hill Elementary School and 
Lunenburg Middle High School.  Walking to school for many of 
these students is dangerous due to the lack of sidewalks near 
these buildings. 
 
We do understand that Massachusetts Avenue is under state 
control, therefore the town has no jurisdiction over sidewalks 
there.  We are also writing to our state legislators to ask for 
help on Mass. Ave., particularly east of school property.  
 
The town is able to make some of our local roads safer by 
adding sidewalks.  We would be happy to walk these roads 
with members of the Selectboard, DPW, Police or any local 
citizens to check out the viability and necessity of sidewalks 
around our school buildings. 
 
These sidewalks would not only help protect our 
schoolchildren but would add safety to all citizens on our 
streets. 
 
We thank you for your consideration. 
 
Sincerely, 
 
Lunenburg School Committee
