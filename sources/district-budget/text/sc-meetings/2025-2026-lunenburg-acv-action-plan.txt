PRIORITY AREA 1:
SYSTEMS, 
STRUCTURES AND 
SCHEDULING
Freshman students at the high school have limited 
access to Visual Arts and Music classes, often 
being placed in Phys Ed or study halls instead. This 
restricts their exposure to a broader curriculum 
and prevents some students from benefiting from 
creative courses early in their academic journey. 
The goal is to increase student access to a wider 
variety of classes and build an arts pathway.
KEY CONCERNS:
Increase student access to and enrollment in 
Visual Arts and Music courses by ensuring 9th 
graders are informed of their course options, 
establishing a recommendation process for Visual 
Arts similar to Band and Chorus, and implement 
quarter-length classes to broaden creative 
exploration while meeting graduation requirements.
GOAL:
Tier 1: Foundational Access & Awareness
1. Create a proposal to Administration to allow for 
interested students to sign up for Arts Courses 
instead of being required to take 9th grade Phys Ed.
Rationale: This change immediately opens up 
scheduling space for students to access Arts courses 
earlier, addressing the core issue of restricted access.
2. Create a proposal to Administration to allow the 
Middle School Art Teacher to make 
‘recommendations’ in PowerSchool for interested 
students.
Rationale: Builds an arts pathway for students’ 
awareness and encouragement, building upon the 
successful model used for Band/Chorus.
Tier 2: Expanded Curriculum Options
3. Develop quarter classes for Visual Art and Music at 
the High School level.
Rationale: Increases variety and flexibility in arts 
education while giving students a voice and choice in 
the programing and to meet graduation requirements 
in an engaging way.
Tier 3: Long-Term Scheduling Optimization
4. Maximize access to arts and leveled academic classes 
and avoid class conflicts in scheduling.
Rationale: Important for equitable access, but more 
complex and dependent on broader systemic 
scheduling changes.

PRIORITY AREA 2:
TRANSITION FROM 
MIDDLE SCHOOL TO 
HIGH SCHOOL 
ELECTIVES
Make Enrichment classes more meaningful and 
student-centered by offering more student voice 
and choice through shorter, varied options and 
assigning term grades to reflect student effort and 
course value.
KEY CONCERNS:
Ensure Enrichment classes are meaningful and 
student-centered by allowing students to select 
from a wider variety of shorter enrichment options, 
and by assigning a term grade to reflect the value 
and effort put into these courses.
GOAL:
Tier 1: High-Impact Student Engagement
1. Survey students to identify interests for Enrichment 
offerings.
Increases student voice and choice and ensures 
relevant, engaging options.
2. Propose mini Enrichment courses based on survey 
results.
Provides meaningful choice and makes Enrichment 
more student-centered.
Tier 2: Structural Support for Equity & Clarity
1. Develop a grading rubric for Enrichment that includes 
partial attendees (e.g., Band/Chorus).
Ensures fair, consistent evaluation and reinforces 
course value.
2. Establish a communication policy for notifying 
Enrichment teachers of student absences.
Improves planning and fairness in assessment.
Tier 3: Community Building & Integration
1. Engage high school students to share Art class 
experiences with middle school students.
Fosters mentorship and reduces transition anxiety.
2. Integrate Unified Arts teachers into the Freshmen 
Academy.
Promotes equity, continuity and visibility of arts 
education at the high school level. Gives the Arts 
Department level footing with other departments.

PRIORITY AREA 3:
FACULTY RETENTION, 
POSITION and  
PROGRAM FUNDING
Sustaining a strong Arts program requires consistent 
funding for materials, equipment, and full-time 
faculty.. In recent years, the Arts department has 
faced ongoing faculty deficiencies, resulting in limited 
resources and repeated vacancies, particularly in the 
music department. These challenges have weakened 
the program’s ability to deliver high-quality, equitable 
instruction and have hindered student engagement 
and access to meaningful arts experiences.
KEY CONCERNS:
Secure adequate funding to fully staff music 
faculty positions, retain quality educators, equip 
music and visual arts programs, and support 
opportunities like field trips to enhance student 
learning and engagement.
GOAL:
Tier 1: Immediate Impact on Student Experience
1. Enhance Music Booster group funding capacity.
Rationale: Directly supports student opportunities and 
program sustainability through increased access to 
resources and events.
2. Create a Visual Arts booster group to support field trips.
Rationale: Quickly expands experiential learning for visual 
arts students through community support.
Tier 2: Strategic Program Growth & Visibility
3. Create a proposal to generate revenue (e.g., lesson fees, 
art auctions, Arts PD, District-Wide Arts Day).
Rationale: Provides long-term financial support while 
increasing community engagement and visibility of the 
arts.
4. Consider hosting a District-Wide Arts Day to showcase 
the arts department at all levels.
Rationale: Builds school-wide and community 
engagement, awareness and pride in arts programs, 
encouraging ongoing support.
Tier 3: Long-Term Departmental Planning
5. Create a short and long term plan to grow the arts 
department, including feeder programs at every level.
Rationale: Sets a strategic vision for sustainable program 
development across all grades in the district..

PRIORITY AREA 4:
ACTIVE and 
EQUITABLE 
PARTICIPATION IN 
ARTS PROGRAMMING
Younger students have limited access to arts 
engagement opportunities, and there are no 
school-supported community or family arts 
programs. Additionally, upcoming disparities in 
Visual Arts classroom space at the Primary and 
Elementary levels raise concerns about equitable 
access.
KEY CONCERNS:
Develop a district-wide plan to provide Visual and 
Performing Arts programs for families outside of 
school and restore dedicated Art and Music 
classroom spaces at the Primary and Elementary 
levels to ensure equitable, appropriate learning 
environments.
GOAL:
Tier 1: Foundational Access & Learning Environment
1. Work with Administration to restore the Primary and 
Elementary School Art Room and Elementary Music 
rooms for their intended use.
Rationale: Provides young learners with a proper space 
for hands-on, creative exploration and access to a full 
curriculum —critical for early development.
Tier 2: Community & Family Engagement
1. Create Elementary and Preschool family art workshops.
Rationale: Strengthens family-school partnerships and 
nurtures creativity and connection at a formative stage.
3. Develop a collaborative relationship with the Library.
Rationale: Expands access to creative resources and 
community programming that supports arts learning 
beyond the classroom. 
Tier 3: Enrichment & Showcase Opportunities
3. All-school show with craft activities.
Rationale: Builds school spirit, celebrates student 
creativity, and offers hands-on enrichment.
5. Craft workshops (possibly led by NHS students).
Rationale: Encourages peer mentorship and leadership 
while expanding arts exposure.
6. AP & Honors Thesis Show.
Rationale: Valuable for older students to showcase 
work, but impacts a smaller, specialized group.
