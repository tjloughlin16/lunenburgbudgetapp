Why the Airline and Not the Bus?
● Bus driver shortage began in 2019
● ABA regulations on drivers’ hours are the most strict they have ever 
been
● COVID signiﬁcantly affected the bus industry
BENEFITS OF AIR TRAVEL
● Shorter travel time
● Flying is safer than bussing
● More time in DC for the itinerary vs. travel time
● Special experience for students
