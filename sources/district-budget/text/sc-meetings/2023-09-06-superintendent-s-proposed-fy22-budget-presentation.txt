Mission
The Mission of the Lunenburg Public Schools is to provide all students with the skills, 
confidence, and passion for life-long learning so that they may find their own paths to 
successful careers, active citizenship, and rewarding lives.
Vision
The Lunenburg Public Schools is committed to becoming a leader in education by 
focusing on its values of attending to the personal well-being of its students, providing 
high-quality curricula and student-centered instruction for deeper learning at all levels, 
and promoting the development of attributes and skills necessary for continuous 
learning for all.
===PAGE 3===
To ensure teaching and learning are student-centered 
and inclusive by establishing effective teams of 
educational leaders to accomplish the essential work

To improve operational efficiencies, including 
communication, in order to increase the organization’s 
capacity to execute the district’s mission and vision. (DSP

high-quality education, that is collaborative, interactive, 
and relevant with an appropriate level of support and 
challenge so that they can become well-rounded, resilient, 
confident, and capable young adults adequately prepared 
for future challenges. (DSP Goals 1, 6)

FY22 School Department Budget Process
August & September
District Leadership Team reviewed and revised the Strategy for Continuous Improvement with a COVID-19 
needs lens.  The following were aligned with the Strategy objectives: School Improvement Plan goals; 
superintendent, administrator and teacher educator plan goals. 
October 
Department leaders and building administrators developed FY22 budget requests focused on highest 
impact on teaching and learning and alignment with Strategy objectives. 
November 
Budgets were submitted to the Superintendent and budget line requests were reviewed. Leadership Teams 
reviewed and revised the 5-Year Staffing Plan. 
December
The 5-Year Staffing Plan was finalized and submitted to the Town Manager to be incorporated into the 5-
Year Financial Forecast to be presented in January. The Year-1 positions were presented to School 
Committee as identified needs. 
January 
The Leadership Team prioritizes the Year-1 positions and identifies those deemed most critical to be 
included in the FY22 budget. Three budget scenarios were developed.

Lunenburg Public Schools
Social Worker
Supports the Staff  –
• Support and provide consultation for staff  in providing best practice Tier 1 interventions in the classroom through social 
emotional lessons and interventions that encourage development of  social awareness, self  awareness, self  management, 
responsible decision making, and relationship building skills (CASEL standards).
• Support and provide consultation for best practice trauma informed education interventions across all settings (inclusion and 
special education).
• Provides more support during the morning arrival and afternoon dismissal times.
• Work closely with school nurses to provide support and clinical consultation as needed for students accessing health offices.
Supports the Students –
• Tier 3 in school counseling services utilizing evidence based interventions.
• IEP direct service for social/emotional and behavioral goals (counseling and consultation).
• Tier 2 evidence based groups to target anxiety, depression, and trauma.
• Supporting students with emotional needs by establishing a relationship.
• Connect student and families to outside mental health supports (i.e. outpatient therapist, psychiatric services, in home therapy, 
family therapy, etc.).
• Consistent support of  SEL throughout the building (promote positive behaviors and build relationships at lunch time, breakfast 
time, arrival and dismissal time).

Social Worker
Supports the Principal –
• Plan, develop and implement universal mental health screenings to proactively identify students, provide 
resources, and connect students to trusted adults.
• Conduct student safety assessments and provide guidance in the event of  a mental health crisis.
• Consultation and coordination with the Department of  Youth Services (DYS) and Department of  Children and 
Families (DCF).
• Support reformative justice practice by providing clinical consultation on student discipline.
• Work closely with administration and school resource officer to develop truancy prevention plans.

Undetermined factors requiring the department to estimate line 
items: 
● Chapter 70 Funding: At the time of this document, the Governor has not released his 
FY22 Budget; we are anticipating the Town’s projected amount by the end of January. 
● Circuit Breaker: Amount will not be released until end of FY21; expecting a value to cover
65% of costs. 
● Federal & State Grants: Allocations are typically not announced for entitlement grants 
until late July; the district is expecting level funding. 
● COVID Grants: An entitlement grant is expected with an unknown amount at this time; 
there is uncertainty on additional grants from expected stimulus packages.
