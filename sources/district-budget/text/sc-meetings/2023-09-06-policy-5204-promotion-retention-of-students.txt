GRADUATION REQUIREMENT 
 
Prior  to  graduation,  each  student  must  meet  the  competency requirements  imposed  by the 
Commonwealth of Massachusetts. 
 
The following requirements are understood as having to be successfully completed (passing 
grades) in order for the diploma to be granted at Lunenburg High School. 
English - 4 credits 
Social Sciences* - 3 credits 
Science** - 3 credits 
Mathematics*** - 3credits 
Foreign Language**** - 2 credit 
Physical Education - 1.5 credits 
Unified Arts - 2 credits 
Health Education - 0.5 credit 
Electives - 5 credits 
Civics Project+  
                                      
 
Total Graduation Credits Required – 24 
 
* U.S. History I, U.S. History II, World History are required. 
** Two credits must include lab. 
*** Students are required to take Math for four years , however they are required to pass Algebra 
I & II and Geometry. 
**** Two consecutive years of one foreign language are required.  
+Requirement can be met by successful completion of specific Social Sciences/English 
courses/electives. 
 
A student who has met graduation requirements is not eligible to enroll for a voluntary fifth year. 
Students who fall short of the credit requirement by 2 credits or less would be required to attend 
summer school for credit recovery. 
Students who fall short of the credit requirement in excess of 2 credits would be allowed to enroll 
as a full-time student for a fifth year or attend summer school for credit recovery.
