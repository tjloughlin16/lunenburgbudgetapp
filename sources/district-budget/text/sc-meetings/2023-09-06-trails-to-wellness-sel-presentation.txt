What needs are counselors seeing from students this year?
At the High School, we have seen a signiﬁcant number of students reporting symptoms of anxiety and depression.  Other 
issues include negative peer interactions, lack of motivation to engage in academics, school avoidance, substance use 
and limited mental health resources outside of school.
Middle School Counseling department has seen an increase in mental health needs of students manifesting in a variety 
of ways; including self harm, thoughts of self harm, behavioral challenges, emotional dysregulation and family system 
breakdown. We have also seen the need for basic hygiene instruction, direct instruction on coping strategies and 
executive functioning coaching. We also provide a safe place for kids to share their thoughts and concerns.
At the Elementary School, the counselors see students working on social skills, presenting with symptoms of trauma, 
coping with anxiety, and managing social and home stressors. 
At the Primary School, we work with children experiencing anxiety, separation anxiety, diﬃculties with executive 
functioning and self control/regulation, home stress, trauma histories, and diﬃculties with social and friendship skills.  
We also work with many students who just need to check in to share their feelings and talk to the counselors for a few 
minutes.
