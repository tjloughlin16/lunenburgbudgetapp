Goal 1: Financial Sustainability and Efficiency 
Objective: 
Ensure the long-term financial stability of the district by developing a budget that balances 
sustainability, competitive compensation, and efficient resource management. Collaboration 
with key stakeholders will be prioritized to maximize resources and efficiency. 
Action Steps: 
 Work collaboratively with staff, unions, administrators, parents, students, and 
community members, as well as town partners including the Select Board, Finance 
Committee, and Town Manager, to create a fiscally responsible budget that supports 
sustainability and long-term planning. 
 Collaborate with the Budget Task Force Committee to establish and communicate long-
term financial goals for the district. 
 Prioritize competitive salaries to attract and retain talented staff members. 
 Maintain transparency by engaging in open discussions with stakeholders to balance 
educational needs with fiscal realities. 
Benchmarks: 
 Passage of a budget that reflects sustainability and long-term planning goals. 
 Regular updates and reports on the district’s financial status and progress toward 
improving efficiency and cost-effectiveness. 
 
Goal 2: Facilities and Capital Planning 
Objective: 
Develop a comprehensive plan that focuses on the current maintenance as well as future needs 
of school buildings and athletic fields, ensuring safety and sustainability. 
Action Steps: 
 Develop a long-term facilities plan that addresses the district’s needs, with special 
attention given to Turkey Hill School and the turf field. 
 Collaborate with town officials and the community to ensure that planned projects and 
upgrades are well-documented and presented at public forums, such as town meetings. 
 Establish a long-term plan for all district buildings, outlining timelines and budget 
estimates for repairs, upgrades, and sustainability improvements. 
Benchmarks:

 Completion and public presentation of a detailed long-term facilities plan. 
 Public documentation and acknowledgment of facility projects and upgrades through 
official town records or meetings. 
 
Goal 3: Transparent and Effective Communication 
Objective: 
To improve communication efforts within the district, ensuring that the public receives accurate 
and timely information while reducing misinformation and fostering greater trust with the 
community. 
Action Steps: 
 Create a communication strategy that provides clear and timely updates on key topics 
such as school committee decisions, budget developments, and facility plans. 
 Enhance public access to information by developing platforms where community 
members can easily ask questions, provide feedback, and stay informed about school 
operations. 
 Actively counter misinformation by sharing accurate, consistent messages across various 
channels, including social media, email, newsletters, and public forums. 
Benchmarks: 
 Implementation of a communication plan with measurable goals to track community 
engagement and feedback. 
 Regular updates to the community on important topics like budget priorities, facility 
projects, and operational changes. 
 
Goal 4: Operational Excellence and Employee Support 
Objective: 
To streamline operational processes, improve overall efficiency, and support employee 
retention by addressing challenges. 
Action Steps: 
 Review and document key operational processes, such as hiring, mandatory training, 
and employee evaluations, to ensure consistency and transparency. 
 Implement programs that address employee burnout and promote staff well-being, 
including clearer process documentation and support structures.

 Create strategies to improve the quality of school operations, with a focus on staff 
retention, operational continuity, and reducing administrative friction. 
Benchmarks: 
 Publication of easily accessible documentation on all key operational processes for 
district employees. 
 Implementation of new initiatives or changes aimed at improving employee satisfaction 
and retention.
