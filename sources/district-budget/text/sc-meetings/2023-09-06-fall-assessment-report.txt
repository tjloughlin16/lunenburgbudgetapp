SPRING 2022 MCAS
● 2022 was ﬁrst full MCAS administration for grades 3-8 since 2019
● Grade 10 students had not taken an MCAS test since 2019 (grade 7)
● State results showed increase in math scores, a decline in ELA scores, and a slight increase in 
science scores as compared with 2021 scores
● Absenteeism was a challenge across the state during the 21-22 school year
● 1.7 million days of school were missed due to positive COVID-19 cases.  (This does not include 
days missed for contact tracing and other illnesses.)
● Lunenburg students missed 13,930 days of school and Luneburg teachers missed 2,990 days 
during the 21-22 school year

MTSS & CURRICULUM CHANGES 22-23
● New high-quality, standards-aligned curriculum for grades K-5 in both ELA and math
● Full-time literacy and math specialists supporting and coaching teachers in grades K-8
● Workshop model of learning in grades K-5 being implemented
● Multi-tiered system of support being ﬁne-tuned throughout district
● Students in grades K-8 required to complete 30-45 minutes per week on iReady My Path, a 
personalized learning platform, in both reading and math
● Targeted interventions based on student needs during intervention time and through Title I
● Review of iReady, MCAS, and classroom data
● 2-year Student Learning Goal for grade K-8 ELA and math teachers - 80% of students on grade 
level
