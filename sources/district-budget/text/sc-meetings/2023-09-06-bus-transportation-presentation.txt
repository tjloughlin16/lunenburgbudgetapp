TRANSPORTATION                                                                                    3600
 
TRANSPORTATION:  DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES                    3601
OF THE SCHOOL COMMITTEE
 
The purpose of school transportation is to get pupils who live an unreasonable walking distance from home to school and back in 
an efficient, safe, and economical manner.  Other purposes include the provision of transportation for academic field trips in 
direct support of the curriculum, transportation for the support of the co-curricular program (athletics, music, drama, and the 
like).
 
As of August, 2005 the Lunenburg Public Schools will transport all Kindergarten, Grade 1, and Grade 2 students and students in 
grades 3-12 who live one and one half miles or more from the school. The Lunenburg Public Schools does not provide 
transportation for pre-school students unless this service is required by the student’s Individualized Education Plan (IEP).
 
 
 
Approved:  April 27, 2005
