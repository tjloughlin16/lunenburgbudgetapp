 Dear Dr. Fortuna,  Mr. Sculimbrene and Members of the Committee, 
 I am writing to bring to your immediate attention a critical staffing need at the Primary School 
 involving a kindergarten student who is currently exhibiting significant behavioral challenges and 
 poses an ongoing safety risk to herself and others. 
 While the student is not yet on an Individualized Education Program (IEP), we are working with 
 the parents to initiate the special education evaluation process due to the severity and 
 persistence of these concerns. In the interim, the student requires constant adult supervision 
 throughout the school day to ensure a safe learning environment. 
 At present, the building principal, assistant principal, and school guidance counselor have been 
 providing direct support and supervision. While we are committed to ensuring this student’s 
 safety and access to education, this current arrangement is not sustainable. It has significantly 
 impacted our ability to remain visible, accessible, and responsive to the needs of the rest of the 
 student body, staff, and daily operations of the school. 
 All existing paraprofessionals are fully allocated based on current IEP service delivery grids, and 
 their reassignment is not an option without violating compliance requirements. Therefore, we 
 are requesting the immediate addition of a paraprofessional to support this student while the 
 evaluation is in progress and potentially beyond, depending on the outcome. 
 We appreciate your prompt attention to this matter and are happy to provide further 
 documentation or clarification as needed. 
 Sincerely, 
 Carla Squier 
 Primary Principal
