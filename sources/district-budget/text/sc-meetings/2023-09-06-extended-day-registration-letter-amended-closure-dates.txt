Dear Families: 
The Extended Day Program is located in the T.C. Passios Building for grades Pre -K-
5 for families of students enrolled in the Lunenburg Public Schools. Children are 
required to be toilet trained to attend.  A 5 week summer program is also offered 
to students K-5th grade. Important information is listed below so that all families 
will be informed about the program.  There is a separate registration form for 
Extended Day Program, the Early Learning Center and the Summer Program. 
Enrollment Cap: 
● There is an enrollment cap of 75 students in both the AM and PM sessions.  
Schedule 
● Hours of operation are from 7:00 AM-9:00AM and 3:15PM-5:30PM 
● There will be a set schedule for registrations.   
● “As needed” registrations are not accepted. 
● A separate sign-up form will be available in person  for any early release 
days one week prior.  (the Extended Day program will be closed during the 
December, February  and April school vacation) 
● We will be closed on all school cancellation snow days and delays will be 
decided on a case by case basis by the Superintendent depending on the 
weather conditions. Cancellations or delays will be communicated via 
email/phone message,  and posted on your local TV station and the district’s 
main webpage. 
● It is necessary to sign your child in and out.  Please see the adult 
supervisor upon arrival.  Your child will only be dismissed to those individuals 
listed on the dismissal list. 
 
===PAGE 2===
● Please pay by check payable to the “Town of Lunenburg”, or through our 
online payment system RevTrak.  If a payment arrangement has not been 
made with the Director a $20 fee may be assessed for late payment.   
● If your and your child’s last names are different, please note that in the 
memo section of your check.  
● A fee will be charged to parents/guardians of $1.00 for every minute they 
are late for pick-up after 5:30pm.  If a pattern of tardiness is noted, the 
child will be removed from the program. 
Important Information 
● There is no nurse/medical staff available in the building. 
● If a child’s behavior continues to be detrimental within the program or 
towards students, administrative intervention will be sought up to and 
including suspension or removal from the program. 
● Parents are required to review the Extended Day handbook which is available 
online. A signature of a parent/guardian will be required. 
● Please label all removable articles of clothing (jackets, hats, etc.) 
● Please make sure you review all monthly notices included with your bill for 
updated information. 
● You must complete a separate registration form for half days.  Cost of a 
½ day is $30.  A bagged lunch must be provided. A $20 fee will be 
assessed for any student who is registered but does not attend without 
advanced warning.
