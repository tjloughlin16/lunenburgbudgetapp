MIAA Rule 53: Student Eligibility: Middle School Students on Senior High Teams 
  
“A middle school the student is eligible to represent a senior high school on its athletic teams only when 
the MIAA member high school includes those grades and they are under the direct “jurisdiction” and 
“supervision” of the high school principal (see Rule 51 for definitions). 
  
 Lunenburg controls where students are eligible to play and what level. 
 The majority of our teams have needed middle school students on high school rosters in order 
to fill out rosters. 
 This saves time from the district having to obtain a league vote and then submitting a paper 
to the MIAA for middle level waivers, like we have had to in years past. 
 With this new eligibility rule in place at Lunenburg it could be potentially helpful under certain 
conditions. For example, Lunenburg field hockey the past couple of years has had enough 
high school students to have a varsity and JV team to start, but over the course of the season 
injuries and other circumstances led to roster sizes being depilated forcing the JV team to 
play 7 v 7 due to lower numbers.  
o Having all students eligible to compete would allow a natural feeder system. For 
example, if a varsity player is injured and/or cannot participate a JV player could be 
brought up to fill the spot and then a middle school player could be brought up to the 
JV level to fill that spot and we are able to maintain enough to sustain a full squad at 
all levels.  
 Several other schools we play and that our in our league already complete middle -level 
waivers or are organized  as a 7 -12 school or 8 -12 school which allow 7 th and 8th -
grade students to participate on JV and varsity level teams. 
 Previously, Lunenburg was setup where 8th grade students were participating on high school 
teams at the JV and varsity levels before moving into the current building.
