Dear Families:  
The Early Learning Center Program is open to 3 and 4 year old children of 
Lunenburg residents. Staff reinforces necessary skills for introduction to 
preschool/kindergarten classrooms in a playful but intentionally structured 
environment.  
What We Offer:  
● Skills reinforced such as: name writing, color/shape & alphabet 
identification, cutting skills, etc.  
● Areas of developmental growth: reading, listening center, science, art, etc. 
● Free exploration of classroom for social development  
● Theme related weeks(ex. jungle, community helpers)

Important Information:  
● 6:1 adult/student ratio per session (not to exceed 12 students) 
● Slots filled on a first come/first serve basis  
● To enroll, we require 3 current proofs of Lunenburg residency within the 
last 30 days.  
● An online registration form is required which includes medical & 
emergency information.  Completing this is not a guarantee of 
acceptance into the program.  You will be notified directly from the 
Director of Community/School Programs if/when you have been 
accepted.  
● ELC follows the dates on the school calendar for early release days, vacation 
===PAGE 3===
weeks, kindergarten screening, etc.  
● Billing is monthly. Please make checks payable to the “Town of Lunenburg” 
and write “ELC” in the memo section. You may also use our online pay system 
RevTrak  
● A $25 non-refundable fee will be required if you are accepted into the 
program and will be used as payment towards first months bill.  
● Once registered for specific days, you will be billed regardless of 
attendance  
● Children should be toilet trained, no diapers. 
  
Program Cancellations:  
● ELC will not operate on full PD Days  
● We are closed on snow cancellation days, school vacations  
● The morning session is canceled when the Primary School has a delayed start 
● The afternoon session is canceled for school early release days 
 If the last day of school is an early release day, the program will not 
operate. 
 If an early release falls on a Friday before a school break, the program will 
not operate.
