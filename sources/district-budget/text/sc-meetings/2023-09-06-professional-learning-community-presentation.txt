Learning Walks 
● Instructional Leadership Team (Superintendent, Principals, Assistant 
Principals, Director of Special Services, Director of Teaching & Learning) visits 
four classrooms in a given building in teams
● Looking for active student engagement, instructional strategies, 
social-emotional learning, and observing classroom environment
● Debrief following observations to calibrate regarding observations and feedback

===PAGE 3===
Instructional Rounds (Sweeps) 
● Principals, Assistant Principals, Director of Teaching & Learning and Title I 
Coordinator visit content area classroom across all grade levels 
● Looking for consistency in grade level instruction and active student 
engagement
● Admin debrief using Notice and Wonder protocol following visits and provide 
feedback to teachers
● Three times per year in each building for ELA and Math.  Additional sweeps for 
other content areas.
===PAGE 4===
Classroom Visits 
We use the TeachPoint unannounced observation form for basic information about 
lesson objective, strategies for engagement, instructional practices and checks for 
understanding.  
We highlight the positive components of social emotional learning that are evident 
in a classroom:  community building, positive student/teacher relationships, 
routines and procedures
We’ve also implemented follow up interviews so teachers and administrators can 
engage in a reflective dialog about what was observed.

Positive Behavior Interventions and Supports 
(PBIS)
PBIS is a set of behavior expectations for each school setting throughout the day, 
creating a uniform set of expectations.
PBIS has been shown to improve social, emotional, and academic outcomes for all 
students.
THES and LPS use similar language.
