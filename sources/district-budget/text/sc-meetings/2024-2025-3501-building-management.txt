BUILDINGS AND GROUNDS MANAGEMENT 3501 
 
While the care, custody , and safekeeping of all school real estate is the general responsibility of 
the Town, each building principal shall have general authority over the building and surrounding 
grounds that they supervise. 
 
The Superintendent shall establish procedures as may be necessary in regard to the condition, 
upkeep, and general/necessary repairs to all school buildings and related surroundings.
