TO:    Dr. Kate Burnham, Superintendent 
FROM:    Dr. Deborah E. Bookis, Director of Teaching and Learning  
RE:  Arts and Cultural Vitality Grant 
DATE: June 2025 
 
 
 
In the fall 2025, Lunenburg Public School was awarded the second year of the Arts and 
Cultural Vitality (ACV) Grant. The goal was to create an action plan from data that was 
collected as a result of the information of the DESE Arts and Cultural Vitality in Schools 
grant program in year one, 2023-24. This year, the second year cohort created an action 
plan for stakeholders based on specific indicators provided by DESE, research, and 
survey results. This data was collected and analyzed for: student learning, available 
student opportunities and experiences. The resulting action plan is the next step in the 
process to close equity gaps and create robust Arts and Culture Vitality in the 
Lunenburg Public Schools. The District plans to apply for year three of the grant when it 
becomes available in late August.
