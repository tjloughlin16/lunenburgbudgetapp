TO:    Dr. Kate Burnham, Superintendent 
FROM:    Dr. Deborah E. Bookis, Director of Teaching and Learning 
RE:  Competitive STEM Grants 
DATE:  March 2025 
 
 
We are requesting permission to write grants for a redesign of the STEM labs at both the Primary 
School and the Turkey Hill Elementary School, which we hope will also include some of the 
materials and equipment that the  FY26 budget was unable to support this year. 
 
The first grant is from Inventionland® Education. Their program is meant to financially support 
schools, districts and other local educational agencies who desire to leverage Inventionland® 
Education’s award-winning Innovation Labs®,  . .  with the goal to offer all educators and 
students the same opportunities to experience the magical world of innovation, creativity, and 
STEAM-related learning. We would request money to support a redesign of the existing STEM 
classrooms (not new construction). These spaces act as a catalyst for creativity, and are perfect 
for maker spaces, technology labs, and project-based learning. 
 
The Inventionland Grant is considered a “stackable grant,” which means that they would write a 
letter of support for an additional grant that would complement their grant (cover costs they do 
not.) The first step is to apply for the Inventionland grant. If awarded, we would then apply for 
both the Reiman Foundation grant and the SC Johnson grant, both of which have rolling 
applications. 
 
 
Due Date for the Inventionland Grant:  March 31, 2025
