PURPOSE- USER FEES
To offset the costs associated with running sports programs 
These costs can include expenses such as:
Coaching Stipends: Salaries for coaches across various sports.
Equipment and Uniforms: Purchasing, maintaining, and replacing sports equipment 
and team uniforms.
Facility Maintenance: Costs associated with maintaining athletic fields, gyms, and 
other sports facilities.
Transportation: Providing buses for teams to travel to away games and meets.
Officials and Game Management: Paying referees, umpires, and event staff for 
managing competitions.
Operational Costs: Other expenses such as insurance, league fees, or medical staff.
