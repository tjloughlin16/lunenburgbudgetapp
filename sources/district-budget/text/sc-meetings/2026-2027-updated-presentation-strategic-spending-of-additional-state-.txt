FY27 BUDGET ALLOCATION
$119,458
1.0 FTE District Support Role
This professional allocation funds a full-time Board 
Certified Behavior Analyst BCBA) dedicated to 
district-wide student support. The role is central to our 
commitment to stabilizing the learning environment 
through specialized, evidence-based behavioral 
intervention planning and strategic staff collaboration.
What does a BCBA do?
Behavioral Assessment & Planning: Conducts functional 
behavior assessments FBAs and designs individualized, 
evidence-based behavior intervention plans BIPs for 
students.
Crisis Intervention & Stabilization: Provides targeted support 
during behavioral crises, establishing immediate strategies to 
safely stabilize classroom and learning environments.
Staff Training & Collaboration: Delivers professional 
development and coaches classroom educators and support 
staff on implementing behavioral techniques and positive 
reinforcement systems.

Board Certified Behavior Analyst BCBA
 Need: Complying with New State Regulations
1.0 FTE
District Support Role
This allocation funds a full-time BCBA dedicated to 
district-wide support. Robust BCBA staffing is now 
critical to safely transition schools away from reactive 
seclusion/time-outs toward proactive, evidence-based 
behavioral management.
Why Now? New DESE Regulations 202627
● Strict Seclusion Limits: New MA DESE mandates restrict 
seclusion to extreme, immediately monitored emergency 
situations, requiring a shift to proactive de-escalation over 
reactive containment.
● Redefined Time-Outs: Under updated guidelines, 
time-outs are strictly defined as voluntary, demanding 
robust, research-backed positive behavioral interventions 
designed by certified analysts.
● Evidence-Based Prevention: Specialized BCBAs conduct 
FBAs, design BIPs, and train educators to de-escalate crises 
before emergency restraint or exclusion is ever considered.

Stabilization & Cost Avoidance
BCBAs provide a massive return on investment by

while building robust internal capabilities.
Key Organizational Impacts
● Avoiding Private Placements: By managing behavioral 
cases in-district, schools prevent costly alternative 
educational enrollments and complex transportation fees.
● Reducing Teacher Burnout: Providing educators with 
professional behavioral intervention frameworks directly 
alleviates daily classroom stressors.
● Decreasing Staff Turnover: Empowering support staff 
and teachers with specialized behavioral tools 
establishes a sustainable, stable, and collaborative 
learning environment.
