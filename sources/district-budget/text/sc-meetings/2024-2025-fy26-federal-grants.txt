SUBJECT: Approval to Apply for FY26 DESE Entitlement Grants
We are requesting the School Committee's approval to submit our intent to apply for the
FY26 entitlement grants administered by the Massachusetts Department of Elementary
and Secondary Education (DESE). These grants include:
• Grant 240 - Special Education: IDEA (Ages 6-21)
• Grant 262 - Early Childhood Special Education (Ages 3-5)
• Grant 274 - Individuals with Disabilities Education Act Part B (IDEA) Federal
Targeted Special Education Program Improvement Grant
• Title 1 - Improving Basic Programs
• Title II, Part A - Supporting Effective Instruction
• Title III - English Language Acquisition
• Title IV, Part A - Student Support and Academic Enrichment
DESE requires districts to submit their intent to apply by May 7, 2025. These funds are
critical to supporting our students' academic, social-emotional, and language acquisition
needs. Your approval will allow us to meet the state deadline and move forward with the
application process.
Please see the table below outlining the funds received in previous years. We anticipate
receiving similar allocation amounts for the upcoming fiscal year. Below the table are
more detailed descriptions of each of the entitlement grants.

Grant 240 - Special Education: IDEA Part B (Ages 6-21)
• Purpose: Supports services for students with disabilities aged 6 through 21.
• Details: Funded under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) Part
B, this grant helps districts provide special education and related services for
eligible students.
Grant 262 - Early Childhood Special Education (ECSE)
• Purpose: Provides services for children with disabilities aged 3 to 5.
• Details: Funded under IDEA Part B, Section 619, this grant ensures that eligible
preschool-aged children with disabilities receive a free and appropriate public
education, including special education and related services designed to meet
their individual needs in the least restrictive environment.
Grant 274 - Individuals with Disabilities Education Act Part B (IDEA Federal Targeted
Special Education Program Improvement Grant
• Purpose: is to support schools and districts to implement IEP Improvement
Project, with a strong emphasis on transitioning to utilization of the newly revised
forms and processes.
===PAGE 3===
• Details: Grant funds are to be used to implement IEP Improvement Project, with a
strong emphasis on transitioning to utilization of the newly revised forms and
processes. All expenditures must be for the costs related to special education IEP
program improvement.
Title I - Improving Basic Programs
• Purpose: Provides financial assistance to schools with high numbers or
percentages of children from low-income families.
• Details: A federal program under the Elementary and Secondary Education Act
(ESEA) providing financial help to schools to improve academic achievement,
especially in reading and math.
Title IIA - Supporting Effective Instruction
• Purpose: Improves teacher and principal quality.
• Details: Funds professional development, recruitment, and retention of
high-quality educators.
Title III - English Language Acquisition
• Purpose: Supports English learners (ELS).
• Details: Funds programs to help EL students attain English proficiency and meet
academic standards.
Title IV, Part A - Student Support and Academic Enrichment (SSAE)
• Purpose: Enhances student academic outcomes by supporting well-rounded
education, safe and healthy students, and effective technology use.
• Details: Flexible funding to improve access to arts, STEM, mental health services,
and digital learning.
