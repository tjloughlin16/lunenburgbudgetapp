NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
  
The Lunenburg School Committee in compliance with M.G.L. and the Town of Lunenburg Charter 
advises all citizens the proposed FY27  Budget is available for review at the Of fice of the 
Superintendent, 129 Northfield Rd , Lunenburg, MA, between the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 4:00 
p.m., Monday through Friday by appointment only .  Copies are also available on the website 
(www.lunenburgschools.net) on the school budget information page. 
  
A public hearing on the FY27 proposed budget will be broadcast remotely via Zoom as well as in 
person at the LMS Collaborative Room #D132 on Wednesday, February 25, 2026 at 6:30 p.m.  The 
proposed FY27 restoration budget requests $28,663,186 for personnel and non-personnel related 
expenses; a 9% increase over the FY26 budget.
