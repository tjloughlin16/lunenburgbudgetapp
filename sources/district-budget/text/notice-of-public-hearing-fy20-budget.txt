NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
The Lunenburg School Committee in compliance with M.G.L. and the Town of 
Lunenburg Charter advises all citizens the proposed FY20 Budget is available for review 
at the Office of the Superintendent, 1025 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA, 
between the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m., Monday through Friday.  Copies are also 
available at each school and on the website (www.lunenburgschools.net). 
A public hearing on the FY20 proposed budget will take place on Wednesday, April 3, 
2019, 7:00 p.m., at the Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA.  The Lunenburg 
School Committee will vote on the FY20 Budget in the regular session of their April 24, 
2019 meeting.  The proposed FY20 budget requests $20,795,863 for personnel and non-
personnel related expenses; a three percent (3%) increase above the FY19 budget.
