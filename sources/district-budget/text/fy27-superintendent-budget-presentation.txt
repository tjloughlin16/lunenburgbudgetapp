Superintendent’s 
Introduction
The Core Theme: Restoration, Not Expansion
● The Reality: This budget request is higher than the previous fiscal year.
● The Distinction: Personnel requisitions are identified strictly as needed 
positions (restoration) rather than discretionary enhancements 
(expansion).
The Strategic Approach
● Three-Year Plan: This proposal serves as the foundational year of a 
systematic process.
● Objective: Restore critical roles and functions that were eliminated during 
the FY26 budget cycle.
The Primary Goal: Bridging the Gap
● Essential Service Levels: Return the district to operational stability.
● Outcomes:
○ Stabilize class sizes.
○ Reinstate critical support services.
○ Meet requisite standards for student success.

===PAGE 35===
Justiﬁcation for 
Interventionists
The Data: The Case for Support
● Current Reality: Longitudinal data consistently show that 
approximately 50% of our students are currently not meeting 
expected grade-level standards in ELA and Reading.
● The Implication: These students require additional, targeted 
support to access the full curriculum and reach their potential.
The Research: The "Compounding Gap"
● Skills Build Skills: Educational research demonstrates that early 
proficiency creates momentum. Conversely, small gaps in 
foundational skills tend to widen over time if not specifically 
addressed.
● The Critical Window: Interventions implemented before Grade 3 
are significantly more effective at closing these gaps than 
remediation attempts in later grades (Stanovich, 1986; Torgesen, 
2004).
The Solution: Academic Interventionist
● Role Function: Deliver high-frequency, small-group instruction to 
accelerate student progress.
● Strategic Goal: Ensure students bridge the gap towards meeting 
grade level standards securing their long-term academic success.

Central Ofﬁce 
Restructuring
Position Cost: $10,000
Insurance Cost: $0
Eliminate 1.0 Literacy Coach - Grant Funded
Transition 1.0 Math Coach to Instructional Specialist - 
~$10,000
Rationale for Change
● Evaluation of Strategy: While instructional coaching is a proven 
model in theory, internal data indicates the current implementation 
is not yielding the projected Return on Investment (ROI).
● Strategic Pivot: Shifting focus from indirect support (teacher 
coaching) to direct student services to maximize budget efficacy.
Personnel & Budget Adjustments
● Elimination: The Literacy Coach position will not be included in 
the FY27 budget.
● Restructuring: The Math Coach position will transition to an 
Instructional Specialist.
○ 60% Direct Service: Small group acceleration focused on 
conceptual math understanding.
○ 40% Systems Coordination: Leading the District’s 
Multi-Tiered System of Supports (MTSS).
