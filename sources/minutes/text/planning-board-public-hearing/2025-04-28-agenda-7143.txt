PUBLIC HEARING
MONDAY, 2025-04-28 – 6:05 P.M | 17 Main St, Lunenburg MA
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on 2025-04-28 at 6:05 p.m., Town Hall, 17 Main 
Street, Lunenburg, MA to make a recommendation to Annual Town Meeting 2025 on alterations to Code of 
the Town of Lunenburg, MA, Chapter 250, Zoning, to conform with provisions of the Massachusetts 
Affordable Homes Act, being Chapter 150 of the Acts of 2024, specifically relative to accessory dwelling units
(ADUs); and Article IV, to replace in entirety the Floodplain bylaw section (§ 250-4.8) to allow for mandatory
updates to the Flood Insurance Rate Maps (FIRM) and required zoning bylaw and map for participation in the 
National Flood Insurance Program.
Materials are available for viewing at the Planning Office, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA. This 
meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, on Facebook Live on the Public Access Facebook 
page (https://www.facebook.com/LunenburgPublic-Access-Cable-157427270975305) and will be posted to the 
LunenburgAccess YouTube channel within 24 hours after the meeting.
To participate remotely: from a computer, please use the link below to join the free public meeting webinar: 
https://us02web.zoom.us/j/88251881562, or from a telephone, dial the toll free number (888) 475-4499 and 
enter Webinar ID: 882 5188 1562.
