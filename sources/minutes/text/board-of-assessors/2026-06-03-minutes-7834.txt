   Sarah Cammer and Rena Swezey will attempt to work together to review 
  neighborhood factors and codes.  The ﬁrst areas of focus is site codes 1A

were included. These are trying to be narrowed down and documented.

town website for residents to view.  Sarah Cammer suggests printing out 
some of the sales for reference.

make sure we do not surprise the taxpayers.
