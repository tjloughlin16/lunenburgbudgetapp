To All Interested Parties:  
 
In accordance with General Laws, Chapter 39, Section 10, and Article 2, § 2-5 of the Town

voters of the Town of Lunenburg, the Select Board, at the meeting of July 27, 2026, voted to call

Middle/High School Auditorium.

close the warrant on August 7, 2026 at 5:00 p.m.

135, Lunenburg, MA 01462 by 5:00 PM on August 7, 2026.
