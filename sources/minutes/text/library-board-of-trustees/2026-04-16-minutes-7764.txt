   FY26 Budget Update  
 
   FY27 Budget Update  
Some sharing of information amongst Library Directors in CWMARS around budget situations. Not 
everyone is in dire straits but many libraries are facing cuts, some will be filing waivers, and many are 
preparing for multiple years of challenging budget context. 
   Staff Update 
Our April 3 partnership with 8th Grade ELA classes was spectacular. Two Trustees in attendance and 
almost 60 people, many more parents than I expected. I’ve already expressed interest in repeating 
the program next year. More than 20 students signed up for library cards as part of the opportunity. 
April 10th Public Access Cable interviewed me and filmed for their next installment of “Welcome 
to…” series. Glad to have the additional effort of spreading word about the library from their team.  
I’ve been considering additional means of studying the unreported/uncaptured data from our 
staffing study last year. I have registered for an online workshop on “Invisible Labor” in hopes that 
there will be some guidance in this context. I’ve also started planning a follow-up supplementary 
study that I would pencil into the schedule for next fall/winter.

LUNENBURG PUBLIC LIBRARY DIRECTOR’S REPORT 
Tomorrow night a staff team will compete in the teen Club’s annual “Are You Smarter than a Sixth 
Grader?” I did also attend a talk from Mass Higher Education Consortium that turned up a new 
vendor for furniture and library materials that I was unaware of, called Mackin that seems promising. 
One topic of discussion at the Friends meeting was the possibility of a donated clothing shed on the 
property. I am awaiting feedback from the Town Manager on whether this would be permitted or 
prohibited. 
I submitted payroll and bills last week, and no obvious errors made. This was the first payroll I’ve 
completed without Lydia since before the pandemic! Scheduling: Marabeth will be out in early June 
with a surgery that she needs. Cheryl should be back from her leave by that point, though, able to 
cover some of those gaps. 
   Programs 
Volunteers from the Friends will help set up and run our May puzzle swap (5/9) with Lydia and Cheryl 
both out on leave during that month. 
We’ve had a number of remarkable programs this past month. Joslyn’s storytimes are really 
knocking it out of the park. Monday of this week had an attendance of 43!  Our April 3 8th grade 
poetry recitation was a big crowd, and created a lot of excitement for poetry. We also showed Come 
See Me in the Good Light, the documentary about poet Andrea Gibson. Small crowd but a great 
format for a poetry event. I have a few more fantastic documentaries about poets that we could 
show in the future. 
The first Ukulele meetup turned out 12 strummers! 31 attendees at the Minecraft Madness show, 45 
came for the drop-in Axolotl Craft, 19 for Incredible Earthworms. The Social Security program was 
attended by a huge number of people, too. At least 75! 
Coming up: 4/17 Spring Tinctures WKSP, Next is our April Vacation programming! 4/21 Spring Painting 
with Julie, 4/22 Seed Kits Take-Home and Terrific Terrariums, 4/22 Arthur James blues guitar, 4/23 
LARPing for kids, 4/24 Video game night. 4/25 Toddler Yoga, 4/28 Uke Meetup, 4/29 Kate Chadbourne 
Poetry & Song, 4/30 Dashbots! 5/1 Sons of Blarney concert, 5/7 What’s Your Next Adventure with 
James Nehring. 
   Outreach 
We broached the subject of merchandise fund raiser sales at the Artisan Market with the Friends. We 
will continue that discussion next month. Seems like an opportunity to raise funds and awareness to 
have people wearing or carrying LPL swag around town. 
Upcoming Events: 4/22 Earth Day Info Booth, 4/27 FSU Farmer’s Fair booth, 5/18 Seven Hills Info Fair 
at Devens, Cruise Night in May, 5/23 Vendor Fair, Artisan Market on 5/24, 6/7, 6/21, 7/19, 8/2, 8/30.

The access panels for the water fountains are in the Community Room storage closet, so Wade and I 
moved boxes out of the way for the Facilities team to access. They need to get in there to finish their 
work. It remains to be seen whether the water bubblers will be able to be repaired or if we will need 
to replace both units. Almost all new units are the bottle filler type, though we have a large number 
of children who will not have water bottles, so we might choose to maintain at least one of these 
“old” style bubblers. 
▪ Technology 
Met with Kara and Mason to discuss near future hardware replacement plans for the Library. As we 
mentioned in past meetings, Lydia’s All-in-one will go to Sherri, and Lydia will receive a new laptop. 
Instead of replacing another pair of early literacy stations, I suggested we replace a few of the 
ancient chromeboxes. Although our last Teen Librarian had expressed strong resistance to the idea 
of replacing the devices with new chromeboxes, I see too many reasons that we should stay with 
this format. The three stations in the Teen Room, and three chromebox stations just inside the entry 
to the  Children’s Room are almost always in use during the afternoon and evenings when young 
patrons are visiting the library.

Highlights:  
CANDLE MAKING: A fun Saturday of back-to-back candle making! We started off strong with a 
crowd of excited kids making colorful candles (with excellent family feedback)! Then we had a 
small but mighty group of tweens who enjoyed the calmer, more meditative process of melting 
wax and adding spring fragrances. All the kids left happy, and all the tweens came back to pick 
up their settled candles within a couple of days – eager to try them out.  
    
 
DROP-IN CRAFTS: Leprechaun Traps and Paper Roll Axolotls were both a hit. While I haven’t 
heard from any of the kids on whether they successfully trapped a leprechaun (despite me 
specifically requesting updates...), they still left in good spirits (maybe because of the sugar in 
the Lucky Charms but I like to think it was because the craft was so fun). Unsurprisingly, we had 
many eager faces show up to make axolotls: an animal that is currently all the rage with the 
kiddos. Only one person burned themselves with the hot glue gun (me), so it was a success!  
     

===PAGE 9===
 
MINECRAFT MADNESS MAGIC SHOW: Jungle Jim was amazing! He had excellent magic tricks (as 
you can see by the kid’s reactions below), tons of humor, and a natural talent for working with 
kids. I have NEVER seen the kids laugh this much at any program. Even the adults had huge 
smiles and laughs during the grand finale (Jim basically becoming a giant balloon himself). 
   
 
IN-BETWEENERS BOOK CLUB: I had 3 new attendees for April’s book club meeting! Which 
worked out perfectly considering 3 of my regulars had sport conflicts that day... They joined in 
with the rest of the group seamlessly, were visibly disappointed to learn that we only have one 
meeting left this year, and demanded (along with the others) that we bring the book club back 
in the fall... So, I already have some September plans it seems!
