LUNENBURG PUBLIC LIBRARY DIRECTOR’S REPORT 
 
   
 
 
   Staff Update 
Due to some extended delays at the town hall, our new hire for the Library Assistant – Children's 
Room will be starting on Friday, January 16th. She will assume the full schedule for the position 
during the week of January 26th while working the next couple Fridays to learn more about the front 
desk and basic library workflow. 
Sherri and I started discussing how to best manage homebound book deliveries through the COA 
Meals on Wheels program. She and I are working on creating a system to track and manage loans 
while still balancing the patron’s right to full borrowing privileges in network. We plan to have a 
system in place by next week at the latest. 
Marabeth has almost completed another full inventory of the library’s collections. She started last 
April and works in small batches when cataloging is slow, and she has some extra time. The inventory 
helps us identify missing or lost items in the collection and was last done during the closure for 
COVID in 2020. She’s made excellent progress in this important collection task!  
I attended a town department head meeting on January 8th and communicated the budget needs to 
Muir. Our Town Manager gave a brief overview of the anticipated state of the budget for FY27, and it 
will require Muir’s input as to how it affects the library. He and I have been in communication about 
what support I can offer him in this process during his leave, but all proposed changes will ultimately 
need to come from him at this time. 
   Programs 
January got off to a slow start for adult programming, most likely due to a combination of cold 
weather and cold illnesses keeping patrons home instead of venturing out to the library. Our DIY 
“make it monthly” evening program was canceled due to the weather, with a rescheduled date TBD, 
and the morning session had low attendance but great feedback. However, our first yoga class and 
winter sowing program were big successes for the library. Thank you to Robin Venezia and her crew 
of volunteers for making another wonderful winter sowing program at the library! 
Joslyn has been booking some excellent new programs for the children’s room and is seeing a lot of 
interest in the toddler yoga sessions, in addition to the return of her drop-in crafts and the usual 
recurring events. We have been working on creating a more robust STEM offering for the spring, 
especially during the school vacation weeks, and booking more diverse programming in general. 
In partnership with art teacher Scott Barry-Grove from the middle high school, there will be a new art 
exhibit installed on January 15th running through February 12th showcasing work from all students in 
the Lunenburg schools. We are hosting a reception for artists and their families to see the exhibit on 
Thursday, February 5th.  
Coming up: 2/8 Colored Pencil Drawing class, 2/12 Copper Bookmark workshops for teens and adults, 
2/13 Silent Film Night with Live Music, 2/18 Afrobeats kids dance class, 2/21 Winter Puzzle Swap.

▪ Outreach 
Melanie is prepared for our second event at Smokestack Roasters on Sunday, January 18th to reprise 
the Library of Things petting zoo program during a more popular time. Joslyn will be going with her 
to highlight the children’s STEM kits and be an extra set of hands for library inquiries.  
The February visit to Baker Brook/Tri-Town apartment buildings is still on. Melanie has prepared a 
presentation on Libby to give to residents. 
▪ Building & Maintenance 
I communicated with Chris in the facilities department to ensure the library receives adequate snow 
clearing on weekends. We had a snowy Saturday with facilities crews arriving in the afternoon, well 
past when we opened. Luckily, Marabeth and I were able to clear sidewalks for patrons by our 10:00 
am opening, but I did stress to Chris the importance of having assistance prior to the 9:00 am arrival 
of staff. All should be fine for the remainder of the winter season, but it is something I will follow up 
on if needed.  
▪ Technology 
Our new color printer is a bit more finicky than the last one and requires more oversight than 
anticipated, but it does help to have the added features and is overall a good addition.
