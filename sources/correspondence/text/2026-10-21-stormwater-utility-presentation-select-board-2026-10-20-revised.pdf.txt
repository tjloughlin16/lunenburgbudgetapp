What It Is 
A stormwater utility provides dedicated funding for stormwater 
management.  Over 30 communities in Massachusetts have implemented a 
stormwater utility and many more are working towards adoption. 
How It Works 
• Fees are based on stormwater program needs 
• Rates may vary based on property type and impervious area 
• Revenue is dedicated to stormwater-related activities 
Why It Helps 
• Provides a stable, predictable funding source 
• Supports ongoing maintenance and permit compliance 
• Reduces reliance on individual Town Meeting funding requests

Stormwater Utility Implementation 
 Day 1: Stormwater utility bylaw adopted and stormwater budget approved at Town 
Meeting 
 Year 1: No abatements.  This is necessary to establish the required stormwater 
budget.  Clearly define guidelines to receive abatements. 
 Year 2: Start the abatement process: 
– Single & Two-Family Residents: 50% abatement to property owners who have BMPs on their 
property that are inspected and maintained annually. 
– All Others: 25% partial abatements to be assessed with full or part-time property managers 
with established Operating & Maintenance plans that address stormwater management and 
verification of compliance. 
 Year 3: Enable 50% abatement on all properties meeting inspection & maintenance 
requirements.  
 Year 4: Recalculate fee based on results of the abatement process to ensure required 
budget is being met.
