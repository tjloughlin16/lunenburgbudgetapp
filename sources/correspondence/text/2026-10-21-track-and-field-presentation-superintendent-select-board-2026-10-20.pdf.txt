TURF CONDITIONTurf condition and expected lifespan
Lunenburg Public Schools  •  Selectboard presentation06
A limited service life requires replacement planningSynthetic turf systems commonly have an expected lifespan of approximately eight to ten years. This is a general planning benchmark. Actual service life depends on the field’s condition and use.Our turf is very worn and nearing the end of its useful life. That condition makes replacement planning timely alongside the documented structural concerns at the facility.Engineering will evaluate the field and underlying conditions to establish the appropriate scope.

PROJECT SCOPEScope of the engineering proposal
Lunenburg Public Schools  •  Selectboard presentation08
The July 2024 Collins study recommends putting the turf-field drainage, track drainage, and outlet drainage in the same construction contract. The drainage design depends on the turf system selected.Gale’s proposal addresses the track, turf, retaining walls, bleachers, press box, and drainage as one facility project. Coordinated engineering can address the underlying drainage deficiencies and inform the sequence of construction.The engineering request supports a permanent response to the documented problems, with scope and construction costs informed by design.
