IDEA funding and Maintenance of Effort
LUNENBURG PUBLIC SCHOOLS
03
IDEA grant rules
FY27 allocations: 0240
FY27 allocations: 0262
FY27 IDEA allocations: $423,413 (0240) + $13,967 (0262) = $437,380.
Restricted federal aid
IDEA supports allowable services through an approved grant budget. An allocation does not establish cash received or the balance available.
Maintenance of Effort
Lunenburg must maintain local, or combined state and local, special education support. Budget and actual spending tests allow total or per-student measures.
Permitted reductions
Specific federal exceptions and adjustments require documentation. Examples include fewer students with disabilities or fewer out of district placements. Budget pressure alone does not qualify.
We must check MOE before reducing spending or shifting costs to IDEA. Failure can require repayment from nonfederal funds, and impact getting future entitlement grants.

===SLIDE 4===
Circuit Breaker covers part of eligible costs
LUNENBURG PUBLIC SCHOOLS
04
Eligible costs
Statute
Thresholds and rates
For a standard claim at 75%: reimbursement equals 75% of eligible costs above the student’s threshold. (4x’s the foundation)
Costs that may qualify
Documented IEP instruction, qualifying related services, eligible tuition, required outside transportation, extended school year services and qualifying individual equipment.
Costs outside the claim
Examples include evaluations, IEP coordination, administration, district employee benefits and building adaptations. DESE pricing and documentation rules apply.
Example: $100,000 in an eligible FY26 out of district placement − $53,431 threshold = $46,569. At 75%, reimbursement is $34,926.75.
Lunenburg pays the full bill first. The threshold, unreimbursed share and excluded costs still need funding.

===SLIDE 7===
Circuit Breaker funds have specific spending rules
LUNENBURG PUBLIC SCHOOLS
07
Circuit Breaker FAQ
Accounting rules
Documentation practices
The Special Education Reimbursement Fund is a restricted account with its own spending authority and deadlines.
Receipt and authority
The Town Treasurer records reimbursements for the School Committee. The Committee may spend them on eligible expenses without another Town Meeting appropriation.
Spending window
Regular FY27 receipts may support eligible FY27 or FY28 expenses. They cannot carry into FY29. 
Clear accounting
Track and reconcile opening balances, receipts and expenditures separately. Assign each eligible cost to its funding source once, and retain claim documentation.

===SLIDE 9===
A separate reserve can help with unplanned costs
LUNENBURG PUBLIC SCHOOLS
09
Reserve statute: c. 40, §13E
Circuit Breaker claim cycle
New student needs, tuition increases and transportation changes can create costs after the budget is adopted.
Establishing a reserve
A special education reserve under M.G.L. c. 40, §13E requires majority votes of the School Committee and Town Meeting, plus lawful funding by appropriation or transfer.
Access and limit
Distributions require majority votes of the School Committee and Select Board. The balance may not exceed 2% of annual net school spending.
Planning implication
The reserve is separate from Circuit Breaker. Stable district enrollment does not ensure stable costs, and higher receipts can reflect earlier spending rather than current capacity.
This presentation does not establish that Lunenburg has funded this reserve.
