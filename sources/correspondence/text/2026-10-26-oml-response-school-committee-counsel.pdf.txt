The Open Meeting Law requires that public bodies create and maintain accurate minutes 
of all meetings. M.G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a). Further, the Open Meeting Law requires that “[t]he 
minutes of an open session, if they exist and whether approved or in draft form, shall be made 
available upon request by any person within 10 days.” M.G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c). However, “the 
Open Meeting Law does not require that meeting minutes be posted to a website…” See OML 
2026 – 15; OML 2020 – 118; OML 2023 –218; OML 2023 – 134. As such, the Committee has 
not violated the Open Meeting Law by not posting minutes on its website. 
 
Furthermore, as to the allegation that the Committee did not timely respond to your 
request, after review of the Complaint, the Committee has determined that it did not provide you 
with a response to your request within ten (10) calendar days. However, the Committee has only 
received requests from you for meeting minutes for the years 2024 through 2026. The Committee 
has not received any requests for minutes from you for the years 2022 or 2023, therefore the 
Committee has not failed to respond to such a request where such a request has not yet been 
made.  
 
Accordingly, the Committee is taking all requisite and appropriate remedial action to 
provide you with the minutes you requested on September 8, 2026 as expeditiously as possible. 
Moreover, School Committee members will engage in refresher Open Meeting Law training by 
December 31, 2026 to review their obligations under the Open Meeting Law. We appreciate your 
patience as we work to process your request and provide you with the requested minutes as soon 
as possible.
