# The Capital Planning Committee's final rankings, FY2022 and FY2024-FY2027

These did not come off a website. The Chair of the Capital Planning Committee sent them to TJ
Loughlin on 20 November 2026 in answer to a records request, so the email is the address
(rule 12). The register row is `2026-11-20-cpc-rankings` in `notes/reference/records-requests.csv`.

## How it reached us

**TJ Loughlin asked Peter Beardmore, Chair of the Capital Planning Committee, on 20 November
2026 at 12:23 PM** (copied to Damon McQuaid and Jennifer Warren) for the committee's rankings
for the past five years. **He replied the same day.** TJ pasted the reply into this project and
uploaded the four emails it forwarded, as `.eml`, and five attachments that evening.

The reply and the four emails are transcribed whole in
`correspondence/2026-11-20-cpc-rankings-cpc-chair.txt`, with every email address removed. The
`.eml` originals and the paste are held in the private bucket, whole; see *Privacy* below.

## What each file prints (read 20 November 2026)

- **FY2024 Rev. B**, one sheet: 29 requests sorted by `Composite RANKING`, with the project
  number, `FY2024 Capital Costs`, `Collins Score` and five members' rankings headed `dpassios`,
  `gmartin`, `mbrenner`, `pbeardmore` and `talonzo`. No rank number column: the order is the
  rank. Every figure equals `a194-fy24-composite-project-ranking-by-capital-planning-committee`.
- **FY2025**, one sheet `FY25`: 35 requests by department, with `Dept Priority`, project number,
  `Collins Score`, `FY2025`, five members' ranks (`TA`, `PB`, `MB`, `CM`, `GM`) and `AVERAGE`.
  Every figure equals the `FY25 Inputs` sheet of the CPC workbook already held,
  `fy25-capital-requests-by-department-final-cpc.xlsx`.
- **FY2026**, one sheet: `Ranking` 1-25, department, project, cost and cumulative cost, ending at
  $2,915,877. The same 25 rows are in the email body.
- **FY2027**, four sheets: `individual rankings` (five members, headed by first name),
  `calculations (3)` (each request's `Average` and `Median` by formula), `rankings on average`
  (the 22 ranks, typed averages and cumulative cost, ending at $3,267,208, as in the email body)
  and `rankings on median`.

## Privacy

Every person named is a public official, a board member or a member of the Capital Planning
Committee, writing in that role (`notes/process/DATA-PRIVACY.md`). The `.eml` originals carry
email addresses, including two personal ones, and mail-routing headers. There is no redactor for
`.eml`, so each original is held whole in the private bucket (`withhold` in
`sources/data/redactions.csv`). The public record of their text is our transcription, which
removes every email address and keeps every word of every message. The paste also carries TJ's
own address and is held the same way. The five attachments were screened (`pii_screen.py`: clean
on the four spreadsheets; the PDF has five picture-only pages, read by eye: a title slide, a
section divider, the debt schedule, the IT title slide and a software-cost screenshot) and are
published as delivered.
