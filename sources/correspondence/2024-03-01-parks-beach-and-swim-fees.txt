Fees were voted on tonight and the following was passed:


Resident Season Pass
Family Pass $65
Individual Pass $44
Senior Pass $25


Resident Daily Pass
$6 Adults with up to 3 children age 15 and under
$3 additional children
$3 children 16-18 years of age
$3 Seniors


Non Resident Daily Rate:
$12 Adults with up to 3 children ages 15 and under
$6 additional children
$6 children 16-18
$6 Seniors


Swim Lessons
$30 per child per session capped at $90 per session per family.


For beach pass purposes: A family is defined as two adults and three children under the age of 18 living in the same address. Additional dependents (up to two) living under the same roof, $5.00 per person added on. A senior is defined as anyone over the age of 60. An individual is defined as anyone age 3 and above. A child is defined as anyone under the age of 18.
