# The ski co-op invoice, 2025-26 season

**File:** `2026-03-30-ski-coop-invoice.xlsx`
**sha256:** `f44b5357a801fb5e52a586a489d84f9a15719139523f43239cae771321903588`
**Dated:** 30 March 2026 (cell `A3`), for the 2025-26 season — FY2026.
**Titled:** `A2 = '25-26 Lunenburg High School Co - op Ski Team'`

## How it reached us

Sent to this project by a resident, September 2026. It is the invoice Lunenburg High
School's athletic department issues to the other towns in the co-op: `A44 = 'Please make
checks out to Town of Lunenburg'`, `A45 = 'Mail to: Lunenburg High School Attn Athletics'`.

There is no publisher address. It did not come off a website, and the town does not
publish it.

## What it is, and what grade of evidence

**A sheet somebody assembled — `stated`, under rule 13a**, not a printout from the
accounting system. It carries no account numbers, no period, no warrant reference. What it
does carry is the town's own arithmetic for how the bill is split, from the department that
issues it, and it foots: the line items sum to the stated total and the per-athlete figure
divides that total by the stated roster.

## Why it matters

The district's by-sport workbook prints ONE figure for the ski team — the whole co-op's
cost. This invoice is the only document held that shows Lunenburg does not pay it.
Lunenburg runs the co-op, pays the mountain and the coach, and bills the other towns for
their athletes' shares.

For 2025-26 that is **$12,392 gross and $2,655.43 to Lunenburg** — 3 of 14 athletes.

## What it does NOT establish

- **It is one season.** Nothing here says how FY2025's $12,984 or FY2024's $10,960 split,
  or whether the co-op had the same members in those years.
- **It is a bill, not a receipt.** It states what the other towns were invoiced. It does not
  show they paid. Two lines record $325 already paid by an athlete direct to Lunenburg;
  the rest is `Total Amount Due upon receipt`.
- **Where the reimbursements land is not shown.** A payment from Leominster to the Town of
  Lunenburg lands in some account, and which one decides whether it offsets the athletics
  line, the revolving fund, or general revenue. Nothing here says.
