## What this establishes, and what it does not

- **Who signed the batch cover sheet: shown.** The signature reads as the School Committee
  Chair's name. The reading of a handwritten name is ours; the form prints no name under the
  line.
- **When: not shown.** The 5853 signature is undated. The sheet's printed date is 6/30/2026,
  and the batch's proof list was generated 07/07/2026; the invoices were stamped OK to pay
  6 July and the check is dated 16 July. The signature could have been made any time up to
  the town's processing of warrant 68 26.
- **A signature by the Chair is not a vote of the School Committee.** The cover sheet's text
  is a department head's certification to the Town Accountant that the bills are approved
  and correct. Whether the Chair's signature stands for a committee approval, and under what
  authority, the sheet does not say. No School Committee minutes held record a vote on this
  batch (`money-gaps.csv`, the row on check 218995).
- **Why the district's copy is blank and this one is signed: not established.** One copy
  may have been scanned before signing. Nothing here dates either scan.
- **The Select Board's signature on warrant 68 26 was not asked for** (TJ, 22 Nov: *"I'm
  looking for who signed off from the schools, not really the select board"*), and is not
  in either file.
- **The `OK to Pay` initials: not identified.**
