## What it says

The Committee reviewed the complaint in executive session on 7 October 2026 and authorised
counsel to respond. The letter quotes the complaint's counts of meetings announced and
minutes available, 2022 to 2026, then:

- **On posting:** *"the Open Meeting Law does not require that meeting minutes be posted to a
  website"*, citing OML 2026-15, OML 2020-118, OML 2023-218 and OML 2023-134. *"As such, the
  Committee has not violated the Open Meeting Law by not posting minutes on its website."*
- **On the request:** *"the Committee has determined that it did not provide you with a
  response to your request within ten (10) calendar days."* That is the request of
  8 September 2026 for the minutes of 35 meetings, under M.G.L. c. 30A §22(c).
- **On 2022 and 2023:** the Committee *"has only received requests from you for meeting
  minutes for the years 2024 through 2026"*, so it has not failed to answer a request for
  2022-2023 minutes.
- **Remedy:** *"taking all requisite and appropriate remedial action to provide you with the
  minutes you requested on September 8, 2026 as expeditiously as possible"*, and OML refresher
  training for members *"by December 31, 2026"*.

What it does not address, what it decides and the refiling window (4 November to
30 December 2026) are in `notes/HANDOFF-FILINGS.md`. The first of the 35 minutes delivered
under this remedy is `sources/meetings/PROVENANCE-school-committee-2026-10-27.md`.
