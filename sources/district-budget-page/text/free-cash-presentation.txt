Purchase TouchView screens for the Primary School  (4 = $10,400; 23 = $59,800) 
Currently the school has 2 touchview screens - 1 in 1st grade and 1 in 2nd grade (THES each 
classroom has a touchview). Touchviews have been critical to our educational initiatives but have 
unfortunately been removed from the budget over the past two years due to funding constraints. 
Touchviews are essential tools for enhancing the learning experience in our classrooms. These 
interactive displays offer a dynamic way for students to engage with educational content, making 
lessons more interactive, memorable, and effective. They are particularly valuable where hands-on 
learning and real-time feedback can significantly improve understanding and retention. Without 
these devices, we are limited in our ability to provide the best possible learning environment for our 
students, especially as educational technology continues to evolve. Our current resources, while 
functional, do not meet the growing demand for interactive learning in a tech-driven world. Research 
has shown that classrooms equipped with advanced technology like Touchviews see improved 
student performance, engagement, and enthusiasm for learning. This a one time purchase and this 
investment will serve us for years to come, enhancing both the teaching process and student 
outcomes.

What is 7D Transportation?
Definition:7D transportation, as defined in Massachusetts General Law Chapter 90, Section 7D, 
refers to the transportation of school pupils in vehicles that are not school buses but are used 
for transporting students.  
Vehicle Type: These vehicles are typically passenger vehicles with a gross vehicle weight rating 
of 10,000 pounds or less, and they can have a maximum of 11 seats, including the driver. 
Purpose: 7D vehicles are used to transport students to and from school, extracurricular 
activities, or other school-related events.

Transport Vans -Legal limitations and 
liability 
Pursuant to state and federal law and regulations, fifteen-passenger vans do not comply with state 
or federal law for transportation of students to and from school and school-related activities. See, 49 
United States Code §30112 and 540 CMR 7.09(1). See also generally, School Pupil Transport Vehicles 
(7D), p.3., published by the Massachusetts Registry of Motor Vehicles, January 2023 (copy 
attached).
Federal law explicitly states that “a school or school system may not purchase or lease a new 15-
passenger van if it will be used significantly by, or on behalf of, the school or school system to 
transport preprimary, primary, or secondary school students to or from school or an event related to 
school” 49 USCode §30112 (a)(2).
Transporting students in a 15-passenger van would potentially expose the District to increased 
liability; if transporting students is in fact a significant purpose of the District in acquiring such a 
vehicle, then any third party injured in the course of the District’s use of the vehicle might allege 
that the District was violating federal law in acquiring the vehicle, which could potentially affect the 
applicability and coverage of any insurance the District might have for injuries resulting from the use 
of the vehicle to transport students, and could also adversely affect the District’s ability to assert 
limitations on its liability under the Tort Claims Act and/or based on any release and indemnification 
provided by a student and/or parent.
