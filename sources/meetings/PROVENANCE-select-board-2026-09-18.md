# Select Board minutes delivered 18 September 2026

Sixteen sets of Select Board minutes, none of which the town had published. They are
filed under `sources/meetings/select-board/` like every other set, with the file_id
`rr2026-09-18` in place of an AgendaCenter document number — because **there is no
AgendaCenter document number**. Inventing one would fabricate an address (rule 12), and
the id is the one place a reader can see how a document arrived.

Sent to this project by the Chair of the Select Board, September 2026, in response to a
question about the gap in posted minutes. The requester convention in this archive is to
name the office rather than the person where the delivery is official business.

## What is in it

**Eight approved** — `…-minutes-rr2026-09-18.pdf`:

    2024-08-13  2025-03-18  2025-04-22  2025-09-09
    2025-09-15  2025-10-07  2025-10-14  2025-10-21  2025-11-17

Seven of the eight were approved by the Board at its meeting of 15 September 2026, which
is why they arrive together and more than a year after the meetings they record.

**Seven DRAFT** — `…-minutes-rr2026-09-18-draft.pdf`:

    2024-10-22  2024-11-05  2024-11-19  2025-01-14
    2025-04-01  2025-04-15  2025-05-06

**A draft is not the record.** Minutes become the official record when the body votes to
approve them; until then they are a clerk's account of the meeting, and the Board may
amend them before approving. The `-draft` in the filename is a fact about the document
and travels with it everywhere the path does.

## Why it matters

The archive held **no minutes at all** for any of these sixteen meetings — checked
against `sources/meetings/select-board/` before the copy. For three of them
(7 October, 14 October and 21 October 2025) the town had published the **agenda** and
never the minutes, so the gap was visible on the board comparison as a meeting that
happened and produced no record.

## What this does NOT establish

- **It does not say when the town posted anything.** These came by email. Nothing here is
  evidence about the AgendaCenter, and the posting dates the board comparison measures are
  unchanged by this delivery.
- **A date of approval is not a date of posting.** Seven were approved on 15 September
  2026; whether and when they were then posted is a separate question this delivery does
  not answer.
- **The drafts may change.** Any figure or motion read out of a draft is provisional, and
  the approved version may differ.
