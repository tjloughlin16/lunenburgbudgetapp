Beardmore School Comm; Matt Brenner Planning Board
Guest: John Petrin, Community Paradigm Assoc. LLC
The meeting convened at 6:30pm
Murphy called the meeting to Order
There were no minutes to review.
Community Paradigm provided an overview of the interviews conducted for the profile and
presented to the committee the draft Position Statement (emailed last week).
A discussion ensued about the Ideal Candidates section. Beardmore questioned the order of the
list. Petrin stated it is not in any prioritized order. The committee reiterated the need for the "soft
skills" eg leadership, communication
Alonzo asked that the language about attracting and retaining employees clarify that it is not unique
to Lunenburg,
Murphy asked that we require candidates to provide a written project and make a presentation.
Petrin suggested that for the interviewees it be something they have done before, and it be limited
to a few minutes. It was agreed all would like to see the candidate's presentation skills.
The position statement was approved unanimously. Petrin stated it would be placed with MMA
tomorrow and mailed to a list they have of town managers, administrators and department heads in
other towns,
Dates and agendas moving forward were agreed as follows:
November 18* 7pm to review all applications and to sort into 3 tiers
Dec, 11" and 12" at 6pm to interview, preferably up to 6 candidates (3 each night)
Community Paradigm will conduct the initial interview, with committee members
engaging in follow-up
After a Motion, the meeting was adjourned at 7:17pm.
Respectfully submitted
Tim murphy
