9/28/26 Minutes 
 Roll Call: M. Brenner, A. Reid, A. Burney, A. Nicastro, T. Willsner 
1. CALL TO ORDER 
M. Brenner called the meeting to order at 6:04 pm. 
 2. BOARD COMMENT 
None. 
 3. CHAIR / DIRECTOR’S REPORT 
Planning Director Steve Wallace reported that meetings with several Town boards and 
committees had been scheduled regarding proposed zoning amendments. The Board scheduled 
a public forum on the proposed amendments for December 1, 2026, at 7 pm, at the Senior 
Center to provide information and receive community feedback. 
4. PUBLIC HEARINGS 
 30 School Street, Site Plan Approval (PBSA-26-6) 
The Board opened the continued public hearing for the proposed relocation of the Lunenburg 
Community Food Pantry to 30 School Street. Representatives for the project reviewed plans to 
install a temporary modular structure while a permanent facility is pursued. 
Discussion focused on parking availability, ADA accessibility, utility connections, lighting, and 
permitting requirements. The applicant explained the anticipated operations of the food pantry 
and the expected duration of the temporary use. The Board also discussed the status of a zoning 
variance application that remains under review by the Zoning Board of Appeals and will be

149 Summer Street Continued from 9/14/26 Modification to Site Plan Approval PMOD-26-5 
The applicant presented revised plans in response to comments raised at the previous hearing. 
Updated drawings included additional dimensional information and clarification regarding site 
layout. 
Board members discussed parking circulation, storage locations, loading and unloading 
activities, and operational restrictions associated with the existing approval. The applicant 
addressed questions regarding daily business operations and the use of outdoor areas. 
Motion:  A Burney to close the public hearing. 
Second: A. Reid 
Vote: Unanimous. 
The Board reviewed the proposed conditions of approval and discussed operational limitations 
intended to protect adjoining properties and maintain compliance with zoning requirements. 
Motion:  A Burney to approve the modification to the Site Plan Approval subject to the 
conditions contained in the decision. 
Second: A. Reid 
Vote: Unanimous. 
 10 Parmenter Road Continued from 5/11/26 Stormwater Permits (PBSW 26-12 & PBSP 26-
13) and Special Permits (PBSW-26-3 & PBSP– 26-4)  
The Board continued its review of draft decisions associated with Phase 1 of the proposed 
development. Discussion focused on permit conditions, construction sequencing, stormwater 
management requirements, inspection procedures, reporting obligations, and long-term 
maintenance responsibilities. 
Several revisions were made to the draft decision to clarify compliance requirements during 
construction and operation of the project. 
Motion:  A. Burney to close the public hearing. 
Second: A. Reid 
Vote:   All in favor 
The Board then reviewed the amended draft decision and discussed final permit conditions. 
Motion:  A. Burney to approve the Special Permits and Stormwater Permits as amended. 
Second: A. Reid 
Vote:   All in favor
