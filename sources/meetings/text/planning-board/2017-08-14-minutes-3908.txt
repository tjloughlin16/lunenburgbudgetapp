BOARD COMMENT/CONCERNS:      
5 & 9 Electric Avenue -  G. Bittner initiated a discussion of the parking situation at Asian Imperial Restaurant.  An application 
was submitted late winter 2016/early spring  2017, but later withdrawn .  To date, 9 Electric Avenue has not been properly 
permitted for parking.  G. Bittner feels sufficient time has passed for the Board to initiate a review of the existing Permit.  He 
would like the Board to review all permits issued by the Planning Board , Zoning Board of Appeals  and the Conservation 
Commission in order to identify any  compliance issues and formalize a request for enforcement by the Building 
Commissioner/Zoning Official for 9 Electric Avenue.   
 
The property owner will be invited to the Board meeting to address any issues.  If prior to the September 11 th Board meeting a 
Site Plan Approval Application is submitted, the Board will forego the review.  
 
D. Prokowiew inquired as the second parking lot is unapproved, is the Town liable if anyone gets injured.  G . Bittner responded 
that could be addressed during the permit review process. 
 
D. Prokowiew, Motion, to initiate a permit review for 5 & 9 Electric A venue, Second, M. Jeffreys,  Discussion, K. Chenis 
inquired if the primary concern is people using the vacan t lot for parking, G. Bittner responded that as they are not permitted, 
they do not meet the requirements for accessory parking under the Bylaw.  He went on to note that there also violations under  
the existing Permit and that the existing conditions do no t reflect what was approved by the Planning Board  in 2011 for 5 Electric 
Avenue.  Motion modified, D. Prokowiew, to initiate a permit review for 5 & 9 Electric Avenue to take place on September 
11, 2017, Second, M. Jeffreys, roll call vote, M. Jeffrey s, ay e; D. Prokowiew, aye; M. Allison, aye; G. Bittner, aye  K. 
Chenis, nay.  Motion passed 4-1.
