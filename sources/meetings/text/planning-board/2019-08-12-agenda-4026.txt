TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
PUBLIC HEARING 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board and Lunenburg Tree Warden will hold a  Public 
Hearing on August 12, 2019, 6:35 PM, Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA 
to hear and review a request to  prune and/or remove trees through the Storm  
Resiliency Program, along Leominster Road from West Street to Lancaster 
Avenue, and along Lancaster Avenue from Massach usetts Avenue to Rolling 
Acres, under M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road Designations and under 
M.G.L. Ch. 87, Section 3, Cutting of Public Shade Trees .  Subject trees range 
from eight (8) inches to thirty (30) plus inches and consist of the following  
species: Maple, Red Pine, Norway Spruce, Silver Maple, Red Maple, Sugar 
Maple, Cherry, Elm, Norway Maple, Hemlock, Sycamore Maple, European 
Beech, Ginkgo, Black Locust, Magnolia, White Pine, Spruce, Ash, Hickory, Oak, 
and Red Oak.  Owners are the Town of Lunenburg and various private parties .  
Applicant is Unitil, 357 Electric Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462.  T he Storm 
Resiliency Program is available for viewing in the Planning Office.
