Due to Family Illness, A. Burney participated remotely under Section 2.02 Remote Participation (Adopted on 05 /22/2013;

The Hearing continuation was to hear and review an application under Chapter 325 Subdivision Regulations, Article III, Section 
325-3.4. Definitive Plan of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg  for a six-lot subdivision consisting of six duplex buildings at 101 
Pleasant Street (Map 095, Parcel 00 03), Lunenburg, MA .  Owner/applicant was MA Pleasant LLC, P O Box 903, Ashburnham, 
MA.     
 
There was no representation from the applicant  and the Planning Office had not received any reques t for an extension of the 
public hearing.  A. Burney would reach out to the applicant for a signed letter of continuance.  A. Burney was aware that the 
applicant had requested some zoning interpretations from the Building Commissioner/Zoning Official.  Boa rd members had 
concerns with the Declaration of Protective Covenants (Homeowners Association Agreement) provided by the applicant’s 
attorney.  If the timeclock runs out, the Board would have to either deny the project or approve with conditions.  A. Burney  would 
request revised homeowners’ association documents from the applicant’s attorney and also a signed letter of continuance.   
 
P. Bertram made Motion to continue the Hearing for 101 Pleasant Street to October 25, 2021, 6:35 p.m., M. Allison 
Seconded, all aye.  Vote passed 3-0. 
 
Hearing recessed 8:38 p.m.
