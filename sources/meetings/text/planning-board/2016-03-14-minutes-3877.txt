Minutes 
Informational Meeting 
WalMart - 301 Massachusetts Avenue 
March 14, 2016 
 
Meeting Posted:  Yes 
Time:   6:35 PM 
Place:  Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  01462 
Present:  Damon McQuaid, Kenneth Chenis, Gregory Bittner, David Prokowiew, Adam R. Burney 
Absent:  Matthew Allison 
 
The Information Meeting was  to hear and discuss an application for a development plan review under Section 8.4. of the 
Protective Bylaw of the Town of Lunenburg for outdoor display and sales of goods at WalMart, 301 Massachusetts Avenue. 
 
WalMart was represented by store manager Mark Masicotte and engineer Alan Carpenter , PE of CPH, 520 Hartford Turnpike, 
Vernon, CT.   
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals granted a Special Permit on  October 28, 2015 for outdoor display area of four (4’) feet by ninety -
five (95’) foot area to the left of the entrance to display seasonal items.  
 
Applicant wishes to utilize a 4x95 foot area (380 square feet) to the left side of the s tore under the exist ing canopy for outside 
display.  The display will extend four feet out from the side of the building.  The pedestrian access area in front of the store 
extends out ten feet so ample walkway will remain for pedestrians.  Outdoor display will be yearly from April 1 to November 1.  
The outdoor display area will not impact any parking spaces.   
 
Motion, K. Chenis to approve plan for outdoor display, Second, D. Prokowiew, all aye.  
 
Information Meeting closed 7:00 PM.
