DISCUSSION WITH BOARD OF SELECTMEN The Board of Selectmen, in discussing the status of the 
Whalom Overlay District in regard to the Town Meeting Article, raised the question of whether or not 
the Planning Board would be  willing to move forward with the article based on the concerns , raised by 
the Building Commissioner, that there are potential spot zoning issues and legal concerns for the future 
of the Emerald Place development?  The Planning Board felt they were ready a nd willing to continue 
with the article.  
 
PUBLIC HEARING, HIGHFIELD VILLAGE (cont’d) - Motion, Mr. McQuaid moved to accept the applicant’s 
Letter of Withdrawal dated July 20, 2016, Second, Mr. Prokowiew.  All aye.
