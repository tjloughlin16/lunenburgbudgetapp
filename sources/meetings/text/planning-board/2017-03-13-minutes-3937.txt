ANR PLAN:  Alan Maki, 171 Lancaster Avenue- Plan presented by Larry Sabean, Hannigan Engineering, Inc..  Plan will create 
Parcel A consisting of 2.31 ± acres.  There was discussion of firming up the ANR process to require submittal to the office two 
weeks prior to a Board meeting.  This will allow the office time to send out the plan for departmental comments, which would then 
be available to the Board at the “formal filing” of the plan.   
 
PUBLIC HEARINGS:  274 Prospect Street (cont’d from 2/27)- see separate minutes. 
 
DISCUSSION WITH TOWN COUNSEL:  There was general discussion with Town Counsel on various topics  to include, but not 
limited to, integrating 40B w ith the Master Plan, Safe Harbor and regulating ownership model.  A. Burney note d that the Town 
has an approved Housing Production Plan.  When a project is first approved, that is when it qualifies for the Subsidized Housing 
Inventory (SHI) list.  Town Counsel noted regulating ownership would have to be defined in the decision.  Zonin g regulates form, 
structure, and use; ownership is not controlled by zoning.
