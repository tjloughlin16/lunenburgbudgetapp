Public Hearing – Stormwater Permit 
360 Flat Hill Road 
August 23, 2021 
 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  8:52 p.m. 
 
CALL TO ORDER:  Present:  Tanner Cole, Matthew Allison, Paula Bertram, Matthew Brenner, Adam R. Burney  
Absent:  Erik Torgersen  
 
Due to Illness, P. Bertram participate remotely under Section 2.02 Remote Pa rticipation (Adopted on 05/22/2013; Amended 
8/12/2020) 
 
The Hearing was to hear and review a stormwater management permit under Chapter 204, Stormwater and Storm Sewer s, for 
360 Flat Hill Road (Map 087, Parcel 0025), Lunenburg, MA.  Owner/applicant was Matson Homes II Inc., 129 Mason Road, New 
Ipswich, NH  03071.    
 
The applicant/owner, Ken Matson, represented himself along with Mark Elbag, M.A. Elbag Engineering.  
 
This was a filing for eight (8) lots, with two common driveways, all serviced by private we lls and on-site septic.  Roof water would 
go into individual chambers.  Driveway water would go into recharge trenches and infiltration.  Each lot would also have a ra in 
garden for overflow .  The rain gardens would be placed near each driveway entrance to be highly visible for homeowner 
maintenance.  P. Bertram wanted the stormwater management plan, containing the operation and maintenance plan, referenced 
on individual deeds.  M. Allison noted pre-development runoff was lower than the post-development runoff.  M. Elbag responded 
that the calculations d idn’t account for some of the ponding behind the driveways.  Design was very conservative and there 
would be a reduction in al l storms.  A. Burney would confirm with reviewing engineer, Jeffrey Walsh, Graves E ngineering, Inc.  
Cultec chambers would take all roof water.  Stormwater was modeled on the assumption that all driveways would be paved.  All 
driveways had an infiltration swale to capture driveway volume.   
 
Glen Logan, 5 Watt Street- Voiced concern that if individual homeowners did not maintain stormwater maintenance, runoff would 
come down to his property.  M. Elbag responded  there would be a swale along G. Loga n’s back property line to direct flow away 
from his property towards the frontage on either Lot 6 or Lot 7.

Susan Whalen, Flat Hill Road - Had concerns that the property was previously an apple orchard.  Would any possible pesticides

T. Cole noted the rain garden on Lot 5 appeared to be too close to the stone wall.
