250 Whalom Road, Lunenburg Village - Richard Heaton, H&H Associates LLP gave the Board the comprehensive permit 
background of the project through the Zoning Board of Appeals.  The applicant, who originally submitted the project as 
condominiums, is now seeking to modify the existing Comprehensive Permit to all rental units  (120) and the Board is inquiring if 
this changes the project from a 40B to a 40R.  Mr. Heaton noted to the Board that this project would still fall under a 40B , and the 
Town will get credit towards its Subsidized Housing Inventory (SHI) for the rentals .  Town will receive less tax revenues if project 
goes from condominiums to rentals.  Seventy (70%) percent of the units would have “local preference”.  Mr. Heaton noted that 
the Town has an approved Housing Production Plan (HPP).  Under that Plan, if the Town produces half of one percent of its 
housing as affordable every year, the Town can be exempt from 40B.  For Lunenburg, that would be approximately 20 units/year.  
The Zoning Board of Appeals will inform the developer that the Planning Board would like him to come before them with a 
presentation.
