Tri Town Landing - Maintenance Shed- Great Bridge Properties, represented by William Caselden, presented a request for a 
second shed on the site i n conjunction with the construction of the fourth building.  There is currently one shed already on site at 
full capacity.  The requested shed, which will be located approximately in the middle of the site, will also have an overhang  for 
residents’ bicycle storage.  A. Burney noted to the Board this is considered a Minor Change to the plan  and does not require a 
public hearing as it is a  functional upgrade and does not change the site density.   K. Chenis questioned roof runoff.  The shed 
will be on a grasse d area, with a concrete walk at the front.  Drainage will be to the grassed area, not the parking lot.  Shed will 
be used for storage of lawn equipment.  No vehicles will be stored in the shed.  Motion to approve Minor Change, K. Chenis, 
Second, D. Prokowiew, all aye.
