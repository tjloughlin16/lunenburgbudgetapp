Public Hearing 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on August 28, 2017, 6:35 PM, Town

Special Permit and Site Plan Approval  under Sections 4.15. and 8.4. of the Protective Bylaw of

installation at 994 Northfield Road (Map 037, Parcel 0013).  Applicant/Owner is 994 Northfield 
Road, LLC, William J. Gustus, 19 Burke Street, Lunenburg, MA.  Materials  are available for 
viewing at the Planning Office, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA 01462.
