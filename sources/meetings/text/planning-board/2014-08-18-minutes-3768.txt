Workshop on Village District Bylaw- 
Discussion of mixed use district vs overlay district.  N. Lockwood prepared a Use Table.  C. Fleck noted tha t the Board does a 
“double process” in requiring both a special permit and a development plan review.  It should be one or the other.  That 
distinction can be made with the Village District Bylaw (if it’s not an overlay district).  Would have to do both pr ocesses with an 
overlay district.  Board could determine whether the Zoning Board of Appeals or Planning Board would be the permitting 
authority for a special permit.  Board used the Village Center Overlay District (VCOD) Model Bylaw as a template.
