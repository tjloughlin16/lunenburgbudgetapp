Minutes
Public Hearing
5 Summer Street
March 27, 2023
Meeting Posted: Yes
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA
Time: 6:05 p.m.
Present: Matthew Allison, Matthew Brenner, Amanda Reid, Timothy Willsmer, Adam Burney
Absent: Erik Torgersen
The Hearing was to hear and review an application for site plan approval under Chapter 250 of the Code of the Town of
Lunenburg for a Brazilian restaurant at 5 Summer Street. Owner-M.C.M.S. Realty Trust, Erin E. Donnelly Trustee, 72 Rinnock
Road, Fitchburg, MA; applicant- Marlon Mei, 395 Cross Street, Boylston, MA 01505.
The applicant represented himself. The restaurant was located in a vacant existing unit. The site contained ~79 parking spaces.
There were ~15 at the site rear, which would be used for employee parking.
The unit had not been used for a restaurant
previously. This triggered a change in use, which required site plan approval. The property had a direct connect to Fitchburg
sewer and Fitchburg was made aware of the application. Furthermore, in order to obtain a permit for a food service operation,
the applicant needed to submit detailed kitchen plans to the Board of Health.
M. Brenner made Motion to close the Hearing for 5 Summer Street, A. Reid Seconded, passed unanimously.
Hearing closed 6:15 p.m.
Application for Site Plan Approval, dtd 3.1.23
