April 11, 2016 
203 West Townsend Road

Present:  Damon McQuaid, Kenneth Chenis, Gregory Bittner, David Prokowiew, Adam R. Burney 
 
Matthew Allison recused himself. 
 
The Public Hearing  was to hear and discuss a Stormwater Management Special Permit under Section 6.7. of the Protective 
Bylaw of the Town of Lunenburg  for construction of s ingle family homes and associated site work on Lots 1 -5, 203 West 
Townsend Road.     
 
The Applicant, Mark Horgan, was represented by Rob Oliva, David E. Ross Associates (DER), Inc .  On January 11, 2016 the 
Board endorsed an ANR Plan which created the five lots.  Section 6.7. requires approval of a stormwater management plan for 
all development projects that disturb one acre or more.  The Applicant submitted an Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan, 
EPA Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP), and an Erosion & Sedimentation Control Plan.    
 
Total area consists of approximately 16.4 acres with 4.1 acres being disturbed for construction of the houses.   The purpose of 
the stormwater special permit is to limit runoff during and after construction that would harm adjacent properties or the Town’s 
stormwater system.  As required, a Notice of Intent ( NOI) will be submitted to the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) noting 
there is a SWPPP in place.  The development of the lots does not include any permanent st ormwater management systems.  As 
part of the house construction, s tone recharge trenches will be installed along each of the foundations along the roof drip edge.  
The Applicant is responsible for putting erosion control in place, but m aintenance of the tr enches will ultimately be the 
responsibility of the individual homeowner.  A silt fence will be installed on the downhill side of each lot. 
 
Motion, K. Chenis to close the Public Hearing, Second, G. Bittner, all aye.  
 
Hearing closed 7:50 PM
