3. CHAIR / DIRECTOR’S REPORT  
 S. Wallace reminded the Board that a public forum regarding proposed zoning amendments 
for the 2027 Annual Town Meeting would be held on December 1, 2026, at the Adult 
Activity Center. The forum will be recorded and aired by Public Access Television. He also 
reported that Town Counsel had completed a review of the draft zoning amendments and 
that staff would be working through the comments received. In addition, the Montachusett 
Regional Planning Commission completed the economic development mapping project and 
the Planning Directors has distributed the results for future discussion. 
4. APPROVAL NOT REQUIRE (ANR) PLANS 
 460 Howard Street (ANR-26-6) 
Kevin O'Brien presented a plan to create four buildable lots from a portion of a previously 
approved subdivision. The applicant explained that the original subdivision layout would 
not be pursued as approved and that a revised development concept would be submitted in 
the future. The Board discussed frontage, access, a proposed common driveway, future 
stormwater permitting requirements, and designation of a non-buildable lot shown on the 
plan. The Board determined the plan met the requirements for ANR endorsement. 
Motion:  A Burney moved to endorse the ANR for 460 Howard Street 
Second: T. Nicastro 
Roll Call Vote:  A Nicastro – Aye, . Burney – Aye, A Reid – Aye, M. Brenner – Aye 
 35 Leominster Road (ANR-26-8) 
Ryan Proctor, representing the applicant, presented a plan to divide a 17-acre parcel into 
two lots. One lot contains an existing single-family dwelling, while the remaining land 
would remain available for future development. The Board discussed zoning districts, 
frontage, and lot configuration and found the proposal satisfied ANR requirements. 
Motion: A Burney moved to endorse the ANR for 35 Leominster Road 
Second: T. Nicastro

6. PUBLIC HEARINGS 
760 Goodrich Street, Special Permit, (PMSP-26-10) Shared Driveway Special Permit and 
De Minimis Stormwater Modification  
The Board opened the public hearing to consider a Special Permit for a shared drivew and 
a related modification to a previously approved stormwater permit. While originally 
proposed as a shared driveway, the project was eventually approved as single driveways for 
each of the three building lots.  Now the developer want to have a shared driveway due to 
site grading and topography and requested approval to return to the original design. Graves 
Engineering reviewed the revised plans and confirmed that prior comments had been 
addressed.  
 The Board discussion focused on driveway location, traffic circulation, fire department 
access, stormwater management responsibilities, and long-term maintenance requirements. 
One abutter spoke in support of the shared driveway configuration. 
Motion:  A. Burney to close the public hearing for PBSP-26-10.  
Second:  T. Nicastro  
Roll Call: A. Nicastro – Aye; A. Burney – Aye; A. Reid – Aye; M. Brenner – Aye.  
Motion: To approve PBSP-26-10 with all previous stormwater permit conditions and a 
condition requiring execution, recording, and submission of a homeowners association or 
similar maintenance agreement for the shared driveway and stormwater system prior to 
issuance of occupancy permits.  
Second: A. Nicastro.  
Roll Call: A. Nicastro – Aye; A. Burney – Aye; A. Reid – Aye; M. Brenner – Aye.  
The Board then considered the requested stormwater modification and determined that the 
changes qualified as de minimis.  
Motion:  A Burney to find the requested stormwater modification de minimis.  
Second:  T. Nicastro  
Roll Call: A. Nicastro – Aye; A. Burney – Aye; A. Reid – Aye; M. Brenner – Aye. Motion 
carried.

7.  DECISIONS and BUSINESS 
 110 Townsend Harbor Road 
 The Board reviewed a request to extend a $4,000 stormwater performance bond associated 
with a five-lot development. Three homes have been completed, while two remain 
unfinished. Staff reported that the existing bond was scheduled to expire and that the 
applicant requested additional time to complete the project. The Board discussed the history 
of the approval and agreed that an extension was appropriate. 
Motion:  A. Burney to extend the performance bond for 100 Townsend Harbor Road
