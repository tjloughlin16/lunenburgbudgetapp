TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board and Lunenburg Tree Warden will hold a public 
hearing under M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road Designations and under 
M.G.L. Ch. 87, Section 3, Cutting of Public Shade Trees  on February 28, 2022 , 
6:05 p.m. via Zoom video conference to hear and review a  request to remove 21 
trees and prune 2 trees  along Leominster Road from Sunny Hill Road to Kilburn 
Street through the Reliability Project.  Subject trees range from above three (3) 
inches to thirty (30) plus inches and consist of the following species:  Hickory, 
Black Cherry, Maple, Cherry, Elm, Red Oak, Red M aple, Sugar Maple, and 
Norway Maple.  Owners are the Town of Lunenburg and various private parties.  
Applicant is UNITIL, 357 Electric Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462.  The 
application is available for viewing in the Planning Office, 960 Massachusetts 
Avenue, Lunenburg, MA .  This meeting will be broadcast live through local 
access cable and on FacebookLive on the Public Access Facebook page 
(https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-
157427270975305/) and will be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access 
YouTube channel within 24 hours after the meeting.  To Participate Remotely: 
From a computer, please use the link b elow to join the public  meeting webinar: 
https://us02web.zoom.us/j/7663167607 or from a telephone, dial the toll free 
number (888) 475-4499 and enter Webinar ID: 7663167607
