Minutes 
Public Hearing (continuation) 
151-181 Leominster Shirley Road 
May 13, 2019 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  6:37 PM 
Present:  Matthew Allison, Kenneth Chenis, Paula Bertram, Adam R. Burney 
Absent:  Tanner Cole 
 
The Hearing continuation was to hear and discuss an application for Modification to Site Plan Approval under Section 250 - 8.4. 
of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg, MA  to permit the current operation of gravel manufacturing and processing at 151 (Map 
146, Parcel 27) and 181 (Map 146, Parcel 8) Leominster Shirley Road, Lunenburg, MA.  Applicant/Owner is Steven Powell, PSG 
Realty 393-435, LLC, 133 Leominster Shirley Road, Lunenburg, MA.  
 
The Applicant/Owner was represented by Brian Marchetti, McCarty Engineering, Inc.  The process started in fall 2018 for 
approval of the current operation at 151 -181 Leominster Shirley Road.  A por tion of the property is zoned Residential 
(approximately 8 acres), but being used for commercial purposes.  By citizen petition, an Article was presented, and passed, at 
May 2019 Town Meeting for consistent rezoning within each lot to extend to the propert y boundaries.  K. Chenis made Motion 
to approve Modification to Site Plan Approval, P. Bertram Seconded, all aye.   
 
Hearing closed 6:47 PM
