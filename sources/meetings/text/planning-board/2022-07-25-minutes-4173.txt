ANR PLAN: 
101 Pleasant Street-  The Plan was presented by Graz Engineering, LLC.  The Board had previously endorsed an AN R Plan in 
November 2021; but t he Conservation Commission requested the applicant shift the building further from the wetland line.  To 
accomplish this and meet the 175 foot lot width requirement of the Zoning Bylaw, th e Plan presented tonight  adjusted the 
boundaries of Lot 1 and Lot 2.  M. Brenner made Motion to accept the ANR Plan for 101 Pleasant Street, A. Reid 
Seconded, passed unanimously.   
 
PUBLIC HEARINGS:

of construction would be set at 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Monday through Friday, and 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Saturdays.  M. Brenner made 
Motion to approve the Stormwater P ermit for Northfield Road and Holman Street with Conditions a s written, A. Reid
