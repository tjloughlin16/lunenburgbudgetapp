Minutes 
Public Hearing 
1263 Reservoir Road & 435 Leominster-Shirley Road 
(aka 535 Leominster-Shirley Road +/-) 
September 13, 2021 
 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  7:21 p.m. 
 
CALL TO ORDER:  Present:  Tanner Cole, Matthew Allison, Paula Bertram, Matthew Brenner, Erik Torgersen, Adam R. Burney 
 
The Hearing continuation was to h ear and review applications for site plan approval and stormwater management permit under 
Ch. 250, Section 8.4. and Chapter 204 respectively of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg  for construction of a 372,000 square 
foot warehouse and distribution building off Leominster -Shirley Road at 1263 Reservoir Road (Map 142, Parcel 0006) , owner 
Paul Keating (Heirs) c/o Mary Coburn, et. al., 998 Reservoir Road, Lunenburg, MA  01462 and 435 Leominster -Shirley Road  
(Map 143, Parcel 0007), Lunenburg, MA, owner J&A Realty Trust, Daniel L. McNiff Trustee, 280 Great Road, Shirley MA 01464.  
Applicant is JMC/TBG Lunenburg, LLC, 100 Grandview Road, Suite 203, Braintree, MA  02184.   
 
In attendance were project engineer s Chris Anderson and Bill Hannigan, Hannigan Engineering ; representatives from The 
Brennan Group, Inc.; Eric Riese, Greenman-Pedersen, Inc.; and Peter Mahoney, John M. Corcoran & Co.   
 
Subsequent to the August 23 rd hearing, the applicant modified the site plan and attended meetings with both the DPW Director 
Rob Oliva and peer reviewer Jeff Walsh, Graves Engineering, Inc.  The site entrance was modified for improved accessibility 
along with drainage changes to accommodate same.  Additional signage was proposed along Leominster -Shirley Road due to 
concerns raised by  both the Planning and Select Boards.  The Conservation Commission granted an Order of Conditions for 
work within the buffer zones.  T. Cole suggested the applicant meet with the DPW Director for discussion of the intersection of 
Reservoir Road and Leominster-Shirley Road.  He was of the opinion that if earthwork took place along the side of the road  to 
clear the shoulder and improve sightlines oppos ite the entrance to Reservoir Road, it could possibly negate the costly need to 
the applicant for repaving, lights, grading, etc.

The Tr affic Study was based on the Institute for Transportation Engineers Trip Generation Manual based on the building’s 
square footage.   
 
As no users have been identified for the facility, no hours of operation could be defined. 
 
W. Hannigan added clarification that the truck driver personnel were included in the employee count.   
 
P. Bertram noted abutter concerns with truck noise from the site that compared it to the Keatin g construction vehicles.  P. 
Mahoney noted the beeping from a tractor trailer backin g up would be at a lower decibel than the Keating large construction 
trucks.  There would also be a natural 25 foot high berm at the rear of the site versus the open Keating site.   
 
Mary Perkins, 62 Ruth Street -  Concerned with the back -up beeping and would like to see a cement noise barrier erected  in 
addition to the 25 foot berm.   
 
Laura Boussom, Robbs Hill Road - She could hear the machinery noise and trucks from the Keating site.  She had concerns with 
individuals cutting through Robbs Hill to get to Route 2 if traffic was backed up at Reservoir Road.   
 
Steve Bean, 27 Fire Road 15- Reiterated above public concerns with noise and traffic.   
 
David Rodgers, 82 Hig hland Street-  He noted his support of increasing the tax base in Lunenburg, but was concerned with the 
lack of specificity as to the hours of operation and number of employees.  He asked if this was considered a high-bay distribution 
warehouse.  He mentioned that the property across the road from the proposed project was for sale.  He opined that w hat was 
done with the proposed project could have a significant impact on the property across the road.  He also inquired if the applicant 
planned to retain ownership of the property once developed, or sell.   
 
C. Anderson noted the intent was to add a sec ond, 11 foot wide turning lane for entry only into the site.  That entrance would be 
on the east side of the property with the exit on the west side.  As there would be a turning lane into the site, there would not be a 
need for any east -bound traffic to s werve around trucks waiting to enter the site.  He reviewed the on -site traffic pattern.  Truck 
bays would be located on the west side of the building.  Employee parking would be at the front of the building, along the east 
side and additional spaces along  the north side.  A total of 405 spaces would be provided, inclusive of both employees and 
visitors.  The septic system would run along the front of the property.   
 
M. Perkins- She felt there would be a negative real estate impact on homes adjacent to the facility.

M. Brenner stressed that the site had been zoned Office Park/Industrial for a number of years. 
 
P. Bertram wanted a more substantial sound barrier with data on the decibel level it could handle.  Both M. Allison and P. 
Bertram felt there were a lo t of unknowns with the project, e.g., the use of the facility , the number of shifts, and hours of 
operation.   
 
T. Cole requested a peer review of the Traffic Study.  He re iterated M. Brenner ’s comment  that this was industrial land, an 
approved use under the bylaw, and that the applicant and property owner have the right to develop that use.   
 
D. Rodgers- He again asked if this would be a high -bay facility and if the applic ant planned to retain the property once 
developed.  P. Mahoney responded it would be considered a high-bay use.   
 
P. Bertram asked if the Board could set the hours of operation under site plan approval  and inquired as to a lighting plan.  C. 
Anderson note d the photometric plan was contained within the plan set  and sum marized there would be stand -alone lights 
throughout the parking area, security lighting along the perimeter of the westerly bay area, and wall lighting on the buildin g itself.  
A. Burney could not find anything in the bylaw that allowed the Board to restrict hours, but felt the Board could “Condition” quiet 
hours.   
 
P. Bertram made Motion to require a peer review of the project’s Traffic Study, M. Brenner Seconded, all aye.   
 
P. Bertram made  Motion to continue the hearing for 1263 Reservoir Road and 435 Leominster -Shirley Road  to 
September 27, 2021, 6:35 p.m., M. Brenner Seconded, all aye.   
 
Hearing recessed 8:32 p.m.
