Public Hearing – Scenic Road 
360 Flat Hill Road 
September 27, 2021 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  7:42 p.m. 
 
CALL TO ORDER:  Present:  Tanner Cole, Matthew Allison, Paula Bertram 
Absent:  Matthew Brenner 
 
Under Section 2.02 Remote Participation (Adopted on 05/22/2013; Amended 8/12/2020), the following individuals par ticipated 
remotely: 
Erik Torgersen, Geographic Distance (departed the meeting approximately 8:00 p.m.) 
Adam Burney, Family Illness  
 
The Hearing continuation was to hear and review a request under M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road Designations and 
under M.G.L. Ch. 87, Section 3, Cutting of Public Shade Trees for the cutting of approximately three (3) trees and construction  of 
four (4) driveways onto five (5) lots, with Lots 2 and 3 sharing a common drive  at 360 Flat Hill Road  (Map 087, Parcel 00 25), 
Lunenburg, MA.  Owner/applicant was Matson Homes II Inc., 129 Mason Road, New Ipswich, NH  03071.    
 
Present were the applicant, Ken Matson, Matson Homes II Inc. and Mark Elbag, M.A. Elbag Engineering.  A revised Plan was 
submitted reconfiguring the driveway for Lot 1 to avoid a significant street tree.  Electric service and poles were also added to the 
Plan.  The Plan showed wiring for Lot 1 from Pole 23.  K. Matson informed the Board that the Plan would be revised for Pole 24 
to service both Lots 1 and 2.   If any underground trenching disturbed the wall, the wall would be re paired to retain its character.  
The only overhead wiring proposed was along the common driveway for Lots 2 and 3, which would then split into underground to 
service 2 and 3.   
 
The Plan would be updated to reflect Pole 24 servicing both Lot 1 and 2.   
 
At the Board’s next meeting of October 25, a draft approval would be available for Board review and possible approval of final. 
 
P. Bertram made Motion to continue the Hearing to October 25, 2021, 6:35 p.m., M. Allison Second ed, all aye.  Vote 
passed 3-0.  
 
Hearing recessed 8:07 p.m.
