EARTH REMOVAL: A draft bylaw was provided to Board members.  T he current Earth Removal Bylaw is a general bylaw  under 
the jurisdiction of  the Board of Selectmen .  The  Board of Selectmen feels it should fall under the Planning Board , yet remain a 
general bylaw.  The revised bylaw would cover what activities trigger a permit and also what would be exempt.  There is currently 
no regulation on earth fill; that would also be included in the revised bylaw .  Discussion ensued on the proposed bylaw.  Board 
members will provide their input to A. Burney and Draft #1 will be revised.
