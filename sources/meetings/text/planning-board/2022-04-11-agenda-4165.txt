PUBLIC HEARING

p.m., Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  to make alterations to Code of 
the Town of Lunenburg, MA, Chapter 250, Zoning, Article IV Use Regulations, 
Section 250 -4.15, Non-medical marijuana establishments, Subsection K (1); to 
Section 250-4.9, Water Supply Pr otection District; to Article III Establishment of 
Districts, Section 250-3.2, Location of Districts; and to update the Zoning Map to 
reflect the new boundaries of the Aquifer Protection Zones .  Materials  are 
available for viewing at the Planning Office, 960 Massach usetts Avenue,
