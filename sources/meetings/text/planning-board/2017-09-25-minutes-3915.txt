DIRECTOR'S ITEMS:
2030 Massachusetts Avenue, Ch. 61B- At the September 19t Open Space Ad Hoc Committee, the Committee voted to waive
right of first refusal, but encourage the Planning Board that any future development should consider continuing a possible
Snowriders' trail on the property. K. Chenis noted he felt the Board did not have that authority and A. Burney agreed. K. Chenis
went on to state that if the plan is to expand trails in Town, this would be a good location for a parking lot for individuals to access
the trails. Motion made, D. Prokowiew, that Board recommend to the Board of Selectmen that the property be retained
for purposes of economic development through eco-tourism, trails, stand-alone recreation, etc., Second, M. Jeffreys, all
aye,
