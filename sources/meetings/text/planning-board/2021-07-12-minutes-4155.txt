Minutes 
Public Hearing 
360 Flat Hill Road 
July 12, 2021 
 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  6:35 p.m. 
 
Present:  Tanner Cole, Matthew Allison, Paula Bertram, Matthew Brenner, Erik Torgersen, Adam R. Burney 
Also Present:  Tree Warden Rob Oliva 
 
The Hearing was to hear and review a request under M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road Designations and under M.G.L. 
Ch. 87, Section 3, Cutting of Public Shade Trees for the cutting of approximately three (3) trees and construction of four (4 ) 
driveways onto five (5) lots, with Lots 2 and 3 sharing a common drive  at 360 Flat Hill Road (Map 087, Parcel 0025), Lunenburg, 
MA.  Owner/applicant was Matson Homes II Inc., 129 Mason Road, New Ipswich, NH  03071.    
 
Ken Matson, Matson Homes II Inc. noted two (2) trees, and possibly a third, would have to be cut o n the common driveway for 
Lots 2 and 3; two (2) replacement trees would be planted.  As Lots 2 and 3 were reduced frontage, T. Cole inquired if  each lot 
required its own access inst ead of a common drive.  A .Burney clarified that a common drive was allowable.  Wherever portions 
of the stone wall had to be removed to create the driveways, K. Matson would incorporate those stones back into the wall.  Tree 
Warden Rob Oliva inquired how the trees were identified on the Plan as he felt the photos showed more trees than those 
identified on the P lan.  K. Matson identi fied trees 10” or larger ; the Scenic Road Bylaw required trees 6” and up.  The Hearing 
would have to be continued as t rees would have to be re -identified.  P. Bertram requested better photographs of the stone wall.  
K. Matson would tag the trees identified for removal and the Board members would do a site visit  on July 22nd, 6 p.m .  P. 
Bertram made Motion to continue the Hearing to July 26 th, 6:30 p.m., M. Allison Seconded, all aye.   
 
Hearing recessed 7:02 p.m.
