Minutes 
November 20, 2018 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  7:14 PM 
 
Present:  Matthew Allison, Kenneth Chenis, David Prokowiew, Tanner Cole, Adam R. Burney 
Also Present:  Select Board members Jamie Toale, Bob Ebersole, Damon McQuaid, Katey Adams 
 
Joint meeting with the Board of Selectmen to interview prospective candidate and vote on appointment to the 
Planning Board.   
 
Candidate Paula Bertram stated that while she was serving on the Select Board, she became  interested in seeing 
how the planning process work ed through the Planning Board.  She feels it is an extremely role in the Town.  She 
noted her prior work background with the Board of Health, working with plans and how various Boards/Commissions 
work together.  She feels she would be an asset to the Board and wishes to continue to serve the Town.   
 
B. Ebersole pointed out that this appointment will be until May 2019 elections.   
 
K. Adams noted the lengthy process she went through when she applied for a prior Planning Board vacancy , to 
include separate interviews with both Boards, and felt there was a lack of consistency in the process.   
 
B. Ebersole made Motion t o appoint Paula Bertram  to serve until the next election , D. McQuaid Seconded, 
Vote 8-0 in favor by a show of hands. 
 
T. Cole made Motion to adjourn Planning Board meeting, D. Prokowiew Seconded, all aye.   
 
Adjourned 7:20 PM
