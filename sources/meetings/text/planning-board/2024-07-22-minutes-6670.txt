BOARD DISCUSSIONS: 
Right of First Refusal – 20 Kilburn St 
L. O’Connell explained that the Select Board’s choice to Right of First Refusal (ROFR) was pending the 
Planning Board’s input on potential Town uses for Lots 1 and 3 of 20 Kilburn St. T. Willsmer invited Tom 
Gibbons (attorney for Rick Hillman, Trustee of RIMRIC Realty Trust) to speak. T. Gibbons filed the Notice of 
Intent to Sell under MGL Ch. 61B on May 15th, and Conservation Commission advised the Select Board to 
waive the ROFR.  
 
A. Reid noted the parcel’s proximity to Cherry Hill Farm and Massapoag Pond, asking if Conservation had 
reviewed this. T. Gibbons confirmed that Conservation had voted to recommend waive the ROFR, and 
explained that approximately 81 acres would still remain in Ch. 61B without Lots 1 and 3. 
 
After brief discussion noting that although the Open Space Committee hadn’t had a formal discussion yet, it 
may have some interest in the land, A. Reid moved to recommend to the Select Board that the Planning 
Board did not find sufficient reason from a planning perspective to exercise the Town’s right to acquire 
this property, but it is aware that the Open Space Committee may have some interest. 
 
G. McLeod seconded. 
Voice vote, passed unanimously. 
 
ARPA Funding Requests 
T. Willsmer confirmed that the board doesn’t currently have any active projects which ARPA funding could be 
put towards, but sought board input for potential projects.  
 
G. McLeod suggested upgrades to the sound system for the Bilotta Meeting Room. 
A. Reid suggested another Zoom link for the Planning Board, which R. Harris expected to be difficult due to 
Zoom’s annual licensure conflicting with ARPA’s contracting deadlines.  
 
The Board agreed to compile these ideas in an email to the Town Manager’s office.
