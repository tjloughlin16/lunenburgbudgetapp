TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
PUBLIC HEARING 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a public hearing on April 10, 2023, 6:05 p.m., Town Hall, 
17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to hear and review a request for the cutting of approximately 6 
trees and construction of one shared driveway  at 759 and 763 Northfield Road  (Parcels 038 -
0008-0002 and 038-0008-0001) under M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road Designations and 
under M.G.L. Ch. 87, Section 3, Cutting of Public Shade Trees.  Owner/applicant is Jessica 
Santos, 37 Fairmount Street, Leominster, MA 01453.   The application and plans are available for 
viewing in the Planning Off ice.  This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable 
and on FacebookLive on the Public Access Facebook page 
(https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-157427270975305/) and will be 
able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel within 24 hours after the meeting.  
To Participate Remotely: From a computer, please use the link below to join the public  meeting 
webinar: https://us02web.zoom.us/j/88068452023 or from a telephone, dial the toll free number 
(888) 475-4499 and enter Webinar ID: 88068452023
