Minutes
Executive Session
April 12, 2010
Time: 9:00 PM
Chair called for Executive Session to discuss litigation of O'Brien vs Planning Board. Roll call vote, Mr. Bodkin Jr., aye; Ms.
Bilotta, aye; Mr. Lockwood, aye; Mr. Bakaysa aye, Mr. Saiia, aye.
Discussion ensued pertaining to a conceptual plan presented to the Planning Board on March 22, 2010. Chair opened
discussion noting the plan was not as expected. It was a plan cut into approximately 25-acre subdivisions, but one subdivision
joined another through an interior road system. Discussion ensued mainly that the submitted conceptual plan was similar to the
plan disapproved by the Board. A portion of the discussion revolved around mediation measures, suggesting that the developer
submit a plan with fewer lots.
The Planning Board came to a consensus and noted the following:
The Board voted (Motion, Ms. Bilotta, Second, Mir. Lockwood, Roll Call Vote Mr. Bodkin Jr., aye, Ms. Bilotta, aye, Chair Bakaysa,
aye, Mr. Saila, aye, Mir. Lockwood, aye), to respond to the developer, Mr. Kevin O Brien, through Town Counsel Kopelman anc
Paige, that it has no interest in continuing to pursue the conceptual plan that was brought forth at the March 22, 2010 meeting.
The Board would look forward to considering a plan that is compliant with Section 5.6. of the Lunenburg Zoning Bylaw.
Motion to adjourn Executive Session, Ms. Bilotta, Second, Mr. Saiia, Roll Call Vote, Mr. Bodkin Jr., aye, Ms. Bilotta, aye, Chair
Bakaysa, aye, Mr. Salia, aye, Mr. Lockwood, aye. Adjourned 10:00 PM.
