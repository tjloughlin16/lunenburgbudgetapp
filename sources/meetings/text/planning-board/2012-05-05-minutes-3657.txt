Present:  Emerick R. Bakaysa, Joanna L. Bilotta -Simeone, Thomas W. Bodkin Jr., Rober t J. Saiia, Nathan J. Lockwood, Marion

Chair opened the meeting and noted to citizens in attendance copies of the Bylaw as it will be presented on the Town Meeting 
floor.  David P rokowiew, speaking for the citizens in attendance , stated they we re pleased with the addition and the final 
document.   
 
Process was discussed.  The citizens plan on giving full support to the Article as written by the Planning Board and pass over the 
proposed Article as submitted by the Petitioners.  Discussion ensued as to procedure to fulfill the above goals. 
 
Both the Planning Board and the citizens spoke about the work that went into the final document and expressed appreciation to 
each other for arriving at consensus with a workable bylaw that suits the purpose of protection for neighborhoods and the whole 
Town. 
 
Adjournment:  Motion, Ms. Bilotta-Simeone, Second, Mr. Saiia, adjourned 8:25 AM.
