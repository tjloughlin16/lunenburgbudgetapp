The Public Hearing was to hear and discuss an application for Site Plan Approval under Section 8.4. of the P rotective Bylaw of 
the Town of Lunenburg for the creation of a sixteen-space parking lot at 119 Massachusetts Avenue (Map 076, Parcel 0047).   
 
The Applicant John Walker DMD/Walker Orthodontics was represented by Greg ory Roy, Ducharme & Dillis Civil Design Group, 
Inc. (DDCDG).  There is an existing parking lot at the rear of the site  (compliant with zoning) and the Applicant proposes to add 
an additional parking lot with 16 spaces at the front of the site  due to internal traffic congesti on.  The front of the site is 
Commercial zoning and the rear is Residence A.  The existing septic system has been decommissioned as a connection to 
public sewer was made in the fall of 2016.  The existing Massachusetts Avenue curb cut will be utilized.   The parking area will 
have a handicap space.  There is good sand and grav el for an underground infiltration system  for the parking lot drainage.  
Landscaping details comply with the Protective Bylaw.  A revised site plan was submitted on February 8 th in response to Graves 
Engineering, Inc. (GEI) review letter of February 3 rd.  Upon reviewing the February 8 th submittal, GEI submitted a February 15 th 
review letter.  Outstanding matters noted by GEI are : 1)  DDCDG needs to revise  the site’s screening alo ng the southwest 
property line to comply with Section 6.4.3.d. )3. of the P rotective Bylaw.  DDCDG is requesting arborvitae instead of  a board 
fence.  There was Board discussion over screening the entire southwest property line or just th at portion of th e property line 
where the parking lot will be located.  Board consensus was the Applicant screen the portion of the property line where the 
parking lot will be with non -deciduous plantings and, 2)  the plans are to be prepared using both hori zontal and vertic al datum.  
G. Roy noted the plans were initially set up to correspond with the old septic plans so the Applicant is requesting the Board waive 
the requirement.   
 
The following waivers from Board Rules and Regulations for Site Plan Approval & Special Permits were requested. 
Section 4.1.f.2) – plan at a scale of 1”=40’ 
Section 4.1.f.2) – plan at North American Datum 1983 and North American Vertical Datum 1988  
Section 4.1.h – Traffic, Transportation and Circulation Impact Report 
Section 4.1.h – Public Services and Fiscal Impacts Report 
Section 4.1.h – Environmental Impact Report
