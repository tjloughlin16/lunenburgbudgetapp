DECISION: Review & Endorsement - 39 Main Street - The Board reviewed the draft Decision.  The Boa rd determined 
construction hours of 7 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday through Friday and 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Saturdays.  No construction on Sundays or 
state or federal holidays.  In addition to the Standard Conditions, two Special Conditions (#18 and #19) were added.  Number 18 
stated that “the two outstanding items remaining on the 8 May 2023 review letter from Graves Engineering (the bollard detail and 
the outlet control structure detail) will be issued a clean review before the commencement of construction of each i tem”.  Number 
19 stated that “no construction vehicle will be parked in a manner to obstruct the passage of pedestrians or other travelers o n the 
public sidewalks adjacent to the project site ”.  M. Brenner made Motion to approve the site plan for 39 Main S treet with all 
Standard Conditions, the two additional Conditions, and construction hours of 7 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday through Friday 
and 8 a.m. to 3 p.m. Saturdays.  No construction on Sunda ys or state or federal holidays, A. Reid Seconded, passed 
unanimously.  Decision endorsed by Board.

DIRECTOR’S ITEMS:   
Site Plan Approval- One of the concerns that came out of the Economic Development Committee (EDC) meeting (see above), 
was that a change of use required site plan approval before the Planning Board.  An EDC member noted that spaces under 1500 
square feet usually have similar parking requirements and a change of use could  be an administrative process through the 
Building Commissioner.  This would allow small businesses to move into spac es without a 4 -6 week delay normally involved in  
the site plan approval process.   To accomplish this would require a change to the zoning bylaw.  A. Burney recommended the 
Board wait for the new Building Commissioner to come on board later this month befor e proceeding.  A process needed to be 
developed which was approachable for a lay person, yet also enforceable for the Building Commissioner.
