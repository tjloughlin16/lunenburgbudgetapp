Meeting Posted: Yes

Member K. Chenis participated remotely in accordance with the Remote Participation Policy adopted by the Board of Selectmen

Hearing continuation, 270 Electric Avenue- see separate minutes. 
 
All concerns had been previously met.  Motion, M. Allison to approve the site plan review conditional upon it being based upon 
the submitted plans, Second, K. Chenis.  Discussion ensued as to conditions of the approval .  There was discussion as to h ours 
of construction  being Monday through Saturday, 7 AM to 7 PM, no Sundays and holidays;  the applicant adhering to any/all 
approvals from other Town Boards and committees ; and the West Street entrance being reserved for emergency vehicles, 
scheduled large deliveries and management needs.  M. Allison amended his Motion to include hours of construction to Monday 
through Saturday, and large deliveries to the West Street entrance.  Second, K. Chenis.  Roll Call Vote - K. Chenis, aye; M. 
Allison, aye; D. McQuai d, aye.  (Note:  Gregory Bittner and David Prokowiew were not on the Board at the date of the initial 
Hearing.) 
 
Approval Conditions will note hours of construction and the use restrictions for the West Street entrance.
