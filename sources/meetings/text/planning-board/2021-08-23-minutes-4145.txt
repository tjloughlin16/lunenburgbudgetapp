Minutes 
Public Hearing 
97 Cross Street, 125 Cross Street, 430 Mulpus Road, and 440 Mulpus Road  
August 23, 2021 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  7:11 p.m. 
Present:  Tanner Cole, Matthew Allison, Paula Bertram, Matthew Brenner, Adam R. Burney 
Absent:  Erik Torgersen 
Due to Illness, P. Bertram participate remotely under Section 2 .02 Remote Participation (Adopted on 05/22/2013; Amended 
8/12/2020) 
 
The Hearing was to hear and review an application for a stormwater management permit under Chapter 204 of the Code of the 
Town of Lunenburg  for 97 Cross Street, 125 Cross Street, 430 Mulp us Road and 440 Mulpus Road.  (Map 014, Parcels 0005, 
0003, 0002, 0004 respectively), owner/applicant Harbor Classic Homes, LLC, 493 Lancaster Street, Leominster, MA  01453.   
 
The owner/applicant Ron Koivu, Harbor Classic Homes  made the presentation as his engineer was not available .  The plan was 
in the process of being updated per the August 20, 2021 review letter of Jeffrey Walsh, Graves Engineering, Inc.   
 
The Stormwater Management Plan noted numerous maintenance /inspection items.  P. Bertram questione d how completion of 
those items would be monitored by individual homeowners.  R. Koivu was unfamiliar with the stormwater permitting process and 
questioned if that was something that would have to be in the individual deeds.  P. Bertram was concerned that homeowners 
would not understand the stormwater requirements on the lots.  M. Allison noted Lots 2 and 4 shared a recharge area and 
questioned how that would be managed with individual lot owners.  He also noted that the driveways would be crushed stone  
(pervious), which would impact the drainage calculations.  That would need to be a “condition” of the Board’s Decision.  He also 
noted there would need to be easements from one lot to another as the infrastructure crossed lot lines.  A. Burney suggested the 
possibility of a “Maintenance (Homeowners) Association” to ensure  follow-through on maintenance/inspection items on the lots.   
T. Cole summarized the issues that needed to be addressed:  drainage easement and ongoing maintenance detail ed in the 
Stormwater Management Plan.  T. Cole advised the applicant to see if there was a way to separate the stormwater  management 
for Lots 2 and 4 to make each lot  owner individually responsible.  The Board noted there were numerous issues that needed to 
be addressed by the project engineer.   
 
M. Allison made Motion to continue the Hearing to September 13, 2021, 6:35 p.m., M. Brenner Seconded, Roll Call Vote -  
M. Brenner, aye; M. Allison, aye; P. Bertram, aye; T. Cole, aye.  Vote passed 4 -0.  Hearing recessed 7:42 p.m.
