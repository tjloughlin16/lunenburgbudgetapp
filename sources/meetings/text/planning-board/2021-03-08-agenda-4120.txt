Public Hearing 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on March 8, 2021, 6:35 PM, via 
Zoom video conference to discuss revisions to the Board Rules and Regulations for Site 
Plan Approval  and Special Permits .  Materials are available for viewing at  the Planning 
Office, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA . In accordance with the Governor’s  
Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open Meeting Law, G.L.c.30A, Section 20, all 
public meetings are being conducted remotely.  This meeting will be broadcast through 
local access cable, on FacebookLive on the Public Access Facebook page 
(https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-157427270975305/) and will 
be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel within 24 hours after the 
meeting.  To participate remotely:  from a computer please use the link below to join the
