PUBLIC HEARING 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on October 2 4, 2022, 6:05 p.m., Town Hall, 17 
Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to  make alterations to Code of the Town of Lunenburg, MA, Chapter 250, 
Zoning, Article IV Use Regulations, Section 250 -4.1G Use Table, by changing 4.1K(10) Motel in the 
Commercial District from “Y” to “SP -Z”.  Materials are avail able for viewing at the Planning Office, 960 
Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.  This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, on  
FacebookLive on the Public Access Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-
Cable-157427270975305/) and will be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel within 
24 hours after the meeting.   To participate remotely: from a computer, pleas e use the link below to join the 
public hearing webinar:  https://us02web.zoom.us/j/83904066060 or from a telephone, dial the toll free 
number (888) 475-4499 and enter meeting ID: 83904066060.
