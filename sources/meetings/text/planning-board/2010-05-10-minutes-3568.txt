Minutes
Executive Session
May 10, 2010
Time: 7:30 PM
Chair called for Executive Session for purpose of clarification of joint executive session with Board of Selectmen and not to return
to general session. Roll call vote, Mr. Bodkin Jr., aye; Ms. Bilotta, aye; Mr. Lockwood, aye; Mr. Bakaysa aye, Mr. Salia, aye.
Discussion: Concern over the minutes prepared by the Planning Director for the Executive Session minutes of April 13, 2010 with
the Board of Selectmen concerning the law suit brought forth by O'Brien Homes. Member Bodking felt that the two minutes of the
two Boards should reflect the same verbiage.
Director will request copy of the Board of Selectmen's minutes. Director proposed that if the Board was satisfied with those
minutes, a signed acknowledgement of same should be sufficient because the meeting was called by the Board of Selectman.
The Board engaged in discussion concerning the lawsuit. Mr. Bodkins spoke on avenues of settlement. Board engaged in open
discussion. No conclusions were presented.
Chair called for adjournment of the Executive Session. Motion to adjourn was made by Ms. Bilotta and seconded by Mr.
Lockwood. Roll call vote: Mir. Bodkin, Jr., aye; Ms. Bilotta, aye; Mr. Lockwood, aye; Mr. Bakaysa, aye, Mr. Salia, aye.
