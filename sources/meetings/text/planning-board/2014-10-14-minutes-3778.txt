Housing Production Plan  (HPP)-  Sam Lawton, MRPC  had requested Board review draft of his 10.06.14 presentation for this 
evening’s meeting.   Comments presented by N. Lockwood (on file in Planning Office) to augmenting with more specifics.  M. 
Allison noted projected population growth is slower than past ten years.  Add Table of Contents .  D. McQuaid opined that the 
Town should not rush to attract affordable housing.  The Town is  currently on track to meet the threshold with developments 
scheduled to be built in Town.  J. Bilotta-Simeone questioned how to get elderly housing (TC Passios or old Primary School) in 
the center of Town.  A. Burney to research the possibility of a 40B applying to a mixed used development, e.g., commercial on 
second and third  floors and  residential on first floor.  A. Burney noted Plan should include discussion of reimbursement 
percentage under t he Community Preservation Act.  Not sacrificing commercial land.  Looking to encourage mixed use.  N. 
Lockwood, Motion to move forward, Second, M. Allison, all aye.
