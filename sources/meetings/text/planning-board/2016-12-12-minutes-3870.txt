COMMITTEE REPORTS: 
Stormwater Task Force -  Task Force met with Ed Himlan, Executive Director, Massachusetts Watershed Coalition and Mike 
Clark, Design Consultants, Inc. to go over the basis of a stormwater management plan for the Town to move forward with its 
notice of intent for MS4 permit compliance.    
Agricultural Commission-  Meeting 12/15/16.  
Building Reuse Committee -  An Article at 11/28/16 Special Town Meeting (STM) , which would have given the Board of 
Selectmen authority to enter into an RFP (Request for Proposal) process for the various buildings, was amended on STM floor to 
include that any proposals as a result of the RFP process will  go before a town meeting.  D. McQuaid opined that the Committee 
felt that will complic ate the RFP process and possibl y deter developers  due to the additional time involved in the statutory 
timeline of scheduling a town meeting.     
Capital Planning Committee-  Meetings continue; met with the DPW Director.   
School Building Committee-  Meeting 12/14/16. 
Green Community Task Force (GCTF)-  Meeting 12/20/16.   
MJTC-  Waiting on right -of-way resolution and 100% plan with Summer Street project.   As a result of Board discussion on 
3/24/16, K. Chenis brought up the Complete Streets Program with MJT C.  A content-sensitive policy can be put forth  identifying 
specific areas of Town (possibly the V illage Center District and Summer Street Revitalization Overlay  District) for a Complete 
Streets Policy.  MJTC is gauging communities’ interest in offering a bike rack program for town -owned buildings.  Communities 
would initially pay for the racks, but would be reimbursed.       
MRPC-   Meeting 12/13/16. 
Open Space Ad Hoc Committee-    No meeting scheduled.
