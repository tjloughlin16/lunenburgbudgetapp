Public Hearing 
711 Massachusetts Avenue 
August 23, 2021 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  6:53 p.m. 
Present:  Tanner Cole, Matthew Allison, Paula Bertram, Matthew Brenner, Adam R. Burney 
Absent:  Erik Torgersen 
Due to Illness, P. Bertram participate remotely under Section 2.02 Remote Participation (Adopted on 05/22/2013 ; Amended 
8/12/2020) 
 
The Hearing continuation was to hear and review applications for site plan approval and stormwater management permit under 
Ch. 250, Section 8.4. and Chapter 204 respectively of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg  for construction of four 2 -unit 
townhouse buildings totaling eight (8) units at 711 Massachusetts Avenue Lt 4  (Map 0 59, Parcel 00 30), Lunenburg, MA .  
Owner/applicant was EF Realty, LLC, 654 Elm Street, Leominster, MA.    
 
The owner/applicant was represented by Wesley Flis, Whitm an & Bingham Associates, hereinafter “W&B” and Eric Fowler, EF 
Realty, LLC.   
 
The site plan had been revised to 7/26/2021.  The plan addressed the sight line issue and review comments of Jeffrey Walsh, 
Graves Engineering, Inc., hereinafter “GEI”.  GEI rev iew letter dated August 20th confirmed sight line issue had been addressed 
along with any other outstanding issues.  The applicant entered into a Memorandum of Agreement with the property owner at 
733 Massachusetts Avenue for a grading easement to allow th e applicant to meet the sight line issue.  W&B believed the only 
remaining issue, per GEI review, was to identi fy existing significant trees per Board Rules and Regulations for Site Plan Approval 
& Special Permits, Section 4.1 f. 16).  As the site was main ly wooded, W&B did not identify trees.  A waiver would be requested.  
P. Bertram noted the DPW Director’s review comments suggesting an alternate location for the 8” sewer  be considered  to 
eliminate the need to build a sewer manhole over the existing sewer  main.  W&B stated his plan was to have a discussion with 
the DPW Director.  T. Cole observed a portion of the grading easement was located within the right-of-way.  W&B would have to 
submit to MA DOT for a curb cut permit.  As the project was located in t he Water Supply Protection District, t he building 
footprints were reduced to keep the amount of disturbance under 30%.  P. Bertram noted that the plans stated that prior to 
construction soil testing would be performed to ensure the groundwater offset was achieved.  This would be “conditioned” as part 
of the Board’s Decision.   
 
P. Bertram made Motion to close the hearing for 711 Massachusetts Avenue, M. Allison Seconded, Roll Call Vote - M. 
Brenner, aye; M. Allison, aye; P. Bertram, aye; T. Cole, aye.  Hearing closed 7:10 p.m.
