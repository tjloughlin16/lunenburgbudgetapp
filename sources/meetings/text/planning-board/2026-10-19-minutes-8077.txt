 3. CHAIR / DIRECTOR’S REPORT 
Planning Director Stephen Wallace provided an update on outreach regarding proposed 
zoning amendments. He reported that presentations had been made to the Zoning Board 
and Conservation Commission and that additional meetings are scheduled. A public 
forum on the proposed zoning amendments is planned for December 1, 2026. Comments 
from boards and committees are being requested by the end of November to allow time 
for revisions before the 2027 Annual Town Meeting process. 
Mr. Wallace also reported that Town Counsel recommended issuing separate decisions 
for Stormwater Permits because stormwater permits are subject to a different appeal 
period than other Planning Board permits. Future decisions will reflect this distinction.

Lunenburg Food Pantry to 30 School Street. The applicant reported that the Zoning 
Board of Appeals had approved the requested variance for the project. Additional plan 
revisions were submitted showing the location of recycling bins and a donation drop-off

 Zoning Amendments for Special Town Meeting  
The Board opened a public hearing to consider several housekeeping amendments to the 
Zoning Bylaw and General Bylaws for the November 17, 2026 Special Town Meeting. 
Proposed amendments included: 
· Replacing references to "development plan review" with "site plan review." 
· Removing obsolete references to the former Retail Commercial District. 
· Replacing references to the Land Use Director with Planning Director. 
· Removing the bylaw provision allowing two dwellings on one lot. 
· Revising language regarding residential common driveways. · Authorizing the 
Planning Board to establish stormwater permit fees.  
Mr. Wallace explained the purpose of the proposed amendments and noted that they 
were intended primarily to correct outdated references, improve consistency, and 
address administrative issues identified during application reviews. Board members 
discussed the history of the Retail Commercial District and the reasoning for removing 
the remaining obsolete references.  
Motion: A. Burney moved to close public hearing 
Second: T. Willsmer 
All in favor 
Motion:  A Burney recommend all proposed zoning and general bylaw amendments 
favorably to the Special Town Meeting 
Second: T. Willsmer 
All in favor
