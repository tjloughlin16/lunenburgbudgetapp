PUBLIC COMMENT: Katey Adams, Williams Drive - had concerns regarding recreational marijuana. Noted that even though
Ballot Question 4 (Massachusetts Marijuana Legalization) (2016) passed by a majority vote in Lunenburg, popularity should not
be involved in the setting of the regulations in Town. She is concerned with the impact on people under the age of 18 and trying
to make sure children live a drug-free lifestyle.
