4. PUBLIC HEARINGS 
• 475 Leominster Shirley Road, Request for Modification to Site Plan  (PMOD-26-4) 
Chris Harrison (Hanigan Engineering) was present and stated that they are requesting a 
minor modification to the use of the building to include manufacturing.  In the office 
industrial zoning district, manufacturing is allowed by right, which is a change in use 
and modification to the site plan.  S. Wallace stated that this is a very minor change.  
The proposed manufacturing will be similar to Ecological Fibers and self contained 
within the building.  Potential noise concerns were addressed.   
A. Burney moved to close the public hearing.  A. Reid Second 
All In favor. 
A Burney moved to approve the Modification to Site plan with the condition that all 
previous conditions of the original permit and previous modifications covey to this 
approval.  A. Reid Second. 
All in favor.
