MASTER PLANNING WORKSHOP 
Procedure Planning:  Ms. Benson presented a plan to facilitate the updating of the Master Plan .  MRPC has been involved in 
assisting towns in updating master plans.  A town can work in different ways  with them.  In a preliminary conversation with 
MRPC it was noted t hey can assist by outline and input as to procedure.  As a third party  connection with the Planning Board , 
they can supply the latest data with the Planning Board being the  decision makers.  Processes such as surveys and other data 
gathering documents can be accomplished in house.  Ms. Benson recommended that the Board meet with representatives from 
MRPC to determine best practice options and processes to achieve goals.  Board consensus is for Ms. Benson to contact MRPC 
again and set up meeting. 
 
Center Planning:  Board discussed the first choice of study to be the Town Center.  This study can fit into several of the Master 
Plan Elements.  Topic discussed was based on outline provided by Mr. Lockwood.  Particularly noted  was center area 
determination, gas station area, possible vacant school, and parking spaces for business .  Ms. Benson to  check on HUD status 
for old Primary School and Riley site re-use.  Also discussed was Design Standards.   
 
Mr. Bakaysa noted continuation after meeting with MRPC at next Master Planning Workshop .  Tentative date of June 18, 2012. 
 
Motion to adjourn, Mr. Bakaysa, Second, Mr. Lockwood, adjournment 8:00 PM.
