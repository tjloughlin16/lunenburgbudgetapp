Town Of Lunenburg 
Public Hearing 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on July 27, 2020, 6:35 
PM, Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to hear and review a request 
under M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road Designations and under M.G.L. 
Ch. 87, Section 3, Cutting of Public Shade Trees.  Application is to remove six 
small trees, one large tree leaning towards Northfield  Road, and create two new 
driveway entrances off Northfield Road which will be located across the street 
from 499 Northfield Road.  Current site address is 300 Holman Street West (Map 
021, Parcel 0011), owner Daron C. Massey, Lunenburg, MA.  Applicant is Bray 
Ave 9 LLC, 493 L ancaster Street, Leominster, MA.  Materials are available 
digitally on request by emailing mboggio@lunenburgonline.com.  In accordance 
with the Governor’s Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open Me eting
