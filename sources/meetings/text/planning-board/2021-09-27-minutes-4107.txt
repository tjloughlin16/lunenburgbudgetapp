PUBLIC HEARINGS: 
1263 Reservoir Road & 435 Leominster-Shirley Road (aka 535 Leominster-Shirley +/-)  
 ANR Plan -  Bill Hannigan, Hannigan Engineering noted that at the previous Board meeting, the Board expressed 
concerns with easements shown on the site plan, but not reflected on the ANR Plan.  The question had also been 
raised regarding the amount of fronta ge that could be obstructed by easements.  The ANR Plan was revised with a 
reduced easement.  An ingress and egress for the project would be located on either side of the proposed easement.  
The Building Commissioner issued a letter stating that “…Upon reviewing the submitted site plan, it is my opinion that 
there is approximately 155 feet of non -encumbered frontage which is over the required 150 feet of non -encumbered 
frontage….the frontage would not be an issue and would not prevent the issuance of a Buil ding Permit.”  M. Allison 
made Motion to accept the ANR Plan, P. Bertram Seconded, all aye.  Vote passed 4-0.   
 Hearing- See separate minutes.

360 Flat Hill Road, Stormwater Management Permit- See separate minutes.   
 
97 Cross Street, 125 Cross Street, 430 Mulpus Road & 440 Mulpus Road -  The applicant had decided to let go of  the 
engineering firm they had been using and retain the services of a different firm.  They were requesting leave to withdra w without 
prejudice to allow a new engineering firm adequate time to review and redesign the project.  T. Cole did not see an issue with the 
hearing being continued for an extended period of time for the new engineering firm.  He had concerns with the appl icant trying 
to circumvent the stormwater management process by  breaking up the project/doing one lot at a time.   A. Burney had discussion 
with the applicant’s attorney noting that the hearing could be continued, only requiring re -advertising and re -noticing, but it was 
the attorney’s perspective it would be in his client’s best interest to withdraw without prejudice.  It would be more beneficial to 
return to the Board with a new application and a single engineering firm.

Master Plan Update-  A. Burney reached out to fellow planners for feedback on the typical cost for updating a full master plan  -
