Minutes 
Public Hearing 
97 Cross Street, 125 Cross Street, 430 Mulpus Road, and 440 Mulpus Road  
December 13, 2021 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  7:23 p.m. 
Present:  Tanner Cole, Matthew Allison, Paula Bertram, Adam R. Burney  
Absent:  Matthew Brenner, Erik Torgersen 
 
The Hearing was to hear and review an application for a stormwater management permit under Chapter 204 of the Code of the 
Town of Lunenburg  for 97 Cross Street, 125 Cross Street, 430 Mulpus Road and 440 Mulpus Road.  (Map 014, Parcels 0005, 
0003, 0002, 0004 respectively), owner/applicant Harbor Classic Homes, LLC, 493 Lancaster Street, Leominster, MA  01453.  
 
Representing the owner/applicant Ron Koivu, Harbor Classic Homes was  Brian Marchetti, McCarty Engineering, Inc.   The 
property consisted of ~14 acres previously subdivided into five (5) lots through the ANR Pla n process.  Lot 1 contained the 
existing house; the remaining four lots were being presented for a stormwater permit.  Septic systems have been approved.  
Each lot had its own frontage and driveway.  All driveways were graded for sheet flow to one side of the driveway.  There were 
pea stone diaphragms along the length of the driveways  that would provide pre-treatment of the stormwater from the driveways.  
There were also vegetated swales and rain gardens.   
 
The Board was in receipt of Graves Engineering, Inc. review letter dated December 13, 2021.  B. Marchetti ran through the items 
listed in the letter.   
 
P. Bertram noted that the O perations & Maintenance P lan (O&M) addressed the removal of dead plantings, but did not address 
re-plantings.  The O&M would be amended.   
 
P. Bertram made Motion to continue the hearing to January 10, 2022, 6:35 p.m., M. Allison Seconded, passed 
unanimously. 
 
Hearing recessed 7:45 p.m.
