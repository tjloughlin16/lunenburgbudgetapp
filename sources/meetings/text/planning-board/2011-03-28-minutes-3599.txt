CLURPA- Director submitted a fact sheet to the Board on the Comprehensive Land Use Reform and Partnership Act (CLURPA). 
The proposed act is the first major updating of the Commonwealth’s planning/zoning and Subdivision Control Statutes in many 
years.  The proposed act will have  a public hearing shortly.  The format outlined will affect the Town’s Master Plan and Zoning 
Bylaw.  The Direc tor recommended to the Board that they review the outline of the Act as it relates to the Zoning Act c40A, 
Master Plans, c41 & 81D, Subdivision Control Law c41 and Land Use Partnership Act c40U.
