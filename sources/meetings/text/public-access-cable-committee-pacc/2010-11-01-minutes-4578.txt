PACC Meeting Minutes
Nov.!
Oct. 14th, 2010
Lunenburg Town Hall
Members Present: Rhonda Lisio, Fred Crellin, Martha Cheesman
Also Present: Ernie Sund, John Dextraze
The meeting was called to order at 7:05.
Minutes from the Oct. 14" meeting were read and accepted.
The Coordinator Candidates Discussion_revolved around the splitting of the position and the resulting job
descriptions. It was unanimously decided to recommend Jo McLaughlin for the position of Public Access
TV Coordinator and John Gugarty for the position of Public Access TV Production Coordinator.
Job Descriptions for the new positions were discussed and agreed to.
There was no news concerning Studio and Editing space.
The Bill Paying Authorization form was signed by all members present.
New Business It was agreed that there would be a meet and greet session with our new coordinators.
The meeting was adjourned at 8:35.
