PACC Emergency Finance Meeting Minutes 
Oct 24, 2016 
Roll call: 
1.  Meeting called to order at 6:15pm. 
2.  PACC members present were Jeff Bajko, Steve Walker, and Faith Beall. 
3.  Non-members present were Jo McLaughlin and Terri Burchfield. 
 
Finance Report 
By motion at the PACC meeting last night, the following was approved for inclusion the Warrant for the upcoming 
Special Town Meeting: 
 
PACC asks that the Town will appropriate an additional $110,142.36 from the Public Education Government Access 
and Cable Related Fund Revenues, beyond the $74,030.76 appropriated at the May 2016 Town Meeting, to 
accommodate potential salary increases ($10,142.36), Capital Expenditures ($50,000), reserves for catastrophic 
failure ($25,000) and expenses to initiate studio improvements in anticipation of the new studio space becoming 
available ($25,000).This will bring the total appropriation for FY17 to $184.173.12..Of this total, operational 
expense are $84,173.12 and capital expenses are $100,000,00. 
 
Note that all of this comes from the PEG reserve fund, and has no impact on the Town's budget. 
 
Steve Walker 
Chair, PACC 
 
Respectfully submitted, Jeffrey Bajko, PACC Clerk 11/11/16
