PACC Meeting Minutes
October 7°, 2010
L.H.S. Rm. 228
Members Present: Martha Cheesman, Tania Fernandez, Rhonda Lisio, Fred Crellin
Also in attendance: Mike Wallmann
The meeting was called to order at 7:07.
Public Comments: none
Updates:
Mike Wallmann, a student at L.H.S. interested in joining PACC, was introduced.
Martha reported that she had met with Kerry Speidel and found that the Town Hall
space for a potential studio was apparently spoken for. There had also been discussion of possible use
of the Brooks House, with the conference room used as a studio (when available) with a locked room
upstairs for equipment storage, editing, etc.
Fred asked if there was any update on Drawing with Mark. There wasn't.
Minutes:
Because of a problem with the printer, we were unable to distribute copies of the
minutes from September 13". They will be distributed and voted on at the next meeting.
Candidates:
The resumes of the candidates for coordinator were discussed and four candidates were
selected to be interviewed. They are Jo McLaughlin, John Gugarty, Timothy Daoust, and Jason Kearns.
An attempt will be made to contact them and schedule interviews on Oct. 14th.
New Business: There was discussion about the creation of a subcommittee to study Article XlI and
propose any changes.
The meeting was adjourned at 8:26.
Next Meeting : October 14th
