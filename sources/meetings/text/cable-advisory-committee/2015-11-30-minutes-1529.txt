CABLE ADVISORY COMMITTEE MINUTES
November 30, 2015
The Cable Advisory Committee met in the Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room as scheduled with Tom Alonzo, Paula Bertram
and Steve Walker present.
PUBLIC COMMENT
None
ANNOUNCEMENTS
Chairman noted with regret the resignation of Rhonda Lisio.
MINUTES/CORRESPONDENCES
1. Preview Questionnaire Responses from Town/School - Committee reviewed municipal responses from Adam
Burney, Planning Director; Jack Rodriquenz, DPW Director, Susan Doherty, Council on Aging Director; Patrick
Sullivan, Fire Chief; Mike Sauvageau, Building Official; Victoria Whipple, Middle School Library Media Specialist and
Jennifer Biery, LHS Field Hockey Coach. The Committee noted the following recommendations/needs based on
responses received:
a. A wider range of meeting rooms wired for sound and video.
b. Equipment to allow for participation by committee members remotely.
c. The ability to broadcast public informational sessions by various departments.
d. Mobile equipment/capability.
e. Portable wireless microphones.
2. Review Town wide Survey Draft - Committee reviewed draft survey created on Survey Monkey. Steve Walker
suggested modifications to the age brackets in question 1. Questions 7-10 were modified to include a reference to
programming on the public access channel.
The Committee voted unanimously to release the survey with minor modifications. A link to the survey will be
available online and printed surveys will be available at the Town Hall, Library and Senior Center. Tom Alonzo will
check with Kerry about including either a reference to the survey or the actual survey in the tax bills. Citizens will
also be notified of the survey via the Lunenburg Ledger and the Scroll on the public access channel.
Survey responses will be collected for 30 days following distribution.
3. Review PACC budget Updates - Steve Walker informed the Committee that PACC is working on the budget and
business plan and hope to have a draft in February.

To All-
As our current contract with Comcast draws to a close next year, the
Cable Advisory Committee, has been convened to enter into negotiations
with the cable provider for the next 10-year contract. In preparation
for these negotiations, we will be reaching out to all residents (both
subscribers and non-subscribers of cable services) to share their
experiences, assessments and perspectives of the past 10 years of cable
service in the Town of Lunenburg and to encourage them to share their
vision for improvements, modifications and/or additions of that service
going forward.
With this email we are also seeking much-needed input from all Town and
School Departments as well as all elected and appointed bodies
regarding their current and projected use of the available broadcast
services and channels. Additionally, we are requesting that you please
provide us with your experiences in dealing with the Public Access
Cable Committee (PACC) in offering, producing and airing programs
important to your department. To this end, we ask everyone receiving
this email to read and consider the following questions (below), and,
subsequently, have discussions within your department and with your
department head about them. /*Most importantly, we ask that you email
your answers AS A DEPARTMENT OR PUBLIC BODY to the Cable Advisory
Committee for review at our next meeting scheduled for Monday, November
30th*/. We encourage you to be as candid and detailed as possible in
your responses as the answers to these questions (and the resident
surveys, too) will provide vital information that will drive our talks
with Comcast.
Should you have any questions about what we are asking you to provide,
please contact me directly at 978-582-7956.
Again, your input is critical to PACC's business plan and, ultimately,
our negotiations with Comcast. Thank you, in advance, for taking the
time to assist us in our mission to further develop Lunenburg's
broadcast services and programming offerings.
Best Regards,
Tom Alonzo
Chair, Cable Advisory Committee
