New Business 
Bill Bernard reviewed the potential hiring of a Seasonal Cemetery Laborer that was a candidate.  He felt the 
candidate had more experience than they usually found for these roles and suggested that this candidate 
should be hire in at Grade 4 step 3.  He has two openings for this type role and it is a part time role up to 19 
hours weekly.  J. Belliveau also recommended this request be approved as the references came back very 
supportive and the candidate had previously worked in a similar role in Lowell. 
S. Andrews made a motion to accept the recommendation of the Assistant Town Manager/HR Director to 
make this current hire into Grade 4 Step 3. 
Second:  W. Bertrand 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor
