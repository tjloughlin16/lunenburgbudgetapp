New Business 
New Hire Form 
 
Julie Belliveau presented a new hire document outlining a request to make an offer to a candidate 
that will fill the role of Principal Assessor on an interim basis.  The candidate has extensive 
experience in this role in other municipalities. Due to her current status as municipal retiree, she 
can only work a limited number of hours and is not a candidate for the permanent full time 
position. Based on her experience, the recommendation is to place her in grade 15, step 8.   
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to accept recommendation from the HR Director to offer this role at the 
proposed grade and step. 
   
Second:  S. Andrews
