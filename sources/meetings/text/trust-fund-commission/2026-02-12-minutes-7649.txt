Table  of  Contents   
Purpose  &  Scope  This  manual  establishes  uniform  criteria  and  procedures  for  Trust  Fund  eligibility  determinations  
including
 
income,
 
residency,
 
FPL
 
application,
 
documentation,
 
and
 
appeals.
 
Definitions  %FPL :  income  divided  by  100%  FPL  monthly  
Federal  Poverty  Level :  HHS  guideline  
Gross  Monthly  Income :  income  before  taxes  
Household  Size :  all  persons  sharing  income

•  Determine  loan  terms

child
 
support).

annual
 
÷
 
12.
 
(See

B/C.)

other
 
benefits
 
are
 
available.
 
7.  Duplication  of  Benefits:  Ensure  Trust  funds  do  not  duplicate  aid  already  provided  for  the  same  need.
