Meeting Minutes 
AGRICULTURAL COMMISSION 
July 18, 2019 
Ritter Memorial Building 
 960 Massachusetts Avenue 
 
Present: M. Allison, R. Stober, J. Marzuca, M. Fontaine 
Chair called the meeting to order at 7:04pm.   
 
R. Stober moved to approve minutes of May 16 and June 20, J. Marzuca 2nd, all aye 
 
General discussion began with J. Marzuca talking about the ‘New entry program’ that his family 
is involved with.  Also discussed was creating a list of people with land willing to sharecrop, and 
another of people looking for more land to farm. 
 
Discussion about hosting a BBQ began with M. Allison sharing conversations with various 
vendors and what was available for local food.  There is much interest.  Suggestions included 
holding it at Dragonfly Café or having Bad Larries or Grange put it on.  R. Stober moved that we 
investigate how to host a BBQ after the farmers market, J. Marzuca 2nd, all aye.  M. Allison 
reported that Hollis Hills had lent 2 cornhole setups to the farmers market.  M. Fontaine reported 
there are a total of 27 full season and per diem vendors at the farmers market.  Everyone is 
happy and there have been no issues.  There was a brief discussion about commissioning hats 
and tee-shirts to promote the farmers market and Lunenburg Agricultural Commission.  
Members will look into potential sources. 
 
 
R. Stober moved to adjourn, J. Marzuca second, adjourned at 8:18pm.
