firm, which did all of the work in-house and did not rely on subcontractors, was a better fit for
Lunenburg. There was general agreement.

400; CEI at $32,400. Town Manager Lemieux felt that there may be additional costs associated with
working with CEI, as tasks may be added; and that the Woodard and Curran price could perhaps be

had initially bid on Year 2 work at the beginning of the calendar year, had a cost of $98,600 associated

Task 3 - IDDE Program
The draft SWMP Plan references an Illicit Discharge Detection and Elimination (IDDE) Plan that
has been prepared as an appendix, however was not provided as part of the RFP. As the IDDE Plan
is required to be completed in its entirety by June 30, 2019, CEL assumes that the following items
have been completed by others:
• Delineation of catchment areas based on topography and/or stormwater infrastructure for
known outfalls and interconnections;
• Outfall catchment inventory, ranking, and prioritization;
• Identification of system vulnerability factors (SVFs);
• Identification of key junction manholes;
• Procedures for dry and wet weather outfall screening and sampling; and
• Catchment investigation procedures for implementation during future years.
As such, it is assumed that this task is intended to focus on implementation of the IDDE Program
through the completion of field work. The MS4 Permit requires that all outfalls categorized as either
High or Low priority must be inspected during dry weather conditions by the end of Year 3, or by
June 30, 2021. Therefore, it is recommended that the Town begin to complete dry weather screening
during Year 2 in order to ensure that enough time remains to meet program deadlines. This task can
also include other field efforts, such as completing supplemental mapping while field staff are
already performing other investigations.
Based on information provided in the Notice of Intent and SWMP Plan, it is assumed that the Town
has approximately 250 outfalls. CEI recommends attempting to inspect approximately 30% of
outfalls during Year 2 as this will help the municipality obtain a better estimate on the level of effort
that will be required during Year 3 to complete remaining outfall inspections. This will also allow a
reasonable budgetary reserve to complete remaining items required during Year 2 while still getting
a head start on Year 3 requirements.
Under this Task, CEI has allocated up to four field days for a crew of two people to perform dry
weather screening with a goal of inspecting 75 outfalls within the allotted budget. It is assumed that
a knowledgeable member of the Town will accompany CEI field staff to aid in locating outfall
structures where needed. CEI will attempt to complete inspections in an area with a relatively high
density of outfalls to make inspections go quicker. CEI will complete inspection forms for all
located outfalls and collect water samples for parameters required under the standard permit, as well
as Appendices Fand Hif needed. Results will be tabulated and reviewed for any recommended
follow-up actions.
