Meeting Minutes 
December 2, 2024 5:30pm 
 
 
Members Present: Brian Lehtinen, Michael-Ray Jeffreys, Matthew Allison, Tom Gray, Greg 
Roy, Kristina Masaitis 
 
Members absent: David Blatt 
 
Town Employees: Chris Ruth 
 
Community Guests: Anthony Sculimbrine  
 
Meeting called to order at 5:30pm. 
 
 
The Chair noted that public participation is encouraged and public comments may be 
welcomed during committee discussions at the Chair’s discretion. 
 
The committee discussed the town’s long-standing efforts to address municipal building 
needs. Initial planning began in the 1980s, followed by a Building Reuse Committee in the 
early 2010s. Recommendations for Town Hall did not move forward, and prior project costs 
(e.g., the TC Passios project) were deemed unviable for addressing all municipal building 
needs.  The Select Board reorganized the committee to focus on a comprehensive solution, 
including renovations to Town Hall, the Ritter Building, and the Middle School. 
 
The committee has developed a conceptual plan to repurpose Town Hall, the Ritter Building, 
and construct a new Town Hall annex. Measurements will be taken at municipal offices for 
improved accuracy. Focus will be on record storage solutions, favoring digitization to 
minimize physical storage costs. Jason Varney and Mark Webster from Simpson Gumpertz 
Hager introduced themselves, highlighting their expertise in historic buildings. 
 
Discussion included the Ritter Building’s limited parking and the potential for parking on 
nearby parcels. Historical Society easements and land use at 30 School Street were also 
reviewed.  Departments requiring space in the new design include the Treasurer/Tax 
Collector, Building Department, Board of Health, Planning, Recreation, and Veterans Affairs. 
The historical commission will require an office and a transaction counter. 
The committee discussed whether PACC (Public Access Channel) should be included in the 
project. PACC has unique needs, such as first-floor access and proximity for vehicle

loading/unloading. Concerns were raised regarding PACC’s budget and the potential costs of 
building a new studio. 
The committee will meet on December 16, 2024, and subsequently on January 6, January 22, 
and February 3, 2025. 
Soil and utility assessments at the Ritter parcel will be reviewed. 
Reports, drawings, and other materials will be distributed before upcoming meetings. 
The Chair thanked committee members for their time and dedication. 
A reminder was issued regarding the Chair’s term ending in May, with a decision about 
future service pending. 
 
Motion to adjourn by Anthony Sculimbrine at 6:44pm, seconded by Michael-Ray Jeffreys, 
unanimously passed.
