Minutes: 
Motion to approve the minutes of the September 21, 2026 meeting made by Matthew

RFP scope, timing, and objectives 
• Chair summarized committee charge and upcoming Select Board warrant preparation 
for November town meeting; asked whether the committee should submit an article 
asking for funding at November town meeting. 
• Consensus: Too early to put an article on the November 2026 warrant. Reasons cited: 
• Insufficient definition of scope and dollar figure to present to Town Meeting. 
• Need to refine RFP and messaging. 
• Action: Chair to draft message to Select Board Chair stating committee’s 
recommendation: defer submitting funding request for November. 
• Next target: Spring town meeting (discussion to continue). 
RFP content and programmatic requirements  
• Primary goal for current RFP-like package: narrow required square footage for 
municipal offices (consolidation of town staff), estimate cost per square foot, and 
produce a product an architect can use to move into schematic design. 
• Key data to gather / include: 
• Town personnel counts: estimated ~30 employees working in Town Hall 
(figure cited in discussion). 
• Visitor assumption used in planning: up to 10 visitors at a time (committee 
member suggested 10 might be high but chosen to cover events). 
• Conference/meeting room needs: 

===PAGE 2===
• Smaller conference rooms for Town Hall staff group: several 4–6 
person rooms and one 6–8 person room. 
• Ritter group / larger meetings: one large conference room for 12–20 
people to accommodate developer/board presentations; preference for 
locating large room near an entrance so other town offices can be 
closed off while meetings run. 
• Accessibility: emphasize accessibility beyond mere ADA baseline (accessible 
routes, entry, restrooms). Committee agreed to highlight accessibility as a 
priority in the RFP. 
• Public restrooms / ability to open restrooms to public during after-hours events 
if cost-effective; considered valuable if minimal added expense. 
• Town green / events: discussion of using TC Passios area as primary town 
green and importance of following the Master Plan; committee recommended 
RFP include an option to consider flexible front/green-facing design if 
cost/feasibility allows but TC Passios topic ultimately to be handled 
separately. 
• Storage and basement use: 
• Interest in making Town Hall basement usable for long-term storage; 
prior estimate cited: approx. $100,000 to replace Town Hall slab / 
make basement usable (reference: Taylor & Burns study figure). 
• Concerns raised about archival storage below grade 
(environmental/health/allergy issues, moisture). Committee discussed 
fieldstone foundation waterproofing pros/cons; consensus: solution 
possible but requires engineering input; any waterproofing work 
should consider foundation breathability; architect/engineer will 
assess. 
• Mechanical / utility systems: expectation that architect will identify baseline 
full-service upgrades; mechanical rooms and service upgrades anticipated and 
will be evaluated during walkthrough. 
• Parking: RFP should define parking needs by employees on-shift + expected 
visitors; do not require detailed design in initial scope but include parking 
assumptions and required counts. 
• Annex options: discussion of existing one-story annex vs. replacing with new 
two-story annex; committee members (Chris) advised that demolition and 
rebuilding may be more cost-effective than attempting to re-level or dig down 
under existing annex due to plumbing and slab issues. Committee discussed 
potential futureproofing (unfinished upper floors) but noted elevator and cost 
implications. 
• Cost sensitivity: committee emphasized strong desire to minimize cost and 
avoid “project creep”; RFP should request firm estimates of hours and cost for 
the study phase rather than prescribing a fixed dollar-limit that might exclude 
qualified firms. 
• Meeting room scheduling analysis: Chair to request Town Clerk provide 
calendar of monthly meetings to determine typical simultaneous meeting 
demand (used to estimate number and size of meeting rooms). 
• RFP product expectation clarified: a holistic package that helps select board 
convert it to a formal RFP or use as basis for architectural work, including 
recommended square footage ranges and cost-per-square-foot guidance. 
Committee scope: Town Hall + Ritter building

• Confirmed scope: Committee will concentrate on municipal functions tied to Town 
Hall and Ritter (consolidation of municipal staff supervised by Town Manager, select 
board meeting space, public access). TC Passios / senior housing / community center 
topics are outside the committee’s charge at this time per Select Board direction; 
committee agreed to remove TC Pasios/housing from current agenda. 
MHP grant / TC Passios discussion 
• Brief discussion: Land use director flagged an MHP grant (housing-focused) that 
could conceivably relate to TC Passios redevelopment; committee discussed the TC 
Passios and senior housing are outside the committee’s charge following prior Select 
Board guidance; item to be removed from committee agenda. School Committee 
representative also requested the property remain available given anticipated MSBA 
process submission in spring.  
• Tony raised that TC Passios is subject to Architectural Preservation District 
Commission (APDC) review; any demolition must be vetted with APDC prior to 
Town Meeting action; caution against putting demolition or straw-poll articles on the 
warrant without APDC review and zoning enforcement officer confirmation. 
 
Public comment:

committee to provide clear recommendations on consolidation viability as the
