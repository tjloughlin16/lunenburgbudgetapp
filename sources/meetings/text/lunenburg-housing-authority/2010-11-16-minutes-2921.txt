MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
Members Present:
Member absent:

John Romano, Chairperson
Sheila Lumi, Member
Elizabeth Murphy, Member
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member
Susan Szocik, Vice-Chairperson
Also Present:
Eugene Capoccia, Executive Director
There was a large contingency of residents present. Tenants questioned why
the heaters in the units were different. The Director informed the tenants that
the Rinnai Company which the heaters originate from changed the model
and the L/M building received the new heating models.
The Chair declared a quorum present at 7:00 p.m. and began the meeting.
Minutes of the October 19, 2010 meeting were presented. On a Motion by
Elizabeth Murphy, seconded by Sheila Lumi; the minutes were approved as
presented. All were in favor.
The Executive Director provided his report. He gave an overview of the
construction. The construction is over 80% completed. Mr. Capoccia also
informed the Board that ARRA Federal Stimulus Fund will be funding the
five new furnaces for the 705 units.
Mr. Capoccia then proceeded to review the Financial Report. He indicated
that the Housing Authority was doing well and are ahead of the subsidy
projected by $22,301. On a motion by Sheila Lumi, seconded by Elizabeth
Murphy, the Financial Report was approved, all in favor.
Mr. Capoccia then presented the following Resolutions;
