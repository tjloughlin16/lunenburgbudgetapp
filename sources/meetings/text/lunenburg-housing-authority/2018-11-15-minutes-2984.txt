LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY 
 
MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING 
 
November 15, 2018 
 
Members Present:   Paul Doherty, Vice Chair 
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member 
     Linda McDonald, Member 
 
Members Absent:   Caroline Fortin, Member 
 
Also Present:    Benjamin Gold, Interim Executive Director 
Denise Ivaldi, Recording Secretary 
      
1. The Chair declared a quorum present and opened the meeting at 6:30 p.m.   
 
2. The minutes of the October 25, 2018 meeting were presented.  Deborah Christen made a motion 
to accept the minutes ; Linda McDonald  seconded.  All  were in favor and the minutes were 
accepted. 
 
3. The Executive Director Report: 
• FHA/LHA Update – Fitchburg is separating, interviewing for Executive Director.  Hope 
to have someone in place by January 1, 2019.  We are working to move FHA employees, 
computers and phones to Fitchburg soon. 
• Ben Gold will be attending the NAHRO Fall Conference December 3-4. 
• We received a letter from the laundry company stating there will be no rate increase  as 
previously posted, their error. 
• December board meeting on the 13th, if needed as Ben Gold will be out of the office on the 
20th.  The board was fine with the change in date. 
 
4.  Financial Report (October 2018): 
• Page F2 – Reserves are at 38.35%. 
• Page F3 – We are a little behind. 
• Utilities are caught up. Maintenance is a little behind; hope to even out by year-end. 
 
Deborah Christen made a motion to accept the October 2018 financial reports. Linda McDonald 
seconded; all were in favor. 
 
5.  Modernization Report:    
• No discussion; no on-going projects. 
 
6.  State-Aided Housing Report: 
• We received 28 standard applications in September; 1 4 elderly/young disabled and 14 
family. 
• There are 889 applicants on the waiting list. 
• No lease-ups; one vacancy 
• We are 98.15% occupied.

9.  Other Correspondence:
