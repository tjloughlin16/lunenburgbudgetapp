At 6:30 p.m. the Chair declared the meeting opened. Minutes of the January 17, 2013
meeting were presented. On a Motion by Elizabeth Murphy, seconded by Paul Doherty;
the minutes were approved as presented. All were in favor.
The Executive Director then presented his report. He reported that a number of
Corporations went out to bid on the paving project. The three lowest bids as follows;
Sunshine Paving $72,000; Lazaro Paving Corp. $78,900; A.F. Amorello & Sons $91,950.
He also mentioned that during the last snow storm the maintenance personnel John Siart,
actually spent the night in the community room office, to prevent getting stuck and not
being able to get back to the Housing Authority.
