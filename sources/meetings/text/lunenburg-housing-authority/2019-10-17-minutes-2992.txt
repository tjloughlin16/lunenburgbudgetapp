Legislative Committee.

Ben Gold explained that DHCD PHNs 2018 -09 and 2018 -23 allow for a 10% increase in 
ANUEL. 
 
RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE AMENDMENT TO THE  
MANAGEMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT WITH THE  
LEOMINSTER HOUSING AUTHORITY 
 
Resolution 201917 
 
Whereas: DHCD Public Housing Notices 2018-09 and 2018-23 provide guidance on 
applying Annual Non -Utility Expense Level (ANUEL) increases toward 
management fees collected under a current, DHCD-approved Management 
Services Agreement between an Owner LHA and a Management Agent 
and 
 
Whereas: LHAs may amend their Management Services Agreements to increase the 
management fee eac h budget year in accordance with the Owner’s 
ANUEL increase published in the DHCD Budget Guidelines for that 
budget year, so long as both parties agree to the increase and 
 
Whereas: DHCD has increased the FY 2020 budget ANUEL by 10% and set a 
maximum Management fee of $42,726. 
 
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, 
 
That the Lunenburg Housing Authority Board of Commissioners 
authorizes the amendment to the Management Services Agreement with 
Leominster Housing Authority to increase the management fee to $39,204 
contingent on DHCD approval. 
 
RESOLVED, Motion made by Karin Menard to authorize the amendment to the 
Management Services Agreement with the Leominster Housing Authority 
to increase the management fee to $39,204 contingent on DHCD approval. 
 
SECONDED, Ronald Dionne seconded the motion, and the motion was passed by a vote 
of 3 to 0.
