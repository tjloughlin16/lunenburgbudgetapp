1. The Chair declared a quorum present and opened the meeting at 6:41 p.m.   
 
2. The minutes of the April 18, 2019 meeting were presented.  Deborah Christen made a motion 
to accept the minutes; Karin Menard seconded.  All were in favor and the minutes were accepted. 
 
3. The Executive Director Report: 
 Ben Gold welcome new board member Karin Menard. 
 Ben Gold asked for a moment of silence with the sad loss of Chair Paul Doherty in May. 
 Met with residents following the CIP meeting to addresses any issues/concerns they had. 
 DHCD On-Line Board Member Training – will impact this year’s PMR so if you haven’t 
completed please do so. 
 Conflict of Interest Law Training to be completed every two years. 
 
4.  Financial Report – April – July 2019  
 Page F2 – Reserves are at 64.92%. 
 Page F3 – Shelter rents are doing well, on target.  Utilities and Administrative Expenses 
are good.  No concerns at this time. 
 Page F4 – Budgeted $20,000 for extraordinary maintenance, spent $4,000 on vacancies 
 Monthly payroll passed around for two board member signatures. 
 
Deborah Christen made a motion to accept the April, May, June and July financial reports. Karin 
Menard seconded; all were in favor. 
 
5.  Modernization Report:    
 Underground Fire Alarm Wiring Replacement – old, replace before issues arise. 
 Septic System Replacement – Project is with DHCD, soliciting for designer. 
 705 Roof Replacement – Received six (6) bids, low bidder was E5 Bu ilders LLC in 
Leominster for $24,900. 
 Work Order Report – Six (6) work orders received, all completed.

6.  State-Aided Housing Report: 
 CHAMP – There are some applicant selection process changes which are being 
implemented now. 
 Applications received in July – 15 elderly/young disabled and 8 family. 
 Applicants currently on the waiting list – 266 elderly, 220 young disabled, 668 family 
two bedroom and 350 family three bedroom. 
 We are currently at 96.23% occupancy. 
 In July there was one new lease-up and one new vacancy.
