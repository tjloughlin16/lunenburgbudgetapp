LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY 
MINUTES OF THE SPECIAL MEETING 
 
131 White Street, Lunenburg, MA 
 
September 5, 2019 
 
Members Present:   Linda McDonald, Vice Chair 
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member 
Karin Menard, Member 
      
Also Present:    Benjamin Gold, Executive Director 
Denise Ivaldi, Recording Secretary 
      
1. The Chair declared a quorum present and opened the meeting at 5:33 p.m.   
 
The purpose of the Special Meeting was to approve two resolutions on the 705 -roof replacement 
at Pearl Brook. 
 
2.  New Business:  The following resolutions were presented: 
RESOLUTION ACCEPTING LOW BID WITHDRAWAL FROM E5 BUILDERS LLC   
FOR THE 705 ROOF REPLACEMENT AT PEARL BROOK 
DHCD Project # 162032 
Resolution 201915 
WHEREAS: E5 Builders LLC has withdrawn their bid of $24,900.00 for the roof 
replacement at the Pearl Brook (705) development (see attached memo). 
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, 
 That the Lunenburg Housing Authority Board of Commissioners accepts 
the low bid withdraw al from E5 Builders LLC, 45 Granite Street, 
Leominster, MA 01453 for the 705-roof replacement at Pearl Brook. 
RESOLVED, Motion made by Linda McDonald to accept the low bid withdrawal from 
E5 Builders LLC, 45 Granite Street, Leominster, MA 01453 for the 705-
roof replacement at Pearl Brook. 
SECONDED,  Karin Menard seconded the motion, and the motion was passed by a 
unanimous vote.

RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE ACCEPTANCE AND  
APPROVAL OF THE SECOND LOWEST BID FOR THE 
 ROOF REPLACEMENT AT PEARL BROOK (705)  
 
DHCD Project # 162032 
Resolution 201916 
WHEREAS: The Lunenburg Housing Authority advertised for bids for roof replacement 
at the Pearl Brook (705) development, and 
WHEREAS: The bids were due by 2:00 p.m. on Wednesday, August 14, 2019 and six 
(6) bids were received (see attached bid results) and 
WHEREAS: The lowest eligible and responsive bidder E5 Builders LLC withdrew their 
bid. 
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, 
 That the Lunenb urg Housing Authority Board of Commissioners approve 
the second lowest eligible and responsive bidder JJS Universal 
Construction Company, 63 Airport Road, Dudley, MA 01571 with a bid of 
$26,640.00 and further authorizes the Chair or their designee to sign said 
contract and/or any other documents necessary. 
RESOLVED, Motion made by Deborah Christen to award the contract for roof 
replacement at the Pearl Brook (705) development to the second lowest 
eligible and responsive bidder JJS Universal Construction Com pany, 63 
Airport Road, Dudley, MA 01571 with a bid of $26,640.00 and further 
authorizes the signing of the contract and/or any other documents necessary 
by the Chair or their designee. 
SECONDED,  Linda McDonald seconded the motion, and the motion was passe d by a 
unanimous vote. 
3. Deborah Christen  made a motion to adjourn the meeting at  5:37 p.m.  Linda McDonald  
seconded, all were in favor, and the meeting was adjourned.
