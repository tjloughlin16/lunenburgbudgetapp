LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY
MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
April 19, 2011
Members Present:
John Romano, Chairperson
Susan Szocik, Vice-Chairperson
Elizabeth Murphy, Member
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member
Sheila Lumi, Member
Also Present:
Eugene Capoccia, Executive Director
Jen Benson, State Representative (D-Lunenburg)
There were a number of tenants present. Residents brought up a few complaints such as
visitor's speeding at the complex, tenants feeding wild animals, and length of time to
connect to answering service.
The Chairman asked to speak to the Board and all guest present.
Mr. Romano expressed that after 15 years on the Board of Commissioners it was his time
to move on.
State Representative Jen Benson (D-Lunenburg) presented Mr. Romano a plaque signed
by the Speaker of the House commemorating his fifteen years of service.
The Executive Director also presented Mr. Romano a plaque thanking him for his
dedicated service. Mr. Capoccia indicated that it was nice to have a partner working
together to improve Pearl Brook.
The Chair declared a quorum present at 7:00 p.m. and began the meeting. Minutes of the
March 15, 2011 were presented. On a motion by Elizabeth Murphy, seconded by
Deborah A.H. Christen, the minutes of the meeting were approved.
The Executive Director provided his report. He informed that all units were rehabbed and
ccupied. There were two incidents of a toilet overflow involving the same tenant, whic
aused major damages and has been submitted as an insurance claim. Mr. Capoccia als
mentioned that as a reasonable accommodation a front entrance stair railing has been
installed at F-1. He also mentioned that bids have been requested for the heating and
cooling system of the Community Center.
