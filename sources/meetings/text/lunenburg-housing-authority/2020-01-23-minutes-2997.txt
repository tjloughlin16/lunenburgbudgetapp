LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY

131 White Street, Lunenburg, MA 
 
January 23, 2020 
 
Members Present:   Linda McDonald, Vice Chair  
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member 
Karin Menard, Member 
Ronald Dionne, member 
 
Also Present:    Benjamin Gold, Executive Director 
Adam Gautie, Assistant Executive Director

1. Vice Chair Linda McDonald declared a quorum present and opened the meeting at 6:34 p.m.   
 
Opportunity for Public Comments: 
 
 Parking at corner, people parking in no parking area.  Housing authority is working on this 
issue. 
 
Ben Gold asked for a moment of silence for former Executive Director Gene Capoccia who passed 
away earlier this month.  Gene was Executive Director for 25 years with t he Leominster Housing 
Authority.   He was a top-notch guy; let’s take a moment to think about him and his wife. 
 
Ben Gold introduced our new maintenance supervisor Scott MacKenzie; who was previously at the 
Ayer Housing Authority.  Scott told the Board he was glad to be here; he is a Lunenburg resident.  
He takes pride in his work and the complex. 
 
Election of Officer put on hold until a future meeting. 
 
2. The minutes of the November meeting will be provided next month. 
 
3. The Executive Director Report: 
 We received DHCD FY2023 formula funding award letter in the amount of $70,196. 
 Mass NAHRO v DHCD, E.D. lawsuit (some Executive Directors involved in this). 
The claim is that DHCD is over -reaching with E.D. contracts, providing more than general 
guidance. 
4.  Financial Report (November 2019): 
 Reserves are at 70.27%.  We are doing well for Utilities; Maintenance and Extraordinary 
Maintenance are both good.  Motion to accept the November 2019 financial report was 
made by Karin Menard.  Deborah Christen seconded; all were in favor.  Monthly payroll 
passed around for two board member signatures.

5.  Modernization Report:    
 Underground Wiring for Fire Alarm Panel (Fish #162030) – DHCD waiting on revised fee 
proposal from Nangle Engineering. 
 Septic System Replacement (Fish #162031) – Pumping all three systems at 705s; hopefully 
all three systems will be replaced this summer. 
 FY2020 Health & Safety Exterior Lighting (Fish #162034) – DHCD awarded an additional 
grant of $10,000; will use for outs ide lighting near the dumpsters in addition to the front 
parking lot. 
 Work Order Report – 32 routine work orders received, 22 completed; 3 emergency work 
orders, 2 completed. 
 
6.  State-Aided Housing Report: 
 12 applications received in December – 7 elderly/young disabled and 5 family. 
 No lease-ups or vacancies in December. 
 We are currently at 100% occupancy. 
 
7.  Unfinished Business:  None 
8.  New Business:  The following resolution was presented by the Chair: 
Resolution 202001 Quarterly Unrecoverable Debt Write-Offs. A tenant of the Lunenburg Housing 
Authority vacated while owing rent and/or tenant balances of $ 296.77 not recoverable per the 
Housing Manager; this balance is older than ninety (90) days as of December 31, 2019.   Motion 
made by Deborah Christen to write-off $296.77 as uncollectable debts.  Karin Menard seconded 
and the motion was passed by a 4 to 0 vote.

11. Deborah Christen made a motion to adjourn the meeting at  7:06 p.m. Karin Menard seconded,
