LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY
MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
Members Present:
Member absent:
March 15, 2011
John Romano, Chairperson
Susan Szocik, Vice-Chairperson
Elizabeth Murphy, Member
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member
Sheila Lumi, Member
Also Present:
Eugene Capoccia, Executive Director
There were a number of tenants present. They brought up several issues such
as the dumpster being too small, several door knobs not working correctly
and visitors driving too fast in the complex. Mr. Capoccia assured that these
issues would be addressed.
The Chair declared a quorum present at 6:45 p.m. and began the meeting.
Minutes of the November 16, 2010 were presented. On a motion by
Elizabeth Murphy, seconded by John Romano the minutes of the meeting
were approved.
The Chair then proceeded to present the Minutes of the February 15, 2011
meeting. On a motion by Deborah A.H. Christen, seconded by Susan Szocik
the minutes were approved.
The Executive Director provided his report. He gave an update on the
construction. All units are 100% occupied. Installations of the furnaces are
complete. He also mentioned that they are looking into spring plantings.
