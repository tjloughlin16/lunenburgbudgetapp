LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY
MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
Members Present:
Member absent:

December 14, 2010
John Romano, Chairperson
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member
Susan Szocik, Vice-Chairperson
Sheila Lumi, Member
Elizabeth Murphy, Member
Eugene Capoccia, Executive Director
There were a number of tenants present. Tenants had questions on the
progress of the construction. The Director gave the tenants an update.
The Chair declared a quorum present at 6:45 p.m. and began the meeting.
Minutes of the November 16, 2010 meeting were presented. Minutes were
not approved at this meeting due to the fact that two Board members present
at the November meeting were absent at this meeting.
The Executive Director provided his report. He brought the Board up to date
with the use of the construction contingency
Mr. Capoccia then proceeded to review the Financial Report. He indicated
that the Housing Authority was doing better than expected. On a motion by
Deborah Christen, seconded by Susan Szocik, the Financial Report was
approved, all in favor.

The Director also informed the Board that UNITIL provided a check in the
amount of $32,445 and the Housing Authority had made arrangements to
install all new fridges in the senior complex.
Mr. Capoccia then presented the following Resolutions;
