There were a few tenants present, no comments.
At 6:30 p.m. the Chair declared the meeting opened. Minutes of the April 18, 2013
meeting were presented. On a Motion by Elizabeth Murphy, seconded by Paul Doherty;
the minutes were approved as presented. All were in favor.
The Executive Director then presented his report. The parking resurfacing project has
been completed. Mr. Capoccia brought the Board up to date on the past MassNahro
conference he attended. He then informed that Bernie Kirsten has accepted a new
position with the Worcester Housing Authority and is no longer working with us. The
new Assistant Director for Leominster Housing Authority will be Peter Proulx, who has
been highly recommended and is very well known for his housing, development,
planning and management experience.
Mr. Capoccia informed the Board about the new checks and balance of collecting rents.
Tenants who are not set up for ACH (automatic checking withdrawal) as of August 1st
2013, they will mail checks or money orders along with a coupon generated by the
Housing Authority to a lock box located at the Fidelity Bank.
The Executive Director then presented the Financial Report along with the bills for
payments. He indicated that Housing Authority was performing well. Financial report
including a review of accounts payable was accepted, on a motion by Deborah A.H.
Christen; seconded by Paul Doherty, financial was approved as presented.
Mr. Capoccia the presented the following Resolutions;
