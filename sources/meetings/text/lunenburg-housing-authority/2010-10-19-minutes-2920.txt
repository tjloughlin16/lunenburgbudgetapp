LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY
MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
Members Present:
October 19, 2010
Elizabeth Murphy, Member
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member
Susan Szocik, Vice-Chairperson
Sheila Lumi, Member
Member absent:
John Romano, Chairperson
Also Present:
Eugene Capoccia, Executive Director
There were a number of residents present. Tenants were looking for an
update of the construction. Mr. Capoccia brought the tenants up to date.
The Chair declared a quorum present at 7:00 p.m. and began the meeting.
Minutes of the September 21, 2010 meeting were presented. On a Motion
by Elizabeth Murphy, seconded by Deborah Christen; the minutes were
approved as presented. All were in favor,
•The Executive Director provided his report. He informed that the Housing
Authority might be receiving energy related rebates coming as a result of the
Modernization project.
Mr. Capoccia then proceeded to review the Financial Report. He indicated
that the Housing Authority was doing much better. The bottom line for
statement ending September 30 showed that the Housing Authority is
$13,568 ahead of the budget projected. The Board was pleased with the
Housing Authority progress.
Being no further business, on a motion by Susan Szocik, seconded by
Elizabeth Murphy, the Board voted to adjourn the meeting at 8:30 PM. All
were in favor.
