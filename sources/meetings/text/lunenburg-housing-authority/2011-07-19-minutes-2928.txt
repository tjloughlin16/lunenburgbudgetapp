LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY
MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
July 19, 2011
Members Present:
Susan Szocik, Vice-Chairperson
Elizabeth Murphy, Member
Amaryllis Leet, Member
Member absent:
Sheila Lumi, Member
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member
Also Present:
Eugene Capoccia, Executive Director
Tenants present had no comments.
At 6:30 p.m. the Chair declared the meeting opened. Minutes of the
April 19, 2011 meeting were presented. On a Motion by Elizabeth Murphy,
seconded by Susan Szocik; the minutes were approved as presented. All
were in favor.
The Executive Director provided his report. He informed that Lunenburg's
Formula Funding allocations have been completed. Part of the funding will
be used to redo the asphalt road and parking lots. The community room had
two efficient heat pumps installed, providing heating and cooling systems,
which was paid by funding from UNITIL energy credits.
The vice-chair person then turned the gavel over to Mr. Capoccia for the
purpose of electing new officers. After a brief discussion Susan Szocik
proposed the following; Susan Szocik, Elizabeth Murphy, Vice-Chair,
Amaryllis Leet, Member, Sheila Lumi, Member, Deborah A.H. Christen,
Member, Eugene Capoccia, Secretary. Motion seconded by Amaryllis Leet;
all in favor.
Mr. Capoccia then went over the Financial Report. Mi. Capoccia indicated
income was up for the first six months by $29,000. In exception of
===PAGE 2===
maintenance, the budget was in line with earlier projections. The Director
pointed out that the operating budget was projected to need $48,000 in
subsidy for the first six months. However, the Housing Authority only
needed $11,000 meaning that the Housing Authority was $36,582 to the
good. On a motion by Susan Szocik and seconded by Elizabeth Murphy, the
Financial Report was approved, all in favor.
Then Mr. Capoccia presented the following motions;
