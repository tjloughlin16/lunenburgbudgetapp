LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY 
 
MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING 
 
September 21, 2017 
 
Members Present:   Paul Doherty, Vice Chair 
     Deborah A.H. Christen, Member 
     Caroline Fortin, Member 
Linda McDonald, Member 
 
Members Absent:   Elizabeth Murphy, Chair 
 
Also Present:    Peter Proulx Executive Director 
     Denise Ivaldi, Recording Secretary 
 
Residents were present with comments.        
   
1. The Chair declared a quorum present and opened the meeting at 6:35 p.m.   
 
2. The minutes of the August 17, 2017 meeting were presented.  Caroline Fortin made a motion 
to accept the minutes ; Linda McDonald  seconded.  All  were in favor and the minutes were 
accepted. 
 
3. The Executive Director Report: 
 We are receiving a little extra money from DHCD for Capital Items: 
1. Patio area 
2. Deck – spring next year 
3. Reshingle Community Room roof 
4. Maintenance shed – remove roof, make higher and re-side. 
 
The E.D. will talk to the residents about the deck and discuss other possible uses for the space. 
 
4.  Financial Report (August 2017): 
 Monthly payroll was passed around for two board member signatures. 
 Page F2 – Reserves at 72.86%.  Much higher than should be; spending a little money to 
use some up. 
 Page F3 - $11,084 ahead of budget.  We will be pretty close to budget by year end. 
 
Paul Doherty made a motion to accept the August 2017 financials; Linda McDonald seconded 
and all were in favor. 
 
5.  Modernization Report:    
 Building Envelope Repair – this project is completed. 
 
6.  State-Aided Housing Report: 
 Overall occupancy is at 100%; we have one vacancy coming in September. 
 Work order report included. 
Board member asked how long work orders are left open.  It depends on what is needed.

RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE ACCEPTANCE 
 OF THE LOWEST BID FOR THE WINDOW SASH REPLACEMENT AT THE PEARL 
BROOK  
667-1 ELDERLY DEVELOPMENT  
WORK PLAN 5001; DHCD FISH 162026 
    
Resolution 201715 
 
WHEREAS: The Lunenburg Housing Authority has advertised for bids for the window 
sash replacement project at the 667-1 elderly development, and 
 
WHEREAS: A bid opening was held on September 8, 2017 and the Authority received 
six (6) bids to complete the work (see attached list), and 
 
WHEREAS: Vareika Construction, Inc., 219 Walnut Street, Suite B, West Bridgewater, 
MA 02379 submitted the lowest eligible and responsible bid in the amount 
of $47,300.00, with DHCD’s approval. 
 
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, 
 
 That the Lunenburg Housing Authority authorize the Chair, or their 
designee, to award the contract for the window sash replacement project to 
the lowest eligible and responsible bidder, Vareika Construction, Inc., in 
the amount of $47,300.00. 
 
  This vote shall further authorize the Executive Director to enter into the 
contract in connection with this bid. 
 
RESOLVED, Motion made by Linda McDonald to award the bid for the window sash 
replacement project at the 667 -1 elderly development to the lowest eligible  
and responsible bidder, Vareika Construction, Inc. in the amount of 
$47,300.00. 
 
SECONDED,    Caroline Fortin seconded the motion and the motion was passed by a 
unanimous vote.
