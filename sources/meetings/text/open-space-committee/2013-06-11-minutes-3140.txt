OS Attendees at Joint Session June 11:
M. Benson, R. Verge, J. Carrier, R. Brockelman, B. Kibbe, S. Jordan
The BOS assigned 4 action items to the OS Committee:
1) Create a list of priority criteria to determine the conservation value of available and potentially available
properties, including lands coming out of Chapter 61 and tax title/ town-owned land
2) Create a policy and town process for responding to rights of first refusal for lands coming out of
Chapter 61. NOTE: the BOS requested that the Town Manager copy the OS Committee on Chapter
ROFR notices when the BOS is notified.
3) In the next OS plan, include updates on: a) impacts of the sewer system expansion and the recent
Reservoir St. town well, b) population growth in reference to the comprehensive waste water
management plan numbers, & c) stress levels on the Mulpus and Catacunamoug water source basins.
The next iteration of the plan will be after 2014 and these factors will be considered at that time.
4) put the OS plan in its own directory (as well as in the existing location) on the town web site
