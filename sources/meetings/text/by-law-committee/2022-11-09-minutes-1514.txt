Lunenburg Capital Planning Committee 
November 9, 2022 
Meeting Minutes 
 
Location: Ritter Building  
 
Present: Chairperson Tom Alonzo, David Passios, George Martin, Matthew Brenner, and Peter Beardmore 
(remote). 
Guests: Town Manager Heather Lemieux, Lunenburg Police Chief Thomas Gammell, Lunenburg Fire Chief 
Patrick Sullivan, Lunenburg resident Rhonda Lissio 
Call to Order: Chairperson Tom Alonzo called meeting to order at 6:01 p.m., and read a statement regarding 
the Open Meeting Law and how all public meetings are legally being held remotely, and how to attend and 
participate in those meetings. Link to recorded meeting: https://youtu.be/3quxwv-W0xs 
Public Comment:  
CPC Comment: Mr. Passios commented on the senior center parking lot discussion at the November 
9 meeting, and added that the item was omitted from last year’s capital plan because there was not 
enough information about the project. 
Public Comment: Rhonda Lissio expressed her concern about large purchase requests such as 
ambulances and fire trucks, and what it costs to maintain vehicles that are not replaced as a result of 
a denied request from a department head.

FY29: $252,410 
Replace School Resource Officer cruiser (2017 Ford Explorer) $80,160 
Firearms replacement $20,000 
Replace Patrol Cruiser (2023 Ford Explorer Hybrid) $80,160 
Additional unmarked vehicle to current fleet –second detective position $71,090 
 
FY30: $165,130 –regular replacement of vehicles 
 
FY31-33: Regular replacement of cruisers, administration vehicles, portable radios –unknown costs at this 
time. Chief Gammell aims to convert entire fleet to hybrid vehicles with the exception of the K9 cruiser. 
 
Mr. Martin expressed his concern over how charging stations would be handled looking out to 2030. Chief 
Gammell responded that the Facilities Director would advise on that situation when the time comes. 
  
Mr. Alonzo asked how many vehicles the Police Department has. Current fleet 17 vehicles, Detective car will 
make 18. 
 
Mr. Beardmore asked whether the School Resource Officer program would expand in coming years, and 
include canines. Chief Gammell said he is looking to expand the program if the budget allows.  
Mr. Beardmore also asked how the vehicle rotation program is changing. Chief Gammel explained he’s trying 
to keep the mileage down on the current fleet, and hopes to do so by separating the vehicles by shift in order 
to take the cars off the road for 8-16 hours downtime. Mr. Beardmore asked to see a timeline for vehicles so 
people could follow along.  
 
Mr. Alonzo asked whether the pavement management plan has affected vehicle maintenance. Chief Gammell 
said he’d look into maintenance records. 
 
Mr. Passios encouraged the use of fleet management software for all the vehicles in town that would allow 
access to maintenance records at a moment’s notice.
