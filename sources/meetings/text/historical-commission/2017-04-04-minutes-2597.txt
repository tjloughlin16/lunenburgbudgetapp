TOWN OF LUNENBURG
Lunenburg Historical Commission
Meeting Minutes:
April 4. 2017
Members Present:
Becky Lantry, Cullen Dwyer, Richard McGrath and Lois Lewis
Members Absent:
Jennifer Sanderson
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order at 6:40 p.m.
Minutes Approval:
Minutes were approved for March 7, 2017.
Correspondence and Communications:
There was no correspondence.
Present Business/New Business:
Richard McGrath said there have been no demolition delay requests.
An article in the Lunenburg Ledger for historic village district residents interested in
obtaining and/or having installed bronze plaques for their residence has prompted no
response as of this date.
Becky Lantry will schedule a meeting with Joshua Herrick of the Eagle Scouts to move
the plaque project forward and install these plaques should any resident request
assistance.
This year, June 7".
', is Lunenburg's Historical Day. Becky Lantry, in appropriate dress,
will be at the Town Pound to explain its history and significance.
The Building Reuse Committee submitted bid proposal plans to sell the town's historical
buildings, the town hall, the Ritter Building and the primary school and the possible
relocation of the town offices to the Passios School. No interest has been expressed.
Voted: Cullen Dwyer will send an advisory letter to express the concerns of the
Fistorical Commission members about the sale of the town's historical buildings
individually or collectively and the possible relocation of the town offices to the Passios
School to the various town boards, commissions and committees.
