TOWN OF LUNENBURG
Lunenburg Historical Commission
Meeting Minutes:
September 8, 2015
Members Present:
Becky Lantry, Amory Phelps, Lois Lewis, Cullen Dwyer and Richard McGrath
Members Absent:
None
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order at 6:40 p.m.
Minutes Approval:
Minutes were approved
for June 22, 2015 and June 27, 2015 and
Amended Minutes for April 7, 2015 and April 13, 2015
Correspondence and Communications:
1) Letter dated July 14, 2015 from the building inspector, Michael Sauvageau, in
regard to the Stone Farm Property, 790-792 Massachusetts Avenue and the
Demolition Delay Bylaw. This letter had been presented previously to the July
27, 2015 meeting at which the Historical Commission invoked the Demolition
Delay provision.
2) Letter from the Nolumbeka Project, Howard Clark, Senior Researcher/
Anthropologist, who requested the protection and preservation of any known
valuable historical cites sacred to the American Indians that may now or in the
immediate future be subject to retail or commercial development.
3) Notice was received Hannaford Market, Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg has
requested to display various plants in the adjacent parking lot.
4) Notice was received there would be a meeting of the Planning Board August 24,
2015 to consider the construction of eleven double units (22) townhouse
condominiums at 274 Prospect Street, Lunenburg.
5) Notice was received for the proposed building of sixty six housing units on the
south side of Northfield Road between Massachusetts Avenue and Northfield
Road.
6) Notice was received for a planned pet service facility at 339 Electric Avenue,
Lunenburg.
