Members Present:
Becky Lantry, Cullen Dwyer, Richard McGrath, Jennifer Sanderson and Lois Lewis.
Members Absent:
None.
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order at 6:40 p.m.
Minutes Approval:
Minutes were approved for April 4, 2017.
Correspondence and Communications:
Notice has been received from the Town Clerk's office Jennifer Sanderson, a new
member of the Historical Commission, has been officially sworn in at a Board of
Selectmen meeting.
The Massachusetts Historical Commission will meet May 12, 2017 in Worcester.
Freedom's Way Heritage Area survey regarding Lunenburg Center and historical
buildings cannot be properly addressed with the current compact disc of the Center.
Present Business/New Business:
Richard McGrath reported there have been no new demolition delay requests.
Richard McGrath advised any necessary repairs and work at the Town Pound should start
within the next two weeks. General repairs include painting the sign, installing a new
padlock on the gate, addressing any of the missing stones in the stone walls and trimming
the shrubs and any encroaching tree limbs.
A request to have maintained the town cemeteries will be forwarded to Jack Rodriquenz,
Director of Public Works.
Voted: The Historical Commission will encumber the monies, three thousand dollars
($3000.00) allotted in the town budget for the Historical Commission
Voted: It was voted by a majority (with one abstention) to renew the subscription to the
Lunenburg Ledger for the Historical Commission.
