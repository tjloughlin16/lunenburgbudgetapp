LUNENBURG HISTORICAL COMMISSION  
JANUARY 11, 2022 
MINUTES 
Meeting commenced at 7:05 PM with a quorum present.  December minutes were read by Kevin 
Bousson, and approved and accepted by all present.

questions on the demolition delay for 30 School St.

The APDC has started a Facebook page…. 
Bill Lakso will talk about the K-House survey at our next meeting, to be held February 8, 2022
