Lunenburg  Historical  Commission  joint  meeting  with  APDC    October  2025   The  Lunenburg  Historical  Commission  met  with  the  APDC  at  the  Ritter  Building  on  October  
20th.
 
Members
 
McGrath,
 
Lakso,
 
and
 
Boussom
 
were
 
in
 
attendance.
 
The
 
meeting
 
began
 
at
 
8:32
 
PM
 The  proposed  merger  of  the  APDC  and  Historical  commission  was  intended  to  streamline  
efficiency
 
for
 
both
 
commissions.
 
However,
 
there
 
has

pushback
 
from

members
 
of
 
the
 
community,

that

 Bill  Lakso  questioned  why  anyone  would  feel  this  has  been  kept  a  secret,  since  every  meeting

is

and

 The  Historical  Commission  was  asked  if  they  were  familiar  with  the  bylaw  of  the  APDC,  which

to
 
protect
 
just
 
the
 
national
 
historical

 There  was  concern  that  giving  control  to  the  Historical  Commission  would  be  giving  them  too

 Anthony  Sculinbrini  of  the  APDC  explained  he  understands  how  this  merger  could  be  viewed  as

 Richard  McGrath  explained  that  over  his  25  years  on  the  Historical  Commission  he  has  seen  a

 Public  question  was  asked  as  to  why  the  amended  bylaw  changed  “architectural”  to  “historical”.

 Nathan  Lockwood  of  the  APDC  pointed  out  that  we  should  probably  consider  delaying  the

There

 A  motion  was  made  by  the  APDC  and  the  Historical  Commission  to  include  the  merger  in  the

 With  no  further  business,  the  meeting  adjourned  at  8:58  PM.
