TOWN OF LUNENBURG
Lunenburg Historical Commission
Meeting Minutes
November 2, 2015
Members present:
Becky Lantry, Amory Phelps, Richard McGrath, Cullen Dwyer
Member absent:
Lois Lewis
Call to Order-6:40 p.m.
Correspondence and Communications
Richard McGrath talked with the Cemetery Commission about restoring the receiving
tomb at the North Cemetery. The cemetery needs grants to accomplish the necessary
work. The Historical Commission is going to give $ 500.00 toward this project. Richard
will speak to Kerry Speidel about additional available funds or grants.
Historically Speaking article for November was Cullen Dwyer and Amory Phelps is
scheduled for December.
Historical Markers for Lunenburg historic homes are a hot topic. The National Register
offers these markers. Becky Lantry is following up on the procedures to make this
possible for the community.
John Bishop from the Fitchburg Sentinel attended our meeting to learn more about the
Historic Markers this Commission is working on presently.
Discussed the APDC and the protection it will give the Historic District
Cullen Dwyer and Richard McGrath will request to be members of the APDC.
Adjournment: 7:45 p.m.
The next meeting will be December 1, 2015
