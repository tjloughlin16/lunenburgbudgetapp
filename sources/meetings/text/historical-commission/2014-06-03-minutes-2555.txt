Correspondence and Communications:
Received a forwarded email from the Lunenburg Planning Board asking for input regarding the impact
on historic resources of a cell tower located on Leominster Shirley Road. The Commission feels that the
only historic concern would be for Lancaster Ave. as it has been designated a scenic road. Any
construction should be sensitive to the views from Lancaster Ave. Becky will pen a response letter.
Old Business:
Town center imaging plan
Damon Expressed his desire for help from the Historic Commission to run an imaging session with the
goal of reaching a common understanding of the history of the town center in provide a solid foundation
moving forward in the writing of the village district zoning by-law. Becky expressed her concern that if
the Historic Commission puts on this event then it would seem as if the commission is endorsing
development in the town center. As a result of the conversation Damon will put the imaging session
together himself.
