TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
OFFICE OF THE SELECT BOARD 
 
Public Hearing Notice 
In conformity with the requirements of Section 22 of Chapter 166 of the General Laws (Ter. Ed.), you are hereby notified 
that a public hearing will be held at the Town Hall, 17 Main Street, 2nd Floor, in the Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 
Lunenburg, MA, on Tuesday, November 24, 2026 upon petition of Verizon New England, Inc. for permission to erect or 
construct, and a location for, a line of wires, poles and such other fixtures as may be necessary to sustain or protect the 
wires of the line, for the transmission of electricity, upon, along, across, or under the following public ways of said Town: 
 
7:10 p.m. COUNTRY ROAD (1) Place one (1) two (2) inch underground telecommunications conduit along the 
northeast side of Country Road beginning at the existing utility pole T.1, located at the northerly corner of the intersection 
of Country Road and Chase Road, and running in a northwesterly direction a distance of approximately three hundred 
sixty-eight (368) feet to Pedestal 1 as on plan number 1A8H7UN dated July 13, 2026.  
 
7:15 p.m. CHASE ROAD (1) Place one (1) two (2) inch underground telecommunications conduit beginning at existing 
Pole T.6, located on the southeast side of Chase Road and running in a southwesterly direction along the southwest side 
of Chase Road a distance of approximately one hundred fifty (150) feet to a point, then continuing across Chase Road a 
distance of approximately fifty (50) feet to a point on the northwest sides of Chase Road, then continuing approximately 
six hundred (600) feet to existing Pole T.51. as on plan 1A8H7UD dated July 9, 2026 
 
7:20 p.m. WILLIAMS DRIVE (1) Place one (1) two (2) inch underground telecommunications conduit along the 
southwest side of Williams Drive beginning at existing utility pole T.7, located at the northwest corner of the intersection 
of Williams Drive and Island Road, and running in a northwesterly direction a distance of approximately six hundred 
(600) feet to Pedestal 1 as on plan 1A8H7VP dated  
 
Purpose: To extend and provide improved services to the neighbors along Country Road; and to provide for the 
distribution of intelligence and telecommunications.  
 
If you have any questions, contact the Select Board Office at 978-582-4144 
 
If you would like to participate remotely via ZOOM, please check the Select Board agenda posted on the Town 
website calendar at https://www.lunenburgma.gov/calendar for sign-in instructions. Agendas are posted 48 
hours in advance of the meeting.
