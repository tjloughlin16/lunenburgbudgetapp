HEARING NOTICE 
 
The Lunenburg Licensing Authority will hold a public hearing on Tuesday, October 1, 2024 at 
7:25 P.M. at Town Hall, 17 Main Street, 2
nd Floor, Lunenburg, MA on the application submitted 
by Bedrock Bar and Grill, LLC, d/b/a Bedrock Bar and Grill for the transfer of alcohol license 
#00039-RS-0636 (Restaurant All Alcohol), located at 324 Electric Avenue, Unit #4. The 
premises is described as a Lakeview Plaza end unit consisting of bar, dining room, two 
restrooms, kitchen cooking area, seating for 100 people with an entrance and exit.     
 
The following information is also provided for members of the public that would like to participate 
in the meeting remotely:
