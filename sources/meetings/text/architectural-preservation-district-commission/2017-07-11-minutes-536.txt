Meeting Minutes 
Tuesday July 11th, 2017 
Ritter Building 
 
 
Call to Order: At 7:00PM 
 
Present:  Chairman McGrath, Corcoran, LaVeck and Dwyer 
 
Review of Minutes:  Minutes from the regular meeting on 5-16-17 had previously not been approved.  The minutes were read 
and approved by a majority (La Veck did not vote because he was absent).  Minutes from the regular meeting on 6-20-17 were 
also read and approved unanimously. 
 
Communications: McGrath had communicated with Administrative Assistant to the Town Manager Elaine Peterson, who said 
that she was prepared to mail out a copy of the guidelines to APD residents, and inform them of the upcoming second meeting 
with the BoS, as soon as the new draft of the guidelines was ready.  She is also running an advertisement in the Lunenburg 
Ledger seeking to fill the vacancy in the APDC.  It was rumored that Katherine Adams had submitted a talent blank form, and 
may apply. 
 
New Business: 
Edits to guidelines discussed in light of A Burney’s commentary.  The APDC did not see any reason to change the guidelines 
again. 
 
Dwyer proposed that the APDC members draft mock proposals for review at future meetings, in order to practice our procedures 
and how we would evaluate proposals. 
 
Next Meeting: August 1st, with BoS, 7:20PM 
Next regular meeting to be held Tuesday August 15th. 
 
Adjourned: 7:25
