Town of Lunenburg 
Architectural Preservation District Commission 
 
Chair: Richard McGrath 
Vice Chair: Jim LaVeck 
Clerk: Cullen Dwyer 
Meeting Minutes: Feb. 9th, 2016 
7:10 Meeting called to order 
Minutes from 1-16-16 approved 
The board reviewed the APD of Ipswich bylaw as it currently stands. It was agreed that it would 
be a good idea to include a review of architectural styles found in Lunenburg. Also, the idea of 
including a flow chart to guide users through process. 
 
Certain temporary structures are exempt from the Lunenburg APD bylaw.  The board 
recognized the need to generate a list of possible temporary structures, and delineate 
guidelines on how long and how big they may be before they are not exempt from the ADP 
bylaw.  It must also draught design guidelines for temporary buildings that are not exempt. 
 
The board then discussed how the APD design guidelines might mesh or conflict with the VDB. 
 
The board recognized the need to first create an outline for the APD guidelines. 
 
The idea was proposed to hire a consultant to help the board identify architectural building 
styles in the Lunenburg APD.  McGrath said he would check to see if the APDC had a budget 
available for such measures. 
 
Adjourned 8:20 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
Cullen Dwyer, recording clerk
