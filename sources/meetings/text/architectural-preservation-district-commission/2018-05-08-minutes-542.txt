Meeting Minutes
Tuesday May 8th, 2018
Ritter Building
Call to Order: At 7:01PM
Present: Chairman McGrath, Vice-Chairman LaVeck, Corcoran and Dwyer
Also: Tanner Cole, who is running for planning board, attended.
Review of minutes: Approved
CTA Applications review: A representative of 99 Lancaster Avenue appeared to submit the $25 filing fee and defend the appli-
cation for a CTA. The alteration is a re-roof of the garage. The replacement shingles where chosen to match the existing shin
gles on the house a much as possible. The APDC voted to waive any further requirements and approve the application.
Some confusion arose as to the serial number that should be assigned to the application. Dwyer said he would begin a register
of CTA applications with serial numbers for easy reference.
New Business: LaVeck reported that at town meeting the town had voted to postpone a vote on Articles 20 and 24 regarding the
removal of the Brooks House and Old Primary School.
Dwyer suggested that the APDC develop its own redevelopment plan for the Primary School and offer it to the BoS and the town.
Some discussion followed.
Old Business
53 Lancaster: Dwyer reported on his efforts to pump water out of the basement. McGrath reported that the owner intended to
sell the property in the next two years and move out, and so the job of the APDC should be not to restore the property but to
simply arrest the decay and let the next owner restore it. He recommended the following immediate measures:
• repair or replace gutters and downspouts, and extend spouts away from house.
• Eliminate flow of water from front stoop toward house (probably by removing stoop)
• Inspect attic and determine source of leak in north side of house
• Clear vegetation on and around house.
Dwyer volunteered to organize efforts to clear the vegetation.
