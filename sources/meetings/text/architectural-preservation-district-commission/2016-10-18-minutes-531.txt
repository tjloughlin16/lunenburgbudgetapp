Meeting Minutes: Tuesday October 18th, 2016 
 
Call to Order: 
meeting open and called to order at 7:05 PM with all members present, except for Mr. Kherer. 
 
 
Old Business 
Land Use Director Adam Burney attended the meeting to offer input on the draft of the APDC 
guidelines.  He felt that the document the Commission had put forward was not usable and not 
ready to present to the Board of Selectmen.  He praised the style guidelines that the town of 
Ipswich had published for their APD.  He also recommended that the Lunenburg APDC follow 
their lead: by publishing rules, regulations and procedures separately from the style guidelines.  
He further suggested finding a published and widely available guidebook on architectural styles, 
and using it an authoritative reference for the guidelines.  Finally, Mr. Burney presented his own 
recommended outline of the documents, and promised that he would submit the beginnings of a 
draft to the APDC in the coming weeks. 
 
Review of Minutes 
Minutes from 9-13-16 were read, voted on and approved. 
 
New Business: 
The Commission discussed the contraction and restoration work that had been taking place on 
Leominster rd.  A stone wall had been taken down for underground work, but afterwards re- 
stored.  The APDC had not been notified.  McGrath had discovered that permits for this type of 
excavation were granted not by the building inspector, but by the Department of Public Works.  
This was noted by the commission as being of procedural importance. 
 
Adjourned 8:40 PM
