Richard McGrath, Chair

Meeting Minutes 
Tuesday, August 23rd, 2016 
 Location: Ritter Memorial Building 
 
Call to Order: McGrath called the meeting to order at 7:03 
 
Review of Minutes: Minutes from July 19th, 2016 were reviewed.  McGrath motioned to accept 
them and LaVeck seconded the motion.  The motion carried unanimously. 
 
Old Business 
Corcoran submitted the application form.  After discussion, McGrath motioned to approve the 
application with amendments.  LaVeck seconded and the motion carried. 
 
LaVeck presented the latest version of the guidelines.  McGrath motioned to accept them.  
LaVeck seconded and the motion carried. 
 
McGrath motioned to institute a policy to have the APDC meet on the second Tuesday of each 
month.  Corcoran seconded and the motion carried. 
 
Since the previous meeting, Mina Scholten of 3 Oak Ave contacted the APDC regarding 
replacement of windows of her house.  Since the APDC guidelines and procedures for 
application for Certificate to Alter were incomplete, and the alteration as described was not very 
problematic, the APDC Chair did not take further action on the matter.  The Commission 
discussed the details of what happened and how it might learn and be better prepared for future 
applications. 
 
Plans were made to get Kehrer’s section of the guidelines on Sheathing incorporated into the 
guidelines and ready for review at the next meeting. 
 
Next meeting scheduled for Tuesday September 13th. 
 
Adjournment: at 8:04PM, McGrath motioned to adjourn.  The motion was seconded by Corcoran 
and carried unanimously.
