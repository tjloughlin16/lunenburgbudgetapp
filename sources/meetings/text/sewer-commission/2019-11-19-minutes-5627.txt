maintenance, corrective maintenance and billing with SWSS. Jack added that it is unfair if SWSS is called in

profitable for SWSS. Carl pointed out that the contract was structured to encourage maintenance. He is also
concerned about the reliability of the system since callouts are increasing. John replied that the age of the system
must be considered and that they have installed new pumps. Jack noted that resident issues, such as mopheads, are
causing the big problems. Ordering spare parts was discussed. (Dave M. entered the meeting.) Jack added that it
may look like an increase in callouts, but it is the same as in the past. Deb noted that SWSS is available 24/7. The
Commission discussed whether they needed to rewrite the scope and percentage increases to the contract amount.
Deb reviews the work performed line by line on Smartsheets to determine what is billable and her goal is to figure out
the gray areas in the billing. Barb added that she and Jack discuss every bill. Joan suggested making a firm plan as
to what should be done going forward. Mike said that the contract should be profitable for SWSS and that we rely on
