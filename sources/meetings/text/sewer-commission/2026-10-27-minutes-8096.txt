---424 Sunnyhill Road- Payment Plan Request – Brett asked if the homeowner was present. Bill stated she is not 
but there was an anonymous viewer and suggested continuing with the discussion. Brett noted that the customer has 
been in arrears for a few years.  Andrew reported the customer currently owes about $1,526 and there was a tax lien 
of $1,430 put on for last year. The customer came in and asked for a second payment plan. He had previously 
offered a $100/month payment plan and 2 pool waivers but no payments have been received. Andrew added that he 
tries to work with everyone as long as they try to make an effort, but he has not seen any effort so far. Bill noted the 
last payment on this account was made on 5/18/2023. Scott asked if the amount owed for 2023/24, added as a tax 
lien, was recouped. Andrew replied the tax collector confirmed the customer has been paying property taxes through 
their mortgage company. The Treasurer’s office stated the amount due on the account currently is $1,529.21 and is 
accruing interest. Andrew noted that he had expected the customer to be in attendance and delinquent accounts 
need to be put on tax liens within the next week and a half so the Assessors can add them to the December tax bills. 
The Commissioners discussed and agreed that Andrew has gone above and beyond to work with the customer. Dan 
asked about the pool waiver and Andrew replied that the customer received 2 pool waivers in the past but he denied 
this year due to the lack of payments. The waiver is based on the average usage over 4 quarters and the excess 
water usage for filling a pool. Brett asked what their options are for customers that do not pay and Bill noted they 
cannot shut off sewer.  However, he recommended the current amount goes on tax lien and to give her a payment 
plan of $100/month for next year. Scott pointed out that customers that are in arrears can pay make partial payments 
on the Uni-Pay platform, but there has been no effort with this customer. He suggested letting the balance go on a 
tax lien, which is the only way they have received payment. The Commissioners discussed and agreed to not offer a 
payment plan. Andrew asked for a vote for the record. Bill will contact the customer about their decision and tell her 
to watch the meeting. The Commissioners discussed the wording for the vote.  
Motion: Dan P.                                                                                                                                    Second: Scott D.  
To direct the Sewer Business Manager to send the balance of fees to the tax collector for 424 Sunnyhill Road 
and not offer a payment plan at this point.                               Roll Call Vote: Dan-aye, Scott-aye, and Brett-aye. 
 
--- Sewer Business Manager/ DPW Director Updates– 
    ---CTE Contract – Bill reported that the RFR (Request For Response) has gone out and the bids are due 
11/12/26. That will give enough time to choose the new contractor before the current contract expires.  
    ---Q2 FY 27 Billing – Andrew completed the Q2 bill run and has dated the bills for 10/29/26, with a due date of 
11/30/26. They will be mailed on 10/29 and customers can call him with any questions.  
    ---Sewer Demand Fee rate – Andrew and Bill met with Town Counsel in regard to options. The State interest 
maximum is 14% and Lunenburg is currently at 12%. They can increase the Demand Fee from $10 to $20 or they  
can increase the interest to 14% and be on par with the rest of the state.  Bill noted the $10 fee is to reimburse for 
expenses but he does not think it covers their expenses and he suggested going up. Lunenburg is currently the 
lowest of area towns. Andrew said he will get figures for what other towns charge and the Commissioners agreed to
