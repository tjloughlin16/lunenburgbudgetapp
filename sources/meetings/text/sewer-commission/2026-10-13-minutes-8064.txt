To accept the minutes of 9/29/26.                             Roll Call Vote: Scott-aye, Chip-aye, Dan-aye and Brett-aye. 
 
--- Sewer Business Manager/ DPW Director Updates– 
     ---Transfer of Funds from Capital Plan Line – Andrew reported that they have had a few emergencies and the 
only line item available is the Capital Plan Line. He is asking to take the full amount of $45,500 to transfer to the 
Sewer Connection Fees Line. The other line items have amounts encumbered for the rest of the year. He is able to 
move monies back and forth but right now he needs to pay Connection Charges to Leominster. This year’s budget 
was set up with only $10,000 which was too low since each connection to Leominster is $3,500. There have been 15 
connections this year and he needs to pay Leominster $24,000, in addition to the $7,000 he already paid. Fitchburg’s 
connection charge is $2,000 but most connections are made to Leominster.  When a property connects, they pay the 
connection fee of $3,500, which covers the costs and it will all balance out next year.  
Motion: Dan P.                                                                                                                                   Second: Scott D. 
To transfer $45,500 from the Capital Plan Line to the Sewer Connection Fee Line.  
Roll Call Vote: Dan-aye, Chip-aye, Scott-aye, and Brett-aye. 
 
     ---786-796 Mass Ave Connections – Andrew reported that the customer brought in a bank check for their 
Connection Fees to cover the first one that bounced. The Building Commissioner halted work on the property until 
the fees were collected and everything is ok now.   
 
     ---CTE Contract  - Andrew stated that the wording in the Contract was sent to Town Counsel a few times and the 
Contract is now with Procurement to finish and push it forward. He confirmed that Procurement is aware of the 
current contract deadline.  
 
Brett asked Andrew if they had met with Town Counsel regarding the issue of billing arrears and Andrew replied they 
had just got the schedule for Counsel hours. After they meet with him, they will have it on the agenda.  
 
---Future Agenda Items- CTE Contract  
 
PUBLIC COMMENT – Scott stated they are looking for another Commissioner and anyone interested can email 
Andrew. He added that the driving range is for sale for over $1 million and Andrew noted it was not in the sewer 
district. Dan said he believed the owner did not want it to be in the sewer district which would require payment of 
multiple betterments. If it is sold and they want to put in Sewer, they would have to go to town meeting for approval. 
Dan suggested the Commissioners need to think about whether they would support it going to Town Meeting for 
connecting to sewer. Andrew added that if they connected it to Sewer, they would need to pay betterments or they 
could do private septic instead.
