In accordance with the requirements of the Open Meeting Law, please be advised that this meeting will be 
recorded and can be found on Lunenburg Access channel on YouTube within 48 hours of the meeting. 
      
The Town of Lunenburg, in response to the COVID-19 (Corona Virus), is currently following the guidance from 
the Lunenburg Board of Health, Massachusetts Department of Public Health and the CDC regarding the virus 
and steps communities can take to prevent the spread and all town facilities closed to the public and open by 
appointment only.  In accordance with the Governor’s Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open 
Meeting Law, G. L. c.30A,§ 20, all public meetings are being conducted remotely. The following information is 
also provided for members of the public that would like to participate in the meeting remotely: To Participate 
Remotely: From a computer, please use the link below to join the public meeting webinar:           
https://us02web.zoom.us/j/83128245881 or from a telephone, dial the toll free number (888) 475-4499 and enter 
Webinar ID #: 831 2824 5881.  If you have not registered with Zoom, you will be asked for your name and an 
email address. NOTE: No participant ID is needed, just press # when asked.  Telephone users may dial *9 to 
request to speak after joining the meeting.  Computer and app users may use the “raise hand” feature to request 
to speak.
