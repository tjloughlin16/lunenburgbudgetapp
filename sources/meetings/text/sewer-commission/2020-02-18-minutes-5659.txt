Brett came forward and said that he is a local stone mason and has been in Lunenburg for 15 years and that he was
asked to consider joining the Sewer Commission. After attending a few meetings, he found it intriguing and
something he would like to learn more about. He will work with others to make the best decisions to keep the system
running smoothly.
Mr. Alonzo asked Brett if he intends to run in May. Brett said he thought he was just being appointed but he will run in
May.
Joint Vote:
Motion: Mr. Jeffreys
Second: Mr. McQuaid
To appoint Brett Ramsden to the Sewer Commission until the next annual election.
Roll Call Vote for Board of Selectmen: Mir. Jeffreys, Ms. Adams, Mr. McQuaid, Ms. Luck and Mr. Alonzo-aye
Roll Call Vote for Sewer Commission: Carl Luck, Dave MacDonald and John Reynolds - aye
Mr. Alonzo and Ms. Luck thanked Brett for volunteering.
The Joint Meeting ended at 7:57pm
