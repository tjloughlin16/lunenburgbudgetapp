for members of the public that would like to participate in the meeting remotely: To Participate Remotely: From a

telephone, dial the toll free number (888) 475-4499 and enter Webinar ID #: 896 2869 5943.  If you have not 
registered with Zoom, you will be asked for your name and an email address. NOTE: No participant ID is needed, 
just press # when asked.  Telephone users may dial *9 to request to speak after joining the meeting.  Computer 
and app users may use the “raise hand” feature to request to speak.
