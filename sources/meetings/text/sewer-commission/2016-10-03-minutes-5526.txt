Minutes of Sewer Commission Meeting on October 3th, 2016, DPW, 520 Chase Rd.

SRF Betterments - MOTION: Read by Carl Luck: MOTION FOR FINAL BETTERMENT ASSESSMENT ORDER - That
acting as Sewer Commissioners pursuant to General Laws Chapter 80 and General Laws Chapter 83, Sections 14
through 25, we order final betterments to be assessed by the uniform unit method upon those properties receiving a
special benefit from the sewer project described in an Order Assessing Final Sewer Betterments in the total amount of
$1,364,753.33, representing 97.5 units at $13,997.47 per unit, with the amount of $1,322,760.92 to be assessed as
betterments on those properties listed on the Schedule attached to said Order, and the amount of $41,992.41 to be
reserved, and collected as privilege fees in the future; that we adopt said Order and that we certify said betterment
assessment in the amount of $1,322,760.92 to the Assessors for commitment to the Collector, which amounts, if not
paid in full within the time permitted by law, will bear interest at the statutory rate of 5%
John Reynolds made the motion, Joe Anderson seconded, Passed Unanimous. Commissioners signed the Order of
Assessment. Carl will take it to the Clerk's office for notarizing. Bob will announce the change to the rate at the Selectmen
meeting on Tuesday 10/4/16
