Village
To discuss strategy with respect to litigation pursuant to G.L. c. 30A s 21(a)(3) regarding
enforcement of the Allocation Agreement between the Sewer Commission and JEG Holdings
because discussion in an open meeting may have a detrimental effect on the litigating position of
the Sewer Commission and the chair so declares.
Roll Call Vote: John R. - Yes, Joe A. - Yes, Carl L. - Yes

discussion between their counsel, Kopelman & Paige and JEH Holding counsel/client in June.

Vote: Unanimous
