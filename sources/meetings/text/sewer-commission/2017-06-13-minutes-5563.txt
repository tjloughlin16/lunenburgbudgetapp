APPOINTMENTS
6:30pm - Dan Proctor, 380 Electric Ave. Car Wash Proposal - Greg Roy from Ducharme and Dillis
presented plans for a carwash to the Sewer Commission. They proceeded to discuss the plans, water
usage and how it will connect to the sewer. They are still waiting on Leominster regarding their
application. The Commission had questions on the process and some technical details which were then
addressed. There was also a question on whether there had been a peer review. Greg R. will be giving a
revised set of plans to Jeff Walsh with Graves Engineering and will ask him to review for the Commission.
The Commission discussed with Dan the combination of lots and the easement for the pump station. The
Privilege Fee was discussed. Dan stated that it was too high and would put an end to the project. The
Sewer Commission discussed Jack's email of 6/13/17. The Commission will ask Counsel for input on
reducing the fee as a consideration. Dave Rodgers(Highland St) came forward and shared his concern
about the high cost, how it was calculated and how this will be handled going forward. Timing of the
construction was reviewed and Greg R. Will send the Sewer Commission a revised set of plans.
CURRENT BUSINESS
Election of Officers - The Commission discussed getting clarification on the charter before electing
officers. Joan P. presented an organizational flowchart to the Commission. This was not on tonight's
agenda and will be discussed by the Commission at a future meeting. Commission discussed having a
workshop to discuss roles and responsibilities within and of the Sewer Commission itself. Once this is
understood the relationship with DPW will be addressed. The Commission will have the workshop on
6/20. There was discussion on first meeting as a team before possibly inviting the Town Manager, a
Selectmen and Rob Bowen to a future meeting. They then discussed a time limit for having the delayed
elections.
