Appointment:     Hearing/Forum for the benefit of the public.       
The Lunenburg Conservation Commission, under authority granted by Massachusetts General Law Chapter 40, Section 8C, is 
revising the rules and regulations governing the use of Lunenburg land and waters under its care and control. The Conservation 
Commission will hold a public forum/hearing for considerations of revisions to commission land use policy. The supporting 
documents are available for review at the Conservation Commission office by calling 978-582-4146 ext 431 by appointment. Emailing 
a PDF is also an option on request by emailing lcc@lunenburgonline, the meeting is scheduled for April 21, 2021 at 7 PM    Public Hearings 
 
Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws a public hearing 
on a Notice of Intent by Bill Ferrebee will be held. Work proposed is the replacement of a septic system within 100-feet of a wetland 
resource area at 9 Boutwell Street. The supporting documents are available for review at the Conservation Commission office by 
calling 978-582-4147 ext 431 by appointment. 
 
Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws a public hearing 
on a Notice of Intent by Christopher and Suzanne DeGeorge will be held. Work proposed is improvement of access to Lake Shirley 
and control of erosion within 100-feet of a wetland resource area at 48 Pearl Street. 
 
Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws a public hearing 
on a Notice of Intent by MA Pleasant LLC will be held. Work proposed is construction of a six lot subdivision , Roadway, utilities and 
a proposed wetland crossing at 101 Pleasant Street
