11. Lise Fracalossi- Invasive removal/seed collection
Lise Fracalossi (Red Trillium) wants to lead an invasive species removal effort at the Lane Conservation Area.
It would be open to the community. An educational session would be held, possibly at the library followed by a
field day. J. Pewtherer noted that proper procedures must be used, orit can worsen the situation and would like
a more detailed plan.
She also wanted to collect seeds from local plants on town property to propagate her nursery stock. T. Doucette
expressed concern about allowing a single resident to collect without sharing. Would this lead to being forced to
allow anyone/everyone to collect? Suggested a 'seed swap day' or something similar. After a motion to approve
by T. Doucette, R. Bursch suggested this should be an agenda topic, so residents will be better in formed of a
decision that could benefit an individual's business. It was agreed to add the seed collection to the agenda of the
next meeting (May 6).

c. 10 Parmenter Road: NO1-26-12
Notice of Intent by Benjamin Builders for the extension of water main from Reservoir Rd to the following: Round
Rd, Parmenter Rd, Ruth St, and Fire Rd 15.
Ryan Proctor (Dillis & Roy) represented the applicant. Ali Benjamin (Benjamin Builders) was also present. Teh
water main extension will be jurisdictional in a few locations. First on Round Road and at the end of Ruth Street.
The water main will pass under the road and has been approved by the water and fire departments. An erosion
control barrier is proposed. At the fork between Round Road and Parmenter, the water main will be on the
opposite side of the road from the wetland area, again with erosion control during construction. At the next fork,
the water main will be on the same side of the road as the wetlands as the lots to be serviced are located on that
side. At the termination of the water main on Ruth Street there will be one hydrant. There are no DEP comments.
J. Pewtherer noted that the area towards the end of Round Road has an active OOC (paved to be turned into
gravel), but DPW has asked the work to be on hold due to a culvert needing repair. Coordination with DPW
would be required. R. Bursch asked why the Water Department was not the entity requesting the installation and
was told it is privately funded, but it will be a municipal line once activated. He also noted that wetland flagging
was inconsistent. H. Jaime has been out seven times and indicated the flagging was correct. R. Bursch suggested a
3r' party review or noting that wetlands delineation is not approved since all the work is under the road.
