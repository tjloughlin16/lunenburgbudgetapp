5. NEW PUBLIC HEARINGS
a. 763 Northfield Rd- NOI-25-19 Video link
A Notice of Intent by the Town of Lunenburg-DPW for the replacement of the deteriorated culvert at the
Northfield Road crossing over Mulpus Brook to mitigate further deterioration, prevent potential
flooding caused by the existing undersized culvert, and meet the Massachusetts River and Stream
Crossing Standards to the maximum extent practicable. The proposed culvert replacement is a 38-foot,
4-foot diameter precast reinforced concrete piped culvert with precast headwalls at both pipe ends. The
proposed culvert will match the existing culvert's alignment.
Matt Lundsted (Comprehensive Environmental) represented the Lunenburg DPW and described the
project. DEP file number is 208-1358. DEP wondered why only a 4-foot pipe is planned. M. Lundsted
will be responding that there is little 'head space' with little 'fluvial geomorphic advantage' to going to a
larger pipe. M. Wilson noted she did not find a wetlands delineation report. M. Lundsted said they
were attached to the paper copies dropped off and the Town should have uploaded them. J. Pewtherer
found this information in the NOI starting on page 81. DEP wanted to know if the property owner of
763 Northfield was aware of the work. It was confirmed that Jessica Santos is the owner and is on the
abutter list. Additional DEP comments were read and the proposed responses shared. J. Pewtherer
requested details on the wetlands mix plan as the notes provided are not very detailed. M. Wilson
asked for the ratio for the replication. M. Lundsted indicated 2:1. Erosion control is staked compost
filter tube at each quadrant as they conform better to this topography. M. Wilson wanted the limit of
work identified. M. Lundsted agreed to add this to the plan.
M. Wilson stated the intent is straight-forward but that we cannot give permission to work on someone
else's property as well and concern about how the 30, 50, 100 lines were drawn. This is in addition to
the limit of work concern previously noted. M. Larouche wants Herlin's review.
Motion:
M. Wilson, to continue to the October 1 meeting with the following requirements:
1. Updated delineation and explanation of delineation
2. Property owner permission or DPW Director explanation as to why it is not
needed
3. Herlin's review of the site and project
4. DEP responses
