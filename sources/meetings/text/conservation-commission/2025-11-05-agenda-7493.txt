revetment for stabilization and protection of the shoreline, which has experienced significant washout

b. 34 Pine Grove Rd – RDA-25-13 
Pursuant to MGL Ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal 
Bylaws, a public hearing on a REQUEST FOR DETERMINATION OF APPLICABILITY by Marc and Linda Chase 
for emergency repairs to a 50+ year old retaining wall on the shore of Lake Whalom.  The wall 
measures 27'-0 long and is within 10'-0 of the lake shore.  The wall is seriously damaged, and our 
consulting engineer found that it was in danger of collapsing.  The repairs will consist of placing 
concrete retaining wall blocks against the existing wall.  The work will include preparing a concrete 
base for the blocks and grading the yard behind the wall. (OpenGov.com Record ID# RDA-25-13) 
c. 32 Sunset Ave – NOI-25-14 
Pursuant to MGL Ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal 
Bylaws, a public hearing on a NOTICE OF INTENT by Robin Corbett to repair and reinforce the retaining 
wall that has eroded on our property. (OpenGov.com Record ID# NOI-25-14)

A public hearing on a Notice of Intent by the Town of Lunenburg-DPW for the replacement of the deteriorated 
culvert at the Northfield Road crossing over Mulpus Brook in order to mitigate further deterioration, prevent 
potential flooding caused by the existing undersized culvert, and meet the Massachusetts River and Stream 
Crossing Standards to the maximum extent practicable. The proposed culvert replacement is a 38-foot, 4-foot 
diameter precast reinforced concrete piped culvert with precast headwalls at both pipe ends. The proposed 
culvert will match the existing culvert’s alignment.
