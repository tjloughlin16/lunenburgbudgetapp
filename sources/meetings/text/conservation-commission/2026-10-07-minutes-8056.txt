Archery Season Notice 
Ryan Proctor informed the Commission that Massachusetts archery season for white-tailed deer opened 
October 5, 2026 and extends through January 30, 2027. He reminded users of conservation properties, 
particularly during dusk or dawn, to wear fluorescent clothing while recreating on conservation land during 
hunting season. 
Mushroom Walk Report 
Andrea Burnap reported on the recent mushroom walk held at the Lane Conservation Area in partnership 
with the Lunenburg Public Library. Approximately 67 people attended. The event was led by Jonathan 
Kranz and included a large turnout of families and children. Commission members viewed photographs 
from the event.  Ms. Burnap also expressed concern regarding dog waste observed along the trails. 
Discussion followed regarding existing regulations requiring pet owners to clean up after dogs and 
maintain control of pets. Commissioners suggested additional signage at trail entrances may be warranted 
and agreed the issue could be placed on a future agenda. 
Zoning Draft Amendments Presentation 
Planning Director Steven Wallace presented draft zoning amendments currently under review by the 
Planning Board for consideration at the 2027 Annual Town Meeting. 
The amendments include: 
· Senior Housing provisions intended to encourage additional housing options for older residents.· 
Inclusionary Zoning revisions, including affordable housing requirements and potential payment-in-lieu 
provisions. 

===PAGE 2===
   
 
· Updates to the Cluster Housing bylaw, including reducing the minimum parcel size from 25 acres to 10 
acres. 
· Creation of a Minor Site Plan Review process intended to streamline permitting for reuse of existing 
commercial and industrial buildings. 
Mr. Wallace requested comments from the Commission by the end of November and encouraged members 
to review the proposed amendments posted on the Planning Board website.No formal action was taken. 
Pollinator Habitat 
Mr. Larouche provided an update on the pollinator habitat at 123 Hollis Road. 
Discussion included: 
· Seasonal transition activities and winter preparation. 
· Draining and storing water containers for winter. 
· Planned final mowing of the season. 
· Potential pruning of a crabapple tree during the dormant season. 
· Continuing bloom activity from asters and other late-season native plants. 
 The public was reminded that the habitat remains open for visitors.

5. NEW PUBLIC HEARINGS 
a. 3 Wilderwood Avenue RDA-26-12 Request for Determination of Applicability by Matt Proctor for the 
replacement of a retaining wall. Mr. Proctor presented plans to replace an existing failing fieldstone 
retaining wall with a block retaining wall. The proposal included removal of two mature shoreline 
maples and requests for additional pine removal. 
Commission discussion focused on: 
· Necessity of tree removal. 
· Replacement tree requirements. 
· Existing shoreline erosion. 
· Stabilization of a sandy beach area. 
· Options for limiting impacts to mature trees. 
The Commission expressed support for retaining the pine trees and requested: 
· A revised plan clearly identifying vegetation to be removed. 
· A replacement planting schedule. 
· Additional details regarding shoreline stabilization and beach protection.
