a. 1625 Massachusetts Avenue: NOI-26-10 (Continued from October 7, 2026)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   
Greg Roy presented a substantially revised concept intended to reduce wetland impacts while providing 
ADA-accessible access to the observation platform. The redesign shifts access to the Mulpus Road side 
of the property, reducing direct wetland alteration and allowing floodplain mitigation to occur within the 
project area. 
Commission discussion addressed: 
· Tree removal and replacement plantings 
· Proposed parking layout 
· ADA trail design standards 
· Wildlife movement 
· Culvert sizing 
· Potential improvements to parking access 
The Commission expressed support for the revised concept and directed the applicant to proceed with 
Planning Board and Natural Heritage review while incorporating comments received. 
Motion:  M. Wilson to Support advancing the revised concept to Planning Board review.
