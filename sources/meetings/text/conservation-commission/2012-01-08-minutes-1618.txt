The proposed project is for expansion of back & side yards next to a wetlands resource. Level non-
wooded part of lot; remove all trash and hydro seed.~ Andrzej presented pictures of before and after of
the yard, basically it was a junk dump. He has cleaned up most of the trash, car parts, dolls, etc.

trees along property lines as well as have a garden in the area. JJ/Sharon Negative 3

1:30 PM ~ Pleasant & West Streets ~ CANCELLED ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the
Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public meeting on a Request
for Determination of Applicability filed by Michael Borkowski of EPG Solar LLC. The proposed project is for
installation of approximately 3.8 megawatt photovoltaic generation system with approximately 16,000
panels arranged in arrays. The project is located at Pleasant & West Streets. The hearing will be held at
7:30 PM.
Public Participation
Hickory Hills ~ Tom & Paula Bertram, Dave & Betty MacDonald. Representing Hickory Hills Lake
Association they were inquiring as to the steps necessary if they proceeded to drop the level of the
lake. The lake has a fanwort problem that it may be necessary to lower the level substantially. A
handout was given to everyone outlining the different directions that they could go to alleviate this
problem. Matt stated that an amendment to the current Order of Conditions would be all that is
necessary as it would fall under part of their lake management project.
John Martin and Jay Haueisen inquired as whether they could cut deadwood on Conservation land.
The ConCom emphatically stated no. If there was any change in the process we would let them
know.
