Town of Lunenburg
Conservation Commission
MINUTES
April 20, 2011
7:00 PM
Ritter Memorial Building
Attendees: Rob Verge, JJ Cote, Jack Byrne, John Lattanzi, Mark Murphy, Sharon Jordan, Jan Carrier
7:00 PM ~ Dan Cyr ~ Bay State Forestry ~ will talk to us about forest planning and timbering. Dan presented an
open table discussion on forestry and forest cutting. Dan is a private consultant forester. Dan is a long term
forester and has done many high profile jobs. First and foremost we need to establish a management plan
outlining boundaries, streams, etc., our goals and objectives to harvest timber. Bids should be rewritten every
10 to 15 years. Bid for Services and Proposals should be separate.
7:30 PM ~ 12 Oaks Landing ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands
Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent filed by Steve Marsden of Marsden
Engineering for Carol & Brian O'Hern. The proposed project is for construction of a retaining wall within the no-touch
zone. Steve Marsden presented. The wall is for the purpose of controlling erosion and sediment in to the lake.
It will be no more than 1 to 1½ feet high. JJ/Sharon Unanimous
7:45 PM ~ 1461 Main Street ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands
Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Request for Determination of Applicability filed by
Marie & Knute Alfredson. The proposed project is for leveling an area, within the buffer zone, where trees were
uprooted and reseed grass in same area. Marie Alfredson presented. The work will consist of removing fallen
trees and filling in holes where the stumps were uprooted from the ice storm. Negative 3 Jack/John
Unanimous
7:55 PM ~ 168 Goodrich Street ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands
Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent filed by Nathan Mahonen of
Harrigan Engineering Inc. The proposed project is for construction of a single family home with driveway and
associated utilities. Nate Mahonen presented. Nate explained the placement of the driveway and that it will
serve two houses (Lot 4 & 5). Matt suggested that the Narrative on Project Description be included in the Order
of Conditions. JJ/Jack Unanimous
8:05PM ~ 283 Cross Road ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands
Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Request for Determination of Applicability filed by
Michelle Marshall. The proposed project will entail removal of approximately six inches of existing surface and replace
with a sand/clay mixture which is more suitable for equestrian riding. No show, continued to next meeting on May
4th.
