2. A Notice of Intent by Proctor Mass Ave realty Trust for the extension of an existing paved driveway, the
construction ofa single-family dwelling and removal of vegetation within the 100ft, 50 ft and 30 ft wetland buffer
at 1523 Massachusetts Ave (OpenGov.com Record ID# NOI-25-6) (DEP # 208-1345).
Greg Roy represented the applicant. 1529 Mass Ave shares the driveway with this lot. The only work proposed
within the 30-foot no-touch zone is a small part of the extension to the current driveway to the extended driveway,
which is the only access point. A small excursion into the 30-food zone will be needed to connect to the utility
pole using an underground connection. Site walks identified 6 trees along the fence that will need to be removed
as they are physically where building walls will be erected. Also noted was a small wetlands area that has been
disturbed. The applicant has committed to restoring the area. There were no DEP comments. M. Wilson noted
that the property touches an NHESP protested area which requires a letter of notification to Mass NHSP and to
gain their approval. After discussion, M. Wilson agreed to validate the need off-line and get back to Greg if it is
indeed required.
