7:15 PM ~ 56 Brookview Terrace ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the 
Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing 
on a Request for Determination of Applicability filed by Michael Nault.  He proposed project 
is for a 10 x 20 storage shed to replace two old sheds that are or will be removed.  Mike 
Nault appeared before the commission.  He was informed of an Enforcement Order 
that has been filed regarding the stone wall, patio and wetland area.  He stated that 
the stone wall was put together using existing stone and is all of 17” high.  Mike 
stated that he will have an independent agent look at the wetlands and will have a 
new plan drawn up.   A motion was made in agreement with Mike’s plan and he is 
scheduled to appear before the commission on October 3, 2012 @ 7:45 PM.  JJ/Rich 
Unanimous 
     
7:30 PM ~ 58 Spring Street Extension ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the 
Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing 
on an amendment to the Order of Conditions (DEP #208-0989) filed by Chris MacKenzie.  
The amendment proposed is an existing deck to be moved to the Lake side of the house, 
removal of three trees for leaching area and altered site grading.   Rich/JJ    Amendment 
approved unanimously. 
 
7:45 PM ~70 Lesure Avenue ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of 
Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a 
Notice of Intent filed by Khan Mohiuddin.  The proposed project is for the demolition of an 
existing single family house within the buffer zone and a new two family home constructed 
outside the 100 foot buffer zone.  Refile of DEP#208-0837    JJ/Rich   Unanimous
