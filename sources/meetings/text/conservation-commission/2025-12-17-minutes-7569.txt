APPOINTMENTS <Video link>
a. Tom Anelons at 7:10 PM to go over plan to groom trails for mountain biking
T. Anelons presented the plans, which are the same as last year. Keeping the trails groomed for hikers takes a good deal of time. Plans are
to groom areas at least 4 inches deep without widening the trails or making new ones. J. Pewtherer mentioned the Commission wants a list
of trail names, but T. Anelons stated many trails are just color coded without names. M. Wilson suggested using the trail maps and a black
marker to note which trails he expects to groom. T. Anelons suggested that all colored trails be included.
Some new trails exist that have not been added to the map nor pre-approved by the Commission. These appear to be hiker paths used long
enough to become trails.
Any ramps used to groom the trails must also be pre-approved. Wood chips are not approved due to the possibility of bearing invasive
plants. All ramps and bridges, even on the ground, are alterations that require pre-approval.
M. Wilson and R. Bursch noted that the Commission is grateful for all volunteers. R. Bursch noted that the funding used to purchase these
lands restricts it to passive recreation. M. Wilson requested a map of all trails proposed, with GPS coordinates, for all properties, prior to
further grooming. R. Bursch requested that we continue the discussion after we have the list of trails.
