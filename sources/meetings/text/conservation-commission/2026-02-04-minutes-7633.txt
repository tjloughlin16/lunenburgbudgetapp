A Notice of Intent by SafStor Real Estate Co., LLC for a project that involves floodplain fill and associated compensatory
floodplain compensation.
Greg Roy (Dillis & Roy) represented the applicant. Peer review with Planning Board is complete. Responses to question
shave been provided but final report is still outstanding. Proposal is a 4-story indoor climate controlled public storage facility.
There would be a reduction in the impervious area on the site. He reviewed the responses to the DEP comments. There are no
wetlands or rivers associated with the proposal. Portions of the site are within the 100-year flood plain. While DEP suggested
the local Conservation Commission may require a habitat evaluation, G. Roy explained they felt it was not needed since the
existing site is paved over. The project will actually create more green spaces.
M. Larouche wondered if an integrated pest management system would be included. G. Roy will have to check as this was a
new requirement for him. There is concern that a storage area could attract rodents. T. Doucette asked if any of the trees
around the area would be removed. G. Roy felt yes but would have to check. These trees would not be in the commission's
jurisdiction.

A Notice of Intent by Steve Bean for a project that involves the demolition of an existing cottage and construction of a
replacement house and deck on the property.
