The meeting was called to order with Clerk Carl Luck residing as Chair. (members attendance above in red) 
Call to Order 
1. Meeting with Town Manager Heather Lemieux – The Commission discussed issues with the budget 
process and a $3,000 dollar potential addition for trail maintenance.  It was confirmed that the $3,000 that 
the Commission asked be included in the budget was removed before submittal to the Town Manager  
without LCC notification. It was agreed that a better process needed to be in place before the FY 19 
Budget season. Bob Pease volunteered to oversee the process next year as LCC Treasurer. There was 
some discussion on possible funding for trail work coming from the forestry program but the 
Commission explained to the Town Manger that any revenue from this program would not be available in 
FY 18. The Commission also discussed the dynamics concerning a receipts based account for fees and a 
revolving account for trail maintenance.  There were no conclusions reached.  
2. TAX TAKING: the Commission expressed interest in some of the properties that were on the list being 
reviewed by the Town/BOS. A list of properties of interest was given to the Town Manger. An updated list 
would be provided for LCC review and comment. The intention of the Town would be to sell the Liens to 
some or all of these properties. 
3. Work Session : Regulatory Review The Commission reviewed sections 10.2 to 10.5
