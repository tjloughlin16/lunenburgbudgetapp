7:00 PM ~ Public Discussion
7:15 PM ~ 611 Reservoir Road ~ Michael Gromley ~ Enforcement Order regarding activity of wall construction within 30 feet of
the high water mark, posts in the lake and excavation in the restricted no-touch zone. Mike Gromiey presented. Owned
property since 1987, belongs to the LS/C, just maintaining property. The Commission requested that he file NOI with his
plans for wall repairs, etc. Will file later, does not plan to do work until October.
7:30 PM ~ 283 Cross Road ~ Steven E./Michelle A. Marshall ~ Enforcement Order regarding illegal disposing of horse manure
near wetlands. Steve & Michelle Marshall presented. They did not realize they were in violation. The field was used as a
hayfield and manure was spread on it every year. They have now moved manure pile to another area. The Commission
requested that they file a RDA.
1:45 PM ~ 44 Peninsula Drive ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and
Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent filed by Steven Sears of David E. Ross Associates, Inc. for
Edward/Doris Kukkula. The proposed project involves raising of house #44 and installing a concrete foundation. The foundation
will be in the same footprint as the existing house, which is partially within the 100' buffer zone of Hickory Hills Lake. Dan Proctor
presented. Foundation will be five feet as house sits above grade by three feet. Will occupy same footprint as original
house. No room to move house as it is a very small (typical HH) lot. JJ/Jack Unanimous
1:55 PM ~ 647 Northfield Road ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection
and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent filed by Steven Sears of David E. Ross Associates, Inc. for
Mary Violette. The proposed project involves the replacement of a failing cesspool with a new sewage disposal system and related
grading within the 100 feet buffer zone of a bordering vegetated wetland, (BVW). Dan Proctor presented. Plans to utilize area
so as not to interfere with gardens and orchard. Jack/Sharon Unanimous
8:05PM ~ Tom Murphy from Unitil will have a presentation on maintenance and replacing static wire. Brian Waterman from
Waterman Design Associates and Tom Murphy from Unitil presented.
