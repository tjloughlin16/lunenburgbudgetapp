7:00PM PUBLIC COMMENT
Mr. Alonzo spoke and said that in wake of the tragic events that happened last Friday in Connecticut he would like to say
that his heart goes out to everyone who was affected in yet another senseless tragedy and loss of life. He went on to say
that although things like this are tough to deal with he hopes something positive comes out of it such as possibly opening
up dialogs with things such as reasonable gun control, funding of mental health services, and just the whole culture of
violence that seems to surround many parts of the country as a whole. He believes that many other industrialized nations
do not seem to have this problem and that it's not just a proliferation of guns but seems to stem from a larger cultural issue
where everything we do seems to have violence. He hopes that if anything positive comes from this tragedy, it is that we
can address these issues and "stem the tide" of them which seem to be happening much more frequently.
Mr. Matthews reminded people that there is an opening on the Planning Board and that this is a fine opportunity for
someone who wants to have a voice in the future of Lunenburg.

6. Town Manager Reports or Department Reports - Ms. Speidel suggested that the BOS make a decision at their first
neeting in January whether or not to move forward with the Borrego project. Borrego is waiting on one last piece o
information from Unitil which was due last Tuesday but was not received. This has been an ongoing problem and th
Town Attorney had contacted Borrego who said they would not be opposed if the Town chose not to move forward with
the project. Ms. Speidel stated that she was hesitant to suggest this to the BOS because Borrego has shown due diligence
on this project but it appears that this is not the best location for this project based on all of the upgrades that would need
to be made to that system. Until the Borrego project is settled, the Mass PV1 negotiations cannot be finalized:
Mr. Matthews stated that the BOS really wanted this project to go forward but due to the high cost of interconnect, it
looks like this will not be moving forward.
Ms. Speidel stated that January will be a busy month for the BOS. A work session meeting will need to be scheduled with
the Finance Committee, School Committee, and the Public Employee Health Insurance negotiating group to discuss
Health Insurance changes which would be beneficial to the Town and to the employees.
Ms. Speidel is working on setting up a workshop with the APR Program (Agricultural Preservation Restriction).
Ms. Speidel will be presenting the 5 Year Forecast which will also need to be discussed in January.
Ms. Speidel mentioned that she had emailed the BOS a copy of this years Capital Improvement Plan requests and said that
there is a complete copy of the requests in the office for the BOS to review or copies will be made available to them.
===PAGE 5===
Mr. Matthews mentioned that the CORI Policies & Procedures were discussed at the last meeting and need to be moved
on quickly. He also said that the unlit flags at Veterans Memorial Park and Upper Town Common were also a topic at
that meeting and this needs to be taken care of also.
Mr. deBettencourt questioned whether or not the new Regional Dispatch will need to submit a request to the Capital
Planning Committee for new/updated GPS devices in police cruisers. Ms. Speidel replied that it is not being proposed
right now but that it had been discussed in the past. Any dispatch, monitoring or software equipment that needs to be
obtained for the facility will be purchased by the Regional Dispatch District.
Mr. deBettencourt would like to see the new design and specifications regarding the Townsend Harbor Road and Mulpus
Road intersection. He would also like know how the traffic count numbers were generated because he thinks that the
numbers are high. Mr. deBettencourt believes that installing stop signs will slow down traffic so much as to cause a
problem with traffic jams in that area.
Ms. Speidel said that the BOS will have review again and will have the final say before anything is done with this plan.
Mr. deBettencourt mentioned that he had received a phone call from a person who has concerns about the Yield sign at
the rotary in Whalom. The caller said that the Yield sign had been moved back approximately 100° which is causing a
traffic hazard at that spot.
Ms. Speidel will investigate this matter.
