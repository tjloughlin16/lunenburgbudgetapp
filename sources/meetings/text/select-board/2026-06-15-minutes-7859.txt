Town of Lunenburg  Select Board Meeting Minutes Town Hall, 17 Main Street and via Zoom  Monday, June 15, 2026  Members Present: Chair Renee Emkey, Vice-Chair Brian Lehtinen (remote via Zoom), and Clerk Glenn McLeod Members Absent: None Also Present: Jennifer Warren-Dyment, Town Manager Public Comment Participants are listed in the minutes  CALL TO ORDER AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE Chair Renee Emkey called the meeting to order at 7:00 PM and led the Pledge of Allegiance. Chair Emkey advised those present that the meeting was being recorded and broadcast live over the Lunenburg Public Access Channel, on Facebook Live, and would be uploaded to the Lunenburg Access YouTube Channel following the meeting. She explained that the agenda listed topics that may be discussed, but not all items may be addressed and other items not listed could be brought up as permitted by open meeting law.  NEW BUSINESS Interviews With New Applicants and Applicants Seeking Re-Appointment to Boards, Committees, and Commissions  Chair Emkey noted that the sole agenda item for the evening was the annual appointments and reappointments to boards, committees, and commissions. She stated her intention to proceed alphabetically and encouraged the evening to be approached as a conversation rather than a formal interview. She asked each applicant to address whether they had observed or attended meetings of the committees of interest, whether they had spoken with current members, and to clarify their ranked preferences where multiple committees had been indicated.  Architectural Preservation District Committee (APDC)  Two seats were available due to expiring terms. Current members Nathan Lockwood and Andrew Gillis had both sought reappointment; new applicant Mali Wagner also expressed interest in the APDC. Nathan Lockwood, a long-standing member who helped draft the APDC bylaw in 2015, voluntarily stepped back from seeking reappointment upon learning of the qualifications of the incoming applicants. He noted that having "a structural engineer to my left and structural engineer to my right with passions for historical preservation" represented an ideal transition and stated he would remain available for consultation. Mali Wagner, a structural engineer and architectural designer who holds a degree from MIT's architecture department, expressed strong passion for historic preservation and the built environment. She acknowledged being new to town volunteering and stated she wished to learn before taking an active lead. She noted she also serves on several school booster organizations but confirmed that the APDC aligned most with her professional passions. Vice-Chair Brian Lehtinen expressed support for Ms. Wagner's appointment, noting her engineering background as a significant asset to the committee. Clerk Glenn McLeod agreed, noting that while residency within the Architectural Preservation District is desirable, it is not disqualifying, and that the committee can compensate through proactive community outreach. Mr. Lockwood concurred

Americans with Disabilities Committee  Town Manager Jennifer Warren-Dyment advised the Board that the Americans with Disabilities Committee had been in a largely defunct status since prior to her arrival in February 2025, with no meetings held and former members no longer serving. She characterized the appointments as a reinvigoration of the committee, noting that while the bylaws remain in place, the appointees would effectively be reconstituting the body. Richard Taylor, a lifelong Lunenburg resident attending remotely, expressed his desire to give back to his community. He noted his background in biology, experience in social work with children with autism, and that he himself has a disability, which he cited as personal motivation for his interest. After being appointed, Mr. Taylor indicated he preferred to focus solely on this one committee given the level of energy it would require to restart. Heather Gleason, a retired state employee with 28 years of residency in Lunenburg who works part-time in memory care, expressed interest in multiple committees and stated she had no strong preference among them. The Board and Town Manager identified the Americans with Disabilities Committee as a fitting placement given her background in care services. Town Manager Warren-Dyment recommended the appointment of both Richard Taylor and Heather Gleason, and the Board ratified that recommendation unanimously. Both appointees were directed to be sworn in by the Town Clerk. Motion to ratify the Town Manager's appointment of Richard Taylor and Heather Gleason to the Americans with Disabilities Committee was made by Chair Emkey and seconded by Clerk McLeod. Roll call vote: Vice-Chair Lehtinen – aye; Clerk McLeod – aye; Chair Emkey - aye. The motion passed unanimously (3–0-0).  Council on Aging  Three seats were available: one reappointment and two open vacancies. The following applicants were considered: Elena Christopher (seeking reappointment), Christopher Johnson, David Bartlett, Pauline Roy, and Heather Gleason. Elena Christopher, a resident with ties to Lunenburg dating back to 1965 and a holder of a master's degree in public administration, spoke to her two years of service on the Council on Aging, her involvement in subcommittees, and her desire to continue ongoing projects. Christopher Johnson, a 32-year Lunenburg resident and retired 47-year computer professional, described being introduced to the Adult Activity Center through the SHINE program and expressed a personal interest in the center's fitness programming, as well as a desire to be helpful to others. Pauline Roy, a 24-year resident and former Council on Aging outreach coordinator, emphasized her passion for the older adult community in Lunenburg and her familiarity with the Board, having attended and taken minutes at its meetings in the latter part of her employment. She confirmed with the Board that she had clearance from her current employer to attend daytime meetings.

Conservation Committee  Two seats were available: one expiring term held by Richard Bursch (not seeking reappointment was in question) and one open vacancy. Vincent Acito, a six-year Lunenburg resident working in HVAC for Johnson Controls and a former volunteer firefighter, presented as the primary new applicant for the committee. After discussion, it was determined that several committees Mr. Acito had indicated interest in were either elected or at full capacity. Chair Emkey and Town Manager Warren-Dyment identified the Green Communities Committee as a potentially better fit given his professional background in HVAC and energy systems. Vice-Chair Lehtinen offered to connect Mr. Acito with Green Communities Chair David Blatt. Mr. Acito expressed strong interest in pursuing that avenue and agreed to submit a formal application for Green Communities. The Board reappointed Richard Bursch to the Conservation Committee for a three-year term. The second vacancy remains open; Chair Emkey noted that the Conservation Commission Chair had indicated additional interested parties may be forthcoming. Motion to reappoint Richard Birch to the Conservation Committee for a three-year term was made by Clerk McLeod and seconded by Select Board Member Lehtinen. Roll call vote: Vice-Chair Lehtinen – aye; Clerk McLeod – aye; Chair Emkey - aye. The motion passed unanimously (3–0-0).  Cultural Council  One vacancy was presented. Green Communities Committee  One full member vacancy and two associate member vacancies were identified. Dave Passios, whose full membership term was expiring, was confirmed to have expressed willingness to continue serving. The Board agreed to reappoint Mr. Passios as a full member. Historical Commission  Two vacancies were identified: one expiring term where the incumbent is not seeking reappointment, and one pre-existing open seat. No applicants had submitted forms for the Historical Commission without having already accepted appointments to other committees. Chair Emkey noted that with these two vacancies, the commission would be at its minimum membership for quorum with only three members. The Board encouraged public interest in these open seats.  PACC  One vacancy exists on PACC. No applicants had submitted forms for this position. The vacancy remains open.  Personnel Committee  The Personnel Committee had not been active since sometime in 2024. Three applicants, David Bartlett, Pauline Roy, and Tanya Dall, were considered to reconstitute the committee, which per the town bylaw consists of five members. Tanya Dall, a Lunenburg resident employed at Harvard University's Bloomberg Center for Cities, described her role as HR liaison for her center and union representative for the Harvard Kennedy School, and noted that through her work she has supported more than 100 municipalities in talent management and public service workforce development. She is also continuing education in public policy and law. Ms. Dall expressed enthusiasm for the committee and acknowledged prior concerns

she had raised about certain bylaw provisions, noting that the committee itself could review those provisions and make recommendations to the Select Board and Town Manager in collaboration. Pauline Roy described her interest as rooted in her time as a town employee, during which she became acutely aware of the importance of the Personnel Committee for staff wellbeing and retention. She acknowledged she lacks a formal HR background but expressed strong commitment to the town's workforce. David Bartlett drew on 40 years of hotel management experience, overseeing staff of 300–400 employees in properties with up to 1,000 rooms, as the basis for his interest. Town Manager Warren-Dyment clarified for the record that the Personnel Committee does not have a role in staff discipline or individual coaching, and that the bylaw does not contemplate that function. Vice-Chair Lehtinen and Clerk McLeod both expressed strong support for all three appointees, emphasizing the diversity and complementarity of their backgrounds. Per the town bylaw, the initial members of a reconstituted committee must be staggered: one member serving a one-year term, two serving two-year terms, and two serving three-year terms. The Board assigned terms as follows: David Bartlett to a one-year term, Pauline Roy to a two-year term, and Tanya Dall to a three-year term. All terms begin upon swearing in. The Board noted that the remaining two seats on the five-member committee remain open, and encouraged the newly appointed members to recruit additional volunteers. All appointees were directed to be sworn in by the Town Clerk. Motion to appoint David Bartlett for a one-year term, Pauline Roy for a two-year term, and Tanya Dall for a three-year term to the Personnel Committee was made by Vice-Chair Lehtinen and seconded by Clerk McLeod. Roll call vote: Vice-Chair Lehtinen – aye; Clerk McLeod – aye; Chair Emkey - aye. The motion passed unanimously (3–0-0).  Remaining Vacancies At the conclusion of appointments, the following vacancies were noted as still open: ● Conservation Committee: 1 vacancy (full member) ● Historical Commission: 2 vacancies ● PACC: 1 vacancy ● Green Communities Committee: 2 associate member vacancies ● Americans with Disabilities Committee: Committee being reconstituted; quorum requires a staff member and a Select Board representative in addition to the two newly appointed members; first meeting anticipated no earlier than August. ● Cultural Council: An additional applicant was expected to come before the Board in July.
