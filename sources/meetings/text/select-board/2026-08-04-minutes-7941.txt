The certificates were circulated for signature.  • Discussion on Lunenburg Municipal Building Design Committee Meeting that was held on July 28, 2026  Chair Emkey read the three motions passed by the Municipal Building Design Committee at its most recent meeting: ● The Committee found that there is no municipal use for the Brooks House and that the Select Board may decide how best to use the property (unanimous). ● The Committee found that TC Passios in its present configuration does not work for any municipal town functions (5-1). ● The Committee stated that TC Passios may be able to support community activities, which requires additional evaluation (unanimous). Vice-Chair Lehtinen explained that the focus is on the gym and the food pantry in the southeast wing. The Committee's view was that voters should decide, at the lowest practical cost, whether to keep those functions. He said voters should also be offered a demolition 
===PAGE 5===
Select Board Meeting Minutes August 4, 2026   5 
option with a price estimate. He stated that he personally supports demolition but wants voters to have clear choices. Town Manager Warren-Dyment reported that heavy rain the previous day caused active leaking in areas of TC Passios that had not previously leaked, including the gymnasium. She stated that there is no operating budget to heat the building this winter and that services at the building will pause regardless of any decision. The roof is necessary to prevent further degradation, and any other work would likely trigger ADA compliance and other systems issues, including an oversized boiler and outdated controls. She stated that the building itself may force a decision if the roof fails. She asked the Board to consider how much to spend on outside estimators for cost estimates, noting that Town Meeting previously funded a project estimator line from free cash. She discouraged placing building articles on the September 3rd warrant, citing the existing warrant, the potential for low attendance, and the need to follow the normal capital planning process in the fall. She noted that a roof project funded in May 2027 could go out to bid no earlier than July 2027, with construction likely in fall 2027 or spring 2028. Clerk McLeod stated that voters must be educated and convinced that the Town's needs cannot be met by the current building before any demolition is approved. Vice-Chair Lehtinen agreed that demolition could take years and would be challenging. The Board directed that the Facilities Director continue developing cost estimates, including a timeline of needs versus wants and ADA implications. The Board will keep the item on upcoming agendas. The Board also discussed exploring a nonbinding or advisory article for the fall Town Meeting, such as authorizing the Select Board to prepare a demolition plan or a nonbinding article on the Brooks House. The Town Manager will consult legal counsel on an appropriate format. A posted tour of TC Passios and the Brooks House with the Facilities Director was tentatively set for Tuesday, August 11th, at 4:00 PM, pending the Facilities Director's confirmation.  OLD BUSINESS  • FY27 Select Board Goals (possible vote)  Chair Emkey proposed adding a goal on the use of opioid settlement funds. Town Manager Warren-Dyment reported that the Town has not spent any of its funds, has approximately $295,000 on hand, and is estimated to receive $722,000 in total through 2039. She noted that other communities have funded treatment, prevention, public safety supplies, regional partnerships, and local task forces, and that uses must be opioid-related. Vice-Chair Lehtinen did not believe a formal goal was necessary but wanted the topic on future agendas and wished to hear from the Police Chief. Clerk McLeod was comfortable with it as a goal or as a standing agenda topic. The Board agreed to leave it off the goals but to commit to bringing in the Police and Fire Chiefs and others to review available resources and options. Chair noted that the goals are a living document, to be shared with the new members and reviewed in January.  Motion to approve the FY27 Select Board Goals as presented was made by Vice-Chair Lehtinen and seconded by Clerk McLeod. Call for vote: Three - aye. The motion passed unanimously (3-0-0).  • September 3 Special Town Meeting  Chair Emkey reviewed the draft warrant, which currently contains Article 1, the School Committee's article with a dollar amount, and Article 2, a citizens' petition with no stated dollar amount.
