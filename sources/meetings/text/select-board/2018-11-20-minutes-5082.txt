Update from the Conservation Commission:
The following is an update from the Conservation Commission Charman Richard Bursch about a
grant the Commission applied for through DCR and were awarded to develop a Forestry for the Birds
Stewardship Plan at the Hollis Road property. There is no cost to the town for this service as a
consultant will perform the work at the cost of the
grant. Under this type of plan, the forester will initially walk the property to develop a site map with
land features and characteristics, as well as an inventory of the woodlands, over story and understory
torest growth, wetlands, streams or other items needed for the bird habitat assessment. The certified
forester will assess the forests looking for the unique characteristics needed for bird habitats. Once the
initial assessment is performed by the forester, a bird biologist from MassAudubon will review the
data and perform his own on-site assessment of the property and develop recommendations for
developing areas of the property best suited for the different migratory birds. The biologist will then
work with the forester to develop a plan based on his recommendations. Once completed, the biologist
will perform a public walk on site to explain his recommendations and how best to achieve them. He
and the forester will present the plan at a public meeting. There is no cutting involved in this plan, it is
just a review of the property by professionals and their recommendations for use of the property. The
plan must be submitted to DCR by March 1st.
Charman Richard Bursch shared these sentiments as well: "The Commission is very excited about
moving forward on this property. It currently has a pollinator habitat being developed on this property
with native shrubs and flowers planted to help attract bees, butterflies, birds and hummingbirds. The
goal of the Commission is to develop the entire property into a diverse wildlife park. They envision
adding forest walking path loops and wildlife blinds for residents to walk and enjoy the fields,
wildlife, and forests. This 60+ acre parcel will allow residents to get out and walk varying terrain
based on time, physical abilities and viewing preference all within just a few minutes from their
homes, apartments and condominiums in the Whalom area as well as all townspeople."
