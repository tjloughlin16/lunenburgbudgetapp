Town of Lunenburg Select Board Meeting Minutes Lunenburg Middle/High School Auditorium 1079 Massachusetts Avenue Tuesday, November 17, 2026  Members Present: Chair Renee Emkey, Vice-Chair Brian Lehtinen, Clerk Glenn McLeod, Member Tom Alonzo, and Member Damon McQuaid Members Absent:  Also Present: Jennifer Warren-Dyment, Town Manager  Public Comment Participants are listed in the minutes   CALL TO ORDER AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE  Chair Renee Emkey called the meeting to order at 6:30 PM.   Article 2 Amendment of the FY27 Omnibus Budget  To appropriate the remaining State aid ($53,065.00) and remaining Winter Recovery Assistance Program (WRAP) Special Revenue ($5,541.00) totaling $58,606.00.   Member Tom Alonzo moved to recommend Article 2 on the amendment of the FY27 omnibus budget. Seconded by Vice-Chair Brian Lehtinen. Call for the vote: Five-aye. The motion passed (5-0-0).  Article 3 Authorization to Pay Expenses of a Prior Fiscal Year  One bill of a prior fiscal year for Nexamp in the amount of $5,196.66  Member Alonzo moved to recommend Article 3 on bills of a prior fiscal year. Seconded by Vice-Chair Lehtinen. Call for the vote: Five-aye. The motion passed (5-0-0)  Article 9 Middle/High School Track and Field Engineering and Related Services  The Select Board deferred Article 9 to Special Town Meeting floor.   Vice-Chair Lehtinen moved to adjourn at 6:32 pm. Seconded by Member Alonzo. Call for the vote: Five-aye. The motion passed (5-0-0).  The meeting adjourned at 6:32 pm.   Respectfully Submitted, Melissa Richard Executive Assistant to the Town Manager and Select Board  These Minutes were reviewed and approved by the Select Board on: Tuesday, November 24, 2026
