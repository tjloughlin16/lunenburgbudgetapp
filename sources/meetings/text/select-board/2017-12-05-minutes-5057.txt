Ms. Lemieux announced the ratification of the collective bargaining agreement between the Lunenburg
Professional Firefighters PPFM Local 4358A and the Town. Both parties have voted to ratify the agreement,
which is for three years starting on July 1, 2017 and ending July 1, 2020.
The main provisions of the agreement are as follows:
There was a market adjustment for the fiscal year 2018 wages and a 2% cost of living increase for fiscal years
2019 and 2020. It provided for two additional paid holidays that will be consistent with the other unions and the
non- union employees. The agreement reduced the recall period for employees that were laid off from 2 years to
18 months. A new position of Firefighter/Paramedic was added to the compensation schedule. It established a
flat stipend for a member of the unit less than the rank of Captain who is assigned as the department's Fire
Prevention Officer and provided clarification on statutory updates to certain articles within the agreement.

year's budget to cover the first year of the contract.
