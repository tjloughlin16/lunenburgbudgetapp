Town of Lunenburg  Select Board Meeting Minutes Town Hall, 17 Main Street and via Zoom  Tuesday, July 14, 2026  Select Board Members Present: Chair Renee Emkey, Vice-Chair Brian Lehtinen (remote via Zoom), and Clerk Glenn McLeod Select Board Members Absent: None. SCHEDULED MEETINGS End of Year Transfers with Finance Committee  Town Manager Jennifer Warren-Dyment explained that year-end transfers are authorized during the last two months of a fiscal year and the first 15 days of the next. She presented three shortfalls to be covered: ● $10,000 for legal expenses ● $1,081.51 for the Sealer of Weights and Measures ● $7,000 for printing the town report The total was $18,081.51. The proposed funding source was the Planning Board Director's salary line, which had sufficient funds because the position was vacant for a substantial portion of the year. There were no questions.

Only the Town Manager's draft goals had been circulated at that point.  Members' priorities: Clerk McLeod cited the Department Head feedback as an excellent blueprint. His priorities were: ● reviewing the charge of the Municipal Building Design Committee ● community alignment and responsiveness ● draft a resolution supporting the voter-approved law authorizing a State Auditor audit of the Legislature ● acknowledging affordability concerns and keeping upcoming capital liabilities in view ● greater transparency that includes the lack of state funding, alongside Proposition 2½ and inflation

● familiarity with the Master Plan and its implementation  Vice-Chair Lehtinen proposed a three-to-five-year strategic plan covering financial stability, buildings, economic development, housing, public safety, parks and recreation, and community events. He also proposed increased public engagement and transparency, diversified revenue and economic development, and stronger relationships with other boards and committees. Chair Emkey described the coming year as one of "operations and processes," following last year's focus on the budget. She cited the heavy staff turnover and loss of institutional knowledge, including a split-fiscal-year payroll at year end. Her proposed goals included: ● Select Board policy review: the flag policy, appointment policy edits, the special event policy, and a timeline for reviewing the town fee schedule. ● Onboarding materials and training for board and committee members, including open meeting law, conflict of interest, and ethics training with Town Counsel. ● Staying current on minutes. ● Pre-town-meeting public information sessions on warrant articles and informational videos with Public Access. ● Board tours of municipal buildings and regular "coffee with" office hours.  T.C. Passios (TCP). The Town Manager and Chair Emkey discussed the condition of the building, including active water intrusion in the decommissioned section. The discussion covered the following points: ● Facilities Director Chris Ruth estimated roughly $120,000 per year to maintain the building and about $1,000,000 for a roof on the remaining structure. ● A prior capital request for asbestos abatement of the decommissioned portion was a little shy of $400,000. ● The capital planning cycle begins in the fall, so the decision between demolition and renovation is needed soon, or the funding timeline slips to FY29. ● The decision belongs to the Select Board rather than to Town Meeting. ● Chair Emkey stated that the land and location are of greater value than the building. ● Clerk McLeod urged input from the Municipal Building Design Committee before a decision. ● Vice-Chair Lehtinen asked to hear from Mr. Ruth. By consensus, the Board agreed to the following: ● tour the buildings in August, once a full Board is seated after the election ● request a joint meeting with the Municipal Building Design Committee in mid-to-late August for an advisory recommendation ● have Chair Emkey formally ask the committee chair to discuss and make a recommendation on demolition versus renovation ● include the evaluation of the committee's charge and composition as a goal  Chair Emkey noted that the Facilities Director favors a permanent building committee with greater staff involvement. The Town Manager advised that the Master Plan assigns responsibilities to the Board and that a goal should be to implement the Year 1 items assigned to the Select Board. Members agreed to add it as a standalone goal.  Finance and policy. Chair Emkey proposed asking a Department of Local Services representative to present on budget topics. The Town Manager reported that the Finance Committee prioritized policies on OPEB, revenue projections, stabilization and reserves, and free cash. Members agreed that a tri-board departmental budget series like last year's is not feasible. Early department budget previews were expected to begin with the Finance Committee in December, with a possible tri-board budget kickoff.  State advocacy. Clerk McLeod urged more active lobbying and transparency on state funding. Vice-Chair Lehtinen and Chair Emkey supported continued letters, resolutions, and legislative outreach as regular agenda items rather than as a formal goal. They suggested inviting legislators in the spring.  Next steps: Chair Emkey will compile the Board's goals into a single draft with timelines. The Board will revisit the draft once the full Board is seated after the election.   Town Manager FY27 Goals: Town Manager Warren-Dyment presented her draft goals, which focus on restoring and supporting core operations: ● Processes and training. She proposed written baseline procedures in each department, with first drafts due in October. She proposed three additional Munis trainings and three other staff trainings. She also proposed a cost-benefit analysis of outsourcing payroll by the third quarter of the fiscal year, digitization of payroll submissions, and a standardized employee onboarding process. ● Municipal property. She proposed winding down TCP operations, including election equipment, the food pantry, and paper files, and coordinating the Board tour. She would pursue a roof replacement or demolition depending on the Board's decision. She also proposed reasonable improvements at Town Hall and Ritter, and a follow-up goal if the 925 Massachusetts Avenue RFP is unsuccessful.

● Budgeting and finance. She proposed early budget views to the Finance Committee in December and five-to-ten-year staffing plans. She also proposed support for the Planning Director's zoning proposals, a pre-town-meeting forum, and an FY28 budget web page. Vice-Chair Lehtinen asked about adding organizational culture to the first goal. The Town Manager responded that culture issues are largely process issues and that staff are collaborating well. Clerk McLeod asked about adding a Brooks House report, and the Town Manager stated that she could delegate it to Mr. Wallace.
