TOWN MANAGER PRESENTATION OF CAPITAL PLAN
What qualifies as a Capital Project?
===PAGE 7===
• any planning, feasibility, engineering or design study related to a capital project or to a
capital improvement program consisting of individual projects.
• equipment for public improvements when they are first constructed such as
furniture, office equipment, or playground equipment;
• major equipment which is expensive and has a relatively long life such as a fire
apparatus, garbage trucks, and construction equipment.
Capital Planning Process
> The Capital Planning Committee (CPC) creates a Capital Plan that consists of all
capital requests submitted by each town department for the next ten fiscal years.
› The CPC's first meeting to begin the FY24 Capital Planning process was on August
3rd, the Capital Planning documents were sent to departments in September and the
Capital Planning Committee met with departments from late October until the end of
November.
• This year the Town Manager transitioned the forms to the same software used for
budgeting, ClearGov, and all the relevant information was submitted through this
platform.
> The data collected is based on the UMass Collins Center Capital Planning documents
that include various categories such as effectiveness of government, the effect on
operating costs, effect on revenues, whether the project is mandated by the State or
Federal government, whether grant funds are available, and how the project contributes
to such things as economic growth, public safety, public health and education. Each
category receives a rating and an overall score based on the aggregate score of all the
categories (the higher the rating, the higher the priority).
> The Capital Planning Committee evaluates capital requests based on the responses to
these categories and as a group, the CPC reviews the merits of all the projects. Each
member submits their ranking of each project and these are averaged to achieve a
composite ranking.
> The CPC prioritized 29 capital project requests for FY24 and provided the prioritized
list to the Town Manager on December 28th.
> The Town Manager may modify this list of capital projects as part of the proposed
budget for the next fiscal year. This capital budget is presented at the Annual Town
Meeting, amended as necessary to reflect the will of Town Meeting, and voted on.
> This plan reflects the same projects that the CPC provided the Town Manager with
differences in the prioritization of some of the projects.

FY 2024 Capital Plan TM Priority #1
Project Title: FD17-03 Replacement of Rescue 1
Project Cost: $506,000
Description and Justification: This project is to replace the 2014 Ambulance. Based on
maintenance costs, wear and tear on the current ambulance, and increased use, the lifespan is
projected at 10 years. The average delivery time is currently near 2 years from time of order. The
ambulance is at its reasonable lifespan, the replacement will increase efficiencies/effectiveness,
reduce potential liabilities, contributes to public safety and the public health/wellbeing of the
community.
FY2024 Capital Plan TM Priority #2
Project Title: LPS23-08 THES Exterior Door Upgrades/Replacement
Project Cost: $160,000
Description and Justification: This request is for the installation of new vestibule that will
require visitors to be buzzed in through 2 sets of doors to create enhanced, more reliable
security. Door replacement must be done before access control upgrade. Current doors are over
their reasonable lifespan, the project will reduce liabilities/threats, adds/expands customer
service, contributes to public safety and aesthetics.

security cameras at all exterior doors, other interior areas, playground. Additional motion
detectors.
This equipment is at its reasonable lifespan, the project increases effectiveness and efficiencies,
reduces potential liabilities/threats, adds/expands customer service and contributes to education
and public safety.
