Municipal Vulnerability Preparedness (MVP) Planning Grant:
The kick-off meeting with our consultant (CEI) was held on Monday, March 9th. The Land Use
Director will be sending out a poll to all the individuals/groups that our consultant has

beginning of April in order to be able to apply for the project funding, which should be released
sometime in April. After the Planning Workshop, there will be a Public Listening Session to
hear the results of the Planning Workshop.

FY21 Town Counsel Retainer:
At the end of this fiscal year we will have completed three years with Mead, Talerman and
Costa providing Town Counsel Services. During this three years, we have not seen an increase
in our retainer agreement; Mead, Talerman and Costa is proposing an amendment of
agreement with them that would increase our monthly retainer $455/month. This fee increase
is proposed to adjust for inflation since 2017 and based on their effective hourly rates which
they track on a month by month basis (the effective hourly rate is calculated by dividing the
retainer amount by the billable hours. I have asked Attorney Costa to send an amended
agreement but my recommendation is to continue services with MTC at the proposed retainer
fee that increases the average hourly rate by $10.95/hr. I have reviewed the Legal Expense
account and based on the previous year's expenses, the current year's projected expenses and
that we will only be negotiating one collective bargaining agreement next year that is billed
from our labor counsel, there are sufficient funds in that account barring any un foreseen legal
expense that would be extraordinary.
