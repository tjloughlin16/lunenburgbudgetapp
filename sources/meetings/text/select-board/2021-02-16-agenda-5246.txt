i. Action: Review the existing process step-by-step as well as any existing written policies that might pertain to the re-
appointment process. Also review the Charter for exact list of Select Board appointments.  
ii. Action: Ask for initial input from all affected appointed bodies.  Discuss improvements that might be made to 
streamline, clarify and strengthen the process. Write a draft policy and distribute to all affected appointed bodies for 
their review, suggestions and recommendations. Also make it available to the public for their input as well. 
iii. Action: Collect all input from the review cycle of the draft policy.  Finalize the reappointment process after discussing 
all the suggestions and recommendations that have been made and write the final policy for distribution.
