Clerk; Hannah Anderson, Member: John Henshaw, Member; George Martin, Member; Mare Luescher, Member
Mr. Toale rose during the Finance Committee meeting Public Comments and stated that a quorum of the
Selectmen was present and a meeting agenda had been posted. He would call the meeting to order (7:01 PM)
and any comments by Selectmen would be made at the podium. He also stated that he was not expecting any
additional deliberations and that any votes would be held until the April 3, 2018 Board of Selectmen meeting.
During the Finance Committee discussion of Warrant articles for the Annual Town Meeting, Mr. Ebersole
stated at the podium in reference to Article 24, that as a point of information, any demolition of the Town Hall
or the Old Primary School buildings would have to go before the Architectural Protection District Committee
(APDC).
During a discussion of Articles 28 & 29, Mr. Ebersole stated from the podium that the Charter Review
Committee had announced a public hearing on April 5, 2018 and that the proposed Charter may be revised as a
result of discussion at that public hearing but any revised Charter recommendation would be made available
prior to the Annual Town Meeting.
No other deliberation occurred and no votes were taken during the meeting.
Motion to Adjourn made by Mr. Ebersole and seconded by Mrs. Luck was approved unanimously and the
meeting was adjourned at 9:22 PM.
