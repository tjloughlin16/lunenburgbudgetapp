Town Facilities Charrette:
Town Manager Lemieux thanked everyone for coming. She noted Land Use Director Adam Burney would be
assisting in the facilitating of the charrette this evening. She reported the Building Reuse Committee was
formed in 2013. Their goal was to look at the reuse and future use of certain town buildings; the Town Hall,
T.C. Passios, Brooks House, Eagle House, Ritter Memorial Building and the old Primary School building. The
Building Reuse Committee dissolved earlier this year. The Board of Selectmen from that point made one of
their goals to look at an infrastructure plan for Town facilities, including buildings and land. This past August
they held their first workshop and had another one in November. We received a detailed report on Town
buildings in September. Since that time we have had many discussions on the buildings including one at the
Special Town Meeting at which seven of the twenty articles was based on Town buildings and land in one way
or another.
A charrette is a planning session where citizens, officials and other stakeholders collaborate on a vision for
development. It provides a forum for ideas and offers the unique advantage of giving immediate feedback.
Participants become included in the process for developing a vision for the final design.
Why are we doing a charrette?
• This is a way to get ideas from the public on this topic
• Increase public participation on the future use of town buildings/land
• All participants will have a voice
What do we intend to achieve?
• To build consensus around future uses of our town buildings/land

Mr. Burney moved on to a Thought Exercise and asked everyone to consider the following:
Think of a place you like to visit, either to shop, do an activity at, etc. What about it makes it inviting?
Describe what that place looks like and the features that make it a place you like to go to. He encouraged people
to sit with someone they don't know. He moved on to the talking points:
Talking Point #1
• What do you see as the future of the Town Hall, Ritter Memorial, old Primary School, the Brooks
House, and the TCP?
• Can you envision the Town Hall, Ritter Memorial, old Primary School, the Brooks House, and/or the
TCP being used for non-municipal purposes?
• What non-municipal uses would you envision in the Town Hall, Ritter Memorial, old Primary School,
the Brooks House, and/or the TCP?
Talking Point #2
• Would you like to see more town green space in the center and if so, where? Why?

• What activities would you see taking place on this green space?
Talking Point #3
• What amenities/services do you expect to receive from municipal offices?
• What does consolidating Municipal Offices under one roof look like to you?
Talking Point #4
