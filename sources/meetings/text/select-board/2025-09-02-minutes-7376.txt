PUBLIC HEARINGS AND SCHEDULED MEETINGS 7:00 PM Recognition of FY2025 Special Merit Awardees The Select Board recognized the inaugural recipients of the FY2025 Special Merit Awards, which were established at Special Town Meeting in FY2024. The following individuals were honored for their exemplary service to the Town: Communications Award: Kennedy Jackson, Police Executive Assistant  Efficiency Award: Marabeth Balboni, Library Collections and Timothy “Eric” Foster, Production Technician  Pinnacle of Excellence: Brian Gingras, Building Commissioner  The Board and Town Manager Jennifer Warren-Dyment commended the award recipients for their professionalism, dedication, and significant contributions to Town departments and the Lunenburg community. 7:10 PM Presentation by DPW Director William Bernard on Trash Bag Fees  DPW Director William Bernard presented an overview of the Town’s Pay-As-You-Throw trash program and discussed the need to consider an adjustment to trash bag fees. Mr. Bernard explained that trash removal costs have increased at a faster rate than revenues generated through bag sales, resulting in an operating shortfall in recent fiscal years. He reviewed historical and projected costs under the Town’s hauling contract, trends in bag usage and household waste generation, and several potential fee increase scenarios, including a 2%, approximately 5%, and 10% increase. Mr. Bernard discussed the anticipated impact of each option on average households and recommended a rounded increase of approximately 5% to better align revenues with expenses while minimizing impact to residents. Consider Fee Proposal The Select Board discussed the presentation, asked questions regarding program sustainability, resident usage patterns, future contract uncertainties, and comparative private disposal costs, and expressed general support for maintaining the enterprise fund model. Following discussion, the Select Board considered a proposed adjustment to the Town’s Pay-As-You-Throw trash bag fees as presented by the DPW Director. Motion by Chair Nuzzolo to approve the fee changes as included in the rounded close to 5% column, effective October 1, 2025. Seconded by Clerk Emkey. Call for the vote: Four-aye. Ratification of the Collective Bargaining Agreement with New England Police Benevolent Association, Local 353, FY26-FY28  Town Manager Warren-Dyment presented the negotiated three-year collective bargaining agreement with the New England Police Benevolent Association, Local 353, covering FY2026 through FY2028. She summarized key provisions, including annual wage increases of three percent for each fiscal year, adjustments to educational incentive percentages, and the addition of several stipends, including accreditation manager, translator, scheduling supervisor overtime, and public information officer stipends.  VIII. OLD BUSINESS Discussion on Warrant Articles to be Sponsored by the Select Board  The Board discussed potential warrant articles for the upcoming Special Town Meeting. Discussion included previously identified items such as funding for warrant postcards and a standing article related to amending the fiscal year budget, including potential Chapter 70 education funding adjustments. The Town Manager stated she planned to continue discussions with the School Superintendent prior to bringing any related proposal forward. The Board also discussed the status of a potential personnel committee bylaw change. Town Manager Warren-Dyment provided background on the current absence of a personnel committee and noted that many communities have moved away from this structure. Board members discussed the need for a replacement framework before any change, including possible Select Board policy oversight and review by the Bylaw Review Committee.  Recommendations to the Master Plan Steering Committee on Master Plan Update  The Board reviewed draft recommendations compiled from public feedback for the Master Plan update. Discussion included housing diversity, preservation of open space, senior and assisted living needs, economic development, community gathering spaces, infrastructure, environmental resilience, and municipal facilities planning. Board members discussed which recommendations fell within the Select Board's purview and which were under other boards or committees. Town Manager Warren-Dyment noted she would compile Board feedback and assist in responding to the Steering Committee.  Discussion on Tri-Board Meeting of the Select Board, Finance Committee, and School Committee  The Board discussed the upcoming Tri-Board meeting scheduled for September 30, 2025. Topics identified for discussion included public safety, DPW, facilities, and health insurance cost trends. Additional potential topics raised included the Water District and Board of Trust Commissioners. Board members noted the importance of coordination among the Tri-Board chairs when finalizing the agenda. Discussion on Acceptance of Sick Leave Bank Policy  The Board reviewed a revised Sick Leave Bank Policy, including clarifications to definitions, committee structure, eligibility, appeal process, and use of leave. Town Manager Warren-Dyment explained changes made since prior drafts and responded to questions regarding accrual, return of unused time, implementation timing, and formation of the Sick Leave Bank Committee. The Board agreed to adopt the policy but delay implementation until the committee is established and enrollment materials are prepared. Motion by Clerk Emkey to adopt the Sick Leave Bank Policy with the changes discussed, to be implemented at a later date once the Sick Leave Bank Committee is established. Seconded by Member Lehtinen. Call for the vote: Four-aye. The motion passed unanimously (4-0-0).
