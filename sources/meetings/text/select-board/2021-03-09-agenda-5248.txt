In accordance with the requirements of the Open Meeting Law, please be advised that this meeting is being recorded and 
broadcast over the Lunenburg Public Access channel.

Board of Health, Massachusetts Department of Public Health and the CDC regarding the virus and steps communities can take to 
prevent the spread and all town facilities are open by appointment only. In accordance with the Governor’s Order Suspending 
Certain Provisions of the Open Meeting Law, G. L. c. 30A, § 20, all public meetings are being conducted remotely. This meeting 
will be broadcast live through local access cable, on FacebookLive on the Public Access Facebook page 
(https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-157427270975305/) and will be able to be found on the 
LunenburgAccess YouTube channel within 24 hours after the meeting.
