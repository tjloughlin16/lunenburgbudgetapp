Ms. Luck reported the Storm Water Task Force met on April 12' to discuss a potential 319 grant application.
She will be attending a Request for Proposals meeting at the Dept. of Environmental Protection and tomorrow
morning will meet with consultant Ed Himlan. Due to DPW Director Rodriquenz not attending the SWTF
meeting we were unable to ascertain the status of our MS4 permit annual report which is due May 1s!. At that
meeting Mr. Burney brought information about the Municipal Vulnerability Preparedness Program (MVPP).
It's intended to work with communities to help them move towards nature based solutions for some of their
infrastructure specifically for stormwater and other items. It is a voluntary opt-in program where you work with
a trained provider. There are about 70 agencies and 280 trained providers. They hold charrettes or stakeholder
meetings and the community goes through what its goals are and the trained provider facilitates. In doing so you
have access to certain capital for specific projects and when you apply for other park grants, Massworks etc.,
you get an extra point towards your application score.
