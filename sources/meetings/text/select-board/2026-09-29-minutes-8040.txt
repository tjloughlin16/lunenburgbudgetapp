Town of Lunenburg Select Board Meeting Minutes Town Hall, 17 Main Street and via Zoom Tuesday, September 29, 2026  Members Present: Chair Renee Emkey, Vice-Chair Brian Lehtinen, Clerk Glenn McLeod, and Member Tom Alonzo Members Absent:  Member Damon McQuaid Also Present: Jennifer Warren-Dyment, Town Manager  Public Comment participants are listed in the minutes   CALL TO ORDER AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE  Chair Renee Emkey called the meeting to order at 7:00 PM and led the Pledge of Allegiance. She explained that the agenda listed topics that may be discussed, but not all items may be addressed and other items not listed could be brought up as permitted by open meeting law. APPOINTMENTS, RATIFICATIONS, AND RESIGNATIONS  Ratification of the Town Manager's Appointment of Ian Pereira as Full-Time Police Officer effective October 5, 2026  The agenda item was taken out of order. Pereira has served with the Groton Police Department since August 2024 and graduated from the Northern Essex Police Academy in February 2025. He holds an associate's degree in criminal justice and is certified by the Massachusetts POST Commission. Chief Thibodeau reported that his background check was very positive. NEW BUSINESS  • Discussion and Potential Action Regarding Open Meeting Law Complaint Filed by Caesar Nuzzolo on September 15, 2026  Chair Emkey summarized the complaint. It alleged that the August 18th agenda item "review select Board liaison representative assignments" did not give adequate notice of a discussion about filling a Bylaw Review Committee seat. The Board had discussed the seat as a vacancy, and Member Alonzo had volunteered, but no vote was taken. The Board later recognized that the seat was not vacant and that the committee, which is appointed by Charter, requires a vote. Members confirmed that the Select Board's appointments remain Jen Duffy and Caesar Nuzzolo. Member Alonzo noted that the error was recognized at the September 15th  meeting, before the complaint was received. Clerk McLeod stated that he had assumed a similar vacancy when he became liaison to the Council on Aging. Chair Emkey said the matter prompted her to review the language in the bylaws and policies governing Board appointments. Motion to accept the findings as drafted in the response letter, and to authorize the Chair to sign the response on behalf of the Board and submit it to the complainant and the Attorney General's Office, was made by Member Alonzo and seconded by Vice-Chair Lehtinen. Call for vote: Four - aye. The motion passed unanimously (4-0-0).  • Ratification of the Employment Agreement for Fire Chief  Town Manager Warren-Dyment announced the selection of Captain Kenneth Jones as the next Fire Chief. The contract begins upon the current chief's retirement on November 7th, 2026, and runs through June 30, 2029. The initial base salary is $136,500, with 2.5% annual increases. Board members praised Captain Jones's long service.

Town Manager Warren-Dyment explained that the November 3rd general election requires a location vote, with mailed notice to voters and postings at the sites. The registrars and the Chair discussed the TC Passios building as an option, but the Chair and Town Manager cited rapidly deteriorating conditions, including water intrusion in the gym and wet equipment earlier in the season. The Town Manager reported that Turkey Hill Elementary is unavailable because of a professional development day and that the Superintendent made the Middle-High School gymnasium or cafeteria available. The cafeteria does not require an elevator, and side doors can be propped open to shorten the walking distance. She also stated that the early voting site is not on Town property and that she asked the Town Clerk to consult the Town's insurer. Facilities staff will move the registrars' equipment. Vice-Chair Lehtinen asked that a walk-through of the cafeteria be arranged, and the Town Manager agreed to coordinate one with the Superintendent. Motion to designate the polling place at 1079 Massachusetts Avenue, also known as the Lunenburg Middle High School, in the cafeteria, contingent on finalization of the accessibility study, was made by Member Alonzo and seconded by Vice-Chair Lehtinen. Call for vote: Four - aye. The motion passed unanimously (4-0-0).  • Update on Food Pantry License Agreement  Town Manager Warren-Dyment reported that a draft modified license agreement had been received from Town Counsel. She said it would be reviewed at a meeting with the food pantry and its counsel later in the week. She stated that the agreement includes a date-certain term of about three years unless extended by the Board, and a surety provision covering site restoration, including septic pumping and discontinuation of the septic system, utility shutoff, and structure removal. The Planning Board reviewed the site plan the prior night, Zoning Board of Appeals (ZBA) approval is pending, and the Planning Board could act October 19th. The manufactured units have a four-week lead time, and installation is expected in mid-November or later. The units will sit on a crushed stone pad. Clerk McLeod expressed concern about the shift from a "temporary" use to a more permanent structure. Member Alonzo noted that the building commissioner had determined the structure is not a temporary building. He also said the use is not permitted by right in the Village District and urged that the Planning Board, ZBA, and APDC reviews be completed in the open and in good time. The Town Manager clarified that the units proposed have not changed. Chair Emkey noted that the site has nearby utilities.  • Update on Flat Hill Road Bridge Reconstruction Project  DPW Director Bill Bernard reported that contractor Kodiak Corporation will begin mobilizing on or after October 1th, when lake levels are lowered, and expects to be fully onsite October 19th. The bridge is expected to be in place by the end of January. Paving cannot occur in winter, so the road is tentatively scheduled to open May 1st, subject to weather. Town Manager Warren-Dyment added that much of the previously fabricated culvert can be reused, and that the Town's engineers will observe critical pours. A press release will follow, based on the memo in the Board packet.  • Discussion of Municipal Law Workshop
