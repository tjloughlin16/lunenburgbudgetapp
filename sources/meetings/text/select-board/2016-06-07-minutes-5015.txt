Bertram, Member, Phyllis Luck, Member
Others in attendance: Kerry Lafleur, Town Manager; Elaine Peterson, Executive Assistant
This session concerned Police union negotiations for FYs 2017, 2018, and 2019. Ms. Lafleur informed that the
union had requested an off the record meeting initially. Their first proposal was a 3/3/3% COLA increase. Their
big issue is the market; they feel they are paid below the current market. Labor counsel believes so as well. Ms.
Lafleur pointed out they only have five steps in their schedule versus eight in the other groups. Suggestion was
made to do a long-term market analysis and a comparison of benefits of comparable towns before making a
counter offer. Discussion about merit pay and annual evaluations followed.
