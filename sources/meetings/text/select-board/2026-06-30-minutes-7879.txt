Town of Lunenburg  Select Board Meeting Minutes Zoom Only Tuesday, June 30, 2026  Members Present: Chair Renee Emkey, Vice-Chair Brian Lehtinen, and Clerk Glenn McLeod Members Absent: None. Also Present: Jennifer Warren-Dyment, Town Manager Public Comment Participants are listed in the minutes  I. CALL TO ORDER Chair Renee Emkey called the meeting to order at 7:02 PM. Chair Emkey advised that the meeting was Zoom only. She explained that the agenda listed topics that may be discussed, but not all items may be addressed and other items not listed could be brought up as permitted by open meeting law.  II. NEW BUSINESS Consider Discontinuing the Use of Electronic Voting Machines for the August 8, 2026 Special Town Election The Chair advised the Board that this item would be passed over. The Chair explained that guidance received from the state confirmed that discontinuing the use of electronic voting machines is not a permissible option, and that their use is mandatory for the August 8, 2026 Special Town Election.  III. OLD BUSINESS Annual Select Board Appointments  The Chair presented the annual Select Board appointments for consideration, noting that the full list had been distributed to Board members in advance. The Chair moved to appoint the following individuals to their respective positions: • Animal Control Officer-Kathy Comeau  • Fence Viewer- Brian Gingras  • Field Driver- Kathy Comeau  • Forest Warden- Chief Patrick Sullivan  • Municipal Hearing Officer- Jennifer Warren-Dyment  • Parking Clerk (Select Board)- No past appointment- Melissa Richard for FY27  Motion to appoint the annual Select Board appointments as read was made by Chair Emkey and seconded by Vice-Chair Brian Lehtinen. Roll call vote: Clerk Glenn McLeod – Aye; Vice-Chair Lehtinen – Aye; Chair Emkey– Aye. The motion passed unanimously  (3-0-0).
