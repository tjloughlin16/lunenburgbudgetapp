VIII.  WORKSHOP:  SELECT BOARD GOAL ON THE OLD PRIMARY BUILDING 
 
To identify, define and propose at least one project to the 2021 Annual Town Meeting regarding the use and/or re-use of the Old 
Primary School building, and/or the land on which it sits (a continuation of last year’s Select Board Goal – Objective 2, with 
additional actions). 
i. Action: Have open sessions with other boards, committees and commissions as well as with the general public in discussing 
the options defined from last year’s goal. Evaluate the pros, cons and costs for each option, potential variations as well 
as possible timelines. 
ii. Action: Review exist information and documents and/or engage the appropriate professionals to create such documents that 
provide for the accurate assessment, including cost, of, at least, (a) the demolition of the existing building; (b)  removal 
of hazardous materials from the building and site; (c) conceptual rendering(s) of the property post -demolition; (d) a 
design (or designs), prepared by a landscape architect, of potential concepts for the property, specifically focusing on 
open space and a “Town Common” theme 
iii. Action:Determine one (or two, if applicable) option(s) to be fully developed into a proposed project for the building and/or 
site to be presented to the 2021 Annual Town Meeting for vote, including approximate costs, plans and necessary 
funding.
