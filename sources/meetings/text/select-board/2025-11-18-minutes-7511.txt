 Town of Lunenburg  Select Board Meeting Minutes Lunenburg Middle/High School Auditorium 1079 Massachusetts Avenue Tuesday, November 18, 2025  Members Present: Chair Caesar Nuzzolo, Vice-Chair Amanda Moore, Clerk Renee Emkey, Member Brian Lehtinen, and Member Glenn McLeod Members Absent: None  CALL TO ORDER  Chair Caesar Nuzzolo called the meeting to order at 6:30 PM.  NEW BUSINESS • Discussion on November 18, 2025 Special Town Meeting and Warrant Articles o Potential Votes on Deferred Warrant Articles  Vice-Chair Amanda Moore moved to recommend approval of Article 3: I move the town authorize the payment of prior year expenses as follows. (i) raise and appropriate the sum of $4,617.32, transfer the sum of $61,382.68 from Article 5 of the May 1, 2021 Annual Town Meeting and transfer the sum of $24,000 from Article 6 of the May 7, 2022 Annual Town Meeting. All for the payment of prior year expenses to the City of Fitchburg. Said some or sums to be reimbursed to the General Fund from the Water Enterprise Fund or the Retained Earnings thereof in future fiscal years. (ii) I move the town authorize the payment of prior year expenses as follows. One, pay the sum of $266 from the FY26 Physical line to Haywood Hospital for an unpaid bill of the prior year. two, pay the sum of $390 to Advanced Print Technology from the FY26 Facilities and Buildings- Park Department line for an unpaid bill of a prior year. And three, pay the sum of $3,500 to CBIZ from the FY26 Town Accountant line for an unpaid bill of a prior year. Seconded by Clerk Renee Emkey. Call for the vote: Five-aye. The motion passed unanimously (5-0-0).  ADJOURNMENT  Member Brian Lehtinen moved to adjourn. Seconded by Vice-Chair Moore. Call for the vote: Five-aye. The motion passed unanimously (5-0-0).  Respectfully Submitted, Renee Emkey  These Minutes were reviewed and approved by the Select Board on: Tuesday, August 4, 2026
