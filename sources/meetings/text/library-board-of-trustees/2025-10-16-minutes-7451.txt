   Staff Update 
We have hired a new Children’s Librarian. Joslyn Davis has been our Library Assistant in the Children’s

meet with the Town Manager to further discuss these plans in the next few days. 
I am working with Pauline and Deb at LAAC to submit our Emerson community grant. We will also

LUNENBURG PUBLIC LIBRARY DIRECTOR’S REPORT 
The Town’s Financial Department has been decimated lately, two resignations and a retirement, so 
payments are a bit behind. The Town Manager has made every effort to smooth payroll, but she 
does anticipate a few hiccups while payroll is being processed by a consultant. 
   Programs 
Joslyn’s Monday Around the World Storytimes have been SUPER popular, 4 session with 124 
attendees so far! Books clubs continue to gather steady interest. Tween D&D has been steady, and 
the last few concerts in our Monthly Music Series have been fantastic in quality and high in 
attendance.  
We are entering a transitional period with fewer staff available temporarily and then three parental 
leaves. The frequency of programming will drop slightly, even though we are making arrangements 
to keep our momentum carrying forward. 
Coming up: DIY Halloween Masks for Tweens/Teens 10/16, Homeschool Craft Corner 10/20, Tween 
D&D 10/22, “Murder in Rockport” Book Talk with Authors 10/22, Anniversary Closing Ceremony and 
Concert 10/24, Tech Help Drop-In with Melanie 10/25, Storytime Costume Parade 10/27, Book 
Bedazzling for Adults 10/29, Wildcrafted Apothecary Workshop 11/1, Book Sale 11/7+8, Small Works 
Exhibit Opens 11/15 
   Outreach 
Upcoming outreach: Shrieks and Krieks with The Club 10/18 (3+ staff), Literacy Night at the Primary 
School 10/22 (2 staff), LBA’s Trunk or Treat 10/25 (2 staff), 10/28 Outreach to Baker Creek apartments. 
▪ Building & Maintenance 
The building HVAC has been receiving attention as we prepare to transition to heating season.  
The rainwater garden has been planted, with one small snafu that has been remedied. Still waiting 
on the curb cuts, but native grasses are already germinating! We are making preparations for Spring 
to removed the juniper ground cover in front of the Community Wing to make way for more native 
plantings. 
▪ Technology 
Our Staff Color printer will be getting replaced in the next few days. This device is one of the oldest 
pieces of hardware in the building and has served us well. The IT department had a barely used 
surplus printer that should work for us. Town-wide, we are reducing the number of standalone 
printers to streamline costs. The library currently has 5 printers in addition to the copy machine.
