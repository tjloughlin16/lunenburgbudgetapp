LUNENBURG PUBLIC LIBRARY DIRECTOR’S REPORT 
October 15, 2026 
   FY27 Budget Update 
 
   Staff Update 
To organize our efforts during the transition to a new Assistant Director, we have a binder with 
details from the programs Lydia scheduled, so we can delegate duties to a variety of staff members. 
This arrangement will be similar to when Lydia was on leave.  
Our Financial Report was submitted and we will be submitting a petition to waive the funding 
requirement for FY27, as our appropriated budget came in just under the MAR. We have been 
discussing a partial funding of PT Staff librarian to help us with some YA services, which if funded 
would allow us to withdraw the petition. 
Our FY27 Outreach Plan is just about finalized. Melanie has put in a lot of work, so that it can reflect 
some of her passions, and some of our data gathered during the period of the initial plan. This will be 
an action focused plan and bridge the rest of FY27 until we have a new strategic plan, anticipating 
that there may be some changes in priorities as a result of that process. 
After school activities in the Teen Room have seen steady engagement, with some growing 
participation. I have counted as many as 52 Young Adults arriving in the first few minutes of the rush. 
Typically, participation in the activity of the day is between 6 and 12. A few days lately, we’ve seen 
more than 20. Button-making, 3d printing, “junk” journaling, painting have been some activities. We 
also hosted Karyn Giuliani to lead an activity last week, she guided the kids through making a 
backpack bling bar with beads (20+ participants). She was effective and enjoyed working with the 
teens. We will have her back. I have also booked a training with Christi Farrar of the MLS for the staff

I have also shared goals for the Library with the Town Manager based on staff goals and trustee

   Programs 
Some great events went on in September and early October. 72! Came to the Bluegrass concert on 
9/24! Fermented Hot Sauces on 9/23 was also a hit, lots of men attending, and 30 total attendees.  
Similarly, nearly 70 attended our Mushroom Walk with Jonathan Kranz at the Lane Property. At least 
10 children came with families and had a great time. This type of educational/informational activity 
seemed to appeal to the homeschool crowd. We will aim to bring more similar things in the future. 
Storytime has been off to quite a start, averaging over 30 attendees each meeting. Kids book groups 
have also been active and off to a strong start. Our September Blood Drive and the vaccine clinic 
both saw lots of visitors. 
Coming up: “HELLO!” student art exhibit with LMHS Art Department starting 10/20; memoir 
workshops on 10/20 and 10/22; Wills and Trusts presentation 10/21; Weight Strengthening with Niki 
continues, as does the Ukulele meetups; Storytime Costume Parade on 10/26; Felted Pumpkins craft 
for ages 8-12 on 10/26; Halloween Puzzle Race on 10/28; From Book to Screen: the Greatest Film 
Adaptations of All Time 11/5; and the Book and Bake Sale on 11/7, with Friend preview period on 11/6; 
our annual Small Works Exhibit will open 11/14. 
   Outreach 
Open House Night at the schools was very successful and helped highlight Library Card Signup 
month for us. We will plan to attend next year again. Out Library Card Signup month promo offered 
chances to win donated gifts cards to three local businesses and encouraged new card applications. 
Thanks to Smokestack Coffee Roasters, Busy Bees Play Center, and Cherry Hill Ice Cream for 
donating and for marketing the opportunity in their places of business. 
Events coming up: 10/17 Family Scare Fest at Shrieks and Krieks, where Minna, Joslyn and I will be the 
guest readers for the “haunted hayride.” LBA’s Trunk or Treat on 10/24 has been cancelled. 
▪ Building & Maintenance 
Our Bob Cole giraffe sculpture has been repaired. It is a relief to have the sculpture returned to the 
rear of the Main Hall. I have moved one picnic table from the patio to near the staff entrance and 
break room, so that it is more accessible to staff. 
▪ Technology 
The “making space” in the YA Librarian office is coming together. Once fully prepared this space will 
be bookable through our website, like study rooms.
