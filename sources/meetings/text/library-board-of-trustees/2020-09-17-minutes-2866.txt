Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes September 17, 2020 
Remote meeting via conference call and video link. All votes were done by roll call.  Meeting was recorded. 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, Maia Wentrup, 
Laura Boussom, Michael-Ray Jeffreys 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:47 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
o Harry did a roll call for attendance. 
o Harry read the requirements for remote meeting. 
 
Welcome to New Trustee 
• Harry welcomed new trustee member Laura Boussom. The BOT each gave a quick introduction of themselves.  
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o The minutes for August 20th were reviewed and accepted as amended. The minutes for the special meeting 
on September 8th were reviewed and accepted as presented.  
 
• Director’s Report 
o This is the last FY20 report. We are about $400 off from being 100% expended. 
o FY21 budget is 17% expended. The CWMARS membership fee has been paid. An annual subscription fee 
will be paid soon so the book line will be expended. There are typically a few large renewals in the 
springtime.  
o There are two staff vacancies. The search is still ongoing for the YA position. Several applicants have been 
interviewed. 
o Muir is working on the annual library report and the Financial report for state aid. Th e deadlines have been 
postponed until early October. 
o The Fall reading challenge has kicked off and is going well. There are 39 readers enrolled in the fall reading 
challenge. The adult reader’s advisory survey is also going well. 
o The Children’s book club is coming up. Storytime on the patio had very go od attendance. 
o In-person browsing has started. On 9/17 all the reservation slots were full. Curbside pickup is still ongoing 
and has been popular. 
o The library Zoom account has been upgraded. This will help with virtual programs. 
o The library has obtained funding this year for a consultant to help with strategic planning. Muir gave a short 
description of what should be in a strategic plan, some of which is required by MBLC for building grants and 
library technology programs. The plan will contain multi-year goals and objectives and an action plan will 
come out of the multi-year goals. The process should take three to six months. 
o Kate said consideration should be given to reviewing the library physical space and usage of space.  
o Harry and Dick both suggested that the BOT should be actively involved in assisting Muir with updating the 
strategic plan. 
o Harry suggested we appoint a subcommittee to work on the plan. The subcommittee could report to the full 
group during regular monthly meetings. 
o Harry asked for volunteers for the subcommittee. Laura, Maia and Dick volunteered to help. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update

o The Friends have not met. The September meeting was cancelled. They will meet again on October 5 th. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Bob asked about the board meeting in person. Michael-Ray pointed out that the town has not yet approved 
in-person meetings for town boards. They are working on plans to commence with that. 
o Michael-Ray gave an update on the town charter changes approved and signed by the Governor. The senate 
bill to allow the library to invest their trust funds is being reviewed by the Senate counsel. There should be an 
update next month. 
o Kate gave a shout-out to the library staff and especially Lydia for help with finding material and being so 
positive. 
o Maia asked how in-person browsing is going. Muir said there have been no major issues. The demand has 
been fairly low. 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick, seconded by Kate. Meeting was adjourned at 7:51 p.m.
