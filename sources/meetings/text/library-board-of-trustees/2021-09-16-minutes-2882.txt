Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes September 16, 2021 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup 
 
Absent: Muir Haman – Director LPL 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:54 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
• Harry asked that we finalize the schedule of BOT meetings. The schedule was accepted as sent out.  
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of June 17, 2021 as amended.  Seconded by Laura. The motion 
passed unanimously. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o Review of this was postponed until the October meeting since Muir was absent.  
o Kate asked about line items not expended. Harry said that some bills have not come in. 
 
• Strategic Plan Review and Vote 
o Maia noted that the deadline from the state is October 1 st. She said the committee wants the document to 
reflect the spirit of the library and be a ‘living document’. 
o There are five focus areas required by the state: mission statement, user needs assessment, goals, planning 
methodology, and sign-off from the committee. 
o Harry asked about goal number 2 on pg. 6: underserved and new populations. How will this be done? Dick 
asked if the strategic plan needs that level of specificity? Those details will emerge in the future.  
o Harry said a goal to add should be close or closer cooperation with the school system. Maia said this can be 
added under the information on children and teens. 
o Harry asked about the projected expenses for additional staffing. Said it could be clearer and should be 
before the appendices in the document. Laura said the expense estimate should show increased circulation 
numbers to bolster the argument for increased staff. 
o There was some discussion about e-books and digital media and how it relates to the future of the library in 
the digital age. How to keep the library relevant as a cultural and social center. 
o The ‘Message from the Director’ in the beginning of the document was edited a bit. 
o Some other edits were suggested – add ‘working with the school’ under the section ‘What does the library 
do?’ on pg. 14. 
o It was decided to send out a new version with tonight’s edits receive comments back by the 28 th and have a 
special Zoom meeting on the 29th to vote on approving the final version. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o There was a meeting last Saturday. The new representatives from Edward Jones attended the meeting. 
There was discussion on the allocations of the Friend’s investments which are currently 50% stocks and 50% 
bonds, earning around 8% per year. The consensus was to leave the allocation as it is.

o There was also discussion on book sale dates and whether to have it inside or out.  There was a proposal for 
four Saturdays in October but no dates have been decided. There won’t be a bake sale. 
o The pop-up book sale brought in around $150 last month. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Dick expressed his thanks for all the work done on the strategic plan.  
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick seconded by Maia. Meeting was adjourned at 8:13 p.m.
