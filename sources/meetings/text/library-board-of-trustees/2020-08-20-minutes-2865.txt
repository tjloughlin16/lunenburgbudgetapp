Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes August 20, 2020 
Remote meeting via conference call and video link. All votes were done by roll call.  Meeting was recorded. 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, Maia Wentrup, 
Michael-Ray Jeffreys 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:47 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
o Harry did a roll call for attendance. 
o Harry read the requirements for remote meeting. 
  
  
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o The July 23rd minutes were reviewed and accepted as amended. Dick made a motion to accept, seconded 
by Maia. Passed unanimously. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o All budget lines should be expended except for electricity charges. Waiting for a few invoices and vouchers 
to go through, 
o FY21 is still in the early stages. Mostly just salary expended at this time. CWMARS membership and Book 
Service fees will be due next month. Have been some delays due to a server going down.  
o The YA job has been posted. The assistant Town Manager, along with the new HR director is reviewing 
some six resumes with relevant experience. No applications have been received through MBLC but will post 
again there. The experience level has been low for the applicants thus far.  
o Summer reading – had at least 179 readers from June 26th through July 27th, about four weeks. It was about 
half the normal number but good under the circumstances. 
o Still waiting for plexiglass shields that have a useable slot at the bottom for passing books back and forth.  
o Had some server problems during July. 
o Looking to reopen for browsing on Sept. 1st. Will be two hours a day for 40-minute slots. Can handle 10 
reservations per hour. The first of those hours will be for vulnerable patrons. The front door entry will have 
hand sanitizer. Masks will be required. Any handled books will be quarantined.  
o Dick asked if we can provide gloves for patrons? Muir said that hand sanitizing should be sufficient. There 
will also be wall-mounted units for this. 
o Maia asked if curbside pickup will continue. It will still be available. Also asked about times of day for 
browsing. Browsing will be available the times when the library is normally staffed.  
o Dick asked about the Children’s area? It will be available. No age limits for children will be in place. Toys, etc. 
have been put away. Upholstered furniture has been put away and computers will not be available. There will 
be a self-checkout station in the Children’s area. 
o Muir said the library has been getting a lot of requests for home schooling materials. Debbie has put together 
some book bundles.  
o Harry asked if we can provide masks if needed? Muir said we can. State recommendation for masks is age 2 
and up.  
o Lydia is working on ‘My Next Great Read’ surveys to assist patrons in finding books. It’s a reader’s advisory 
based on what they have enjoyed in the past and uses potential matches that are currently available in the 
library.

o Debbie is working on an outdoor storytime. She is also working on a Lunenburg-wide story time on the public 
access channel. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o Dick and Harry went to the annual meeting. Said it was well attended. A new budget was approved. Some of 
the library passes were looked at carefully because of closures and loss of opportunity to attend.  
 
• Update on Potential Board Candidate 
o Kate has a candidate to recommend: Laura Boussom. Kate read a statement from Laura and made a motion 
to consider Laura for the temporary appointment. Dick seconded. Motion passed unanimously. 
o The BOT will meet with the Selectman on Sept. 8 th to process the appointment. 
• Board Comments 
o Dick: Thank you to Muir and the library staff for all the services they have provided and kept up. Harry and 
Donna both mentioned that they have used the curbside pickup and it has been great. Sherry has been very 
helpful with getting books. 
o Michael-Ray said he has been redesignated as the BOT liaison for the next year. 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick, seconded by Donna. Meeting was adjourned at 7:48 p.m.
