Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes November 16, 2023  
Attending: Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup, Jana-Lee Balish, Michael-Ray Jeffreys - BOS 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:48 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
•  Michael-Ray said he was happy to be able to attend our meeting. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of October 19, 2023 as amended, seconded by Maia. The motion 
passed unanimously. 
o Approval of executive session minutes from October 11 th were deferred until our next meeting. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o The FY23 budget is now closed out. 
o FY24 budget is 32% expended. We are in month 5 of the fiscal year. Expenditures are a bit behind schedule 
because of staff vacancies. Expenses for materials,  programs, office supplies, and library materials have 
been a bit lower this month. 
o Muir and Lydia have implemented a new time-off request procedure to help prepare for Muir’s leave. 
o Muir attended the User’s Council meeting on November 14 th. There was discussion of a proposal to increase 
fiber connection speeds without raising costs. 91% of the libraries in the network no longer collect fines. 
o There was a legislative hearing on H.3239, regarding access to electronic books. The expense for libraries is 
much more than consumer costs. Michael-Ray suggested Muir send a request to the BOS so they can send a 
notice of support for this. 
o Susan will be leaving to take a position at the Worcester library. Muir has discussed library openings with the 
town HR director and three positions will be posted, including the assistant director. 
o There are a lot of holiday programs coming up. The small works exhibit will be installed for an opening on 
November 18th. The estate planning program had a good response of 14 people. The children’s programs this 
time of year have been very successful. 
o Muir has made some good progress on the technology issues. He is still working on a second quote for a 
copier. 
o New staff workstations have been installed. Five of the thin client replacement devices have been installed 
with four more to go. 
o A capital request has been submitted for the replacement of the security cameras. Muir has quotes of $80k for 
24 cameras, $40k for 12 cameras, $35K for a lower tier of camera devices.  
o Michael-Ray said to share the plan with him and he can help Muir fine tune it. 
o Muir shared a breakdown of the requested annual budget: 
▪  The library is about 1.4% of the annual town budget. Approximately 1/3 of the $601k budget is 
dedicated to fixed salary costs. 
▪ The clerical union staff contracts were just approved last Tuesday. The contract includes an 18-
20% increase over 3 years, plus a 4-6% market adjustment. 
▪ Michael-Ray suggested breaking down the $14k requested for building maintenance into more 
detail and coordinating the budgeting with the DPW head. 
▪ There is a request for an additional $9k for programs, $20k total. Muir said program costs have 
been increasing the last few years by almost 50%. Michael-Ray suggested Muir detail a 
comparison with previous years to justify the increase. Harry pointed out that programming is very 
important to the community and community involvement.

▪ The CWMARS Network membership assessment is increasing by $3120 to $23k. 
▪ Request for office supplies increased by 50%, from $6k to $12k. Michael-Ray suggested including 
a new line item, i.e. children’s supplies. Harry said the Friends have been covering many of these 
costs, particularly supplies for the Children’s programing. 
▪ Library Materials line item has a $7500 increase, to $93k. 
▪ Training and Professional development is a new line item. $2k is requested. Michael-Ray said Muir 
should provide some specific examples with associated costs. 
▪ Technology purchase is a new line item. $14k is requested. Michael-Ray said Muir needs to 
provide some justification for this. 
▪ Muir mentioned two possible future projects: Solar panels and EV charging. Michael-Ray said 
these is a possible solar project on the site of the former town dump. There are no current town 
plans for EV charging stations. 
 
• Library Invested Funds Update 
o The funds have been pretty static. 
o Muir has stayed up to date with the reporting to the town. 
 
• Library Sign Update 
o Muir showed a sample drawing. He got an estimate of $6387 for the sign only.  
o We need to have some gooseneck lighting above the sign. 
o The general consensus was that the sign was too scrunched near the bottom of the space.  
o Maia said we should consider the entire outdoor entrance area for design. 
o Michael-Ray said we should consult with the Architecture Preservation District Commission to see if there are 
rules or guidelines we may need to conform to. 
 
• ARPA Update 
o Michael-Ray recommends that we spend the money ASAP. 
o Harry said we want to make sure the funds are spent within the required time. 
 
• Director’s Evaluation Plan/Timing 
o All the individual evaluation forms have been completed and turned in. 
o The personnel committee will meet to compile them into a single form for final review. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The book sale brought in about $1500. 
o The revival of the bake sale is still being discussed. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Dick noted that the monthly music series report was very well done. 
o Harry received some overdue notices in the mail. He said we should relook at the procedure for notifying 
patrons about overdue materials to possibly reduce postage expenses. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick. Seconded by Laura. Meeting was adjourned at 8:41 p.m.
