Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library Personnel Subcommittee Meeting
APPROVED BY Lunenburg Library Board of Trustees 5/17/12
April 25, 2012
Subcommittee Members Attending: Richard Mailloux, Jeanne Raboin, Len
Smetana
LPL Trustee Attending: Lisa Krowitz
The meeting came to order at 6:40 PM.
There was no public comment.
The purpose of the meeting was to compile the LPL Director's 2012
Evaluation using the evaluation forms submitted by the members of the
LPL Board of Trustees.
The members of the subcommittee went through the submitted forms
and tallied all of the rankings onto a master sheet. There was a question
regarding the pursuit of additional funding by the director. It was noted
that goals were needed to address section III-D and III E. There was also
a question as to what period of time was covered by the evaluation since
the board had completed the last evaluation in November of 2011. It
was decided that even though that was the case, the evaluation covered
from July 2011-June-2012.
There was discussion of a possible personnel subcommittee mtg. in
May/June to finalize the Director's evaluation for the June LPL board
meeting.
Lisa Krowitz gave input from her evaluation that hadn't been submitted
prior to the meeting.
Motion to adjourn was made by Jeanne Raboin and seconded by Len
Smetana. The meeting adjourned at 7:45 PM.
Respectfully submitted: Richard Mailloux
