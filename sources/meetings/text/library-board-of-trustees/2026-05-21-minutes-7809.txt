   FY26 Budget Update  
 
   FY27 Budget Update  
FY27 Library Budget will be $598,960.20. No overrides passed on the ballot and we will operate on 
the balanced budget that was passed at Annual Town Meeting. This is approximately a 5% decrease 
on FY26. We will not be able to hire a Young Adult Librarian. 
   Staff Update 
This week will be the return of 5th grade visits on Tuesday and Thursday. This will be the first time 
since 2020 that we’ve been able to make these visits happen. We will meet with the new THES 
administration this summer to prepare and plan for a more comprehensive set of visits next school 
year. 
Recent weeks have certainly focused on the FY27 budget and preparations for Annual Town 
Meeting, which happened May 2. This year was an approximately 8 hour meeting. The Selectboard 
offered an informal info session on the Tuesday before. This novel format did bring out some visitors, 
perhaps three dozen. All who I spoke to were staunch Library supporters. 
Main Street Bank has moved to an every other year award structure for their community support 
grants, and they chose to not fund our summer Lunch Program this year. The Friends did vote to 
provide the funds. I also met with an Assistant Vice President from Worker’s Credit Union to discuss

Child Highlights:  
APRIL BREAK: Julie Harrold returned on Tuesday to, once again, instruct a class full of focused 
faces and impressive pieces of art! Julie’s classes are always followed by demands for more. On 
Wednesday we have NEBG at Tower Hill for a filled-up terrarium program. I was surprised by 
just how cute every single terrarium turned out and heard only good things from kids and 
parents. On Thursday The Story School hosted a King Arthur inspired LARP adventure. Chris of 
The Story School is great with the kids and was able to force the kids to use their minds to solve 
puzzles as well as get out all their energy on the villainous Red Dragon with their foam swords. 
The kids were so enthused that a large group even stayed behind insisting on taking a group 
photo (featured below). Plus, I got to play a role in the story and was having just as much as all 
the kids. Overall, it was a very lively and happy April Break week!  
   
   

===PAGE 12===
 
DASHBOTS: “Robot Soccer” was a big hit (a surprise to no one). There was shockingly good 
teamwork and tons of laughs as they attempted to program their robots to score goals.  
    
 
CATERPILLARS: While it’s quite possible that the person who enjoyed the caterpillars the most 
was me... I promise the kids enjoyed it too! While I was hoping they would last just a little 
longer so I could release them on our upcoming Caterpillar Day (that I believe at the time of this 
meeting has already happened...), they managed to grow and transform quite quickly. One 
child who visited the library two days in a row almost didn’t believe me when I insisted they 
were the same caterpillars from the day before. Luckily, I was able to release them at my final 
Sounds and Music storytime for the year. The toddlers were enthralled through all 10 releases!  
   
 
TWEEN/TEEN Highlights: 
TWEEN GAME NIGHT (AGAIN!): Another success! Opening this event up to the tweens has 
proved to be a worthwhile change. Good attendance, happy faces, and plenty of new folks!
