Adult Services Department 
March 2026 
 
Summary of Last Month 
➢ The yoga and breathwork classes really took off in February! We had two full classes 
and lots of positive feedback from participants. 
➢  Our final Adults Only Make It Monthly crafting sessions were also big hits this 
month, drawing a total of 29 people to the library between the two events.  
➢ On February 13th we held a silent film screening of “Girl Shy” with live piano 
accompaniment. There were nine people in attendance, but the program was 
fantastic and something I would highly consider booking here again! Though it 
wasn’t a huge draw, the audience was engaged and the presenter was talented. 
➢ February’s Monthly Music Series concert was on February 18th featuring an evening 
of Scottish music, which was another big hit, and saw 44 people join us. 
➢ We finished the month with two puzzle events: the winter swap on February 21st and 
the puzzle race on February 25th. Both are popular annual programs that drew 55 
and 17 people respectively. Our next puzzling programs are scheduled for May.  
➢ For our virtual program offerings, there were 22 total registrations across the six 
programs held this month. Some were more popular than others, but it is 
encouraging to see the engagement rising. There are eight virtual events planned for 
March, so we will see if the trend continues or starts to dip as the year progresses.  
Recurring Program Updates 
➢ Book clubs went well in February. I informed both groups that March may be the last 
meetings I can attend before maternity leave, and we made plans for how to 
proceed until I return in June/July.   
o 100 Books Bucket List is discussing Alice's Adventures in Wonderland by 
Lewis Carroll at the next meeting on Tuesday April 7th  
o Between the Bookends is discussing The Queens of Crime by Marie Benedict 
at the next meeting on Wednesday April 8th 
➢ No significant changes to our drop-in tech help sessions with Melanie, though her 
tech-help appointments at other times of the week seem to be increasing.  
➢ The Saturday morning music clinics with Jason Graham have also been growing in 
size lately. We routinely see between 15-20 people drop-in to this program that is 
part instruction and part open jam session for players of all skill levels to improve.
