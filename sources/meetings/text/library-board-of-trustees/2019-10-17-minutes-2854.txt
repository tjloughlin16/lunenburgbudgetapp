Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes  
October 17, 2019 Sudolnik Meeting Room, Lunenburg Public Library  
  
Attending: Dick Mailloux, Maia Wentrup, Kate McCarron, Donna Saiia, Muir Ha man – Director LPL, Bob 
Conroy, Harry Kubetz., Myleen Mallari – Town Treasurer, Karen Brochu – Town Accountant, Jim 
Besarkarski – Chairman of Trust Fund Commission, Sarah Davis – Edward Jones 
Representative, Nick Davis – Edward Jones Representative 
Absent:  Lars Widstrand 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:49 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz. 
Public Comment 
 There was no public comment 
  
Agenda Items 
 Approval of Minutes 
o The minutes for September 2019 were reviewed. Motion to approve minutes was unanimously approved. 
 Trust Fund Expenditure Guidance/Process/Progress 
o Harry provided an overview of the library trust funds and the current situation. He noted the difference in 
returns between Bartholomew and Touchstone. There is approximately $46k in Bartholomew of which $11k 
is usable. The Touchstone fund has approximately $25k. 
o Some funds are principal non-expendable, i.e. the principal can’t be touched. 
o Donna noted that there is a spreadsheet available which specifies the usage. 
o Myleen: The Library trust funds are under the town tax ID even though they are not town funds. There are 
still some outstanding questions on what we can do with these funds. 
o Nick Davis asked for account statements and any available trust fund documents and volunteered to 
investigate these questions and gave us some general guidelines on what we should look for in a financial 
advisor. 
o Myleen and Muir volunteered to represent the BOT and work with Edward Jones to help answer questions.  
o Karen and Jim suggested we should meet with Bartholomew to check all options that might be available. 
 Director’s Report 
o The library is one third of the way through the year and is on schedule budget-wise. 
o We are at full staff. 
o Muir is working on a spreadsheet for the MBLC annual report. 
o Muir estimated our state aid for next year at $16,524. 
o Harry posed a question on how to measure ‘value to community’? Muir said that CWMARS has some 
calculators based on certain circulation numbers. Some discussion followed on how and what might be 
quantified. 
 Friends of LPL Update 
o Book and bake sale generated just under $2600 for the Friends. 
 Board Comments 
o Dick mentioned again that the BOT is not listed on the town website under Boards and Commissions.  
o Dick commented on the Director’s spring evaluation and raise. Noted that we should get the evaluation and 
raise into the budget upfront before the budget is done so we can build any increase into the budget.  
 Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Donna, seconded by Dick. Meeting was adjourned at 8:15pm.
