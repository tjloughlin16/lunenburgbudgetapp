1. 7:04 PM - Called to Order by Mike Mackin, Chair.
2. Approved Meeting minutes from March 28, 2012.
3. Review scoring of RES Proposals and Select Finalists: Mr. Londa shared tabulation sheets based
upon the scores provided by Committee Members and using the ranking system previously
approved by the committee. The ranked scores showed that four of the proposals had received top
scores by a clear margin. Mr. Barney made a motion that the top four firms be invited to
interview; Mr. Erickson seconded the motion. All voted in favor. Collaborative Partners, Joslin
Lesser Project Management, Dore & Whittier Management Partners, and Skanska Integrated
Solutions will be invited to interview with the School Building Committee.
4. Finalize Interview Schedule: Interviews will be conducted on October 2 and 4. Two interviews
will take place each night, with the presentation by the first firm to begin at 6:15 and the second
firm to begin at 7:30. This schedule allows 45 minutes for each presentation, 15 minutes for
Q&A, and 15 minutes after each interview for the Committee to have discussion. Mr. Londa will
put together a template for Committee members to use for note taking during the interviews. Mr.
Londa and Superintendent Calmes will begin checking references for the finalists prior to the
interviews. The finalist chosen by the committee will be determined at the October 10, 2012,
School Building Committee meeting. This allows us to stay within our planned timeframe and
present our OPM selection to the MSBA on November 4, 2012.
5. Adjournment - At 7:39 Mr. Erickson made a motion to adjourn. Mr. Barney seconded.
