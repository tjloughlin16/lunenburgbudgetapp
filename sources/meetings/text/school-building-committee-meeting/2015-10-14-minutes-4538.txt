Approval of School Building Committee Minutes
The Lunenburg School Building Committee reviewed minutes from the following
meetings:
• September 10, 2015 LSBC Meeting
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the meeting
minutes from September 10, 2015. The motion was unanimously approved.
Monthly Invoices and Commitments
Joslin Lesser (JLA-NV5) presented monthly progress invoices for work completed
through September, 2015 for Joslin Lesser - NV5 (OPM), Tappe Architects (Designer),
Shawmut Design & Construction (Payment Requisition No.14), UTS Testing Services
(September 2015).
LSB Committee asked to review testing budget and understand if the project is on
track. JLA-NV5 will review and address at the next month's meeting.
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the September,
2015 invoices as presented by Joslin, Lesser (JLA-NV5). The motion was unanimously
approved.
Monthly Approvals
Shawmut (SDC), Tappe Architects(TA) and Joslin Lesser (JLA-NV5) presented Change
Order #4 Package as well as a log with pending potential change orders.
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve Change Order #4
as presented by Shawmut, Tappe Architects and Joslin Lesser (JLA-NV5). The motion
was unanimously approved.
