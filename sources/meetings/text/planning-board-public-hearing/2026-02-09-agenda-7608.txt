PUBLIC HEARING
Monday 2026-02-09 – 6:05 P.M | 17 Main St, Lunenburg MA
1083 Northfield Rd (Scenic Road Alteration and Tree Removal)
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a joint   Public Hearing   with the Tree Warden,   in accordance with provisions of 
Massachusetts General Law Chapter 8, Sections 3 and 4, on 2026-02-09 at 6:05 p.m., Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to 
hear and review:
An application for Scenic Road Alteration, pursuant to M.G.L. Ch. 40 § 15C  by Ryan Proctor (on behalf of Tanner S Cole), for 
removal/relocation of approximately 40 linear feet of the existing stone wall along the property frontage and the Removal of 2 
White Pine Trees, 30-inch diameter each.
SUBJECT PROPERTY INFORMATION:
Location: 1083 Northfield Rd(Assessor’s Map 55 Parcel Number(s) 2).
Owner: Tanner Cole, 856 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA 01462
Objections to the removal of these trees must be received in writing prior to or at the public hearing. Please direct questions about this 
proposed removal to the Tree Warden.
