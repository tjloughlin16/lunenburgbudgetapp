PUBLIC HEARING
MONDAY, 2025-09-22 – 6:05 P.M | 17 Main St, Lunenburg MA
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a public hearing on 2025-09-22, Town Hall, 17 Main St Lunenburg MA to 
make a recommendation to Special Town Meeting (November  2025) on alterations to the Code of Town of Lunenburg 
MA Chapter 250 Zoning , to conform with the provisions of the Zoning Act, MGL c40A  Section 3A , a.k.a. The MBTA 
communities law. 
HOW TO VIEW MATERIALS:
 Via EMail request to the Planning Office (PLANNING@LUNENBURGMA.GOV)
 In-person at the Planning Office (960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA).
