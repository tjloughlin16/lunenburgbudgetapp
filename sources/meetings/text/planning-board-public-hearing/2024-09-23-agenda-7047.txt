 PUBLIC HEARING 
2024-09-23 – 6:05 P.M. 
 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing via Zoom and in-person on MONDAY, 
SEPTEMBER 23RD, 2024 at 6:05 p.m., Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to make alterations to the 
Code of the Town of Lunenburg, MA, Chapter 250 (Zoning). The changes are summarized as follows: 
 Amend § 250-2.1 (Zoning – Definitions – Terms defined, Word Usage) to insert a definition regarding 
Accessory Dwelling Unit 
 Amend § 250-3.4 (Zoning – Establishment of Districts – Two dwellings on lot) to exempt Accessory 
Dwelling Units 
 Amend § 250-4.1 (Zoning – Use Regulations – Permitted and prohibited uses) to edit section H 
subsection (1) “Accessory Dwelling” regarding criteria applicable to accessory dwellings 
 Add § 250-4.1 section H subsection (2) to delineate Accessory Dwelling Units by Special Permit from 
Accessory Dwelling Units by right 
 Amend § 250-5.2 (Zoning – Dimensional Regulations – Setbacks and yards) section E “side and rear 
yards” paragraphs (1) and (2) regarding accessory dwelling units in Residence A, Residence B and 
Outlying Districts 
 Amend § 250-4.1G (Use Table) to reflect the difference between Accessory Dwelling Units by right and 
Accessory Dwelling Units by Special Permit 
 
The complete text of the proposed amendments is available for public viewing at: 
 Planning Office (960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg MA 01462) 
 Town Clerk’s Office (17 Main St, Lunenburg MA 01462)
