PUBLIC HEARING 
MONDAY May 11, 2026 
Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on MONDAY May 11, 2026, at 6:00 pm, Town Hall, 17 Main Street, 
Lunenburg, MA to hear and review: two Stormwater Permit applications covering twelve single-family house lots at the 
following addresses: 28, 36 & 44 Parmenter Road, 2, 8, 14, 22, 28, 38, 44, 50 & 56 Ruth Street pursuant to Chapter 204 
(Stormwater and Storm Sewers) of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg. The Planning Board will also hear and review two 
shared driveway Special Permits that will serve seven single-family house lots at the following addresses: 8, 14, 22, 28, 44, 50 
& 56 Ruth Street pursuant to Chapter 250-6.4.A(7) of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg. 
 
Subject Property Information: 10 Parmenter Road, Lunenburg, MA (Assessor’s Map 136, Parcel Number 29). Applicant: 
Dillis & Roy Engineering, 1 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA, 01462. Owner: PSG Ground Road LLC, 133 Leominster Shirley Road, 
Lunenburg, MA 01462.
