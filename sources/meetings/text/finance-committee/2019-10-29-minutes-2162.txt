1. Call to Order
• Terri Burchfield, Chair called the meeting to order at 6:30pm. This
meeting is being recorded for broadcast on the Lunenburg Public Access
channel.
2. Meeting Overview
• This was an informational symposium and joint meeting including the
Lunenburg Board of Selectmen, The Lunenburg School Committee, and
The Lunenburg Finance Committee.
Terri Burchfield opened the meeting with an explanation of the purpose of
the meeting and groundrules.
Land Use Director, Adam Burney, spoke from a prepared PowerPoint
presentation, outlining the historical population and building growth of
the town, anticipated development and population growth rates (including
age-related demographies), and development opportunities.
• Superintendent, Dr. Kathleen Burnham, spoke from a prepared
Powerpoint presentation, outlining the strategic changes that are
underway in the Lunenburg School District, an assessment of existing
resources (buildings, faculty, etc) and a forecast of emerging needs over
the next 10 years.
Town Manager, Heather Lemieux, spoke from a prepared PowerPoint
presentation, outline needs and projections from all town departments
including public safety (police and fire), DPW, Library, and Council on
Aging.
