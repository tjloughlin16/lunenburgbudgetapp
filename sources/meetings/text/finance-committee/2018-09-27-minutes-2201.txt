3. Discussion regarding Free Cash. -JH Lead
John talked about new growth in the last 11 years and how it effects the town in
revenue. John says we cannot plan on new growth being a specific amount or percent
because it has a wide variance. It's not predictable or stable year to year like free cash
and the standard 2.5% levy is.
Terri asked if residential new growth includes places like Tri-Town? Yes
Terri asked if residential growth also includes building improvements on their home? Yes
Terri asked if in column 8, looking at one year at a time, i.e. 2015, the 1.4% is in there
and then the 2.5% is applied on top of that? Yes
John wanted to say there is an association between new growth and free cash, when we
budget for new growth it is about $375K but as shown in FY17 & FY18 new growth was
$488K & $481K making it a little over $100K in those years.
Karen Brochu said, if there is an increase from new growth from what was actually
estimated at Annual Town Meeting then those monies are appropriated during Special
Town Meeting in the Fall.

7. Town Manager/Financial Officer Reports
Karen Brochu- Balance sheet was submitted for the Department of Revenue today for
Free Cash Certification and she estimates it will be $1,530,000 and some change.
The town just received the growth from RRC, the company that does the towns personal
property values. Originally when estimated it was $50K but the actual is about $97K.
Based on the total estimated growth being $375K versus what we think it will be now,
there is $37,904 additional dollars available. That takes in account the personal property
component of the growth, not the real estate. There is a number from Harold and he
was firm from the real estate number so if that is the case the total new growth is $37K
higher than the $375K budgeted right now.
