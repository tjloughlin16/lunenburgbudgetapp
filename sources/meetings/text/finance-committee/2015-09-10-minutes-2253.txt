4. Committee discussion on the FY2017 budget process
a. Constraints on Setting Priorities and/or reallocating funding:
i. The annual growth of revenues (historically it ranges from 3.5% to 3.8%)
limits opportunities to pursue new initiatives.
ii. By statute, a large percentage of Town expenditures are either non-
discretionary or only partly discretionary, which further limits changes the
committee might propose.
b. Terri handed out copies of Anatomy of a Priority-Driven Budget Process, an article
published by the Government Finance Officers Association www.gfoa.org which may
help frame approaches to the Town's FY2017 budget.
i. Terri circulated an article on a priority driven budget process.
• Mark suggested we familiarize ourseives with her article for a discussion at
the September 24 meeting.
c. John has begun to look at school department expenditures over time but was the
spreadsheet was not yet ready for committee review.
