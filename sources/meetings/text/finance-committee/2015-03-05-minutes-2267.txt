5. Council on Aging (COA) FY16 Budget Presentation by Doreen Noble
A.
The COĂ provides a wide range of services to seniors-those who are 60 years old or older. These
services comprise dozens of activities, events, and programs that range from providing meals,
transportation, diet and exercise, companionship... Indeed the COA program offering touches the lives
of seniors who live in Lunenburg as well as those who live out of town.
i. The COA summited an FY16 budget of $107,214, $12,652 above FY15, and $3,404 above the
Town Manager's preliminary budget, $103,810.
ii. The COA budget amounts to about ½ of one percent of the total Town budget.
B. The main difference between the COA's budget and the preliminary budget is a $4,500 reduction in the
MOC Nutrition line from $5,000 to $500.
i. The MOC nutrition line had been increased in FY15 to improve the quality of meals at the
Eagle House Senior Center and make them less dependent on vendors. Doreen stated that
one aim had been to make salads available more often. This reduction may affect the quality
and nutritional value of the meals.
6. School Department FY16 Budget Presentation by Loxi Calmes.
While acknowledging the Town's financial realities, the budget the school department submitted aims to
recognize personnel needs.
i. Accordingly the school department recommends adding/reinstating personnel, including three
classroom teachers, 15 paraprofessionals, a behavioral specialist, and an out of district
coordinator.
•
Two of the teachers would rectify class sizes of 30 or more students in the fourth and
sixth grades in the 2015-2016 school year.
ii. The cost of all recommended personnel additions is $549,119, not including health insurance
costs.
lii. Increased costs related to school choice and special education make the revenue/expense
situation even more challenging.
B. Accordingly, the School Department submitted a FY16 budget of $18,273,840. This would be
$1,518,977 more than projected expenditures in FY15, a 9.1% increase.
i. The Town Manager's preliminaryFY16 budget for the schools is $17,398,402, which would be
an increase of $643,539 or a 3.8% increase over FY15.
For reference, the total projected increase in general fund revenue from FY15 to FY16 is
$1,149,553, 3.2%.
