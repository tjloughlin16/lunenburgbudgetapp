3. Town Manager/Town Accountant report and discussion:
Town Accountant: All transfers that needed to be done were able to be done internally with out
the 4453B authorization that requires the FinCom signatures by the Town Manager.
Karen gave an overview of the year end appropriations and expenditures.
Total revenues for FY17 were 1.07% more than what our revenue estimates were.
Some of the highlights are:
Collected 16.36% more in local receipts than estimated. One of the major increases came form
motor vehicle excise at $253,604 more than estimated
Miscellaneous non reoccurring revenues $156k more than what was budgeted. Mostly due the
water betterment's from Meadow Woods trailer park. $62,781. This is a one time occurrence.
Taxes collected are at 99.02% of the Levy. Even though its $241K less than what the Levy
estimate was, we received $96K in tax lien revenue that was not accounted for in the estimate.
State aid: Received $50,285 more than estimated. Majority of increase is Charter School tuition
On the expenditures side, we expended and or encumbered 99.49% of budgeted expenditures.
$358,084 in appropriations close outs. But we also paid $170,787 more than our State
assessment figure, of which the majority was because of Choice and Charter school tuition
adjustment for students that go out of district. After discussion with the School committee, they
made some budget adjustments to compensate for the differences in revenues and expenditures
to balance the budget at the end of the year.
The snow deficit was able to be covered.
Free cash is estimated at $800K for this year.
The Town will be holding an auction to auction off five parcels of land on August 29th 2017 for
tax title.
