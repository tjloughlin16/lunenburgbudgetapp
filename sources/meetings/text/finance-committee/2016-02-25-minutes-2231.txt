3) Department of Public Works, FY2017 Budget. Presented by Jack Rodriquenz, Director of the DPW.
Department of Public Works Fiscal 2017 Budget Presentation by Director Jack Rodriquenz.
Below is a summary of the presentation. For details you can access the presentation on the Finance
Committee Page on the Lunenburg On-line site.
Overview:
The FY17 recommended budget is $40,191, or 2.7% higher than the FY16 recap.
The budget includes one additional staff member in the highway labor account at
$35,702. This is to fill an open requisition from a couple of years ago. The increase
net of this addition is $4,489, or 0.3%.
Effects on Current Service Levels:
•
The FY17 recommended budget funds the DPW at a level sufficient to provide services
at the same level as in the past few years.
• However, there is nothing extra in the budget for increases in expenses (e.g. salt and
sand or gasoline) that are beyond the department's control.

These line items are requests for funding of some above target items in their FY17 Budget:
• $600 in materials to repair and maintain bridges with volunteers providing the labor.

Dear Mr. Rodriquenz,
Enclosed is the Tree Risk Management Inventory on an Excel spread sheet as per your agreement.
This inventory was started and completed in November 2015. All trees on the 95 plus miles of road in the
Town of Lunenburg were visually inspected, including trees leaning or hanging over the roads that may
potentially fail and fall into the right of way. One thousand one hundred eighty one (1,181) trees were
recorded on the inventory data base. This data base is listed in column form and includes location using
street name, pole numbers or house number (where we could find one). Tree species are identified using
the common tree name, tree location private or town property, estimated diameter and estimated height.
The trees are placed in the following risk categories; Extreme, High and Medium. Extreme is number one
priority 1, High is number two priority 2, and Medium number three priority 3. You will see
abbreviations where there was not enough space in the comment column: CD= Codominate stem (2
stems), MS= Multi-stems, (more them 2 stems), clumps = more than 2 separate trees in the close
proximity. The diameter of the Codominant and multi-stemmed trees were added together which is
indicated by the larger number in the estimated DBH column of the excel data. If you need additional
information please contact me.
