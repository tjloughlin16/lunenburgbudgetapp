FY 2019 Budget Presentations
2. Public Access Cable Committee (PACC) FY2019 Budget presentation by Steven Walker, Chair of the Public
Access Cable Committee
a. Income:
• Funds come from the franchise fee that Comcast pays quarterly. This fee averages slightly over
$28,000 a quarter.
• Steve projects an FY2018 ending balance in this franchise fee account of $253,500.
• PACC is in negotiations with Comcast over a renewal of the franchise fee including the increase in
that fee from 2.5% to 5% which would be in line with the franchise fee to other towns.
b. Expenses for FY19:
• Operations: $142,600 including salaries, health insurance, and lease of studio space.
• A proposal to Increase staff hours would mean higher operation costs, due in part to increased
cost of benefits.
• Capital: $50,000 - Studio improvements. replacing/upgrading equipment.
• An article on the FY19 ATM warrant requests $192,600 to meet these anticipated expenditures.
3. Monty Tech FY2019 Budget presentation by Sheila Harrity, Superintendent of Monty Tech and Tammy
Crocket, Business Manager
a. FY2019 Assessment for Lunenburg:
• This assessment is largely set by the state required minimum.
• Although the per-student cost increased by about $500 or (see point below), Lunenburg's enrollment
in Monty Tech declined by 10 students to 82 students. Thus, the Town's assessment declined from
$876,381 to $824,011, a decrease of 5.9% or $52,370. For context, Monty Tech is proposing a 3.2%
increase in its total FY19 budget.
• Monty Tech's per student assessment: FY2018 $9,525.88, FY2019: $10,048.91.

5. Unclassified Budgets: Update presented by Heather Lemieux, Town Manager
a. Health Insurance: The updated percentage growth from FY18 to FY19 is 6.82%. Originally the expected
growth was 8%.
b. The other categories comprising unclassified budget have remained the same as of now. Other budgets
within Unclassified have been level funded, except for general government.
6. Department of Public Works FY2019 Budget Recommendation Presentation by Jack Rodriquenz, Director
of the DPW
a. The Dept. of Public Works encompasses:
• Road maintenance including the pavement management program, pot hole repair, traffic signs &
devices, and snow and ice plowing & sanding/de-icing.
• Facilities and grounds
• Vehicle maintenance
• Tree Removal
• Cemetery
• Parks and Recreation.
b. Except Vehicle Maintenance all is level service funded.
c. The proposed budget for FY19 includes a recommendation to add $37,000 for an assistant mechanic to
augment the one mechanic the DPW has.
• There were reductions in DPW staff several years ago during a tight budget times. The second
mechanic position was never refilled. They have not made up the lost positions.
• The mechanic on staff is tasked with repairs and services every piece of motorized equipment
including vehicles, pumps, generators, etc. the Town has. The charge to contract repairs exceeds
$100 per hour.
• It's too much for one person to do.
