Lunenburg Finance Committee
Minutes
3/21/2013
Location:
Present:
Town Hall
M. Erickson, B. Laffond, J. Male, J. Smith, T. Burchfield, S. Raboin
1. Meeting called to order at 7:00 pm.
2. Communications
• ME read the notice inviting people interested in filling the Finance Committee vacancy to apply.
• ME noted that the public hearing will be held on 3/28/2013, after the sewer commission budget presentation, to make
recommendations regarding the budget and any warrant articles having fiscal impact.
• The Annual Town Election will be Tuesday, April 30" and the Town Meeting will be May 4th.
3. Department Budget Presentations
Shelley McCaie presented the Treasurer/Tax Collector's budget.
The new Technology Director, Steve Malandrinos discussed the current technology status and needs of the town. He feels
we are in better shape than he expected, and looks forward to working to meet the town's technology needs.
Kerry Speidel reviewed costs associated with benefits and debt service.
Loxi Jo Calmes reviewed the School budget. The School Committee will also be holding a budget hearing on April 35°
Which Will be televised. Key points included reinstating the part time Music Teacher and an administrator to manage
Student Services and Grants. Over the past few years, the district has reduced staff due to enrollment trends and budget
limitations but is concerned that the reductions cannot be sustained.
The Montachusett Regional Vocational Technical School District budget was presented.
Next Meetings
• Last department budget presentations and Public Hearing Thursday March 28".
5. TB motioned to adjourn at 9:08. BL seconded, approved 6-0.
Minutes respectfully submitted by Terri Burchfield.
