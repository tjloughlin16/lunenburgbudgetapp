*           Review Charge and Timeline; We reviewed Charge of  “To discuss and make recommendations on          
school calendar on vacations, start/end date and start and end time, and report back to school                                 
committee our findings”. 
Proposed calendar needs to be presented to school committee by December 3rd meeting, to be                               
finalized by December 2014.  Start and End times need to be submitted by March 2015. Issues discussed were 
the interest of half day and scheduling of half days.  DOE regulations on hours and days – 990 Hours as 
minimum, 180 days, with teacher contract being 183/ 184 new teachers.  3 days used for Full Day Professional 
Development, ½ days used for more PD, MCAS dates, contractual pieces that if changed would need to be re -
negotiated. 
 
Teachers commented they liked having the 2 days PD before school started, felt better prepared for students 
arrival. 
 
Question brought up about surrounding districts/towns and what their school calendar looked like.  Specifically 
start date (before or after labor day), Observation of religious Holidays, Length of December break, Second 
Semester vacations, amount of half days, PD days and End of School year date.  Looking to obtain copies from 
Westford, Fitchburg, Lancaster, Littleton, Groton, Ayer/Shirley, Leomi nster, to use for a data point in 
recommendation. 
 
Question was raised if we moved to a single vacation in second semester, would that impact our Athletic 
Departments in terms of practices and games.  Copy of Athletic Department schedule will be requested.  Us e for 
a data point in recommendation.

Question was raised regarding our town demographics.  Can we determine how many dual employed families, or 
single working families, to help determine population and their child care needs.  Use for a data point in our  
recommendation. 
 
Suggested to bring a survey to the district – parents and students.  Use Survey Monkey, Twitter, and Lunenburg 
Ledger.  3 questions to gauge communities interest in the following areas: 
 
1) do you support starting before labor day or after 
2) do you prefer an extended December break 
3) are you in favor of a single second semester vacation week. 
Other areas would be filled in around this – 4 day weekends, ending school earlier in June.  Same result is 180 
school days, just the shifting around of vacations and start and end dates. 
 
Discussed our role as Calendar Advisory Committee – we bring recommendations to the School committee on 
school beginning and ending dates, vacations and start and end times.   Noted that there is another Calendar 
committee, a faculty only committee, mandated by their teacher contract, also goes to School committee.   This 
is the first year of the Calendar Advisory Committee.
