*      Initial Calendar Recommendations:  
 
The Committee discussed the Guidelines proposed to standardizing the calendar.   
This gives a starting point each year when making the school calendar, and allows of people to know the general 
start date, vacation weeks, PD days and understand there will be no large shift year to year.  
 
Also talked about an informational piece to the calendar.  Letting people know there are 180 days, 5 snow days 
built in, however all need to be made up. 
 
Talked about consistency of working – vacations vs breaks, also having full school weeks – if we have a holiday, 
then the PD day should be that same week.. have a full week, then a 3 day week – not 2 ”4” day weeks 
 
Dr. Gary Asher made a motion to vote on approving the guidelines.  Katie McGuire second ed the motion.  
Vote: Unanimous
