Athletic Advisory Council 
Meeting Minutes 
April 5, 2021 
  
Attendees: Brian Lehtinen, Robin Bursch, Jane Gallagher, Eric Short, Kathleen Jones, Ernie 
Friend 
 
Absent:, James Bunnell, Nichole Bourdelais, Audra Sprague, Jennifer Gendron, Kim Smith, 
Colleen Shapiro, Tammy Yourk 
 
Meeting called to order at 6:32pm.   
 
Public Comment: none 
 
Review of Minutes from November 16, 2020 meeting. Minutes approved unanimously. 
 
New Business: 
 Athletic Director update: none 
 Discussion on best practices for covid fundraising: Jane Gallagher stated the bracelets 
went well and allowed them to fundraise following protocols and distancing. Eric Short 
stated fundraising was not the same but raffles were able to take place and players raised 
funds for the injured Bishop Feehan player and they had success with using Venmo to 
assist in social distancing while raising funds. Ernie Friend stated soccer had success with 
having vendors assist in social distancing in regards to shipping.  
 
Old Business: 
 Fundraising Requests:  none. Discussion took place that many teams and boosters have 
cut back on fundraising.   
 
Future Meetings: Monday, June 7, 2021, 6:30pm, prior to summer break and for fall season 
planning. Monday August 30, 2021, 6:30pm, prior to start of fall if fall sports have additional 
fundraising requests. 
 
Public Comment: There was a question on when the spring season starts and what the conclusion 
dates were. Comments were made on length of season changes, special spring season sports and 
corresponding dates, and spring season dates, as well as pod playoffs instead of MIAA state 
tournaments.
