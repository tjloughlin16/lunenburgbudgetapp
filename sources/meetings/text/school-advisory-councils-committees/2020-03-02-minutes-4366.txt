Athletic Advisory Council 
Meeting Minutes 
March 2, 2020 
  
Attendees: Brian Lehtinen, Jo Weigold, Jean Proctor, Nichole Bourdelais, Ernie Friend, James

Meeting called to order at 6:30pm.   
 
No public comment.  
 
Review of Minutes from January 6, 2020 meeting.  Not a quorum to approve. 
 
New Business: 
 Vote for New Secretary was tabled. 
 School Committee Policy 1303 on fundraising was discussed. State Law 501.3c was 
discussed for boosters as well as whether boosters have obtained a EIN, tax exempt 
number, file taxes every year. Discussion took place on insurance and protecting boards.

graders are eligible, lacrosse boys, softball, girls lacrosse, tennis is with Fitchburg. 
Friends of Baseball donated a batting cage, this Thursday, March 5th will be a community 
reading day with 10 senior athletes volunteering.  
 
Old Business: 
 Fundraising Requests: Lacrosse $10 fundraiser approved. Baseball canning fundraiser 
approved. Track selling bracelets approved. 
 One Booster Club: It was discussed that a unified booster would be important and need to 
grow over time but at this time we are looking for volunteers, possibly outside this 
committee who would like to take this on this initiative.  
 Community Service update: All high school kids should know about it and athletes 
Coach driven activities that are team building to promote service. It was discussed that all 
boosters promote team bonding local community service work that is built into the 
program in or outside the season communicated from Boosters to Coach.  
 Student Representative: no discussion. 
 Next Meetings: Monday, June 8th, off cycle, August 31st, off cycle.
