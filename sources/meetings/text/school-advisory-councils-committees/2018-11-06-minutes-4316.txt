Lunenburg Public Schools Athletic Advisory Council Meeting Minutes 
Tuesday, November 6, 2018 
 
Members Present:  (MJ, do you have this list who was there?)  
 
Meeting called to order at 6:36PM by Chair. 
 
Minutes for October 2, 2018 were accepted as presented. 
Public comment: 
 None 
New Business: 
 Scheduling activities over vacation breaks - members present discussed whether this was an 
issue. Most believed students are aware of consequences of not participating, but further 
discussion moved to next month due to lack of quorum. 
Old Business: 
 Fundraising 
o Basketball booster club requested approval of fundraising event:  Cash calendar over 2 
weeks in December (10th-21st).  It is not mandatory for any student to participate in 
sales.  Each player is requested to sell 10 calendar spots at $10 each - if more are sold, 
there's a money-grab incentive. Family cap is to in place and funds will be used for 
banquet, awards, acknowledgements.  Winners will be notified by email. - Approved 
(quorum achieved at this point) 
 
 Concession Stand 
o Dr. Burnham shared current information about the concession stand and being able to 
use it. The group discussed making the kitchen like any other kitchen in the school and 
indicated in doing so, there would be a cost/rental fee and kitchen staff would have to 
be hired (assumed at $26/hour).  Boosters felt that reasonable and requested 
Superintendent proceed with gathering information. 
 
 Community Service 
o Currently looking at Sleep in Heavenly Peace.  This is an organization that helps build 
beds for children that do not have beds. 
 
 Admission Fees 
o It was mentioned that these fees are inconsistently collected, however group didn't feel 
it necessary to discuss further. 
Next meeting-  Tuesday, December 4, 2018 at 6:30PM 
Meeting adjourned at 7:43PM
