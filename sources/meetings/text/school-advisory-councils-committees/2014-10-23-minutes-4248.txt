*          Review & Discussion of Major Events in 2015-2016 and 2016-2017 Calendars & Survey Update: 
 
Sports director advised that generally they still have practice and games during vacation weeks and Holiday 
weeks. 
 
Discuss arose that it is generally staff driven the preferences of Feb/Apr vacation week. February vacation week 
is good if we have a bad season of Flu in February, however shouldn’t be used as a reason because Flu is 
unpredictable. 
 
Brandon Kibbe brought up the issues of all the disruptions.  Interrupts learning opportunities with multiple half 
days, snow days, days off.    Staff feels that snow days (unanticipated breaks) are the only days that really disrupt 
learning. 
 
Discussion of survey - try to get as many people as possible to take the survey.  Superintendent Calmes will try 
to send the results in a spreadsheet form before our next meeting. 
 
Issue - Strong need to have school end as early in June 2016 as possible and start as late as we can in September 
due to move into new school.

Possible ideas to have PD days before first day and after last.  Could be helpful to teachers to have a few days to 
prep before students and also with having one after last day, more opportunity to clean out and move.
