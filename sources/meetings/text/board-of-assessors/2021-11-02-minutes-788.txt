Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: November 2, 2021
Members present: Chairman, Richard Letarte, Board Members, Christine Higdon and Kevin Fish and
Principal Assessor, Chris Kumar and Administrative Assessing Assistant, Sheila Craigen
Rich opened the meeting at 4:30 pm
Open Meeting:
• Rich made a motion to approve the October 5, 2021 minutes. Christine seconded the motion. All in
favor.
• Chapter Land application (61B) on Page St: Chris updated the BOA (Board of Assessors) on her
discussion and findings after speaking with the property owner. A unanimous vote was made to deny the
application.
• Chapter Land application (61A) on Flat Hill Rd: Chris presented and members reviewed new application
on Flat Hill Ra. Kevin made a motion to approve. All in favor.
• Chris explained she received a call on land (Rolling Acres) that is currently in the Chapter Land
program (61B). The property is in the process of being sold and the potential buyers intend to keep in the
Chapter Land program. Chris explained an error was made on the release in 1995. Chris explained the
process and what needs to be done. BOA members agreed and signed off on the necessary paperwork.
• Chris presented and members reviewed Exemption Applications. Ultimately, the BOA approved 2 (two
Clause 37, 2 (two Clause 17, 12 (twelve) Clause 22A-F, 2 (two) Clause 22E and 2 (two Clause 41C
applications. Note: Since the October 5, 2021 meeting, no additional applications have been denied.
• BOA reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, payroll, abatements, warrants and commitments.
Open Discussion:
• Chris answered questions on the Chapter Land program and stated she will send members an
electronic copy of the brochure from DCR (Department of Conservation & Recreation).
