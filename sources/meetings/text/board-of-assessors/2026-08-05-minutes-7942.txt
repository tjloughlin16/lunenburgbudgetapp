•Appointment with Storm Water Task Force to understand their ask of the Assessors.
 John Beale was present on behalf of the Stormwater Task Force.
John Beale had numerous questions regarding mapping issues and record cards. 
The Stormwater Task Force needs to collect information to propose a stormwater 
utility to close the gap within the Department of Public Works (DPW) budget 
shortfall.
Weston and Sampson (W&S) were hired as contractors to complete data 
collection. Sarah Cammer states the DPW Director needs to ask CAI to host a 
layer for them to obtain access.  The focus would be on impermeable area.
There was discussion about the MS4 requirements related to stormwater. Majority
of the work is completed by the DPW (surveying and cleaning catch basins, street
sweeping, testing for discharge detection, etc.).
The Stormwater Task Force anticipated a stormwater utility to operate as a Mass 
enterprise fund. Properties will be assessed and bills will be sent to property 
owners once a year.  Fee will be calculated based on impermeable area.
Below is the Information needed from the assessor database:
Property Owners Name
 Address
 Use Code
 Total Assessed Parcel Value
John Beale requests a master list or log of all properties and their owners from the
Board.  The information can be shared via a CSV file for electronic manipulation.
Brian Laffond states there is a new Principal Assessor, Brian Fernandes, who will 
be the point of contact regarding databases used by assessors going forward.
