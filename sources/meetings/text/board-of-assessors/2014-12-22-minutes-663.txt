119 ROLLING ACRES  
 
Chris MacKenzie met with the Board of behalf of the owner.  This system is in failure.  This site 
has high groundwater and a sloping lot.  The lot is served by town water.  An offset to 
groundwater is being requested.  Agent Jim Garreffi has advised that he would like the contractor 
to use a monolithic pump chamber that has cast in place rubber boots on the inlet and outlet. 
 
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Passios, the variance was approved.

60 SOUTH ROW ROAD  
 
Chris MacKenzie and Bob Proctor met with the board on behalf of the owner.  A Title 5 variance 
is required.  The appropriate notice was not mailed to the abutters.  Mr. MacKenzie gave an 
overview of the proposal.  This is a camp house on the peninsula.  This is a difficult site.  There 
is only one place to dig and it is all fill.  This hearing is continued to January 5, 2015 at 7:00 pm 
due to the lack of notification.
