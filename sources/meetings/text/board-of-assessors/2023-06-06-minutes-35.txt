Location: In person at the Lunenburg Town Hall and broadcast over the Lunenburg Public Access
Channel.
The meeting was opened at 3:00 PM.
Members Present: Principal Access CJ Carroll, Administrative Assessing Assistant Sheila Craigen,
Board Member Kevin Fish, Chairman Richard Letarte, Board Member John Rabbitt, Interim
Principal Assessor Rena Swezey.
Abatement Application Update
The last batch of abatements applications has been received. The board has been able to review
20 to 25 applications per hour. They anticipate having all abatements completed by June 15th in
order to have the tax bill in the mail on time.
The tentative schedule for remote meetings is as follows; June 7 at 2 PM, June 8th at 3 PM, and
June 15th at 3 PM.
Reorganization of the Board of Assessors
John Rabbitt nominates Richard Letarte for chair, seconded by Kevin Fish. All in favor.
Public Comment
Dave McDonald - inquires if the board is still making calls to residents. John Rabbitt states that
calls were being made only to alert people that the rest of their paperwork was received. Dave
McDonald wants to know what the process is if there is a discrepancy. CJ Carroll states that
once the tax bill is received there is a 90-day window to apply the ATB. Within those 90 days
residents can come and discuss with the board.
Ken Kohlstrom - 16 Ruth Street - had submitted a possible error on the property card with the
abatement. Inquires if that is going to be reviewed or if that is something separate. John Rabbitt
confirms that it will be viewed.
Adjournment
Motion to adjourn and enter into executive session and not return to the open meeting by CJ
Carroll, Seconded by Kevin Fish. All in favor.
The meeting adjourned at 3:20 PM.
