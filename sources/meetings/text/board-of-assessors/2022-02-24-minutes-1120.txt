Minutes from the BOA (Board of Assessors) meeting held: February 24, 2022
Members present: Chairman, Richard H. Letarte, Board Members, Christine Higdon and Kevin Fish.
Also present Principal Assessor (Interim), Rena Swezey and Administrative Assessing Assistant, Sheila
Craigen
Rich opened the meeting at 10:01 am
Open Meeting:
• Kevin made a motion to approve the January 20, 2022 minutes. Rich seconded. All in favor.
• Rena presented real estate abatement applications received to date. After much discussion, the BOA
ultimately approved 14 (fourteen) and denied 2 (two) residential applications. The BOA also reviewed
commercial applications. Ultimately 5 (five) were denied and 1 (one) approved.
• At the request of the BOA, Julie Belliveau, Assistant Town Manager/HR Director, made an appearance
to speak briefly. Julie stated an offer has been made and accepted for the position of the Principal
Assessor.
• BOA reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, abatements, warrants and commitments.
Adjournment:
• Kevin made a motion to adjourn the meeting at 11:29
am. Christine seconded, All in favor.
Respectily Syried.
Sheila Craigen
Administrative Assessing Assistant
Office of the Board of Assessors
