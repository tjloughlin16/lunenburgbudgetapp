Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: July 13, 2021
Members present: Chairman, Richard Letarte, Board Member, Christine Higdon and Principal Assessor,
Susan Byrne and Administrative Assessing Assistant, Sheila Craigen
Rich opened the meeting at 10:34 am
Open Meeting:
• Christine made a motion to keep Rich as chairman to the BOA (Board of Assessors)
• Sue stated she has been doing some research on the Elderly Exemption Clause 41C. Members
discussed and it was decided that Sue will submit an article for Special Town Meeting in the fall. The
article will request the Town to vote to adjust the income and asset limits for the Elderly Exemption
(M.G.L. Ch. 59 & 5 cl. 41C) to Single person income limit $31,500., Asset limit $52,700. Married persons
income limit $39,600., Asset limit $72,700 and to increase the exemption amount to $1,000.
Adjournment:
• Meeting adjourned at 11:15 am
