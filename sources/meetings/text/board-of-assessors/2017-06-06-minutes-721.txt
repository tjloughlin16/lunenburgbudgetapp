Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: June 6, 2017
CERK OFFICE
The meeting opened at 5:45 pm.
Present: Chairman, Louis Franco, Board Members, Rena Swezey, Richard
Letarte, Associate, Regional Assessor, Harald Scheid, and Louise Paquette, Administrative Assessor.
Board reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, abatements, warrants, commitments, May 9, 2017
minutes and payroll.
Reorganization: Lou Franco was reelected on May 20" to continue serve on the Board of Assessors for three
years. Rich motioned that Lou continue to serve as Chairman of the Board and was seconded by Rena. Lou
thanked the Board members for their support and agreed to continue serving in this capacity.
Omitted and Revised Assessment Report For FY17:
Personal Property Omitted - $0.00
Real Estate Roll-Back Tax - $0.00
Louise reported that the billing process for the FY18 preliminary real estate bills went well and the estimated
date of mailing is June 28"h
The Board reviewed and approved the FY17 Preliminary RE Commitment:
TOTAL RE PROPERTIES: 5,043
TOTAL RE PRELIM TAX: $ 12,688,379.75
Louise reported last years' "prelims" for FY16 Preliminary RE bills committed were 5035 bills totaling
$12,143,957.24.
RE & PP Contract Services: BOA voted to accept the contracts for Property Tax Assessing Services for the
period of July 1, 2017 - June 30, 2020 as follows:
Regional Resource Group, Inc. - Harald Scheid - Assessment Services
Real Estate Research Consultants, Inc. - Brian Pelletier - Personal Property Assessment Services
Louise stated that a Notice of Award will be sent upon the acceptance of the contracts by the Town Manager,
Board of Selectmen and Finance Director.
Verizon Settlement: The Board voted to abate personal property FY10 Bill # 407 in the amount of $6,891.40 as
agreed upon on 5/30/2017 Docket # C301034 and PP FY11 Bill # 399 in the amount of $7,806.41, Docket #
C306624. Abatement Notification will be sent to Verizon New England, Inc. C/O Will Hazel, Esq., Chu Ring &
Hazel LLP, 241 A Street, Suite 300, Boston, MA 02210 Louise reported that the directives in the settlement
stated that Lunenburg will have 60 days from its receipt of the Verizon NE Withdrawals to cause a check
payable to "Verizon New England, Inc." in the amount of $14,697.81 to be received by the Verizon NE's
attorneys.
Harald provided "hand-outs" (see attachments) and reported on Lunenburg's Residential Market Trend for years
2007 to 2016 & FY 2018 Single Family Ration Study for calendar year 2016. He stated that market sales within
a 10 year period had declined by 28 - 30%; however, 2016 sales are now up by 15%. He reported that there
were 160 sales in 2016 and the overall sales indicate a residential increase of up to 7%. After much discussion
and review of Harald's study report, the Board authorized an overall increase of residential assessments by 5-
6%.
Board voted to meet on Monday, July 10" at 4:00 pm. Meeting adjourned at 7:20 pm.
Respeatfully Submitted,
