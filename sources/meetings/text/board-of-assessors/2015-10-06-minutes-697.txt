Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: OCTOBER 6, 2015
The meeting opened at 5:45 pm.
Present: Chairman Louis Franco, Board Member Richard Letarte,
Board Member Matthew Papini Sr, Harald Scheid, Regional Assessor and Rebecca Boucher, Associate
Regional Assessor.
HS introduced RB to Board.
Board reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, abatements, warrants, commitments, payroll
and September 8,
minutes.
RL made a motion to approve 15 statutory exemption applications totaling $10, 100 for FY2016, the
motion was seconded by MP and it was so voted aye by all.
Board discussed their opinions of how to act on late Chapter Application filings. The Board's opinion was
not to be lenient with late filings. The Board tabled taking action on Chapter Land applications for FY2017
until next meeting
RB explained to Board that the property lookup at the Community Software Consortium had been
changed in appearance.
A Special Town Meeting will be held November 16, 2015.
Report by Assessor. HS and RB provided an update on the valuation update and New Growth
calculation. Goal is for submittal to Department of Revenue by end of October. Personal property
accounts were discussed. RL expressed to Board that he is interested in learning how to field inspect
personal property accounts, if Board was agreeable. Administrative Assessor Louise Paquette had a list
of a few accounts that need to be field listed.
There was discussion regarding date of the next meeting and it was set for November 3, 2015 at 5:45PM.
Meeting adjourned at 6:35 pm.
