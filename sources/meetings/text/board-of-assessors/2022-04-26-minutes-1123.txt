Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: Tuesday April 26, 2022
Members present: Chairman, Richard H. Letarte, Board Members, Christine Higdon and Kevin Fish and
Principal Assessor, CJ Carroll and Administrative Assessing Assistant, Sheila Craigen
Rich opened the meeting at 1:35 pm
Open Meeting:
• Kevin made a motion to approve the March 22, 2022 minutes. Christine seconded. Minutes approved.
• Cu stated he had a meeting with Heather (Town Manager) to discuss the pending ATB (Appellate Tax
Board) case with Nugen Capital Management. CJ previously filed a motion to dismiss which was denied
and the ATB will probably move forward. Some discussion on pilot agreements.
• Cu stated the town is considering a new program for Senior Tax Relief (in addition to the program
already in place). The new Tax Relief would be based on Massachusetts Senior Circuit Breaker Tax
Credit. Cu is working with Heather on this research.
• Cu stated he will be working on omitted bills soon.
• The BOA reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, payroll, abatements, warrants and
commitments.
Open Discussion:
• Some discussion on future meetings. It was decided the BOA will try to meet the first Wednesday of
every month. Afternoons are preferred.
Adjournment:
Meeting adjourned at 3:00 pm.
Sheila Craigen
