LUNENBURG BOARD OF ASSESSORS  
NEIGHBORHOOD AND SITE INDEX WORKING GROUP, ZOOM MEETING 
Meeting Date: Tuesday, October 15, 2024 
  
Meeting Minutes  
Attendees:  Sarah Cammer BOA Lead 
  Ana Lockwood, Resident at Large 
  Carl Luck, Resident at Large 
Not present: Rena Swezey, Outside Assessor/Contractor  
Jackie Favreau, Resident/Realtor member 
 
The meeting was called to order at 6:30. There was no board or public comment. 
The working group discussed the need to review neighborhoods 0-5. Sarah Cammer shared a 
visualization of the parcels with the indexes listed 0-5 and reported the board was concerned with 
identifying parcels that were not yet in the tax role, and discerning what the site indexes seemed to 
stand for based on site locations and how they were coded, as well as trying to re-evaluate site index 3, 
which was a large group of parcels. The group decided they needed to first clarify what the 
neighborhood classification appeared to be based on.  
The working group reviewed parcels and identified descriptors of the neighborhoods based on the 
location and the parcel information. The working group confirmed that there were neighborhoods listed 
0-5, and hypothesized that the neighborhood characteristics seemed to be:  
0-Land held in common, public and 
private 
1,1a,1b-Lake 
2-Newer colonials buil t in the 90’s 
3-default 
4-Lake access but not lake adjacent 
5- Homes near very busy roads   
Based on discussions with the board of assessors, Sarah Cammer reported that 0’s also could contain 
some properties that may contain parcels that should be on the tax roles. The working group desired to 
examine a spreadsheet of the properties to attempt to classify properties in 0’s. They divided the list to 
share the work to do separately prior to their next meeting. 
There were no public board comment or comments from the public. A motion to adjourn was made at 
7:30, accepted unanimously.
