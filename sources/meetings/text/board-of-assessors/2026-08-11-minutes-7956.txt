TOWN OF LUNENBURG
BOARD OF ASSESSORS
Minutes, August 11, 2026
Executive Session
Executive Session opened at 5:00p.m.
Attending the executive session were the Chair, Brian Laffond, Board members Sarah 
Cammer and Bryant Moscon.  Also present were Robin Nano and Karen Barrett.
A Clause 18 application was discussed. The Board decided more confirming asset and 
income information is required before we can reach a finding on the application. We 
will send a letter to the applicant asking them to provide:
1.) a copy of their 2025 Federal Income Tax Return,
2.) copies of current bank statements and
3.) copies of investment statements.
The Board would also like to understand the “rental income” source, does the applicant 
own another property or is it from the “in-law” apartment that was permitted a few 
years ago?
The Board reviewed an application for Clause 61B at 801 Lancaster Avenue.  Motion to 
approve, Sarah Cammer, seconded, Bryant Moscon.  All in favor.
2026 Motor Vehicle commitments #3 for $161,787.08 and #4 for 119,769.72 were 
approved and signed.
A total of 58 Real Estate exemptions were reviewed.
A total of 3 Clause 37A exemptions were approved.
A total of 8 Clause 41D exemptions were approved.
A total of 17 Clause 22 exemptions were approved; 3 required additional review.
A total of 7 Clause 22D exemptions were approved.
A total of 20 Clause 22E exemptions were approved.
The Board discussed the situation at 255 Sunny Hill Road (includes 0 Leominster Road 
and 0 Sunny Hill Road).  Based on the series of events stretching back several years, the 
Board recommends having the Finance Director hire a forensic accountant to determine
