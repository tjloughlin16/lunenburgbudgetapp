Abatement Application Update
The board will be accepting additional public comments today related to the abatement
process. Each participant will be limited to 2 minutes per person.
At the last executive session, there were 50 applications reviewed. For those applications, the
homeowner will receive the certificates, a notice, and a copy of what is going to be sent to the
Department of Revenue. The certificate will have the old tax and new tax information and the
letter will include the new property values. If the board is unable to complete the applications
by the end of the executive session, then a meeting will be held on May 30 to finalize.
An additional batch of 250 abatement applications will soon be received.
