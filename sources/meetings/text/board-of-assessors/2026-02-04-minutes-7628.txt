TOWN OF LUNENBURG
BOARD OF ASSESSORS
Minutes, February 4, 2026
Executive Session
Executive Session started at 9:08 p.m.
Attending the executive session were the Chair, Brian Laffond, Sarah Cammer and
Board member Brian Marple. A total of 4 real estate exemptions were reviewed with
Lynda Cajka, the Assessor. All of the 4 real estate exemptions were approved by the
Board of Assessors.
The breakdown of the 4 real estate exemption applications that were approved include:
2 of Clause 22/ Veteran Applications
1 of the Clause 22E Application
• 1 of the 41D Clause Application
The Assessor also discussed the 12 Real Estate Abatements for FY2026. The Board of
Assessors approved 4 of the real estate abatements. A total of 8 abatements were
denied which consisted of 2 real estate abatements and 6 personal property abatements.
The 6 personal property abatements were denied as the abatements were submitted by
a solar company that leases their solar systems to residents and solar is not taxable for
residential properties.
Meeting adjourned 9:52 p.m.
