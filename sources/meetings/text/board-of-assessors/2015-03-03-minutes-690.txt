Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held March 3, 2015
The meeting opened at 5:45 pm. Present: Chairman, Louis Franco, Board Members, Matt Papini, Sr.,
Christopher Comeau, Assessor, George Bourgault and Louise Paquette, Administrative Assistant.
Feb. 3,, 2014 minutes, current expense vouchers, abatements, warrants, commitments & payroll.
Louise presented (2) two Chapter 61B Recreational Land releases and re-recordings for applications that
were previously reviewed and approved. The releases were to correct ownership on the properties which
were recorded under their parents' names.
Louise reported that the Collector has received the Roll-Back taxes committed on 12/23/14 in the amount
of $4,712.38 for the property located at 90 West Townsend Road. Louise will contact the previous owner
regarding the procedure in releasing the Chapter 61B recording.
The Board reviewed and voted to grant (2) two Exemption applications totaling $1,150.00. (V1 & E2)
Louise informed the Board that the MVE bills for the 1° Commitment for 2015 and 7th commitment for
2014 were mailed on February 24, 2015. The 18 Commitment for 2015 includes 10,823 invoices totaling
$1,134,116.25. The 7" commitment for 2014 includes 325 invoices totaling $8,723.37.
Before beginning the scheduled review of this month's abatement applications, George Bourgault under
the advisement of Harald Scheid, requested that the Board review again the application from Centro GA,
Lunenburg Crossing LLC of 301 Massachusetts Ave. The Board reviewed and discussed the reason for
the filing of the abatement application. The Applicants' focus is on the in-line retail space which has
struggled and is experiencing considerable vacancy. After reviewing, the Board voted to affirm their denial
to not grant the abatement.
They recognized that even a small adjustment to the estimated anchor rents
would yield a value that in in agreement with the valuation, Moreover, they felt that the value for this
property at approximately $76.00 per square foot is conservative and is supported by strip center sales.
Louise reported that the Abatement Notification and Findings will be mailed to the applicant within the
next week.
George presented (11) eleven Real Estate abatement applications and (1) one Personal Property
abatement application.
Two applications for properties located at 73 Pine Grove Road and 64 Pine
Grove Road were denied by the Board.
Granted: (9) Nine Real Estate applications & (1) Personal Property applications
