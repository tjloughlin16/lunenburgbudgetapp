Harald reported to the Board that there have been many inquiries lately from property owners of "Chapter
Land" parcels seeking information regarding the Right of First Refusal process. He reported that several
roll-back tax amounts have been prepared; however, property owners have yet to follow through with the
process.
Town Meeting was held on Saturday, May 6* and the FY18 Assessors Budget was approved which
includes contracting for Personal Property Services.
Preliminary Calendar: Louise reported that Rebecca will be creating the FY18 Real Estate bill file within
the next couple of weeks. Prelim bills will be mailed on or about June 28, 2017.
Harald reported that Inspectors are very busy working on periodical inspections. Harald started that he
expects that approx. 783 properties will need to be inspected this year which will leave about 600+ in
2018 before the FY19 DOR audit. He explained that there are approx. 4100 accounts and that the last
major inspection took place in 2006. The Board questioned whether these inspections also included the
homes' interiors. He responded that every property has received a questionnaire and they are being
reviewed and then the interior will be inspected if warranted with the cooperation of the homeowner. The
Board requested that they would like to review an annual report on what properties are inspected
annually.
Harald spoke of his work in progress. He is currently working on solar projects which includes the activity
on Chase Rd. and Pleasant St. He stated that he has been reviewing PILOT AGREEMENTS and is
investigating why the Pleasant St property never had an agreement in place.
Board voted to meet on June 6, 2017 at 5:45 pm. Meeting adjourned 7:30pm.
