The meeting opened at 5:45 pm.
Board Members, Richard Letarte, Rena Swezey, Regional Associate
Assessor, Rebecca Boucher, and Louise Paquette, Administrative Assessor. Chairman, Lou Franco,
was not in attendance.
Board reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, abatements, warrants, commitments, and
payroll. August 7, 2016 minutes reviewed and approved by Richard Letarte; approval of minutes tabled
upon Lou accepting them.
Statutory Exemptions: 82 applications for exemptions were reviewed. Board approved 81 and one V1
exemption application due to residency requirements. Total amount granted: $48,763.00.
Signature Authorization for DLS Gateway Entries: BOA authorized Rebecca & Harald to sign Sales
Report & Tax Rate Forms in DLS Gateway on behalf of the Board.
Interim Year Adjustment: Rebecca reported that the property value ratio is at approx. 95%; stating that
single family properties increased by 5% of value. She also reported that some condo values decreased
in value, no changes in values for apartment buildings, commercial and industrial properties and two
neighborhoods saw an increase in value according to 2016 sales. BOA were satisfied with the revised
assessment level.
Louise reported that a certified letter has been received in office regarding the Right of First Refusal for
property located at 2030 Mass Ave. This property is currently under the Chapter 61B Recreational Land
Program.
Rebecca presented to the Board the Pro Forma of Taxes on the five properties that were auctioned on
August 29, 2017. (See attached report: Calculation of payment in Lieu of tax - Sale of Town Land MGL
Chapter 44, Section 63A).
Rebecca spoke briefly on the Settlers Solar, LLC; property owned by 994 Northfield Road LLC. This
project is still in the planning stage and will be addressed further at a later time with Harald Scheid
Board voted to meet on October 3, 2017 at 5:45 pm. Meeting adjourned at 7:04 pm. RECEIVED & FILED
