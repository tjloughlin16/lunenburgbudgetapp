Minutes from BOA (Board of Assessors) meeting held: May 25, 2023.
Location: In person at the Lunenburg Town Hall and broadcast over the Lunenburg Public Access
Channel.
The meeting was opened at 3:02 PM.
Members Present: Principal Access CJ Carroll, Administrative Assessing Assistant Sheila Craigen,
Board Member Kevin Fish, Chairman Richard Letarte, Board Member John Rabbitt, Interim
Principal Assessor Rena Swezey.
Abatement Application Update
During the executive session on Tuesday, the board voted on 50 abatement applications.
Notices will be going out in the mail tomorrow. The certificate will include the old tax, the new
tax, and the abatement amount.
The final batch of nearly 250 should be received from MFS soon.
The Board hopes to complete the first 160 abatement applications by May 30.
Public Comment
No public comment.
Adjournment
Motion to adjourn and enter into executive session and not return to the open meeting by
Kevin Fish, Seconded by John Rabbitt. All in favor.
The meeting adjourned at 3:07 PM.
