Real Estate Research FY21 Proposal: Louise reported that we are in receipt of RRC FY21
proposal in the amount of $5,420.00 & an additional $1500 for software support. Louise
reported an increase in tax revenue committed from FY19 to FY20 in the amount of $81,037.70
and Growth value increased by $8,428,030. BOA voted to accept the FY21 proposal. In addition
to the proposal Brian Pelletier, President of RRC stated that there could possibly be additional
fees associated due to the requirements of the DOR relative to the class 504 utilities.
ClearGov / Budget Software: Louise reported that budget planning season is upon us and the
process for submitting the FY2021 has been revised to an electronic submission through new
software called ClearGov. This will replace the procedure used in the past. Louise stated that
the FY2021 proposed budget for the Town Manager is due by January 2nd.
She expressed
concern about budget planning due to the uncertainty of the current 3 year contract for Regional
Assessors Salary / Measure and List which expires on June 30, 2020. She also informed the
BOA that she plans to retire this spring possibly on April 1st of this year. She explained that this
could also affect the planning under Assessor's Admin. Salaries for the FY21 Budget. Rena
stated that she has reached out to the Town Manager and expressed her desire that the Town
may want to explore hiring a full time Assessor. She feels at this time a restructuring of the
office may need to be addressed to better service the needs of the Town. Harald expressed that
he wants the opportunity to share his thoughts on future staffing possibilities, working along with
Board in order to keep the office running well.
