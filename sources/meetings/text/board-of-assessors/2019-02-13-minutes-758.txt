 RE Assessment Software:  Harald reported to the Board that we have been under contract with the 
State DOR CAMA Assessment Software for the last 30 years and it is no longer supported.  He 
suggested that a RFP (Request for Proposal) be drafted as soon as possible.  Louise reported that we 
have been in communication with Vision Government Solutions as recommended by the BOA. Louise will 
reach out to them to set up a time for a demonstration of the software.   The BOA is recommending that 
this happen as soon as possible.  
 
Registry of Motor Vehicle Software Upgrade : Louise reported that the Registry of Motor Vehicles is in 
the process of developing and implementing new motor vehicle software called “Atlas” and it will go “live” 
on November 12. 2019 . They plan the first run for bills on  December 16, 2019.   The website linking the 
new product is: atlas.massrmv.com.  She stated that there are webinar programs available to track the 
progress and provide understanding of the new software.  
 
Report by Assessor:  Harald reported that the Town has b een approached by property owner of 40 
Pioneer Drive who is seeking Tax Increment Financing. Harald explained that  TIF provides a direct 
upfront benefit to a Developer in the form of tax relief. The money saved on taxes helps pay the project's 
construction costs. He explained that the taxpayer is requesting this now after the buil ding has doubled in 
size and construction  has been completed.  He explained that the request will need to go before the 
Selectmen and then an article will need to be presented to the towns’ people at Town Meeting.  
 
New Growth:  Louise reported that she received an email from RRG’s Regional Associate Assessor 
Diane Peterson in regards to new growth.  She stated, “I have reviewed the 5 year historical numbers and 
the past two years of actual properties that contributed to new growth. I am still reviewing building permits 
with the assessor, and with that, it appears 300K would be a new growth projection at this time. We will 
continue to follow local progress on new construction and with March 1 being the deadline for personal 
property returns; we can provide updates as they happen.  Louise stated that she has been in touch with 
the Building Department requesting a report of all building permits & occupancy permits fr om January 
2018 to the current date and also stated that RRC will be handling the growth report for Personal 
Property.
