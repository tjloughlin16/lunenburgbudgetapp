21 MAY STREET
Dan Proctor met with the board of behalf of the owner. This system is in failure due to high ground
water. This lot is small, has high ground water and three wells with 100 feet of the proposed system. Due
to the fill on the lot, a perc test would not have been safe. A variance for sieve analysis is being
requested.
The design for the proposed system will utilize the existing tank. Variance as also requested for offset of
the tank to the cellar wall. If this design plan were not approved, the only alternative would be a tight
tank.
Agent Jim Garreffi is recommending replacement of the septic system with a FAST or JET system and
the testing of all wells within 100 feet.
After discussion and input from the installer relative to physical difficulties with the lot, on motion by
Passios and second by Fortin, the variance was approved without the requirement for use of a FAST or
JET system but with the requirement that all wells within 100 feet be tested. All in favor.
1024/1034/1048 NORTHFIELD ROAD
Ron Smith, one of the owners of the property (Steve Anderson is also an owner) met with the board. The
proposed house locations are at distances greater than 100 feet from their proposed leaching areas.
Due to the configuration of the lots and the ledge that is present, the design presented is necessary.
Lengths for each lot are as follows:

Lot 5 (1024) - 116 feet
Lot 2 (1048) - 152 feet
Lot 4 (1034) - 127 feet
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Passios the variances were approved with the
requirement that the proposed forced mains are self-draining and are buried deep enough to prevent the
lines from freezing. All in favor.
126 YOUNGS ROAD
Dan Proctor met with the board on behalf of the owner. The board approved local upgrade approvals at
their September 19, 2016 meeting. The system installer, Dan Proctor, is asking if the tank can be moved
to improve the construction of the system. The movement of the tank would require an additional
variance as the tank will now be ten (10) feet closer to the well. Agent Jim Garreffi does not have a
problem with the new location of the tank provided the tanks are monolithic in construction and the
installer clears the move with the design engineer.
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Fortin, the request was approved. All in favor.
