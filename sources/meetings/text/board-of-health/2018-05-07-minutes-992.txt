The owner of the above property, Ed Perry, met with the board. A housing complaint was received
and Agent Jim Garreffi completed a housing inspection. All violations were completed with the
exception of the replacement of windows. At their December 4, 2017 meeting, the board gave Mr.
Perry additional time to complete window replacement until the end of January, 2018. The
replacement has not been completed.
Mr. Perry stated that the windows are not that bad and only "drop a little from the top when unlocked".
Chairman George Edmond advised Mr. Perry that windows must function in the manner in which they
are designed. Dropping a little from the top when they are unlocked are not functioning in the matter
in which they are designed.
Mr. Perry stated that he does not have the money to replace the windows. That he has just recently
returned to work.
Chairman Emond suggested that the board give Mr. Perry to the end of the month to repair or replace
the windows.
Member Dave Passios asked how many windows needed repair. Mr. Perry stated that he thought it
was seven or eight.
