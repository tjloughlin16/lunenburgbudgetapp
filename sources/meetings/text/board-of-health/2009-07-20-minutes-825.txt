802 CHASE ROAD - VARIANCE REQUEST
George Sullivan of David E. Ross and Associates met with the board to request variances
for the replacement of a failed system. This is a small lot that is served by a well and is
located in a high water table. The house is sited very close to the road. A new well is
being proposed to be sited in the front yard, with the new septic system in the rear. Due
to its proximity to the roadway, the well will be sealed after installation.
Mr. Sullivan has maximized the offsets for the wells and septic system located on the
surrounding properties. He further advised that he has made numerous attempts to
contact the abutting property owner to the north without success.
===PAGE 2===
After discussion and on Motion by Sund and second by Rabbitt, the request was
unanimously approved.
408 CHASE ROAD - VARIANCE REQUEST
The owner of this residence is suffering with a low water yield and, occasionally, no
water. They are proposing the installation of a new drilled well in the general vicinity of
the old well. This proposal requires a variance to distance from property line from 50
feet to 43 feet.
After discussion and on Motion by Shea and second by Sund the request for variance was
unanimously approved
