1170 MASS AVENUE 
 
Jack Maloney met with the board on behalf of the owner.  This system is in failure.  This property is 
connected to public water.  The topography of the lot is difficult.  Testing showed gravel soils with an 
estimate groundwater at 30 inches.  Perc is 8 MPI.  A Presby system is proposed. 
 
Variances requested are offset to groundwater from 4 feet to 3 feet; the use of a sieve analysis and the 
use of an AI system (Presby). 
 
After discussion and on motion by Fortin and second by Rudd, the variances  were approved.  All in 
favor.
