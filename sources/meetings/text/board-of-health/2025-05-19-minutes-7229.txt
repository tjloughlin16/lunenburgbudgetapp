LUNENBURG BOARD OF HEALTH 
MEETING MINUTES 
Monday, May 19, 2025 
 
The Board of Health met at 4:30 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building  
 
Members Present:  Perry Jewell, Paul Fortin, Matt Proctor, Lorraine Rudd and Nick Pirro 
 
MINUTES APPROVED 
 
No minutes were approved. 
 
155 ISLAND ROAD 
 
The septic system for this property was installed, with a permit, in 2013.  The paper work was never 
completed to generate a Certificate of Compliance.  In completing the paperwork it was noted that the 
septic tank was not swapped out for a 1,500 gallon tank.  There was conflicting information on whether 
it was required.  Steve Anderson did swap out the septic tank but noted that he could only meet an offset 
to foundation of 7 feet.  Regulations require 10 feet and a 3 foot variance would be required.   
 
After discussion and o n motion by Proctor and second by Fortin, the variance was approved.  All in 
favor. 
 
BURRAGE STREET – PENNIMAN PROPERTY 
 
Andrea Schnepf reported that Cara Olejarz and Andrea Schnepf were able to set up a meeting with town 
counsel with regard to the proposed subdivision on Burrage Street.  Two members of the board, two 
members of Nashoba and Andrea Schnepf were present.  The board will be issuing a letter stating that 
there is not enough information to approve this project. 
 
Having no further matters before the board and on motion by Jewell and seconded by Fortin, the meeting 
was adjourned at 5:40 pm, all in favor.
