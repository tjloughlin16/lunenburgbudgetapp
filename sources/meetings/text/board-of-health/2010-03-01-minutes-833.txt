BOARD OF HEALTH

March 1, 2010
The Board of Health met at 7:00 pm at the Ritter Administration Building
Present:
Chairman Dave Shea, George Emond, Ernie Sund, Perry Jewell and Jack Rabbitt
TITLE 5 INSPECTION REPORTS REVIEWED:
232 Burrage Street
389 Mulpus Road
SEPTIC PERMITS SIGNED:
188-190 Hollis Road
26 SPRING STREET EXTENSION
George Sullivan met with the Board of behalf of the homeowner. Jim Van Buren was also present on
behalf of an abutter, Paul Shea, Scott Laney and Jeff Stone. The system is in failure. This is a small,
lake side lot with the well location less than 100 feet.
George Sullivan stated that the plan as submitted is for a two bedroom home. There was a permit
issued in 2002 which has since expired. The state approved the plan but nothing was ever done with it.
The board discussed the location of the tank. Agent Jim Garreffi is not in favor of the current plan or
well location. Abutters are concerned with the location to area wells. They would like the wells test to
get a benchmark of current well quality.
At the very least this system would need to be deed restricted to 2 bedrooms.
After further discussion, this matter was continued until March 15, 2010 at 7:100 pm.
193 PAGE STREET
Tim Beauchim met with the Board on behalf of the owner. This system is in failure. This lot has
extensive wetlands. Because of the wetlands the system has been placed to achieve the greatest offset.
After discussion and on motion by Emond and second by Sund, the variance requests were approved
subject to the board receiving an amended letter and plan. All in favor.
Having no further business before the Board, the meeting was adjourned at 7:42 pm.
