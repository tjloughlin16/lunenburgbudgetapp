234 CHASE ROAD 
 
The system on this property is in failure based on soil testing.  The lot has very high seasonal 
groundwater so perc testing was conducted.  The site slopes toward Chase Road, the well is behind the 
house. 
 
In order to maintain the 100 foot offset from the well, the leaching area is proposed for the front yard.  
To maintain the breakout fill needed for the site, a variance of one foot to groundwater is being 
requested.   
 
After discussion and on motion by Jew ell and second by Passios, the variance was approved.  All in 
favor. 
 
39 RANGELEY ROAD 
 
The owner of this property would like to replace his system, believing it is in high groundwater.  A 
Presby system is being proposed.  Because of high groundwater, perc  testing was not possible so a 
sieve analysis was performed.   The applicant is requesting a groundwater variance from four feet to 
two feet (Presby System), the use of a sieve analysis and a reduction in the 12” separation from the

tank inlet tees.  The separation will be 7 inches tank inlet, 4.5 inches for tank outlet, 4 inches for pump 
inlet and 3.8 to pump outlet. 
 
After discussion, and on motion by Jewell and second by Fortin, the board voted to approve the 
requested variances.  All in favor.
