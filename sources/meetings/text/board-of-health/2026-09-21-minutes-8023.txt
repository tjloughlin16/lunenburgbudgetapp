Continued to October 5, 2026 at 4:30 pm 
 
27 FIRE ROAD 15A 
 
Steve Bean, owner, met with the board.  Mr. Bean is tearing down a cottage and building a new 2 
bedroom home and using the existing well fr om the cottage.  Although there are regulations for new 
wells, there is a gap in regulations for existing wells.  There are no records for the existing well.  Prior 
to signing off on the building permit for the new home, the water must be proved potable.  Mr.  Bean 
sampled and tested the water and provided the results.  The water sample meets t he Primary Standards 
set forth by the Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection and the LBOH well Regulations 
for a potable source of water.  Additionally, the Sodium levels exceed the Massachusetts Guidelines for 
individuals on a low/restricted sodium diet. 
 
After discussion and on motion by Pirro and second by Jewell, the board accepted the water test results 
as potable.  All in favor. 
 
757 CHASE ROAD 
 
Frank McPartlan of Dillis and Roy met with the board on behalf of the owner.  This was continued from 
July 20th.  The owner is requesting to retain the 2nd well on the property for an irrigation/fire suppression 
purpose.  Initially the request also include the use of car washing but the owner does not want to install 
the required tank.  This became the second well on the property when a new Zone 1 well was drilled. 
 
This property is to be redesigned and rebuilt within the next year.  Currently  there is no need for either 
an irrigation well (there is nothing to irrigate) and the fire suppression system will not be needed until 
the new building is completed.

After discussion, and on motion by Jewell and second by Fortin, the board voted to allow the 2nd well to 
exist on the property, completely disconnected from service and when there is a time that there is a use 
for the well after construction is complete, to come back to the board for discussion.  All in favor. 
 
30 SCHOOL STREET 
 
This is the old Primary School Pro perty.  The system on this property was approximately 6 years old 
when the New School opened.  The decision was made when the school was demolished to not 
decommission the septic system so it could be used for a future use. 
 
Currently the Food Pantry is looking for a temporary location.  The board voted to allow up to $50,000 
from the grant fund to be used to bring the septic system back up to working.  This is large system that 
was installed to service the prior school.  The Food Pantry has very small sanitary needs and are asking 
for a change in use for the septic system to  a civic and or community use and approval of the site plan 
before the Planning Board. 
 
On motion by Fortin and second by Rudd, the change in use and site plan approval approved.  All in 
favor. 
 
141 PLEASANT STREET 
 
Bob Proctor met with the board on behalf of the owner.  This system on this property is in failure.  
High groundwater is at 20 inches.  Due to the small size of the property, high groundwater and 
wetlands, a Presby system is proposed.   
 
Variances requested are: 
 
Use of an I/A System 
Reduction in distance to cellar wall 20 feet to 11 feet 
Offset to wetlands 50 feet to 25 feet 
The use of sieve analysis 
 
This proposed system is not within 100 feet of any wells and does not utilize a groundwater offset. 
 
After discussion an on motion by Pirro and second by Jewell, the variances were approved.  All in 
favor.
