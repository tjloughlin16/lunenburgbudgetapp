229 LANCASTER AVENUE – SEPTIC VARIANCES 
 
Dan Proctor met with the board of behalf of the owner, Marilyn Stokes.  This sewage disposal 
system has failed a Title 5 Inspection.  Soil testing was conducted in preparation for the design of 
a new system and at the time the soil was too damp to perc.  A soil sample was taken and a sieve 
analysis was performed.   
 
The sewer line servicing the system is too close to the pressure water line of the Lunenburg Water 
District, 5 feet instead of 10 feet.   
 
Should the building sewer line need to be replaced, Jim Garreffi is requesting that same be 
sleeved as it passes the water line. 
 
After discussion and on Motion by Shea and second by Daniels, variances were approved.  All in 
favor. 
 
 
Meeting adjourned at 6:45 pm.
