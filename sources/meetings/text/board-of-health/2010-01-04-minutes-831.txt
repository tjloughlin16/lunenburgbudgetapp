25 MAY STREET
The owner, Louis Herman, met with the board to discuss drilling a new well at the above
property. This is a small, lake front lot. There is limited space for a new well.
After discussion and on motion by Emond and second by Jewell, variances were
approved with the following conditions:
1. The new well be a drilled well'
2. The new well casing shall be advanced 50-70 feet into competent bedrock and be
sealed with bentonite. All in favor.
64 FIRE ROAD 12
Steve Marsden met with the Board on behalf of the owner. This system is in failure. The
is a small, lake front lot. The plan, as proposed, maximizes the offsets to wells and
wetlands (lake). The existing well will be replaced as it is too close to the new system.
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Rabbitt, the variance requests
were approved. All in favor.
