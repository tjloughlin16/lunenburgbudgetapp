125 HOWARD STREET - BARN/KENNEL REQUEST
Owner Gary Burton met with the board to explore the options to either expand the existing septic
system or install a second septic system on this property in preparation of building a barn. Chairman
George Emond stated that he will contact DEP and report back to Mr. Burton as well as the Board.
SEPTIC DESTRUCTION PERMIT
Bob Ebersol, Chairman of the Sewer Commission, met with the board to discuss the System
Abandonment Permit. It was stated that the Sewer Commission gives permission for the connection.
Currently, the Board of Health does not witness the destruction of the system, however, an agent for
the Board of Health executes an affidavit that the work has been completed. Chairman Ebersole
suggested that an application for destruction be given out at the same time the Sewer Department
office provides the application to connect.
This discussion was continued to give the Board time to review the permit and create a permitting
process.
Meeting adjourned at 7:55 pm.
