LUNENBURG BOARD OF HEALTH 
MEETING MINUTES 
Monday, April 7, 2025 
 
The Board of Health met at 4:30 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building  
 
Members Present:  Perry Jewell, Paul Fortin and Matt Proctor  Also Present Lorraine Rudd  
 
MINUTES APPROVED 
 
March 3, 2025 
 
On motion by Proctor and second by Fortin, the minutes were approved. 
 
1178 NORTHFIELD ROAD 
 
Dan Proctor met with the board on behalf of the owner.  This system is in failure.  Although the property 
contained 1.63 acres, it is predominately wetlands.  Perc rate is 8 MPI with groundwater at 34 inches. 
 
Variances requested are the use of an IA system, a Geomat 
Offset to wetlands from 50 feet to 34 feet 
Use of a sieve analysis 
 
After discussion and on motion by Fortin and second by Proctor, the variances were approved.  All in 
favor. 
 
PORTA TOILETS ON TOWN PROPERTY 
 
Today, Recreational Director Angela Clement provided Andrea Schnepf with the required information 
to place porta toilets on town property for events run by the Town. 
 
After discussion and on motion by Proctor and second by Fortin, the board voted to accept the 
Recreational Department plan for porta toilets. 
 
Having no further matters before the board and on motion by Jewell and seconded by Fortin, the meeting 
was adjourned at 5:15 pm, all in favor.
