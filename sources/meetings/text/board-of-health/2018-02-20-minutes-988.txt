44 BROOKVIEW TERRACE 
 
The matter was before the Board on July 18, 2016.  At that time variances for offset to wetlands, off to 
property line and offset to cellar wall were approved.  There is a new owner of the property that wished 
to re-design the system in a way that will be less of a burden on the property and will reduce the 
number of variances required.  The new system design requires offset to wetlands (shoreline) and 
requires the use of a monolithic septic tank.  This is a long narrow lot with water on two sides. The 
new design moves the system the proper distance from the house put closer to the water.  This new 
location grading does not require a retaining wall. 
 
After discussion and on Motion by Fortin and second by Jewell, the changes and associated variance 
were approved.  All in favor. 
 
BUDGET 
 
The yearly budget for Board of Health was prepared.  The budget is level funded with an increase to 
salary based on employment contract and a 5% increase to Nashoba Associated Boards of Health also 
based on agreement.   
 
After discussion and on motion by Fortin and seconded by Jewell the budget was approved.  All in 
favor.
