168 SUNNY HILL ROAD
Chris MacKenzie met with the Board on behalf of the owner. This system would be in failure because of the
high ground water. Soil tested was completed on most of the site and did not have the required 4 foot of
pervious, naturally occurring material that is required by Title 5 due to ledge. The best area found was in the
back corner of the lot.
Title 5 allows for the use of as little as 2 feet of pervious soil with a variance from DEP.
After discussion and on motion of Perry Jewell and second by Jack Rabbitt, the variance requests were
approved. All in favor.
