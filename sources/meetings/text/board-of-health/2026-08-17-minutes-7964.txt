165 GILCHREST  
 
In March of 2023 a resident was interested in obtaining a Residential Kitchen License which allows 
jellies and jams and bread  to be prepared in a residence .  The permit came to the board for approval 
because Nashoba believes that this type of permit is disallowed under Title 5.  At the time, A. Schnepf 
spoke with James Laughlin who stated that there was nothing in Title 5 to disallow a Residential Kitchen 
License on a septic system and it was to go to the Board of Health for approval.  The license was approved 
and has been renewed yearly.  At that time the Board wanted all Residential Kitchen Licenses to come 
to the local board for approval. 
 
Recently a resident asked about obtaining a Residential Kitchen License.  They were directed to Nashoba 
who, according to the resident, were told the Residential Licenses were not issued to addresses with 
septic systems.   
 
J. Garreffi, Director of Nashoba, was present and asked for direction on issuing these permits.  N. Pirro 
stated that the Board is in favor of continuing to issue these licenses with all the proper paperwork and 
inspections.  Moving forward residents can continue to apply for a Residential Kitchen Licen se in 
Lunenburg to be approved by the board after completing all the necessary steps.

27 FIRE ROAD 15A 
 
Steven Bean, owner, met with the board.  This is a unique property that has three small cottages and 3 
separate well.  There is a 4 bedroom septic system currently on the property. 
 
What is being proposed is demolishing one of the co ttages and building a new 2 bedroom home.  The 
remaining cottages would have 1 bedroom each.  One of the three wells would provide water for the new 
structure.  Because of the uniqueness of the property, overall the combined structures have 4 bedrooms 
but have 11 rooms total, above that allowable number of rooms with a 4 bedroom septic.  Mr. Bean is 
requesting a deed restriction on the property of 4 bedrooms with 11 rooms total. 
 
On motion by Fortin and second by Rudd, the above the deed restriction was approved.  All in favor.
