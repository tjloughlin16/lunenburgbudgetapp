64 ARBOR STREET
An Order was issued to repair/replace the septic system for the above property on June 19, 2018, the
replace of the system to be by the end of August, 2018. The owner advised agent, Jim Garreffi, that
his attorney would be contacting the Board and/or the agent. Neither the owner or his attorney have
been in contact with the Board or its Agent. Agent Garreffi has recommended that this matter be
pursued in housing court.
After discussion and on motion by Fortin and second by Passios, the board voted to pursue this mater
in housing court. All in favor.

accordance with regulations.
