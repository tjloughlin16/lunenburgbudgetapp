LUNENBURG BOARD OF HEALTH 
MEETING MINUTES 
Monday, June 6, 2022 
 
The Board of Health met at 7:00 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building  
 
Members Present:  George Emond, Perry Jewell and Lisa Normandin and Paul Fortin  
 
MINUTES APPROVED 
 
NONE 
 
34 RANGLEY ROAD 
 
Dan Proctor m et with the board on behalf of the owner.  This system is in failure with a replacement 
Presby system having been designed.  The lot slopes and the proposed design incorporates the slope 
and allows for a gravity system.  This lot is served by public water. 
 
Variances requested are offset to groundwater from 4 feet to 3 feet and the use of a sieve analysis. 
 
Perry Jewell commented on the close proximity to the adjoining lot.  Mr. Proctor stated that there will 
be a poly barrier installed.  He also stated that there is no abutting house due to the large wetland. 
 
After discussion and on motion by Normandin and second by Jewell, the variances were approved.  All 
in favor. 
 
COVID UPDATE 
 
The two week case counts, as reported by the Department of Public Health, h ave been fairly consistent 
since the beginning of May (32-37) but it is likely higher due to the number of home tests being used. 
 
Nashoba Associated Boards of Health received an email from the Department of Public Health that 
Nashoba towns are eligible fo r free home test kits from the State.  Lunenburg would receive 2160 kits 
amounting to 4,320 tests (2 per kit).  Chairman Emond authorized ordering the kits.  Distribution of the 
kits will be at the discretion of the Board. 
 
Having no further matters before  the board and on  motion Fortin and seconded by Jewell, the meeting 
was adjourned at 7:45 pm, all in favor.
