395 Hollis Rd - Upgrade 
The septic system for this site is in failure. Soil testing was conducted which revealed 
sandy loam soils with high ground water (Estimated at 24”). The proposed design will 
utilize I/A technology (Presby enviroseptic). The owner has requested the following 
Local Upgrade approvals and Local Variances: 
1. 310 CMR 15.405 (1) (i): the owner is requesting approval to use of a sieve analysis. 
2. 310 CMR 15.405 (1) (j): The owner is requesting a reduction in inlet and outlet tees 
(12” required, 4” proposed). 
3. Section 300-4.3:The owner is requesting a local variance from Lunenburg’s local 
regarding the offset to groundwater. (4’ required, 2’ proposed offset to groundwater) 
 
4. Section 300-4.16: The owner is requesting approval to use Innovative/Alternative 
Technology 
 
After discussion and on motion by Proctor and second by Fortin the variances were 
approved. All in favor.
