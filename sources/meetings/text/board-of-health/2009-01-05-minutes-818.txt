OUTDOOR HYDRONIC HEATERS (WOOD FIRED OUTDOOR FURNACES)
The DEP has issued its regulations for Outdoor Hydronic Heaters. The members
reviewed the regulations and had the following comments:
A permit, either through the Board of Health or the Building Department is
required;
All currently existing furnaces must be identified as to size and location and
be provided with current regulations.
Chairman Emond will contact DEP to discuss these new regulations, the role of the local
Board of Health in enforcing these regulations and report back to the Board.
MEADOWOODS MOBILE HOME PARK
Subsequent to the recent ice storm portions of the Meadowoods Mobile Home Park were
again without water, primarily in the "lettered" portion of the park. Health Agent Jim
Garreffi was able to secure water and implement a plan to continue to provide water to
the residents of the park on a short-term basis. Chairman Emond spoke with Andrea
Lemerise at DEP who indicated that a public water hydrant would be hooked up to the
"lettered" portion of the park in the immediate future.

WASTE WATER MANAGEMENT PLAN
The members of the Board again discussed the role that they believe they should play in
the final Waste Water Management Plan to be submitted for Town Meeting. After
discussion it was decided that the Board does not believe that the Final Waste Water
Management Plan should be at the sole discretion of the Sewer Commission and will be
attending the next meeting and/or workshop to discuss the major issues that the Board of
Health disagrees with.
FLOOR DRAIN REGULATION
The Board received a letter from the Lunenburg Water District requesting that the Board
consider the adoption of a floor drain regulation. After discussion and on motion by Shea
and second by Jewell the Board voted unanimously to reject the adoption of a floor drain
regulation.
Having no further business before the Board, the meeting was adjourned at 8:35 pm.
