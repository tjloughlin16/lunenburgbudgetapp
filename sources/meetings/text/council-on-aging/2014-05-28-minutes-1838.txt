Town of Lunenburg
Council on Aging
Board of Development Committee Minutes
Date of meeting - May 28, 2014
Present - Barbara Brown, Judy Tarbell and Connie O'Dea
Excused - Joyce Wass
The meeting was called to order at 8:30 am by Chairperson Barbara
Brown.
No other people attended the meeting.
No Minutes needed approval.
Current Business: Ms. Brown stated that a "slate" of Board Members
for the 2014-15 year needs to be established and presented at the
upcoming Annual Meeting of the COA to be held on June 10, 2014.
She also expressed that she was very pleased with the attendance of all
the prospective members at the May COA meeting and that as of May
28* we have received completed application forms from Sue Andrews
of Horizon Island Road, Kevin McNally of Hemlock Drive, Caroline Griffis
of Spring Street and Diane Nowd of Island Road.
Paul and Dee Anderson and Lorraine Mendoza were present at the May
COA meeting, as well as Pete Lincoln who has already been appointed
to the COA as of July 1, 2014. Paul expressed that he has quite a few
commitments at this time but was interested and Lorraine would like to
attend a few more meetings before making a commitment.
===PAGE 2===
Ms. Brown explained that because of the upcoming vote that will be
taken at the June meeting which pertains to "Term Limits"
, it will be
necessary to establish two different slates of Officers and Members for
the upcoming year, but only one will be submitted. Slate #1 will have
Jane Doyle and Barbara Brown remaining on the Board if the Board
votes to waive or no longer use the "Term Limit " policy, thus only
choosing two prospective members or Slate #2 will be submitted if it is
voted to keep "Term Limits"
, thus choosing all four prospective
members that have already submitted the necessary paperwork.
Pete Lincoln and Barbara Brown have agreed to serve as Chairperson
and Vice-Chairperson respectfully depending on the outcome of the
voting at the June meeting. (SEE Attached Slate #1 and Slate #2)
Ms. Brown stated that she will send letters to all prospective members
expressing our appreciation for their interest in serving on the COA and
explain the reason (if that is the case) for not choosing all members and
also ask for their approval to keep their names in our file for future use.
Barbara will type slates to be submitted.
Meeting adjourned at 9:15.
Respectfully submitted
Barbara Broun
Barbara Brown
Chairperson of the Board of Development
