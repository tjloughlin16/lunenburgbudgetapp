TOWN OF LUNENBURG
COUNCIL ON AGING
Meeting Minutes
Tuesday, October 13, 2026
Lunenburg Aduit Activity Center
25 Memorial Drive, Lunenburg, MIA
Call to Order: 9:48 a.m.
Absent: Pauline Roy
Announcements
None.
Public Comment
None.
Board Comment
Ana Lockwood informed the Board that she had contacted Aging Services of North Central
Massachusetts to inquire about services available to Lunenburg's homebound residents.
Approval of Minutes
The September meeting minutes were unanimously approved.
New Business
1. Assessor's Office Hours / Disability Commission Liaison
The Assessor's Office will hold office hours twice monthly at the Lunenburg Adult Activity
Center.
Chairperson Deb Lincoln suggested appointing a liaison to the Disability Commission. Jane
Rabbitt volunteered to serve in this role.
2. Policies and Procedures
Board Member Jack Rabbitt presented a PowerPoint slideshow outlining his research regarding
existing policies and procedures, including areas needing clarification, changes, and updates.
===PAGE 2===
Following a lengthy discussion, the Board voted unanimously to establish a subcommittee to
further review the concerns presented.
Subcommittee members:
Ana Lockwood
• Susan O'Sullivan
Christopher Johnson
• Jack Rabbitt
3. COA Tree for Eagle House Supporters' Festival of Trees
The Board agreed to create at least one, and possibly additional, decorated trees on behalf of the
Council on Aging for the Festival of Trees fundraiser.
4. Town Report Entry
The Board previously voted to submit a Council on Aging entry for the Annual Town Report in
addition to the Director's report. Chairperson Deb Lincoln will follow up with the Town
Manager regarding the submission.
5. By-Law Review Committee
Tabled.
6. Council on Aging Priorities for FY 2028 Budget
Discussion focused on the need for additional space to accommodate the growing needs of the
Adult Activity Center.
7. Eagle House Facility Space Needs
Continuing the discussion of space needs, a recommendation was made to explore the process
and requirements for having an architect develop plans for a possible building addition.
8. Formula Grant Explanation and Status
The Director provided Board members with a handout explaining the Massachusetts State
Formula Grant.
The grant provides $16 per senior, based on Lunenburg's population of 3,120 seniors. Funds are
used at the Director's discretion to support services and programs benefiting seniors.
9. Friendly Visitor Program
===PAGE 3===
Currently, one individual is receiving visits through the program. Volunteer training has been
held, and LAAC staff will continue promoting the program to increase participation.
10. Housing
A request was made to establish a liaison to the Housing Authority. Amended 11/10/26 that
Elena Christopher volunteered to fill this roll.
11. Building Use Policy
The Town currently has an application process for building use; however, no formal policies
were located. The Director will research applicable policies and present information to the COA
Board.
Old Business
1. Final FY 2026 Financial Report
Not presented.
2. Bylaws Discussion
Tabled.
3. Tea Party Update
Preparations for the upcoming Tea Party are complete. Anyone interested in volunteering or
donating a gift basket is encouraged to contact the Adult Activity Center.
4. Parking Lot Update
The project continues to move forward. Ana Lockwood stated that once the process is
established, she will explore potential grant opportunities.
5. 41A Update
Tabled.
6. Hospital Update
The Director reported speaking with the Lunenburg Fire Chief, who confirmed that Lunenburg
Hobulances will not transport residents ta the Croton facility because it is not classified as a
Director's Report
===PAGE 4===
The Director presented information regarding upcoming programs and events at the Lunenburg
Adult Activity Center.
Special appreciation was extended to the Town of Lunenburg Facilities Department for its
continued care, maintenance, repairs, and improvements to Eagle House.
Eagle House Supporters Report
Eagle House Supporters President Barbara Coulombe provided updates on upcoming trips and
fundraising activities, including:
• Toll Booth Fundraiser: Saturday, October 24, from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. at Town
Center. Volunteers are welcome.
• Festival of Trees: November 19-21, 2026.
Notices and Communications
None.
Board Comment
Chairperson Deb Lincoln thanked Board members for their participation, involvement, and
commitment to maintaining an active Council on Aging Board.
Adjournment
A motion to adjourn was unanimously approved.
Meeting adjourned at 12:08 p.m.
