Minutes Accepted by COA at 11/12/13 Meeting 
 
 
Minutes 
Town of Lunenburg 
Council on Aging Finance Committee 
 
 
A meeting of the Council on Aging (COA) Finance Committee was held on Tuesday, October 8, 2013 
at 11:45 a.m. at the Eagle House Senior Center.  Present were committee members Deb Seeley, Shirley 
Bertone, Tony Bertone, Connie O'Dea, and Judy Tarbell. 
 
Ms. Seeley gave a brief overview of the COA Finance Committee's role in the preparation of the COA 
Annual Budget and suggested that a next meeting be schedule with Doreen Noble, COA Executive 
Director, for the purpose of further explanation of the budget and the timeline required by the Town 
Manager.   
 
The Committee had a brief discussion about the organization of the Committee and will nominate & 
elect a Chair and Vice-Chair at it's next meeting. 
 
The next meeting of the Finance Committee will be held on Monday, November 4, 2013 at 9:00 a.m. At 
the Eagle House.  Ms. Seeley will submit the meeting agenda to Sue Doherty for posting of the 
meeting. The meeting adjourned at 12:00 p.m. 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
Deborah J. Seeley 
Member, Finance Committee
