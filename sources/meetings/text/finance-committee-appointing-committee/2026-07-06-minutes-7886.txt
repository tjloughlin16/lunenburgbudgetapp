TOWN OF LUNENBURG
2026 FINANCE COMMITTEE APPOINTING COMMITTEE
MINUTES July 6, 2026
Remotely on Zoom and in person at Bilotta meeting room, Town Hall 17 Main Street
Attendees Tim Murphy, Moderator, Rene Emkey Selectboard; Laura Brzozoski School Committee
The meeting was convened at 6pm
Ms. Emkey made a motion that Mr. Murphy be the Chair, seconded by Ms. Brzozoski and the
Motion was approved.
The Agenda called for the Committee to consider re-appointment of Mr. Chris Menard and Mr. Jay
Simeone. Both were interviewed, separately.
At the conclusion of those interviews, the Committee then considered the talent bank application
of Peter Beardmore. Mr. Beardmore was then interviewed remotely.
After a brief discussion Ms. Brzozoski made a Motion to appoint Mr. Menard and Mr. Simeone to
three year terms, and Mr. Beardmore to fulfill a a two year vacancy, all commencing upon their
swearing in with the Town Clerk. Ms. Emkey seconded and the Motion was approved unanimously.
Ms. Emkey that moved to approve the draft minutes, as updated, to be typed and filed by the chair.
Ms. Brzozosku seconded and the motion was approved.
Ms Emkey then moved that the meeting adjourn, Ms. Brzozoski seconded and the meeting
adjourned at 6:18pm
