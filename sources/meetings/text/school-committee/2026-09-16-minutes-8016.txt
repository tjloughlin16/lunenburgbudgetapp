8. Superintendent’s Report 
Dr. Fortuna reported that much of the District’s recent work had focused on reviewing its financial 
operations and procedures an d ensuring that information presented to the Committee was accurate and 
complete. 
Business Administrator candidate Derek Andrews withdrew his candidacy. The position was reposted 
with a revised job description and increased salary range to attract candidat es with significant school 
finance and operations experience. 
Dr. Fortuna, the Town Manager, the Select Board Chair and the School Committee Chair met with a 
representative from the Edward J. Collins, Jr. Center for Public Management at UMass Boston to discuss 
a potential review of the District’s finances and financial procedures. A proposed scope of work was 
expected. 
The District continued to explore funding the Special Education Stabilization Fund. Dr. Fortuna was 
seeking clarification from the Departmen t of Elementary and Secondary Education regarding whether 
funding must be included as a separate line item in the District’s annual budget rather than appropriated 
through another source. She had worked with the Town Manager to preserve the District’s opti ons by 
considering a placeholder article for the Special Town Meeting warrant. 
The Committee would not vote that evening to re-ratify the paraprofessional contract because errors were 
discovered in the conversion of certain hourly rates to the annual salar ies shown on the salary scale. The 
calculations and appointment letters would be reviewed for accuracy, and the corrected contract and salary 
scale were expected to return for ratification at the next meeting.

b. Request for LHS Parent Teacher Conference Date Change 
 
The High School requested that its spring parent-teacher conferences be rescheduled from March 4 to March 18, 
2027. The additional instructional time would allow teachers to provide more effective feedback regarding 
students’ second-semester progress and reduce the potential effect of snow days. The administration was also 
considering workshops, course information and other options to make the conferences more useful to families and 
increase participation. 
 
Members discussed participation in the prior year’s spring conferences, scheduling limitations during the fall 
conferences and the ways families receive information regarding student progress. 
 
A motion was made to approve the High School’s request to change the spring parent-teacher conference 
date to March 18, 2027. The motion passed unanimously by roll-call vote, 5-0.

e. Initial Approval 8th Grade Washington DC Trip 
 
Eighth-grade teachers Jen Rios and Lisa LeBlanc presented the proposed Washington, D.C. trip scheduled for 
May 25–28, 2027. The itinerary was expected to include Smithsonian museums, national memorials, Arlington 
National Cemetery and the Capitol, with flexibility for weather and other conditions. 
 
The proposal included traveling by Amtrak from Route 128 rather than by motor coach. The presenters explained 
that train travel would reduce travel time and traffic-related delays, improve access to restrooms and food, 
accommodate students with mobility or medical needs and simplify group transportation. The proposed tour 
company would also provide group communications, student debit cards, fundraising opportunities and 
scholarships. 
 
The estimated student cost was $1,425, including transportation from the school to the train station. Members 
discussed affordability, scholarship and fundraising opportunities, advance notice to families, chaperone 
requirements, student safety and the possibility of allowing families to contribute toward the participation of other 
students. 
 
A motion was made to approve the eighth-grade Washington, D.C. trip as presented. The motion passed 
unanimously by roll-call vote, 5-0.

 Discussion 
f. Draft Superintendent Goals 
 
Dr. Fortuna presented four proposed goals for the 2026–2027 school year: 
1. Increase the districtwide median student growth percentile in English language arts for students in grades 
4–8 from 46 in 2026 to at least 55 in 2027.  
2. Strengthen instructional leadership and professional culture by developing a shared understanding of 
rigorous, standards-aligned ELA instruction and coordinating professional learning cycles in which 
educators apply new practices, review student-learning data and identify next steps.  
3. Develop, obtain local authorization for and submit a complete Statement of Interest to the Massachusetts 
School Building Authority for Turkey Hill Elementary School by the applicable 2027 deadline.  
4. Strengthen the District’s financial leadership and operational capacity by recruiting and onboarding a 
qualified Director of Finance and Operations, facilitating a comprehensive review of the District’s 
financial systems and establishing clearer responsibilities and more reliable reporting and budget-
development practices.  
 
Members supported the proposed areas of focus and emphasized that Dr. Fortuna should be evaluated on the 
processes within her control rather than on outcomes determined by outside entities, such as acceptance into the 
MSBA program. The Committee also discussed the purpose and structure of professional learning cycles and the 
importance of providing relevant, collaborative professional development. No vote was taken, and the goals 
would return for consideration at a future meeting. 
 
g. Finance Office Staffing 
The Committee discussed the reposting of the finance leadership position under the title Director of Finance and 
Operations, with an annual salary range of $160,000 to $165,000. A comparison of salaries and finance-office 
staffing in surrounding districts indicated that the revised salary was competitive, although members noted that 
regional school districts may have different financial responsibilities and staffing requirements. 
Dr. Fortuna reported that five applications had been received since the position was reposted and that three 
appeared potentially viable, subject to further review. Members discussed the need for an experienced candidate 
with strong financial, accounting, operational, analytical and communication skills who could lead the office, 
assist with implementing recommendations from the independent financial review and evaluate the District’s 
existing policies and procedures. 
The Committee also discussed current staffing levels and the additional financial and human-resources 
responsibilities being handled by central-office employees during the vacancy. Dr. Fortuna reported that she had 
contacted the Massachusetts Association of School Business Officials regarding the possible temporary assistance 
of a retired school-finance professional, who could also support the transition once a permanent director was 
hired. No vote was taken.

Discussion 
b. Resuming Tri-Board Discussion 
The Committee discussed whether resuming the Tri-Board process should remain one of its goals. 
Members expressed concerns that the previous meetings had consisted primarily of presentations, lacked 
meaningful discussion and had not produced a clear or effective budget process. Members agreed to 
remove the Tri-Board process from the School Committee’s goals. The Committee remained open to 
participating in future meetings that had a clearly defined purpose and included meaningful discussion 
regarding the Town’s priorities and values. No vote was taken.
