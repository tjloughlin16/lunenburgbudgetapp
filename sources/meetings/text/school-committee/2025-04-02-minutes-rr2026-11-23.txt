3. Public Comment – Mr. Beardmore Since our last school committee meeting there has been a lot of 
commentary about the roles of the school committee and finance committee and our ability or lack 
thereof to work together.  I come at this from the position of somebody who about six years ago had 
some issues with the school department budget and was encouraged by a finance committee chair to 
volunteer for the finance committee.  I served on the finance committee for three years, my last year 
there as a chair person.  I learned an awful lot during that three year period.  One of the things that I 
recommended immediately when joining that board was that the finance committee entertain 
department heads through the time period prior to what we know as budget season, to get out on the 
table, issues that we though as finance committee members would be pertinent when looking at the 
budget in the coming year.  This would give the department heads opportunities to prepare for what 
we were looking for and it also gave the department heads an opportunity to let the finance 
committee know well in advance of things that might be coming down the pike that may be out of 
the ordinary.  We did that for each of the three years I was on the finance committee and I think it 
served us pretty well.  Also during that time I served as finance committee liaison to the school 
committee and during that three year period with the exception of a very brief period at the end of 
my last year as chair, I watched or attended every school committee meeting.  I think that we have 
lost our way a little bit, when it comes to sharing information and setting expectations between these 
two particular boards.  I would encourage the finance committee to reestablish the position of 
finance committee liaison and to ensure that whoever that representative is, is very active as I was 
during that period of time.  Secondly, there are clear delineations of roles and responsibilities
