Action 
 
a. FY26 Capital Plan Dr. Burnham reports we were awarded a Green Communities Grant of 
$200,000 and additional rebate saving that could be applied to the cost of phase 2 of the mini 
split installation at the Primary School.  There was a discussion of the different ways we 
could secure the remaining funds for the project and it was determined the best resolution is 
to amend the Capital Plan requests for 2026.  Mr. Beardmore makes a motion to endorse 
the town manager and the superintendents plan to modify our request and extend the 
request back to the town manager, Mr. Sculimbrene seconds the motion all approve via 
roll call vote
