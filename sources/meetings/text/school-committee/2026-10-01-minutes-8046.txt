b. Appointment of Temporary Director of Finance and Operations 
 
Dr. Fortuna recommended appointing William Frangiamore as Temporary Director of Finance and 
Operations. She explained that she had contacted MASBO, which provided his contact information 
from its list of retirees available to assist districts. 
Mr. Frangiamore met with the hiring team and brought extensive municipal and school district 
experience, including experience in districts similar in size to Lunenburg. Dr. Fortuna reported that 
he interviewed well, asked pertinent questions about the district’s needs, and was eager to begin. He 
was available to work three days per week, on Mondays, Tuesdays, and Fridays. 
His initial responsibilities would include ensuring that FY2026 was properly closed out, that FY2027 
was built correctly, and tha t day-to-day financial responsibilities were addressed. Dr. Fortuna had 
provided his résumé to the Committee. 
Dr. Fortuna recommended an overlap between Mr. Frangiamore and the eventual permanent Director 
of Finance and Operations so that he could continue  assisting with financial cleanup while the 
permanent director focused on developing the FY2028 budget. 
Chair Brzozoski supported the recommendation, noting that Mr. Frangiamore arrived at the interview 
familiar with the district’s budget and asked specifi c questions addressing its financial challenges. 
She explained that the temporary appointment would also provide coverage while a permanent 
candidate fulfilled any required notice period with a current employer. 
Chair Brzozoski clarified that the Committee  was considering a temporary appointment rather than 
conducting an interview for a permanent administrative position. 
 
A motion was made to approve Mr. Frangiamore as Temporary Director of Finance and 
Operations. Ms. Russell seconded the motion. There was no further discussion. 
Roll call vote: Ms. Russell—yes; Mr. Sculimbrene—yes; Ms. Gilman—yes; Chair Brzozoski—
yes. The motion passed unanimously, 4–0. 
 
Chair Brzozoski confirmed that Dr. Fortuna would handle the contract and had the Committee’s 
approval to hire Mr. Frangiamore.
