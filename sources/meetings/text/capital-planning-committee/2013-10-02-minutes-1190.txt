Ms. Benson noted electronic mail from the DPW Director increasing the cost of Eagle House ADA-compliant ramp construction 
from $25,000.00 to $40,000.00 a fter conferring with David Ross Associates.  He also noted there is a similar issue with the

Ms. Speidel noted she has not yet update d the financial forecast .  There is nothing new at the present time other than the

need to discuss what is Capital and what is not Capital.  She noted the pavement management p lan.  The Town is working with

item is not being requested.  She also suggested, regarding Passios and the High School, that the Committee put in the figure s 
the Director of Building and Grounds had in 2016 going forward so it’s reflected; with a footnote subject to change.   
 
The upcoming schedule was noted.  October 9- School Department, October 16- Police Department, and October 23- DPW.   No
