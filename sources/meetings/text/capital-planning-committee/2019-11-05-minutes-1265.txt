1. Meeting Called to order at 5:04 by Mr. Henshaw
2. Public Comment - None
3. Approval of Minutes: The Committee tabled until next meeting
4. The Committee reviewed the FY21-FY25 Capital Plan requests with the Fire and Police Chiefs
For FY21 the Fire Department has requested:
1. $55,000 to equip one of the ambulances to the Paramedic Level. This includes $32,000 for a cardiac
monitor/defibrillator, $10,000 for ancillary equipment for cardiac monitor, $9,300 for controlled access
pharmaceutical dispenser, $3,500 for initial outlay for paramedic level required medications.
2. $115,000 to replace the remaining Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus. The Town received an earmark in
FY20 for $100,000 to replace ½ of the Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus inventory; this would complete the
entire replacement of inventory and by replacing the rest of the units in FY21 it will keep all units uniform for
interoperability.
3. Due to the $100,000 earmark for Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus in FY20, Capital Request #FD17-05-A
in the amount of $156,000 will be removed from the Capital Plan.
4. The Fire Chief requested Capital Request #FD17-06 for Radio Frequency Forced Migration in the amount of
$200,000 be removed from the Capital Plan as it is not needed at this time.
5. A new capital request (#FD20-01) was added in FY21 for a Mid-Life Overhaul of Squad 3 in the amount of
$65,000. By performing an overhaul of this vehicle, it will prolong the life of this vehicle.
For FY 21 the Police Department has requested:
1. $67,000 for Police vehicle, marked, new: The Police Chief reviewed the rotation of vehicles by officers and

7. A total of 23 other new requests were added to the Police Department's capital plan. These include cruiser
replacements out to 2030, duty gear, taser replacements, portable radios, bulletproof vests, handguns, and a
traffic data recording system replacement.
5. Other Business/Action Items: None
6. Next Meeting is November 19 at 5:00 p.m. at Town Hall.
7. Public Comment:
Ms. Lisio expressed the need to prioritize capital requests with safety issues first then approve requests that would
benefit a large part of the community. This feedback was based on her discussions with other members of the
Town of Lunenburg. One of the members stated that safety has been a deciding factor when approving capital
requests.
8. Meeting adjournment at 6:20 p.m.
