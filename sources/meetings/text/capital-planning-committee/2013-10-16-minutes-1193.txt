Ms. Benson noted difficulty with scheduling the School Department to ensure attendance by both the School Superintendent and 
Director of Grounds.  Committee consensus was to move DPW to October 30, School to November 7, and not hold a meeting on 
October 23. 
 
General Discussion: 
ADA Ramp- Submitted by DPW for the Eagle House.  Concerns with the requested amount changing from $25,000 to $40,000.  
Ms. Benson will notify the DPW Director that this will be a topic of discussion during his  appointment with the Committee.  She 
will also note to him that the Committee is looking for possibilities of out -sourcing the Sweeper.   
The Committee will be looking for specifics to justify requests from all departments.   
 
Fire Department:  Chief Sullivan’s main priority is replacement of Car 2 and Engine 1.   
 
Vehicles: 
Engine 1 :  Twenty-two years old  (1991 model) .  Chief questioned dealership on rebuilds , but was i nformed it is  not 
recommended for pumpers over 15 years old.  New truck would become the primary pumper for fire calls.  Disposition of current 
truck would be trade-in (possibly $15,000-$20,000?).  Committee would like to see quotes for both new  and used; plus photos of 
the current Engine 1.   
 
Car 2:  Car 2 would be r eplacing a ten year old police cruiser , which is u sed as an inspectional vehicle, for investigations and 
support work.  The Police Department does not currently have any used vehicles  that could be passed to the Fire Department .  
Car 2 is c urrently the most heavily used vehicle in the fleet  and has 130,000 – 140,000 miles.  Committee would like him to 
narrow down to type of vehicle he wants  and provide quotes for both new and used.  He will check with John Londa, School 
Facility/Grounds Director, to see what vehicle would qualify under Green criteria .   
 
Secondary to that is the radio infrastructure which was submitted in conjunction with the Police Department.  Police Chief Marino 
went over this at the 10-09-13 Capital Planning Committee meeting.    
 
Facilities:  Redo floors- currently have epoxy with grit and floors are too rough to clean/sanitize.
