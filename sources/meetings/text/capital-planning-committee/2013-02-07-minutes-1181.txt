General Review/Prioritization of Capital Requests-  
 
Reconfiguration of Townsend Hbr Rd & Mulpus Rd Intersection and 4 -way Flasher on Lancaster Ave @ Goodrich St -  
Ms. Benson noted she corresponded with DPW Director John Rodriquenz  regarding the two intersection requests.   She also 
corresponded with Police Chief Marino who noted 12.31.09 to 12.31.12 accident count  of nine at Lancaster Avenue and 
Goodrich Street and 12.31.07 to 12.31.12 accident count of four at Mulpu s Road and Townsend Harbor Road.  Chief Marino 
noted these are only “reported” accidents.  The Committee had inquired of any financial assistance available from Montachusett 
Regional Planning Commission ( MRPC).  Correspondence to the Committee Chair noted that a project usually takes one to two 
years to be included and that the Transportation Improvement Program (TIP) for 2013-2016 has already been approved.  Noted 
process to get a project in the TIP .  Board consensus was to take both requests off the FY201 4 Plan and have them brought 
back in the future with grant funding  information.  Committee also requesting h azardous intersection study for 
Lancaster/Goodrich.   
Unanimous vote to delete both requests from Plan at a total of $65,000, with the stipulation of future consideration. 
 
Town Hall Repainting, Chimney, and Roof Repair -  Ms. Benson requested breakdown from Chair, Historical Commission.  
Chimney - $18,500.  Roof - $40,000.  Painting - $40,000.   
First priority is the chimney at $18,500 for FY2014.  Unanimous vote.   
Roof and painting deleted for FY2014.  ($32,000 minus’d as amounts deleted under prior prioritizations.)    
 
Utility Vehicle- Committee received information noting Chelmsford using new Ford Interceptors.  Ms. Benson’s research notes 
Sedan cost is $26,641.00, Utility cost is $27,432.00 (difference of approximately $800).  Ms. Benson spoke with Chief Marino; he 
noted several sites to pur chase from – can shop around .  Sedan get s average mileage of 16 /gallon with 23 on the highway .  
Utility gets average mileage of 16 /gallon with 21 on the highway.  There is not a lot of info rmation concerning maintenance as 
they are so new.  There is 100,000 miles,  three years guarantee reported (no cost).   Ms. Benson noted reasons the Committee 
has seen and heard from other communities is that the Utility serves departments better i n size.  The back seat of the Sedan is 
narrow with a narrow width  and a short floor depth .  This can be difficult to place a prisoner in, especially if they are combative.   
There is no room in the trunk  after the placement of equipment ; some of it has to be placed in the  front seat.  More importantly, 
the sedan is not a ve hicle for a large individual.  They have difficulty getting out of it quickly which is a safety factor.  The 
Committee prioritizes the Utility over the Sedan .  The Committee de cided not to rec ommend the unmarked car.  Previous 
prioritization noted $45,000 for cruiser.  Cost will note Utility /interceptor for $45,000.  This includes $15,000 for outfitting, $50 00 
of which is a radio required under State law.  Unanimous vote for Interceptor at $45,000.   Plan will not e breakdown - 
Interceptor (base unit) for $30,000, equipment and outfitting mandated by State regulations $15,000.

Demolition of Holman St Cemetery Bldg & West St Park Bldg - Lengthy discussion ensued.  The Committee discussed the 
possibility of selling the property “as is”.  It was noted that the Cemetery Department plans to use the land for storage onc e the 
building is demolished.   
 
Prior to voting on the demolition, Ms. Sha piro made Motion to reconsider the DPW Pickup with Plow, Second, Mr. Sund.  Mr. 
Sund felt the pickup with plow should come before the building demolition.  Ms. Shapiro felt not enough information was avail able 
for demolition.   
 
Motion, Mr. Raboin, to vote  on removing DPW Pickup with Plow from FY14 Plan, Second, Ms. Shapiro, Mr. Raboin, aye; Mr. 
Mannone Jr., aye; Mr. Sund, aye; Ms. Shapiro, aye; Ms. Benson, nay.  DPW Pickup with Plow removed from Plan.  
 
Discussion continued on demolition.  Mr. Mannone Jr. felt not enough information was available.  Mr. Raboin felt they needed to
