3. The committee determined the priority of the 12 capital improvement requests we recommend funding in FY17.
• Based on the governor's budget that was recently released, Kerry estimated that the Town's capital
budget in FY17 will be between $600,000 and $700,000.
• Requests will be funded in order of priority and, at this level, the Town could fund the nine highest

If more funds become available, the Town may be able to fund priorities 11 and/or 12.
Dennis M. made a motion to shift the asbestos abatement for Turkey Hill School to priority 11, ahead of
the all-terrain vehicle request. Matt A, seconded the motion. The committee approved the motion by a
