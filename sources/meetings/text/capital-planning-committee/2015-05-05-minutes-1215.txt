Minutes May 5, 2015 Meeting
Meeting Posted:
Yes
Location:
Bilotta Meeting Room, 2nd Floor, Town Hall
Present:
Kerry Speidel, Dennis Mannone, Jr., Heather Sroka, Jamie Toale, John Henshaw
Absent:
Joanna Bilotta-Simeone
1. Meeting called to order by Jamie Toale at 4:02 pm.
2. No public comment.
3. Minutes: Heather made a motion to approve the minutes of the March 31 meeting, Dennis seconded the
motion, and the committee voted 4-0 to approve the minutes.
4. John Londa described potential plans for Turkey Hill Middle School (THMS) and what that would entail. The
main points:
a. THMS is now 46 years old and the threshold for a decision about renovation or replacement of
buildings are usually when they are 50 years old.
• Major areas that would need to be renovated to prolong the useful life of THMS are the
roof, and the unit ventilators.
• Structurally the building is sound.
• John estimated that renovation would take three years if THMS were in use during the
renovation, and 18 months if it were not occupied. TCP could support three grades during
construction.
• The primary concern is the spray-on asbestos, and that could be abated while the building
was being renovated.
b. Limited resources available for capital projects are a constraint.
• Kerry pointed out that Town Hall was a project that would need to be addressed soon.
• Town Meeting would decide how to use any unexpended balance in the contingency
account for the new Junior-Senior high school project.
5. Public Comment: John Londa said the reason the pipes in the library froze was lack of circulation of the
water.
6. Adjournment: John made a motion to adjourn the meeting; Heather seconded the motion and the
committee approved the motion 4-0. The meeting adjourned at 5:29.
