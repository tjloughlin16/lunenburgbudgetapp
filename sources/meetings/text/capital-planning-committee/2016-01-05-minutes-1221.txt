Background:
There is still $17,000 unexpended balance from the FY15 capital
funding for the first phase of the multi-year project to abate the
asbestos in Turkey Hill Middle School [THMS]. This money
would be applied to subsequent phases of the project.
• Since the School's FY15 capital requests, the scope of the
project they to be funded as capital projects has narrowed to
the friable asbestos over the ceiling tiles.
• The other asbestos abatement (e.g. floors, and the frames
around the windows) would become a part of the project when
THMS is renovated.
The current request:
• John Londa explained that the state told the Town that we need
to abate the asbestos in THMS and that the Town must pay for
this abatement. The state said the Town shall have this done as
funds to pay for it are available.
• This request is the first of three remaining stages in this part of
the THMS asbestos abatement project. The three phases total
$434,000. Each phase would be completed during summer
break and is a stand-alone project independent of the other
phases. If the Town funds this phase it would not commit
funding of subsequent phases in successive years as laid out in
the School Department's capital requests.
The phone system has an expected life of 15 years: When the plan
is to renovate THMS the phone system would be replaced. Total
costs over the 15-year life of this system of either alternative
approach is more expensive.
The current system old and in poor repair, (e.g. another amplifier
has been Gerry-rigged for power). It is not capable supporting the
ALICE concept for student security, which the department is
migrating to.
