December 11, 2013 
 
Meeting Posted:  Yes 
Place: Ritter Memorial Building, 960 Massachusetts Avenue  
Time: 9:30 AM 
Present:  Chair, Marion M. Benson, Jamie Toale, Steve Raboin, Colleen Shapiro, Dennis Mannone, Jr. 
 
12-04-13 minutes approved. 
 
The Committee is requesting the Town Manager’s presence at its December 18 th meeting.  Meeting time will be changed to 
9:00AM.   
 
At its December 4 th meeting, the Committee  requested the Fire Chief provide actual vehicle firefighting usage for the past five 
years.  Data provided for calendar years 2007-2012 for Engine 1, Squad 3 and Engine 4; broken out by call type.   
 
Ritter HVAC added to the Prioritization as #21.   
 
School Technology revised- Priority 1- from $30,000 to $40,000; Priority 2- from $35,000 to $25,000. 
 
Committee reiterated that the DPW Street Sweeper would remain a Priority 3.  
 
Committee will hold off on taking its vote on the Capital Plan FY 2015, pending Town Manager comments.  
 
Committee will tentatively plan to meet in January to take a formal vote and prepare a comment sheet for Town Meeting. 
 
Adjourned 10:45 AM.
