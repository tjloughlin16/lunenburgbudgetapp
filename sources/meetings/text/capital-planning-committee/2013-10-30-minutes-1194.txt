General Discussion:  Committee viewed Town-wide summarization sheet.  Edits will be made.   
Town Manager  noted she  requested DPW Director to identify 1) when things need to be replaced and , 2) then work on

Department of Public Works:  Jack Rodriquenz, DPW Director presented.   
 
Vehicles- 
Street Sweeper, FY15- $180,000- Occurs Town-wide spring into summer.  Sand in roadway, danger to traffic and detrimental to 
drainage with serious environmental impact.  One person dedicated to sweeper and one person dedicated  with dump truck  to 
follow sweeper.  Mr. Rodriquenz opined that o utsourcing is not financial feasible.  Would be approximately $40- $50,000 to do 
each street once and anything else would cost extra.  Cost is only for sweeper and operator.  Cannot rent a sweeper by itself ; 
have to also pay for the operator.  Town would still have to supply dump truck and operator.  It cost approximately $10,000 to 
repair for last year.  Maintenance costs were discussed.  Mr. Rodriq uenz noted that a one -year warranty would cover basic 
maintenance, but would probably request a five-year warranty.   
Asphalt Roller , FY15- $35,000- Would be replacing a 1968 model taken out of service three years ago.  Have been renting 
since at approximately $7,000/ yr., but not cost effective.  As a necessity, r ental cost currently built into DPW operating budget.  
Ms. Speidel noted possibility of lease/purchase financing  and pay through operat ing budget.  Cannot do same with Street 
Sweeper as not in operating budget.    
 
Facilities-   
Updated Facilities Five Year Plan provided: 
Eagle House,  ADA Ramp , FY15 - from $25,000 to $40,000 .  Figure includes excavation of banking and concrete ramp and 
railings set to ADA standards.  Will need an engineer’s design.  Discussion ensued.  Possible sidewalk down Memorial Drive?  A 
sidewalk would not have ADA slope issues.  Is a ramp required?  Who se request (Eagle House)?  Original intent proposed by 
previous Eagle House Director was to make upper parking adjacent to Teen Center accessible to s eniors from the lower parking 
lot next to the Eagle House.  Will have more discussion with Eagle House Director at November 13th meeting.
