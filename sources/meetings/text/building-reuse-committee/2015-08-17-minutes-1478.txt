Building Re-Use Committee
Meeting Minutes 8/17/2015
Members Present: Jamie Toale, Ron Albert, Damon McQuaid, Dave MacDonald, Mark Erickson
Members Absent: Rebecca Lantry, Mike Mackin,
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order August 17, 2015 @ 7:04pm
Public Comment:
None
Current Business:
Interview with DRA
Kenneth Best AlA was present to represent DRA. He went over their RFQ package.
He fielded questions from the board in regards to the scale of previous projects,
findings from similar projects and if he felt that the proposed fee was appropriate for the
scope of work.
Interview with Tappe Associates
Christopher Blessen and Damian Liddiard stood in for Charlie Hay who could not attend
the meeting. They provided a presentation of previous work and fielded similar
questions as were asked of DRA.
Discussion of Qualifications
After a brief discussion of initial reactions the individual board members filled out the
required spreadsheets to be recorded with the town. The results were as follows
DRA 77.5 pts.
Tappe 80pts.
M. Erickson-
DRA 87.5pts.
Tappe 90pts.
Tappe 85pts.
D. MacDonald- DRA 57.5pts.
D. McQuaid - DRA 77.5pts
The board then discussed why they ranked the applicants the way they did
McQuaid cited that the presentation quality and utilization of emerging technologies by Tappe
has the potential to be a valuable asset to the town.
R. Albert noted that both firms were qualified to do the requested work but Tappe had a slight
edge.
Dave MacDonald made a motion to recommend moving forward with Tappe Associates. Mark
Erickson second. All in favor.
