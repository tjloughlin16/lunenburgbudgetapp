The Planning board has drawn up a working draft and will host public discussions to solicit

discussion. Multiple members of the Reuse committee expressed the desire for a joint
meeting with the school committee to discuss the location of the property line.

Jamie and Mark have been working to compile cost/revenue data on all town buildings.
