Building Re-Use Committee
Meeting Minutes 11/24/2015
Members Present: Jamie Toale, Ron Albert, Damon McQuaid, Mike Mackin, Mark Erickson
Members Absent: Dave MacDonald, Rebecca Lantry,
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order November 24, 2015 @ 6:32PM
Public Comment:
None
Minutes Approval:
Motion to approve minutes from 7/6/2015, 8/8/2015, 8/17/2015, 9/21/215 (M. Mackin) 2° (M.
Erickson) All in favor
Current Business:
Review progress on feasibility study by Tappe Associates.
Charlie Hay of lappe Associates presented two hand outs labeled "Lunenburg
Municipal Study 11/03/15 one consisting of graphic depictions of 4 options for reuse
and the other an evaluation matrix to rate each option. Option "4" a renovation and
partial demolition of the Passios building was the highest rated option according to the
matrix.
Ron Albert suggested the idea of showing option an "4A" that would leave the entire
Passios building intact for future use or use by the FLAC collaborative.
Tappe associates asked that the board make a preliminary ruling on which option to
move torward with so that they could tocus their most detailed cost estimating on one
option. The motion was made to recommend option 4. (R. Albert) 2n° (M. Mackin) all in
favor.
The Committee also gave Tappe Associates direction to do a cost estimate to stabilize
the Town Hall exterior only.
Draft Q&A's about building reuse
Mark Erickson generated a list of anticipated questions that the committee should be
able to answer (See attached hand out) the committee members were instructed to take
the list home and attempt to develop responses.
Discuss the use of the Passios Building by FLAC
See above discussion of option 4A. The recommendation of Tappe Associates was that
the cost vs. return of operating and maintaining additional square footage is not
favorable. The consensus of the board was that we would keep the option open pending
more information.
