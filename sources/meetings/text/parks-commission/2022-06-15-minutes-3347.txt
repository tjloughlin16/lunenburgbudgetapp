Next item on the agenda, Update on meeting with the Water District. 
K. Menard gave the commission an update and stated herself and D. Passios visited the Water District to discuss the parcel 
that abuts McNally field, and the potential for the Parks Commission to use the parcel. The Water District seemed 
interested in looking at ideas for use. K. Menard indicated they will revisit the Water District next month.

D. Passios noted the Water District has no desire to give up the ownership of the property but are interested in partnering 
with the Parks Commission. 
Next on the agenda, Request for Bench Donation 
Karen Blocher requested via email the placement of a memorial bench at the Town Beach in honor of her sister Regina 
McGee and asked what the process was. 
K. Menard had replied back to her that she would discuss with the commission as there was no current process. K. 
Menard asked if the Commission was interested in a program. 
C. Sullivan expressed concerns with the room for benches. A. Lockwood suggests looking at putting a policy in place. 
P. Hakey suggested replacing an existing bench in need of repair with a commemorative bench. 
K. Menard indicated she will research and see how this can work for Lunenburg. 
Next item on the agenda, Skate Park discussion including location change to match master plan design. 
Present for this discussion: Anthony Caiozzo and Greg Roy of Dillis and Roy. G. Roy pulled up the Marshall Park Master 
Plan design with the location of the Skate Park overlay and explained the impacts of the skate park going in at the planned 
location: 
●The playground equipment would be impacted and need to be relocated. 
●Underground electric feed to transformer runs under the Skate Park. 
●The Skate Park location would eliminate the existing gravel access driveway. 
The commission discussed the relocation of the playground equipment. K. Menard explained the equipment would need 
to be brought up to code if relocated. A. Lockwood expressed concern with removing equipment and not having a plan/
timeline for replacement. C. Sullivan stated he would be comfortable with a short term loss for the long term gain. 
C. Sullivan suggested possibly moving the Skate Park down and relocating the sidewalk. 
D. Passios suggested leaving the design alone and work out the logistics for the skate park.  
P. Hakey noted he was not concerned about the electric line under the concrete. 
K. Menard told the commission that since the selected design has the skate park in a different location that the 
commission would need to rescind and revote on the new location.  
K. Menard echoed the statement, “a short term loss for the long term gain”. 
G. Roy noted the temporary loss of the playground is substituted by the Skate Park, which will be putting in a quarter 
million dollars into Marshall Park. 
  
D. Passios made a motion to rescind the approval vote for the location of the Skate Park that was approved on November 
22, 2021, and vote to support and recommend the location that is in the presently accepted Master Plan created by WOLA 
and the conditions set in the November 22, 2021, carry to the new location. P . Hakey seconded.  
Roll Call Vote: 
K. Menard - yes 
C. Sullivan - yes 
D. Passios - yes 
A. Lockwood - yes 
P . Hakey - yes

Next item on the agenda, Marshall Park Graffiti 
K. Menard said that Chris Sullivan had shared some photos of a graffiti incident we had at Marshall Park. She asked R. 
Thompson if the issue was resolved. R. Thompson indicated the DPW is aware of the graffiti and is working with the 
schools to resolve the issue. K. Menard asked R. Thompson to also notify the Police Chief. D. Passios noted the graffiti is 
not overly visible. 
Next item on the agenda, Marshall Park Public Urination 
K. Menard stated players using the 90 foot diamond are not using the Little League portable toilets. K.Menard spoke to 
the Police Chief regarding this and confirmed there were laws around this. 
K,Menard suggested R. Thompson sends a reminder to the school groups, who are using the field. K.Menard suggested R. 
Thompson speak with Joe Ruth, Phillies manager and offer the option to place an additional portable toilet on site. 
Next item on the agenda, Marshall Park Master Planning 
K. Menard explained the plan to include the overall plan, phases, funding and steps to secure funding. 
D. Passios suggested having conversations with the user groups before meeting with the Select Board. 
The Commission will meet with the Select Board August 2, 2022. 
The Commission discussed the order of the phases they would like to see for Marshall Park. 
D. Passios noted the wetlands flagging will be done next week, per Matt Moreau. D. Passios discussed with K. Ostermier 
doing the schematic design for Marshall Pond picnic area. A. Lockwood suggested funding phases 1, 3 and 4. A. 
Lockwood suggested asking the Town Manager for the funding to get the schematic design done. 
K. Menard stated she will go to the Town Manager and ask for the full funding for the entire schematic design fo the Park. 
A. Lockwood suggested an independent article at Town Meeting if this is not in Capital Planning. 
A.Lockwood asked about using the remaining funding for the “pocket park”. D. Passios believes  the continued 
engineering cost is an extension of the Master Plan and possibly expand the scope of work for WOLA and ask K. 
Ostermier. 
Next item on the agenda Marshall Park Survey 
K. Menard indicated she would reach out to the Town Manager and Assistant Town Manager to get information on the 
scope of work. K. Menard noted she asked Bill Bernard, DPW Director, if he could help with this. K.Menard noted they 
are required to get 3 quotes, no RFP is required, and the money will be available July 1, 2022. A. Lockwood stressed they 
need to work on it and move it forward. D. Passios stated he had a brief discussion with Matt Marro and he should be able 
to do the wetland flagging. 
Next item on the agenda Marshall Park septic/sewer 
D. Passios noted there was sewer add into the WOLA quote. D. Passios is in favor of the septic  with a 2 seat toilet 
facility. K. Menard noted it meets an immediate need. 
D. Passios made a motion that the Parks Commission commit to installing the 2 toilet septic that the Board of Health 
offered to fund and match the location shown on the Master Plan from WOLA. A. Lockwood seconded.
