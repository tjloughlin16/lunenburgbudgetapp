LUNENBURG PARK COMMISSION
Minutes for December 16, 2011 meeting
Meeting called to order at 5:45 pm
Present: Jeff Johnson, Tom Leppala, Bob Robuccio, Cliff Worthley, Pete Patno
The meeting was scheduled to discuss Jr Basketball custodial pay to the school and see if we
could come up with a way to pay the Jr Basketball director something for his work. Pete Patno,
JI Basketball director, asked that we could forego any payment to him and instead use that money
to help pay the custodial fees. Jeff brought up the fact that Petes' position is six months of
planning and running the league and should be compensated at some point, but we appreciate his
offer and will accept it for at least one more year.
Voted 3-0 to pay the custodians out of the Park Expenses line in our budget and any additional
will come out of our revolving account.
Voted 3-0 to forego paying the Jr Basketball Director for at least this year.
Voted 3-0 to accept the minutes from the last meeting
Meeting adjourned at 7:10
