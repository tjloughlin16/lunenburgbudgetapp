Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
CONTINUANCE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
In accordance with the Open Meeting Law, please be advised that this meeting  will be 
broadcast live through local access cable, on Facebook Live on the Public Access Facebook 
page https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-157427270975035/) 
and will be able to be found on the Lune burg Public Access YouTube channel with in 24 
hours after the meeting.  
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, December 27, 2023 at 7: 00 P.M. at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 
2nd Floor, Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 
01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of  Raymond & Gloria Clouthier, 438 
Sugar Road, Bolton, MA  01740.   The property which is the subject of this case is owned 
by the Clouthier Family Realty Trust and located 37 Pearl Street, Lunenburg, MA 
01462. 
 
The applicants are seeking a Special Permit to  raze the existing structure and construct a 
new dwelling on the property.   
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit.  The basis for 
the Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Code of Lunenburg, §250-7.2 B & 7.4 A (1).  
 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in 
person, by agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town 
Clerk’s office or the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
