Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, June 12, 2019  at 7:00 P.M. at the Lu nenburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor, 
Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Scott & Nicole Kirouac, 32 Pearl Street, 
Lunenburg, MA  01462 .  The property which is the subject of this case  is located at 32 Pearl 
Street, Lunenburg, MA  01462.   Assessors Map 122-38. 
 
The petitioners are seeking a Special Permit for dimensional variation to construct  an addition 
that exceeds the height and side yard requirements. 
 
If you wish to appear in support  for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by 
agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or 
the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
