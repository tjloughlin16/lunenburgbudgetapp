LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, May 27, 2015 at 7:00 P.M.  at the Town Hall, Joseph F. Bilotta 
Meeting Room, 2nd Floor, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition o f Empire Management, 171 Great 
Road, Acton, MA  01720  and known as North End Subaru and North End Mazda .  
The property, which is the subject of this case, is located at 739, 747, 757 and 777 Chase 
Road,  Lunenburg, MA  01462.   
 
The petitioners are seeking a Special Permit Modifi cation for the proposed construction 
of an eight (8) bay service garage between the two existing auto dealerships and pave an 
existing gravel vehicle storage lot located at 739 Chase Road to the south of North End 
Mazda dealership. 
  
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizi ng the Special Permit.  The basis for 
this Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Lune nburg Protective By-law, Section 
4.6.3. If you wish to appear in support for or in o pposition to the petition, you may do so 
in person, by agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be viewed in the Town 
Clerk’s office or the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
