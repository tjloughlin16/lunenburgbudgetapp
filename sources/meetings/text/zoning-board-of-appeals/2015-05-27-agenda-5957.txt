Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, May 27, 2015 at 8:00 P.M.  at the Town Hall, Joseph F. Bilotta 
Meeting Room, 2nd Floor, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition o f Richard A. Bursch, LTN Realty 
Trust, 308 Electric Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462.    The property, which is the subject 
of this case, is located at 270 Electric Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462.   
 
The petitioner is seeking a Special Permit for the construction and operation of a “Self 
Storage Facility”.  It will consist of indoor units  for rent to the general public for the 
purpose of storage.  There will be a small office f or the management and operations of 
the facility.   
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizi ng the Special Permit.  The basis for 
this Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Lune nburg Protective By-law, Section 
4.6.3.1.j). If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do 
so in person, by agent or by attorney.  Copies of t he application may be viewed in the 
Town Clerk’s office or the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
