LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
CONTINUANCE 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, September 27, 2017 at 7 :00 P.M. at the Town Hall, 2nd Floor 
Conference Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Robert Barry, 191 Cross Road, 
Lunenburg, MA  01462.  The property which is the subject of this case is located at 191 
Cross Road, Lunenburg, MA  01462 and owned by Robert D. & Lisa A. Barry. 
 
The petitioner is seeking a Special Permit for Dimensional Variation to install a 23.04 
Kw ground mount solar array on an 8.2 acre parcel.  
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit.  The basis for 
this Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Lunenb urg Protective By -law, Section  
8.3.2.1.b)4. If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may 
do so in person, by agent or by attorney.
