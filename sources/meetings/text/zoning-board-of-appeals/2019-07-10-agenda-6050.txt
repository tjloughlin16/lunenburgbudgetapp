Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, July 10, 2019 at 7:15 P.M. at the Lun enburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor, 
Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Thomas A. Reynolds & Danielle T. 
Thompson, 129 Peninsula Drive, Lunenburg, MA  01462.    The property which is the subject 
of this case is located at 129 Peninsula Drive, Lunenburg, MA  01462. Assessors Map 42-16. 
 
The petitioners are seeking a Special Permit to construct a two car garage and workshop.  The  
setback requirement is 40 ’ in the R-A Zoning district .  The proposed structure is approximately 
30’ back from the front property line which requires a Special Permit for dimensional variation 
as provided in §250-5.2 A(1) of the Code of Lunenburg.    
 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by 
agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or 
the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
