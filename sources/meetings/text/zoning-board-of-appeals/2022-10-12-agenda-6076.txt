Notice of Public Hearing 
Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
Notice is hereby given that t he Zoning Board of Appeals will hold a public hearing on October 12, 2022 
at 7:00 P.M. at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor, Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, 
Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Pond View Commons Comprehensive Permit, 
LLC, Pond View Commons, LLC, The Homes at Aro Estates, LLC, owner Kevin O’Brien, 18 Cassimere 
Street, Andover, MA 01810.  The property, which is the subject of this case, is located at 318, 390, & 400 
Howard Street, Lunenburg, MA  01462; known as Pond View Commons , Assessors Map 6, 17, & 
17; Parcel 24, 20, & 34 respectively. 
 
The applicant is seeking 40B approval for a Comprehensive Permit for the purpose of developing a two 
hundred (200) unit apartment community at 318, 390, & 400 Howard Street, Lunenburg, MA  01462.   
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Comprehensive Permit, 40B.  If you wish to 
appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by agent or by attorney.  
Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or the Zoning Board office during 
normal business hours. 
 
This meeting will broadcast live through local access cable, on FacebookLive on the Public Access 
Facebook page ( https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-157427270975305/) and 
will be abl e to be found on the Lunenburg Public Access YouTube  channel within 24 hours after the 
meeting.  To participate remotely: from a computer, please use the link below to join the public webinar:  
https://us02web.zoom.us/j/7927370353 or from a telephone, dial the toll free number (888) 475-4499 and 
enter meeting ID:  7927370353.
