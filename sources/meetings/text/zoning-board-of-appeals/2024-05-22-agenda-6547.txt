The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Timothy J. Loughlin, 207 Rolling 
Acres Road, Lunenburg, MA  01462.  The property, which is the subject of this case, is 
located at 207 Rolling Acres Road, Lunenburg, MA 0146.  Assessors Map 70/26. 
 
The applicant is seeking a Special Permit for Dimensional Variation to construct a farmer’s 
porch.  Section 250-5.2A.(1) requires all setbacks for building be located forty (40) feet 
from the front property line.   
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit.  If you wish to 
appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by agent or 
by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or the 
Zoning Board office during normal business hours.
