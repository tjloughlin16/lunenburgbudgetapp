Notice of Public Hearing 
 
 
 
Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that t he Zoning Board of Appeals will hold a public hearing on 
September 28, 2022 at 7 :00 P.M.  at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor, Joseph F. 
Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Daniel & Jean Proctor, 1529 
Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.  The property, which is the subject of this case, 
is located at 1325 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462 owned by Daniel & 
Jean Proctor.  Map 62 Parcel 3. 
 
The applicants are seeking a Special Permit for a contractor yard per Section 250 -4.1 G.  
In addition:  A Special Permit for a dimensional variance to vary the regulation of 
Section 250 -4.9 E. (6) Natural vegetation.  No more than 30% of existing natural 
vegetation on any lot may be disturbed in an underlying residential district, nor more 
than 50% of existing natural vegetation on any lot may be disturbed in an underlying 
nonresidential district. (Proposed 90% disturbance). 
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit.  If you wish 
to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by agent 
or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or 
the Zoning Board office during normal business hours.
