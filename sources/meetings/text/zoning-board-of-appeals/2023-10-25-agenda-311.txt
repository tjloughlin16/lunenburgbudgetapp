Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public hearing on 
Wednesday, October 25, 2023 at 7:00 P.M. at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor, Joseph F. Bilotta 
Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01 462.  Public hearing also available by 
ZOOM remote in accordance with the requirements of the Open Meeting Law. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of David Butler, 87 Carriage Court, Apt. 208 , 
Leominster, MA 01453.   The property which is the s ubject of this  case is located at 56 Pine Street , 
Lunenburg MA  01462.  Assessors Map 63-16. 
 
The petitioner is seeking a Special Permit for the demolition and reconstruction of a new single family 
home that does not conform to the current Zoning By -law requirements. This action was  previously 
approved July 28, 2020, the Special Permit has since expired. 
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit. The basis for this Appeal for 
Special Permit is found in the Lunenburg Protective By-law, Section 250-7.4A. 
 
This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, on Facebook Live on the Public Access 
Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg -Public-Access-Cable-157427270975305/) and 
will be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel within 24 hours after the meeting.
