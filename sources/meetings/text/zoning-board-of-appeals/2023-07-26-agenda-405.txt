NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, July 26, 2023 @ 7:15 PM . at the Town Hall, 2nd Floor, Joseph 
F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, on Facebook Live on the Public 
Access Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg -Public-Access-Cable-
157427270975305/) and will be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel  
within 24 hours after the meeting.  
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Greg & Terry Smolka, 15 Cliffview Terrace, 
Lunenburg, MA  01462.   The property which is the subject of this case owned by 15 Cliffview 
LLC, 33 Orion Way, Groton, MA 01450 and is located at 15 Cliffview Terrace, Lunenburg MA  
01462.  Assessors Map 30-36. 
 
The petitioner hereby makes application as follows: Appealing as persons aggrieved by the ruling 
in the March 30, 2023 letter from Casey Burlingame that short term rentals are prohibited in the 
Residence A Zoning District.
