Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, February 9, 2022 @ 7:00 P.M. at the Lunenburg  Town Hall, 
2nd Floor, Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 
01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of  John & Cathy Beale, 116 Island 
Road, Lunenburg, MA 01462 . The property which is the subject of this case  is located 
at 12 Horizon Island Road, Lunenburg, MA  01462.  Assessors Map 24/22. 
 
The applicants are seeking Special Permit approval to vary the dimensional requirements 
to add an addition to the existing garage and to construct a porch on the front of the house 
and expand the deck on the southerly side of the house.    
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Spe cial Permit authorization.  
The basis for the  Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Code of Lunenburg, §250-
8.3B (2) (d) and Section §250-5.2 A (1).  
 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in 
person, by agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town 
Clerk’s office or the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
