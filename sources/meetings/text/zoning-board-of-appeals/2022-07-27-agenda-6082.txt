LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, July 27, 2022 at 7 :00 P.M. at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd 
Floor Conference Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Peter Cowley, 72 Peninsula 
Drive, Lunenburg, MA  01462.  
 
The property  which is the subject of this case is located at 72 Peninsula Drive,  
Lunenburg, MA  01462, and owned by Peter Cowley. 
 
The petition  is an appeal for  a person aggrieved by the Building Inspector’s 
determination dated May 12, 2022  with regard to a fence erected by the owners and the 
Building Inspectors order to reduce the height of two fence panels.    
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider the petition,  The basis for this Appeal for  
persons aggrieved is found in the Lunenburg Protective By-law, as per §250 6.6 O (1).  If 
you wish to appear in support for or in opposition  to the petition, you may do so in 
person, by agent or by attorney.
