Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
In accordance with the Open Meeting Law, please be advised that this meeting will be 
broadcast live through local access cable, on Facebook Live on the Public Access Facebook 
page https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-157427270975035/) 
and will be able to be found on the Lune burg Public Access YouTube channel within 24 
hours after the meeting.  
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, December 13, 2023 at 7: 30 P.M. at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 
2nd Floor, Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 
01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Daniel Proctor, 1529 Massachusetts 
Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462.   The property which is the subject of this case is owned 
by Bowerbank 6 LLC, 15 29 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg,  MA 01462  and 
located at 1295 and 1315 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  014632 
 
The applicant is seeking a Special Permit for a Shopping Center per §250-4.1k (20).  Also 
seeking a Special Permit for Dimensional Variation to vary the requirement of Section 250-
6.5 F.(4)(b)”The sign shall be a minimum of twenty-five (25) feet from the front property 
line and a minimum of 20 feet from the side property line.  ( Proposed: 5-feet to the front 
property line, 102-feet to the side property line). 
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit for dimensional 
variation.  The basis for the Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Code of Lunenburg, 
§250-4.1k (20). & §250-6.5.F. (4) (b).  
 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in 
person, by agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspecte d in the Town 
Clerk’s office or the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
