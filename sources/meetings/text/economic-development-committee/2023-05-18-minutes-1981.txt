Adam Burney (Land Use Director), Cathy Clark (Economic Development Chair), Jeanne Dupre (Economic Development Member), Joeci Gilchrest (Lunenburg Business Association), Maryrae Holman (Agricultural Commission) Anna Lockwood (Open Space Representative).

PUBLIC COMMENT
No comment.

TOWN OFFICIAL MEET &amp; GREET
Joeci Gilchrest met with the LBA (Lunenburg Business Association) and discussed hosting an economic meet and greet between local businesses. Similar to the economics committee the LBA has shown interest in creating this event. The Lunenburg diner offered their diner as a potential location. Maryrae Holman also suggested hosting at the farm at Low Places Ranch. There was a discussion about narrowing down the potential date. From the committee discussion, it seems as though a Tuesday or Thursday night for a couple of hours would be ideal. 

Joeci Gilchrest will be in contact with Adam Burney to discuss which town officials may be available to attend. 

MASTER PLAN UPDATE
The elected consultant has started collecting data. The fieldwork of walking around the village district, parks, and major roads to get a feel of what is here in town has started. A kick-off meeting is scheduled for June 15th to introduce the consultants and talk about the master plan.
