• Upcoming Grant Opportunities for Lunenburg Discussion with Kelly Brown 
o Coming in February 2024: "Program Opportunity Notice" (AKA Green Communities 
Grant Application). 
• Grants are split into Spring and Fall "blocks". Fall blocks are more popular for 
timelines (budgeting meetings, school availability during summer). Funding is 
targeted for a 50/50 split. A maximum of 1 grant per year is granted per town 
(up to $200k). Application due Feb 7th for Spring block. Application due April 12 
for Fall block. 
• To apply for new grants, current projects must be finalized (streetlight project - 
on track to finalize this week)  
• There are some new aspects to the application 
  
• Introduction of New Decarbonization Grants with Kelly Brown 
o Larger project funding up to $500,000 with 25% municipal match. Criteria includes 
"fossil-free" buildings. If this grant is awarded, the town is not eligible for other grants 
for a few years. 
  
• Potential Projects Q&A with Kelly Brown 
o David Blatt: Are grants moving from LED lighting towards heating/cooling? Water 
Heaters? 
• Kelly Brown: LED lighting is still funded but at a reduced amount. Lunenburg 
eligible for $200,000 grant (interior non municipal lighting). 
• Other fund possibilities: 
▪ Not funding fossil fuel replacements or window replacements.  
▪ Fire dept/police can combine infrared with heat pumps due special 
circumstances, including firetruck interior/exterior areas that are not 
feasible for only heat pump. 
▪ HVAC application can to be combined with weatherization. Heat pump 
applications require weatherization - whether assessment indicates no 
additional can be added, or what will be added. Partial project 
applications can be submitted if exceeds grant amount and exceeds are 
funded by the town. 
o Facilities and DPW Potential Projects: 
• Bill Bernard: Are electric pumps for sewer systems eligible for grants? Quotes 
are already provided, timeline is as soon as possible. 
▪ Kelly Brown: Yes, still allowed under program. On the application: 10hp 
or less is a prescriptive project. Great than 10hp is a traditional project.
