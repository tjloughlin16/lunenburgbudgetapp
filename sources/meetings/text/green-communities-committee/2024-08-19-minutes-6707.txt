Public Comment Tyler Proteau (84 Robbs Hill Road) presents Styrofoam improvement proposal 
plan via slide deck. Current recycling issues (provided by Harvey). Harvey 
separates waste, unknown final location of expanded polystyrene (styrofoam). 
35% of landfills are Styrofoam. Possible improvement (Federal, state, local 
funding possible, more research needed. 100k state funding likely eligible): 
Create styrofoam collection points to recycle styrofoam. Requires community 
education/outreach. 
 
David Blatt: What is the collection point? 
Tyler: Could be large bins, or a drop-off point at school, public safety building. 
Chris: Would we use the town trash bags for these? 
Tyler: Different bags, or just the materials 
David Blatt: Would we bring it to the recycling company, or would they pick it 
up? 
Tyler: Likely bring it, unless grant available 
Kim Duffy: Concerns include creating large enough scale for citizens to use, 
reliable company that ensures recycling. Home-compostable materials vs facility-
compostable materials for utensils, etc. 
Dave Passios: Where is the closest facility? 
Tyler: Worcester, MA or Leicester, MA 
Dave Blatt: Previously the town had a cardboard dumpster that kept the materials 
from blowing away. Also interested in large scale, pickup at a location for 
styrofoam recycling. 
Dave Passios: Recommends finding a successfully implemented styrofoam 
recycling plan in another town or contact Hannaford or other local business
