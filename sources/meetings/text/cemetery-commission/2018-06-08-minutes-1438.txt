Minutes
Cemetery Commission Meeting
June 8, 2018
Cemetery Building
75 Holman St.
The Cemetery Commission met at 4 PM on June 8, 2018 with a representative from
Monty Tech to gather information on the construction of a new garage at the site of the
old garage on Holman St. Discussion followed and Chairman May called for a motion to
pursue a cost estimate from Monty Tech Vocational High School for the construction of
a new 2 bay garage that is to be built by the students from Monty Tech Vocational High
School. Commissioner Tyler made the motion and Commissioner Mendoza seconded his
motion and upon no further discussion, the motion passed unanimously.
Meeting was adjourned at 6:00 PM
Respectfully submitted
