Town of Lunenburg 
  
 
Cemetery Commission 
 
 
William Tyler , Chairman 
Lorraine Mendoza, Vice- Chairman 
 
 
Minutes 
Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Tuesday, Jan 22, 2019 6:00 PM 
Lunenburg Public Library  
Conference Room 
1023 Mass Ave 
Lunenburg Ma. 
 
Meeting was called to order at 6:00 pm by Chairman Tyler, also present was 
Vice-Chairman Mendoza.  
 
Nancy Foss Yasko was present as she is seeking the appointment to fill the 
vacancy left by the passing of former Chairman Lance May. 
 
Discussion ensued involving duties of a Commissioner and the procedure for the 
appointment. 
 
Commissioner Tyler and Mendoza agreed that Nancy Foss Yasko would be a 
great addition to the Commission and will recommend that she fill the remainder 
of the late Lance May’s term until the next Town election. 
 
Meeting was recessed to reconvene at the Town Hall for an appointment with the 
BOS at 7:15 pm 
 
Chairman Tyler informed the BOS that it is the recommendation of the 
Commission to appoint Nancy Foss Yasko to the Cemetery Commission to fill the 
remainder of the term of the late Lance May until the next Town election. Ms. 
Yasko introduced herself and gave the BOS her reasons for wanting to join the 
Commission. 
 
On a motion by Selectman Adams, seconded by Chairman Tyler to appoint 
Nancy Foss Yasko to the Cemetery Commission until the next Town election. 
With a joint vote between the BOS and the Cemetery Commission is was 
unanimously voted to appoint Nancy Foss Yasko until the next Town election. 
 
With no further business before the Commission, meeting was adjourned at 8:15 
pm.
