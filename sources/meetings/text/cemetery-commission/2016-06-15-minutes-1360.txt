THE LUNENBURG CEMETERY COMMISSION MET ON JUNE 15, 2016 AT THE LUNENBURG PUBLIC
LIBRARY @7:00 pm.
MEMBERS PRESENT WERE LANCE MAY, LORRAINE MENDOZA AND STEVE SCHAFF WITH TODD
HOLMAN, CEMETERY SUPT AND GUEST HISTORIAN, RICHARD MCGRATH.
MINUTES FROM MAY 25, 2016 WERE READ AND ACCEPTED.
NO PUBLIC DISCUSSION TOOK PLACE.
THE MEETING CAME TO ORDER WITH CHAIR MAY RECOGNIZING HISTORIAN RICHARD MCGRATH,
WHO APPEARED BEFORE THE COMMISSION TO FURTHER DISCUSS HIS PROPOSAL TO RESTORE
THE NORTH STREET CEMETERY TOMB.
DURING THIS FOLLOW-UP PRESENTATION, HE OFFERED TO RESTORE THE BRICK WORK, RE-
CALK AND RE-POINT THE BRICK AND GRANTE FOUNDATION, SCRAPE, PAINI, AND CAL THE
• WOODEN FASCIA AND BRACKETS, TRIM THE HEDGE 15 FROM THE BUILDING, REMOVE POISON
IVY AND PROVIDED BIDS HE HAD SOLICITED FOR THE ROOF WORK.
RICHARD ALSO STATED HE WOULD INSTALL A NEW DOOR, COMPLETE WITH HINGESAND LOCK.
HE STATED ALL OF THE MATERIALS AND WORK WOULD BE DONATED AND PAID FOR THRU
GRANTS AND DONATIONS, THE TOTAL ESTIMATED COST TO BE $6,000.00. $ 9,000.00 LESS BECAUSE
THE ROOF WILL BE RE-PLACED WITH (EPDM), A RUBBER ROOF MEMBRANE WHICH HAS A 30
YEAR WARRANTY. HE WOULD SEEK A ROOFING SUB-CONTRACTER TO DO THE ROOF.
RICHARD ASKED THAT THE PURCHASE OF THE DOOR BE MADE BY THE CEMETERY COMMISSION.
RICHARD STATED THAT THE LUNENBURG HISTORICAL COMMISSION VOTED TO APPROPRIATE
$500.00 TOWARDS THE PROJECT. HE FURTHER STATED THAT A BOND WAS UNNECESSARY PER HIS
CONVERSATION WITH THE DPW AND INSURANCE IS UNNECESSARY BECAUSE HE IS COVERED BY
THB TOWN (INDEMNIFIED BY HIS SERVING ON A TOWN APPOINTED BOARD), AND SIGNED
WAIVERS ARE ALSO NOT REQUIRED.
RICHARD FURTHER STATED THAT HIS LABOR WILL BE DONATED AND THE $6,000.00 WILL BE
RAISED BY DONATIONS.
HE EXPLAINED HE ORIGINALLY SPOKE TO THE SELECTMEN'S OFFICE AND HIGHWAY SUPT AND
WAS TOLD HE IS NOT REQUIRED TO POST A BOND AND THAT ANY VOLUNTEERS INVOLVED WITH
THIS PROJECT WILL SIGN A WAIVER ABSOLVING THEM OF ANY LIABILITY SHOULD DAMAGE
INCURE TO THE BUILDING OR INJURY SUSTAINED WHILE WORKING ON THE BUILDING.
HE FURTHER STATED THE LUNENBURG HISTORICAL COMMISSION VOTED AND APPROVED THE
PROJECT AND IT WILL TAKE 30 DAYS TO COMPLETE ONCE, APPROVED BY THE CEMETERY
COMMISSION.
CHRMN MAY STATED THAT HE HAD BEEN IN CONTACT WITH THE TOWN MANAGER, KERRY
LAFLEUR ABOUT THIS WORK AND HIS PROPOSAL.
