Minutes 
Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Tuesday, November 29, 2022 
 
 
Meeting was called to order at 10:05am.  In attendance was Chairman Bill Tyler, Vice 
Chairperson Nancy Foss-Yasko and Clerk David Dorren.   
 
 
Chairman Tyler presented the Capital Funds email from Bill Bernard with notes attached 
from Town Manager Heather R. Lemieux.  David brought up the Earmark Funding for the 
Cemetery through State Rep. Michael Kushmerek’s Office.  Nancy will call Michael 
Kushmerek’s Office and see what is needed and how to earmark the Cemeteries needs 
for the next year and beyond.  
 
 
Chairman Tyler read the October 20, 2022 minutes.  It was approved 2-0 with Vice 
Person Nancy Yasko abstaining since she was not at that meeting.  
 
  
Chairman Tyler went over the existing Cemetery Budget.   
 
 
Vice Chair Yasko discussed the dogs racing loose without a leash in the North Cemetery 
and reiterated the dog leash rules to be enforced at said cemetery.   
 
 
Meeting adjourned at 11:02 am approved 3-0 
 
 
Next meeting set for December 20, 2022 at 1pm. 
 
 
Respectfully Submitted 
David Dorren, Clerk
