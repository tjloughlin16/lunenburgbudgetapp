LUNENBURG CEMETERY COMMISSION 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
MEETING MINUTES 
 
Tuesday, October 31, 2023 – 11:30AM 
Lunenburg Public Library 
1023 Massachusetts Ave., Lunenburg, MA 
Suldonik Conference Room 
 
 
11:30am – The Cemetery Commission Meeting was called to order at 11:31am by 
Chair, Nancy Foss-Yasko, other members present were David Dorren, Vice-Chair & 
Clerk.  Also present was Bill Bernard, DPW Director.  
Public Comment – Nancy Foss-Yasko, Chair asked if there was any Public Comment?  
There was none. 
 
DPW Director Updates –  
DPW Director Bill Bernard mentioned he wanted to clarify the total number of hours that 
the Town Manager mentioned at the last meeting.  Bill mentioned that the maximum 
number of hours for the Superintendent and 2 seasonal workers in a year is 2,770.  The 
calculation is 5 days per week at 40 hours per week x 52 weeks = 2,080 hours. The 2 
seasonal workers would work 690 for the year.  The total 2,080 + 690 = 2,770.  This 
does not include overtime.  Chair person Nancy Foss-Yasko asked for a printed validation 
of the difference between the 3,952 hours stated at the previous Select Board Meeting 
and the 2,770 hours that was stated at this meeting. What is the source/validation of 
these numbers?  
 
Bill further stated that the DPW/Cemetery Budget totaled $113,027.48 for fiscal year 24. 
This included payments to Helen for flowers and watering of front of North & South 
Cemeteries, The Beaulac Brothers - $9,990 for the hedges along Holman Street and 
$3,920 for the weed whacking within the North Cemetery, supplies for the cemeteries, 
gasoline, uniforms and Purchase of Services.  
 
To save on time and therefore save on man hours Vice-Chair Dorren suggested that the 
DPW fix the blower that Director Bernard stated was not working.  By fixing the blower 
he can blow leaves into the truck instead of using hand blower and putting into piles and 
then into barrels and then loading into trucks. Vice-Chair Dorren also made the 
comment that The Superintendent of Cemetery stated in one of the Commissions 
meetings he starts mowing at one end of the cemetery then goes to the other end of the 
field, then comes back toward the middle making it at least 4 sweeps back and forth

Holidays -  
In discussing Memorial Day and Veterans Day the DPW Director stated he will look into 
the State Purchasing plan for small new flags for the cemeteries for those holidays.   
 
Rules and Regulations -  
The Cemetery Commission made a motion to accept the Rules and Regulations as 
amended on 10-31-23.  Motion was passed 2-0. DPW Director Bill Bernard mentioned 
that he cannot meet the Standards of the New Rules and Regulations.  The Cemetery 
Commission then recommended ways to help the Superintendent achieve meeting the 
Rules and Regulations to the point of bringing in private company for mowing and 
landscaping which will give the Superintendent more time to accomplish his other duties.  
 
Motion was made to adjourn by Vice–Chair and Clerk, David Dorren. With no further 
discussion the meeting was adjourned at 12:38pm.  Motion passed 2-0 
 
Next meeting scheduled for November 30,2023 at 9am.
