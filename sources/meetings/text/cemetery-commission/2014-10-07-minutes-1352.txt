The Kunenburg Cemetery Commission met on October 7, 2014 @ 5:30 pm @ the Cemetery Building on Holman
St.
Present were Cemetery Commissioners Lance May, Steve Schaff and Cemetery Supt, Greg Bingham.
Commissioner Daryl Demers was not presentoue to work conflicts from new job.
Guests included the Chr of the Kunenburg Mistorical Commission, Reberca Kantry and members of the Kunenburg
Historical Commission.
The Cemetery Commissioners opened the meeting with Steve Schaff making a motion to do so and Lance may
seconded his motion to meet with members of the Lunenburg Historical Commission and tour the Holman Street
Property-as a part of a Site visit to determine the structural integrity of the building along with the building's historical
significance in Lunenburg.
After the tour was completed, The Cemetery Commissioners, Lance May and Steve Schaff along with the Supt of
Cemetery's, Greg Bingham followed the Historical Commission members back to the Ritter Building to attend a
hearing with The Historical Commission @
6:30 pm on the Holman Street property. Commissioner Daryl Demers did not attend the hearing.
After attending hearing and having no further business before the commission and with no public comment,
Commissioner Steve Schaff moved for adjournment and Commisioner Lance May seconded the motion, the motion
passed unanimously to adjourn.
Time of adjournment was 8:30 pm.
Respectfully submitted,
Steve Schaff.
