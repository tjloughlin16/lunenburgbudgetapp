AUG 17 2015
LUNENBURG TOWN
CLERK OFFICE
Members present were Lance May and Steve Schaff. Daryl Demers was not present due to work commitments.
Greg Bingham, Supt of Cemetery's was also present.
Guests included the Supt of The Lunenburg Highway Dept, Jack Rodriquenz.
The 1st order of business was re-organization. Steve Schaff nominated Lance May to be chairman.
Lance May seconded the motion and a vote was taken to unanimously elect Lance May Chairman.
Lance May nominated Steve Schaff for Vice Chairman, Steve Schaff seconded the nomination and Steve Schaff was
unanimously elected Vice Chairman.
Daryl Demers was not present and prior to the meeting indicated he would not be attending and did not wish to be an
officer.
Under Public discussion, Jack Rodriquenz introduced himself to the Commission and sought cooperation between the
Commission and The Highway Dept. He also offered some suggestions to improve the landscape around the North
Cemetery by completing the access Road.
The Commissioner's thanked Jack for attending the meeting and his input and also wanted to offer their cooperation -as
well.
Discussion followed on equipment needs, where the budget $ is derived from, a 5 year capital plan, road paving, need 3
bids).
Greg brought up the concept of building a "Niche Wall"@ The North Cemetery and offered to comeback to the Committee
with some design plans.
The Commissioners agreed to review this plan at the next meeting. Steve will work with Greg
on this project.
No further business came before the Commission and there was no further public comment, a motion by Steve Schaff and
seconded by Lance May to adjourn was unanimous.
Time of adjournment was 11:00 am.
Respectfully submitted,
Steve Schaff.
