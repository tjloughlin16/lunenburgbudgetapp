Minutes 
Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Tuesday, December 20, 2022 
 
 
Meeting was called to order at 1:00pm.  In attendance was Chairman Bill Tyler, Vice 
Chair person Nancy Foss-Yasko, Clerk David Dorren and DPW Director Bill Bernard. 
 
 
Chairman Tyler went over the Capital Funds from the Town of Lunenburg.  The Turf 
Project was #17 and the Headstone Project was #20 out of a total of 29 Projects. Vice 
Chair Yasko suggested we go through funding from State Rep. Michael Kushmerek’s 
Office.  Chairman Tyler suggested we do both, we would have a better chance getting 
funding.  Motion made to go through both Capital Funding and State Rep Michael 
Kushmerek’s Office. 3-0 
 
 
Bill Bernard stated Todd Holman received a quote for the Stone Wall for both cemeteries.  
Quote was $375,000 for both.  The Pavement for the DPW Building Driveway at the 
North Cemetery will be earmarked by DPW for State Funding.  Bill also said he put in the 
budget for the Hedges at the North Cemetery to be done after July 1, 2023. 
 
 
Motion was made by David Dorren to approve the November 29, 2022 minutes.  
Chairman Tyler 2nd the motion.  Motion approved 3-0 
 
 
Motion made to adjourn meeting at 2:30pm 
 
 
Next meeting set for January 10, 2023 at 1pm.  
 
 
Respectfully Submitted 
David Dorren, Clerk
