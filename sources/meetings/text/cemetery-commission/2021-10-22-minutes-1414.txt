Minutes 
Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Friday, October 22, 2021- 3:30 pm 
DPW Building – Conference Room 
 
Meeting was opened at 3:44pm by Chairman Tyler . Commissioners Yasko and Dorren were 
present along with DPW Director Oliva. 
 
There was no public attendance. 
 
Chairman Tyler opened the meeting with a discussion of the Hedges at the North 
Cemetery. DPW Director Oliva discussed the budget.  He will talk to Bolac Brothers 
Landscaping for an Estimate.  Depending on the estimate the discussion then turned to 
working on the first part of the hedges on Holman Street to the South Ave. Entrance.   
 
Chairman Tyler discussed the Revolutionary War Monument for the South Cemetery.  
Estimated Cost is $16,000.  To reduce the costs topic was brought up regarding 
Superintendent Todd Holman doing the excavation for the monument.  DPW Director 
Oliva to talk to Todd.  
 
Motion to approve minutes from August 5, 2021 and September 3, 2021 approved and 
passed by Chairman Tyler and Commissioners Yasko and Dorren. 
 
Next topic for discussion was the increase in fee’s and charges for the North and South 
Cemeteries.  It was decided to check with the area towns on their pricing.  
Commissioner Yasko will check with the area towns and report back to the Cemetery 
Commission at their next meeting.  
 
Motion to adjourn the meeting  and was seconded and affirmed by roll call vote.  
Meeting was adjourned at 4:44pm 
 
 
Next meeting TBD 
Respectfully Submitted 
David Dorren, Clerk
