THE LUNENBURG BUDGET PROJECT — SOURCE ARCHIVE
=============================================

This is a mirror of public records about the budget of Lunenburg, Massachusetts,
kept by an independent project at https://lunenburgbudgetproject.org.

IT IS NOT AN OFFICIAL SOURCE. Nothing here is published by the Town of Lunenburg,
the Lunenburg Public Schools or the Commonwealth. It is a copy, made because
documents move and disappear: 57 of the district's own links stopped working in a
single day in August 2026, and several documents here are no longer available at
the address they came from. Where a figure matters, check it against the publisher.


HOW TO CHECK A FILE
-------------------
Every file has a sha256 recorded before it was stored. The index — path, size,
sha256, and the publisher's own URL where one was recorded — is published at

    https://lunenburgbudgetproject.org/data/archive-manifest.csv

That index is not kept in this bucket, deliberately. Objects here cannot be
changed once written, so a copy of the index stored beside them would be
permanently out of date about them.

Each file is also served from the site itself, at

    https://lunenburgbudgetproject.org/docs/<the same path>


WHAT IS IN EACH FOLDER
----------------------
The folders say how a document reached us, which is the one thing about a document
that never changes. What year it covers and what it is about are in the index, not
in the path, because most documents carry several of each.

  town-budget/            The Town's budget documents, warrants and financial
                          statements, from lunenburgma.gov
  town-supplementary/     Everything else the Town publishes that is not a budget
  town-annual-reports/    The annual town reports, FY2011 to FY2025
  town-ledgers/           Accounting reports from the Town's MUNIS system, obtained
                          by records request. PROVENANCE files say who sent what
  district-budget/        The school district's budget page, mirrored in full
  budget-workbooks/       Spreadsheets behind the district's budget documents
  contracts/              Collective bargaining agreements and employment contracts
  meetings/               Agendas and minutes for every Town board, 2025 onward
  state-dese/             Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary
                          Education — school finance data
  state-dls/              Massachusetts Department of Revenue, Division of Local
                          Services — free cash certifications
  peer-districts/         Budget documents from neighbouring school districts
  correspondence/         Letters and emails with Town and district officials
  analyses/               OURS. Written by this project, not published by anybody
  data/                   OURS. Data extracted from the documents above

Alongside most originals is a `text/` folder holding the extracted text, and in a
few places `ocr/` and `pages/` holding what was read off scans that had no text
layer. Those are our renderings of somebody else's document, not the document.


WHY THE FILES HERE CANNOT BE CHANGED
------------------------------------
The bucket carries a lock: for ten years, an object cannot be deleted or
overwritten. A published document does not change, so if our copy ever differed
from what was uploaded, that would be a defect and not a new version. Freezing them
is the point.

The consequence, said plainly: our own extracted text and derived data are here too,
and those DO change. The copies in this bucket are a snapshot and cannot be updated.
The current version of anything marked OURS above is on the site and in the
project's git repository, and that is what the site serves.
