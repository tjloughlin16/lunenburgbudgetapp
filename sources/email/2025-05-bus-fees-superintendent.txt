Good Afternoon:

This is a friendly reminder that Bus Fee Payments are due by June 1st.  A page on Revtrak
has been set up for bus fee payments  https://lunenburg.revtrak.net/r2#/v/bus-fees  or you
can pay by check made out to the "Town of Lunenburg".  Please put "bus fee" in the memo
section of the check and mail to: Lunenburg Public Schools Central Office, T.C. Passios
Elementary Building, 1025 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA 01462.

Payment Guidelines

Grades K-6 :  0-1.99 miles are charged

Grades K-6 :  2 or more miles no charge

Grades 7-12 :  all students charged

Fee Structure

$180 Families with 1 student

$50 Families with 1 student that qualify for a reduced fee (through free/reduced
application which will be verified)

$270 Families with 2 or more students

$75 Families with 2 or more students that qualify for a reduced fee (through free/reduced
application which will be verified)

Free - Families that have submitted a Free/Reduced Application and qualify which will be
verified

Free & Reduced Meal Information

Any family that believes they may qualify for a free or reduced fee has to complete and
submit the application AND the sharing information form found in the links below.  Families
that do not submit the sharing form checking the third box on the form for Transportation
fees, will not be considered for a free or reduced fee.
