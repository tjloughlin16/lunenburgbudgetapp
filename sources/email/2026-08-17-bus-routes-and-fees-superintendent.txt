Good Evening:

Attached please find the 2026-2027 Bus Routes for Dee Bus Regular Transportation.  This
does not include Van Pool Special Education bussing.

2026-2027 Bus Route Listing.pdf

If you have not filled out a transportation form and need transportation, please follow the
instructions below.  Please be aware that there may be a delay in transportation if you did
not sign up by the May 1st deadline.

To sign up for the 2026-2027 school year, please follow these steps:

Go online to www.lunenburgschools.net.
Scroll down to IMPORTANT TRANSPORTATION INFORMATION.
Click the link for the Transportation Form Link for the 2026-2027 School Year.
Read the instructions carefully, click Next to complete the form, and click Submit at the
end of each section.

Bus Fee Information:

One child: $180.00

Family rate (2+ children): $270.00

$75 Families w/2+ children that qualify for a reduced fee(will be verified)

Free-Families that have qualified(this will be verified)

You may pay using one of two options:

Online via RevTrak: Please note that RevTrak charges a small processing fee.

By Check: Make checks payable to the Town of Lunenburg and include your student's name(s)
in the memo line.  Send to Lunenburg Public Schools Central Office, 129 Northfield Rd,
Lunneburg, MA 01462

Thank you and enjoy the last weeks of summer!

In Partnership,

Jodi L. Fortuna Ed.D
Superintendent Lunenburg Public Schools
