## How it reached us — NOT ESTABLISHED, and that is the point

**Nothing in this archive records how these files were obtained.** They are not on the
town's website, and no email, records request or meeting packet is recorded for them.

Rule 12 is explicit that a document which did not come off a website still has an
address — a request and its date, an email and who sent it, a packet — and that
"obtained from the Town" is an address while "public" is not. **We do not have one for
these.** Writing a plausible route would be inventing provenance, which is worse than
recording the gap.

**What would settle it:** one sentence from whoever obtained them. Until then this file
exists so that the absence is visible rather than assumed away.

The FinCom deck and memo that arrived with these are in `town-budget/docs/` — they are
Finance Committee material rather than ledger reports, and they share this gap.

## The period-12 report is different

`PROVENANCE-fy2026-p12.md`, beside this file, has a full address: sent by email on

That is what a recorded address looks like, and it is the contrast worth seeing.
