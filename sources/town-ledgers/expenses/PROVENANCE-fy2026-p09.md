## How it reached us

**A public records request made by Matt Nazarenko to the Town Manager, answered by the
Town, and sent to this project by him directly.**

There is no URL. These documents were not published; they were asked for under the public
records law and handed on. The request is the address.

**We are one remove from the town.** The request was not ours and the town's response did
not come to us, so what we hold is a copy of what the requester received. That is a real
address and it is not the same thing as having asked ourselves — it is recorded this way
so nobody later reads "records request" and assumes the correspondence is in this archive.

## What is bounded rather than known

**The request date is not established.** What is known is that it was made **before 14
August 2026**, and the reports themselves were generated by the Town Accountant on **11
August 2026 at 11:59**, which is the town producing them in answer to it. So the request
precedes 11 August and the handover falls between then and 14 August.

Three things would close the rest, and none of them changes what the documents are:

- the date the request was filed, and its wording — which decides what was asked for and
  therefore what a *complete* answer would have contained
- the town's response message, which is where any caveat about the figures would be. The
  period-12 package has one — the Town Manager writing that figures were "likely to
  continue to adjust as we continue the year-end reconciliation process" — and that
  caveat bears directly on how a period-9 figure should be read. **Nothing here says
  whether a similar note accompanied these.**
- **The answer was wider than what is filed here.** TJ, 20 September 2026: the same drop
  also carried `Finance Committee Memo 3-31-2026.docx`, which this archive does not hold.
  So this package is a SUBSET of what the town produced, and the question is no longer
  whether anything is missing but what else was. Not chased — it is a memo rather than a
  ledger, and nothing on the money pages rests on it — but recorded, because "we hold the
  answer to that request" would now be wrong.

## Why this matters more than it used to

The trust and agency report in `fund-balances/` is now the source of the balances
published on `/analysis/stabilization-funds` — every stabilization fund's holdings,
revenue and expenditure. It replaced figures read off a photograph of a printed table, and
it is far better evidence (rule 13a: a sheet the accounting system printed is proof).

A document that carries a published figure has to carry its address with it, which is why
this note was completed rather than left as a recorded gap.
