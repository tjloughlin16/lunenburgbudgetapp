# School department journal detail, FY2023-FY2026 -- the Town's second answer to the 4 September request

Covers `account-details-fy2023-fy2026-p13-school-journal.pdf`, which is **held private and
never published** (below), and our redacted reading of it in `sources/data/`.

## What the document says about itself

Quoted from the printed copy (every page carries the same header and footer):

- **Program ID** `glytdbud`, `YEAR-TO-DATE BUDGET REPORT`, **`FOR 2026 13`** and
  **`JOURNAL DETAIL 2023  0 TO 2026 13`** -- the same year-to-date report as the period-13
  spreadsheets beside it in `expenses/`, run with journal detail switched on, from FY2023
  period 0 to FY2026 period 13
- **Report generated** `10/07/2026 21:47` -- the day after the period-13 spreadsheets
  (`../expenses/PROVENANCE-fy2023-fy2026-p13-school.md`, run `10/06/2026`)
- **Generated by** the Town Manager, whose name the last page prints
- **1,744 pages.** Page 1,743 is the `GRAND TOTAL` line; page 1,744 is `REPORT OPTIONS`,
  which records `Print journal detail: Y`, `From Yr/Per: 2023/ 0  To Yr/Per: 2026/13`,
  `Include budget entries: Y`, `Incl encumb/liq entries: Y`, `Multiyear view: D`
- **Scope, read off the page headers:** fund `0100 GENERAL FUND`, school accounts only
  (every account string is `0100-3-...`), pages 1-679; then every school special fund --
  revolving funds 1300-1315, school lunch 2200, the grant funds 2481-2962 and 4201-4604,
  the school capital articles 3000-3123, scholarships 8130 and 8134, and 9002 custodian
  special details -- pages 680-1742. 3,356 accounts.
- **What a journal line carries:** journal number (year/period/sequence), effective date,
  source code, amount, vendor number and name, invoice or purchase-order reference, a
  description, and on paid invoices a check number. Payroll (`PRJ`) posts as one line per
  warrant per account (`WARRANT=6P23 RUN=1`), not per employee.

It is a printout from the accounting system (CLAUDE.md 13a): account codes, periods,
journal and warrant references, and a GRAND TOTAL the report foots itself.

## The Town's redactions, and what we checked about them

Checked 18 October 2026:

- **163 redaction overlays on 28 pages**, drawn where the text has been REMOVED from the
  text layer, and **3 pages (34, 643, 1080) flattened to pictures** with the redactions
  burned in. Every one of the 1,744 pages was tested for text sitting under a black box and
  none holds any (a box drawn over live text, as a control, was caught). These are real
  redactions, not boxes over live text.
- **The redaction is incomplete on the rendered page.** Page 643 prints what reads as a
  child's first name and last initial in a tuition line, and part of one in the next; pages
  34 and 35 print a two-letter code in a column boxed on every other line; page 1080 leaves
  an address fragment beside a boxed payee. The values are written down nowhere in this
  repository.
- Vendor lines that name individuals -- game officials, reimbursements -- were not redacted
  by the Town and were not meant to be; its stated scope was children and citizens named in
  the special education fund.

## What is published, and what is not

- **The PDF is held private and never published** (`sources/data/redactions.csv`). It is
  the Town's file and its redaction misses a child.
- **What is published is our redacted reading of it:** `sources/data/munis-school-journal.csv`
  (every journal line) and `munis-school-journal-accounts.csv` (every account), built by
  `scripts/extract_munis_school_journal.py`, which states the redaction rule in its header:
  people paid as vendors become `individual (redacted)` with no vendor or invoice number;
  children, and people named in a description, become `[redacted]`; businesses and public
  bodies stay. Every distinct vendor name and every distinct description was read for a
  person before it was written.
- **The three picture pages** were read by eye into
  `sources/data/munis-school-journal-image-pages.csv`, already masked. The extractor proves
  that reading: the three accounts those pages fall in close to the cent only with it.
- **Still to come from the Town:** the redaction log, and the same report as a spreadsheet.
