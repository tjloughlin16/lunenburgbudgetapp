SUMMER 2024 SOCCER CLINIC
RUNS Monday 7/22 - Friday 7/26
9:00AM - 12:00PM

LOCATION: Lunenburg Middle/High School Turf
Ages 5-6: Players learn the importance of a good warm-up through fun games and activities. Coaches will develop basic foot skills, passing, and
shooting through structured games with every player actively involved. Players will also learn the basics of soccer match play via 3v3 tournaments
focusing on positions, start of play, and dead-ball situations. The games and matches develop safe play, teamwork, communication, and interest in
soccer while still having lots of FUN!!
Ages 7-12: As players learn and grasp the basic fundamentals of soccer, focus can shift towards the WHEN, WHERE, and WHY in order to get a
better understanding of the game. The players will also develop their positional sense, and spatial awareness in a tournament played at the end of
every session. Players will be divided by age and skill level to ensure an appropriate environment for a challenging and fun week.

NRWA Summertime Eco-Adventures
 If you are looking for an outdoor activity for your child this summer, look no further than the Eco-Adventure program ran by Nashua River
Watershed Association. The Lunenburg Recreation Department is excited to partner with NRWA for a three-day activity exploring our local trails,
water sources and wild life. Please note that this is a parent drop- off program!
Dates: Monday July 29th- Wednesday July 31st
Time: 9:00AM - 11:00AM
Ages: 8- 11 Years Old
Location: Lunenburg Community Pollinator Habitat
Cost: $80/participant
Join NRWA educators for a three day, two hour long exploration of two of Lunenberg's beautiful conservation properties and the amazing
Lunenburg Community Pollinator Habitat. During this drop off program, youth participants will hike and explore three ecosystems, learning about
the diversity of life found within each one. Using hand held viewers, binoculars, and field guides participants will be given the opportunity to get a
close look at some fascinating organisms that live side by side in our meadows and forests!
