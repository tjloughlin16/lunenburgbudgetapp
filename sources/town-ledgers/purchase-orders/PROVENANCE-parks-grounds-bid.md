# Parks grounds-maintenance bid, year one from 1 July 2024

`po-closed-fy2025-parks-grounds-bid.pdf` — a contractor's bid sheet for municipal grounds
maintenance (mowing, weeding, turf, clean-ups, infield work) priced per park for the year
beginning 1 July 2024. Obtained from the Town by public records request, August 2025, in
the same delivery as the Parks & Recreation reports; the full provenance, the publisher's
filename and the sha256 are in `../account-details/PROVENANCE-parks-2025.md`.

A bid is an offer, not a purchase order and not an invoice: it is filed here because it is
the closest folder to what it is, and it is `stated` — the vendor's figures, on the vendor's
authority. What the town actually paid for grounds work is in the FY2024 Parks budget
report beside it (`../expenses/glytdbud-expense-fy2024-p13-gf-parks.pdf`, image only) and
would be in the FY2025 one, which the archive does not hold.
