Lunenburg Board of Trust Fund Commissioners 
Policy on Verification of U.S. Citizenship and Municipal 
Residency 
1. Purpose 
This policy establishes standardized documentation and procedures to verify U.S. citizenship and 
Lunenburg, Massachusetts residency for applicants seeking grants or loans from trust funds 
administered by the Lunenburg Board of Trust Fund Commissioners. The policy supports 
compliance with donor intent, fiduciary obligations, and consistent, fair administration. 
2. Authority 
This policy is adopted under the Board’s fiduciary duty to ensure distributions are made only to 
eligible beneficiaries, consistent with trust instruments and applicable Massachusetts law and 
municipal practices. 
3. Scope 
This policy applies to all applicants for distributions (grants or loans) from any trust fund under 
the custody of the Lunenburg Board of Trust Fund Commissioners. It also governs internal 
review by Board members and designated staff. 
4. Definitions 
Applicant. An individual requesting a distribution from Board-administered trust funds. 
Primary Document. A document that, by itself, is sufficient to establish the required element 
(citizenship or residency). 
Secondary Document. A document that contributes to proof when used together with other 
secondary documents. 
Residency. The applicant’s current physical domicile in the Town of Lunenburg, Massachusetts. 
5. Documentation Requirements 
5.1 U.S. Citizenship Verification 
Provide ONE Primary Citizenship Document OR TWO Secondary Citizenship Documents: 
• Primary (one required): U.S. Passport or Passport Card; Certified U.S. Birth Certificate; 
Certificate of Naturalization (N‑550/N‑570); Certificate of Citizenship (N‑560/N‑561); 
Consular Report of Birth Abroad (FS‑240/FS‑545/DS‑1350); Federally recognized tribal 
enrollment documentation.  
• Secondary (two required if no primary): Delayed birth certificate; Letter of No Record from 
vital records; Early public records (school, medical, baptismal); Notarized affidavits from U.S. 
citizens with direct knowledge.

5.2 Lunenburg Residency Verification 
Provide ONE document from List A and TWO documents from List B (List B dated within 60 
days): 
1. Massachusetts driver’s license or state ID listing a Lunenburg address 
2. Voter registration or precinct confirmation 
3. Property tax bill 
4. Mortgage statement or deed 
5. Government benefits/correspondence (e.g., MassHealth, SSA, DTA, VA) 
6. Court or jury summons 
List B (provide two): 
• Utility bill (electric, water, sewer, gas, internet) 
• Lease or rental agreement 
• Bank or credit union statement 
• Homeowners or renters insurance declarations page 
• USPS change‑of‑address confirmation letter 
6. Procedures 
7. Application Intake: Applicant submits the Citizenship & Residency Verification Form with 
required documents. 
8. Initial Review: Designee confirms document completeness and dates; requests any missing 
items within 10 business days. 
9. Substantive Review: Board (or designee) verifies authenticity and sufficiency; additional 
documentation may be requested. 
10. Decision: Upon verification, eligibility is determined and recorded in meeting minutes; 
approvals/denials are communicated in writing. 
11. Recordkeeping: Copies of documents are retained in accordance with municipal records 
retention schedules and privacy practices. 
7. Privacy and Records 
All applicant documents are confidential municipal records and will be safeguarded from 
unauthorized disclosure. Access is limited to Board members and designated personnel with a 
need to know. Records will be retained and disposed of per applicable retention schedules. 
8. Nondiscrimination 
The Board administers trust funds without discrimination based on race, color, national origin, 
religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, veteran status, or any other 
protected class. 
===PAGE 3===
9. Amendments 
This policy may be amended by majority vote of the Board at a posted public meeting. Changes 
take effect upon adoption unless otherwise specified.
