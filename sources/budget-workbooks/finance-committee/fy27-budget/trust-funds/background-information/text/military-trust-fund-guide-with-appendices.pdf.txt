Trust Fund Disbursement Eligibility Instruction Manual 
For the Trustees of the Lunenburg, Massachusetts Board of Trust Fund Commissioners 
Table of Contents 
 
1. Purpose & Scope 
This manual guides Trustees through a consistent, auditable process to determine eligibility for disbursements from Military Personnel–Related Trust 
Funds. It focuses on discharge types (punitive and misconduct‑based administrative) and the DD‑214 fields required to make determinations. 
2. Definitions 
Punitive discharges: Dishonorable Discharge, Bad Conduct Discharge (BCD), and Dismissal (officers) imposed only by court‑martial. 
Administrative discharges: Honorable, General (Under Honorable Conditions), Other Than Honorable (OTH), and Entry‑Level (Uncharacterized). 
DD‑214 Blocks: Key fields: #24 Character of Service; #25 Separation Authority; #26 SPD Code; #28 Narrative Reason. 
3. Step‑by‑Step Eligibility Determination Process 
1. Obtain the applicant’s DD‑214 (long form). 
2. Record the Character of Service (Block 24). 
3. Record the Narrative Reason (Block 28). 
4. Record the Separation Authority (Block 25). 
5. Record the SPD Code (Block 26). 
6. Screen for restrictive categories: any Dishonorable, BCD, Dismissal, and most OTH tied to misconduct; flag for verification. 
7. If flagged, verify the SPD code with the Service authority (Army S‑1/Transition; Navy PSD/PERS‑832; Air Force/Space Force MPF; Marine Corps 
IPAC; Coast Guard SPO/PPC).

5. Appeals & Exceptions

Appendix B — SPD Verification Workflow & Contacts 
1. Confirm DD‑214 is the long form; ensure Blocks 24–28 are present. 
2. If discharge is Dishonorable/BCD/Dismissal or OTH tied to misconduct, initiate SPD verification. 
3. Contact the appropriate Service office: Army S‑1/Transition; Navy PSD/PERS‑832; Air Force/Space Force MPF; Marine Corps IPAC/Manpower; 
Coast Guard SPO/PPC. 
4. Request written/email confirmation of the SPD meaning and the narrative reason. 
5. Attach the confirmation to the case file and proceed with determination.
