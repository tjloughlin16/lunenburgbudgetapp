Disclaimer: This Preliminary FY27 Capital Program is being transmitted to the Lunenburg Select Board and Finance Committee in accordance with Section 6-7 of the Town Charter, which requires the Town Manager to submit a capital improvement program at least 150 days before the start of the fiscal year to the Select Board and Finance Committee.Under typical circumstances, the Town’s Free Cash would be certified prior to this submission.  However, due to transitions in the Finance Department, Fiscal Year 2025 has not been closed out and Free Cash has not yet been certified.  In order to meet the intent and timing requirements of the Charter, this preliminary FY27 Capital Program has been formed using a conservative estimate of Free Cash.  Once Free Cash is certified, additional meetings of the Capital Planning Committee are anticipated.  Accordingly, this FY27 Capital Program should be considered preliminary and subject to revision.
