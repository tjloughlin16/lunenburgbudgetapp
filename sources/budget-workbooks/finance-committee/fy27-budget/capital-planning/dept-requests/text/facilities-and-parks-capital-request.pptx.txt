FM 23-33 – Public Safety Roof Replacement
We have had several leaks on the asphalt shingle section and the shingles are showing signs of serios deterioration.
The rubber roofing over the fire garage is also showing its age and is in the early stages of roof failure.
This capital project would be to remove all existing roofing and replace the shingled areas with new 25 year architectural shingles with 100% ice and water shield coverage. The rubber roof over the garage will be replaced with a white TPO roof which will help keep the building cooler in the summer months.
The cost for this project is $494,500
Pricing came from cost per sq/ft numbers derived from roof projects last year with a contingency.

FM XX-XX – Public Safety VCT Flooring Replacement
The current VCT flooring in the public safety building is original and at the end of its useful life.
Carpets for this building were replaced last year, but the VCT in hallways and common areas was not touched.
This project will replace the VCT with another flooring of similar type and life span.
Estimate for this project is $38,600
Pricing came from cost per sq/ft numbers derived from floor projects last year with a contingency.

FM XX-XX – Parks – Fitzgerald Field Improvements
This project is to re-grade and re-seed the fields at Fitzgerald field.
Right now the fields are unusable in the spring due to heavy water infiltration from a nearby stream.
This wasn’t always the case, but due to years of erosion it now needs the repairs.
This project will also include fencing repairs for Fitzgerald field and the town beach.
Total Costs Estimated @ $55,600
Pricing came from verbal quotes obtained from our landscaping contractor in October of 2025
