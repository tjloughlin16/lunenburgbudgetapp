Library,Library Staff Salaries,"Reduced to 226,077.79","Level funded at 269,048.83","Level funded at 269,048.83","Restored in Tier 1- This reduction eliminates the position of Young Adult Services Librarian, a 30 hr./week employee.  The community would lose a key professional librarian role (1 of 5 Master’s-level librarians) who works with the important young adult demographic. The loss would be especially impactful shortly after town leaders committed a significant amount of capital to improving the Teen Room with more than $120,000 from ARPA funds. The reduction also decreases staff availability for absence coverage to meet operational safety requirements.   The library will need to eliminate structured after-school activities and Teen Advisory Board volunteer opportunities. Additionally, the community will see reduced quality and frequency of Summer Reading Program for Young Adults, book displays and collection maintenance, and outreach and partnerships.  
Operational impact will include increased strain on remaining staff to cover evening shifts, potential scheduling gaps, negative impact on staff morale and increased difficulty to retain staff talent. To partially counter the morale and retention impact, the library might close one evening per week, but this reduces community access by 25% for evening space use, creating significant impacts on local groups such as Scouts, and would result in lost shifts for one Library Page. Other impacts will be less immediately visible but will reduce teen-focused programming and increase reliance on outside funding sources.  The Young Adult Services Librarian represents 11% of total staff hours and plays a critical role in supporting teen engagement, literacy, and safe after-school space. Reduced staffing and resources will directly affect service quality, accessibility, and the overall user experience for the community. "
