Funding Sources
The department’s funding sources include general fund support, grants, and fees, ensuring fiscal responsibility and transparency while maintaining essential services for our community.
Department Funding is solely from the General Fund.  Grants and other sources are used to supplement the existing budget and can vary from year to year.  In general, other funding sources include-
SAFE/SENIOR SAFE Funds (State)- $7,430
Firefighter Safety Grant (State)*- $15,000-19,000
EMPG (State/Federal)- Normally $3750.  As of now not funded this FY
Donations- (Various)- 
+
 $5000.  Can vary from year to year
Nashoba Mitigation funds**- $165,000
*Safety Grant was extended one more year; Unsure if will continue
**One time funds to be expended in FY26 &amp; 27 to offset EMS strains due to NVMC closure

Budget as requested provides 3 Career Staff 24/7 with a per diem FF supplementing 16 hours/day.  Provides better working conditions for staff and better emergency coverage for the Town.  (Can staff the 2
nd
 ambulance more of the time);  Also addresses shortages in the budget due to rising costs and general inflation.
Level funded budget would continue to place undue strain on staffing, possibly resulting in loss of experienced personnel, further straining the system.  Decreased buying power in budget will limit replacement and supplies.
A less than level funded budget will reduce some per diem coverage and result in more shifts running at a reduced level.
