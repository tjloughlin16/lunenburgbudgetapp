Dr. Fortuna, 
I am writing to raise a budget item for consideration as we prepare for the FY27 School
Department budget, specifically the line item related to school-based social work services.
The employment of social workers within the district was introduced during the COVID period to
provide additional support for students. The proposed FY26 budget reflects an approximate cost
of $370,000 for these services. Given the broader fiscal context and the information shared to
date indicating that an override may be required to maintain a level-service budget, I believe it is
appropriate to revisit this item.
At last year’s budget hearing, I asked whether the social work services provided in our schools
are reimbursable through health insurance and whether these services could potentially be
subcontracted to a private provider. At that time, the response indicated that in-house services
were believed to be more effective, though the question of insurance reimbursement was not
fully clear to me.
In order to prepare for this year's budget meetings, I have started looking at what other districts
in Massachusetts, that are similar to ours in total enrollment and demographic, and wanted to
share some early findings. 
There are organizations that provide school-based social work services and are able to bill
insurance directly. For example, Seven Hills Foundation notes that, in most cases, health
insurance covers the cost of services, with applicable co-payments and deductibles.
https://www.sevenhills.org/programs/school-based-services
In addition, a brief review of nearby districts suggests a wide variation in how these services are
delivered and funded. As an anecdotal comparison, Winchendon Public Schools, with a student
population of approximately 1,127, budgets roughly $50,000 for social work services.
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1cualzUa8LE9JfltbjBnWjm3ePEZrUCBt/view
Based on publicly available budget materials, Littleton Public Schools and Ayer-Shirley Regional
School District do not appear to employ social workers as dedicated school staff positions,
though I have not yet contacted either district directly to confirm this information.
Littleton: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Lx2tZdNjs_eigac1DELGYx5AlUKToLXr/view
Ayer-Shirley Regional:
https://resources.finalsite.net/images/v1767973461/asrsdorg/n7ptnhkyetc4aj7bpgak/FY26ASRS
DRe-CertifiedBudget12-17-25pdf.pdf
I am sharing this information now because I believe the district should, at a minimum, explore
alternative models for continuing to support students, including the possibility of contracting with
