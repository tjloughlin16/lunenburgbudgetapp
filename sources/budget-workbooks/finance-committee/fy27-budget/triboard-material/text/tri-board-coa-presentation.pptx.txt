FY25 
Kitchen 
Reno
The COA Director 
was notified by the previous interim Town Manager that there were funds totally $22,000 that had been previously allocated for kitchen renovations by June 30
th
 
The project began in redesigning and creating a commercial kitchen earlier this year.
All the old wood cabinets were removed along with the flooring.  There were mechanical issues that needed to be addressed.  After lining pipes, updating electrical, preparing and painting floors and ceilings, our new equipment was installed.  A new 3 bay sink, stainless steel countertops and beautiful gas range and a new heater/proofer that was required to keep Meals on Wheels at temperature and our meals too.
We use the kitchen daily and are so pleased with the way it turned out.  We recently had our inspection with the Nashoba Board of Health and passed with no issues.
Oh, what fun it was!
Thank you, Chris Ruth!

LUNENBURG COUNCIL ON AGING
The Council on Aging will be working with area groups such as Nashoba Board of Health, Senior Connections, Lunenburg Public Library and Parks &amp; Recreation to provide new services and programs designed to benefit the health and well-being of Lunenburg’s senior population.
COMMUNITY HUB
Reorganizing the staff’s duties and responsibilities will allow the Council on Aging to better serve the senior community.  It will change the dynamics ensuring coverage and cross training of positions.
A Program Coordinator would have the time and ability to schedule and implement new programs with an emphasis on educational programming.
STAFFING
The Council on Aging will be focusing on reaching our younger seniors with a special focus on our caregivers.  
Harvard University study results indicated that 70% of caregivers over the age of 70 die before the person they are caring for.  
We need to do better to support these caregivers.
