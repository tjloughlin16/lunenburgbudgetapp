Budget changes include: 
12
The major changes to this budget are the inclusion of TCP operating costs which were typically not included before. The bulk of these increases are in utility costs as well as additional cleaning, rubbish and repair costs. This also includes taking on landscaping costs which will shift to the town after FY2026
Target reflects current contracts with DPW union as well as SAP agreement. Subject to future town meeting votes and new contract with DPW employees.
We are adding a generator fuel line for the FY26 fiscal year.
Our water usage line has gone up due to some additional irrigation coming on line.
An increase of $10,000 under the Parks and Recreation Contracted Services line to address an increase repair costs not associated with regular landscaping services. Prior to this, regular landscaping maintenance took up the majority of the budget allowing no room for extra repairs or improvements.

TCP Costs and Decommissioning
13
As part of the FY26 budget we are decommissioning roughly 1/2 of the TC Passios Building with the hopes of lowering the operating and maintenance costs for an underutilized building. This will also allow us to begin asbestos abatement in the decommissioned portion of the building, which is extensive.

TCP Budget
Overall
20XX
PRESENTATION TITLE
14
Historically the TCP building was managed by the school dept until the last couple of years, and now the school dept has completely vacated the building with the exception of the garage space until the end of FY2026. 
In the last few years there was no real budget for this building and costs were spread between a TCP revolving account and other sources of free cash.
Now that the care and custody of the building is back in the towns hands we are re-assessing all costs and revenue sources for the building in order to properly budget this within the regular facilities budget.
With the decommissioning of the back half of the building we are expecting a significant drop in some of the utility costs. Maintenance costs are expected to remain the same or increase due to years of 
deffered
 maintenance on the building.
