Acceptance by Town of 
M.G.L. Chapter 32B, Section 19
- 2008
Requires a 
written agreement between the Town and the PEC
Requires contracts with a health insurance carrier(s) to be in conformity with the PEC agreement 
PEC is comprised of a representative of each collective bargaining unit (90 % of vote divided proportionally-weighted-by eligible employees in each bargaining unit) and a retiree representative (10 % of vote). 
Vote of PEC is binding on all subscribers- active and retired
