M.  FY2027 PROPOSITION 2 ½ OVERRIDE 
(Submitted by the Select Board and Town Manager)   
To see if the Town will vote to raise and appropriate such sums as may be necessary to supplement the 
general operating budgets of the Town and the Lunenburg Public Schools for Fiscal Year 2027; provided, 
however, that such appropriation shall be expressly contingent upon approval by the voters of a 
Proposition 2½ override question, so-called, in accordance with G.L. c. 59 § 21C(g), as follows: 
• $2,400,000 (Tier 1 Override)  
• $3,300,000 (Tier 2 Override)  
===PAGE 7===
   
 
   
 
with the understanding that each amount shall be presented to the voters as a separate ballot question, and 
that if more than one question is approved, only the highest approved amount shall be implemented; or 
take any other action relative thereto. 
FINANCE COMMITTEE RECOMMENDATION:  
SELECT BOARD RECOMMENDATION:  
VOTE REQUIRED: Majority  
Summary:  This article asks the Town to consider a general override of the levy limit under 
Proposition 2½ to support the Fiscal Year 2027 operating budgets of both the Town and the 
Lunenburg Public Schools. 
The Town has developed three budget scenarios for Fiscal Year 2027: 
 
• Balanced Budget (No Override): $49,925,699.99  
• Tier 1 Override Budget: approximately $52,283,502.68  
• Tier 2 Override Budget: approximately $53,171,145.83  
 
This article provides the additional appropriation for the Tier 1 and Tier 2 budgets ($2,354,551.69 
and 3,245,445.84 respectively).  The tiered structure provides voters with more than one funding 
option. The Town Meeting vote alone does not authorize or approve an override.   
 
Each tier will be voted on separately at the Town Election.  The Election Warrant will contain two 
separate questions.   
 
• Tier 1 Override: $2,400,000  
• Tier 2 Override: $3,300,000  
 
Voters may approve one, both, or neither option at the Annual Town Election on May 16, 2026.  If 
both options are approved, only the higher amount (Tier 2) will be implemented (the amounts do 
not combine or “stack”).  If neither question is approved, the Town will operate under a balanced 
budget within the existing levy limit.  If only Tier 1 is approved, the Tier 1 budget will be 
implemented.  If only Tier 2 is approved, the Tier 2 budget will be implemented.  If both are 
approved, the Tier 2 budget will be implemented.  
 
The override, if approved, would increase the Town’s levy limit on an ongoing basis and fund 
general municipal and school operating expenses. For more information, please visit the Town and 
School budget websites.

FINANCE COMMITTEE RECOMMENDATION: Recommend Approval (5-0-0).  
SELECT BOARD RECOMMENDATION: Recommend Approval (5-0-0). 
VOTE REQUIRED: Majority  
 
Summary:  OPEB stands for "Other Post Employment Benefits". These are benefits, other than 
pensions, offered to employees after they leave employment (retiree medical, dental, vision and life 
insurance). All Towns that offer retiree benefits have always had OPEB liability. However, the 
amount of liability was largely unknown until the Government Accountability Standards Board 
(GASB) directed all municipalities to quantify their liabilities in 2001. Initial estimates of these 
liabilities were first released in 2014. The Town is under no obligation to fund its OPEB liability.  
 
The OPEB Sewer Trust Fund provides for post-employment benefits, other than pensions, offered 
to employees whose jobs are funded in any part by the sewer enterprise.

Summary: OPEB stands for "Other Post Employment Benefits". These are benefits, other than 
pensions, offered to employees after they leave employment (retiree medical, dental, vision and life 
insurance). All Towns that offer retiree benefits have always had OPEB liability. However, the 
amount of liability was largely unknown until the Government Accountability Standards Board 
(GASB) directed all municipalities to quantify their liabilities in 2001. Initial estimates of these 
liabilities were first released in 2014. The Town is under no obligation to fund its OPEB liability. 
However, funding OPEB liability is one of the elements considered by agencies when determining a 
Town's bond rating.  Lunenburg’s OPEB Liability as of June 30, 2024 is $52,294,353 and we are 
2.69% funded.  Lunenburg’s Financial Policies call for a transfer of 10%, or $335,437, of Certified 
Free Cash into the OPEB Trust Fund.

Article IX Open Space Committee 
 
§ 12-24. Membership and Appointment 
 
A. There shall be established an Open Space Committee consisting of seven (7) voting members 
who reside in Lunenburg who shall be sworn to the faithful performance of their 
duties.   Membership shall include:

• One member or designee of the Conservation Commission

===PAGE 19===
   
 
   
 
• One member or designee of the Agricultural Commission  
• Three at-large community members.  
   
B. The Open Space Committee at-large members shall be appointed by the Select Board.  The four 
Board/Commission members shall be appointed by their relevant Board/Commission.  Committee 
members shall serve two (2) year staggered terms, with the majority of the first members 
appointed for a two (2) year term and the remaining initial members appointed to a one (1) year 
term.  Thereafter, each member shall serve for two (2) years or until a successor has been 
appointed.  The positions will not be term-limited.  
   
C. The membership shall consist of individuals with experience, training, or a dedicated interest in 
open space preservation, land planning, conservation, real estate acquisition, mapping, 
fundraising, communications, and such other professions as could be helpful to the Committee’s 
work.    
   
§ 12-25. Duties 
 
The Open Space Committee shall have the following responsibilities and duties:    
   
A. Assist and advise other Town boards/committees and departments as they may request 
such assistance with regard to the review of applications for land development permits with the 
goal of securing open space areas for the community.   
 
B. Serve as an information source and advocate for the acquisition of land for conservation, open 
space, agricultural, recreation, and related purposes and make recommendations for its 
purchase to the Select Board.    
 
C. Update and implement Lunenburg’s Open Space Plan and Recreation Plan pursuant 
to Massachusetts Department of Conservation and Recreation guidelines.    
 
D. Maintain a publicly-owned open space inventory.    
 
E. Work with Lunenburg residents, Town Departments, Town Boards and Committees, state and 
federal officials and agencies, and private non-profit land conservation organizations to further 
the preservation of Lunenburg's open space resources.    
 
F. Encourage philanthropy and private efforts to preserve open space.    
 
G. Develop community education programs and communication tools regarding the value and use of 
open space areas.    
   
§ 12-26. Officers 
 
The Open Space Committee shall choose its own officers and may adopt rules of procedure regarding its 
functioning and operation in compliance with Massachusetts Open Meeting Law.    
 
§ 12-27. Guiding Principles 
 
In performing its work, the Open Space Committee shall be guided by the Lunenburg Master Plan and 
the Lunenburg Open Space and Recreation Plan.
