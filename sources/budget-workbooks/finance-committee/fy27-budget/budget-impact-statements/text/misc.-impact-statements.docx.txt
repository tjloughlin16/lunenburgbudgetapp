

Select Board
Line Item: 11222-531003-Contracted Services:

FY26 Budget: $6,000
FY27 Tier 1/Core: $13,500
FY27 Balanced: $6,000
Total Reduction: $7,500

Impact: The Town of Lunenburg contracts with John Guilfoil Public Relations LLC to provide professional crisis communication, media relations, and strategic content support. The Tier 1 request reflects the actual annual cost of maintaining the full service agreement. Funding the line at the FY26 level would require reducing or eliminating these services.

JGPR provides 24/7 on-call crisis communication support and assists the Town during high-risk or high-visibility incidents such as officer-involved incidents, fatal accidents, major emergencies, or other events requiring immediate public messaging. Services include review of sensitive information prior to release and  preparation of coordinated media statements.  They also have the ability to deploy a large response team during a mass critical incident to assist with on-scene media coordination and public information management.

In addition to emergency response, the contract includes routine communications support, with up to three pieces of content per month such as press releases, stakeholder letters, fact sheets, FAQs, and website or social media messaging.

Reducing the line item to $6,000 would limit the Town’s ability to maintain a full-service contract and would likely result in the loss of 24/7 crisis response coverage, reduced professional review of public statements, and reduced capacity to provide timely, accurate information to residents during emergencies or controversial issues. In that event, responsibility for media relations and crisis communication would need to be handled internally with existing staff or with a one-time emergency-services contract, which comes at a high rate.

Impact: This line item is proposed to provide limited funding for activities of the Economic Development Committee (EDC), specifically the annual Town Official Meet &amp; Greet. Line Item: 11992-521001-Office Equipment Mtc.

FY26 Budget: $7,500
FY27 Tier 1/Core $7,500
FY27 Balanced: $6,500
Total Reduction: $1,000

Impact: This line item provides centralized funding for the purchase of office furniture and similar larger items that cannot typically be absorbed within individual departmental office supply budgets. The account is used primarily, but not exclusively, for Town Hall and Ritter Building departments.

Typical expenses include desks, chairs, file cabinets, tables, shelving, and other office furnishings needed to replace worn or broken items or to equip workspaces. Reducing the line by $1,000 will limit the number of replacement or upgrade purchases that can be made during the year and may require departments to defer non-essential furniture purchases or continue using older equipment longer than planned. Core operations would not be affected, but flexibility to address workspace needs would be reduced.

Board of Health
Line Item: 15121-511000-Admin Assistant BOH Salary

FY26 Budget: $52,630.56
FY27 Tier 1/Core: $54,204.12
FY27 Balanced: $31,182.19
Total Reduction: $23,021.93

Impact: This line item currently funds a full-time Administrative Assistant assigned to the Board of Health. As part of a broader reorganization of Land Use and regulatory departments, staffing is being restructured to provide shared administrative support across multiple departments rather than maintaining separate full-time administrative positions within each office.

Under the current structure, the Planning Department, Building/Zoning Department, and Board of Health each have their own dedicated administrative staff.  With the implementation of online permitting, the amount of in-person processing and manual paperwork has been reduced. As a result, the level of hands-on administrative staffing required to support these functions has decreased.

Based on these efficiencies, the Town is moving toward a consolidated staffing model in which the departments of Planning, Building/Zoning, Conservation, Board of Health, and related land use functions will be supported by two full-time staff members.  Specific details are still being discussed. 

As a result of this reorganization, the Board of Health will no longer be served by a single full-time employee assigned exclusively to that department. Instead, staff time will be shared among the departments based on workload and operational needs. This change is expected to occur regardless of the final budget level, although the exact allocation of hours and responsibilities may vary depending on funding.

The balanced budget reflects a reduction in the amount charged directly to the Board of Health salary line to account for this shared staffing model.

Band Concert
Line Item: 16932-520000- Band Concert

FY26 Budget: $6,000
FY27 Tier 1/Core: $6,000
FY27 Balanced: $0.00
Total Reduction: $6,000

Impact: This line item funds the Town’s summer band concert program. The program typically consists of five concerts held on evenings during the summer and features a traditional New England town band performing a variety of music, including patriotic selections, marches, show tunes, folk music, jazz, and music associated with the armed services. The funds in this account are used to pay the performers for these concerts.

Because this program is recreational in nature and not part of core municipal operations, the balanced budget eliminates funding for the concerts. If the line is not funded, the summer band concert series would not be held unless an alternate funding source, such as donations, sponsorships, or other non-tax revenue, is identified.
