
Impact Statement FY27 Lunenburg Adult Activity Center

Line Item # Salary – Assistant Meal Site Manager Reduction: $19,825.74
Service: Dietary Aides
Impact: Dietary Aides provide essential operational support to the Meal Site Manager, including meal setup, dining area sanitation (tables, chairs, and counters), dishwashing, basic food preparation, table setting, meal service, and coordination of the Meals on Wheels program, including distribution to volunteer drivers. Elimination of this position will require the Meal Site Manager to assume these duties, resulting in decreased operational efficiency and a likely reduction in the quality and timeliness of meal services provided to program participants.

Line Item # Kitchen Supplies Reduction: $1,500
Service: Paper goods and kitchen supplies
Impact: The Meal Program operates as a self-supporting service funded by participating seniors, with no dedicated food budget line. Maintaining affordability is critical to ensuring access. A reduction in this line item would necessitate absorbing the cost of paper goods, cleaning supplies, cups, and kitchen equipment (e.g., knives, serving utensils) into other budget lines, such as Office Supplies. This shift may create budgetary strain or result in increased meal costs for participants.

Line Item # Programs Reduction: $2,000 Service: Programming
Impact: Program funding supports a range of fitness, wellness, social, and educational activities that promote health, independence, and community engagement among older adults. A reduction in funding will limit the scope and frequency of program offerings, thereby reducing access to services that support the well-being of the senior population.
