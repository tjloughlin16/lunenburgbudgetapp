To:  Jennifer Warren-Dyment, Town Manager
From: Christopher Ruth, Facilities Director

RE: Budget Impact Memo – March 19, 2026

Dear Jen,

If the town proceeds with the balanced budget shown, the following impacts will be felt by the town mainly in the parks department. The specific impacts are listed below:

Line Item – Park Supervisor Line
Reduction - $30,919.54 to $0
Service - This line covers the salary for the recreation director. The main responsibilities of this role are to provide recreation programming for the town such as: summer sports camps, stem programs, parks and recreation billing, management of the beach staff and operations, management of field / park rentals.
Impact - This would eliminate the recreation director position entirely as well as any recreation programming, meaning the after school and summer programs that kids love and parents depend on would be gone.
The elimination of this position will also add additional work load to the facilities director, as that position will now need to be more involved in the beach staff hiring and managing.
This also adds additional work load to the facilities admin staff who picks up the responsibility of parks billing in the absence of a recreation director.
Line Item – Town Beach Salaries
Reduction - $10,000.00
Service - This line covers the salary for all of the beach staff including the beach director, lifeguards and swim instructors. The main responsibilities of these roles are to provide life guard coverage for free swim at the beach and teaching swim lessons to the kids. The beach staff conducts roughly 165 swim lessons per summer and 2,200 users for the summer.
Impact - The overall effect of this change remains to be seen. In the past the beach salaries have overed around $25,000, this year the parks commission voted to increase the rates to use the beach in a proactive effort to offset costs. Beach fees for residents increased by roughly 40% and non residents increased by roughly 50%.
The parks commission hopes the anticipated additional revenue will offset the cut to this line, but we do not yet know what the rate increase will do to actual usage and revenue.
The beach program bridges fiscal years, but funding for the program would cease after the summer of 2026.

Line Item – Salaries Programming
Reduction - $5,000 to $0
Service – The salaries programming line is typically used to offer free programs to the residents or to occasionally offset the cost of being 1 or 2 people short to run a program.
Impact – With the loss of this funding in combination with the loss of the recreation director will again mean that there will be no recreation programs at all.

Line Item – Purchase of Service
Reduction - $20,000
Service – This line item covers all maintenance and repair to the town parks and fields. The primary users of these fields are the Lunenburg Schools baseball and softball programs, the Lunenburg youth baseball and softball league as well as a couple of rentals for various leagues. 
Impact – The reduction to this line item will limit us to regular maintenance only such as mowing at a reduced frequency, seasonal clean up activities and some infield maintenance. This will also leave the parks department with minimal unforeseen repair money and no money for improvements such as fence repairs, field repairs, improvements to parking areas and repairs to structures. This is a line the facilities and parks department has been trying to increase for the last few years in an attempt to tackle some of the deferred maintenance on all of parks locations.

Line Item – Departmental Expenses
Reduction - $400
Service – The departmental expenses line is used for parks related office supplies, some repairs materials and beach supplies.
Impact - The departmental expenses line has been lowered slightly and will have a minor effect on what we can purchase for operational supplies and repair materials.

In summary, this budget basically eliminates the entire recreation program, and will limit our ability to maintain our parks.
