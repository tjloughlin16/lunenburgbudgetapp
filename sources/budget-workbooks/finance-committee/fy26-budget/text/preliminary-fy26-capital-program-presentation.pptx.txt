Public Safety Building 2nd Floor Design – 
Office Space &amp; File Storage: $42,000
This request is for funding to complete design and engineering work to fit out the unused space on the 2nd floor of the Public Safety building.  This space will be used for record storage as well as additional office space for the Police Department.  This project is a high priority for the Department as they are currently out of space for their Officers and Detectives and have already converted their conference room into office space.

FY26 Capital Program

Town Hall, Window Replacement: $210,000
The current Town Hall windows have been replaced before (with the exception of (5) original basement windows) with vinyl windows. These windows have gotten to a point where many of them are inoperable, some of the windows also extend above the ceiling level inside the space. The plan for the new windows is to have a fixed transom at the top and a regular double hung window below.

Compact Backhoe: $115,000

The compact backhoe will be used primarily in the Cemeteries to excavate graves and will be used for drainage repairs, roadway repairs, and snow and ice.
