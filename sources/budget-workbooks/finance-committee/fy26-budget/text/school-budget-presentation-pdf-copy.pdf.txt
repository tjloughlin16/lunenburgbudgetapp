SECTION 6-6A CTION ON THE BUDGET
(b) Review - The finance committee shall consider, in open public meetings, the detailed expenditures 
proposed for each town agency and may confer with representatives of each such agency in connection 
with its review and consideration. The finance committee may require the town manager, or any other 
town agency, to furnish it with such additional information as it may deem necessary to assist it in its 
review and consideration of the proposed operating budget.
SECTION 2-3 COMMITTEES
(c) Finance Committee Powers and Duties
(1) The finance committee shall have the primary responsibility to report to town meeting on the proposed 
budget of the town manager and all warrant articles having a fiscal impact on the town, as more 
particularly detailed in the charter, town by-laws, and the laws of the commonwealth; before preparing its 
final recommendations, the finance committee shall hold 1 or more public hearings to permit public 
discussion on the subject matter of the articles contained in the warrant.

School Department Budget Request
1. Provide employee headcount data from FY2020 to FY2025, including all funding sources.  
(Example: Hudson Public Schools FY2025 Budget Handbook  , we don’t need a graph, data in a spreadsheet is 
sufficient - or Lunenburg Superintendent's FY21 Budget - Identified Needs  page 12 as long it includes the current 
employee headcount from all funding sources and the staff additions and reductions are also of all funding 
sources)
2. Present a comprehensive Operational Budget for FY2025 and FY2026 that encompasses all 
School Department operations, irrespective of the funding source. (Similar to the Sewer 
Department Budget)
3. Detail all revenue sources for the School Department, irrespective of the purpose or origin 
of the funds.
4. Future budget presentations to the FinCom should show changes from the last publicly 
presented budget to the FinCom, not just the latest budget version approved by the School 
Committee. This approach ensures consistency, transparency, and alignment with 
publicly available information.

School Department Budget Request
Why These Requests Are Critical to Our Community and Town Meeting
1. Ensures Transparency and Accountability:
• Helps anticipate and mitigate financial disruptions from funding fluctuations.
• Transparency builds trust, particularly when seeking additional funding through 
overrides.
2. Supports Accurate Budget Analysis:
• Enables holistic evaluation of resource allocation.
• Leads to more informed, data -driven budget discussions and sound fiscal 
decisions.
===PAGE 9===
3. Assesses Financial Risks:
• Identifying all the School Department operations helps the community understand the 
department’s financial challenges.
• Clearer data helps demonstrate that resources are used efficiently
4. Aids Decision-Making:
• Proper data comparison to past budgets supports evidence -based recommendations.
• Ensures the community is fully informed of the scope and costs of services provided 
by the School Department.
• With a second consecutive year of significant deficits impacting staffing and services, 
a comprehensive understanding of the School Department's operations and finances 
is crucial for this and future fiscal years.
