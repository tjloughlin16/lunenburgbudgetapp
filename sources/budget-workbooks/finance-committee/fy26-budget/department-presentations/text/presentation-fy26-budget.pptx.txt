How do we address these needs?
The #1 issue on our radar for this year, is food insecurities resulting in health issues.
Food Insecurities are not just lack of food items, but 
the fact that 
many seniors, especially those who are living alone, are not preparing and eating healthy meals at home.
By offering hot nutritional meals at the Lunenburg Adult Activity Center, whether breakfast, lunch or dinner, we are covering this basic need for good nutrition.

The changes in this year’s budget include replacing the position of a part-time 
(19-1/2 hr.) 
Assistant Meal Site Manager with two 
(10 hr. each) 
Kitchen Aide positions.  
The Meal Site Manager position, in order to cover food services 5 days a week, will be 36 hrs. wk.
Other changes are a slight increase in programming, office supplies and mileage.
This request is a modest increase of $30,011.42.  
This is the minimum that is needed to address the needs of the growing senior community.

Thank you!
This requested budget would allow the Lunenburg COA/ Lunenburg Adult Activity Center to extend services for the critical needs of the Town of Lunenburg’s senior population for FY26.
The requested Council on Aging budget breaks down to approximately $72 per Lunenburg senior for FY 26.
The Lunenburg COA would not only continue to expand the current programming, supportive services but be able to increase our food service program by providing additional hot meals on site throughout the week along with educational programs and specialty programs, in addition to our current Veterans’ Breakfast and Memory Cafés.
