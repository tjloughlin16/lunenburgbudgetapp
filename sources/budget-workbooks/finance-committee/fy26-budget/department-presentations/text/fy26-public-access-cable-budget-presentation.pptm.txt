Preamble - Financial
While PAC is a Town department, we are self funded at present
The PAC revenue from Comcast is reserved for PAC Capital Improvements and Operations and does not affect Town budgets
The Enterprise Fund is reserved for fitting out and equipping our new studio, and other expenses
6

Income Sources
The franchise fee from Comcast is the principal source of PAC income, although this is reducing due to video service revenue dropping
If this trend continues, PAC will need financial support from the Town to maintain and improve our present services
In addition, PAC receives interest monthly
Some of the Enterprise Fund (under advisement from the Town Treasurer) has been invested in short term bonds, presently yielding about $35,000 annually, which is reinvested
7

Franchise Fees
8
Cable Cutting is a misnomer- it’s video services that gets cut, and that’s what the franchise fee is based on
Last calendar year, Comcast’s video service revenue from Lunenburg dropped by 2.39%
This was used to estimate PAC’s Franchise fee revenue for FY25
Franchise fees are governed under Section 622 of the 
Cable Communications Act of 1984
. Section 622, states that municipalities are entitled to a maximum of 5% of gross 
revenues
 derived from the operation of the cable system for the provision of cable services such as 
Public, educational, and government access
 (PEG) TV channels.
