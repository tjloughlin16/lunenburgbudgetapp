FY26 ™ Budget  (Insurance Calculation)
Lunenburg has 4 health insurance plans (HMO Blue Fam, HMO Blue Ind, Blue Care Fam, Blue 
Care Ind, Blue Select. The 14.84 % increase is the monthly rate of increase per each plan from 
FY25 to FY26. The number of employees on each plan is in the table. Due to life changes, the plan 
can change monthly.
Choose your own Adventure 
Example: Choose a Health Plan, Start with the Monthly Cost in FY25, Multiple by 1.1484 (this is 
the 14.84 increase), See if you agree with the FY26 Monthly Cost per the plan you selected, then 
multiple by the number of employees getting that plan, and you will come up with a monthly cost 
per that plan. Sum the monthly cost of all plan participants. Multiply by 12 months and you come 
out with the district cost per the insurance. Check your number with the Annual Cost box above.
