BACKGROUND•The Sewer Commission was established in 2006 and operates under the Sewer Enterprise Fund (prior to 2006 sewer under Select Board).  •System consists of  16 miles of gravity sanitary sewer, 3.9 miles of force mains, 2.3 miles of low-pressure sewers and 10 pumping stations.•Vendors are contracted to ensure system operates efficiently.•Intermunicipal agreements in place with Fitchburg and Leominster to treat wastewater.

Recent Emergency Repairs –Mass 1Problem:The dresser coupling in the wet well of Mass 1 corroded and was subject to popping off. If the pipe became disconnected the wet well would fill quickly and potentially overflow.Solution:  Drained the force main using a local pumping service (15,000 gallons). Flow maintained by Pump during repair.   Staging installed within wet well.  The 12’ 8” pipe was cut and replaced.  A steel riser was installed to support the pipe so that this problem will not reoccur.Cost:  Approximately $40,000

Rate Increase 
Due to increasing costs, fees, new NPDES requirements, and capital needs sewer rate will increase byapproximately $12.34 per month for the average residential customer.  The average residential unit is currently $233 per quarter, and will increase to $270. The minimum charge will increase from $115 to $126.The connection fee will increase from $2,250 to $3,900 due to Leominster increase.
