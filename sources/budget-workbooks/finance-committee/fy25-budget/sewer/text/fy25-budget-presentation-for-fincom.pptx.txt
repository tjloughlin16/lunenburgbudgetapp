BACKGROUND
The Sewer Commission was established in 2006 and operates under the Sewer Enterprise Fund (prior to 2006 sewer under Select Board).  
System consists of  16 miles of gravity sanitary sewer, 3.9 miles of force mains, 2.3 miles of low-pressure sewers and 10 pumping stations.
Vendors are contracted to ensure system operates efficiently.
Intermunicipal agreements in place with Fitchburg and Leominster to treat wastewater.

Current Issues/Concerns

Significant infiltration &amp; inflow
Pump stations in need of numerous upgrades/repairs because they are nearly at the end of their design life (20/25 years)
Increased fees from Leominster &amp; Fitchburg
Fitchburg is increasing the sewer rate 7% each year for the next five (5) years
Leominster rate increasing 16.17% in FY25 and an additional 3% in FY26
Rising labor &amp; material costs
New reporting requirements as co-permittee of Leominster’s NPDES Permit

Strategic Plan

Infiltration &amp; inflow (I/I)
I/I is rain or groundwater that is getting into the system and being sent to Leominster &amp; Fitchburg facilities increasing cost.
