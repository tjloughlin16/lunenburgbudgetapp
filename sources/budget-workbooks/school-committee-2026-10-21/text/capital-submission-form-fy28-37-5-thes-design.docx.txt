2. DESCRIPTION AND JUSTIFICATION
Description and scope:      Funding is requested for architectural and engineering services to develop preliminary design and schematic plans for a new 400-student school. The scope will include site planning, educational space requirements, conceptual floor plans, building systems, accessibility, energy efficiency, preliminary cost estimates, and project scheduling. The completed plans will provide the foundation for future design, permitting, funding, and construction.
