2. DESCRIPTION AND JUSTIFICATION
Description and scope: Seeking funding for a man lift.  Improved safety: Provides a stable, purpose-built platform for employees performing work at elevated heights, reducing reliance on ladders and makeshift equipment. 
  Routine building maintenance: Allows Facilities staff to safely perform tasks such as replacing lights, servicing ceiling-mounted equipment, inspecting roofs and gutters, and making repairs in high-ceiling areas. 
  Cost savings: Reduces the need to hire outside contractors for routine elevated maintenance that school Facilities staff can safely perform themselves. 
  Faster response to maintenance needs: Staff can address repairs and maintenance issues more quickly without waiting for contractor availability or equipment rentals. 
  Reduced equipment rental costs: Frequent rental of lifts can become expensive over time. Owning a unit provides access whenever needed. 
  Emergency response: Provides the district with equipment immediately available for urgent repairs or building issues involving elevated areas. 
  Improved efficiency: A man lift allows staff to access difficult-to-reach areas more efficiently than ladders or scaffolding. 
  Long-term asset: A properly maintained lift can serve the district for many years and be used across multiple school buildings. 
  Minimizes disruption: In-house maintenance can often be completed more quickly, reducing disruptions to students, staff, and school operations. 
  District-wide use: The equipment could be shared among all Lunenburg public schools, maximizing its value and usefulness.
