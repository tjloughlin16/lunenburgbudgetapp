2. DESCRIPTION AND JUSTIFICATION
Description and scope: Funding will allow for purchase and installation of age-appropriate, inclusive and
accessible playground equipment based on recommendations of a playground designer with input from
the community and stakeholders.
● Benefits: Playground equipment will be smaller in
scale, potentially reducing injuries. Playground
equipment will be selected to be accessible to students
with disabilities.
● Cost/Benefit Analysis: Funds invested in smaller scale
equipment which are more appropriate for the age of
the students at the Primary School and accessible by
students with disabilities may reduce liability and
legal costs in the event of legal action based on injury
or discrimination.
● Maintenance Analysis: Currently equipment is in
good condition.
● Maintenance History: Broken equipment is repaired
or taken offline. Rubber mulch bed is maintained at
12 inches deep.
● Green Communities Eligibility and Feedback: N/A

Consequences if not funded:        Continued use of outdated and potentially inadequate playground equipment may increase safety and maintenance concerns. 
  Students with disabilities may not have equitable access to playground activities and equipment. 
  Younger students may not have equipment appropriately designed for their age, size, and developmental needs. 
  Delaying the project may increase repair and maintenance costs as existing equipment continues to deteriorate. 
  The school may face limitations in providing a safe, inclusive, and accessible outdoor recreation area for all students. 
  Failure to upgrade may limit opportunities for physical activity, social interaction, and inclusive play. 
  Deferring the project could result in higher future replacement costs as additional equipment and site components require replacement. 
  Continued reliance on aging equipment may lead to portions of the playground being taken out of service, reducing available play space.
