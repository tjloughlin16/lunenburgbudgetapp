2. DESCRIPTION AND JUSTIFICATION
Description and scope: Conduct a comprehensive evaluation of the school’s existing boiler system to determine its current condition, operational efficiency, remaining useful life, and capacity to meet the building’s heating demands. The study will identify deficiencies, maintenance concerns, and potential code or safety issues and will evaluate options for repair, replacement, or system upgrades.The study should include recommendations for appropriately sized, energy-efficient replacement equipment, estimated project costs, potential energy savings, and a phased implementation plan to support future capital planning and budgeting.

Consequences if not funded: If the boiler study is not funded, the school district may lack the information necessary to properly plan for the replacement or rehabilitation of the existing heating system. Continued operation without a comprehensive assessment could result in:Unexpected boiler failures and costly emergency repairs.Loss of heat during the school year, potentially disrupting school operations.Increased energy and operating costs from inefficient equipment.Delayed identification of safety, code, or system deficiencies.Higher future replacement costs due to emergency procurement or inflation.Difficulty developing an accurate capital budget and long-term replacement plan.Reduced ability to evaluate energy-efficient alternatives and potential savings.Increased risk of having to make short-term repairs to aging equipment rather than planning a cost-effective replacement.
