III.   Custom’s Solution 
 
Based upon our understanding of Lunenburg Public Schools  District’s needs and our experience designing 
and implementing VOIP solutions, CCS has proposed a design for Lunenburg Public Schools District based on 
Cisco technology.  Cisco is a leader in the Collaboration industry.  The design includes Cisco Call Manager for 
Call Control, Unity Connection for Integrated Messaging, Emergency Responder for E -911, Voice Gateway, 
and IP Phones. 
 
To start, we have proposed the Cisco Unified Communications Call Manager and Unity system utilizing one 
Cisco Business Edition 6000 (BE6000) appliance.  This appliance will provide centralized Call Processing (Cisco 
Call Manager) and Cisco Unity Connections for the  school.   The Cisco BE6000 is built on virtualized Cisco 
Unified Computing System™ (Cisco UCS®) products, which are designed for performance and density over a 
wide range of business workloads.  This server would be housed at LMHS and serve all 3 schools and Central 
Office.  This server would be a prerequisite before implementing VOIP at any of the buildings. 
 
BE6000M is a medium -density model that supports between four to six collaboration applications.  The 
BE6000 ships with a suite of integrated voice applications and utilities including:   
 
• Cisco Unified Communications Manager (CUCM):  CUCM is the call- processing engine of BE6000, 
extending voice and video features to network devices such as IP phones, telepresence endpoints, 
media-processing devices, gateways, and multimedia applications. Fully integrated instant messaging 
and presence  services are also included.  The system delivers integrated IP telephony, gateway, 
voicemail, and Automated-Attendant functions, allowing you to deploy one device to address all your 
business needs, thereby simplifying management, maintenance, and operations and delivering a lower 
TCO. 
• Cisco Unity® Connection: Unity integrates voice-messaging and voice-recognition functions to provide 
continuous global access to calls and messages. Its advanced convergence -based communication 
services allow you to use natural- language voice commands to place calls or listen to messages in 
hands-free mode and to check voice messages from your desktop, either through integration with your 
email inbox or by using a web browser. It also provides robust Automated -Attendant functions, 
including intelligent routing for incoming calls and easily customizable call- screening and message -
notification options.

For call contro l, access to the Public Switched Telephone Network (PSTN)  and for interconnectivity to the 
existing PA System in each school, we are proposing a Cisco 8200-1NT Voice Gateway.  At LMHS, one gateway 
will be populated with the 1 -port PRI card for access to the PSTN and the other will be populated with (2) 
ports of FXS and (4) ports of FXO for interconnectivity to the PA system and for fail- over POTs lines.  These 
lines would be used for fail-over and local outbound calling, if the PRI or the Call Control Server goes down. 
Turkey Hill Elementary School and Primary School would receive one gateway each populated with (2) ports 
of FXS and (4) ports of FXO for interconnectivity to the PA system and for fail-over POTs lines.     
  
To provide for survivability in case of failure, the  routers will be configured with SRS Telephony Services 
(SRST).  SRST automatically detects a failure in the network, and, using the Cisco Simple Network Automated 
Provisioning (SNAP) capability, initiates a process to intelligently auto -configure the router to provide call-
processing backup redundancy for the IP phones in that room. The router provides c all processing for the 
duration of the failure, ensuring that the phone capabilities stay up and operational. Upon restoration and 
connectivity to the network, the system automatically shifts call-processing functions to the Cisco BE6000.  
 
For interconnectivity to the existing paging system and for paging through the IP Phones, CCS has included 
additional licensing for Singlewire Informacast. 
 
Finally, for IP Phones, CCS has included the following models: 
• Admin and Main Office:  Cisco 9861 IP Phone has a 5 -inch Color LCD, 800 x 480 resolution, backlit  
and 10 line buttons.  For the Main Office clerks, these phones will also have an additional 20 lines. 
• Offices and Classrooms:  For all other areas, the Cisco 9841 IP Phone with 3.5 -inch Grayscale LCD, 
384 x160 resolution, backlit  and 4 physical lines will be provided.  Phones will be wall- mounted, 
where designated on the provided plans. 
 
CCS has included 1 -year of warranty support for all equipment .  The IP Phone licensing is proposed for 3 
years and the paging licensing is proposed for 1 year. 
 
Finally, Custom has included new Category 6 cable runs for all phones at Primary School and all wall-
mounted phones at LMHS and Turkey Hill Elementary School.

IV. Scope of Services 
 
Scope of Services:  
 
The success of your project will be ensured via assignment of a Project Manager and appropriately skilled 
Systems Engineers to complete a phased implementation as follows: 
 
Phase 1: Design Review 
 
CCS will collaborate with the appropriate representatives of the Lunenburg Public Schools District to review 
the functional requirements for the solution and determine final configuration parameters. Items to be 
reviewed include, but are not limited to:  
 
• IP Planning Session: Our Engineers will work with the Lunenburg Public Schools District to develop an 
IP scheme to be implemented within Lunenburg Public Schools District and verify the IP Scheme for 
the networks. Once finalized, the Engineers will configure the VOIP equipment with the defined IP 
scheme. All devices statically addressed by CCS will be documented and included as part of the As-Built 
documentation package.  
• VLAN Planning Session: CCS’ Engineers will work with Lunenburg Public Schools District to review the 
separation of the locations through the use of  VLANs and subnets and develop a plan to implement 
the auxiliary VLANs required for the VOIP network. Once finalized, the VLAN and subnet schemes will 
be applied to all network equipment. Auxiliary VLANS will be documented and included as part of the 
As-Built documentation.  
• Telephony Integration Planning Session:  CCS’ Engineers will work with Lunenburg Public Schools  
District to understand and establish the call routing patterns for local, long -distance, internal, and 
external calling. Also, the use and configuration of Auto Attendant will be defined, if needed. Telephony 
fail-over procedures and configuration will be discussed. Call routing patterns and Auto-Attendant call 
flows will be included as part of the As-Built documentation.  
 
The information gathered from the design review, will be documented as the final system design to be used 
in support of the implementation. 
 
Phase 2: Equipment Configuration and Implementation:  
Lunenburg Public Schools District local and long-distance telephony provider(s) will most likely be required 
to be available during installation and testing of the system. A list of extensions, voice mailboxes, and trunk 
lines will be required from a representative of the Lunenburg Public Schools District or outsourced provider 
prior to the installation. Configuration, telephone numbers, and IP address information along with analog 
functionality are pre-requisite for the installation of the routing equipment.

CCS will have a documented cutover and testing plan including the anticipated schedule, implementation

to be available for any technical issues that may arise or help the end users with any questions.  During the 
time of testing, we will monitor the live production environment and make any final configuration 
adjustments as necessary.  
 
Phase 4: Close-out and Knowledge Transfer  
During the project’s close-out, CCS will provide Knowledge Transfer to the Lunenburg Public Schools District’s 
technical staff. We will review all final configurations, as-built documentation, and provide basic training on

provided (8) hours of Administration training and up (16) hours of end user training.   
 
CCS will deem this project complete with acceptance sought upon completion of knowledge transfer 
activities, and the transfer of all project documentation to the Lunenburg Public Schools District.
