2. DESCRIPTION AND JUSTIFICATION
Description and scope: The installation of a new wheelchair lift is necessary to provide safe, reliable, and equitable access to all areas of the school for students, staff, and visitors with mobility limitations. The new lift will improve accessibility, support ADA compliance, and provide dependable access where the existing system may be outdated, unreliable, or inadequate. This improvement will also reduce barriers to participation in classrooms, programs, events, and other school activities while improving the overall safety and accessibility of the facility.

Consequences if not funded:      If the new wheelchair lift is not funded, the school may continue to face accessibility and safety concerns. Continued reliance on an aging or inadequate lift may also result in increased maintenance costs, unexpected breakdowns, and disruptions to school operations.
