Scope of Services
As part of this engagement, CCS will deliver a structured and comprehensive Scope of Work (detailed below) to ensure 
a smooth transition and minimize business disruption. Our approach includes careful planning, documentation, and 
execution across all key components of the Lunenburg School District Camera Replacement Oct 2026
Scope of Work:
CCS’s Scope of Work will include the following: 
Discovery & Planning
 Project kickoff call with client stakeholders
 Site walkthrough to confirm mounting locations, cable paths, and rack space
 Confirm camera fields of view and coverage at each location
 Confirm door controller mounting, reader placement, and power locations
 Confirm IP address schema and naming conventions with client IT
 Review floorplans and finalize device placement map
 Confirm firmware and licensing requirements
IP Camera Installation & Configuration   
 Unbox, assemble, and inventory all cameras
 Update firmware on all camera units
 Assign static IP addresses per IP schema; apply standard device settings
 Mount and install all interior cameras at locations per approved placement map
 Mount and install all exterior cameras at locations per approved placement map
 Acquire all camera feeds into video management system
 Configure recording schedules, retention settings, and motion detection
 Verify live video feed, recording, and field of view at each location
 Create user accounts and configure VMS dashboard for client viewing
Documentation & Closeout
 Deliver as-built documentation including IP address schema and device inventory with MAC addresses and 
serial numbers
 Conduct knowledge transfer session with designated client staff — system operation, user management, and 
alert configuration
 Provide user guide and configuration summary document
 Support first-time login for client staff 
Closeout Meeting & Project Documentation Review
 A close-out meeting to review pertinent project documentation including but not limited to:
o Overview of project deliverables
o Address any questions, or concerns
Assumptions: 
 Cameras will be powered via power over ethernet switch ports or power cubes that already exist onsite or will be 
provided by Lunenburg School District
 Network configuration including VLAN assignments already exist onsite or will be provided by Lunenburg School 
District
 The installation environment will be free of obstructions or conditions that could prevent the proper installation 
and functioning of the door access controllers.
 Existing cabling meets the necessary specifications and function properly. Remediation of deficient cabling is 
outside this scope.
 PoE-capable switch ports are available for all devices unless a switch is included in this scope.
