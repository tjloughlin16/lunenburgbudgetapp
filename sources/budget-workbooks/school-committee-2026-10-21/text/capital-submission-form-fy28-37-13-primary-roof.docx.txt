2. DESCRIPTION AND JUSTIFICATION
Description and scope: The existing school roof is approximately 20 years old and is approaching the anticipated end of its useful service life. Replacement is recommended to prevent water infiltration, protect the building structure and interior finishes, reduce ongoing maintenance costs, and minimize the risk of unplanned emergency repairs. The project will provide an opportunity to improve insulation and energy efficiency, address applicable building code requirements, and extend the useful life of the facility. Proactive replacement will help preserve the district's investment in its school buildings, protect the learning environment, and support long-term facilities planning and budget management.

Consequences if not funded: The existing school roof is approximately 20 years old and requires evaluation for replacement based on its age, condition, and remaining useful life. Failure to fund the necessary roof replacement may increase the risk of water infiltration, damage to interior building materials and equipment, deterioration of structural components, and potential indoor air quality concerns.Continued reliance on temporary repairs may result in increasing maintenance expenses and unanticipated emergency costs. Water intrusion can damage ceilings, insulation, walls, flooring, electrical systems, and other building components. If these conditions are not addressed promptly, the district may incur additional costs beyond the roof replacement itself.Roof deterioration may also disrupt school operations, require the relocation of classrooms, limit access to portions of the building, and interfere with instructional time. In more serious circumstances, affected areas may need to be closed until repairs or remediation are completed.
