LUNENBURG PUBLIC SCHOOLS 
Office of Teaching & Learning 
MEMORANDUM 
To: Lunenburg School Committee 
From: Kimberly Sarfde, EdD, Director of Teaching & Learning 
Date: October 7, 2026 
Re: Fair Share Act (H.2518) Earmarks – Plan and Request for Authorization 
Purpose 
The district has two Fair Share Act (H.2518) earmarks available. This memorandum presents the 
administration’s plan for these funds and requests School Committee authorization to carry it out. 
Our Plan for Each Earmark 
Physical Improvements – $175,000. The district’s plan for these funds covers HVAC upgrades; an 
electrical upgrade at Turkey Hill Elementary School; exterior doors; handicap-accessible doors at 
the Primary School and Central Office; a Primary School generator; gym and hallway bleachers; a 
high school coil replacement; and server infrastructure. 
Playground Design & Planning – $30,000. These funds will be used for the design and planning of 
the Primary School playground replacement. These funds do not cover construction. 
How We Will Carry Out the Plan 
Every project will meet the following standards: 
• Priority and need. Projects will be completed in priority order, with safety, accessibility, 
building operations, and the learning environment first. 
• Eligibility. Every project fits the earmark’s approved purpose and grant requirements. 
• Cost. Spending is based on actual quotes and will not exceed the allocation. 
•  Procurement. All purchases will follow Massachusetts procurement law and district policy. 
• Timeline. Each funded project will be completed within the required time frame. 
Because needs exceed the $175,000 allocation, the district will fund projects in priority order until 
the allocation is fully used. To protect every dollar, if a project cannot be completed on time, the 
district will move directly to the next project on the list. Any remaining projects will stay on the 
district’s capital needs list. 
Requested School Committee Action 
The administration requests authorization to carry out this plan as presented. 
Suggested Motion: 
I move that the Lunenburg School Committee authorize the administration to carry out 
the Fair Share Act (H.2518) earmark plan as presented.
