Subject: Fair Share Special Support Earmark (FC 1192) – Classroom Instructional 
Technology 
Purpose 
The administration respectfully requests the School Committee’s approval to expend the Fair 
Share Special Support earmark (FC 1192) on classroom instructional technology for Lunenburg 
Public Schools. The total budget is $20,000, which will be used for interactive Touchview 
display screens and document cameras. 
Description of Planned Purchases 
Interactive Touchview Display Screens – $18,000 
These funds will support the purchase of interactive Touchview display screens to replace 
aging or non-interactive presentation technology in classrooms across the district. The touch-
enabled displays allow teachers to annotate, manipulate digital content, and engage students 
directly at the board, strengthening whole-group and small-group instruction and reducing 
downtime caused by outdated or malfunctioning equipment. 
Document Cameras – $2,000 
These funds will support the purchase of document cameras that allow teachers to project 
texts, student work, and manipulatives in real time. Document cameras make it easier to model 
close reading, demonstrate problem-solving steps, and share exemplars, particularly in English 
language arts and mathematics, while replacing outdated or unreliable equipment. 
Recommended Action 
The administration recommends that the School Committee approve the expenditure of the FC 
1192 allocation as described above. All purchases will be made in accordance with applicable 
procurement requirements, grant requirements, and district policy. 
Suggested Motion: 
I move that the Lunenburg School Committee approve the expenditure of the Fair 
Share Special Support earmark (FC 1192) as presented.
