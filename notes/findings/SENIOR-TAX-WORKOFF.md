# Senior Property Tax Work-Off: what the record holds

Research notes for a report requested by a resident, 26 October 2026. Not a published page.
Every figure below carries its document, its line or page, and its raw text. Participants are
private individuals: no name, address or individual amount appears here, even where the source
prints them (the FY2015 and FY2016 annual-report wage lists, and several committee and library
minutes, do). Counts and totals only.

**The program is M.G.L. c.59 §5K.** The veterans' equivalent (§5N) was searched for and **no
Lunenburg §5N program appears in anything searched** (the one "5N" hit, Select Board
2011-11-22 line 95, is a time, "6:30 on the 5n"). It is not discussed further.

---

## 0. Read this first: which "year" a figure belongs to

The program runs on a **calendar year, January 1 to November 1** (FY2015 annual report line 2654:
*"receive up to $1,000 off their property tax bill in the calendar year rather than the fiscal
year"*), and the credit lands on the **3rd-quarter bill of the following fiscal year** (FY2020
annual report line 2250: *"The 2020 tax abatement maximum is $1,500 and applied to your 3rd quarter
real estate tax bill"*). So one season has three labels:

| program season | annual report that describes it | fiscal year the credit is charged to |
|---|---|---|
| CY2016 (Jan-Nov 2016) | *FY2016* annual report | FY2017 (committee minutes 2016-11-15 line 23: *"the total amount to be charged for the FY17 allowance"*) |

The annual reports from FY2015 onward print the **calendar year of the same number** as the
edition (the FY2025 edition reports January-November 2025). The FY2011 and FY2014 editions print
a count and hours with **no year stated** (see section 3). The Assessors label the same money by
the fiscal year it is charged to. The table below is keyed on the **program season**.

[ASSUMING] that each Assessors fiscal-year total is the season ending the November before
(FY12 credit = CY2011 work). The 2016-11-15 committee line above states this for one year; the
others are inferred from the meeting dates (every approval is November-February).

### Observed vs derived, by year

- **Observed** (printed by the source): every participant count, every hours figure, and the
  totals for CY2010-2012, CY2014, CY2016, CY2018-CY2025.
- **Derived by us**: CY2015 $8,655.00 and CY2017 $8,620.00 (hours x the printed $10 rate). Both
  assume every hour was credited. The reports say hours past 100 *"were considered volunteer
  hours"* (AR 4121 L2655), so these are ceilings if anyone passed 100 hours. CY2016's printed
  $8,372.50 equals 837.25 x $10 to the cent, so for one year the assumption is confirmed.
- **Gross vs net is not consistent.** CY2022 is printed *after* withholdings; CY2024 and CY2025
  are printed *before*; CY2018-CY2021 do not say. The policy says the credit is net of federal,
  Medicare and OBRA withholding (Select Board 2021-09-21-minutes-5267, policy section V.4). So
  **the totals are not one series** until each year's basis is known. CY2022 cannot be put on a
  gross basis from what is printed: the $15/h rate (L2136) gives 587.25 x 15 = $8,808.75, but the
  report states the rate at the time of writing, not the rate paid in 2022.
- **Derived effective rate** (printed total / printed hours), offered as a consistency check only:
  CY2018 $10.0000, CY2019 $10.4640, CY2020 $12.7520, CY2021 $13.5000, CY2022 $12.8579 (net),
  CY2024 $15.0000, CY2025 $15.0000. CY2019 between $10 and $12 fits the mid-year change. CY2020 and
  CY2021 fit the Council on Aging's report of a move to minimum wage (section 2). That fit is a
  hypothesis: the state minimum-wage schedule is not in this archive.

## 2. The program's rules, as the documents state them

**Statute.** The town's own 2021 policy paraphrases it (Select Board 2021-09-21-minutes-5267,
from L308): *"Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 59, §5K provides for the establishment of a
program to allow persons over the age of 60 to volunteer to provide services to a town ... no such
person shall receive a rate of, or be credited with, more than the current minimum wage of the
Commonwealth per hour for services nor shall the reduction of the real property tax bill exceed
$1,500 in a given tax year."*

**Adoption: two dates, never reconciled in the documents.**
- *"Est. 1998"* -- every annual report from FY2015 to FY2018 (AR 4121 L2640; AR 4123, 4124, 4125
  under the heading).
- *"the town initially approved this law in May of 2000"* -- Town Manager's report, Select Board
  2019-03-05-minutes-5150 L209-210.
- The 2021 policy's introduction leaves the chapter **and** the date blank: *"The Town of Lunenburg
  voted to accept the provisions of Massachusetts General Laws Chapter to the Town of Lunenburg."*
  (2021-09-21-minutes-5267, policy section I).
- **No acceptance vote is held in this archive.** Town meeting booklets held start in 2015
  (`sources/town-budget/text/a162-...`); annual reports start FY2011.

**Cap and rate over time.**

**Eligibility, as printed (CY2025, AR 4130 L2992-3015):** age 60+; owner and occupant as of July 1;
tax bill in own or spouse's (60+) name; household income $50,000 (one person) / $75,000 (two),
proof required; CORI for new applicants; re-apply annually. Earnings are exempt from state tax
only, subject to federal and FICA/Medicare withholding, and to OBRA where the participant has no
Worcester Regional or Teachers' pension. The 2021 policy adds: one applicant per household; hours
cannot be shared (policy V.3); first come, first served when applicants exceed positions (VI.C).

**Withholding since 2014.** BOA 2013-12-10-minutes-644: the Town Accountant found payments were
*"being processed incorrectly. According to the IRS, any resident who participates in the program
must be treated as an employee of the Town and issued a W-2 ... It would then be the net pay that
is credited to the tax bill."* So **a $1,500 cap is not a $1,500 reduction** for most participants,
and the gross/net confusion in section 1 dates from here.

**Where the money comes from (not settled).** BOA 2024-09-04-minutes-6733: *"include the potential
liability of senior tax work-off as it likely comes out of overlay"*; BOA 2026-02-04-minutes-7628
records a member asking *"if those funds come from the overlay account"*. Neither states it as
fact. COA 2010-01-12: *"$12,000 in funding for the program."*

**Live documents (the 2026 season).** `sources/town-supplementary/text/494-...brochure`: *"$15.00 per
hour, up to 100 hours, to receive the $1500 abatement"* -- 100 x $15 = $1,500, consistent. The
application (`3358-...`, catalogued as "Application 2025" but headed **2026**: the town overwrote
the file in place) lists **22+ department requests**, from clerical to school lunch monitoring.
**Those are openings offered, not places anyone worked.**

## 6. What was searched, and what each held

- **Annual town reports FY2011-FY2025**, text layer `sources/town-annual-reports/text/` (16 files),
  for `work[ -]?off|workoff|tax work|5K|5N|senior tax|hours of labor|clocked`. Where the text
  came up short, the OCR `sources/town-budget/ocr/<id>.tsv` was searched too: FY2012, FY2013,

  FY2011 and FY2014; nothing FY2012, FY2013, FY2023.

  `work[- ]?off|workoff` (grep route), plus `senior (citizen )?(property )?tax ?(work|abate)|taxation aid|5K exemption|clause 5K`,
  and the phrases `"tax work off"` and `"work off program"`. The town-document coverage the
  script printed: **11,062 of 12,252 documents searchable (90%)**; 1,140 held without text; 50
  not held. 248 town documents matched the regex. Every minutes hit with a figure was read in
