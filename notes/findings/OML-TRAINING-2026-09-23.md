## What the session covered

### Open Meeting Law

- **Definitions** ([0:04:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=266s)). Subcommittees are public bodies. Deliberation includes email, text and messaging apps. Repeating something the AG has already ruled a violation makes it intentional ([0:06:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=401s)).
- **Not a meeting** ([0:08:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=511s)): site visits, conferences and social events, attending another body's posted meeting, and town meeting. Members may not deliberate at any of them.
- **Notice** ([0:12:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=736s)): 48 hours ahead, excluding weekends and legal holidays. It must list topics "the chair reasonably believes will be discussed". The presenter said it is "the town clerk who's responsible for posting the notices", and that a late request to the clerk is no emergency ([0:22:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=1327s)).
- **Agenda detail.** An AG case found a conservation commission agenda insufficient for not labelling an item a public hearing ([0:15:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=932s)). Executive session notices must reveal what they can without compromising the purpose ([0:17:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=1044s)).
- **Posting location** ([0:19:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=1164s)). If the town adopts an alternative such as the website, "the town clerk has to notify the attorney general" ([0:20:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=1252s)).
- **Remote participation** ([0:23:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=1399s)). The old rules required a physical quorum and roll-call votes. The COVID-era rules are extended "to June 30th of 2027" ([0:27:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=1662s)). The presenter recommended agendas say the meeting continues if the remote feed fails ([0:28:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=1733s)).
- **Public comment** ([0:29:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=1792s)). There is no public right to comment outside a hearing. Offering comment creates a "limited public forum", which allows time, place and manner limits only. The presenter cited the Southborough case (captioned "Baron versus Kenda") ([0:35:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=2123s)).
- **Abstention vs. recusal** ([0:38:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=2309s)). Abstaining does not cure a conflict. The presenter advised members who recuse to leave the room.
- **Email** ([0:41:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=2474s)): "even the one-way communication is a violation" if an opinion reaches a quorum. In an AG case, completed voting grids sent by reply-all were violations ([0:43:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=2609s)).
- **Executive session** ([0:44:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=2655s)). Enter by roll call, citing a numbered purpose. The presenter walked through all ten purposes ([0:45:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=2712s)).
- **Complaints** ([0:54:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=3247s)): "filed with the public body and the town clerk" within 30 days. The body then has "14 business days" to take remedial action and copy the Division of Open Government.
- **Remedies** ([0:56:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=3374s)): the Division can order compliance or training, release minutes, or void an action. It can fine "$1,000 per intentional violation", which the presenter said is rare. New members must certify receipt of the law and of their body's violations "within the past 5 years" ([0:58:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=3496s)).

### Conflict of Interest Law

- **Scope** ([1:11:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=4270s)). Chapter 268A covers anyone serving the town, paid or unpaid ([1:12:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=4333s)).
- **Gifts** ([1:13:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=4436s)): none "at $50 or more annually".
- **Misuse of position** ([1:14:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=4491s)), for oneself or others.
- **Self-dealing** ([1:15:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=4549s)) covers the member's own financial interest and that of a spouse, child or parent. The presenter's examples were a school committee member married to a teacher and a select board member whose daughter is a police officer ([1:17:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=4662s)). Only appointed officials can get an exemption from their appointing authority ([1:18:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=4689s)).
- **Appearance of conflict** ([1:20:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=4810s)): disclose publicly first. Elected officials file disclosures "to the town clerk" ([1:22:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=4922s)).
- **Confidential information** ([1:22:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=4934s)). This rule bars leaking what was said in executive session.
- **Multiple positions** ([1:23:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=5011s)), **after-hours work** ([1:25:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=5127s)) and **"special municipal employees"** ([1:26:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=5189s)).
- **After service** ([1:27:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=5232s)): a permanent ban on matters the person worked on, and a one-year ban on matters under their authority.

### Public Records Law

- **Definition** ([1:34:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=5663s)): "regardless of physical form", including metadata.
- **Exemptions** ([1:36:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=5768s)) include personnel files, policy drafts until final, unopened bids and appraisals. Attorney-client communications are also exempt ([1:38:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=5925s)).
- **Records access officer** ([1:40:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=6041s)). Absent another designation, "it is the town clerk". Contact details must be posted at town hall and online.
- **Requests** ([1:43:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=6196s)) may be made in person, by mail, email or fax. The town need not take them by telephone.
- **Deadlines** ([1:44:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=6266s)): respond within 10 business days or lose the right to charge a fee. Production can be extended "within 25 days" ([1:45:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=6351s)), and up to 30 days more on petition ([1:48:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=6498s)).
- **Fees** ([1:48:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=6523s)): "5 cents" a page and no more than "$25" an hour.
- **Harassing requests** ([1:52:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=6738s)). Relief is "very difficult" to get, even after "150 requests in a 12-month period" ([1:53:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TwY3W18bNzM&t=6813s)).
