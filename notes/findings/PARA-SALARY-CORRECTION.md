# The paraprofessional salary correction: what changed between the two scales

9 October 2026. Two scales for the same agreement (AFSCME Council 93, Local 503, 1 July 2025
to 30 June 2028):

- **POSTED** — `sources/contracts/pdf/paraprofessional-salary-fy26-fy28.pdf`, held since
  20 August 2026, byte-identical to the district's own Drive copy (CONTRACTS.md). One page,
  headed `FY 26- NEW SALARY SCHEDULE`, with `3.00%` over FY 27 and `2.00%` over FY 28.
  Columns `PARA 1` to `PARA 4`.
- **CORRECTED** — `sources/contracts/pdf/paraprofessional-salary-fy26-fy28-corrected-2026-10-07.pdf`,
  received by records request on 9 October 2026, delivered as *"Para Contract Signature Page
  with Corrected Salary Schedule Approved by SC 10-7-26.pdf"*. Pages 1-2 are the scale,
  headed `Lunenburg Public Schools Paraprofessional Salary Scale`; page 3 is the MOA's
  sick-leave page signed by four School Committee members and three union signatories.
  Columns `High School - Column 1`, `Associate's - Column 2`, `Bachelor's - Column 3`,
  `Licensed Bachelor's - Column 4`. No percentages are printed on it.

Both are scans (`pdf_kind`). Every figure below was read off the page images, and the 240
figures of each scale were reconciled to the OCR text beside it and, for the corrected scale,
to a spreadsheet forwarded anonymously the same day (section 4). The comparison is
our arithmetic over those readings; the full cell-by-cell table is the appendix.

**Column mapping is ours.** `PARA 1-4` are matched to Columns 1-4 by position. The match is
supported by the top step of FY2026, which is identical in all four positions on both
scales ($24.59, $25.98, $27.24, $27.84), and by the MOA, which names the four columns in that
order. Neither document states the mapping.

---

## 1. What the documents show

**Every rate went up or stayed the same. None went down.** Of 120 hourly rates, 107 are
higher on the corrected scale and 13 are unchanged.

| year | rates compared | higher | unchanged | rise, steps 1-8 | largest rise, hourly | largest rise, annual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| FY2026 | 36 | 32 | 4 (step 9, every column) | +3.85% to +4.02% | +$1.00 | +$1,254.50 |
| FY2027 | 40 | 34 | 6 (step 10, every column; step 9, Columns 1-2) | +3.77% to +4.04% | +$1.04 | +$1,304.68 |
| FY2028 | 44 | 41 | 3 (step 11, Columns 1-3) | +4.18% to +4.55% | +$1.19 | +$1,500.59 |

- **Steps 1-8 rose about 4% in FY2026 and FY2027 and about 4.5% in FY2028.** Column 4 (the
  licensed column) rose a little less than the others in every year: +3.85% to +3.89% in
  FY2026 against +3.97% to +4.02% elsewhere.
- **The top step did not move.** FY2026 step 9, FY2027 step 10 and FY2028 step 11 print the
  same figures on both scales in every column, except FY2028 Column 4 step 11: $32.27 posted,
  $32.28 corrected (+$0.01; annual $40,692.47 to $40,705.08).
- **The steps just below the top rose by less.** FY2027 step 9 rose +1.39% in Column 3 and
  +1.29% in Column 4 and not at all in Columns 1-2. FY2028 steps 9-10 rose between +0.42%
  and +1.89%.
- **Step 1, posted to corrected, hourly:** FY2026 $16.82 / $17.00 / $17.18 / $17.78 became
  $17.49 / $17.68 / $17.87 / $18.47. FY2027 $17.32 / $17.51 / $17.70 / $18.31 became
  $18.02 / $18.21 / $18.40 / $19.00. FY2028 $17.67 / $17.86 / $18.05 / $18.68 became
  $18.47 / $18.67 / $18.86 / $19.46.

**The year-to-year raise on the scale changed too.** Comparing each step with the same step
a year earlier:

| | FY2026 to FY2027 | FY2027 to FY2028 |
|---|---|---|
| posted | +2.95% to +3.03% (the `3.00%` it prints) | +1.97% to +2.03% (the `2.00%` it prints) |
| corrected | +2.87% to +3.04%, except step 9 in Columns 3-4 (+4.44%, +4.35%) | **+2.41% to +2.54%** |

So the corrected scale does not carry the posted scale's FY2028 `2.00%`: its same-step rise
into FY2028 is about two and a half percent. Neither the MOA nor the agreement's Article VI
(as read) prints a percentage, so which figure was bargained is not something these documents
say. CONTRACTS.md's "3.0% · 2.0% · 2.0%" came from the posted scale's markers.

**The annual salary is the hourly rate times a fixed number of hours, on both scales.**
FY2026 and FY2027 use 1,254.5 hours; FY2028 uses 1,261. The difference is 6.5 hours, one
day at the MOA's *"6.5 hour day"*, and the corrected scale says what it is: *"Added
Patriot's Day to the annual salary in accordance with the contract"* (the agreement's
Article XXI lists `Patriots' Day (in FY28)` among paid holidays). 1,254.5 is also 193 days of
6.5 hours; that reading of it is ours, and no document states 193 days.

## 2. The claim tested: "the hourly rates did not calculate accurately into the annual salaries"

The words are from our machine captions of the School Committee meeting of 16 September 2026
(the Superintendent's report, [0:06:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XY8Ru12S9Xg&t=413s)),
a finding aid and not a record: *"the school committee will unfortunately not be able to vote
this evening to reratify the paraprofessional contract with the corrected salary scale. While
preparing new appointment letters, we discovered that in some instances, the hourly rates did
not calculate accurately into the annual salaries presented on that scale."*

**Read with its context, the claim is about the CORRECTED scale as it stood on 16 September,
not about the posted one.** "That scale" is the corrected scale the committee was to vote on.
The internal email released with the response shows the sequence: on 11 September the
Superintendent sent counsel *"the correct paraprofessional salary scale and signed MOA"*; on
16 September she told payroll *"The 'new' salary schedule will be approved by the school
committee tonight"*; the captions record that the vote was then put off; the filename of the
scale delivered says it was approved on 7 October.

**Tested against both scales, cell by cell, at 1,254.5 hours (FY2026-FY2027) and 1,261
(FY2028):**

| scale | annual figures | equal to hourly x hours, to the cent | do not |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| posted | 120 | 119 | FY2028 Para 2 step 9: $27.29 x 1,261 = $34,412.69; printed **$34,412.29** (-$0.40) |
| corrected, as approved 7 Oct | 120 | 118 | FY2026 Column 2 step 8: $24.92 x 1,254.5 = $31,262.14; printed **$31,263.70** (+$1.56). FY2027 Column 3 step 4: $22.35 x 1,254.5 = $28,038.08; printed **$28,038.75** (+$0.67) |

- **Against the posted scale the claim does not hold in any material sense.** One annual
  figure in 120 misses its hourly rate, by forty cents. Whatever was wrong with the posted
  scale, it was not that its annual salaries failed to follow from its hourly rates: they
  do, at a constant number of hours. What differs between the two scales is the hourly rates
  themselves (section 1).
- **Against the approved corrected scale it still holds, slightly.** Two annual figures do
  not equal hourly x hours, by $1.56 and $0.67. The version the Superintendent described on
  16 September is not held, so whether these two are the remainder of a larger set of
  miscalculations that were fixed, or were never noticed, is not established.

## 3. What the other three documents are, and what they change

**`MOA Para 2025-2028.pdf`** — `sources/contracts/pdf/paraprofessional-moa-fy26-fy28.pdf`, two
pages. A memorandum of agreement between the School Committee and AFSCME Council 93, Local
503, *"to be in effect from July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2028"*; terms not modified are
*"carried forward"*. What it changes:

- Article VI, §6-1: *"Beginning in FY26, a salary scale restructure will occur for all
  paraprofessionals from an hourly wage previously to salary"*, into four columns (High
  School; Associates; Bachelors; Bachelors with License), column by most recent degree, step
  by past experience, a step each 1 July.
- §6-4: a new step each year: step 9 in FY26, 10 in FY27, 11 in FY28.
- §6-10: pay over 22 or 26 cycles (the agreement as posted says 21 or 26), election by
  1 August; a new (c): time used beyond what has accrued is docked in the same pay period,
  and missed deductions are doubled next pay or paid by check.
- Article VIII, §8-1: sick and personal accruals in days rather than hours; use by the hour
  allowed, deducted on a 6.5-hour day.

Its signature page is signed by the Superintendent and one union signatory only; the five
School Committee lines (Chair, Vice-Chair, Secretary, two members) are blank and nothing on it
is dated. **Page 3 of the corrected-scale PDF is the same sick-leave page signed again**: by
four School Committee members and three union signatories, with the Superintendent's line
blank and, again, no date. The offices differ between the two (the MOA's Chair is the
corrected page's Vice-Chair, and the reverse; one member differs). The MOA carries **no
salary figures**; it does not attach a scale.

**`Draft Union Agreement - Para Wages.docx`** — blank and unsigned, *"Agreed to September
2026"*, file created 14 September and last saved 15 September 2026 by the district's counsel
(its metadata). It recites that *"the District and Union recently uncovered that
paraprofessionals received incorrectly calculated wage payments during the 2025-2026 school
year and a certain period of the 2026-2027 school year"*. Terms: affected paraprofessionals
**who sign an individual waiver** get **one additional personal day** in 2026-27 and **a lump
sum "reflecting retroactive payment for the 2026-2027 school year"**, by a date left as
`<DATE>`; the union agrees not to grieve or otherwise challenge the agreement in any forum;
no precedent. One recital speaks of *"the paraprofessionals' underpayment of **longevity**
compensation"* — the only place in the delivery that word appears; nothing else in it says
the underpayment was longevity.

**`Draft Individual Waiver - Para Wages.docx`** — a blank template: the employee is
`[_________________________]`, no amount, no date. Same recital, same consideration (one
personal day, a lump sum for 2026-27). The employee waives *"any and all rights and claims"*
under the Massachusetts Wage Act, M.G.L. c.149 §§148 and 150, *"related to compensation
received during the 2025-2026 school year and the 2026-2027 school year (through the date of
the execution of this Agreement.)"*

**What the drafts and the email together establish, and only this:**

- Retroactive pay is offered for **2026-27 (FY2027) only**. The Superintendent, 11 September:
  *"retro pay for FY27. I believe this will be acceptable to the union given that they only
  requested FY27 retro pay."*
- For **2025-26 (FY2026)** the drafts offer no money. They offer a day of leave, and the
  waiver releases Wage Act claims for that year as well as for the part of 2026-27 before
  signing.
- *"approximately 52 paras are impacted by the retroactive FY27 pay"* — the Superintendent's
  estimate, 14 September, in her words *"My sense is"*. It is a count of people, not of FTE or
  dollars.
- These are DRAFTS. Whether they were signed, by how many, on what date, and for what sums
  is not in the delivery.

## 4. The anonymously forwarded spreadsheet is the same scale

`sources/budget-workbooks/forwarded-anonymously-2026-10-09/corrected-lunenburg-paraprofessional-salary-scale-fy27-fy28.xlsx`
(sheet `Salary Scale`, `A1:I60`) holds **the same 240 figures in the same cells** as pages 1-2
of the approved PDF: every hourly rate and annual salary, every column, step and year,
including the two cells that do not compute (`E15` = 31263.7, `G24` = 28038.75). Its title,
its Patriot's Day line (`A33`) and its footnote (`A48`, `A49`: *"Hourly rates and annual
salaries are transcribed as shown in the source schedule. / Source: Para Salary Scale.pdf"*)
are the PDF's, word for word. The values are typed numbers, not formulas, so the sheet does
not compute its own annual salaries either. Its file metadata says it was created
14 September 2026 and last saved 8 October 2026, the day after the vote.

Checked by: every figure in the sheet found in the PDF's OCR in the same column order (the
FY2028 Column 1 block, where the OCR interleaves columns, checked against the page image by
eye).

## 5. What none of this establishes

- **Why the posted rates were wrong.** The documents show the rates moved and by how much;
  the only explanation in the delivery is the drafts' *"incorrectly calculated wage
  payments"*. Two readings fit the numbers and nothing here separates them: that the
  posted FY2026 steps 1-8 omitted part of an increase applied to the new top step; or that
  the restructure into salary columns re-based the scale and the posted grid was an earlier
  draft. *Hypotheses, both; the FY2025 scale (not held) would test the first.*
- **What the correction costs.** That needs the number of paraprofessionals in each column
  and step and their appointed hours. The scale is a full-year, 6.5-hour figure; an aide on
  fewer hours is paid less than the annual column says. Registered in `money-gaps.csv`.
- **Whether the FY2027 budget carries the corrected rates.** The paraprofessional lines in
  the FY2027 budget were built before 7 October.
- **That ~52 people were underpaid by the amounts in section 1.** The difference between two
  scales is not a payment. What each person was actually paid in FY2026 and FY2027 is payroll
  data, and none is held.
