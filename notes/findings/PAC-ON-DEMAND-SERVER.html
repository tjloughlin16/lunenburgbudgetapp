PAC on-demand server

How Lunenburg's cable-access CASTUS system was replaced and its video-on-demand site launched · Oct 2025 – Nov 2026

Town Meeting approved it on 2 May 2026: $77,000 from PEG retained earnings (Article 15).
The reasons given were that the old system was at end of life, and that buying in FY2026 would beat a 15–20% tariff price rise due 1 July. The system went live in August, with video on demand launched in September. Since then PAC has reported few viewers on the new site and several problems.

Why it was replaced

  • End of life. The warrant summary: The current system is at end of life. CASTUS is the software and server PAC uses to broadcast and live-stream.
  • Age. The manager told FinCom the old server had run about 8 years, and expected the same life from the new one. (video) Problems go back to at least February 2021, when the Select Board was told Zoom broadcast issues were related to the CASTUS server.
  • Tariffs, which is why the purchase moved to FY2026. From the warrant: The vendor has advised that, due to tariffs, pricing is expected to increase by 15-20% as of July 1, 2026; therefore, this article seeks to authorize the purchase in FY 2026.
  • New features, pitched as public benefits: closed captioning, video on demand instead of going to YouTube, agenda chapters, Roku, Fire TV and Apple TV channels, and a mobile app.

Open questions

  • What was actually spent against the $77,000, and what is left in the appropriation.
  • $1,795: monthly or yearly? The 6 April minutes say monthly; the video of the same meeting says every year. That is a factor of 12.
  • Recurring cost: about +$9,000 a year from FY28 (November estimate). PAC must be self-supporting, and the franchise fees it runs on are falling.
  • Do viewers use it? PAC's own reports say people still go to YouTube.
