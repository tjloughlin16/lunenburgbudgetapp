# Track and turf as a capital item, FY2014-FY2025 (early cycles)

Data: `early.csv` (46 rows).

- **FY2014:** Town Manager said (Sept 2013) she meant to add replacement to the capital plan. Art. 14, $652,483, prints no item list, so a field line is neither shown nor excluded.
- **FY2015:** Not in the FY2015 plan (19 rows read, $1,114,152). Finance Committee and Parks Commission put reconstruction at $600,000-$800,000 "in the next few years" (25 Sep 2014).
- **FY2016:** Not in the plan (13 rows). DPW report: track complex "in dire need of repair".
- **FY2017:** CPC listed Turf Field $800,000 (27 Oct 2015) and track surface $150,000 within a year (12 Jan 2016). Not in the 12-item FY17 plan. Handled as separate Art. 28 at the 5 May 2016 ATM: **$770,000, borrowed**, 2/3 declared (warrant said $600,000; warrant says submitted by the Board of Selectmen, minutes say the Parks Commission). Turf laid fall 2016; track resurfaced June 2017.
- **FY2018:** Bond issued January 2017, **$760,000, 2.88%, 12 years** (SB 17 Jan 2017). Unissued authorization at 30 June 2018: "-". Not in the FY18 plan.
- **FY2019-FY2021:** No field line in any capital plan read. Only turf item is debt service from the Turf Revolving Fund.
- **FY2022:** One-year plan has no field line. First request appears in the ten-year table: LPS20-04 retaining wall, $90,000 for FY2027. "LPS20" first appears in the Jan 2021 deck, suggesting a request written May 2020-Jan 2021 (inference).
- **FY2023:** Not in the plan; LPS20-04 became $98,000 for FY2025.
- **FY2024:** Schools requested $50,000 feasibility study (LPS23-06); School rank 5 of 6; CPC average 18.6; "not included" in the preliminary plan. Voted FY24 plan: $1,039,010 total, item list unreadable. The study was paid from ARPA (contract $49,990).
- **FY2025:** $200,000 Phase 2 (LPS20-04-A); School Committee rank 7 of 9; composite 19; "not included" in the preliminary plan; ATM voted $1,317,119.98 with no confirmed field line.
- **After:** $1.43M for FY27 in the Sept 2024 schools plan; a spring 2025 stand-alone article was removed (captions).
