# The track and field history: the Select Board Chair's compilation beside ours

23 October 2026. NOTES ONLY, so nothing here is on a page. Nothing in
`sources/data/thread-history.csv` has been changed. The proposed additions and
corrections are in the table at the end, waiting for TJ.

**The two sides.**

- **Hers.** *Track and Field complex history.pdf*, ten slides. The Chair of the Select Board,
  Renee Emkey, assembled it for the 20 October 2026 meeting and emailed it on 21 October:
  *"I did it manually so do not assume it is complete."* It is held in the private bucket,
  `pending`, as `correspondence/2026-10-21-track-and-field-complex-history.pdf` (provenance:
  `sources/correspondence/2026-10-21-select-board-chair-presentations.md`). Under rule 13a it
  is `stated`. **This note quotes figures from a document that is not yet public.** None of
  them is personal, but the note should not be published before the document is.
- **Ours.** The `turf-field-study` thread: 46 rows in `thread-history.csv` and the rounds in
  `thread-briefing.csv`. Also `sources/data/capital-plan-field-lines.csv`, which
  `scripts/build_turf_plans_topic.py` reads for `/athletics/turf-field-capital-plans`.
  **The threads `athletic-complex-turf` and `turf-field-repairs` do not exist.** Neither id
  appears in `threads.csv`, `thread-history.csv`, `thread-briefing.csv` or
  `thread-moments.csv`. The turf history is all in `turf-field-study`.
- **The Superintendent's deck.** *A Timely Next Step for the LMHS Track and Field Facility*,
  shown to the Select Board on 20 October 2026, held publicly as
  `correspondence/2026-10-21-track-and-field-presentation-superintendent-select-board-2026-10-20.pdf`.
  Its figures are **as presented, not voted**.

Each disagreement below was settled against the vote or the plan it rests on. Neither account
was preferred. A town meeting vote is cited from the town's annual report, which prints the
warrant article and the vote together.

## 5. What this does not show, and the hypotheses

- **Why the 2021 article and the 2016 figure came out wrong in both decks is not shown here.**
  *Hypothesis:* both were taken from a warrant rather than from the vote. The $600,000 is the
  2016 warrant's figure word for word, and the 2021 article appears with its warrant amount.
  The fix is to read the vote. Nothing here tests the hypothesis.
- **Whether the Select Board ever voted the ARPA money for the study is not established.** The
  minutes searched do not have it. A meeting whose minutes are not held may. The question
  closes with the ARPA ledger, or the Select Board's list of ARPA commitments showing the
  $49,990.
- **Whether the FY27 request was formally withdrawn, on what date, and at what printed figure
  is not established.** It closes with the schools' FY27 capital request as submitted to the
  CPC for 29 Oct 2025, which is not held.
- **The construction cost is not known to anybody yet.** The deck says so. Every plan figure
  above predates the design.

**Three documents would settle most of the open rows, and none is held:** the Collins
feasibility study (July 2024), the Gale proposal (December 2025), and the Reynolds condition
assessment (15 October 2026). Under rule 7c they belong in `sources/data/money-gaps.csv`. They
are proposed for it here and not added.
