**THIS FILE IS THE FIRST THING WRITTEN AND THE THING WORKED FROM.** Every step of
`notes/process/READING-A-REPORT-PAGE.md` is already a row below, at `todo`, before any page
is opened -- which is the whole point. A step that is not on the page gets skipped, and
FY2014 skipped step 1 three times without anybody noticing until it was over.

**Status is one of `todo`, `doing`, `DONE`, `blocked`, `n/a`, and every row needs a COMMENT.**
A status with no comment is not a report. `DONE` with a figure in it is worth ten without:
*`60 of 60 rows, all six columns tie`* rather than *`read it`*.

**Sections are worked DOWN this page, in page order.** Do not jump to the easy one.
