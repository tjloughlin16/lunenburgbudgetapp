# FY2012 annual town report — run sheet

`sources/town-annual-reports/docs/4118-fy-2012-annual-town-report.pdf`, 116 pages.

**Every page of this report is a photograph.** `pdfplumber.extract_words()` returns 0 words
on all 116, tested per page. Each page holds ONE CCITTFaxDecode image, 4400x3400, 1-bit,
DeviceGray, 518 dpi.

**`stream.get_data()` RAISES on every page of this year** — `PDFValueError: None` out of
`ccittfaxdecode`, because `/K` is absent from `/DecodeParms`. So the documented
`Image.open(io.BytesIO(stream.get_data()))` recipe fails outright here, and under a
`try/except` it reads as *this page has no picture*. What works: take `stream.rawdata` and
wrap it in a minimal single-strip TIFF header — Compression 3 for G3 and 4 for G4,
PhotometricInterpretation from `BlackIs1` — then `Image.open`.

**ORIENTATION IS PER PAGE and was tested on each.** The stored bitmap is landscape
(4400x3400) on every page. pp.60–65 and 112–115 are 90° from upright; p76 is upright at 0°;
**p77 is bound UPSIDE DOWN (180°)**.

### What the year taught

**A subtotal that two different pairs satisfy has no power to choose between them.** The
28 September pass corrected the Traffic Signs & Devices line from $37,563.79 to $37,663.79
on the strength of its printed subtotal $41,208.17 — and $3,544.38 + $37,663.79 and
$3,644.38 + $37,563.79 are both $41,208.17. The page prints the second pair. What settles
it is a check the subtotal cannot make: p65's own legend, `fwd - forward from FY 2011
$207,450.46`, against which the sixteen flagged carry-forwards across pp.60–65 sum exactly.
**Before accepting a correction, ask whether the check that justified it could have failed.**

**The flag-word legend at the foot of a run is three free independent checks and nobody was
using them.** `fwd` $207,450.46 over sixteen cells, `srt` $7,320.50 over one and `rft`
$44,393.02 over two, all exact, all spanning six pages. They check cells that no column
total touches on its own.

**A page the tracker does not list is not a page without a table.** p113 is 136 names,
sitting between two tracked pages, invisible because a page enters the tracker on fifteen
RECOGNISED money figures and recognition could not read it.

**One document defect, three printings, two reports.** Mass Water Pool 9 prints a FY2024
principal of $418,704 with no interest cell and no total cell, and the schedule then
disagrees with itself by exactly that amount in three places: the FY2024 `TOTAL DEBT`
column, that issue's own lifetime `TOTAL`, and the grand corner. The FY2013 report prints
the same schedule a year later and fails the same identity by the same amount. The gap is
already registered in `money-gaps.csv`; this is a second, independent corroboration of it.
