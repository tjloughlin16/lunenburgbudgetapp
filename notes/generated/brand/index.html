All three at the size that decides them

32 pixels is where a mark survives or does not. Magnified 8× for the silhouette, then actual size on light and on dark.

On the brand blue. --brand: #12428f is the Blue Knights' royal — the school district's colour. On the site it reads as affection for the town; alone on a Facebook banner it reads as the district talking, and this project's only authority is that its figures are checkable. It appears in option A only, as the lake, and in neither of the others.

No figure appears on any of them, deliberately: a banner cannot be corrected and it is seen more than anything else here.

The live favicon today is not the purple file. fy28/index.html carries an inline rel="icon" data URI of the 🏫 school-building emoji, so that is what every tab shows; fy28/public/favicon.svg is the purple template leftover and nothing references it. Replacing the mark means changing the tag as well as the file.
