# Every known problem with the data

Generated by `scripts/build_data_problems.py`. Three kinds of problem, kept apart on purpose: a **document** that is defective, a **reading** of ours that is thin, and a **question** nobody published an answer to. Mixing them is how a project ends up telling a resident the town does not publish something it publishes every year.

## 1. The document is defective

**1.** The town published something incomplete or inconsistent. Nothing this project does closes these; they are recorded so that a figure read off one is never mistaken for a measurement of the world. Typed by hand, because a defect in somebody else’s printing cannot be derived.

**FY2022 — FY2022 annual town report, Fire Department.** The Fire Department roster is cut off. The book prints the Chief the Deputy Chief and the nine career firefighters and then stops mid-entry; the Call Firefighters section that every other year carries is not in the book at all.

- *how it was found:* measured: the department states its own strength in the same report — 10 career and 30-35 on call — and the roster names twelve
- *evidence:* page 85 ends `Karen Weller, Administrative` with no page number between it and the Police Department letterhead on page 86; the printed page numbers 85 and 86 are consecutive, so nothing is missing from our scan
