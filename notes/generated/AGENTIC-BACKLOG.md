**Counted above and NOT really work.** Three of the generic table extracts inside `Reconciling the annual-report tables` should not be read as a backlog anybody will clear:

- **`report_officials`, 237 rows, none checked.** It is not the officials listing. Its FY2011 rows are `Nancy L Woodruff 79` and `Robert E. Tucker WWII 82` — a memorial page — and 35 of its rows carry NO LABEL and a value equal to their own page number. The listing itself is read properly by `extract_personnel.py` into `town-personnel.csv`, 2118 rows, every one tied to a post and a year, with a `size_check` against the membership each heading states.
- **`report_gross_wages`, 3545 rows, none checked.** The same pages are read by `extract_gross_wages.py` into `gross-wages.csv`, 2946 rows of name and amount. NEITHER is published and both are honest about why: the town stopped printing the department beside each name after FY2016, so the list cannot be split by department, and the two-column layout loses a third to a half of the given names. Registered in `money-gaps.csv` rather than shown.
- **`report_trust_funds`, 642 rows, 12 checked** — and the part of it anybody asks about is already read. The STABILIZATION funds are in `stabilization-balances.csv`: 30 fund-years across 14 years, every one proven against an identity the table states about itself, published at `/analysis/stabilization-funds`. What is genuinely unreconciled is the REST of the trust table — the cemetery, library and scholarship funds — so this row is a real backlog, but it is not the stabilization backlog it reads as.

Left in the count deliberately rather than quietly subtracted — a number that moves because somebody changed what it counts is worse than one that is too big and says so.

**Not in any stream, by choice:** conclusions on the finance pages are a per-owner writing job rather than a batch. Minutes-writing is NOT in this category: `sources/data/recording-minutes-policy.csv` sets the order every board is written in, not which boards qualify.

**Proposed, not built:** *Town Meeting vote displays.* Town Meeting runs electronic voting and puts the VERBATIM motion text and the counted tally on screen, and the whole meeting is on video -- so every appropriation motion is recoverable exactly, with a tally arithmetic can check, rather than paraphrased from captions. Article 3 of the 3 September 2026 Special Town Meeting is the worked case: the display reads `$30,308.00` and `224 / 29 / 0 / 253`, and our recording minutes record it as the single word `passed`. `sources/data/official-votes/` holds 30-odd boards and no `town-meeting/` at all, so the body that actually appropriates is the one captured least precisely. Same machinery as `ocr_scanned_minutes.py` (macOS Vision, local, free), pointed at sampled frames instead of scanned PDFs.
