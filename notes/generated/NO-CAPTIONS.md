## What this means

27 of the 102 have no agenda or minutes in the town’s AgendaCenter for the same date either, so the recording is the only record and it cannot be searched. A transcript for those would need the channel owner to switch captions on (YouTube generates them free once enabled), or a transcription made from the audio — which this project has not done.

The date on each row is read from the video title by `build_youtube_classification.py`; a recording whose title carries no date is not in the fetcher’s scope and is not listed here.
