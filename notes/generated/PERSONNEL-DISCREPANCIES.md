**The right column is long and that is expected.** It is almost all volunteer boards — a body with no paid staff has nobody for a staff directory to list, and the Finance Committee and the Library Trustees are not missing from it, they are not the kind of thing it holds.

**The left column is the one to read, and four of it are deliberate.** Animal Control sits inside the Police Department here because the department’s own FY27 chart puts `ACO Kathy Comeau` under the Chief, and the Electrical, Plumbing/Gas and Weights & Measures inspectors sit inside Building. What is left is **Accounting** and **Human Resources** — two departments this project could not see at all until the directory was fetched, because neither files an annual report and neither head is an APPOINTED OFFICIAL, which is the single blind spot both of the other sources share.
