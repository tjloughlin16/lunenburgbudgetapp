## What this is for

It ranks the backlog by what it will COST rather than by how many pages are left.
FY2024 has the fewest pages outstanding of any year and only one of them is digital;
FY2014 has more and every one carries its text. Those are not the same job, and the
page count alone cannot tell them apart.
