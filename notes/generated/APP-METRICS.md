# The Lunenburg Budget Project, by the numbers

*As of 2026-11-14. Every figure below is computed from the archive itself by `scripts/build_app_metrics.py`; none is typed.*

## What the town and the state published — and we hold

- **16,880 documents** — PDFs, spreadsheets, Word files and slide decks — 53,215 files and 11.36 GB in all, each with its address, its publisher’s filename and a checksum.
- **16 kinds of source**: budget-workbooks, contracts, correspondence, district-budget, federal-bls, meetings, peer-districts, state-census, state-dese, state-dls, state-law, state-massgis, town-annual-reports, town-budget, town-ledgers, town-supplementary.
- **12,314 meeting documents** from **51 town boards**: 4,787 sets of minutes and 7,527 agendas, 2009-01-05 to 2026-11-13 — **18 years**.
- **27,799 annual town report appropriation lines**, FY1959–FY2025, read page by page.
- **12,278 names** on the town’s printed staff rosters, FY1959–FY2025.
- **9,503 school budget line-years**, FY2014–FY2027, and **983 ledger lines** from the town’s own accounting system.
- **23 state (DESE) data tables** — enrollment, staffing, class size, special education, Chapter 70, per-pupil spending.

## What we made from it

- **4,562 hours of meeting recordings transcribed** — 2,716 meetings, 190.1 days of audio end to end, 2012-05-02 to 2026-11-12. Machine captions, ours, a finding aid: they locate a moment; they do not settle what was said.
- **2708 meetings with our own minutes** written from those recordings — **9174 substantive votes** and **1106 transfers** logged, each linked to the second of the video.
- **A database of 458,730 rows in 243 tables**, rebuilt from the documents on every run and queryable by anyone at `/api/query`.
- **8 analyses** and **271 published conclusions**, every figure recomputed by a script before it ships.
- **193 cuts announced in writing** traced across budget cycles; **71 rates** in the projection, each backtested against the district’s later budgets.
- **654 registered gaps** — questions the published record cannot answer, each with the one document that would close it.
- **856 pages** on the site.

## Where it came from

- `www.lunenburgma.gov` — 12,928 files
- `massago.hylandcloud.com` — 5,821 files
- `drive.google.com` — 1,553 files
- `www.doe.mass.edu` — 664 files
- `docs.google.com` — 100 files
- `api.schoolspring.com` — 58 files

Everything above is checkable: every document is downloadable at its own address, every figure carries its source, and the whole database is one file anybody can open.
