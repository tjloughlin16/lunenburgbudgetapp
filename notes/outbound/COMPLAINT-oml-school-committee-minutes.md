# Open Meeting Law complaint — Lunenburg School Committee minutes

**Filed with:** Laura Brzozoski, Chair, Lunenburg School Committee
**Copy to:** openmeeting@mass.gov (AG's Division of Open Government)
**Form:** the AG's Open Meeting Law Complaint Form — mass.gov/how-to/file-an-open-meeting-law-complaint
**Date of alleged violation:** ongoing; most recently 1 October 2026

---

## Section: description of the alleged violation

The School Committee is not creating, approving and making available minutes of its
meetings, as M.G.L. c.30A, §22(a) and §22(c) require.

**The most recent minutes the Committee has made available are dated 24 June 2026.**
Since that date the Committee has announced and posted agendas for eight meetings — 15
and 29 July, 26 August, 2, 9, 16 and 25 September and 1 October 2026 — and no minutes for
any of them are available. Section 22(c) requires minutes to be approved "in a timely
manner," and a meeting held in July that has no approved minutes in October is not timely.

**This is not a recent lapse.** Counting only meetings the Town itself announced on its
AgendaCenter, against the minutes posted there for the same body:

| calendar year | meetings announced | minutes available | not available |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2022 | 33 | **0** | 33 |
| 2024 | 36 | 27 | 9 |
| 2025 | 46 | 30 | 16 |
| 2026 (to 1 Oct) | 24 | 14 | 10 |

Every figure above was read from the Town's own AgendaCenter on 4 October 2026. I am not
inferring that these meetings occurred — the Committee published a notice for each one.

**I also requested these records directly and received no reply.** On 8 September 2026 I
emailed the Chair asking for copies of the minutes of 35 named meetings, each identified
by date and by the Town's own agenda URL, and offering that where a meeting had been
cancelled or no minutes existed, saying so against that date would be an equally useful
answer. As of 4 October 2026 I have had no response of any kind, and none of the 35 has
since been made available. Section 22(c) requires minutes to be available on request.

## Section: the remedy I am seeking

1. That the Committee create and approve minutes for every meeting it has held, and
   adopt a schedule for doing so.
2. That for any date listed above where the meeting was cancelled or no minutes exist,
   the Committee say so against that date.
3. That the Committee bring its minutes current and keep them within a stated interval
   going forward.
4. That the minutes be posted alongside the agendas on the AgendaCenter, as the
   Committee already does for the meetings it does minute.

I am not seeking to invalidate any action the Committee has taken. The minutes are the
only record of what was decided for a resident who cannot attend or watch a three-hour
meeting, and for most of the meetings listed above no written record exists at all.
