# School Committee meetings with an agenda posted and no minutes

Generated 8 September 2026 from the Town of Lunenburg AgendaCenter,
as listed by the town itself. Every row below is a meeting the town announced.

## What this list is, and the one thing it is not

**It is every date where the AgendaCenter carries an agenda for a School Committee
meeting and carries no minutes for the same date.**

**It is not a list of meetings held.** An agenda can be posted for a meeting that was
later cancelled, and minutes for a meeting may exist without being posted. Those are
different things and nothing published distinguishes them — which is part of why the
request is worth making.

## For the request itself

Each meeting above is identified by the date the town itself published. The
agenda link is the town's own address for that meeting, so a clerk can match each
row without needing anything from us.

Massachusetts Open Meeting Law (M.G.L. c.30A, §22) requires minutes to be created
for every meeting and made available on request. It does not require them to be
posted online — so a meeting on this list may well have minutes that simply are not
on the website, which is exactly what a request would surface.
