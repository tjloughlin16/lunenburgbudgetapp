Why is the school budget always tight? Here's where you can actually find out, plus general town metrics, tax reports, growth and commercial development and more!

I've spent months building a free public tool that gathers everything Lunenburg, the schools and the state have published about the town's money — and explains it a topic at a time.

https://lburg.org/

PICK THE THING YOU'VE WONDERED ABOUT:

▪️ lburg.org/health-insurance — what the town pays, what the law actually allows, and how we compare to other towns
▪️ lburg.org/athletics and lburg.org/what-sports-cost — what a season really costs, and what the fees cover
▪️ lburg.org/sped — special education, the piece almost nobody explains
▪️ lburg.org/salaries and lburg.org/teacher-contracts — what's bargained, and what that commits us to
▪️ lburg.org/override — what one is worth, who pays, and what it doesn't fix
▪️ lburg.org/fees — what user fees can and can't raise
▪️ lburg.org/state-aid — Chapter 70, and why it isn't the same thing as "state aid"
▪️ Also: /transportation · /classrooms · /enrollment · /free-cash · /monty-tech · /youth-sports · /parks-and-recreation

AND THE TOWN, NOT JUST THE SCHOOLS:

▪️ lburg.org/tax-bill — what the average home is worth, what it pays, and how that compares to the towns around us
▪️ lburg.org/growth — commercial development and new growth, as the state certifies it, FY2002 to today
▪️ lburg.org/by-the-numbers — who lives here: how many, how old, owning or renting, on what income
▪️ lburg.org/owners — how long people have held their homes, and what a long-held one pays
▪️ lburg.org/homes-and-students — the town keeps building; the schools have not grown

Each one lays out what's established, what it costs, what the options are — and, just as importantly, what the numbers don't prove.

THE RECORD BEHIND ALL OF IT:

📄 13,111 documents — every budget, warrant, contract and annual report, downloadable
🗓️ 12,105 agendas and minutes from 51 boards, back to 2009
🎥 3,369 hours of meetings transcribed, searchable by the word
📊 150,793 rows anyone can query

THINGS YOU CAN USE EVERY WEEK:

▪️ This week — every meeting coming up, with its agenda
▪️ The budget feed — everything budget-related said at any board, on one page
▪️ Minutes we wrote ourselves — 159 meetings had a recording but no published minutes, so we wrote them from the video: 672 votes and 201 transfers, each linked to the second it happened
▪️ Subscribe — a feed per board, so new minutes come to you

Anything we produced is labelled as ours, and every figure links back to the document it came from. 140 questions are listed that the published record simply cannot answer — each with the one document that would close it.

The Town publishes a great deal. This doesn't replace it; it makes it findable.
