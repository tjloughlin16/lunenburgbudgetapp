# Records request — Parks & Recreation funds, FY2023 to FY2026

**To:** Jennifer Warren-Dyment, Town Manager (or the Records Access Officer for the Town's
financial records)
**Subject:** Public records request — Parks & Recreation fund detail, FY2023–FY2026

---

Dear Ms. Warren-Dyment,

Under M.G.L. c. 66, § 10, I request the records below for Parks & Recreation (department 650).
They are reports the Town's accounting system (MUNIS) already produces. If a different report
holds the same information, I would gladly take that one instead.

**Funds covered:** 1500 Park Revolving Fund, 1523 Park Gift Fund, 1529 Skate Park Fund,
1532 Marshall Park Restoration Fund, 8126 Playground Fund, and 3108 Marshall Park Design.

**Period:** fiscal years 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026, through the year-end close (period 13)
where the year has closed.

## From MUNIS

1. **Account Detail / journal export** for each fund above, for each year: every revenue and
   expense posting, with its date, amount, account, journal number, source, vendor, reference
   and description. This is the same kind of export the Town has produced for the school
   department's funds.
2. **Who paid.** Journal descriptions are cut off at about thirty characters and often leave out
   the payer. For every receipt to these funds, please provide the cash receipt detail showing
   the payer's full name. Where a payer is a private individual rather than an organization, I
   do not need the name.
3. **Year-to-Date Budget Report, revenue and expenditure,** at the year-end close, for each fund
   above. For fiscal 2024 and 2025 the annual town reports print a balance only.
4. **General fund revenue accounts 436200 (SR RENTAL) and 437200 (REC FEES):** the Account
   Detail for each year, or confirmation that nothing posted.
5. **Transfers** into or out of any fund above, showing the other side of each transfer.

## From Parks & Recreation

6. **Field and facility use fees:** the fee schedule in force each year, and each agreement,
   contract, invoice or payment record for an organization using Parks fields. That includes
   Lunenburg Youth Baseball and Softball and any other league. The Commission's minutes of
   6 November 2024 record user fees at Marshall Park and refer to a contract.
7. **The use agreement between Recreation and the schools,** described in the Commission's
   minutes of 30 June 2021 and 6 November 2024 as a mutual or reciprocal arrangement for fields
   and facilities.
8. **MyRec Program Sales and Membership Sales reports** for fiscal 2023, 2024 and 2026. I
   already hold fiscal 2025, so please do not reproduce it.

I am not asking for children's or participants' names, and they may be removed. Please send
everything electronically in whatever format the Town already holds; Excel is the most useful
for the MUNIS reports. I am happy to receive the records in batches.

If any fee would apply, please send an estimate before starting. If any part is withheld, please
state the exemption and release the rest.
