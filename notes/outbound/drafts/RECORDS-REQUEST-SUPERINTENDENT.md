# A few district documents, and three questions

Dear Dr Fortuna,

I run an independent archive of Lunenburg's budget documents at
lunenburgbudgetproject.org — every figure traces to a document anyone can download, and
where the district's own budget shows careful work I try to say so. I have a separate
request in with the Town Accountant for ledger reports. This one is only for things the
district holds, and I have kept it off that list so nobody is asked twice.

## 1. DESE End of Year Financial Report

**The submissions for FY2023, FY2024, FY2025 and FY2026**, in whatever form you file or
retain them.

This is the one I would take first if only one were possible. It reports spending **by
fund**, which is the distinction I cannot make from anything else I hold: the town's ledger
shows the general fund and nothing beside it, so a budget line that rose because the
district grew looks exactly like one that rose because a grant stopped paying part of it.
The EOYR separates those.

It bears directly on something in the FY2026 books. Every paraprofessional account in the
district ran over budget that year, while two grant funds — 2813 and 2814, which I believe
are the federal special education award — spent $229,398 and $179,637. Those may have
nothing to do with each other. I cannot tell from outside, and I would rather not write it
up until I can.

## 2. A crosswalk between the budget and the ledger

**Which MUNIS account each line of the school budget is built from.** Any one year would do;
it is the mapping I need, not the amounts.

This is the only thing on either of my requests that is not a report, and the only thing
that would let a single budget line be followed into the town's books. At the moment the
budget prints a line name and the ledger prints a ten-character account name — `MS GUIDANC`
and `HS GUIDANC` are both function 2710 — so matching them is inference rather than
tracking, and I would rather not publish inference.

The budget is built in a workbook, so whoever builds it may already hold this in some form.
If it exists only in somebody's head, a rough version is still worth more to me than none.

## Three questions

**Is fund code 240 the federal special education grant?** Funds 2813 and 2814 in the town's
special revenue report are labelled `FY25 #240` and `FY26 #240`. I am reading a code, not a
document, and everything I might write about grant-funded staffing turns on whether I have
read it right.

**Does anything record whether a budgeted position was filled?** A ledger records dollars
and not people, so from outside an unfilled post and an underspent salary line are
identical. One FY2026 account was budgeted at $98,784 for a school psychologist and paid
nobody at all; I can see that the money was not spent and nothing else. If a position
control report or staffing roster exists, that would explain several of the largest
variances in the year.

**Out-of-district placement counts, by setting and year.** For FY2023 onward: how many
students were placed privately, how many through a collaborative, how many in district.

I am asking for **counts only** — no names, no identifiers, nothing about any individual
student, and I do not need anything below the level of a total per setting per year. If any
category is small enough that a count would identify someone, please merge it with another
or leave it out and say so; a suppressed number is a perfectly good answer and I would
publish it as suppressed.

The reason I ask is narrow. In FY2026 the private placement line spent $522,629 less than
budgeted and collaborative tuition spent $434,108 more, and the two nearly cancel. That
pattern has an obvious reading — that children moved from one setting to another — and it
has several others that fit the same numbers just as well, including that one line was
simply over-budgeted for years. **Dollars cannot tell those apart and counts can.** Without
them the honest thing to publish is that the data does not say, which is what I have
published so far.

## If any of this is easier some other way

I would rather be corrected than given the thing I asked for. If a document does not exist
in the form I have described, or exists under a different name, or would take real work to
produce, telling me so is genuinely more useful than the document — I can usually rework the
question around what does exist.

Thank you for your time.
