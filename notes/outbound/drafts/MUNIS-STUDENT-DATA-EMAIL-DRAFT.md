**Subject:** Student information in MUNIS purchase order descriptions

Dear Dr. Fortuna,

Thank you for Report 1, the journal detail for the school accounts. The redaction on it was
clearly careful work, and we know it took real time across 1,744 pages.

Reading it raised a structural issue that we think is worth fixing at the source, because
it will recur on every report MUNIS produces, not just this one.

**Where the information comes from.** Student names and initials are being typed into the
**description field of purchase orders**, mostly on special education placement, tuition,
transportation and related-service lines ("Day placement for [initials]…", "Tuition for
[name]…"). MUNIS then copies that description onto every later line that references the
purchase order: the PO entry, each liquidation when it is paid, and the invoice payment.
So one entry typed once appears several times in the general ledger, and in every report
that prints journal detail.

Two related patterns:

- **The service type can identify a student when it sits next to initials.** Lines for
  vision services, assistive technology, home/hospital tutoring or an individual
  evaluation carry a disability or medical context. Paired with initials in a small
  district, that is identifying.
- **Families appear as vendors.** Lunch balance refunds, reimbursements and returned
  checks are paid to or recorded against parents by name, in the vendor and description
  fields.

**Why redaction at the report end does not solve it.** Every records request, audit
export or year-end run carries the same text, and each one has to be redacted by hand,
line by line, on every copy. That is a lot of staff time, and with thousands of lines a
single missed box exposes a child. The only reliable fix is to keep the information out
of the fields in the first place.

**What we would suggest:**

1. **Do not enter student names or initials in PO or invoice descriptions.** Use a
   non-identifying code that the special education office can map back internally. Some
   lines already do this ("CODE 5", "PROG"), so the practice exists; it just needs to be
   the rule.
2. **Correct the descriptions on open purchase orders** where MUNIS allows it, so they do
   not carry forward into next year's records.
3. **Use a generic vendor record for family refunds and reimbursements** (for example,
   "Lunch refund – family" or "Parent reimbursement"), with the payee's details kept in
   the supporting paperwork rather than the ledger.
4. **Write it down for whoever enters POs and invoices** — a one-paragraph data-entry
   note, on both the school and Town side, so it survives staff changes.

This would also make future records requests much faster for your office, since the
ledger itself would no longer need redacting.

On our side, we are holding the PDF privately and will not publish it. What we publish is
our own extract, with every student and private individual removed.

We're still looking forward to the redaction log and the Excel export Jennifer mentioned.

Thank you again for the quick turnaround.

Best,
TJ Loughlin
