## One question, if it is quick

**Is there any way to see what state aid actually paid for?** Here is what I can see and
what I have inferred from it, which are not the same thing.

What I can see: `0100-01001-450600` CH 70 AID received $6,870,136 in FY2026. It sits under
the `0100 GENERAL FUND` header of your revenue report, no revenue account I hold carries a
category code, the state aid accounts share no organisation code with any expense account,
and there is no separate Chapter 70 fund anywhere in what I have — unlike the district's
grants, which each have their own fund and can therefore be followed.

What I have inferred: that Chapter 70 lands in the general fund unrestricted and is
thereafter indistinguishable from property tax, so nothing records which dollar paid which
bill. **I want to be careful that this is a reading of a report header rather than of the
account itself** — the printed report does not show the full account string, so the fund is
where the row sits on the page rather than something the row states. A spreadsheet export
would settle it on its own, and so would one sentence from you.

I have included the revenue accounts in item 2 so I can look at the transaction detail
rather than keep assuming, since a reference or journal number on a receipt might tie to
something I cannot see from the annual totals. **If you already know the answer is that no
such link exists, saying so saves us both the exercise** — I would take that as a finding
rather than a refusal, and stop asking.

Separately, I am asking the Superintendent for the district's **DESE End of Year Financial
Report** submissions, which report spending by fund. Those should settle the neighbouring
question — whether a grant or the town paid for a given cost — even if they cannot settle
the Chapter 70 one, since Chapter 70 sits inside the general fund rather than beside it.
Nothing in this request duplicates that.
