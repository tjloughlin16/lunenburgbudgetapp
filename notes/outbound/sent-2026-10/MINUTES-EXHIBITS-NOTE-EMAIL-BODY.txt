I just wanted to send a note along that I think it would be a good idea to remind minutes takers that Open Meeting Law (OML) requires the minutes to contain a list of documents and exhibits used during the meeting.

I've done an analysis across all minutes provided, and I found very very very few following that.
It was recently discussed by the Town's lawyer when giving an Open Meeting Law refresher, which is posted on YouTube.
I think this is a critical piece of the minutes because then the public can know exactly which files to request in a records request (which I will be doing), or ideally, all of those same documents/exhibits are already posted somewhere online ahead of time (I know the school doesn't post all of them automatically, as I found with the para contract and a few other things).  

Otherwise, the public doesn't know which files to ask for, and the only way for the public to get this information without this is to say, "Please send me all the documents related to the meeting last Wednesday". I can think of a few places this would have prevented serious issues recently.
