What neither request fixes

Everything before FY2023. Both requests cover four years. Fourteen years of ledger sit behind that and are not asked for — a request reaching back to 2009 would not be filled, and the questions this project is answering are about recent years.

The whole-archive figure still moves only a little — 13% to 29% of 247 cells. Nineteen years wide this is a small change; four years deep, where the questions are, it is the difference between 23% and 98%.

And two things that are not documents at all — whether a budgeted position was filled, and out-of-district placement counts. Both are asked of the Superintendent as questions. Neither has a row here, because a completeness matrix counts documents and these are facts nobody publishes in one.
