How to read the outside column

labelmeans
tracedan account or a journal shows this money going here.
restrictedthe fund exists for this and nothing else, so the money almost certainly goes here — but no expense report for the special revenue funds exists, so it is not observed. A presumption, not evidence.
NOT FOUNDthe money is known to be collected and cannot be located in any ledger we hold.

Every restricted row on this page becomes traced with one document: glytdbud-expense run for the special revenue funds — the same report the town already produces for the general fund and for each of its four enterprise funds.

Athletics, in full, as the worked case

The town appropriates $518,334 for athletics inside dept 300 — coaches, transport, the athletic director, the trainer, insurance. The district’s own athletics documents record a further $335,856 through the revolving fund. So the programme costs about $854,190 and the town’s budget shows $518,334 of it.

The town ledger and the district’s documents give different figures for the fund, on different bases and periods. Neither is wrong; they answer different questions. Every fee-funded programme has this shape — athletics is only the one where both halves have been found.
