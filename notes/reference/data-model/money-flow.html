The pension line is the one that cannot be split. COUNT[Y] RET covers town and school employees together and no published document says in what proportion. Teachers are not in it at all — they belong to the state system, whose cost Lunenburg never appropriates and never sees. The Worcester Regional Retirement System publishes an annual actuarial valuation by member unit; that is the document that would settle it.

Why there is no line from Chapter 70 to the schools

Because there is no such line to draw. Chapter 70 arrives as unrestricted revenue into fund 0100 and is thereafter indistinguishable from property tax. The town apportions general fund revenue across departments by share when it presents a budget, and that is a reasonable convention, but it is a convention: no dollar of state aid can be followed to a classroom.

The old diagram drew seven such lines, each labelled with a figure. This one refuses to, and states the refusal on the pot. The edge is not missing data. It does not exist.
