LUNENBURG  
BOARD OF ASSESSORS MEETING 
NOTICE 
 
 
 
 
WEDNESDAY, DECEMBER 7, 2022  
2:30 PM   
           
BILOTTA MEETING ROOM, TOWN HALL 
                                              17 Main Street  
 
 
 
The agenda lists all the topics which may be discussed at the meeting and are those  
reasonably anticipated by the Chair. Votes may be taken as a result of these 
discussions. Not all items listed may in fact be discussed and other items not listed may 
also be brought up for discussion to the extent permitted by Open Meeting Law. 
 
 
CALL TO ORDER 
 
 
OPEN MEETING 
  
 Approve Minutes of November 2, 2022  
 Updates from the Principal Assessor  
 Review Exemption and Abatement Applications 
 Review Applications for Chapter Land 
 Review and Approval of Expense Vouchers, Abatements and Payroll 
 
 
OPEN DISCUSSION
