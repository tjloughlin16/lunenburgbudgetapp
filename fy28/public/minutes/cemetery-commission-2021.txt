Minutes 
Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Thursday January 10, 2019 
Lunenburg Public Library Conference Room 
1023 Mass Ave 
Lunenburg, Ma. 
7:00 pm 
 
Meeting was called to order by Comm. Tyler at 7:00 pm. Members present were 
Vice-Chair Lorraine Mendoza, Clerk William Tyler and Supt. Todd Holman. 
 
Public Comment made by Dave Passios noting Comm. Tyler’s comments at the 
BOS meeting regarding the service of the late Lance May and also his remarks at 
the Charter Review Committee public hearing. He also suggested that we look at 
participating in Wreaths Across America next year and volunteered to assist in 
the cleanup. 
 
A letter was received from Patricia Potter , who lives in South Carolina, daughter-
in-law of Howard who died in 1985. They have 6 lots in the South Cemetery and 
requested the Commission to buy back the plots. It is our policy not to buy back 
unused lots, but they can be donated back to the Cemetery. We cannot re-sell 
them.  
Clarence Floyd, who knew Sergeant Kilpi, who has a duplicate headstone found 
at the old Cemetery building, stated he has tried to contact his son with no luck. 
It was decided to wait a month or so and then destroy the stone in accordance 
with protocol. 
 
Reorganization- Comm. Mendoza made a motion to nominate Comm. Tyler to 
Chair the Cemetery Commission. Motion was seconded by Comm. Tyler and 
voted unanimously to appoint Comm. Tyler . 
 
In old business, the foundation for the new garage has been completed, and bids 
for the remainder of the construction will be reviewed and discussed at our next 
meeting. Our FaceBook page is up and running and Comm. Tyler will continue to 
post items of interest. The grant for the handicap access ramp and parking lot 
was not approved, the Town Manager stated that we can apply again, and she 
would advise us when it comes time to submit it. 

===PAGE 2===
 
The FY 2019 budget was reviewed, and vouchers were signed totaling 
$14,707.36. After some discussion, it was decided to re-visit the budget at the 
next meeting when we will have a clearer idea of the rest of the expenses for FY 
2019. Geri Grigas was reimbursed $50.00 for overpayment on engraving their 
niche in the Columbarium. 
 
Supt. Holman gave an update on the Fall cleanup and said both Cemeteries are 
in good shape for the Winter . Beaulac Bros. Landscaping was contracted by the 
DPW to finish the leaf cleanup and a bill of $1500.00 was given to the DPW. Supt. 
Holman also stated he is in the process of compiling the burial records for 
submission to the 2018 Town Report.  
 
Next meeting is Tuesday, Jan 22, 2019 at 6:00 pm for discussion and 
recommendation for appointment to fill the late Lance May’s term until the Town 
election in May. One Talent Bank form submitted by Nancy Foss Yasko. 
Commission will recess and reconvene at the Town Hall for a joint vote with the 
BOS on the appointment at 7:15 pm. 
 
Next regular meeting is Thursday, Feb. 14, 2019 at 7:00 pm at the Lunenburg 
Public Library. 
 
With no further items on the agenda, a motion was made by Chairman Tyler to 
adjourn, seconded by Comm. Mendoza. Meeting was adjourned at 9:05 pm. 
 
Respectfully submitted 
Lorraine Mendoza, Vice-Chairman

Cemetery Commission Minutes 
February 18, 2021 Meeting 
Via Zoom 
  
The meeting was called to order at 4:00 pm by Chairman Tyler . Commissioners 
Yasko and Mendoza present along with DPW Director Oliva. 
 
There was no public comment. 
 
First order of business was the discussion and vote on the Commission’s FY22 
appropriation at this years Town Meeting. Chairman Tyler explained our annual 
expenses are relatively the same, with the only exception being the improvement 
to the Goodrich family burial yard at the corner of Fish and Lincoln streets and 
the cost of the fieldstones to continue the wall on two sides to enclose the yard. 
Labor is being donated by Richard McGrath, along with two granite posts and an 
iron gate. The cost of these stones is approximately $5500.00. Chairman Tyler 
recommended appropriating the sum of $15,000.00 to fund our operating costs 
for FY22. A motion was made by Commissioner Yasko to appropriate the sum of 
$15,000.00 from the Sale of Lots account at this Spring’s ATM. It was duly 
seconded by Commissioner Mendoza, and a roll call vote was unanimous to 
proceed with the warrant article for that amount. 
 
A brief discussion ensued on the progress at the building site, and all agreed 
that Monty Tech is doing a great job, considering the weather and the time they 
have actually been there. 
 
The North Cemetery parking lot expansion was discussed, and the conceptual 
plans were reviewed. DPW Director Oliva updated the Commission on the 
process going forward with the bidding of the job. Once the final plan is 
complete and a cost estimate is provided, the project will go out to bid. This 
project, because of the funding source, needs to be completed by the end of the 
fiscal year . The Commissioners all agreed that the plan looks fine and gave 
Director Oliva the approval to go ahead with final plans. 
 
With no further business before the Commission, a motion was made to adjourn, 
duly seconded and by roll call vote was voted to adjourn at 4:50 pm. 
 
Respectfully Submitted, William Tyler , Chairman

Cemetery Commission Minutes 
April 22, 2021 Meeting 
Via Zoom 
 
 
The meeting was called to order at 6:30 pm by Chairman Tyler, with Commissioners Yasko and Mendoza 
along with DPW Director Oliva present. 
There was no public comment. 
The first order of business was to approve the minutes of the February 18, 2021 meeting. The minutes 
were read and with no additions or corrections, were approved unanimously. 
Director Oliva gave an update to the Commission on the building project and reported that it is essen-
tially done, with the final grading and hydroseeding done recently. The front will be cleaned up when 
time allows, and the container moved to the back of the building. 
The North Cemetery parking lot expansion project is waiting on the final engineering plans, and hope-
fully would be put out to bid soon, as there is a June 30th deadline looming for the grant funding to be 
expended. Director Oliva said he would speak with the Town Manager to see if an extension is possible 
and also to see if we could bypass the bid process and possibly solicit some bids from local contractors. 
Also discussed with the Director was the stump grinding to be completed at the Goodrich family burial 
yard. Once he has a number on the remaining balance in the maintenance budget for the cemeteries, he 
will let us know. 
Chairman Tyler brought up the issue of the grass condition at both cemeteries, as grub damage is preva-
lent in many areas. The possibility of a grub treatment program was discussed, and Director Oliva stated 
that he would work with the Superintendent to come up with a plan. Further plans on restoring the 
grass will be made once the grubs have been eliminated. 
Commissioner Yasko asked about the condition of some of the roads in the cemetery as there are nu-
merous cracks developing. Director Oliva stated that he would take a look at the problem areas and dis-
cuss at a future meeting. 
The cost for the final grading at the Holman St. garage came in at $5900.00. Powell was willing to do the 
work now while they had a window to get it done but will not bill us until after July 1st. The hydroseeding 
was done by Lakeview and cost $700.00 and will have to paid from the FY 21 appropriation. 
Two vouchers, one in the amount of $14,423.93 for the final billing on the garage project that will come 
from the FY 21 appropriation and one for $512.00 for the new cemetery department sign, which will 

===PAGE 2===
come from our gift account, was duly motioned to pay, and voted unanimously. This will leave $1108.67 
in the FY 21 appropriation account and $209.39 left in the gift account. 
An issue involving a dog problem at North Cemetery was discussed pertaining to a certain area that dog 
owners are not picking up after their dog. A camera may be put up to try and get an identity of the per-
son or persons. 
Lastly, Chairman Tyler suggested that we send a letter of thanks to Monty Tech for their help in the con-
struction of the new garage on Holman St. He will draft a letter and discuss at the next meeting. 
Before adjournment, Chairman Tyler thanked Lorraine Mendoza for her 6 years of service to the Com-
mission and the town as she is not running for re-election. Commissioner Yasko echoed those comments 
as well.  
Next meeting to be determined as the Town elections are May 15, 2021 and once a new commissioner 
is sworn in, we will schedule a meeting at which time the Commission will be re-organized. 
Motion to adjourn was made, duly seconded and with no further discussion, meeting was adjourned at 
7:22 pm. 
Respectfully submitted, 
William Tyler, Chairman

Minutes 
Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Thursday, May 20, 2021- 6:30 pm 
Via Zoom 
 
Meeting was opened at 6:30 PM by Chairman Tyler . Commissioners Yasko and 
Dorren were present along with DPW Director Oliva. 
 
There was no public comment. 
 
DPW Director Oliva updated the Commission on the progress in preparing the 
Cemeteries for the upcoming Memorial Day holiday. Crews would be in both 
Cemeteries providing help to the Superintendent to insure they are ready. He 
also discussed the North Cemetery parking lot project, and explained the 
engineered plans were complete and an RFP would be published in the Ledger 
and also distributed to local contractors. There is a critical timeline involved as 
the project needs to be started and finished by the end of the fiscal year in order 
to qualify for the ADA grant of $32,000.00. He also updated the Commission on 
the seasonal positions that were approved at the ATM. Job openings and 
descriptions would be advertised soon. Chair Tyler asked about the timeline for 
getting the hedges at North Cemetery cleaned out and trimmed. Director Oliva 
stated that he would work with the Superintendent to get some bids on the work 
to hopefully be done after July 1st. Lastly, since in person public meetings are 
able to be conducted after June 15th, due to the lifting of Covid 19 restrictions, 
convenient times were discussed, and it was thought that afternoons would be 
the best times. No exact time was set. 
 
The next order of business was to re-organize the board as there is a new 
member . The following seats on the Commission were duly nominated and 
seconded- William Tyler , Chairman- Nancy-Foss Yasko, Vice Chairman and David 
Dorren, Clerk. After a roll call vote, all seats were voted unanimously in favor . 
 
The minutes from the April 22, 2021 meeting were reviewed and with no errors or 
omissions, a motion was made and duly seconded to accept the minutes as 
written. A roll call voted in favor unanimously. 
 
In old business, the dog problem at North Cemetery involving people not picking 
up after their dogs was brought up again, as the problem has begun again after a 
few weeks of it not being an issue. After discussion, it was decided to make 

===PAGE 2===
another public plea on our Facebook page for help in identifying the individual 
responsible, if they happen to see it happening. Video surveillance is an option 
as well that will be researched along with the proper signage. In other old 
business, a thank you letter as sent to the Superintendent of Monty Tech, 
thanking the students and instructors for the great job on the new building. 
 
In new business a voucher was presented to the Commission for authorization to 
pay the following four vendors totaling- $912.95. Lakeview for hydroseeding the 
area around the new building, Needam Electric Supply for wiring to the overhead 
door , Doors Unlimited for remote control device for the same overhead door and 
lastly Unitil for electric service to the building. After July 1, 2021, this electric 
charge will be paid from the DPW Cemetery Supplies account. 
 
The final Cemetery Accounts report for FY 21 from the Financial Director were 
reviewed and discussed. Commission Dorren inquired about donations to the 
Gift account and Chair Tyler thought there would not be any issues involved if a 
donation wanted to be given from another organization. 
 
Tentative date for the next meeting is June 24, 2021 at the DPW Conference 
Room at a time TBD. 
 
With no further items on the agenda, a motion was made and duly seconded to 
adjourn and by roll call vote was affirmative. Meeting was adjourned at 7:51 PM 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
William Tyler , Chairman

Cemetery Garage 
Holman St. 
 
Meeting was called to order at 10:30 AM by Chairman Tyler. Also present were 
Commissioners Yasko and Dorren, along with DPW Director Oliva and Cemetery 
Superintendent Holman. 
 
There was no public comment. 
 
Only agenda item was a discussion of the cemetery rules and regulations as it 
pertains to decorations and adornments placed by plot owners. A situation 
occurred where a plot owner had placed a stone decoration on their plot and 
since according to our rules and regulations, it is not allowed, it was removed. 
 
It was the Commission’s opinion that since the stone decoration posed no 
interference with grounds maintenance and was put in such a place as not to, it 
should be allowed. It was determined that subjects such as this, do fall into the 
category of day-to-day maintenance and because of that, going forward, the 
Commission will have input into any further discussions as it applies to 
decorations that hinder the maintenance of the cemeteries. However, the final 
decision will be made by the DPW Director . 
 
Meeting was adjourned at 11:45 AM 
 
Respectfully submitted 
William Tyler, Chairman
