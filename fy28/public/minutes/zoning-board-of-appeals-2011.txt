ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS
MINUTES
February 9, 2011
The Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals held a public hearing on Wednesday February 9, 2011 at 7:00
PM. The hearing was held at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor Conference Room, 17 Main Street,
Lunenburg, MA.
The petitioner Priestly A. Bisong, 41 Hamilton Street #16, Leominster, MA, Asek-Ojong Enterprise LLC;
d/b/a Best Auto was seeking Special Permit approval for the storage and display of twenty motor vehicles
for sale. The property, which is the subject of this case, is located at 40 Summer Street, Lunenburg, MA
01462. The owners of the property are Kelly Brothers, 40 Summer Street, Lunenburg, MA 01462
Board Members present: Donald F. Bowen, Chairman, James Besarkarski, Alfred Gravelle, Hans Wentrup
and Sheila Lumi. Present but not voting Paul Doherty and David Blatt.
Others Present:
Michael & Peter Kelly, property owners.
7:00 PM The public hearing was opened and Chairman Donald Bowen introduced the Board members and
explained the hearing procedure to the audience. The petition was read into the public record and the
petitioner Priestly Bisong said he would answer any questions from the Board. No one spoke in favor or
opposition to the petitioner's request.
James Besarkarski asked if the use was going to remain the same. Michael Kelly one of the property
from this location and an abutting location from 1996 to 2008. In 2009 they rented the property to
Valentine Miller and received the proper approvals and permits to sell twenty motor vehicles. Mr.
Valentine closed his business in June of 2010.
Alfred Gravelle asked if the layout of the cars on the lot would remain the same. Mr. Michael Kelley
indicated that Mr. Bisong is seeking the same number of cars (twenty) and the layout would be exactly the
same as Mr. Miller. Mr. Kelly further explained that he made Mr. Bisong aware of the by-laws that govern
used car sales with regard to signage and lighting. Mr. Bowen asked if the hours of operation were going
to change. Mr. Bisong requested the hours of 9:00 A.M. to 7:00 P.M. Monday through Saturday. Mr.
Kelly said that the automobiles would be late model sticker ready vehicles. Hans Wentrup asked if the plan
submitted was to scale. Mr. Kelly informed the Board that he used the Assessors map to create the parking
layout and the paved area of the parcel is outlined on the plan, they will maintain a twenty foot setback as
required in the Protective (Zoning By-law. Snow will be plowed to the rear non-paved area of the car lot.
Mr. Bisong indicated that there would be no repair work, he would only engage in the cleaning of his cars.
Mr. Bowen, Chairman, outlined the standard conditions for operating an auto sales business. The applicant
had no problem complying with the enumerated conditions.

ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS
MINUTES
August 24, 2011
The Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals held a public hearing on Wednesday, August 24, 2011
at 7:00 PM. The hearing was held at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor Conference Room, 17
Main Street, Lunenburg, MA.
The petitioner Christopher J. losua, 12 Town Street, Lunenburg, MA 01462 was filing as a
person aggrieved by the decision of the Building Official. The applicant was seeking approval to
construct an accessory housing unit on an existing two-family home. The property is located at
12 Town Street, Lunenburg, MA 01462.
Board Members present: Donald F. Bowen, Chairman, Raymond Beal, Alfred Gravelle, Sheila
Lumi, and Paul Doherty. Present and not voting: James Besarkarski.
Others Present:
Christopher losua, property owner.
7:05 PM The public hearing was opened and Chairman Donald Bowen introduced the Board
members and explained the hearing procedure to the audience.
DISCUSSION
Lisa Normandin read the petition into the record. There was no public discussion. Donald
Bowen reviewed the zoning by-law with respect to accessory housing. The by-law defines
accessory housing for a single family home but not a duplex. Mr. Bowen indicated that he
contacted town counsel and counsel reviewed the data and supported the fact that an accessory
unit can be added to a duplex. Raymond Beal reviewed the petition, he felt that the filing was
deficient in that it had no recorded plan of land, a building permit application was not attached
and the plans did not include metes and bounds. He felt that while it was a large parcel of land
that was high and dry it lacked exact dimensions and septic plans in the submission.
Donald Bowen indicated that there was specific criteria for an accessory housing unit, one of the
items indicates that the new duplex unit attached to the accessory unit needs to be owner
occupied. In addition the accessory unit as submitted does not meet the minimum requirement of
twelve hundred square feet. Alfred Gravelle agreed with the comments of the Board however, he
felt the only question in front of the Board was the matter of a person aggrieved by the Building
Inspectors failure to issue a building permit for an accessory housing unit on a duplex. Mr. Iosua
indicated that the accessory unit was not being used at this time and a new septic system is
currently being installed at the property that would support three units.
===PAGE 2===
James Besarkarski agreed that the paperwork filed by the petitioner was deficient and did not
include the well or septic locations.
Alfred Gravelle reiterated the fact that the issue regarding the building plans and locations of well
and septic are all things that are reviewed at the time of the building permit process. The onl
natter before the Board was the issue of an accessory unit on an existing duplex. The Boar
agreed with Mr. Gravelles' assessment and discussed it at length.
MOTION
After due deliberation Paul Doherty made a motion and specifically found that an accessory unit
can be attached to a duplex. Sheila Lumi seconded the motion.
Hearing Adjourned at 8:20 P.M.
Minutes submitted by Lisa A. Normandin, Board Secretary
