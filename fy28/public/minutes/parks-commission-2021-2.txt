4. Town Manager/DPW Director/Recreation Director Updates-R Oliva stated that the gate at Marshall Park 
by the softball field got closed after they had seen tire tracks at the main field. He mentioned someone 
accessed the park from Chestnut Street also. He’s trying to find a solution of granite blockage. The landscaper 
did the fall cleanup of the field and did a good job. D Passios mentioned that the DPW crew did a great job on 
the cleanup that the landscaper was not responsible for. A Lockwood asked about an update on the town 
beach, R Oliva did request it but didn’t get the information but will ask again. The light post will be put on their 
agenda to pull up, the area had to dry up for work to commence and the DPW gripper claw was out of service 
but is fixed. She also asked about the weeds growing in the town beach and that there is a grant available 
through the Open Space Committee and asked for information. R Oliva mentioned that it’s under state control 
so he’s not sure of process if chemically treatment was sought. K Menard was going to talk to R Pease for 
information. D Passios gave the history of the light post at Marshall Pond. D Passios left the meeting. 
5. Recreation Director Hiring Updates and Discussion with Town Manager-H Lemieux opened a 
discussion on the appointment of Rob Thompson to fill the permanent Recreation Director Position. It will go 
before the Select Boards meeting on the 21st for ratification. She stated that R Thompson has been the 
temporary director since September. The position was posted in October and expired in November. She 
received two applications. She added that Rob stood out as an obvious choice because he had prior 
experience as a recreation manager for the US Army in Germany and at Alternative Perspectives in Devens. 
He has 13 years’ experience in the field. He was highly recommended from his reference checks. This is a part 
time position and the job description as the previous Directors’. Discussion followed from the members in 
support of his appointment and discussed the line of communication and responsibility between the Parks 
Commission, the DPW and the Recreation Director.
