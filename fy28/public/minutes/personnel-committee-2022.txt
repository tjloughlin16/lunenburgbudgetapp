Town of Lunenburg 
Personnel Committee 
 
Minutes: January 24, 2022 – Zoom Meeting 
 
 
Attendees: 
Joanna Bilotta, Susan Andrews, Eric Arnold, Wendy Bertrand; Donna Fortune 
 
Also Present:   
Julie Belliveau, Asst. Town Manager/HR Director; Atty. Marc Terry, Town of Lunenburg, Labor 
Counsel; Susan Doherty, COA Director; Heather Lemieux, Town Manager, Robert Oliva, DPW 
Director;  Barbara Lefebvre, Sewer Business Manager; Nancy Forest, Payroll & Benefits/Risk 
Manager; Pauline Roy, Outreach Coordinator 
 
Meeting began at 5:04 pm with all attendees present, E. Arnold Committee Chair presiding 
 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes of the December 13, 2021 meeting reviewed. D. Fortune noted that her absence from that 
meeting should be noted on the minutes. 
 
Motion by J. Bilotta made a motion to accept minutes as presented, with the addition of indicating 
D. Fortune as absence. 
Second: W. Bertrand 
 
Roll Call Vote: Donna Fortune abstained as she had not been present at that meeting, all other  
Members voted in favor of acceptance. 
 
No Old Business 
 
New Business 
Changes to SAP 
Marc Terry was introduced to committee and he outlined the work that had been undertaken to 
update the Salary Administration Plan by-law. This was done to better align it to the Town Charter 
and also to adjust several areas that were in conflict with other town groups, where these areas had 
changed as they were amended per the group’s contract changes.  He reviewed where his input 
merely clarified areas where the SAP outlined practice, but were outside their charter, as well as 
several areas that were updated to deal with changes driven by practices in other town groups. 
 
The Town Manager and HR Director had met with the SAP employees with the draft of proposed 
changes and the reasons for those changes.   
 
The information was quite extensive and a discussion with all parties in attendance was helpful in 
identifying several areas that needed more thoughtful discussion.  Issues like the actual scope of 
the Personnel Committee, that this group of employees felt they had no recourse if there were 
disagreements; that other groups may have negotiated for other changes when making changes like 
adding a new holiday to their holiday schedules; and the same issue as it relates to holiday accruals 
and longevity payments.  There was also a discussion on how potential layoffs would be managed 
and by whom, as well as issues with definition of “time-worked” for the purpose of consideration 
of overtime.   
 
===PAGE 2===
Eric proposed that with so many issues to be addressed that the committee members review the 
material, and notate the areas they felt needed a more through review, so that the committee could 
begin to more thoroughly examine the most significant changes at the next meeting 
 
Review Timetable for SAP compensation review 
Julie reviewed history of recent dis cussions that would make changes to the Salary Grid . The 
Personnel Committee had previously discussed starting the grid at minimum wage and condensing 
the 20 grades into a more reasonable pay grid. 8 steps might be better managed with less levels and 
less steps. She also outlined that she has requested pay data from comparable commu nities and is 
awaiting responses.  She will continue work on amending the grid, to be presented at future 
meetings. 
 
New Position/Amended Current Position 
Susan Doherty , COA Director,  outlined changes that had taken place during the last two years, 
driven by the pandemic and the need to provide different services as on site services changed.  The 
previous role of Meal Site Manager did not broadly include management of budgets, food selection 
and prepara tion, meal manage ment, food quality standards, inventory management and kitchen 
leadership.  She asked the group to consider adding a new role of Assistan t Meal Site Manager to 
the SAP.  She also outlined that she would update the current Meal Site Manage r’s job description 
to better include those duties that had become essential to the role. The new role better defined 
what the old work was of the Meal Site Manager.  These changes would take place as the budgets 
for the new year would be presented for approval. 
 
Motion by E. Arnold to accept the proposed job description changes to the current Meal Site 
Manager including the new SAP grade level of Grade 6 ; submit at the annual Town Meeting for 
Final Approval. 
Second:  J. Bilotta 
Roll Call Vote:  All in favor 
 
Motion by W. Bertrand to accept the proposed  new job description for an Assistant Meal Site 
Manager as a Grade 3; submit at the annual Town Meeting for Final Approval. 
Second: S. Andrews 
Roll Call Vote:  All in favor 
 
Public Comment 
Visitors expressed gratitude for being able to p articipate in these important discussions. Donna 
Fortune expressed thanks for the long years of service Dave MacDonald provided for this 
committee. 
  
The group identified the importance of continuing to meet regularly to complete the work of 
updating the SA P and completing the compensation analysis needed to provide to Town Meeting 
for adoption.  Next meeting, Monday January 31, 2022 at 5:00 via Zoom. 
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to adjourn the meeting at 7:18 pm 
Second:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote:  All in favor

Town of Lunenburg 
Personnel Committee 
 
Minutes:  February 16, 2021 – Zoom Meeting 
 
Attendees:   
Joanna Bilotta, Susan Andrews, Eric Arnold, Wendy Bertrand, Donna Fortune 
 
Also Present: 
Heather Lemieux, Town Manager, Julie Belliveau, Asst. Town Manager/HR Director, Sue 
Doherty, COA Director, Nancy Forest, Payroll & Benefits Coordinator, Casey Burlingame, 
Building Commissioner, Barbara Lefebvre, Sewer Business Manager 
 
Meeting began at 5:02pm with all attendees present, E. Arnold Committee Chair presiding 
 
Public Comment: 
Some members of the committee referenced the previous meetings discussions regarding the role 
the Personnel Committee and their concerns about the nature of the committees support to SAP 
employees.  Several issues were voted on at that meeting that did not support the active 
engagement with employees in several areas such as the grievance process. The group reviewed 
several of these items, and an open discussion ultimately lead to referencing the actual votes that 
took place at that meeting, which confirmed the committee’s decision on each. 
 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes of the January 31, 2022 meeting reviewed 
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to accept and approve the minutes as presented 
Second:  S. Andrews 
 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Ongoing Business: 
 
Group continued to review and assess the recommendations for changes to the Salary 
Administration Plan by-law.  Section 17 Paid Vacation section included a new condition to request 
summer vacation plans by May 1, to better facilitate planning.  Various discussions questioned the 
need to add this as year end closing was very busy and there didn’t seem to be long term issue with 
vacation planning.  It had been added to some of the contracts in other groups, the need to add it to 
this group did not seem needed.   
 
Motion by W. Bertrand to keep current language and not add the May 1 condition. 
Second:  E. Arnold 
 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
===PAGE 2===
 
Continued review of this section then covered the next paragraphs reference to employment 
termination.  The changes recommended the elimination of several sentences that just explained 
how time was calculated and group felt their removal was appropriate for clarity. The use of the 
term unused vacation was also discussed and the group felt a better way to express that was 
accrued vacation. 
 
Motion by E. Arnold to accept the recommended elimination as proposed, but change the words 
unused vacation to accrued vacation. 
Second:  J. Bilotta 
 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
The next paragraph outlined the approval process of vacation, with a recommendation that the 
reference to special conditions that should be met for approval of summer vacation be deleted.  
The group also felt the language in the initial paragraph was confusing, so recommended a few 
additional changes.  They felt the final paragraph’s recommendations were acceptable. 
 
Motion by E. Arnold to accept the recommended changes in both paragraphs as well as amend the 
initial sentence of the first paragraph as follows:  “Vacation shall be approved by the Department 
Head/designee for such time or times as best serve the public interest.”  
Second:  S. Andrews 
 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
The group then started to cover recommendations made in Section 18, Paid Holidays.   The group 
had a lengthy discussion on the impact of the towns Friday closings and how it impacted holidays 
that fell on a weekend.  There was considerable dialogue that the policy did not seem appropriate 
as it was the town’s decision to close on Fridays, covering some of the lost hours on Tuesday and 
Thursday early evening hours.  This resulted in employees not being scheduled to work on Fridays, 
so a Saturday holiday would be lost.  As the group was unable to continue its zoom access past 
6:30pm due to another meeting scheduled to use the technology, they decided that the discussion 
on this issue would be continued at the next meeting. 
 
Next Meeting: 
 
Wednesday, March 2, 2021 Zoom Meeting 5:00pm 
 
Motion by W. Bertrand to adjourn meeting at 6:34pm 
Second:  J. Bilotta 
 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor

Town of Lunenburg 
Personnel Committee 
 
Minutes:  March 2, 2021 – Zoom Meeting 
 
Attendees:   
Joanna Bilotta, Susan Andrews, Eric Arnold, Wendy Bertrand,  
 
Not in Attendance:   
Donna Fortune 
 
Also Present: 
Heather Lemieux, Town Manager, Julie Belliveau, Asst. Town Manager/HR Director, Nancy 
Forest, Payroll & Benefits/Risk Manager, Barbara Lefebvre, Sewer Business Manager 
 
Meeting began at 5:03pm with all attendees present, E. Arnold Committee Chair presiding 
 
Public Comment: 
No comment from either committee or the public attendees. 
 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes of the Feb 16, 2022 meeting reviewed 
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to accept and approve the minutes as presented 
Second:  E. Arnold 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
New Business: 
J. Belliveau presented a new hi re form for hiring a new principal assessor.  She recommended that 
we consider hiring this candidate at Grade 15 Step 3, due to his extensive experience which 
included some familiarity with Lunenburg.  He had previously worked on a project in town as a 
consultant.  His resume was reviewed and his experience discussed.   
 
Motion by E. Arnold to accept the recommendation of the HR Director to hire the presented  
Principal Assessor at Grade 15 Step 3. 
Second:  W. Bertrand 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Ongoing Business: 
 
Group continued to review and assess the recommendations for changes to the Salary 
Administration Plan by-law.  Section 18 covers Paid Holidays.  There were several areas that the 
legal counsel recommended be examined.  He also noted that the state had mandated that towns 
recognize Juneteenth as a holiday and all town halls would be closed on that day.  That would 
require adding Juneteenth as an additional holiday in Lunenburg.  A lengthy discussion covered 
===PAGE 2===
the issue of how holidays that fall on Saturday should be managed, given that the town hall no 
longer was open on Fridays and therefore employees were not scheduled to work the day before a 
Saturday holiday.   
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to amend the language of paragraphs A, B, & C to reflect that all employees 
who are eligible for paid holidays would receive a Floating Holiday if a designated holiday fell on 
Saturday. 
Rewording: 
Paragraph A: Employees shall receive paid holiday leave on the day the Town observes the 
holiday based on the base hourly rate of the employee for the number of hours the employee is 
regularly scheduled to work on the day of the week on which the holiday is observed. 
Paragraph B:  Eliminated  
Paragraph C:  Employees shall receive a floating holiday for any holiday that falls on a day that 
they are not regularly scheduled to work.  Holiday leave will be granted on the day the Town 
observes the holiday, when possible.  If the holiday falls on a Saturday, it will be observed on the 
preceding Friday.  If the holiday falls on a Sunday, it will be observed on the following Monday.  
If an eligible employee is required to work on the day the Town observes the holiday, they will be 
entitled to a Floating Day which has to be used within 30 days, by agreement of the supervisor and 
employee.  If the department head determines that holiday leave cannot be granted, the employee 
shall receive payment at one and one=half times the base hourly rate for all hours worked on the 
holiday, in addition to his or her holiday pay.  Final determination is to be made by the Town 
Manager. 
Second:  W. Bertrand 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to add Juneteenth as an observed Paid Holiday to the Lunenburg SAP bylaws 
Second:  E. Arnold 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
The recommendations for several areas that were to clarify the Section 19 Sick Leave were 
generally acceptable, with the exception of adding language that implied employees might be 
required to see a town appointed doctor.  This would have been a new requirement and the group 
generally felt was not necessary.   
 
Motion by E. Arnold to accept the recommended changes by Legal Counsel in Paragraph A, B, 
and C as noted, and amend recommended changes  in Paragraph D, to remove the 
recommendations suggested regarding seeing a town appointed doctor, leaving the previous 
sentence regarding providing a doctors note . 
Second:  S. Andrews 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
The recommendations for Section 20 Personal Leave were generally acceptable with the exception 
of reducing the number of days employees were eligible for.  The recommendation to reduce the 
number to offset the addition of Juneteenth was reviewed and the committee did not support this 
change. The group also clarified the expression of what Personal Leave time was, by 
recommending the timing be noted as days rather than hours. 
===PAGE 3===
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to amend Paragraph A noting the entitlement of personal leave be 3 days 
rather than 24 hours, and that other recommendations in Paragraph A, B, and C be accepted. 
Second:  E. Arnold 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
The recommendations for Section 22 Bereavement Leave were discussed at length.  The group 
identified what other groups within the Town did with this benefit and ultimately determined that 
they felt that both the length of time allowed and the types of relationships supported in this 
section needed to be updated. 
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to amend this entire section and update it to the same language that the 
Clerical Group now has in place, with the number of days and the relationships covered changing 
to the same benefit. 
Second:  W. Bertrand 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
The recommendation from Legal Counsel was to entirely eliminate Section 27 from the SAP 
bylaws.  This is because Discipline is clearly outlined in the Town Charter and does not fall into 
the purview of the Personnel Committee.   
 
Motion by E. Arnold to accept the recommendation to eliminate Section 27 from the SAP by laws. 
Second:  S. Andrews 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
The recommendations for Section 28 Definitions was generally acceptable, with just an expansion 
of types of benefits noted in Paragraph C and the reference to the Benefit Formula noted in 
Paragraph D.  Those words seemed to need clarification as to what was included in such a formula.  
We determined it was referencing just vacation or sick leave, so decided we should note it that 
way.  The statement of the use of a “Benefit Formula” might also be utilized on other sections, so 
any change in the use of this term should be reexamined.  (Section 17 and Section 19, perhaps 
others) 
 
Motion by E. Arnold to accept the recommended changes to Paragraphs A, B, E, F, and G of the 
Definitions Section 28. 
Second:  J. Bilotta 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Motion By J. Bilotta to amend the changes in Paragraph C to add Holiday in the explanation of 
what benefits were covered, and in Paragraph D amend the term “Benefit Formula” to 
Vacation/Sick Leave Formula. 
Second:  S. Andrews 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
===PAGE 4===
The recommendation for Schedule A was to update all references to the Board of Selectman to

Town of Lunenburg 
Personnel Committee 
 
Minutes:  March 28, 2022 – Zoom Meeting 5:00pm 
 
Attendees:   
Joanna Bilotta, Susan Andrews, Eric Arnold, Wendy Bertrand, Donna Fortune 
 
Also Present: 
Julie Belliveau, Asst. Town Manager/HR Director, Nancy Forest, Payroll & Benefits/Risk 
Manager, Barbara Lefebvre, Sewer Business Manager,  Sue Doherty,  COA Director 
 
E. Arnold opened the Meeting at 5:10pm with all members in attendance 
 
Public Comment: 
There was no public comment from the committee or the public 
 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes of the March 21, 2022 meeting were reviewed. Two minor administrative errors were 
noted. 
 
Motion by W. Bertrand to accept and approve the minutes of the March 21, 2022 meeting, 
amending the administrative errors noted. 
Second:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote: All in Favor 
 
New Business: 
New Hire Form –   Sue Doherty presented a request to hire a new COA van driver at a higher step 
in Grade 4.  It has been difficult to recruit for this role and a current employee has graciously 
agreed to stay till this replacement is brought on.  The candidate has previous experience as a 
school bus driver, a good driving record and her recommendation is to bring the candidate in as a 
Grade 4 STEP 3 $15.26ph.  This is a part time role and the step increase will be equal to the other 
driver shortly. 
 
Motion by S. Andrews to accept the recommendation of the Director of COA and supported by the 
HR Director and hire this van driver at Grade 4 step 3. 
Second J. Bilotta 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Ongoing Business 
Review of SAP revisions 
The committee did a final review of the revisions of the SAP bylaws.  There were some minor 
administrative adjustments needed in sections 10 and 11.  In Section 17 the group reexamined 
paragraph E which, had, in the original document, a reference for the ability of each employee to 
be able to take a summer vacation.  That paragraph had been changed to eliminate the reference to 
===PAGE 2===
this provision.  The group discussed this again this evening and decided that the reference had 
value and provided clarity so should remain as it was noted in the original version.   
Motion by E. Arnold to return paragraph E in Section 17 Paid Vacations, back to its original status, 
supporting that employees should be allowed a summer vacation. 
Second:  W. Bertrand 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
There was also a discussion on Section 21 Family and Medical Leave.  Legal counsel advised that 
this was not required to be noted in the SAP by laws as it was a mandated program, and the Town 
was required to provide all employees a copy of the FMLA Policy. As this policy might change 
overtime the Select Board is responsible for adopting the policy.  The group had taken this advice 
and deleted the section in our amendments.  The group thought that it might be better to just note 
that a copy of the FMLA was available on the Towns website. 
Motion by S. Andrews that we just note in Section 21 FMLA, that a copy of the FMLA Policy is 
available on the Towns website. 
Second:  J. Bilotta 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
This completed the group’s review of all the changes proposed and approved for the SAP by laws. 
Motion by J. Bilotta to accept the completed SAP by law amendments as noted, and submit the 
document for final approval at the upcoming Town Meeting. 
Second W. Bertrand 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Public Comment 
The committee members and the public expressed thanks for the effort the committee made in 
updating this document. 
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to adjourn meeting at 6:21pm 
Second:  W. Bertrand 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor

Personnel Committee 
 
Minutes:  June 6, 2022    
Town Hall, 2nd floor 6:30pm 
 
Attendees:   
Susan Andrews, Eric Arnold, Wendy Bertrand, Donna Fortune 
 
Also Present: 
Julie Belliveau, Asst. Town Manager/HR Director, Nancy Forest, Payroll & Benefits/Risk 
Manager, Barbara Lefebvre, Sewer Business Manager, Pauline Roy, Senior Center; Deborah A.H. 
Christe n, resident; Tom Alonzo, Selectperson 
 
E. Arnold opened the Meeting at 6:33pm with members noted in attendance 
 
Public Comment: 
Their was a recommendation that in the future submissions like the one we just did with the SAP 
refresh, with so much being changed…should have broader and earlier distribution so town 
employees might be better able to review material. The current submission did not allow that time 
so did not feel transparent. 
There were no comments from committee 
 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes of the May 2, 2022 meeting were reviewed. 
 
Motion by W. Bertrand to accept and approve the minutes of the May 2, 2022 meeting as 
presented  
Second:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote: All in Favor with S. Andrews abstaining as she was not at meeting. 
 
Ongoing Business: 
T. Alonzo came to address the committee and other attendees, after reading the memo that Select 
Board members received that represented some disagreement with how the SAP approvals at the 
Town Meeting were managed.  He was not representing the Select Board, but his own thoughts on 
how the particular warrant issues were handled.  He understood there was some tension on the part 
of some members of the Personnel Committee and the changes that were adopted at the Town 
Meeting.  The two significant changes regarded the awarding time off for Juneteenth, and how 
holiday time off was managed, given the nature of holidays falling on weekends and their 
reassignment to other days.   
He expressed his appreciation on the extent of the work that the committee had done to update the 
SAP, acknowledging that ultimately the residents of the town did not support the committee’s 
recommendations on these two items, which is the appropriate review and approval process.  The 
committee’s recommendations on all the other changes were accepted.   
The committee members asked his guidance on the plans to review and perhaps upgrade the SAP 
job descriptions, making fewer levels and perhaps different step progressions in an effort to 
===PAGE 2===
simplify the grid.  He acknowledged it sounded like a good project, and the Select Board was 
certainly open to simplifying things, but cautioned that his experience in matters similar to this was 
that employees resisted this type of change, lacking trust in any process that changed their pay or 
status.  If the committee could gain the employees understanding and acceptance, he thought the 
Select Board would be favor of it. 
 
The committee members also asked about recommendations that would be acceptable to the Town 
regarding the percentage increase to the SAP level grid.  As the committee had only submitted one 
year’s recommendation for 2023, we would not need to address 2024 and 2025. 
 
J. Belliveau reviewed that she and H. Lemieux had discussed how a review of job descriptions and 
job grades might be done.  A review like this may require an outside consultant. Any funding 
would need town meeting approval. 
 
Public Comment 
There was no additional public comment  
 
Next Meeting: 
The group agreed to meet again Monday July 11, 2022, at 6:30pm in person. 
 
Motion by to adjourn meeting at 7:34pm by D. Fortune 
Second:  W. Bertrand 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor

this project, thus removing the n eed to take the request to Special Town Meeting . She presented the 
proposal that has been accepted from Mary Beth Bernard, an HR Consultant with broad experience in

Second:  W. Bertrand 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
New Business: 
J. Belliveau presented a new hire request from the PAC for a Videographer.  J. McLaughlin the PAC 
Manager outlined the broad experience of the candidate, much more in-depth experience than outlined in 
the Job Description.  Based upon this greater experience, and with the more recent difficulties in hiring into 
this group, she requested that the candidate be place in Grade 3, Step 3.  General discussion on any adverse 
impact this might have on current employees was discussed. There would be no such impact with current 
employees. 
Motion by W. Bertrand to accept the recommendation of the HR Director to hire this new videographer as a 
Grade 3, Step 3. 
Second:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Ongoing Business 
J. Belliveau gave an update on the Reclassification Project, which, as of this date, is on target as outlined in 
the original proposal timeline.  There will be onsite interviews on 12/19/22 to review any open issues in the 
preparation of the job descriptions.   
 
Public Comment 
There were no comments from committee or from the Public

As needed and regular meeting January 9, 2023 6:00 pm
