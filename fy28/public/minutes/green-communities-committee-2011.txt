Green Community Task Force 
Meeting Minutes   
January 11, 2011 
  
Present:   Members:          S.Marsden, P.Slattery, S.DiCampo, D.Blatt  
Town Liaison:   M. Benson, E. Sund, J. Londa 
Guest:   Neal Sullivan 
       
1. Minutes were reviewed and approved for 11/16/2010 meeting. 
2. S. Marsden advised that Courtney Zivojinovic  has notified him that he is leaving the Green 
Communities Task Force due to schedule conflicts.   
3. M. Benson introduced Neal Sullivan who is interested in being a member of the Green 
Communities Task Force.  Discussion was held to acquaint him with the history, topics and 
function of the Task Force. M. Benson shall notify the Selectmen that Mr. Sullivan has been 
endorsed by the Task Force to fill the opening created by E. Sund becoming a liaison. Two other 
openings exist. One to fill the C. Zivojinovic opening and one to fill the position of Alternate 
which has remained unfilled since the appointment of J. McQuaid as a full member.  
4. Two additional names were put forth for consideration to fill the vacancies - Zachary Cutting and 
Fernando Siera. P. Slattery will meet with each to introduce them to the Task Force functions to 
insure action by the Selectmen in early February.  
5. M. Benson advised that action was being taken by the Selectmen to hire a web page design firm 
to improve content, layout and access.   
6.  A lengthy discussion was held regarding the solar RFQ as it applies to the Power Provider RFP 
solar project at the former landfill which qualifies as a “brown field  project”.  J. Londa requested 
direction on a number of issues to finalize the RFQ. M. Benson will work with Town Manager  to 
secure a copy of the DEP Closure and Enforcement Order; as well as a plan of the landfill gas 
venting which is available from the Assessors/ DPW. A Google Earth overview map is also 
required. The size of the project is tentatively felt to need 30 acres of the existing 42 acre site to 
insure a facility capable of generating 2MW of power.   
7. D. Blatt spoke about getting a paper recycling dumpster in a convenient Town location near the 
Center.   
8.  S. Marsden will follow through with MRPC regarding his contact of WPI to determine if a 
student led project with professor certification would provide a validated wind study for funding 
through MEC.  
9. Discussions of upcoming seminars and webinars were held. S. Marsden to attend webinar this 
week on solar photovoltaic installations. 
10.   A notice about a new State, solar, residential hot water heating program will be posted on the     
web site. M. Benson to have J. McQuaid add that to Task Force web page. 
       11.  Future meeting scheduled for February 1st, 2011 at 6:30PM 
       12.  Meeting adjourned at 8:15 PM 
 
      
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Patrick J. Slattery, Secretary

Green Community Task Force 
Meeting Minutes   
March  9, 2011 
  Present:   Members:          P. Slattery, J. McQuaid, D. Blatt, N. Sullivan, E. Sund,  
     S. DiCampo, Z. Cutting,   
Town Liaison:   M. Benson, J. Londa, J. Breault 
Guest:    S. Marsden 
       
1. Minutes were reviewed and approved for 2/15/2011 meeting with one correction to change  
B. McQuaid to D. McQuaid. 
2. P. Slattery chaired the meeting and announced the recent resignation of Chairman, Steve Marsden 
from the Task Force and confirmed the resignation of C. Zivojinovic. Noting that the Committee 
needed to reorganize a discussion followed regarding member availability, meeting time 
preferences, direction and agendas. Elections were held with the roster as follows: 
 
Patrick J. Slattery, Chairman   Marion Benson, Advisor 
David Blatt, Vice Chairman   John Londa, Advisor 
Joanne McQuaid, Secretary   James Breault, Advisor 
Ernie Sund, Member 
Steve DiCampo, Member 
Neal Sullivan, Member 
Zachary Cutting, Member 
 
3. P. Slattery advised that it was his intention to serve on the Committee for another 6 months and 
then leave the Task Force in September due to increased time commitments in other areas.  
4. P.Slattery requested that J. McQuaid update the web page and the information on the cable 
channel listing to reflect the new roster.    
5. Zachary Cutting was welcomed as a new member of the Task Force.  
6. M. Benson announced that her research indicated that accepting the IEBC standards in lieu of the 
Stretch Code would not satisfy the requirements for becoming a Green Community. P. Slattery 
advised that the Task force should work on and expect to introduce a Green Community by-law 
for the Fall Town meeting. 
7. Recent Chairman S. Marsden was in attendance and explained the professional work related  
reasons for leaving. M. Benson and E. Sund commented on his expertise and untiring efforts on 
behalf of the Task Force over the last 2 years. Mr. Marsden was thanked by all present.  
8. J. Londa gave an update of progress on the proposed 1 ½ MW solar system planned on the Town 
owned Youngs Road site. Land layout was reviewed over the 22 acre site. Placement details on 
cap were discussed, questions about engineering costs to be borne by the Developer and grid 
connection details were reviewed.   DPW issues have been resolved and a conference call is 
scheduled with K. Speidel and Unitil to resolve net metering and distribution questions. 
Expectation is that the RFP will be out to bid before the end of March.   
9. Recycling RFP is ready for bidding. Separate quotes are being requested for recyclables and 
trash.  
10.   Z. Cutting was instructed to see L. Klaft at the Town Clerk’s office to sign documents relating to    
        Open Meeting laws and take the online ethics test as required.  
11.  Future meetings shall occur on the first Monday of each month. Next meeting April 4th, 6:30 PM. 
       12. Meeting adjourned at 7:50 PM 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
Patrick J. Slattery, Secretary

Green Community Task Force Meeting Minutes 
April 4, 2011 
 
        Present:   Members               P.Slattery,  J.McQuaid, E.Sund, N. Sullivan, Z. Cutting 
                           Town Liaisons     M. Benson, J. Londa 
 
1. M. Benson called the meeting to order at 6:35 presiding for P.Slattery  until  7:00 P.M.   
The minutes for 3/9/2011 were reviewed and approved. 
2. J. McQuaid commented that there has been difficulty accessing the website. E.Sund noted 
that the conversion to a new town website that will be launched on 5/1/2011 may be 
causing the problem.  She will hold off on uploading any new information until then. 
3. No new development on recycling at this time. 
4. The Board of Health raised concerns over proper channels for approval of the proposed 
solar site at the landfill.  George Emond confirmed through the DEP Solid Waste Bureau that 
it is acceptable to install a solar field on the capped landfill. 
5. Z. Cutting commented on the new solar hot water rebate noting that is was more economical 
for new construction than retrofitting. He will work with D. Blatt and S. DiCampo to prepare 
information for the public. 
6. E. Sund reported on the feasibility of wind projects noting that 5-6 potential sites have been 
identified on Town property for 1-3 turbines but more in depth studies are needed. Also a 
financial partner would be desirable but they usually deal with 50+ MW and ours would 
only be around 1.5 – 2 MW. 
7. M. Benson mentioned that the Montachusett Regional Planning Commission is working with 
WPI to develop a model of possible energy sources in the region. 
8. J. Londa stated that the solar landfill site has been reduced to 18-20 acres possibly lowering 
the capacity to 1.3 MW. The Selectmen are concerned about an increase in cost to the 
ratepayers.  Unitil has a 2MW cap on purchasing renewable energy but it’s unknown how 
much of that is already committed. The RFP went out for bid on 3/30/2011. He will hold a 
pre-bid meeting with J. Breault on 4/14 and bids are due by 2:00 P.M. on 5/5/2011. 
9. Unitil did an energy audit for the T.C. Passios building.  Upgrades to lighting and heating 
could save an estimated $5,775.00 annually. Unitil would contribute $32,000.00 and the 
Town $16,000.00 with a payback of 2.8 years. 
10.  There was a discussion on how various departments could recapture energy savings to be 
applied to future energy conservation measures. 
11. M. Benson noted that Felipe Schwartz reiterated that we couldn’t substitute the IEEC for the 
Stretch Code to become a Green Community. She would keep looking for a different opinion 
of the criteria before we present it to the fall Town Meeting.  There appears to no penalty to 
opt out of being a Green Community once accepted. 
12. We need to spread the word about zero interest energy loans available to homeowners. 
Unfortunately they need to spend money to save money on energy cost which could raise 
their taxes. 
13. Next meeting date set for May 2, 2011 at 7:00 P.M.  Meeting adjourned at 8:15 P.M. 
 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Joanne McQuaid, Secretary

Green Community Task Force Meeting Minutes 
May 2, 2011 
 
   Present:   Members                 P.Slattery,  J.McQuaid, Z. Cutting, D. Blatt, S. DiCampo 
                   Town Liaisons      M. Benson, J. Londa, J. Breault 
 
1. P.Slattery called the meeting to order at 6:35P.M.  The minutes for 4/4/2011 were 
reviewed and approved. 
2. New Town website has not yet been launched.  M.Benson will correct the Planning Board’s 
webpage for the Task Force updating the current members. 
3. Several classes at the Primary School are looking for ways to make their building greener. 
Goals will be set after an impact study week. J. McQuaid will follow up with the teachers 
for ideas to incorporate into an educational brochure. 
4. Once the Town signs a new recycling contract D. Blatt said he could evaluate if a separate 
town wide paper recycling bin would be profitable as a certain tonnage has to be attained. 
A convenient location would also have to be found. 
5. The bid opening for the Landfill Solar Field is 5/5/11. Proposals were sent to 30 
companies, 12 came to the pre-bid meeting. D. Blatt will evaluate bids with J. Londa to 
check for minimum qualifications. The Task Force will review the list and select 3-4 to 
recommend to the Town Manager. 
6. The solar panels are on the roof at THMS and being connected. The inverter is on site and 
the project has passed the DOER inspection. J.Londa reported that completion is about 30 
days out and he has met with the Fire Department about safety precautions. A possible 
ribbon cutting ceremony was mentioned. 
7. J. Breault will consult with the Fire department about hot water use to see if solar hot 
water is feasible. The DPW building does not have high enough demand to justify the use. 
8. The IECC cannot be substituted for the Stretch Code, which is 10% above the IECC. A 
recommendation needs to be made to the Board of Selectmen with pros and cons of 
becoming a Green Community. Grant money ($175,000) if available to designated towns. 
9. A statement drafted by P.Slattery describing the activities of the Green Community Task 
Force for 2010 was accepted for the Annual Town Report.  A newspaper article 
highlighting the green projects of area towns featured a picture of the THMS solar project. 
10. The WPI study concerning energy needs of the region will not be available for 2-3 months. 
Twenty towns are included Fitchburg, Leominster and Gardner. 
11. Z. Cutting had a home energy audit through the MassSave program.  Free light bulbs and      
air sealing are available as well cost sharing on insulation. He will write up a report so we 
can promote it to homeowners. 
12. An audit was performed for Town Hall and Ritter Building.  J.Londa will put out a bid for a     
lighting upgrade on 5/9/11, opened on 5/26/11. Unitil will pay for 75% of the cost.   
       13. P.Slattery reiterated that he would be stepping down in September. 
       14. Next meeting date set for May 18, 2011 at 7:00 P.M.  Meeting adjourned at 8:22 P.M. 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Joanne McQuaid, Secretary

Green Community Task Force Meeting Minutes 
June 6, 2011 
 
        Present:   Members -   D.Blatt, Z. Cutting, Steven DiCampo, J. McQuaid, E. Sund,   
                    Town Liaisons - J. Breault, J. Londa 
 
 
1. D. Blatt called the meeting to order at 7:00 P.M.  
2. Presentation was made by EOSOLAMERICAS regarding a proposed 25-30 acre 
solar field at 651 Chase Road on a 116 acre parcel including a potential future 
commercial enterprise at the frontage. D.Blatt inquired if the Town could buy 
power from them. They are going through procedures with their engineers, 
Stantec, and hope to be generating power by this time next year. 
3. The minutes for 5/18/2011 were reviewed and approved. 
4. Borrego update- J.Londa and Z.Cutting have permission from the Town Manager to 
negotiate with Borrego for a lower cost of electricity and net metering. Their bid 
was just an initial proposal. Borrego sent a timeline for milestones. On June 24th they 
will update the Town on permitting and interconnection processes. Their goal is 
execute the contract by late June/ early July. 
5. D.Blatt suggested getting an offer from EOSOLAMERICAS to purchase electricity for 
comparison to Borrego’s quote. Z.Cutting pointed out that with Borrego’s offer the 
Town will own the system after 20 years but not with EOSOLAMERICAS. 
6. Recycling- D. Blatt to look for rates on a paper retriever for the Town. The 
schools generate (24) 96 gal. totters of single stream recyclables every week 
during the school year. J. McQuaid will check on terms of new contract with 
Casella. 
7. J.Londa reported that the THMS solar project is waiting on final inspections.
