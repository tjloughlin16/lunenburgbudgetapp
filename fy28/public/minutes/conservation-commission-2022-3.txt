Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal 
Bylaws a public hearing on a Notice of Intent by Mardsen Engineering for replacement of a septic system and 
installation of a recharge trench within the 100’wetlands buffer at 37 Spring Street. 
 
Steve Marsden shared the project plan.  There is a need for offsets and distances between this site and abutting 
wells.  The septic system is using a jet tank for aeration to remove suspended solids and BOD and also a 
geomat sand filter for additional nitrogen removal.  The proposed work will be outside the 30’ buffer and 
inside the 50’ wetlands buffer, there will be one bush in the 30’ buffer which needs to be dug up and raised 
along with the bed.  They are also proposing gutters, downspouts, and recharge trenches along the sides of the 
house.  The trenches are designed for the 100-year storm event.  RB asked if the work will affect the house 
foundation, and how they will reseed.  SM said he did not think the foundation would be affected as it is quite 
high, and that sod will be used to revegetate the soil.   
Kathleen Vorce is an attorney for David McHeartland who is an abutter at 51 Spring Street.  He has had 
significant drainage problems from a development project uphill being done by BM and other building 
partners.  KV said that the line of trees that runs along the left also includes a drainage ditch on 37 Spring 
Street which holds considerable runoff.  The prior owner used to run sump pumps from the basement to the 
trench, and KV asked if the new setup will also involve pumping to the drainage ditch.  She also asked where 
the trenches were for the runoff. SM showed where the drywell trenches will be on the plan.  KV asked if the 
sump pump would be going to the old trench or to the new drywells.  BM said the sump pump will be tied into 
the new recharge trenches.  KV asked if the Commission would want to ask that the existing channel be 
depicted on the plan.  BM says the existing trench will not be touched but that he can add it to the plan. 
 
JV asked if spring street is a town road, BM said that it is private.  RB said that the 100-foot wetlands buffer 
which crosses is the end of the Conservation Commission’s jurisdiction, as is the street.  JV asked what will 
happen to the existing leach field, RM said the tank will be crushed and filled and the leach fields will be left 
alone. 
 
 RB/ML M/S to continue the public hearing to September 7, roll call vote unanimous to approve.
