Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Wednesday April 20, 2022 
 
 
Meeting called to order at 3:05pm.  In Attendance was Chairperson William Tyler, 
Vice-Chairperson Nancy Foss-Yasko and Clerk David Dorren  
 
No Public Comment 
 
Chairperson Tyler started the meeting with the topic of Perpetual Funds and how 
said funds were to be dispersed.  The current Principal amount is $210,602 with 
the interest in the Perpetual Funds being $5,000. Recently a request was made 
through the Town Manager to use interest from the Perpetual Care account to help 
purchase a new lawn mower for the DPW. Because of time constraints and the 
need for an answer to the request, the Chairman replied to the Town Manager that 
purchases of this nature were not an appropriate use of these funds. The 
Commission decided on a unanimous vote (3-0) that all Expenditures of Perpetual 
Funds will be voted on in the future, as per Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 
114, Section 20. 
 
At a recent Selectboard meeting it was suggested that the Cemetery Commission 
used funds from the Sale of Lots account to create a memorial at the West St. 
Terrace burial ground of the Arnold/ Litch family. This memorial was funded by the 
Historical Commission and the Historical Society, and created by volunteer efforts, 
not by the Cemetery Commission. 
 
The topic of the trimming of the hedges at North Cemetery was tabled until after 
the July budget is approved and passed.  
 
The Commission started reviewing CHAPTER 310 of the CEMETERY RULES AND 
REGULATIONS.  The commission decided to review as many sections of Chapter

310 at each meeting as possible.  This month the Commission revamped Section 
310-1 Fees and Charges.   
 
 
Meeting was Adjourned at  4:45pm

Meeting called to order at 2:35pm.  In Attendance was Chairperson William Tyler, Vice-
Chairperson Nancy Foss-Yasko and Clerk David Dorren  
 
No Public Comment 
 
First topic of discussion was to approve the minutes of March 1st and April 20th.  
Commissioner Dorren made the motion to approve said minutes.  VP Yasko seconded the 
motion.  Minutes were approved 3-0 
 
Commissioner Dorren brought up the topic of raising more money in the form of Grants 
for the Cemetery Commission to use for: 
 
Old mausoleum, Repair of stone walls, Turf in North Cemetery, Head stones in North and 
South Cemeteries (especially our veterans), Roadways that are in disrepair and 
cameras.  It was suggested that Commissioner Dorren initiate a conversation with 
Heather R. Lemieux , Town Manager since she has had experience in grant writing .   
VP Yasko also suggested talking to Tanner Cole about the cost of redoing the turf 
and the shrubs.   Motion was passed 3 -0. 
 
The topic of a Petroleum Odor at the North Cemetery was discussed. All Local and State 
Departments have been notified, however, as a Commission it is our responsibility to 
ensure that the Commission and the Town are protected from future environmental 
concerns. Ms. Yasko has volunteered to be the point of contact with Chief Sullivan and 
the DEP. She will report back to the Commission on a regular basis to give us updates on 
the situation.  
 
Chapter 310-2 of the Cemetery Rules and Regulations were discussed.  It was decided to 
leave all paragraphs in place except for paragraph 3 which would be moved to section 
310-1.  That paragraph read: 
Full and Cremation burials and Columbarium interments on Saturday are $500.00 for the 
first 4 hours and $500.00 for the next 4 hours.

Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Thursday, August 18, 2022 
  
 
Meeting called to order at 2:30pm.  In Attendance was Chairperson William Tyler, Vice-
Chairperson Nancy Foss-Yasko, Clerk David Dorren, DPW Director Bill Bernard and 
Tanner Cole from Gatsby Grounds 
 
No Public Comment 
 
Chairperson Tyler started the meeting with the discussion of the Capital Plan for year 
2023.  Main topic was the Turf situation at both the North and South Cemeteries.   
 
Tanner Cole from Gatsby Grounds was asked his opinion on the turf at both North and 
South Cemeteries.  Tanner stated that the Soil was heavily disturbed, no water or 
irrigation available in either cemetery and there were grubs in the soil.  Tanner 
suggested improving the soil structure with organic matter that makes the soil rich, put 
a top dressing (bio compost) on top of the soil.  
 
In looking over the cemeteries Tanner suggested that the South Cemetery needs work 
on 4-5 acres and the North Cemetery needs help on 7-8 acres.  The amount to Top 
Dressing needed would be 30-45 yards per acre.   
South Cemetery 5 acres x 45 yards = 225 yards 
North Cemetery 8 acres x 45 yards = 360 yards 
 
Tanner suggested Top Dressing in October and Grub prevention in July. 
 
Approximate cost would be as follows: 
 
$35,000 for both North and South Cemeteries for Top Dressing 
$ 7,500  for both North and South Cemeteries for grass seed 
$ 7.500  for both North and South Cemeteries for Grub/Weed Control 
Unknown pricing for N and S Cemeteries for Lime Control

Minutes 
Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Thursday, September 22, 2022    2:30 pm 
 
Meeting was called to order at 2:30pm by Chairman Tyler . Commissioners Yasko and Dorren 
were present along with DPW Director Bill Bernard.     There was no public attendance. 
 
Chairman Tyler opened the meeting with a discussion of the Capital Plan for 2024. The 
Commission is asking for a total of $81,600 under the 2024 Capital Plan.   
 
$ 11,000 for the completion of the hedges at North Cemetery 
$ 50,000 for the Restoration of turf at the North and South Cemeteries 
$   4,100 for the Survey and Layout of plots in the new section of the North Cemetery.   
$ 16,500 for the Repairs of the 16th and 17th century Headstone repairs 
$ 81,600 Total  
 
If the Capital Plan is not approved the Commission will be asking for an extra $4,000 to 
do the North Cemetery Hedges which will be added to the $ 10,000 purchase services 
requested to finish the Hedges.   Vote was 3-0 to approve the Capital Plan  
 
The Commission asked DPW Director Bill Bernard to look into removing the wood that is 
being stored at the new extension area of the North Cemetery to get ready for the 
surveying and potential use of the land for new plots in the future.  
 
The Minutes of August 18, 2022 meeting were amended and approved by a vote of 3-0.   
 
Motion was made to send Helen Balcom a Thank You card for doing the flower beds in 
front of the North and South Cemeteries.  Motion passed 3-0 
 
 
Chapter 310 Cemetery Rules and Regulations draft were reviewed and will await 
comments from DPW Director. 
 
Meeting adjourned at 3:35pm  Motion passed 3-0 
Next meeting Thursday October 20, 2022 at 2:30pm 
Respectfully Submitted 
David Dorren, Clerk

Minutes 
Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Tuesday, December 20, 2022 
 
 
Meeting was called to order at 1:00pm.  In attendance was Chairman Bill Tyler, Vice 
Chair person Nancy Foss-Yasko, Clerk David Dorren and DPW Director Bill Bernard. 
 
 
Chairman Tyler went over the Capital Funds from the Town of Lunenburg.  The Turf 
Project was #17 and the Headstone Project was #20 out of a total of 29 Projects. Vice 
Chair Yasko suggested we go through funding from State Rep. Michael Kushmerek’s 
Office.  Chairman Tyler suggested we do both, we would have a better chance getting 
funding.  Motion made to go through both Capital Funding and State Rep Michael 
Kushmerek’s Office. 3-0 
 
 
Bill Bernard stated Todd Holman received a quote for the Stone Wall for both cemeteries.  
Quote was $375,000 for both.  The Pavement for the DPW Building Driveway at the 
North Cemetery will be earmarked by DPW for State Funding.  Bill also said he put in the 
budget for the Hedges at the North Cemetery to be done after July 1, 2023. 
 
 
Motion was made by David Dorren to approve the November 29, 2022 minutes.  
Chairman Tyler 2nd the motion.  Motion approved 3-0 
 
 
Motion made to adjourn meeting at 2:30pm 
 
 
Next meeting set for January 10, 2023 at 1pm.  
 
 
Respectfully Submitted 
David Dorren, Clerk
