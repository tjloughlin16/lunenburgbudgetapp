Department of Public Works,

Call to Order
Meeting convened at 7:02pm. In attendance were:
Commissioners: Mike Masciarelli, Bob Robuccio, and Corinne Scouten
Review/Approve Minutes
Motion was made to approve minutes. Motion was seconded and minutes were approved 3-0.
Marshall Parking Lot Update
DPW Director - Jack Rodriquenz was unable to attend and provide an update this evening.
Turf Fees/Usage
Two bills - one for Lunenburg Bengals and one for LYSA (soccer) were reviewed by the
commissioners prior to sending. Both bills reflected $10,000 for the prior two years field use
===PAGE 2===
(2013 & 2014) that were not previously billed. The Bengals bill can be send to Bob Coppenrath
- Bengals President and the LYSA bill can be sent to the LYSA Treasurer.
Question: Is the school current through the 2014 school year? Bob Robuccio will get an
accounting from Karen Brochu at Town Hall to review.
Corinne Scouten will bring a sample template of a User Fee Schedule to the next meeting. She
raised the question of how will the Commission monitor who is using the fields and when.
Some type of schedule needs to be set up and maintained in order to track use. There should
be signage placed at the turf to direct people who to call or email to reserve.
Mike Masciarelli reviewed with the commissioners the hours of usage the different groups from
town have used the turf: LYSA - 353 hrs, Adult Soccer - 20 hrs, Truax Conditioning Camp - 12.5
hrs, Sports Camp - 24 hrs, Miscellaneous - 20 hrs. This does not include school or school
team's usage.
Bob Robuccio stated that he has been unable to find out about the MIAA playoff games and
how we can collect our user fees for those games. Apparently the MIAA won't talk to anyone
but high school principals. Mike Masciarelli will talk to Brian Spadafino and find out what he
can.
Concession Stand Fees/Usage
The Music Aiders sent in $300 for 6 uses of the concession stand during the fall football season.
Mike Masciarelli signed off on the receipt. Corinne Scouten noted that she was asked (via
email) if the music aiders could be provided a key so they don't have to track down someone
every time they need to get in. Consensus is that we don't want to hand out keys - but could
have a central pick up location - maybe Corinne's house.
Key/ Other Park Properties
Mike Masciarelli stated that we need to find out where other keys are for all of the different
gates and/or buildings on our Park properties. He believes all gates at Marshall have the same
key. The Commission should have a master key set kept at the DPW.
Corinne Scouten is the only Commissioner that does not have a key to the DPW at this time.
Fuel Bill
The Commission was presented with a fuel bill. What is this bill for? Jack Rodriquenz will have
to be asked to explain.
Other
Mike Masciarelli reported that Rhonda Malatos, Phys. Ed. Teacher at the high school unofficially
approached him and would like to hold a Ping Pong Tournament and asked if the Park
===PAGE 3===
Commission would like to sponsor. Commissioners did not want to entertain this idea and will
no official request, no action was needed. Perhaps this is an activity the Teen Center could co-
sponsor with her and benefit them as well.
Adjourn
Motion to adjourn meeting was made and seconded. Vote in favor of adjournment 3-0.
Time adjourned was 8:04pm.
Minutes Accepted on: 2/26/15
Mike Masciarelli
Robert Robuccio
Corinne Scouten

Call to Order
Meeting convened at 7:02pm. In attendance were:
Commissioners: Bob Robuccio and Corinne Scouten
LYSA Representative: Stephanie Quinn (150 Houghton's Mill Rd, Lunenburg)
Review / Approve Minutes
Motion was made to approve minutes. Motion was seconded and minutes were approved 2-0.
Turf Fees/Usage
Following the emailing of the Turf Use bills to LYSA and the Bengals, both organizations replied
back questioning the bills and how the amounts were arrived at. Emails were received from
Amy Arpano/Bengals and Karin Menard/LYSA.
===PAGE 2===
Stephanie Quinn, in attendance at tonight's meeting was able to tell the commission that LYSA
had made their last payment, to and as per their agreement with LTAFA, in November of 2012.
Ms. Quinn wanted to know if we could provide a basis for the amounts we were charging
stating that they had no agreement with the Parks Commission that they were aware of.
Robuccio stated that we issued the bills based upon the best information we had regarding past
payment history and that the commission would be more than willing to have LYSA provide us
with an estimate of hours they felt they actually used the turf (they were bumped by both the
Bengals and the school multiple times) and then work out an agreement from there.
Bob Robuccio suggests that all three groups - Bengals, LYSA, and the school sit with the Park
Commission to talk about usage both past and future. Field availability will continue to be an
issue until the new school facility is complete.
Ms. Quinn also asked about availability of other fields around town - the outfield at Marshall?
What is available? Is the Park Commission thinking of finding any other field space which is
lacking? Park Commission response - at this time - no search is occurring and the outfield at
Marshall is unavailable in the spring due to heavy baseball use/schedule. LYSA is searching for
out of town fields to play on.
Bob Robuccio obtained several years of account printouts from the town accountant in order to
try to determine and track the payments received for turf use. Further clarity is needed to
understand many of the entries on these reports. Perhaps we can have Jack Rodriquenz or the
town accountant sit with us and go through the entries to explain.
Adjourn
Motion to adjourn meeting was made and seconded. Vote in favor of adjournment 2-0.
Time adjourned was 8:00pm.
Minutes Accepted on: 3/26/15

Call to Order
Meeting convened at 7:01pm. In attendance were:
Commissioners: Mike Masciarelli, Bob Robuccio and Corinne Scouten
DPW Director - Jack Rodriquenz and Director of Public Facilities at Lunenburg Public Schools -
John Londa
Review /Approve Minutes
Motion was made to approve minutes. Motion was seconded and minutes were approved 3-0.
Executive Session
DPW Director Jack Rodriquenz requested a short executive session regarding Marshall Parking
Lot issue. The three commissioners voted in favor of recessing into a short executive session
at 7:03pm. Mr. Londa excused himself during this time. Executive Session ended at 7:25pm
and Mr. Londa returned.
===PAGE 2===
Marshall Parking Lot
Mr. Rodriquenz presented updated plans for the parking lot project at Marshall. The commissioners
reviewed and vote in favor 3-0 to accept the plans as designed from Ducharme and Dillis.
Marshall Baseball Fields
Snow clearing took place at Marshall. Fields are almost ready. Adult coaches in charge can use their
own judgment as to when that is. Mr. Rodriquenz stated stone dust will be laid. Mike Masciarelli stated
the gate in center field (softball) is collapsed. DPW will fix.
Budgets
The commissioners requested a budget breakdown and explanation of expenses from DPW Director.
Mr. Rodriquenz will get this information to them.
Turf Fees
Mr. John Londa was present to aid in the discussion of turf fees. The electricity will be coming from the
new school once on line. This will save a separate and very expensive demand cost. Mr. Londa has
some historical data that he can pull to help the commission determine an electric charge for the fee
schedule. He hopes to see the tie in before April 7t. Mr. Londa will prepare a proposal of his thoughts
on how to set up the lights fees.
Tennis Camp
Mr. Londa asked about the tennis camp being offered again this summer. They state they are a Park
sponsored activity yet the website they are using does not show fees as being payable to the town. The
www.sites.google.com/site/youthtennisclub. Mr. Robuccio said the money in fact is
deposited through the town. The commissioners will follow up with Sandy Laserte and Justin Sparks
who run the camp.
Next Meetings
Suggest two meetings in April. April 15th - Gen Nass would like to come and discuss weed issue at Town
Beach. April 29th - Marshall Parking Lot Issue - invite all interested/involved parties.
Adjourn
Motion to adjourn meeting was made and seconded. Vote in favor of adjournment 3-0.
Time adjourned was 8:34pm.
