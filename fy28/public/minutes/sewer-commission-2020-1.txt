LUNENBURG BOARD OF HEALTH 
MEETING MINUTES 
January 6, 2020 
 
 
The Board of Health met at 7:00 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building 
 
Present: Perry Jewell, George Emond, Paul Fortin David Passios, and Warren Mayhew 
  
MINUTES APPROVED: 
 
December 16, 2019 
 
TITLE 5 INSPECTION REPORTS ACKNOWLEDGED: 
 
SEPTIC PERMITS SIGNED: 
 
322 FLAT HILL ROAD 
 
John Boardman, Engineer, met with the board on behalf of the owner.  This matter was continued from 
December 16, 2019 at the request of Mr. Boardman.  As a summary:  The owner is requesting a well 
variance from 50 feet to 14 feet.  The owner has a large parcel of land and is proposing dividing it into 
three lots.  One will contain the existing house and the two remaining lots will be to the rear of the 
property off of a common dr iveway.  The lot which has the original house on it is served by a private 
well.  Once subdivided, this well does not meet the offset requirement from the newly created common 
drive. 
 
George Emond stated that he does not believe that this is a variance tha t the board can grant.  The 
owner has created this situation by subdividing the property and believes that a new well will have to 
be drilled. 
 
Dave Passios stated that he agrees, the owner has created this hardship.  The board does grant 
variances in cases where there is no other alternative, but in this case it is possible to drill a new well 
and feels that a new well should be drilled. 
 
After discussion, and on motion by Passios and second by Emond the variance was not approved.  
Votes as follows: 
 
Mayhew: Approved 
Passios: Denied 
Emond: Denied 
Fortin:  Denied 
Jewell:  Denied 
 
Meeting adjourned at 8:15 pm

FY21 Budget discussion and approval- The Commission reviewed Barb's updated draft budget. Barb noted that
last quarter 29% of the customer base is billed the minimum charge of $97.20. Dave said that they all know they
need to raise rates, but asked if they would need to do it now or after town meeting? Commission discussed options
for the timing of the rate increase. John added that as a rate payer, he would be concerned if he only heard about an
increase at the town meeting. Mike suggested getting all the facts first before going to the town meeting. Dave said

down. The Commission discussed the Leominster rates and the IMA, which states that Lunenburg pays per the cubic
foot for what flows to Leominster. The Commission reviewed the figures for Leominster usage and the increase in
projected costs due to the IMA. Barb added that the Fitchburg rate will increase 8% each year. Carl stated that the
Leominster IMA was negotiated in 2004 and is due to be re-negotiated in 2024. The Commission discussed the
large increase in costs for the FY21 Small Water Services contract. Barb added that SWSS has a new billing and
tracking system, plus they lost a long-term employee. Carl pointed out that there are more call outs and system is not
as reliable as in the past. The Commission discussed clarifying the contract, writing a new RFP and looking for bids.
They will discuss the Small Water Services (SWSS) contract with Town Counsel. The Commission discussed the
increase in costs for generator maintenance. They also discussed having a separate line for capital expenditures and
figures for infrastructure costs for the pump station repairs. They further discussed the revenue lines in the budget,
including the Reserve Capacity Fund transfers, which will be used towards payments to Fitchburg for increased
capacity. Barb said that Town Counsel will come to the meeting on 2/11 to discuss the SWSS contract and SWSS
will come to the 2/25 meeting. All agreed that major parts should be a capital expense. Barb added that there has
been discussion with Flow Assessment regarding the telemetry of the meters and there may be a cost in upgrading
the flow meters. The Commission discussed other "Purchase of service" amounts, including legal costs, engineering
and the flow metering contract. They also discussed working directly with Weld for generator maintenance. Barb
added that they should start seeing results from the solar credits in April. She noted that in the FY20 budget, postage
was reduced in anticipation of outsourced bill printing, but she is waiting on that until the meter import project is
completed. The Commission discussed the figures for increases for Leominster usage. Carl questioned if the liability
insurance for pump stations is adequate as they are valued at $2.8 million. The Commission requested that Barb ask
Heather if the pump stations are adequately covered. Commission also discussed the shortfalls, potential rate

PUBLIC COMMENT-Dave announced that Jack Rodriquenz, DPW Manager, will be retiring and he thanked Jack for
all he has done for the town and the Sewer Commission. He added that Jack is a man of his word and he will be
missed
CURRENT BUSINESS -
RFP- Pumping station Operations & Maintenance-Barb had received the final RFP and has emailed it to the
Commissioners. The RFP was finalized with help of the Town Manager, Adam (Counsel) and Jack. Barb will be
sending it to the Central Register and Ledger. Julie (Assistant Town Manager) will send it to the state purchasing
program, CommBuys. The Commissioners were all in agreement with the RFP. Barb stated the schedule: 8/12 -
pre-proposal conference, 8/21- Proposals due, and 9/1- contract awarded. Dave added the next step will be the
interviews with responders.
Irrigation (Deduct) Meter Policy revision- Barb noted that they are changing the name to Deduct Meter Policy in
order to avoid confusion, as not all meters are for irrigation. She added that there was also a change to the section
on the fees, how they are assessed and what will happen if not paid. Also that the Building Department handles the
Plumbing permits. Barb said that the Commission needs to vote to approve the policy changes and they will hold 2
public hearings before it is officially accepted.
Motion: John R.
Second: Mike D.
To accept the Deduct Meter policy and credit program as updated on 7/28/20.
Vote: John-aye, Brett-aye, Mike-aye and Dave-aye
Barb added that she will put it on the agenda and post the change in the Lunenburg Ledger in advance of the
hearings to alert customers.
Bright Farms Project discussion- Barb reported that it is still in the planning stage and reviewed the project. It will
be a lettuce growing greenhouse business and will be located on the former Highfield Village property. Dave added
that they are still going through the approval process with other departments and they may request a special meeting
with the Sewer Commission, as they are on a tight schedule.
Grease Trap annual renewal- Barb mailed out the annual renewal letters. The Commission had agreed to waive the
fees, but the renewal application still needs to be sent in.
YTD FY20 Budget- Barb reported that the FY20 Budget is still not final because they have been asked to not
process invoices for encumbered purchase orders. Dave asked if the town budget has been affected by the Covid
crisis and Barb replied that mostly the invoices have been paid as normal. Dave added that the Commission will be
working on simplifying the budget for the future.
Minutes for 7/14/20 -
Motion: Mike D.
Second: Brett R.
To approve the minutes for 7/14/20.
Vote: Brett, Mike, Dave- aye. John - abstain.
Business Discussion- Counsel hours - Barb explained that the town appointed Mead, Talerman and Costa as
Town Counsel and Adam Costa provides counsel open hours. A Doodle poll is sent electronically each month for any
staff or elected commission member to sign up for a 20-minute session with Adam. Anything outside of the counsel
hours is billable. Dave added that Barb should be the point person and the Commission discussed how to use the
counsel hours. Barb added that they cannot go to counsel as a group but could have Adam come to a meeting for a
group discussion. Barb said she has gone to counsel hours in the past to get an opinion on processes and reports

looking at the I/l data. She added that the meter ids need to be entered first. Dave said that Jane can work with Barb
to get this done and the cost will need to be approved by Heather, but he did not think it was part of the contracts
scope of work. Barb clarified that it was something they asked Woodward & Curran (WaC) to do to correlate the
billing usage vs the water usage. Dave suggested sending Carl's recommendation to the Town Manager. The
Commission discussed the issue and asked Barb to send the scope and project out to the Commissioners. Barb

Member Agenda Inputs for Future Meetings- Dave encouraged all members to give their suggestions for future
