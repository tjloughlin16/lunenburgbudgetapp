TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
 
Lunenburg Historical Commission 
 
Meeting Minutes: 
October 4, 2016 
 
Members Present 
: 
Becky Lantry, Lois Lewis, Cullen Dwyer and Richard McGrath 
Amory Phelps has requested to withdraw as a member of the Historical Commission. 
 
Members Absent : 
None 
 
Call to Order :  
Meeting called to order at 6:40 p. m. 
 
Minutes Approval: 
Minutes were approved for September 6, 2016. 
 
Correspondence and Communications : 
There was no correspondence received. 
 
New Business/ Present Business  
Richard McGrath reported no new demolition delay requests have been received. 
 
Becky Lantry has written two letters to advise Michael Sauvageau, the building inspector, 
the properties at 203 West Townsend Road and 37 Burrage Road have not complied with 
the demolition delay plan. 
 
Both Cullen Dwyer and Becky Lantry will write letters to Jack  Rodriquenz, at the  
Department of Public Works,  to express the need for the care and maintenance of the 
Town Pound.  Of a particular concern is the amount of traffic in that area and the use of 
heavy trucks and equipment.  Richard McGrath will also write to Jack Rodriquenz, at the 
Public Works Department for the installation of a lock on the gate at the Town Pound. 
 
Cullen Dwyer suggested he will put notices on the older homes in the area for any 
interest in membership on the Historical Commission to fill the vacancy left by Amory 
Phelps. 
 
Meeting Adjourned : 
Meeting adjourned at 7:35 p.m. 
 
The next monthly meeting will be November 1, 2016 at 6:30 p.m. at the Ritter Building.

TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
 
Lunenburg Historical Commission 
 
Meeting Minutes: 
November 9, 2016 
Members Present 
: 
Becky Lantry, Lois Lewis, Cullen Dwyer and Richard McGrath 
Members Absent : 
None 
Call to Order :  
Meeting called to order at 6:30 p. m. 
Minutes Approval: 
Minutes were approved for October 4, 2016. 
Correspondence and Communications : 
There was no correspondence received. 
New Business/ Present Business 
A discussion ensued for historical markers to be placed on historical buildings in the 
village center district.  The Boy Scouts of America have expressed interest in this project. 
 
A request for the demolition of the property at 37 Burrage Street under the six month 
demolition delay plan, now over two hundred fifty three days, has been received.  The 
house has been sold, but not the barn. 
Voted:  The demolition request for 37 Burrage Street is approved. 
Richard McGrath will visit this property at Burrage Street, and take photographs prior to 
its demolition. 
Demolition Delay Requests have been filed for and do not apply to the following 
properties: 
1)  925 Chase Road demolition completed prior to a requ est for demolition 
delay review (not submitted to the Historical Commission). 
2)  675 Reservoir  Road 1957 
3)  9 Electric Avenue  1957 
4)  70 Oak Ridge Road 1948 
The building inspector, Michael Sauvageau, will be notified demolition delay review 
does not apply to these properties. 
Richard McGrath has suggested a tour of the three cemeteries, North, South and Fish 
Street, would be advisable to determine their present status. 
Lunenburg Ledger Historically Speaking  articles: 
 December Richard McGrath 
 January  Cullen Dwyer 
 February Becky Lantry 
 March  Lois Lewis 
Meeting Adjournment 
The meeting adjourned at 7:50 p.m. 
The next monthly meeting will be December 6, 2016 at 6:30 p.m. at the Ritter Building.

TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
 
Lunenburg Historical Commission 
 
Meeting Minutes: 
December 12, 2016 
 
Members Present 
: 
Becky Lantry, Lois Lewis and Richard McGrath 
 
Members Absent : 
Cullen Dwyer 
 
Call to Order :  
Meeting called to order at 7:15 p. m. 
 
Minutes Approval: 
Minutes were approved for November 9, 2016 as corrected. 
 
Correspondence and Communications : 
There was no correspondence received. 
 
New Business/ Present Business 
Lois Lewis will inquire from the Town Moderator, Attorney Timothy Murphy, about the 
Lunenburg Ledger  article for the Special Town Meeting, November 28, 2016, and the 
vote and amendment to limit the authority of the Board of Selectmen to determine the 
value of the Town Hall, Ritter Building and the Primary School (the old high school) 
building for the listing and sale of these town buildings. 
 
Becky Lantry will inquire why the property at 925 Chase Road was demolished prior to a 
review by the Historical Commission. 
 
Although a demolition delay request was filed for the property at 203 West Townsend 
Road the house and barn have been sold.  The new owner must now file another or 
second application to demolish the barn. 
 
Becky Lantry is preparing a notice for public dissemination to explain the demolition 
delay review plan for residential and commercial properties over seventy five years of 
age or possessing historical value. 
 
The Boy Scouts will publicize for the town residents the availability of historical plaques 
for older buildings.  Becky Lantry will order the requested plaques.  The Boy Scouts have 
offered to mount the plaques.  The Historical Commission will research the names, if 
requested, of the early or first owners of the buildings for inclusion on the plaques.
