Green Community Task Force Minutes 
February 6, 2012 
 
 Present:    Members    D. Blatt, J. McQuaid, E. Sund 
                  Advisors     M.Benson, J. Londa 
 
1. Joint Meeting with Lunenburg Planning Board called to order at 6:34 P.M. by T. Bakaysa    
      and D.Blatt. 
2. The Planning Board would like input from the Green Community Task Force regarding 
the Town solar bylaw. M. Benson provided a handout of the Model As-of Right Zoning Bylaw, 
which is the only current one from the State. 
T. Bakaysa said that the Planning Board met with Unitl last week regarding procedures for 
large-scale solar projects. They are only required to accept Net Metering up to 1% cap for 
residential and 2% for municipal projects. D.Blatt said that if more power is produced than net 
metering limits  the power can be wholesaled. 
 3.   Regarding the proposed solar field on the Youngs Road landfill D.Blatt noted that the GCTF 
       is gathering all the financial information and Unitil approval before presenting the project 
       for review to the PB. He suggested that if proposed large scale solar projects defined the scope      
       of their projects (size and interconnect application) prior to seeking approvals from town 
       officials that if would provide much needed information required to make informed decisions.  
4. T. Bakaysa asked D.Blatt for suggestions on setbacks for ground mounted solar installations. 
D. Blatt offered an idea of doubling the size of the array. E.Sund noted the difference between 
commercial and residential solar applications. T.Bakaysa questioned if the size of residential 
ground mount projects could be limited. 
5. M.Benson accepted a copy of the Pioneer Valley Planning Commission on Bylaw Writing  
submitted by a visitor. 
6. At 7:07 the Joint meeting with the Planning Board ended and the GCTF meeting  
continued. 
7. Solar Landfill Project: J. Londa said that Borrego has the contract back from Town Counsel    
and should hear from Unitil by 1/14/12 regarding the interconnect. On 10/18/12 the estimated  
cost to upgrade the lines was $200,000.00 but they will be giving a more accurate cost soon.  
The project size is still at 1.5 MW. The Development Plan Review needs to proceed through the 
Planning Board to the Building Inspector.  The landfill site includes both Commercial and 
Residential zones. 
8.   The THMS solar project has produced 4,000 kW of power in January. 
9.   D. Blatt will write a series of articles for the Ledger addressing “What is in it for you, the  
      taxpayer”.  They will start in late March. J. Londa stressed that there is indeed a financial  
      impact to the Town through savings in energy reduction and grants to achieve that goal. 
      An informational sheet on energy produced and savings realized from the THMS solar project     
      would reinforce that point. 
10.  J. Londa is working on compiling at list of suggested lighting upgrades on a town wide basis   
      that would be suitable for  Energy Performance Contracts to help reduce cost. 
11. The next meeting date was not established. 
     Meeting adjourned at 7:30 P.M. 
 
Respectfully Submitted, 
 
 
Joanne McQuaid

Green Community Task Force Minutes 
June 6, 2012 
 
 Present:    Members    D. Blatt, Z.Cutting, J. McQuaid, N.Sullivan 
                  Advisors     M.Benson 
 
1. Meeting called to order at 7:05 P.M. The minutes from 1/4/12 were approved. 
 
     2. Solar Landfill Project: J. Londa stated that Borrego is possibly reducing the size to 500 – 750    
   Kw because the original interconnect fee is too expensive making the project non-viable.  
Borrego is trying to get Unitil to discuss fees and procedures with National Grid and WMECO 
for price comparison. 
 
 3. Twin City Solar Project/EPG: M. Benson noted that they are not “grandfathered” under the 
new Solar By-Law. It was submitted as an ANR (Approval Not Required) land lease plan which 
protects it as a solar field. It currently has been submitted to Developmental Plan Review but 
they have not submitted a Zoning Application, which has related fees. 
 
4. Chase Road Solar Project: M.Benson noted that this is a land purchase, not a lease and 
has been ANR approval as a solar field but with new dimensions. 
 
5. Green Community Act: D. Blatt said that it was not on the Spring Town Meeting Town 
Warrant because there were too many ongoing issues. The informational articles will be 
republished before the Fall Town Meeting. 
 
6. Question: In what direction should the Green Community Task Force move? 
Need ideas for projects to increase revenue or cut cost with low cost to the Town. Need 
quick return on investment; small to medium projects 
Suggestions: Revenue from recycling. Westminster is making a million dollars on regional    
                     unit.  Maybe a Paper Retriever? 
                     Public Safety Building- Solar Hot Water 
                     Performance Contracts- still cost the Town money 
                     Re-Education- Solar has a bad reputation right now  
                     Revolving Account for the GCTF to keep gains realized for new projects 
                     Recruit new members 
 
7. Next meeting: Possibly in late July. Members will be sent an email to set the date. 
 
8. Meeting adjourned at 8:07 P.M. 
      
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Joanne McQuaid

Green Community Task Force Minutes 
July 18, 2012 
 
Present:  Members       D. Blatt, Z. Cutting, J. McQuaid 
               Advisors        J. Londa 
 
1. Meeting called to order at 7:03 P.M. The minutes from 6/6/12 were approved. 
 
2. Solar project updates: 
     Landfill: J. Londa met with Borrego and the Town Manager. The interconnect fees are still an 
issue as well as the means of connection. Unitil will do a study in August looking at different 
inverters and the scope of the project. The cost will be $12,000.00 payable by Borrego who has 
already invested one year of planning and $18,000.00. There is uncertainty whether the Planning 
Board will require the 200’ setback per the by-laws which would reduce the developable area 
significantly. Perhaps a variance would have to be requested. 
    EPG: They are interested in net metering the electricity to the Town. If 100% is sold to   
Lunenburg then Unitil will reimburse the distribution charge which could ultimately be paid by the 
rate payers. The discussion is ongoing with the Planning Board. 
     Mass PVI: In M.Benson’s absence no other members were aware of updates. However an 
observer at the meeting indicated that they think MassPVI has gone to the Attorney General 
claiming that the new by-laws were making the project unfeasible. 
   
 3. The Planning Board is requesting possible additional sites for renewable energy projects within 
the town. We should look for quick Return on Investment (ROI) projects D. Blatt will follow up 
with M. Benson. Unitil may already have a list for solar sites. Large scale wind does not seem 
feasible. Z.Cutting stated that energy conservation would probably be the best option. A solar hot 
water system for the Public Safety Building may not be a quick ROI but an option for savings. 
 
4. Town Buildings Update: J. Londa said that cardboard recyclers are being installed at the schools 
to generate revenue and it would be available to residents. Selective lighting changes at the 
Primary building are warranted as well as changing the high school lighting from T-12 bulbs to T-
8 because the T-12 bulbs are no longer available. Perhaps an Energy Savings Contract or 
assistance from Unitil would be possible. Z. Cutting indicated that the price of LED lighting 
should be dropping soon. D.Blatt wondered if Monty Tech students could assist with lighting 
upgrades for the Ritter Building. 
 
5. Other business: The Green Community Act may be on the Fall Special Town meeting. D. Blatt 
will verify with the Board of Selectmen. Is there a new Building Code coming in 2014? 
 
6. Next meeting TBA.  
    Meeting adjourned at 8:04 P.M. 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
Joanne McQuaid, Secretary
