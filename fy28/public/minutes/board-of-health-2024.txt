LUNENBURG BOARD OF HEALTH 
MEETING MINUTES 
Monday, May 6, 2024 
 
The Board of Health met at 7:00 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building  
 
Members Present:  George Emond, Perry Jewell, Paul Fortin and Jim Garreffi 
 
MINUTES APPROVED 
 
March 4, 2024 
 
135 HIGHLAND STREET  
 
This property was subject to a fire.  The owner is now in a position to rebuild this house.  The owner is 
proposing to keep the same room count but increase the size of the rooms, thereby increasing the square 
footage of the house. 
 
At the time the septic system was approved, there was a DEP policy in place that stated one could not 
increase the square foot of the dwelling with Class B soils.  That DEP policy has since expired.  After 
review by Nashoba, it was determined that a new septic tank would be required.  The septic tank has 
been applied through Nashoba. 
 
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Fortin, the Board voted to allow the increase of 
square footage in a property with Class B soils as long as the room count remains the same and that Class 
B soils are now considered pervious.  All in favor. 
 
747/757 CHASE ROAD 
 
Jason Cole and Frank McPartlan met with the board.  A new septic design as approved contingent on the 
“new” Zone 1 well being installed.  The well was begun 10 years ago but was not completed.  Mr. Cole 
and Mr. McPharlan would like to discuss the possibility of signing off on the COC for the septic system, 
even though it is contingent upon the new Zone 1 Well being completed. 
 
Mr. Cole stated that since 2020 they have been steadily doing work to bring the well on line.  They ran 
into issues with the fire suppression system among other problems. 
 
Jim Garreffi stated that he has no documentation of the status of the well or progress of the well 
completion.  Before determining whether a  sign off on the septic system could be considered, Mr. 
Garreffi would need to be in possession of those documents. 
 
On motion by Normandin and seconded by Fortin, the board voted to continue this matter until May 20, 
2024 at 7 pm to allow the parties to provide Mr. Garreffi with the necessary paperwork.  All in favor. 
 
 
SHADY POINT CAMPGROUND 
===PAGE 2===
 
The Board reviewed and unanimously approved the annual request for the use of portable facilities at 
Shady Point Campground. 
 
 
Having no further matters before the board and on motion by Emond and seconded by Fortin, the meeting 
was adjourned at 7:45 pm, all in favor.

142 WHITING  
 
Seth Mayer met with the board as the beneficiary of the Trust that holds this property.   
 
Sarah Mayer contacted the Board of Health for a housing inspection on the above property.  Agent Cara 
Olegarz and Bridget Brailey completed the inspection.  An Order to Repair was issued for numerous 
violations at the property.  To date, only a small number of those violations have been corrected. 
 
Mr. Mayer stated that he and his wife are in an acrimonious divorce situation.  By Order of the Probate 
Court Mrs. Mayer was giving possession of the Whiting Street property.   He further stated that Mrs. 
Mayer was to move to a different property and has not done so.  Mr. Mayer believes that Mrs. Mayer is 
responsible for part of the cost of repair.  It was explained to Mr. Mayer that the board cites the record 
owner, which is the trust.  Anything beyond that is a matter for the Probate Court. 
 
The board advised that they do not have any power to enforce an Order from the Probate Court.  That 
any issues concerning his divorce need to be resolved through the Probate Court.  The Order issued for 
this home is separate and apart from his divorce proceedings and the pending Board of Health violations 
need to be repaired. 
 
On motion by Jewell and seconded by Fortin, the board voted to grant an extension to complete the 
repairs at which time Mr. Mayer is required to be present .  This matter was continued until August 19, 
2024 at 7 pm.
