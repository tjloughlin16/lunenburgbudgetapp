Economic Development Meeting
July 28, 2022
Ritter Memorial Building 960 Massachusetts Ave. Lunenburg, MA 01462 
Call to order 6:05PM 
ROLL CALL
Cathy Clark (Economic Development Chair), Adam Burney (Land Use Director), Joeci Gilchrest (Lunenburg Business Association), Jean Dupre (Economic Development Member), Lou Franco (Select Board Clerk).
PUBLIC COMMENT	
Donnelly’s location is closing and there is concern that a cannabis shop is interested in the location. This will be the first cannabis shop opened in Lunenburg and the town is only allowing 2 licenses for cannabis shops. The main public concern is security, maintaining traffic flow, and worry due to number of locations popping up.  The public has been assured these issues have already been discussed. The town will also receive 3 percent of all revenue in tax money.

SURVEY RESPONSE UPDATE
Currently, 6 to 8 people have voted.  The survey needs to be re-circulated among town pages/websites. Joeci will discuss this with LBA at the upcoming meeting. 
Cathy inquiries about exit survey to be given to businesses leaving. Previously these surveys were mailed, in leger, and posted on town page.
BUSINESS UPDATES
A Rough draft of “How to Open a Business” guide has been made. Once completed these packets will be added to the town website. Anticipated to be completed by Adam by September.
Lunenburg Central Warehouse Project- 475 Leominster Shirley Road – most of the foundation has been laid and walls and roof to be built within the next month. Anticipate being open in upcoming spring. 
Parcel across the street from 475 Leominster Shirley Road is up for sale and has been getting significant interest in potential warehouse or distribution center.
El forno is replacing white tent with a building. This building will not be connected to preexisting building and there will be no occupancy increase. Anticipate new building to be up by next summer.
LUFA Farms -361 Mass Ave- company from Quebec whose mission is to grow food where people live and grow it more sustainably. They will work in partnership with local farmers and discussion about potential virtual farmer’s market with local farmers. The building is to consist of half hydroponic grow and half head house with a greenhouse on the roof. This is anticipated to bring 350 jobs. 
925 Massachusetts Ave – Town has a judgement from the foreclosure board and town now owns the property. EPA (Environmental Protection Agency) will clean up lot including asbestos abatement and then will be sold by the town.  
Old Primary School Building - 250 grand appropriated to finish abatement, planning, and demolish the building. Eventually, will be scrapped back into a hill. Timeline is fluid.
COMMITTEE DISCUSSION/CONCERNS
Cathy and other members want a Facebook business page for Economic Development. This will display general information and advertise open locations up for purchase. Adam will speak to the town manager/selectboard as there may be certain protocols/policies. 
Adam will add Lunenburg Business Association under key contacts in “How to Open a Business” guide. 
LBA (Lunenburg Business Association) is being requested to come to the august meeting. Joeci will invite LBA to the next meeting to discuss and outline areas of interest that we can do to help them and vice versa.   		
VII. MEETING SCHEDULE
August 25th at 6 PM.

VIII. PUBLIC COMMENT
Wendy Drennan from LBA wants to advertise the charity golf tournament for Boys and Girls Club on September 11th on the town page.  Adam will talk to town manager about publishing the event to Facebook. Member will reach out to Selectboard to have them publish the charity golf tournament under their announcement section. 

X. MOTION TO ADJOURN made at 7:30 pm by Jean Duprius, seconded by Joeci Gilchrest.

Economic Development Committee Meeting
August 25, 2022
Ritter Memorial Building960 Massachusetts Ave.Lunenburg, MA 01462 
Call to order at 6:08 PM 
ROLL CALL  - Cathy Clark (Economic Development Chair), Adam Burney (Land Use Director), Joeci Gilchrest (Lunenburg Business Association), Jean Dupre (Economic Development Member), Ana Lockwood (Parks Commission), Maryrae Holman (Agriculture Commission).

PUBLIC COMMENT
No comment.

POTENTIAL MEETING WITH THE LBA (Lunenburg Business Association)
Economic Committee and LBA are scheduled to meet on September 22nd at 4 pm.
Joeci shares the upcoming events hosted by the LBA.
1st Annual Charity Golf Tournament scheduled for September 11 from 1 pm-4 pm at Settlers Crossing Golf Course -994 Northfield Road.
Still looking for half-a-hole sponsorship, food truck vendors, and gift prizes/donations for raffles. All proceeds are going to the Lunenburg Boys and Girls Club.
Networking meeting to follow from 4 pm – 6 pm
3rd Annual Trunk or Treat scheduled for October 29th starting at noon at the Lunenburg High School 
Winter- Welcome to Lunenburg Document is anticipated to be released. This will list important phone numbers, businesses, resources, events, and attractions.
Discussion with Sue Doherty regarding re-branding of Equal House Team.
LBA wants to meet with the Parks and Recreation Commission regarding the Marshall Park Project.  Looking into potential sponsorship from local businesses to advertise at the park.

EDC MISSION STATEMENT 
Committee will be looking into other mission statements from local towns. The objective is to promote project goals, support growth, and the reasons why this committee was established.
BUSINESS UPDATES
LUFA Farms, 361 Mass Ave.
They are in the process of picking an engineering firm to do their design. Anticipate construction to start in the spring and be operational by 2025. 

Lunenburg Central, 475 Leominster Shirley Rd. 
Walls are up and they are working with the DPW to remove/replace signage on Shirley town line to Fort Pond Road. Stripping intersection at Fort Pond Road and Leominster Shirley Road. Currently don’t have tenants but numerous companies have been interested and in contact, but no leases signed. Anticipate being operational by spring of 2023. 
Uma Flowers- As of Monday, August 22nd the planning board has approved Uma Flowers. Anticipate being under construction in 60 days. They have a provisional license from the cannabis control commission and order conditions from conservation. Site plans have already been filed. The town of Lunenburg is capped at 2 cannabis companies.
The commission wants to promote and advertise the empty business spaces for sale/rent. 
- 5 Summer Street Plaza – a few spaces are vacant.
- 308 Mass Ave. – previously a Dollar Tree location.
- 50 Mass Ave. – previously Bootlegger restaurant.
- 165 Mass Ave. – previously Kabob-e-Licious- the property has been split between a house and a restaurant. The house portion has been sold.
- 88 Mass Ave. – a few spaces are vacant.
-1259 Mass Ave. – previously medical office.
- 466 Electric Ave. – previously coffee shop.
Cathy will bring a list of empty business spaces to the next meeting.
COMMITTEE DISCUSSION/CONCERNS
The committee is interested in making a Facebook page/group. Adam will research what the selectboard social medial policies are. 

MEETING SCHEDULE	
September 22nd at 4 pm – LBA will be in attendance.

PUBLIC COMMENT
LUFA Farm is interested in partaking in the farmer’s market before the end of the season. 

MOTION TO ADJOURN was made at 8:00 pm by Jean Dupre, seconded by Ana Lockwood.

Economic Development Committee Meeting Minutes
November 10, 2022
Ritter Memorial Building
960 Massachusetts Ave.
Lunenburg, MA 01462

Call to order at 6:05PM.
I. ROLL CALL
Matt Brenner (Planning Board), Adam Burney (Land Use Director), Cathy Clark (Economic Development Chair), Jeanne Dupre (Economic Development Member), Lou Franco (Select Board), Joeci Gilchrest (Lunenburg Business Association), Anna Lockwood (Open Space Representative).
II. PUBLIC COMMENT
There was a reminder of the upcoming town meeting on November 15th.
III. REVIEW OF MEETING WITH THE LBA
Joeci Gilchrest and Adam Burney met with Wendy Drennan from the Lunenburg Business Associates (LBA) in October. The LBA is looking to help connect businesses with assistance, support, and exposure to other businesses. These businesses are mainly small but are eager to work with the town and community to give back, especially since these local businesses revolve around the people who live and work in the community.  
A priority of the LBA is to create more community-focused events within the town as well as starting new events. The LBA is interested in re-starting the town fair with the help of the Lions club and town participation. Additionally, Anna Lockwood and the parks commission have shown interest in hosting events such as movies at Marshall Park. 
The LBA also wants to collaborate and provide material for the Information packet that is being created. 
IV. EDC MISSION STATEMENT 
Additional discussion about creating a town mission statement specifically for this group. Without the people, business owners don’t have anything and without businesses, we don’t have an economy. Cathy Clark proposed a drafted mission statement to “help facilitate economic growth in the community and provide resources and tools to promote future growth”. 
The goal of the mission statement must place emphasis on: 
Present and future business and growth within the town.
Facilitating economic growth in the community.
Attracting new business.
Provide, identify, and connect businesses with resources.
Facilitate mutual awareness of existing businesses and cross-pollination of businesses.

Cathy Clark will type up her statement and provide an email with a rough draft for members to review and add additional information.
V. PROPOSED ZONING CHANGE 199 
This location is formally the Chapdelaine auto dealership. A smaller portion of the land abutting the street is listed as commercial land. The remaining acreage is listed as residential. A citizen petition was presented at a town meeting to rezone the entire 16 acres as commercial land. There may potentially be a change of use or expansion of use for this location. The planning board is petitioning to change the use to a special permit. 
VI. BUSINESS UPDATES
A. LUFA Farms, 361 Mass Ave.
No additional updates besides the purchase and sales agreement have been signed.
B. Lunenburg Central, 475 Leominster Shirley Rd. 
A large building is in the process of being constructed. Currently, the use of the building has not been specified. There are roughly a half dozen residents looking into occupying this building. Once the building is weathered tight, they will start negotiating leases. The anticipated date of operation is April 2023. 
C. 88 Mass Ave.
The final approval and special permit have been completed by the planning board for their site plan. A special permit from the zoning board is for a shopping center.  In addition to the businesses already at this location, the new addition will be dividing the building into 2 to 3 uses. All the property line issues have been squared away.
VII. COMMITTEE DISCUSSION/CONCERNS
The primary school is potentially getting torn down starting in the spring. The bids have been awarded.   
Cathy Clark has created a Facebook page but has not been published. Adam Burney has researched and investigated social media policy and believes it should be okay if it’s informational and not editorial. Adam Burley will speak with Heather Lemieux to confirm endorsement. There will be restrictions on not letting the public post pictures and publish comments. 
Feedback on Business Development Guide 
There was a brief discussion about the Business Development Guide.  An email with a rough draft will be sent to board members to review and submit feedback to Adam Burney. 
VIII. MEETING SCHEDULE
The next meeting is scheduled for December 22nd at 6 PM.
Roy Nascimeto at December Meeting?    
Roy Nascimeto has reached out to Cathy Clark regarding attending the upcoming meeting. Roy is the head of the North Central Chamber of Commerce and has previously presented to the Lancaster Economic Development Committee.  Cathy Clark will email the economic development plan that was developed by the North Central Chamber of Commerce for members to review prior to meeting. 
IX. PUBLIC COMMENT
There was no public comment. 
X. ADJOURN
Motion to adjourn made at 7:40 PM by Anna Lockwood, seconded by Joeci Gilchrest.

The meeting opened at 5:3 4  PM .
1. Public comment
 There were no comments from the public.
2. Vote to approve minutes
 There was a motion to approve the minutes of January 13, 2026,  by  Jeanne 
Dupre  Dupre, seconded by Cathy Clark . Roll C all V ote: Joeci Gilchrest  aye, Jeanne 
Dupre  aye, Judy Dragoslis  aye, Cathy Clark aye .
3. Update from Planning Board & Director - bylaw  changes, etc.
Stephen Wallace confirmed that the Planning Board has approved 4 zoning 
amendments to be reviewed over the summer in anticipation of fall town meeting.
Ma tt Brenner shares that the Massachusetts legislators are reviewing Bill H  3680  
to extend commuter rail services to bring revenue from a hypothetical casino. The
location proposal considers a location in Fitchburg, Leominster, Ashburnham, 
Westminster or Lunenburg.  Due to this information, Stephen Wallace will be 
updat ing  the development constraints map  for the commercial and industrial 
districts.   This map will include all water and sewer lines. It will also show how 
much buildable land there is in the commercial and industrial districts.
4. EDC related  changes in Planning Office - notifications of new businesses, etc.
 Going forward, Stephen Wallace  will b e getting notification s from the Clerk ’s 
Office  when a business fills out a business certificate. This information will then 
be relayed to the Economic Development Committee.
There was discussion about the possibility of hosting ribbon cuttings. Stephen 
Wallace and Joeci Gilchrest will obtain additional  information from  Nashoba 
Valley Chamber of Commerce a nd the N orth Central Massachusetts  Chamber of 
Commerce .
===PAGE 2===
 Stephen Wallace confirms a warrant article has been put forward to create a 
revolving fund for the Economic Development  Committee . The article proposes a 
$3,000 budget.
O n April 21 at 6:30  PM there will be a public forum at the library to obtain 
community  input regarding the old gas station site located at 925 Mass Ave.
5. EDC ’s “wishlist ” in Planning Dept.
 Stephen Wallace has reviewed the business guidelines  created by the 
Committee and suggests trimming down th e document.
Cathy Clark suggests  adding information to the Economic Development 
Facebook page she created years ago. Cathy Clark will send an invite to Judy 
Dragoslis  and Joeci Gilchrest,  so they can  edit and create posts.
Stephen Wallace provided information about the SCORP program that 
businesses can utilize. It consists of retired business executives that can help put 
together a business plan.
6. Business Overview from Cathy Clark
 Ruby Donuts , Empire Athletics, and Busy B ee s Preschool  are now open for 
business.
The Freedom of Movement pilates  studio located in the village will be closing on 
May 1 st . Elena ’s  Gourmet Grocery , also located in the village, is still vacant and is now
listed on MLS.
Dunkin Donuts has pulled their application for the former Main Street Bank 
location.
7. LBA updates - 2026 Fair, LBA resource page on EDC web
 Joeci Gilchrest received permission from the Committee to share  LBA updates.
The Lunenburg Business Association has been sponsored by the Parks 
Department to partake in the 2 nd  annual Lunenburg Fair taking place on May 30 th  from 
11 AM- 5 PM.
8. Topics for next meeting
 - Review and approve minutes of February 10, 2026  
 - Information session regarding the old gas station in the center of town.
 - Ribbon cutting discussion 
- Updates regarding the Town Meet and Greet
9. Committee  comment
 There were no Committee comments.
10. Adjourn
 There was a motion to adjourn by Cathy Clark, seconded by Judy Dragoslis . Roll
