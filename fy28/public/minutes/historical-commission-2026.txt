Lunenburg  Historical  Commission   January  2026   The  Lunenburg  Historical  Commission  met  January  6th  at  the  Ritter  building  at  7:00  PM.  
Members
 
McGrath,
 
Lakso,
 
Sanderson,
 
and
 
Boussom
 
were
 
in
 
attendance.
 
 December  minutes  were  reviewed  and  accepted.   A  reply  was  received  from  Mike  Dickinson  of  the  Templeton  Historical  Society  regarding  the  
1776
 
Lunenburg
 
document,
 
and
 
it
 
will
 
be
 
donated
 
to
 
the
 
Historical
 
Society.
 
 Returning  to  the  Hometown  Heroes  project,  Jen  Sanderson  plans  on  choosing  a  banner  design  
which
 
will
 
be
 
used
 
for
 
the
 
first
 
10
 
banners
 
funded
 
by
 
the
 
commission.
 
After
 
those,
 
the
 
plan
 
is
 
to
 
pass
 
the
 
project
 
along
 
to
 
Veterans
 
Affairs,
 
which
 
will
 
continue
 
the
 
project.
 
 It  has  also  been  confirmed  that  68  Elm  street  is  in  fact  from  the  mid  18th  Century,  but  still  no  
response
 
has
 
been
 
had
 
from
 
the
 
current
 
owner.
 
 With  no  further  business,  the  meeting  adjourned  at  7:10  PM.

Lunenburg  Historical  Commission   March  2026   The  Lunenburg  Historical  commission  met  March  10th  at  the  Ritter  Building.  Members  Richard  
McGrath,
 
Bill
 
Lakso,
 
and
 
Kevin
 
Boussom
 
were
 
in
 
attendance.
 
The
 
meeting
 
began
 
at
 
7:00
 
PM.
 
 A  demo  permit  has  been  approved  for  922  Flat  Hill  Rd  after  it  was  found  to  have  no  historical  
significance.
 
 Todd  Holman  of  the  DPW  plans  to  contact  the  landowner  of  which  the  Arnold  Litch  cemetery  
resides,
 
in
 
hopes
 
that
 
we
 
can
 
come
 
to
 
an
 
agreement
 
to
 
preserve
 
the
 
site.
 
 There  are  also  plans  to  soon  begin  work  restoring  the  headstones  and  walls  at  the  Goodrich  
Cemetery,
 
as
 
well
 
as
 
addressing
 
the
 
table
 
stones
 
and
 
hanging
 
tree
 
limbs
 
at
 
the
 
South
 
Cemetery.
 
 DPW,  APDC,  and  parks  commission  need  to  be  contacted  to  address  the  deteriorating  walls  of  
the
 
town
 
pound.
 
 The  commission  also  plans  to  begin  work  on  the  historic  site  markers  this  spring,  so  we  will  work  
on
 
finalizing
 
a
 
list
 
of
 
the
 
most
 
important
 
sites.
 
 In  other  news,  the  MA  historic  commission  just  announced  the  32nd  annual  Mass  Preservation  
Project
 
Fund.
 
 With  no  further  business,  the  meeting  adjourned  at  7:17  PM

Lunenburg  Historical  Commission   April  2026   The  Lunenburg  Historical  commission  met  April  7th  at  the  Ritter  building.  The  meeting  began  at  
7:02
 
PM.
 
Members
 
McGrath,
 
Lakso
 
and
 
Boussom
 
were
 
in
 
attendance.
 
 An  update  from  the  building  inspector  regarding  763  MA  Ave  reports  that  the  building  is  under  
construction
 
to
 
be
 
converted
 
to
 
a
 
duplex.
 
This
 
will
 
be
 
followed
 
by
 
two
 
additional
 
townhouses
 
being
 
built
 
on
 
the
 
front
 
lot.
 
 Todd  Holman  of  the  DPW  reported  that  no  response  has  been  received  from  the  owner  of  the  
Arnold
 
Litch
 
property.
 
They
 
do
 
have
 
plans
 
to
 
begin
 
digging
 
for
 
the
 
wall
 
at
 
the
 
Goodrich
 
cemetery
 
starting
 
next
 
week.
 
 Two  items  of  question  on  the  Town  Survey  included  389  Sunnyhill  road,  which  was  highlighted  
as
 
370.
 
The
 
town
 
bandstand
 
was
 
also
 
reported
 
as
 
being
 
built
 
in
 
1905,
 
however
 
the
 
current
 
bandstand
 
is
 
not
 
the
 
original,
 
and
 
was
 
most
 
likely
 
built
 
much
 
later
 
on.
 
 Chairman  McGrath  donated  time  and  materials  towards  the  repair  of  the  Leominster  Road  stone  
directional
 
marker.
 
The
 
lettering
 
was
 
repainted,
 
brush
 
was
 
cleared,
 
and
 
new
 
crushed
 
gravel
 
was
 
laid
 
around
 
the
 
marker.
 
 A  final  list  of  important  historic  sites  to  be  marked  includes  the  Dickinson  House,  the  Houston  
House,
 
the
 
Buttonwood
 
tree,
 
and
 
the
 
site
 
of
 
the
 
mill
 
at
 
the
 
end
 
of
 
Holman
 
street
 
on
 
the
 
Mulpus
 
river.
 
 With  no  further  business,  the  meeting  adjourned  at  7:22  PM
