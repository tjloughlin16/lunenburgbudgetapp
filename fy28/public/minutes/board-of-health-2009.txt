OUTDOOR HYDRONIC HEATERS (WOOD FIRED OUTDOOR FURNACES)
The DEP has issued its regulations for Outdoor Hydronic Heaters. The members
reviewed the regulations and had the following comments:
A permit, either through the Board of Health or the Building Department is
required;
All currently existing furnaces must be identified as to size and location and
be provided with current regulations.
Chairman Emond will contact DEP to discuss these new regulations, the role of the local
Board of Health in enforcing these regulations and report back to the Board.
MEADOWOODS MOBILE HOME PARK
Subsequent to the recent ice storm portions of the Meadowoods Mobile Home Park were
again without water, primarily in the "lettered" portion of the park. Health Agent Jim
Garreffi was able to secure water and implement a plan to continue to provide water to
the residents of the park on a short-term basis. Chairman Emond spoke with Andrea
Lemerise at DEP who indicated that a public water hydrant would be hooked up to the
"lettered" portion of the park in the immediate future.
===PAGE 2===
WASTE WATER MANAGEMENT PLAN
The members of the Board again discussed the role that they believe they should play in
the final Waste Water Management Plan to be submitted for Town Meeting. After
discussion it was decided that the Board does not believe that the Final Waste Water
Management Plan should be at the sole discretion of the Sewer Commission and will be
attending the next meeting and/or workshop to discuss the major issues that the Board of
Health disagrees with.
FLOOR DRAIN REGULATION
The Board received a letter from the Lunenburg Water District requesting that the Board
consider the adoption of a floor drain regulation. After discussion and on motion by Shea
and second by Jewell the Board voted unanimously to reject the adoption of a floor drain
regulation.
Having no further business before the Board, the meeting was adjourned at 8:35 pm.

322 FLAT HILL ROAD - VARIANCE
Chris McKenzie met with the Board. Currently, this system is in failure. The system is a
two bedroom system. Due to the high ground water on this lot, a sieve analysis was

Mr. McKenzie stated that fill would be brought in and the system raised. The final grade

552 MASSACHUSETTS AVENUE - FAILED SYSTEM
Ms. Szocik, the owner, and Steve Marsden met with the Board. This is a rental property.
The tenant called to report a failed septic system. Agent Garreffi inspection the site and
was shown an area of discharge in the backyard. The tenant ran water in the house to
determine the severity of the failure and the water immediately discharged to the surface
as observed by Agent Garreffi.
Correspondence was sent on January 5, 2009, via certified mail, ordering the owner to
repair the system within 120 days. In addition, the owner must pump the tank and keep it
pumped until a new system has been installed.
The owner advised that the tenant has vacated the property and the tank has been
pumped. Ms. Szocik has retained Mr. Marsden to engineer a new system. After
discussion and on motion by Sund and second by Jewell, it was agreed that the section on
pumping the tank would be waived as the property is vacant. Further, there would be no
occupancy of the property until the new system is installed. All in favor.

intent of the Commission to use Title V volume restrictions (i.e. 110 gals/day
per bedroom) or the 57 gals. Per day also mentioned. Our concern it that
should a lot owner wish to construct a home of greater size when the lot could
support an adequate septic system, it would not be possible within the Sewer
District while it would be allowed outside the district where subsurface
disposal systems would be the norm.
2. Septage Management Plan - Our Board has stated that it would not consider a
Septage Management Plan until the CWMP has been completed and
approved.
===PAGE 2===

V and I/A systems cannot be implemented by the Sewer Commission. Boards
of Health are the only entities within cities and towns empowered to do so.

the Sewer Commission can exercise townwide after a sewer District has been
created. i.e raise fees or otherwise raise funds by modification of usage
amounts without oversight, restrict building, placement of treatment
facilities? With the current By-law as proposed, you are also regulating the
areas that are not in the district.

as determined in the past, have already been connected to sewer. Current
