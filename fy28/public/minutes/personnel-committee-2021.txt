Personnel Committee 
Minutes:  February 24, 2020 - Ritter Memorial Bldg. conference room 
Attendees: 
Scott Chase (Chair), Sue Andrews, Dave MacDonald, Erik Arnold 
Also Present: 
Heather Lemieux, Town Manager, Julie Belliveau, Asst. Town Manager/HR Director 
Jo  McLaughlin, Director, Local Public Access  
Sue Doherty, Director, Council on Aging

Old Business 
Minutes from January 27, 2020 reviewed  
Motion by D. MacDonald to accept the minutes presented; Second: E. Arnold 
Vote:  All in Favor 
Ongoing Business 
Job Description Review: 
Jo McLaughlin presented a revised job description for the LAT, Senior Videographer, as 
discussed in the January 27, 2020 meeting. Revisions included several additions outlining  
project leadership responsibilities, which better reflect this senior role. 
Motion:  D. MacDonald:  Approve SR Videographer role as submitted as a Grade 6 and submit 
for final approval at next appropriate Town Meeting.  2nd:  S. Andrews  Vote:  All in Favor 
New Business 
With the above completion of all LAT job descriptions, we are now able to complete the plan to 
transition of these contract employees into town employee roles.  Each employee will be 
placed in the appropriate SAP job role and put into the step closest to the current wage they 
receive, in order to put them appropriately in the SAP salary grid.  New Hire forms on each of 
these LAT employees were completed and signed by the committee members.   
New Hire Form - Dietary Aide - This role is a SAP Grade 2, and the initial steps are currently 
below the Massachusetts minimum wage.  As the committee has previously acknowledged that 
although a municipality may hire below that wage, they support Lunenburg having a practice of 
not employing anyone below the states minimum wage.   
Motion:  E. Arnold:  Approve the new hire as a Grade 2 step 5 in order to insure this hire would 
be above Mass Minimum Wage.  2nd:  S. Andrews  Vote:  All in Favor 
===PAGE 2===
Discussion: 
Principal Assessor - This role is now being outsourced and there is no need to change SAP role 
at the present time.   
Seasonal DPW Laborer/Truck Driver - Heather reported that, per this groups previous 
instructions to the Director of the DPW, he and Heather have reviewed the need to make a 
change to the current contracted work done by roles like this with the union representatives to 
insure they support changing this specific role to a town employee role.  The union understands 
the recent need to change the status and supported this change.  This impacts specific 
temporary roles like snow plowing, where previously non-town employees provided the 
temporary service utilizing town equipment.  The state mandates that utilizing town equipment 
can only be done by town employees.  A job description will be prepared and presented at next 
meeting to determine SAP Grade level. 
State Minimum Wage - Julie Belliveau presented several salary grade level alternatives that 
support all employees having a starting wage that is consistent with Massachusetts State 
Minimum Wage.  She included additional alternatives to change the current SAP which has 20 
grade levels, down to only 10 grade levels.  This would require some "banding" of current roles, 
but the committee agreed that it was a reasonable alternative to the current longer grid. More 
discussion will take place as she continues to refine the work. 
Position Rating Guide - Julie Belliveau also lead a discussion regarding a more formalized 
position rating guide to utilize for town roles. She provided an example of such a rating system.  
The committee will continue a review of this topic in future meetings as Julie explores the 
options available and considers the impact on our current town process. 
Next Meeting Target:   Monday, April 13, 2020 5:30pm 
Motion:  S. Andrews:  Adjourn meeting 6:30pm 
Second:  D. MacDonald   Vote:  All in Favor

Old Business 
 
SAP Longevity Amendment  
 
J. Belliveau prepared a spreadsheet that represented the impact this amendment would have on  
  town employees.  She explained the process and justification that supported the need of the 
amendment.  She outlined the positions impacted over time in the spreadsheet, comparing the 
potential change with both the Police/Fire Longevity Benefit and the Clerical Longevity Benefit as 
outlined in their respective contracts. The proposed amendment would change the benefit from a 
percent of salary to a flat rate. The Personnel Committee previously recommended approval of the 
changes, but because they received a request by town employees to re-examine the change, they 
requested a review of the impact this change would have on employees. 
 
A general discussion regarding all the elements of this potential change was reviewed.  It was 
clarified that two contracts had changed the longevity benefit from a percentage to a flat dollar 
amount during their last contract negotiations and that no employees were grandfathered into the 
old benefit.  Some employees in each of those groups may have experienced some decline in their 
annual longevity amount due to this change.  It was unclear what, if anything might have been 
===PAGE 2===
traded off for this change during those negotiations.  The SAP town employees are not represented 
under a contract, so they have no similar negotiation process. 
 
Two disputed areas were reviewed (1) should the Clerical Longevity Benefit be the comparison to 
the SAP Longevity Benefit or should it be the Police/Fire Longevity Benefit (2) should current 
SAP employees be grandfathered in the current Longevity Benefit and only new employees would 
be placed in the amended benefit. 
 
Several of the visiting attendees expressed their belief that the benefit should not be changed for 
current employees, and that the comparison to the Clerical Longevity Benefit was not a reasonable 
comparison, as some of the SAP jobs were a higher level of responsibility then clerical roles. The 
committee agreed that the department heads should be more closely compared to the Police/Fire 
longevity amounts which were approved at a higher benefit.   
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to grandfather current employees in existing SAP Longevity Benefit, with any 
new employees participating in the amended benefit, and that the amended benefit utilize the 
Police/Fire Longevity Benefit for SAP Department Heads and all remaining SAP employees 
receive the Clerical Longevity Benefit. 
 
Second: D. MacDonald 
 
Roll Call Vote:  Three in favor of motion; two not in favor of motion 
Motion Passes 
 
New Business 
 
Town Meeting Article 
 
The Article incorporates the addition, into the SAP, the following positions: Assistant to the Sewer 
Business Manager be added and assigned to Grade 8; the position of Intermittent Heavy 
Equipment Operator be added and assigned to Grade 9; Reserve Officer to be added and assigned 
to Grade 10; the position of Network Administrator be added and assigned to Grade 13; and to 
amend Chapter 70 Section 26, entitled “Longevity Pay”. This Article will be presented at the May 
Town Meeting. 
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to amend the article to reflect the Personnel Committees current 
recommendations as noted above. 
 
Roll Call Vote:  All in favor of motion 
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to adjourn the meeting at 6:00pm    
Second:  D. Fortune 
 
Roll Call Vote:  All in favor

Old Business 
 
SAP Longevity Amendment  
 
E. Arnold opened the meeting by informing the Committee that he attended the Select Board 
meeting on Tuesday, March 16, 2021 for the submission of the Personnel Committees Town 
Meeting Article. The Select Board suggested that the longevity amendment be put on hold until the 
Fall Town Meeting. The language of the amendment was “confusing”. The Article was written so 
that department heads would receive the amounts that mirror the Police and Fire Union amounts 
and any position that is not a department head position would receive longevity amounts mirroring 
the Clerical Union amounts. The question was asked what qualifies as a department head. There 
are positions that are management positions, but not necessarily department heads. 
 
E. Arnold suggested modifying the Article, rather than using the department head method to assign 
longevity amounts, go by grade level. For example, all employees in XX grade level and up will 
===PAGE 2===
receive the Police and Fire longevity rates, those below that grade level will receive the Clerical 
longevity rates. Employees hired under the current longevity language of the SAP will remain 
grandfathered for longevity. S. Andrews pointed out that the Personnel Committee may modify the 
SAP grid by reducing the number of pay grades and if the number of pay grades were reduced, this 
would affect the longevity rates if we were to use a grade level based longevity system. E. Arnold 
agreed that this process would take precedence. J. Belliveau also indicated that was a good point 
and supported the Select Boards request for the Committee to hold off until the Fall Town Meeting 
and continuing working on it, and that this meeting is to grant authority to a Personnel Committee 
member or herself to ask the Select Board to remove the longevity amendment of the Article.  
 
Motion by D. MacDonald to grant J. Belliveau the authority to request that the Select Board 
remove the longevity amendment from the Town Meeting Article. 
 
Second: J. Bilotta 
 
Roll Call Vote:  All in favor 
 
Motion by J. Bilotta to adjourn the meeting at 5:18pm 
Second:  S. Andrews 
 
Roll Call Vote:  All in favor
