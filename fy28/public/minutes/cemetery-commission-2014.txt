Toon of Zunenburg
Cemetery Commission
Lance 2. May
Daryl Demers
G. Stephen Schatt
RECEIVED & FILED
AUG 17 2015
LUNENBURG TOWN
CLERK OFFICE
Members present were Lance May and Steve Schaff. Daryl Demers was not present due to work commitments.
Greg Bingham, Supt of Cemetery's was also present.
Guests included the Supt of The Lunenburg Highway Dept, Jack Rodriquenz.
The 1st order of business was re-organization. Steve Schaff nominated Lance May to be chairman.
Lance May seconded the motion and a vote was taken to unanimously elect Lance May Chairman.
Lance May nominated Steve Schaff for Vice Chairman, Steve Schaff seconded the nomination and Steve Schaff was
unanimously elected Vice Chairman.
Daryl Demers was not present and prior to the meeting indicated he would not be attending and did not wish to be an
officer.
Under Public discussion, Jack Rodriquenz introduced himself to the Commission and sought cooperation between the
Commission and The Highway Dept. He also offered some suggestions to improve the landscape around the North
Cemetery by completing the access Road.
The Commissioner's thanked Jack for attending the meeting and his input and also wanted to offer their cooperation -as
well.
Discussion followed on equipment needs, where the budget $ is derived from, a 5 year capital plan, road paving, need 3
bids).
Greg brought up the concept of building a "Niche Wall"@ The North Cemetery and offered to comeback to the Committee
with some design plans.
The Commissioners agreed to review this plan at the next meeting. Steve will work with Greg
on this project.
No further business came before the Commission and there was no further public comment, a motion by Steve Schaff and
seconded by Lance May to adjourn was unanimous.
Time of adjournment was 11:00 am.
Respectfully submitted,
Steve Schaff.

The Kunenburg Cemetery Commission met on October 7, 2014 @ 5:30 pm @ the Cemetery Building on Holman
St.
Present were Cemetery Commissioners Lance May, Steve Schaff and Cemetery Supt, Greg Bingham.
Commissioner Daryl Demers was not presentoue to work conflicts from new job.
Guests included the Chr of the Kunenburg Mistorical Commission, Reberca Kantry and members of the Kunenburg
Historical Commission.
The Cemetery Commissioners opened the meeting with Steve Schaff making a motion to do so and Lance may
seconded his motion to meet with members of the Lunenburg Historical Commission and tour the Holman Street
Property-as a part of a Site visit to determine the structural integrity of the building along with the building's historical
significance in Lunenburg.
After the tour was completed, The Cemetery Commissioners, Lance May and Steve Schaff along with the Supt of
Cemetery's, Greg Bingham followed the Historical Commission members back to the Ritter Building to attend a
hearing with The Historical Commission @
6:30 pm on the Holman Street property. Commissioner Daryl Demers did not attend the hearing.
After attending hearing and having no further business before the commission and with no public comment,
Commissioner Steve Schaff moved for adjournment and Commisioner Lance May seconded the motion, the motion
passed unanimously to adjourn.
Time of adjournment was 8:30 pm.
Respectfully submitted,
Steve Schaff.

G. Stephen Schaff
The Kunenbburg Cemetery Commission met on Mouember 18, 2014 @ 7:00 pm @The Kurenburg
Public Library with Commissioners Lance May and Stebe Schaff present. Supt of Cemetery's Greg Wingham was
present and Commissioner Daryl Demers did not attend due to work conflicts.
Commissioner Schaff made a motion to open the meeting with Commissioner Lance May seconding his motion. The vote carried
to open the meeting
No Public Discussion took place.
No minutes were available for discussion.
The Meeting came to order with discussion taking place on the crack filling of road ways in the
North and South Cemetery and reviewing 3 bids. Black Dawg Of NH submitted a bid for $3,300.00, Prescott Landscaping
submitted a bid for $3,503.80 and Walts Drive Way submitted a bid for $2,800.00. Steve Schaff made a motion to award the bid
to Walts Drive Way for $2,800.00, Lance May seconded his motion and with no further discussion, the vote was unanimous.
Discussion on the hearing results from the Lunenburg Historical Commission on the Holman Street Property took place along
with a clean-up of the property. Greg was to follow-up on the property's clean-up.
Discussion followed on the current condition of the Ferris lawnmower and Greg's suggestion to update
this piece of equipment at some point. He will follow up with Shepard's Service and speak to Glen Shepherd on the pricing-as the
state bidding procedures would have to be followed.
The plans and building of a niche wall was discussed by Greg -as he has contacted Monty Tech to assist with the construction.
Proposed costs were discussed for material and labor. But, with-out knowing how much Monty Tech's commitment would be,
this would have to be discussed at another meeting. A time table to begin the project should be established once, we know of
Monty Tech's Time Line for availability. Greg is to send a letter to Monty Tech to get their commitment to do the project and then
follow-up with Barbara Reynolds, the Lunenburg Representative to the Monty Tech School Board.
Greg is also to follow-up on excavating bids that will be required to pour a footing.
Greg requested that the commission look the current fee structure for week-end or overtime burials
-as he felt it should increase. He suggested the fee should be increased from $300 to $500.00-as other
area cemetery's had increased their fees.
Steve Schaff made a motion to increase the week-end /overtime fee from $300.00 to $500.00. Lance May seconded the motion
and with no further discussion the motion to increase this fee was passed unanimously.
No further business came before the commission and there was no further public comment
Steve Schaff made a motion to adjourn and Lance May seconded... with no further discussion, an unanimous motion carried.
Time of adjournment was 10:00 pm.
Respectfully submitted,
Steve Schaff
