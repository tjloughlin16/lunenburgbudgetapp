Senior Citizen Property Tax Work-off Program Committee
Minutes, October 28, 2013
Present: Doreen Noble, David Manzello, Kathy Herrick, and Shelley McCaie. Absent: Jean Larkin.
Doreen Noble opened the meeting at 2:30 p.m.
• The members in attendance reviewed the time slips submitted by the department heads that worked with
the candidates in the program. One candidate did not submit a time sheet and has stated to be excused
from the program in the future.
• The members in attendance discussed proposed sites and jobs for next year. Doreen Noble mentioned
that the school Superintendent was still interested in getting help in the cafeteria. The school was also
looking for assistance in the Special Education department. Doreen felt that a person working in the
Special Education department should have appropriate training before working there. All members in
attendance agreed. Kathy Herrick also reminded the committee that the purpose of the work the
candidates should be doing is helping departments with busy work that is above and beyond what the
department can handle not to replace paid positions. David Manzello made a motion that both jobs the
school was looking for help with do not qualify for work under this committee, Kathy Herrick seconded.
On vote, 4 in favor, 0 opposed.
• The number of available slots for next year's work-off program remains to be seen. David Manzello
suggested that the department heads should submit in writing what hours they have available to commit
to the program. It is important to make sure there are enough hours available to fulfill the time the
candidates need to work.
• Suggestions were made on how to better promote the program. Shelley McCaie suggested the
possibility of the Lunenburg Ledger interviewing one of the candidates so they could share their
experience. David Manzello suggested that an ad be placed on the local access channel. Kathy Herrick
suggested maybe the Public Access Cable Committee could video a worker and highlight their
experience.
• The next meeting will be on Tuesday, November 5, 2013 at 10:00 a.m. in the Clerk's Office located at
the Town Hall, 17 Main St., Lunenburg, MA.
• Kathy Herrick moved to adjourn the meeting. David Manzello seconded. On vote 4 in favor, 0
opposed.
Meeting adjourned at 3:02 p.m.
RECEIVED & FILED
Respectfully Submitted
Shelley McCaie, Committee Member
Senior Citizen Property Tax Work-off Committee

Senior Citizen Property Tax Work-off Program Committee
Minutes, January 27, 2014
Present: Doreen Noble, Jean Larkin, Kathy Herrick, David Manzello and Shelley McCaie.
Doreen Noble opened the meeting at 2:30 p.m.
• 6 applications were reviewed. The committee approved all 6 applications. There are 9 slots remaining
in the program.
• Doreen asked if one of the committee members could reach out to Department Heads to determine if
there are any new requests for proposed jobs. Doreen was contacted by Amanda at the DPW/Sewer
department; she stated they have a need for someone on a regular basis. Kathy Herrick will send an
email to all departments requesting they submit their requests by 2/6/14. This will help the committee
determine how many applicants can be approved based on the number of jobs.
• Jean Larkin made a motion to allow Doreen Noble to approve prior applicants without the approval of'
all committee members. New applicants would have to be reviewed by all committee members, Kathy
Herrick seconded. On vote 5 in favor, 0 opposed.
• Jean Larkin made a motion to increase the tax work-off deduction from $750.00 to $1000.00 to be voted
on at Town Meeting. Kathy Herrick seconded. Doreen Noble will prepare an article for Town Meeting.
On vote 5 in favor, O opposed.
• The committee discussed how to better promote the program. Jean Larkin mentioned running an ad in
the Lunenburg Ledger or having a table set up at Town Meeting. Kathy Herrick suggested an interview
with some of the committee members on the local access channel. Doreen Noble is going to look into
running an ad or getting an interview.
• The next meeting will be on Monday, March 3, 2014 at 2:00 p.m. at the Eagle House located at 25
Memorial Dr., Lunenburg, MA.
• Kathy Herrick moved to adjourn the meeting. Shelley McCaie seconded. On vote 5 in favor, O
opposed.
Meeting adjourned at 3:08 p.m.

MINUTES OF MEETING – NOVEMBER 15, 2016 
Meeting called to order by Sue Doherty at 2:00PM, at Lunenburg Town Hall, 17 Main St. 
Members present: Sue Doherty, Nancy Forest, & Myleen Mallari. 
Not present: Sheila Craigan 
MINUTES APPROVAL 
A motion was made and seconded to approve the minutes of the June 23, 2016 meeting.  All approved, motion carried. 
CURRENT BUSINESS 
1. Discussion criteria for 2017 applications and programs:  Members discussed the proposed 2017 Property Tax Work-
Off application criteria and agreed that before the applications are made public, Ms. Doherty will check with tax 
professionals to ensure that the criteria is correct.  Applications should be available to the public on 12/1/2016 and the 
deadline for return will be 1/20/2017. 
Town Departments have been petitioned for participation in the 2017 program.  As of the date of this meeting, there 
have been nine departments that have shown interest. 
2. Discussion increase of property tax -work off abatement: Ms. Doherty received an email from the Executive Office of 
Elder Affairs stating that the state has increased the maximum abatement from $1,000 to $1,500 in the Property Tax 
Work-Off program .  After discussion the board determined that each municipality sets their own abatement amount 
and decided to leave the amount at $1,000 for calendar year 2017.   
3. Review returned calendar year 2016 time sheets:  All 2016 have been returned and the total amount to be charged 
for the FY17 allowance for abatement/exemptions is $8,372.50 
4.  Discussion Property Tax Work-Off board vacancy:  Ms. Doherty will make a request to the Board of Selectmen that 
an announcement is made at each of their televised meetings regarding the vacancy on the Property Tax Work-Off 
committee for one member at large.  Preferably a candidate with tax knowledge will come forward. 
5. Set date for next meeting: To be determined. 
NOTICES & COMMUNICATIONS:  None 
BOARD COMMENT: None 
PUBLIC COMMENT: None 
ADJOURNMENT:   A motion was made and seconded to adjourn the meeting.  All approved, motion carried. 
Meeting adjourned at 2:28 PM 
Respectfully Submitted, Sue Doherty
