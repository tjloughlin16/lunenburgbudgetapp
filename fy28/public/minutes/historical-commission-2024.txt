 Minutes of Meeting 
Thursday, January 4, 2024 @ 7:00 pm 
Ritter Memorial Building located at 960 Massachusetts Ave, Lunenburg, MA 01462 
 
 
- call to order 7 pm 
- McGrath, Lakso, Sanderson, and Lordan in attendance 
- secretary not in attendance so minutes from Dec 7, 2023 unavailable 
- Cemetery Commission requests Chari McGrath be in attendance at their February 15, 2024 meeting to 
discuss North and South cemeteries historic status, grants, and subsidies available for necessary 
preservation and upgrade work 
- McGrath brought up a discussion about how the Community Preservation Act could help fund 
necessary preservation work and upkeep at the cemeteries as well as for other town projects. The CPA 
could be used, not only by the Cemetery Commission, but also by the Historical Commission and the 
Architectural Preservation District Commission, which have voted to advocate for the Town’s adoption 
of the CPA.  
- Lakso reported that the application to the Massachusetts Historical Commission for a grant for the 
townwide survey of the existing houses on the 1833 Cyrus Kilburn Map was progressing and would be 
ready for submittal soon. 
- Sanderson reported she was working on getting all the details for the Home Town Heroes Banner 
program in line. Meetings would be upcoming with the Town manager and Unitil.  
- McGrath reported that the partially built Arnold/Lynch Memorial on West St Terrace would be 
dismantled and protected until it could be reerected on the exact cemetery plot where the burials have 
been found. 
- McGrath discussed the use of the Historical Markers as an important educational tool to draw 
attention to important Town sites to promote public recognition of the value of these places and events 
to the quality of the Town’s fabric. 
- The Commission reiterated its commitment for the use of its budget to initiate the Home Town Heroes 
Banner project, as well as for the Historical Markers outside the APDC. 

===PAGE 2===
APDC Update 
- Sanderson presented the Home Town Heroes Banner program to the APDC.  
- McGrath spoke to the APDC in favor of the CPA. He outlined how it could offer funding for 
preservation of town buildings and other projects in the APDC. 
 
- next meeting Feb 8, 2024, at 7 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building. 
- Adjournment 7:24 pm

Proper notice having been posted,  the Commission held a special meeting to discuss an Open Meeting Law

3:00 p.m. April 22, 2024 at the headquarters of the Lunenburg Historical Society, Inc., 10 School Street, 
Lunenburg, Massachusetts.  Present were Mr. McGrath, C. William Lakso, Jennifer L. Sanderson,  and Megan 
Lordan.  Absent was Kevin Boussom .  Attorney
Steven Chaplin represented Town Counsel.
Chairman McGrath stated that discussing the complaint in open session would have a detrimental effect on the 
commission.  On motion regularly made by Ms. Sanderson  and seconded by Ms. Lordan,  the Commission 
unanimously voted to meet in executive session. 
Mr. McGrath declared the executive session open at 3:05 p.m.
Mr. McGrath closed the executive session at 3:45 p.m. p.m. and declared the meeting open .
On motion regularly made by Mr.  Lakso and seconded by Ms. Sanderson , the Commission unanimously voted: 
To approve the  response to Mr. Menard as prepared by Town Counsel, with copies distributed as required by 
law.
Mention was made relative to a second complaint, filed by Karin Menard, but not received by the Commission 
in time to be included on the agenda for this meeting, and that a second meeting will be needed.
Attorney Chaplin  recommended that members of the Commission review and participate in Open Meeting Law 
training.  He further recommended the Commission not use abbreviations in agendas, and expand on detail of 
listed items.

North Cemetery, other cemeteries in town, as well as throughout Massachusetts. McGrath commented on his 
experience with towns and viewing projects that other towns have adopted and received funding from the 
Community Preservation Act. He pointed out that cemeteries in Medford, Boxboro, Somerville, and Stoneham 
have received money for wall repair and restoration, as well as gravestone and general cemetery restoration, 
survey, and documentation.
