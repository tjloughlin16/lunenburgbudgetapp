MEETING MINUTES 
May 16, 2018 
7:00pm 
DPW 
520 Chase Road 
Lunenburg, MA 01462 
1) Dennis Mannone called the meeting to order at 7:03pm. Attendance: Karin Menard, Dennis Mannone 
and DPW Director Jack Rodriquenz. 
2) Karin Menard motioned to accept the minutes of April 11, 2018 - approved 2-0. 
3) DPW Director / Town Manager Updates -  
Jack quickly went through a list of items that he and Karin had discussed early in the week. One of those 
items was the playground area at the beach. Dennis is concerned that the ground surface doesn’t meet 
state requirements for use as a playground. Karin suggested picnic tables for families to have lunch at 
and maybe incorporate some sort of shade structure. Dennis though those were good ideas and we can 
add it to our master plan as we create it. 
Jack updated the commission that the first accident damage to the fence at the Town Beach has been re-
paired. He mentioned that as far as he knew this was an uninsured driver. The town inquired to their in-
surance company and he said they don’t cover this type of damage. Karin suggested he ask his adminis-
trative assistant to track down the name of the driver and send a bill. The damage from the second acci-
dent is in process of being repaired. 
Jack also mentioned that Chestnut Street parking still remains a concern during ball games. Parking is 
only allowed on one side but it is not a wide road so it easily bottlenecks causing concern that emer-
gency vehicles would not be able to get through. Jack also mentioned that he had sent an email earlier in 
the year to the Police Chief, Fire Chief and Town Manager offering solutions but he got no response. 
Dennis thinks the concern is big enough to continue having discussions with the Fire Chief and Police 
Chief. Karin mentioned that the commission has already talked to the groups using the fields and re-
quested that their players and spectators use the main lot at Marshall. 

===PAGE 2===
Dennis Mannone motioned that we minimize street side parking on both sides during ball games and to 
encourage people to park off of 2a in the main parking lot due to concerns over public safety access on 
chestnut street. Approved 2-0. 
4) Town Beach - General 2018 Discussion - Karin met with Adam Burney to discuss getting set up on 
the online payment system to accept online registrations for beach passes. Adam was going to do some 
leg work on his side and then reach back out to her. 
5) Wallis Park Playground - Karin updated the commission that there would be a ribbon cutting on 
Tuesday at 7:15am. The playground build was completed on May 5 and the only current concern is the 
smoking by visitors on and near the kiln dried wood chips that were just spread. She reported that tem-
porary no smoking signs were put up last week but we may need to work on better signage if the prob-
lem continues. 
6) Whalom Luxury Apartments - Playground/Land Update/Discussion/V ote - Gary Lorden’s office pro-
vided a sketch and plans for the playground they would like to install at 250 Whalom Road as part of an 
agreement they have with the Zoning Board of Appeals. The playground would be installed on a .78 acre 
piece of property that would be donated to the town. Dennis expressed concern that this is being forced 
on the Park Commission. Karin agreed saying she had mentioned to Garry Rhodes, when they met a few 
weeks ago, that agreements that involve other boards should be discussed with them prior to approval. 
Both Karin and Dennis felt something different may have been done if they knew another playground 
was going in 2 blocks away from Wallis Park. Karin said the Z.B.A. was looking for the Park Commis-
sion to give their approval for the playground and location. Dennis said that he would need to recuse 
himself from a vote as he works for a company that does work for Benjamin Builders. Karin said she 
would go back to Garry Rhodes and see how he would like to proceed since it appears that only two 
members of the commission are currently active. Dave Passios asked why the Bus Shelter was being put 
on land being donated to the town. Karin explained that both the bus shelter and the playground were 
required to be put on the piece of property being donated by the ZBA. 
7) Spring 2018 Baseball Field Usage- Karin mentioned that the Little League payment did not include 
tee ball plays and that she has no notes of discussing waiving those player fees for the 2018 season. 
Dennis suggested Karin follow up with them to see and see what they say. Karin also noted that we had 
not heard any more from Dan Proctor about using the 90’ diamond at Marshall Park this summer but she 
would try and find his email to reach out to him. 
8) General Parks - Request by Dave Passios to discuss questions / concerns raised at Annual Town Meet-
ing - Dave Passios was in attendance to discuss questions and concerns raised at the Annual Town Meet-
ing. His research showed that there has been a difference of over 139k  between what was budgeted and 
what was spent over the last 5 or so years. He mentioned that it was not appropriate to take money from 
one line and move it to another when the fields need work. He showed pictures of the bleachers at Mar-
shall Park that were in a state of disrepair that could have easily have been taken care of with that mon-
ey. Karin agreed and pointed out that although the grass seems to be adequately cared for there are other 
things that need to be addressed such as fencing, removal of old equipment like the skate ramp and some 
repairs to the Beach house. Jack gave a bit more background and provided an overview of the organiza-
tional chart for the DPW. Jack said with him overseeing 5 of the 6 areas (exception was Cemetery divi-
sion) and the passing of the Pavement Management Program there was only so much of him to go 
around. Karin said she felt strongly that the commission needs to make their needs known to the Town 
===PAGE 3===
Manager in hopes of brining in someone to oversee the day to day park needs. Dennis said we can’t 
wade through the past and we need to come up with the big picture and also be getting regular budget 
updates. 
9) Discussion on long term plan / ideas / capital items for Parks - Both Karin and Dennis agree that we 
do not have enough field space. Dave Passios asked how many outside groups use the fields and if there 
was conflicts. Karin said the main user of the baseball fields was the Lunenburg Little League but the 
High School also uses the fields for the JV and Varsity Softball/Baseball teams and that there were a few 
smaller groups that also used them. She mentioned that there were currently no conflicts since everyone 
has been able to work together on their schedules but that other sports groups in town who used the high 
school fields were not always able to get the field time they needed due to the high number of kids in-
volved in high school sports. She said that although giving baseball and softball programs a place to 
play, it would be nice to be a bit more well rounded and include field space for other sports. Dennis 
though a feasibility study or survey to get more information would be beneficial. Jack reminded the 
commission that years ago a resident in town was interested in a land swap and we could look further 
into that. 
10) Other Business 
       a. Unanticipated items - none  
      b. Correspondence, Announcements and Public Comment - none 
11) Karin motioned and Dennis seconded to adjourn at 9:10pm. Approved 2-0

Meeting Minutes
June 27, 2018
6:00pm
DPW
520 Chase Rd.
Lunenburg, MA
1) Dennis Mannone called the meeting to order at 6:00pm. Attendance: Karin Menard, Dennis Mannone and Jack 
Rodriquenz. Absent: Pete McCauliff
2) Dennis Mannone motioned to approve minute from May 16, 2018. Karin Menard seconded. Approved 2-0. Minutes 
from June 19th, 2018 were not ready.
3) Reorganization of Committee - Dennis Mannone will remain as Chair, Karin Menard will remain as clerk.
4) DPW Director / Town Manager Updates 
1) Adult Fitness - Karin provided information from Heather Lemieux about an adult ﬁtness facility. Karin was 
concerned about the fundraising that would need to be done above and beyond what would need to be 
contributed by the town. Dennis felt as though there were currently more pressing things. Karin agreed. 
Will add to the list of ideas for future discussion.
5) Request from Pastor Rodriguez of the Seventh Day Adventist Church to perform a baptism at the Town Beach - Karin 
advised the commission that Town Manager had received a request from the Seventh Day Advent Church to use the 
town beach for a baptism. They are requesting access for a 1 hour block of time when the beach is not opened and  
they have signed the needed release of liability form with the town.
Karin Motioned to notify BOS that there are no conﬂicts that we are aware of and ok the use of the beach 
for this purpose. Dennis Mannone seconded. Approved 2-0.
6) Spring / Summer 2018
1) Central Mass Youth Basketball has 2 teams (5th and 6th grade boys) playing this year. Karin recommended 
charging the same $10 fee per player that is currently being charged to other youth groups using Park 
facilities. 
Karin motioned and Dennis seconded to charge Central Mass Youth Basketball teams $10 per player for 
use of Wallis Park. Approved 2-0.
2) Karin updated the commission that the Phillies and Little League have paid their Summer 2018 user fees. 
Outstanding is payment from Life Long Learners, Central Mass Youth Baseball, and Central Mass Youth 
Basketball. Karin will ask Samantha Tucker, Admin for DPW, to send out bills.

===PAGE 2===
7)Town Beach - General policy, procedure and summer 2018 discussion and votes.
1)Jack concerned with the beach being open during inclement weather with few, if any, pass holders using it. 
Karin expressed concern about the budget and didn’t think it made sense to keep the beach open when the 
weather questionable if the beach and that closing when it was raining and there were no patrons would 
help to close the budget gap. Karin and Dennis both agreed that the beach should be closed during 
inclement weather. Jack will send an email to the beach coordinator.
8)Request from Main Street Bank for “We Love Lunenburg” event at Town Beach - Karin received an email from Main 
Street Bank looking to do some sort of shaved ice or ice cream truck event at the Town Beach on one of the swim   
lesson days as a way to give back to Lunenburg. Dennis had no concern. Karin will let her contact with Main Street 
Bank know we thought it was a great plan.
9)Wallis Park - Karin advised the commission that she had received a call from Think Tank, a company on the 
Lunenburg / Fitchburg line, inquiring about the condition of the hoops at Wallis. They felt that the court was an 
important community facility and gets a lot of use. Think Tank asked about whether a sponsorship program was in 
place and Karin had told him there wasn’t but would talk to the commission. Karin had reached out to the Town 
Manager to see if the town had a sponsorship policy before tonights meeting and Heather had replied via email that 
there was not one. Jack recommended we ask Think Tank to give him a call or to come in and discuss their thoughts 
and give more information on what they would like to see done. Both Dennis and Karin agreed that would be a good 
idea. Karin will email Think Tank and ask them to contact Jack.
10) 250 Whalom Rd. - Benjamin Builders - Z.B.A. agreement update. - Karin updated the commission that she attended a 
Z.B.A. meeting at their request. Benjamin Builders had a plan in hand but the Z.B.A. felt that things were being rushed 
and they weren’t ready to commit to that plan. In order to not hold up the occupancy permit, they decided to have 
Benjamin Builders put the funds into an escrow account with the town. Jack will reach out and get more information.
11) Open Space Ad Hoc Committee - Open Space requested Parks delegate a representative.
 Karin motioned to designate Dennis with Karin as the alternate. Dennis seconded. Approved 2-0.
12) Long Term Vision - Jack suggested either together or separately. Dennis to send over dates that work for him for a 
walking tour. Karin will post if a majority of commissioners will be present.
13) Capital Plan - Commission decided they will discuss after a walking tour.
14) Other business matters that may legally come before the board not known at the time of posting - none
15) Correspondence, Announcement and Public and Board Comment - no public comment
16) Next meeting: July 26, 2018 6pm @ DPW
17) Karin motioned to adjourn and Dennis seconded to adjourn at 7:10 pm. Approved 2-0.
