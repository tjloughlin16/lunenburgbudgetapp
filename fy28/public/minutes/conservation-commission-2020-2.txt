location was optimized to allow DPW and police vehicle traffic. KJ feels this is important to move on as 
rapidly as possible to get the tanks out of the ground. The above ground tanks area a better alternative 
and more readily observable and that the area proposed was the only area that it could be safely 
completed. He feels there would be more safeguards in place.  CL noted that while he supports JR’s 
comments he also understands the constraints to the town that KJ outlined. He also noted that his
