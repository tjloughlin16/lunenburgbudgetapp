Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: January 11, 2011
The meeting opened at 6:00 pm. Present: Chris Comeau - Chairman, Board Members
Matthew J. Papini, Sr., Louis J. Franco, Harald Scheid - Regional Assessor, and Louise
Paquette- Administrative Assistant.
Board reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, abatements, warrants & minutes.
Louise reported that the bills have been mailed for the Actual FY 11 RE & PP. The committed
amount for Real Estate is $17,551,928.72 and Personal Property is $454,884.48. Louise also
reported that there were 4 omitted Real Estate bills totally an additional $55.628.79. The
commitment and warrant for these omitted bills were also reviewed and signed. An Omitted
& Revised Assessment Report will need to be submitted for this Fiscal 2011 year no later than
June 20th
Applications are now available for abatements. Louise stated that she has not received many
inquiries compared to former years. The abatement filing deadline is Tuesday, Feb. 1, 2011.
Harald informed the Board that David has created a question & answer sheet called "Things to
Consider before Applying for a Real Estate Abatement" which is available in the office and is
available along with the applications on the Town's website.
One Elderly 41C exemption and two Veteran exemptions were reviewed and approved totally
$1,550.00. The Board re-reviewed an application from an applicant who e-mailed Louise
contesting the denial of his exemption. The Board voted to uphold their previous decision
unless the applicant can provide the necessary supporting documentation as requested.
Louise reported that the applicants (jointly owned) are aware of the of the Boards' request.
Two applications (one former applicant & one new applicant) for Clause 41A Tax Deferrals
(Financial Hardship / Elderly Persons) were reviewed and approved upon. Louise will submit
the Statement of Entry into Tax Deferral and Recovery Agreement (State Tax Form 97-2/99-2)
to the Registry of Deeds for recording.
A proposed Memorandum of the FY12 Assessors' Budget was received on December 30, 2010.
Harald reported that he & Louise have reviewed the budget and stated that a meeting has
been set up with Kerry on the 24*
. The budget proposal comes in at about a 3.3% increase
over last year (due to salary & CAMA membership increases).
Harald spoke about the GIS (Global Informational System) software that will become
available to the public soon. It will be installed and maintained by Cartographic Associates,
Inc. Louise stated that the contract was signed on December 16, 2010. She reported that she
along with David and Nancy Strom (ITT) met with "Cartographics" to see a demonstration of
===PAGE 2===
the software program. This mapping tool uses relational database technology to integrate
data and has the capability to access many sources of information from within one program.
Harald spoke to the Board how efficiently it can generate an abutters list, create property
and topography maps and is capable of many other functions. A training session will be
offered to all departments once it is up and running.
Louise informed the Board that renovations have been started in the Town Hall (monies were
approved at the Special Town Meeting held in December). It appears that the plan shows the
Assessors' Office to remain where it is, however, the plan shows that the area will be
enlarged due to the change in the infrastructure of the bathrooms and taking down the wall.
We will gain another few feet of space. Louise stated that she was told that we probably will
be the last office renovated.

beginning April 7' through May 12'h
". Matt & Lou stated that they are interested in attending

Louise will post notice in three places in Town.

Louise reminded the BOA that the Town Meeting will be held this Sat., May 7, 2011 at the HS at 9 am.
The election will be held May 21, 2011. Louis Franco is the only candidate running in the election for the
BOA is in receipt of 5 ABC applications (Religious & Charitable Organizations) seeking property tax
exemptions. Harald presented all the applications and the Board voted unanimously in favor of granting a
full exemption for all but one of the applications. The Board reviewed the application for the United Parish
Assembly, Inc., located at 39 Main Street. BOA reviewed and discussed the guidelines of the DOR:
Taxpayers Guide to Local Property Exemptions For Religious and Charitable Organizations to determine
if it meets the specific eligibility criteria and qualifications. Under M.G.L. c.59 s.5., Clauses 10 & 11, it
states that the religious organization exemption is impacted if "any part of the property regularly leased
or occupied for any purposes is taxable"
. Statute M.G.L. c.59, s. 5, Clause 3, states that, "all other real
property owned by a charitable organization is taxable. This includes property (a) leased to individuals
and non-taxable entities. , (b) occupied or used for non-charitable purposes",
, etc.
After reviewing the application, Harald informed the Board that the United Parish leases the lower level
of the property to a "for profit" child daycare center. Chris informed the Board that the daycare center
which is leased by a private person, occupies the property five full days a week. With much discussion,
the Board voted that the property does not qualify for a full tax exemption. Motion was made to grant a
partial exemption and Board voted unanimously in favor to grant a partial exemption. Harald will notify all
the organizations in writing of the Assessors' decisions.
BOA reviewed and signed Commitment /Warrant for FY 2011 RE Supplemental Billing. Louise informed
the Board that the bills will be mailed on Friday, May 6*
". The 15 property bills compiled by RRG,
warranted an additional tax due to the fact that there was a 50% or more increase when comparing
additional value to total valuation of land /bldg. of prior bill. The total supplemental tax amount is
$23,634.77. Louise stated she informed Kerry of the additional commitment amount.
Harald spoke briefly on the growth updates on Emerald Place and Lunenburg Village. Emerald Place
continues to develop; they are presently moving forward in building more "garden style condo-
apartments". To our knowledge, there have not been any properties sold. Harald stated that they are
offering them leased to own. To date, Lunenburg Village (Whalom area has yet to break ground but all
the foundation permits have been pulled.
Louise reported that she has electronically submitted the final Statutory Exemption report to the state.
The Board reviewed and signed the "hard" copy and will be filed. Total exemptions reported to the state
are $95,611.76 which includes two tax deferrals. Louise stated that the report does not take into account
the tax work off program which totaled: $10,500.00 in exemptions.

property affect the grade. Mr. Blatt responded that the property has not changed. David stated that the
property record was inaccurate not reflecting the true value of the property. After a lengthy discussion,
the Board offered several recommendations:
1. Property owner needs to set up an appointment to have the Assessors re-inspect the property (last
inspection was in 2007).
2. Property owner needs to check the square footage and compare it to the square footage listed on the
property record card.
3. Property owner should have a market analysis / appraisal of the home.

Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: December 6, 2011
The meeting opened at 6:00 pm.
Present: Board Members: Louis J. Franco & Matthew J. Papini, Sr., Associate Assessor, David Manzello;
and Louise Paquette, Administrative Assistant. Chris Comeau, Chairman not present.
Board reviewed and approved abatements, expense vouchers, commitments, warrants, and minutes from
the meeting held on November 1, 2011
Louise presented 11 statutory exemption applications. The Board reviewed and approved 11 applications
totally $11,300. The Board reviewed and approved 13 Tax Work-Off exemptions in the amount of
$8,766.00.
Special Town Meeting was held last night and Matt gave a brief synopsis of the meeting. He stated that
there was much discussion on the article pertaining to adopting the stretch energy code. The article was
presented by the Green Communities Task Force. He reported that the article did not pass at Town
David spoke regarding the 2012 recap and tax rate. He reported that the tax rate is close to being
finalized at $16.83 per thousand. He stated that there is approximately $448,000 of new growth due to
the Emerald Place Development and Tri-Town Landing apartments. He reported $18,865,867.25 in levy
for FY12. David explained all the recap forms / reports required by DOR. The BOA reviewed and signed
all the documentation.
Louise reported on the billing process. She stated that a newer version of Munis has been installed and it
has been challenging.
There have been some changes to the software and it has been necessary on
occasion to request support from Munis. She reported that the bill process will need to be completed and
turned over to the Collector by 12/16.
David suggested to the Board that they meet later in January due to the filing of abatement applications.
He explained that taxpayers have until 2/01/2012 to file their applications. The Board approved the
recommendation and voted to meet on Tuesday, January 24, 2012 at 6:00pm.
Meeting adjourned at 7:10 pm
