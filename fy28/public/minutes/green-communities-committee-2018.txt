John DiNapoli presented information regarding converting lights to LED, and/or purchasing the
streetlights. Using tariff information, they can project cost savings for LED conversion. The
town would be responsible for unappreciated costs of the assets, costs of removing the old
lights and installing new lights, but not the cost of the bulb itself. We would also need to
estimate costs of police details for the work. John reports it would take 3-4 weeks to analyze
the data. This is an informal inquiry, the Town Manager would need to email John to advise if
we are purchasing or staying with unitil to provide maintenance of the lights. It was noted these
lights are not kept in stock and we need to consider time to order the LED lights. If we want to
use a bulb not on Unitil supply list, we would need to provide info to Unitil so engineers could
review specifications. A member of the public asked if conversion could be phased in based on
age of the bulbs; with oldest first. J. L mentioned this will not allow us to reap the most savings
as quickly as possible, a consideration for the grant. The Town Manager will send the request to
Unitil to perform the tariff investigation/audit.
The proposed project spreadsheet was reviewed next. The Turkey Hill envelope project
proposal was handed out by J.L., the return on investment for this is 9.6 years. The Turkey Hill
lighting project ROl is 4.7 years with the rebate. John Londa will oversee the school projects.
The project team for non-school projects will be Heather Lemieux and Jack Rodriquenz. The
expectation is to complete projects by the end of the year. Karen noted that the grant allows for
administrative costs of up to 10% for project management expenses.

Minutes for May 10, 2018
Present: David Blatt- Chair, Neal Sullivan, Liz Murphy, John Londa
Meeting was Called to Order at 6:30 PM.
One member of the public, David Passios, commented that if the town has a reasonable option
to go with separate company for the maintenance of street lamps, he would encourage this.
Documents from Unitil regarding street light LED conversion were presented and reviewed.
Documents present an approximate $30,000 annual savings with a 2.5 year payback of the
investment.
J.L. presented the options as he sees them:
1. Do nothing.
2. Convert to LED with Unitil maintaining lights, save on the energy costs.
3. Purchase the lights, convert ourselves, save on the maintenance and energy costs.
For reference, Tanko gace an estimate of $156,000 to convert the street lights-just the head,
not the pole-plus $300 per light when the head needs replacement.
If we let Unitil replace, we would save on those costs, as they own the fixture/pole. Uniti's
proposed changes & charges include maintenance costs.
Tanko's maintenance costs were $4000 changing bulbs, and on call service but doesn't include
the cost of the bulbs. Could we keep bulbs in stock and find a lower cost company to provide
on-call maintenance?
J.L suggested we might be missing a cost- should there be another charge to hand some of the
owner of the pole, (AT&T?)?
N.S expressed concern about the maintenance contract and J.L recommended putting all of the
numbers together on a spreadsheet to provide a detailed comparison for Selectmen to use
when making a decision. J.L also suggested the selectmen will want to understand the
feasibility of the service contract. D.B stated he will create the spreadsheet and include
assumptions such as number of hours lights are on and noting the estimates coming from Unitil.
J.L. suggested talking to the town manager about options for funding.

Solutions, the Onics energy filter and smart LEDs. Showed a video of the filter on a Leany
Clinic project. According to the presentation, the filter can reduce power usage by 15-19%. The
