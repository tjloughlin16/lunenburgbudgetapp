Public Comment: John Fortune of Hickory Hills Landowners Inc. States his presence in regards to the 
previous concerns of the well use controlled by LWD. BP recuses himself from this discussion and MW is 
to act as Chair. JF stated he was out at the location to take water samples as well as take pictures. JF 
continued to share updated photos of the site to the commission. Photos consisted of hose placement, 
haybale placement, and possible erosion. 
 
Mark Bursch, Chairman of the Board of Water Commissioners, is present to represent LWD in regards to 
this matter. MB states that previously the superintendent of LWD and the Conservation Administrator at 
the time would meet and discuss the pumping of the well without having to file. MB also states that this 
pumping has been continuous throughout the year with the approval from the Conservation Admin, and 
also have tested the Lake water and well water. MB exclaims that this proposal of pumping does not allow 
their engineer firm to file anything with the Commission along with being a costly task. MB asks to 
continue past practices with the engineers, LWD superintendent, and Conservation Admin without having 
to file.  
RB asks to confirm that the LWD does not have accurate tests of the well water versus the Lake. 
MB confirms, only accuracy is manganese and iron. MB also stated that the levels of these substances 
tested to be higher in the Lake itself compared to the well water.  
RB asks if these tests results can be presented? 
MB confirms that the results can be provided to the Conservation Admin and asks welcomes JF to include 
his test findings.  
RB asks if the commission or admin have yet to receive any form of proposals for the well pumping 
activity? 
MB says there has never been any form of proposals submitted in the past and has usually always been in 
contact with the Conservation Admin and follow through with the recommendations made. MB continues

RB addresses MW in regards to seeing no problem with fund submittals for an RDA. RB also states that the

Paula Bertram presented a comment that due to the duration of the upcoming project with Tighe & Bond, 
the recommendation of a filing is appropriate and LWD should be required just as any other public entity 
would.  
MB asks the commission, if an RDA submittal is required, to waive the abutter's notification due to the 
number of abutters and the expense.  
RT asks if the positioning of the haybales from the picture presentation was intentional? RT also asks if the

MB replies that haybales should've been connected, though seem to be moved. MB also answers the second

NEW PUBLIC HEARINGS 
Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal 
Bylaws a public hearing on a Notice of Intent by Nancy Malady for the replacement of one lakefront retaining 
wall and two non-lakefront retaining walls, the removal of an existing deck, and a 386 square foot addition to 
a single-family residential structure at 406 Sunset Lane (DEP #208-1284). 
 
BP states that this submittal has yet to have an issued DEP # as well as DEP comments. BP makes a standard 
statement that applicants forward a cancelled check to the DEP to expedite the issuance of a DEP with 
comments. 
 
Homeowners are present as well as Greg Roy of Dillis & Roy to represent them.  GR states that the property 
owners are planning on this lot to be their permanent residence. GR explains the layout of the house and that 
the homeowners intend on expanding the living space of the existing home, though using the existing deck as 
the footprint for the addition. The overall footprint will stay the same. The other proposed plans are to replace 
the existing rail tie retaining walls with natural stone walls. Erosion control description on plans is to show 
phases of work; Phase 1 (house) Phase II (retaining walls). GR points out the number of trees that are going to 
be removed that are in close proximity to the deck/proposed addition.

JV asked if the Title 5 was passed once sold and the number of bedrooms in the home. 
 
GR states that it did pass Title 5 and is just one bedroom. 
 
JV asks if the construction of the wall will be done house-side or lake-side? 
 
GR states that they can get this answer from Brett Ramsden, who will be installing the wall.  
 
JV suggests replanting the trees, as well as expresses his agreement upon the addition as well as replacing the 
retaining walls. 
 
BP informs the commission that he had received a chat comment from Brett Ramsden at the time and confirms 
that the replacement of the wall will be done land-side. 
 
RB wants confirmation if there is to be a proposed basement level for the addition. 
 
GR says that the homeowners would like to have a basement level. 
 
GR describes the layout of the deck and porch, and which portions will be removed and kept.

GR states that there is an opportunity for that.

BP restates the suggested changes to be done for next continued hearing being: plan showing that the wall will 
be natural stone rather than block, proposal of dry wells, replanting plan and number of trees taken down, and 
construction schedule. 
 
JV suggests editing in possible plans for dock removal/replacements. 
 
Nancy Malady, homeowner, states that they will not be removing docks, though repair one of the docks.

Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal 
Bylaws a public hearing on a Notice of Intent by Matthew Proctor for retaining wall replacement at 189 
Hemlock Drive (DEP #208-1282). 
Matthew Protector to represent himself. MP explains that the existing timber wall will be removed and 
replaced with a fieldstone wall, in which the existing wall will be pushed back away from the waterline. 
 
AP provides pictures from the site as well as site drawings.   
 
MW asks if any trees are going to be removed? 
 
MP responds that there is no intention of removing trees closer to the water line. MP states that there is one 
tree that is directly behind the retaining wall that could possibly be damaged or cause a problem. MP says that 
the tree might have to be removed if needed but the homeowners wish to save it if possible.  
 
MW states they she would like to see the tree replaced if it has to be removed. 
 
RB asks if the sketch depicts the existing wall being 15 feet from the water’s edge? RB also asks that the wall 
is 27 inches tall and 30 inches added which creates 57 inches above the grade below the front wall? 
 
MP Confirms. 
 
RB asks if the new wall will be the closest to the lake and will be approximately 48 inches? 
 
MP says that will be the targeted height.  
 
RB says typically wall replacements are wall for wall, and the only possible problem is if the T-wall was going 
to be any closer to the water line compared to the current face wall.  
 
MP states that it was not be any closer and do not plan on changing the grading outside the scope of work.  
 
RB asks about erosion control? 
 
MP would like partial beach access and have silt fence at the high-water mark as long as the lake stays drawn 
down.  
 
RB asks when work will begin? 
 
MP hopes to start this winter, and once permitted will have project completed in approximately 2 weeks.  
 
RT asks how the timbers will be disposed of? 
 
MP states that they will be disposed of properly at a dump site facility outside of town. 
 
RT asks if the one tree is to be removed, will it be replaced? 
 
===PAGE 3===
   
 
   
 
MP confirms and will be open to suggestions for trees that are native.  
 
ML asks what the diameter of the tree is? 
 
MP is unsure. AP believes it could around 6 inches in diameter. 
 
ML asks to include the diameter of the tree; if the tree needs to come down and if under 12 inches 1 for 1 rule 
for replanting, and if over 2 for 1 rule.  
 
RB asks if the frost is set in the ground? 
 
MP says it hasn’t frozen due to conditions of this season and does not completely freeze. 
 
RB is concerned for equipment movement and rutting in the soil close to the water mark. RB also states 
another line of silt fence should be installed for if the water level rises. RB says moving forward, better 
documentation and plans should be submitted. 
 
JV asks for the current state of the water level at the lake? 
 
MP states lowered 18 inches, low enough for access.  
 
MW asks how far away the existing front wall is from the water? 
 
MP does not have exact number.  
 
MW asks if AP can retrieve measurements that was inquired. 
 
RB/JV M/S to approve with a standard Order of Conditions, special conditions being: Tie-in wall no 
closer to the waterfront, the stop work with equipment when soil rutting happens and repair ruts, 
erosion control with no erosion into lake, tree that is at risk to be replaced with native species for 1 for 1 
if under 12 inches, 2 for 1 if over 12 inches, new wall will not exceed 48 inches in height, notify the 
Administrator when working below water line as well as erosion control in place. Roll Call vote, 
unanimous to approve.
