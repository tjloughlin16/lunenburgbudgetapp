CABLE ADVISORY COMMITTEE
MINUTES
MAY 5, 2015
BILOTTA MEETING ROOM, TOWN HALL
Those present: Cable Advisory Members: Steve Walker, Rhonda Lisio, Jamie Toale, Tom Alonzo,
Paula Bertram
Others present: Jo McLaughlin, Advisory Member; Kerry Speidel, Town Manager, Elaine Peterson
Bill Hewig, Kopelman & Paige, Kathy Herrick, Town Clerk
Those not present: Steve Malandrinos, Advisory Member
Meeting was called to order by Ms. Herrick at 6:00 p.m. Ms. Herrick welcomed all attending and
requested nominations for officers. Ms. Herrick exited the meeting.

To appoint Mr. Alonzo as Chairman
Motion: J. Toale
To appoint Steve Walker as Vice Chairman
2'': R. Lisio
Vote: All in Favor
2nd. R. Lisio
Vote: All in Favor
Mr. Hewig entered the meeting and all present were introduced to him. Mr. Hewig passed out handouts
on the negotiation and ascertainment process for the cable license renewal. The cable license expires in
September 2017. Mr. Hewig stated negotiations are basically for funding. Programming and rates are not
negotiable; Programming is determined by the market.
The license can be denied for two reasons:
1) The provider is not in compliance with the current license after notice and opportunity to cure and
2) The provider has not met reasonable community demands from the town.
One goal of this committee will be to ascertain what the town needs over the next ten years. Requests
should be "cable-related, "such as equipment, a remote drop site, studio etc.
Mr. Hewig asked the committee to consider if an educational channel is desirable as the town does not
have one currently. Does the committee want and educational channel based at the new school? This
could be a training opportunity for students as well as a center for community volunteerism/events.
Mr. Hewig advised conducting a survey of subscribers to hear what they want. He presented several
samples from other towns. He also advised contacting department heads, committees and boards, and
community organizations etc. to see what their cable-related needs are. He encouraged the committee to
invite the public to their meetings to get feedback. Main concerns to keep in mind are customer service
and signal quality.
In summary, the committee will need to hold one public hearing at some point, determine what the town
needs will be going forward over the next ten years, and present a business plan with a proposed annual
budget for negotiating purposes.

CABLE ADVISORY COMMITTEE MINUTES
November 30, 2015
The Cable Advisory Committee met in the Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room as scheduled with Tom Alonzo, Paula Bertram
and Steve Walker present.
PUBLIC COMMENT
None
ANNOUNCEMENTS
Chairman noted with regret the resignation of Rhonda Lisio.
MINUTES/CORRESPONDENCES
1. Preview Questionnaire Responses from Town/School - Committee reviewed municipal responses from Adam
Burney, Planning Director; Jack Rodriquenz, DPW Director, Susan Doherty, Council on Aging Director; Patrick
Sullivan, Fire Chief; Mike Sauvageau, Building Official; Victoria Whipple, Middle School Library Media Specialist and
Jennifer Biery, LHS Field Hockey Coach. The Committee noted the following recommendations/needs based on
responses received:
a. A wider range of meeting rooms wired for sound and video.
b. Equipment to allow for participation by committee members remotely.
c. The ability to broadcast public informational sessions by various departments.
d. Mobile equipment/capability.
e. Portable wireless microphones.
2. Review Town wide Survey Draft - Committee reviewed draft survey created on Survey Monkey. Steve Walker
suggested modifications to the age brackets in question 1. Questions 7-10 were modified to include a reference to
programming on the public access channel.
The Committee voted unanimously to release the survey with minor modifications. A link to the survey will be
available online and printed surveys will be available at the Town Hall, Library and Senior Center. Tom Alonzo will
check with Kerry about including either a reference to the survey or the actual survey in the tax bills. Citizens will
also be notified of the survey via the Lunenburg Ledger and the Scroll on the public access channel.
Survey responses will be collected for 30 days following distribution.
3. Review PACC budget Updates - Steve Walker informed the Committee that PACC is working on the budget and
business plan and hope to have a draft in February.

subscribers and non-subscribers of cable services) to share their
experiences, assessments and perspectives of the past 10 years of cable

vision for improvements, modifications and/or additions of that service
going forward.

important to your department. To this end, we ask everyone receiving

department head about them. /*Most importantly, we ask that you email

with Comcast.

Rhonda Lisio stated that the comments made by Paula Bertram relative to the inability to broadcast live trom locations
other than Town Hall were incorrect. Steve Walker indicated that currently PACC is able to broadcast from other
locations using vpn, this functionality has been tested in the high school auditorium but has not been in tested other
locations as of yet.
Minutes/Correspondence
Steve motioned to approve the minutes of 12/18/17 with the addition of adding Paula being present. Paula seconded.
Motion passed unanimously.
Current Business
Discuss Comcast contract proposal - Tom indicated we would be discussing the two side letters received from Comcast
and their proposed counter offer.
Bill Hewig had sent the committee's counter offer via email to Greg Franks of Comcast (minutes of 12/18/17).
Comcast's counter offer is to:
(1) Pay 50% of our $170K capital in year 1, and the balance 2 or 3 years later;
RECEIVED & FILED
(2) Construct a fiber video return line to the High School, or a digital upgrade, but not both;
(3) Convert the Leominster channel to Lunenburg E channel; and
MAR 27 2018
(4) Give a Standard Senior Citizen Discount side letter [see attached sample letter]
MINENRURG TOWN
CLERK OFFICE
The Committee questioned what the proposed digital upgrade is? Mr. Hewig was not aware what Comcast was
referencing, a call was made to Steve Malandrinos who indicated he was not aware what was included in a digital
upgrade. Mr. Malandrinos added that currently the upload speeds at the high school excellent, he is not sure we need
the video return line. The Committee asked Mr. Malandrinos about upload speeds at the Ritter, Library, and Eagle
House capacity. Steve responded that those locations don't have upload capacity - 10 or 15 mb sufficient to push to
Town Hall, these sites have potential for SD but not HD.
Paula Bertram made a motion to authorize Tom Alonzo/Bill Hewig to contact Comcast to determine what is included in
the digital upgrade and what would the Town be required to purchase to comply. Steve Walker seconded, passed
unanimously.
The Committee then discussed the two side letters offered by Comcast. Attorney Hewig pointed out that side letters are
not a legal obligation.
The Committee had requested:
1. A meeting within 1 year to assess the availability of HD/EPG for PEG;
2. Should HD for PEG become an option, Comcast will not withdraw SD for those customers still wanting it;
===PAGE 2===
Committee discussed potential impacts to customers with the removal of SD channels. Tom Alonzo pointed out that all
issues will be discussed and reviewed, when HD becomes available and at that time Lunenburg could insist on SD.
The Committee was agreeable to a meeting in two years to discuss HD/EPG.
Following discussion the Committee found that the terms of the side letters are acceptable. Bill Hewig will work with
Comcast to draft.
The next time the committee meets it should be to approve contract terms and recommend acceptance to the Board of
Selectmen.
The next cable meeting was tentatively scheduled for Wednesday, January 7, 2018.
Meeting adjourned at 6:37 pm.
Respectfully submitted,
Paula Bertram

Steve motioned to approve the minutes of 1/24/18. Paula seconded. Motion passed unanimously.
Current Business
Discuss Comcast Contract Proposal - Committee discussed latest contract proposal from Comcast. The proposed
contract includes the following:
1. Capital of $170K 50% to be paid in year 1, 25% in year 2 and the remaining 25% in the third year
2. 5% of the Subscriber Fees
3. Construct a fiber video return line to the Middle/High School within 2 years.
4. Convert the Leominster channel to Lunenburg channel within 2 years
In addition Comcast has provided two side letters regarding:
1. Standard Senior Citizens Discount
2. High Definition Programing
Tom Alonzo reviewed the option of a fiber video return line or digital upgrade and determined that the digital upgrade
didn't make sense. Steve Malandrinos informed the Committee that the video return line will come into the head end
room at the Middle/High School.
Steve Walker made a motion to recommend to the Board of Selectmen to approve the proposed contract. Paula
seconded. Motion passed unanimously.
An appointment with the Selectmen has been scheduled for April 3, 2018 to outline the proposed contract and officially
make our recommendation. All committee members are encouraged to attend, therefore it will be a posted meeting.
Paula read the draft minutes to the Committee. Steve Walker made a motion to approve the minutes, Tom Alonzo
seconded. Passed unanimously.
Meeting adjourned at 6:20 pm.
Respectfully submitted,
Paula Bertram
