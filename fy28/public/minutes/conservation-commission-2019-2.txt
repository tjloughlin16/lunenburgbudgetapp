          Plan- Progess meeting with the Commission, Gary Gouldrup and the public at large  
          Gary Gouldrup and Jeff Ritterson appeared before the commission. Mr Gouldrup recounted the principles and        
          methods used in the planning of the bird habitat. Mr Ritterson explained the Mass Audubon program and the  
          training for creating bird habitat through forestry. He noted after review of the plan he thought the plan as written  
          by Mr. Gouldrup was well written. Per request of the chair, Mr. Ritterson went thorough his background and  
          qualifications. Patch cuts were discussed by the power lines and how it was designed for specific species of  
          that are in decline due to habitat loss and the principle of the cut was to attract them. He did not notice any of the  
          species on site due to the time of the year but he did analyze the construct of the forest. He explained that  
          building off the power line right of way would expand the type of habitat needed as the species are area  
          sensitive and the power line is not large enough to help in the early successional habitat. Areas of the forest  
          were discussed with respect to a monoculture versus a more complex bio diverse structure of the forest and the  
          understory. MM inquired about the value of the understory. Mr. Ritterson outlined the value of maintaining dense 
          understory for bird habitat. RB reviewed what snag trees were and what the value is. Mr. Ritterson reviewed the  
          value of woody debris. KJ asked if there was any statistics on success rates. Mr. Ritterson related that the  
         monitoring is still in progress, however he has noted that there has been an increase . Gary Gouldrup noted that  
          in what he has learned about bird habitat, it confirms much of what he has practiced over a long period of time.  
         JR evaluated the seasonal benefits and the benefits of best management practice of soils.  
 
          Richard Tomkins 601 flatt hill road- are we losing species? Mr. Ritterson noted that with any management action  
         there is going to be winners and losers. He did noted there are many bird species that are doing well and the goal
