May 12, 2011 
 
PERSONNEL COMMITTEE MEETING MINUTES 
 
At 6:00 p.m. the Chair called the meeting to order. Present were Chair Robert Rand, William Murray, 
Doreen Noble, and Deborah Christen. The May 5 
th  meeting was postponed until May12th.   
 
On-going Business: 
1. Public Comment: None. 
2. Personnel Actions to Review and Approve:   
3. Personnel Change/Hiring Notifications:  
4. Review of Previous Meeting Minutes: Minutes of A pril 17, 2011 (William 1
st , Deb 2nd ) meeting 
were approved.    
 
Current Business: 
 
1. Job Description Format Project: On hold until ne xt meeting. Chair will meet with Town 
Manager and designated HR personnel. Chair will also meet with Council on Aging Director..    
2. Discussion on, Review of Progress and Assignment s of LPC Initiatives: No action.  
3. Next Meeting Agenda: Standard On-going (Public C omment, Personnel Actions/Approvals, 
Personnel Change/Hiring Notifications, and Review of Previous Minutes) and Current 
Business to include Job Description Format Discussion,  Discussion on, Review of Progress 
and Assignments of LPC Initiatives for 2011, Changes to LPC website, and Next Meeting 
Agenda.  
 
The next scheduled meeting will be on June 2, 2011 at 6:00 p.m. at the Lunenburg Public Library. The 
meeting adjourned (Deb 1
st , William 2nd ) at 6:10 p.m.  
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Deborah Christen, Secretary
