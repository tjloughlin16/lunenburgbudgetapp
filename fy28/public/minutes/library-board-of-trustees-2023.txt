Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes January 19, 2023  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux-remote, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup-remote           
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:56 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Kate made a motion to accept the minutes of December 15, 2022 as amended, seconded by Laura. The 
motion passed unanimously by roll call vote. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o The FY23 budget is 48% expended and we are 58% through the fiscal year. Heating costs have still been 
somewhat low. 
o Muir received the FY24 target budget from the Town Manager. It’s a flat budget, as usual. Muir met with the 
budget subcommittee and got requests submitted by January 11 th. He will meet again with the Town Manager.  
o Some budget items include the MBLC 16% materials requirement, small increases for mileage and training, 
and postage. Some slight increases will also be requested for heating and electricity.  
o Muir has a Finance Committee presentation scheduled for February 23 rd. 
o Francesco will present ADA accessibility training. 
o There will be a legislative breakfast at the Lancaster Library on February 17th. 
o Muir and Dick attended a Pathways meeting with the superintendent of scho ols, our local legislative 
representatives and Michael-Ray Jeffreys. 
o There will be interviews this week for the Library Assistant opening. There are two candidates who will be 
invited back. Muir is considering splitting the positions to better adapt to the  candidates. 
o The reclassification of the Staff Librarian to Assistant Director is in process.  
o There were 50 people for the Polar Express program, 14 for All About Bears, and 14 people showed up for the 
new Open Mic. 
o The new book groups are going well. 
o There have still been HVAC issues. The heat was out on the weekend of 1/14-1/16. 
o A dedicated workstation for parents has been installed in the Children’s area.  
o Bob noted recent news articles about Drag Story Hours and asked if that had ever been considered here . Muir 
said the library always attempts to serve target audiences but there hasn’t been an identified demand there. 
There are policies in place for possible situations in the Children’s area. We have not generally had any 
controversial programs but keeping it in mind particularly in the YA area for materials development. 
 
• Trust Fund Update 
o The Edward Jones account hasn’t been opened yet. Harry had to provide additional information to the town 
and designate signatories. The type of organization is not defined in the context of town investments. 
o There is a little over $80k in the current accounts. 
o Harry is just about ready to request the transfer of funds from the town.  He noted that we should have input in 
the investment options. 
 
• Library Sign Discussion 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
o Muir presented a few pictures from the architects with ‘Public Library’, in 30 inch letters, in the space above 
the entrance. A suggestion was made to add an additional ‘Lunenburg’ line above.  
 
• ARPA Funds Update 
o Muir discussed this with the budget subcommittee, Laura and Dick. Requests under consideration are the 
redesign and construction of the HVAC system, estimated to be around $150k and a backup generator, 
estimated to be around $25k. Some other ideas under consideration are $50 for the memorial garden, a 
possible expansion of the teen room into the Director’s office space. 
 
• Outreach Ideas 
o The board decided to skip over this agenda item for this month. 
 
• Rules and Regulations Review, Discussion, and vote 
o Dick suggested we move this up in the agenda and review during the February meeting.  
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o No Update. The Friends are on hiatus until March. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Kate mentioned that Jean Proctor can come to the February meeting to discuss ideas for the memorial 
garden. 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Laura. Seconded by Kate. Meeting was adjourned at 8:24 p.m. 
 
• Attached Documents 
o January 19, 2023 Agenda 
o January 2023 Director’s Report 
o January 2023 Adult Services Report 
o January 2023 Teen Services Report 
o January 2023 Youth Services Report

Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes February 16, 2023  
Attending: Kate McCarron-remote, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux-remote, Donna Saiia-Remote, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup-remote , Jean Proctor – Guest, Jana Balish - Guest , Robin Venezia  
- Friends of the Library Assistant Treasurer.     
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:47 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
 
• Proctor Memorial Garden Discussion 
o Jean Proctor joined us to give us some background on the development of the Proctor Memorial Garden 
behind the library. 
o Her husband Dan was on the library building committee and her family took on the task of maintaining the 
garden once it was completed. It was designed to be an outside extension of the library. 
o The garden has not been well maintained over recent years because there have been some problems with 
vandalism and it became a burden for the family to keep it up. 
o Harry said it sounded as if the library administration at the time was not as supportive  of the Memorial Garden 
as they could have been. 
o Harry asked Muir about the vision for the future. It could be a great space for a meditation garden. He was 
thinking in terms of the pandemic and the healthful aspects of outdoor spaces. It could also be used for 
programming. 
o Dick asked if it might be possible to incorporate the side lawn area into the back garden.  
o Kate noted that in the past the library had many programs outside in that area. 
o Robin asked if design ideas could consider more maintenance-free options. She also said the library is 
probably better staffed now than it has been in the past to be able to maintain the garden. 
o Harry asked about the Friends’ willingness to provide some funds for some design and renovation. Robin said 
they have $20k available from a bequest.  
o Muir said the idea is to revitalize the space and possibly expand it along with improving the grounds in 
general. 
o Harry asked if the maintenance could be done by the DPW. Maia suggested that local youth groups could be 
approached for volunteers to help with maintenance. 
o Harry: Where do we go from here? Can we get some designs on paper with costs?  
o Muir said he will discuss some ideas with Jean and put together proposals for grants/funding that will be 
required. 
o Laura asked if we are just looking at the Memorial Garden alone? Muir said we should try to do more.  
o Harry asked how long it might take to come up with a proposal. Muir said he would coordinate putting a 
proposal together. 
 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Laura made a motion to accept the minutes of January 19, 2023 as amended, seconded by Donna. The 
motion passed unanimously by roll call vote. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o The FY23 budget is 53% expended and we are 66% through the fiscal year. Salary costs are on schedule. 
This includes two vacant positions. Utility costs are a bit behind. They tend to be more expensive towards the 
end of the fiscal year. 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
o Programs are only at 34% of the budget but a lot of programs will be coming up in the spring. CWMARS 
Network expenses have increased. 
o Postage expenses have been way over normal due to fine-free/overdue notifications. 
o Office supply expenses are up a bit due to the replacement cost of a laptop.  
o Muir met with the Town Manager on the FY24 budget. Library requests were mostly funded in the FY24 
budget. Some requests that were not funded included electricity increases and the copy machine. $18k was 
requested for the technology budget and $15k was funded. This includes the replacement of security cameras 
and $9k for three staff workstations. 
o Muir has made two conditional offers for the library assistant positions and one has been accepted. The job 
reclassification of deleting a staff librarian and adding an assistant director should go forward. 
o Under programs - there was a student art exhibit. The first teen video game night will be tomorrow night. Next 
week will be Intro to Shark Biology. The book clubs have had a steady attendance. On 2/27 there will be a 
Celtic music concert. Zumbini will be coming back. 
o Harry asked if open Fridays have still been going well. Muir said yes, they have still been a very busy time. 
 
• Trust Fund Update 
o The Edward Jones account should be funded in the next week. Approximately $80k will be transferred.  
 
• Library Sign Discussion 
o Muir asked the architect about adding a second ‘Lunenburg’ line. This should be fine. Muir suggested we 
should do a mock up of the letters to see onsite. He will reach out to some sign companies for some quotes.  
o We should consider lighting options. 
 
• ARPA Funds Update 
o Muir discussed this with the budget subcommittee, Laura and Dick. Requests under consideration are the 
redesign and construction of the HVAC system, estimated to be around $150k and a backup generator, 
estimated to be around $25k. Some other ideas under consideration are $50k for the memorial garden, a 
possible expansion of the teen room into the Director’s office space. 
 
• By-Laws Review, Discussion, and vote 
o We reviewed the document. It will be amended and voted on at the next meeting. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The next meeting will be held on March 6th, 2023. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Dick noted that the remote system was working well for this meeting. 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Bob. Seconded by Dick. Meeting was adjourned at 8:41 p.m. 
 
• Attached Documents 
o February 16, 2023 Agenda 
o February 2023 Director’s Report 
o February 2023 Adult Services Report

Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia,

The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:47 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• Robin wanted to give a shout out to Donna for the grant for the art class from the Cultural Council. It was a big 
success. 
 
Agenda Items 
 
• Proctor Memorial Garden Discussion 
o Muir, Robin and Jean Proctor met twice to discuss plans. The first priority is to restore the area and expand it. 
Phase two would be to convert existing open space into a plant demonstration area. 
o Muir presented a draft proposal. Jean is coming up with some numbers for construction and maintenance.  
o Harry said he is excited about enhancing the outdoor space. He asked how we can ensure that the space 
stays clean and well maintained. 
o Robin said she would like to have a hands-on space for the kids, similar to the herb garden out front. 
o Harry said the ARPA request should include all three phases. 
o Robin has a draft plan for the front area. She suggested a pocket pollinator garden with multiple plantings and 
a central walkway. 
o Harry made a motion to authorize the purchase of plants. Seconded by Dick. The motion passed unanimously 
(and enthusiastically). 
 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Kate made a motion to accept the minutes of February 16, 2023 as amended, seconded by Dick. The motion 
passed unanimously. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o The FY23 budget is 60% expended and we are 75% through the fiscal year. Heating charges are still a bit 
behind. The program line has gone up considerably, as expected. A number of materials costs will be coming 
up, mainly online materials such as Kanopy, Mango, and online databases.  
o Harry asked about the contracted services line. Muir said it is kind of open right now and may be used for 
painting the community room. 
o Muir presented the FY24 budget to the finance committee on 2/23 and met with the town manager for the 
preliminary budget. The preliminary budget is $539k. Most of the requests were funded. The copy machine 
lease and camera upgrades were funded in the town central purchasing and IT budgets.  
o Muir will be meeting with the Fitchburg and Leominster public library directors on potential points of 
collaboration. 
o We are still looking for a library assistant and Francesco’s replacement. There should be some people coming 
in for interviews. 
o The town and HR are working with a consultant and are recommending increased pay rates for the SAP 
library staff in order to come up to market rates. 
o Joselyn Davis, the new library assistant, is doing very well. She is assisting with the Children’s room and 
LSTA grant programming. 
o There is a high school art exhibit in the community room. There will be a Celtic Chocolate program on 3/29. 
o Muir met with Chris Ruth, the new facilities director on 3/13 and gave him a tour of the library.  
o Muir met with National Grid to talk about heat pumps for possible HVAC replacement.

o Muir has started to replace the thin clients. Nine units will be installed. 
 
• Trust Fund Update 
o Harry and Dick met today with Heather. The Touchstone funds can be transferred. The others are listed as 
Trust Funds and would have to be transferred one item at a time. Dick pointed out that these are not Trust 
Funds, there are no trust documents. 
o The funds should be transferred by Wednesday next week. We can’t touch the principle but the interest is still 
a large percentage of the funds.  
o Harry will email the town and reiterate what was agreed to and what should happen.  
 
• Library Sign Discussion 
o No current update.

o There are two people interested in running for the BOT. 
o Kate will not be running for reelection this year. 
o Dick will attend the meeting.

o They are discussing looking for possible sponsorships. One possibility is NE Subaru.  
o Harry gave a Trustee update.

o Harry tried to call on Wednesday when there was a snow closure. He went to the website and the date of the 
closure message was not very clear. Muir said he would look at it.  
o Donna noted that the art class was filled but the updated info was not posted on Facebook.

o Motion to adjourn by Dick. Seconded by Donna. Meeting was adjourned at 8:46 p.m.

Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes May 18, 2023  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup, Nicole Groskreutz – Edward Jones, Kristen Munroe – Edward 
Jones 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:51 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
 
• Trust Fund Update 
o Nicole and Kristen from Edward Jones attended the meeting. Nicole asked for some clarity on our investment 
goals and what our timelines are. Harry said we want to preserve the principal, but we can possibly be more 
aggressive with the interest that has accumulated and think in terms of a longer-term growth strategy. 
o Nicole suggested a one-year CD as a possible option. They are currently getting around 5% return. She also 
had three options for Edward Jones Advisory Solutions balanced funds with moderate risk  although the 
returns can vary widely depending on market conditions. 
o Dick suggested we start off conservatively and wondered if the debt ceiling issue would affect markets short 
term. Nicole said that debt ceiling problems don’t historically have any long-term negative effects. 
o Harry polled the group for ideas on investments and what our risk tolerance might be.  
o Donna asked what the library might need in liquid funds. Muir said around 2k to 3k. 
o After some discussion Dick made a motion, seconded by Laura, to invest 40% in a one-year CD, 2% in some 
liquid account, and the remaining 58% in an Advisory Solution fund.  The motion passed unanimously.  
 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of April 20, 2023 as amended, seconded by Kate. The motion 
passed unanimously. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o The FY23 budget is 75% expended and we are 92% through the fiscal year. We have a surplus in the staff 
salary line. There is a fairly large gap in the heating costs as they have been less this year. There is a gap in 
the library materials line but that will be filled with renewals and payments coming up.  
o The preliminary library budget of $539,126.28 was passed at Town Meeting.  
o Muir attended a department-head meeting on 4/25. Karen Brochu is retiring. Muir said she has been very 
helpful to him. 
o Muir worked with Jane Crook and Gail Kerr from the Friends on an agreement with NE Subaru to do an 
annual sponsorship of $3600 for a monthly music series that will begin in June. The first concert will be 
Saturday June 10th. 
o The pocket pollinator garden will be planted this coming weekend. 
o Robin is doing a talk on ecological garden design tonight. 
o The summer reading kick-off will be on June 21st and will have a return of the ‘Eyes on Owls’ program. They 
brought in 7 or 8 owls and a bird of prey for a previous program. 
o The staff librarian vacancy will not be filled. The position will be replaced with the Assistant Director position. 
The Digital Services position is still open. 
o A full calendar of summer programs is being planned. Muir is working on some way to provide free lunches. 
o 45 people attended the North Sea Gas music program last week. The puzzle swap was successful again. 
Over 40 people attended the craft lab for flower lanterns. 

===PAGE 2===

Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes December 21, 2023  
Attending: Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup 
Absent: Jana-Lee Balish 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:51 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Executive Session 
o Dick made a motion to enter executive session at 6:52, seconded by Donna. 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of the October 16th executive session as amended, seconded by 
Laura. 
o Maia made a motion to adjourn the executive session, seconded by Dick. Executive session was adjourned at 
6:53. 
 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of November 16, 2023 as amended, seconded by Laura. The 
motion passed unanimously. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o December is month 6 out of 12. The budget is 41% expended. Electricity usage is a bit down, have had 
credits. Heating charges are down a bit. Office supplies is behind schedule by about $500. Annual renewals 
are coming up in the spring for library materials. Some databases have been added which will increase 
materials costs by about $8-9k. 
o Muir had some good interviews for the open staff positions. Some second interviews are scheduled for 1/4/24.  
o Lydia is now officially the Assistant Director. 
o Muir met with Chris Ruth to discuss ARPA projects. The HVAC work will be a project under the town capital 
budget. Muir also met with the Town Manager to discuss library budget needs.  
o Muir met with Anthony Sculimbrene of the Architectural Preservation Commission to discuss the library sign 
project. He stressed the importance of unified signage across town properties and mentioned that guidance 
for signage should be included in the upcoming Master Plan. 
o Muir submitted a letter of intent for the next round of LSTA grants and an updated action plan for FY25 in 
support of the strategic plan. This includes the Civics Hub project, designed to improve communication 
between town government and the public. 
o A patron was banned for an indeterminate amount of time after being abusive to staff and acting  
inappropriately with some of the school age patrons. 
o Programs have been very successful lately. The Gingerbread house program had 70 attendees and the craft 
and cocoa night had almost 100. The Celtic concert and sourdough workshop also had very good attendance.  
o Some good progress has been made on the outstanding IT projects/issues.  
o Muir verified with Verdaka that the estimate to install 12 cameras was within the average range and they 
offered to review any layout plans. 
o The backup UPS system failed last month and new batteries were ordered and installed.  
 
• Vote to Request Selectboard Communicate Support for H. 3239 
o This bill is about access to E-books and E-audio books.
