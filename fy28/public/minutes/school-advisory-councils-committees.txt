decision was made by committee to meet on Thursday, March 27th, Friday, March

schedule was decided in order for original committee members to attend all 
evenings.

There is typically 15-18 questions per candidate.  Committee was tasked with 
reviewing each section of questions and choosing or revising questions as 
appropriate to narrow down the number of questions asked.
