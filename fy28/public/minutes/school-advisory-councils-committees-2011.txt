THMS SCHOOL ADVISORY COUNCIL 
December 20 th , 2011 
Minutes of Meeting 
Meeting called to order at 5:05PM.    
Present:  Timothy Santry, Greg Berthiaume, Linda Hallisey, Kim Thomas, and Heidi Champagne 
RTI Update 
Announcement regarding use of grade 8 math certified teacher as an “interventionist”, someone who 
will target students who score at 228 and below on 6 
th  and 7 th  grade MCAS tests.  Amy Raboin will fill 
that role, meeting with small groups once a week. 
Math Curriculum 
Clarification – math curriculum is aligned to the common core frameworks; however, the math 
consultant will continue to align our grade level maps with the new curriculum.  We will focus on 
identifying standards that are possibly new responsibilities for different grade levels. 
Groupings and Vertical Teaming 
 We are using student growth percentiles to review placements for students in math classes.  Analysis of 
data seems to indicate students who are on the cusp between algebra or pre-algebra seem to be less 
successful if pushed into algebra.   
We are looking to establish data  based criteria for transition into high school honors or regular math 
classes instead of relying strictly on teacher recommendation. 
Results Teams 
Results faculty meetings are dedicated to the following issues: 
•  Identifying areas of student strength and weakness 
•  Noting where in our curriculum map these concerns are addressed 
•  Planning how to measure success 
iPad training 
PTO funds of $1000. Tim recommends using this to hire an Apple representative to provide professional 
development for the best use of the iPads in the classroom 
Project 351 
Candidate Josephine Bowen, community volunteer
