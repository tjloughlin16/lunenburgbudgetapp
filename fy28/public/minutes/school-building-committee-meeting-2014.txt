LSBC were informed the Project funding letter from the MSBA had not yet been received.  Inquiries have

Lunenburg School Building Committee (LSBC) members held a brief discussion of roles and positions of the 
LSBC seeking confirmation of the positions of the Committee.  The following positions were voted on: 
Michael Mackin as Chairperson; Mark Erickson as Vice-Chairperson; Tim Sentry as Advisor.  Letter from the 
District will be issued to the MSBA confirming these positions.       Motion: The Lunenburg School Building 
Committee voted to approve the May 2014 invoices as presented by Joslin, Lesser + Associates.                                                                                                                                             
Project Design Status Update 
Community Outreach Efforts
JLA and LSBC discussed scheduling  another Abutter's meeting for late August or wait till school in the 
Thomas Passios School's Cafeteria.  Methods to inform Community members of the next meeting were 
briefly discussed.  JLA explained they have a list of attendees from last meeting with email addresses but 
were concerned on how to notify Abutters that do not receive email .  LSBC requested other methods of 
advertising the meeting be explored. (i.e.. Lunenburg Tab, Project website, postcards)
Tappe briefed the LSBC on the current progress of Interior design for the new school.  Several slides were 
shown to the LSBC  with color palettes.  Mixed reactions to several selected colors with Tappe explaining 
final colors have not been selected , palettes shown were fro discussions and further examples would be 
generated.  Tappe recommended a Working Group be developed to review building palette choices and 
selections. Follow-up meetings to be scheduled.
Adjournment
The meeting was adjourned at approximately 7:42 pm.
School Building Committee briefly discussed scheduling a Ground Breaking Ceremony after school is in 
session. A committee will be selected to plan the event.
Project Web-Cam for the project will explored and discussed at the Construction Kick-off meeting yet to be 
scheduled.  Prices and capabilities to be discussed.
Other Business
Early Release Construction Package No.1 
JLA and Shawmut Design & Construction briefed the LSBC on the results from the bids of the Early Site 
Package.  Best and final estimates were received with JLA working with Shawmut to develop an iGMP No. 
1 for this referenced work.  Separate meeting will be required to approve iGMP No. 1 for the Early Site 
Package.
