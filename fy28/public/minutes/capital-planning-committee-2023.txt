CAPITAL PLANNING COMMITTEE 
Wednesday, November 20, 2023 
Ritter Memorial Building 
960 Massachusetts Ave. 
Lunenburg, MA 01462 
 
Present Members: Chair Tom Alonzo, Clerk Peter Beardmore, Planning Board Representative 
Matt Brenner, Finance Committee Member Chris Menard, Board Member Dave Passios 
1.Call to Order 
 The meeting opened at 7:00PM 
 
2. Public Comment 
a. CPC Members 
No member comments. 
b. Public 
Rhonda Lisio- 181 reservoir road- Rhonda addresses the problems with 
drainage wells on her street. Rhonda agrees that the project does not 
meet capital planning criteria, but the town made an error in 2016 when 
an asphalt berm was added at the beginning of the driveway and on the 
opposite side of the street. Since this was added water is now funneled 
down to her property and increased the water table. Tom Alonzo agrees 
there has been additional support from neighbors. Matt Brenner states 
that Stormwater has more stringent policy’s/bylaws within the past few 
years. There is a suggestion that Stormwater and the planning board 
meet to discuss.  
 
3. Presentation of Capital Planning Requests: School Department 
Speakers present: Dr. Kathleen Burnham, Business Manager Michael Cassidy, Director of 
Facilities and Grounds Eric Hevy. 
The presentation was split into 10-year plans by building. 
 
Lunenburg Middle High School 
1. Turf field, site survey and drainage improvements. There is consistent damage from 
heavy rain to the field and structures.  Will be split into 4 phases: 
a. Phase 1: Initial assessment that is being funded through ARPA funding:   
  $50,000 
b. Phase 2 FY 25: Detailed engineering survey with recommendations: $200,000 
c. Phase 3 FY 26: Extensive rehabilitation and construction projects recommend 
 from the feasibility study: $300,000 
d. Phase 4 FY 27: Additional extensive rehabilitation and construction projects 
 recommend from the feasibility study: 1.2 million 
 
===PAGE 2===
Turkey Hill Elementary School  
FY 25: Study for renovations, assessments, and schematic design:  $275,000  
FY 26: Complete window replacement (plans only): $290,000  
FY 26: Update classroom technology: $40,000 
FY 27: New roof: $625,000  
FY 30:  HVAC building wide upgrade: $4.349 million 
FY 31: Fire suppression system (currently doesn’t exist): $2.615 million 
- Currently there is no fire suppression system in the building. 
FY 31: Tile floor replacement: $225,000 
FY 31 Plumbing upgrades building wide: $261,882 
- Annual water testing is flagging elevated lead levels. 
 
Primary School 
FY 25: Designer service for playground equipment: $30,000 
FY 25: Feasibility study for entry and driveway expansion: $30,000 
FY 25: Classroom AC units’ Phase 2: $160,000 
FY 26: Gas condensing boilers (plans only): $30,000 
FY 27: Age appropriate and accessible playground equipment: $170,000 
FY 27: Driveway and parking expansion: $175,000 
 
All Schools 
FY 25: Installation of 3M window safety film totaling: $130,000 
- Primary School: $38,120 
- Turkey Hill Elementary: $33,569 
- Middle High School: $42,618 
FY 25: Replace 1-ton dump truck with plow for maintenance and grounds: $74,500 
FY 27: Replace ¾ ton or 1-ton pickup truck with plow: $72,000 
FY 29: 13-person passenger van for athletics, student groups and misc. use.: $40,000 
FY 33: Replace lawnmower: $55,000 
 
Questions 
• During a tour of Turkey Hill Elementary School Matt Brenner noted that classrooms only 
have 2 electrical outlets per classroom. Matt Brenner questions if the current electrical 
infrastructure can be implemented regarding updates to the technology in the 
classrooms. 
• The board inquires if there are any grants available. Dr. Kathleen Burnham confirms 
there may be grants through MSBA program, but funding can be competitive based on 
the submissions each year.

There was no additional discussion regarding capital planning requests.

CAPITAL PLANNING COMMITTEE 
December 4, 2023 
Ritter Memorial Building 
960 Massachusetts Ave. 
Lunenburg, MA 01462 
 
Present Members: Chairman Tom Alonzo, Clerk Peter Beardmore, Planning Board 
Representative Matt Brenner, Member George Martin, Finance Committee Member Chris 
Menard. 
 
• Call to Order 
The meeting opened at 7:04 PM. 
 
• Public Comment 
a. CPC Members: No member comment. 
b. Public: No public comment. 
 
• Discussion of Capital Planning Requests and Preliminary Prioritization 
There were 2 revisions received from the prior school presentation and the facilities and 
parks presentation. Peter Beardmore states there was a slide missing from the 
presentation and there is a discrepancy in terms of what the slides listed compared to 
the school committee priorities list. There is a question of why on page 69 there a line 
with is $15,000 but there is no label. Peter Beardmore will inquire to get more 
clarification.  
 
Turkey Hill Window Replacement and Design Work 
There is an accelerated report program, but it is limited to windows, roof, and 
boilers. To qualify for the accelerated repair program, it was found that there is a 
requirement to commit to that building for the lifespan of the project.  
Matt Brenner doesn’t believe the safety film is a top priority and is struggling 
with the cost.  Peter Beardmore states there is a safety concern relative to the 
perimeter strength of the building. This would slow activity down to allow for a 
response. There are vulnerabilities in every building. Turkey Hill has floor to 
ceiling glass while the other two buildings have single pain glass.  
 
Senior Center Parking Lot 
The senior center parking lot project has been separated into a design and a 
construction phase. The design phase is estimated to total $35,000. The 
committee questions what the main goal of this project is. Heather Lemieux 
thinks this is a project that addresses safety issues, and the project could add an 
additional 19 parking spaces. There is also a question about the location of kid’s 
kingdom and the potential to relocate to create more space.  
 
 
===PAGE 2===
 
Ritter Floor Replacement and Ritter Stair Cover Access 
Chris Menard doesn’t think the floor replacement is a medium priority. Tom 
Alonzo also agrees it’s not deteriorating the building. Peter Beardmore believes it 
is all cosmetic. Chris Menard thinks if this is a safety issue then it should be 
closed off as an entrance. This can’t be used as an emergency entrance since it’s 
too dangerous. 
 
Marshall Pond Treatment 
The original request was for $256,000 for treatment of the entire pond. It has 
now been scaled back and will potentially be done in sections starting at an 
estimated cost of $50,000. Matt Brenner thinks this is a low priority. The main 
question posed by the committee is how to keep the lily pads from continuing to 
come back. Tom Alonzo and additional members believe this will not be a one-
time treatment. Peter Beardmore agrees that the pond is being used for 
recreational services. Additionally, there are a lot of lake acreage in Lunenburg 
but a very small portion I available to the public. 
 
Wallace Park Basketball and Tennis Court 
Peter Beardmore questions if this was a bad job when created or if it’s just 
surface issues. Additionally, Peter Beardmore questions if maintenance is being 
done to minimize the issues. There will be a discussion with the chair to get 
additional information.  
 
DPW Roof 
Overall, the committee seems the price is very high for the amount of space 
being repaired. 
 
DPW Front End Loader with Plow 
Peter Beardmore shares that Lunenburg has always only had 1 front end loader.  
Peter Beardmore doesn’t believe there is a need for an additional loader and to 
also keep the current front-end loader. Chris Menard questions the prioritization 
of repairing infrastructure versus the risk of equipment breaking and having 
redundant equipment. Heather Lemieux confirms that the directors meet with 
staff to find out what their needs are and there’s a lot of conversation with the 
mechanics about equipment and trucks. It’s not just the opinion of 1 person. 
 
Stone Wall in Cemetery
