Notice of Public Hearing 
 
 
 
Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that t he Zoning Board of Appeals will hold a public hearing on 
September 28, 2022 at 7 :30 P.M.  at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor, Joseph F. 
Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Paul Block, 292 Arbor Street, 
Lunenburg, MA  01462.  The property, which is the subject of this case, is located at 451 
Townsend Harbor Road, Lunenburg, MA  01462 owned by Paul J. & Lois L. Block.  
Assessors Map 29 Parcel 8. 
 
The applicant is seeking a Special Permit to raze the existing structure and construct a  
new three bedroom dwelling on the property per Section 250-7.4 A (1).   
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit.  If you wish 
to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by agent 
or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or 
the Zoning Board office during normal business hours.
