5.  Sewer Commission Computer Request as part of the Technology Capital Requests.  
a.  Background:  Technology capital projects include a $900 computer requested by the Sewer 
Commission. However, the commission has stated their intent to pay for all their capital 
requirements from the Sewer Enterprise Fund.  Discussion centered on whether the Town’s 
technology capital equipment request may include a request for a computer for the Sewer 
Commission.  If so, should the Town pay for the computer for the Sewer Commission?   
b.  Resolution:  After discussion, John moved that “The Sewer Commission could include any of their 
requests for technology equipment purchases in the Technology capital request plan.  However, the 
Sewer Commission will reimburse the Town for the cost of these purchases upon delivery to the 
commission.”  Dennis seconded the motion.  The Capital Planning Committee voted 4-0 in favor of 
this motion.  Note:  Heather S. had left the meeting by this time. 
c.  Jamie will write a letter to the Sewer Commission informing them of the decision taken by the CPC.  
  
6.  Discussion of Building re-use issues in advance of the December 2 meeting. 
a.  Jamie summarized the issues that will come before this committee at the next meeting regarding 
Town Hall.  These issues include painting Town Hall (at least the front of the building), and 
commissioning a study to determine the options, costs, and practicalities of renovation/re-use of 
Town Hall. 
 
7.  The agenda for the December 2 meeting was approved and John will send the approved agenda to the 
Town Clerk to post.  
 
 
8.  Adjournment:  John moved to adjourn the meeting; Dennis seconded his motion, and the committee voted 
4-0 to adjourn at 6:46 pm.
