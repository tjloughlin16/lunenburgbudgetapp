November 27, 2018
Joseph Bilotta Meeting Room, Town Hall
Damon McQuaid, Matthew Allison, Caroline Griffis, John Henshaw.
Heather Sroka
David Passios, Jack Rodriquenz, DPW Director.
1. Meeting Called to order at 5:00 by Mr. McQuaid
2. Public Comment: Mr. Passios asked about the procedure the Capital committee will use to prioritize the
capital requests. The Committee responded that Heather Lemieux would like to use the Collins Center Scoring
method to prioritize the requests.
3. Jack Rodriquenz reviewed the Capital requests for the DPW with the Committee. Jack reviewed the issues
the DPW Department is current facing due to a shortage in staffing. This has been a major reason projects have
not been completed. Extra funds are required for engineers and architects and have extended the time period
required for projects to be completed.
The DPW Director discussed two capital projects. The first capital request from the DPW is $36,000 to refur-
bish the Wallis Park basketball court and the fencing around the court. No additional Capital requests were
made due to the need to finish current projects. The fuel tank removal and replacement project will require a
capital expenditure of $220,000. No Vehicles required for FY20.
Jack handed out a copy of the VERTEX report that lists Facility repairs that need to be done within one year.
The community needs to decide what the Facility plan will be before the DPW can prioritize projects.
4. Jack Rodriquenz and John Henshaw discussed some changes that need to be made to the Capital Spreadsheet.
5. The Committee approved minutes from the 11/6 meeting.
6. Action items for the December 4" meeting with a start time of 5:15: 1) Review minutes for 11/20 (changes
needed) and 11/27; 2) Prioritize Capital requests
7. Public Comment: None
8. Adjournment at 6:15 p.m.

Heather Lemieux, Town Manager; Phyllis Luck, Selectman
1. Meeting Called to order at 5:10 by Mr. McQuaid
2. Public comment: None
3. Heather Lemieux reviewed the Collins Center scoring method used to review capital requests. The Collins
Center method is based on a points system using criteria such as public safety. The Committee agreed to start
using the Collins method next year after review with Town Manager.
4. Cost of election machines was discussed. This capital project request is for four new voting machines for a
total cost of $26,500.00.
5. Heather Lemieux provided the Committee an update regarding the capital project request for costs associated
with the Town's Master Plan. A Master Plan is used to guide decisions for capital planning on Town buildings,
infrastructure, etc. John Henshaw provided the Committee an update on his work on the Google Document
rollup sheet, that he needs to input project numbers for some requests. John Henshaw also relayed that Fire
Chief Sullivan needs to have a fire truck repaired. The $10,000.00 for repairs will be budgeted in operating ex-
pense and are expected to last one year.
6. Minutes from 11/06/2018 approved, minutes from 11/27/2018 changes will be reviewed at the next meeting.
7. Capital requests were discussed and the list was prioritized.
8. Action items for next meeting: Damon McQuaid will reissue the capital spreadsheet. The Town Manager
will provide the Committee with a firm number for the capital budget.
9. Public Comment: None
10. Next meeting January 8 with a start time of 5:15
11. Adjournment at 6:00
