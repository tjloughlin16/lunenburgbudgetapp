Lunenburg Council on Aging Meeting Minutes       Minutes Approved at the 2/14/12 COA  Meeting 
January 10, 2012 
 
Present : Pete Lincoln (Chairperson), Mary Lynn Conrad (Vice Chair), Doreen Noble (Director), Barbara Brown,  
Fred Crellin, Jane Doyle, Jim Hays, Sis Montuori, Deb Seeley, Joyce Wass, and Susan Doherty (Admin. Asst.) 
 
Guest:
  Lloyd Carlson, John Male, Judy St Arnaud, and Armand St Arnaud.  
 
Welcome 
:  Pete Lincoln, Chairperson, welcomed the guests and called the meeting to order at 9:30 a.m.  
 
Minutes: 
  A motion was made and seconded to accept the December 14, 2011 COA meeting  
minutes as presented.   All approved, motion carried. 
 
Director’s Report: 
   The Director, Doreen Noble introduced herself to the guests and began her report.  She informed the 
board that she met with the Cable Access representa tive, Jo McLaughlin, and filmed a generic “Eagle Ho use Updates” 
without being specific on dated activities so that the program information that runs on the television will not be out of date 
too quickly. 
 
Finance/Informational Report :  Doreen met with the COA Finance Sub Committee be fore today’s meeting and gave the 
members of that committee a report on COA budget li ne items, which were generated by the Town Account,  to review.  
She said that everything is up to date on being pai d although we are waiting for a “Request for Transf er” approval from 
the Town Manager to transfer funds from the gas uti lities line to the electric utilities line to pay a  Unitil bill.  This is not 
uncommon and was expected to happen at this time of  year due to fluctuating increases/decreases in rat es from the utility 
companies. 
 
The Director is currently working on a MARTAP grant for $850.00 which if received will be used to improve our existing 
transportation program with new safety equipment for the van. 
Another grant which is being looked at is the Execu tive Office of Elder Affairs Service Incentive Gran t.  This would 
involve a consortium of area Councils on Aging, which border the town of Lunenburg, applying for a $20,000.00 grant.   
Doreen is also researching a grant which is availab le from the Community Foundation which will provide  funds for a 
wellness program with a paid instructor. 
 
A revised “Director Job Description” draft was comp leted by Doreen to help aid in the Director’s Evaluation process.  She 
has not submitted this to the Lunenburg Personnel Committee yet. 
A discussion ensued about the proposed “Executive S ession” which the Chairperson wanted to hold after this meeting to 
discuss the Director’s evaluation.  Sue Doherty inf ormed the board that the Town Clerk had advised her  that going into 
executive session is not necessary to discuss this matter, therefore it was not added to the January COA agenda.   
Doreen also commented in saying that she prefers this issue to be discussed in a public forum. 
 
Old Business: 
 
 
Parking Lot:   The septic mound behind the Eagle House Senior Center has been removed.  The Friends of the Eagle 
House Inc. funded the removal which cost $12,000.00.  The contractor will be back in the spring to seed and clean up the 
area.  After this is completed the area can be used for outdoor recreational activities only at the Eagle House.  In the mean 
time there will be no parking allowed in that area.  Request for construction of a parking lot have been included in the 
COA Capital Planning request with the town. 
The COA board thanked Lloyd Carlson, President of the Friends of the Eagle House and Pete Lincoln for all of their help 
with this project. 
 
Janitor for Eagle House:  The Director thanked the COA board for their help with pursing the issue of janitorial help for 
the Eagle House with the Town Manager.  The Department of Public Works has hired a part time 19 hour per week person 
to assist with Jim Breault the Facilities Manager.  This person will be at the Eagle House for 1 hour every morning to help 
with setup/break down of furniture and other tasks they may be asked of him by the Director. 
The Tax Work off person for the Eagle House will also be returning at the end of January to help with janitorial duties at 
the Eagle House.   
 
===PAGE 2===
Director’s Evaluation:   Pete Lincoln had previously contacted  Kerry Speidel, Town Manager and asked about the 
necessity of the COA board doing an evaluation on the Director of the senior center.   Kerry replied to him that she finds 
our material helpful in her evaluation process of the Director. 
Prior to this meeting, Pete requested that Doreen come up with ideas on what she thought the Director of the COA should 
be evaluated on. 
Discussion about the evaluation was held and it was decided that Deb Seeley will chair the Evaluation Sub Committee 
along Fred Crellin and Jim Hays as members. 
 
Fred Crellin left the meeting for another engagement at this time. 
 
New Business: 
   
 
Eagle Heights:  The Eagle Heights project was not funded this yea r by the state but they will re-apply for funding a gain 
in the spring of 2012. 
 
COA Resignation:  A letter of resignation was received and read from COA board member Sandra Flanagan.  Sandra also 
informed Sue Doherty that she had sent a copy to the Lunenburg Board of Selectmen. 
A discussion then ensued about term limits and the possibility of waiving them in order to keep people  who want to 
remain on the board over the 6 year limit.  Doreen reminded the board that Executive Office of Elder Affairs recommends 
that term limits be kept in place.  This discussion  led to another regarding COA Board members serving on the Friends of 
the Eagle House Board to which Doreen reminded the board that Executive Office of Elder Affairs recomm ends that the 
this not be done. 
 
FY13 Budget:   Discussed under Director’s Report. 
 
Senior Center Evaluation/Survey:   Doreen suggested that an overall evaluation of th e center and its programs be 
designed as it may prove helpful to the Director’s Evaluation Committee.  The COA board felt that an e valuation of the 
center by its participants might be helpful but not necessary to be done in order to do the Director’s evaluation. 
 
Friends of the Eagle House Inc.:   Lloyd Carlson informed the COA board that the Fri ends have not paid the last half of 
the invoice to William Proctor Excavating for the work they have done on removing the septic mound at the Eagle House.  
He reassured them that they do have enough funds to  do so and will submit the final payment after the Friends next 
meeting. 
A discussion was held in relation to what the next step is in completing the parking lot construction.   Pete Lincoln stated 
that he would contact the Town Manager to ask what the Town of Lunenburg’s plan is for this project. 
Multiple board members commented on how they have h ad a difficult time in the past parking at the Eagl e House due to 
large functions that were going on at the center. 
It was reiterated again that there was to be no par king behind the Eagle House on the grass or the dir t and that there were 
concerns from the School Committee about not being informed about the mound removal, and that the exca vation work  
encroached on school property. 
 
Sub Committee Reports: 
   
 
December minutes from the Board Development meeting  and the Advocacy Committee meeting were passed out  for 
review and both will be voted on at the February COA meeting. 
  
Correspondence: 
   No correspondence was read at this meeting. 
 
General Discussion: 
    
 
Comments were made about how the Friendly Seniors n eed to seek other accommodations for different meet ings due to 
the room capacity in the large function room not being able to accommodate them.  
Doreen gave kudos to the President of the Friendly Seniors, Lisa Hays saying that she does a great job with her functions. 
 
Adjournment: 
 A motion was made and seconded to adjourn the meeting.  All approved, motion carried.    
 Meeting adjourned at 10:59AM 
 
Respectfully Submitted, Susan Doherty Administrative Assistant

Lunenburg Council on Aging Meeting Minutes       Minutes approved at the 3/13/2012 COA Meeting 
February 14, 2012 
 
Present : Pete Lincoln (Chairperson), Mary Lynn Conrad (Vice Chair), Doreen Noble (Director), Barbara Brown,  
Fred Crellin, Jane Doyle, Jim Hays, Sis Montuori, Joyce Wass, and Susan Doherty (Admin. Asst.) 
 
Excused: 
  Deb Seeley 
 
Guest: 
  Lloyd Carlson and John Male  
 
Welcome 
:  Pete Lincoln, Chairperson, welcomed the guests and called the meeting to order at 9:30 a.m.    
 
After introductions a discussion was held concerning term limits for Council on Aging board members.   
Board members agreed that they liked the language in the COA Bylaws concerning term limits which allows  
the term limit policy to be waived by vote of the COA.  Pete Lincoln asked if Judy St.Arnaud, who attended the  
January COA meeting, was interested in becoming a COA board member.  Joyce Wass informed him that  
due to other commitments, she would not be available for a few months. 
 
Minutes: 
  A motion was made and seconded to accept the January 10, 2012 COA meeting  
minutes as corrected.   All approved, motion carried. 
 
Director’s Report: 
    
 
Finance/Informational Report :  Doreen reported that she did not anticipate any upcoming financial problems, although 
due to her being out sick for several days, she is two weeks behind in processing the Meals on Wheels mileage 
reimbursement.  
 
The intern from Mount Wachusett Community College w ill not be continuing at the Eagle House.  Doreen d id not feel 
that the “fit” was right for the senior center. 
 
The MARTAP grant for $850.00 which if received will  be used to improve our existing transportation pro gram with new 
safety equipment for the van, has been applied for.   
Due to time constraints and illness the Executive Office of Elder Affairs Service Incentive Grant was not applied for. 
 
Doreen informed the board that Lunenburg Selectman Ernie Sund, is looking for a representative from th e Council on 
Aging to join a Memorial Day Committee for the town of Lunenburg. 
 
Doreen Noble and Meal Site personnel Chris McCarthy  and Ruth Swiecicki will all be attending Serve Saf e certification 
classes soon.  This is necessary in order to have a meal site on the premises.  
 
Old Business: 
 
 
Parking Lot:   Lloyd Carlson, who was in attendance representing the Friends of the Eagle House Inc., reported that the 
seeding and finish work on the septic mound removal at the Eagle House will have to wait until spring to be completed.  
Although this will not be used as a parking lot, Pete Lincoln reported that the Town Manager Kerry Speidel has given 
approval for things such as a horse shoe pit and a bocce court to be installed.  She also told Pete that arrangements could 
be made for the prisoners from the Worcester County Sherriff’s department to clean up any debris that may have been left 
behind from the mound removal. 
 
Director’s Evaluation:   Not discussed at this meeting. 
 
FY13 COA Budget:   Doreen said that due to her recent illness she needed to cancel a meeting twice with the Town 
Manager concerning the FY13 COA budget.  She will schedule a meeting with her A.S.A.P. There will be a public 
presentation on March 8, 2012 at 7PM at Lunenburg town hall where the COA budget for FY13 will be presented. 
 
Memorial Day Committee:   Discussed under Director’s report 
 
 
===PAGE 2===
 
New Business: 
   
 
Friends of the Eagle House Inc.:   Not discussed at this meeting. 
 
Sub Committee Reports: 
   
 
A motion was made and seconded to accept the Board Development and Advocacy Committee December 2012 mi nutes 
as presented.  All approved, motion carried. 
 
Pete Lincoln reminded all sub-committee’s to have t heir end of year reports prepared for the Annual CO A meeting in 
June. 
 
Jim Hays distributed COA Financial Committee minute s for the board to review and approve at the March 2012 COA 
meeting. 
 
Sis Montuori informed the board about the “Mature H omeowner’s Discount” where elder homeowners can get  a 
significant discount on the homeowners insurance, w hich she claims that insurance companies don’t seem  to promote too 
openly to the public. 
 
Correspondence: 
   No correspondence was read at this meeting. 
 
General Discussion: 
    
 
Doreen informed the board that she will be on vacation from June 23, 2012 to July 9, 2012. 
 
Adjournment: 
 A motion was made and seconded to adjourn the meeting.  All approved, motion carried.    
 Meeting adjourned at 10:25AM 
 
Respectfully Submitted,  
Susan Doherty Administrative Assistant  
Lunenburg Council on Aging

Lunenburg Council on Aging Minutes       Minutes Approved at the 1/8/13 COA Meeting 
December 11, 2012  
 
Present: Mary Lynn Conrad, (Chairperson), Pete Lincoln (Vice-Chairperson), Doreen Noble (Director), Barbara 
Brown, Jane Doyle, Deb Seeley, Fred Crellin, Jim Hays, Sis Montuori, Joyce Wass, and  
Sue Doherty (Admin. Asst.) 
 
Guest    
Whitney Lacka, Prospective COA Member 
 
Welcome   
Mary Lynn Conrad (Chairperson) opened the meeting at 9:30AM 
 
Minutes    
Pete Lincoln asked for clarification from the Director regarding items in the November minutes concerning new 
hires, Holiday Luncheon, new kitchen equipment and side walk installation, and Boston Post Cane Ceremony.   
After discussion, Sis Montuori motioned to accept the minutes of the November 13, 2012 COA Meeting.  Joyce 
Wass seconded.  All approved, motion carried. 
  
Director's Report    
Finance/Informational Report:  Ms. Noble informed the COA Board that the Boston Post Cane Ceremony for 
Thelma Katkin was held on November 20 th .  Although many of Ms. Katkins family members were in attendance 
the only Town Official who attended was the Town Manager, Kerry Speidel.  State Rep. Jennifer Benson’s 
office had contacted the Council on Aging giving regrets that Ms. Benson could not attend but they would be 
sending a Citation for Ms. Katkin through the mail.  Unfortunately the Citation did not arrive in time for the 
ceremony.  Although it was an enjoyable time, Ms. Noble was disappointed in the turnout from the community 
for this special ceremony. 
 
All new kitchen equipment which was purchased through the Capital Plan, except the wall oven, has been 
received and installed.  Invoices have been received and are in the process of being paid through the Town 
Accountant. 
 
On Thursday, December 6 
th  the MOC Nutrition congregate meal was received and deemed not fit for 
consumption by the Director and Meal Site Manager.  The meal that day was called “Friendship Stew” and the 
Director documented it with a picture which was sent on to MOC Nutrition along with the Town Manager.  
Hotdogs and Potato Salad were purchased to replace the meal.   
When contacted, MOC suggested adding celery and carrots to the meal to make it more appealing and still 
offer it to the clients.  There were approximately 18 clients attending lunch this day, all refused the MOC meal 
except for 2 of them, and after trying, did not eat it. 
The Lunenburg representative on the MOC Nutrition Advisory Board is Bessie Lindsey and she reports any 
problems our Meal Site may have to them. 
 
The new Meal Site hours and menu will begin on January 2 
nd  at the Eagle House.  Lunch will begin at 12 
Noon, Monday – Wednesday and will remain at 11:30AM  on Thursday.  Every Tuesday, there will be an 
alternating menu consisting of Hot Dogs one week and Pizza the other.  The Director realizes that this is a 
large undertaking but would like to ease into a different menu without losing the MOC meal program. She 
believes that funding for this can be done through the COA Gift Fund at the Town Hall. 
The Pepperell Meal Site is currently doing a Pilot project through MOC where they prepare their own meals 
which, are subsidized with funding through MOC.  They will update Ms. Noble periodically on how this program 
is working for them. 
 
Ms. Noble is the Montachusett Area Rep. for MCOA and is currently contacting COA’s to push Legislature to 
restore funding to $7 per elder.  Approximately 25% of the population in Massachusetts is over 60 and funding 
has been cut to $6.40 per elder. 
===PAGE 2===
The Director reported that the COA budget is holding its own but that $1600 needed to be transferred out of 
the Direct Energy Line and put into the Sewer ($1000) & Water lines ($600) to cover bills.  It is anticipated that 
another transfer will need to be made into the Sewer & Water lines again before the end of FY13. 
 
The Director had attended a Department Head meeting where the planned FY13 budget cuts were the 
discussion.  The Town Manager informed them that the Town of Lunenburg did not receive cuts from the State 
as bad as was previously thought and that all departments should continue operations as is. 
 
 
Old Business  
 
Capital Plan:  The Director will be attending the Capital Plan meeting this morning at 10AM where she will be 
discussing the request for the purchase of a generator for the Eagle House Senior Center.  Ms. Noble stated 
that the Town Manager was dismayed with her that she had begun researching the purchase of a generator 
and said that it was up to the Highway Superintendent to do this.  As of this meeting Ms. Noble has not 
received any information from the Highway Superintendent about the purchase of a generator.  Ms. Noble 
does not believe that Capital Planning will approve this request. 
 
New Business    
Budget Cuts FY13:  Discussed under Director’s report.  
 
FY14 Budget:  The Director reported that the Town Manager has not provided FY14 budget packets yet but 
hopes they will be here sometime in mid-January.  The Town Manager has told all Department Heads that the 
FY13 cuts will remain in FY14.  Ms. Noble will make a recommendation for the COA  FY14 budget to be 
restored in full so the Senior Center can re-open on Fridays. 
 
Sub-Committee Report    
Sub-Committee Meeting: Discussion was held concerning the posting of agendas for sub-committee 
meetings.  All COA sub-committees had been reorganized at the beginning of FY13 but Chairpersons to these 
sub-committees were not chosen at that time.   
At the November COA meeting sub-committee board members set meeting dates and times to be held on 
December 11 
th  for four of the COA sub-committees.  The Administrative Assistant reminded board members to 
send agendas to her for the Town Clerk to post within 48 business hours of that date.  Only one agenda was 
received and as a result, one of the meetings could not be held.  The Director and the Administrative Assistant 
were able to set agendas for the other two for that day. 
Board members were confused about who should be posting the agendas considering that there were no 
Chairpersons to the newly arranged sub-committees, therefore nobody replied to the agenda requests.  Board 
members decided that if this situation should arise again, it would be the responsibility of the former 
Chairperson to set the agenda, of the particular sub-committee which will be meeting, with the Administrative 
Assistant in a timely manner for posting.  
 
Chairpersons to COA sub-committees were assigned as follows:
