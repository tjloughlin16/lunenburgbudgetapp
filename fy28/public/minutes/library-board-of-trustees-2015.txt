January 15, 2015; Mary Ann Sudolnik Conference Room, Lunenburg Public Library 
 
Attending: Jeanne Raboin, Dick Mailloux, Harry Kubetz, Gare Thompson, Robin Venezia, Susan Visser, Lars Widstrand, Martha 
Moore (by telephone), Teresa Hathaway, Robert Ebersole (Selectmen representative), Joanna Bilotta-Simeone (Planning 
Board), Jamie Toale (Building Re-use Sub Committee), Adam Burger (Land Use Director) 
Absent:  
 
The meeting was called to order at 6:45 p.m. by Jeanne Raboin. 
 
 Public comment: None 
 
 Approval of LPL Trustee Meeting minutes 12-18-14, with revisions recommended by the Trustees.  Motion by Gare, 
second by Dick; voted all in favor. 
 
 Joanna Bilotta-Simeone (Planning Board) and Jamie Toale (Building Re-use Sub Committee) 
o Village District Bylaws draft, Passios Boundary and Memorial Drive 
o Background information from Adam Burger 
o Planning board has made an 18 month look at Village; small scale commerce; walkable; in harmony with rest 
of Lunenburg’s appeal. 
Village is considered ¼ mile radius from the intersection 2A, Lancaster Ave. Desired design is something that 
work with existing buildings, town hall, library, etc., that does not break the style of a New England small 
town. Most homes are mid-18th century (some exceptions).  Residential district feel to be maintained.  
o Question from the trustees concerning the plan and how it impacts the Library and town  (Jeanne) 
 The library would be within the ¼ mile radius district. 
 Toale: school owns T.C. Passios and Brookes’ house, town hall and Ritter building owned by town; 
changes in ownership of them requires town meeting vote, old primary school, old West St. hw. 
Dept. building.  Zoning decision steers how to dispose of surplus buildings, so nothing has been 
set “up for sale” yet. 
 Gare: important that we [the library/trustees] are kept informed about the processes, and that we 
get a representative on the reuse/zoning discussion – the entrance road to T.C. Passios was 
invested in by the library – do we get any compensation if the road is “taken”? 
 Jeanne: talked about campus – we constructed the library in conjunction with the needs of the 
school district, in a safe environment (roads limited use).  Possible parking issues, (donated) 
garden – how that it is affected, safety for seniors, kid’s kingdom, teen center… Possible 
commercial properties in the area, max 5000 sq. ft. (CVS @ Whalom is 15,000 sq. ft. or three 
commercial uses) 
 Joanne suggests that we get a rep. for School Committee meetings to listen in and representation, 
since the schools decisions can affect the LPL 
 26 Jan, 9 Feb, 23 Feb (information) 7 p.m. Planning board meetings before the public hearing. 
 
 LPL Draft Budget FY16 
o Martha – discussed the budget proposal, she questioned how the materials and energy portion for the 
upcoming year are anchored. 
o Teresa on utilities, deficit to cover the rest of FY-15 to cover energy costs, total ~$14.5k, this should go in the 
letter to the FinCom. 
o Motion for Teresa/Martha to submit amended budget proposal as presented to Kerry Speidel, motion by 
Lars, second by Dick, voted all in favor.

Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes  
July 14, 2015; Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 2nd floor, Town Hall 
 
Attending: Harry Kubetz,  Dick Mailloux, Gare Thompson, Susan Visser, Lars Widstrand,  
Absent: None  
 
The meeting is a joint meeting between the Lunenburg Public Library Board of Trustees (LPLBoT ) and the Lunenburg Board of 
Selectmen (LBOS). 
Attending from the LBOS: Jamie Toale, Chairman; Robert Ebersole, Vice-Chairman; Thomas A. Alonzo, Clerk; Paula Bertram, 
Member; Kerry Speidel, Town Manager 
Absent: Phyllis Luck, Member LBOS 
 
The LPLBoT meeting was called to order at 7:05 p.m. by Chairperson Dick Mailloux. 
 
 Public Comment 
o No public comment 
 
 Agenda items 
o Consideration of appointments to two (2) vacant seats on the Library Board of Trustees (Failure to elect, 
Sections 3-1, 7-10, and any other relevant provisions of the Town Charter) 
o Dick Mailloux introduced the three candidates: Donna Saiia, Kiirja Paananen and Joy Ruth Cohen to the 
LBOS.  The candidate presented themselves; gave brief backgrounds and declared their interest in the 
Lunenburg Public Library and serving on the LPLBoT.  The LBOS asked questions of the candidates. 
o All three candidates were nominated for the [two] positions. 
o Voted: 
 Joy Ruth Cohen, 4 for, 5 against 
 Kiirja Paananen, 5 for, 4 against 
 Donna Seiia, 5 for, 3 against, 1 abstain 
 
 Motion to adjourn 
o Dick made a motion to adjourn the LPLBoT meeting, second by Gare, voted all in favor @ 7:30 p.m. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
Lars Widstrand 
Secretary, Trustee of Lunenburg Public Library
