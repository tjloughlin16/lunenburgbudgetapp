Construction  Activity Update 
Inga Knox (JLA) and Jonathan Hirst (Shawmut) presented a construction update to the 
committee. 
Monthly Invoices and Commitments 
Joslin Lesser & Associates (JLA) presented monthly progress  invoices for work 
completed through May, 2015 for Joslin, Lesser & Associates (OPM), Tappe Architects 
(Designer), Shawmut Design & Construction (Payment Requisition No.10), UTS Testing 
Services (April/May 2015).
 
Motion:  The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the May,2015 
invoices as presented by Joslin, Lesser & Associates.  The motion was unanimously 
approved. 
The Lunenburg School Building Committee reviewed minutes from the following 
meetings:
• May 13, 2015  LSBC Meeting 
Motion : The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the meeting 
minutes from May 13, 2015.  The motion was unanimously approved. 
Approval of School Building Committee Minutes 
Note: Weekly Construction Meetings are on Wednesdays at 10:00am in the Shawmut 
trailer. Everyone is welcome to attend and walk the site. Rob Alger is available to set 
up time for site visit. 
Re-Use of TCP building and Brooks House 
Public Comment 
No Public Comment.
The Committee reviewed a proposed lot line and property line concepts as they relate 
to the TCP building property allocation. The Re-use Committee approved the proposed 
property line plan which be used as the TCP building is marked for possible reuse. 
LHS Murals 
It was noted to the Committee for record that the project team is looking in to reusing 
one actual mural from the existing school and digital images of other murals. The 
locations and the logistics of the the mural design within new space are being vetted 
through with the design team.
