Monthly Approvals - Invoices
Joslin Lesser & Associates (JLA) presented monthly progress invoices for work
completed through December, 2014 for Joslin, Lesser & Associates (OPM), Tappe
Architects (Designer), Shawmut Design & Construction ( Payment Requisition
No.5), UTS Testing Services, Miscellaneous Printing Invoices and GAW Aerial
Photography.
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the
December 2014 invoices as presented by Joslin, Lesser & Associates. The motion
was unanimously approved.
Monthly Approvals - Commitments
Construction Amendment #3 for Final GMP was presented for record.
Mation: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the
December 2014 Commitments as presented by Joslin, Lesser & Associates. The
motion was unanimously approved.
Construction Activity Update
Jonathan Hirst (SDC) presented an overview of a construction progress. JLA
presented progress photos which are also available on the School Building
Project Website.
Progress Update
• Foundation work is on ongoing. Most of this work will take place during weeks
of December 12, 2014 and December 19,2014.
• Concrete sub contractor demobilization is scheduled to begin on December 15,
2015.
• Foundation and retaining wall on the North and South of the building is
scheduled for this week.
• Weather permitting, the work related to the underground electrical,
transformer vault and generator pad will take place during the week of
December 19,2014.
• Underslab coordination will take place during the week of December 20, 2014.
• Site mobilization & preparation for steel delivery is complete. Steel delivery
commenced on December 8, 2014.
• Steel erection at Area A has commenced. Second floor decking installation will
begin during the week of December 28, 2014.
• Drainage and sewer work is ongoing.
Note: Weekly Construction Meetings are on Wednesdays at 10:00am in the
Shawmut trailer. Everyone is welcome to attend and walk the site. Rob Alger is
available to set up time for site visit.

Change Order Review & Approval Process
Change Order process was reviewed by the Committee. It was decided to follow
up on the process after first change order is issued.
School Storage Garage
Currently the school uses a storage garage which will be demolished during
construction. The school may need at least a two bay storage garage built. This
topic will be reviewed at a later date once it is determined if funds may be
available for such a construction.
Topping Off Ceremony
Topping Off Ceremony will take place late spring. It will take about 8 weeks to
complete organization of the event. It has been suggested to have two painted
beams (painted with Intumescent paint) available on school premises where
students can access sign them. Additionally it may be possible to set up an online
signature option for those who will not be able but willing to sign the beam.
The committee will review and finalize details during following meetings.

Monthly Approvals - Art Room Modifications
Joslin Lesser (JLA-NV5), Tappe Architects and Shawmut Design & Construction
presented CR-68C, a change request which for modifications of the Art Room storage
and Kiln. These modifications originated from a faculty walkthrough during FF&E
(Fixture, Furniture & Equipment Review). The modifications work best for the faculty
and for functionality of the art programs.
If approved this change will be included in the next change order, however, the
Construction Team was looking for Authorization to Proceed with the work in order to
stay consistent with the schedule and sequencing of construction.
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to grant Authorization to
Proceed with this work pending final approval of total cost which will be included in the
next Change Order Package in January, 2016. The motion was unanimously approved.
