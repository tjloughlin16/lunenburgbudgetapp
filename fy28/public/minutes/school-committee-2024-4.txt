1. Call to Order by Carol Archambault at 6:30 
 
2. Pledge of Allegiance 
 
3. Public Comment  
Mr. Beardmore thanks all appropriate parties in budgeting process and execution of the override votes.  I was skeptic, but I 
was surprised and incredibly proud of our town.  I hope this is a giant leap for the brand of Lunenbur g, to be a town that 
prioritize our children and education.  I know there are a lot of people in Lunenburg that don’t agree with that, but we need to 
continue to work to make sure that the vision continues to become a reality 
 
4. Chair’s Report Ms. Archambault congratulates our retirees for their service to the children and families of Lunenburg, it is 
greatly appreciated. To the Lunenburg High School class of 2024, the ceremony on Saturday was a reflection of you.  Thank 
you to the high school administrators and staff for their preparation and to Mother Nature, for a glorious day, and we wish the 
graduates all is that good as they move forward.  Representative Scarsdale has scheduled a regional meeting with DESE to 
discuss school budget issues.  Mr. Waters and Dr. Gilman, Ms. Lemieux, Dr. Burnham and our school committee chair are 
invited to this meeting.  It will be scheduled either July 12 or 19 at 9 am or 1, she is looking for availability, I will no longer 
be the chair at that point, but my thought is we reply that we will have someone available at that meeting.  She is also 
requesting a brief overview of our school district with points that would be helpful to DESE .  As the fiscal year draws to a 
close I feel it is important for the public to know that the superintendent and her team, despite the lack of a business manager 
have made sound fiscal decisions and our financial consultants tell us that we will close out the fiscal year strongly.  We a re 
awaiting their final statements as they are awaiting confirmation through the town, difficulty with Munis, the town’s financial 
software has caused the fiscal year close out to be more challenging than normal, but we are looking Godward to a financial 
report at our June 26th meeting. 
 
Lunenburg Public Schools 
 
 Dr. Kate Burnham, Superintendent of Schools

5. Review & Approve Minutes 
a. Regular Session 3/6/24 &3/27/24 – Mr. Lehtinen makes a motion to approve minutes, Ms. Brzozoski seconded 
the motion, all approve 
 
6. Review & Approve Warrants & Line Item Transfers 
a. Warrant Total 5/20/24 to 5/30/24 $244,162.63 Ms. Archambault has signed 
b. Line Item Transfer $76,336  Mr. Livingston our contracted financial consultant suggests a transfer of funds 
between lines based on reconciling accounts and open issues from FY 23 and FY 22 lingering open, grants that need 
to be reconciled and Munis issues have made this an even more challenging situation Mr. Beardmore makes a 
motion to approve line item transfers as listed, Ms. Brzozoski seconds the motion all approve  
 
7. Superintendent’s Report Our retirees this year, we had several, Kristine Bassett who has been with our district for 32 years 
Sarah Sabatini, 31 years with the district, Tim Normandin, 31 years with the district, Erinanne Buroughs 30 years with the 
district, Mary Whitaker, 24 years with the district, and Martha Sullivan, 23 years with the district.  A report of plans for the 
retirees is shared.  Also the last day of school is June 12 th and will be a half day, and we look forward to having everyone 
back in August. 
 
8. Student Representative Report Tatum Furman reports the last day of school is June 12 th which is a half day for all schools.  
The Primary School had a second grade concert last night.  Turkey Hill Elementary School field day was today and there is a 
third and fourth grade music concert on June 7th. The Lunenburg Middle School has a fairy tale dance coming up on June 7th. 
Mrs. LeBlanc’s 8th grade civics class took civics MCAS.  Graduation for the 8th grade is on Wednesday June 12th at 8:20 a.m.  
At the Lunenburg High School we said goodbye to our seniors at graduation last week on Saturday June 1 st, it was a great 
day.  Finals start tomorrow and go until Tuesday. 
 
9. New Business 
Action 
a. FY25 School Handbooks All changes in the handbooks have been highlighted for review.  The consistency 
throughout the handbooks for the school is appreciated.  Mr. Beardmore makes a motion to approve the 
handbooks for FY25, Mr. Lehtinen seconded the motion, all approve 
 
b. 2024-20225 Parent- Teacher Conference Pilot Mr. Adams shares that at Turkey Hill, parent teacher conferences 
will be held in January, to occur after some i-ready testing and report cards have been sent out.  Ms. Squier reports 
the Primary School will also hold parent teacher conferences in January.  T he Middle High School has decided to 
keep their parent teacher conferences as scheduled in late October and not change them.  All schools will offer zoom 
options, as well as welcome any parents/guardians to make appointments with us and do not have to wait until 
conferences if they want to meet with us sooner.  Ms. Brzozoski makes a motion to accept the changes at the 
Primary and Turkey Hill schedule of parent teacher conferences, Mr. Be ardmore seconds the motion, all 
approve. 
 
c. Unaffiliated Salary Schedule There are a handful of staff that are not in a particular bargaining unit, these are 
primary the extended day program staff.  We generally bring a 3 year plan forward for i ncrease, however, with 
budget uncertainty, we are bringing a plan for just the next fiscal year.  This represents a 2.5% increase.  Mr. 
Lehtinen makes a motion to approve the increase, Ms. Brzozoski seconds the motion, all approve  
 
d. Hiring Screening Committee Appointment Our town accountant is resigning on August 4 th, we will need 1 school 
committee member to be on the screening committee, Mr. Beardmore volunteers.  Ms. Brzozoski makes a motion 
to appoint Mr. Beardmore, Mr. Lehtinen seconds the motion all approve  
 
e. Payroll Modification The town manager has sent an email to notify employees of a change in July for payroll, 
currently payroll is paid in advance however, based on some timing of overtime, this will change.  The committee 
discuss concerns over the lack of notification timing, as some employees will go a week without a check and feel 
this is unacceptable.  The members do not agree with this process of change.  Dr. Burnham suggests we wait a few 
days to see what options the town comes up with to combat the difficulties of weeks without payment. 
 
Discussion 
f. LHS Activity Fees Dr. Burnham shares a presentation of fees that are charged at each school level with an 
explanation of what the fee covers.  The committee agrees that even though they would rather not char ge fees, 
understand this is necessary, and would like a very transparent look into specifically the high school fees and what 
they actually pay for.

11. Public Comment  
Dr. Burnham thanks the administrators for staying throughout the entirety of the meeting and being available for any 
comments or questions.  Ms. Archambault thanks Ms. Lorenzen for the nice snack assortment put out today. 
 
12. Reports 
Mr. Lehtinen reports the Finance Committee met on 5/23, there was no special town meeting needed for the override, they 
were also looking for support on research and recommendations on collaboration of the town and school specifically with 
facilities, similar to what Mr. Beardmore talked about earlier today.  They are looking for us to research and look for 
recommendations on how we can collaborate for the future.  The town manager specifically mentioned about healthcare, the 
option for HAS and how that process will work. A rep from each of the unions will form a committee and vote on those 
changes, the town manager emphasized this would be a difficult process.  Some of the committee member’s struggled with 
the ideas that the school is using excel for budgeting and again without a finance director.  Even though many organizations 
use excel, once it does get to a certain level it does usually go into some other system, which then shows trends over time.  
Members are working on getting their term cycle back track, also frustrated with the minutes process in general for all 
committees and feel it’s out dated. 
Mr. Beardmore reports the Capital Planning Committee intended to continue our meetings through the winter to do a 5 year 
plus capital plan, however, a meeting was never called, as we were knee deep in budget, it is our intention to have a meeting 
next Monday, however, I haven’t received any notice or agenda to confirm that is happening.  The high school advisory 
committee met several weeks ago, I have asked for further clarification from the vice principal at the high school is a 
potential new program involving work credit for some students, this is a new state program that can potentially target at ris k 
students and organize some sort of school credit around a program relating to part time jobs outside of school.  The Athletic 
advisory committee has not met, but I have gotten some feedback from a  couple of the booster club presidents that maybe 
we need to take another look at that policy around sports club fundraising and the athletic advisory program, as we have set 
up in our policy.  A couple of the clubs have kicked off their spring/summer fundraising programs for fall sports and they ar e 
running them in different nature then previously, we aren’t selling baked goods or raffle tickets as much, now its text and 
web based appeals for funding, we aren’t getting the level of participation and compliance that we set out to get  
Ms. Archambault reports the master planning committee met, but I was unable to attend the meeting, as I was presenting 
senior awards, they are meeting Thursday night.

4. Chair’s Report I understand there is some concern in the community regarding the cuts being made despite the passing of 
the override as promised before the override the following cuts are being made, a Humanities teacher at the high school, an 
intervention teacher at the high school, a class room teacher at the middle school, a Wilson reading teacher at the elementary 
school, a PE teacher that is split between the Primary School and the elementary school, and a soc ial worker in the Primary 
school, as well an instructional coach districtwide, these are the cuts being made due to financial reason.  These cuts do equal 
the ESSER funds when cuts are made per contract.  The remaining personnel do move around as their certification permits, 
paraprofessionals are not part of this cut, there may be a reduction in this area for other reasons ; the need for individual 
paraprofessional positions is constantly being reassessed.  I hope that answers the questions people have concerning this, if 
you have any additional questions I would ask you to follow up with the superintendent or w ith the school committee chair.  
As you are aware I have one more year in my term on the school committee, but I am stepping down from being chair, I wish 
to thank the members of this committee both past and present for their hard work and keeping me on track.  I would also like 
to thank the superintendent and Mrs. Peterson for always being available, knowledgeable, and flexible.  One more very 
special thank you is to elected officials from the other boards particularly Renee Emkey and Evan Waters for their 
collaborations. 
 
 
 
Lunenburg Public Schools
