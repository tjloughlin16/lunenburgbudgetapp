obtain approvals for the harvest at small town forest (STF). RB  asked him what was the main target of the STF harvest.

the interior of the parcel. GG outlined the process of using a feller buncher on site.  GG strongly recommended the 
walking of the small town forest so he can show the details of skid trails and the interior portion of the cut.  In response 
to Linda Hillson’s question on the skid trails it was clarified by GG that the trees would be dragged out bu the skidder 
eliminating the need for turnarounds, thereby reducing the amount of skidder trail. The issue of the 20to 40% of tree 
harvesting was in reference to a controlled basal volume and not necessarily the number of trees relative to the total.  
GG outlined the goal of overall species diversity on a long term basis and not actively managing the area it becomes 
susceptible to disease, weather concerns and the establishment of a monoculture. Rick Hillson stated he did not like 
the terms harvest or farm. It was also questioned if the only motivation for the activity was for profit only, The

small town forest management plan were not called in as part of the equation for Robb’s Hill. Lisa Lento of 782 Flatt Hill
