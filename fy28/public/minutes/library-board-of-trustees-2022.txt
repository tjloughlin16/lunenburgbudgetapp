Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes January 20, 2022 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup, Michael-Ray Jeffreys 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:49 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Harry read rules for remote meeting. Took a roll call. 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of December 15, 2021 as amended.  Seconded by Laura. The 
motion passed unanimously.  
 
• Director’s Report 
o We are in the 7th month. There have been no major issues. Heating costs are still low. Muir received the 
FY23 target budget from the town manager and they met on Jan. 6 th. 
o Muir will be meeting with the town manager to discuss library requests. These include two new positio ns for 
Friday hours, corrected director’s salary, 9% increase for utilities and additional money for programs.  
o There will be an increase in the membership assessment for the CWMARS network. The library is 
transitioning over three years to a full membership. This is the second year.  
o There will also be a requested increase for library materials. The budget needs to be 16% of the municipally 
appropriated amount. 
o There is a request for funding to purchase technology. 
o Maia asked if the CWMARS membership upgrade had to do with the town population, Muir said that is one 
piece. It also includes electronic materials, circulation and the library internet connection. 
o Muir will also be meeting with the head of the finance committee to discuss the requests and will attend the 
February finance committee meeting. 
o Michael-Ray asked a few questions. 1) Has the library conducted an inventory to decide what needs to be 
bought or replaced? Muir said the priorities are the phone system, the server, and the public access 
computers. 2) Why would the 16% not be part of the technology budget line? The MBLC specifies library 
materials as anything in circulation. 3) On the impact of adding a business day, ie. Friday hours, how did you 
arrive at the 9% increase in utilities and is this too low? Muir calculated the number of additional hours to be 
an 11% increase in hours. 
o The Director’s contract was redrafted to start on July 1, 2022 to match the fiscal year schedule. The contract 
included a one-time payment to cover the salary increase differential for the first 6 months of 2022. Dick 
made a motion to approve this one-time payment of $2258.97, seconded by Kate. The motion passed 
unanimously. 
o Collection Development Policy: There was a complaint from a patron regarding the Children’s book ‘Hookers 
and Blow Save Christmas’. Muir gave the patron the challenge form. Harry responded to the patron via email 
and informed him that after a discussion with the Director and the Children’s librarian the book would be 
removed from circulation. Some discussion ensued on the complaint process and the nature of this particular 
book. 
o Laura asked about the staff update, whether the job requirements had been chang ed for the open position as 
discussed in our last meeting. Muir said the job description and requirements had not been modified but they 
have been more relaxed in choosing candidates to interview. 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
o The trust fund legislation is now before the senate and should be passed very soon. 
o There have been a mix of virtual and in-person programs. The take home kits in the Children’s area have 
been very popular. 
o The town caucus will be held on March 14th, 2022. Donna and Dick are both up for reelection. 
o In response to his recent evaluation and new contract Muir expressed his gratitude to the board for their 
support. 
 
• Trustee Handbook Discussion 
o Muir covered the Chapter 6 review – Personnel. 
 
• By-Laws Review Discussion 
o Harry asked if any old By-Laws documentation could be found? Dick said he didn’t think so. 
o Muir suggested we emulate the Lancaster public libraries by-laws. 
o Policy sub-committee will meet to review and present some suggestions for the board.  
 
• Town Caucus and Election Information 
o The town caucus will be held on March 14th, 2022. Two trustees are up for re-election: Dick and Donna. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The Friends had a short meeting. Fund-raising continues to go well. They will be holding off until February for 
book donations.  
 
• Board Comments 
o Kate asked about possible Friday hours, would it be half days? Kate suggested we have a food truck on 
Fridays as a way to promote the new hours. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick seconded by Donna. Meeting was adjourned at 8:27 p.m.

o January 2022 Director’s Report 
o January 2022 Adult Services Report

Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes February 17, 2022 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup, Michael-Ray Jeffreys 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:43 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Harry read rules for remote meeting. Took a roll call. 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Maia made a motion to accept the minutes of January 20, 2022 as amended.  Seconded by Kate. The 
motion passed unanimously.  
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of January 6, 2022 as presented. Seconded by Donna. The 
motion passed unanimously.  
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of February 2, 2022 as amended. Seconded by Kate. The motion 
passed unanimously.  
 
• Director’s Report 
o We are 2/3 of the way through the fiscal year. The budget has been 56% expended. Utilities have increased 
but are coming closer to the normal expected bills. Salaries have seen the biggest changes in the past 
month.  
o Muir has met with the Town Manager and the Finance Committee rep and will be meeting with the full 
finance committee on February 24. The funding for Friday hours has been requested. 
o A draft of the LSTA grant has been sent to a consultant for feedback. 
o There have been four interviews for the Digital Services position. Two more interviews are scheduled. 
o This is an in-between time for programs. Some have had sparse attendance but others have been very well 
attended. The take-home kits and craft labs have been very popular. 
o We have had some issues this past month with the DVR/Security cameras. We also lost heat this month and 
had to close the library. Laura asked if there was some remote low-temperature notification if this happened 
on a weekend. Muir said there are sensors and alerts in case that happens. 
o Some temporary changes have been made to the teen room to increase shelvin g. We will be getting rid of  
the spinning racks to make more room for teens. 
o The latest discussion on technology has been on the thin clients for public computing. Muir is looking for 
funding sources to replace the thin clients. 
o The mask requirement has been lifted for town facilities and employees. 
o Dick commented on the annual report – “well done”. 
 
• By-Laws Review Discussion 
o Harry thanked Dick for taking the lead on the by-laws process. After the By-Laws are approved Muir will bring 
the doc to the town counsel for review. 
o The board read through and discussed the By-Laws draft document.  
o There was some discussion of the ‘Advocacy’ section whether trustees had an obligation to support general 
legislation that may benefit other libraries. 
o Dick made a motion to accept the draft document as our up-to-date By-Laws as amended. Seconded by 
Kate. Motion passed unanimously. 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
 
• Trustee Handbook Discussion 
o Muir covered the Chapter 7 review – Budgeting and Financial Management. 
o Kate asked about the Finance Com meetings – are they live? The answer was that meetings are currently 
hybrid – live with remote accessibility. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The Friends had another short meeting. They talked about pop-up book sales. There was some discussion 
on allowing the friends to encourage their members to participate in Amazon Smiles for library fund-raising. 
 
• Board Comments 
o No board comments. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn Dick by seconded by Donna. Meeting was adjourned at 8:06 p.m.

Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes March 17, 2022 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup, Michael-Ray Jeffreys, Robin Venezia – Membership Chair Friends 
of the Library 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:48 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o Robin attended the meeting to give an in-person Friends update. The Friends website has been updated. 
There is now a Gmail contact email address and an Amazon Smile donation link. 
o There have been a lot of discussions about fundraisers. They are planning a native plant sale. The miniature 
golf fundraiser for the skate park is still under discussion but no plans have been made yet. The pop -up book 
sales are ongoing and doing very well. A full book sale will be planned for June. 
o They are trying to come up with ideas to make members feel more appreciated such as discounts or early 
admission to the semi-annual book sales. 
o They are also looking for ideas on how to attract new members and new members for management 
positions. 
 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of February 17, 2022 as amended.  Seconded by Kate. The 
motion passed unanimously.  
 
• Director’s Report 
o We are 75% of the way through the fiscal year. Utilities expenses have been as expected. 60% of the salary 
budget has been expended. The Digital Services position is still open. There are materials expenses that will 
be coming up in the spring. Some periodical renewals will also be coming up. 
o The presentation to the Finance Committee on 2/24 went very well. 
o The funding for the Friday hours has been included in the FY23 budget. This will be a ½ day or four 
additional hours. It will include two new staff positions. Laura asked whether the utility increase was 
approved. 
o The technology funding request for $8k is not currently in the FY23 budget. Michael-Ray pointed out that the 
technology request did not have a detailed list on exactly what would be funded. Muir said he would provide 
this. 
o Teen behavior has been a problem in the afternoon in the past few weeks. Muir said they had a few 
strategies for dealing with this. 
o The movie app Kanopy has seen a lot of usage. 
o The LSTA grant is still in progress. Most proposals will be funded. LPL has applied for a $10K grant. 
o The library will be switching over to a new version of Evergreen in April 
o Muir is working with Lydia on improving engagement for programs.  The number have been up and down. 
The take home kits are still very popular. Children’s programs in general have been very well attended.  
o There have still been some issues with the security cameras but these have been resolved. Four cameras 
were not recording and had some remote access issues. 
o The library will be working to move some files over to Google docs. Muir would like to replace the thin clients 
and he will get a detailed list to the town manager. 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
 
• By-Laws Review Discussion 
o Muir met with the town counsel. A formal, legal set of by-laws would have to go through town meeting and be 
voted on. Any subsequent changes would need to go through the same process.  
o Town counsel recommended that we change the name to ‘Rules and Regulations of the Lunenburg Board of 
Library Trustees’ to maintain a more informal document. 
o Maia asked if we maintain an informal document would it be possible for some future bad actor to ma ke 
unwanted changes to policies? Harry pointed out that changes require a majority vote of the board and Muir 
said he would question the town counsel further regarding this. 
o The document needs to be consistent with the town charter. 
o Trustees should read the current document in the February google folder and we will vote on approval at the 
next meeting. 
 
• Trustee Handbook Discussion 
o Muir covered the Chapter 8 review – Library Support: Fundraising, Foundations and Friends. 
o Muir discussed sources of funding: funding from the town, fundraising and the Friends group. 
o Also covered in chapter 8 is the libraries relationship with the Friends: understanding roles and working 
together. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Dick noted that he and Donna attended the town caucus and both were nominated for another term as 
Library Trustees. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn Dick by seconded by Donna. Meeting was adjourned at 8:37 p.m.

Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes May 19, 2022 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 7:03 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of April 21, 2022 as amended.  Seconded by Kate. The motion 
passed unanimously.  
 
• Director’s Report 
o We are in the final month, 91% of the way through the fiscal year.  Utility costs are still ok. Some invoices will 
be coming in. The library materials budget line is still short but the funds will be expended before the end of 
the year. Harry asked what happens to any unspent money. Muir said it goes back to free cash for  next year. 
o We have a new Digital Services Librarian, Francesco Buccella. Muir is discussing some new IT upgrades. 
o Susan will be attending the MLA conference. 
o The FY23 budget is $492k and will include two new positions for Friday opening. The hours will be 2 to 6 
P.M. This will hopefully start on July 1st. Planning to do food trucks on Friday afternoons. 
o We need to hire two part-time employees. This should be done before the school year starts. Also 
interviewing for library pages this week. 
o The union employees will be getting retroactive pay for the past 11 months to match the new bargaining 
agreement. 
o The summer reading program has been planned and there will be an outdoor kick -off party. Harry asked Muir 
if he had reached out to Main Street Bank for some funding and Muir said he will be meeting with them. 
o Muir talked to Heidi about restarting the school/library cooperative program this fall.  
o Muir had a meeting on the LED project. It should take about five days to complete. Security camera domes 
have been cleaned. They were accumulating moisture. 
o Replacements for the front desk computers, Maribeth’s computer and the cataloging computer are in the 
works. 
o There was an incident with three teen girls regarding changing screen savers in the Children’s area to 
disturbing images. 
o The LSTA grant is waiting to be voted on but it looks good. 
• Trustee Handbook Discussion 
o Decided to skip chapter 10 – Building a Library, as it is not applicable. 
 
• Trust Fund Update 
o There was a question about whether these funds should be taxable. We thought not, but Michael-Ray will 
look into the tax status. 
o We had some discussion on appointing a Treasurer to manage this process. Dick suggested that the 
Treasurer role should be added to the new By-Laws document. 
o Dick made a motion, seconded by Harry, to agree to go forward with Edward Jones and meet with their rep. 
Motion passed unanimously. Maia asked who other libraries use. Muir said he would check.  
 
• By-Laws Review Discussion 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
o Discussed adding the Treasurer role under ‘Officers of the Board’. Sign-off / directives would require two 
board members: the Treasurer and the Chair. 
o Dick made a motion, seconded by Harry, to postpone the vote to accept the By-Laws until the June meeting. 
Motion passed unanimously. 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The book sale will be held on Saturday June 4th. Setup will be Friday morning at 9 am. 
o There will be a members-only pre-sale on Friday night. 
o Robin gave an update on the plant sale and there was a treasurer’s update. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Harry went to a Craig Grossi presentation, author of ‘Craig and Fred: A marine, a stray dog, and how they 
rescued each other’. He recommended that we get him to give a presentation in Lunenburg, possibly in 
coordination with the schools. 
o Harry spoke to Michael-Ray. He will be becoming chair of the Select Board but he will continue as the library 
liaison. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick by seconded by Kate. Meeting was adjourned at 8:45 p.m. 
 
 
• Attached Documents 
o May 19, 2022 Agenda 
o May 2022 Director’s Report 
o May 2022 Teen Services Report

Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes June 16, 2022 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup, Michael-Ray Jeffreys - BOS, Nicole Groskreutz – Edward Jones 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:45 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Meet and Greet with Edward Jones 
o Nicole Groskreutz gave us a short introduction to Edward Jones and what they could do for us.  
o Our investments depend on the timelines and needs of the board and our risk tolerance.  
o Harry asked if she could tell us why we should work with her. She cited her background in be havioral analysis 
and the importance of community connections. She said she would want to support the board in their role in 
the community. 
o Donna asked how often our investments would be reviewed. Nicole said there are monthly reviews and there 
are also notifications that can be set up to flag changes. Their Advisory Accounts have various models and 
categories that auto balance. 
o Dick asked if the Friends use Advisory Accounts. They do. 
o Donna asked Muir how he sees our usage of these funds. Muir said he would use the same model as the 
Friends Endowment. 
 
• Board of Trustees Reorganization 
o After some initial discussion Maia made a motion, seconded by Kate, to keep the current slate of officers and 
add Donna as Treasurer. The motion passed unanimously. 
o Donna asked to be removed from the Budget Subcommittee. 
o The Strategic Plan Subcommittee was dropped as it is no longer needed.  
o Kate made a motion, seconded by Laura, to accept these subcommittees as amended. The motion passed 
unanimously.  
 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of May 19, 2022 as amended.  Seconded by Maia. The motion 
passed unanimously.  
 
• Director’s Report 
o We are 98% of the way through the fiscal year.  Excess salary funds will be redirected to office supplies and 
library materials. Utility charges are 83% and 84% respectively but will be expended close to 100% by the end 
of the fiscal year. 
o Kate asked why office supplies are so low (49%). Muir said they try to be conservative during the year and 
make it up at the end of the year.  
o Maia asked if the staff could get bonuses. Muir said there is no mechanism to do that.  
o Harry asked if a printer breaks isn’t that part of the town IT budget? Muir said it is not easy to get funds from 
the town IT budget for unexpected things. This is also complicated by the fact that the relationship between 
Dan (town IT) and the new consulting company is somewhat in flux. 
o The new Digital Services librarian, Francesco, is settling in well. 
o The library website is currently broken. The immediate need/goal is to have something up next week for the 
summer reading program. 
o Muir is interviewing for library assistants. He has a good candidate and will probably extend an offer. He is 
hoping the two assistants will start by July 1st.  
===PAGE 2===
 
 
o The first open Friday will likely be July 15th or July 22nd. Kate asked how we can effectively advertise the 
Friday opening. Some discussion followed on good ways to do this. 
o The staff evaluations are done. Muir will be meeting individually with each staff member. 
o Lydia met with school staff to demo available databases. 
o Next Wednesday is the summer reading kick-off. 
o A bereavement group has been meeting at the library, sponsored by UMass Health Alliance.  
o Volunteer Bill Tyler has been doing a lot of outside work around the library grounds. Robin and Lydia have 
also been doing outside work. 
o The shades will be replaced in the conference room. Some display hardware will be added for presentations.  
o The LED project will be happening next week. The contract scope changed and caused some delay. 
 
• Trustee Handbook Discussion 
o Chapter 11: Beyond Your Library: Resources and Support.   We agreed that there was really not much to 
discuss about this chapter. 
 
• Trust Fund Update 
o Michael-Ray expressed some concern about the language in the legislative change for the library trust funds. 
The language seemed to indicate that the town treasurer would retain control of the funds. This brings up the 
question of whether we can direct the investment of the funds and will we have the option to reinvest the funds 
as we choose. If this is the case can we still achieve what we want working with the town treasurer? Michael -
Ray will investigate this further and hopefully come up with a side-agreement between the Library Trustees 
and the Town of Lunenburg that will enable the Library do manage their trust funds as they intended.  
 
• By-Laws Review Discussion 
o Maia made a motion to table this until our September meeting, seconded by Dick. The motion pa ssed 
unanimously. 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The book sale raised about $1500, with $300 raised on the Friday night pre -sale for members. 
o The Friends have received 1800 membership renewals but would like to attract new active members.  
o The money for the Spring plant sale in 2023 has been allocated. 
o The annual meeting will be held on Saturday July 23rd at 9 am. 
o Starting on July 1st the Friends will be accepting book donations again. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Muir showed us a quick mock-up of a possible new sign for the building, with ‘Lunenburg Public Library’ on the 
front of the building above the main doors. He said he would explore design ideas and asked that we bring 
ideas to our next meeting in September. 
o Maia and Harry both noted that they have received very positive feedback about the library and the staff. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Kate by seconded by Dick. Meeting was adjourned at 9:17 p.m. 
 
 
• Attached Documents 
o June 16, 2022 Agenda 
o June 2022 Director’s Report 
o June 2022 Adult Services Report 
o June 2022 Teen Services Report 
o June 2022 Youth Services Report 
o LPL BOT Subcommittees 2022

Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes September 15, 2022 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy-remote, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia- remote, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup            
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:48 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of June 16, 2022 as presented, seconded by Kate. The motion 
passed unanimously by role call vote. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o The FY22 budget is 100% expended. The office supplies line is higher because some technology items were 
rolled into office supplies. We are ¼ through the new fiscal year and the  FY23 budget is 21% expended. The 
office supplies line Includes new laptop for new library assistant to be hired. The conference room has been 
upgraded. 
o State reports are going out. 
o Circulation has risen to about ¾ of pre-pandemic levels. This includes a significant increase in electronic 
circulation. Usage has continued to grow. 
o One of two new library assistants is on board. Julie Bozicas is the new assistant in the children’s area. We 
now have a third library page. Muir is still looking for a second library assistant and has a few good 
candidates. 
o We had a very good turnout for the first Friday opening. Next Friday there will be a movie. Muir is hoping the 
Lions club will be able to come to serve food.  
o The CWMARS assessment came in as expected.  
o We had a good summer reading program. There was a little less participation than last year. 359 people and a  
total of 218k minutes. Lunenburg no longer qualifies for the free lunch program so that wasn’t an option this 
summer. Muir will try to make alternative arrangements for the future. 
o HVAC has required some regular maintenance this summer. A leaky outside faucet has been replaced. 
Volunteer Bill Tyler has done a lot of landscaping work. 
o The conference room and community room blinds have been replaced as well as adding blinds to the rest of  
the children’s room windows. Some of the salary surplus paid for the blinds.  
o The new Cricut machine can be utilized by the public for craft projects. There will be some introductory 
workshops.   
o The library of things now has podcasting kits. Francesco will be doing some introductory workshops for the 
podcasting kits. 
o Muir plans on replacing the two workstations at the front desk. 
o The town received $3.5M from the Commonwealth of Massachusetts. The town manager is working with a 
consulting firm for eligible uses of the funds and will be accepting proposals. Proposals are due Sept. 30th. 
Maia suggested a zen/community garden adjacent to the library with a mental health focus.  
o Laura asked about the library of things. How do we know what’s available? Muir said items are catalogued 
and there is a display next to the front desk. 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
o Laura asked about the stem grant for snap circuits. There is a LSTA grant of $12,970 for stem learning and we 
will have a stem program for kids. There has been some discussion about using the stem kits to promote 
engagement with the Turkey Hill students. 
o Kate asked about promoting the new Friday hours along with increasing student engagement with the library. 
 
• Trust Fund Update 
o The bill did not get acted on during the normal session. However, the bill did get a final reading and is on the 
Governor’s  desk for signing. 
o The final bill enables the town treasurer to manage the library funds independently from other town funds. This 
was what was approved at town meeting. Harry talked to town manager, Heather, and noted that this was not 
what we originally intended. Michael-Ray is working with Heather to possibly instruct town treasurer, Myleen  
to act as the BOT directs, ie. we would direct her on our investments. 
o We should be able to create an account using the town tax id and the account should be tax free. The signed 
bill will be presented to Edward Jones to set up an account for the BOT.  
o Myleen asked for another study of the origin of the trust funds so she could responsibly manage them. All the 
documentation that is available has already been produced and made  available to the town. There was a lot of 
back and forth via email over the summer. 
o Harry said that based on the current information the library should be able to use these funds as we want to. 
o Laura asked how can she get background information on this as a new member. She also asked about the 
mechanism for using funds from the Friends.  Muir and Harry gave an overview of the procedures that are 
followed. 
 
• Library Sign Discussion 
o There was some discussion on the placement, font, and lettering. Also whether it would be directly on the 
building or on a backing plaque. 
o Maia said the sign on the old Ritter building could be confusing. Having the building readily identifiable is more 
inclusive and welcoming. 
o Muir will reach out to Johnson Roberts, the original architects, for possible ideas. 
 
• By-Laws Review,  Discussion, and vote 
o Dick suggested we defer to next meeting. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o Rob Bowen came to talk about incorporation for the Friends. Can get some umbrella insurance policy. 
o They discussed a prospective database to track changes in membership. They would like to accept credit card 
payments for donations and membership fees. 
o There will be a book sale in November. They will have a Friends only pre-sale in Friday night. 
o The Friends are still looking for some new younger members. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Dick asked Muir if he could update the BOT on his activities during the year so that info rmation can be 
included in his annual review.  
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick. Seconded by Maia. Meeting was adjourned at 8:38 p.m.

o September 15, 2022 Agenda 
o September 2022 Director’s Report 
o August 2022 Youth Services Report 
o September 2022 Youth Services Report

Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes November 17, 2022

The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:47 p.m. by Dick Mailloux.  
 
Public Comment 
• Robin wanted to thank the BOT for their help in setting up and running the Friend’s book sale. The Friend’s made 
$1339 from the recent sale. 
 
Agenda Items 
 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Kate made a motion to accept the minutes of October 19, 2022 as presented. Seconded by Maia. The motion 
passed unanimously by roll call vote. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o We are 1/3 expended, typical for this time of year. 
o Fridays are still very popular. Harry asked which is the busiest day? Muir said Wednesday is due to programs 
and meetings. Saturday between 10 and 12 is busy. Friday afternoon is the busiest weekday.  
o Victoria, the page, is leaving. Julie B. is re-retiring. Some interviews are scheduled for 11/21 and 11/22 and 
the opening has been reposted. 
o The updated strategic plan has been submitted to MBLC. 
o Lydia has had a number of programs. The ‘Bucket List Book Club’ has had significant interest. There was an 
indigenous history program that 20 people attended. 
o There was a teen program to make pronoun buttons. 
o The Turkey Hill school visits have been delayed. Muir will revisit this with a possible Jan/Feb start.  Maia asked 
if the 6th grade classes have been invited to come to the library? Muir said he would look into this. 
o The LED project is finally done. The HVAC system was a top priority for the capital plan requests. Exterior 
painting is also a high priority. Muir got a quote for this for $36k.  
o Some temporary computer stations have been set up just inside the entrance to the Children’s area.  
 
• Director’s Review 
o Individual reviews from the BOT members are in. Harry scheduled a subcommittee meeting for December 11th 
at 11 am to compile a completed review. We should be able to vote on it at the December meeting. 
 
• Trust Fund Update 
o Harry had a meeting with the town, Edward Jones and Bartholomew. Edward Jones will talk with Harry 
tomorrow about what is required to move forward. 
o Dick pointed out that we could just stay with Bartholomew and still choose our own investment strategy. He 
also said we should sit down in person with Heather and Mylene to work out a detailed list on how to proceed.  
o Donna asked if the BOT gets to decide how the funds will be invested. 
o Kate asked where the Touchstone funds are currently. Muir said they are in with the town funds.  
 
• Library Sign Discussion 
o Muir has been in touch with Johnson-Roberts Associates, the library architects, and they are willing to help us 
with some design ideas.  

===PAGE 2===
 
 
 
• By-Laws Review,  Discussion, and vote 
o Discussion was postponed until the December meeting. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The Friends did not have a quorum so there wasn’t a meeting. There was some discussion about ideas for 
chocolate tasting. 
• Board Comments

Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes December 15, 2022 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Maia Wentrup, Laura Boussom-remote, Michael-Ray Jeffreys 
     
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:45 p.m. by Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Kate made a motion to accept the minutes of November 17, 2022 as amended. Seconded by Dick. The 
motion passed unanimously. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o We are 37% expended. One half through the fiscal year. Susan attended a two-day YALSA conference in MD, 
requiring a $500 mileage reimbursement. Kanopy and some Digital Services expenses are coming up.  
o Harry asked about the 82% expended office supply figure. Muir said there was a one-time expense for a 
laptop. 
o A library page has been hired and will be starting after Christmas. The library assistant positions have been 
reposted with more detail and some interviews are scheduled for next week.  
o Muir purchased some professional development E-books for the staff, 14 or 15 titles. 
o Muir is participating in the CWMARS by-laws committee working on policies for the network. 
o Harry asked about the borrowers’ liability for lost items for the Library of Things. Muir said there is a policy of 
stating replacement cost when items are checked out. 
o Muir met with the directors of the Fitchburg and Leominster libraries on opportunities for collaboration and 
resource sharing. 
o 18 people participated in the Cricut machine workshop. There were 22 people at the Highwaymen concert. 90 
people came out for the craft program after the town tree lighting. 
o Zumbini for kids has been very popular. There will be an ‘All About Bears’ program on 12/28 put on by Mass. 
Audubon. 
o HVAC maintenance has been required about once a month. 
o The library has been using the new LunenburgMA.gov email accounts. 
o Michael-Ray asked about the impact on revenue of the fine-free policy. Muir said we are probably a few 
hundred dollars down. Some expenses have been incurred in tracking materials and sending out notifications. 
o Michael-Ray gave us an update from the select board. 
▪ The town received $3.5 million in ARPA funds from the federal government. The Select Board 
came up with criteria for grant proposals and did a town-wide email to solicit proposals. There will 
be two rounds of funding, one in Feb/March and one in the Fall. 
▪ The Select Boeard have been revising various policies. These include the appointment policy – 
appointing people for various town roles; and many ideas for environmental policies:  
• How can we be more energy efficient? 
• How we procure utilities. 
• Possible solar panels on the old town dump. 
• Species identification: Identify trees in the center of town.
