Lunenburg Council on Aging                                              Minutes Approved at March 8, 2022 COA Meeting 
Meeting Minutes 
January 11, 2022 
 
CALL TO ORDER:  9:35 
 
Present:  Director: Sue Doherty,  Chairperson: Deb Lincoln, Jane Rabbitt, Susan O’Sullivan, Barbara 
Brown, Pete Lincoln, Diane Nowd, Betty DiGiacomo, Cheryl Moisan, Judy Tarbell 
 
Absent:  Sarah Grant, John Pesenti, and Administrative Assistant, Amanda Koeck 
 
GUESTS: Lou Franco: Select Board representative. 
 
ANNOUNCEMENTS:  Deb thanked all for coming out on this frigid morning and for complying with the 
reinstated mask mandate.  
 
MINUTES APPROVAL:  Ms. Doherty requested that the amount of $26,000 that was recorded in the 
December 14, 2021 minutes for the FY23 COA Budget increase be amended to $17,000. 
Mr. Lincoln mentioned that his name was not included in the December 14, 2021 minutes as a 
contributor to the COA history segment of the New Board Member Orientation package and would like 
to amend the minutes to reflect that. 
 
Mr. Lincoln moved to approve the minutes as amended from the December 14, 2021 meeting. Susan 
O’Sullivan seconded. On vote, motion carried unanimously. 
 
CURRENT BUSINESS:  
1. New Council on Aging Board Member Orientation.  Susan O’Sullivan re-wrote the table of 
contents for our updated COA Orientation manual. Amanda updated the COA contact list. 
Ms. Doherty updated the acronyms list (Alphabet Soup). The orientation packet is complete 
with the exception of COA history. This will be completed by COA Board member Barbara 
Brown, Peter Lincoln, Judy Tarbell and Director Sue Doherty.  
2. The review of our By-laws is tabled at this time until Lunenburg town By-laws are reviewed.  
3. Ms. Lincoln asked Mr. Franco what the procedure is for COA members who would like to 
continue serving on the COA. He will look into this. 
4. The Lunenburg Finance Committee Meeting/Budget review takes place on 2/24/22 at 7 PM. 
5. Executive office of Elder Affairs Annual Report. The state continues to use results from the 
2010 census as the 2020 census has not yet been completed. Sue reported that the 
“unofficial” number of residents over age 60 is app. 3300. 
6. A new van driver has been hired. Benjamin McGinnis.  
7. The Personnel committee meeting regarding the Assistant Meal Site Manager/Meal Site 
Manager positions was cancelled. 
8. Memorial Drive Boulder Report. Mr. Franco reported that the Select Board discussed 
alternatives at their last meeting. The town survey was completed and it was determined 
that the boulders on Memorial Drive were over the town line. Two options were discussed. 
The first option was the Self Help option. The town would remove the boulders and the 
owner would pick them up at the Highway dept. The second option was to file with the 
State Superior court to get a court order for the owner or the town to  remove the boulders. 
The Select Board voted for the second option as there were concerns regarding Town 
===PAGE 2===
liability. The COA members expressed hope that this will be resolved soon as this issue has 
been going on at least since 2007. 
9. Director’s Report.  
 Covid 19 Report.  The Mask mandate has been re-instituted town wide by the Town 
Manager.  Ms. Doherty reported that large gatherings at the Eagle house have been 
temporarily cancelled (Bingo, Cribbage, Pancake Breakfast, Mrs. Bellow’s Breakfast 
Bar). The town of Lunenburg has purchased Covid-19 Rapid test kits. The Senior 
Center was given 100 test kits that are available free to Senior citizens starting 
today.  
 Building and Grounds Report.  The former company contracted as grounds keepers 
for the Eagle House are continuing with shoveling of snow this winter. They missed 
shoveling a sidewalk and were notified of this. The DPW were able to complete the 
shoveling. 
 Upcoming Events. 
Taste the Greek Diet, Friday January 14th at 10 AM. 
Veterans Informational Expo, Tuesday January 25th at 2 PM. This is a multi-agency 
event. 
Sand for Seniors. This program is limited to Seniors who are unable to purchase/pick 
up sand on their own due to physical limitations. 
Zumba with Amy Leblanc. This is a six week class for $25.00 Fridays at 1 PM, 
beginning on January 14th, 2022. 
Yoga for Vitality Class time is changing from six PM to 5:30 PM on Wednesdays. 
 
EAGLE HOUSE SUPPORTER’S REPORT: 
Pat Kennedy was unable to attend today’s meeting. Elaine Murphy was added to the Eagle 
House Supporter’s Board. There are still two vacancies on the Board. It was suggested that Lou 
Franco announce these vacancies at the next Select Board meeting when reporting on this 
meeting. 
 
NOTICES & COMMUNICATIONS:  None. 
 
ADJOURNMENT: Mr. Lincoln moved to adjourn the meeting. Ms. O’Sullivan seconded it. On vote, this 
motion was carried unanimously.  
 
UPCOMING Meeting SCHEDULE:  
Next Meeting: Tuesday, February 8, 2022 @ 9:30 AM 
 
Respectfully Submitted 
Jane Rabbitt, Council on Aging Vice Chairperson

Lunenburg Council on Aging                                             
Meeting Minutes  
March 8, 2022 
 
CALL TO ORDER: Ms. Lincoln called the meeting to order at 9:30 a.m. 
 
Present:  Director Sue Doherty, Chairperson Deb Lincoln, Diane Nowd, Pete Lincoln, Judy Tarbell, 
Barbara Brown, Susan O’Sullivan 
 
Absent:  Jane Rabbitt, Cheryl Moisan, Betty DiGiacomo, John Pesenti 
 
Guests: Eagle Supporters President Pat Kennedy   
 
ANNOUNCEMENTS:  Ms. Lincoln declared a moment of silence for COA member Sarah Grant and Boston 
Post Cane recipient Alice Quinn, both of whom passed away since the January meeting.  
 
MINUTES APPROVAL:  Ms. O’Sullivan motioned to approve the minutes from the January 11, 2022 
meeting, Mr. Lincoln seconded. On vote, motion carried unanimously.  
 
CURRENT BUSINESS:  
1. New Council on Aging Board Member Orientation Packet: Ms. Brown and Ms. Doherty 
worked together on developing a summary of the history of the Lunenburg Council on 
Aging. The board agreed to include a list of chairpersons on the board from its inception. 
The board also agreed to commemorate all members of the Council on Aging by listing their 
names on a plaque. Ms. Doherty suggested using money in the Council on Aging Gift Fund to 
purchase the plaque.  
2. FY23 COA Board Member Terms: Five terms are up this June: Mr. Lincoln, Ms. Rabbitt, Ms. 
Tarbell, Ms. O’Sullivan, and Mr. Pesenti. There is one year left of Ms. Grant’s term. Ms. 
Lincoln suggested making Mr. Pesenti an honorary member, as he wants to remain involved 
with the board as much as possible, but recognizes he may not be able to fulfill all the duties 
of a board member. Mr. Lincoln suggested he take the last year of Ms. Grant’s term, in order 
to fill that seat, and open up a full term to a prospective member. Mr. Lincoln, Ms. Tarbell, 
and Ms. O’Sullivan prefer to be re-appointed for another term. Ms. Lincoln visited Town Hall 
to see if they received any Talent Bank Forms, but there were none. Ms. Lincoln volunteered 
to get in touch with the Select Board to discuss staggering the terms as five out of eleven 
members’ terms are up in June.  
3. Lunenburg Finance Committee Meeting/COA Budget Review 2/24/22: Ms. Doherty is 
requesting an $11,344.01 increase in the COA budget for FY23. The increase would put 
another $2,000 in the office supply line, $1,358 will go toward the new Meal Site Manager 
position, and $7,985 will go toward the Assistant Meal Site Manager position. Formula Grant 
funds and the municipal budget will fund the Assistant Meal Site Manager position.  Ms. 
Doherty highlighted outside funding from various grants in FY22, along with prospective 
grants for FY23. Ms. Doherty also reviewed senior center stats from FY21. 
4. FY21 Executive Office of Elder Affairs Report: Ms. Doherty informed the board that there is 
a new way of reporting to the EOEA, and restrictions have loosened on how funds are spent. 
Ms. Doherty also reported that the U.S. Census numbers did not come back as anticipated. 
The FY23 Formula Grant should come out to $33,000, based on the $12 per capita figure.  
Unanimously Approved by COA at 
April 12, 2022 meeting 
===PAGE 2===
5. FY23 COA Capital Plan: Ms. Doherty reported that the patio project has been pushed out to 
FY24. Ms. Doherty has applied for a $50,000 grant from AARP to fund the project.  
 
6. Director’s Report:  
 Ms. Doherty informed the board that retired driver Jim McGuigan has been filling in 
once a week while she tries to hire a replacement. Meanwhile, the Thursday/Friday 
driver has given his notice, and will stay on through the month of March. Monday 
van service has been suspended until another driver is hired. Ms. Doherty also 
looked into turning transportation over to MART, but riders would not be given the 
same level of service.  
 Ms. Doherty informed the board that the Lunenburg Friendly Seniors donated 
$1,000 to the Council on Aging Gift Fund. 
 Covid-19 Report: No report 
 Building and Grounds report: see above 
 Upcoming Events: Ms. Doherty announced upcoming events for March at the Eagle 
House 
  
 
EAGLE HOUSE SUPPORTERS REPORT: The Supporters’ next meeting is March 11. Ms. Kennedy informed 
the board that there is a fundraiser for the Supporters at Hannaford where people get the opportunity 
to donate their change on their way out of the store. Ms. Kennedy also said her board is hoping the 
police department holds the car show this summer so the Supporters can hold a bake sale at the event. 
The Supporters board is hoping to take on two new members, Marilyn Talley and Barbara Kolk.  
 
NOTICES AND COMMUNICATIONS:  There has been no response from property owners about the rocks 
lining Memorial Drive.  
 
BOARD COMMENT:  none 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT none 
 
ADJOURNMENT At 10:56 a.m. Mr. Lincoln motioned to adjourn, Ms. Brown seconded. On vote, motion 
carried unanimously.  
 
UPCOMING MEETING SCHEDULE Tuesday, April 12, 2022 at 9:30 a.m.

Present:  Director Sue Doherty, Chairperson Deb Lincoln, Cheryl Moisan, Diane Nowd, Jane Rabbitt,

Mr. Lincoln informed board members that former board member Gloria O’Brien has passed away. 
 
MINUTES APPROVAL:  Mr. Lincoln motioned to accept the minutes from the March 8, 2022 meeting, 
Ms. O’Sullivan seconded. On vote, motion carried.  
 
CURRENT BUSINESS:  
1. FY23 Board Member Terms: Ms. Lincoln reported that she has consulted with the Town 
Manager’s executive assistant and Chairman of the Select Board about how to fill the seat 
left vacant by Sarah Grant. In the meantime, the Select Board’s office received a Talent Bank 
Form from former board member Jacqueline Dwyer, and was appointed to the Council on 
Aging to finish out the recently vacated seat. The board also discussed changing the way 
seats are reappointed because five seats are up this year.  
Ms. O’Sullivan motioned to recommend a one-time temporary appointment for two years, 
for one seat, and the remaining three seats will be three-year terms. Ms. Nowd seconded. 
Upon discussion, board members agreed to have Mr. Lincoln occupy Mr. Pesenti’s seat, 
while Ms. Rabbitt, Ms. O’Sullivan, and Ms. Tarbell’s terms will remain three years long. On 
vote, motion carried unanimously. 
Ms. Rabbit motioned to support the reappointment of Ms. Tarbell, Ms. O’Sullivan, Ms. 
Rabbitt, and Mr. Lincoln. Ms. Moisan seconded. On vote, motion carried unanimously.  
 
2. New Council on Aging Board Member Packet: The board agreed to add all former and 
present COA board members to the packet, and to denote the chairs of the board. Ms. 
Brown added that she’d like to add the history of the Bellow’s House (Now known as the 
Eagle House) to the packet, and she’d have a draft ready by July.  
 
3. Director’s Report:  
 Council on Aging Turns 55 Celebration: Ms. Doherty informed the board that 
she’d like to host an event at the Eagle House to celebrate the Council on 
Aging’s 55th birthday. Mr. Lincoln suggested tracking down phone numbers and 
addresses of former board members in order to invite them. Ms. Doherty said 
she’d call entertainers, and see if the police and fire departments would work 
the grill for a cookout. Ms. Doherty is also looking into getting an ice cream 
truck for the celebration. 
Approved as amended unanimously 
by COA at May 10, 2022 meeting.

for $10,000 to purchase a My Senior Center account, which would be used to 
accommodate the police and fire departments, as well as Nashoba Nursing, 
along with the office at the Eagle House to use for communications purposes 
when dealing with at risk seniors in town. 
 
 Covid-19: No report 
 
 Building and Grounds: Ms. Doherty reported that a tree fell down across the

CURRENT BUSINESS:  
1. Lunenburg Tax Relief: Ms. Doherty spoke with Principal Assessor CJ Carroll who informed 
her that establishing tax relief for seniors is not a fast process, and pointed out that only two 
towns in Massachusetts actually do provide tax relief for their seniors. Mr. Franco added 
that the town has established a group consisting of Select Board members Tom Alonzo and 
Lou Franco, the Principal Assessor, the Town Manager, and Finance Director Karen Brochu. 
The group is taking into consideration who would be eligible in terms of age, income, and 
assets. Mr. Franco emphasized the importance of getting the relief to those who are most 
deserving, and most in need. Mr. Lincoln asked what other towns do provide tax relief to 
seniors. Mr. Franco responded that Wareham and Andover have tax relief, and suggested 
that the variables between those towns and Lunenburg are very different from each other, 
and Lunenburg will have to devise its own plan. Once the concept is finalized it will need to 
be reviewed by the Select Board and then approved at Town Meeting, and added to the 
Town Charter.  
 
2. New Council on Aging Board Member Orientation Packet: Ms. Brown had copies of the 
packet printed, and distributed it to board members to edit. The packet will be discussed at 
the next meeting.

stigma around aging. Board members agreed that Lunenburg 50+ or Lunenburg Adult

agreed to create a column in the Lunenburg Ledger. Mr. Lincoln volunteered to write
