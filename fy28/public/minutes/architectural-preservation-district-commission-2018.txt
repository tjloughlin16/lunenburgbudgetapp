Meeting Minutes
Tuesday March 20th, 2018
Ritter Building
Call to Order: At 7:00PM
Present: Chairman McGrath, Corcoran, LaVeck and Dwyer
Review of minutes: Minutes from February 13th, 2018 were read and unanimously approved.
Administrative Review: Leslie Sziabady appeared to defend his application for a CTA at 920 Massachusetts Avenue. The
application was to replace the asphalt-shingle roof with another of the same sort. The commission readily approved the applica-
LaVeck felt that reviewing applications like Sziabady's was an inefficient use of time. He proposed a review of recent applica-
tions in three months time to consider ways of amending the guidelines streamlining the application process for situations like
this. The commission concurred.
Old Business: McGrath met with DPW director Jack Rodiquenz regarding the posting of signs to mark the APD. Rodiquenz
recommended that the signs be larger than those that McGrath originally proposed. McGrath will note the size and type of signs
used in other towns' districts and size accordingly. The DPW will provide posts and installation. The signs themselves would
normally be manufactured by D&G graphics in Fitchburg. The DPW has a budget for signs, which could be used for this project
with the town manager's approval. State approval will not be required for signs along route 2A. Rodiquenz was concerned
about "sign pollution" and recommended that historic district and APD signs be combined on one post.
McGrath also plans to help with restoration of the town pound: the gate will be sanded, painted and provided with a lock. Brush
will be trimmed back and certain trees may be removed if they appear to be threatening the structure.
Dwyer discussed his comparison of the current APD bylaw with the model bylaw suggested by Mass. Historic. He felt the current
bylaw was okay for now, and that more time would be needed to discuss and agree on any changes. LaVeck had also reviewed
the bylaws and agreed that it was too complex to propose a new bylaw for the spring town meeting. He also thought a 24-month
delay should be considered.
Dwyer also presented a letter to be sent to the owner of 53 Lancaster, which appears to be in the process of demolition by ne-
glect. The letter was approved with certain edits.

Meeting Minutes
Tuesday April 23rd, 2018
Ritter Building
Call to Order: At 7:00PM
Present: Chairman McGrath, Vice-Chairman LaVeck, Corcoran and Dwyer
Review of minutes: None reviewed
Special Business: The commission convened in a special meeting to discuss Warrant Articles 20 and 24 to be voted on at the
May 5th, 2018 Town Meeting
The wording of Article 20 makes its intent unclear, but Land Use Director Adam Burney had explained to McGrath that it was to
allow the Board of Selecimen to sell the Brooks House to a buyer who would move it to another location, leaving the lot empty.
The commission felt that it would be unlikely to grant aCTA to remove the Brooks House from the APD, since that would lower
the historical value of both the house and the district, and also because it was unclear if the APDC would retain any authority
over how the house was used once it left the district. The article was poorly thought through, as it raises many procedural ques-
tions, and was put forward without consulting the APDC. LaVeck motioned for the APDC to not recommend approval. McGrath
seconded the motion and it carried unanimously.
Article 24 intended to allow and fund the demolition of the old Primary School. A consultant called Vertex has reviewed the cost
of renovating town properties, and found that this building would be the most expensive: about $1 Milion. A group of citizens
petitioned to put Article 24 on the ballot, but had not followed the town process or open meeting policy, and had not consulted
with the APDC. Seeing as the article ran contrary to the mission of the APDC, Corcoran made a motion that the APDC not rec-
ommend approval. McGrath seconded the motion and it carried unanimously.
Next Meeting: Scheduled for May 8th.
Adjourned: 7:45 PM

Meeting Minutes
Tuesday May 8th, 2018
Ritter Building
Call to Order: At 7:01PM
Present: Chairman McGrath, Vice-Chairman LaVeck, Corcoran and Dwyer
Also: Tanner Cole, who is running for planning board, attended.
Review of minutes: Approved
CTA Applications review: A representative of 99 Lancaster Avenue appeared to submit the $25 filing fee and defend the appli-
cation for a CTA. The alteration is a re-roof of the garage. The replacement shingles where chosen to match the existing shin
gles on the house a much as possible. The APDC voted to waive any further requirements and approve the application.
Some confusion arose as to the serial number that should be assigned to the application. Dwyer said he would begin a register
of CTA applications with serial numbers for easy reference.
New Business: LaVeck reported that at town meeting the town had voted to postpone a vote on Articles 20 and 24 regarding the
removal of the Brooks House and Old Primary School.
Dwyer suggested that the APDC develop its own redevelopment plan for the Primary School and offer it to the BoS and the town.
Some discussion followed.
Old Business
53 Lancaster: Dwyer reported on his efforts to pump water out of the basement. McGrath reported that the owner intended to
sell the property in the next two years and move out, and so the job of the APDC should be not to restore the property but to
simply arrest the decay and let the next owner restore it. He recommended the following immediate measures:
• repair or replace gutters and downspouts, and extend spouts away from house.
• Eliminate flow of water from front stoop toward house (probably by removing stoop)
• Inspect attic and determine source of leak in north side of house
• Clear vegetation on and around house.
Dwyer volunteered to organize efforts to clear the vegetation.
