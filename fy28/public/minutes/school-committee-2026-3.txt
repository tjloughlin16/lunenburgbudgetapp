3. Public Comment Eden Perrine 18 White Tail Crossing I am going to advocate about school sports 
counting as gym credits for high school students.  I have multiple reasons behind why this would be 
beneficial for students and for the athletic department.  The first supporting reason this would be 
beneficial is that it would increase the number of people involved in school sports.  If students could free 
their schedule of a gym class and instead play an afterschool sport it would allow students to fulfill other 
credits while still being able to take classes they are interested in taking. An increase in the number of 
kids trying out for sports they would be interested in participating would be instrumental as the teams 
would be bigger.  In the fall of 2025, the boys varsity and jv soccer teams barely had enough students to 
field a game. They resorted to a large number of players swinging for both teams.  With more students 
on the team, they would be able to have two full teams, which would fill the field positions and still 
leave substitutions. There have been multiple studies showcasing that students who participate in after 
school sports have overall better mental health.  A study published in the journal of adolescent health 
found that students who play an afterschool sport had less stress and better mental health as young 
adults.  In a study, they surveyed 850 students from 10 different schools about participation in school 
sports and met up with them again 3 years after graduation and found those that were involved in school 
sports had lower depression symptoms, lower stress, and better mental health than those who did not 
play any sports.  The study co-author Katherine Sabistston even made the statements “team sports offer 
a heightened emphasis on group goals, social support, and sense of connection that provides more 
opportunity for learning adaptive coping strategies that can be essential or long –term mental health” 
She explains that students playing after school sports had a good increase in mental health even as they

months really did engage in a collaborative process to build budgets.  The town charter makes clear that 
we are responsible for the school district’s budget and the town and select board are responsible for their 
budget.  While the select board has an oversight role over the entire budget, they really are separate and
