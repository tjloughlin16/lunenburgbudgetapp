Minutes for 5/05/2016
Present: D. Blat, Greg Bither, Neal Sullivan, John Londa, Kelly Brown, Adam Burray
1. Meeting Called to order at 6:32 PM. No minutes were available to review/approve.
2. Review of new projects- J. Londa present a proposal for a new project lighting for Lunenburg
Primary School and Turkey Hill Middle School which would be aimed at conversion of T8
fluorescent lighting to LED lighting tubes with removal of ballasts. J. Londa explained that
he thought that the projects would have energy payback in the 6.7 year for Lunenburg
Primary and 3.9 year payback for Turkey Hill after energy rebates. The proposed library
project was discussed as not being able to be awarded to Guardian as they project needs to be
competitively bid to be a useable bid. Kelly Brown of DOER confirmed that Guardian does
not have the vendor status with Unitil that it has with National Grid and cannot undertake the
project as proposed.
3. Review Grant Extension and new time table- Kelly Brown suggested that the Committee
provide the information for the Re-Purpose Table and the DOER can get a quick turn around
on approval of new projects. The committee voted to have J. Londa combines the two school
projects and the town projects into a single Request for Proposal (RFP) with the Library and
the Teen Center included as alternates. The committee will meet after the bids are received to
approve the Repurpose table based upon the cost of the various projects.
4. Set date for next meeting- No meeting date was set for the next meeting.
5. Meeting adjourned at 7:38 PM.
Respectfully Submitted,

Minutes for 7/28/2016
Present: D. Blatt, Greg Bittner, Neal Sullivan, John Londa
1. Meeting Called to order at 6:36 PM. No minutes were available to review/approve.
2. Review of Present Projects- J. Londa presented the results of the re-bid of the Municipal
LED project results. DMH Electric of Sterling MA was the low bidder for work at Luneburg
Primary School ($88,000) and Lunenburg Public Library ($37,500). Three other bidder
submitted bids. A notice to proceed will be sent to the Town Manager for signature and
contract document will be send to DMH Electric in the coming week. The Teen Center and
Turkey Hill were not included in the bid. Lightec Inc provided a proposal to perform the
work at the Teen Center. J. Londa was directed to solicit a price from DMH to perform the
Teen Center work and then award the work to the low bidder.
3. Review of new projects- J. Londa present a proposal for a new project for Turkey Hill School
which would be aimed at improving the building envelope. This is likely a candidate for the
next round of grant funds.
4. Review Grant Extension and new time table- J. Londa informed the committee that DOER.
approved an extension of the grant funds until April 2017.
5. MS-HS and DPW solar projects: J. Londa provided an update on the solar projects for the
two buildings. Both projects will be present on the August 22 Special Town Meeting to allow
the Select Board to enter into a long term (20 year plus) contract.
6. Update on Net Metering. - J. Londa present information on the town serving as host for a
solar project located in Fitchburg. The Special Town Meeting scheduled for August 22d will
consider allowing the Select Board to enter into a long term contract with Borrego to purcase
the Net Metering Credits.
/. Approval of the G/ Repurpose Table- J. Londa presented a proposal to modify the projects
the Teen Center lighting projects to DOER on the repurpose table. The Committee voted
unanimously to approve.
8. Set date for next meeting- No meeting date was set for the next meeting.

Minutes for 10/05/2016
Present: D. Blatt, Greg Bittner, Neal Sullivan, Liz Murphy, John Londa
1. Meeting Called to order at 6:30 PM. Minutes for 05/05/2016 and 7/28/2016 were reviewed
and approved unanimously.
2. No Public Comment
3. Review of Present Projects- J. Londa presented the status of the Teen Center, Public Library
and Primary School lighting Project. The Director of the Library had requested that the
occupancy sensors be removed from the project. The committee continued to support the
installation of the sensors as a sound energy conservation measure. The projects were
expected to be started shortly
4. Review of new projects- J. Londa presented a proposal for a new project for Turkey Hill
School which would be aimed at improving the building envelope. This is likely a candidate
for the next round of grant funds. Elements Management had been requested to provide an
estimated cost and became the basis of the requested amount
5. MS-HS and DPW solar projects: J. Londa provided an update on the solar projects for the
two buildings. Both projects are still pending approval by the Select Board and are being
worked with Town Counsel and the interim Town Manager.
6. Approval of the GC7 Repurpose Table- J. Londa present a request to modify the grant by
including a request to fund an exterior envelope audit for the Turkey Hill Elementary School.
The Committee voted unanimously to approve the request. The Committee expressed a
desire to identify a vendor to look at the cost of conversion to natural gas at the public safety
building and to do a lighting survey of that building to support future grant requests.
7. Set date for next meeting- No meeting date was set for the next meeting.
8. Meeting adjourned at 7:45 PM.
Respectfully Submitted,
