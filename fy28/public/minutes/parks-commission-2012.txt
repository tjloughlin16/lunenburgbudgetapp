LUNENBURG PARK COMMISSION
Minutes for January 5, 2012 meeting
Meeting called to order at 4:30 pm
Present: Jeff Johnson, Tom Leppala, Bob Robuccio, Jack Rodriquenz
The meeting was called by Jack to find out what our priorities for next years budget would be
and if we had any questions for him. Our priorities are fully funding the Town Beach and Park
Expenses line at their present level. The beach is a very popular program and the expenses line
covers Jr Basketball custodians for the approx. 400 kids who participate. Jack asked if there
were any wishes if the override passes and we told him we would like to see a full time Park
person if it would work with a fully funded budget. He said he could'nt guarantee anything, but
would do the best he could.
Voted 3-0 to make the Beach and Park Expenses line our priority with a level funded budget and
a full time Park person if the override passes.
Voted 3-0 to accept the minutes from our last meeting.
RECEIVED & FILED
APR 25 2012
LUNENBURG TOWN
CLERK OFFICE

LUNENBURG PARK COMMISSION
Minutes of March 19, 2012 meeting
Meeting convened at 6:00 pm at the 23 West Street Office
Present: Jeff Johnson, Tom Leppala, Bob Robuccio
We received a packet of sheets from the CAFO looking for info about Park
programs, land controlled by us, participation in programs that the Park offers, and use of
various facilities. Each of us had reviewed the sheets prior to the meetings and filled each
other in on our respective areas. We found out that the Town Beach is not included as a
pool for swimming because it is located on a lake and is classified differently, nothing
that pertains to us. The sheets that pertained to the Park Dept were filled out and it was
decided that we could turn these back in to Kerry and have her contact us with any
questions.
Voted 3-0 to turn in the sheets
Voted 3-0 to approve the minutes from the last meeting
Meeting adjourned at 7:45
