THE COMMONWEALTH OF MASSACHUSETTS
TOWN OF LUNENBURG
ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS
April 5, 2018
Special Permit for Dimensional Variation
A Special Permit for Dimensional Variation was granted to Jacob R.J. & Catherine A. Proctor,
1431A Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA 01462, under the Code of Lunenburg § Section
250-5.2E (1) [b]. The petitioners were seeking a Special Permit for Dimensional Variation to
allow for the construction of two decks to be added to the existing home and didn't meet the rear
yard spacing requirement. The subject property is located at 173 Hemlock Drive, Lunenburg,
MA 01462.
The Board of Appeals of the Town of Lunenburg hereby certifies that a Special Permit for
Dimensional Variation has been granted pursuant to the Code of Lunenburg § Section 250-5.2E
(1) [b].
Board Members present: Alfred Gravelle, Chairman, James Besarkarski, Hans Wentrup, David
Blatt and Robert Cadle. Paul Doherty present but not voting.
Special Permit for Dimensional Variation has been issued to Jacob R.J. & Catherine A. Proctor,
1431A Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA 01462, affecting the rights of the owner with
respect to land and buildings 173 Hemlock Drive, Lunenburg, MA 01462, Book 8962, Page 187
and dated November 17, 2017 as described in plans submitted with the petition. Assessors Map
41, parcel 10.
And the said Board of Appeals further certifies that the decision attached hereto is a true and
correct copy of its decision granting said Special Permit, and that copies of said decision, and all
plans referred to in the decision, have been filed with the Planning Board and the Town Clerk.
The Board of Appeals also calls to the attention of the owner or applicant that General Laws,
Chapter 40A, Section 11 (last paragraph) provides that no variance or special permit, or any
extension, modification or renewal thereof, shall take effect until a copy of the decision bearing
the certification of the Town Clerk that twenty days have elapsed after the decision has been
filed, that it has been dismissed or denied, is recorded in the Registry of Deeds for the county and
district in which the land is located and indexed in the grantor index under the name of the owner
of record or is recorded and noted on the owner's certificate of title. The owner or applicant shall
pay the fee for such recording or registering.

and for his family.
Paul Doherty informed Mr. Proctor that no roofing can be attached to the decks in the future and
if granted must be constructed as proposed and in accordance with the engineered plans. Mr.
Proctor said that he was amenable to that.
Hans Wentrup asked if the construction would encroach on any other setbacks to property lines.
Alfred Gravelle explained that he visited the property and was familiar with the home; he said
that no other setbacks would be affected. Mr. Gravelle also added that the dwelling needed some
TLC and that the application and request before the Board made sense.
Matthew Proctor, 1431 Massachusetts Avenue (father of the petitioner) explained that he met
with the structural engineer and that the plans were available for the Board to review. Paul
Doherty requested that the engineered plans be included in the decision if granted.
Hans Wentrup and Chairman Gravelle agreed that the addition would be compatible to homes in
the area the location is consistent with the neighborhood. The Board members agreed that the
granting of a special permit would not adversely affect the neighborhood.
MOTION
After due deliberation James Besarkarski made a motion to grant the Special Permit for
Dimensional Variation as requested in accordance with the plans submitted and subsequently on
file with the Zoning Board of Appeals and with the condition that the engineer plans remain with
the file. The motion was based on the finding that it would not derogate from the intent of the
by-law or be more substantially detrimental to the neighborhood. David Blatt seconded the
motion.
