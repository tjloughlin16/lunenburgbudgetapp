Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
Continuance 
 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, October 24, 2018 at 7:30 P.M. at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor, 
Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Donna Carlo, 1432 Lancaster Avenue , 
Lunenburg, MA  01462 .  The property  which i s the subject of this case  is located at 1432 
Lancaster Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462 and owned by Theresa R. Carlo.  Assessors Map 
140-8. 
 
The petitioner is seeking a Special Permit to operate an in-home doggie daycare and boarding 
facility. 
 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by 
agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or 
the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.

NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, November 14, 2018 at 7:00 P.M. at the L unenburg Town Hall, 2nd 
Floor, Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Kenneth Tully, P.O. Box 595, 
Dunstable, MA  01827.    The property which is the subject of this case is located at 54 Maple 
Parkway, Lunenburg, MA and o wned by Kenneth A. Tully & Kevin  R. Tully.  Assessors 
Map 57/26. 
 
The petitioner is seeking a Special Permit for dimensional variation to allow a proposed dwelling 
to have a reduced front setback  due to the location of wetlands on the property as provided in 
250-8.3B.(2)(d) of the Code of Lunenburg.    
 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by 
agent or by attorney.  Copies of the applicatio n may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or 
the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
