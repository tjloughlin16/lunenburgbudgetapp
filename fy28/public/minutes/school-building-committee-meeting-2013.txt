Overview of the Preliminary Design Program Submission
Charlie Hay, Tappe provided an update to the ISBC on the March 21 Preliminary Design Program (PDP)
Submission to the Massachusetts School Building Authority (MSBA). The submitted document was shared
with the LSBC prior to the meeting. The PDP describes the fundamentals of the project, documents
existing conditions and District's educational program. Charlie noted that feedback collected at the early
March Educational Visioning and Planning workshops helped to develop the PDP. Charlie staed the PDP
includes Project options, space summary templates and noted this information will be revisedchange
during the planning process as needed. The next submission for the Lunenburg District will be the
Preferred Schematic Report, due on April 18 to the MSBA. This report will include three (3) options that
will be studied during the cureent feasibility phase including a preferred option.

Thomas C. Passios Elementary School Long Term Plan
The LSBC discussed the future of the Thomas C. Passios Elementary School as it narrowed down the
options that it would like to continue studying as part of the project. Loxi Jo Calmes mentioned that John
Londa drafted a white paper about the school (previously shared with the LSBC) and that both she and he
are available and would be pleased to discuss it with interested Town Committees or individuals. They
plan to brief the Board of Selectmen on this topic at its next meeting.
Project Option Matrix Review
Greg Cohan provided the LSBC with a copy of an options matrix for its review. Greg mentioned that the
matrix is intended to be a tool for the LSBC to use as it continues to compare options to project goals and
determine the preferred option for the project. LSBC members will review and fill in the matrix before the
April 10 LSBC meeting. JLA and Tappe will also fill in the matrix and share it at the April 10 meeting as
well. At this meeting, the LSBC will continue a discussion about the options and select the preferred
option that will be submitted in the Preferred Schematic Report.
2e.
Construction Delivery Methods Presentation
Greg Cohan summarized Design-Bid-Build and Construction Manager (CM) at Risk construction delivery
methods, explaining the points listed in the materials that were shared with the LSBC prior to the meeting.
The LSBC discussed the relative merits of both methods. The LSBC will select the construction delivery
method for the project by vote at its April 10 LSBC meeting. The Lunenburg Board of Selectmen will also
have an opportunity to be briefed on the two methods at its next meeting on April 2.

Call to Order
Michael Mackin, Chairperson of the Lunenburg School Building Committee (LSBC), called the meeting to
order at 5:05 PM. The purpose of the meeting was to review Lunenburg Middle/High School project
schematic design cost estimate to determine if any additional value management items could be
identified by the project team.
Review of Lunenburg Middle/High School Cost Estimates and Value Management Discussion
• Greg Cohan briefly reviewed the project schematic design cost estimate data that was reviewed at the
Lunenburg School Building Committee meeting on September 30, 2013.
• Mr. Cohan also distributed a file that the MSBA assembled tracking schematic design cost estimate
data on school projects (this information was requested by the Working Group and distributed in advance
of the meeting). Mr. Cohan explained that it is challenging to compare any of the projects on the
spreadsheet to the Middle/High School project. Some of the projects are more than a few years old and
the construction market has changed since then. In addition, the spreadsheet does not provide additional
context on the projects such as special circumstances that make the project unique (such as site
conditions, etc.).
• Mr. Hay distributed a file generated by its cost estimator, PM & C. The file provided some cost estimate
data on some recent school projects.
• The Working Group engaged in a discussion about the estimates and the potential value management
choices that it can consider. One approach would be to identify stand alone scope items that could be
listed as additional alternates. The team discussed that beyond the Greenhouse that it may be difficult to
identify additional alternates. Any alternates would need to be covered by Owner's Contingency (all costs
would be excluded by the MSBA).
• Mr. Hay distributed a list of additional potential value management items for the Working Group to
review and consider. None of the items on the list would negatively effect the project's educational
program/vision. Tappe and Shawmut will analyze these items for cost and the Working Group will be
provided with updated cost estimate data at its next meeting.
• Some members of the Working Group had general questions about the cost of green projects. Mr. Hay
provided some clarification on this topic. Designing a sustainable design school is not optional, it is
required. Under the MSBA program, every school project must qualify for CHPS or LEED in order to receive
any funding from the state. The MSBA requires that you meet the base standard of CHPS "verified" for
participation in project funding. They do not provide any added reimbursement for meeting this base

Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 6:00 PM.
Review of Project Presentation
The main purpose for tonight's meeting was to preview the project update presentation that will be
presented to the Finance Committee at 7:00pm. The LSBC reviewed the project update presentation and
proposed some additional changes and edits.
Monthly Approvals
The meeting minutes from the November 21, 2013 LSBC meeting will be reviewed for approval at the LSBC
meeting scheduled on January 6, 2014. The November 2013 invoice from Tappe Associates will be
reviewed for approval at the LSBC meeting scheduled on January 6, 2014.

• Next LSBC Meeting - Monday, January 6, 2014 at 6:00pm (Lunenburg High School Auditorium)
• Special Town Meeting - Tuesday, January 7, 2014 at 7:00pm (Lunenburg High School Auditorium)
• Special Town Election - Saturday, January 11, 2014 from 7:00am-5:00pm (Thomas C. Passios Building)
