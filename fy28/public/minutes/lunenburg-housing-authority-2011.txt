LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY
MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
October 18, 2011
Susan Szocik, Chairperson
Elizabeth Murphy, Vice Chair
maryllis Leet, Membe
heila Lumi. Membe
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member
Also Present:
Eugene Capoccia, Executive Director
Tenants present had no comments.
At 6:40 p.m. the Chair declared the meeting opened. Minutes of the
September 20, 2011 meeting were presented. On a Motion by Susan Szocik,
seconded by Sheila Lumi; the minutes were approved as presented. All were
in favor.
The Executive Director provided his report. Mr. Capoccia brought the Board
up to date on the renovations and insurance settlement. He reminded the
Board of the grand opening taking place on 10/20/2011. He mentioned that a
new energy TV (40" LED) had been purchased and installed in the
community room with the funding from UNITIL.
Mir. Capoccia then went over the Financial Report in detail. He noted tha
he Housing Authority continues to do well. On a motion by Sheila Lumi;
seconded by Elizabeth Murphy, the Financial Report was approved, all in
favor.
November
The Chairperson, Susan Szocik, presented a letter of resignation as of
2011. Ms Szocik explained she was moving out of
Lunenburg.
Being no further business, on a motion by Amaryllis Leet, seconded by
Susan Szocik, the Board voted to adjourn the meeting at 8:30 PM. All were
in favor.
