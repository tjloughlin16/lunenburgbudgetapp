  Public Hearing 
The Conservation Commission will hold a public hearing on a Notice of Intent by Eric Fowler for the development of a 4 lot residential 
subdivision at 711 Massachusetts Ave. 
 
Wes Flis appeared on behalf of the applicant. BP noted that a DEP number was issued and read the 
comments for the record. Wes Flis introduced the project and outlined the existing conditions the 
wetland resource areas and the orientation of the wetlands in relation to the proposal. He further 
described the methods of compliance with stormwater management standards and the review 
results from Graves Engineering they conducted for the Planning Board. Mr. Flis also outlined the 
proposed grading and erosion control proposal. He reviewed the total proposed disturbances in the 
riverfront showing it was under 10% not including the stormwater as it is permitted under the river 
protection act and outlined other options for construction of the proposal of the site. He cited 
===PAGE 2===
compliance with zoning regulations that applied and the economical feasibility of the proposal as 
presented. He noted that planning board and zoning board of appeals approved the proposed 
project, They will also need to apply for An access permit from Mass DOT from the main road (route 
2A) and they will apply for federal NPDES.  
 
JR asked if planning board granted stormwater permit. RB noted the DEP mentioned the 
alternatives analysis and wanted to know why it could not be reduced in buildings or configuration. 
Mr Flis noted that there was economic feasibility that was tied into the 4 units. RB asked for some 
evidence of the economic viability. Mr. Flis will consult with his client. RB asked about the current 
state of development of the property. It was noted that there are cart paths along the property, 
however it was mostly undeveloped. CC requested if there were any vegetation that would be 
removed within the 30 foot no touch zone. Mr Flis showed the commission on the plans that the 30 
foot zone was left proposed as untouched. BP noted that his site visit noted early successional 
growth with a high density of invasives. He noted that the area where the lots were proposed has 
piles of broken and plastic pots. Mr Flis noted that would be cleaned up. JR asked if the area was in 
at special environmental sensitive zones. Mr Flis reviewed the GIS database and noted it was not. 
The meeting was continued to February 2nd 2022 to allow the applicant to put together an economic 
feasibility narrative by a motion of RB and a second by JV with a unanimous roll call vote to approve 
the motion.

1.Mary Wilson – Report on Conservation Commission Earth Day event 
 
Mary Wilson made a presentation of the earth day activities from Sunday April 24, 2022. There were  
38 volunteers including the Lunenburg Snow Riders, the Honor Society and the Girl Scouts, with 
advertising from the Ledger. There were 146 volunteer hours put in for the efforts. She outlined the

2. Kroll- 398 Sunset Lane Request for field change 
 
Greg Roy outlined the prior approval history and the work involved. Mr Roy noted the revised house 
plan is smaller than what was previously approved, resulting in the house being pulled away further 
from the lake. He noted that the offsets were maintained. He noted the net result within the work of 
the 30 and 50 foot zones are reduced. RB noted that due to the reduced work, he has no objection 
of a field change. JR examined the docks to ensure they were previously approved. Mr Roy noted 
that the change is that one of the docks is being converted to a temporary dock. A motion by ML 
was made to approve the filed change. JV seconded and the commission voted unanimously to 
approve the motion.

Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 and Lunenburg Wetlands Bylaw and regulations as listed in chapter 239, The Conservation Commission 
will hold a public hearing on an amendment request by Nick Eaton to a previously issued Order of Conditions 208 -1240 for 411 
Townsend Harbor Road. The amendment request is for a wall replacement and replanting plan adjustment  
 
Jack Maloney appeared before the commission and outlined the changes requested for the 
amendment. JR noted he would like to see more aggressive tree painting that was proposed. RB 
mentioned he would agree with JR. ML asked about how long would it take to have shade value as 
proposed. Mr Maloney noted that there is preexisting pines trees that line the shore line and still 
have little value. He noted that the site has limited room and if the planting is too dense it could 
compromise the health of the trees. JR expressed the opinion there was room for a half dozen new 
trees.  
 
Nick Eaton noted that he felt he was fulfilling a request of cleaning out the trees and the beach and if 
there is too much planting, then he may withdraw his request for an amendment. BP noted that he 
has an approval for the prior septic system and all we are approving is the removal of 2 trees and 
replanting.  
 
MM noted that in his opinion that we can only re-examine the area under consideration for and 
perhaps under regulation six trees is sufficient. Mr Eaton reiterated that he thinks there are too many 
trees to be planted. He is attempting to fix the issues created by the contractor.  
 
MM reviewed with Jack Maloney the trees that were removed and the trees to be removed and 
reviewed the local by law and the 30 foot no touch zone. He recommended the commission examine 
a further combination of tree and shrub planting JR examined the say involved with the Hickory Hills 
landowner association. Mr Maloney made the commission aware that the association was notified 
as an abutter. JV expressed the opinion that three within the 39 and three within the 50 foot could be 
sufficient to re-establish the shade value. Associate member Tremblay examined the areas where 
the trees were proposed to be planted on the plan within the 30 foot zone. MM reviewed the shade 
pattern on the lot. JR moved that the amendment be approved with six maples to be planted in 
locations to be determined in working with the administrator to determine the locations. JV asked for 
clarification of location relative to the 30 and 50 foot zones. RB noted that placing within 30 feet 
would be consistent with the regulation. JR revised his motion to be consistent with the 30 foot zone. 
RB seconded. Nick Eaton asked for clarification. BP summarized the motion. Nick Eaton asked for 
three to be in the 30 and three to be in the 50-foot zones.  The motion was approved by a 5 to 1 vote 
with JV dissenting. Nick Eaton noted he would leave the wall and keep the trees. BP noted that it 
was his decision.

Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 and Lunenburg Wetlands Bylaw and regulations as listed in chapter 239 ,the Conservation Commission 
will hold a public hearing on a Notice of Intent by Glenn Maki to construct replace a culvert at 160 Massachusetts Ave.  
 
Mika Lassilla from SJ Mullaney represented the applicant. He outlined the plan for the commission 
as to the current condition of the culvert and the repairs necessary. He outlined the replacement 
dimensions of the culvert, the erosion controls and the methodology of how the culvert maintains 
flow, and wetland hydrology above and below the culvert. He reviewed the channel condition with 
JR. JR reviewed the lowering of the pond. It was noted the pond would be lowered ½ to one foot. It 
was noted the pond is 8 feet deep on average. RB asked if there was a schedule of work. The work 
outline was reviewed. It was noted that DEP has not issued a file number at this point. RB moved to 
continue to May 18 with a second by CC and a unanimous vote to approve 
 
 
Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 and Lunenburg Wetlands Bylaw and regulations as listed in chapter 239, The Conservation Commission 
will hold a public hearing on a Notice of Intent by Fitchburg Land property Owner LLC to construct a 367,000 square foot ware house 
and distribution facility with parking and storm water controls at 260 Fahlulah Road 
 
Chris Anderson reviewed the scope of work, the limit of work in relation to wetlands and the current 
condition of the site in question. He noted the restoration work previously approved will commence 
within the summer months. He outlined the condition of the river front as being disturbed. He also 
reviewed the limit of work and the direction of storm water flow. He reviewed the proposed alteration 
versus restoration areas as required under the wetland protection act. Jr asked the location of the 
200 foot river front. RB examined the parking areas outlined on the plans . He also examined the 
invasive species area. BP asked the applicant who was reviewing the project for the planning board. 
It was also noted the project is also in front of Fitchburg Conservation. The Applicant requested a 
continuance to May 18. RB asked if the commission could consider potentially hiring a third party 
inspector. RB requested that that an area of invasive species be remediated and the applicant 
agreed to do so. The owner of the property indicated a willing ness to fund a third party inspector. 
The meeting was continued until May 18 by a motion of ML and a second by RB with a unanimous 
roll call vote approving the motion.

Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 and Lunenburg Wetlands Bylaw and regulations as listed in chapter 239, The Conservation Commission 
will hold a public hearing on a Notice of Intent by Matthew and Tara Nazarenko for the construction of stairs, a landing and a dock 
within the 100 foot buffer zone at 160 Sunset Lane. 
 
Matthew Nazarenko was present to represent himself. He outlined the updated plan showing stairs 
and a landing. He outlined existing conditions and asked for a 120 square foot deck landing. He 
noted it was the same size that was approved for the Beers lot. It was noted that current site 
conditions has no growth. As such the applicant has proposed adding plantings that would slow 
erosion and prevent foot traffic. JR reviewed the square footage. He noted that he would be 
amenable to a smaller structure. Matthew Nazarenko noted that there was no trees going to be 
removed. MM noted this may be exempt from MESA review. JV noted he was satisfied with the 
plan. MM reviewed the MESA regulations and showed it was exempt. JR revisited the MESA 
exemption. JR proposed an alternate scope.  Matthew Nazarenko reviewed photos of the site and 
why he is proposing his current scope. CC indicated she could vote on the proposal. ML stated that 
he feels he is not in favor of the current proposal. JV reiterated it was a reasonable attempt to keep 
foot traffic off areas prone to erosion and the also noted previous approval was granted to another 
site. JR asked the applicant if he was willing to continue to the next meeting. The applicant noted he 
provided JR was invited to go onto the property and he declined. JR noted it was due to the 
presence of a quorum of members and didn’t want to discuss and cause an OML violation. JV 
moved to issue an Order of Conditions. CC seconded and the motion was approved by a vote of 3 
for (BP, JC and CC). Two were against. ( ML and JR) Lia Ellowitz noted that there still may be an 
NHEPS issue. BP asked MM to speak to town counsel concerning the regulation.
