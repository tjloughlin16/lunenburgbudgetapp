Meeting to order at 7:00 PM and A. Lockwood read “This meeting is being broadcast live and digitally recorded.”  
First item on the agenda, Facilities Director Update 
C. Ruth gave the following updates to the Commission: 
• The Town Beach House, all major structural work is complete. 
• Snack Shack at McNally field, contractor has been hired to remove the top half of the building. C. Ruth reminded the 
contractor to save the sign. 
• A Recreation Director has been hired. Angela Clement will be starting on Monday, March 27, 2023. C. Ruth stated her 
days will be Monday, Wednesday and Friday. 
• Town Beach staff, Beach Director and the lifegaurds from last year will be returning. 
• Budget update, an additional $5,000 in staffing has been added for programming and $10,000 for the purchase of service 
accounts. 
• Marshall Park, revised quotes have been received for the schemetic design. 
• Working on permitting to get the weeds treated at the Town Beach and Marshall Pond. 
• Marshall Pond light post will be taken out by the DPW. 
• Wallace Park basketball court surface is on the Capital Plan. 
• Veterans Park upkeep, C. Ruth will take the landscapers around when the weather breaks. 
• Fitzgerald, fence is on and locked and the sign has been rehung. The baseball field needs some work 
• Town Beach sand will be addressed in the Spring. 
• Powell Field vandalism damage had been repaired. The outfield needs to be seeded. 
• Marshall Park Softball Field, to be addressed when the snow is gone.

Joint meeting with Select Board 
Ana Lockwood called the Parks Commission meeting to order at 7:38 p.m. Present - A. Lockwood, K. Menard, D. Passios 
A.Lockwood presented the attached power point presentation to the Select Board. 
The board thanked Ms. Lockwood for the presentation. Mr. Alonzo asked what amount of ARPA funding is uncommitted. 
Ms. Lemieux stated $2.3 million. ARPA funds must be committed by December 31, 2024 and spent by December 31, 
2026. Ms. Lockwood stated her best case scenario would be breaking ground next summer after baseball season. 
Construction to completion would take 18 months; breaking ground by 2024 and completion by 2026. 
Discussion about the grant application process and deadlines for applying. K. Menard noted that their April 19th meeting 
is a community meeting with WOLA via ZOOM and we are starting the schematic phase. This will be the first of multiple 
community meetings. 
The Parks Commission also reported they are continuing on with their Earth Day projects and it's been very successful so 
far. Last week the Cub Scouts helped work on Veterans Park and two groups working at the Lane Property (Holman 
Street) removing invasive plants with more projects the upcoming weekend. More information is on the town website, 
Town Facebook page and also the Parks and Recreation page. 
K. Menard motioned and D. Passios seconded to adjourn at 8:14pm. Passed 3-0.
