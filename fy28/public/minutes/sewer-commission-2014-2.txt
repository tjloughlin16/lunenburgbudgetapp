now, as they’d prefer to pay the bills then collect it fr om the residents since they do not want a blemish on the  title.  Carl noted he 
likes the idea of a concise process to follow. Dave asked  about doing up a form similar to a ticket, showing the lega l information,

$50/day, noting that the lien holder is copied, and includ e a copy of the last letter. Carl continued on the list.   37 Graham was the

would charge a reconnection charge for new owners, wh ich helps us recoup the cost of cutting the pipe.  Bar b to serve notice to
