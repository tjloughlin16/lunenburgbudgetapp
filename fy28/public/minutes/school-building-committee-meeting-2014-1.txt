Revlew ofiMSBA Module 6- Design Details
Charlie Hay from Tappe Associates provided the LSBC with an update on the design development phase.
Mr. Hay mentioned that a series of design development workshops and meetings would be set up over
the next few weeks. These meetings will engage district faculty, staff, and administrators in the design

commence this spring. LSBC members expressed interest in informing local contractors about the project
in an effort to invite them to submit qualifications should they be elieible. The LSBC also discussed future
LSBC meeting dates. It was determined that the LSBC will meet every second Wednesday of the month.

confirm the 2014 meeting schedule and distribute it to the ISBC.

ELECTRICAL SYSTEMS
1. Main Electric Service:
A. A new primary service will be extended from Utility co services via underground
ductbank and manhole system to new Utility Co. pad mounted transformer
B. Secondary service from new pad mounted transformer at 480/277V. 3phase, 4
wire to main switchboard.
2. Normal Distribution System:
A. Main switchboard with surge protection (SPD) and ground fault protection on
main.
B. Distribution system will consist of conduit and wire feeders run from switchboard
to panelboards and larger mechanical equipment. Panelboards and dry type trans-
formers will be located in electric closets located throughout the building to serve
concentrated loads and manage voltage drop.
C. Surge protection will be provided on 120/208V receptacle panels.
D. Provisions will be included to serve proposed Green House.
3. Emergency Distribution System:
A. An exterior, ground mounted, self contained sound attenuated Diesel emergency
generator will power the following:
1) Emergency/Life Safety: egress lighting and exit lighting in corridors, assembly
areas, toilets and stairwells.
2) Standby: Miscellaneous systems such as kitchen walk-in coolers and freezers,
telephone system, security system, IT head-end, fire alarm system, one boiler
and associated circulator pump and controls.
B. Emergency panels will be located in two-hour rated electric closets.
C. Non-emergency (standby) panels will be located in separate closets fed via sepa-
rate automatic transfer switch.
D. Emergency feeders run outside two-hour electric rooms and shafts will utilize Mi
Cables.

Alternative Site Layout
An alternative site layout was briefly discussed that would either maintain student parking
in current design location and have a road that connected it over to the main project
access drive or relocate parking to the west side of fields with access from lot to main
access drive. It was noted that the negative attributes of these options was significant in
terms of traffic issues. Students entering and exiting into the main drive could create
significant traffic congestion at this location. It is anticipated that students exiting onto
access drive and turning left to exit onto Mass Ave will also create added congestion and
backups at this location. It was agreed by all present that there did not appear to be
enough of a benefit to pursue this option further.

6 . . Project Traffic Study Discusslon
Changes to Vehicular Circulation were discussed by the Group, Shawmut Design & Construction Is
working on a traffic circulation map for the duration of the construction. This map will be finalized and
distributed to school members, parents & abutters. The goal is to identify delivery truck entrance as well
as student drop off / pick up circulations.
There is a concern about traffic & road logistics not just on premises of the school but also around the
school area, A preliminary traffic study was performed by Tappe Architects during the feasibility study
phase, However, the impact on abutting roads is hard to predict without an extensive study of the future
traffic volume. Such study is possible however it will be at a cost which will have to be taken out of the
owner's contingency budget. The School Building Committee agreed not to proceed with the study at
this time. This item will be noted for future consideration if the Group feels that such study is necessary.
In order to attend to the Abutters' traffic Impact concern, Design & Construction Team will take the
following measures:
• A landscape architect will be present at the next abutters/ neighborhood meeting to revlew and
discuss the details of the planned landscaping as well as sidewalk and crosswalk locations.
• Shawmut Design & Construction wlil be providing additional signage at the entrance to the site for
clarity.
