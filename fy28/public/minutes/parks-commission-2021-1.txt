3.Rec Director, Kayla Wright, Updates:
! Winter programs are opened. Skating rink ordered. Fire dept will ﬁll it. DPW will

4.Non Departmental Beach Usage Discussion and Policy Creation:
! Kayla has researched several other towns policies to help come up with ways to 
approach our policy creation. Most had policies where the applicant had to be a beach 
pass holder. several required that everyone attending the event had to get day passes. 
All had requirements that a life guard be on duty, some with additional charges for that 
life guard/s. Karin highlighted some of the issues that we have had in the past. Beach 
rental requests have gone through the town manager, not parks. Rentals are often 
requested for Sundays or times when the beach is not open and she has had to go 
down to let the renters in. Policy needs to be created so that it is clear what our 
expectations are. Chris asked about lifeguards- suggested that we should require a 
separate lifeguard for events, even if the beach is open and staffed. Ana suggested 
having that in a ﬂat fee, instead of parsing out a separate lifeguard fee. Susanna 
clariﬁed that we should always require a lifeguard, even if people aren’t going into the 
water. Karin and Kayla suggested a two week out rental request, to allow time to 
schedule lifeguards. Karin expressed an interest in making sure that Parks properties 
===PAGE 2===
are usable and an asset to the town. Kayla will work on getting pricing/policy to us for 
next meeting. Chris asked if there are additional costs incurred from rentals, ie trash, 
electricity, etc. Kayla will base the fee to include all of that. All renters will need to have a 
Certiﬁcate of Liability. Jared Bowser, guest and lifeguard at the town beach, was present 
for this discussion. He advised to not have too many events at once, given the size of 
the beach. Karin asked Jared about Sunday events and extra lifeguard work. He 
thought that would not be a problem. Dave commented that he agreed that there should 
be a designated lifeguard for all rentals. He requested that there be some language 
added to the policy for other town departments that might want to rent the lakefront for 
an event.
5.Hunting on Parks Properties and Vote:
! Parks commission has been asked by Conservation Commission to decide if we 
will allow hunting on Parks properties. Karin asked the commission if anyone had any 
strong feelings either way. Chris suggested we say no to all hunting, with any weapons, 
on Parks properties. Ana added to clarify that this should include bow and arrow, not 
just guns, and not just for hunting but also for target practicing,etc. Chris ﬁnessed the 
point to say “outside of an ofﬁcial park program”-leaving space for an archery class to 
be held at Marshall Park, for instance. All in agreement. Policy discussion-Karin 
suggested we say “No Hunting or discharging of ﬁrearms or weapons on properties that 
come under the care and control of Parks commission, unless sanctioned by the 
recreation department.” Trapping will be added at our next meeting. 
6.Marshall Track:
! The track might not be workable until late spring given the water table/drainage 
there. Dave proposing to revitalize the cinder track at Marshall by removing the organic 
matter off of the surface of the track, tilling the cinder, and compacting it. He has a 
volunteer to help with this. Dave hasn’t had the chance to talk to the DPW director about 
it yet but he will follow up. Dave wants us to think about the No Dogs policy in Marshall 
Park-would like us to consider allowing dogs on the track but not anywhere else in the 
park. 
7. Spring 2021 Volunteer Clean Up Days
! Dave is thinking that park users and others could come up with a list of things 
that we as a community could do to tidy up some Parks properties. Little League has 
done something like this in the past. Karin likes the idea. Chris suggested an Earth Day, 
April 22, kick-off. Dave suggested we start somewhere other that Marshall in late April, 
as Marshall has a high water table. Karin had a discussion with Conservation 
Commission recently about trail creation over at our newly acquired Northﬁeld Rd 
property. Chris suggested that we emphisize the clean up days as “family” oriented so 
as not to dissuade anyone.

Chris said that there seems to be a consensus on the commission that if there is a 
skateboard park in town then it should be in Marshall park. He would like to see a dog 
park area there too. Dave mentioned a seasonal skate rink on a paved area, otherwise 
used in the other seasons. Chris asked about skating on the actual pond. Dave 
responded that it is less reliable in terms of safety. Dave brought up horseshoe pits. 
Karin brought up the Little league and was wondering what their needs are, given theri 
reduced numbers. We could possibly gain some space at Marshall that way. Karin 
requested that we consolidate and clarify this list. Susanna suggested that the list could 
easily be consolidated without losing any of the content because several of the points 
were redundant. Karin wants to be sure to include seniors in our planning of the park. 
Dave suggested a picnic table near the town pound on the pond. Ana pointed out that 
there are several things on our list that should be part of standard maintenance of the 
park. Susanna suggested that referring to benches beside the pond as a “pocket park”

install separately. Karin requesting that we remove the items that don’t belong on the list

total survey done professionally as soon as possible. Karin reminding the commission 
about our upcoming meeting w the Skateboard Park. Dave asked the commission-do 
we want to get something on the warrant for May-for consulting help or a survey of 
Marshall? Warrant for submission closes March 15. We will revisit in our February 
meeting. 
9.Discussion about creation of Parks Policy and Procedures related to maintenance 
and general use 
Karin: It’s time to be proactive instead of reactive. What priorities do we want to focus 
on? Lets work on policy in chunks instead of trying to do it all at once. Susanna 
suggested we go park by park to think about what each parks issues are. Ana 
suggested we approach it piece by piece and build a policy over time. Karin is thinking 
to start with maintenance. Procedure on how organizations need to report things to the 
Parks commission that need our attention. Examples are broken fences, trash, mowing. 
Karin will put it on our next agenda to discuss maintenance policy.

account that we could be under? Dave is concerned that we would be limited on what 
we could use the money for. Spending down under $30k v not having to. Karin not 
against changing to a different form. Susanna not feeling informed enough to discuss it 
right now. Will do homework for next meeting. Karin will add it to the next agenda.

1.Karin called meeting to order at 7pm. Attendance: Karin Menard, Susanna Jewell, Dave Passios, Ana Lockwood, Chris 
Sullivan, Recreation Director Kayla Wright. 
2.Discussion with Lunenburg Skate Park group regarding the pros and cons of building a skate park within Marshall Park and 
potential decision/vote on supporting a park and park size at this location. 
Griffin Caiozzo and Chris Roy did a presentation about why the Skate Park group feels that the Town of Lunenburg needs a skate 
park, how they got here to this point and the current status of their work.   
Karin asked the group to talk about the pros and cons of Marshall Park and added that this was the only property that the Park 
Commission has any control over out of the locations that Griffin and Chris mentioned. Karin said that a pro that she sees is that 
this is a piece of property that the Commission currently has control over so there is no other group involved in the governance of 
this property if a skate park did go in at Marshall Park. Chris added that the Commission is in the beginning process of laying out 
a Marshall Park master plan and this piece could fit in nicely and be visible and not tucked away. Ana Lockwood said she doesn’t 
agree with all of the cons that the skate group presented.  She said there are two access points to Marshall Park - one off Mass Ave 
and one off of Chestnut - and the Town is working on getting funds for building more sidewalks. She also said that the previous 
skate park location may not have been ideal but with the amount of acreage we have to work with we can come up with a better 
location. She thought the previous park had high use for awhile but had no care and maintenance. She cited the new playground at 
Wallis Park as an example of not using history to determine the future. She said she didn’t think the cons, in the way the Skate 
Park group presented them, were necessarily deal breakers. She what said she hoped to gain from this conversation was what it 
would take for the Skate Park group to say that Marshall Parkwas a good option but if Marshall Park isn’t a place they would 
consider she didn’t think there was much more to discuss. Aiden Moore said they are open to our suggestions but they think the 
original location is still the best but that a good park could still be built at Marshall depending on the location within Marshall 
Park. Aiden said that they would be concerned about the lack of visibility at Marshall Park. Dave Passios mentioned that his 
concern with the TCP location, which he also brought up at Capital Planning, is that there has been discussion about a small 
parking area for the Senior Center and part of the TCP property may be needed to complete that. He also didn’t think that 
aesthetically it would be a good location for it if TCP becomes town offices. Dave said that there are two locations at Marshall that 
he thinks would be very visible locations from the street at Marshall - where the old bathroom used to be is very visible from 
Chestnut Street and that the old growth trees in that are are already gone and also along the entrance driveway to the right as you 
are driving in from 2A. He also thought a pull station for emergencies may be a good addition to deal with emergency responses. 
Dave said that because this location is already a Park more grant funding may be available than if they went forward with the TCP 
location. Susanna said she really likes the idea of putting it in around the entrance area and asked Dave if he had taken a look at 
the size of the area. Dave said that he hadn’t but would like to get a better idea from the Skate Park group what the overall size

were a group of kids who were skating on Memorial Drive area and there were a lot of complaints. He said that when the first 
skate park in at Marshall one of the concerns expressed was that skaters wouldn’t use it because it was too far away from where

from people who will work or spend a lot of time at what is expected to become Town Hall and those at the Eagle House property

on them for the location at the center of town there should be a discussion with all the stake holders to see if something can be 
worked out. Karin chimed in that she has not heard anything from Town Hall or the Senior Center and that original decision that 
Parks made almost a year ago not to support that location was done independently. She also said that the piece of land by TCP is 
not owned by the Park Commission and its not their land to give so the only thing in her opinion that the Park Commission could 
do is to decide on whether to support or not to support and that is what the Park Commission did. She also said the vote by the 
Park Commission that Parks has no interest in maintaining or supporting a skate park on that property was not a vote saying not to 
build at that location. Ana added that there was nothing sneaky going on and that this board is interested in pursuing a skate park 
at Marshall Park. Peter added that at the end of the day there was property that was pledged to the skate park group and they have 
raised money all with that property in mind and the expectation that the skate park if built would be accessible to the student and 
the kids that it is intended to service. He said that the concerns that the student members of the skate park have are the same 
concerns that he has especially around transportation and he doesn’t think kids will be motivated to travel 1/2 to 3/4 of a mile to 
Marshall Park and that some of the opportunity of this project may be lost if its moved down there. He also said he doesn’t think 
there has been enough discussion with the stake holders about the TCP property. Ana said that we can only discuss things from the 
Park Commission side and we as a commission had a previous vote to not support the TCP location and that what is now in front 
the Commission is whether to support a skate park at Marshall Park and that our vote does not change what the skate group does 
and they can continue to pursue the original location. Karin let Peter know that he could reach out to any of the Park 
Commissioners to further discuss and that she doesn’t disagree with many of his points around the original location. She also said 
that the original choice of property never involved Parks in the discussion so it is a situation where one group made a decision 
without another knowing. She said that she believes a skate park could still go in there and believes it could be overseen by 
another group and that it wasn’t the goal of the Park Commission to try and strong arm anyone to move to a location that they 
don’t want to be in. Chris added that he has now seen the direction Marshall Park is going and feels this is a project that would fit 
in well there. Griffin asked if there was anything they could do to put the TCP location land under the Park Commission. Karin

TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
PARKS COMMISSION 
MINUTES 
Wednesday July 14, 2021 
6:30pm via Zoom 
 
1. Meeting called to order at 6:30. In attendance was Dave Passios, Chris Sullivan, Karin 
Menard and Ana Lockwood. 
2. Public and Board Comments none 
3. Approval of Minutes none 
4. DPW Director 
 1. Summer Programming Status and Updates. / 2. Free Summer Programs  
K Menard presented that The Ninja Warrior camp is currently running this week at TC Passios 
and another session is running next week.  The summer multi-sport camp is scheduled for the 
weeks of July 26th and Aug. 9th also at TC Passios. Both camps are run by Viking Sports. Refunds 
have been issued to all registrants for camps that have been cancelled. 
 3. All Other Updates.  
The town has advertised for a temporary park director and are currently accepting applications 
for the position.  The beach and swim lessons seem to be operating normally other than the last 
couple weeks of rainouts. 
5. Marshall Park Master Planning-steps for defining IFB and Scope of project. K Menard had 
circulated an email she received about the Marshall Park priorities list. She had a conversation 
with H Lemieux about where the responsibility lands for the Request for Proposals and that the 
Assist Town Manager would help.  Discussion on the lists and providing an overall scope of the 
commission ideas. Parking needs to be addresses, installation of a skate park and how it could 
be built, include a track and walking area, addressing the wetlands in the woodland areas, 
possible restrooms are some of the wish list points. K Menard will talk with J Belliveau to get to 
process going. 
6. Portable Toilet and Dumpster discussion and policy Select board would like a policy in place 
for the parks. The Board of Health has no regulations of porta pottys. K Menard got quotes 
from companies. One company quoted $100 a month for a standard unit and $140 for a 
handicapped unit. Included is a once weekly pumping, cleaning and restocking. A winter fee 
from November to March is an additional $10 for salting. K Menard quoted the price of $4-$500 
a month depending on how many units are needed. The policy created would be for the 
===PAGE 2===
Commission’s providing of facilities as opposed to the outside groups to bring in their own 
toilets, a different set of regulations that need to be created. Discussion on the park user’s 
responsibilities on bathrooms.   
K Menard gave an overview of the policy- 
1. Placement of portable toilets at all properties under park commission custody is at the 
discretion of the parks commission and shall be requested at time of field use application or no 
less than 7 days prior to delivery. 
2. A copy of the renter and service provider shall be provided to the Recreation Director, DPW 
Director, and Park Commission no later than 7 days prior to deliver.   
3. Portable toilets will be locked daily no later than sunset.  
4. Portable toilets shall be removed between November 1 and April 1 at all locations or when 
the location isn’t anticipated to be used by the renter for a season. 
 5. Cleanliness and upkeep is the responsibility of the renter. The Recreation Director, DPW 
Director and or Park Commission reserve the right to have portable toilets removed at the 
expense of the renter if units are not properly maintained including sanitization or failure to 
comply with these policies. 
A Lockwood made a motion to approve the portable toilet policy as written titled Parks 
Commission Sanitation Facilities Policy, D Passios seconded, vote was unanimous 
Dumpsters discussion K Menard reported that a camp’s trash was found in the dugout of the 
Little League.  There has been dump offs of TVs and couches.  We have a carry in carry out 
policy. A Lockwood reported trash left and dumped around the pond. K Menard sees three 
choices to address trash.  To institute trash cans back and have them emptied by the DPW 
Director rescinding the carry in carry out policy, not allow dumpsters at all or to allow them and 
create a similar policy of the portable toilets. 
K Menard gave an overview of the policy to allow dumpster. 
1. Placement of dumpsters at all properties under park commission custody is at the discretion 
of the parks commission and shall be requested at time of field use application or no less than 7 
days prior to delivery.  
2. A copy of the renter and service provider shall be provided to the Recreation Director, DPW 
Director, and Park Commission no later than 7 days prior to deliver.   
3. Dumpsters will be kept fully secured so it cannot be accessed outside of the renter’s events. 
4. All trash accumulated in the vicinity of the dumpster is the responsibility of the renter’s. 
5. Dumpsters shall be removed between November 1 and April 1 at all location  
===PAGE 3===
6.  Recreation Director, DPW Director and or Park Commission reserve the right to have 
dumpsters removed at the expense of the renter if units are not properly maintained and 
secured or failure to comply with these policies. 
Chris Sullivan made the motion to accept policy as written titled Lunenburg Park Commission 
Dumpster policy, D Passios seconded. Vote was unanimous. 
7. Discussion and consideration to rescind reciprocal agreement with Lunenburg Public 
Schools for use of Park fields. K Menard gave a recap on the history. The Recreation Director 
entered an agreement with the superintendent of the public schools that they could use the 
fields at Marshall Park for programs in exchange for park’s programming to use the outdoor 
field space at the schools for our programming needs. A request from the Rec Director got 
kicked back saying that because the vendor was a for-profit group, they would be charged a $50 
fee. The Camp got cancelled because they couldn’t afford the fee. School committee chair Carol 
Archambault and Superintendent Burnham joined the meeting. C Archambault commented 
that it was her board’s intent to work something out and the issue didn’t get to the board in 
time to vote to waive the fee. D Passios would like to have a consistent contact to communicate 
within the school system. Discussion on what falls under school committee jurisdiction or the 
Town boards. A School Board meeting will be scheduled to address waiving the fees and setting 
new policy. 
 
8. Discussion on invasive plants at part properties. D Passios talked to DPW Director about the 
maintenance contract with the landscaper at Marshall Park and the issue of poison ivy control. 
Chemical applications need to be posted when used is a question to be clarified. Will pass to 
next meeting. 
9. Open Space and Municipal Property Update A Lockwood reported that the title or status of 
Stump Cove has not been defined yet. The Open Space Committee is working on that. 
Discussion on the Pleasant Street, West Street and the wooded property that is offered to the 
Committee from Open Space.   
10. Other Business 
 a. Other business matters that may legally come before the board no known at the time 
of posting. None 
 b. Correspondence, Announcements and Public board Comment K Menard updated that 
the shed on the easement land has been removed. K Menard asked Commission members to 
take notice of any possible issues on the Commission’s properties adding that the Town legal 
counsel can take care of it. 
11. Next meeting date/agenda July 28th with Dog Policy, Poison Ivy at Marshall Park, Invasive 
Species. 
===PAGE 4===
12. Adjourn C Sullivan made a motion to adjourn at 9:12, A Lockwood seconded, the vote was 
unanimous. 
 
Respectfully submitted 
Dawn Pavone 
Minutes Secretary
