MINUTES OF HISTORICAL COMMISSION MEETING October 4, 2010
Members present: Patrick Slattery, Richard McGrath, Chriztine Foltz, Becky Lantry,
Vera McCarthy.
Meeting to order at 6:40 PM.
Minutes of September 14, 2010 meeting approved with correction of one typographical
error.
Marion Benson to send a letter re: grant money for historic projects. The clock tower
work in the Town Hall a possible recommendation.
Discussion about photos needed for the survey of the Town. We could apply for grant
from the MASS HISTORIC. It was decided to table this till next year - not enough time
to put together criteria for grant. Chriztine to speak with Chris Skelly and also to Kerry
Speidel about this project.
All members received the paperwork on the Ethics, Open Meetings and Conflict of
Interest Laws.
Jones House RFP has been filed and recorded. Pat Slattery had a discussion with Marion
Benson and Kerry Speidel re: trash compactors for Emerald Place that were to be
donated. Also Pat reported that Emerald Place will pay for the digitizing of material as a
donation.
Pat Slattery posed a question re: the demo delay at 86 Lakefront, a property owned by
Emerald Place. Was it possibly to allow Emerald Place to entertain offers of parting out
the property, rather than wait for the demo delay to expire? Chriztine reported that it was
At
access and it would be out of the area being worked upon at this time.
Article for the Ledger on Adaptive Reuse of Town Buildings as written by Pat Slattery
for insertion into the Lunenburg Ledger was presented, but Chriztine had to "red pencil"
it, so we did not make the deadline.
Elections were held for positions on the Commission: Chair - Chriztine Foltz
Vice Chair - Becky Lantry - Secretary - Vera McCarthy
Next meeting, November 1, 2010
Meeting adjourned @ 8PM
Respectfully submitted,
Vera McCarthy, Secretary

Lunenburg Historical Commission
Meeting Minutes
November 1, 2010
Present: C.Foltz, B. Lantry, P.Slattety, R. McGrath
1. Meeting called to order at 6:35PM(
2. Minutes were reviewed and approved for 10/4/2010 meeting with one typo noted.
3. Correspondence was noted as receipt of 2 magazines related to Preservation.
4. A brief follow-up discussion was held about the Demolition Delay vote for 86 Lakefront
Avenue. P. Slattery questioned why another patcel was allowed to be dismantled and sold ir
lieu of waiting thru the 6 month period while the subject parcel was not allowed the same
provisions. C. Foltz & B. Lantry stated that it was because the vote had already been taken
for the Lakefront property and could not be redone. Because of other considerations related
to the Developers of Emerald Place and their funding of the digitizing project the matter
was tabled.
5. A discussion of the Jones House article for the Town Meeting was held. C. Foltz will
provide a statement from the Commission for the Town Meeting outlining the historic
nature of the property. P. Slattery questioned the status of the tenants for the property. C.
Foltz reported that the expectation is that the property will be vacant by the end of the
P. Slattery reported that the proposal to have ScanSmart digitize the 34 rolls of microfilm
had been presented to the Developers of Emerald Place and they have agreed to donate the
money to cover the entire cost. The Commission voted unanimously to accept the donation
pending approval by the Town Manager. P. Slattery will contact K. Speidel.
7. B. Lantry distributed an article on the Houghton House for publication in the Lunenburg
Ledger for the town wide edition at Thanksgiving. C.Foltz requested that it be sent to her
electronically so she could review it further. The Town Hall article by P. Slattery is still
pending release. No articles have been submitted for an extended period.
P. Slattery presented the preliminary layout plans for the proposed addition to the
Congregational Church on Main Street for which he is the Architect. He noted that the
presentation was informational only and no action is required by the Commission.
9. B. Lantry to investigate brass plaques for historic properties, noting that the Jones House
already has a plaque in waiting.
10. Discussion of MHC grant possibilities was held. C. Foltz to update grant outline
pt
Respectfully submitted,
Patrick J. Slattety, Acting Secretary
