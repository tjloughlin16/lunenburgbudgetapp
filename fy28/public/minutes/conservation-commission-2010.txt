Town of Lunenburg
Conservation Commission
MINUTES
November 3, 2010
Rob Verge, Rich Bursch, Mark Murphy, John Lattanzi, JJ Cote, Jack Byrne, Sharon Jordan, Matt Marro, Jan Carrier
HEARINGS AND NEW INFORMATION FOR REVIEW AND COMMENT
7:15 PM ~ LUNENBURG VILLAGE ~ Gary Lorden to discuss footbridge ~ Gary discussed the possibility
of putting a footbridge over a wetland area. The ConCom was definitely against it. This was not a
hearing, just informational.
Sharon updated and gave us info on Park Watch and ATV's. A 4-H group might be interested in trail
work.
7:30 PM ~ 392 SUNSET LANE ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg
Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Request for Determination of
Applicability filed by Richard Tonole. The proposed project is for replacement of an existing railroad timber
retaining wall with stone/cement pavers. Only the wall will be replaced, no regrading or vegetation removal
proposed. No Show-will call
7:40 PM ~ MBTA RIGHT OF WAY ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg
Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent filed by Holly
Palmgren of M B TA (Lars Carlson of the Jacobs Engineering Group, Inc.). The proposed project consists of
bridge repair, track work and signal work within the existing right of way. Refer to Exhibit B for detailed
description. Mike Turgeon presented. A small amount of track will be shifted and replaced in several
sections. Reminded him to check on new gas line. He stated that there would be 52 square feet of
impact. SG-1 General Notes - Concerned about materials going into brook. He stated that they have
notified EPA and ConCom. National Heritage has been contacted regarding a trench of 60 feet long
and 30 inches deep. JJ/Rich Unanimous
7:50 PM ~ 7 OAKES LANDING ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg
Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent filed by Robert
W/Mary M Marchetti. The proposed project is for construction of a retaining wall to correct an erosion issue.
Would like to erect wall to protect wetland filter. The filter was a 301 Grant Project and was not
thought thru very thoroughly. JJ/Sharon
1 No 4 Yes
8:00 PM ~ 125 HOWARD STREET ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s. 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg
Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent filed by Gary T
Burton. The proposed project is for construction of a barn within the buffer zone.
Steve Marsden
presented. Barn will be used for a kennel only and part of it willo be inside the 100 foot buffer zone.
The ConCom had no comments or questions. Mark/Rich Unanimous

Town of Lunenburg
Conservation Commission
MINUTES
December 15, 2010
7:00 PM
Ritter Memorial Building
NO HEARINGS SCHEDULED
7:00 PM ~ Gaynor Biegelbach from Shirley will speak to the Commission about opportunities
for conservation projects that Americorps volunteers could offer us regarding trail repairs and
other possibilities.
Gaynor spoke to the commission about the volunteers from
Americorps. She stated that in February she will have scouts that will be working with
her on different projects. They will build wood duck house, eastern bluebird houses
and place them in different areas on conservation land. They will also do trail
maintenance. The commission was very receptive to her ideas and she will contact us
before the work takes place.
7:15 PM ~ Monica Farley ~ hunting, trash, etc. on ConCom property (Robbs Hill area and
Burrage Street).
Monica Farley is concerned about hunting on town property, she stated that she walks
with her dogs and is concerned about hunters that are bow hunting. The commission
stated that bow hunting is allowed but no discharge of firearms on town property is
allowed.
Members told her to be sure to wear "Hunter Orange.
Sharon mentioned the stewardship program we hope to have up and running by
spring, and hopes she will join.
7:30 PM ~ Signatures ~ 4
New Business ~ NONE
Public Discussion ~ Judith Graves of 21 Beachview Road issued a complaint
about water on her property as a result of the new house and landscaping next door
(11 Beachview). The commissioners would like to take a look at the site before
commenting further.
