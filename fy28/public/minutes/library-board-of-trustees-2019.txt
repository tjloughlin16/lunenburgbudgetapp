 Lunenburg Public Library  Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes  January 17, 2019 Wallace Community Room, Lunenburg Public Library    Attending: Dick Mailloux, Maia Wentrup, Kate McCarron, Martha Moore – Director LPL, Donna Saiia, Bob Conroy,  Michelle Walton – FinCom representative, Lars Widstrand, Harry Kubetz (participating remotely)   The LPLBT meeting was moved from the Sudolnik Room to the Wallace Community Room due to heating issues and called to order at 6:50 p.m. by Chairperson Dick Mailloux. Dick asked that all votes be taken by roll call as to include Harry.   Public Comment -There was no Public Comment  Approval of Minutes The December 20, 2018 minutes were reviewed and amended by Dick, Kate and Donna.  Kate made the motion to accept them as amended. Lars seconded.  Motion unanimously approved.     Director’s Report (Presented by Martha)  FY 2019 Budget – 58% into the budget year and 43% spent see attached  Budget Planning for FY ’20- Martha met with Heather Lemieux to discuss the budget and explain the need for an increase in staff hours and the advantages of adding an additional union position for Marybeth Balboni. Heather was not in favor, explaining that the town cannot accept additional benefit costs and retirement liability. Martha has contacted the union to file an application with them and try a different approach. Dick recommended increasing the Director’s Salary line from $60,000 - $70,000 to reflect current salaries paid to directors in towns of similar size, thus increasing the ability to attract and negotiate with highly qualified applicants. On 1/27/19 there will be a 5 cent increase in postage stamps so Donna recommended a $50 increase in that line item. The TM budget target is $413,592 and the LPL request reflecting the additional monies for director and stamps is $434,200. Donna made a motion to accept the amended budget, seconded by Lars, unanimously approved.                                                                                                                                                                                         Trust Fund Update combined with Trust Fund Expenditure Guidance –Still investigating how to access funds and the original intent regarding Bartholomew accounts. There are 6 interest-only funds. Michelle said there’s no problem spending money from the earnings and that those funds not designated as interest only require only a source document and then can be spent. Touchstone Funds are either expendable or non-expendable and whoever makes a donation must sign off on an intent for the money. Martha submitted a Donor Intention Form to Myleen and Karen for their approval. Dick made a motion to accept the donor form, Bob seconded, and the board voted unanimously to accept. Donna emphasized the need for a tie-back to that form on all expenditures.  Staff – Teen Room renovations are done and all is well in the Children’s Room! (see attached) Muir asked if he could attend future board meetings to be better prepared for a smooth transition for whoever replaces Martha and the board enthusiastically replied yes.

 Approval of Minutes 
o The minutes for April 18, 2019 were reviewed. Some amendments were suggested. Motion to approve 
minutes as amended was unanimously approved by roll call.

o Chair: Harry Kubetz was nominated for chair by Kate McCarron. Dick Mailloux was nominated for chair by 
Lars Widstrand. Harry Kubetz was elected chair by a roll call vote.  
o Voting for Dick were Lars, Donna, and Dick.

scheduled for the week of May 27th. One is a second interview.

budget planning cycle. 
o The board determined that the evaluation forms should go out after the Sept. 2019 meeting – around Oct. 
21st and be back by Nov. 7th to give the Personnel Committee time to review the evaluations and make salary 
recommendations for the Director.

Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes  
September 19, 2019 Sudolnik Meeting Room, Lunenburg Public Library  
  
Attending: Dick Mailloux, Maia Wentrup, Kate McCarron, Donna Saiia, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob 
Conroy, Lars Widstrand, Harry Kubetz. 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 7:00 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz. 
Public Comment 
 There was no public comment. 
  
Agenda Items 
 Approval of Minutes 
o The minutes for June 20, 2019 were reviewed. Two small changes were suggested. Motion to approve 
minutes as amended was unanimously approved. 
 Director’s Report 
o FY19 budget is 98% expended. 2% will be returned to the general fund. Electricity and heating have been 
the largest expenses. 
o Library is one quarter through FY20 and is on target budget-wise. 
o Tech updates have been completed: Added three wireless access points.  
o State aid will be decided in January 2020. There are two decision rounds. The town is currently around 11k 
population and is gradually approaching the 15k population threshold which will affect future state aid. 
o Muir gave a staff update: Lydia Gravell is the new Adult Services Librarian, Cheryl Stillman is the new Library 
Assistant and page Harrision Taylor has left. 
o Muir provided an extensive update on the THES library visits. 
 Three teams per week will visit for one hour. 
 They will alternate between the craft room and library areas, mostly the children’s room. 
 There will be no special changes to library procedures to accommodate them.  
 Library staff will not assist with decisions on what they can access. 
 They can check out books from the teen/YA room. 
o A long discussion followed on the new THES policies and the packet of information which will be provided to 
parents of students. (These are detailed in attached documents below) 
 Trust Fund Expenditure Guidance/Process/Progress 
o Harry did some work on this over the summer. 
o The current rate of return is low and the consensus is to transfer the funds to Touchstone. Muir can currently 
access the Touchstone funds. 
o Harry met with town counsel. Some questions that have arisen: Should we appoint a treasurer? Should we 
direct the town to manage the funds? Based on the returns, should we use Touchstone? 
o Dick pointed out that these are not town funds and we should direct the town to put them where we want 
them. Myleen is looking for direction from the board and we should tell her to transfer the funds. 
o Dick made a motion to submit a request to transfer the funds from Bartholomew to Touchstone, pending a 
recommendation from town counsel. Motion seconded by Donna. Motion passed unanimously. 
 Trustee Goal Setting 
o This agenda item was tabled for a future meeting. 
 Plan for Director Evaluation 
o Already covered in a previous meeting. 
 Friends of LPL Update 
o Book and bake sale will be held on Sat. Oct. 5th. Volunteers are needed on Friday for setup and Saturday 
afternoon for cleanup. 

===PAGE 2===
 
o Some plans are under discussion to utilize the catch basin next to the library for a rain garden and possible 
educational opportunities. 
o Robin had a Friends table at a recent farmer’s market in the center of town. Also attended by Harry with one 
of his bull dogs. This attracted significant interest in the friends and resulted in 10-15 applications, $25 in 
donations and $30 in book sales. 
 Board Comments 
o Donna mentioned a recent ‘Beatles band’ concert in Townsend and suggested it might be nice for 
Lunenburg. 
o Harry: Jeanne Raboin pointed out that the library is not listed on the town website under boards and 
committees. 
o Dick: Mentioned a first amendment activist group that is filming in various libraries. There is no current 
prohibition on filming in the Lunenburg library. Is this an issue for town counsel? 
o Dick noted that the flag in the Sudolnik conference room needs to be replaced. 
 Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick, seconded by Lars. Meeting was adjourned at 8:48 PM

Glowacki – Bartholomew & Company, Brian Jamros – Bartholomew & Company. 
Absent:  Maia Wentrup, Donna Saiia  
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:45 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz. 
 
Public Comment 
 There was no public comment 
  
Agenda Items 
 Trust Fund Expenditure Guidance/Process/Progress 
o Since Representatives from Bartholomew were joining us, the Trust fund agenda item was taken up first. 
o Muir provided the group with an overview of current situation and what we had done so far. Harry noted that 
we have not been happy with the rate of return. 
o Brian gave us a brief presentation on Bartholomew and what they do. Talked about how and why we might 
transfer the library funds from the ‘Legal List’ funds which would give us more flexibility in terms of 
investment. 
o Dick questioned why the library funds are linked to the town’s stabilization funds. This is due to state laws 
which govern categories of investment. 
o Harry questioned the historical responsiveness of Bartholomew and Brian responded that the onus was on 
the BOT to research the documentation and that the lack of documen tation limits our options. 
o Myleen said she may have found some additional documentation and noted that we need trust documents to 
transfer any funds. 
o An extended discussion followed on our various options: 
 Kathleen: Moving the funds into prudent investments, a different category, could provide us with 
more equity options. 
 Harry: Why should we choose Bartholomew going forward? 
 Brian: We have extensive experience in managing municipal funds and may be the cheaper 
alternative for the BOT. 
 Dick: Can we move Touchstone funds to prudent investments? 
 Myleen: The BOT must certify the fund is a gift not a trust. 
 Karen B.: We don’t have adequate documentation on these funds. 
o Result of the discussion was that Brian suggested we go before town meeting to re -categorize the funds, 
which would allow us to move them to BOT or library accounts, give the BOT control over the funds and 
allow us many more investment options. This also requires a change by the state legislature. 
o Brian will forward some sample language for the town meeting warrant article based on some other town’s 
experience. (This sample has already been forwarded to the BOT by Myleen) 
 
 Approval of Minutes 
o The minutes for October 2019 were reviewed. Motion to approve minutes was unanimously approved wit h 
two minor edits. 
 
 Adult Services Report 
o Lydia provided us with an overview of adult services and an update on some new groups and programs.  

===PAGE 2===

o Muir mentioned some restrictive policies on E-Books by MacMillan that seemed to display a lack of 
understanding on how libraries work. 
o A quote has been received for the redesign and replacement of a row of shelving in the Children’s area. 
There is a possibility that Hannaford may donate money to fund this initiative and that is being explored. 
o We had a discussion of library fines and the possible option of operating fine -free. 
 Muir presented data from 2012-present on fine collection numbers. 
 Some fines may be paid online through CWMARS. We receive a credit for these which is used for 
E-Books. 
 This is the beginning of what we anticipate will be an ongoing discussion of the possibility of going 
fine-free. 
 Harry asked that Muir come in with a recommendation for the BOT. What to do and how?  
 
 Friends of LPL Update 
o Friends have scheduled the 2nd annual ‘Cookie Walk’ fundraiser and are discussing other fundraising ideas. 
 
 Director Timeline & Update 
o Dick: When does the budget have to be submitted? 
o Muir: Early January. Salary should be included. 
o Muir will make a budget presentation to Fin-Com in Mid-February. 
o Looking forward for BOT: We should have Muir’s evaluation done for the December 2019 meeting.
