Town of Lunenburg  
Land Acquisition Committee  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
                                        
                                             MEETING AGENDA 
Wednesday, April 24, 2019; 6:30 PM 
Ritter Building, 960 Massachusetts Avenue 
Upper Conference Room 
Lunenburg, MA 01462 
 
AGENDA 
 
 
 
1) OPEN MEETING 
 
2) NOMINATE & VOTE FOR CHAIRPERSON 
 
3) SET MEETING SCHEDULE 
 
4) ENTER EXECUTIVE SESSION PER M.G.L. c. 30 § 21, REASON # 6;  To consider the

detrimental effect on the negotiating position of the governmental body. 
 
5) REVIEW PROPERTY & COMMITTEE CHARGE 
 
6) DISCUSSION WITH PROPERTY OWNER & REPRESENTATIVE  
 
7) EXIT EXECUTIVE SESSION 
 
8) ADJOURN
