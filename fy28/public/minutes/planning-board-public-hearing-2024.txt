The hearing was to review an application for Site Plan Approval and a Stormwater Management Permit for 
236, 240 & 244 Summer St (Assessor Map-Parcel Numbers 117-0028, 117-0029, and 117-030). Applicant is 
S&L Property Management, LLC, 236 Summer St. Owner is S&L Property Management, LLC, 236 Summer St, 
Lunenburg, MA 01460. 
 
Bill Hannigan of Hannigan Engineering represented the applicant. S&L Property Management is owned by 
Stan and Lydia Miller of Miller’s Paving. There are no jurisdictional wetlands in or around the property. The 
project is situated on several parcels, a plan to merge them into one lot has already been recorded with the 
registry of deeds. The two structures on the property will be razed and replaced with a new building. The 
property’s driveway is wide enough for vehicles (i.e. dump trucks) to turn around. The side of the property 
facing residential buildings has a solid-wall fence which will be extended and continued to the front of the 
property, with proposed landscape screening on the left side. 
 
Catch-basins on the property would be directed to a water quality unit which discharges to an underground 
storage system of chambers beneath the parking lot. This would include infiltration into the ground and peak-
rate mitigation, which then dissipates into an existing ditch in the back of the property. 
 
For the sewer system, a gas trap is located just to the west of the building which connects to a conventional 
sewer line. The building doesn’t need a fire protection line as it’s less than 7500 square feet.  
 
The exterior will include night-sky compliant wall lighting for security purposes, as the building won’t be used at 
night. 
 
 
 
===PAGE 2===
p. 2 of 2 Planning Board Minutes, 2024-06-24 
B. Hannigan requested several waivers of certain requirements of the Planning Board’s Rules and Regulations 
for Site Plan Approval & Special Permits: 
 
1. 4.1(f)(2) requires a scale of 1”=40’ for plans. Hannigan Engineering provided 1”=20’. 
2. 4.1(f)(22) requires a photometric map showing on-site footcandle information. Hannigan Engineering 
requests a waiver since the project would only include night-sky compliant wallpack lighting units above 
doors and around the office areas, rather than any pole-mounted lighting. 
 
He noted that a comments #1 and #2 in Graves Engineering, Inc’s 2024-07-18 letter were not applicable as 
they erroneously cited requirements of the Summer St Overlay District, which the project wasn’t being 
submitted under. He outlined each of the upcoming changes to the plan which would address GEI’s other 
comments.  
 
T. Willsmer asked R. Harris whether the above described changes could be included as a condition of approval 
for a Building Permit. He confirmed that it was, but advised the Board to accept or decline the waivers before
