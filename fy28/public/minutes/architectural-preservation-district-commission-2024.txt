Ritter Building 
Call to Order: At 7:30 PM  
Present: Chairman Sculimbrene, members  Lockwood, McGrath, 
Public Comment: 
 
1) None 
Minutes Approval: 
 
1) 9 April 24 Meeting – Motion to approve by Member McGrath, seconded by Lockwood, Approved by all 
 
Correspondences: 
 
1) Chair met by text and email with Chris Ruth to determine the status of the Town Hall and Ritter projects. 
Ruth did not have any defined plan to move the projects forward. Chair will send another note to Ruth and 
the Town Manager asking for a plan to move the projects forward. 
 
Public Hearing: 
1) The hearing convened at 7:54 pm. The chair described the purpose of the Public Hearing as reviewing and 
approving the Architectural Preservation District Design Guidelines. He provided a summary of those 
changes and requested comments from the public and members of the APDC. There were none. A motion 
to approve the revised guidelines was made by Lockwood with a second from McGrath. They were 
approved unanimously by a roll call vote of Sculimbrene, Lockwood and McGrath. 
2) The chair brought the public hearing to a close. 
 
New Business: 
 
1) Sculimbrene and Lockwood met with Town Counsel to discuss expansion of the Architectural Presevation 
District (APD). Counsel advised it was possible but would require a vote at Town Meeting. The issue of 
solar panel installs on historic properties in the APD. Counsel indicated that public law encourages solar 
installs with few restrictions. Offering financial incentives to property owners to assist with historic 
preservation work on historic properties would require special legislation and a vote at Town Meeting. 
Counsel was not aware of any community in the state that offers such incentives and more research would 
be needed.. 
2) There was discussion among the members of the APDC regarding a potential merger of the Historic 
Commission and the APDC. Additional research would be needed prior to pursuing such a plan. 
a.  
3) 925 Mass Ave 
a. The chair provided an update on the meeting hosted by the Town Manager with various officials 
on the subject property. While outside of APDC jurisdiction (the building is not 75 years old) the 
===PAGE 2===
chair offered his support for reuse of the existing building. He did have concerns about the 
potential clean up standards as all of the necessary environmental screening and evaluation work 
has not been completed. The consultant hired by the town via a state program will prepare a final 
recommendation on use of the site for consideration by the Select Board. 
 
 
Old Business: 
 
1) 50 Main Street-a list of contractors for the property owner to consider for repairs is still pending. 
 
Other Business: 
 
1) 763 Mass Ave proposed demo-Member McGrath provided an update on the property. No action has been 
taken by the owner as required by the Demolition Delay letter issued by the Historical Commission.  
 
Public Comment: There was none. 
 
Next Meeting:  Meeting Schedule:   
Thursday, 13 June, 2024 
 
Adjourned: 8:29 PM by motion from Lockwood, second by McGrath and approved by all.

Meeting Minutes
Thursday, July 11, 2024
Ritter Building
Call to Order: At 7:38 PM 
Present: Chairman Sculimbrene, McGrath, Lockwood
Public Comment:

None

Minutes Approval:

Minutes for 9 May were not available. Minutes for 13 June  need some revisions so those will be approved at the meeting in August.

Correspondences:

Chair Sculimbrene reported on his appearance at the Select Board. It was suggested by the vice chair of the Select Board that a meeting be held to provide some background information on the APDC to the new board chair, Ceasar Nuzzolo. Chair Sculimbrene will take the lead in setting up that meeting.

Current Business:

CTA for 920 Mass Ave.
It was anticipated the applicant, Courtney Zivojinovic, was to be in attendance. The chair had identified to town staff upon receipt of the CTA and drawings, that missing information on materials was needed. Since the applicant was not present and that information requested by eamil had not been provided there was discussion about approving the CTA subject to receipt of that information. Member Lockwood was concerned this approach could lead to further delay. Chair Sculimbrene said it was not his intent to approve or disapprove the specific items only to ensure the submittal was complete. Chair Sculimbrene moved to approve the CTA for 1010 Mass Ave upon receipt of the missing information with a second from Member McGrath. All voted to approve the CTA conditionally.
Old Business:

Brooks House Preservation-no additional information on developing a plan.
Work on the Town Hall and Ritter Building-no status update on the work. 
763 Mass Ave-there’s been no report provided by the current owner as required by the Demolition Delay letter. 
Letter to APD property owners-there was extensive discussion about sending the letter. It was agreed the letter would not be sent at this time. Member Lockwood indicated he would prepare a draft change to the APD bylaw for the meeting in August. 
50 Main Street-no change in condition of the steeple. 

Next Meeting: 	Meeting Schedule:  
Thursday, 8 August 2024

Adjourned: 8:40 PM
