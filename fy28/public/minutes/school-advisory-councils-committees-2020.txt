Minutes Lunenburg Schools Athletic Advisory Council  
Monday, January 6th, 2020, 6:30 pm  
Location: Lunenburg Middle/High School, Room D132 
 
Brian Lehtinen: Lunenburg School Committee, member, LHS AAC Chair Jim Bunnell: Lunenburg 
Schools Athletic Director 
 
PRESENT:   Kim Mannone,  Jo Weigold, James Bunnell,Lou Bettencourt, Brian Lehtinen, Jean 
Proctor, Robin Bursch, Jane Gallagher, Ernie Friend 
 
ABSENT: Amanda Waltz,  Brenda Pajari, Mary Jean Davis,  Rhonda Jardini-Yeates, Kim Hedlund, 
Nicole Bourdelais, Tammy York, Kim Hedlund, Kathleen Jones, Kelley Laine. 
 
1. CALL TO ORDER: 6:31 pm  
2. PUBLIC COMMENT: NONE 
3. MINUTES: Review of Minutes November 4, 2019, Approved unanimously  
 
4. New business:  
● Vote for a new secretary- none one nominated or volunteered was tabled for next 
meeting.  
● Updated Members- no comments. 
● Athletic Director update: Summary of the Winter Season briefly, coop swim with 
Leominster is not happening and has been disbursed due to Fitchburg State pool being 
closed. Lunenburg will be looking for another co-op.  Boys and Girls Track are doing 
great and underway. Picture times for the yearbook will be posted. Football Coach 
Boone stepped down and we will be posting soon.  
● Priorities for 2019-20: discussion took place on increasing community service 
opportunities for our athletes outside of NHS requirements. Jean Proctor and Robin 
Bursch will take the leads in a subcommittee to explore this. Jean Proctor inquired about 
a baseball cage and fundraising for indoor baseball equipment. She will be in contact 
with James Bunnell as this progresses and on the details of the location of the baseball 
cages.   
 
Old Business: Fundraising requests: Cross Country and Track and Field fundraiser at Chipotle on 
February 1st, 33% of proceeds go to Cross Country and Track and Field. Approved unanimously.  
Fundraising request of Indoor Track and Field to do a Snapraise fundraiser. Initially was 
approved but later denied by School Committee policy 1303 due to its “crowdfunding” nature. 
 
 
6. FUTURE TOPICS  
Community service opportunities for athletes. 
One Booster to support teams and other boosters in need. 
 
7. MEETING SCHEDULE:  Next meeting is planned for March 2nd 2020, 6:30 pm

Athletic Advisory Council 
Meeting Minutes 
March 2, 2020 
  
Attendees: Brian Lehtinen, Jo Weigold, Jean Proctor, Nichole Bourdelais, Ernie Friend, James 
Bunnell, Jane Gallagher, Kelley Laine, Kim Mannone, Robin Bursch, Audra Sprague 
Absent: Kathleen Jones, Louis Bettencourt, MJ Davis, Tammy Yourk, Eric Short 
 
Meeting called to order at 6:30pm.   
 
No public comment.  
 
Review of Minutes from January 6, 2020 meeting.  Not a quorum to approve. 
 
New Business: 
 Vote for New Secretary was tabled. 
 School Committee Policy 1303 on fundraising was discussed. State Law 501.3c was 
discussed for boosters as well as whether boosters have obtained a EIN, tax exempt 
number, file taxes every year. Discussion took place on insurance and protecting boards.  
  Athletic Director update: lacrosse will be doing a 2 year coop, hockey and basketball 8th 
graders are eligible, lacrosse boys, softball, girls lacrosse, tennis is with Fitchburg. 
Friends of Baseball donated a batting cage, this Thursday, March 5th will be a community 
reading day with 10 senior athletes volunteering.  
 
Old Business: 
 Fundraising Requests: Lacrosse $10 fundraiser approved. Baseball canning fundraiser 
approved. Track selling bracelets approved. 
 One Booster Club: It was discussed that a unified booster would be important and need to 
grow over time but at this time we are looking for volunteers, possibly outside this 
committee who would like to take this on this initiative.  
 Community Service update: All high school kids should know about it and athletes 
Coach driven activities that are team building to promote service. It was discussed that all 
boosters promote team bonding local community service work that is built into the 
program in or outside the season communicated from Boosters to Coach.  
 Student Representative: no discussion. 
 Next Meetings: Monday, June 8th, off cycle, August 31st, off cycle. 
 
Meeting Adjourned at 7:34pm

with covid safety pre-cautions was approved unanimously. Football yard sale with covid 
safety pre-cautions was approved unanimously. Football boosters plans to encourage all 
players to fundraise with the gold cards and split the players on the yard sale and car 
wash. A golf raffle with covid safety pre-cautions was approved unanimously.
