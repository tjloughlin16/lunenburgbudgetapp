2. Appointment- meeting with LSIC – Joanna Billotta noted that the town of Shirley approved of the plans as 
submitted. Dominic Mergingolo presented the annual treatment plan and noted the dates o f the surveys they 
conducted. He noted that there was a difference in species in high abundance that had not been predominant 
along the shore line before. Species include leafy and grassy pondweed, clasping leaf pondweed ,snail seed 
pond weed , tape grass and non native curly leaf pond weed. He attributed the increase to the lack of satisfactory 
drawdown and general environmental conditions allowing them to germinated He reviewed the locations of the 
64 points noted on the maps as well as data points added to ensure proper representation of the species 
detected. Mr. Meringolo explained the methodologies used in determining the extent of treatment for this year.He 
further explained the chemicals to be used and the dosages. 
 
Wendy Gendron noted to the commission she had also toured the shoreline after receiving Dominic Meringolos 
data. JR asked if the treatment will make the 40 percent weed cover for a healthy lake. Ms. Gendron noted that 
there would be surface area that are not treated and treatment is not 100% effective. Mr. Meringolo noted that the 
treatment areas are very narrow and named different species in the lake that would contribute to diversity and a 
healthie percentage cover. JR also asked about fanwort management. Mr. Meringolo noted that they are 
designated is considered more of a pilot area instead of  viewing it as an experiment CL expressed satisfaction 
on the criteria used for consideration of treatment. Cl asked for clarification of the 2020 numbers. Mr. Meri ngolo 
noted that the data was for 2019 and 2020 was not entered yet as that would happen after the fall survey. CL’s 
examination of the numbers with Wendy revealed that percentages appeared to be skewing to a higher density.  
Dominic cautioned that the data tables for last year’s data were from the spring and not the fall ( total year of 
2019). CL asked for clarification of the data for the fall report. Cl asked about areas 10 -13 for treatment and the 
rationale behind its selection.  Mr. Meringolo noted that this area was one where the newer weeds were density 
had increased. CL expressed support for the selection of the area for testing fanwort treatment. CL asked if 
abutters were notified of the use of clipper in the cove? Mr. Meringolo noted that there was no water use 
restriction other than the 5 days irrigation restriction similar to the Diquat restrictions the residents have 
historically encountered. Mr Meringolo noted that due to the thickness of the fanwort the cove was an excellent 
candidate. CL asked if there were any native weeds susceptible. Mr Meringolo noted that the effects were similar 
to Diquat and noted that the area they are treating is predominantly fanwort, although there may be some Lilly 
pad morbidity.  
 
JR compared the use of chemicals for fanwort between Hickory Hills and Lake Shirley as they were slightly 
different. Mr Meringolo noted the chemical used for Lake Shirley was more advantageous due to costs and the 
small area to be treated. JR moved to accept the presentation as stands CL se conded and the commission 
unanimously approved except RB as he abstained due to not hearing the entire presentation
