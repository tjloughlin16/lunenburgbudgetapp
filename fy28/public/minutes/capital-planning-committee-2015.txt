a. Jamie notified the Library of the low priority of their request for an added $28,476 to move and

6. Borrowing and Prioritizing Capital Requests:
Please refer to PRIORITY WORKSHEET Third Pass for TM discussion.xisx distributed by Jamie to the
committee on January 7 as a follow up to our January 6 meeting. This is especially germane to
points 6.b. and 6.c. below. As described in point 6 b., the priority changed as a result of this
meeting.
a. The CPC agreed that borrowing is an option when funding large capital requests, provided
borrowing adheres to the following guidelines:
• The Town may Borrow to fund projects costing $100,000 or more.
• The term of the loan will not exceed the projected useful life of the asset.
• Total debt service costs are less than 4% of the tax levy or 3% of the total omnibus
budget, whichever is lower.
• The Town Manager will determine the term of borrowing, whether to make a down
payment, and if so what amount the down payment should be.
b. Regarding the priority of funding capital requests, based on the current list, John moved to raise the
priority of $34,000 for upgraded technology for Town Departments from 11 to priority 9. Joanna
seconded his motion. This would dramatically increase the probability that the upgrade to Town
technology is funded. The motion was approved 5-0.
• The priority of the street sweeper and the police speed sign dropped from 9 to 10 and 10 to
11 respectively.
c. After a discussion on funding, Jamie moved that the CPC recommend that the Town fund the ten
highest priority items with a total estimated cost of $1,028,778, and that the CPC recommend the
Town Manager consider borrowing to pay for one, two or all three of the capital purchases that cost
$100,000 or more the dump truck, the backhoe, and the street sweeper. Heather seconded his
motion and the committee voted 5-0 in favor.
• Kerry stated that as this exceeded the capital budget by $178,778 she needed to determine
whether the Town could afford this in FY2016, or in the case of borrowing, whether the
debt service costs in future years would affect the Town's ability to fund requested capital
purchases in those years.

• This was an old bid-actual costs would be higher than the $40,000 requested.
• The scope and specific requirements of the Town Hall renovation were not yet settled.

done without additional funding.

Trustees the Library concur to use these funds to replace the boiler and the controller.

contributed to the burst pipes in the library and also to boiler is inefficient.

with the way the network is used.
• This upgrade would support the new Junior-Senior High School now under construction.
c. The Police Department: The funds left over would allow funding of the next highest priority item,
the speed monitoring and recording sign which costs $12,000.

motion, and the committee voted 4-0 to approve the minutes.
4. John Londa described potential plans for Turkey Hill Middle School (THMS) and what that would entail. The
main points:
a. THMS is now 46 years old and the threshold for a decision about renovation or replacement of
buildings are usually when they are 50 years old.
• Major areas that would need to be renovated to prolong the useful life of THMS are the
roof, and the unit ventilators.
• Structurally the building is sound.
• John estimated that renovation would take three years if THMS were in use during the
renovation, and 18 months if it were not occupied. TCP could support three grades during
construction.
• The primary concern is the spray-on asbestos, and that could be abated while the building
was being renovated.
b. Limited resources available for capital projects are a constraint.
• Kerry pointed out that Town Hall was a project that would need to be addressed soon.
• Town Meeting would decide how to use any unexpended balance in the contingency
account for the new Junior-Senior high school project.
5. Public Comment: John Londa said the reason the pipes in the library froze was lack of circulation of the
water.

committee approved the motion 4-0. The meeting adjourned at 5:29.

asbestos abatement project) that had be considered annually in isolation
rather than in total.

may be out of the scope of the CPC:

budget over several years.

the discussion.
