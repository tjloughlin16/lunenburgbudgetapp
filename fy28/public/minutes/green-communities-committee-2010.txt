Town of Lunenburg 
Green Community Task Force 
Meeting Minutes   
February 2, 2010 
  
Present:   Members:           S.Marsden, P.Slattery, D. Blatt, S.DiCampo, L. Krowitz,   
C.Zivojinovic  
Town Liaison:   M. Benson, J. Londa, J. Breault  
   Alternate:   J. McQuaid 
   Absent:  E. Sund 
 
1.  M. Benson advised that she will be serving on t he MRPC Committee regarding energy reduction. 
More information to follow.    
2.  Group discussed the question of whether the designation of becoming a Green Community was 
achievable given the 5 required criteria for same. Specifically the stretch code requirements and 
the 20% energy reduction plan for municipal buildings. No Mass. cities or Towns have been able 
to do it yet. An opinion from Town Counsel seemed to further cloud the criteria as it relates to the 
stretch code.  L. Krowitz advised that she would like to have our consultant return to explain 
options available to the Town.  
3. D. Blatt made a motion which was seconded to hav e the consultant return to make another 
presentation with specific emphasis on the stretch code requirements which are in conflict with 
the pending Mass. Building Code Energy Code update due on July 1,2010 and the likelihood of 
achieving the 20% mandated reduction over 5 years given the new buildings in Lunenburg. 
Motion passed by majority vote. 
4.   M. Benson will contact consultant with directe d questions and set a meeting when consultant has 
a Tuesday evening available.  
5. J. McQuaid related her discussion with Unitil re ps on planned program concerning streetlight 
conversion to LED.   
6. Discussed the use of energy software available a nd its ability to track energy use on Town 
buildings.    
7.  J. McQuaid reported that there are 6 KW meters available for borrowing through the Lunenburg 
Public Library. Lunenburg is the first town in Mass. to have same available.   
8.  S. DiCampo advised that if the Green Community criteria are unable to be satisfied as presently 
legislated, the Task Force should be prepared to act proactively with our State representative to 
work on making it achievable for Lunenburg. 
       9.   P. Slattery reported on ongoing studies related to an expansion of  the Lunenburg DPW 
             building and items being considered for energy efficiency. 
10.   J. McQuaid spoke about the website and ongoing efforts to refine same.  
11.   J. Londa reported that he received a request for additional information related to the   
grant submittal currently under review. 
12.  A brief discussion on the current cardboard recycling methods being employed and what   
alternate options are available. Discussion is based on perceptions of costs to taxpayers of  
recycling program. J. McQuaid & J. Londa to report back to Committee. Information can be put  
on website once formulated. 
13.   Future meeting schedule: to be determined 
14.   Meeting adjourned at 7:25PM 
      
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Patrick J. Slattery, Secretary

Green Community Task Force 
Meeting Minutes   
March 16, 2010 
  
Present:   Members:          S.Marsden, P.Slattery, D. Blatt, L. Krowitz, E. Sund,  
     S.DiCampo, C.Zivojinovic  
Town Liaison:  M. Benson, J. Londa, J. Breault 
   Alternate:  J. McQuaid 
   
1. Minutes were reviewed and approved for 3/2/2010 meeting.  
2. L. Krowitz has advised the Selecmen that she has resigned from the Task Force as of 3/11/10. 
The Selectmen have not acted on her resignation as of this date.  She was invited and encouraged 
to participate in the meeting. She advised that she would determine at the end of the meeting 
whether her resignation was still in effect. 
3. A lengthy discussion was held regarding Ms. Krowitz’s  accusatory transmittal directed at 
particular members of the Task Force.  It centered on her desire to have the Task Force place an 
article on the upcoming Town Meeting for adoption of the Stretch Code.  The concensus of the 
other 6 members is that there are still too many outstanding questions related to the adoption of 
an article, at this time, as no advisory has been received from the Building Commissioner and no 
follow up has been received from the Consultants. 
The majority of members agree with the schedule of placing an article before the Fall Town 
Meeting 
4. A review of the 5 criteria for becoming a Green Community was held with the following 
conclusions: 
#1-  by right zoning  is in place  
#2 - expedited permitting process is in place 
#3 – 20% energy savings over 5 years is viewed as unattainable without grant 
        monies to implement initiatives. 
#4 – fuel efficient vehicle purchase is viewed as attainable 
#5 – stretch code adoption is possible but its affect on the Town is not clear 
         especially if criteria #3  is unattainable..  
5.   J. Londa reported that progress to date with data entry into the energy use software program is 
slow due to the difficulty of retrieving the record data.  The Selectmen will be asked to direct 
Department Heads to gather the information required.  
6.  A discussion of Criteria #3 relative to the 20% energy reduction plan for municipal buildings was 
held.  The consultants had advised that the baseline starting point should probably be 2007 which 
would mandate compliance within 5 years, by 2012.  J. Londa feels that we should start the 5 
years when the energy audit is complete this year, making the 20% criteria extend to 2015 as 
compliance is not attainable without grant monies for municipal building upgrades.  
7.    The outstanding question of what would happen if by 2012 the Town demonstrated less than a 
20% saving but had received substantial grant monies to achieve the mandated reduction was 
discussed.  The Consultants assured the Committee that the Town would not be penalized or 
required to return grant monies if the threshold was not reached as it is a target. The desire of the 
Committee is to get an official opinion from DOER as to whether that is correct. P. Slattery and 
M.Benson will construct a question to be submitted to DOER through Kelly Brown. The question 
format will be distributed to members for input and approval prior to release. 
 
 
 
 
===PAGE 2===
8.    There has been no response from E. Studer – Planning Consultant, relative to  a written submittal 
for criteria #3 related to the required baseline inventory and 20% reduction program.  
9.  L. Krowitz restated her intention to resign from the Task Force at the conclusion of the meeting   
     10.   Future meeting schedule will be either 3/24 or 3/29  to meet with of M. Sauvegeau. 
     11.   Meeting adjourned at 10:10PM 
 
      
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Patrick J. Slattery, Secretary

May 26, 2010 
  
Present:   Members:           S.Marsden, P.Slattery, E. Sund, J. McQuai d

1.  Minutes were reviewed and approved for 4/27/2010 meeting.  
2.  An update on the Stretch Code status was discussed. M. Benson presented a plan showing which 
Mass. cities and towns had adopted it. C.Zivojinovic continues to study the effect for the 
Committee. P. Slattery & S. Marsden related conversations with various Town and City officials 
who had adopted the code noting that there was nowhere near the in-depth discussion and 
analysis given, in many cases, as Lunenburg has done. It was also noted that none of the allocated 
money for being a Green Community has been distributed. The general expectation is that the 
Stretch Code will be proposed for adoption at a Fall Town Meeting. 
3.  P. Slattery advised that because of the attitude among cities & towns, that it’s all about getting the 
money, it will be proposed and adopted by a majority of cities & towns, regardless of its practical 
effects.  
4.  J. McQuaid reported on recycling initiatives including a matching grant of $750. Grant deadline 
is June 2 
nd.   Committee approved application for grant. M. Benson will write a letter. 
5.  J. McQuaid advised that as part of a town wide recycling program Lunenburg can use the 
Townsend facility. 
6.   It was reported that the need for Hers raters as a component of the Stretch Code may be removed 
by Jan. 1 st .  
7.    E. Sund gave a wind energy update. It was voted and approved to send a letter to the Town 
Manager requesting an amount of $70,000 for a wind study as she previously suggested that she 
may be able to aid in securing funding for the necessary wind study. M. Benson to do letter to 
Kerry Speidel. Approval through the State is a 2-3 year process. 
8.    E. Sund gave a progress report on his personal wind turbine noting that it is still being fine tuned. 
His system stores energy in batteries and at this point it hasn’t reached a level to compensate for 
the lack of darin on the batteries. 
9.    J. Londa noted That his contact with the State for Mass Energy Insight had left the Dept. and no 
new hire has occurred as of this time.  
10.  J. Londa reported on progress with the Energy Baseline project. All accounts are now entered in 
the Mass Energy Insight program. Department heads need to manually input data into the system 
as the next step. It was suggested that a letter be sent to State Rep., J. Benson relative to having 
Unitil assist with making account information interface more readily with software requirements 
as is the case with other utility companies. 
      11.  Future meeting is scheduled 8/25/10  at 6:30PM. 
      12.  Meeting adjourned at 7:50 PM

Green Community Task Force 
Meeting Minutes   
July 19, 2010 
  
Present:   Members:           S.Marsden, P.Slattery, E. Sund, J. McQuai d, D. Blatt  
Town Liaison:   M. Benson  
       
1.  Amended minutes were reviewed and approved for 5/26/2010 meeting noting two spelling errors.  
2.  An update on the Stretch Code status was discussed. S. Marsden explained that in his review of 
area cities and towns, most are opting in for the short term understanding that they will fail to 
meet the other criteria for Green Community status and be dropped eventually.  It was also noted 
that none of the allocated money for being a Green Community has yet to be distributed. Current 
expectation is that the Stretch Code will be proposed for adoption at the Spring Town Meeting 
but further discussion is necessary and will be held at the August meeting.. 
3.  E. Sund reported on the proposal made to the Selectmen about power amalgamation.  Unitil has 
indicated no interest in Green Power proposals and N. Grid would be a better choice if available. 
No clear advantage to involving a middle-man  to act on behalf of the Town is apparent although 
the Selectmen continue to discuss the power amalgamation approach.  
4.  Power Provider contracts are the best approach for the Town per the opinions of S. Marsden  & 
D. Blatt. 
5.  S. Marsden reported on the possibility of utilizing the former landfill as a solar farm site. He has 
had discussions with various solar companies including Alteris, Inc.  There is renewed interest on 
the part of solar companies as the program enabling same expires at the end of 2010.  M. Benson 
will contact Town Manager about setting up a presentation before the Selecmen. S. Marsden will 
coordinate contact with solar companies. 
6.   A discussion of the possibility of moving forw ard with a wind study was held. Initial thoughts of 
finding funding, through the Town Manager has not been successful. S. Marsden will contact 
WPI to determine if it might be initiated as a student led project with professor certification and 
whether that approach would provide a validated wind study for funding through MEC. S. 
Marsden will report back at August meeting. Approval through the State is still a 2-3 year 
process.  
 7.  J. Londa has recommended to the Selectmen, acc epting the solar proposal provided by Broadway, 
Inc. in comparison to those submitted by Greenbridge as well as Ostrow. The analysis revealed 
cost and service benefits for the Town.  
        8.  Future meeting is scheduled 8/25/10  at 6:30PM. 
        9.  Meeting adjourned at 7:35 PM 
 
      
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Patrick J. Slattery, Secretary
