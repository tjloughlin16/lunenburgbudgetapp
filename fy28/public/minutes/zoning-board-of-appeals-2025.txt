NBUR
ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS MINUTES
Monday, November 17, 2025
The Town of Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals held a public hearing on Wednesday November 12,
2025 at 7:00 PM. The hearing was held at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 17 Main Street in Lunenburg, MA,
2nd Floor Conference Room.
The petitioner was seeking Special Permit approval for construction of a 104,700 SF, 4-story, climate
controlled self-storage facility. The property which is the subject of this case is located at 30 Summer
Street, Lunenburg, MA 01462 and owned by Summer Street Autos, LLC.
Board Members Present
Alfred Gravelle Chairman, James Besarkarski, Anthony Nicastro and Donald Gurney.
Other Attendees
Ryan Proctor, of DILLIS & ROY Civil Design Group, Inc., 1 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA 01462, as
Representative of the Petitioner: SafStor Real Estate Co., LLC., 444 Seabreeze Boulevard, Suite 840,
Daytona Beach, Florida 32118, and Lisa Normandin, Secretary of the Lunenburg Zoning
Board of Appeals.
7:00 PM The public hearing was opened by Chairman Alfred Gravelle, who introduced the Board
members and explained the hearing procedure to the audience. Lisa Normandin read the petition into
the public record
250-4.5 A (3)
Any use allowed by right or special permit within the zoning district provided that:
(a) They are associated with the primary use of the property.
(b) They comprise no more than thirty five percent (35%) of the gross floor area of the building.
(c) Any permissible Accessory Use that comprises greater than 35% of the gross floor area may
be allowed by Special Permit from the Zoning Board of Appeals.

In all other districts, except the Tri-Town Smart Growth District, the maximum height, as measured in

Hydrants shall be provided every 500 running feet on one side of each street or lesser distance unless a

250-4,5 A (1) (g)
Outside lighting or music, if any, shall not extend beyond ten (10) PM and shall be limited in so far as

After due deliberation Anthony Nicastro made a motion to approve the request as shown on the Plan
No. 8663, dated 09/29/2025, submitted by the applicant. James Besarkarski seconded the motion. The
petition was granted. Hearing Adjourned at 7:22 P.M. Minutes submitted by Deborah Crowley.
