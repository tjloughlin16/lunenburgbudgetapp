  100 Townsend Harbor Rd Select Board has not yet discussed this property or asked

Commission is not on the list of boards typically asked

the town population. 
  
Jackie made a motion to state the recommendation of the 
Agricultural Commission is for the town of Lunenburg to 
preserve the property at 100 Townsend Harbor Road 
citing the reasons stated below. Kathy seconded. 
Commission unanimously approved. 
The town has the opportunity to preserve the 
agricultural aesthetic and open space in this 
Chapter 61a property we should take the 
opportunity considering other properties are 
coming up as private sale (no ROFR). 
Currently, several large properties in town are 
already seeing development such as the stone farm, 
funeral home, and patten's farm (already 
townhouses).  
This property has potential for multiple passive 
recreation and a community garden which would 
serve the entire community.  
Surveys and general comments in town 
consistently express interest to maintain the 
farming community feeling. The town needs to 
step up to take advantage of these opportunities. 
Once farms and open space are subdivided and 
sold, there is not an opportunity to get them back.

conjunction with CMRPC and Central Mass Grown. 
  
The purpose of the project is to convene a collaborative 
partner network including municipalities, landowners, 
service providers, Historically Underserved Farmer 
(HUF) organizations, and educational institutions to 
address the four core Farmland Action Plan activity 
areas: farmland protection, access, viability, or a 
combination thereof. Key deliverables will include 
interactive regional dashboards, peer-led farmer support 
networks, and focus groups informed by compensated 
ambassadors from underserved communities.
