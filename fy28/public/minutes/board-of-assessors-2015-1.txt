Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held June 5, 2015
The meeting opened at 5:45 pm.
Present: Chairman, Louis Franco; Board Member, Matt Papini, Sr., Newly
Elected Member, Richard H. Letarte, Regional Assessor, Harald Scheid and Louise Paquette, Administrative
Assistant.
Board reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, abatements, warrants, commitments, payroll and May 5,
2015 minutes.
Reorganization: Richard H. Letarte was elected on May 17" to serve on the Board of Assessors for three years.
Matt motioned that Lou continue to serve as Chairman of the Board and was seconded by Rich. Lou thanked the
Board members for their support and agreed to continue serving in this capacity.
Harald spoke on the process of the FY16 Preliminary Billing and explained that the 1st & 21° quarter tax bills should
include an additional amount for the construction of the new High School/Middle School project. With this
information, he requested that the Board take a vote to increase the Prelim Tax calculation percentage to offset the
impact to taxpayers on the 3'd & 4" quarter Actual RE bills. He explained that the percentage in calculating the
estimated tax amount is typically 50.00 percent, but due to New Growth he suggested an increase to the valuation
factor by 2½ or more; approx. 102.5%. Louise explained that the billing process using the 50 percent valuation
factor had already been entered within the training environment and that the billing process had advanced to the
point that the bills were already created and generated in the training environment. She reported that Munis
strongly recommends running through the billing process in the training environment prior to generating bills in the
live database. However, the process had yet to be completed in LIVE. She also explained that the testing of the
real estate import programs and layouts should be well in advance of the billing season; thus providing plenty of
time to research, diagnose and adapt the import program in order to accept any changes that may be necessary.
She expressed concerns due to time constraints and the fact that the TRAIN process will need to be refreshed -
"initial steps will need to be performed again". She explained that the process is quite challenging and stated that
