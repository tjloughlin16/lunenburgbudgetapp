LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY 
 
MINUTES OF THE MONTHLY MEETING 
 
March 16, 2017 
 
Members Present:   Elizabeth Murphy, Chair 
     Paul Doherty, Vice Chair 
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member 
Linda McDonald, Member 
 
Members Absent:   Caroline Fortin, Member 
 
Also Present:    Peter Proulx Executive Director 
     Denise Ivaldi, Recording Secretary 
 
No residents were present. 
           
1. The Chair declared a quorum present and opened the meeting at 6:34 p.m.   
 
Mr. Proulx noted that Sue Honeycutt from Fenton &  Ewald was present to go over the year -end 
and January 2017 financials.  If the Chair would like, we can have her present the financials first. 
 
Sue Honeycutt went over the year-end financials first with the following comments: 
 Page F2 – Lunenburg H.A. is in pretty good shape, did very well on utilities. 
 Earned $27,000 in subsidy, not as much as expected.  This included $3,600 for the AUP 
(per DHCD). 
 Went over some of the expenses. 
 Page F3 – Went over what was spent, non-routine items. 
 DHCD owes Lunenburg H .A. $15,000, can ask for this money if there is a cash issue.  
Out of $27,000 subsidy, only received $11,500. 
 Once over $70% Reserves should start using some money, currently at 65%. 
 Receiving a lot of Capital Funds this year for the generator, patio, etc. 
Paul Doherty questioned if money could be spent on the lighting at the back Community 
Room door.  The E.D. replied that was a good idea and he will look into it (there is only 
one light out there now). 
 
A revised first page  (F2) for the January 31, 2017 f inancials was handed out to board members.  
The January financials will be reviewed during the financial portion of the meeting. 
 
2. The minutes of the January 19 , 2017 meeting were presented .  Elizabeth Murphy  made a 
motion to accept the minutes ; Deborah Christen seconded.  All  were in favor and the minutes 
were accepted. 
 
Linda McDonald asked about the waiver query from the January meeting.  Mr. Proulx explained 
that waivers are normally due to move -out inspections, painting, application process, etc. DHCD 
allows 60 days for the housing authority to turn over a vacant unit.  If more time is needed, a 
waiver can be requested from DHCD.  We usually requir e 90 days for turnovers.  Mr. Proulx 
then went over the units currently with waivers. 
 
 
 
===PAGE 2===
 
3. The Executive Director Report: 
 DHCD Board Member On -Line Training.  DHCD felt there was not a large enough 
turnout at previous training sessions; they decided to go with on-line training. 
 NAHRO Legislative Day is April 1 3th.  Mr. Proulx and Mr. Gold have set up 
appointments with Legislators on new bills, etc.  Let Denise know if you are interested in 
attending. 
 NAHRO Annual Conference is May 21 -23.  Let Denise know  if you are interested in 
attending. 
 
4.  Financial Report (January 31, 2017): 
 We are in good shape; $12,000 subsidy ($200 per unit for some work). 
 $8,000 negative in utilities. 
 The E.D. will check with Sue Honeycutt on page F3 – utilities, etc. 
 
Paul Doherty inquired about the snow removal costs so far this year.  The E.D. replied snow 
removal is at a fixed fee now.   
 
The E.D. noted that the $6,886 amount under total operating expenses may be in error.  Elizabeth 
Murphy made a motion to accept the Jan uary 2017 financials with the exception of the $6,886  
calculation error; Paul Doherty seconded and all were in favor. 
 
5.  Modernization Report:    
 Building Envelope Repair – We have a resolution to award contract later in the meeting.  
Mr. Proulx went over the work to be done.   Elizabeth Murphy asked if the handicapped 
ramp would be replaced.  Mr. Proulx replied no, it will be removed and will be repaved. 
 
6.  State-Aided Housing Report: 
 340 total applicants currently on the waitlist; 143 Elderly/YD and 197 Family. 
 Waitlist update will be going out to all 340 applicants which usually reduces the number. 
 100% occupied. 
 Work Orders – 15 during February.
