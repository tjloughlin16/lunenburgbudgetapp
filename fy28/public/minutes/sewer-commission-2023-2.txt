Minutes of 5/30/23 Sewer Commission meeting – via Zoom (Video Conference)  
Attendees: Chair- John Reynolds, Vice-Chair- Brett Ramsden, Members: Dave MacDonald and Mike Nault, DPW 
Director- Bill Bernard, Business Manager-Paula Bertram, Minutes Clerk- Jane Ranley 
Guest: Mr. Brambila (A&D Crossroads), Mike Mackin 
John opened the meeting at 6:00pm. and did a roll call of attendees. The Commission began the meeting by saying 
the Pledge of Allegiance. 
PUBLIC COMMENT- Brett noted he was sworn in for his new term. Dave thanked Jane for working at polls and said 
he liked the new check-in system. He said Mike Mackin was interested in the Commission and would be observing 
the meeting.  
ANNOUNCEMENTS – none 
CURRENT BUSINESS -  
Minutes of 4/11/23- 
Motion: Brett R.                                                                                                                                 Second: Dave M. 
To approve the minutes of 4/11/23.                             Roll Call: Mike N.-aye, Dave-aye, Brett-aye and John-aye. 
 
Minutes of 5/9/23-    
Motion: Mike N.                                                                                                                                 Second: Brett R. 
To approve the minutes of 5/ 09/23.                             Roll Call: Mike N.-aye, Dave-aye, Brett-aye and John-aye. 
 
CURRENT BUSINESS 
ARPA Funding- Dave asked if the ARPA funds were being taken back as part of the debt ceiling discussion, but no 
one had any information. John complimented Paula on her ARPA presentation package. She noted the Town 
Manager requested the Commission put the presentation together and have a member present it. The Commission is 
requesting $200K for manhole repairs. Paula went through the manhole inspection reports of 2016 and 2020. She 
focused on Electric Ave., Mass. Ave. and a few areas off of Mass. Ave. Joe Progen and Jack Welsh (DPW) visited 
the manholes on the initial list and agreed on all. The ARPA request would repair the following manholes: 19 on 
Electric Ave. ($47,420), a frame replacement at the High School ($1,890), 41 on Mass. Ave. ($95,410), 3 at 
Meadowwoods ($6,930), 4 at Primary School ($15,120) and 15 on Richard’s Way ($ 28,550). The repairs to these 84 
manholes would cost $195,320. Paula stated the yearly budget is limited and they have only been able to fix 8-10 per 
year out of the 168 in need of repair. She has put together a PowerPoint presentation to explain that need. Paula 
noted the 2nd component of the request is for $300K for the pump stations. Paula, Bill and Al put the following list 
together: 6 Dialers (Dana, Electric, Twin City, Francis, Leominster Rd, and Mass 1, 4 Pumps (Leominster, West, Twin 
City and Francis), Panel Upgrade at West, Motor Shaft at Mass 1, Impellers (Dana, Leominster and Mass 1), and 
Generators (Electric, Twin City and Meadowwoods), for a total of $305K. Paula noted the cost of repairs to each 
manhole may differ, depending on the amount of repair to be done. The prices were based on Wright Pierce’s 2015 
pricing, updated using a cost escalator. Joe (DPW) stated Tango Construction did the repairs on 2A, and the 
concrete was brought up too high, which caused damage. Dave suggested having the manholes inspected prior to 
adding asphalt. Bill said he has the DOT plans that show how to rebuild up to grade. Paula will also include 5 maps 
of the highlighted manholes. John pointed out the repairs were based on the 2015 study and manholes may now be 
worse off.  Paula asked the Commission to vote to send the proposal. Mike asked about manholes that could be 
eliminated. Paula replied if they have some close together, they could line the pipe, and eliminate some manholes 
from the list. The presentation will be 6/6 and John will be able to attend. 
Motion: Dave M.                                                                                                                            Second: Mike N. 
To recommend sending the ARPA proposal to the Board of Selectmen as soon as possible for their review, 
considering today is Tuesday and go from there as far as  when they do the presentation.  
Roll Call Vote: Mike N.-aye, Brett-aye, Dave-aye and John -aye. 
   
APPOINTMENTS:   A&D Crossroads – Non-Compliance Hearing – Mr. Brambila - Paula stated she met with a 
representative from Ixtapa Restaurant and detailed numerous issues with getting reports for pump outs. Multiple 
emails and certified mail letters also were sent. The system was due for a pump out on 10/12 but they did not receive 
===PAGE 2===
 
 
a report in November or December. The Commission voted to implement the non-compliance process and requested 
the report by January 26th or fines would be implemented from 11/11/22 to 3/1/23. They received a report on 2/17/23 
and the Commission voted to implement the fine of $25/day, plus the $50 non-compliance fee.  (Mr. Brambila had 
difficulty logging on but finally was able to join the meeting.) Paula noted they had sent numerous communications to 
the property owner, who is the brother of Mr. Brambila, but received no response. Finally on 3/1/23, they received the 
report they were looking for and sent A&D Crossroads a fine invoice of $2,525. Again there was no response, so a 
hearing notice was sent to the property owner, Ixtapa and Darios restaurants.  Darios has since closed their 
business. Paula met with a representative from Ixtapa and a translator to discuss the situation. They stated the 
system was pumped on 5/24 and Paula received a report from the pumping company but the report did not have all 
the required information on the amount of FOG and sludge. She sent an email, asking for the correct report. They 
have been out of compliance for 99 days. Mr. Brambila stated he received the letters and emails which he forwarded 
to his brother. He has scheduled pumpings every 3 months with the company. The system was pumped 9/28, 2/8 
and 5/24. John asked when the non-compliance began and Paula replied the system was due to pump 10/12, but the 
report did not arrive until 2/17/23. The report is due 30 days after a pumping. Brett said over several months, the 
business manager and the Commission spent lot of time. They need something to cover for all the time and 
frustration.  Dave asked Mr. Brambila why he did not call back and he replied his brother hired the company to do the 
pumping but they did not send the reports on time. Dave said the Commission is upset because they did not respond 
for 5 months. Paula stated the big problem is the pumping company was not doing the reports correctly. The report 
they finally received on 2/17 but it did not have the relevant information filled out. Wind River pumped on 5/26, but it 
was not the correct report. Mr. Brambila noted they have hired a new company - Bay State Sewage Disposal, Inc.-to 
do the pumpings. Paula reiterated the fine is $2,525 for 11/11/22 to 2/17/23. The Commission discussed and agreed 
Mr. Brambila could have saved a lot of frustration and time if he had responded on time. Also this was not the first 
time they were non-compliant. Dave suggested reducing the fine to $750, with more severe fines, if it happens again. 
The Commission agreed.  
Motion: Dave M.                                                                                                                                 Second: Brett R. 
To reduce the fine for A & D Crossroads to $750 towards his $2,525 fine, to be paid immediately.  
Roll Call: Mike N.-aye, Dave-aye, Brett-aye and John -aye. 
Paula asked Mr. Brambila to have Wind River send the correctly filled-out report to the office within a week in order to 
be compliant. He also needs to pay the $750 and remain in compliance. Mr. Brambila agreed to do so. 
CURRENT BUSINESS (con’t)  
Woodard & Curran Sewer System Infiltration Review- Paula screen-shared her notes from the Woodard & Curran 
2020 report. John stated they have been talking about I/I (Inflow/Infiltration) in the Whalom area and this report 
shows I/I issues outside of the Whalom district. John asked all to take a close look at the report and possibly re-
evaluate where they attack the I/I.  They need to look at the report in context of the manholes to I/I and the Capital 
Budget. Bill said they need to repair manholes to address I/I issues and to look at the operation of the pump stations. 
The report supports the Commission’s request. John noted they need to get pump station reports on a regular basis.  
Paula received some reports for January 1st through May from Al Packard and Bill had some questions about the 
data. Paula and Bill will meet with Al to go over the reports he submitted.  
IMA Negotiations- John stated they cannot go into executive session to discuss and he would like to keep it to a 
non-quorum group meeting with Leominster Sewer Dept., with updates to the rest of the Commission. Paula 
confirmed that Town Counsel said they cannot use Executive Session to discuss the negotiations, except in the final 
stages. Paula read the email from Town Counsel that stated negotiations need to be done within a small non-quorum 
group, bringing it to the full public body when necessary. The Commission gave Dave permission to talk to Town 
Counsel during open hours on 6/21. Dave said the next meeting with Leominster has not been scheduled. Paula 
noted Wright Pierce said the testing requirements in the new NFTS permit draft are onerous and costly. Kevin Olsen 
(Wright Pierce) said many communities are pushing back on the requirements. Paula asked for Commission approval 
to send a letter in support of eliminating those requirements, based on Jake Fleming’s comments. The Commission 
agreed.  Dave said the meeting went well and thanked Paula, Bill and the Town Manager for their assistance.  
===PAGE 3===
 
 
Capital Planning- Bill & Paula met with Al Packard on 5/24 and things have changed. They need panels at West St. 
and Francis St., but the dialers and seals can wait. The revised list is: 3 cranes, block heaters, a panel and a dialer at 
Meadowwoods for a total of $45,237.80. Paula noted the budget will cover that and she sent the Commissioners an 
updated sheet. Al is recommending going with the SKADA system. Bill and Paula asked Al for access to the system 
which can be done off-site remotely.  Al would like to put Dana and Mass 1 in the SKADA and estimates they will be 
$16K each. He also suggested getting on a program for battery replacement every 3 years and Paula developed a 
database to track that. Also he asked to do annual pump outs November/December. Bill stated some of the fences 
around the pump stations were damaged and Al suggested fixing them. Paula and Bill suggested tracking inventory 
on-site, in space at the DPW building. Al will check if they already have some of the pumps. John asked if the Town 
or Al would track the inventory and Bill replied they will work together. John offered to help and would like to learn 
more about the SKADA system. Paula and Bill want to get familiar with SKADA and Al will be getting them access.   
Dave suggested meeting with Leominster to discuss technology and the system they are using. Paula said she would 
reach out to Jake Fleming regarding the manhole lining and SKADA. Dave suggested having the meeting via zoom.  
Leominster -Shirley Corridor Development/Mass Works Grant - Paula reported the Selectmen are working on a 
grant for next year due to the possible construction of 5 large commercial buildings across from the large new 
warehouse. She told the Town Manager the Commission would like to have input in the grant process. 
Assistant Business Manager - Paula noted the position is on hold. It is currently 24 hrs. with benefits, but Paula 
does not believe it needs to be 24 hrs. or benefited.  John agreed and Dave suggested offering the position to a job 
training program. Paula said the job needs to be more than clerical and she will need someone who can cover when 
she is out for any extended period. Bill agreed and the Commission discussed the position. Paula has a candidate 
interested in the non-benefited position but they cannot change the job unless it is negotiated with the union. They 
are currently in discussions for the new contract and if they change the position, they will have to do collective 
bargaining. Bill agreed the position can be less than 20 hours because Paula has streamlined the business but they 
need someone that can learn the day-to-day job. Paula asked the Commission to vote on the change.    
Motion: Brett R.                                                                                                                                  Second: Mike N. 
To change  the hours for the Assistant Business Manager position from 24 hours benefited to under 20 hours 
unbenefited.                                                           Roll Call Vote: Mike N.-aye, Brett-aye, Dave-aye and John-aye. 
 
Action Items- Dave suggested looking into Leominster’s ideas on manholes.  
Business Manager Report – FY23 Budget Update -Paula sent out the current budget and upped the amount for I/I, 
to address some manholes other than the ARPA funding. They have added $28K for jetting. Bill noted he obtained an 
estimate of $4,800 per week to rent a jetting truck. If they hire a company such as Clean Basins, their cost is $2,500-
2,800/day. Bill said the DPW crew would do the jetting and Al Packard will assist. He will rent the truck for 2 weeks 
and start jetting lines. John asked if the DPW labor would come out of the Sewer or DPW budget and Bill will look 
into it. Paula added there may be some fees for Al. Dave suggested Bill invoice the Sewer Department for time and 
labor. Bill plans on jetting 3 miles. System Maintenance Update - Paula and Bill gave the ok to CTE to order 3 
cranes. Weld Generator has changed out batteries. They are looking at replacing some screens, did a pump at 
Meadowwoods, control panels at West St. and Francis. Paula said she has a log at each station which she can send 
to the Commissioners and is tracking maintenance and parts replacement at each station. Grease Trap Compliance 
- all reports are in except Conrad’s and Paula will follow up with them.  Il Forno -Paula did not get their report on time 
and sent an email. She has since received it. Vacation- Paula gave her dates for vacation in July and August.  
PUBLIC COMMENT –John thanked Michael M. for sitting in on the meeting and hoped he would join them. Mike M. 
said he enjoyed the discussions and he is interested. Dave noted he would need to be appointed by the Select 
Board. Dave also congratulated Brett on getting 2nd place in number of votes at the town election and thanked him for 
stepping up for another term.

Minutes of 6/27/23 Sewer Commission meeting– via Zoom (Video Conference) 
Attendees: Chair- John Reynolds, Vice-Chair- Brett Ramsden, Members: Dave MacDonald and Mike Nault, DPW 
Director- Bill Bernard, Business Manager-Paula Bertram  
Absent: Minutes Clerk- Jane Ranley 
Guests: Mike Mackin 
John opened the meeting at 6:04pm. and did a roll call of attendees. The Commission began the meeting by saying 
the Pledge of Allegiance. John welcomed Mike Mackin who is interested in joining the Commission and will be on 
board after the Commission meets with the Select Board. Dave asked Paula to pass on their prayers to Jane, for her 
husband’s recovery.  
 
PUBLIC COMMENT- Dave thanked Mike Mackin for joining the Commission – just a matter of paperwork to make it 
official. Dave thanked John for his great presentation to the Select Board last week. Brett said he was very impressed 
with the DPW’s repairs on the potholes on Mulpus Road, using the grinding machine. He wanted to commend Bill B. 
on his action and approach.  
 
ANNOUNCEMENTS – John stated they received an email from Paula regarding some sewage backup in a Mass. 
Ave. residence, near the center of town. The backup was cleared but they are still looking into what happened and 
John wanted all the Commissioners to be aware of the problem. Paula received the call about the backup but was 
unable to get Clean Basins out right away. Instead RM Ratta was contacted, who came and pumped the manhole, 
where the sewage was up to the top. They then proceeded down Mass. Ave. from the center of town to the pump 
station. The lines were cleared and they are in the process of determining what happened. Paula noted there was a 
significant grease blockage. John and Paula visited the establishment which has a grease trap and asked for more 
information. They are waiting for the reports and noted there was a history of a problem in the area, between 2 
establishments. The issue is on the property, where there is a junction of several pipes. The property owner had 
previously contracted someone  to service those lines to determine why they were having a backup. Paula is waiting 
for more information about what happened, when it occurred and what pumping had been done. Also they want to 
pop the manhole covers at the site to see what is causing the issue. The backup was from 926 Mass. Ave. to the 
pump station, heading towards Fitchburg. John will pass on information as available.   
 
CURRENT BUSINESS -  
Minutes of 6/13/23- 
Motion: Mike N.                                                                                                                                   Second: Brett R. 
To accept the minutes of 6/13/23.                        Roll Call Vote: Mike N.-aye, Brett-aye, Dave-aye and John-aye. 
 
ARPA Funding- Paula reported the Select Board asked for a modified ARPA request and she has sent the draft to 
the Commissioners. She put together the information that John presented to help the Select Board understand the 
effects of Covid on the sewer system, as well as the challenges they are facing from new regulations and financial 
constraints. John asked the Commissioners to give Paula their input, so he and Paula could compose the final letter 
to the Select Board. The Commissioners agreed that the request looks good. Dave suggested saying more about the 
testing for NPDES/Covid aftermath and the potential costs between Fitchburg and Leominster. Paula stated the 
numbers in the document are from Wright Pierce and are for Leominster. Their document also articulates their 
estimated costs for the flood plain monitoring and additional contaminant monitoring if they are required. Dave asked 
if they are attaching an engineer’s report to the request and John replied the information from Wright Pierce was their 
professional opinion but not a paid for study. Paula added Wright Pierce is working with other towns to provide 
comments on the draft NPDES and those towns are in the same position as Lunenburg.  Dave noted that the state 
will be offering grants and monies for the water departments to deal with PFAS issues but has not seen anything 
being offered to Sewer departments for dealing with these issues.  John replied that the PFAS issue is a much 
greater unknown than the flood plain issue, which could be $200-300K. He added that right now it is an unfunded 
mandate. Testing for PFAS would be one cost, but if Fitchburg and Leominster have to remove PFAS, it would be a 
very expensive proposition. Dave noted the costs would then come from the sewer budget. John added there has 
===PAGE 2===
 
 
been a 50% increase in flows to Fitchburg and Leominster, which is significant. Paula stated the modified request 
needs to go to the Select Board for their 7/11 meeting. The Commissioners will send input to Paula asap.  
FY23 Encumbrances- Paula reported there is just over $50K in encumbrances. The pump station cranes have been 
ordered but will not arrive until September. Martinez Road Construction repaired 16 manhole covers on Mass. Ave. 
for just under $10K but she has not received a bill yet. Clean Basins will be jetting lines soon for $9,800. Paula has a 
proposal of $9,100 from Wright Pierce for evaluation of feasibility of redirecting flows.  She also ordered more 
manhole covers for $3,624 and she has estimated bills for telephone and utilities. The cost of waste disposal and 
asphalt from PJ Keatings is $1,195. The emergency jetting on Mass Ave is estimated at $2,800 plus the money for 
police details. She noted the funds will be coming from various line items in the budget for a total of $51,592.50 and 
will carry over from this year’s budget to next. Paula asked for a vote and signatures of Commissioners. 
Motion: Dave M.                                                                                                                                   Second: Mike N. 
To accept the encumbrance of $51,592.50.        Roll Call Vote: Brett-aye, Dave-aye, Mike N.-aye and John-aye. 
 
Action Items- John suggested future discussion on grease traps in light of the recent overflow issue. Dave 
suggested pulling all the records for grease traps in that area and Paula replied they had started that process 
already. She also found out that there has been some jetting done, for which she has not received reports.  The 
Commission discussed and agreed they need to make sure it does not happen again. Paula noted she and John had 
been investigating, along with visiting the establishment involved, but found out there were other issues on other 
properties that were dealt with in the past. More investigation will be done. Dave suggested looking at how they 
would deal with this issue during non-business hours. Paula gave a shout-out to DPW workers Jack Welsh and Jim 
Stickler for an amazing job in dealing with the issue. Bill stated if an issue occurs after hours, people can call the 
police, who will notify the DPW and they have personnel who will respond. Dave suggested adding wording to the 
DPW answering machine, to call the police station (not 911), for a sewer problem. 
 
Business Manager Report –  
Update on System Maintenance & Upgrades – Paula reported they recently replaced pumps at Meadowwoods, 
Francis and Mass Ave. There were 2 in stock and 1 was ordered. The cranes have been ordered and they are 
installing guards on the panels, to prevent shocks. Al Packard is looking at other upgrades and he has updated some 
reports but she has not received the final reports. She will follow up with him. 
FY23 Budget-Paula sent out the updated budget but it is difficult to find out where they are because the accounting 
department is severely understaffed. As of 6/27, they have $945,366.77 in User Charges, which is much less than 
the forecasted $1.1 million. Until the receipts for May and June are posted, she will not know where they are. Paula 
estimated conservatively there will be $975K in User Charges but will end the year positive due to moving money 
from other line items and encumbrances. They will not have the money they usually put into Retained Earnings, due 
to the personnel shift and upgrades done. There will be a surplus of about $20K which may increase if the Revenue 
figure increases. She suggested looking at more bids and spending more than $10K on jetting, manholes and I/I. 
Right now, they are limited to spending less than $10K on contracts. If more than $10K, they need 3 bids and if more 
than $50K, it will need to go out for bid.  
System Maintenance – Paula stated they received 1 bid from Chad Empey and it was significantly more than they 
anticipated. Bill has been working on adjusting the proposed hours in the bid to bring the cost down but they will need 
to rebid. He talked to Chad Empey and they are willing to extend the current contract out 2 months. They need to 
revise the IFB to reduce the preliminary quantities to come in under budget. John said they discussed combining the 
generator contract with the Operation & Maintenance, but Julie (Assistant Town Manager) thought they may have 
gotten more bids if they were separated. John asked the Commissioners if they would want to split them since they 
are going back out for bids.  Bill noted one company asked if they could just bid on the generator maintenance. Dave 
suggested keeping them separate to help control costs. The Commission discussed the options. Bill said he favors 1 
contract and noted on Chad Empey’s bid, the generator cost was only 11% overall. John suggested putting the IFB 
out again as one contract and Paula stated Bill has already modified the quantities, so they are ready to send it out 
now. Dave asked to see the modified quantities and asked about the increases from Chad Empey.  Bill replied the 
===PAGE 3===
 
 
monthly and hourly charges went up some. Paula added the increases were due to the increase in the quantity of 
hours compared to the current contract.  
July 25th & August 8th Attendance- Paula noted she will be able to attend the 7/25 meeting via Zoom but will not be 
able to attend the 8/8 meeting. She will be doing the agenda early for the 7/25 meeting and has tentatively scheduled 
Wright Pierce to attend that meeting to discuss their proposal for redirecting flow. Brett had asked for an update on 
Pond View and has added it to 7/25 agenda. John noted in regard to system maintenance, the manholes that were 
fixed a few years ago used a cement/concrete which degraded into powder. Paula stated she added into the modified 
ARPA request the fact that when Mass DOT did repairs during the repaving of 2A, the concrete essentially turned to 
dust. This may happen to all the manholes repaired by the DOT. Bill stated they potentially used a fast-setting 
concrete which has a tendency when below grade to turn to mush when wet and then to dust. Dave asked if there is 
are regulations for material used and Bill replied yes. Dave suggested going back to Mass DOT with the repair costs 
and Bill noted they would need to do a chemical analysis to prove their point. Paula added she has information on 
what repairs were done on a database and could compare a manhole done by Mass DOT to one done otherwise. 
The Commission discussed if the state is responsible for redoing the manholes when they resurface or were they 
were asked to do them by a past DPW Director.  They also discussed the crack sealing done around many 
manholes. Paula will discuss the issue with Adam during the next Town Counsel hours and she recommended 
having Bill talk to Joe Progin to do more investigating into other manholes.  Dave also suggested adding pictures of 
the new sewer covers to the DPW Facebook page to let the public know about work being done, as well as traffic 
delays. 
 
PUBLIC COMMENT – Dave wished everyone a safe holiday. John thanked Mike Mackin for joining the meeting and 
is looking forward to having him join the Commission. Paula asked about posting a joint meeting on 7/11 with the 
Select Board for Mike Mackin’s appointment and the Commission agreed.
