Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: July 13, 2021
Members present: Chairman, Richard Letarte, Board Member, Christine Higdon and Principal Assessor,
Susan Byrne and Administrative Assessing Assistant, Sheila Craigen
Rich opened the meeting at 10:34 am
Open Meeting:
• Christine made a motion to keep Rich as chairman to the BOA (Board of Assessors)
• Sue stated she has been doing some research on the Elderly Exemption Clause 41C. Members
discussed and it was decided that Sue will submit an article for Special Town Meeting in the fall. The
article will request the Town to vote to adjust the income and asset limits for the Elderly Exemption
(M.G.L. Ch. 59 & 5 cl. 41C) to Single person income limit $31,500., Asset limit $52,700. Married persons
income limit $39,600., Asset limit $72,700 and to increase the exemption amount to $1,000.
Adjournment:
• Meeting adjourned at 11:15 am

Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: August 31, 2021
Members present: Chairman, Richard Letarte, Board Members, Christine Higdon and Kevin Fish and
Principal Assessor, Susan Byrne and Administrative Assessing Assistant, Sheila Craigen
Also present: Town Manager, Heather Lemieux and Christine Kumar
Rich opened the meeting at 10:01 am
Open Meeting:
• Rich made a motion to approve the July 13, 2021 minutes. Christine seconded the motion. All in favor.
• Heather spoke to the BOA (Board of Assessors) and explained several applications were received and
2 (two) persons were selected for interviews. Heather introduced Christine Kumar who accepted the
position as the new Principal Assessor. Christine Kumar talked about her work experience and answered
questions from the BOA. Christine stated her start date is September 7, 2021.
Sue Byrne stated her last day is September 23, 2021. She is currently making a calendar and
instructions for ongoing projects. Sue talked about the progress made over the past year and offered
suggestions for the upcoming years. Sue stated the BOA should consider keeping the measure and list
program in place and/or consider hiring a part time data collector.
• Sue presented the article that she needs to submit for the upcoming Special Town Meeting. BOA
reviewed, approved and signed the document.
• BOA reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, payroll, abatements, warrants and commitments.
Adjournment:
• Meeting adjourned at 10:40 am
Respectfully Submitted,
Sheila Craigen

Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: OCTOBER 5, 2021
Members present: Chairman, Richard Letarte, Board Members, Christine Higdon and Kevin Fish and
Principal Assessor, Chris Kumar and Administrative Assessing Assistant, Sheila Craigen
Rich opened the meeting at 4:30 pm
Open Meeting:
• Kevin made a motion to approve the August 31, 2021 minutes. Christine seconded the motion. All in
favor.
• Chris explained the Chapter Land Program to the BOA (Board of Assessors). Chris then presented the
applications received to date. The BOA discussed concerns on an application for a parcel on Page St.
Rich made a motion that an onsite inspection is needed for clarification. Members concur. Chris will
contact the applicant to set up a date and time.
• Chris presented Exemption Applications received to date. Ultimately, the BOA approved 3 (three)
Clause 37, 48 (forty-eight) Clause 22, 5 (five) Clause 22D, 19 (nineteen) Clause 22E and 14 (fourteen)
Clause 41C applications.
A unanimous vote was made to deny 1 (one) application for Clause 22E as the applicant does meet the
criteria. Chris will draft the denial letter for the BOA to sign.
• BOA reviewed and approved Commitment #4 Motor Vehicle Excise
• BOA reviewed and approved current Expense Vouchers, Abatements and Payroll
Open Discussion:
• Chris updated the BOA with status of the denials made on the 5 (five) Chase Rd properties. ATB had
been filed for all of the denials. Chris stated negotiations were made by the former Assessor. It was agreed
that 2 (two) abatements should be granted and the remainder 3 (three) are still denied. ATB for all 5 (five)
properties have been withdrawn per this agreement.
• Chris talked to the BOA about getting back to regular monthly meetings. BOA members mutually agree
the first Tuesday of every month would work.
Adjournment:
• Meeting adjourned at 5:06 pm

Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: November 2, 2021
Members present: Chairman, Richard Letarte, Board Members, Christine Higdon and Kevin Fish and
Principal Assessor, Chris Kumar and Administrative Assessing Assistant, Sheila Craigen
Rich opened the meeting at 4:30 pm
Open Meeting:
• Rich made a motion to approve the October 5, 2021 minutes. Christine seconded the motion. All in
favor.
• Chapter Land application (61B) on Page St: Chris updated the BOA (Board of Assessors) on her
discussion and findings after speaking with the property owner. A unanimous vote was made to deny the
application.
• Chapter Land application (61A) on Flat Hill Rd: Chris presented and members reviewed new application
on Flat Hill Ra. Kevin made a motion to approve. All in favor.
• Chris explained she received a call on land (Rolling Acres) that is currently in the Chapter Land
program (61B). The property is in the process of being sold and the potential buyers intend to keep in the
Chapter Land program. Chris explained an error was made on the release in 1995. Chris explained the
process and what needs to be done. BOA members agreed and signed off on the necessary paperwork.
• Chris presented and members reviewed Exemption Applications. Ultimately, the BOA approved 2 (two
Clause 37, 2 (two Clause 17, 12 (twelve) Clause 22A-F, 2 (two) Clause 22E and 2 (two Clause 41C
applications. Note: Since the October 5, 2021 meeting, no additional applications have been denied.
• BOA reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, payroll, abatements, warrants and commitments.
Open Discussion:
• Chris answered questions on the Chapter Land program and stated she will send members an
electronic copy of the brochure from DCR (Department of Conservation & Recreation).
Adjournment:
