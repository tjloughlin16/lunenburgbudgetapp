PACC Meeting Minutes 
Jan 7, 2019 
Roll call: 
1. Meeting called to order at 6:04pm. 
2. PACC members present were Jeff Bajko, Steve Walker, Faith Beall, Peter Lincoln, and Steve 
Archambault. 
3. Non-members: Jo Mclaughlin. 
Public Comments: 
1. Cable companies are trying to pass a law where they do not have to pay as much to Public Access 
channels.  Mass access is planning strategic meetings to help fight this.  
Review and approve meeting minutes from Dec. 10th, 2018. 
1. Committee reviewed and approved the minutes.  
Manager’s update: 
1. Ending balance of Dec is $157,248.08. 
2. Meadow will increase her hours in January. 
3. December’s calendar included tree lighting and school concerts. 
4. Manny and Gabe are new hires that will start in January. 
5. Basketball games will start to be broadcast this month. 
6. 53% local broadcasting. 
7. Letters to Santa is completed. 
8. Jo went to a Castus user meeting to learn how to stream through You Tube.  
9. Knight’s round table with the high school principle was recorded. 
10. The receiver for the wireless microphone is being repaired. 
11. Jo presented the committee a quote for new wireless microphones.  The committee asked Jo to reach out 
to Access A/V and ask for other quotes. 
12.  Jo presented the committee with a draft of the FY18 report.  The committee will bring corrections to the 
next meeting. 
13. The committee made a motion to raise the salaries for Meadow Galbo and Kevin Boussom from ten 
dollars an hour to twelve dollars an hour.  The committee passed the moti on. 
 
Budget discussion: 
1. The packets were sent to the committees 
2. Committee was given last year’s budget to review.  Changes were made and a motion to approve the new 
budget was made and approved.    
FY20 budget discussion: 
1. The committee started to plan the budget for FY20. 
2. The committee made a motion to allow Steve to make the changes the committee made to the budget 
and present it to the Town Manager. 
Adjournment: 
Next meeting is Feb 4th.             
Meeting adjourned at 7:56pm.

PACC Meeting Minutes 
Dec 9, 2019 
Roll call: 
1. Meeting called to order at 6:02pm. 
2. PACC members present were Steve Walker, Jeff Bajko, Faith Beall, and Peter Lincoln.  
3. PAC members missing: Steven Archambault 
4. Non-members: Jo Mclaughlin.   
Public Comments: 
1. An email was sent stating that it is recommended to have a Lunenburg email when discussing town 
business.  
Review and approved meeting minutes from 11.4.19: 
1. The committee reviewed and approved the minutes from last meeting.  
Manager’s update: 
1. Ending balance is $668,517.88 
2. The town departments’ video is completed and getting a lot of views on youtube.  
3. A salvation Army project will be completed. 
4. Basketball will be recorded starting next week. 
5. Try lighting and school concerts were recorded. 
6. Channel 9 is 70% local programming. 
7. Talking Baseball has been downloaded to mass access and several communities have picked it up. 
8. Suggestions were made to switch contract workers to town employees.   
9. Jo presented to the committee videographer and senior videographer’s job descriptions.  The 
committee reviewed, made recommended changes. 
10. Jo will present it to HR for recommendations. 
11. Jo will then send it to the committee chairman for final approval. 
 
Discussion of audio options: 
1. The gooseneck audio holder fixed the audio problem in the Billotta room. 
Adjournment 
Next meeting is Jan 6th.               
Meeting adjourned at 7:13pm. 
 
Respectfully submitted, Jeffrey Bajko, PACC Clerk 2/24/20 
 
P.S. to the minutes that "The job descriptions were approved without further discussion and with 
a majority vote on December 11th"
