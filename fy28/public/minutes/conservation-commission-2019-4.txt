William Hannigan was present to represent the applicant. He outlined the history of the violation. 
The Leominster Conservation Commission was notified and they will work to echo the towns 
Order of Conditions. He outlined the construction of the original building and lot  was prior to 
the river protection act. Mr. Hannigan then proceeded to exhibit historical ortho photos outlining 
the work that was completed prior to the river protection act and after the enactment of the river 
protection act. He also outlined the ownership history as part of the work has been completed on 
the FRA land and the MBTA property directly adjacent. Mr. Hannigan noted that the current 
principle business is replacement of manhole structures for Mass Highway. The site is currently 
used for old manhole product storage. The applicant is still in negotiations with the FRA on 
purchasing a section of the property. The Mean annual high water mark and wetland delineation 
was marked by Charles E. Caron Environmental. 
 
The proposal is to first install the erosion control and take the grindings and storage containers 
out and re-established. The area closest to the brook is proposed to be restored first as a priority.

that the redevelopment authority is in agreement with the restoration proposal. He outlined the 
total restoration.  The FRA would eventually will move to develop the property and firming the 
riverfront numbers now could be useful to both the commission and the FRA in the future.
