The Lunenburg Cemetery Commission met on January 31, 2017 at The Lunenburg Public
Library @ 7:00 p.m.
Members present were Lance May, Lorraine Mendoza, and Steve Schaff. Todd Holman
was unavailable due to plowing snow from an existing snow storm.
Supt of Highways, Jack Rodriquenz and Police Chief, Jim Marino were present.
Chair Schaff called the meeting to order at 7:00 p.m. and the minutes from October 16, 2016,
were reviewed and upon a motion from Commissioner Mendoza to accept them -as read, and a
second by Commissioner May and without any further discussion, the motion passed
unanimously.
Commissioner May read one letter of correspondence from The Selectmen's Office requesting an
article be submitted to them for the Town's 2017 Annual Report. Chair Schaff was to speak to
Todd to submit this article.
Under appointments, Jeff Smith of 60 Valley Rd, Lunenburg was given 15 minutes to speak and
he began with a concern about vehicular traffic and access to the cemeteries during the winter
months. It was pointed out to him that The Cemeteries are closed to vehicular traffic during the
winter months due to liability and to keep traffic off of unplowed roads that encompass plot
bound markers of those owned plots, (burial sites).
Mr. Smith, also inquired about the lack of handicap accessibility and felt this was not allowing
for safe access and the town was in violation of the Americans for disability act, both federal and
state. He went onto say that from previous attempts to bring this and other issues before the
town manager, police chief and highway director that he was denied his right to due process and
wanted his comments to be placed on record!
Chair Schaff informed him that he was beyond his 15 minutes and his time was up. He continued
to speak and was given a warning about being removed from the room should he continue.
At this point during the meeting Chair Schaff called for a recess to set up tables for the 7:30 p.m.
appointment of Leominster Monument Co.
At 7:35 pm The Commission mẹt with Leominster Monuments to discuss the submitted
engineered drawings from two additional Columbarium Companies. The drawings were
reviewed and questions were asked. However, due to the disruption and questions being asked by
Jeff Smith during the discussion with Leominster Monuments, Chair Schaff suggested the
commission take this material under advisement and thanked Leominster Monuments for
attending the meeting during a snowstorm.
===PAGE 2===
At this point in the meeting, Mr. Smith was informed for the 4 time, his opportunity to speak
was out of order and he was asked to be seated. He continued his out of order questions and the
police were asked to escort him from the room which they did at 8:40 p.mm.
After bringing order back to the meeting, Chair Schaff brought up for discussion the review of
the current cemetery rules that he had submitted, (after Commission review), to the Town Mngr's
office.
One rule, (310, 2-D...winter burials), was reviewed by the Commission, concerning burials
during the winter months in a closed, (to vehicular traffic), cemetery.
Under Mass State Law, anyone who wishes to be buried in a cemetery, especially during the
winter months must be accommodated. Upon a motion by Commissioner May and a second by
Commissioner Mendoza, This rule is amended to read -as follows, "Burials may be made during
CLOSED seasonal cemetery conditions with prior approval of the Supt. of Cemeteries and in
consultation and approval with the Lunenburg Cemetery Commission's Chairman."
No further comment was made on this motion and it passed on a unanimous vote.
Next Meeting will be on March 16, 2017.
No further business came before the cemetery commission and a motion was made by
Commissioner Mendoza and seconded by Commissioner May to adjourn. Without further
discussion, Chair Schaff called for the vote and it was unanimous to adjourn.
Time of adjournment was 9:09 p.m.
Police Chief, Jim Marino provided the Commissioners with an escort from the building.
Respectfully submitted,
Lance D. May, Clerk

The Lunenburg Cemetery Commission met on March 16, 2017 at the Lunenburg Public Library.

Commission was to place on that warrant and after reviewing the current need for week to week
operations and adding into that amount the need for the cost associated with the Colum-barium
project, (part II), it was decided to request an amount of $84,200.00.
Commissioner Mendoza made a motion to put the amount of $84,200.00 onto the May Town Meeting

a vote and the vote was unanimous to put this amount of $84,200.00 onto the Town Meeting Warrant and
to give this information to the Town Manager's Office by March 27, 2017.
At 7:15 pm, Bobby and Kelly Pearson from Leominster Monuments were asked to join the meeting to
present to the Commission for their review -a follow-up on preliminary plans that were presented at the
January meeting for the building of the Colum-barium and Niche wall at the North Cemetery. Further
discussion took place on the cost, design and materials -as well -as establishing a time line for the
establishment of bids. It was determined by the Commission that the over-all cost to build this Colum-
barium should not exceed $50,000.00 and that the bids and construction should not exceed this amount. It
was also determined that the Bid specs would be written -up to select the design and type of materials to
be used and where possible, adding the words), "or equal materials" with additional language added for a
performance bond and a prevailing wage statement to be requested from each bidder and the words "The
Lunenburg Cemetery Commission reserves the right to accept or reject any and all bids".
Additionally, a time-line was established to prepare the bids by March 27 and to get them sent to
perspective bidders, so-those bidders would have the time during April to assemble their bids and forward
them to Todd Holman's attention before the Commission's next meeting on April 27, 2017
Commissioner Mendoza made a motion to prepare the bids and time-line -as discussed. Commissioner
May seconded her motion and with-out further discussion, Chair Schaff called for a vote and the vote was
unanimous to proceed ahead in this direction.

Chair Schaff reported that the update on the rules are still being reviewed by Town Council and has no

The Lunenburg Cemetery Commission met on September 7, 2017 at the Lunenburg Pu6VE Far At 7: boE
pm. Members present were Chairman Lance May, Vice-Chairman Lorraine Mendoza, William Tyler, Clerk
and Todd Holman, Cemetery Supt.
Chairman May called the meeting to order at 7:00 pm.
No minutes were available for approval.
A letter from Geire Geigas dated July 17, 2017 was read, thanking Supt. Holman for his continued
dedication to caring for the Cemeteries in Town and how good they look.
In the Public Comment portion of the meeting, Mike Clark described the Eagle Scout project his son
wants to do. It involves placing wreaths at all Veterans graves prior to Christmas. He intends to do the
North Cemetery and possibly the South would be done as a future Eagle Scout project. It is in
conjunction with Wreaths Across America. It was decided to take under advisement until more details
were available.
Under old business, the North Cemetery Receiving Tomb and Niche Wall was discussed and Supt.
Holman stated that the last week of September was the starting date for construction. Rock of Ages was
to call a week before to confirm.
The subject of Rules and Regulations was brought up, and it was agreed that the opening and closing of
the Cemeteries was our call. Also, it was decided to re-write the vehicular traffic regulations next year.
Supt. Holman mentioned that the Lions Club is working to replace one of the Blue Spruce trees that they
had donated, because it didn't survive.
Under new business, sealed bids were opened and reviewed for the North Cemetery paving project,
including the State mandated regulations for handicap access. It was decided to take them under
advisement until the June meeting because of the time frame for appropriations
The tree cutting project at South Cemetery Section #1, was discussed and after reviewing the three bids,
Commissioner Mendoza made a motion to accept the bid of T. Bennett Tree Service for the amount of
$7200.00. Commissioner Tyler seconded, and with no further discussion, the motion passed
unanimously. The three bids for Section #2 was discussed, and a motion by Commissioner Tyler was
made to accept the bid of $4800.00 by M&M Tree Service and was seconded by Commissioner
Mendoza, no further discussion ensued, so the motion passed unanimously.
The paving project at South Cemetery which consists of paving a section of roadway 100 feet by 10 feet
was discussed with three bids before the Commission. Commissioner Tyler made a motion to accept the
bid of $2000.00 by Miller's Paving, with a second from Commissioner Mendoza, and without further
discussion the motion passed unanimously.
New street signs at the North and South Cemetery was discussed and a motion by Commission Tyler was
made to accept the bid from Atlantic Broom for 39- 6- inch signs and 22 crosses, for a total of $1077.44.
Commissioner Mendoza seconded and the motion passed unanimously.
After some discussion, it was decided to send a letter to Mr. David Dunn, 22 Bennett Rd. Gardner, Ma. A
letter requesting the bench be removed from their gravesite along with the sneakers that are hanging
===PAGE 2===
from a pole. According to our regulations, the gravesite may contain either a headstone or a bench, not
both. Adornments such as the sneakers are not allowed.
Lastly, the subject of entry signs that display Cemetery rules was brought up, because of individuals
letting their dogs run loose. It was decided to include just 2 rules for the present, as other rules could be
added at a later time. One being that dogs must be leashed and shrubs must be kept trimmed. A motion
was made to purchase 2- 3 x 5 foot signs from D&G Graphics, not to exceed $2500.00, one for North and
one for South Cemetery, by Commissioner Mendoza, seconded by Commissioner Tyler, with no further
discussion, the motion passed unanimously. Supt. Holman was to report back in October with further
rules and regulations.
With no further new business, Chairman May asked for a motion to adjourn, with Commissioner
Mendoza offering that motion. Commissioner Tyler seconded, and it was passed unanimously to adjourn
the meeting. Meeting was adjourned at 10:05 pm.
Respectfully Submitted,

The Lunenburg Cemetery Commission met on October 12** at the Lunenburg Public Library.
The meeting was called to order at 7:00 pm. Members present were Commissioners Lance May,
Lorraine Mendoza, William Tyler and Cemetery Supt. Todd Holman.
Minutes from the September 7 meeting were reviewed and upon a motion by Commissioner
Mendoza to accept the minutes and a second by Commissioner May, and with no further
discussion, the motion passed unanimously.
No correspondence was received.
During Public Comment, Steven Archambault from the Charter Review Committee, mentioned
that the review process would be beginning shortly, and we would be invited to participate.
Under Old Business, Commissioner Tyler stated that he would draft a letter to Mr. Dunn
concerning the adornments and bench at the South Cemetery that are in violation of the Rules
and Regulations under Chapter 310.
The Columbarium was installed on October 10* at the North Cemetery under the direction of
Supt. Holman and the crew from Rock of Ages and Leominster Monument. It was suggested
that a photo should be taken of the Columbarium with members of the Commission, Supt.
Holman, former Supt. Bingham, former Commission Chair Steve Schaff and a representative of
Leominster Monument, for insertion into the Lunenburg Ledger.
Rates for the Columbarium Niche Wall was discussed, and a motion by Chairman May was
made to set the rate of $1200.00 per niche, which would hold 2 cremations, and would also
include the engraving. Also in the motion was to have only Leominster Monument do the
engraving. The motion was seconded by Commissioner Tyler, and with no further discussion
was passed unanimously. We also have three extra cover plates, so that the niches will always
be sealed during engraving.
Chairman May mentioned that he is going to draft a letter to the Town Manager concerning
Supt. Holman's availability for year- round burials since the Niche Wall will be accessible during
the Winter months.
Supt. Holman presented the Commissioners with several invoices including repairs to the old
Ferris mower, tree work in the South Cemetery, shrub replacement for shrubs that were
===PAGE 2===
damaged by accident and an invoice for the new Ferris mower recently purchased from
Sheperd's. These were all approved with the exception of the new Ferris mower. The
Commission had concerns that since we were buying it, we wanted to make sure it was used
only by Supt. Holman and only for the Cemeteries.
Town Manager Heather Lemieux was present as an invited guest and agreed to draft a letter
stating that the new Ferris mower would be the property of the Cemetery Dept, and would not
be used for any other purpose. After some discussion a motion was made by Commissioner
May to pay for the new mower, understanding that a letter would be forthcoming. The motion
was seconded by Commissioner Tyler and with no further discussion was approved
unanimously.
Lastly, Supt. Holman mentioned that he would like to attend Dept. Superintendent meetings,
and the Town Manager said she would make sure he is included in the future.
With no further new business to discuss, a motion was made by Commissioner Tyler to
adjourn, seconded by Commissioner May, with no further discussion, the meeting was
adjourned at 9:11 pm
Respectfully Submitted
William Tyler, Clerk.

The Lunenburg Cemetery Commission met on November 30, 2017 at the Lunenburg Public Library.
The meeting was called to order at 7:10 pm. Members present were Commissioners Lance May, Lorraine
Mendoza, William Tyler and Cemetery Supt. Todd Holman.
Minutes from the October 12' meeting were reviewed and upon a motion by Chairman May to accept
the minutes, seconded by Vice-Chairman Mendoza, and with no further discussion, the motion passed
unanimously.
Chairman May's letter to Town Manager Heather Lemieux was discussed concerning Supt. Holmans
availability for Winter burials since the new Columbarium has been installed. The Commission discussed
the November 27* letter that was sent to the Town Manager, Heather Lemieux, and agreed to direct the
Chairman to follow-up with her should no reply be received.
Nancy Gray, a member of the Charter Review Committee appeared before the Commission to discuss
our involvement in the process. It was decided to have the Charter Review Committee contact us for a
time and date for a meeting.
Account expenses was the next topic, and with an update from the Town accountant, there is
$4892.87 left in the Capital Article. The only expenses left to be subtracted are $422.00 for loam and
$100.00 for the outhouse the prisoners from the Worcester County House of Correction use while
working at the Cemeteries. It was also discussed whether the prisoners might be available in the Spring.
Bids for the lettering of the niches at the new Columbarium will be opened and awarded at the
January meeting.
It was decided that the Rules and Regulations will be amended to include rates for the new Niches in
the Columbarium. Also, Steve Malandrinos, IT director, will be contacted to reflect the changes in our
Rules and Regulations, on the Town's website.
Kenneth Chester and Bruce Chester both wrote letters of thanks for Supt. Holman in regards to their
recent loss, and how grateful they were for his help.
The Boy Scout project is almost completed at North Cemetery, with concrete poured and benches to
be installed around the Flag pole, in honor of our Veterans.
With no further business to discuss, a motion was made by Chairman May to adjourn, seconded by
Vice Chairman Mendoza, the meeting was adjourned at 9:30 pm.
Respectfully submitted, William Tyler, Clerk
