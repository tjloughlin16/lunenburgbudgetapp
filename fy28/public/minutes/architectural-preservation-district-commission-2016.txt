Town of Lunenburg  
Architectural Preservation District Commission  
Richard McGrath- Chair 
Jim LaVeck- Vice Chair 
Cullen Dwyer- Recording Clerk 
Meeting Minutes, January 4
th , 2016 
7:15PM- meeting called to order with all three members present.  Also present was Selectman Bob 
Ebersol to assist the Commission at its first meeting. 
Officers were elected by unanimous vote: 
 Richard McGrath- Chair 
Jim LaVeck- Vice Chair  
Cullen Dwyer- Recording Clerk  
Certain errata were noticed in the town bylaw as ratified: 
1)  The bylaw states that the APD consists land within the Lunenburg Village District or 
within the Lunenburg Historical District (or both).  The map attached to the bylaw 
shows the APD as only the Historic District.  Mr. Ebersol commented that the map 
currently attached to the bylaw stands and defines the district.  Land Use Director 
Adam Burney is currently working to draw a corrected map.  
2)  There is certain confusion in the wording of the bylaw as to how many members the 
APDC shall have: five or six.  
3)  In section 8, one passage reads “thirty(45) days”.  The passage should read “forty-
five(45) days”. 
The bylaw will need to be amended to correct these mistakes at the Town Meeting in May 2016. 
There was also discussion regarding the APDC having less than its proposed number of members.  Mr. 
Ebersol commented that the BoS was concerned that having too many residents of the APD on the APDC 
might lend an anti-development bias to the board.  At present the BoS has no viable additional 
candidates for the APDC. 
Since “Demolition by Neglect” is forbidden by the bylaw, there was some discussion of how we would 
enforce this rule, and whether the intentions of the violator might be a factor in the enforcement.  No 
conclusions were reached. 
The Commission recognized its obligation to provide design guidelines for alterations of existing 
contributing properties in the APD.  These must be brought before public hearing and accepted by the 
BoS within 180 days of this meeting.  McGrath agreed to contact Mass Historic to obtain an example of 
===PAGE 2===
 
 
historic design guidelines.  LaVeck agreed to contact the Town of Ipswich and research other towns with 
historic preservation bylaws to see if he could find a set of guidelines to serve as our model. 
The Commission also resolved to meet on Saturday the 16 th  at 8AM for a site visit of the APD, to help the 
APDC formulate design guidelines.  Mr. Ebersol recommended that the APDC inform the police of its 
intentions and to carry identification. 
Mr. Ebersol also suggested that all members acquire and use Town of Lunenburg email addresses for all 
APDC-related business. 
The APDC agreed to have its next meeting on Jan 19 
th , 2016 at the Ritter Building.  McGrath agreed to 
contact the police about the site visit, and to contact the Town Clerk to post the next meeting. 
8:30PM Meeting Adjourned.

Meeting Minutes, January 19, 2016 
Approved: 2-9-16 
Richard McGrath- Chair 
Jim LaVeck- Vice Chair 
Cullen Dwyer- Recording Clerk 
 
7:10 Meeting called to order with all members present. 
 
Mr. Dwyer was not able to print out the minutes from the previous meeting, so approval of 
minutes was forestalled until next meeting. 
Members discussed ideas generated from the APD site-visit on 1-16-16.  Mr. Dwyer presented 
notes on his own reflections from the site visit, which the board agreed would be helpful moving 
forward. (See supporting document “Reflections from Site Visit, 1-16-16”) 
The board recognized that many repairs and improvements that would effect the overall 
appearance of the building would not require a building permit, and therefore would fall under 
exemption 6b of the bylaw.  Mr McGrath resolved to learn what needs or does not need a 
building permit. 
The board agreed that it should consider specific topics before drafting design design guidelines 
for the APD, including: 
Roofs: shingles and materials, dormers and roof additions, skylights, solar panels, windmills, 
vents, heat traces, chimneys, roof pitch, antennas and communication improvements, weather 
vanes, cupolas. 
Facades and sides visible from public way: siding (clapboards, aluminum, vinyl, brick and 
stucco), windows, doors, decks, porches, balconies, steps, railings, exterior lighting, patios, 
walkways, trim, ballisters, foundations. 
 
For Design Guidelines regarding signs, the board must first review the Village District Bylaw to 
see what guidance that document offers. 
 
Design guidelines will be written to cover the majority of cases.  The APDC will retain the right to 
make exceptions to preserve the unique historical character of a building. 
 
The appeals process described in section 13 of the bylaw is still unclear to the board, and 
requires further research. 
 
The board will meet again at 7PM in the Ritter Building on 2-9-16 
 
8:30PM Meeting Adjourned.  
Respectfully submitted, 
Cullen Dwyer, Recording Clerk

Clerk: Cullen Dwyer

Meeting Minutes 
Tuesday, March 8th, 2016 
 
Richard McGrath opened the meeting and called the board to order at 7:08PM 
 
The Committee welcomed new member Brian Corcoran 
 
Minutes from the meeting on 1-4-16 were voted on and approved 
 
Richard McGrath reported on his visit to the building inspector, who told him that all work 
requires a building permit other than repairs, fences under six feet tall, and sheds/play-
structures of less than 200 square feet. 
 
Jim LaVeck suggested that the APDC build its own website, explaining the powers and duties of 
the APDC. 
 
The board discussed the guidelines from the Ipswich APDC.  They provide a valuable 
framework.  The Lunenburg APDC will need to strengthen section IV, to give specific treatment 
of topics like roofs, sheathing, foundations etc.  It will need to give specific recommendations, 
tell property owners what to avoid, and explain what the APDC forbids.  The Ipswich APDC 
guidelines contain photographs and examples of important historic buildings.  A similar review 
was deemed valuable for the Lunenburg guidelines.  To that end, Richard McGrath will contact 
the assessor’s office for existing information and photographs, and recover the existing 
materials gathered for Lunenburg’s Survey of Historical and Architectural Resources (work in 
progress). 
 
The board then turned to the Lunenburg APDC bylaw and discussed the details of the 
application process: what forms were needed, when and how property owners would be 
contacted, time frames, and how internal and official documents would be stored and recorded. 
 
Using the Ipswich guidelines as a model, the board members will each individually draught 
Lunenburg’s guidelines for sections I and II, and compare draughts at the next meeting. 
 
Next meeting set for 3-29-16 
 
Adjourned, 8:40PM 
 
Respectfully Submitted, 
Cullen Dwyer, Recording Clerk, Lunenburg APDC

minutes as read, and LaVeck seconded the motion.  The board voted approval.

None.

from APDC oversight.  The board recognized that special procedures would be needed to deal 
with these situations.

bylaw.  Hard copies were distributed covering topics of roofs, cellars and foundations, temporary 
buildings, and alternate versions of sections 1 and 2 of the guidelines.  The board agreed to 
read these over and discuss them at the next meeting.

Meeting Minutes: Tuesday September 13th, 2016 
 
Call to Order: 
meeting open and called to order at 7:08 PM with all members present, except for Mr. Kherer. 
 
Review of Minutes 
Minutes from 8-23-16 were read, voted on and approved. 
 
Old Business 
A draft of the “Application for Certificate to Alter”
 was presented, reviewed and accepted into the 
guidelines. 
Mr. LaVeck presented the web content for the APDC website, and the commission approved of 
all content, and had nothing to add at that time.

guidelines to the Board of Selectmen, and intended to make the draft of the guidelines available 
to the public by Friday, September 23rd, so that the Commission could hold a public hearing at 
its next meeting on Tuesday, October 11th, 2016.  Once that is done, the guidelines will be 
available for review and acceptance by the Board of Selectmen. 
 
Next meeting set for the second Tuesday of October, the 11th.

Architectural Preservation District Commission 
 
Richard McGrath -Chair 
Jim LaVeck -Vice Chair 
Cullen Dwyer -Clerk 
Brian Corcoran -Member 
Matthew Kherer -Member 
 
Meeting Minutes: Tuesday October 18th, 2016 
 
Call to Order: 
meeting open and called to order at 7:05 PM with all members present, except for Mr. Kherer. 
 
 
Old Business 
Land Use Director Adam Burney attended the meeting to offer input on the draft of the APDC 
guidelines.  He felt that the document the Commission had put forward was not usable and not 
ready to present to the Board of Selectmen.  He praised the style guidelines that the town of 
Ipswich had published for their APD.  He also recommended that the Lunenburg APDC follow 
their lead: by publishing rules, regulations and procedures separately from the style guidelines.  
He further suggested finding a published and widely available guidebook on architectural styles, 
and using it an authoritative reference for the guidelines.  Finally, Mr. Burney presented his own 
recommended outline of the documents, and promised that he would submit the beginnings of a 
draft to the APDC in the coming weeks. 
 
Review of Minutes 
Minutes from 9-13-16 were read, voted on and approved. 
 
New Business: 
The Commission discussed the contraction and restoration work that had been taking place on 
Leominster rd.  A stone wall had been taken down for underground work, but afterwards re- 
stored.  The APDC had not been notified.  McGrath had discovered that permits for this type of 
excavation were granted not by the building inspector, but by the Department of Public Works.  
This was noted by the commission as being of procedural importance. 
 
Adjourned 8:40 PM 
 
Postscript: these minutes approved at 11-8-16 APDC meeting. 
 
Respectfully Submitted, 
Cullen Dwyer, recording clerk
