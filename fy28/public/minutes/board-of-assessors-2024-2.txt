 Clarification of our current situation regarding Exemptions.  
Lynda McQuade spoke with Jim Crowley who is an attorney for the Department 
of Revenue regarding 41C. For 41C to apply you must be 70 years old as of July 1
and the exemption amount is $750. For 2024 we must go by the income 
limitations which is $315 for single person and $396 for a married person. The 
asset limitation for a single person is $527 and a married person is $727. COLA 
must be adopted to have 41D as an exemption. That will then allow for COLA 
yearly and be able to follow the legislature income limitations.
