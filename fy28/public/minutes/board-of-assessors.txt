- Senior Means Tested Exemption/H.3911 Plan
 John Rabbitt and Lynda McQuade did some research and found that in towns 
like Concord and Westford have different applications and forms related to 
the means tested exemption. There were questions about the qualifying age 
and whether it was age 70 or age 65. Due to the changes we made to 41C it 
was determined the minimum age would be 65. Lynda McQuade provided 
the Board with a copy of the law that was signed by Governor Healey on 
January 6th.
The qualification list includes:
 - Resident must be 65 or older.
 - Resident must have lived in the town and occupied a home 
for not less than 15 consecutive years.

 - If they haven’t received a circuit breaker then they do not 
qualify.
