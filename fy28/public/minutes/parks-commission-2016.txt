1) Call to Order - 7:06
Attendance: Dennis Mannone, Karin Menard, Jack Rodriquenz, Gen Nass
2) June 2, 2016 Minutes - Approved 2-0
3) Board Reorganization - Chair: Dennis Mannone, Clerk: Karin Menard
4) Turf Complex Update and Discussion - Town Manager is working with bond council. Project
timing is up in the air and may be started over the Summer. More information will be forthcom-
ing. Memorandum of Agreement between schools and Parks Commission will need to be dis-
cussed and tweaked in the future.
5) Town Beach - Gen Nass, Beach Director
a) Reviewed Christians Law (Massachusetts General Law c. 111, §127A½)- allows for in
fants, toddlers and those below a level III in Red Cross to wear life jackets while partici-
pating in "municipal and recreational programs and camps". Question surrounding
whether or not the town beach falls under these guidelines as we are neither a program or
a camp. Parks Commissioners will review law and Gen Nass's proposal.
b) Weeds have continued to thrive in the roped swim area. There is currently no weed
control on the lake or within roped area. Too late for action this season but we can re-
search for next year and can preliminarily contact Conservation Commission to see if
they need to be involved. Lake Shirley Improvement Corporation uses Solitude (contact:
Dominic Meringold) and they might be a good place to start the research.
===PAGE 2===
c) Board of Health requesting access to town beach when not opened for water quality
testing. Per Jack Rodriquenz, they just need to call the office and someone from the
DPW will go down and open the gate when requested.
d) Fees and Schedule - Current family fees start at $40 for beach passes and Swim
Lessons are capped at $30 per family per session. Fees have not been looked at in many
years. Beach has historically been open for 8 weeks, closing a week or two before school
starts. Thought should be given as to whether we should extend to a 9 week season
startinging in 2017. Since the summer season is already upon us, discussion was tabled
on both matters until the winter.
e) Request for an on call, as needed, life guard be hired in case one of their regular full or
part time life guards are unable to work their shift. Ms. Nass recommends hiring her
grandson, Ethan Gengler, for this position. Ms. Nass also forwarded a letter of disclosure
as advised by the Ethics Commission as well as the Disclosure by Non Elected Municipal.
Employee of Financial Interest and Determination by Appointing Authority form.
Motion made by Dennis Mannone / Seconded Karin Menard - Approve as presented
hiring of an as needed part time seasonal employee to cover life guard duties. Approved -
2-0
6) Marshall Park Update
a) Per Jack Rodriquenz nothing to report on parking lot
b) Waiting on Surfside gave quote but waiting on sketch. Only group that we know of
using the main field on Chestnut are Babe Ruth (approximately 6 practices). Temporary
decision to halt games and batting tunnel required for practices is still in place.
7) DPW Director Update - Waiting on checks for usage fees from the Bengals and Little League.
8) Public / Board Comment - Bob Robuccio resigned from the Parks Commission effective
6/22/16
Adjournment 9:00
Next Meeting - July 21, 2016 at 7pm.

3.
Town Manager Updates - Verizon Wireless plan to build cell tower is moving along and
will be filing special permit to build tower. This will take time but she is anticipating
rental fees for tower to begin coming in this fiscal year.
4.
Old Business
a. Turf Complex - Pete McCauliff gave update on timeline. Explained that the
process was considerably more demanding than expected. Working with out-
side agencies to draw up plan to go out to bid. Mr. McCauliff is hopeful for a late
August start date and estimates 3-4 weeks for completion of turf and 3-4 days
for completion of track once started. Town Manager reported that information is
being provided to bond council for financing and they are reviewing agreements
to determine which interest rate we will fall under.
b. Town Beach
i. Gen Nass reports that the season is going well and there has been high atten-
dance. First session of swimming lessons coming to and end and the second
session starting next week. Trash has not been picked up on a regular basis and
it needs to be as there are only three barrels at the beach and although they are
only filling one or so a week, they are getting full and need to be emptied. Karin
Menard stated she would contact DPW and request regular pick up.
===PAGE 2===
ii. Kerry LaFleur updated the commission and Gen Nass on the hiring proce-
dures of the town. Any position, whether seasonal as needed or otherwise
needs to be posted and the formal hiring procedure needs to be followed per
town charter. At this time, the request and subsequent approval of hiring a part
time as needed lifeguard has been tabled due to time lines and upcoming end to
the beach season. We should be looking to be hiring for the summer beach sea-
son in April or May. Kerry also stated that the current conflict of interest laws
are tougher now and that she has an opinion from labor council that an employ-
ee can not be supervised by a relative. She said Nancy Forest is up to speed on
seasonal employment and could provide information on the process. Karin
asked how the hiring process works for seasonal positions as the same life
guards seem to automatically come back season after season. Kerry explained
that the Parks Commission should reappoint people on a yearly basis. Dennis
added that we need to create a review process in the off season and that the
beach should be on the agenda over the winter for review of policies, proce-
dures and fees.
ili. Gen Nass requested permission for the Parks Commission to sponsor the
Card Board Boat Race. She said that the race has become a popular event dur-
ing the summer but the new children's librarian, who has organized it in the past,
is not organizing it this year. Since the event has always happened at the beach
and staffed with lifeguards, she wanted to know if it was possible to organize it
with the approval of the Parks Commission.
Motion made by Karin Menard, seconded by Pete McAulliff - for the Parks
Commission to Sponsor the Card Board Boat Race - approved 3-0.
iv. Gen said the current plan is for the beach to close on August 14th but she
would like to extend the season, due to the great weather and the high atten-
dance, to August 19th. Karin pointed out that without the DPW director present
that she wasn't sure if there was enough funds in the budget to cover it.
Motion made by Karin Menard seconded by Dennis Mannone to extend the
beach season through August 19th pending confirmation of available funds with
the DPW director. passed 3-0.
c. Marshall Park
i. Kerry provided background that the FY15 Capital Plan approved a park
ing lot at Marshall Park off of Chestnut Street. Due to a series of events and the
vendor doing the work was available sooner than expected and that no letter
went out to abutters before work started. As soon as work started, an abutter
contacted the town stating work had been done on her land. Work was halted
and the town worked with the abutter to mediate boundary issue as there was a
===PAGE 3===
conflict between the towns deed and the abutters deed. There is a warrant arti-
cle for the Special Town Meeting in August, that in a nut shell would use the
stone wall as the boundary marker, not require remediation of work done on
abutters side of the wall and allow Parks and the DPW to decide what to do on
the town side.
Motion by Pete McCauliff and seconded by Dennis Mannone to support the
town creating a boundary agreement with the adjacent property owner a town
meeting. Passed 3-0.
ii. Pete McCauliff said he joined the DPW director and other interested parties at
the Chestnut Street field for a brief presentation on the plan to install a netted backstop that
would help eliminate damage being caused by foul balls. He had no concerns that it would
negatively impact games held at that field enough to be concerned. The DPW director was not
in attendance but Pete said that he was continuing to work on it.
d. Usage fees - topic passed over.
5. New Business
a. Policy discussion, scheduling of fields, courts and beaches under the Park
Commission umbrella
i. Scheduling of turf field will remain with the Athletic Director until turf/
track project completed this fall.
ii. Pete McCauliff stated that due to the turf resurfacing and the shortage
of fields due to continuing demolition/construction, Lunenburg Public
Schools would like to request use of Marshall Park for some of their sports pro-
grams. Currently, there are no other requests for usage so there are no sched-
uling conflicts at this time.
Motion made by Dennis Mannone / Seconded by Karin Menard - Approve short
term usage of Marshall Park (Chestnut Street field) by Lunenburg Public Schools
for fall sports programs. Approved 2-0 (PM abstained from voting)
ill. Brief discussion on timing of policy creation for fields and beaches that fall
under the park commission umbrella. Parks Commissioners all agree that this
will be a lengthy process best dealt with in the upcoming months.
6. Other business
a. no unanticipated items
b. no correspondence or announcements.
Motion to adjourn 9:01 by Karin Menard seconded by Pete McAuliff.

1) Call to Order - 7:01
Attendance - Pete McCauliff, Dennis Mannone, Karin Menard, Jack Rodriquenz.
2) Motion to approve Minutes of August 18, 2016 made by Karin Menard, seconded by Dennis Mannone-
approved 3-0
3) DPW Director / Town Manager Updates - Fitzgerald Field has been used by a team for fall ball. Not
sure who they are but Jack will investigate to see which organization they are with. He will also follow
up with Steve Chow regarding Fall Little League. There has been some concern regarding playground
equipment meeting current standards. In order to investigate further, photographs should be taken to
forward to someone with expertise in this area to determine next steps. Karin will photograph and email
photographs to Jack.
4) Ongoing Business
a) Turf Complex - discussion and update
i. Pete updated that RAD will be starting demolition on Tuesday. Rubber is to be deliv-
ered early October and they are hoping about a 2 week job. Once turf is removed they
will be able to give a better timeline. Dennis asked about field lines and Pete responded
that RAD will put all necessary lines on the field. Track will be done as soon as turf is
completed. No games scheduled until both are completed.
ii. Leominster Lacrosse responded with email with more details about their request to use
the turf for their LAX program in the Spring of 2017. Per email of 8/18 that was sent to
Karin, Larry Cordio, Leominster Lacrosse President said they are requesting use of the
turf April 2nd, 9th, 23rd, 30th, May 7th, 14th, 21st and June 14th, 2017. They expect to
play between 4 and 8 games per Sunday for both their girls and boys teams. This trans-
lates to 125-250 players per Sunday and he estimates 300-600 spectators or so. These
===PAGE 2===
dates are their regular season games. Discussion amongst commissioners to determine a
price structure followed.
Motion made by Karin Menard, seconded by Dennis Mannone - to allow Leominster
Lacrosse to use the turf field for 8 Sundays at a rate of $7500 for the season. Additional
expense for field attendant (set up and clean up) which is estimated at $25-$50 per hour
as well as police detail at the discretion of Lunenburg PD to be the responsibility of
Leominster Lacrosse. passed 3-0
5) New Business
a) request from local Triple Play team for Marshall Park usage - Arthur Thibeault, coach for the
Lunenburg Iriple Play team contacted both Dennis and Karin about using Marshall Park (Chest-
nut Street field). Dennis believes they are looking for Wednesdays. Pete said Field Hockey is
using the field this fall for their practices and he would have to wait until Field Hockey is over to
begin theirs. They are there M-F and possibly a Saturday. Discussion continued about pricing.
Motion made by Karin Menard, seconded by Dennis Mannone to allow Triple Play Fall Baseball
to use Marshall Park at a rate of $25 per hour: Insurance certificate must be provided prior to
first practice. Schedule needs to be passed through PM to cross reference availability. passed
3-0.
b) Policy/procedure discussion, fees, and scheduling of fields, courts and beaches under
the Parks Commission umbrella.
Development of field request form started. Reviewed policies and permit applications of other
towns and created a list of what we felt was needed for Lunenburg's field usage.
Dennis asked Jack if he could provide an analysis of the costs to maintain the fields so we could
better understand the yearly maintenance and upkeep fees. Jack said he would research and get
the information to us at a future meeting.
Currently we are charging Lunenburg Little League $10 per player per season. Karin will request
more information from Steve Chow, Lunenburg Little League President, for their participation
numbers so we can better gage the fees.
Karin will take the information we discussed and create a policy and application document that.
we can review at our next meeting.
6) Other Business
a) No unanticipated items
b) No Correspondence, Announcements and Public Comment
7) Next meeting: October 20, 2017.
8) Motion for adjournment made at 8:31 by Karin Menard, seconded by Pete McCauliff. passed 3-0:

b: October 20, 2016 - Dennis Mannone motioned to accept minutes and Pete McCauliff seconded. Ap-
proval passed 3-0.
3) DPW Director / Town Manager Updates - not present
4) Ongoing Business
a. Leominster Lacrosse Turf Request - Trent Larson and Chuck Courtemarche were present to repre-
sent Leominster Youth Lacrosse. Chuck explained that a good number of players in their program
were from Lunenburg and some teams were comprised 100% of Lunenburg kids so it made sense
to be looking in the Lunenburg area for field space. Trent explained that they are currently using
Doyle but due to the growth of their program they would like to split between locations and that if
they could use the turf in Lunenburg there would be 6 games tops each Sunday for 8 Sundays.
Pete said that there are two things that wear the field - sun and usage - but felt that 8 Sundays
would not make or break us and it might be a good test run. Trent relayed that the pricing structure
that Karin sent to Larry Cordio, club president, earlier in the fall was too steep for them and they
are hoping to work out a deal for $100/hour or $600 a day preferably less. Pete said it would be
$28+ an hour for a field monitor but that he thought we would be able to work out $6300 for the 8
===PAGE 2===
weeks (56 hours/$112.50hr/minimum 3 hours). He also told Trent and Chuck, and reminded the
rest of the commission, that the turf would be resurfaced in the Spring which may bump them one
weekend. Karin asked when the schedules come out since exact usage would depend on their
game needs. Trent said teams are firmed up by the end of February and games, which start the first
week of April, are solid by mid March. Trent and Chuck would take this information back to their
group and get back to us.
b. Turf Complex Update and Discussion - Pete advised the the turf is finished and that temporary
lines are painted. Track will be resurfaced in the spring. The materials have been paid for and the
installation is outstanding as he is asking for clarification on what still needs to be done before he
does so. The labor for the track is also included in the bill he received but he said that won't be
paid until the work is done. There are also extra pellets and turf that will also need to be stored in
case it is needed in the future for repairs.
c. Continuation of policy / procedure discussions, fees and scheduling of fields, courts and beaches
under the Parks Commission umbrella - Form is coming together for field usage. Karin will work
on creating an in-town youth group form for the groups that pay per player (i.e. Lunenburg Little
League) or have a financial agreement with the town (i.e. Bengals and LYSA). Dennis also would
like to ask Amanda to put together some sort of cost schedule so we can compare it to the current
Lunenburg Little League per player fee that they are currently paying. Karin said she would con-
tact Amanda and get back to the commission with the information.
5) Other Business
a. Unanticipated items - none
b. Correspondence, Announcements and Public Comment - non
6) Next meeting January 19, 2017.
