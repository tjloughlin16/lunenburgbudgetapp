AGRICULTURAL COMMISSION 
February 20, 2020 
Ritter Memorial Building 
 960 Massachusetts Avenue 
 
Present: M. Allison, R. Stober, G. Burns, J. Marzuca, M. Fontaine, Y. Fusaro,  
   M. Holman, K. McClellan 
Chair called the meeting to order at 7:03pm.   
 
General discussion began welcoming Maryrae Holman and Kira McClellan, and discussion of 
their interests and involvement with agriculture. 
 
R. Stober corrected a spelling error in the minutes of September 19, 2019 and moved to accept 
the minutes of Sept as revised, Oct and Nov as presented.  G. Burns 2 
nd , all aye 
 
Mention was made of interested party for Economic Development Committee and warrant 
articles were discussed by mention only.

2020 market rules and application as previously discussed.  The opening day was moved up to 
the last Sunday in May, the time and fees remained the same.  Discussion included pros and 
cons of starting earlier.  G. Burns moved we accept the 2020 LCFM rules and regs.  G. Burns 
2
nd , all aye. 
 
There was a group discussion on all the ongoing agenda items for the benefit of potential new 
volunteers.  Discussion moved to what was involved, the difference between member and 
alternate, and how one goes about the application and appointment process.  Each decided to 
apply.  R. Stober moved that Maryrae Holman be recommended for appointment as a member.  
G. Burns 2 
nd , all aye.  G. Burns moved that Kira McClellan be recommended for appointment as 
alternate.  J. Marzuca 2 nd , all aye. 
 
Next meeting set for March 19. 
 
R. Stober moved to adjourn, J. Marzuca 2nd, adjourned at 8:39pm.

Meeting Minutes 
AGRICULTURAL COMMISSION 
September 17, 2020 
Zoom Meeting 
 
Present: M. Allison, R. Stober, J. Marzuca, M. Holman, K. McClellan 
Chair called the meeting to order at 7:05pm.   
 
Public Comment/Open Discussion: none 
 
Reorganization: R, Stober nominated M. Allison for chair, J. Marzuca 2nd.  Roll call, all aye. 
J. Marzuca nominated R. Stober for vice-chair, M. Holman 2nd.  Roll call, all aye. 
R. Stober nominated J. Marzuca for clerk, M. Holman 2nd.  Roll call, all aye. 
 
-Farmers Market report:  M. Allison, official bathroom keeper, reported market is going very well.  
There were 19 vendors for last 3 weeks.   
-Economic Development Comm. appointment was discussed.  R. Stober asked if the Select 
Board had even mentioned it.  M. Allison responded not to his knowledge. 
-BrightFarms application was discussed.  Discussion was had on the potential impact to local 
farmers.   
-Letter to town officials, RE: farm complaints.  M. Holmans draft was reviewed and discussion 
had on who to send it to.  R. Stober moved to approve the letter, J. Marzuca 2nd.  Roll call, all 
aye. 
-Draft a flyer:  M. Holman suggested single sheet, tri-fold with map of farms on one side.   
-Farm inspections: Discussion included protocol for farm/ barn inspections, backyard chickens 
and coops, farm/animal complaints, animal control officer (ACO), livestock vs. pets, field driver 
and pound keeper appointments. 
-Lanni Orchard request for infrastructure:  Jerry Lanni had requested sewer extension from 
Sewer Commission, Select Board, Planning Board, and had reached out to AgCom for any 
assistance.  Sewer service would significantly improve the ability for commercial agriculture on 
the 70 acres in Agricultural Preservation Restriction (APR) 
-Public market:  Review of ability to have year round market space. 
-Bylaw references to farm size:  Review of 5ac vs. 2ac farm size requirement. 
-AgCom holding land:  Review of other commissions, discussion of what would be needed, and 
mention of current bid process of Town owned Ag fields. 
-Farm map:  Discussion on types of farms shown on map vs. farm listing. 
 
Next meeting set for October 15, 2020. 
 
R, Stober moved to adjourn, J. Marzuca 2nd.  Roll call, all aye.  Adjourned at 8:21pm.

The Town of Lunenburg, in response to the COVID-19 (Corona Virus) is currently following the guidance 
from the Lunenburg Board of Health, Massachusetts Department of Public Health and the CDC regarding 
the virus and steps communities can take to prevent the spread and all town facilities are currently closed 
to the public.  In accordance with the Governor’s Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open 
Meeting Law, G. L. c. 30A, § 20, all public meetings are being conducted remotely.  This meeting may be 
broadcast live through local access cable, on Facebook Live on the Public Access Facebook page 
(https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-157427270975305/ ) and will be able to be 
found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel within 24 hours after the meeting.  The following 
information is also provided for members of the public that would like to participate in the meeting 
remotely: To Participate Remotely: From a computer, please use the link below to join the public meeting 
webinar: https://us02web.zoom.us/j/82235971157  or from a telephone, dial the toll free number (888) 475-
4499 and enter Meeting ID: 822 3597 1157 If you have not registered with Zoom you will be asked for your 
name and an e-mail address.  Note: No participant ID is needed, just press # when asked. Telephone 
users may dial *9 to request to speak after joining the meeting.  Computer and app users may use the 
“raise hand” feature to request to speak.
