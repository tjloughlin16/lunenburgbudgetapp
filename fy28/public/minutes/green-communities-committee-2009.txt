Town of Lunenburg 
Green Community Task Force 
Meeting Minutes 
March 30,2009 - Amended April 8,2009 – Added D. Blatt to Members attending 
 
Present:   Members:   S.Marsden, P.Slattery, E. Sund, 
Lisa Krowitz, C.Zivojinovic, S.DiCampo, D. Blatt 
 
  Town Liaison:  M. Benson, J. Londa, J. Breault 
  Alternate:   J. McQuaid 
 
1.  Meeting was called to order by Kathryn Herrick Town Clerk, who distributed 
Open Meeting and Conflict of Interest law info. 
2.  Ms. Herrick swore in attending members, liaison s and alternate. 
3. Motion was made by P.Slattery to nominate S.Mars den as Committee Chairman. 
 Motion was seconded and approved unanimously. 
4.  Motion was made by E.Sund to nominate P. Slatte ry as Secretary. 
 Motion was seconded and approved unanimously. 
5.  Each Committee member introduced themselves wit h a brief background 
statement. An email address listing was circulated to enable contact. 
6.  M. Benson shall be the coordinating liaison for  the Town. 
7.  S. Marsden briefly outlined and discussed Energ y Code By-Law models that exist 
along with alternate energy sources. 
8.  Following a brief discussion of the need for a Town Building Inventory, 
P.Slattery distributed a draft Town Building Inventory Form for review, comment 
and amendment. M. Benson will formalize the document and provide copies to 
Town liaison members for completion. 
9.  M. Benson made the Committee aware of directive s from the State which require 
 Wind Energy By-Laws to be available as a matter of  right to citizens. Lunenburg 
presently requires a Special Permit from the Planning Board. Marion noted that 
the State advises it should be handled as part of the Planning Board Development 
Review process and requested a letter from the Committee to the Planning Board 
denoting same. Motion was made, seconded and passed unanimously. P. Slattery 
will provide the letter by 3/31 for the Planning Board meeting. 
10. J. Londa and J. Breault reviewed a grant opport unity for an Energy Audit of Town 
buildings. John and Jim to work toward application letter for 4/15 submittal.  
11.  M. Benson asked Committee to review the Wind E nergy By-Law which is 
available on Town website 
12.  J. McQuaid discussed Unitil packet of informat ion which is available for 
customer use. She will provide a sample packet at the next meeting. 
13.  M. Benson will provide a web link to Green Act  for members. 
14.  Meeting schedules and availabilities were disc ussed. It was decided to meet next 
on Wednesday April 8 
th  at 6PM.  
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
Patrick J. Slattery, Secretary

May 6, 2009 
 
Present:  Members:  S.Marsden, P.Slattery, E. Sund,

1.  Previous meeting minutes were approved as submitted  
2.  Mssrs. Vachon & Patterson gave a comprehensive presentation on the potential 
for Wind Power in Lunenburg. They outlined background, experience and factors 
necessary for consideration of viability of potential wind energy for both private 
and municipal use.  
3.  P. Slattery left meeting at 6:45PM to attend another meeting and returned at 
7:30PM for balance of Vachon Assoc. presentation

 P. Slattery chaired meeting in his absence.

should pick a less busy time to encourage more involvement by the public.

assigned. 
  E. Sund- provided contact and presentation by Vachon Associates 
 P. Slattery – reported that Energy Mangement system on a campus wide 
basis for Town buildings was not a viable consideration at this time. He 
also advised of a seminar he is attending in New York City 6/4/09 on 
Green Design vs. Historic buildings as it was felt this would be a 
beneficial resource for the Task Force and Historical Commission. 
 S. DiCampo – reported on his photo-voltaic research and his attendance 
            at the Lakeview Nurseries open house. He also noted that John Carrol of 
Moss Hollow is a local resource for P/V systems and is available and 
interested in being available to discuss potentials for systems. 
 C. Zivojinovic – reported on MMA seminar results. Distributed Unitil 
energy saving resource brochure and advised of his interest and 
availability of his wife to help develop a web site similar in scale and 
scope to grotonlocal.org. 
 J. Londa- reported on status of Energy Audit initiative. Lunenburg is #84 
in line of Towns that applied and grant decision is due by April 2010. 
Lighting upgrades at middle school from T-12 bulbs to Super T-8 is 
scheduled for funding this year. 
===PAGE 2===
 
J. Breault- reported on the possibility of selling composting material with 
proceeds to go to Town. 
      D.Blatt – reported on viability and costs of solar hot water systems. 
Recommended further info gathering and analysis of potentials for use in 
private and public applications 
 J.McQuaid – reported on a suggested recycling survey of town Depts. She 
advised of lack of recycling at the Housing Authority complex on White

printer cartridges and recommended that recycling dumpsters be placed at

 S. Marsden- reported on potential for methane capture from landfill as not

Town of Lunenburg 
Green Community Task Force 
Meeting Minutes 
July 14, 2009 
 
Present:  Members:  S.Marsden, P.Slattery, E. Sund, 
Lisa Krowitz, D. Blatt 
  Town Liaison: M. Benson, J. Londa, J. Breault 
  Alternate:  J. McQuaid 
  Absent: C.Zivojinovic, S.DiCampo  
 
1.  Previous meeting minutes were approved as submitted  
2.  J. Londa reported on a meeting with Sunlight Solar and their review of a solar array for 
the Turkey Hill Middle School. He advised that the potential would be a system of 45 
KW for that facility. 
3.  PJS advised of his involvement on a project with Alteris Renewables, Inc. who are 
providing a large scale photo-voltaic installation at Phoenix Park in Shirley, MA which 
will in the end provide nearly all the electrical energy for the park 
4.  S. Marsden presented information on the potential for solar roof arrays for a company 
that rents roof area as a possible source of enhanced revenue for the Town. 
5.  A meeting with the Selectmen is scheduled for August 4th. Committee needs to be 
prepared to make recommendations for Town action. Committee needs to analyze and 
propose projects and identify funded versus non funded efforts. 
6.  Solar hot water, wind energy, photovoltaic and education are the most viable approaches 
toward Green Community goals for Lunenburg. 
7. PJS requested clarification of costs related to selling power back to the grid. S. Marsden 
advised that costs are related to what utilities pay to buy their power which is different 
from what they sell power for. 
8. J. McQuaid advised that a key component of savings is reduced demand  
9. E. Sund and S. Marsden discussed wind study costs at $100K 
10. M. Benson advised that streetlighting is not a viable project to pursue. The Town 
streetlights are owned by Unitil and not the Town. Parking lot lighting is not a practical 
project at this time. 
11. Solar hot water possibilities were discussed noting that the public Safety building and 
middle Schhool buildings offer potentials for same. 
12.  J. McQuaid noted that the Education component including brochures, web site with 
appropriate links and school initiatives provides the desired Town wide approach. 
13.  Brief discussions about paper retrievers and mercury thermostat replacements was held. 
14.  D. Blatt advised vthat the only true Town wide solution is wind energy. S. Marsden 
advised that the wind farm approach was viable noting that the wind farm area need not 
be necessarily located in Lunenburg. 
15.  E. Sund presented a listing of possible wind energy sites in Lunenburg  
16.  Web design is in process through the subcommittee for same. 
 
17.  M. Benson reported on DOER and stretch codes. S. Marsden advised of the difficulties 
with adopting the stretch code at this time. 
18.  S. Marsden requested that Committee members report any information for inclusion in 
the presentation before the Selectmen to him no later than July 24th. 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
Patrick J. Slattery, Secretary

Benson, advisory member). Bid will be opened at Town Hall at 2:00 P.M. on November 24, 
2009.  Meeting will follow at the Ritter at 4:00 P.M.

invitations from the bid results. 
Meeting will be held on December 3, 2009 at 4:30 P.M. at the Ritter with interviews scheduled at

fields. Ensuing discussion noted small energy possibilities.

Kopelman & Paige.

Green Community Task Force 
Workshop Meeting Notes   
October  27, 2009 
  
Present:   Members:  S.Marsden, P.Slattery, Lisa Krowitz, C.Zivojinovic 
  Town Liaison: M. Benson, J. Londa   
  Alternate:  J. McQuaid 
  Absent: D. Blatt, S.DiCampo, E. Sund, J.Breault  
Guest:   D. Zivojinovic 
 
1. Website design and layout was discussed at length and viewed online. Consensus is that 
tabbed home page and keyed graphics for each component is best approach. D. 
Zivojinovic will continue to work on layout and expects to have it in presentable form for 
Committee review on November 17,2009.  
2. Committee reviewed, edited and approved a disclaimer to be appended to web page. 
3. J. McQuaid reported that 100 copies of the Energy Star brochure are being delivered by 
courier shortly for Task Force distribution.  
4. C. Zivojinovic will be the single source contact for the web page. Members wishing to 
work on or provide information for the website should coordinate that through C. 
Zivojinovic.  
5. J. McQuaid will coordinate cable tv bulletin page information regarding Green 
Community Task force information. 
6. Next meeting will be November 10, 2009. 
7. Workshop adjourned at 5:30PM 
      
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Patrick J. Slattery, Secretary

Green Community Task Force

November  17, 2009 
  
Present:    Members:   S.Marsden, P.Slattery, L. Krowitz, E. Sund, C.Ziv ojinovic 
  Town Liaison:  M. Benson, J. Londa, J.Breault  
  Alternate:   J. McQuaid 
  Absent: D. Blatt, S.DiCampo,  
Guest:   Kelly Brown – MA Dept of Energy Resources   
 
1.   Website design and layout is being finalized and is online for Member review.  
Courtney discussed particular questions and details. Agenda, minutes and calendar were 
items that could be accessed through “Our Team” tab . Geo-thermal info is also being 
added as a tab item.   
2.   Minutes approval delayed pending receipt by all members. 
3.   Kelly Brown Worcester County regional Rep from DER reviewed Committee efforts to 
date. An involved discussion was held about the viability of adopting the “stretch code” 
and what benefit it brought to Lunenburg. It appears to be a redundant requirement given 
the pending Energy Code upgrade being readied by the BBRS as part of the Mass. 
Building Code and yet it is a requirement of program compliance.  We reviewed the 
Town’s Green Community Application noting complianc e with applicable criteria. At 
this time Lunenburg definitely meets 1 of the criteria and possibly could meet 2 
additional ones. This efforts is directed at being eligible for receipt of a portion of an $8 
million funding source. She noted that additional information is available on the DER 
website and and encouraged members to view same.   
4.   Briefly discussed Solar & Wind By-law articles for Dec. 1 
st  Town meeting. S. Marsden 
will speak on the article.    
5.   E. Sund reported on Wind Energy initiatives noting a lack of grant money available. He 
is also working on Data Logger efforts.  
6.   Future meeting schedule:  November 24, 2009 @4:30P M 
   (review solar panel bids) 
   December 1, 2009 @4:30PM 
   December 3, 2009 @ 5PM to 8PM 
   (Interview 3 low solar project bidders) 
   December 8, 2009 @ 6:30PM 
7.  PJS reported on Utility Task Force meeting he attended, noting that this will be a long 
process with undefined expectations of direction and success. 
8.  Meeting adjourned at 6:30PM

Town of Lunenburg 
Green Community Task Force 
Sub-Committee Meeting Notes   
December  1, 2009 
  
Present:   Members:  S.Marsden, P.Slattery, L. Krowitz, S.DiCampo  
  Town Liaison: M. Benson, J. Londa, J.Breault  
    
 
1.  J. Londa reported on bid process for photo-voltaic panels planned for municipal 
buildings. Bids were due today at 2PM. Only 1 bid was received and it was 
delivered later than the 2 PM deadline. Bid received was from Green Bridge Co. 
and limited the bid proposal to a 90kw system on 1 building, the Turkey Hill 
Middle School.  
2.  A letter was received from Moss Hollow Solar explaining their reason for not 
submitting a formal bid. Specifically, with government funding unavailable at the 
present time they felt that the project as proposed would not be economically 
viable.   
3.  J. Londa reviewed a spreadsheet he prepared on the basis of the Green Bridge 
proposal noting that without the government reimbursement the project would 
not be beneficial to the Town. It did show that a 15 year proposal was the most 
advantageous to the Town.  
4.  The Green Bridge bid information was felt to be helpful to J. Londa and his grant 
proposal due in Boston by 12/7/09. 
5. J. Londa will send a letter to Green Bridge thanking them for their effort in 
bidding the project and telling them that the Committee is taking their bid under 
advisement for discussion at their next meeting.   
6.  Briefly discussed Solar & Wind By-law articles for this evening’s Town meeting. 
S. Marsden will speak on the article.    
7.  Future meeting schedule:  December 8, 2009 @ 6:30PM 
8.  Meeting adjourned at 5:10PM 
      
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Patrick J. Slattery, Secretary

Town of Lunenburg 
Green Community Task Force 
Meeting Minutes   
December  8, 2009 
  
Present:   Members:  S.Marsden, P.Slattery, D. Blatt, S.DiCampo, E. Sund,  
  Town Liaison: M. Benson, J. Londa,  
  Alternate:  J. McQuaid 
  Absent: L. Krowitz,  C.Zivojinovic, J.Breault 
 
1.  Scheduled guest David Demers was ill and will reschedule appearance for a later date to 
talk about Fire Prevention Regulations as they relate to roof top solar panel installations.    
2. Minutes of 12/1 workshop were approved and initialed. 
3. Kelly Brown Worcester County Regional Rep from DER has followed up with 
information. M. Benson has had trouble opening it but will report on content.  
4.   M. Benson advised of dedication of Town of Princeton Wind Turbine installation which 
will provide 40% of Town’s power. 
5. J. Londa reported that solar grant request has been submitted to Boston. 
6. M. Benson will contact new Town Tech Director, Nancy Strong, to enable web page 
access for J. McQuaid. 
7.  J. McQuaid to contact MassSave about obtaining kilowatt meters for the Town residents 
and have them distributed on a borrowing basis through the Library.  
      8.  D. Blatt reported on cash incentives for recycling initiatives. 
      9.   D. Blatt reported on feasibility of small scale solar or residential installations of 
solar noting that with the Commonwealth Solar fund depleted for large projects,   
 practicality is limited. 
10. E. Sund reported that he had received the first building permit issued for a wind 
 Turbine in Town following the passage of the wind energy bylaw.  
11.  S. Marsden will make an update presentation to the Selectmen in January   
 outlining accomplishments and activities of Commission. 
12.  J. McQuaid reported on conversation with Town Manager about possible 
 availability of up to $3,000 for recycling education initiatives. M. Benson & J.   
 McQuaid will do further follow-up with Town Manager  
13.  A solar hot water link is need for the website. L. Krowitz and J. McQuaid will   
 work to enable it. 
14.  D. Blatt reported on availability of wind testing equipment that could facilitate 
 identifying wind studies for potential Town wind turbine sites. Equipment cost is  
 in a range of $2,000-$3,000. He will research and report further info at next  
 meeting. 
15.  Future meeting schedule: January 12, 2010 @ 6:30PM 
16.  Meeting adjourned at 8:00PM 
      
Respectfully submitted,
