Meeting Minutes 
May 18, 2015 
 
Roll call: 
1)  Meeting started at 6:00pm. 
2)  Committee members present were Jeff Bajko, Rhonda Lisio, and Steve Walker. 
3)  Non committee members present were Jo McLaughlin. 
Public comments: 
1)  Issue with last meeting posting. 
Review and approve meeting minutes from April 6, 2015: 
1)  Committee reviewed and approved minutes with recommended changes. 
Coordinator's monthly budget report: 
1)  Jo informed us that she needed 3 quotes for AV system improvement proposals. 
2)  Quote from Unique Media systems: $24,069.   
3)  Recorder is not included into the price of Unique Media System provider.  The 
price would be $1300.00.   
4)  HB communications showed no interest after reaching out to them for a quote. 
5)  Jo spoke to Kerri Spiedal about the difficulty of obtaining a third quote.  Kerri just 
told Jo when submitting quotes; include emails attempting to request a quote. 
6)  Committee approved and voted for the bid from Access A/V to upgrade the 
control room in Town hall. 
7)  Jo met with Emily Bodkin, a student who will be volunteering next school year 
2015-2016 at PAC. 
8)  Another Volunteer open house for the first Thursday in June for high school 
students. 
9)  Jo wants to announce a summer video contest for students 7 th  through 12 th 
grade.  Dates will be June 24th  through August 24th .  Objective it to create a 5 to 
10min. video highlighting a person, place, or thing in Lunenburg. Winner will 
receive $250 and will be announce the first week of school.  
10)   Jo purchased three new cameras.  Total cost: $5854.14.    
11)   Jo showed PAC coverage:  Lacrosse games, Pops concert, and double session of 
yoga with Linda. 
12)   Program analysis: 77% is local coverage. 
13)   Ending balance at the end of April is 290,336.35 
14)   Ending balance as of May 18 th  is 288,589.13 
15)   Committee reviewed and approved $2500 from vender line to service line for 
Jason’s salary.       
Bulletin Board Policy: 
1)  The policy will be emailed to Jo and the rest of the committee for review.  
Changes will be made and discussed at next meeting. 
Discussion of salaries/payment and evaluation process of PAC staff: 
1)  Jo is under the administration payment policy. 
2)  Step increase is every two to five years.  
===PAGE 2===
3)  Jo is the only town salary administration plan for PAC.  Everyone else is classified 
as vendors. 
4)  PACC decides salaries, raises, and hires for PAC.  
5)  PACC will evaluate Jo and submit its evaluation to the personal committee, which 
will decide compensation. 
 
Discussion of cable access committee: 
1)  Committee that is looking into renegotiating cable contract with Comcast. 
2)  Town council is asking for a 10 year business plan.  
Agenda Items for next meeting: 
1)  Roll call 
2)  Public comments 
3)  Coordinators monthly report 
4)  Review Vendor payments. 
5)  CAC discussion.  
Adjourned: 
1)  Meeting adjourned at 7:25pm. 
Next Meetings:  
1)  June 8, 2015. 
2)  July 6, 2015. 
 
 
 
Jeffrey Bajko 5/18/15  Approved.

12.  Robert Haig, a distributor of Leightronix, will be giving a product demonstration at the Acton Access

Space Needs: 
1.  The PACC needs to create a wish list of space needs for the studio, office, archives, and accessibility.

3.  The PACC will also explore other avenues, 
4.  Jo will also meet with John to assess needs. 
CAC:

Meeting Minutes 
Nov 5th, 2015 
Roll call: 
 1.  Meeting started at 6:02pm. 
 2.  Committee members present were Jeff Bajko, Rhonda Lisio, Steve Walker and Faith Beall.  
Public Comments:  
 1. Rhonda Lisio announced that she will be stepping down as the chair of PACC and leaving the 
committee. 
Review and approval of coordinators job description 
 1.  Approved with recommended changes and sent to the town human resource department 
for final approval.   
Review draft studio space: 
1. Jo presented a draft of space needed to the building reuse committee. 
2. Jo requested the space to be about 3200-4000 sq. 
3. The reuse committee did not contact the PACC about space needed, but they did directly 
contact Jo due to time restraint. 
4. PACC requested the following for the new studio space:  
•  Town meeting video equipped room (1000sq). 
•  Garage (12 x 24: 300sq) 
•  Parking for eight dedicated spaces 
•  24 hour access/ external access 
•  Total square footage would be around 5000  
•  Utility requirements: 2 bathrooms, full kitchen, and alarm system 
•  Environmental friendly space.  
5. Jeff will rewrite and submit to PACC and reuse committee; Jamie Toale 
jamietoale@comcast.net  for review. 
6. PACC approved of the submisal of the new studio space to the building reuse committee. 
Future of PACC: 
1. Steve, Faith, and Jeff are still committed to the PACC. 
2. Steve will become the Chairman. 
Coordinator’s monthly report: 
1. End of starting balance was $265,970.11 
2. Ending balance of October $276,246.55 
3. Step student evaluation of Emily Bodkin was filled out by Jo and submitted it back to Pete 
McCauliff, her advisor.  
4. 15 new shows were added this month. 
5. Another step student will start in the spring from Lunenburg high school. 
6. Jo is going to video tape at the Eagle House. 
7. A $130,000 mobile unit from Dracut was displayed at the video show. 
8. Steve received information about a drone- $10,000. (Itv) 
9. Jo was contacted about Bridging the Gap through the Boy Scouts for volunteering at the PAC. 
10. Volunteers will be from Fitchburg, Ma, and go through an interview process.

11. Robert from Access\AV suggested towns Jo can go and check out the studios. 
12. Telvue wants to do a product demo for PACC. (HD server) and the committee approved. 
13. Jo will be taking a few days off at the end of Dec. 
14. PACC would like a clearer report of reallocation of funds on production for each show. This 
will be provided next meeting in an Excel sheet by Jo. 
15. Taping of sports is expensive.  PACC wants to make sure that meetings are being taped over 
sports. 
Discussion of Mission Statement: 
1. The committee agreed on the following statement for our PACC mission statement: 
 
Develop, modernize, establish, and operate the Town of Lunenburg’s Public and Government 
(PG) Access Channel(s) to reflect what matters to Lunenburg.  
 
Strategic planning: 
1.  Steve presented five bullet points and wants each member to bring five points as well to 
discuss. 
Discussion of Castus system:

CAC update:

3. Committee asked Jo to directly ask the school if they want an educational channel. 
PACC Comments:
