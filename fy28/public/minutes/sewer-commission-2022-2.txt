Minutes of 7/12/22 Sewer Commission meeting- via Zoom (Video Conference)
Attendees: Chair-John Reynolds, Vice-Chair- Brett Ramsden, Member- Dave MacDonald, DPW Director- Bill
Bernard, Business Manager-Barb Lefebvre, Asst. Business Manager-Paula Bertram, Minutes Clerk- Jane Ranley
Absent: Mike Nault and Mike Deveikis (Clerk)
John opened the meeting at 6:01 pm. and did a roll call of attendees. The Commission began the meeting by saying
the Pledge of Allegiance.
PUBLIC COMMENT- none
ANNOUNCEMENTS - Barb announced they sent out the annual permit applications for grease traps (FY23) and
they are due back by 8/10/22.
CURRENT BUSINESS -
Exploration of Billing Software options- Paula screen-shared a presentation of the Muni-Link Utility Billing
program. She noted she has gone through 1 billing cycle and the next billing cycle will be early August. She added
the billing process is extremely cumbersome, with a lot of data manipulation going on. With Barb's approval, Paula
contacted a few firms, attended several demos and found one software vendor (Muni-Link) with a lot of functionality
that would streamline efficiencies. Barb and Bill also attended a presentation by Muni-Link and were interested.
Paula summarized the current billing process, which requires the use of Microsoft Excel, Microsoft Access and Munis
(the town's accounting system). Access is used to create customer numbers, reports, manipulate data for irrigation
meters, check for errors and import readings via Excel from the Water District. The numbers from Leominster and
Fitchburg are entered manually. Excel is used to track ownership changes and account adjustments. They generate
a worksheet to compare data from last billing to the current billing to see if it matches up. Another worksheet tracks
final reads so they can correct any invalid readings. The Northern Data file from the Water District is imported to
Munis and they correct any errors. The bills are printed, stuffed and sent out in-house. Demands for over-due bills
are done through Munis and sent out from the office. In between billing, there are a lot of data changes made in all 3
programs. Paula shared copies of sample data and worksheet screens. She added it takes a minimum of 30 minutes
for every ownership change. The Water district provides customer readings in CSV format and Excel. Leominster
provides readings in Excel, but Fitchburg provides readings in pdf, and they have to be entered manually. The entire
billing process takes 2 weeks, not including final reads, ownership changes and adjustments. The goal for finding a
new software system is to streamline, simplify and easily import from multiple places. They also need to import and
export data to Munis, eliminate any manual entries, decrease errors and save time. Paula shared a comparison of
the products from Northern Data, Muni-Billing and Muni-Link. Northern Data is just a billing outsource mailing system.
Muni- Link has the most advantageous platform. Muni-Link has a 1- time fee of $8K and $400/month for 920
customers. Bill generation can be done for an additional fee per piece. Muni- Billing has a 1-time fee of $5-7K, and
$600/month. Muni-Link has all documents for an account in one place and they can attach photos, videos, etc. E-Bills
and E-payments can be done. They can also communicate to field staff in real time regarding problems. Paula talked
to a couple of references, that gave Muni-Link great ratings. She said the next step would be authorization from the
Commission to proceed. Then they would need to talk to the Town Manager, Treasurer, Accountant and Munis about
the process. Once funded, the implementation process would take about 7 months and they would bring 3 years of
data over to the new system. Dave asked if they provide technical support and Paula replied they provide live
customer support. Dave also asked if she talked to other town departments, or Leominster and Fitchburg about the
system. Paula replied they have not and wanted to approach the Commission Dave said he is in favor of the
program. Brett asked how long it would take to do bills with the new system and Paula replied it should cut the billing
to a couple days. It would also flag any errors. They would need to keep Munis but eliminate Access & Excel. Bill said
it would be a great improvement and he recommends it. John added it would save time and he is just concerned
about what the Town Manager and Accountant say. In regard to funding, Dave suggested taking it from capital or
maintenance. Barb reported they came out ahead for FY22 and in FY23, they budgeted $4K for billing mailing. They
could put $4k down and pay the second $4K when the process was complete. If needed, they could go to the special
town meeting. Dave suggested budgeting $15K and Paula replied it could be budgeted over 2 years. His concern
===PAGE 2===
was the tech support, and he recommends they go forward. Paula said the next step is to talk to Town Accountant,
Manager and Treasurer first. Muni-Link could also come in to do a quick demo.

To accept the recommendations from Paula Bertram and Barb for the new program for the billing system and
strongly recommend implementation.
Roll Call Vote: Dave-aye, Brett-aye and John-aye.

ways to do the other work not performed by CTE.
