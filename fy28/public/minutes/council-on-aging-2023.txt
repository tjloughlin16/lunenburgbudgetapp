Lunenburg Council on Aging                                             
Meeting Minutes  
February 14, 2023 
 
Present:  Chairperson Deb Lincoln, Vice-chairperson Jane Rabbitt, Betty DiGiacomo, Diane Nowd, Pete Lincoln, Judy 
Tarbell, Jim McGuigan, Susan O’Sullivan, Amanda Koeck COA Administrative Assistant 
 
Absent:  Director Susan Doherty, Cheryl Moisan 
 
GUESTS:  COA Liaison to the Eagle House Supporters Marilyn Talley, Select Board member Lou Franco, Lunenburg 
resident Cathy Beale 
 
CALL TO ORDER: 9:30 a.m. 
 
ANNOUNCEMENTS:  Ms. Rabbitt introduced Cathy Beale, who filled out a volunteer application indicating her interest in 
serving on the Council on Aging 
 
MINUTES APPROVAL:  Mr. Lincoln motioned to approve the minutes from the January 10, 2023 COA meeting, Mr. 
McGuigan seconded. On vote, motion carried unanimously. 
 
NEW BUSINESS:  
 
1. Discussion: Communication with Lunenburg Residents: Ms. Lincoln drew attention to the communication from 
Town Hall advising town employees not to advise people on how to file for abatements for their real estate 
taxes. Ms. Lincoln expressed her concern over how to communicate with those seniors who may need help, but 
don’t use the internet to learn about services and workshops that are offered. Board members agreed they’d 
like to host a workshop at the Lunenburg Adult Activity Center informing property owners of how they could file 
an appeal if their abatement application is denied.  
 
Ms. Lincoln also expressed her concern about the possibility of senior tax relief being a lower priority for the 
Assessor’s Office because of the reaction to the recent real estate tax hike. Mr. Franco commented that senior 
tax relief is not a low priority for him, and that the sub-committee working on the issue is forming an article to 
be placed on the Warrant for Annual Town Meeting in May of 2023. Ms. Lincoln added that she read in the 
Board of Assessors meeting minutes that the town would need to hire more people to work on senior tax relief.  
 
2. Possible Warrant Article for May Town Meeting: Ms. Lincoln recapped the Capital Planning meeting she and 
Ms. Doherty attended in December. The Capital Planning Committee opined they needed more information on 
the plan for the proposed parking lot in order to support it. The Capital Planning Committee suggested the COA 
get more information on the plan and then to put the request in as a Warrant Article to be voted on at Town 
Meeting.  
 
Ms. Lincoln added that the Town Manager asked Ms. Doherty to scale back her FY24 budget request. Ms. 
Lincoln pointed out that the COA budget is low, compared to other departments in town, and that the COA 
relies heavily on grants and outside funding sources to operate. Ms. Lincoln opined that all COA employee 
salaries should be covered by the municipal budget, and not need to be funded by grants.  
Mr. Lincoln motioned to allow the Chairperson to speak on behalf of the COA on the budget. Ms. O’Sullivan 
seconded. On vote, motion carried unanimously.  
 
CURRENT BUSINESS:  
 
1. Council on Aging Board Member Search: The board thanked Ms. Beale for her interest in a position on the 
board. Ms. Lincoln reminded board members that there is still one more vacancy to be filled. 
 
Unanimously approved by COA at 
their March 21,2023 meeting.

2. COA Orientation Packet: Ms. Lincoln suggested members go through their older information, and compare 
it to the draft packet. 
 
3. COA Board Members Town Email: No report 
 
4. Lunenburg 300th Celebration: Ms. Lincoln informed the board that she and Mr. Lincoln have been appointed 
to the 300th Anniversary Celebration committee. Board members agreed they’d like to create a float to 
display, and also would like to feature something at the Lunenburg Adult Activity Center each month to 
commemorate the 300th anniversary of the town.  
 
DIRECTOR’S REPORT: In the Director’s absence, Ms. Koeck gave a report. 
 
 COA FY24 Budget Adjustment: Ms. Koeck reported that the Town Manager did ask Ms. Doherty to scale 
back her budget requests, and the Director found several ways to shave her numbers down, then later 
that day, the Town Manager called back, and said that Ms. Doherty could actually keep her original 
budget requests. Ms. Koeck reviewed the highlights of the budget discussed at the last COA meeting. 
 
 FY22 Annual Report: Ms. Koeck reported that the Director is working on the report. 
 
 Capital Plan: see “Possible Town Warrant Article” above 
 
 Building and Grounds Report: Ms. Koeck informed the board that a pipe froze and burst over a record 
setting low temperature weekend. The pipe was repaired, and some paperwork was lost.  
 
 Upcoming Events: Ms. Koeck highlighted special events in February and March.

their board. 360 bags have been collected for the Epilepsy Foundation fundraiser. The foundation will be picking up the 
bags April 29, 2023, and the Supporters will need help loading the bags into the truck that day.

replied that luckily, the kitchen door was closed and there were no damages.

unanimously.

Lunenburg Council on Aging                                             
Meeting Minutes  
April 11, 2023 
 
CALL TO ORDER: 9:34 a.m. 
Present:  Director Sue Doherty, Chairperson Deb Lincoln, Vice-Chairperson Jane Rabbitt, Susan 
O’Sullivan, Jim McGuigan, George Martin, Cathy Beale, Judy Tarbell, Deb Lincoln, Pete Lincoln, Betty 
DiGiacomo 
Absent:  Cheryl Moisan, Diane Nowd 
 
GUESTS:  Town Manager Heather Lemieux, Facilities Manager Chris Ruth, Select Board Member Lou 
Franco, COA Liaison to the Eagle House Supporters Marilyn Talley 
 
ANNOUNCEMENTS:  Ms. Lincoln informed board members that terms are up for Ms. Nowd, Ms. Moisan, 
and Mr. Martin, as he filled Jacquelyn Dwyer’s seat. Ms. Nowd, and Mr. Martin will seek reappointment. 
Ms. Moisan cannot commit to another term. Everyone on the board expressed their appreciation for 
Ms. Moisan’s years of service.  
Ms. Lincoln introduced new Facilities Manager Chris Ruth. 
Mr. Franco informed the board that the article for senior tax relief has been finalized in time for this 
spring’s Annual Town Meeting Warrant. 
 
MINUTES APPROVAL:  Mr. Lincoln motioned to approve the minutes from the March 22, 2023 meeting. 
Ms. DiGiacomo seconded. On vote, motion carried unanimously.  
 
NEW BUSINESS: none 
 
CURRENT BUSINESS:  
1. Discussion: Communication with Lunenburg Residents: Ms. Doherty has drafted an 
informational post card with the new Lunenburg Adult Activity Center logo to send out to 
residents.   
 
2. Discussion: Warrant Article for May Town Meeting/Senior Tax Relief: Ms. Lincoln wants 
the board to be informed about the Senior Tax Relief article so they can help people 
understand. Mr. Franco explained that if passed at Town Meeting, the concept of senior tax 
relief will be requested on the state level as a Home Rule petition. Ms. Lemieux added that 
Home Rule petitions can take between 18 months and 2 years to be decided.  
To be eligible for tax relief, property owners will need to be at least 65 years old, and live in 
town for 15 consecutive years before they apply. The applicant must not have any excessive 
assets, and must have applied for and receive the circuit breaker tax credit. The town will 
match the amount of the circuit breaker tax credit, which should be no more than $1,200. If 
the property owner is 70 years old, they must have already applied for the pre-existing 
exemption (Clause 41C). The application for senior tax relief must be filled out annually. Mr. 
Franco went on to explain that the relief will max out at $300,000 (1 percent of the levy). 
The funds will generate from all property taxes of those who are not eligible for senior tax 
relief, which has been estimated to cost approximately $50-$60 per household per year.  
 
3. COA Board Member Orientation Packet: The board made several changes to the latest 
document, and asked for a new draft to be printed reflecting those changes.  
Approved by COA at May 16, 2023 
meeting.

4. COA Board Member Email: All but one have established their new town email accounts. 
 
5. Lunenburg 300th Celebration: Ms. Lincoln informed the board that the 300th celebration 
committee will be meeting soon to brainstorm ideas. Ms. Lemieux added that there is an 
article on the town warrant to spend $5,000 on the celebration.  
 
DIRECTOR’S REPORT:  
 Capital Plan: Mr. Ruth reported that a bid package for the patio project will soon be 
published. Responses are due May 1. The project will be awarded to the lowest 
responsible bidder. Phase 1 of the project will take most of the summer.  
 
Mr. Ruth will be in contact with engineers to draw up new plans for additional parking. 
A new parking lot will be requested for a future capital plan.  
 
A bid package for the generator requested in the FY23 Capital Plan is about to go out for 
bid. 
 
 Building and Grounds: The kitchen rehab requested in the FY20 Capital Plan should get 
started in September of 2023 by Monty Tech students. Mr. Ruth is looking into plumbing 
adjustments to address a hot water issue.  
 
 Upcoming Events:  Ms. Doherty listed upcoming events 
 
EAGLE HOUSE SUPPORTERS REPORT: Ms. Talley reported that the Supporters have collected 792 bags 
for their clothing drive fundraiser. The Supporters are recruiting volunteers to help load the truck on 
April 28, 2023. The next fundraiser is the June raffle calendar, which will be shared with the Lunenburg 
Skate Park. The calendars will go on sale May 1. The goal has been set to sell 300 calendars, which would 
bring in $3,000 if achieved.

Lunenburg Council on Aging                                                   Minutes Approved at 11/14/2023  COA Meeting 
Meeting Minutes 
October 10, 2023 
 
 
CALL TO ORDER    Chairperson Deb Lincoln called the meeting to order at 9:35 am.  
 
PRESENT    Deb Lincoln (Chairperson), Susan Doherty (Director), Pete Lincoln, Diane Nowd, Betty 
DiGiacomo, Jim McGuigan, Cathy Beale, Lou Franco, Helen Balcom, Jane Rabbitt & Michele Beauvais.  
 
EXCUSED    Judy Tarbell, Susan O’Sullivan & George Martin 
 
GUESTS  - Lou Franco, Lunenburg Selectperson 
 
ANNOUNCEMENTS   The passing of Jim Hayes was announced. The board observed a moment of silence 
in his honor.  
 
PUBLIC COMMENT   None 
 
MINUTES APPROVAL   Ms. Lincoln motioned to approve the September 12, 2023 minutes & Ms. 
DiGiacomo seconded the motion.  All approved and motion carried.  
 
NEW BUSINESS 
Discussion:  Potential LAAC Recreation Area Programs       
Ms. Lincoln asked COA Board members to think of new program ideas for the newly improved 
recreation area. She also thanked Helen Balcom, Pete & Susan Doherty, & Bob Hamel for 
spreading mulch. Ms. Rabbitt suggested having Bocce tournaments. Ms. Balcom suggested 
having band concerts at the new outdoor pavilion. She will contact Kathy Herrick to discuss 
further. 
 
CURRENT BUSINESS 
1. 100 Club  -  Mario Patacchiola is turning 100 in December. Plans are being made to induct 
him into the club.  
2. Ribbon Cutting Ceremony -  Wednesday October 11 @ noon. The Dogfather Hotdog truck 
& the Sunshine Boys will be available for the ceremony. 
3. Lunenburg Master Plan – The Facebook page is not available yet.  The website should be 
up and running at the end of the week.  
4. Lunenburg Senior Tax Relief – No update 
5. Council on Aging Board Members Town Email    Ms. Lincoln & Ms. Rabbitt will contact Mr. 
Nadareski with any ongoing email issues.  
6. Lunenburg 300th Celebration – We are currently still in the planning stages.  
 
 
 
===PAGE 2===
DIRECTOR’S REPORT     
• Capital Plan – The Facilities Manager reported to Ms. Doherty via email that he is working with 
coordinating with Monty Tech on a schedule. They have cabinet students available but not 
carpentry.  The generator is ready to send out to bid. However, we are still waiting on the Town 
Manager for final review.  The roof is also ready to send out to bid. Currently, waiting on 
Assistant Town Manager for final review.  
• Building & Grounds Report – Tables & chairs are on the way for the grand opening of the 
pavilion. We had an overabundance of sod left over from the pavilion project. It was used to 
dress up the area in between the LAAC & Kids Kingdom. Discussions were made to have a small 
brick wall over by the Pollinator Garden. Ms Doherty will contact a local brick mason for ideas. A 
sign has been ordered, thanking the major donors to the patio project.  
• Upcoming Events –  
• UFO’s & Spy Balloons is scheduled for Friday 10/13/23. 
• Spice Club of the Month continues to meet the first Monday of the month.  
• Brain Health Presentation will be on November 6. 
• Pickle Ball will resume again at TC Passios beginning on 11/3/23. 
• Artificial Intelligence – We will be having a meeting on 11/20/23. This is a meeting to discuss & 
understand new technology.  
• History of Fort Devens – The Historical Society will  have a program on the History of Fort 
Devens. This will be on Friday 11/17/23 @ 1:00. 
• Memory Training-  Pauline Roy will start a memory-training program on 11/27. This will be a 3-4 
week program.  
 
EAGLE HOUSE SUPPORTERS REPORT    No report 
  
NOTICES & COMMUNICATIONS 
 
BOARD COMMENT    Mr. Lincoln commented that Chris Ruth was a gem. He greatly appreciated his 
efforts regarding the cupola. 
 
ADJOURNMENT     Mr. Lincoln moved to adjourn the meeting. Ms. Digiacomo seconded the motion. All 
approved and the motion was carried.  
 
UPCOMING MEETING SCHEDULE     Tuesday, November 14, 2023 ~ 9:30 AM 
 
*MEETING CANCELLATION POLICY: 
If the COA board is unable to meet on the regularly scheduled 2nd Tuesday of the month, the meeting 
will be rescheduled to the following Tuesday.
