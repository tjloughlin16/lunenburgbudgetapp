Town of Lunenburg 
  
Cemetery Commission                                                                  
Lance D. May, Chairman 
Lorraine Mendoza, Vice- Chairman 
William Tyler, Clerk     
 
Minutes 
Meeting July 12, 2018 
Lunenburg Public Library Conference Room 
 
Meeting was called to order at 7:00 pm by Chairman Lance May. Present were 
Vice-Chair Lorraine Mendoza, Clerk William Tyler and Superintendent Todd 
Holman. First order of business was approval of minutes from the June 20, 2018 
meeting. On a motion by Chairman May, seconded by Commissioner Mendoza, 
and with no errors or omissions and no further discussion, it was voted 
unanimously to accept the minutes as written. 
 
Since there was not any public comment or correspondence to report, Old 
Business was the next topic on the agenda. 
 
After some discussion on the matter of invoices from M&M Services for the use 
of a backhoe, because the equipment was not available from the DPW for 3 
burials this Spring, a motion was made by Commissioner Tyler to decline paying 
these invoices that totaled $1080.00 and seek to reach an understanding from 
the Town Manager when their equipment is needed and pre-arranged, but 
unavailable for use. Also, in the motion was to have Chairman May draft a letter 
to the Town Manager on this subject. Motion was seconded by Commissioner 
Mendoza, and with no further discussion, it was voted unanimously to approve 
the motion. 
 
On the subject of technology, Commission Tyler said he would follow-up with the 
Town Manager on the Facebook page that was discussed at the June 20 meeting. 
 
On the Holman St. building and proposed demolition, it was announced that the 
Cemetery Commission is scheduled to meet with the Historical Commission on 
July 31, 2018 at 7:00 pm at the Ritter building for a hearing on the demolition 
application that was filed with the Building Inspector . 
 
There was nothing to report on the Handicap access plans for the Niche Wall at 
North Cemetery. 
 
Chairman May stated that the subject of future growth with the Montachusett 
Regional Planning Commission, is still pending and will be followed up at a future 
meeting. 
 

===PAGE 2===
Chairman May presented the Commission with several handouts regarding 
safety issues as it applies to excavation and burials. Commissioner Tyler will 
follow up with more research on the subject. 
Commissioner Tyler is in the process of determining how to get seasonal help 
for the Superintendent next year, through any means necessary. He will report  
back at the next meeting. 
 
Superintendent Holman reported that the headstone straightening project by a 
member of Troop 1728 for his Eagle Scout project is complete, and he and other 
members straightened 80 headstones in the North Cemetery. He also reported 
that mowing and trimming is ongoing and the Cemeteries are in good shape. 
 
There will be no meeting in August 
Next meeting is scheduled for September 13, 2018 at the Lunenburg Public 
Library Conference room. 
 
With no further items on the agenda, a motion was made to adjourn by 
Commissioner Mendoza, seconded by Commission Tyler . Meeting was adjourned 
at 9:30 pm. 
 
Respectfully submitted 
William Tyler, Clerk

July 31, 2018 
Lunenburg Public Library 
6:30 PM 
Historical Commission 
Ritter Memorial Library 
7:00 PM 
 
Meeting was called to order by Chairman May at 6:30 PM. Other members 
present were Lorraine Mendoza and William Tyler. The purpose of this meeting 
was to discuss upcoming meeting with the Historical Commission concerning 
our application for demolition of the Cemetery Building on Holman St. After 
discussing the application, the meeting was reconvened at 7:00 PM at the Ritter 
Library for a joint meeting with the Historical Commission. 
 
Chairman May expressed his opinion on the applicability of any delay in 
demolition based on the buildings current condition. He further added that 
building maintenance falls under the jurisdiction of the DPW and nothing has 
been done in the way of preventative maintenance for many years. 
 
Historical Commission Chair Lantry, after hearing the Commission arguments 
for demolition, stated that they would take our request under advisement and 
render their decision as to the applicability of the 6 Month Demolition Delay 
Protocol. 
 
A date of Tuesday, August 7, 2018 at 7:00 PM was set for that determination. 
 
Meeting was adjourned at 8:30 PM 
 
Respectfully submitted 
William Tyler, Clerk

Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Minutes 
September 13, 2018  
Lunenburg Public Library Conference Room 
 
Meeting was called to order at 7:00 PM by Chairman May, other members 
present were Lorraine Mendoza, Vice-Chair, William Tyler, Clerk. Also present 
was Todd Holman, Cemetery Superintendent. 
 
Minutes were read from meetings of July 12 th, July 31st - August 7th and 
September 5, 2018. Upon a motion by Chair May to accept the minutes as written, 
seconded by Vice-Chair Mendoza, minutes were approved unanimously. 
 
With no correspondence or public comment, the budget was reviewed and 
vouchers were signed for Ramaker for website maintenance and Lunenburg 
Ledger for advertising. 
 
Comm. Tyler has been in touch with the building dept. at Monty Tech concerning 
the building schedule and will continue to keep the Commission informed of any 
new information. After meeting with the building inspector, it appears we will 
need a plot plan drawn up for the new building in order to get a building permit. It 
was decided to contact Whitman and Bingham, Civil Engineers to get a price and 
after some discussion, a motion was made by Comm. Mendoza seconded by 
Comm Tyler to put a cap of $1000.00 for the plot plan, with no further discussion 
it was approved unanimously. 
 
There was also some discussion of a container to house all the lumber and other 
items in the current building before demolition. No decision was made at this 
time and will be discussed at the next meeting. However, a letter is to be sent to 
the Town Manager, requesting the Superintendent be available for all tasks 
involved with this project. A backhoe will be needed to remove old gravestones 
and other material that is around the base of the building. Comm. Tyler will draft 
the letter and send it to the Town Manager . 
 
Superintendent Holman stated that the prisoners will be available the week of 
November 26-30 for help with leaves and other clean-up duties. 
 
It was decided to discuss Cemetery rates at the next meeting. 
 
With no further business before the Commission, a motion was made to adjourn 
by Chair May, seconded by Comm Tyler and with no further discussion, meeting 
was adjourned at 9:15 PM 

===PAGE 2===
Next meeting, October 11, 2018 @ 7:00 PM 
 
Respectfully Submitted 
William Tyler, Clerk

Cemetery Commission Minutes 
October 11, 2018 Meeting 
Lunenburg Public Library Conference Room 
 
Meeting was called to order at 7:05 pm by Chairman May. Other members 
present were Vice-Chairman, Lorraine Mendoza, and Clerk, William Tyler . 
 
A motion was made by Chairman May to change the order of the agenda, to 
accommodate a member of the Charter Review Committee, who has a previous 
engagement. Seconded by Vice-Chair Mendoza, voted unanimously. 
 
First order of business was re-organization of the Commission. The following 
nominations were put before the Commission. Lance May, Chair , Lorraine 
Mendoza, Vice-Chair and William Tyler , Clerk, with no further discussion, it was 
voted to accept the nominations as presented unanimously. 
 
Minutes from the September 13th were read and upon a motion by Chair May, 
seconded by Vice-Chair Mendoza, the minutes were accepted unanimously. 
 
Steve Archambault from the Charter Review Committee was present to give the 
Commission an update on the progress of the committee to this point. He 
informed the Commission that the Committee meets the second and fourth 
Wednesdays of every month. Dates to include: 10/24, 11/7, 12/12 and possibly 
12/26. He suggested that we formulate what changes we would want and to 
schedule a meeting with the Charter Committee. 
 
The Holman St. Cemetery building is awaiting demolition, pending a plan to 
remove the old gravestones that are scattered around the building. It was 
decided to get a price from M&M Services for the use of a backhoe and operator, 
since the DPW Director cannot commit to giving the Supt. any help in this 
process. Also, upon a motion by Commissioner Tyler , seconded by Vice-Chair 
Mendoza, to purchase a used 8x20 storage container for the purpose of storing 
whatever useful tools and other items that remain in the building before 
demolition. It was voted unanimously to purchase this container . 
 
Cemetery rates were discussed and the possibility of increasing the Niche Wall 
price to $1400.00 was discussed. It was taken under advisement to be further 
discussed at a future meeting. 
 

===PAGE 2===
Also discussed was the possibility of placing an add in the Fitchburg Sentinel 
advertising the Niche Wall and its availability year- round. Commission Tyler will 
follow up with prices before the next meeting. 
 
The subject of the ADA grant for the handicap access to the Niche Wall at North 
Cemetery was discussed, with Commission Tyler reporting that he met with the 
Town Manager and submitted bids for the paving of parking lot and the ADA 
compliant railings, totaling $30,528. Bid notification will occur on December 3, 
2018. 
 
Notification was received of a private donation in memory of George Reichart, a 
former Cemetery Commissioner . His son wishes to donate a sum of money for 
the purchase of signs showing Rules and Regulations for both Cemeteries. It 
was advised that he send a letter stating his wishes and then we could establish 
a special account in his name that could be drawn on to fulfill his wishes. 
 
Commissioner Tyler is in the process of contacting LUK in Fitchburg for 
assistance in obtaining Summer help next year for the Supt. He will report on 
this at the next meeting. 
 
Next meeting date: November 29, 2018 @ 7:00 pm 
 
With no further business before the Commission, a motion was made by Chair 
May to adjourn, seconded by Comm. Mendoza. Meeting was adjourned at 9:05 
pm. 
 
Respectfully submitted 
William Tyler , Clerk

Town of Lunenburg 
  
 
Cemetery Commission 
 
 
 
Lorraine Mendoza, Vice- Chairman 
William Tyler , Clerk    
 
Minutes 
November 29, 2018 Meeting 
Lunenburg Public Library Conference Room 
 
 
Meeting was called to order at 7:05 pm by Clerk, William Tyler . Chairman May 
was unable to attend due to illness. Other members there were Vice Chair 
Lorraine Mendoza. 
 
Minutes of the October 11, 2018 meeting were read and with no errors or 
omissions, motion made by Com. Mendoza to accept the minutes as written, 
seconded by Com. Tyler , accepted unanimously. 
 
A letter was read into the minutes, from George Reichard who made a $2,500.00 
donation to the Commission for the purpose of signs for both Cemeteries listing 
the Rules and Regs. Supt. Holman will get some quotes for the cost. The 
$2,500.00 will be put in a special account so that we may draw from it to have the 
signs and granite posts purchased. 
 
The Town Manager informed Com. Tyler that the new FaceBook page was up and 
running. He will begin posting photos of past projects and new projects such as 
the new Cemetery building. 
 
The Grant application for the ADA handicap ramp, railings and parking lot at 
North Cemetery was submitted to the Town Manager. She informed us that we 
should hear whether the Grant was approved or not sometime in December . 
 
Discussion on Cemetery rates were passed over until a later time. 
 
The Commissioner signed vouchers for Casella Waste Systems- (dumpster), 
Powell’s (loam and seed), Triumph Modular (storage container) and Lunenburg 
Ledger (add for Columbarium), totaling $1,967.87. 
 
Discussion with Supt. Holman concerning the beginning of construction on the 
new garage on Holman St. After much discussion it was decided to go ahead 
with the foundation portion of the building. The building permit is active until 
April, but the Commission felt that we should not wait until Spring and take 

===PAGE 2===
advantage of the good weather . A motion was made by Com. Mendoza to accept 
the low bid of $6 ,146.00 from R.A. Powell Construction for the excavation work 
needed for the foundation, and a low bid from P .D. Boucher for the foundation 
itself for $7,625.00, seconded by Com. Tyler. With no further discussion the 
motion passed unanimously. 
 
Discussion on the progress of the Fall clean-up at both Cemeteries with Supt. 
Holman. Due the early snow, progress is somewhat behind where it should be. 
With the help of the Worcester County Sheriff’s Dept. hopefully it can be finished 
up soon. 
 
There was discussion on placing an add in the Fitchburg Sentinel for the 
Columbarium. It was decided to wait for the time being. The price will be $50.00 
an insertion. 
 
Com. Tyler attended a meeting of the Charter Review Committed on Nov. 28, 
2018 to submit the proposed changes we had discussed at an earlier meeting. 
There is a Public Hearing on the proposed Charter changes on Jan. 9, 2019. 
 
Lastly, Sandy Chester inquired about the Wreaths Over America , which puts a 
wreath over every Veteran’s grave. With 1,400 veterans it isn’t feasible for us to 
participate. 
 
Next Meeting- Jan 10, 2019 
 
With no further items on the agenda, a motion was made by Com. Mendoza to 
adjourn, seconded by Com. Tyler , with no further discussion meeting was 
adjourned at 9:10 pm. 
 
Respectfully Submitted 
William Tyler , Clerk
