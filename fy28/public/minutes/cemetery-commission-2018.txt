The Lunenburg Cemetery Commission met on January 11, 2018 at the Lunenburg Public Library. The
meeting was called to order at 7:00 pm. Members present were: Commissioners Lance May, Lorraine
Mendoza, William Tyler and Cemetery Supt. Todd Holman
Minutes from the November 30, 2017 meeting were reviewed and upon a motion by Chairman May to
accept the minutes, seconded by Commissioner Tyler, and with no further discussion, the motion passed
unanimously.
Chairman May's meeting with the Town Manager on January 8, 2018 and the follow-up letter sent to
her on January 9, 2018 was discussed with no apparent change in the usage of Supt. Holman at the Town
Cemeteries, from November 27, 2017 to January 11, 2018.
The communication between the Charter Review Committee (CRC) and the Cemetery Commission was
discussed since Chairman May attended their meeting on January 10, 2018, following CRC member
Nancy Gray's visit to our meeting on November 30, 2017. A letter was also sent to CRC Chairman Robert
Ebersole describing the changes proposed to the Charter.
Commissioner Tyler reported to the Commission that his email to Town Accountant Karen Brochu was
answered and it was presented to the Commission concerning finances and expenditures. The only
expenses left to pay at the moment is $422.00 for loam and food and bathroom facilities for the
prisoners from the Worcester County House of Correction.
With the population of Lunenburg increasing, the subject of space left at both Cemeteries was
discussed and it was concluded that we have adequate room left for the foreseeable future. Although
further expansion is possible at the North Cemetery.
A change in policy regarding our Rules and Regulations was presented by Chairman May, involving
changing the delegation and supervision of the day to day care and maintenance of the Town Cemeteries
by the Town Manager, without the prior approval of the Cemetery Commission. A motion was made to
accept this change by Commissioner Mendoza, seconded by Commissioner Tyler, and with no further
discussion was passed unanimously. Commissioner Tyler will send a copy of the change to the Town
Clerk for posting.
The deadline for submission of information for the Town Report is February 15, 2018.
The next scheduled meeting of the Cemetery Commission is February 12, 2018 at the Lunenburg Public
Library. With no further business to discuss, a motion was made by Chairman May to adjourn, seconded
by Commissioner Mendoza. Meeting was adjourned at 9:05 pm.
Respectfully Submitted, William Tyler, Clerk.

Cemetery Commission Meeting
May 10, 2018
Lunenburg Public Library
The Lunenburg Cemetery Commission met on May 10, 2018 at the Lunenburg Public
Library Conference Room. The meeting was called to order by Chairman May at 7:05 pm.
Members present were Chairman Lance May, Vice Chairman Lorraine Mendoza, Clerk
William Tyler and Superintendent Todd Holman.
Minutes from the April 12, 2018 meeting were read as well as the minutes from the May 4,
2018 site visit to North Cemetery. With no corrections or omissions, a motion was made
by Comm. Mendoza to accept the minutes as written. Seconded by Comm. May, and
with no further discussion, motion passed unanimously.
In the Public Comment part of the meeting, Dave Passios was present and wanted to
give the Commission an update concerning one of the wells at North Cemetery. Mr.
Passios volunteers his service to the maintaining of the wells and pumps at both
Cemeteries. The well in question has an accumulation of sand and gravel at the bottom,
making water unavailable later in the summer. He is going to work on resolving this Issue
by trying to extract the sand with help from the Fire Dept.
Correspondence received from the Town Manager concerning our use of the
appropriation requested at Town Meeting. The Town Manager was informed that it was
for our annual operation budget along with other improvements. Our warrant article
passed at Town Meeting with no discussion.
Because of a need to upgrade our back-up server, which stores all the Cemetery
records, an agreement was made between the IT director and Ramaker Associates for
an upgrade to a Cloud based system. The Cemetery Commission would pay $350.00
toward the cost this year with IT paying the remainder. Next year IT would pick up the
total cost of the upgrade with the Cemetery Commission responsible for the annual
$1200.00 fee.
A short discussion was had concerning our current Rules and Regulations, no action
was taken.
Under the topic of future growth and planning, discussion ensued concerning
population growth and the increased number of burials every year, and how the
Commission would respond to that added growth. On that same topic it was decided to
formulate a Mission Statement that would address this growth and establish a 5 year
plan that would address the Town's Cemeteries for the future. While addressing our
future needs, discussion ensued pertaining to the old Cemetery building on Holman St.
and the future of this building. It was decided that the Commission would invite the
===PAGE 2===
during the week of May 14-18 to help in cleanup from the Winter.
The Boy Scouts are planning a service at the newly constructed memorial at the flag
pole at North Cemetery on Memorial Day.
Supt. Holman gave an update on maintenance at both Cemeteries. He reported that a
chain was stolen from the North Cemetery that blocks entrance into the new area. A
Police report was filed. Robin Burch informed the Supt. that the Memorial Fund donated
$100.00 towards the purchase of a new spruce tree.
Comm. Tyler informed the Commission of his idea to start a Face Book page for the
Commission. He felt that Social Media has become a very popular platform for many
people to get the latest updates and information concerning the Town. The Commission
agreed, and it was decided to pursue this with the Town Manager to get her input before
we proceed.
With no further items on the agenda, a motion was made by Comm. Mendoza to adjourn,
seconded by Comm. Tyler. With no further discussion the motion passed. Meeting was
adjourned at 10:05 pm.

Meeting was called to order at 7:00 PM by Chairman May. Members present were: Lance

voted unanimously to accept the minutes as written.
Under correspondence, a letter from Nancy Yasko was read, praising Supt. Holman for
his work in both Cemeteries. She also proposed that the Town find an appropriate
candidate to help the Supt. in his upkeep of both Cemeteries. Considering how the Town
is growing it is unreasonable to think that one person can continue to meet the needs of
a growing Town with two Cemeteries.
Under Public Comment, DPW Director of Operations Jack Rodriquenz, spoke about

emails was brought up, and we were told that the Commissioners would have their own
email account set up by the IT Director. The Town Manager stated that she would have
an update for our next meeting.

the proposed garage to be built on the site by the students from Monty Tech, the Town

the Capital plan from 2014 for demolition of the Cemetery building. The Town Manager
stated that we would have to request that this be used for this purpose.

available for burials. Commissioner Tyler pointed out that since the backhoe was not
