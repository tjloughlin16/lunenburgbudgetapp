Meeting Minutes 
December 17, 2020 5:30pm 
Members Present: Greg Roy, David Passios, Brian Lehtinen, David Blatt 
Members absent: Michael-Ray Jeffreys, Matthew Allison 
Architects:  Ed DiSalvio, Tom Lam 
Town Employees: Steve Walker, Heather Lemieux 
Community Guests: Lunenburg Ledger 
Meeting called to order at 5:33pm. 
Public Comment: Greg Roy stated at 10 minutes to 7pm we will need to leave to due to another 
Lunenburg group using this zoom meeting ID.  
 
Update from Design Team: 
Tom Lam stated he wanted to talk about the toilet counts. The architects made an adjustment and 
explained the plumbing count based on occupancy. The table presented on the zoom listed the 
mathematical calculations from the building code. Architects tried to fit as many chairs and tables and 
were able to reduce the occupancy count and could reduce toilet fixtures and plus or minus one fixture 
and can make that work. Capacity, seating, and layout of meeting rooms were presented. Toilet layout 
was discussed. Dave Passios noted the family toilet and ADA toilets were present. Brian Lehtinen 
mentioned the importance of a family bathroom and proper sink location for staffed redesigned 
bathrooms. Greg Roy stated getting it down to a reasonable level is good in reality and maximum usage 
will not be occurring. Tom Lamm mentioned gynamsium can fit 534 people and each room could have 
signage for maximum occupancy. David Blatt mentioned no urinal in the family toilet. Tom Lamm said 
that it would be taken care of. Tom Lamm mentioned flooring will be discussed in the future. The 
building entrances were shown by the architects. Department entries were shown. Architects plan to 
use existing openings, using wall panels, accent floors, and highlight with entry wall sconce. Signage, 
railings, and fabric boards for notices were presented. Transaction window and accent floors were 
discussed for each department. Dave Passios stated stationary transaction windows. Tom Lamm stated 
he has not finalized those conversations with end users. Dave Passios asked about the signage being an

issue over the doors for ADA compliance. Greg Roy asked if sign should be next to the door not over it, 
like a street sign. David Blatt mentioned a sign instead of a sconce to see the department and assess 
locations. Tom Lamm mentioned cost savings could occur with adding another layer of drywall with 
trim. Dave Passios mentioned colors as an option, as the public enters we would want a key on where 
town offices and what departments there are and their color code on a road map. Dave Passios asked 
about the staff entrance. David Blatt asked if there is a difference in having a solid door or a window for 
safety. Tom Lamm said we will look into side entrance and glass for visual. Greg Roy stated a cohesive 
look will be good but we need to make sure we keep track of bottom line.  
David Blatt stated he would like to discuss the need for a staff side entrance in a future discussion. David 
Blatt also mentioned acoustics should be considered in ceiling options. Dave Passios mentioned staff 
would likely need to use the staff entrance. Ed DiSalvio mentioned sound absorption would be 
considered. Ed DiSalvio said the engineers created a sketch for the property line.  The issues are 
subdividing with need adding legal because of easements and a fair amount of legal work would be 
needed by the town to subdivide. Dave Passios stated you cannot create a non-conforming lot. Greg Roy 
stated that you can possibly under section 81R. Dave Passios stated TCP should be conveyed back to 
town and portioned off the school property with the building.  Ed DiSalvio stated the engineers did use 
all neighboring property lines. Greg Roy stated land was drawn in pencil, not ink for MSBA to define 
middle/high school project limits. Dave Passios stated that nothing was recorded and a moot point. Greg 
Roy stated this is going to be a lot of work and that the easements should not be an issue if all three 
properties are continued to be owned by the town until they are conveyed. Brian Lehtinen stated that 
the subdivision would be an added legal cost for the project and does not seemed to be needed for this 
project, and we can pass this information along to the select board. Greg Roy stated unless you are 
looking to convey part of the land then this is a lot of work to do.  
Tom Lam moved on to the sidewalk discussion. Greg Roy stated we would like to limit the work to 
project being compliant and not try and add things that are not specifically related to that. Greg Roy 
asked the Town Manager and DPW about the paving. The DPW said it would make sense to include the 
paving and that it might make sense to include the loop because we may need to pave in 3 or 4 years.  
The through road is new pavement and would not need to be done, but the other two parts of the 
triangle should be overlaid. Ed DiSalvio stated the engineers would recommend completely replacing 
sidewalks in blue, and the orange would be removed and not replaced. Removing any doors would 
eliminate the sidewalks.  Including paving out front the 2 legs of the triangles and the parking. Dave Blatt 
stated he likes the idea of getting rid of the sidewalks around the building. Dave Blatt asked about the 
pads outside the door of the classrooms. Tom Lamm stated they are for egress. Dave Passios stated the 
existing sidewalks asked about the sidewalks that will stay. Will they be redone? Ed DiSalvio stated he 
would check with the engineers. Greg Roy stated it may make sense to make a connection with the 
sidewalks to the other side of the road, but not if we need multiple curb cuts. If we redo the whole 
sidewalk, this gives credence to overlaying the road. Dave Blatt said we should have a sidewalk so 
people are not walking across the grass. Greg Roy stated the upper parking lot will be used for high 
school events and something to think about and improve the circulation pattern. Ed DiSalvio stated we 
will have the engineers look into that.  Greg Roy stated replacing the sidewalk in front of the building 
would give it a nice clean and fresh look. The parking is also set off the building. There are trees there 
that would need to be removed to do that. Dave Passios stated the trees were on the plan.  Brian 
Lehtinen mentioned taking out spots at the end to cut into the hill less. 
Ed DiSalvio discussed the traffic study and recommendations were to make sidewalks ADA accessible 
and adding signage and sidewalks. Under all scenarios there is no difference in traffic but would 
recommend in 2032 Mass Ave and Lancaster intersection signal because of the growth. The issue with 
parents cueing up, analysis recommends signage for parents and use school personnel to keep parents 
moving.  Dave Blatt asked if it would be a one way around the triangle? Will the west entrance be used?

Ed DiSalvio said the gate was closed and we assumed it would stay closed. Dave Blatt stated it would 
eliminate a lot of traffic if that was opened up. Ed DiSalvio said we can ask them that. Greg Roy asked 
would that overburden the one way and create more traffic by cutting through. Brian Lehtinen asked 
about stop signs being used for safety. Dave Passios worried about unintended consequences to 
increasing traffic near the senior center similar to Whiting St. Brian Lehtinen asked can we get 
information from the town why the gate is always closed. Dave Passios stated it is open sometimes and 
often closed due to children playing in the area. Greg Roy asked if the architects needed more input or 
guidance from the committee for cost estimates. Tom Lamm stated in regards to architect side we have 
enough and as it is put together we will have more questions but it will not slow the process down. If the 
architects need more information they can ask in future meetings. Ed DiSalvio said we have enough to 
continue with civil site work for now. Dave Passios asked if we have another meeting scheduled.  Greg 
Roy stated two meetings in January. Tom Lamm stated the first will be clarifying and the second would 
be a draft of the budget and we will need another discussion after that to adjust.      
 
 Future Meetings:  January 13, 2021 and January 27, 2021 at 5:30pm. 
 
Meeting was adjourned due to zoom disconnection at 6:52 PM.
