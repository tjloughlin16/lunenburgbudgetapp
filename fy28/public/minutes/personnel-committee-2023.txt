Town of Lunenburg 
Personnel Committee 
Minutes:  January 9, 2023    
Zoom 6:00pm 
 
 
Attendees:   
Susan Andrew Eric Arnold, s, Donna Fortune, Wendy Bertrand, 
Later arrivals Joanna Bilotta (6:13pm), Eric Arnold (6:15pm) 
 
Also Present: Julie Belliveau, Asst. Town Manager/HR Director; Thomas Gammel, Police Chief, Bill 
Bernard, DPW Director, Pauline Roy, COA, Nancy O’Rourke, Payroll and Benefits Coordinator/Risk 
Manager, Barbara Lefebvre, Sewer Business Manager, Mary Beth Bernard, HR Consultant 
 
Wendy Bertrand opened the Meeting at 6:07pm with members noted in attendance 
 
Public Comment: 
There were no comments from the Public, W. Bertrand wanted to be sure to get an update on the Recreation 
Directors position. 
 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes of the December 5, 2022 meeting were presented and reviewed. 
Motion by D. Fortune to accept and approve the minutes of the December 5, 2022 meeting as presented 
Second:  S. Andrews 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
New Business: 
Job Description Revision 
J. Belliveau presented the updated Civilian Traffic Member job description, assisting Chief Gammel who 
was having internet connection issues.  The update mainly addressed the addition allowing active public 
safety employees to be considered for the role to  broaden the availability of candidates.  
 
Motion by D. Fortune to accept the recommendation of the HR Director for the update of the Civilian 
Traffic Member job description, and for it then to be submitted at the next available town meeting for final 
approval. 
Second:  S. Andrews 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
H.R. Consultant Update 
Mary Beth Bernard brought the committee up to date on the Job Description/Job Classification Project she 
is responsible for.  The project is on target with rough drafts of 75% of descriptions completed, meetings 
with key stakeholders finished, and collection of outside survey materials in progress. She has also utilized 
available public municipality data.  She has done FLSA reviews and classification reviews as each job 
description completed.  There is an open issue that they are  reviewing to assess if the Administrator role 
should be expanded to create department specific administrators as she has seen a wide disparity in these 
roles.  Some changes noted as departments modernized and roles changed.  She suggested that when final 
job descriptions were approved that employees be asked to acknowledge receiving the job description.   The 
pay plan proposal will be completed for review by February 8, 2023 and final drafts of proposal will be 
February 15, 2023.  There was a discussion regarding the need for more in person interviews, which will be 
considered as this was a voluntary process. 
 
 
 
 
 
===PAGE 2===
Facility Director 
J. Belliveau updated the group on this opening.  There is a candidate that is being reviewed, not yet offered 
the role as of thi s date, but should be reviewed by Select Board shortly. The role will be a grade 16, and 
with this candidate’s background, the pay step is targeted at step 5.  She asked for the committee’s approval 
of this action. 
Motion by S. Andrews to approve the recom mendation of the HR Director to offer this role of Facility 
Director at Grade 16 step 5. 
Second:  J. Bilotta 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Public Comment 
There were no additional comments from committee, or from the Public 
 
Next Meeting: 
As needed and regular meeting February 6, 2023 6pm 
 
 
Motion by to adjourn meeting at 6:45pm by J. Bilotta 
Second:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor

Zoom 6:00pm 
 
 
Attendees:   
Susan Andrew, Donna Fortune, Wendy Bertrand, 
Later arrivals Joanna Bilotta (6:10pm) 
 
Also Present: Julie Belliveau, Asst. Town Manager/HR Director, Bill Bernard, DPW Director, Barbara 
Lefebvre, Sewer Business Manager, Nancy O’Rourke, Amanda Moore, Mary Beth Bernard, HR Consultant 
 
Wendy Bertrand opened the Meeting at 6:03pm with members noted in attendance 
 
Public Comment: 
There were no comments from the Public, W. Bertrand wanted to be sure to get an update on the Recreation 
Directors position. 
 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes of the January 9, 2023 meeting were presented and reviewed. 
Motion by D. Fortune to accept and approve the minutes of the January 9, 2023 meeting as presented 
Second:  S. Andrews 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Ongoing Business: 
H.R. Consultant Update 
Mary Beth Bernard brought the committee up to date on the Job Description/Job Classification P roject she 
is responsible for.   Completed 57 job de scriptions and remainder of interviews.  Spent time with the fire 
chief on the on call employee’s profiles.  The pay plan proposal is still on target to be completed for review 
by February 8, 2023 and final drafts of proposal will be February 15, 2023.  Her initial findings found the 
clerical roles within 5% to 12% off the market, the mid level professionals and mid management 10% to 
15% off the market and the Director levels higher.  Understanding the nature of affordability in small towns 
she expected a c onservative recommendation as she completed this work.  She would recommend that we 
eliminate Grade 1 as it was below minimum wage and suggested an alternative grading system using alpha 
designations.  She would submit her final report and her recommendati ons to Julie upon completion.  Julie 
would then forward this report to the committee members for review.  
 
Public Comment 
There were no additional comments from committee, and Barbara Lefebvre reminded the group that the 
finance committee would need to see the proposal for the SAP pay grade adjustments. 
 
Next Meeting: 
Perhaps a call to review the final proposal on February 27, 2023 and regular meeting March 6, 2023 6pm 
 
 
Motion by to adjourn meeting at 6:57pm by J. Bilotta 
Second:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor

Personnel Committee 
Minutes:  February 27, 2023    
Zoom 6:00pm 
 
 
Attendees:   
Susan Andrew, Donna Fortune, Wendy Bertrand, Joanna Bilotta  
 
Also Present: Julie Belliveau, Asst. Town Manager/HR Director, Barbara Lefebvre, Sewer Business 
Manager, Nancy O’Rourke, Payroll and benefits Risk Manager, Heather Lemieux, Town Manager 
 
Joanna Bilotta opened the Meeting at 6:01pm with members noted in attendance 
 
Public Comment: 
There were no comments from the Public, W. Bertrand asked for clarification on the need to restructure the 
committee given the recent resignation of Chair, Eric Arnold from the committee. J. Belliveau advised that 
no changes could be made till the end of the town’s fiscal year in June, until then the vice chair takes over 
the responsibility for leading the committee. 
 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes of the February 6, 2023 meeting were presented and reviewed. 
Motion by W. Bertrand to accept and approve the minutes of the February 6, 2023 meeting as presented 
Second:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote:  S. Andrews, D. Fortune, and W. Bertrand all in favor; J. Bilotta was not in attendance on 
February 6, 2023 so did not vote on motion. 
 
New Business 
Motion by W. Bertrand to move the new agenda article to beginning of meeting, thus leaving the ongoing 
business of the Job Evaluation Project to the end. 
Seconded:  S. Andrews 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
New Hire – Administrative Assistant IT 
J. Belliveau presented a recommendation to hire a candidate into a new role as administrative assistant in IT 
in Grade 8 Step 6.  This role has been open and interviewing ongoing since September 2022.  Like many 
other open positions in town, qualified candidates have been difficult to source.  This candidate brings 
valuable skills to the role and her experience level is commensurate with the step 6 pay rate.  This is the 
only administrative role in this group and the pay grade and step do not disrupt any other employees pay 
status. 
Motion by S. Andrews to accept the recommendation of the HR Director to hire this candidate into the role 
of Administrative Assistant in IT at Grade 8 Step 6. 
Seconded:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Ongoing Business: 
HR Consultant update - Consultant was unavailable for this meeting; J. Belliveau gave an update on 
progress.  W. Bertrand noted that the overall pr oject seemed to be behind on the delivery of project targets.   
Acknowledging that the project had some delays and that the consultant still had several open issues 
pending even though the project submissions shoul d be completed.  The lack of an updated 
recommendation for a new salary grid was especially worrisome.  Since the updated job descriptions were 
previously made available to members of this committee, they had provided feedback to J. Belliveau.  Most 
were minor notations and did not materially change their outcome.  Several descriptions were reviewed and 
minor issues explained more fully.  The descriptions are in good order and committee will continue to 
review any updated provided.  The group questioned if any descriptions resulted in grade changes.  While 
===PAGE 2===

better identify at our next meeting.   
The group was generally concerned about finalizing this proje ct, especially the proposed salary grid 
recommendations that are  needed to be submitted for the Finance committee and ultimately at town 
meeting.  The group recognized the limited timeline to complete this project and agreed to meet again 
Monday, March 6, 2023 at 6pm on Zoom.  This was done with the request that the committee get updated 
prior to the meeting with an updated salary grid and any other job description changes prior to the meeting 
so they might be review prior to the meeting.

Town of Lunenburg 
Personnel Committee 
Minutes:  March 6, 2023    
Zoom 6:00pm 
 
Attendees:   
Susan Andrew, Donna Fortune, Wendy Bertrand, Joanna Bilotta  
 
Also Present: Julie Belliveau, Asst. Town Manager/HR Director, Barbara Lefebvre, Sewer Business 
Manager, Nancy O’Rourke, Payroll and Benefits/Risk Manager, Pauline Roy, COA Outreach Coordinator,  
Heather Lemieux, Town Manager; Mary Beth Bernard, HR Consultant (6:03pm – 6:23pm) 
 
Joanna Bilotta opened the Meeting at 6:03pm with members noted in attendance 
 
New Business 
Motion by S. Andrews to move the Consultants presentation on the agenda to the beginning of meeting, to 
accommodate her agreement to call in during her vacation. 
Seconded:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Mary Beth Bernard outlined the HR projects goals and current status covering the formal proposal she had 
prepared for the committee. The balance she attempted to build in to deal with recruitment and retention 
issues as well as taking in constraints of the municipalities need to manage under Prop 2 ½.  She also 
submitted a proposed change in the overall SAP salary grid, moving from a 20 level grid to a 16 level grid.  
This proposed grid also has been built to balance the midpoint differentials from grade to grade as well as 
the spread within each grade that more closely aligns with other comparable corporate standards.  As the 
material was delivered to the committee late today, committee members were unable to offer opinions on 
submission.  It was also determined that the material had not been made available to anyone outside the 
committee.  J. Bilotta requested that J. Belliveau make the material available to others. Mary Beth 
suggested the committee consider renaming levels of the grid from numeric to alphabetic to allow for the 
loss of the four grades.  The entire committee agreed that a great deal of analysis was needed to determine 
how to proceed.  Heather and Julie had begun to assess this and would share their findings.  The committee 
would also need to understand what the change would be in actual town spending, either for 2023 or if 
spaced unto 2024. 
 
Public Comment: 
The public comment was regarding the lack of receiving the above mentioned consultants proposal.  J. 
Bilotta addressed this as noted in new business notes.  D. Fortune noted that she had gone back to the job 
descriptions in the drop box, that she had commented on to be sure the comments were there and found no 
evidence of that accurately happening.  J. Belliveau/H. Lemieux would go back to IT to research this issue. 
 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes of the March 6, 2023 meeting were presented and reviewed. 
Motion by S. Andrews to accept and approve the minutes of the March 6, 2023 meeting as presented 
Second:  D. Fortune  
Roll Call Vote:  All in favor 
 
Ongoing Business 
The committee explored the issue of our limited timeline to complete this project and present our proposal 
to both the Finance Committee and ultimately the Select Board for delivery at Town Meeting.  The 
updating of the job descriptions needed to be assessed as much previous work might have been lost.  
Extensive review of the entire proposal especially the SAP salary grid would be needed.  Julie and Heather 
indicated they had already started to assess the impact and would share their findings with the group as it 
become available.  The group agreed to meet again the following Monday, hoping that additional analysis 
would become available and open issues like the commenting of the job descriptions would be corrected. 
===PAGE 2===
 
New Business 
The initial milestone for the committee was presenting an article to be submitted for the town meeting.  J. 
Belliveau presented a proposed article for that submission.  The need for full disclosure of our proposal was 
reviewed and it was determined that the article was a generalized statement and would not include any 
specifics to a final submission date of March 30, 2023 would be met.  A minor clarification was added to 
the article. 
Motion by S. Andrews to accept the recommendation, with one minor clarification, that had been proposed 
by the HR Director for the submission of the committees Town Meeting Article. 
Second:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Public Comment 
B. Lefebvre acknowledged her appreciation for the extensive work that the committee was doing on this 
important project.  Committee comments were from J. Bilotta regarding the loss of the chairperson E. 
Arnold and the diminished committee membership, noting the need to recruit another member.   
 
Next Meeting: 
Monday, March 13, 2023 7pm Zoom 
 
 
Motion by to adjourn meeting at 6:55pm by J. Bilotta 
Second:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor

There were no public or Committee comments 
 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes from the November 13, 2023 meeting of the Personnel Committee were reviewed  
             Motion by D. Fortune to accept the minutes as presented for the November 13, 2023 Personnel Committee Meeting 
Second:  S. Andrews 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Ongoing Business: 
The Committee continued to examine the re-rating of the COA positions.  D. Fortune and S. Andrews had adapted the 
current spreadsheet for easier review and the Committee began examining the various roles within each grade level, 
focusing especially on the COA and library roles as they related to similar roles in each grade level.   
The Committee identified the following recommendations to be considered with the Department Managers: 
Dietary Aide:  The 11/13/23 meeting identified that Confidentiality seemed to be weighted to high; at this meeting we 
identified that the description the consultant drafted showed an average weight of  up to 30# and the previous 
description showed up to 60#.  Needs to be clarified 
Asst. Meal Site Manager:  The 11/13/23 meeting identified that the confidentiality measure seemed to be weighted too 
high.  No other items identified at this meeting. 
COA Outreach Assistant:  The current JD seems very limited without much content, recommend revision; perhaps we 
were missing a page?   
Meal Site Manager:  The 11/13/23 meeting identified that the confidentiality measure seemed to be weighted too 
high; at this meeting we identified that we felt that the work experience should be weighted higher (D2 to D3), and 
perhaps the supervisor role lower and work environment higher. 
COA Van Driver: The 11/13/23 meeting identified that the confidentiality measure seemed to be weighted too high.  
At this meeting we identified both judgment and physical demand seemed to also be too high. 
COA Outreach Coordinator:  The Committee felt that the experience level of this role should be measured higher than 
noted and the Occupational Hazards might be too high.   
COA Director: The Committee felt that the experience (D3 to D4) and supervisory measures should be higher (D5 to 
D6). 
 
The Committee had a general discussion on measuring Directors and were there different levels of Directors, 
example: Beach Director perhaps is more reasonably noted as Beach Supervisor. 
 
Library Assistant: The Committee questioned if the level of experience was too high; unclear if this is PT high school 
student assignment or someone more skilled ie: education HS Diploma or equivalent. 
Staff Librarian:  The Committee questioned that the level of education should be higher i.e. Masters Degree; and that 
level of experience might be lower? 
Library Page:  There are currently no employees in this category 
Videographer:  The Committee questioned that the Occupational Hazards should be measured higher (D1 to D2). 
Sr. Videographer:  The Committee questioned that the Experience should be higher (D3 = 3-5yrs) 
Admistrative Asst – Conservation:  This description opened a conversation on the differences in these types of roles; 
each department appears to utilize their employee if various ways utilizing different skill requirements.  This will be 
explored as we move ahead.  For now the Conservation role seems to have higher weighting in several areas that 
seems too high:  accountability, judgment, complexity and personal contact.  Needs more review. 
Administrative Asst. – IT:  The Committee questioned if this is more a customer service type role rather than 
administrative Assistant, but ratings seemed appropriate:

   Town of Lunenburg 
Personnel Committee 
Minutes:  December 20, 2023   
5:00pm – Town Hall - in person meeting  
 
Committee Attendees:   
Wendy Bertrand, Susan Andrews, Joanna Bilotta, Donna Fortune 
 
Also Present  
Julie Belliveau, Assistant Town Manager/HR Director 
 
J. Bilotta opened the Meeting at 5:06pm with members noted in attendance 
 
Public Comment: 
There were no public or committee comments 
 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes from the November 28, 2023 meeting of the Personnel Committee were reviewed.  
             Motion by D. Fortune to accept the minutes as presented for the November 28, 2023 Personnel Committee Meeting 
Second:  S. Andrews 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
 
Ongoing Business: 
None 
 
New Business 
The Sewer Business Manager Job description was not available for review, will be done at next meeting. 
The Committee began to examine several positions that previously were noted as having some question regarding 
degree rankings.  PACC Production Tech both items in question, Education and Occupational Hazards were 
determined to be correctly represented. Exec Asst to TM and SB, Personnel Contact was changed from Degree 6 to 
Degree 4, and Confidential was correctly represented. PS Head Clerk, Confidential was correctly represented. PACC 
Manager, Experience was changed from Degree 3 to Degree 4. Seasonal Cemetery Laborer was changed in a number 
of areas; Experience was changed from Degree 4 to Degree 3; Accountability was changed from Degree 3 to Degree 
2; Judgment was changed from Degree 3 to Degree 2; Physical Demand was changed from Degree 4 to Degree 3. 
Payroll and Benefits Coordinator/Risk Manager, the Committee felt that the job description was incomplete and 
needed to be rewritten. Tax Collector/Treasurer, the Committee found no reason to change current grading. 
Intermittent Heavy Equipment Operator, Supervision should be noted as Degree 1. Asst. TM/HR Director, 
Accountability was changed from Degree 5 to Degree 6.  Network Administrator, this job description was found to 
have been correctly graded. Principal Assessor, Supervision Received was changed from Degree 5 to Degree 6, 
Complexity was changed from Degree 5 to Degree 4, and Personnel Contact was changed from Degree 4 to Degree 5.  
Beach Director, the Committee felt the title needed to be changed to Supervisor/Coordinator to better reflect the level 
of responsibility.  The final roles were from the fire department and all were left unchanged. 
 
Public Comment 
There were no comments from the public or the committee 
 
Our next meeting will be January 17, 2024, at 5:00pm, location to be determined 
 
And potential following meeting on January 31, 2024, location to be determined 
 
W. Bertrand made a motion to adjourn the meeting at 6:51pm 
Second:  S. Andrews  
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor
