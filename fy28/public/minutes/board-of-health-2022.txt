LUNENBURG BOARD OF HEALTH 
MEETING MINUTES 
Monday, January 10, 2022 
 
The Board of Health met at 7:00 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building  
 
Members Present:  George Emond, Perry Jewell, Paul Fortin , Lisa Normandin  and Evan Watters .   
Agent Jim Garreffi was also present. 
 
MINUTES APPROVED 
 
December 20, 2021 
 
On motion by Jewell and second by Normandin, the above minutes were approved.  All in favor. 
 
138 HOWARD STREET 
 
Kimberly Murphy, owner  met with the board .  This is a failed system on public water.  A Presb y 
system is being proposed.  A groundwater variance from 4 feet to 3  feet is being requested.  This lot is 
a fairly big lot with gravely soils and no wetland issues.  Agent Garreffi was present at the meeting and 
had no issues with the granting of this offset to groundwater variance. 
 
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by  Fortin, the board unanimously approved the 
requested variance. All in favor. 
 
390 NW TOWNSEND ROAD 
 
The permit on the above property has expired.  This permit is for new c onstruction no variances were 
allowed.  During the course of Covid the Commonwealth of Mass enacted an emergency declaration to 
extend permits on a State Level.  Local permits were extended for 60 days past the expiration of the 
emergency declaration.  This permit meets all state and local codes. 
 
At the time that the extension was in place, the excavation had been done.  There is now a new owner 
and the permit has been transfer. 
 
Based on the Commonwealth’s extension of permits and discussion of the board and on motion by 
Normandin and second by Jewell, the board voted to extend this permit for a period of one year or until 
January 10, 2023.  All in favor. 
 
1078 LANCASTER AVENUE 
 
Engineer Mark Malbag met with the board on behalf of the owner, Jeff Laundry, who was also present.  
This an old camp built in 1953 by the current owner’s grandfather.  This is an extremely small lot on 
Massapoag Pond which is currently served by an old leaching pit. 
 
Mr. Malbag is before the board requesting a variance for the inst allation of a 3,000 gallon tight tank on 
the site.  Due to the size of the lot and location on Massapoag Pond, there is no other alternative 
available for this property. 
 
===PAGE 2===
After discussion, and on motion by Watters and second by Normandin, the board voted t o approve the 
request for the installation of a tight tank with the condition of a 2 bedroom deed restriction and a 
maintenance agreement in place.  Further, the tight tank installation must be completed by May 15, 
2022. 
 
FLAT HILL ORCHARD – MATSON 
 
Jim Garreffi was present to speak with the board  with regard to this matter.  Mr. Matson purch ased a 
portion of land from Flat Hill Orchard for a housing development .  The land purchased was previously 
used as part of Flat Hill Orchard.  As a part of the approva l for this subdivision, soil testing to 
determine if there was soil contamination from orchard spraying was required. 
 
This property had been used as an orchard since the 1930’s where  chemicals were sprayed on the trees 
including phosphates, arsenic and lead.  Jim Garreffi asked that testing be conducted in at least the top 
one foot of soil. 
 
Mr. Matson contracted with a company to complete the testing but Agent Garreffi does not believe that 
manner and reporting of the results are adequate.  Agent Garreffi  is requesting that the testing results 
be submitted in a form that is readable.  If the results obtained are under the standards then the soils are 
ok.  In addition, there will be wells located on the individual building lots.  Those wells will also be 
tested for pesticides, as well as standard Lunenburg tests. 
 
COVID UPDATE 
 
Agent Jim Garreffi reported that there were 148 cases of Covid last week.  The two week rolling 
average doubled to 148 from the previous week of 72.  With the exceptionally high number of case is it 
becoming harder to contact trace for all cases.  Nashoba has recently invested in a new system that will 
send out an automated text to those that test positive. 
 
The CDC is changing guidance frequently and the different guidance for differe nt groups makes it 
difficult.  Enforcement is difficult.  Mask mandates are hard to enforce. 
 
Nashoba will be holding vaccine and booster clinics and will be able to offer other vaccines besides 
Moderna. 
 
Having no further matters before the board and on  motion Fortin and seconded by Jewell, the meeting 
was adjourned at 8:35 pm, all in favor.

LUNENBURG BOARD OF HEALTH 
MEETING MINUTES 
Monday, February 14, 2022 
 
The Board of Health met at 7:00 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building  
 
Members Present:  George Emond, Perry Jewell, Paul Fortin, and Evan Watters.   Agent Jim Garreffi 
was also present. 
 
MINUTES APPROVED 
 
January 7, 2022 
 
On motion by Jewell and second by Normandin, the above minutes were approved.  All in favor. 
 
68 WILDWOOD 
 
Dan Proctor met with the board on behalf of the owner.  This is a failed system that had been installed 
in the 1970’s.  Testing showed a perc rate of 8 MPI.  A replacement Presby system is being proposed.  
Variance requested is offset to groundwater from 4 feet to 3 feet.  The property is served by public 
water. 
 
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Watters, the board unanimously approved the 
requested variance. All in favor. 
 
444 CHASE ROAD 
 
Dan Proctor met with the board.  This system is in failure.  The property was too wet to do test holes so 
a sieve analysis was completed instead and showed a perc  rate of 5 MPI.  The only variance being 
requested is the use of a sieve analysis. 
 
After discussion and on motion by Fortin and second by Jewell, the board voted to approve the 
requested variance.  All in favor. 
 
1 VALLEY ROAD 
 
The septic permit for this property expired in April of 2021, during Covid.  The current owner of the 
property would like to begin installing the system and is asking for a one year extension. 
 
After discussion, and on motion by Watters and second by Fortin, the board voted to appro ve the 
request for a one year extension until February 14, 2023.  All in favor. 
 
64 ARBOR STREET 
 
Mr. Woodward, the owner, met with the board.  He is proposing to subdivide his property retaining a 
lot with the current house and splitting off a building lo t.  As part of this process, Agent Jim Garreffi 
supplied Mr. Woodward with the Board of Health regulations regarding dividing land .  Those 
Regulations require a Title 5 report be completed on the existing house and that the requirement to 
===PAGE 2===
show the location  of a new replacement system must be shown on the subdivision plan .  This is 
required to insure that required property for a replacement system is not being subdivided off. 
 
Mr. Woodrow did not believe that he should have to submit an additional Title 5 an d did not want to 
invest additional funds in showing where a replacement could be located. 
 
After much discussion, the Chairman, with the support of the board,  again informed Mr. Woodard 
again that he would have to comply with Title 5 Subdivision rules by providing a current Title 5 
Report and show the location of a replacement system on remainder lot on the subdivision plan. 
 
Mr. Woodrow indicated to the board that he would be resubmitting the plan with the required 
information. 
 
COVID UPDATE 
 
Agent Jim Ga rreffi reported that Covid cases within the Town are significantly lower.  Cases are half 
what they had been. 
 
Nashoba will continue to hold  vaccine and booster clinics and will be able to offer other vaccines 
besides Moderna. 
 
BUDGET 
 
The budget was prese nted to the Board.  There is a substantial increase in the assessment to Nashoba  
associated Boards of Health.  The increase is due to the yearly 5% increase as well as an adjustment for 
the increase in population derived from the census in 2010 to the census in 2020. 
 
Having no further matters before the board and on  motion Fortin and seconded by Jewell, the meeting 
was adjourned at 8:35 pm, all in favor.

JENNA MONTGOMERY 
 
Jenna Montgomery, Public Health Educator/Communications Specialist , met with the Board.  Ms. 
Montgomery stated that she joined Nashoba Associated Boards of Health in September under a grant.  
The grant is to run for two years.  She stated that her position/job is to support Public Health Education

drinking water standard is  “10” or below however the current nitrate level is too high for Title 5 
standards. 
 
Agent Jim Garreffi is recommending p assing this Title 5 report as the nitrate level is below the

236 CROSS ROAD

Variances requested are offset to groundwater f rom 4 feet to 2 f eet, reduction in inlet and out let tees

February 14, 2022

100 CHESTNUT STREET 
 
Chris MacKenzie met with the board on behalf of the Lunenburg Parks Commission.  This discussion 
is for the replacement septic system as Marshall P ark.  Soil testing was conducted and a new  
replacement Presby system has been designed.   The system as designed is a pump system with a 3000 
gallon tank and 1,500 gallon pump chamber.  Soil Testing showed a perc rate of 6 MPI. The design as 
presented will allow for 1,000 gallon per day or 200 people in accordance with Title 5 Guidelines for a 
park/playground. 
 
Variances being requested are offset to wetlands from 100 feet to 58 feet 
Groundwater offset from 4 feet to 3 feet 
Less than 4 feet of pervious material to 2.33 feet 
Inlet and outlet tees less than 12 inches to 4 inches 
 
Agent Jim Garreffi stated that he doesn’t have any issues with the requests and that the addition of 
bathroom facilities at the park would be a public good. 
 
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Watters, the board unanimously approved the 
requested variances. All in favor. 
 
STELLA’S 
 
The owner, Allan Souza met with the board.  Mr. Souza advised that he was able to obtain a test date 
for April 4, 2022 for the Restaurant Managers Certification.  
 
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Watters , the board voted to approve the 
extension.  All in favor. 
 
1 VALLEY ROAD 
 
The septic permit for this property expired in April of 2021, during Covid.  The current own er of the 
property would like to begin installing the system and is asking for a one year extension. 
 
===PAGE 2===

1432 LANCASTER AVENUE 
 
This is a continuation from Board of Health meeting on March 7, 2021. 
 
The Board is now in receipt of the Title 5 report for this property.  After much discussion and on 
motion by Jewell and second by Fortin, the Boar d voted to Pass the Title 5 report with the conditio n

PERMIT EXTENSIONS 
 
441 Country Road 
205 Sunset Lane
