October 25, 2022 
 
Meeting called to order at 14:25 by Chief Gammel at NVRDD, 270 Barnum Road, Devens, MA.

APPROVAL OF MEETING MINUTES 
• A motion was made by Chief Hanson and seconded by Chief Clark to accept the September 13, 2022 
submitted meeting minutes. The motion carried unanimous. 
 
EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR REPORT 
• Staffing:  
a. We currently have 1 new dispatcher entering the final phase of training and 1 new dispatcher in 
the State 911 Training Academy. He will be graduating from the academy on 11/04 and will 
report to NVRDD on 11/07. 
b. We have hired a new district accountant, and he began working on 10/03/2022. His name is Neil 
Vaidya and he has over 19yrs experience in municipal accounting. 
• Events:  
a. I was just notified by Chief Legendre that LIV Golf is coming back to Bolton in June of 2023, so we 
will most likely begin planning in the new year.  
b. We also have for 2023 Devens Day, and The Bolton Fair. 
• Projects:  
a. We have completed the first update project for the facility. We now have card access to the two 
main doors and our server room.  
b. In the first week of February, we will be completing the replacement of the dispatcher’s desks. 
This will be a live replacement and I will coordinate between our IT vendor, Radio Vendor and 
911. 
c. I have our Radio Vendor working on licensing NVRDD’s base radios under each of your 
frequencies. The frequencies still belong to each of the towns, and should be mainta ined that

NVRDD operates on. 
d. Citrix Update – we have purchased a new Citrix Virtual Connection, and will be replacing the

NVRDD. 
• Additional Towns: 
a. Chief Babu and I met with the Boxborough Police and Fire Chiefs on Monday. It was a good initial

APPROVAL OF MEETING MINUTES 
• A motion was made by Chief Sullivan and seconded by Chief Babu to accept the December 20, 2022 
submitted meeting minutes. The motion carried unanimous. 
 
EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR REPORT 
• Trainee Courtney Perry has completed all training requirements and has been released from training. 
Trainee Zach Lewis is in phase 1 of the on-the-job training program, he was placed on an improvement 
plan and has shown improvement in the last few days. We have one new hire Ashley Wirta she has begun 
training with State 911 this week. With this latest hire, we have 3 supervisors, and 10 dispatchers. I will 
not fill the 4th supervisor position (which will make us fully staffed) until the end of the fiscal year to allow 
for training to be completed and to ensure we’re balancing out our OT budget.  
• I have presented the final draft of the FY24 Budget to the Finance Committee. There was a lot of

1. General Description 
 
A police detail is described as the assignment of one or more officers for a particular specific 
duty. A police detail is typically present at any road work where traffic must be diverted or 
at events with special safety or security concerns.  
 
In order to provide the most direct access to those who are looking to hire a police detail 
and the officers themselves, the role of Nashoba Valley Regional Dispatch District (NVRDD) 
Dispatchers in the detail request process will be limited in function.  The Dispatcher will 
transfer the caller to a Police Detail Voicemail and advise the respective officer in charge 
(OIC) or designated police employee of the call so that the OIC/designated police employee 
can contact the caller and obtain further information for processing the request. 
 
If the caller requesting a detail is another police department with one or more details, the 
dispatcher will notify the OIC over the air of the request and to contact said department for 
details regarding the open details.

2. Policy 
 
It shall be the policy of the Nashoba Valley Regional Dispatch District to adhere to the 
following procedure for taking and recording detail requests. 
 
3. Procedure 
 
When a detail request is received from a private company/citizen/police department for 
one of our service towns, the Dispatcher will ascertain which department the detail is for 
and transfer the caller to the respective town police detail voicemail.  The OIC or designated 
police employee will be notified over the radio of the transferred detail request.   
 
The Police Detail Voicemail has a function to send an audio recording of the message to 
whichever email addresses are programmed into the system.  The officer/designated police 
employee will then follow their department policy/guidelines on filling the detail. 
 
If the caller requesting a detail is from another police department with details for multiple 
of our service towns, the dispatcher will notify the OIC over the air that the requesting 
department has open details and to contact them directly.  If the OIC is interested in the 
open detail, then they will contact the agency directly and follow their policy/guidelines on 
filling the detail. 
 
Requests to cancel, change or update a detail request or requests for emergency detail jobs 
will be logged in IMC and then transferred to the respective detail line for further 
documentation. The dispatcher will log the date, time, location, caller’s name and business 
in CAD, and will notify the OIC over the air and assign him/her to the call, immediately upon 
receiving the call of the cancellation, change or update.  
  
 
4. Questions

concerning the above policy to their immediate Supervisor or Department Union

I. CALL TO ORDER:    
 
With both the Administration Board and Operations Committee quorum present and acting, 
the meeting was called to order by the Administration Board Chair at 9:33am. 
 
II. DISCUSSION OF STATE 911 REQUEST RE: BOXBOROUGH'S 911 CALLS 
 
Director Camaro was given the floor to present the request from State 911 Department, for 
NVRDD to take over and process 9-1-1 calls for the Town of Boxborough for 60 days. 
===PAGE 2===
 
 
Director Camaro also presented the concerns previously sent to her by members of both the 
Admin Board and Operations Committee: 
• What happens at the end of the 60 days, if the Town of Boxborough is not able to 
staff their communications center? 
• What is the Town of Boxborough doing to mitigate the issue? 
• Why isn’t their back-up center able to take over their calls? 
• There’s a concern about taking on another community in an ‘emergency’ situation, 
and being forced into another “Townsend Situation”. 
 
Monna Wallace from State 911 Department addressed the concerns: 
• Currently the Town of Boxborough has 2 officers and 1 dispatcher certified. They are 
hoping to be back to their optimal staffing (5) by the end of the sixty days. 
• The Town of Boxborough is actively searching, interviewing and training staff to work in 
the comm center. 
• The Town of Stow is the alternate PSAP for Boxborough. They are however, unable to 
take on the calls from Boxborough as they are a single-dispatcher PSAP. 
• The State 911 Department does not believe that the Town of Boxborough would be 
rushing into a regional center without pre-planning. 
 
Administration Board Chair Kristen Rubin opened the floor for other comments, concerns and 
discussion. 
 
Chief Gammel stated his concerns with the arrangements: 
• 60-days is not enough time to get staffing levels back up. 
• NVRDD Communities pay their assessments, and are concerned with taking on a town 
with no financial compensation. 
Monna Wallace addressed the concerns from Chief Gammel, by saying that State 911 will 
continue to monitor and work with the Town of Boxborough to address the staffing issue. Frank 
Pozniak advised that State 911 can provide NVRDD with financial reimbursement for any 
overtime incurred as a result of this arrangement. 
 
Don Lowe also expressed his concerns with regards to the arrangement. He agrees with the points 
brought up by Chief Gammel and added that it will be important to define what happens at day 
61, and he is concerned with NVRDD’s staff getting burned out. 
 
Director Camaro took the floor, and advised she has had a conversation with NVRDD’s 
Dispatcher Union, and that they are in favor. She opened the floor to Supervisor Kinnas 
(scheduler) and Supervisor Casey (Union President). 
 
Supervisor Casey advised of not having a microphone, but that he was in favor of the 
arrangement. 
 
Supervisor Kinnas talked about the scheduling process at NVRDD and that he did not think there 
would be an issue filling the open shifts. He was also a proponent for the arrangement, as he 
believed this arrangement would showcase NVRDD as a good neighbor and willing to work with 
our public safety partners. 
 
Chief Babu took the floor and favored the arrangement, agreeing with Supervisor Kinnas.  
 
===PAGE 3===
 
 
Heather Lemieux took the floor and aired her concern with what will happen after the 60-days. 
Both Monna Wallace and Frank Pozniak advised that they will be monitoring the situation, and 
will continuously provide updates to Director Camaro. 
Chief Babu wanted clarification that NVRDD would only be taking 9-1-1 Calls, and was advised 
that yes, it would only be 9-1-1 calls, followed by a transfer to the Boxborough Police. 
 
With no further discussion, Chief Gammel entertained a motion to accept the State 911 Proposal, 
where NVRDD will answer and transfer 9-1-1 calls originating in the Town of Boxborough to the 
Boxborough Police Department for the period of 60 days.
