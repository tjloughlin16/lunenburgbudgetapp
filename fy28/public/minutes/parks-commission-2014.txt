Meeting convened at 7 o'clock, members present: Commissioners Bob Robuccio and Mike Masciarelli.
No previous meeting minutes to approve, 3/27 meeting postponed due to snow storm.
Recreation Basketball
Bob Robuccio reported no problems with the recreation basketball league this winter. League wrapped
up play Saturday 3/8 with a championship night held at the LHS gym.
Both the adult men and women's basketball league concluded their season at the Lunenburg Primary
School, with no issues.
Turf Field
Mike Masciarelli reviewed Turf Schedule:
LYSA has field reserved for practices/games starting Monday 3/31 to 6/14. Monday thru Friday 5:00
pm - 8:30 pm, Saturdays 8:30 am - 4:00 pm. LYSA also has 3 selected Sundays.
Adult soccer league has also reserved turf for Friday nights after LYSA activities starting in June.
Water at the bathrooms need to be turned on, Bob will contact Jack to arrange for turn on.
Marshall Park Baseball Field
Bob Robuccio reported that Marshall Park Baseball field is still unplayable, alternate arrangements and
schedule changes are taken place until the field is ready for use. The LHS baseball schedule is available
on the schools web site.
Lunenburg Phillies schedule is still not completed, practice/try-out times will start as soon as the field is
. ready. Practice/try-outs are scheduled for every Tuesday, Thursday and. Saturday until league play
===PAGE 2===
begins.
The Lunenburg Police Association has reserved the field for its annual celebrity softball game/fundraiser
on Saturday 6/211:00 pm - 4:00 pm.
Mike Masciarelli reported that a group of parents in the community would like to add some plantings
around the "Mike Dione Memorial Bullpen" to help improve its appearance. Will discuss this matter at
a future meeting.
Other Business
The Turf field fee structure needs to be modified. Meeting with DPW director, LYSA, Pop Warner,
LTFA and the School Dept will be scheduled to start discussing this matter.
Public Comment
Troy Daniels was present at the meeting and stated that the commission has been violating the Open
Meeting Law. The commission thanked him for his input and will work to eliminate this issue.
Next scheduled Park Commission meeting will be Thursday May 8, 2014 7:00 pm @ the DPW.

7pm
Department of Public Works,
520 Chase Road, Lunenburg, MA 01462
Call to Order
Meeting convened at 7pm. In attendance were:
Commissioners: Mike Masciarelli, Bob Robuccio, Corinne Scouten
DPW Director: Jack Rodriquenz
Review/Approve Minutes
Minutes from 6/19/14 meeting were approved, 3-0.
New Park Commission Organization
Bob Robuccio will be stepping down as Chairman. Motion made by Bob Robuccio to appoint
Mike Masciarelli the new Chairman. The motion was seconded by Corinne Scouten. Vote 3 - 0
in favor of appointment. Mike will continue to oversee/handle items relating to the Turf field
and track and Bob will continue to oversee/handle items relating to Marshall Park and the
Town Beach. Corinne will record meeting minutes moving forward.
===PAGE 2===
DPW Director Jack Rodriquenz commented that the Town Beach program ran well this year but
there are concerns about weeds in the beach area. He suggests that we have Gen Nass - Beach
Director come in to speak with the Commission.
OML Complaint
The Parks Commission received an Open Meeting Law Complaint from resident Troy Daniels.
Mr. Daniels contends that the Parks Commission did not post notice of their Joint Meeting with
the Board of Selectmen held on August 12, 2014.
Then Chairman, Bob Robuccio, verified with Town Clerk and Town Manager that the Board of
Selectmen's meeting posting clearly specified the joint meeting of the two bodies and therefore
met the requirements of the Open Meeting Law posting process. Bob will provide a response
to Mr. Daniels.
New Parking Lot at Marshall Park
DPW Director Jack Rodriquenz offered an update on the new parking area being created at
Marshall Park. Trees have been removed on the hill area to the right of first base. Old bath
house will be demolished and entrance to the lot will be by way of the Mass. Ave. side of the
park. The purpose of the new parking is to alleviate the traffic issue caused by vehicles parked
along Chestnut St during games. The hope is that finishing touches will be complete for Spring
2015.
Abutter Elaine Mroz has been in contact with Jack regarding some trees that were removed
that she feels were on her property. Jack will be looking into this further and will report back to
the commission.
Softball Field Improvements/Marshall Park
Mark Sowerbutts who runs the Summer Softball League - The Comets - would like to see the
Softball field at Marshall re-done. Jeff Powell has offered some ideas regarding items such as
new fencing, new dugouts including moving them off of the field area (they are currently
considered "in play") and re-doing the field itself. Mr. Sowerbutts is suggesting that some
fundraising through Little League can be done to fund this project. It was noted that the field
has a sprinkler system.
Steve Chow, Little League President, has voiced concerns about erosion between the softball
field and lower Powell field and has presented a sketch to the Commission (thru Mike
Masciarelli) on how this can be corrected. Included may be an 18" wall. Would this require a
rail to be safe? Dan Proctor has also been consulted and he feels that Little League has the
funds to complete this project. DPW Director Jack Rodriquenz suggests that detailed plans with
===PAGE 3===
funding values including labor and materials be provided to the Commission for any project
being considered. Mention was made of checking insurance coverages of people working on
the fields.
Bob Robuccio reported that the pitcher's mound and sod are coming along nicely on Marshall
Field. High school field hockey will be using the outfield during the school construction project.
Greg Dupuis will be mowing Marshall. He typically mows the school's fields but will now mow
Marshall during the school's use.
DPW Director Jack Rodriquenz suggests a Weed-n-Feed or Turf Management program be
considered.
Recordkeeping/Website/Email
Corinne Scouten noted that the Park Commission link on the lunenburgma.gov site needs some
attention/updating. Schedules, links, and contact names and numbers need to be worked on.
Both concession stand and field use forms should be added as documents.
A general email should be set up for contact purposes as opposed to each commissioner's
personal address. Suggestion is parks@lunenburgonline.com
Corinne will work with DPW Admin Amanda Moore to make these updates/changes.
Turf Fee Structure
Turt Field and Concession Stand Fees need to be re-evaluated and reinforced. Since the
completion of the field, the main users - Lunenburg Schools, LYSA Soccer, and Bengals Football
had been paying a combined total of $25K in user fees each year. These payments went to the
town to help pay down the lease fees. Now that the lease payments are complete, there seems
to be no clear understanding of what fees are and how they will be collected. The Finance
Committee is asking for the Parks Commission to begin working on a plan in this area. The
pressing upcoming concern is that the turf field and track will need complete reconditioning
soon - suggested date is 2017. Estimated cost may be near $800K.
Mike Masciarelli has been researching how other area fields charge for their use and most are
an hourly type rate. How would we establish how many hours the school uses the facility?
How many hours do the Bengals or LYSA use it? Mike will speak with the Bengals and Bob will
speak with Pete regarding the school. LYSA has moved to Camp Lowe during the school
construction.
Currently there is a generator on site for night games for lights, etc. Future lighting cost will be
addressed in conjunction with the new school.
In addition to the Turf Fee Structure, DPW Director Jack Rodriquenz suggests that this
Commission begin thinking about a 5 & 10 year plan and suggest items that may need to be
===PAGE 4===
considered under the Capital Planning Budget - Example: Wallis Court has not been resurfaced
in 20 years. Jack is encouraging the Commission to consider needs at all of our
properties/parks.
Adjourn
Motion to adjourn meeting was made and seconded. Vote in favor of adjournment 3-0.
Time adjourned was 8:33pm.
Minutes Accepted on: 10/2/14
Mike Mascare on
Robert Robuccio le
Corinne Scouten

Others: Jeff Bingham, Tom Leppala, and Cliff Worthley
Review /Approve Minutes
Minutes from 8/28/14, 9/18/14, and 9/25/14 meeting were approved, 3-0.
Veterans' Park Memorial Bricks/Walkway
Cliff Worthley was present to discuss and update the commission on the progress of the brick
walkway at Veterans' Park on Memorial Drive. Cliff reports there are 82 bricks in the current
===PAGE 2===
walkway that are not yet engraved. He also reports that 80 bricks have been sold (through
Town Clerk's office). Monies for these brick sales have been deposited into the Park
Commission account and the same will be used to pay for the completed engraved bricks. The
first 40 bricks are being worked on now at Leominster Monument and the engraving should be
done by the end of October in time to be installed before Veterans' Day. Discussion about who
would install these came up. Jeff Bingham offered to look into this and Mike M. suggested
checking with Jack Rodriquenz to see if he had any DPW manpower to do this.
Comets Softball
Cliff Worthley states softball season is over. He wants to close the softball bank account by
obtaining a bank check and turning it over to the Parks Commission. He believes there is
approximately $3,000. Mark Sowerbutts is still interested in continuing this league next
summer and he is currently holding the uniforms. Typical season expenses are the registration
fee to the league, insurance fee to ASA, purchase of socks, water, ice, and soda. Umpire fees
and one game ball are provided by the league.
Lunenburg Jr. Basketball League
Cliff Worthley wishes to step away from his involvement in the Jr. Basketball program. The
program has funds in a bank account that he typically has cut the checks for. The account has
always used the town's tax ID. The bank has told Cliff that Jr. Basketball can no longer do this
and must apply for its own tax ID. There is no current Jr. Basketball Board in place but when
there was, there was always a Parks Commissioner on the board. There is a question of
insurance coverage and flow of funds. There is a website hosted by Homestead Tech. Cliff's
name is on that as well and he would like that to come off. Corinne Scouten will look into the
tax ID question and report back at the next meeting.
Track/Turf Drainage Issues
Jeff Bingham of LTAFA was at the field and noticed work being done on the new school was in
the direct proximity of a drainage system that was installed during the original building of the
track facility. This system captures rainwater in a containment system to be used for irrigation
of the lower grass practice field. He spoke to someone on-site to ask who he could talk to
regarding the status of the drainage in relation to the new school construction. He was finally
