• , and secretary to serve a one-year term.

• Dave Passios had recommended in May that the committee review the Town's financial policies,
especially the policy on borrowing. As a role of the Finance Committee is to make
recommendations about the financial policies and as they have been in place for a few years now,
the committee began this process at this meeting. At the meeting we all got printed copies of these
policies to review. Links to the policies are on the Board of Selectmen page on the Town's website.
• This committee can recommend changes to the policies and the Board of Selectmen will
decide whether to adopt the recommendations.
• The BOS has modified these policies over the years.
• This committee will continue the review when more members are present, probably at the July 11
meeting.

• Resident David Rodgers: Inquired what the anticipated Chapter 70 and 90
numbers were and how they compare to the town budget. The town
manager and Terri Burchfield reported that the numbers are still not final.
Mr. Rodgers also expressed concern that the budgets for the Parks
Department and Cemetery Commission were not prepared or ratified by

included in the joint meeting that would be discussed later in the agenda.

funds are "discretionary" during the budget process. He advised that the
committee adopt a "cautiously optimistic" plan. Mr. Rodgers also
requested that the committee take a look at the Master Plan work that the
Planning Board is outsourcing and suggested that the work should be done
by the Land Use Director instead.
3. Request for Joint Meeting/Presentations
• Terri Burchfield recommended that in response to the School Committee's
invitation for a joint meeting that the Finance Committee propose a
'symposium' presentation in October; delivered by the Town Manager,
Superintendent of Schools, and Land Use Director; and overseen by the
chairpersons of the Board of Selectmen, School Committee and Finance
Committee. The chairpersons could then call upon their respective
members, and the public, for questions and comment. This would allow
the presenters to focus on delivering relevant information (outside of the
context of any specific budget or budget request) and alleviate the
confusion that comes with moderating a meeting of three committees,
nearly twenty members, and the public. The topic of the symposium would
focus on the anticipated effects of development and shifting demographics
on town services over the next several years.
• The Town Manager expressed support for the 'symposium' concept.
