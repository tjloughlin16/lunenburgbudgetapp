4. Response to Open Meeting Law Complaint from John Bowen – Attorney Costa spoke on behalf of the Cemetery Commission
and Michael-Ray Jeffreys, Chair Select Board.  This discussion was turned over to the Legal Counsel, Attorney Costa – presented
copies of the letter’s that Attorney Costa had written to both the complainant and Division of Open Government - Office of the 
Attorney General to Nancy Foss-Yasko, Chair and Dave Dorren, Vice-Chair.  Explanation of the Open Meeting Law and how 
this process works.  A new process was created and begin in early 2010’s for those that believed that an Open Meeting Law may 
have been violated.  The complaint is submitted by the complainant to the public body, in which a period of time in which the 
public body can review the substance of the complaint, and can then prepare a response.  That response gets sent to the 
complainant, and then filed with the new Division of Open Government, within the Office of the Attorney General; which 
reviews these sort of complaints.  In the event that the complainant is satisfied with the response from the public body, it will 
then end there – end of process.  If the complainant is then dissatisfied with the response, then the complainant can make a filing 
of their own with the Division of the Open Government, will then conduct their own investigation of the complaint; which 
consists of phone call/conversations with the complainant and members of the commission that are alleged to the Open Meeting 
Law violation.  This usually falls within the 45 to 60 days, the Division of the Open Government will make a determination if the
Open Meet Law has been violated, and will order any remedies that might be appropriate. Again, this is the process.  In this case 
an Open Meeting Law Complaint was submitted – originally filed on January 17, 2024.  Filed the Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Form – 2 pages.  Description of the alleged violation complaint completed by Mr. Bowen, and a request for certain actions to be 
undertaken by the public body. Attorney Costa did his own investigation after conducting many investigations for other

communities that he represents; and for probably about a dozen times over the years for the Town of Lunenburg.  The 
investigation was conducted of the facts, and prepared a proposed response.  This was sent in advance, but because of the open 
meeting law, we could not discuss in advance to having this Cemetery Commission Meeting.   This is a straight forward response
a couple of paragraphs with many footnotes that take up the better half of the first page.  The essence of the response of the Open 
Meeting Law is untimely.  The way that the Open Meeting Law is structured, a complainant that believes that the Open Meeting
Law has been violated – has 30 days from the date of the alleged violation to make a request to the Cemetery Commission and 
Division of Open Government; claiming that the violation of Open Meeting Law has occurred.  The 30 days run from the date of 
the alleged violation.  There are a provisions in the law, as I saw some correspondence from Mr. Bowen that he did not realize, or
was not aware that there was alleged violation, and that had occurred then sometime long after the date of November 30, 2023. 
There are some provisions in the Open Meeting Law, within the regulations that say if/or the 30 days have been responsibly 
known or discovered – then the date would change to the time that this was 30 days when discovered.  For example, if an 
Executive Session took place, then the only time you would know if the violation occurred was when the meeting minutes were 
released to the public.  The date would start from the date of the minutes being released to the public.  This was not the case, as 
Mr. Bowen had attended the meeting and was present on November 30, 2023 – Open Public Meeting.  Mr. Bowen contend that a 
violation occurred at a meeting that he attended on November 30, 2023; yet the complaint is dated and was received on January

consists of phone call/conversations with the complainant and members of the commission that are alleged to the Open Meeting
