Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes 
January 16, 2014. 
 
Attending:, Dick Mailloux, Martha Moore, Joanne McQuaid, Lisa Krowitz, Kathy Murray, Robin Venezia, Lars 
Widstrand, Bob Ebersole (Selectmen Representative), Brian Laffond (FinCom Representative) 
Absent: Jeanne Raboin 
 
Meeting called to order at 6:45 pm in the Sudolnik conference room by Vice Chairman Dick Mailloux. 
 
 Public Comments 
o No public comments 
 
 Agenda Items 
o Approval of minutes 
 October 17 minutes as amended; motion by Dick, seconded by Joanne, unanimous approval 
vote. 
November 21 minutes as amended; motion by Dick, seconded by Robin, approve by Dick, 
Joanne, Kathy, Lisa and Robin.  Lars abstained (not present at that meeting) 
 
o Director’s Report 
 Director’s handouts reviewed and discussed: FY-15 budget. 
 Vote on FY-15 Director’s proposed budget of 353,206.00, (town budget):  Motion to 
accept proposed budget by Dick, seconded by Lars, all votes in favor. 
To maintain the Library Revolving Fund at the existing level of $8,000.00 to 
supplement library budget in order to meet state requirement.  Motion to accept by 
Lars, seconded by Robin, all votes in favor. 
 Programs 
 Several adult programs on the books – nutrition; wood turning; Cultural Horizons 
programs to run through March-14. 
 CWMARS Network meeting to discuss Evergreen. 
 Budget 
 Finances – we are on target for mid- fiscal year. 
 Discussion about heating, water and sewer bills for the year.  Questions about the 
interval for the water bills – they arrive “randomly” 
 Salaries – on target 
 Martha has received training on Muni system.  Not fully implemented (rights to enter 
new vendors, etc.) for Martha yet. 
 Weeding collections – teen’s is done, children’s estimated to be done by April, adult 
fiction ongoing. 
 Addressing issues with cleaning company through building/facilities.  Scheduling 
currently not working. 
 Jack put in for capital request for carpeting.  Discussion about the scheduling for 
installation – the replacement will require library closing. 
 Staff 
 Nothing major, Kate and Karen partnering with the Lunenburg School. 
 Discussion about staffing. 
 Library Technology 
 Website update

o January 25, launch date for new website, additional marketing for 
Wowbrary. New URL: www.lunenburglibrary.org.  Martha provided an 
extensive walk-through of the new website.  The trustees were very 
impressed with the layout, added functionality and options. 
 Technology Plan update 
o Desktop virtualization discussion 
o Servers on order; software on order, up and running hopefully by end of

Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Personnel Subcommittee Meeting minutes 
February 6, 2014 
 
Attending: Lisa Krowitz, Richard Mailloux, Jeanne Raboin 
Guest: Martha Moore, LPL Director 
 
Meeting called to order at 6:54 p.m. in the Wallace Community Room by Jeanne Raboin. 
 
•  Public Comment: none 
 
•  Martha Moore presented different aspects to 360 evaluations. Personnel subcommittee members 
discussed these with Martha. 
 
•  Discussion about how to incorporate the 360 evaluation into the Library Director’s 2013-2014 
evaluation.  
 
o Procedure:  
/square4 The current evaluation form and selected questions from documents about 
conducting 360 reviews will be used. (These will be given to the Trustees at the 
Feb. 20 meeting) 
/square4 The selected 360 questions (along with Director’s j ob description and LPL 
mission statement) will be sent to selected town hall staff, all LPL library staff 
and selected Friends of LPL for feedback. Responders will have the choice to 
review anonymously.  If this method is chosen, eval uations will then be sent to 
Trustees via Lunenburg Public Library in a sealed envelope (to be placed in the 
Trustees folder);  
/square4 Library Director will provide a self-evaluation; also Director will provide 
information for sections II and IV from the current evaluation form. 
/square4 If Trustees have a goal/s in mind for Director, these will be added to the 
evaluation form. 
/square4 All evaluation forms to be in April 1. 
 
•  Open Trustees positions: brief discussion on recruiting Lunenburg residents to run for LPL Board 
of Trustees. Trustee chair, Jeanne Raboin, will send a document looking for volunteers to the 
Lunenburg Access Cable Channel. LPL web site and Lu nenburg Ledger.  
 
Meeting adjourned at 7:50 pm 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
Jeanne Raboin, Chair 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library

Public Library’s share of it.  For future budgets, the town will most likely be able to meet (LPL) minimum 
spending requirements.  However, due to upcoming Lunenburg school projects, there will not be much 
“wiggle room”. 
Robin asked if the library can help the town in any way shape or form (i.e. meeting space, seminars, 
etc.).  Kerry made a mental note, but saw no immediate need. 
Jeanne asked to get the Lunenburg Public Library Trustees listed on the town website under the tab 
Boards and Committees.

Friday hours or partial Sunday hours.  No recommendation or plan was drafted at th is time.

roughly ¾ into the current fiscal year some numbers are “high” (heating, etc.) while 
others are “low” (several renewals that have not been billed yet).  This is due to 
seasonal and renewal date variations and are not discrepancies but normal.. 
 Collections 
 Discussed per report. 
 Building/Facilities 
 Discussed per report.

sponsor a re-dedication ceremony of the LPL garden after completion of program.

really trying to engage the Lunenburg youth in “star gazing”. 
 Technology 
 Discussed per report. 
 (Technology, continued)  Re: Network assessment complete – all network cabling 
(CAT-5E, Ethernet) has been electrically categorized, cataloged and marked.
