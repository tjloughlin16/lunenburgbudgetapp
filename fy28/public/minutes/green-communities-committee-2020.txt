for her and staff. We could probably use Fitchburg’s proposal, and a template to adapt for

DB shared a doc from a contractor providing a cold call estimate.  (TANCO?)  Their estimates

ours.   
 
Going back to slides and costs/savings- additional discussion was had about changing the 15-
year savings to 20 years- it would be over a million.  DS -MAPC has a 30% grant for the cost of 
retrofitting street lights and Unitil can own.  DB also researched cost of light arm and cost is $44. 
The company was XTRALIGHT.  Also, some of the cost of light fixtures around 100- 129 per 
arm, with different wattages at different heights.   
 
DB asked JL what our final number for savings was.  JL said it's in the report but 2020 is an 
outlier year. Baseline is 28,827 2019 23221, 2020 17,234MMBtu.  We are almost at 20% 
reduction.  We have $40K of net meter credit against the street light account, if we buy lights, 
we’ll move it to another building.
