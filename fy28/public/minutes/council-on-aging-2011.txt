Lunenburg Council on Aging Meeting Minutes           Minutes Approved at the 2/8/11 COA Meeting     
January 11, 2011 
 
Present : Pete Lincoln (Chairperson), Doreen Noble (Director), Mary Lynn Conrad (Vice Chairperson),  
Jane Doyle, Joyce Wass, Barbara Brown, Sandy Flanagan, Sis Montuori, Jim Hays,  
Deb Seeley and Susan Doherty (Admin. Asst.) 
 
Excused: 
  Fred Crellin 
 
Guests:
  John Male & Jim Halloran 
    Lunenburg High School Principal Mike Barney (Sc heduled for later in meeting) 
 
Welcome 
:  Pete Lincoln, Chairperson, called the meeting to order at 9:34 a.m.  
Pete welcomed John Male and Jim Halloran to the meeting.  
 
Minutes: 
  A motion was made and seconded to accept the December 14, 2010 COA meeting  
minutes as amended.   All approved, motion carried. 
 
Director’s Report: 
  
 
Finance/Informational Report :  
A verbal monthly budget report was given by the Dir ector.  Funds needed to be transferred from the ele ctric line 
to the water line in order to cover the water bill for the 3 
rd  quarter.  All other monthly expenses are on target. 
 
Doreen reported that she had spoken with Jim Breaul t, Facilities Manager, about the unfinished roof re pairs and 
he assured her that the temporary repairs will hold until the roofing company is able to get back to finish the job. 
 
The senior center uses Voice Systems as its telepho ne network.  There were problems with the service d uring last 
summer/fall which the Friends of the Eagle House Inc paid to have fixed. 
An Office Equipment/Repair line will be added into the budget for next year.   
 
The Director has decided that she will not be getti ng an intern this semester from Fitchburg State due  to time 
restraints. 
 
The pancake breakfast which was scheduled for 1/12/11 has been cancelled by Lion’s member  
Dave  Berthiaume, due to a pending snow storm.   
A discussion ensued about last summers grill reques t.  Barbara Brown informed the board that at the De cember 
Lions Club meeting a procedure was put in place sta ting that there would be no free gas grill loans to  any 
organizations.  She also said that Dave Clements sa id at the meeting that the charcoal grill was offer ed to the 
senior center for free.  Sue Doherty, who was in co ntact with Dave Berthiaume about the grill request,  stated that 
she was unaware of any offer of that nature. 
COA Board member Jim Hays asked to move this discussion. 
 
Doreen has withdrawn her name from the Director’s p osition at the Pepperell COA.  Town Manager Kerry 
Speidel has offered her a salary increase, competit ive with what Pepperell had offered her, if she sta ys in 
Lunenburg to which Doreen agreed.  The increase wil l take effect as of July 1, 2011. The COA board 
congratulated her on the salary increase. 
 
Senior Citizen Property Tax Work-Off:   New applications for the property tax work-off pr ogram have been developed 
and applicants have been picking them up at the Eagle House office.  They hope to fill 14-16 positions this year. 
A question about liability for volunteers was raise d.  There is a “Volunteer Service Agreement” which is on file at the 
Eagle House that includes a section on liability.  A copy will be brought to the February meeting. 
 
 
 
 
===PAGE 2===
Old Business:  
 
Parking Lot/CDBG Grant:  Shelly Hatch from MRPC had delivered the completed grant to the Eagle House the week 
prior to this meeting.  Shelly stated that she feared  bonus points may have been lost in the process due to the fact that she 
had not received all of the information needed from Lunenburg officials at town hall.  She had also suggested that Senator 
Jen Flannagan and State Rep. Jen Benson be contacted about supporting this project by speaking to HUD directly at the 
end of February.  The Grant announcement will be made sometime in April 2011. 
 
New Business: 
   
 
Mike Barney Ace/Achieve Program:   Lunenburg High School Principal Mike Barney was in attendance to discuss a 
Memorandum of Understanding in regards to the ACE/ACHIEVE students from the High School concerning 
communication and health/safety guidelines while they are volunteering at the Eagle House Senior Center.  He has 
researched the Center of Disease Control for ideas and would like to arrange a meeting with a COA board member to 
develop the Memorandum of Understanding.  In the mean time, the ACE/ACHIEVE program will not continue at the 
Eagle House until this process is completed and signed by Mike Barney and Doreen Noble. 
 
Committee Reports: 
  The COA Finance Committee had met at 9AM before t he COA meeting and will reconvene after 
this meeting has ended. 
Pete Lincoln reminded all sub-committees that they need to begin dealing with end of year committee re ports.  He also 
reminded the Board Development committee that they should  be actively looking for a new COA board member. 
 
Correspondence: 
  Pete Lincoln read a letter which was sent to the Town Manager written by COA board member Jim 
Hays concerning the town furloughs which were taken last spring. 
 
General Discussion: 
  Jim Halloran, who is a member of the Friends of the Eagle House Inc. was present and was 
concerned about the fact that a new Chairperson for the Friends group has not been selected yet.  Doreen said that she 
would like to give the Friends group guidance in this matter.  Jim Hays and Jim Halloran told her that they did not want 
her to serve as a consultant. 
 
Discussion was held on what the procedure would be, should it snow and the building was closed on COA meeting day. 
A motion was made and seconded that if the building is closed due to a snow day, on a scheduled COA meeting day, the 
meeting will then take place at the same time, on the following Tuesday. 
All approved, motion carried. 
 
Both Debra Seeley and Mary Lynn Conrad will not be in attendance at the February COA meeting. 
 
Upcoming Events 
:  See attached agenda. 
 
Adjournment: 
 A motion was made and seconded to adjourn the meeting.   
All approved, and the motion was carried.    
 
Meeting adjourned at  10:55AM 
 
Respectfully Submitted, 
Susan Doherty, Administrative Assistant

February 8, 2011 
 
Present : Pete Lincoln (Chairperson), Doreen Noble (Director), Fred Crellin, Jane Doyle, Sandy Flanagan,  
Sis Montuori, Jim Hays, and Susan Doherty (Admin. Asst.) 
 
Excused: 
  Barbara Brown, Joyce Wass, Deb Seeley, & Mary Lynn Conrad 
 
Welcome 
:  Pete Lincoln, Chairperson, called the meeting to order at 9:34 a.m.  
 
Minutes: 
  A motion was made and seconded to accept the January 11, 2010 COA meeting  
minutes as presented.   All approved, motion carried. 
 
Director’s Report: 
  
 
Finance/Informational Report : A new vendor, Trans Canada, has been added by the  town to help offset the cost of 
electricity from Unitil.  Trans Canada has been inc luded in the COA vendor list and funds have been pr ocured from the 
Unitil line to cover any bills coming from Trans Canada. 
 
A copy of the Director’s proposed FY12 budget was distributed to members for review and discussion. 
 
Doreen reported that Mike Barney, Principal of Lunenburg High School, has developed a Memorandum of Understanding 
concerning the ACE/ACHIEVE students.  One of the ma jor concerns was sending students to the Eagle Hous e when they 
were ill.  It was decided that if there was a conce rn, the student would be sent to the school nurse f or evaluation.  The 
Director of the Senior Center reserves the right to  “veto” the nurse’s decision of sending a student, if she feels that he/she 
is not well enough to be here. 
 
The roof of the Eagle House has been repaired altho ugh there are still extensive leaks above the foyer  due to ice dams.  
Doreen said that Jim Breault, Facilities Manager, h as worked diligently shoveling the roof and removin g snow from the 
sidewalks at the center but unfortunately it is jus t the “nature of the beast” during this snowy winte r that these problems 
are ongoing. 
There is also a leak in the vent over the stove in the new kitchen which is being looked at. 
 
Old Business: 
 
 
Parking Lot:  Shelly Hatch from MRPC has retired and Glen Eaton will now be in charge of the Eagle House parking lot 
grant.  Doreen stated that phone calls to local politicians asking for their support of the grant should be made at the end of 
February.   The Grant announcement will be made sometime in April 2011. 
 
Memorandum of Understanding ACE/ACHIEVE PROGRAM:  Discussed under Director’s report. 
 
New Business: 
   
 
Conflict of Interest Law: Doreen informed the board that every board member needs to take the state “Conflict of Interest 
Law” test online. 
 
City of Brookline Resource Guide:   Pete Lincoln received the City of Brookline’s Resource Guide and shared it with 
board members.  It included useful agency information for elders in that area.  Doreen said that we also have the “Elder 
Resource Guide” with similar information in it whic h is put out by the Central Mass Agency on Aging. 
 
Sub:Committee Reports: 
   
 
The COA Advocacy Sub-Committee was scheduled to mee t this day at 9AM but there were not enough members  in 
attendance for a quorum.  Another meeting will be scheduled for March 8, 2011 at 9AM.

specifically the title change of CAFO to Town Manager.

Description can be obtained. 
 
Correspondence:

Lunenburg Council on Aging Meeting Minutes     Minutes Approved at the April 12, 2011 COA Meeting 
March  8, 2011 
 
Present : Pete Lincoln (Chairperson), Mary Lynn Conrad (Vice Chairperson), Doreen Noble (Director), Barbara Brow n, 
Fred Crellin, Jane Doyle, Sandy Flanagan, Jim Hays, Sis Montuori, Deb Seeley, Joyce Wass,  
and Susan Doherty (Admin. Asst.) 
 
Guest:
  Lloyd Carlson, President Friends of the Eagle House Inc. 
 
Welcome 
:  Pete Lincoln, Chairperson, called the meeting to order at 9:34 a.m.  
 
Minutes: 
  A motion was made and seconded to accept the February 8, 2010 COA meeting  
minutes as presented.   All approved, motion carried. 
 
Director’s Report: 
  
 
Finance/Informational Report :  Doreen informed the COA board that all monthly e xpenses are being paid on time and 
that the mileage payments for the Meals on Wheels drivers are up to date. 
 
Dr. Julia Byers, a Professor from Leslie College ha d requested with Doreen doing an “Ambassador to Imm igrants” 
program with the senior citizens at the Eagle House which would involve matching them up with new immigrants to show 
them how the American culture works along with the social mores of our society.  Doreen told her that she did not think 
that the program would be appropriate with our peop le at the Lunenburg Senior Center considering that many of the 
immigrants are in the Boston area and have no trans portation.  It would also be difficult for our seni ors to drive to Boston 
to participate.  Dr. Byers also suggested a Positiv e Aging/Mental Health program which would involve u sing Skype on 
the computer.  This will be looked into again. 
 
Lunenburg Public Access Television will be filming a monthly “Eagle House Calendar” with Doreen which will be aired 
on the local public access station.  They have alre ady filmed the Boston Post Cane presentation which currently can be 
seen on the computer on You Tube.  Pete Lincoln sug gested looking into purchasing full size Boston Pos t Cane replicas 
which can be given to recipients along with the stick pin replica which they currently receive. 
Other ideas are being developed with them such as a  “Whalom Park Memories” program and hopefully a “Ve teran’s” 
program. 
 
Budget Presentation:   Copies of the FY12 Council on Aging budget were d istributed to COA members for review.  This 
is the same budget that was previously presented to  the Town Manager. Doreen went through each page wi th the board 
and told them that the Town Manager has suggested a t 12.74 % increase in the COA budget.  The budget w ill need to be 
finalized by voters at the Lunenburg May Town meeting.  The board congratulated Doreen on a job well done. 
 
Old Business: 
 
 
Parking Lot:  Doreen has contacted MRPC and spoke with Glen Eaton who is now the person in charge of the Eagle 
House parking lot grant.  He informed her that the grant is still under review and that announcements should be made in 
April but could come as late as May.  He suggested that now is the time to contact legislators and ask for support of this 
grant.   
Lloyd Carlson, President of the Friends of the Eagle House Inc, was present and asked if the “Friends”  would need to 
contribute anymore funds towards this project besides the $15,000 which they have already contributed.  He was told that 
if the Friends could continue to fund the project for “unexpected” expenses that it would be very bene ficial. 
 
Conflict of Interest Law:  All board members were reminded that they need to complete the  “Conflict of Interest Law” 
test online. 
 
 
 
 
 
===PAGE 2===
 
New Business: 
   
 
Friends of the Eagle House:   Lloyd Carlson has returned as President of the Friends of the Eagle House Inc and informed 
the board that there would be a meeting of the group on Wednesday March 9, 2011 at the Eagle House at 10AM. 
 
Article for Van Revolving Account:   Doreen had written a grant to hopefully procure a new van for the senior center and 
town of Lunenburg.  If this becomes a reality then an account will need to be set up with the town in order to fund the 
vehicle for maintenance, fuel, etc.  This account would be different then the MART revolving account which already 
exists. 
A motion was made and seconded to establish a revolving account to support a new van with funds not to exceed 
$3500.00.  All approved, motion carried. 
 
Sub Committee Reports: 
   
 
There was a brief discussion about the COA Director’s Evaluation.  It was agreed that the Director’s E valuation should be 
based upon the new job description which has not been made available yet as the Personnel Committee is still working on 
them. 
 
The COA ByLaw Committee will meet at 9AM on April 12, 2011 at the Eagle House. 
 
Pete Lincoln asked the COA Board Development committee to prepare a slate of officers for FY12 to be reviewed. 
 
Correspondence: 
  An announcement for a “Texas Hold Em” tournament and BBQ Buffet to benefit the  
Chester Mossman Teen Center was read. 
 
General Discussion: 
   Doreen invited board members to stay for the Fat Tuesday lunch and said that the staff members 
had prepared some Cajun food for sampling. 
 
Deb Seeley informed the board that she is now officially an elder and that she had recently turned “60 ” years of age. 
 
Doreen reminded everyone that the Lunenburg Turkey Hill Family Lion’s Club will be holding a pancake breakfast at the 
Eagle House on Wednesday March 9, 2011 from 8-11AM. 
 
Mary Lynn Conrad informed the board that “Conrads R estaurant” had purchased a large gas grill for the business last 
season which they will not be using this year and they would like to donate it to the senior center. 
 
Upcoming Events 
:  See attached agenda. 
 
Adjournment: 
 A motion was made and seconded to adjourn the meeting.   
All approved, and the motion was carried.    
 
Meeting adjourned at 10:55AM 
 
Respectfully Submitted,

Lunenburg Council on Aging Meeting Minutes       Minutes Approved at the 9/13/11 COA Meeting  
August 16, 2011 
 
Present : Pete Lincoln (Chairperson), Mary Lynn Conrad (Vice Chairperson), Doreen Noble (Director),  
Barbara Brown, Fred Crellin, Jane Doyle, Sandy Flanagan Sis Montuori, Deb Seeley, Joyce Wass,  
and Susan Doherty (Admin. Asst.) 
 
Excused: 
  Jim Hays 
 
Guest:
  Lloyd Carlson, President Friends of the Eagle House Inc.  
 
Welcome 
:  Pete Lincoln, Chairperson, called the meeting to order at 9:36 a.m.  
 
Minutes: 
  A motion was made and seconded to accept the June 14, 2011 COA meeting  
minutes as presented.   All approved, motion carried. 
 
Director’s Report: 
  
 
Finance/Informational Report :  There was no Finance/Information Report for this month. 
 
Doreen apologized for the delay in the start of thi s meeting.  She explained that the hot water heater  which is located in 
the upstairs utility room had broken in July and fl ooded the offices, bathrooms, and janitor’s closet.   Work has begun on 
repairing the walls and carpets in these areas and due to this fact activities and office personnel ha ve to be juggled around 
which makes it confusing at times.   
All programs with 10 or more participants have been cancelled until the work is complete due to the fact that there is only 
1 bathroom available to the public to use. 
The completion of the Executive Office of Elder Aff airs annual report and formula grant has also been delayed due to the 
water heater upheaval. 
 
There have been questions about the new hours of op eration for the Eagle House senior center, whether they are just for 
summer or will they be permanent.  Doreen explained  that this is a permanent change and that they were  put in place for 
the “younger’ senior citizen who perhaps is still w orking and may need assistance later in the day. 
 
During the heat wave in July the Eagle House was de signated as a “cooling station” for the town for tw o days.  The first 
day, which was a Thursday, there were no calls from  the public for assistance, although the center rem ained open until 
7PM.  The next day the Director had a sick day and told the staff that if there were no calls for assi stance, then close the 
center at the normal hour of 12:30PM, and that she would be on call with the Lunenburg Fire Dept dispatcher. 
A sixty-one year old woman called 911 at around 1PM  and said she needed assistance.  Doreen arrived to  find the person 
at the center.  The person did not seem extremely distressed and was more interested in what kind of “ senior services” she 
could receive.  The person stayed until 5:40PM. 
Doreen has decided to develop “guidelines” for stay ing open later for these types of situations.   She  feels that there needs 
to be a “real” concern about the health of an indiv idual in order to justify using the center for a cooling station.   
 
Old Business: 
 
 
Parking Lot:  The Block Grant for the Eagle House parking lot/housing rehab in Lunenburg was denied by the 
Department of Housing and Community Development.  All concerned parties who have been involved with this project 
have been notified. 
The Council on Aging members strongly wish to continue pursuing this project and will look into developing different 
avenues to help this come to completion. 
There is an immediate concern with the parking situation as it stands.  School will be open soon and the students, who do 
not want to pay for parking at the school, tend to take up all of the spots in the municipal lot above the senior center.  The 
idea of using Friends of the Eagle House parking funds to build two stair cases with railings at the upper municipal lot, 
which would lead down to the Eagle House, were discussed.  If this were to happen, the town would be approached to 
place signage with parking restrictions in that lot, and the existing spots at the Eagle House lot would then become 
handicapped only. 
===PAGE 2===
 
 
 
 
New Van Update:   Doreen received a letter from MART stating that the new van has arrived and as soon as paperwork is 
completed it will be ready for delivery to us.   
The title for the old van will be turned over to the Town of Lunenburg and will be operated under the auspices of the 
Council on Aging.  COA board member Jane Doyle suggests that all agreements with MART concerning the two vans 
should be received in writing and kept on file at the Eagle House. 
 
Volunteer Appreciation Day:  Due to the water heater issues and the bathroom shut downs it was decided to change the 
date of the Volunteer Appreciation Day to Tuesday September 27, 2011.   Mary Lynn Conrad was thanked for the grill 
which Conrad’s Restaurant had donated to the Eagle House Senior Center. 
 
Reminder New Hours for Eagle House:   Discussed under Director’s Report. 
 
New Business: 
   
 
RMV Reminder Service:   Jane Doyle reported that the Registry of Motor Vehicles has partnered with a private agency to 
reinstate the RMV reminder service which informs people when their auto registration or license renewal is due.  This 
service can only be initiated “online” and she woul d like to get the word out to the “senior” populati on.  Sue Doherty will 
advertise this in the COA September newsletter, which is mailed to every household in Lunenburg which has a person 
sixty years of age or older living there. 
 
My Senior Center Software:   Doreen reported that the Eagle House Senior Center will be purchasing “My Senior Center” 
software.  The computerized system will allow visitors to the senior center to sign in using a simple swipe card which is 
kept on their keychain.  The system will take the place of the “STAR” system which is used to track st atistics for the state.  
Doreen will be using formula grant funds to purchase the equipment, which is allowed by the Executive Office of Elder 
Affairs. 
 
Sub Committee Reports: 
  There were no Sub Committee reports for this meeting. 
 
Correspondence: 
  There was no correspondence to be read at this meeting. 
 
General Discussion: 
    
 
Doreen informed the board that Liturgical Press cal led and requested use of the Eagle House to do phon e solicitations to 
area businesses to advertise in the Council on Agin g newsletter.  She refused this request but told th em that they could 
stop in while they are working in the area, for coffee, lunch etc. 
 
Olive Coburn came into the meeting with a gift of an electric paper shredder which she donated to the senior center. 
 
Mary Lynn Conrad said that she met with former COA board member, Gloria O’Brien and that she is doing well. 
 
Pete Lincoln asked the board to continue the search  for a new COA board member to fill the eleventh sp ot.  He also 
requested to Doreen that she contact the local cabl e access station and ask them to update the senior center information 
which is taped once a month. 
 
Doreen was asked when the intern from Fitchburg Uni versity would be arriving.  Doreen informed the mem bers that she 
will not be getting an intern this year due to time restraints in her schedule. 
 
Adjournment: 
 A motion was made and seconded to adjourn the meeting.  All approved, motion carried.    
 Meeting adjourned at 11:10AM 
 
Respectfully Submitted, 
Susan Doherty, Administrative Assistant

Lunenburg Council on Aging Meeting Minutes       Minutes Approved at the 1/10/12 COA Meeting 
December 13, 2011 
 
Present : Pete Lincoln (Chairperson), Mary Lynn Conrad (Vice Chair), Jane Doyle, Jim Hays,  
Sis Montuori, Deb Seeley, Joyce Wass, and Susan Doherty (Admin. Asst.) 
 
Excused: 
  Doreen Noble (Director), Sandy Flanagan, Barbara Brown, and Fred Crellin. 
 
Guest:
  Lloyd Carlson and John Male  
 
Welcome 
:  Pete Lincoln, Chairperson, called the meeting to order at 9:30 a.m.  
 
Minutes: 
  A motion was made and seconded to accept the November 8, 2011 COA meeting  
minutes as presented.   All approved, motion carried. 
 
Director’s Report: 
  
 
Finance/Informational Report :  There was no Director’s report at this meeting.  
 
A discussion ensued regarding communication between the Chairperson Pete Lincoln, Director Doreen Noble and  
Town Manager Kerry Speidel, concerning the janitorial and Eagle House septic mound removal issues.   
Sue Doherty relayed a message from Doreen to the bo ard that she had not heard back from the Town Manag er about 
either of these issues.  She said that Doreen had met with Capitol Planning the previous month and that they were in favor 
of connecting the upper parking lot to the Eagle House parking lot.  
Pete said that he had spoken to Doreen and had told  her that in his communication with the Town Manage r, Kerry had 
told him that the DPW would be advertising for a pa rt time janitorial position. She also said that she  had not heard back 
from the Board of Health concerning the mound but that it would be all right to send out for bids for its removal. 
 
Old Business: 
 
 
Parking Lot:   The Friends of the Eagle House have received two bids for the removal of the septic mound.  They voted to 
award the job to Dan Proctor and he would like to start the removal process while the weather is still good.  Lloyd Carlson 
stated that the Friends of the Eagle House Inc would be meeting on December 14 
th  to discuss the funding of the mound 
removal. 
 
Janitor for Eagle House:  Discussed under Director’s report. 
 
New Business: 
   
 
Public Access Eagle House Announcements:   The COA board members were concerned with the  
Eagle House announcements that Doreen tapes monthly for the local cable television station saying that they  
were showing outdated announcements and it makes the COA look bad.  Doreen will be asked to contact the people at the 
cable station about filming new footage which will not be so “date sensitive”. 
 
Director’s Evaluation:   The COA board discussed the issue of how to proceed with the Director’s evaluation. 
A motion was made and seconded to follow the proces s of the Director Evaluation which is in the Counci l on Aging 
Bylaws. 
All approved, motion carried. 
The COA board spoke about perhaps going into Executive Session to discuss this issue after the January 10, 2012  
COA meeting. 
 
Sub Committee Reports: 
   
 
COA board member Joyce Wass reported that the Board Development Committee had met before this meeting and at this 
meeting Sandy Flanagan gave her resignation as a COA board member.  They are actively looking for new people to fill 
vacant spots on the COA board, which they will then recommend appointment to the Board of Selectmen. 
 
===PAGE 2===
The COA Finance Committee has not set a meeting date yet.  They are still awaiting budget information from the Town 
Manager. 
 
 
The COA Advocacy Committee also met before this mee ting where they discussed looking into a new tax ra te for 
Lunenburg citizens over 70 years of age who have lived in the town for 50 years or more. 
 
Correspondence: 
   No correspondence was read at this meeting. 
 
General Discussion: 
    
 
Sue Doherty reported that the new computerized sign  in system is a work in progress and so far things were going well 
with the seniors and their transition to swiping a sign in card for each event. 
 
Adjournment: 
 A motion was made and seconded to adjourn the meeting.  All approved, motion carried.    
 Meeting adjourned at 10:30AM 
 
Respectfully Submitted, 
Susan Doherty, Administrative Assistant
