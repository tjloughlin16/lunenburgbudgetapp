Minutes 
Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Thursday January 10, 2019 
Lunenburg Public Library Conference Room 
1023 Mass Ave 
Lunenburg, Ma. 
7:00 pm 
 
Meeting was called to order by Comm. Tyler at 7:00 pm. Members present were 
Vice-Chair Lorraine Mendoza, Clerk William Tyler and Supt. Todd Holman. 
 
Public Comment made by Dave Passios noting Comm. Tyler’s comments at the 
BOS meeting regarding the service of the late Lance May and also his remarks at 
the Charter Review Committee public hearing. He also suggested that we look at 
participating in Wreaths Across America next year and volunteered to assist in 
the cleanup. 
 
A letter was received from Patricia Potter , who lives in South Carolina, daughter-
in-law of Howard who died in 1985. They have 6 lots in the South Cemetery and 
requested the Commission to buy back the plots. It is our policy not to buy back 
unused lots, but they can be donated back to the Cemetery. We cannot re-sell 
them.  
Clarence Floyd, who knew Sergeant Kilpi, who has a duplicate headstone found 
at the old Cemetery building, stated he has tried to contact his son with no luck. 
It was decided to wait a month or so and then destroy the stone in accordance 
with protocol. 
 
Reorganization- Comm. Mendoza made a motion to nominate Comm. Tyler to 
Chair the Cemetery Commission. Motion was seconded by Comm. Tyler and 
voted unanimously to appoint Comm. Tyler . 
 
In old business, the foundation for the new garage has been completed, and bids 
for the remainder of the construction will be reviewed and discussed at our next 
meeting. Our FaceBook page is up and running and Comm. Tyler will continue to 
post items of interest. The grant for the handicap access ramp and parking lot 
was not approved, the Town Manager stated that we can apply again, and she 
would advise us when it comes time to submit it. 

===PAGE 2===
 
The FY 2019 budget was reviewed, and vouchers were signed totaling 
$14,707.36. After some discussion, it was decided to re-visit the budget at the 
next meeting when we will have a clearer idea of the rest of the expenses for FY 
2019. Geri Grigas was reimbursed $50.00 for overpayment on engraving their 
niche in the Columbarium. 
 
Supt. Holman gave an update on the Fall cleanup and said both Cemeteries are 
in good shape for the Winter . Beaulac Bros. Landscaping was contracted by the 
DPW to finish the leaf cleanup and a bill of $1500.00 was given to the DPW. Supt. 
Holman also stated he is in the process of compiling the burial records for 
submission to the 2018 Town Report.  
 
Next meeting is Tuesday, Jan 22, 2019 at 6:00 pm for discussion and 
recommendation for appointment to fill the late Lance May’s term until the Town 
election in May. One Talent Bank form submitted by Nancy Foss Yasko. 
Commission will recess and reconvene at the Town Hall for a joint vote with the 
BOS on the appointment at 7:15 pm. 
 
Next regular meeting is Thursday, Feb. 14, 2019 at 7:00 pm at the Lunenburg 
Public Library. 
 
With no further items on the agenda, a motion was made by Chairman Tyler to 
adjourn, seconded by Comm. Mendoza. Meeting was adjourned at 9:05 pm. 
 
Respectfully submitted 
Lorraine Mendoza, Vice-Chairman

Minutes 
Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Thursday February 14, 2019 
Lunenburg DPW Building 
520 Chase Rd. Lunenburg, Ma. 
7:00 PM 
                                                   
 
 
 
Meeting was called to order at 7:00 PM by Chairman Tyler, other members present were Lorraine 
Mendoza, Vice-Chair, and Nancy Foss-Yasko, Clerk.  Also present was Todd Holman, Cemetery 
Superintendent. 
 
Minutes were read from meetings of  January 10th and January 22nd, 2019.  Upon a motion by Chairman 
Tyler – to accept the minutes as written, seconded by Vice-Chair Mendoza, minutes were approved 
unanimously.  
 
Commissioner Chairman Tyler, and Vice-Chair Mendoza wanted to welcome Nancy Foss-Yasko – as the 
newest member on the Cemetery Commission Committee.  Nancy was honored to be present and be 
part of  this committee. 
 
Old Business: 
 
Discussion on Cemetery building and what the next steps are.  Commissioner Foss-Yasko asked the 
question why there were not two or three bids proposed to the Cemetery Commission?  Chairman Tyler 
explained that he thought multiple building material quotes were not necessary if  it was under $10,000 
or less.  How do you know if  you have the best deal unless you have something to compare it with?  
Task was given to Chairman Tyler to meet with Lunenburg Town Manager – Heather Lemieux 
regarding the question to Best Practices with bids/proposals.  
 
Discussion on Charter Review Committee- Chairman Tyler is recommending that we pull the submitted 
change to the Charter from the next Charter Review meeting.  Chairman Tyler viewed this deed with 
the Town Counsel, and the land/building belongs to the Cemetery Commission.  The DPW has no 
interest in this building, as this building is intended to store items that will only be used by the 
North/South Cemeteries and Todd Holman, Superintendent. 
 
Brief  discussion on Cemetery Commission Financial Sheet and what is considered as DPW’s 
responsibility regarding maintenance at North/South and Fish Street Cemeteries. 
 
Motion was made to remove submitted change to Charter Review Committee, regarding Town Manager 
control over Cemetery Building.  Vice-Chair Mendoza made motion, and Commissioner Foss-Yasko 
seconded the motion, and with no further discussion the motion passed unanimously. 

===PAGE 2===
 
Superintendent Holman, requested that each Commissioner read the information provided that will go 
into the Lunenburg Town Report.  Commissioners thanked Superintendent Holman for writing this 
article.  Brief  discussions on burials and cremations – there have been 18 full burials and 18 cremations 
at this time.   
 
New Business: 
 
Brief  discussion on 5 year plan and what are some of  the bigger projects that need to be accomplished 
going forward? 
 
Chairman, Tyler explained that Caucus is on March 11, 2019.  Commissioner Foss-Yasko will need to be 
voted on, so that she may be placed on the Town’s ballot in May. 
 
Discussion on the upgrade proposal from Ramaker & Associates – this includes the yearly expense also. 
Superintendent Holman believes that notes from past conversation, this charge will be split between IT 
and the Cemetery Commission.  Chairman Tyler believes that there needs to be clarification on this 
proposal.  Commissioner Foss-Yasko requested that IT should be thinking of  consolidating the town 
with storing important documents to the cloud.  Chairman Tyler and Vice-Chairman, Mendoza all 
agreed that a discussion with IT was needed regarding this matter before moving forward with the 
upgrade. 
 
With no further business before the Commission, a motion was made to adjourn by Chairman, Tyler, 
seconded by Commissioner Foss-Yasko.  With no further discussion, meeting was adjourned at 9:00 PM. 
 
Next meeting will take place on March 7, 2019. 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
Nancy Foss-Yasko, MBA  
Lunenburg Cemetery Commission, Clerk

Lunenburg Cemetery Commission – Meeting Minutes 
Thursday, April 11, 2019 
Lunenburg DPW Building Conference Room 
520 Chase Road, Lunenburg, MA   
 
 
Meeting was called to order at 7:00 PM by Chairman Tyler, other members present were Lorraine 
Mendoza, Vice-Chair, and Nancy Foss Yasko, Clerk.  Also present was the Department of Public 
Works, Director - Jack Rodriquenz, and Todd Holman, Cemetery Superintendent. 
 
Minutes were read from meetings of March 7th and March 21st, 2019.  Upon a motion by Chairman 
Tyler – to accept the minutes as written, seconded by Vice-Chair Mendoza, minutes were approved 
unanimously. 
 
Approve and sign voucher for Arrow (out-house) at the North Cemetery - $1,100.00.  Upon a motion by 
Chairman Tyler – to accept this voucher as stated, seconded by Nancy Foss Yasko, Clerk, voucher was 
approved unanimously.  
 
Commissioner Chairman Tyler started the meeting asking if there had been any new correspondence 
from anyone.  Todd Holman, Cemetery Superintendent, replied stating, “George D. Reichard Family 
requesting the granite signs to be completed in both the South and North Cemeteries.” 
 
Cemetery Commissioners reviewed signage and came up with the following information: 
 
The North Cemetery will read:  First Burial 1770 – Donated by the Reichard Family – in honor of 
George D. Reichard, Former Cemetery Commissioner. 
 
The South Cemetery will read:  First Burial 1728 – Donated by the Reichard Family – in honor of 
George D. Reichard, Former Cemetery Commissioner. 
 
Below the signage will read: 
 
• Dogs are allowed with following rules: 
o Dogs will not be admitted into the Cemetery unless leased. 
o Dogs waste must be picked up and disposed into trash barrels. 
• All trash picked up and disposed into trash barrels. 
• No Alcohol Allowed. 
• No artificial flowers or plants allowed. 
• Nothing on, around or planted at flush makers – only at head stones. 
 

===PAGE 2===
2 | P a g e  
 
 
• No shrubbery or trees may be planted in the Cemetery without the express approval of the 
Cemetery Commission or Superintendent.  The Commission further reserves the right to 
remove trees, shrubs, or any other object that is considers injurious to the functioning or 
appearance of the Cemetery. 
• Automobiles must be driven carefully in the cemetery, while abiding to right of way, and do not 
enter signage. 
• Cemetery Hours – From Dawn until Dusk Only. 
• Full Rules and Regulations available on website. 
• The Cemetery Department maintains on online mapping system for both Cemeteries at 
http://www.map.ramaker.com/ecims/.  
 
Discussion with Jack Rodriquenz, DPW Director – concerning burial policy and procedures.  This 
included an in- depth conversation regarding the possibility of two to three full burials that will occur at 
the same day/time in both the South and North Cemeteries.  
 
Key thoughts: 
 
1. How burials should go going forward. 
2. Have grave site dug at least one to two days before the funeral. 
3. DPW assistance when Todd cannot be at the two cemeteries at the same time. 
a. Jack Rodriquenz – offered his presence if needed in the near future.  Duties may include: 
securing the check and burial certificate. 
b. Bill Tyler – offered his presence if needed in the near future. 
4. Making sure that a representative is present during the funeral and closing the grave site 
afterwards. 
5. Funeral Directors on-site to help with moving the casket to the grave site. 
 
Key discussion on future plans of maintenance/assistance that needs to fall under the DPW 
maintenance budget.  This includes the following: 
 
1. The 150 year old hedges desperately need to be reshaped and trimmed professionally – South 
and North Cemeteries.  
a. Jack Rodriquenz has graciously agreed to research, and speak directly with Lakeview 
Nurseries – May timeframe.  
b. What the next steps are needed going forward with making the hedges look healthy 
again. 
 
2. Review the LUK Program – assistance for Cemetery Superintendent, Todd Holman. 
a. How many hours are needed from June – September, 2019? 
b. What duties are needed to allow Cemetery Superintendent, Todd Holman to do his 
other tasks with both the South and North Cemeteries? 
c. Other towns have at least one other person working the summer months with Cemetery 
Superintendents.  
 
Further discussions regarding Capital Planning and how the Cemetery Commission can proceed.  Jack 
Rodriquenz has generously offered to help in the preparation going forward with the Capital 
Committee.   
 
With no further business before the Commission, a motion was made to adjourn by Chairman, Tyler, 
seconded by Commissioner Foss Yasko.  With no further discussion, meeting was adjourned at 9:00 
PM. 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
Nancy Foss Yasko, MBA
