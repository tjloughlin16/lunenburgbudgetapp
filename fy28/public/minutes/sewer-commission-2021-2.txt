Pond View Commons, LLC 40B discussion- Dave reported he represented the Sewer Commission at the site visit
and Barb attended the 40B workshop, held by Town Counsel. Barb sent the Commissioners the handouts from the
site visit and also the link to the video recording of the workshop. Any comments are due to the Select Board by 8/23.
Dave pointed out that the Commission is just concerned with how they will be getting sewer and utilities to the 40B
site. There are no engineered drawings as of yet. Barb screen shared a list of comments she put together with Rob.
The Commission reviewed the list and discussed the fact that the project is not in the Sewer Service Area. Dave
noted the sewer will be put in by the developer and they need to determine who will pay for the upkeep of the system.
Rob added once the application is approved, they will get engineered drawings and the project will be submitted to
the Zoning Board. Dave asked the Commissioners to send any additional comments to Barb. Mike N. noted the
developer will probably want to include the future development of 120 other houses. He added right now the flow to
Leominster is cheaper and Dave pointed out they will need to consider this volume when negotiating the IMA. One
option could be looking at Townsend, but Rob said Townsend has no sewer. John asked if Leominster or Fitchburg
are aware of this project and Barb replied that she had not communicated from this office yet. Dave also noted the
project has several 2- and 3-bedrooms apartments, which could add a lot to the flow. Mike asked if either city gets to
say 'no' to the flow. Barb replied that Town Counsel said since they have IMAs, a 40B project does not have any
sway over contracts. Also Leominster & Fitchburg will need to approve the flow and plans. John asked how many
pump stations would be needed. Rob replied it would probably be multiple pump stations (2-3) and may require
additional upgrades to the system. He added that they should seek additional financial contributions, beyond user
fees, to maintain the stations. Also since the area is not in the Sewer Service Area, the town should not be on the
hook for extra cost. Rob noted that since this is residential, the flow metering should be based on the water use not
metering for outflow as discussed for Bright Farms. Barb said that Emerald Place and Whalom Luxury Apartments do
not have pump stations and that the metering is done by building. Dave suggested installing a Grease Trap of some
sort, but Rob said they can't separate the grease from other waste on residential developments.

Sewer Commission agrees, he will send it out to the state DEP. He added that the drawing shows the connection
going from 384 and crossing the property at 362, but the town does not allow easements. The Commission still
needs to discuss this and he suggested making the property owners grant an easement. Dave suggested putting it
on the deed and the Commission needs to address the issue. Barb said she will check with Town Counsel. The
Commission discussed and agreed Rob can send the plan to the DEP.
ARPA Monies (American Rescue Plan/Coronavirus Local Fiscal Recovery Fund) - Rob reported there will be about

the money will be shared by the Town. Dave suggested getting input from Al (CTE) and also working with
Leominster on I/l grants.

Minutes of 9/14/21 Sewer Commission meeting- via Zoom (Video Conference)
Attendees: Chair- Dave MacDonald, Vice-Chair- John Reynolds, Clerk- Brett Ramsden, Members: Mike Deveikis and Mike
Nault, DPW Director- Rob Oliva, Business Manager-Barb Lefebvre, Minutes Clerk-Jane Ranley
Dave opened the meeting at 6:00 pm, with the Piedge of Allegiance and did a roll call of attendees.
PUBLIC COMMENT- Dave sadiy reported Peg Gorman of Hickory Hills passed away. She was a dear friend, a big
supporter of the Lion's Club and loved watching the Sewer Commission meetings. She will be greatly missed.
ANNOUNCEMENTS - Barb noted the Town Manager sent out an email regarding signing up for automatic notifications
when any minutes and agendas are posted or updated. Also the Q1 bills are out and are due at the end of next week. If
anyone has not received their bill, they should call the office. Barb reported that major work had been done due to grease
buildup in the pipes and she reminded all customers not to dump grease down the drains or toilets. The 9/28 meeting will be
via Zoom as the Select Board will be using the Town Hall meeting room. Discussion followed on assessing a fine for

Health.

Maintenance Report- DPW Director, Rob Oliva reported Clean Basins has augured the line on Mass Ave. which they
thought was a grease build-up, but the technician said it looked like joint compound. The line is now freed up and running
clean. CTE would like to replace the panel at the Mass 2 station and the cost will be about $5K- the same cost as the
Electric Ave. panel, so they will go ahead to do that. Rob received an estimate from CTE to replace the impeller, gasket &
seals on the Dana St. station pumps for a cost of $10,900. Since the cost is over $10K, he is asking for the go-ahead from
the Commission.
Motion: Brett R.
Second: Mike D.
To approve the $10,900 for repairs to the Dana Street pump station.
Discussion: Rob noted they will be repairing both pumps and this will be the first time they have done minor rebulld since
installation in early 2000. Mike D. asked if they use a megohmmeter to test the motors and Rob replied he will talk to CTE
about it. Brett asked if there is a life expectancy for the bearings. Rob said he will ask AI (CTE) and check if this is part of the
Maintenance Plan. Dave suggested revisiting the Maintenance Plan every couple years. Mike D. asked about a spare motor
for Dana St. Mike N. added preventative maintenance is good and they should have spare parts on hand.
Roll Call vote: Mike D.-aye, Mike N.-aye, Brett-aye and Dave-aye.
ARPA funds - Rob reported there has been no further discussion or updates.
Assistant to the Business Manager - Rob reported, in discussion with Heather (Town Manager), the assistant position
may need to be more than 19 hrs. and should offer benefits. He suggested changing the position to 24 hrs./week with
benefits to attract qualified candidates. They would need to move forward with an amendment to the budget at the Fall Town
Meeting. Barb explained that the estimated cost for the 7 months of FY22 would be $14,400 (salary) and $14,000 (benefits)
for a total of $28,400. They have budgeted $19,600 and would need to add approximately $8,640 to the budget. Mike N.
calculated the yearly cost would be about $49,000. The Commission discussed the costs and Rob added the goal is to hire
someone who can take over when Barb retires. In the future, they could adjust the assistant position back to 19 hours. Dave
suggested being flexible with the hours and the Commission agreed the hours should be fixed. Barb agreed with hiring an
assistant for 24 hours/week and noted that, because of different tasks throughout the fiscal year, it would be good to give
that person a full year to learn the job.
Motion: Brett R.
Second: John R.
To approve the suggestion of Barb and Rob to hire an assistant for 24hrs/week with benefits.
Roll Call vote: Mike D. aye, Mike N.-aye, John R.-aye, Brett-aye and Dave-aye. •

$14,000 be included from the general fund for indirect cost, to allow for addition of a benefited 24 hour/week position. Dave

Minutes of 9/28/21 Sewer Commission meeting- via Zoom (Video Conference)
Attendees: Chair- Dave MacDonald, Vice-Chair-John Reynolds, Clerk- Brett Ramsden, Members: Mike Deveikis and Mike
Nault, DPW Director- Rob Oliva, Minutes Clerk- Jane Ranley
Absent: Business Manager-Barb Lefebvre
Dave opened the meeting at 6:06 pm. and did a roll call of attendees. The Commission began the meeting by saying the
Pledge of Allegiance.
PUBLIC COMMENT- none
ANNOUNCEMENTS- Dave announced the 1s quarter bills were due 9/27. If anyone has past due amounts, they need to
pay them or it will be added to your December tax bill, if they remain unpaid.
APPOINTMENTS:
Parks Commission - Marshall Park request- Dave Passios- Rob reported the Parks Commission would like the Sewer
Service Area (SSA) expanded to include Marshall Park and they may do a warrant article for the November Special Town
Meeting. Dave Passios noted the warrant article is the same as the one passed over at the Spring Town Meeting for
Chestnut St. He added the park is about 28 acres and the Parks Commission is starting to plan a renovation of the entire
park. He noted the old bathroom building was torn down and they found during testing the old septic system would not be
useable. They are doing test holes to see if they can put a new septic system in, which is one option the Parks Commission
has for restrooms on the property. Dave P. noted the Board Of Health has money for soil testing. They are looking to get
the park into the Sewer District with a line out to Mass Ave. He added there are pros and cons for both options. Dave M.
said he would want to put Marshall Park in the sewer district so they can have flexibility to go septic or sewer. John asked if
they know that a septic cannot be put in and Dave P. replied a septic could go in as a repair, in the exact same location.
Brett asked how long a repaired system would work and Dave P. guessed about 20 years. John asked how they would
determine the cost of the betterment. Dave M. replied sewer would just be just for the bathrooms, and he would like to add.
sewer connections at every park being used. Rob said he did not know if the regulations had an exemption for a municipal
connection. Right now, they are looking at just adding the park to the SSA. Mike N. said he would like to accommodate the
park but asked if it would have an effect on other properties. Rob noted the vote is to just add it to the SSA- not for a
connection. He added it is good for the community; it will always belong to the town, and it is not developable. Dave P. noted
they are looking for a septic location in order to determine the space needed. With a sewer connection, they could have
multiple lines in the park. John said he supported adding it to the SSA but adding a connection would depend on the
circumstances.
Motion: John R.
Second: Brett R.
To approve the request from the Parks Department to add a special article to the Special Town Meeting to add
Marshall Park to the Sewer District.
Roll Call Vote: Mike N.-aye, Brett-aye, Mike D.-aye, John-aye and Dave-aye.
Dave M. noted Dave P. and Karen are doing a great job on the Parks Commission and Dave P. said he appreciates the
support of the Commission so they can take the article to Town Meeting. (Mike N. & Mike D. left meeting.)
CURRENT BUSINESS -
711 Mass Ave Condo project-Rob stated this is an 8-unit condo project and the site plan was approved by the Planning
Board on 8/23/21. He had added some comments to the plan and the Planning Board approved the plan with conditions-
one being they had to come to the Sewer Commission for final approval. Rob screen shared the approved site plan and
noted they approved it with 8 sewer connections. He does not see any problems but asked the Commission to be aware of
his comments when they do come before them. Rob noted the Whitman & Bingham engineer for the project did not address
his comments, but that may have been an oversight. Rob said he will discuss his comments with them prior to their coming
to the Commission. John asked if this transpired before Whitman & Bingham was sold to Maine engineering firm and Rob
replied it was before the sale.
Upland Ave extension discussion- Rob was contacted by a party that wants to purchase 71 Upland Ave. and would like to
run an E1 pump system down Upland Ave, past the house next door. He added 61 Upland Ave. put in an E1 pump system
and ran the force main for the pump in the shoulder, down Upland to Electric Ave. for the sewer connection. The person
looking to buy 71 Upland would like to do the same thing, but Rob told him they cannot continue to run multiple force mains
down the shoulder. Rob suggested looking at making sewer available to everyone on the road. Jim Garreffi (Board of

Minutes of 12/21/21 Sewer Commission meeting- via Zoom (Video Conference)
Attendees: Chair- Dave MacDonald, Vice-Chair- John Reynolds, Clerk- Brett Ramsden, Member-Mike Nault, DPW
Director- Rob Oliva, Business Manager-Barb Lefebvre, Minutes Clerk- Jane Ranley
Absent: Mike Deveikis
Dave opened the meeting at 6:05 pm. and did a roll call of attendees. The Commission began the meeting by saying
the Pledge of Allegiance.
ANNOUNCEMENTS - Barb noted the bills that were sent out in November were due on 12/13. If anyone has not
received theirs, they should call the Collectors to get a payment in because the demands have already been
processed. Barb reported that all 3 sewer-related articles were passed at the Special Town Meeting on 11/16. The
articles were: budget article to increase hours for the Assistant position, the transfer to the Capital Reserve
Stabilization Fund and the Parks Commission's request for inclusion of Marshall Park in the Sewer Service Area.
Barb said the delinquencies from the prior fiscal year have been processed and transferred to the owners' tax bills.
John asked it there were many in that category and Barb replied there is $30,000 in back payments due, which was
less than previous years.
PUBLIC COMMENT- Dave asked Rob for an update on Marshall Park and Rob replied a landscape architect firm
has been hired. They are organizing public meetings for public input, and they will come up with some conceptual
plans. The plans will be reviewed by the Parks Commission with input from the public, to come up with a refined plan.
John congratulated Dave on a great job on the Salvation Army boot drive. Dave thanked all that participated and
noted they have served 1200 families. Mike Nault added it was nice to see Michael Ray and Todd Dwyer at the drive.
Dave noted they collected tons of toys and helped a lot of kids that day.
CURRENT BUSINESS -
DPW Director Report- Rob noted that the Commission was sent the RFP for preliminary sewer design for
Upland/Eastern Ave, and White St. He received proposals from Wright Pierce, Tate & Howard, Weston & Sampson
and Woodard & Curran. They were all fairly similar. Wright Pierce bid $13,200, Tate & Howard bid $19,800, Weston
& Sampson bid $42,000 and Woodard & Curran bid $15,000. Based on Wright Pierce's proposal for soil exploration
with 21 probes to search for ledge on Upland & Eastern, Rob would recommend going with Wright Pierce, as the low
bidder and their proposal was exactly what he asked for.
Motion: Brett R.
Second: Mike N.
To accept Rob Oliva's recommendation to use Wright Pierce for the preliminary design.
Discussion: John agreed with using Wright Pierce but asked if the cost would be included in the project. Barb
replied it would be included in the cost, as long as the project goes forward. Dave suggested having some of these
engineers come in to meet the Commission in January. Rob agreed and wanted to introduce Tatum & Howard as a
new resource that he is familiar with. He also noted Wright Pierce was very interested in working with the town again
and they have a new associate on staff who is very knowledgeable on sewer. Dave added he would like to make a
commitment in 2022 to work on expansion and customer service.
Roll Call Vote: John-aye, Brett-aye, Mike N.-aye and Dave-aye.
Rob also reported they have completed work on Elizabeth St. I/l and have filled joints with 7 gallons of chemical
grout. He received a video of their inspections but will not be getting a report. Rob noted Barb reached out to
Fitchburg and Leominster to set up a meeting with the heads of the waste-water divisions. There is a new person in
Fitchburg and hopefully they will get together after the holidays. Rob reported that he received the as-built for 384
Mass Ave. and had submitted it to Mass DOT. Because the as-built showed work was not done in the right of way, he
asked to withdraw the permit app. The DOT obliged and the project has been closed out.
American Rescue Plan Act (ARPA) funds (Coronavirus Local Fiscal Recovery Fund) - Barb reported the federal
government has approved funds but they are waiting on the state. Dave asked Barb to add compiling an ARPA funds
wish list to the January agenda. Rob noted the biggest needs would be the pump stations and the Commission
agreed. Dave said he is concerned about the flow meters and would like to discuss upgrades with Rob. Rob noted
that they recently have done 10 more manholes and have addressed the most critical ones. John suggested doing
===PAGE 2===
more investigation on I/l (Inflow/Infiltration). Dave reiterated he still would like to sit down with Leominster and would
not want to spend any more money on studies- they need to get solutions. Rob agreed they can go back to the
studies for suggestions/recommendations.
Grease Trap Policy- Dave noted he talked to the new Lunenburg Diner owner about the grease trap fees, who
questioned why he needed to pay someone to inspect the traps. Barb replied originally the fees were to pay for the
cost for inspections and the fees were less if they are compliant. The Commission discussed the grease trap policy,
fees and inspections. Barb noted that there is a lot of administrative time spent tracking compliance. Rob agreed
there is still a cost to implement the policy and an annual fee is appropriate. Mike pointed out the fees were put in
place so the restaurants would take the policies seriously and maintain the grease traps. Dave suggested talking to
the Chair of the Board of Health. He added that they should charge large fees for dumping grease. John noted if they
have large fees, they need to follow through. The Commission agreed to discuss the grease traps at a future
meeting. Barb noted that Jane also puts in time, helping her with entering logs and 1st Tuesday reports. She added
that with the Fitchburg Chapter 147 changes, they will be looking at several policies and will add the Grease Trap
Policy to the list so that all can be taken care of in the same public hearings. Barb stated for the record that the
Annual fees for Grease Traps are: Internal trap - $150 if compliant, $ 350 if non-compliant, External Interceptor -
$450 if compliant, $ 650 if non-compliant. Barb confirmed that all fees have been waived for the last 2 years by the
Commission. 1st Tuesday report-all logs are in for November. Barb added that the New restaurant, Lunenburg
Diner, has submitted all that was required.
Victuallers Licensing with Select Board- Barb has communicated with the Select Board that all are compliant, and
she is just waiting for the external pump out report from A&D Crossroads Plaza. She received the invoice, which
confirmed it was done, but is just waiting for a report.
Fitchburg Sewer Use Ordinance-Barb noted that Rob mentioned Jeff Murawski has left Fitchburg and Tony
Murrasa is interim. Adam Costa (Town Counsel) has not submitted his recommendations yet. He is answering from a
legal standpoint, but said some questions need to be answered by someone with technical knowledge - possibly a
consultant. After Adam responds, they can discuss if they need to be reviewed by a consultant or just sent on to
Fitchburg.
Q2 Commitment of $266,302.46 - Barb asked for a verbal approval for the Q2 Commitment.
Motion: John R.
Second: Brett R.
To accept the Q2 Commitment for $ 266,302.46.
Roll Call Vote: John-aye, Brett-aye, Mike N.-aye and Dave-aye.
Commissioners will come into sign.
FY22 YTD Budget - Barb sent out the budget for review and noted they are right on target. Revenues are ahead
(54%) and expenses are right where they should be. Barb confirmed that Fuel is good, and the pump stations were
just topped off for the winter. She has not received the bill yet.
Transfers to Delinquent on FY22 Real Estate taxes $30,593.35 - Barb noted she worked with the Assessor's and
Collector's offices to move any FY21 Sewer delinquencies to the FY22 Real EstateTax bills.
Minutes of 11/9 meeting review/approval- will be put on the next agenda.
Business Manager Report - Invoices- Fitchburg: $15,498.33 (9/26-10/25/21) and $ 18,978.61 (10/26-11/25/21)
and Leominster: $119,387.06 (6/26-9/25/21). All invoices are based on flow meter readings and the Commission
agreed with paying them. John asked Barb if she has an updated billing comparison spread sheet and Barb replied
she needs to do biling algorithm first. She added that Leominster billing for the same period in 2020 was $74,265
but noted that this had been a very rainy summer.
FY23 Budget and tentative 3/24/22 FinCom appointment- Barb received a tentative date from Heather for an
appointment with FinCom on 3/24/22. Dave suggested working on the budget in mid-January and noted that it was a
good presentation last year.

Assistant to Business Manager - Barb reported they are trying to get the position re-posted but has not had an
update from Julie (Asst. Town Manager). Barb read a memo draft to Julie from the Commission, asking to post the

Roll Call Vote: John-aye, Mike N.-aye, Brett-aye, and Dave-aye.
Action Items: Dave said they will discuss the budget at 1/25 meeting.
Public Comment - The Commission wished everyone a Merry Christmas and Happy New Year.
