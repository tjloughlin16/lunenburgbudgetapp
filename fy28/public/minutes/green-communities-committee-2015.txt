Minutes for 1/20/2015
Present: D. Blatt, A.Burney, M.Conway, J.Londa, McQuaid, N. Sullivan (at 7:15 PM)
Observers: Maryellen Ramstack, Dick Mailloux (Library), Venezia (Library), Jay Simeone (Finance)
1. Meeting called to order at 6:32 P.M. The Minutes from 12/23/14 were approved.
2. Public Safety and Library Project Update- J. Londa obtained 3 quotes for both: Guardian $16,683,
N.E. Electric $17,300 and Di-Rock $9,800. All are retro-fits of existing fixtures, new timers were not
included. The bid will go to Di-Rock and have them look at the timer replacement at the same time as
the exterior lights.
3. Library, New Interior Project: D.Blatt asked the Library Trustees to look at the current interior light-
ing configuration to see when lights are needed to be on. Fixture changes may be needed especially
up lights in main hall. The Library Trustees said that Guardian looked at the interior lights, only quot-
ing bulb replacement, They also noted that the heating system is problematic. K. Walker needs to
check it daily to make sure it is running. The 10 yr old furnace may need replacing as well as the
building energy management system. J. Londa noted that it would cost approximately $70,000 to re-
place it. The same company installed the energy system at the Primary school and he's till chasing
problems 10 years later. D. Mailloux said to never worked well. The GCTF will review the quotes
and Return on Investment (ROL) once available and submit them to the DOER.
4. Eagle House- Interior Lighting: D. Blatt made a survey of the existing lights noting that the office fix-
tures seem to be in the wrong position for best lighting of work stations. J. Londa has access to a
light meter to test lumen output. J.McQuaid reminded them to check with Unitil for rebates. D. Blatt
noted that the Town Manger needs to contact MassSave for an energy assessment for attic insulation.
A. Burney noted that the work does not need to go out to bid but we will need to use a Unitil ap-
proved vendor.
5. Solar By Law - A. Burney said that the Planning Board will not even have it on their agenda until
March. It won't be ready for Town Meeting until Late 2015 or Spring 2016.
6. GCTF webpage - J. McQuaid noted that the content is 2-3 years old, as well as the information on the
cable access page. The Members need to review the material and make suggestions for changes.
===PAGE 2===
7. New items - J.L.onda updated us on the Baseline. Another Quarterly Report is due soon. He will start
the draft copy. The photo with the Green Community road signs is still needed. (They still have not
located so new ones may need to be ordered.) A questionnaire for the DOER also needs to be com-
pleted.
8. D. Blatt meet with the Town Manager. A suggestion was made that the GCTF come under the direc-
tion of the TM so a quorum is not necessary for meetings. The Energy Policy still needs to be ap-
proved as part of 20% Reduction plan. The GCTF discussed the need for a new mission now that we
have become a Green Community. What do they want us to do? Will we have any authority to com-
plete projects? A. Burney suggested that maybe they lack knowledge about the projects we are work-
ing on.
9. Re-Organization- D. Blatt noted that he needs to step down as Chair. J.McQuaid submitted her resig-
nation but will stay on temporarily until replacement can be found.
10.Meeting Adjourned at 7:45 PM to join Selectman's meeting. No new meeting date set.
Respectfully Submitted,
Joanne he Said
Joanne McQuaid, Secretary

Minutes for 3/18/2015
Present: D. Blatt, Z. Cutting, J.Londa, McQuaid, N. Sullivan
Observers: Jay Simeone (Finance Committee)
1. Meeting called to order at 6:04 P.M. The Minutes from 1/20/15 were approved.
2. Public Safety and Library Project Update- J. Londa said that Di-Rock submitted the projects to Unitil
and they are awaiting approval to begin work. Z.Cutting suggested getting "Before" pictures.
3. Eagle House- Interior Lighting: J. Londa noted that some new fixtures are necessary because re-
placement bulbs didn't work with the current ballast. Also some need dimmer switches. The project
can proceed in 2 phases: ones with bulbs and ones with fixtures. There is only enough in the Grant to
pay for the bulbs, not an electrician. The electrician will have to put in a bid for the labor. J. Londa
will submit the bid to the vendor. The MassSave rebates will be applied automatically. A motion was
made by D.Blatt to approve the interior lamp replacements minus all the fluorescent tube fixtures
with the DOER Grant money. N. Sullivan seconded. All approved. D.Blatt suggested that Phyllis
Luck of the Select Board and Doreen Noble, the Eagle House Director need to contact Mass Save for
a Energy Assessment prior to the envelope tightening portion of the project. J.McQuaid will follow
up with them.
4. Library, New Interior Project: J.Londa reported that of the 4 timer clocks in place, 2 are out of service
and need to be reactivated. Di-Rock will try to put them back online. Some exterior lights need to be
on from dusk to dawn and others have broken lamps so they can't be tested and are running 24/7 now.
J. Londa felt that if we start woking on the lights we should finish fixing them. He suggested remov-
ing the ballast and replacing the bulbs. Z. Cutting pointed out that replacing the lamps is going out-
side the scope and we won't capture the energy savings. Only $500.00 has been budgeted for the
timers. N. Sullivan pointed out that the Library's input on fixtures is important. He motioned that as
long as Di-Rock is close to the $500.00 budget for T & M that they fix the 2 exit lights by the Chil-
dren's Garden on timers. Second by Z. Cutting. Approved
J. Londa noted that the Library is not currently operating as designed, and is not functioning properly.
After using a light meter he determined that some areas are over lit. J. Simeone suggested we schedule
a workshop with the Library trustees to review current lighting practices.
===PAGE 2===
5. New Projects:
- Possible re-lamping of THMS proposed by J. Londa. The ballast were done in 2009. No new fixtures
will be needed, only lamps and they can be replaced by the staff.
- Replace emergency lights at the Primary School. D. Blatt suggested making 2 projects for it: small
lights and large ones.
- D. Blatt noted that we still need to submit the oil to gas conversion for the Public Safety Building
- J. McQuaid will check with J. Rodriquez for any other potential projects.
- J.LOnda is waiting on a RFP for a 300 kw solar roof installation on the new school (Power Purchase
Agreement) which is about 40% of the energy cost.
6. Meeting adjourned 7:40 P.M. Next meeting TBD.
Respectfully Submitted,
Joanne TeQuaid
Joanne McQuaid, Secretary

Minutes for 5/14/2015
Present: D. Blatt, Z. Cutting, J.Londa, McQuaid, N. Sullivan
1. Meeting called to order at 6:00P.M. The Minutes from 3/18/15 were approved.
2. Project Update-Public Safety exterior lights and Library exterior lights and timers are completed.
The Eagle House is mostly complete; still need to locate a few retrofits to replace some CFL's and
decorative lights. To date $23,457.82 of the first disbursement check ($39,306.25) has been applied
from the Grant total of $157, 225.
Projects Approved but not Completed - THMS envelope tightening in progress, Library bollard lights,
Town Hall lighting retrofit and envelope tightening, Public Safety Building - Gas Conversion Study.
3. New Projects 1) Teen Center - D. Blatt is generating a list of interior and exterior lights. Need to try
one of the LED light bulbs to make sure they work in the existing fixtures. Programmable timers for
the lights and thermostat may be applicable. Noted that insulation is lacking in the building. 2) Li-
brary interior lights 3) Lunenburg Primary - lighting (resubmit) 4) THMS - LED conversion
5) Part-time person to oversee the program and projects. N. Sullivan suggested a collaboration with
the local colleges. Students could collect data to use in projects. He will contact Fitchburg State Uni-
versity to set up a meeting with the group. 6) Z. Cutting proposed solar electric hot water for the
Public Safety Building. 7) Replace emergency lights at the Primary School. 8) Solar-J.Londa submit
ted a RFP for a 300 kw solar roof installation on the new school (Power Purchase Agreement) which
is about 40% of the energy cost.
J. McQuaid will check with J. Rodriquez for any other potential projects. Z. Cutting suggested
combining as many projects as possible into one submission so the ROI will look better for energy
savings.
N. Sullivan proposed and D.Blatt seconded that the next projects submitted should be the Primary
School, THMS and Teen Center. All Approved.
