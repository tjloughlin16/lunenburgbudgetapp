Open Space Ad Hoc Committee
Wednesday, January 8, 2025 – 7:30 PM
Minutes

Members Present: Mike Toohill (Chair), Ana Lockwood, Eric Hinds, Glenn McLeod.
Chairman Toohill called meeting to order at 7:32pm.
September 11, 2024, Meeting Minutes Approval: A. Lockwood moved to approve as presented. G. McLeod seconded. Voted unanimously in favor by roll call.
Committee Reports: G. McLeod reported no updates from Planning Board.
Farmland of Local Importance: M. Toohill describes NRCS program for designating Farmland of Local Importance (FLI), which can increase eligibility for federal farmland preservation funding. M. Toohill notes that there is no cost and little effort required to do so, other than reviewing and confirming land identified as potential FLI by NRCS, and that doing so does not create regulatory obligations or affect property use or valuation. Committee member M. Wilson will present additional details but is not present tonight due to attending Conservation Commission meeting. G. McLeod suggests gathering additional information prior to voting on whether or not to recommend approving, since there is no deadline to do so. The members present concur. 
100 Townsend Harbor Rd: Discussed whether to recommend that town exercise right of first refusal over said property which is being withdrawn from Chapter 61A.  Present members generally agree that the parcel likely does not meet the criteria for acquisition by the town, although all express some regret about potential loss of agricultural space in the town. Further discussion of the challenges created by the OSC not being automatically notified of Chapter 61 withdrawals and frequently having to respond in unrealistically short time frames. As the OSC has not specifically been asked to provide a recommendation, the members present agree to table the discussion and take no action for now.
Public Comment: G. McLeod, in his capacity as a town resident, indicates that the town hall project is moving forward rapidly and that the 30 School St parcel is of interest. G. McLeod recommends that committee members and members of the public become informed about the project.
Next meetings: Next scheduled meetings: February 12 and March 12, 2025. Discussed potential to have some in-person meetings after these two online meetings.
E. Hinds motion to adjourn, A. Lockwood seconded.  Voted unanimously in favor by roll call.  Meeting adjourned at 8:03 pm.
Respectfully submitted,
Eric Hinds
APPROVED at the 2/12/25 Open Space Committee Meeting

Open Space Ad Hoc Committee
Wednesday, May 14, 2025 – 7:30 PM
Minutes

Members Present: Mike Toohill (Chair), Brandon Kibbe (Clerk), Eric Hinds, Glenn McLeod.
Chairman Toohill called meeting to order at 7:33pm.
Committee Membership update:  B. Kibbe announced that he will be resigning his position. M. Toohill reported that Tim Lawless (Parks Commission representative) has also stepped down.  
Approval of Minutes February 12, 2025: G. McLeod motioned, E. Hinds seconded.  Unanimously voted by roll call.
Committee Updates: G. McLeod reported that Planning Board interested in Chapter 61 lands. Open Space Committee may have a joint meeting with the board on June 9.
Open Space Plan Update: State offering a 3-year extension to existing 7-year plans if action item table gets updated and extended.  M. Toohill offered to take the lead. B. Kibbe suggested forwarding to other land use boards for input. M. Toohill suggested Planning, Conservation, Agricultural, Parks, and Select Board together with Town Manager. B. Kibbe recommended reminding boards that having a current, approved Open Space and Recreation Plan makes the town eligible for certain state grants that can be very impactful for achieving town open space and recreation goals, including acquisitions and infrastructure improvements. Maintaining that eligibility should be a prime motivator for other boards to have input if for no other reason. M. Toohill added that updating the open space criteria should also be done as needs and opportunities have changed since first implemented.
Public Comment: No public comment.
Next meetings: June 9, 2025 and July 9, 2025.
B. Kibbe motion, E. Hinds seconded.  Unanimously voted by roll call.  Meeting adjourned at 7:58 pm.
Respectfully submitted,
Brandon Kibbe
