LUNENBURG 300TH ANNIVERSARY COMMITTEE MEEETING MINUTES
FEBRUARY 26,2024  Sudolnik Room, Lunenburg Library 7:00 p.m.
Members Present:  Michelle Belleza, Kathy Comeau, Dawn Johnson, Debbie Lincoln, Pete Lincoln, Tony 
Sculimbrene, Bill Tyler, Liaison Lou Franco
Members Absent:  Laura Brzozoski, Cathy Clark, Lois Filiau, Chris Sullivan
Guest: Ana Lockwood
The meeting was called to order at 7:07 by Chair Pete Lincoln. He stated the general feeling that the 
Committee had been formed prematurely, and probably should not have been organized until 2026. For the 
past two celebrations, teams began meeting three years out. What members can do in the meantime is get 
ideas as to events and places to hold them.
Minutes were approved with the parade date corrected to Sunday, 9/10/2028.
Guest Ana Lockwood presented the idea of a Carnival on Whalom Lakefront, closing Lake Front Avenue. 
Parking could be made available at local businesses and shuttle buses used. Facilities Manager Chris Ruth 
is familiar with ﬁreworks set-ups and has contacts. They would cost $12,000 with barges on Lake Whalom, 
and $6,000 without the barges. We would need to factor cost of the shuttles.
Ana also suggested multiple rides reminiscent of Whalom Park, and perhaps a bouncy house. There are 
vendors who would give us 20% of gross event proﬁts, and pay 50% of ﬁreworks if we held a multi-day 
event. A potential Whalom Day in years leading up to 2028 would help us gauge the event and build up 
anticipation, likely in May with ﬁreworks to begin the tradition.
Ana indicated the biggest park area in town is Northﬁeld Park - 64 acres on the left just before Settlers 
Crossing. Debbie asked if the Selectboard could do a walk through and Lou indicated he would bring it up 
at the next meeting.
COMMITTEE PROGRESS REPORTS
Bill noted the Historical Society is at work on a n update to the “Red Book,” so there is no need for 
Committee involvement at this time.
Dawn said the Lions Club would be available for food booths. 
Kathy said she would work with the Humane Society to set up an Adoption Week.
Pete spoke with a representative from the Woo Sox. Lunenburg can arrange for a Sunday to have a 
Lunenburg Day with a kids run the bases and have the oldest veteran throw out the ﬁrst pitch.
Debbie got the approval from the Cemetery Commission for the Halloween Ghost Walk. It was suggested if 
there was enough interest and resources, there should be walks at both North and South Cemeteries.
Debbie also spoke with Robin Venezia, who oﬀered to spearhead a Colonial Community Garden, with crops 
grown during the 1720s such as ﬂax and potatoes.

===PAGE 2===
NEW IDEAS
Pete suggested Luther Burbank Week, with events such as potato races and a stone marker at the 
Burbank House, and one at the Lunenburg Adult Activity Center where the potatoes were actually grown. 
Dawn mentioned the Lions Club had already talked of having a Potato Festival.
Debbie showed pictures of various styles of historical markers and referred to the Athol Historical Trail, 
which has 24 signs around Athol delineating various homesteads and events. Sign placement could be 
Town Pound, Luther Burbank House, West St. School, Whalom Park, Site of Buttonwood Tree, etc.
Bill brought up the “Ice Out,” where a dummy is placed on Whalom Lake and bets are placed when it will 
fall through the ice. Could also be one on Marshall Pond. 
FUNDRAISING SUGGESTIONS
Michelle suggested namig the “Ice Out” dummy, with prize for the winner
Dawn had looked into what Westminster did for their dogs for the Westminster Cracker sponsorship, but it 
didn’t seem that they made money on it
Dawn  suggested a Facsimile of New Yorker magazine cover made as Lunenburg as a poster.
A motion for adjournment was called for and seconded at 8:39.
The next meeting will be September 9, 2024 at 7:00 at the library in the Sudolnik Room.

LUNENBURG 300TH ANNIVERSARY COMMITTEE MEETING MINUTES
SEPTEMBER 9, 2024 Sudolnik Room, Lunenburg Library 7:00 p.m.
Members present: Laura Brzozoski, Dawn Johnson, Debbie Lincoln, Pete Lincoln, Chris Sullivan, Bill Tyler
Members absent: Michelle Belleza, Cathy Clark, Kathy Comeau, Lois Filiau, Tony Sculibrene
The meeting was called to order at 7:04 by Chairman Pete Lincoln. He commented that the Committee was 
meeting two years too early, and that the only thing to do at present was to concentrate on long-range 
projects until 2026.
Kathy Comeau is resigning from the Committee as she is unable to make the time commitments at present, 
but will be available to help.
A motion for passing the minutes was made by Dawn and seconded by Bill and passed unanimously.
One long-term project is Lunenburg signs resembling the Massachusetts Tercentenary signs erected in 
1930. Pete and Debbie spoke with DPW and Police about placement of signs. Bill asked if it would be 
possible for QR codes to be added to the signs for additional information. We will need to decide on 
locations and verbiage for the signs and determine cost. DPW told us the prisoners make most of the signs 
the town uses.
Debbie presented the ﬁrst ever above ground time capsule, which was done by the Town of Braintree, 
Mass. in 1976, and suggested that we do a similar structure on the Lower Common. Braintree, England 
sent an inscribed rock by special boat, and Debbie suggested we could try to get rocks from Lunenburg, 
Vermont, Nova Scotia, Germany.
Local contractors can be on the lookout for a potato-shaped rock for the Luther Burbank memorial for the 
LAAC backyard.
Debbie met with Interim Town Manager Carter Terenzini, who expressed interest in furthering the property 
on Northﬁeld Road. He has had experience with the town of Spencer and their 250th. Their biggest sources 
of funding were Town Meeting appropriation, corporate donations, state funding through Representatives 
and Senators, but mostly through sales of memorabilia. They made enough money to give the Town back 
what they they had appropriated them. He also stated that the schools could not make money charging the 
300th for events, but expenses like Police details and janitorial fees would have to be decided between the 
school budget and the 300th budget.
Pete and Debbie met with Facilities Manager Chris Ruth, who has considerable background with ﬁreworks. 
He suggested a location behind Passios where ﬁreworks could be set oﬀ and seen at both the high school 
football ﬁeld and the area where the band concerts are now held. A good display would cost $500/minute.
The bonﬁre remains one of the most-wanted events, but a location has still not been determined.
The premiere event of the year would be the Birthday Celebration 7/28-7/30/2028. Proposed activities are 
ﬁreworks, a carnival, and Olympics.
Other ideas were Main Street feasts, contests for branding, Artisan weekend, ﬂoats.
The meeting was adjourned at 8:14.
The next meeting is Monday, December 9 at 7:00 at the Lunenburg Adult Activity Center (Eagle House.) 
Minutes approved unanimously 12/9/24. Submitted by D. Lincoln

LUNENBURG 300TH ANNIVERSARY COMMITTEE MEETING MINUTES
DECEMBER 9, 2024 Lunenburg Adult Activity Center 7:00 p.m.
Members present: Cathy Clark, Lois Filiau, Dawn Johnson, Debbie Lincoln, Pete Lincoln, Chris 
Sullivan, Bill Tyler, Tony Sculimbrene, Liaison Caesar Nuzzolo
Members absent: Michelle Belleza, Laura Brzozoski
Guests: Hailee Attorri, Jeanne Dupre
The meeting was brought to order at 7:07 by Chairman Pete Lincoln.
COMMITTEE COMMENTS:
Cathy Clark brought a variety of Lunenburg Christmas ornaments and suggested they would be a 
good fundraiser.
Pete Lincoln announced the next meeting would be 4/28/25, and that would be the ﬁrst true 
organizational meeting. He also reported that Oﬃcer Sean Zrate would be your liaison for the Police, 
Fire and DPW. Our job as a Committee is not to do everything ourselves, but to encourage 
townspeople and organizations to get involved and have as much community participation as 
possible.
The most-wanted event is the bonﬁre, but where it can be held is a persistent problem. The Fire 
Department is now unionized and not anxious to support the event. Town owned land does not 
seem a feasible location at this time, or even in the near future. Privately owned land would raise 
insurance questions. Bill and Tony are going to look into this, and Dawn will talk with Jim Lattanzi.
Debbie mentioned an encounter with Marc Valentine, the “Flag Truck Guy,” in Halifax, and the 
possibility of his bringing 90’ ﬂags for our birthday celebration.
Dawn suggested a Scavenger Hunt with signs placed around town, and Cathy suggested they be 
sponsored by local businesses.
Hailee is going to try to bring the schools into the celebration, teaching Lunenburg History, also 
involving the Drama Club, and potential Community Service.
All agreed publicity was essential through The Ledger and Facebook and the Committee spreading 
the word.
MINUTES
Motion to approve minutes by Dawn, seconded by Bill, and were approved unanimously with the 
correction of one typo.
PROGRESS ON PROJECTS
Pete and Debbie will fund the signs and the cairn as their personal projects, so no fundraising will be 
necessary for those projects. Pete has several leads on providers for signs.
Dan Proctor is looking for a potato-shaped rock.

===PAGE 2===
There is an All Vendors Fair May 10th sponsored by Parks and LBA. There is a $40 fee. We should 
be a presence at that event. There will be a follow-up to see if we need to pay the fee.
The Marshall Park completion date is still TBD, so there is no certainty we can plan events at that 
venue, which leaves the question of where can large groups assemble?
ACTIVITIES SUGGESTIONS
Tony suggested the use of Memorial Drive for events such as community suppers.
Tony thought an Informational Bulletin Board would be useful, and Chris recommended a Town 
Calendar of Events and volunteered to head up a Facebook page.
An agenda item for the next meeting is how do we best connect with other town organizations and 
committees.
There were street-wide banners for the last celebration, and Lois wondered where they were, and if 
we could do something similar for the 300th.
Dawn motioned to adjourn at 8:06, Cathy seconded, and the motion passed unanimously.
NEXT MEETING: MONDAY , APRIL 28, 2025, 7:00 LUNENBURG LIBRARY , SUDOLNIK ROOM
