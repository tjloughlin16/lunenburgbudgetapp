School Building Committee Meeting - Brooks House
February 2, 2012 - Minutes
• All members sworn in by Town Clerk, Kathy Herrick, at 7:00
• Meeting called to order by Town Clerk in order that a Committee Chair and Secretary
be nominated and elected. Colleen Shapiro nominated Mike Mackin as Chair. Dave
Matthews seconded. Vote: unanimous. Dave Matthews nominated Colleen Shapiro
as Secretary. Mike Mackin seconded. Vote: unanimous. Dave Matthews nominated
Mark Erickson as Vice Chair, Colleen Shapiro seconded. Vote: unanimous.
• Committee members introduced themselves stating their background relevant to the
committee.
• Discussion of meeting schedule. Next meeting February 8, 2012, 7 pm at LHS
auditorium in conjunction with School Committee. Committee will meet twice
monthly on the alternate Wednesdays from School Committee in the coming months
(2"' and 4'' Wednesdays). Dates are February 29, March 14, 28, April 11, 25
(tentatively set). All meetings will be at Brooks House at 7 pm unless otherwise
noted.
• Superintendent Calmes updated the committee on status with MSBA. Vote was taken
by MSBA on January 25, 2012 to move forward with Lunenburg.
• Mr. Londa updated the Committee on the MSBA process. He explained the
opportunities for earning additional reimbursement points depending on our
maintenance practices, building options (new build versus renovation), etc.
• Discussion surrounding creating a list of FAQ. First on the list: Didn't we already do
a Feasibility Study? Also discussion relative to how the SBC will communicate to
the greater community.
• Next Meeting February 8, 2012 LHS auditorium.
• At 8:25 Mr. Erickson moved and Mr. Matthews seconded a motion to adjourn the
meeting. Vote: unanimous.
Respectfully submitted,
Colleen Shapiro, Secretary

1. At 7:02 p.m. Mr. Mackin called the meeting to order.
2. Voted to approve minutes from 02-02-12 and 02-08-12 meetings.
3. Discussion relative to warrant article and cost of Feasibility Study. John Londa reported that he
looked at similar studies being done in the area (Ayer/Shirley $750K and Tahanto $505K - both
Middle/High School studies, Ayer/Shirley being done more recently) and the committee
determined that the warrant article should be for $750K with $30K of that to come from a
reappropriation of funds not used by the Regionalization Committee. Mark Erickson made a
motion to approve such a warrant article and Dave Matthews seconded the motion. All members
voted in favor.
4. Discussion relative to Owner's Project Manager (OPM). Some discussion about when the OPM
would be chosen and how long the OPM would be on the project. It was determined that the OPM
will work with the SBC until completion of the project.
5. Superintendent's update: John Londa is working on the maintenance report to submit to MSBA.
The information included in the report will be used towards a possible 2% reimbursement. While
it is likely that our current maintenance schedule would result in 1.6-1.7% reimbursement, Mr.
Londa stated that if an override is not passed the cuts in next year's maintenance budget will likely
result in close to 0% reimbursement in that area. A very rough estimate (based on building size
standards and per square foot costs) of the cost of a new building is $41 million, therefore 1-2% is
a significant amount of reimbursement the Town could lose if we face maintenance cuts next year.

Request for Designer Services (RFS)
JLA reviewed the Request for Designer Services (RFS) with the committee highlighting several items. The
name of the projects for the RFS will be Lunenburg School Project. The RFS Design fee for basic services
will be listed at $400K with $50K in contingency for additional services such as geotech, traffic study, etc.
The estimated construction budget will be listed at $25 million to $59 million. The milestone schedules
listed on page 5 & 6 were discussed with the advertised milestones remaining as listed. The pre-proposal
onsite briefing was confirmed for 12/04/12 at 3:00 PM at LHS. The SBC requested the RFS be advertised in
the Central Register and a local newspaper, rather than the Boston Globe. The local paper selected was
Worcester Telegram & Gazette. The RFS proposal will be issued from JLA website with link to Lunenburg
website. Lunenburg needed to nominate three (3) representatives to serve on the MSBA Designer
Selection Panel for the meeting scheduled for 1/8/2013. The suggested three (3) representatives, per
MSBA guidelines, are: Kerry Speidel, Loxi Jo Calmes and Michael Mackin.
Motion: LSBC moved to approve Kerry Speidel, Loxi Jo Calmes and Michael Mackin to serve as local
representatives on the MSBA Designer Selection Panel and approval of the Draft RFS for Designer.
The Committee voted to recommend approval.
2. Meeting and Milestone Schedule
Page 1 of 2
===PAGE 2===
JLA distributed a copy of the proposed Site and Design Alternatives matrix to the group tor review and
comment. The matrix is divided into six (6) main headers with seven (7) sub headers denoted with various
topics. JLA explained the matrix is an evaluation tool weighted for each item from high to weak. The
categories can be modified to better suit Lunenburg. Comments or changes should be directed to JLA for
correction and collection.
4. Formation of Project Coordination Group
JLA discussed with the LSBC the formation of a Project Coordination Group (PCG). This group will meet
regularly during the school day for a variety of items under discussion with the results presented to the
LSBC. The First meeting scheduled date will be coordinated by JLA. Eight (8) members were appointed to
the group with an open invitation to anyone else wishing to participate in any meeting. The eight (8)
members selected were: Loxi Jo Calmes, Michael Mackin, Colleen Shapiro, Richard Cohen, Kerry Speidel,
John Londa, Brian Frank and Tim Santry.
Motion: LSBC moved to recommend approval of the Project Coordination Group.
The Committee voted to recommend approval.

JLA recommend at the next LSBC meeting the following topics be discussed: Construction Delivery
methods, set-up of public forum, and review criteria and options on the evaluation matrix.
