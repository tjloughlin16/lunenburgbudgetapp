1. Hudson 90 Hemlock – Touch up Beach with sand. 
 
June Hudson appeared to represent herself.  The plan was reviewed by the membership. Bill 
Hudson noted that the amount was about 5 Cubic Yards. JR asked what would be done to stop the 
erosion in the future. June Hudson Noted that there is edging in place to prevent that in the future.  
JR and BP noted that the sand was 2 inches . RB noted that if much of the beach was grown over, 
restoring he was not in favor of restoring the entire beach however, a portion may be ok to do. BP 
noted that the entire area was there was still there, but the beach had degraded. JR did note that 
the beach still does exist based on his last site visit. JV n oted that if the beach was already in 
existence, then he felt there was a pre -existing right to restore the beach. He would be against a 
new beach, Mr Hudson noted that by wetland flag a -5 on the plan, there was no sand as there is a 
walkway and that area would not have sand placed in that area, John Fortune of 50 Hemlock noted 
that there is grass by the walk and the beach is a little smaller, so the beach area was a little 
smaller than was formerly marked on the plan. Mrs. Hudson confirmed there was an exp ansion of 
the grass area that shrank the beach down.  BP requested a certain number of yards for the beach  
he would entertain in the motion. JR moved to approve a field change to allow 9 yards of sand. JV 
seconded. The motion was passed by BP, JV, JR, ML and CC with RB abstained.  
 
2. 30 Laurel Lane- Field change adding a shed 70 feet from edge of wetland.  
Anthony Jordan appeared before the commission for the  request for a field change. The location of 
the shed was examined by the Conservation Commission. It was noted that the Shed was 
supposed to be 70 feet away. RB examined and noted that there was a potential discrepancy in the 
measurement. MM noted he may not have accounted for the side wetland. A  Motion was made by 
JV to allow a shed at 70 feet from t he wetland. CC seconded and the commission unanimously 
approved the motion.  
 
3. Gerry- Field change 36 Peninsula Dr. : 
Steve Marsden reviewed the plan changes with the commission. He noted the items originally 
approved that was previously approved within the  buffer zone and that the change totaled 1,120 
square feet proposed reduction in impervious.  He noted the reason for the change was to allow a 
family member with ADA requirement to have some accommodation. BP reviewed the patio stone 
to be used in the new proposal. RB noted that there may be a variance required for the work within 
the 50 foot and may require closer examination. Mr. Marsden noted that proposed versus what is 
approved was a reduction in impervious. JR reviewed the areas  of pre-existing, existing and 
proposed. Mr. Marsden’s calculations were reviewed from his Letter. A polli ng of members 
indicated the members wanted more information. It was decided by the members to review the 
information and will review at a future meeting for further detail.

4. Hickory hills Lake Annual presentation. 
John Fortune from 50 Hemlock Drive appeared before the commission for the discussion for the 2021 drawdown 
request. He reviewed the condition form the original Order of Conditions with the Conservation Commission for the 
drawdown thresholds. Mr. Fortune reviewed the spillway work accomplished to stabilize the spillway. He reviewed 
the biomass removed and further erosion control efforts. He also reviewed the copper sulfate treatment and the 
water quality testing. RB asked if there any number that shows excess Phosphorus and asked if there was any 
data showed phosphate. Preexisting phosphate numbers will be submitted. RB asked about fanwort management. 
Mr Fortune noted there was a reduction however there were a large number that were hand harvested. He noted 
that hand pulling versus chemical treatment is being evaluated. RB asked if the fanwort found was widely 
disbursed or in certain areas. Mr. Fortune noted the areas where dashing had not occurred there were more 
concentrated. MM reviewed the Algae bloom numbers. JR moved to approve the 4 foot drawdown for 2021. ML 
seconded. The Commission unanimously approved the motion by roll call vote.

Public Hearing 
 
 
Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws a public hearing o n 
a Notice of Intent by Jeffery Landry the installation of a tight tank at 1078 Lancaster Ave. 
 
Mark Elbag represented. Mr. Elbag outlined the specifics of the site to the Conservation Commission and its relation to the 
resource area and noted the most feasible solution was a tight tank. He noted that the construction outlined on the plans 
would be quick and light for the site. RB asked when the plan was to put the tight tank. RB ask the applicant if the erosion 
protection could be added two and enhanced to account for any heavy rains that may be encountered during the fall. He 
asked for buried and staked silt fencing. MM noted the waddles should go from 6 to 8-inch waddles to 12 inch waddles as he 
felt 6 inch waddles would be ineffective based on the terrain. JR agreed with the expectation of the erosion control. 
 
 A motion was made to approve an order of conditions with added conditions concerning the enhanced the erosion 
control. The motion was made by JR and seconded by ML.  The motion was approved by a role call vote 
 
Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws a public hearing on  
a Notice of Intent by Kevin Hill the Construction of a single family home at 41 Country Lane 
 
 
Wesley Flis was present to represent the commission. He outlined the site for consideration, the condition of the site and the 
location of the resource areas. He outlined the location of the proposed house in relation to the wetland resource areas and 
addressed impacts under the rivers protection portion of the wetland protection act. He reviewed the creation history of the lot 
to note it predated the enactment of the river protection portion of the welding protection act. He further outlined the location 
of the well and septic, specifying the areas of the septic system components and where they were located. He illustrated for 
the Commission how the impacts shown on the plan were proposed to be as minimal as possible. JR reviewed the riverfront 
and the other resource areas with the applicant. It was noted by the applicant that there were no alternatives for the location 
regarding the riverfront and the buffer zone. JR noted he felt the applicant was asking for a waiver from the riverfront and the 
100 foot buffer zone. The applicant noted the exceptions built into the river protection act. JR felt the structure should not be 
as large as the applicant wants.  The applicant noted the provisions are in existence to protect pre-existing lots prior to the 
enactments of the regulations and noted the regulations do not restrict the size of a house development, however it does 
allow for it specifically. The applicant noted that in order to move the house farther out of the riverfront it would move it closer 
to the wetland resource area in the front of the lot potentially intruding into the 50 foot no touch zone. The applicant further 
noted that in accordance with the performance standards in the regulations, they were attempting to provide a 100-foot swath 
of undisturbed vegetation within the riverfront. JR noted he was sensitive to the Mulpus. The applicant expressed concern is 
that reconfiguring the locations of the house, would likely wind up with configurations within the 30 in 50-foot restrictive bylaw 
zones.. JR noted he would not be adverse to the house being turned and going into the 100 foot buffer zone the applicant 
then asked if the Commission was open till work being done in the 50 foot no build zone in order for the house to be moved 
out of the riparian zone.

ML asked if there was a former dumpsite on this property. RB examined moving the garage closer to the front of the house. 
the applicant did note that there was a covered front porch. RB ask if the porch could be eliminated and there could be just a 
set of stairs going to the front door and if the garage was moved up about 15 feet, there could be a lot of impacts mitigated. 
RB indicated he did not have any issue with having an open deck located in the back of the house going into the inner 
riparian zone. It was noted the septic system has already been approved by the Board of Health. CC wanted to know if the 
trees being removed were quantified and if there was a replanting plan that was submitted with the application. The applicant 
noted there was no formal replanting plan. MM clarify there was no work within the 30 foot no touch zone, and as such, there 
was no replanting plan required under the regulations. MM noted he would like to see all the backland closed off with maybe 
some type of conservation restriction. MM noted based on CC’s point, there may be some small trees that might be touched 
and consider evaluating planting replacement along the front of the stone wall and in areas where the canopy naturally thins 
out.  
JR ask the applicant about moving the tanks that were proposed from their current location to the other side of the house. the 
applicant noted that he would check it. RB Noted that he would be more inclined to have the garage go into the front of the 
house. RB noted the house could be smaller however it likely would not be any narrow within 30 feet. 
Noted that JV I was having a hard time logging in and as such was wondering if the meeting should continue. RB noted he

JR building on Ms. Adams comment, his own house had the garage placed at the basement level and suggested perhaps 
installing an elevator.

narrative to ensure compatibility with the replication that is proposed to be planted with what is existing for the surrounding 
wetlands. MM inquired if the replication area was going to require removal of the overstory or if it was going to be dug out in 
such a way that it was going to meander around the existing canopy. The applicant noted most of the trees in the area are 
multi stem saplings. MM also asked if the revised plans were sent to DEP noting that he would like to see any follow-up 
comments if it had been submitted. The applicant noted that he would check into it and ensure it was done. 
RB wanted to know if the crossing was in the same location as originally proposed. It was noted by the applicant that it was 
moved slightly southward as the original subdivision required large radius for turning that no longer exists in this new version. 
The applicant also noted that the common driveway and it's required location along the property lines also dictated the

to stake out the replication area so it could be examined on a future inspection. MM asked the applicant if the septic system

shortly. MM asked about the status with the planning board. The applicant noted that he withdrew the subdivision and the
