Meeting called to order by the Chairman Robin Corbett at 7:10 Pm.
The minutes from 2010 were read by the secretary Mary Ann Sudolnik.
Brent Benson made a motion to accept the minutes. Caroline Griffin seconded. All were
in favor.
No public comment.
The treasurer's report was presented by Jim Ryan. There is $123.79 remaining in the
Town Account. As of June 30, 2011 there was $4306.70 to disperse to the applicants.
Treasurer Jim Ryan has paid $1,110 and has $3,198.70 pending. The money left in Town
Hall was added to the amount granted this year for a grand total of $3993.79 to award to
acceptable applicants in 2012.
Lunenburg requests amounted to $5,173. $6,490 was requested by groups outside
Lunenburg.
Review of applications. There were 19 applications submitted. Amount requested
totaled $12,463.
The Council looked at the Lunenburg/locally sponsored requests first.
These totaled $3857.00. Other requests totaling $1275 were evaluated and both groups
were accepted on a motion that was seconded and approved by all.
The request for "Say It with Magic" was to change the program to this coming year. It
was approved by the group. The $300 allocated last year will pay the fee this year.
A motion was made to move $43.79 for administrative costs and was passed by
all. motion was made and seconded and then passed by all present.

The group discussed and agreed to the following suggestions for FY2013:
The denial letter will be revised to state that we prefer activities that will take place in Lunenburg and
have a local sponsor.
Have the accepted and denied letters ready for the December meeting on the system to streamline the
process.
The Lunenburg Ledger is a good communication tool and will be used to solicit new members, events and
inform the public about our mission.
Brent will get a schedule of events together for FY 2013 and notify Council members so we can attend
some of the events.
The group discussed ramping up the activities of this Council. As an example, other towns in the area
have fund raisers to increase the number and amounts of grants.
This Council is in transition as some of the members have taken on other activities. All of the members
offered help as needed.
The group agreed to schedule the yearly meeting on the second Wednesday of December but make
adjustments as needed each year. Next years meeting: December 11, 2013 at 7 PM.

Minutes of December 3, 2014
Lunenburg Public Library
The meeting was called to order by Chairman Brent Benson. All members were present.
Nomination and voting was conducted for Council officers. Brent Benson was re-elected Chair, Jim Ryan
was re-elected Treasurer and Caroline Griffis was re-elected Secretary.

awards:

appealed.
