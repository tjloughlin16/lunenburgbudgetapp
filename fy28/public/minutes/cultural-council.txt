Lunenburg Cultural Council Meeting
Minutes of November 3, 2015 7:00pm
Lunenburg Public Library
The meeting was called to order by Chairman Brent Benson.  All members were present.
Approved last year’s minutes.
Elected Sarah Coon for secretary
Re-elected Brent Benson for Chairman.
Elected Laura Brzozoski for Treasurer.
Applications for 2016 were reviewed and discussed by members.  The following is a list of the 
grand awards:
1. LHS for their collaborative Youth Concert - $500
2. Hunting the Forrest - $176
3. Fan Fiction 101 - $176
4. Get Bent Yoga for Teens - $204
5. Sunny Skies of Italy - $550
6. Children’s Songs - $450
7. Poetry Anthology - $1250
8. 81st Regional Art Show - $350
9. Lunenburg Town Band - $703
10. Plymouth Plantation ﬁeld trip - $1655
11. Mad Science of Western NE - $250
12. Songbirds of the Northeast - $200
The meeting was adjourned at 8:30p.
