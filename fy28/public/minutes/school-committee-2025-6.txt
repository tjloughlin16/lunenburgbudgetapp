Dawn Gearin, 9 Crescent Terrace  I have lived here for 32 years and I have had many different roles 
within the school and community.  My big goal is to make the point that the athletic director  job  needs 
to be full time.  I am living the nightmare of having a full time job cut and then I could be expected to do
both of those full time jobs, I am lucky enough that I don ’t have too, that Greenhouse job was full time 
job at one point.  From a coach ’s perspective, I cannot believe what an amazing job Yanina is doing, and
I know this from a coach ’s perspective, because I coached this Fall, it was seamless.  From the 
educator ’s perspective, she is at school doing a great job also.  I feel bad for her, it is almost inhumane 
what is happening to her, because of the hours she is working.  I am supporting her as she is doing a 
great job, but my big shout out is that the athletic director needs to be a full time job.  How does it affect 
me?  As a coach, when I need the AD in the morning, I don ’t want to reach out to them because she had 
been there until 9 or 10 the night before, I struggle as a coach to get what I need when I need it.  As an 
educator, thank God, we have the other two administrators that are helping and pulling up the slack, 
which is unfortunate for them also, having more stuff dumped on them, but again I cannot get in touch 
with her until she gets to school, this is too much for one person.  If you know anything about that job, 
you know it is a full time job, the fires you put out every day with parents, kids, and coaches alone is a 
nightmare.  Managing the  budget,  gym, the fields, the transportation and the weather.  In the past they 
have tried to have it be  an educator , and those poor kids, that had that teacher, they were out almost 70 
different days that is not fair to the kids.  You have a great person working their tail off doing a great job
at both of these, because she would never not do a great job.  I am here to express the importance of the 
position being full time.  Sports are a big part of kids ’ lives, more importantly if we lose sports, we will 
lose a lot of great kids.
