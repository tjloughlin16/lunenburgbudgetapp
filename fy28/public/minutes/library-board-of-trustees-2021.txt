Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes January 21st, 2021 
Remote meeting via conference call and video link. All votes were done by roll call.  Meeting was recorded. 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Laura Boussom, Maia 
Wentrup, Michael-Ray Jeffreys 
Absent: Donna Saia 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:45 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
o Harry did a roll call for attendance. 
o Harry read the requirements for remote meetings. 
o Laura mentioned a Facebook post about giving thanks mentioned Debbie and the library staff. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Kate made a motion to accept minutes of December 17 as amended.  Seconded by Laura. Motion passed 
unanimously. 
 
 
• Director’s Report 
o It’s the end of the 7th month of the fiscal year – about 58% of the year is completed. The budget is on 
schedule, salaries are steady. We are entering into the higher cost months for electricity and heating. 
o The CWMARS network membership has been paid. Have some catching up to do on library materials. 
o We have been paying for the strategic plan consultant. 
o Annual membership fees and subscriptions are coming up in the spring. 
o FY22 budget: Muir got the target budget from the town mgr. Budget requests were submitted. Muir has a 
meeting on Monday morning to discuss those. 
o The Finance meeting is coming up in February.  
o Debbie Laffond attended annual CWMARS resource meeting. 
o Muir submitted the continuity of operations outline plan. This documents lists processes for town-wide 
operating during the pandemic or any other emergency situation. 
o Programs are still going on virtually at the library including a Creepy Christmas program and a home 
downsizing program. We are aiming for at least one adult program per month on Zoom. A series of programs 
on travel is coming up to be presented along with some other libraries. 
o STEM programs have been presented about once a month.  
o Laura asked about programs being recorded and posted on YouTube. Muir said some programs are but it’s 
dependent on the permission of participants. 
o The library has purchased a GOPro camera to help create content. It was funded from the library materials 
budget. 
o Muir has been working on a technology plan for upgrades, etc. 
o In-Library browsing has been averaging around 200 patrons per month.  The plan is to continue along with 
curbside pickups. The browsing still keeps the library below the 25% capacity limit. 
 
• Trustee Handbook Review 
o Muir recommended we re-read/review the Trustee handbook.  

===PAGE 2===
 
 
o He reviewed the new trustee checklist and some highlights from the Trustee handbook such as ‘Ten 
important things you should have’, ‘Trustee’s Responsibilities’ and ‘Eight Essential Tips for Success’ . 
 
• Strategic Plan Subcommittee Update 
o The selection of Barbara Friedman as consultant was finalized. The Subcommittee ha s met with her 3 times. 
They have gone over focus group questions and will be going over survey questions that will be sent out 
after the focus groups. 
o Dick noted that she has exceeded expectations on pulling together information and being proactive in time 
between meetings. She has also helped keep meetings focused. 
o Harry thanked the subcommittee members for their time working on this. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o No January meeting. The pop-up book sale is still going on weather permitting. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Dick signed up for Kanopy today – to watch the movie Wave for the upcoming Zoom discussion. He noted 
that it was a great service. 
o Harry thanked Michael-Ray for helping to move up Muir’s salary increase to January. 
o Michael-Ray met with the town manager and our state representatives and said the initial indications from 
the state are positive revenue-wise. Town revenue is on target. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick, seconded by Kate. Meeting was adjourned at 7:49 p.m.

Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes February 18th, 2021 
Remote meeting via conference call and video link. All votes were done by roll call.  Meeting was recorded. 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup, Michael-Ray Jeffreys 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:30 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
o Harry did a roll call for attendance. 
o Harry read the requirements for a remote meeting. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept minutes as amended, seconded by Kate. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
 
• Director’s Report 
o FY21 budget – Feb is the 8th month of the fiscal year. We are two thirds through the year. Heating and 
electricity costs are catching up to normal levels. Salaries are still a bit low with one vacancy still to fill. There 
are no major hiccups budget-wise.  
o Dick asked about custodial supplies. Muir said it is covered under the DPW budget.  Muir said they have been 
trying to be conservative in the first part of the year and are on track. 
o FY22 – have received preliminary budget and sent requests back. Muir is meeting with the Finance 
committee tonight. 
o Staff updates – finished annual report for the town. 
o Marabeth is working on an inventory of library materials, scanning every item. This enables us to delete 
records of unaccounted for items and should improve catalog searches for patrons. 
o The Beanstack winter reading program has concluded – was a big hit. There were predominantly children 
participating but all ages also did. 
o Lots of virtual programs are coming up including Armchair Travel, the short film discussion group, free 
photography course and a garden program. 
o Susan has been doing teen take home kits. 
o STEM programs have been expanded to accommodate more attendees. 
o Security camera upgrades have been postponed until March. 
o Request for proposals for the carpet replacement is in process. 
o The phone system needs some updating. Muir is working on getting estimates for maintenance. 
o No change in reopening plans. The library guidance from the state hasn’t changed. The library will continue 
with curbside pickup and limited browsing. 
 
• Strategic Plan Subcommittee Update 
o The committee has met a few more times with Barbara. They have finished up invitation letters for the focus 
group. 
o Letters/invitations are ready to be mailed out. 
o Focus groups will be the first week of March. 
o They are ready to send out a survey after the focus group sessions have been completed. 
o Barbara is working on an outline of the report to be done after all the analysis is done. 

===PAGE 2===
 
o Hannaford has given gift certificates to give to the focus group participants via a random drawing. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The Friends met in February. They discussed plans for their website and social media updates and revisions. 
o Their plan is to work on a Friend’s Facebook page. They made some small revisions to the Friend’s portion 
of the library website. 
o Pop-Up book sales are still going on and going well. They are discussing whether or not to do a larger sale  
and plan on continuing the pop-ups to cycle through the inventory they currently have. 
o They have invited skate park representatives to talk about collaborating on a fund raiser in the spring. 
o They have discussed ways of using the Letarte poetry fund this year and are planning some virtual poetry 
events for April. 
 
 
• Board Comments 
o No board comments. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Donna seconded by Kate. Meeting was adjourned at 7:09 p.m. 
 
 
• Attached Documents 
o February 18th 2020 Agenda 
o February 2021 Director’s Report 
o February Adult Services Report 
o February 2021 Youth Services Report 
o February 2021 Teen Room Highlights

Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes March 25th, 2021 
Remote meeting via conference call and video link. All votes were done by roll call.  Meeting was recorded. 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 7:02 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
o Harry did a roll call for attendance. 
o Harry read the requirements for a remote meeting. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept minutes of February 18, 2021 as amended, seconded by Laura. The motion 
passed unanimously. 
 
 
• Director’s Report 
o For the FY21 budget, March is the 9th month of the fiscal year. We are three quarters through the year. 
Salaries are on schedule. One staff vacancy is still open. The sewer and water bills were paid today. Annual 
renewals are coming up in the next few months. 
o FY22 budget – no update yet. The Town Manager is making a preliminary budget presentation tonight to the 
Finance Committee. 
o Staff Update: Maribeth has finished the scanning portion of the inventory project. She is tracking down some 
anomalies now. Cheryl has been doing a great job working on the newsletter.  
o The CWMARS users council met last week. The group voted to implement a digital card service with 
Evergreen and to increase the membership fee for small libraries with populations under 10K to 25% of the 
full membership fee. 
o Lots of programs and events are coming up in the next few months. Poetry programs are coming up, 
sponsored by the Mary Ellen and Richard Letarte Poetry fund. 
o The Beanstack adult reading challenge has begun and we have had 15 readers two days into the program. 
o Security cameras were upgraded. The coverage of the outside grounds has been increased and the 
resolution of the inside cameras has been improved. The phone system was repaired and improved. 
o The new cleaning contractor begins on April 1st. Muir has carpet tile samples for the recarpeting project. 
o Town offices are moving to limited hours without appointments. We are in phase 4 for the state 
recommendations on reopening. Town boards are permitted to begin shifting to a hybrid model starting with 
the Selectboard meeting on April 6th. 
o Kate asked whether hours open to in-person browsing can be increased. Muir said the curbside pickup is 
very popular and increasing the browsing time would require decreasing the curbside pickup.  Donna asked 
how having patrons browsing impacts the curbside pickup service. Muir said patrons usually need some sort 
of assistance and staff tend to be busy with that.  
o Dick asked about the status of the staffing vacancy. Muir said he hopes to begin a search soon. He said he 
will check with the Town Manager on whether the budget will allow filling the staffing vacancy this fiscal year. 
o Harry asked about having open browsing within limits. Muir said a possibility is to have  some open browsing 
times without appointments soon depending on state recommendations and capacity limits. 

===PAGE 2===
 
o Harry asked about making sure the person picking up the material is the right person. Muir said the materials 
are matched to accounts and notification messages and they have not had a problem with any kind of fraud.  
o Laura asked about librarians being eligible for vaccines and whether browsers are cooperativ e in wearing 
masks. They are not yet eligible in general although some staff are eligible and have been vaccinated. 
Patrons have been very good about wearing masks. 
 
• Strategic Plan Subcommittee Update 
o Focus groups have been held. The committee is finalizing survey questions. The survey could be sent out 
within the next month.  
 
• Trustee Handbook Discussion 
o Muir reviewed the first chapter of the handbook focusing on the checklist for the new trustee.  
o We will look at chapter two for the April meeting and review any questions.  
o Muir and Dick pointed out that we don’t have any documented bylaws. Should be a goal for next year . 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The Friends met with the skate park representatives to work on a joint fundraiser.  One idea discussed was a 
mini golf course inside the library with individual holes sponsored.  
o There was also some discussion on revising the website and creating a Facebook page. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Dick offered kudos to Lydia for the short film discussion group. Dick also recommended the Armchair Travel 
program. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick seconded by Kate. Meeting was adjourned at 8:19 p.m.

o For the FY21 budget, April is the 10th month of the fiscal year. The budget is 65% expended. We had a large

o The May 1st annual town meeting will finalize the FY22 budget. The preliminary town budget is almost exactly

Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes October 21, 2021 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup, Michael-Ray Jeffreys 
 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:45 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Kate made a motion to accept the minutes of September 16, 2021 as amended.  Seconded by Laura. The 
motion passed unanimously. 
o Donna made a motion to accept the minutes of September 29, 2021 as presented.  Seconded by Kate. The 
motion passed unanimously. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o We are in the 4th month of the fiscal year and the budget is one third expended. The process for the 2023 
budget has been started. 
o The strategic plan and annual financial report have been submitted to the state. Muir will share the action 
plan at our November meeting. The action plan will be submitted to the state in December. 
o Library Insight is going out of business. They provide the software used for the event calendar, room 
reservations and museum passes. We are getting a new vendor and the new software will be an 
improvement. It will provide better reports and better data access. The transition is being worked on now.  
o Summer Reading went very well this season. There were many more participants than last summer. There 
were over 1000 attendees at the children’s programs. There were more than 200 people at the kickoff event 
with the herding dogs. 
o Harry asked how this would compare with other libraries. Muir said he wasn’t sure but that our summer 
reading program is very strong. 
o Kate asked if the extended renewals and fine-free policy has added to wait times for materials. Muir said for 
E-books it would be no. He wasn’t sure about other library materials. 
o Kate suggested we update the fine-free policy on the library website. It still has the old one. 
o Michael-Ray asked about the impact of the fine-free policy. Muir said that the revolving fund is 40% less than 
it was last year but that the impact isn’t fully known yet. We need more time and data  to assess the full 
impact. Michael-Ray asked about how we have marketed the new policy to the student population and the 
public. 
o The elementary school class visits will be resuming this fall. 
o Michael-Ray commented on the budget request for Friday hours. There are some big ticket items coming up 
in the town and even though the $18K for a half year, $35K for the full year is a relatively small request, we 
should wait and go through the normal budgeting process. This would be in July 2022.  
o The Digital Services staff position is still open and the search process has been difficult.  
o The BEAM interactive projector game system has been installed in the Children’s area.  This was purchased 
by the Friends. 
o Muir has discussed Capital Plan projects with Rob Oliva of the DPW. The HVAC system will be prioritized.  
 
• Trustee Handbook Discussion 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
o Harry suggested we defer discussion on the Trustee Handbook. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o Book sales have been the main topic of discussion lately. 
o It was decided to do expanded pop-up book sales events for the Saturdays in October. 
 
• Director’s Annual Review 
o The review is due by December 16th for the December meeting. 
o We will use the existing template. 
o Harry will set a date to collect responses from the BOT. 
o The subcommittee will meet to consolidate comments and draft a review. 
 
 
• Board Comments 
o Harry mentioned that he will miss the December BOT meeting due to his company Christmas party.  
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick seconded by Kate. Meeting was adjourned at 7:58 p.m. 
 
 
• Attached Documents 
o October 21, 2021 Agenda 
o October 2021 Director’s Report 
o October Adult Services Report 
o October 2021 Youth Services Report 
o October 2021 Teen Services Report

Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes November 18, 2021 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom 
Absent: Maia Wentrup 
 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:58 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of October 21, 2021 as presented.  Seconded by Donna. The 
motion passed unanimously. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o We are in the 5th month of the fiscal year and the budget is 37% expended. The process for the 2023 budget 
has been started. For the first round the Town Manager generally level funds everything. The target budget is 
the next step and is expected to be distributed at the end of November. We have until the end of the calendar 
year to present budget requests.   Muir has had some discussions with the Town Manager regarding the 
Friday hours request. Muir will meet with the Town Manager after submitting requests. Department heads will 
then meet with and present to the Finance Committee. 
o The weather has been mild so the heating season has been lighter than usual. 
o The staff vacancy is still open. Lydia’s job is being reclassified because of the additional responsibilities she 
has. 
o Sherri has been working on migrating data to the new system replacing Insight . The new system should be 
ready to launch in December. 
o Dick asked about the cleaning crew. There have been some difficulties. Muir said one problem is that there 
are different people each time. They seem to be missing some details and have set off the alarms a number 
of times. 
o The Tween cooking program has been successful. They get to do it themselves while someone guides them 
through the steps. 
o Muir met with a rep from Suzor IT, the town’s new IT support company. They talked about a succession plan 
for hardware and about existing problems. These include the thin clients. They are old and have had some 
software versioning problems. There are also problems with the phone system. There is no maintenance 
contract for the phone system. Dick asked if they could come in to assess the situation and make 
recommendations. 
o Donna asked about IT security and disaster recovery plans. The staff computers are connected to 
CWMARS. Muir’s laptop and desktop are connected to the town. 
 
• Trustee Handbook Discussion 
o Harry suggested we defer discussion of chapter 4 in the Trustee Handbook. 
 
• By-Laws Review Discussion 
o The BOT has no current bylaws document. MBLC has a number of examples to use as templates. 
 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
• Directors Evaluation Discussion 
o The personnel subcommittee will be meeting tomorrow to review and compile the Board’s comments and 
input. 
 
• Trust Fund Update 
o The last action by the state legislature was on August 16 th and was an order to be read, which means it is in 
process. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o There were three pop-up book sales in November which netted about $400 each week. 
o There is a pop-up puzzle and cookbook sale planned for December 11 th. 
o There was some discussion about the partnership with the skate park group.  
 
• Fine Free Policy 
o The borrowing policy has been updated to include the new fine-free policy. 
o There was also an update on auto renewals. 
 
• FY23 Action Plan for LSTA (Library Services & Technology Act) Grant  
o Item No. 1, adding additional Friday hours, assumes the library will get funding from the town. The request 
needs to be submitted by December 4th.  
o Utilize LSTA grant funding to increase community involvement and programming.  
o Donna asked about item no. 3, access anywhere. Muir said an example might be genealogy software. 
o Dick made a motion to accept the action plan. This was seconded by Kate. The motion passed unanimously. 
 
• Discussion of changing December meeting date 
o Dick made a motion to change the December meeting date from the 16 th to the 15th. Seconded by Laura. The 
motion passed unanimously. 
 
• Board Comments 
o None 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick seconded by Laura. Meeting was adjourned at 8:45 p.m.

Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes December 15, 2021 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:48 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of November 18, 2021 as amended.  Seconded by Kate. The 
motion passed unanimously. Dick made a motion to accept the minutes of November 19, 2021 as presented.  
Seconded by Kate. The motion passed unanimously.  
 
• Director’s Report 
o We are ½ through the year. Salaries are slightly behind schedule. Heating expenses are still reasonable. The 
financials look good.  There are no updates for FY23 yet. They should be coming soon.  There were lots of 
expenses this month for library materials. 
o The Action Plan has been successfully submitted to the state. Muir will be meeting with the staff to go over 
programming. The staff is focusing on LSTA grant opportunities and beginning the planning process for 
summer reading. 
o Muir did a quick demo of the new Beam interactive projector game system. It has been a big hit with th e kids. 
o The new software for the calendar, room booking, and museum passes has been launched. It is available 
now on the library website. 
o A quote has been received from Piper Electric for the LED light replacement project.  
o Early literacy stations for the Children’s room have arrived. There are two stations for ages groups 2 to 7 and 
two stations for age group 7 to 12. 
o Harry asked about some loose boards on the outside of the building. They have not been worked on yet.  
o Muir is still having difficulty filling the staff vacancy. It was suggested that the job description should be 
rewritten. This will be added to the agenda for the next meeting. 
 
• Trustee Handbook Discussion 
o We discussed chapter 5 in the handbook which covers Planning. There are four essenti al areas: Strategic, 
Technology, Disaster, and Facilities. The BOT should be able to give a quick synopsis of library goals and 
plans. Laura pointed out that the mission statement on the website needs to be updated.  
 
• By-Laws Review Discussion 
o Muir gathered some sample Bylaws documents from some area libraries. 
o Harry asked how we would go about creating a Bylaw document. Muir said he would be happy to compile 
one.  
o Donna suggested we make a list of what should be included and we can discuss and compi le it at our next 
meeting. 
 
• Collection Policy Review 
o Muir presented a Collection Policy document. There was some discussion on the policy and the process for 
handling any possible objections or concerns. 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
o Kate asked if it matters how the policy is presented. Is the policy just evident in the collection or do we 
advertise it some other way.  
o Harry asked about the next steps. Muir said he doesn’t think it needs to be changed. It was updated and 
revised on 11/19/20. 
o Dick made a motion to accept the Collection Policy as presented. Seconded by Maia. The motion passed 
unanimously. 
 
• Trust Fund Update 
o The approval by the state legislative committee should be very soon. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The Friends had an excellent book and puzzle sale on December 11th. They took in $872. 
 
• Director’s Evaluation Discussion 
o After a short discussion of the final draft of Muir’s review Kate made a motion to accept the review as 
amended. Seconded by Donna. The motion passed unanimously. 
o There was some discussion about Muir’s contract and the contract period. Harry made a motion to offer Muir 
a 3-year contract, extended from the previous 1 year. Seconded by Bob. The motion passed unanimously.  
 
• Board Comments 
o Harry contacted Main St. Bank and connected them with Muir to investigate opportunities for the bank to 
support library programs. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick seconded by Kate. Meeting was adjourned at 8:52 p.m.
