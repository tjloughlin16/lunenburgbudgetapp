Lunenburg meeting archive — full text, by board
========================================================================================

1422 agendas and sets of minutes across 40 town boards.
Each bundle below is every document for one board, concatenated, with a
header per document giving its date, our own permanent address for it, and
the town's scanned original.

Cite the individual document, never a bundle.

TO SEARCH: do not start with a bundle. The two largest are around 1MB,
which is more than many callers can read at once. Look the word up and
fetch only the documents it names -- three small requests:
  https://lunenburgbudgetproject.org/minutes/find/README.txt      how, in twenty lines
  https://lunenburgbudgetproject.org/minutes/find/<first two letters of the word>.json

The bundles below are still the fastest way to read a board whole, if
you can hold one. Check the size in this list before fetching.
