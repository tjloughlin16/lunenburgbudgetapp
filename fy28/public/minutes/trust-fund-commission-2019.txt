Meeting minutes for the Trust Fund Commissioners
 
 
Date: September 16, 2019
 
 
Meeting was called to order at 6:00.
 
 
 
Attendance: James Bersarkarski, Robert Herrick, Cathy Hennessy
 
 
Review of September 5, 2019 meeting
 
 
Commissioners spoke before meeting with resident who requested assistance
 
Ask resident about good faith loan/look into whether we can ask for money back
 
 
Met with resident who requested assistance
 
 
Asked resident to review the scope of the job and get a quote in writing
 
 
Asked resident to speak to insurance company to see if they will cover if not request a
 
denial in writing
 
 
Asked resident if they would be able to repay any of the money back but we reiterated
 
that it should not be a hardship.  The resident thought that they could manage $100 a
 
month.
 
 
Set another meeting with resident for Tuesday October 15 and Cathy Hennessy will
 
send her a reminder letter for the items
 
 
Commissioners continued with meeting after resident left and need to look into
 
community development block grant
 
 
Select a meeting schedule
 
 
General Discussion
 
 
Adjournment

MINUTES FOR DECEMBER 20, 2019 TRUST FUND COMMISSIONERS
 
 
Attendance: James Besarkarski, Cathy Hennessy and Michael Gale
 
 
Reviewed minutes from November 4, 2019 meeting
 
 
Discussed citizen request for assistance. Discussed asking citizen to speak
 
with her church group for assistance and perhaps assessing the roof for
 
patching instead of replacing the roof.
 
 
We talked about getting a copy of denial from her (the citizen) insurance
 
company.
 
 
We asked Michael Gale questions about the trust fund commissioners role
 
and parameters in regards to distributing money to people requesting it.
 
For instance is a citizen required to pay back any of the money borrowed.
 
No is the answer. Or can the trust fund commissioners ask for some kind of
 
good faith pay back without requiring the person to pay back the
 
money-answer yes. We also asked if we could limit the scope of funds-that
 
was a yes.
 
 
Adjournment: 6:05
