APPOINTMENT TO OPEN SPACE AD HOC COMMITTEE:   One completed Talent Bank Form for the vacancy  was received in 
the office and forwarded to the Open Space Committee  for review/vote.   The Open Space Committee was also meeting this 
evening, but did not have a quorum  and was unable to take a vote on the vacancy.   Discussion tabled awaiting the arrival of G. 
Bittner.   
 
EARTH REMOVAL:  Members were in receipt of Draft #1.  The Board of S electmen has placed this topic on their August 6 th 
agenda; the Board discuss ed attending the August 6 th Select Board meeting.  P. Bertram had concerns with the impact to 
existing businesses if earth removal/fill is prohibited on weekends.  She would also l ike input from the Board of Health regarding 
groundwater analysis.  M. Allison requested bot h the Bylaw and the Rules and Regulations be reviewed by the Board of Health.  
R. Lisio felt limiting  weekend hours would be appropriate if the operations are takin g place in a residential area.  Continued 
discussion took place and revisions will be made to Draft #1. 
 
With the presence of G. Bittner, the Board resumed discussion of the Open Space vacancy.  G. Bittner made Motion for P. 
Bertram to be appointed to the Open Space Committee as an At-Large member, R. Lisio Seconded, all aye. 
 
MASTER PLAN:  Board members viewed goals of the 2002 Master Plan.  A. Burney noted the Visioning Process and Planning 
Goals from the 2002 Master Plan were based on recommendations fro m the late 1980’s - early 1990’s.   He suggested that the 
Board, instead of implementing those goals, hold a visioning session with focus on implementing what comes out of a new 
master plan.  There was discussion on assimilating the new Open Space and Recr eation Plan into the master plan as Element 
V, Open Space and Recreation, Natural and Cultural .  P. Bertram questioned if the Economic Development Element prepared by 
MRPC in 2013 should be updated.  There was Board discussion on what should be included in  the RFP (Request For Proposal) 
for consultant tasks.  T. Cole felt the RFP should not be finalized until the Board knows if it will receive the $50,000 grant.  P. 
Bertram felt that an RFP should be put together with the expectation of having the full $100 ,000, and then have a works hop to 
review the RFP.  A. Burney proposed the formation of a master plan committee, which could then transition into an 
implementation committee of the master plan.

new lots; one consisting of 6.0± acres and one of 1.7± acres.  The remaining property of 33.3± a cres contains a solar field.  
There were Board concerns as to the use of the non -buildable lot of 6.0 acres.  The Board cannot endorse a land -locked non-

lot, and such should be noted on the Plan.  The anticipated purchaser of the 6.0 acres will be connecting it to Map 74, Lot 27.  A

14, 2019 in the event the work is not completed by that date, T. Cole Seconded, all aye.    
 
ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT/ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT COMMITTEE - Draft Committee Charge was viewed by the Board, 
but no vote taken as the Board would like to wait for input from G. Bittner .  Discussion will continue at the Board’s Se ptember 9th 
meeting.   
 
SOLAR BYLAW - P. Bertram asked what were the Board’s objectives with the Solar Bylaw.  The current Bylaw allows for 
ground-mounted systems up to 10 k W.  Most homes need a system larger than 10 k W, which then places them into the larg e-
scale category.  A minimum of 20 acres is required to install a large -scale ground mount.  Historically, there has been concern 
with the location of large MW solar farms within a residential area.  There ha ve also b een past discussion s regarding better 
defined buffer zones.  Further discussion tabled due to G. Bittner’s absence.   
 
ANR Plan, 190 Electric Avenue - D. Ruiz conferred with his attorney and was informed that the land conveyance doesn’t need 
to be on the ANR Plan  for Board endorsement .  The Board disagreed and requested that the lot be relabeled as a parcel being 
conveyed to/combined with another specified parcel.  A Board meeting will be posted for Thursday August 29th, 6:00 PM for the 
ANR Plan.

ANR PLAN:  322 Flat Hill Road - M. Allison made it known that his mother lives across the street f rom the subject address and 
that the applicant is also his colleague.  He went on to note that he will no t derive any financial gain from this land division.  The 
Plan was presented by John Boardman P.E., Places Associates, Inc.  The house is located at the front of a long and narrow 
piece of property.  This Plan creates two backland lots behind the original front lot.  The lots have sufficient frontage and area.  
As the property is registered 61B, Recreation land , it will need to go through the Right of First Refusal process  if taken out of 
Chapter.  The Chapter Land process does not prevent an ANR Plan from being endorsed by the Board.  P. Bertram requested 
that a memo randum be provided to the Select Board, Conservation Commission, and Open Space Ad Hoc Committee notifying 
them of a potential Chapter Land withdrawal.  P. Bertram made Motion to approve the ANR  Plan as submitted for 322 Flat 
Hill Road, T. Cole Seconded, all aye.  P. Bertram made Motion that land use boards be notified of the potential 
withdrawal from Chapter, T. Cole, Seconded, all aye.   
 
335 CHASE ROAD, Proposed Common Drive - At its November 25th meeting, the Board requested information regarding the 
layout and grading of the proposed common drive indicated on the ANR Plan.  Plan presented by David LeRoy, Hannigan 
Engineering.  The drive would be constructed off Northfield Road and would be alm ost 100 feet long.  There is a 25 foot leveling 
area at 2% along Northfield Road.  The drive will steepen to a grade not to exceed 9%.  The drive does not straddle the lot line.  
It was noted that the location of the proposed shared driveway will preserve the existing fieldstone wall and eliminate an 
additional wetland crossing on a  scenic road.  P. Bertram made Motion to approve the proposed common drive at 335 
Chase Road with the stipulation that the field stone wall be retained, T. Cole Seconded, al l aye.  T. Cole was concerned 
that the replication areas could possibly be on Town -owned land.  If so, the applicant w ill probably need permission  from the 
Town and an easement  to replicate the wetlands.  The project still needs to go before the Conservation Commission and the 
Board of Health.   
 
335 CHASE ROAD, ANR Plan- P. Bertram made Motion to approve the ANR Plan for 335 Chase Road, T. Cole Seconded, 
all aye. 
 
ROBBS HILL ROAD & SUNSET LANE:  Proposed Common Drive - Neil Gorman, David Ross Associates noted this previously 
came before the Board in spring 2019.  At that time it was withdrawn without prejudice for the applicant to gather more 
information and for the presence of a  full Board.  Due to the absence of a member this evening  and a member vacancy , N. 
Gorman is requesting to reschedule to the next meeting.  Board was in agreement.  Application will be presented at the January 
13th Board meeting.

EMERALD PLACE AT LAKE W HALOM: Surety Release -  Continued from the last Board meeting as Board members wanted 
to ensure all items on the punch list had been completed ; in particular the sidewalks .  In reviewing the files, A. Burney noted 
there had originally been conflict between  the developer and the Board as to the purpose of the Bond.  When the project was 
approved, the improv ements along the waterfront o n Town property and the roadway  were included in the project.  There were  
Bond releases up to August 2014.  Any final issues were for final pavement and berm repair.  The DPW Di rector has no issues 
with the development and paving was completed to his satisfaction.  Throughout construction there was no flagging of the interior 
sidewalks as an issue.  P. Bertram made Motion to release the surety for Emerald Place at Lake Whalom, T. Cole 
Seconded, all aye. 
 
SOLAR BYLAW:  A draft survey was available for member input.  Comments had been submitted by absent member.  Board

be made to the Survey and discussion will continue at the January 13th Board meeting..
