School Reconfiguration Committee -   Televised meeting to be held  at Town Hall, April 10, 2013 to discuss preferred Options.  
The Committee-preferred three Options have been sent to MSBA (Massachusetts School Building Authority) ; they are 1)  9-12 
new build, 2) 6-12 new build, and 3) 6-12 addition/renovation.  The “educators” prefer a 6-12 new build.

Manager, which have been discussed at Finance Committee meetings.   
 
PLANNING DIRECTOR’S REPORTS/NEW BUSINESS/DEVELOPMENT STATUS REPORTS:  
Agricultural Commission - Board of Selectmen approved development of a commission as a Warrant Article for May 2013 
Town Meeting.  The Right To Farm Bylaw will not be presented at May 2013 Town Meeting.  Commission will be under the Town 
Bylaws and the Right To Farm will be under the Zoning Bylaws.   
MRPC- Board has received MRPC contract approval for economic section of the Master Plan.  Once Board reviews, Contract will 
then go to Board of Selectmen for signature.   
Highfield Village, Northfield Road - Planning Director noted Sewer Commission letter (on file in Planning Office) to EOEA 
(Executive Office of Energy and Environmental Affairs) dated April 5, 2013, which discusses the Draft Environmental Impact 
Report submitted by the Applicant.  Ms. Bilotta-Simeone noted a workshop  will be held with the Board of Selectmen and Sewer 
Commission on April 9, 2013 regarding a new Fitchburg IMA (Intermunicipal Agreement) to secure up to an additional 70,000  
gpd (gallons per day) which is intended to include this project’s needs.  Ms. Bilotta -Simeone made Motion to extend Highfield 
Village’s Planning Board extension to July 1, 2013, pending outcome of workshop noted above and the Fitchburg IMA, Second, 
Mr. Lockwood, Motion passed.   
Aro Estates, Howard Street- Hearing Continuation to be held April 10, 2013, Ritter Memorial Building, 6:30 PM   
New England Farms, Leominster-Shirley Road- Plan being revised per Conservation Commission input.  Once revised, will be 
reviewed with Jesse Johnson, David Ross Associates.   
White Tail Crossing- There are issues that need resolution prior to road acceptance at a town meeting.  Tabled at Ms. Benson’s 
request.

William Hannigan, Hannigan Engineering, Inc. represented Mr. White.  The proposed modification is for Phase III.  The original 
plan for Phase III consisted of five (5) buildings; two (2) of them with three (3) units and three  (3) of them with four (4) units.  Ten 
(10) units in three (3) buildings have been constructed to date.  The Modification application to the existing Special Permit is for 
the remaining eight (8) units to be constructed in four (4) duplex units instead of quad type units.   The requested change will 
allow the buildings to be construct ed in a more cost -effective way and also  eliminate the need for sprinkler units in the buildings.  
The total number of units has not changed.  Waivers originally requested are s till in place.  An additional waiver is being 
requested from the Protective Bylaw of the Town of Lunenburg , Section 5.5.1.2.g – “if there is more than one building containing 
dwelling units on a single lot, there shall be a minimum of fifty (50) feet betwe en such buildings ”.  The applicant is asking for a 
waiver down to 30 feet.  The 30 foot distance is due to potential sunroom on the back side of the units that will now be facing 
Jacklyn Drive.  The minimum distance between the main buildings is approximat ely 40 feet.  (Mr. Hannigan noted a 
typographical error in their Review of Waivers – Modification letter updated May 22, 2013.  Paragraph 3 should be June 28, 2005 
– not June 28, 2013.)  Applicant anticipates that this remaining portion of Phase III will b e completed within the next two ( 2) 
years. 
 
Mr. Allison inquired if there was any affordability aspect to the units.  Mr. Wh ite responded that in good faith he had originally 
offered two (2) out of the first  ten (10) to be sold at less than cost, but found  out they were not allowed to be affordable even 
though in the affordable range.   Mr. Hannigan added that they weren’t allowed to be counted as affor dable units under the 
Town’s affordable count as this was done locally and not under the 40B process.  The State would not recognize them.

ANR- 1401 & 1417 Massachusetts Avenue- Presented by Ms. Speidel, Town Manager. When the Primary School was
constructed, it was discovered, through a land survey that the driveway for 1417 Massachusetts Avenue was on Town property.
At that time a land swap took place whereby the Town gave land for to the property owner for the driveway and in return the
property owner gave a parcel to the Town. For this to occur, the Town had to request special legislation. Article 45, at the May
2006 Town Meeting gave the Board of Selectmen permission to petition the legislature for the land swap. The general court
approved the petition in November 2007. Neither the Town nor the property owner submitted an AN once the petition was
approved. Once tonight's ANR is approved by the Board, the Town will prepare a deed, thereby completing this process
Motion, Mr. Lockwood to approve ANR, Second, Mr. McQuaid, all aye. Mylar will be obtained from Whitman & Bingham
Associates and be available in the Planning Office for Board signatures.
