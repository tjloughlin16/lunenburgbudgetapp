SPECIAL TOWN MEETING:  Stormwater Bylaw Warrant Art icle- The question arose at the last Board meeting whether to 
present the amended Stormwater Bylaw at Special Town Meeting on November 17 th or wait until May Annual Town Meeting.  At 
the last Planning Board meeting, T. Cole noted he had talked to DEP, whic h advised him that the Stormwater Bylaw should not 
be approved at fall special town meeting as changes to the Stormwater Handbook would be out in 2021.  Based on that 
conversation, A. Burney contacted DEP, which advised waiting until annual town meeting in  May.  A. Burney then attended the 
Central Mass Stormwater Coalition training on stormwater bylaw, which advised that if the bylaw was ready to go, it should be 
voted.  He also attended a DEP meeting of the working group on the Stormwater Handbook.  The gr oup projected that it would 
be spring before the Handbook would be ready for public comment; the Handbook could then change based on public comment.  
A. Burney noted there was nothing in the Bylaw that would impede the Town from complying with the Handbook .  He 
recommended to the Board that it move forward for presentation at special town meeting.  The Bylaw stated compliance with the  
Stormwater Handbook.  It was P. Bertram’s opinion that as the Bylaw stated that compliance, there was no rush to get it to 
special town meeting.  Due to probable low attendance at special town meeting, she wanted to wait until spring annual town 
meeting.  A. Burney responded that the Board had been working on the Bylaw for approximately a year; it altered the Bylaw to 
meet mandatory requirements imposed by the Phase II Permit.  If it went to special town meeting, and didn’t pass, the Board 
would have until spring to make changes.  The Town would be out of compliance at the end of June 2021 if the Bylaw didn’t pas s 
at annual town meeting.  This Bylaw was not the Town or the Board imposing new regulations; this was to comply with DEP.  A. 
Burney reminded the Planning Board that zoning warrant articles are typically placed at the end of the town meeting, by which 
time attendees are usually tired and want to finish up.  It would not be an advantageous time to present a stormwater bylaw.  
Board discussion to continue at the next meeting.
