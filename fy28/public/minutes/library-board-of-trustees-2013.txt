January 12, 2013 
Meeting called to order at 10:15 a.m. 
 
 
Attending:  Lisa Krowitz, Lars Widstrand, Robin Ven ezia, Martha Moore 
 
 
Public comment – None 
 
Review and propose revisions to existing policies 
 
Participants reviewed, discussed and offered suggested changes to the following policies: 
•  Library Bill of Rights (format changes only) 
•  Operational Safety Policy  
1.  insert two missing words “will be” between the word s goals and based,  
2.  remove an unnecessary comma between therein and or 
•  Art Exhibit Guidelines – Revise policy so that the legal contract and policy are separate 
documents.  Martha Moore will also address the concern that artwork is not consistently 
removed on a timely basis after an exhibit ends. 
•  Borrowing Policies – Recommended an addition to the  policy to address library cards. 
•  Posting Policy – Discussed renaming and reorganizin g to include related policies in a

Appropriate Library Use Policy, Quiet Study Rooms Policy and Library Signage Policy 
were all tabled until the next subcommittee meeting.

•  Technology Plan 
Martha revealed her newly developed, integrated Technology Plan for the library.  Using a dual 
processor server, NComputing L300 boxes with a firefly solution, the plan provides 19 new 
public computers and 10 staff computers.  All software, security and user controls are installed, 
maintained and updated on the one server.  The plan provides estimates that the new hardware 
system will save over 90% of current electricity costs and 75% of the on-going management costs 
associated with the library’s existing computers.  The funds to purchase this system are available
