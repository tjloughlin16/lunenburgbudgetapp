update on the netted backstop at Marshall Park and inquired about the delay. Jack gave a brief
history of the field usage and the reason for the net. He went on to explain that the contractor
has proven difficult to work with and things were not done in a timely manner. Once it was
brought to Jack's attention that the High School had games scheduled that were going to need
to be moved around, he worked with Steve Powell to put up a temporary net so that games

Palma and Stephanie Knight inquired as to the lack of communication. Dennis explained that
the delay was not a secret and that Jack has been in contact on a regular basis with the con-
tractor to try and get this job completed. Karin pointed out that notification of field use needs
to come from the school and as far as she knew the start dates hadn't been given and that it is
not the responsibility of the Park Commission to track down those that may want to use the
field. Dan Proctor asked if the Phillies or Babe Ruth would be able to play there as well. Jack
said yes as long as the temporary netting is sufficient until the permanent one was installed.

fees.
