Minutes of 10/5/23- Master Plan Steering Committee Meeting
Roll Call of Attendees- Chair: Joeci Gilcrest (Economic Dev.), Vice-Chair: Tim Willsmer (Planning Board), Evan Watters (Finance Com.), Karin Menard (Parks), Debbie Lincoln (Council on Aging), Dick Mailloux (Trustees of Library), Lou Franco (Select Board), Jerry Reid (Resident), Adam Burney (Land Use Director), and Carol Archambault (School Committee)
OUTREACH FOR PUBLIC EVENTS- Adam noted that October 21st will be Homecoming and Shrieks &amp; Krieks. OverUnder, RKG and Anez will create 2 teams. One team will set up a table during Homecoming at the Boys and Girls Club, next to the Lions Club.  They will have some info-graphics, surveys, and bookmarks with the QR code for the survey.  They will also attend the Monster Midway at Shrieks &amp; Krieks, to engage more people with kids. Adam said the first big public forum will be held 11/2 in the Library’s community room. They plan on setting the model up and the existing conditions report should be available. The LBA will be running Trunk or Treat 10/28. Joeci noted it will be a walk through this year and a good opportunity to give out promotional material since a lot of adults attend. Joeci will distribute surveys from her trunk and encourage others to do so. Rami is also interested in having a table there and he said the model would be available for 11/2. Adam said the design files are done for the model. It will be a 5’ x 7’ model of the town of Lunenburg and it will be an exaggerated z-access topographic to show various elevations in town. It will have a clear Lexan top with streets and significant buildings on it. You will be able to walk around and make notations with a dry erase marker. It will become a display piece for the town and will help with development patterns. The Committee discussed asking businesses and community organizations to display the surveys. Carol said the school department can distribute them and Karin added the Parks MyRec.com can blast out events. Lou asked how they developed the model and Adam replied they used Lidar at 2’ which gives an accurate representation. Evan has a contact at Boy Scouts and suggested events at Lanni Orchards. Joeci reported she created a Facebook page for the Master Plan Committee and encouraged all to Share with friends. She talked to Rami and there will be a link from the website to the Facebook page. Adam stated they are creating on a website called PlanLunenburg.com which will be separate from the town website but there will be a link to the town’s website. They will be posting updates, material they create and do surveys through the website. There will be an interactive component for comments and signing up for a newsletter. The website should be live by 10/21. Matt (Planning Board) will be meeting with Rami every 2 weeks and Adam will check on use of the room at Library on 11/2. The Committee discussed giving out surveys at Rolling Acres, Arrive Condos and Hickory Hills Association. Debbie suggested putting information on Channel 8 &amp; 9 cable channels. Adam suggested contacting the Lunenburg Ledger to have someone do column on the Master Plan and/or place an ad. He noted the Master Plan does not have an advertising budget but the Planning Board does for placing ads in the Ledger. Lou said announcements can be made at Select Board meetings. They can email Kari McCarthy to put it on all the socials and Facebook pages. Adam suggested sending the survey QR code to all town newsletters. 
COMMITTEE COMMENT- none
PUBLIC COMMENT – Committee agreed to have a meeting on 10/19 at 6pm to go over details for the 11/2 forum. 

Motion: Joeci G.                                                                                                                               Second: Lou F.
To adjourn at 6:41pm.  Roll Call Vote: Dick M., Karin, Jerry, Evan, Lou, Debbie, Tim, Carol and Joeci -all aye.

Respectfully submitted,

Jane L. Ranley

DECIDE ON DETAILS FOR NOV. 2 ND  FORUM  – Joeci  noted that Rami sent an informational 
flyer that they can post and give out. She will ask someone at Town Hall to make copies and will
email it  to the committee members. Joeci  read the verbiage on the flyer stating  a comprehensive
plan is being developed, looking at land use and resources, to help shape the town for the next 
5-15 years .  They need to create a shared vision, requiring input from all corners of Lunenburg. 
There will be several public engagement opportunities, including a public meeting on 11/2 at 
Smokestack Roasters, 6:30- 8:30pm. Rami stated  they will do a short presentation on what they 
are doing and then have 2 breakout sessions. A question will be given,  and the group will divide 
into smaller groups, led by one of their team  to facilitate  discussion. Fun activities will be used to
break the ice and encourage discussions. One member of each group will then report back to 
the larger group. After that, there will be a final open session for questions. Rami is hoping to 
have the town model available to promote interest. They will attend Homecoming and the 
Shrieks & Krieks  events to get information out  about the public meeting. The website, 
planlunenburg.org  will be live next week. On the website, people will be invited to attend the 
meeting and fill out a survey. High School students and the general community filled out a 
previous  survey with 163 responses.  The second survey will have more focused questions that 
will launch before the public meeting but will also ask for responses at the public meeting. Joeci  
asked how the committee could assist  him on 11/2. Rami said they will come with 4 or 5 of their 
team, but it would be helpful if Joeci  or Matt Brenner could say some introductory words 
regarding  the consultants and it being a town initiative. The Steering Committee team is a liaison
between the consultants and the community. Joeci  reiterated their mission is to bring awareness
to the 11/2 public meeting. She asked Rami if the flyer is good to share on Social Media  and 
Rami said yes. Joeci  will post the flyer on the Master Plan Committee Facebook page. Carol 
said she can email it to the Superintendent ’s office who will get to parents of school age kids. 
     Lou asked if PACC will be there to record the public meeting and Joeci  confirmed they will 
record the front table introductions and resident questions .  Breakout sessions will not be 
recorded, and it will not be live because of the location. Lou said he will put the flyer on his page 
and will platform it at Select Board. Rami added if anyone had questions or comments on the 
flyer to let him know. Joeci  asked where the QR code on the flyer goes,  and Rami replied it 
takes you to the website planlunenburg.org . Currently, you need a password to get in but once 
the website is active, it will go right to the website. He added they could make it live tomorrow. 
Rami screen-shared the flyer for the committee to review. Joeci  noted that Smokestack will be 
open, with their full menu for purchase and it was suggested to add a caption stating  that on the 
flyer.

option  would be near the Football Field. Evan suggested moving to the Football Field at 1pm 
and Rami agreed.

Minutes of 12/14/23- Master Plan Steering Committee Meeting  
Roll Call of Attendees- Chair: Joeci Gilchrest (Economic Dev.), Vice-Chair: Tim Willsmer (Planning Board), Karin 
Menard (Parks), Carol Archambault (School Committee), Debbie Lincoln (Council on Aging), Lou Franco (Select 
Board), Jerry Reid, Rami el Samahy (OverUnder) 
Update from Over/Under- Rami noted they had a successful event in November, as well as at Homecoming events, 
and the Boys & Girls Club event.  They have taken all the information to add to the Existing Conditions report which 
he will be sending to the Committee. There have been 205 survey responses so far, but he is concerned about the 
small number of renters participating and would like advice on targeting that demographic. Overall, the responses 
show a good spread of age, income, and education levels, which is a good representation of the town. There is a lot 
of consensus on preserving the agricultural nature of Lunenburg, as well as preserving and repurposing municipal 
buildings. The survey will be open until the end of January, prior to the next public meeting and Rami proposed 
having that event on February 1st. Rami has been in touch with Matt and Joeci bi-weekly since November. Joeci 
asked if the February meeting will be like the November event. Rami replied he would like to coalesce around vision 
and goals, which the survey is helping to achieve, giving some clues as to where they can find consensus. The 
general format will be like the November event, unless the committee would like something different. Regarding 
different times of the day, usually the evenings are more successful. Rami stated the format would be a short 
presentation and then have 2 or 3 breakout sessions for discussion. Joeci noted there were suggestions to seek out 
a different venue to reach other demographics.  Rami stated the venue was successful, but a different venue may 
attract other groups of people. The Committee discussed other options of using a municipal building and meeting 
during the day. Joeci asked Rami if they can pick a day and have multiple sessions to accommodate different groups 
like seniors or working mothers. Rami replied his team could do 2 meetings in a day, such as a morning and evening 
event. Committee members said they could be available and that weather could also be an issue. Karin suggested 
doing the morning session at the Senior Center’s big room or use the Library and Debbie will check availability at the 
Senior Center. Other options discussed were the TC Passios cafeteria, the High School, and Turkey Hill School. 
Carol said she could check into Turkey Hill. The evening session will be 6:30pm- 8:30pm at potentially Turkey Hill
