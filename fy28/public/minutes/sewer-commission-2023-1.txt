OPENING OF PUBLIC HEARING FOR SEWER USE REGULATION CHANGES 
(as posted in the Lunenburg Ledger 2/16/23; Regulations on file with Town Clerk for Public Inspection) 
 
Fitchburg’s Sewer Ordinance Chapter 147 changes of March 2021  
Article II - Building Sewers, Connections, and Extensions 
 II-1(C) – insert information regarding Tie Card and Stub location assistance 
 II-1(E)- insert requirements of Low-Pressure system installer  
 II-1(F)- remove obsolete DEP requirements  
 II-3(F-1)– remove notary requirement on initial Drain Layer application 
 II-3(F-2)- corrections to Workers Comp Insurance levels for licensed Drain Layers to $100K/500K/100K  
 II-3(F-3)- correct requirements by the DPW for Trench Permits and Road Opening Permits 
 II-3(F-4a)- insert requirement of 48 hours’ notice given prior to inspection 
 II-3(F-4h)- added Pipe and slope requirements  
 New ARTICLE II-4- OWNERSHIP AND MAINTENANCE OF BUILDING LATERALS AND DRAINS  
Article IV – Administration 
 NEW IV-2 (C)– adding requirements regarding Direct Connections for Fitchburg and Leominster   
Article VII - Charges and Fees 
 NEW VII-3 (D-1)-adding that no billing adjustment will be allowed for invoices after 6 months  
 VII-3 (D-2), (also, Appendix K- Deduct Meter Policy) - added requirement for that measurement be in cubic 
feet(cf), removed 10/1/20 reference, and referenced web page for equipment requirements.   
 VII-3 (F)-corrected user charges for new connections or re-connections to be pro-rated by month.  (Also, 
removed section VII-3 B-1a regarding prorations (duplication) 
 VII-3 (F-1) Final Reads- Added verbiage for new and old owners to be prorated by month:. 
 VII-4 Connection Charge –reduction of the reconnection fee from $1000 to $100. 
Appendices  
 Grease Trap Regs (Appendix C)    
o Change annual fee for all Food Service Establishments $150 per year.   
o Change initial non-compliance fee from $50 to $100. 
o Remove referrals to SCDA (Small Commercial Discharge Authorization 
o Miscellaneous typos throughout.  correction Section V.   
 Appendix D Extension Regs – to reference waiver language incorporated into Sewer Bylaw at 5/7/22 at 
Annual Town Meeting under Section 6.0 GENERAL SPECIFICATIONS 
 NEW Appendix L – Oil/Water Separator Regulation
