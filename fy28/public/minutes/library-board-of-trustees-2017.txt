Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes  
February 16, 2017; Sudolnik meeting room, Lunenburg Public Library  
  
Attending: Dick Mailloux, Harry Kubetz, Kiirja Paananen, Donna Saiia, Gare Thompson, Susan Visser, Lars Widstrand 
Martha Moore – Director, LPL; John Londa – Director of facilities 
Absent: Terri Burchfield – FinCom representative; Bob Ebersole – Board of Selectmen representative 
 
 
The LPLBoT meeting was called to order at 6:45 p.m. by Chairperson Dick Mailloux. 
 
 Public Comment 
o No public comment 
Agenda Items 
 LED Migration Update – John Londa, Facilities Director, visiting to present status of project.  (The agenda item was 
moved up on agenda to accommodate John.) 
o 2/16: DMH completed 95% of 4ft fluorescent tube fixtures throughout building; some material shortage: as 
soon as material is here, the remaining emergency-lights will be complete. 
Troffers1 in Martha’s office were supposed to have 24 watt LED light source, however 35 watt LED light 
sources were installed.  Two options were suggested by John: dimming, but that will require a special type of 
dimmer (a low voltage control 0-10 V) or to replace the troffers with the model that was meant to be there. 
The children’s area office has 35 ft. cdl. troffers installed.  They are indeed specified to be 35 ft.cdl., but in 
this small space they are very bright. 
o Intense discussion ensued about how to remediate the situation in Martha’s office – dimmer vs. replacing 
fixtures.  The discussion also revolved around the selected color temperature of the troffers, which is making 
the feeling of the space “cold”. 
The board strongly suggested replacing the troffers with the correct ones. 
o It was also suggested that Martha visit offices at LHS to get a “feel” for the lights (specifically color 
temperature and intensity) installed there. 
o DMH (the contractor) retrofit three of the uprights in the main hall.  Lighting (output) reportedly looked great, 
but the fixtures have “pan fans” for the cooling of the LED source, which were deemed too loud for a library 
environment.  These fixtures were removed and replaced with lower wattage, fan-less fixtures. 
Martha mentioned that she had called Guardian:  they are willing to sell their light fixture solution that does 
not incorporate fans to DMH.  The Board asked John to investigate the Guardian option. 
  
                                                     
1 A troffer is a rectangular light fixture that fits into a modular dropped ceiling grid (i.e. 2' by 2' or 2' by 4').  Troffer fixtures have 
typically been designed to accommodate standard fluorescent lamps (T12, T8, or T5), but are now often designed with integral 
LED sources.  (source: Wikipedia)
