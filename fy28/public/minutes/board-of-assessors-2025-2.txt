December 1, 2025, for 2026).

find parcels that were split that include plans that haven't yet been submitted to
the Assessor's office.

• FY 2026 Growth, FY 2026 LA3 and Interim Adjustments. Other items as needed.
In the past month:
- 4 requests for abutters lists were received.
- 43 motor vehicle excise tax abatement applications were received. All
have been processed and are awaiting payment processing by the
tax collector.
- The FY26 real estate exemption applications were mailed on
September 1" with a deadline of April 1, 2026.
- Final notices for functional and economic obsolescence discounts
were mailed.
- The FY26 smart growth cost reports for motor vehicle excise tax and
additional property tax revenue were submitted to DLS.
- The FY26 growth for residential, commercial, industrial and chapter
land was submitted to RRC. The total was $392,380.
The new amounts for the 41D exemption totaled $1,029. Lynda Cajka called DLS
to utilize the social security deduction. For a single person the deduction is $5,980
and their spouse $2,990. Married couples would receive $8,970 which would
come off the income amount for exemptions. This allows more applicants to
qualify for an exemption.
We have already received a number of Chapter Land applications. Deadline is
December 1, 2025.
