Meeting began at 6:00pm with all attendee's present. Sue Andrews was not in
attendance.
Old Business
Minutes of November 15, 2017 were approved unanimously.
New Business
Susan Doherty, Director of the Lunenburg Council on Aging, requested that the
Committee approve the following change: that Pauine Roy's designation as "Council
on Aging Outreach Worker" (Grade 6) (technically known as "Council on Aging
Outreach Counselor" in Schedule A) be reclassified as "Council on Aging Outreach
Coordinator" (Grade 7).
This would change Pauline Roy from Grade 6, Step 3 ($16.75 per hour) to Grade 7
Step 1 ($17.90 per hour).
Susan Doherty indicated that it was difficult to fill the position in the past year
because the pay grade was not as competitive with other towns (Clinton, Lancaster,
Pepperell).
The demands for this position are projected to increase as the Lunenburg
community ages.
Dave McDonald made a motion to change Pauline Roy to Outreach Coordinator
(Grade 7, Step 1) effective July 1, 2018; Erik Arnold seconded the motion. Approved
unanimously.

Minutes of the May 3, 2018 meeting were presented for approval;

Vote: All in Favor
New Business
Salary Action Request: Heather outlined a request for a step increase, submitted 6/25/18 by
Sue Doherty, Director of the Senior Center. Her request was for Ms. Ricci, who is the newly
hired Meal Site Manager. Ms. Ricci started July 2, 2018. The position was a replacement, and
was posted as a Grade 3 Step 1: $12.42PH. Given the broad experience Ms. Ricci had, Ms.
Doherty felt the new employee should not be at Step 1. She learned during hiring process that
other similar roles in other towns paid more and she felt the increase was justified to insure
comparability to similar roles in other towns. There was general discussion regarding any
impact this decision might have on other hires. Nancy Forest confirmed there was no impact to
others by this action.
Motion by S. Chase: Approval for this new hire to be repositioned as Grade 3 step 3 at
$13.18ph, and the adjustment be retroactive to hire date of July 2, 1018.
