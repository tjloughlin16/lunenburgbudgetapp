Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Special Meeting minutes  
March 14th, 2020, Community Room, Lunenburg Public Library  
  
Attending: In Person: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, 
Via Conference Call: Dick Mailloux, Maia Wentrup, Donna Saiia, Lars Widstrand, Michael-Ray Jeffreys - BOS 
 
 
The LPLBT special meeting was called to order at 11:04 am. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz. 
 
  
Agenda Items 
• Discussion of Coronavirus Response 
o Harry: The purpose of our meeting is to hear Muir’s plans for the library in response to the coronavirus and 
discuss what types of services we should try to provide for our patrons during the closure period. 
o Muir: The town has closed all town buildings with the exception of the public safety building. 
o The library will be closed until Friday March 27th. Staff will still be reporting for regular hours. 
o A message from the town manager was posted on the town website. This message contains info about the 
virus. Public safety is the main reason for the closures. There are currently zero cases in Lunenburg.  
o The library has posted a notice on the library website and the front door.  This information will be updated and 
added to as needed. 
o Muir and Lydia have been making task lists for services that may be provided and plan on working on 
projects and tasks they might not normally have time for during normal business hours.  
o The main priority is the safety of the staff and community. 
o Some ideas for services include: utilizing Facebook for live streaming, digital and online services, newsletter 
lists, links to available activities, usage of Cereal Box apps. 
o The library can still service patrons’ inquiries by phone and email. 
o Harry asked about fine suspension. Muir said that CWMars may relax the rules for renewing materials so 
Lunenburg may be able to renew items from other libraries. People can also call to e xtend borrowing time 
and the library has some leeway in extending borrowing time. 
o Donna asked about books dropped off: Is there any risk to the staff? Muir said that they will quarantine 
returned books for a week unless we receive more specific info about the risks from surface contact. 
Quarantined books will not accrue fines. 
o Muir: There has been some discussion about cancelling ILL Interlibrary loan deliveries. No plans for this at 
the current time. 
o Harry polled the group for ideas and questions. Dick: The main priority should be ensuring the safety of the 
staff. We need a full shutdown now. May likely see many new cases and we don’t know the hotspots to 
avoid. Muir: We wish to err on the safe side. Kate: It’s good to be proactive with a two-week closure. We can 
brainstorm ideas for access to materials during the closure. 
o Maia: Can we possibly have some online activities for younger kids to help keep them occupied while 
schools are closed?  Muir: Debbie has posted a STEM activity on the library Fa cebook page after the kids 
programs were cancelled. Heather is queuing up some messages on activities that are planned.  
o Harry: Can we do webcasts? Story hour online? Muir: We may be able to do some of that on  the Facebook 
page. 
o Lars: The main objective is to keep the staff and patrons safe. We shouldn’t go overboard on providing 
services if it’s only a two-week break. Donna: We really have no idea right now how long it might be.  
o Maia: Will the library be professionally cleaned? Muir: There will be one addit ional cleaning per week for a 
total of three cleanings per week. Muir read a statement from the Town Manager on virus info. There is not 
clear or definitive info on the persistence of the virus on surfaces. 
o Michael-Ray: We are in uncharted territory. The response statewide has been a bit behind. It’s good that the 
town is being proactive. Agree with the two-week decision but we don’t know yet how long this will last. 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
o Maia: Are the library staff equipped to work from home? Muir: Some but not all tasks can be  done from 
home. 
o Harry asked about staff getting paid, we should make sure that they don’t have to worry about getting paid 
during the closure. 
o Donna: Should the staff take their temperature before they enter the building? Muir: We have no capability to 
do that currently. 
o Muir announced that Nicole Piermarini has accepted the position of Assistant Director at the Leominster 
Public Library and her last day will be 3/19. He is working on posting the job.  It has to be posted internally for 
7 days before being posted externally. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Meeting was adjourned at 11:57 am.

Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes  
January 16, 2020 Sudolnik Meeting Room, Lunenburg Public Library  
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Lars Widstrand, Maia 
Wentrup, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, Michael-Ray Jeffreys – BOS 
 
The LPLBT special meeting was called to order at 6:46 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz. 
 
Public Comment 
• Harry welcomed Michael-Ray Jeffreys to the BOT meeting. 
• Harry mentioned the clearing of the public walkways during and after snowstorms . Michael-Ray said the library should 
be covered under the town’s public building insurance if there are any liability concerns. 
 
Approval of Minutes 
o The minutes for December 19th, 2019 were reviewed. Motion to approve minutes was unanimously approved 
with a few edits. 
o Minutes for the December 29th special meeting were also approved. 
  
Agenda Items 
• Director’s Report 
o We are 58% through the fiscal year. Teen material spending is a bit behind.  
o Budget requests have been submitted to the town. The Town Manager’s target FY21 budget is effectively 
flat. 
o A request has been submitted to fund an outside consultant for the strategic plan. 
o A new line for programs has been requested. Program attendance has been very high. 
o Muir met with the Town Manager and with FinCom on 2/20 to discuss the requests. 
o Some discussion followed on a few line items: 
▪ Michael-Ray asked why building repairs were double in FY21 from the previous year. FY20 was 
1k. Muir said the Friends have been funding some of these projects. 
▪ Harry asked about the Friends’ total contribution. Muir: around 50k. 
▪ Michael-Ray mentioned that he is advocating for a town facilities manager so the town can have a 
more proactive approach. 
o Harry: The grant from Hannaford for the new shelf in the Children’s room has gone through. 
o The library is partnering with a Hannaford nutritionist for an all ages programs in February. February is 
Happy Healthy Me month. 
o The Fiction/Non-Fiction signs have been removed and will be replaced after some painting is done.  
o The fifth grade visits are going very well. Muir said the goals are being met. Harry asked that we invite  school 
teachers and administrators to attend a BOT meeting to reconnect and touch base. 
 
• Fine Free Discussion 
o Muir recommended a two-stage implementation. 
o 1) Remove fines associated with most items for a juvenile audience <18. 
o 2) Expand to adult items if data from stage one supports a positive impact. 
o Some discussion followed on the amount of fines collected and current policies on collecting fines. 
o Harry polled the board for opinions on the new proposal.  
o A motion to support Muir was proposed by Lars, seconded by Donna and approved by the board.  
 
• Trust Funds – Next Steps 
o Muir is meeting with the town counsel to work on the language for the town meeting warrant article. 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
 
• Friends of LPL Update 
o The cookie walk brought in around $1300. 
o They are considering a chocolate tasting fundraiser possibly in coordination with the June 6 th book and bake 
sale. 
 
• Director Evaluation Completion 
o Harry reviewed the completed evaluation with the board. 
o Some minor changes were discussed and the board voted to accept the review as amended. Dick made the 
motion and Kate seconded. 
o The board agreed on a 4%/$2800 salary increase along with the review. Dick made a motion to accept the 
salary increase seconded by Kate and the motion was approved. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Donna will be absent for the February meeting. 
o Harry offered condolences to Kate on the loss of her mother. 
o Dick thanked Michael-Ray for attending the meeting. 
o Michael-Ray said he would look into a few issues that came up in the meeting: insurance coverage, whether 
there is a public bus stop at the library, snow removal, and trust funds; a nd he also complimented Muir on a 
good job. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Meeting adjourned at 8:57 p.m.

o January Director’s Report 
o FY2021 Budget document 
o Fine Free Recommendation document 
o January 2020 Adult Services Report 
o January 2020 YA Report 
o January 2020 Children’s Area Report

Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes  
February 27, 2020 Sudolnik Meeting Room, Lunenburg Public Library  
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz remotely via Skype, Dick Mailloux, Maia 
Wentrup 
 
Absent:  Lars Widstrand, Donna Saiia 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:47 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• Dick congratulated Muir on his presentation to the town Finance Committee.  
 
Approval of Minutes 
o The minutes for January 2020 were reviewed. Motion to accept minutes as amended by Dick, seconded by 
Maia. Motion was unanimously approved. 
  
Agenda Items 
• Director’s Report 
o We are 2/3’s of the way through the fiscal year. 
o Heating charges were up the past month but are still under budget overall. 
o Materials spending is a bit behind schedule. 95k was spent on materials in FY19. State regulation requires 
16% of the budget be allocated to materials. Muir said this should not be a problem for the current year.  
o Muir has received the FY21 budget from the Town Manager and has sent back requests. The Town Manager 
has submitted the preliminary FY21 budget. 
o Muir noted that the FY21 budget has zero allocated for building maintenance. That cost will be transferred 
over to the DPW budget which will be increased to cover it. 
o Dick was happy to note that the 5k requested for programs was left intact. 
o Muir will attend a User’s Council meeting on March 17th. 
o The annual report was submitted on February 15 th. 
o Lydia will be getting a new desk. 
o The drains backed up in one of the bathrooms on 2/20 and a plumber had to be called.  
o Muir presented some changes to the meeting room policies. You must have a library card to make a 
reservation. There was also a change to the wording regarding room usage by groups.  
o Dick made a motion to accept the policy change, seconded by Maia, and passed unanimously.  
o Muir reported on his presentation to FinCom. It went well. He received some positive comments and 
thoughtful questions. He also talked with them about the proposed fine free policy.  
o Muir expressed his gratitude to the board for his positive performance evaluation, was pleased  that we noted 
staff morale and collaboration, and said it was good to have clear goals explicated.  
 
• Fine-Free Discussion  
o Fines have been dropping off. Proposal is for fines to be replaced by voluntary donations for overdue 
materials. 
o Circulation is up 14% from FY19 for the July through Feb time period. 
o Muir is gathering data with Marabeth to determine before and after differences.  
 
• Trust Funds – Next Steps 
o Muir has been working on the warrant article with the town counsel. 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
o Jim Besarkarski – Chairman of Trust Fund Commission expressed an interest in combining the library 
warrant article with other town trust fund changes. Muir said it would be clearer for the town and voters to 
have a separate library article.  
 
• Friends of LPL Update - 
o A chocolate tasting is being discussed for April 25 th with a wish to include an instructional element. 
o The next book and bake sale will be scheduled for June 6 th, 2020. 
o The annual meeting is scheduled for July 18th. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Dick noted that the town caucus is on March 16th and that Kate and Harry are both up for reelection. 
o Harry notified the board that Lars is resigning as of June 30 th, 2020. 
o Harry congratulated Muir on his review and stated that he constantly exceeds our expectations and that he is 
also an excellent ambassador for the library and the town. 
o Harry passed on a message from Michael-Ray Jeffreys to convey to him any concerns we might have to 
bring to the BOS. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick, seconded by Bob. Meeting was adjourned at 8:11 p.m. 
 
• Attached Documents 
o February 2020 Agenda 
o February Director’s Report 
o FY2021 Budget document 
o February 2020 Adult Services Report 
o February 2020 YA Report 
o February 2020 Children’s Area Report

Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes  
April 16th, 2020  
Remote meeting via conference call and video link. All votes were done by roll call.  Meeting was recorded.  
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Maia Wentrup,  Donna Saiia 
 
Absent:  Lars Widstrand,  
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 7:08 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
 
Approval of Minutes 
o Approval of February 27th BOT meeting minutes deferred. Minutes for March 14 th emergency meeting have 
been submitted to the town. 
 
  
Agenda Items 
• Director’s Report 
o Muir read the Director’s report for the record. 
o The year is 83% complete. 72% of the budget has been expended. 
o Non-essential hiring and expenditures have been frozen for FY20. 
o The YA staff opening will not be posted for the near future. 
o State aid requirements for expenditures and hours have been revised for FY20. LPL will be in compliance 
with state requirements. 
o Budgets for Ebooks and Eaudiobooks have been increased to meet recent demand.  
o Programs have been continuing online via video with story times and staff blogs. Newsletters have included 
links to online resources. 
o The current closure extends to May 4th. If schools are closed beyond that date the library will also be closed 
to ensure the safety of staff and patrons. 
o Staff is continuing to develop new activities and programs: Discord book grou ps, virtual board game groups, 
book lists, and more. 
o Dick asked for clarification on the MAR requirement for FY21. Muir: We will likely be on par with being level 
funded for FY20 to FY21.  
o Dick congratulated Muir on his work on the continuation of library service and the excellent documentation of 
this. 
o The Adult Services report was read by Harry for the record. 
o The Children’s Area report was read by Harry for the record. 
 
• Pandemic Policy  
o Muir read the Pandemic Policy document for the record. 
o Maia suggested a change to #6: generalize to PPE rather than specific items.  
o Dick asked Muir to read #6 with new changes: ‘Library staff will be provided with Personal Protective 
Equipment.’ 
o Dick made a motion to accept the policy as amended, seconded by Maia. Motion was passed unanimously. 
 
• Responsibility Policy 
o Muir read the document for the record. 
o Discussion followed on the definition of time periods, exceptions and how specific the policy should be.  

===PAGE 2===
 
 
o Muir pointed out that the possible lack of specific definition gives him some flexibility in its interpretation. 
o A 5th bullet point was suggested. Muir added: ‘When a prolonged absence is recognized, the BOT may seek 
to hire a temporary qualified person through the MA Library System BiblioTemps emp loyment service.’ 
o Donna made a motion to accept the policy as amended, seconded by Kate. Motion was passed 
unanimously. 
 
• Operational Safety Policy 
o Muir read the document for the record. 
o Kate suggested making the last sentences more concise. 
o Bullet #4 changed to: ‘This notice will inform patrons of the time of closing, the reason for closing, and 
anticipated time of reopening.’ 
o Dick made a motion to accept the policy as amended, seconded by Maia. Motion was passed unanimously.  
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The Friends have not had a meeting since March. No update. 
 
• Board Comments 
o No board comments. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Kate, seconded by Dick. Meeting was adjourned at 8:42 p.m.

were starting curbside pickup. 
o Two self-checkout stations will be added when the library reopens again. Self service is a little more difficult 
to coordinate and this will be worked on once the staff is back in the building. Harry asked about the 
possibility of people handling someone else’s material. Muir said perhaps patrons can designate whether 
they want self-service. Books could be easily identifiable with their name and possibly with the covers 
showing.

o Summer reading plans are being revised.

o Harry asked if Main St. Bank had reached out recently and asked if it would be helpful for him to contact 
them.  Muir said ‘yes.’ 
o Facebook engagement has been down a bit. Maia asked if events could be posted the night before rather 
than the morning of, in order to give parents a bit more time to plan. 
o The Friends have decided to purchase two software resources: Beanstack and CreativeBug.  
 
• Fine-Free Plan 
o The plan was to use the summer reading timeframe for the first phase, removing overdue fines for Children’s 
and YA materials. Fines will be replaced with voluntary donations. The plan is to proceed with this.  
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o No Friends update. They will be meeting remotely in June.

o This budget will be up again at special town meeting in the fall when the info for state aid is known.

will begin pre-registering for summer reading.

o Plexiglass dividers have not been installed yet. Hand washing stations are in place. There is a town -wide 
control plan but Muir created a library specific checklist. 
o Muir presented the newsletter, children’s area, and facebook engagement stats.  Instagram stats are up for 
adult services with 195 followers and 117 posts. 
o Hannaford sponsored half height shelf has been installed in the children’s area. It opens up the room and lets 
in more light. 
o Kate asked about how the library is paying for the cleaning and PE supplies. A lot of supplies come from the 
DPW.  Library budget doesn’t cover the increased cost of those supplies.
