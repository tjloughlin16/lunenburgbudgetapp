New Business:
Demolition Request for Barn at 133 Leominster-Shirley Road
The applicant has submitted a letter stating that he plans to dismantle the barn and relocate it on
another site. The Commission has decided that the demolition delay by-law will apply to this
building. However, pending more information about the details of the applicants plan to move the
structure, the commission will entertain the release of the demolition delay before the six month
time frame to accommodate the move. Becky will contact the applicant asking that he attend our
next meeting to provide more information about the planned move.
Demolition Request for house and out buildings at 305 Leominster-Shirley Road
The Commission has found that the demolition request is incomplete and does not contain
enough information (i.e. pictures of said structures and approximate date of construction) to make
a decision. Becky will ask that the applicant provide that information to our next meeting for
Board and Public Comment:
Damon noted a break down in communication between the building department and the Historic
Commission as it relates to demolition requests. He will contact the building inspector and work out
a way to streamline the process.

Lunenburg Historical Commission
Meeting Minutes
October 7, 2014.
Members Present:
Becky Lantry, Amory Phelps, Richard McGrath, Lois Lewis.
Members Absent:
None
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order at 6:30 p.m. after a viewing of 75 Holman Street, the North
Cemetery Office Building, at 5:00 p.m.
Minutes Approval:
Minutes from 09/02/2014 were approved as submitted.
Minutes from 09/11/2014 were amended Richard McGrath was present.
Correspondence and Communications:
Letter from Freedom's Way Heritage Area to sponsor a free public program for area's
"hidden treasurers" May 16" and 17", 2015.
Letters from building inspector and fire department in regard to 75 Holman Street for
demolition request.
Old Business:
Demolition Delay Requests:
75 Holman Street. Present from the Cemetery Commission were Commissioners Stephen
Schaff and Lance D. May. Greg Bingham in attendance. After review it was agreed
here were alternatives to demolition. Request was denied with prejudice. Section 2:
Demolition Delay Bylaw, Demolition Plan Review d) 3.
90 West Townsend Road: The Commission voted by a majority for demolition by
neglect.
1790 Massachusetts Avenue Lot 33 (Mobile Home). The Commission voted this 1988
unlivable mobile home did not warrant protection under the demolition delay bylaw.
Becky will send notice to building inspector.

2
Lunenburg Ledger newspaper articles, Historically Speaking.
Richard to write for November
Becky to write for December
Commission expressed interest to participate in the Freedom's Way Heritage Area's
Program for "hidden treasurers" in May 2015.
Discussion designating Flat Hill Road and Northfield Road and classification as scenic
roads. Planning Board has requested a letter on this issue with consideration of 1833
New Business:
Cullen Dwyer present to express his interest in joining the Historical Commission. The
Commission unanimously approved and recommends his appointment.
Adam Burney, Lunenburg Land Use Director, invited to attend next meeting of the
Historical Commission November 4*.
Planning Board meeting October 14" considering solar farm 322 Flat Hill Road.
Discussion of plaque identifying the Jones House as an important historical site.
Meeting Adjournment:
Meeting adjourned at 8:15 p.m.
