Trustees of the Lunenburg Public Library 
January 20, 2011 
 
Meeting was called to order at 6:30 p.m. 
Attending: Noelle Bodkin, Lisa Krowitz, Dick Mailloux, John Mara, Kathy Murray, Jeanne Raboin, Amy Sadkin, Len Smetana 
 
 
1. There was no public comment. 
2. New Business 
 December 16, 2010 minutes approved unanimously. 
 Director’s Report (please see report for more detail) 
o Budget: FY12 budget submitted to Town Manager; FY11 budget on track, salary variances for 
December, YTD and Total Year are under; as of December 2010, FY2011total budget under 
$63, 907. 
o Circulation: a bit under for December 2010 (FY11); Meeting Room usage also down for 
December 2010. Reasons could be due to weather, holiday and School groups using their 
rooms. 
o Staff Update: Sam Reggio hired as “fill-in” help; Amy attended A Director’s Roundtable on 
1/14/11, discussion was about eReaders; LPL Kindle lending program and policy was noticed by 
a Sydney, Australia librarian. 
o Facilities: Clean Carpets did free spot cleaning in December when library was cl osed; snow/ice 
removal has been adequately taken care of and the HVAC is being carefully watched.  
o Technology Update: Jen continues to work on the main hall computer monitor and it will soon be 
ready for use. 
o Programs: ALL of the children, YA and adult programs are very well-attended and successful. 
Herbalist Kenyon Keily will be speaking the last week in January 2011.  
o Friends/Endowment: $5,000 dontatin from the estate of Vida Waller was placed into the 
Endowment Fund; Friends have written a media release describing the mission of the Friends 
and Endowment; fundraisers and programs being discussed for 2012: LPL mini -golf, wine and 
beer tasting, new Cultural Horizons series, Book & Bake sales. 
o Old Mutual Fund account has shown an increase, total earnings for last calendar year: $784.09 
3. Board of Selectmen Meeting – January 18, 2011 
 Trustees are welcome to attend; Amy will present the FY12 budget in the format requested by Kerry Speidel.  
 Highlights: funding for CWMars network fee, 4 additional hours of operation; 3% utilities increase; 5% 
increase for Clerical Union employees, SalAdmin employees 3%. Also an increase in Mileage 
Reimbursement and Professional Fees is included. 
 Salary Adjustments: have been based on Town Manager’s FY12Forecast.  
 Motion made and passed unanimously to accept FY12 Budget Revised Line Items.  
 
 
Meeting adjourned at 7:45 p.m. 
Next meeting: February 17, 2011 at 6:30 p.m. 
 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
 
Jeanne Raboin 
Secretary

Trustees of the Lunenburg Public Library 
February 17, 2011 
 
Meeting was called to order at 6:02 p.m. 
 
Attending: Lisa Krowitz, Dick Mailloux, John Mara, Kathy Murray, Jeanne Raboin (late), Amy Sadkin 
Absent: Noelle Bodkin, Len Smetana 
 
1. There was no public comment. 
2. New Business 
 Motion made by Kathy Murray and seconded by Dick Mailloux to approve the January 20, 2011 minutes, 
passed unanimously. 
 Update of Information of the FY 2012 Budget: Amy will present FY 2012 budget to Fin Comm on March 3 
at 7 pm. 
 Revised Line Items based on Town Manager’s Recommendations for FY 2012 Budget: Discussion about 
revised line items. Motion made and passed unanimously to accept the Revised 2012 Library Budget as 
proposed on February 17, 2011.

 FY 2011 Budget: Salary is under projection; library materials a little under; will try to work with Jim to lower 
electricity usage. 
 Circulation: compared to last year circulation numbers are a little lower. These numbers could be reflecting 
the snow days where the library was closed. 
 Staff update:  
o Amy attended an Emergency Management meeting and has volunteered chairs, 
books, games, etc. and staff is also willing to assist at an emergency shelter.  
o Amy spoke at the Groton Public Library’s February 2011 Legislative Breakfast.  
o Jen attended an eBooks class and has offered to teach staff and patrons about 
the technology. 
o Staff meeting held on February 10, 2011.

 Programs: Attendance is up; some highlights: Valentine’s Day Chocolate Dipping, Yoga program with

 Friends/Endowment: Friends have added Pawtucket Red Sox tickets to the Museum Pass program; 
approved ordering additional library card holders; there are 1,043 households that are members of the 
Friends of LPL.

of Huckleberry Finn by Mark Twain; the computer in the Main Hall is still a work in progress. 
4. Other Library Business 
 Discussion of letter to Trustees relating to use of the Wallace Room.

Trustees of the Lunenburg Public Library 
March 17, 2011 
 
Meeting was called to order at 6:30 p.m. 
Attending: Noelle Bodkin, Lisa Krowitz, Dick Mailloux, John Mara, Kathy Murray, Jeanne Raboin, Amy Sadkin, Len Smetana  
 
 
1. No public comment 
2. February 17 minutes unanimously approved as amended. 
3. Amy: Budget report to Financial Committee went well; Town manager asked us to consider using use state aid to 
support additional hours; figure recommended from last trustees meeting has been accepted; Kerry sent information 
that local aid may not be funded at recommended level. 
4. Town Caucus: March 21, 2011: Kathy Murray and Lisa Krowitz agree to have their names submitted to Town Caucus.  
5. Director’s Report (Please see report for more details.) 
 Budget and Circulation: Staff salaries little adjustment from last month, reconciled with town figures; Kate’s 
longevity, Karen’s in June; salaries still a little under, however, just hired someone for part -time summer 
work, 12.08 per hour, Kerry approved. Person starting Monday is Alex Lent. Rest of budget on track, holding 
steady in all areas. 
 Quick discussion about e-books, number of downloads being limited; CW Mars will be meeting tomorrow to 
try to come to some agreement. 
 Building/Facilities: New hinges for the door not yet installed, door will not be under warranty after new 
installation.  Cub Scout group left running water on in the boy’s bathroom, den mother contacted -  soap 
dispenser snapped off and soap all over the place; many kids on Wednesday, vandalized men’s bathroom, 
police called; sign was placed on door, if need to will begin locking the doors again. DM suggests changing 
the locks to something that self-locks. JM suggests burning a cd from the security tape and sending to the 
middle school principal. 
 Circulation: Slightly down, meeting room usage up in Feb.2011.  Averaging 220 people on Saturdays (see 
report). Weeding the collection: VHS tapes and Books on Tape, both items will eventually be eliminated.  
 Staff Update: AS participated in L.H.S. Career Day; staff participated in webinars on Evergreen and Teen 
Theme/Children’s theme for summer reading programs; staff reviews for clerical union employees sent out, 
also included were director’s suggested goals and own (personal) goal’s. 
 Technology: Website purchased by town, AS having trouble getting information about maintaining, 
transferring information, etc. Jen remains on the committee. 
 Programs: Puzzle and Game Swap; National Poetry Month in April and there will be programs collaborating 
with Kate and local poetry groups. 
 Friends: Investigating new passes; Cultural Horizons and mini-golf in 2012. 
 Other – Historical Commission: dropped off thumb drive with town records dating back to 1721 with original 
land grants, very large files; discussion about how to give the best access to the community for these 
records. Swamped with volunteers, having them switch the barcodes on old items, however does takes much 
staff time to train. $12,000 Revolving fund article submitted to the Town Meeting warrant for FY12.  
 Town Meeting is Saturday May 7th. 
6. State Aid Money for FY 2011: $6,551.68 is due to come to Lunenburg and will be placed into the library. Will have 
received $12,000+ this year. 
7. Staffing levels and scheduling: AS reviews this with the Trustees. Primary issue: to have enough people in the pm, 
goal is to have 4 all day long, more in afternoon if possible – we are short-staffed with 5.5 FTE. One person scheduled 
to be off from now through the summer;  Deb will be out on medical leave for awhile; there are other staff health issues 
with some unexpected leave. Trustees and Library Director discussed time-off request procedures. 
Motion made and passed to adjourn at 7:34 pm. 
Next meeting: April 14, 2011 at 6:30 pm. 
Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
Jeanne Raboin, Secretary

Trustees of the Lunenburg Public Library 
April 14, 2011 
 
Meeting was called to order at 6:30 p.m. 
 
Attending: Noelle Bodkin, Lisa Krowitz, Dick Mailloux, John Mara, Kathy Murray, Jeanne Raboin, Amy Sadkin, Len Smetana  
No public comment. 
1. Minutes – Motion made by Dick Mailloux and seconded by Len Smetana, passed unanimously to accept minutes as 
amended. 
2. Freegal Music: A music service where the library pays upfront, then free to patron to download ; motion made and 
passed unanimously to have Amy obtain more information and report to Board at May meeting. 
Question – Do we track log-ins from home computers? Amy does get counter numbers for use of website and 
databases. 
3. Director’s Report (Please see report for more details) 
a. Highest circulation ever for March; increase in meeting room usage, computer use remains steady; of the 
12,113 items circulated, 1,282 were ILL’s. 
b.  Alex Lent is now on board; scheduling continues to be an issue; Bonnie and Jen have taken on a few more 
hours. One staff longevity is outstanding. 
c. Budget: Salaries a bit different due to 3 pay periods; library materials expenditure is up due to big purchases. 
i. Budget Adjustment FY 2011: Mileage increased, Amy Sadkin thinks it’s due to her being on the 
CWMARS Executive Board suggests taking it out of postage and water/sewer; shouldn’t need too 
much more, probably $100. Motion made by Kathy Murray and seconded by Dick Mailloux to take 
money out of 1st postage, 2nd sewer, and then 3rd water of up to $200 and place into Postage 
account – passed unanimously. 
ii. Utilities will probably even out by the end of the year. 
iii. Materials expenditures will probably be about $65,000 this year. 
d. CWMars Statistics: As of January 2011 LPL had 6,843 patrons, please see report for breakdown by user -
type; 55,709 items in collection. 
e. Amy attended CWMars Executive Meeting and User’s Council Meeting; new ILL delivery service starting July 
1 – Optima is the company. Paperless ILL’s will begin next year. Evergreen won’t be up by July 1; won’t 
continue Millennium circulation service, so may be some issues between Millennium being discontinued 
(contract up in July) and Evergreen coming up in October. 
f. Facilities Update: empty liquor bottles found in Men’s room in March; a Monday night meeting notified that 
they left water running in ladies room and lights on; an interior window in the Children’s room cracked, 
replaced by facilities. 
g. Lion’s club offered to do mulch; students needing community service hours will do raking; Amy will ask 
Friends to pay for lawn care (Lakeview) and removal of dead shrubs; Town will do mowing. 
h. Technology: Jen has computer monitor up and running 
i. Programs: April library programs – Nursery Rhyme Times, story hours, Lego club, May Baskets and Wildlife 
Owl Program. Teen programs – Red Eye, GBC and TAG Book Clubs. Adult programming – knitting, 
computer classes and teen/adult poetry programs. In addition to these the Quilts of Valor are on display 
throughout April. 
j. Town meeting: May 7, 2011. 
 
4. State budget looking good, should help with Town Hall projections; discussion about Town Manager and Fin Comm 
recommendations and budget. 
5. Nothing yet about Town Meeting Agenda and potential questions for LPL.  
6. Town election May 21, 2011 
Meeting adjourned 7:25 pm. 
Next meeting May 19, 2011 at 6:30 pm.

Meeting was called to order at 6:43 p.m. 
 
Attending: Noelle Bodkin, Lisa Krowitz, Dick Mailloux, John Mara, Len Smetana, Amy Sadkin  
 
Absent: Jeanne Raboin 
 
No Public comment. 
 
1. Minutes - Motion made by Dick Mailloux and seconded by Noelle Bodkin, pass unanimously to accept the 
minutes from the April 14 meeting. 
2. Review of Town Meeting – The technology budget included 15 computers, a wireless printer and a color 
copier for the Library. 
3. Director’s Report (see report for details) 
a. Circulation/Budget: Fine money is down year over year; e-mail reminders are helping.  Attendance, 
meeting room usage and computer usage are up. Salaries and utilities are in line. Library materials 
will be up in May due to magazine renewals and purchases of books on CD. 
b. Staff:  
i. Jen attended a CWMARS meeting re:Evergreen and the switch over to the new system, 
the database will be unavailable for four days, all patrons will need to have their library 
cards to check items out.  Signs will be posted.  
ii. Sherri attended a meeting on an alternative meeting room booking software, but found 
the current software better suits LPL’s needs.  
iii. Patty attended an Evergreen update re: cataloguing changes. 
iv. Kate and Karen are working on Summer Programs. 
v. Amy was invited to join the Board of the Mass Library Aid Association, attending 
CWMARS Executive Committee and User’s Council meetings and has been acting as a 
proctor for local residents taking tests  
vi. David Berthiaume, long-time Sr. Tax write-off worker will no longer be doing our yard 
work/recycling. 
c. Technology – Jen’s computer has a virus which affected PCRserve, CWMARS is working with Jen 
to correct the issue. 
d. Facilities: The front door hinges are fixed, an extra set has been ordered. 
e. Programs: On May 14th, the Friends sponsored The BUS-eum, a traveling exhibit about German 
POW’s titled, “Held on the Homefront”, nearly 40 people attended. On May 26th the Never Again

following WWII. Kate wrote a grant and received $1,000.00 for the Teen Summer programs.

passes, Butterfly Place and Garden in the Woods.   Spring Book and Bake Sale is Saturday, Jun e 
4th.

Mara with a second by Len Smetana to try the program with an initial expenditure of $1,440 for 52 weeks/3

Fund.

½% to the steps in 2013. 
6. Discussion about Board Reorganization to occur at the June Meeting. 
7. Other Business: Discussions about the town website and library trustee overview.   Wildberry a monthly e-
mail service is used by Leominster to communicate what’s happening at the library, check it out.  
 
Meeting adjourned 7:43 p.m. 
Next meeting June 16, 2011 at 6:30 p.m.
