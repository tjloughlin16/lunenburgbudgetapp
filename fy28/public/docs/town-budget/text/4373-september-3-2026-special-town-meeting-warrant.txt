TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
SPECIAL TOWN MEETING WARRANT 
September 3, 2026 
Worcester, ss: 
To:  Constable of the Town of Lunenburg, in the County of Worcester, Greetings: 
 
In the name of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, you are hereby directed to notify and warn the 
inhabitants of the Town of Lunenburg, qualified to vote in elections and town affairs, to meet in the 
Lunenburg Middle/High School Auditorium in said Lunenburg on T hursday, t he 3rd day of 
September, 2026 at 7:00 o'clock in the evening (7:00 P.M.), for the purpose of taking action on the 
Special Town Meeting Warrant, and then and there to act on the following articles, viz: 
 
SPECIAL TOWN MEETING ARTICLE(S) 
 
ARTICLE 1:  AMENDING FY27 SCHOOL BUDGET 
(Submitted by School Department) 
 
To see if the Town will vote to raise and appropriate an additional sum of Four Hundred Eighteen 
Thousand Fifty-Six Dollars and No Cents ($418,056.00) for the FY 27 operating budget for the School 
Department, or take any other action relative thereto.   
 
FINANCE COMMITTEE RECOMMENDATION:  
SELECT BOARD RECOMMENDATION:  
VOTE REQUIRED: 2/3rds  
 
Summary:  On July 7, 2026, Governor Healey signed the FY27 State Budget, which made available 
$418,056.00 more in education-specific receipts than the Town’s FY27 budget initially anticipated. 
The School Committee is requesting $418,056.00 from these additional education receipts from the 
state for the FY27 school year. This article would appropriate that amount to the school budget. 
  
ARTICLE 2:  AUTHORIZATION TO SUPPLEMENT THE SCHOOL DEPARTMENT’S 
FISCAL YEAR 2027 OPERATING BUDGET 
(Submitted by Citizens Petition) 
 
To see if the Town will vote to raise and appropriate, or transfer from available funds, a sum of money to 
supplement the School Department’s Fiscal Year 2027 (FY2027) operating budget, or take any other 
action relative thereto.   
 
FINANCE COMMITTEE RECOMMENDATION:  
SELECT BOARD RECOMMENDATION:  
VOTE REQUIRED: 2/3rds if raise and appropriate* and majority if transfer 
 
*Per Section 81-9 of the Town Code, “[n]o money shall be raised or appropriated at a Special Town 
Meeting except by two-thirds vote of the voters present and voting.” 
 
Summary:  This warrant article was submitted by petition requesting a Special Town Meeting for 
the purpose of supplementing the Fiscal Year 2027 operational budget of the School Department.  
 
ARTICLE 3: SUPPLEMENT TO THE LUNENBURG PUBLIC SCHOOLS FISCAL YEAR 2027 
OPERATING BUDGET 
(Submitted by Citizen’s Petition) 
 
To see if the Town will vote to transfer a sum of money in the amount of $151,338.38 from Certified Free 
Cash or available funds to the Lunenburg Public Schools Fiscal Year 2027 Operating Budget for the 
purpose of supplementing the athletic department budget to reinstate middle school sports as well as 
provide transportation for student athletes; or take any other action relative thereto. 
 
FINANCE COMMITTEE RECOMMENDATION:  
SELECT BOARD RECOMMENDATION:  
VOTE REQUIRED: Majority 
 
Summary:  No summary was included with the submitted petition.
