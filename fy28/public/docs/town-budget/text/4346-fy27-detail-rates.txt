agreed upon by contract between the Town and the NEPBA, Local 353 Police Union. Effective on 
Wednesday, July 1, 2026 the following rates for extra service details for off-duty police officers shall be in 
effect: 
1. Hourly Rate   $65.8106 
4 Hour Minimum $263.2424
