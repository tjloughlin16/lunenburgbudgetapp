Goal #1: Codify and Improve Processes and Procedures 
 
• Objective 1: Direct each department to produce a baseline written procedures 
document for its core recurring functions.  First/initial drafts by end of October with 
an expectation that a comprehensive draft would be prepared by end of FY.  These 
are expected to be “living” documents. 
 
• Objective 2: Continue to provide training opportunities around MUNIS- with at 
least 3 trainings in the FY. 
   
• Objective 3: Continue implementing employee development initiatives including 
additional Town-wide training opportunities throughout year so that there is at 
minimum 6 total trainings (including the 3 MUNIS-related trainings).   
 
• Objective 4: Explore outsourcing elements of payroll to Harpers/ADP-type entity.  
Complete cost-benefit analysis on the same by end of FY27 Q3.  Explore digitizing payroll 
submissions ASAP. 
 
• Objective 5: Establish a standardized onboarding and orientation process for new 
employees.  Create an updated orientation materials package and process with a  
target roll-out date of January 1, 2026.

Goal #3: Budgeting and Finance 
 
• Objective 1: Work with the Department Heads to present “early views” of budgets to 
FC in December 
 
• Objective 2:  Work with Department Heads to develop/update 5-10 year staffing 
plans/projections (to include cost projections). 
 
• Objective 3:  Provide support for Director of Planning and Economic Development 
to propose zoning changes to encourage reasonable economic development. 
 
• Objective 4:  Continue to provide opportunities for public engagement on the 
budget by continuing to hold pre-Town Meeting forum that began with the FY26 
budget.  Develop FY27 budget web page in early calendar 2027 and keep updated as 
“real-time” as possible 
 
• Objective 5:  Work with the Finance Committee, Select Board, staff, and other 
stakeholders to complete the review of, and modify as necessary, the draft Financial 
Policies.  This was a FY26 goal which had been tabled due to transition in the 
Finance Director role.  The Finance Committee has identified several policies they 
would like to focus on first: OPEB, Stabilization Accounts/Reserves, Free Cash, and 
Revenue projections.  To achieve this objective I would like to bring in various
