The Town of Lunenburg will offer 
income eligible senior citizens age 60+ 
an opportunity to receive up to $1500 
off their property tax bill. 
Participants work in various Town 
Departments to earn the tax abatement. 
Between January 1, 2026 and November 
1, 2026 a person will receive $15.00 per 
hour, up to 100 hours, to receive the 
$1500 abatement. Any hours worked 
beyond that will be considered volunteer 
hours. 
Application package can be picked up at: 
Eagle House 
Lunenburg Adult Activity Center 
25 Memorial Drive 
Lunenburg, MA 01462 
OR 
Town Hall 
17 Main Street 
Lunenburg, MA 01462 
All new applicants will require a CORI 
background check. 
Earnings are exempt from state 
taxes only. Earnings are subject to 
federal and Medicare tax 
withholdings and are also subject to 
OBRA if participant is not receiving a 
pension from either Worcester 
Regional Retirement or 
Massachusetts State Teachers 
Retirement. 
All necessary paperwork must be 
completed before any work is done. 
All applicants must apply and be 
accepted annually for participation 
in the program.
