FY27 Summary & RecapPAC has experienced a decline in revenue from Comcast over the past two years and has had an increase in hours providing services to the town. Special government presentations, informational sessions, and meetings are on the rise causing a spike in videographer and production hours.  Due to this growing number of recording requests, resources are stretched thin with few options. PAC has reached a crossroad, where it has become important to make immediate and necessary decisions to ensure its future.Moving forward, considering the decline in revenue, PAC will need to seek alternative revenue sources including Town resources, or a reduction in personnel and/or their hours.  A lower headcount will result in a shift in priorities away from content produced locally for the Public and Education channels.  Therefore, there would be no coverage of popular concerts, sports, events and presentations.  The Government channel would attempt to remain at a level service, whether staff is cut or the Town assists in funding PAC, as all available resources would be used for government meetings.Funding assistance from the Town would help us maintain level services on all channels, Public, Educational and Government (PEG).  In addition, unfunded media channels such as YouTube and Facebook could continue to be available. Having the Town supplement the budget would mitigate the shortfall in Comcast revenue and essentially give the Town a stake in PAC’s overall health.  PAC could therefore continue to provide local programming to keep the community entertained, informed and updated. Without the Town’s participation in contributing funds, PAC’s mission and current service levels may not be fully realized.
