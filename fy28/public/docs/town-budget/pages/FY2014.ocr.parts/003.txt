   2|                      The Firefighters and Emergency Medical Technicians of the Lunenburg Fire Department are
   3|                 dedicated to providing the highest quality, most cost effective fire and emergency medical services to
   4|                                                              the citizens of the Town of Lunenburg.
   5|                          In 2014 the Fire Department responded to 1957 calls for service as follows:
   6|                          Fires & Hazardous Conditions                               521                     Emergency Medical                 914
   7|                          Inspections                                                458                     Other                             64
   8|                          Fire Department staffing today is five career staff and forty- forty-five on call firefighters and
   9|                mergency medical technicians. The station is statted Monday through Friday trom b a.m. - 6 p.m
  10|                lowever, on weeknights and weekends all of our personnel respond from home on an "on call" basis
  11|              This adds several minutes or more to our response time as personnel must respond to the station, pick up
  12|              the appropriate equipment and then respond to the call. This staffing model has not seen a substantial
  13|              change in over 30 years, even though we have experienced a nearly 400% increase in calls for service
  14|              during that period. While we have added four additional full time personnel in that time to bolster our
  15|              day time response, it has only resulted in a 12% increase in the time the station is manned. We still rely
  16|               on our on call personnel to be the primary responders 64% of the week.
  17|                          2014 was our busiest year to date, and given the growth occurring in town, I can only anticipate
  18|              further increases in service requests. While the commitment shown by our call force is nothing short of
  19|              amazing, the ever increasing call volume is straining our ability to provide the high level of service
  20|              townspeople have come to expect. These factors lead me to continue to call for 24 hour staffing in the
  21|              station. Sufficient funding to maintain two Firefighter/EMT's in the station 24 hours a day, 7 days a
  22|              week would allow either the first ambulance or first piece of fire apparatus to respond immediately. Not
  23|              only would this reduce our response times to critical emergencies, it would also reduce the "wear and
  24|               tear" on our call staff. This coverage would in no way reduce the need for our on call personnel; it would
  25|               only supplement their response and reduce response times.
  26|                          Our firefighters continue to train for the myriad of situations which can be encountered even in a
  27|              town such as ours. Today's firefighter must be skilled in not only firefighting, but medical care,
  28|               hazardous materials mitigation and technical rescue. Our EMT's must continue to stay abreast of the
  29|              latest treatment protocols and advances in lifesaving skills. Personnel train regularly both here at the
  30|              station and at classes sponsored by other agencies. While they are compensated for some of the training,
  31|              the vast majority is done on their own time. To give an example, a new Firefighter/EMT must attend over
  32|              340 hours of basic firefighting and emergency medical technician training. Once they are certified they
  33|              must complete over 40 hours of continuing education and refresher training every two years, just for their
  34|              EMT. Operators of fire apparatus must complete an additional 65 hours of training in pump and aerial
  35|              operations, hydraulics calculations and vehicle operation.
  36|                           Several of our personnel are members of Regional Technical Rescue units, providing services for
  37|              Confined Space and Cave in Rescue, as well as Underwater Search and Recovery. They stand ready to
  38|              respond as needed throughout Northern Worcester County.

   4|                       early childhood education for all students. We are a community of teachers, students, and parents

  11|                       classes servicing one hundred and ten students. The class sizes in our Kindergarten programs
  12|                       range from 21-22 students. First grade has one hundred and twenty-seven students with class
  13|                       sizes ranging from 25-26. Second grade has one hundred and twenty-three students with class
  14|                       sizes of 24-25 and the third grade has one hundred and thirty two students with class sizes of 26-

  16|                       educational program for all our students.
  17|                                     Our Professional Development this year focused on ALICE training, the Teacher
  18|                        Evaluation process, the Common Core curriculum, and Curriculum Mapping updates. The
  19|                        ALICE training is a proactive satety protocol used in the event of an active shooter. This is
  20|                       necessary to enhance our current safety procedures and processes. All of our classroom teachers
  21|                       continued to use Responsive Classroom strategies as part of our Academic/Social/Emotional
  22|                       Learning Curriculum which includes morning meetings, guided discovery, rules and logical
  23|                        consequences and academic choice.
  24|                                     The Primary School community continues to strive for academic excellence. Common
  25|                       assessments are in place in the fall, winter, and spring for all children in grades K-3 in the areas
  26|                       of reading, writing, and math. These common assessments serve to monitor the effectiveness of
  27|                       teacher instruction and drives instructional programming for our students. We are now able to
  28|                       track progress throughout the year in a faster, more efficient manner in order to improve
  29|                       instruction. Students are identified quickly and additional resources and direct instruction are
  30|                       provided to address the areas of concern identified. The Primary School is a Title One school and
  31|                       we continue to utilize a Response to Intervention (Title One/RTI) approach to address student
  32|                       needs. Our tiered instructional system insures that all students have the instruction necessary to
  33|                       continue good progress with their learning. Tiered instructional services are provided in the area
  34|                       of Language Arts (Reading and Writing) and Math. The first tier involves the largest number of
  35|                       students, and is fitted for universal instruction in the classroom. Teachers are skilled in
  36|                        differentiating instruction to meet a large number of student needs in the classroom. The second
  37|                       tier combines more classroom teacher support and Title One Support for students. Title One/RTI
  38|                       services are based on student needs that are targeted, individual, and involve more intensive
  39|                       interventions. The third tier is the smallest and involves special services with very intense
  40|                       interventions. The tiered instructional model requires that the teachers not only individualize
  41|                       instruction to meet the needs of the students who are struggling with learning but those students
  42|                       who are doing well and need to strive to achieve higher levels of learning. With this model
  43|                       learning is more personalized and students are taught to become independent learners and
  44|                       problem solvers.
