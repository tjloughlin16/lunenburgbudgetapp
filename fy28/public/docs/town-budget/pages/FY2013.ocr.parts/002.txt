  23|              a winter operations procedure based upon a well thought out plan that must be flexible due to the unpredictability of each storm. A

   2|                                                                                     Elaine Blaisdell, Principal
   3|                                      The Lunenburg Primary School is a child-centered school devoted to providing quality early childhood education
   4|                           for all students. We are a community of teachers, students, and parents working together to create a safe and respectful
   5|                           environment for children. Current knowledge and research of child development guides the school's beliefs and practices
   6|                           and provides the foundation for learning for all students.
   7|                                      For the last two years the school serviced Preschool to Third grade students. The February 20, 2014 student count
   8|                           reported to the state was 517 students. Our Preschool program services 19 three year old students and thirty-three four
   9|                           year old students. We have five Kindergarten classes servicing one hundred and twenty-three students. The class sizes in
  10|                           our Kindergarten programs range from 24-25 students. First grade has one hundred and thirteen students with class sizes
  11|                           ranging from 21-22. Second grade has one hundred and twenty-five students and the third grade has one hundred and four
  12|                           students. We are utilizing all available space in the building to provide a child centered enriching educational program for
  13|                           all our students.
  14|                                      We constantly strive to find better ways to meet the collective needs as well as the individual needs of our
  15|                           students. Our Professional Development focused on Race to the Top requirements including the new Teacher Evaluation,
  16|                           and the Common Core curriculum. All of our classroom teachers continued to use Responsive Classroom strategies as part
  17|                           of our Academic/Social/Emotional Learning Curriculum which includes morning meetings, guided discovery, rules and
  18|                        / logical consequences, and academic choice.
  19|                                      The Primary School community continues to strive toward academic excellence. Common assessments are in
  20|                           place in the fall, winter, and spring for all children in grades K-3 in the areas of reading, writing, and math. These common
  21|                           assessments serve to monitor the effectiveness of teacher instruction and drive instruction. We are now able to track
  22|                           progress throughout the year in a faster, more efficient manner in order to improve instruction. Additional resources and
  23|                           direct instruction are provided to address the areas of concern identified through these common assessments. The Primary
  24|                           School is a Title One school and we have continued our tiered instructional system to insure that all students have the
  25|                           services they need to be successful. Tiered instructional services are provided in the area of Language Arts and Math. The
  26|                           first tier is the largest, and is fitted for universal instruction in the classroom. Teachers are skilled in differentiating
  27|                           instruction to meet a large number of student needs in the classroom. The second tier is based on student needs that are
