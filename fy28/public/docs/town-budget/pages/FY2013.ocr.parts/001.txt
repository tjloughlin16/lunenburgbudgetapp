   6|                            In June 2013, the Town's E-911 Service was transitioned over to Nashoba Valley Regional Dispatch District
   7|                            (NVRDD), a newly created district which provides E-911 Service to the towns of Harvard, Lancaster,
   8|                            Lunenburg and to Devens. Lunenburg is the largest community in the district and it was the first town to
   9|                            transition service. The decision to transition to the district was made after years of study and review of two
  10|                            separate regional options. New state requirement to provide Emergency Medical Dispatch in addition to the
  11|                            increasing number of calls for service would have necessitated an increase in staffing levels, resulting in a
  12|                            higher cost to the Lunenburg taxpayer. Ultimately, it was determined that by opting for a regional service, we
  13|                            could meet the new state requirements within our existing budget. From an end-user point of view, there has
  14|                            been no change in service levels. In FY2015, the District anticipates expanding to include an additional town
  15|                            which will reduce the annual assessment to each of the existing district members.
  16|                            The increase was made in recognition of the Town's overall improved financial condition, adherence to
  17|                            adopted financial policies, and fiscal prudence. The increase in Bond Rating comes at a great time and will
  18|                           only help in securing the best interest rates on future borrowings.
  19|                            In November 2013, the Board of Selectmen accepted a $25,000 grant from the Stanton Foundation which will
  20|                            enable the Lunenburg Police Department to implement a K-9 Program. The grant will cover the cost of
  21|                            purchasing, training and caring for the dog for the first 3 years of the program. The K-9 Unit will be used in
  22|                           narcotics detection, assisting in locating missing persons, and in following suspects involved in crime.
  23|                           In 2013, the Board of Selectmen established a Building Reuse Committee, specifically tasking it with the
  24|                           following: identify space requirements for potential combined municipal operations; identify costs to convert
  25|                            municipal space; identify 5 and 10 year capital costs to retain existing buildings including, Town Hall, Ritter
  26|                            Memorial and Eagle House; identify appropriate options for sale or lease of facilities; and identify potential
  27|                           reuse or market for any buildings deemed surplus as a result of consolidation. The Committee made its report
  28|                            of findings to the Board in December. As a result, additional tasks were identified, including: communicating
  29|                           findings and recommendations so they can be considered during School Building discussion; develop a "go
  30|                           to market" plan for the marketable properties; create a Request for Proposals Template after discussion with
  31|                           stakeholders; develop consolidation plans for municipal functions and a renovations plan for Town Hall; and
