   1|                             § 140-1 Purpose.
   2|                              The purpose of this article and associated regulations is to regulate earth removal operations
   3|                              and land filling operations for the protection of human health, public safety, welfare, and
   4|                             the Town's natural resources in the Town of Lunenburg. Further, the provisions of this
   5|                             article are intended to protect abutting property owners from dust, washouts, noise, ponds,
   6|                             and other hazardous land conditions that could potentially be created by poorly managed
   7|                             earth removal and/or filling operations.
   8|                             § 140-2 Definitions.
   9|                             As used in this bylaw, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:
  10|                             ABUTTERS The owners of property within 300 feet of the perimeter of the subject
  11|                             property.
  12|                             APPLICANT The owner of land from which earth will be removed or fill to be placed,
  13|                             or the person of lawful standing in the stead of such owner. Any person, company, or agent
  14|                             standing instead of the actual property owner must have the owner's written permission to
  15|                             do so.
  16|                             BOARD The Planning Board of the Town of Lunenburg, which shall act as the permit
  17|                             granting authority (PGA).
  18|                             EARTH This term shall include soil, loam, sand, gravel, clay, peat, rock, or other
  19|                             earth material in solid form.
  20|                             EARTH REMOVAL OPERATION Any commercial mining, stripping, quarrying,
