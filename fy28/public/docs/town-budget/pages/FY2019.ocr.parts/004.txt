  14|                   Guidelines for Special Events
  15|                   A Special Event is any private event/activity held on public property or an event open to the
  16|                   general public held on private property that involves or affects, directly or incidentally, the
  17|                   ordinary use of parks, playgrounds, fields, public buildings, streets and rights-of-way and/or

  22|                   inspections are in place. Events beyond 6 not permitted without reference to zoning in the
