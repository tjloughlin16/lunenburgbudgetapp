   1|                           Section 1. Purpose and Intent
   2|                           The production and use of thin-film single-use plastic checkout bags have significant impacts on the
   3|                           environment, including, but not limited to: contributing to the potential death of marine animals through
   4|                          ingestion and entanglement; contributing to pollution of the land environment; creating a burden to solid
   5|                           waste collection-and recycling facilities; clogging storm drainage systems; and requiring the use of
   6|                           millions of barrels of crude oil nationally for their manufacture. The purpose of this bylaw is to eliminate
   7|                           the usage of thin-film single-use plastic bags by all retail and grocery stores in the Town of
   8|                           Lunenburg, by June 1, 2019.
   9|                           Section 2. Definitions
  10|                                                      2.1 Checkout bag: A carryout bag provided by a store to a consumer at the
  11|                                                             point of sale. Checkout bags shall not include bags, whether plastic or
  12|                                                             not, in which loose produce or products are placed by the consumer to
  13|                                                             deliver such items to the point of sale or checkout area of the store.
  14|                                                       2.2 Grocery Store: Aretail establishment where more than fifty percent (50%)
  15|                                                             of the gross floor area is devoted to the sale of food products for home
  16|                                                             preparation and consumption, which typically also offers home care and
  17|                                                             personal care products.
  18|                                                       2.3 Retail Store: An establishment that offers the sale and display of
  19|                                                             merchandise within a building.
  20|                                                       2.1 Reusable checkout bag: A bag, with handles, that is specifically
  21|                                                            designed for multiple use and is made of thick plastic, cloth, fabric or
  22|                                                            other durable materials.
  23|                                                       2.2 Thin-film single-use plastic bags: Typically with plastic handles, these
  24|                                                            are bags with a thickness of 2.5 mils or less and are intended for
  25|                                                            single-use transport of purchased products.
  26|                           Section 3. Use Regulations
  27|                                                       3.1 Thin-film single-use plastic bags shall not be distributed, used, or sold for
  28|                                                            checkout or other purposes at any retail or grocery store within the Town of
  29|                                                            Lunenburg.
  30|                                                       3.2 Customers are encouraged to bring their own reusable or biodegradable
  31|                                                            shopping bags to stores. Retail            or grocery stores are strongly
  32|                                                            encouraged to make reusable checkout bags available for sale to
  33|                                                             costumers at a reasonable price.
  34|                                                       3.3 Thin-filmed plastic bags used to contain dry cleaning, newpapers, produce,
  35|                                                            meat, bulk foods, wet items and other similar merchandise, typically without
  36|                                                            handles, are still permissible.
  37|                           Section 4. Enforcement Process
  38|                           Enforcement of this bylaw shall be the responsibility of the Town Manager or his/her
  39|                          incorporating the process into other town duties as appropriate. Any retail or grocery store
  40|                           distributing plastic grocery bags in violation of this bylaw shall be subject to a non-criminal
  41|                           disposition fine as specified in Appendix A of the Regulations for Enforcement of Town
