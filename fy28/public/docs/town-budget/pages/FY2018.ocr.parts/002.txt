  14|                                     It has become a high priority goal of the BOS as Road Commissioners, to achieve a
  15|                         pavement condition of 72%-75% Town-wide. Although State funded Chapter 90 funds remains
  16|                         stagnant, the dedicated Pavement Management line in the operational budget has grown
  17|                         substantially.
  18|                                    Results of the Comprehensive Pavement Management Plan displayed clearly the inequity
  19|                         of the distribution of paving funds toward higher use main and collector roads leaving local
  20|                         neighborhoods unattended. Using the PMP as evidence, residents went to the polls to vote
  21|                         favorably on a four-million-dollar debt exclusion targeting exclusively the local roads. Expending
  22|                         approximately a million dollars in the initial year, and in excess of a million the following year has
  23|                         resulted in a major enhancement of road conditions.

  25|                         the total paving value of years past as we attack a nine-million-dollar backlog of repairs.
  26|                                    Our pot hole log has and is expected to lessen exponentially as new paving increases, a
  27|                         clear benefit of the dollars expended. There is much work yet to do but expectations are high.
