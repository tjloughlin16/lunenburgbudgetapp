  10|             Vision:
  11|             The Mission of the Lunenburg Public Schools is to provide all students with the skills, confidence, and passion for life-

  13|             The Lunenburg Public Schools is committed to becoming a leader in education by focusing on its values of attending to
  14|             the personal well-being of its students, providing high-quality curricula and student-centered instruction for deeper
  15|             learning at all levels, and promoting the development of attributes and skills necessary for continuous learning for all.
  16|             The Strategy is a living document. Each August the District Leadership Team reviews the Strategy, its objectives and
  17|             action plans. The Strategy objectives for the next two to three years are:
  18|                        Transforming Teaching and Learning: To ensure teaching and learning are student-centered and inclusive
  19|                        by: (1) establishing effective teams of educational leaders to accomplish the essential work for transforming
  20|                        practice, and (2) hiring and developing excellent educators who are continually learning and adapting practice to
  21|                        meet the needs of all students.
  22|                        Cultivating Climate & Culture: To create a culture that values data use and fosters continuous learning in
  23|                        a climate that is safe, caring, inclusive and reflective of our core values.
  24|                        Communication & Operations: To improve operational efficiencies, including communication, in order to
  25|                        increase the organization's capacity to execute the district's mission and vision.
  26|                        Equity, Access, Achievement & Opportunity for All Students: To ensure all students are provided equal
  27|                        access to a high-quality education, that is collaborative, interactive, and relevant with an appropriate level of
  28|                        support and challenge so that they can become well-rounded, resilient, confident, and capable young adults
  29|                        adequately prepared for future challenges.
  30|             Over the last several years we have worked to put in place the necessary infrastructure to support the academic
  31|             achievement of all students. We have established curriculum leadership positions, instructional specialists/coaches,
  32|             teacher curriculum leaders; piloted and purchased evidence-based math and literacy curricula for Kindergarten (K)
  33|             through grade 5; implemented a standards-based benchmark assessment in math and literacy for grades K through 8;
  34|             provided job-embedded professional development for teachers in best instructional practices and using assessment data
  35|             to inform their instruction; and hired tutoring/intervention staff for additional academic supports during the school day as
  36|             well as after school in order to close learning gaps. The Director of Teaching and Learning and Title I Coordinator
  37|             positions have been and continue to be integral to all of this work.
  38|             In order for students to be ready and able to learn in the classroom, behavioral health needs must be addressed. To that
  39|             end, we have developed a multi-tiered system of added guidance counselors, social workers and adjustment counselors;
  40|             piloted and adopted a districtwide social-emotional learning curriculum. Students receive social-emotional supports in
  41|             small group with counselors or in a one-on-one setting when student need dictates. Additionally, we have continued to
  42|             provide Care Solace, an online resource with live 24/7 concierge meant to assist families and individuals in finding local

  44|             healthcare resources and providers. The vast database of mental health resources includes vetted local therapists and
  45|             All of these supports have been absolutely critical in order to respond to the increase in significant behavioral health
