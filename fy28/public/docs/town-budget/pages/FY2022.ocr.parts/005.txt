   1|             Message on the Benefits of Creating Director of Facilities and Grounds Position
   2|             The purpose of this reorganization plan is to create a Director of Facilities and Grounds position that would
   3|             oversee, supervise, and facilitate the comprehensive management of all town facilities and grounds. The reason
   4|             this is being recommended to town meeting, is that it will improve the long and short term management of our
   5|             facilities and grounds with an individual whose background and skills relate to property management, capital
   6|             planning, building construction, and grounds maintenance. This position will help the town strategize, prioritize
   7|             and coordinate maintenance, repairs, safety, and efficiencies of municipal properties, as well as play a key role in
   8|             future use and reuse of properties.
   9|             The key benefits of creating a Director of Facilities and Grounds Position:
  10|             1. Hiring a professional level position with a background in facilities management. Facilities management
  11|             experience is not typical for Department of Public Works Directors. An individual with this skill set will enhance
  12|             and improve the town's current and future facilities management plan.
  13|             2. A management level position whose sole focus is long term and short term planning and maintenance of town
  14|             facilities   and grounds. Since the DPW Director is responsible for multiple different divisions, town facilities
  15|             receive only a portion of that position's focus.
  16|             3. More preventative maintenance and less reactionary maintenance of our facilities and grounds.
  17|             4. Collaborate with the School's counterpart position on the overall maintenance plan for facilities and grounds
  18|             and work together with the Town Manager and Finance Director on capital planning and financing alternatives.
  19|             5. Directed effort towards the health and safety of all staff and users of town facilities.
  20|             6. 6. Create and maintain a property condition assessment of all town facilities and grounds utilizing Facilities
  21|                   Management software.
  22|             This position will be appointed by the Town Manager, in accordance with Sections 4-2 (d) of the Town Charter,
  23|             with ratification of the appointment to be made by the Select Board.
  24|             The Director of Facilities and Grounds will have direct supervision for the following existing positions: Facilities
  25|             Superintendent, Facilities Custodian, and Recreation Director. Those positions that are currently in the Laborers
  26|             International Union of North America, Public Employees, Local 39 will continue to assist with snow and ice
  27|             removal operations, mostly related to town facilities and grounds.
  28|             Essential functions of this position:
  29|             1. Responsible for the planning, organizing, directing and control of all aspects of Town facilities and grounds.
  30|                   This will include facility planning, renovation, construction, energy conservation measures and efficiencies,
  31|                   preventative and regular maintenance, and repair of all town-owned buildings and grounds
  32|             2. Responsible for a comprehensive approach to building maintenance and repair coordination, oversight,
  33|                  inspection, oversight of maintenance logs, resolving any warranty issues, establishing facility management

   2|             § 70-1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
   3|             This by-law establishes a Salary Administration Plan, hereinafter called the "Plan", which classifies several
   4|             positions of town services into groups, establishes salary schedules, establishes so called "fringe" benefits, or
   5|             "indirect pay" provisions, such as holidays and vacations with pay, sick leave, and the like, and establishes a
   6|             Personnel Committee to administer the Plan, as hereinafter provided, and to represent the interest of both
   7|             taxpayers and town employees.

   2|             The positions of all employees in the service of the Town, and not otherwise covered by a collective
   3|             bargaining agreement or employees of the school, are hereby classified by titles in the groups listed in
   4|             Schedule A, entitled "Classification and Salary Schedule", as set forth in this by-law.
   5|             § 70-4. TITLE OF POSITIONS
   6|             No person shall be appointed, employed or paid as an employee in any position subject to the provision of the
   7|             Salary Administration Plan under any title other than those of the Classification and Salary Schedule, or under
   8|             any title other than that of the job the duties of which are actually performed. The job title in the Classification
   9|             and Salary Schedule of the Plan shall be the official title for all purposes having to do with the position and
  10|             shall be used to designate the position in all payrolls, budget estimates and official reports, and in every other
  11|             connection involving personnel and fiscal processes.
  12|             § 70-5. NEW OR CHANGED POSITIONS
  13|             The Personnel Committee tentatively may add new job title and descriptions to this Plan, establishing grades
  14|             with minimum and maximum wage or salary brackets for the same, and reclassifying existing jobs, revising
  15|             the description and the minimum and maximum wage and salary brackets therefore, and such addition or
  16|             reclassification to be effective only for the period from the date of such action by the Personnel Committee to
  17|             the date of any vote with respect thereto adopted at a subsequent Town Meeting but in no case later than the
  18|             next Annual Town Meeting unless such later date as may be specified by such vote. Any increase in wages or
  19|             salaries under this section shall be effective only if funds are available for the purpose, and approved by the
  20|             Finance Committee and Beard of Selectmen Select Board.
  21|             § 70-6. RECLASSIPICATION OF EMPLOYEES
  22|             No employee shall be reclassified to a job in another group, either higher or lower, until the Personnel
  23|             Committee shall have determined that such a reclassification will be consistent with the provisions of the
  24|             Salary Administration Plan. Eligibility for a step increase of a reclassified employee will be granted one year
  25|             from the date of reclassification.
  26|             § 70-7. JOB DESCRIPTIONS AND INTERPRETATIONS
  27|             A. The Personnel Committee shall maintain written job descriptions of the jobs or positions in the Plan,
  28|             describing the essential functions, requirements and duties. The descriptions shall not be interpreted as either
  29|             complete or restrictive and employees shall continue to perform any duties assigned by an employee's
  30|             supervisor.
  31|             B. Every three (3) years, the Personnel committee shall review the wage and salary schedules of all positions
  32|             in the Plan, and be in a position to recommend to the Board of Selectmen Select Board any action deemed
  33|             desirable to maintain a fair and equitable pay level.
