   2|             Lunenburg Middle School provides a rigorous academic curriculum as well as strong programs that support
   3|             students' social and emotional growth. These programs ensure that each child becomes a self-motivated,
   4|             creative, lifelong learner in a safe, stimulating environment that encourages diversity and open honest
   5|             communication. The dedicated and accomplished staff of professionals within the school embraces the
   6|             philosophy of educating the whole child academically, socially, and emotionally. This philosophy is a
   7|             means by which all students become positive community members that possess the skills to be successful in
   8|             high school and beyond.
   9|              Lunenburg Middle School is in its sixth year as a co-located middle -high school serving grades six through
  10|             eight. Effective, student centered middle schools embrace interdisciplinary teams of teachers that enable
  11|             teachers to work with smaller groups of students. Smaller groups of students allow teachers to learn and
  12|             engage students in their preferred learning style, making them more confident and successful.
  13|              Our effective middle school model is student centered and provides opportunities to explore a variety of
  14|              different interests and talents through special areas and enrichment classes and extracurricular activities.
  15|              One of our goals is for students to discover something they are passionate about and want to continue
  16|             exploring as they move through high school and beyond. We will continue to seek out opportunities for
  17|             students that foster critical thinking and problem solving skills that prepare them for high school and beyond
  18|              or where their passion may lead them to.

  20|             The global pandemic that began in March of 2020 has disrupted and interfered with the method of
  21|             instruction our school community has become accustomed to. Since that time, we have participated in fully
  22|             remote learning, hybrid model of learning, and in August of 2021, we returned to a full in person learning.
  23|             The pandemic took a toll on students, teachers and families, both academic and socially and emotionally.
  24|             Lunenburg Middle school has prepared and planned for the full in person return, and has placed personnel
  25|             and programs in place to ensure students are serviced in all components of their well-being.
  26|              Lunenburg Middle School now has a full time social worker to work with students who may experience an
  27|             increased level of anxiety in returning to the building full time. The social worker also is able to run small
  28|              groups for students to develop executive function skills, strategies for managing anxiety and stress and help
  29|              develop social skills. Our social worker has also done an outstanding job with managing and facilitating
  30|              crisis management and assessing student's well-being and aligning out of school services for students and
