  20|              Snow and ice present a major challenge. The safety of the public depends on the ability of the DW to
  21|              maintain our roadways at a high level of safety despite the severity or the duration of snow and icing
  22|              events. To that end, we have developed a winter operations procedure based upon a detailed plan with
  23|               flexibility to accommodate the unpredictability of each storm. The expectation of immediate service is
  24|              high and must be delivered to every part of Town in a timely manner.
  25|               Departmental preparedness begins with successful implementation of the Capital Plan. Financial support
  26|              is necessary to provide effective fleet rotation ensuring minimal interruption of service due to breakdowns
  27|              and repairs. In the fall salt is delivered to the DPW facility. Lunenburg is in a consortium with 14 other
  28|              communities in an effort to procure the lowest possible price. From late summer to early fall vehicle
  29|              maintenance staff inspects and prepares the trucks, spreaders, and plows for winter operations. During the
  30|               construction season road crews address right-of-way issues, overhanging branches, and pavement defects
  31|              in order to eliminate obstacles to snow removal. Each division within the DPW is involved in winter
  32|               operations. Typically, DPW staff combines with subcontractors to provide comprehensive snow and ice
  33|              operations.
  34|               Generally, it is not the amount of snow but the duration of the event that determines the overall cost. For
  35|               example, a fast moving snow storm of 6-8 inches will cost significantly less than a long duration freezing
  36|              rain event even though the accumulation is much less. Historically and as allowed by State law, the
  37|               department's snow removal accounts operate in a deficit due to a yearly allocation that is below the cost
  38|               of snow removal operations for an average winter.
