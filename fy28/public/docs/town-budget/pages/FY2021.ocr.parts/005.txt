   2|               The Monty Tech Practical Nursing Program is designed to prepare graduates to practice safely and ethically in a caring
   3|               manner for patients who are experiencing common variations in health status in diverse health care settings.
   4|                On June 24, 2021 a graduating class of 28 students completed the Practical Nursing Program and entered the nursing
   5|               profession. The class has thus far achieved a pass rate of 75% graduates passing the NCLEX-PN exam (National
   6|                Council Licensure Examination for Practical Nurses). Program graduates are currently employed in the health care
   7|               profession throughout Massachusetts, working in various health care settings, such as long-term care, sub-acute care,
   8|               mental health/substance abuse facilities, physician's offices and correctional medicine.
   9|                The Monty Tech Practical Nursing Program continues to strengthen the "LN to BSN (Bachelor of Science in Nursing)
  10|               Bridge" relationship with Fitchburg State University (FSU). Former graduates have successfully transferred into seats
  11|               in the LPN to BSN Bridge program at FSU and will continue their education to a Bachelor's of Science in Nursing
  12|               (BSN). Many class applicants cited their attraction to Monty Tech as the opportunity to complete a ten-month
  13|               accelerated program and then bridge to Fitchburg State University. In the seven -year history of the Bridge Program, all
  14|               of Monty Tech's Practical Nursing graduates reaching graduation at FSU have passed their NCLEX-RN exam. These
  15|                former graduates have gone on to work in hospital settings including Lahey, Beth Israel, and Baystate Medical Center,
  16|                while others have pursued advanced degrees as Critical Care Nurse Practitioners, Nurse Educators, and Family Practice
  17|                Nurse Practitioners.
  18|               In 2020-2021, Monty Tech Practical Nursing students were asked to complete patient scenarios in the Sim Lab on a
  19|                weekly basis in Terms 2 and 3. The Faculty Sim team has implemented National League of Nursing (NN) patient
  20|               scenarios and authored several others consistent with the National Council of State Board of Nursing Detailed Test
  21|               Plan. Instructors have also utilized the Sim lab setting to instruct students in developing nursing skills that they may
  22|               not be experiencing in the clinical setting.
  23|               By successfully developing and implementing a vast library of scenarios reflecting INASCAL Standards (International
  24|                Association for Clinical Simulation and Learning), we hope to prepare our students for any experience they may
  25|                encounter as a newly licensed Practice Nurse. In Maternity Sim Lab Boot Camp, for example, students utilize the
  26|               simulation models to imitate the birthing process, from obstetric office visits through labor and common postpartum
  27|                complications. Well newborn care is also taught. Student feedback has been positive regarding these maternity
  28|                scenarios, stating it better prepares them for an unexpected outcome during a delivery. This expanded content also
  29|               better prepares students for NACE (Nursing Acceleration Challenge Exam) success at FSU.
  30|                Substance abuse and the mental health setting job opportunities have increased with more funding being provided to
  31|               combat the opiate crisis. In addition to having specialty clinical rotations, students participate in simulation scenarios
  32|                involving substance abuse and mental health clients, to help to prepare our graduates to work in a substance abuse
  33|                clinical setting.
  34|                The Monty Tech practical Nursing Program recently completed an accreditation visit from the Massachusetts Board of
  35|                Registration in Nursing, highlighting goals that include stabilization of faculty positions and pursuance of ACEN
  36|                (Accreditation Commission for Education in Nursing) accreditation to more accurately reflect the changing
  37|                environment in nursing education.
  38|                Looking Ahead
  39|                Undoubtedly, the 2020-2021 school year was challenging and difficult for students and staff, yet the school community
  40|                demonstrated a remarkable resilience and commitment to education and service that proves, yet again, that Monty Tech
  41|                is truly a special place to work and learn. While adapting curriculum to the needs of all students both at home and in-
  42|                person, adhering to state and federal safety and health guidelines, and finding opportunities for students to demonstrate
  43|                their creativity and technical skills, certainly kept staff busy, there was always an element of improvement, vision, and
  44|                forward-thinking that kept moving the school forward. Successful grant applications, new coursework, and increased
  45|                school-to-business partnerships that came about during this difficult year are a few of the more notable initiatives that
  46|                we expect to have a positive impact on our school and students in the coming years:
  47|                Grant funding to improve and expand training programs: Monty Tech has applied for and received six Skills Capital
  48|                Grants, totaling $2,029,686. School officials anticipate a FY22 application may be forthcoming, and as a result, have
  49|                met to determine which program is in need of a shop renovation, updated equipment, and/or new technology that
  50|                reflects current industry standards. The school's Dental Assisting program, which effectively prepares students to enter
  51|                the dental field may be the next vocational program to benefit from grant funding, should an application be approved.
  52|                Instructors have advocated for a renovated instructional space, as well as new chairs, tools, and equipment, so that
  53|                students are trained using the exact instruments and technology found in area clinics. This improved facility would
  54|                certainly benefit our high school students, and would also allow Monty Tech's School of Continuing Education to
  55|                develop an all-new evening training program, which would help address the critical shortage for trained Dental
