   4|                       204-1. STORMWATER MANAGEMENT - NPDES PHASE II PERMITS
   5|                        A. Purpose and intent.
   6|                       (1) Regulation of discharges to the municipal separate storm sewer system (MS4) is necessary for the protection of the
   7|                              Town of Lunenburg's water bodies and groundwater, and to safeguard the public health, safety, welfare and the
   8|                              environment. Increased and contaminated stormwater runoff associated with developed land uses and the
   9|                              accompanying increase in impervious surface are major causes of impairment of water quality and flow in lakes,
  10|                              ponds, streams, rivers, wetlands, and groundwater which result in the contamination of drinking water supplies;
  11|                              erosion of stream channels; alteration or destruction of aquatic and wildlife habitat; and flooding. This bylaw
  12|                              establishes minimum stormwater management standards for the final conditions that result from development and
  13|                              redevelopment projects to minimize adverse impacts off-site and downstream which would be borne by abutters,
  14|                              townspeople and the general public.
  15|                       (2) This regulation requires local review and approval of a stormwater management plan for all development and
  16|                              redevelopment projects that disturb one acre or more. In addition to these regulations, the owner and/or developer is
  17|                              also obligated to meet the requirements of the Federal Environmental Protection Agency's (EPA) regulations for
  18|                              stormwater management.
  19|                        B. The objectives of this bylaw are:
  20|                       (1) To require practices to control the flow of stormwater from new and redeveloped sites to the Town's storm drainage
  21|                              system in order to prevent flooding and erosion;
  22|                       (2) To protect groundwater and surface water from degradation;
  23|                       (3) To promote groundwater recharge;
  24|                       (4) To prevent pollutants from entering the Town's municipal separate storm sewer system (MS4) and to minimize
  25|                              discharge of pollutants from the MS4;
  26|                       (5) To ensure adequate long-term operation and maintenance of structural stormwater best management practices so that
  27|                              they work as designed;
  28|                       (6) To comply with state and federal statutes and regulations relating to stormwater discharges; and
  29|                       (7) To establish Lunenburg's legal authority to ensure compliance with the provisions of this bylaw through inspection,
  30|                              monitoring, and enforcement.

  34|                              ultimately disturb equal to or greater than one acre of land draining to the Town of Lunenburg's municipal separate
  35|                              storm sewer system, without a permit from the special permit granting authority. Construction activity does not
  36|                              include routine maintenance that is performed to maintain the original line and grade, hydraulic capacity or the

  15|                       A.    Purpose.
  16|                       (1) Increased and contaminated stormwater runoff is a major cause of impairment of water quality and flow in lakes,
  17|                              ponds, streams, rivers, wetlands and groundwater; contamination of drinking water supplies; alteration or destruction
  18|                              of aquatic and wildlife habitat; flooding.
  19|                           Regulation of illicit connections and discharges to the municipal storm sewer system is necessary for the protection
  20|                              of the Town's water bodies and groundwater, and to safeguard the public health, safety, welfare and the
  21|                              environment.
  22|                       (3) The objectives of this bylaw are:
  23|                       (a) To prevent pollutants from entering the Town's municipal separate storm sewer system (MS4);
  24|                       (b) To prohibit illicit connections and unauthorized discharges to the MS4;
  25|                       (c) To require the removal of all such illicit connections;
  26|                       (d) To comply with state and federal statutes and regulations relating to stormwater discharges; and
  27|                       (e) To establish the legal authority to ensure compliance with the provisions of this bylaw through inspection,
  28|                              monitoring, and enforcement.
