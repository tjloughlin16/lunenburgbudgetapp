  13|            As the school year opened, Monty Tech experienced a welcome growth spurt as the District officially launched the
  14|            inaugural year of the Montachusett Vocational Partnership (MVP) Academy. Created as an innovative proposal to
  15|            address the building capacity issue that results in hundreds of students being deprived of the opportunity to be
  16|            enrolled at Monty Tech, MVP Academy allowed nearly 80 juniors and seniors from three of our sending districts,
  17|            the opportunity to receive workforce training in Electrical or House Carpentry during the school day while remaining
  18|            enrolled in their sending school. Thus, as Monty Tech instructors and students worked to complete the renovation of
  19|            the program's future home, the initial MVP
  20|            instruction began in an outdoor classroom
  21|            located                  on our main campus. As winter

  26|            commonwealth to simultaneously celebrate the

  28|            graduation ceremony of the program's first 40

  31|            fall of 2026.

  33|            burden of costly new initiatives that could fall on the eighteen cities and towns of our regional community.
  34|             Consequently, in order to avoid that scenario, 2024-2025 became noteworthy as the District's most successful in
  35|            terms of accessing competitive grants from a variety of resources. This included opportunities involving
  36|            cybersecurity, equitable access, workforce training in clean energy fields and for adults with disabilities, skills
  37|            capital equipment, partnership/implementation programs, no-cost adult workforce training and even anonymous
  38|            corporate partner donations. In total, during the 2024-2025 school year, Monty Tech was awarded almost $3.7
  39|            million in external grants and donations. Such results clearly reflect the District's commitment to offering innovative
  40|            state-of-the-art workforce training to a variety of highly skilled workers while also being mindful of the financial
  41|            implications such valuable training could have on our sending communities.

  45|            maintained its consecutive run of expanding enrollment across the spectrum of its offerings. Between the 2020-2021
