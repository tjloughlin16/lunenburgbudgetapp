  16|              (1) To require practices to control the flow of stormwater from new and redeveloped sites to the Town's

  21|                     minimize discharge of pollutants from the MS4;
  22|              (5) To ensure adequate long-term operation and maintenance of structural stormwater best management
  23|                     practices so that they work as designed;
  24|              (6) To comply with state and federal statutes and regulations relating to stormwater discharges; and
  25|             (7) To establish Lunenburg's legal authority to ensure compliance with the provisions of this bylaw through
  26|                     inspection, monitoring, and enforcement.
  27|              (8) Establish regulations of land development activities that preserve the health of water resources.
  28|              (9) Require that the amount and quality of stormwater runoff from new development is equal to or better than
  29|                         pre-development conditions in order to reduce flooding, stream erosion, pollution, property damage

  31|              (10) Establish stormwater management standards and design criteria to control the quality and quantity of
  32|                         stormwater runoff.
  33|              (11) Encourage the use of low-impact development practices, such as reducing impervious cover and
