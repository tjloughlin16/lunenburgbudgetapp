   3|               future, and developing and implementing rules, regulations and policies. Under the Town's Charter,
   4|               responsibility for the day-to-day operation, care and maintenance of the sanitary sewers are under the

   6|               side of the system to the Department of Public Works' Director. The Town of Lunenburg does not operate a
   7|               sewer treatment plant. Approximately 85% of Lunenburg sewer is discharged to the City of Leominster and
   8|               the other 15% is discharged to the City of Fitchburg.

  10|               2006. In August 2019, John Reynolds resigned from the Commission and there were 2 vacancies. Former
  11|               Commissioners Dave MacDonald and Mike Nault stepped up to fill the positions on September 3, 2019. At
  12|               the September 24, 2019 meeting, the Commission elected Joan Pease as Chair, Ryan Stober as Vice-Chair
  13|               and Carl Luck as Clerk. In January 2020, Joan and Ryan gave their resignations due to personal reasons,
  14|               and Carl Luck became acting Chair. The Commission welcomed back John Reynolds and new member
  15|               Brett Ramsden in February 2020. With a full board, the Commission elected new officers on 3/10/20 -
  16|               Dave MacDonald-Chair, John Reynolds-Vice Chair and Brett Ramsden-Clerk.
  17|               The Covid-19 crisis resulted in shutdowns during March 2020. The Town of Lunenburg closed all town
  18|               offices with employees working from home. In June offices reopened by appointment only. All meetings
  19|               were held virtually via Zoom for the rest of 2020. The Town Caucus was cancelled and the Town Meeting
  20|               and Town Election were rescheduled to June. The Commission had 4 openings on the Town Election ballot
  21|               and the following were elected: Dave MacDonald - 1 yr. term, John Reynolds- 2 yr. term and Brett
  22|               Ramsden and Mike Deveikis - 3 yr. terms. At the June 23, 2020 meeting, the Commission re-elected Dave
  23|               MacDonald-Chair, John Reynolds-Vice Chair and Brett Ramsden-Clerk.
