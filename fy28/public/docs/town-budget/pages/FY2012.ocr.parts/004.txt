  24|                    Section 4.15 SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS
  25|                    The purpose of this section is to provide siting for solar photovoltaic energy systems for power generation for all,
  26|                    home, commercial or industrial installations. Small-Scale Ground-Mounted Photovoltaic Installations, Roof-Mounted
  27|                    Solar Energy Installations and Large- Scale Ground-Mounted Photovoltaic Installations within the Solar Overlay District
  28|                    may be constructed without need for discretionary approval as set forth herein. Large-Scale Ground-Mounted
  29|                    Photovoltaic Installations outside of the Solar Overlay District may be constructed by special permit provided certain
  30|                    criteria as set forth herein are met. The provisions in this section of the Zoning Bylaw shall apply to the construction,
  31|                    operation, repair, and/or removal of all solar electric systems, and to physical modifications that materially alter the
  32|                    type, configuration, or size of these installations or related equipment.
  33|                    4.15.2 Definitions
  34|                    Small-Scale Ground-Mounted Photovoltaic Installation: A solar photovoltaic system that is structurally mounted on the
  35|                    ground and has a nameplate capacity under 10 kW. Large-Scale Ground-Mounted Photovoltaic Installation: A solar
  36|                    photovoltaic system that is structurally mounted on the ground and has a nameplate capacity of 10 kW or greater.
  37|                    Rated Nameplate Capacity: The maximum rated output of electric power production of the photovoltaic system in Direct
  38|                    Current (DC). Solar Photovoltaic Array: An arrangement of solar photovoltaic panels. Roof-Mounted Solar Energy
  39|                    Installation: Solar photovoltaic arrays placed on the roof of residences, or commercial, industrial or institutional
  40|                    buildings and Town owned municipal buildings. Buffer Strip: A strip of land between the solar photovoltaic arrays, and
  41|                    any structures accessory thereto, and the boundary of the parcel, reserved for plant material, berms, walls or fencing to
  42|                    serve as a visual barrier. Plant Material: Trees or shrubs of a type and height that sufficiently screen the arrays without
  43|                    blocking necessary sun that would hinder the product performance, including ground cover that would screen the
  44|                    lower portion of the arrays.
