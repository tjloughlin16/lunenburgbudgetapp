   7|                 The Lunenburg Primary School is a child-centered school devoted to providing quality early childhood education for all
   8|                 students. We are a community of teachers, students, and parents working together to create a safe and respectful
   9|                 environment for children. Our current knowledge of child development guides the school's beliefs and practices and
  10|                 provides the foundation for student learning.
  11|                 This year the school serviced preschool to third grade students after the closing of the Thomas C. Passios Elementary
  12|                 School. We started the 2012-2013 school year with 506 students in 20 classrooms for an average class size of 23, and 41
  13|                 students in our two preschool classrooms. We constantly strive to find better ways to meet the collective as well as the
  14|                 individual needs of our students. Our Professional Development focused on Reading Comprehension, the Common Core
  15|                 curriculum, and the new teacher evaluation system. We worked with experts in the field of Literacy and established
  16|                 eighteen lab classrooms in the building that are piloting Readers Workshop and Inclusive Practices. All of our classroom
  17|                 teachers continued to use Responsive Classroom strategies    as part of our Academic/Social/Emotional Learning
  18|                 Curriculum, which includes morning meetings, guided discovery, rules and logical consequences, and academic choice.
  19|                 The Primary School community continues to strive toward academic excellence. Common assessments are in place in the
  20|                fall, winter, and spring for all children in grades K-3 in the areas of reading, writing, and math. These common
  21|                assessments serve to monitor the effectiveness of teacher instruction and drive instruction. We are now able to track
  22|                progress throughout the year in a faster, more efficient manner in order to improve instruction.
  23|                The Primary School is a Title One school; we have continued our tiered instructional system to insure that all students
  24|                have the services they need to be successful. Tiered instructional services are provided in the area of Language Arts and
  25|                Math. The first tier is the largest, and is fitted for universal instruction in the classroom. Teachers are skilled in
  26|                differentiating instruction to meet a large number of student needs in the classroom. The second tier is based on student
  27|                needs that are targeted, individual, and involve more intensive interventions. The third tier is the smallest and involves
  28|                special services with very intense interventions. The tiered instructional model requires that the teachers not only meet the
  29|                needs of the students who may be struggling with learning but those students who are doing well and need to strive to
  30|                achieve higher levels of learning. Learning is becoming more personalized and students are being challenged to be
  31|                independent learners and problem solvers.
  32|                Our staff works hard to maintain a school culture that is positive, inclusive, and supportive of each of our members. We
  33|                have high expectations, insist on respectful behavior, and recognize the importance of building a community where
  34|                members feel a sense of belonging and responsibility to contribute. Our students have a right to be in an environment that
  35|                is safe, promotes learning, and enables them to achieve success.

  37|                night serves as the vehicle for students to display/articulate their strengths as a learner. To prepare for Student Success
