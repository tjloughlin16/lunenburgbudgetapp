  18|                Section 4.15 SOLAR ENERGY SYSTEMS
  19|                4.15.1 Purpose
  20|                The purpose of this section is to provide siting for solar photovoltaic energy systems for power generation for all,
  21|                home, commercial or industrial installations. Small-Scale Ground-Mounted Photovoltaic Installations, Roof-Mounted
  22|                Solar Energy Installations and Large- Scale Ground-Mounted Photovoltaic Installations within the Solar Overlay District
  23|                may be constructed without need for discretionary approval as set forth herein. Large-Scale Ground-Mounted
  24|                Photovoltaic Installations outside of the Solar Overlay District may be constructed by special permit provided certain
  25|                criteria as set forth herein are met. The provisions in this section of the Zoning Bylaw shall apply to the construction,
  26|                operation, repair, and/or removal of all solar electric systems, and to physical modifications that materially alter the
  27|                type, configuration, or size of these installations or related equipment.
  28|                4.15.2 Definitions
  29|                Small-Scale Ground-Mounted Photovoltaic Installation: A solar photovoltaic system that is structurally mounted on the
  30|                ground and has a nameplate capacity under 10 KW. Large-Scale Ground-Mounted Photovoltaic Installation: A solar
  31|                photovoltaic system that is structurally mounted on the ground and has a nameplate capacity of 10 kW or greater.
  32|                Rated Nameplate Capacity: The maximum rated output of electric power production of the photovoltaic system in Direct
  33|                Current (DC). Solar Photovoltaic Array: An arrangement of solar photovoltaic panels. Roof-Mounted Solar Energy
  34|                Installation: Solar photovoltaic arrays placed on the roof of residences, or commercial, industrial or institutional
  35|                buildings and Town owned municipal buildings. Buffer Strip: A strip of land between the solar photovoltaic arrays, and
  36|                any structures accessory thereto, and the boundary of the parcel, reserved for plant material, berms, walls or fencing to
  37|                serve as a visual barrier. Plant Material: Trees or shrubs of a type and height that sufficiently screen the arrays without
  38|                blocking necessary sun that would hinder the product performance, including ground cover that would screen the
  39|                lower portion of the arrays.
  40|                4.15.3 Small-Scale Ground-Mounted Solar Photovoltaic Installations and Roof-Mounted Solar Energy Installations
  41|                4.15.3.1 Purpose
