   7|                       Article II. Sale, Exchange or Purchase of Metals
   8|                       Section I. Findings
   9|                       The Town of Lunenburg finds that there is a cognizable risk for stolen metals to end up in the possession of even legitimate
  10|                       businesses which trade in metals, including precious metals. Certain criminals bring stolen metals, including precious metals
  11|                       to dealers in jewelry and scrap metal. The victims of theft have only small hope of recovering their property unless the people
  12|                       and entities who receive scrap metals and jewelry are regulated in a manner which identifies both the metal received by the
  13|                       dealers and the persons who deliver the metal to these dealers. There is an Internet alert system used by the Lunenburg Police
  14|                       Department which reports stolen metals. Monitoring transactions within the town and identifying both the metals and the
  15|                       persons selling or delivering these metals will serve both to deter theft and to enable the Lunenburg Police Department to
  16|                       trace and recover stolen property and return it to the rightful owners.
  17|                       Section II. Definitions
  18|                       As used in Article II, the following terms shall have the meanings indicated:
  19|                       ARTICLE: Any metal which is subject to Article II and is exchanged, sold, collected, bartered or received by a person
  20|                       dealing in metals.
  21|                       CHIEF OF POLICE: The Chief of the Lunenburg Police Department or his designee.
  22|                       EXCHANGE: Receiving, purchasing, selling or bartering metals, including precious metals and industrial scrap metals.
  23|                       METAL: A chemical element that is malleable, opaque and ductile, usually solid, which has a characteristic luster, is a good
  24|                       conductor of heat and electricity, and can be melted or fused, hammered into thin sheets, or drawn into wires, e.g., copper,
  25|                       aluminum, gold, silver or iron or other metals.
  26|                       PRECIOUS METAL(S): Any metal which is considered valuable, such as silver, gold, platinum and copper without regard
  27|                       to the form or amount of such precious metal.
  28|                       BOARD: Board of Selectmen
  29|                       Section III. License Required
  30|                       a) No person shall collect, deal in, exchange, buy, or keep a shop or place for the exchange, purchase, sale or barter of metals,
  31|                       including precious metals and industrial metals, in any building or place within the limits of the town without having a
  32|                       license issued by the Board of Selectman. The license requirement and the other requirements of this Article II shall not
  33|                       apply to commercial haulers having valid, current United States Department of Transportation stickers affixed to their
  34|                       vehicles.
  35|                       b) Board of Selectmen may for a fee set by said board and based upon an application devised by said board, license suitable
  36|                       persons to be dealers in and keepers of shops or locations for the purchase, sale or barter of precious metals at such places
  37|                       within the town as may be designated in such license. The Board of Selectmen may place reasonable conditions on such
  38|                       licenses which shall be inscribed on the face of the license. A license shall be limited to a specific address stated thereon.
  39|                       Subject to the notice and appeal provisions contained in Section IX (E),(2), (3) of this Article II, the Board of Selectmen may
  40|                       deny a license to any person who in the exercise of his reasonable discretion is unsuitable.
  41|                       c) All metal dealer licenses shall expire annually on the last day in December. The license holders shall be responsible for
  42|                       submitting an application for license renewal with the license fee to the Board of Selectmen at least 30 days prior to the last
  43|                       day in December. Upon receipt of an application for renewal, the Board shall consider the performance of the licensee during
  44|                       the previous year and shall determine whether the licensee had been found in violation of this Article II and other ordinances
  45|                       and regulations of the town applicable to metal dealers.
  46|                       d) The Board of Selectmen may promulgate reasonable regulations not inconsistent with this Article II which shall be binding
  47|                       upon all licensees. No regulation shall take effect until the expiration of 30 days from the date the regulation(s) is delivered or
  48|                       mailed to the person(s) then licensed. The Board of Selectmen shall deliver a complete set of regulations and any
  49|                       amendments thereto to the Town Clerk. The Town Clerk shall maintain the complete set of all regulations in a book available

  51|                       e) The obligation to obtain a license as required by this section will take effect 45 days after it is finally enacted.

  34|                       PREAMBLE
  35|                       This by-law establishes a Salary Administration Plan, hereinafter called the "Plan", which classifies several positions of town
  36|                       services into groups, establishes salary schedules, establishes so called "fringe" benefits, or "indirect pay" provisions, such as
  37|                       holidays and vacations with pay, sick leave, and the like, and establishes a Personnel Committee to administer the Plan, as
  38|                       hereinafter provided, and to represent the interest of both taxpayers and town employees.
  39|                       SECTION 1. THE PERSONNEL COMMITTEE
  40|                       There is hereby established a Personnel Committee consisting of five members, each residing in the Town of Lunenburg, to
  41|                       be appointed by the Selectmen and Town Manager, and each member shall serve for a term of three years; provided,
  42|                       however, that of the members originally appointed, one shall serve a term of one year, two for a term of two years, and two
  43|                       for a term of three years, as designated by the Selectmen and Town Manager. However, nothing herein shall be deemed to
  44|                       prohibit the chairman of the Personnel Committee from designating a member of said committee to serve ex officio as
  45|                       representative of said Committee on a study committee of the Town Meeting, nor shall such service affect the tenure of said
  46|                       member of the Personnel Committee. If any member of the Personnel Committee shall resign or otherwise vacate the office
  47|                       before the expiration of their term, the successor shall be appointed by the Selectmen and Town Manager, at their discretion,
  48|                       to serve for the balance of the unexpired term.
  49|                        A Town employee may serve as an ex officio member of the Personnel Committee and may be called upon from time to time
  50|                       to provide information requested by the Personnel Committee so that the Personnel Committee can make well informed
  51|                       decisions and proposals.
  52|                       Said Committee shall elect its own chairman and vice-chairman by September 15" of each year; and in the absence or
  53|                       incapacity of the chairman, the vice-chairman shall act as chairman. The Committee shall keep a record of its official
  54|                       proceedings and actions, may establish its own rule of procedure and may, subject to appropriation therefore, retain such
  55|                                                                                                    189

   2|                        members shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of the business of the committee, and the affirmative vote of three
   3|                        members shall be necessary to any official act of the Committee.
   4|                        The Personnel Committee is hereby authorized to administer the provisions of this by-law, except for such duties as may be
   5|                        specifically assigned by statute or by-law to other Town officers, boards or committees. Appeals for decisions of the
   6|                        Personnel Committee may be taken to the Town at the next Annual or Special Town Meeting.

  20|                       positions filled by popular election, all contractual employees, all police officers under the rank of Police Chief, and those

  30|                        period from the date of such action by the Personnel Committee to the date of any vote with respect thereto adopted at a
  31|                       subsequent Town Meeting but in no case later than the next Annual Town Meeting unless such later date as may be specified
  32|                       by such vote. Any increase in wages or salaries under this section shall be effective only if funds are available for the

  35|                       No employee shall be reclassified to a job in another group, either higher or lower, until the Personnel Committee shall have

  38|                       The Personnel Committee shall maintain written job descriptions of the jobs or positions in the Plan, describing the essential
  39|                       functions, requirements and duties. The descriptions shall not be interpreted as either complete or restrictive and employees
  40|                        shall continue to perform any duties assigned by an employee's supervisor.
  41|                        Every three (3) years, the Personnel committee shall review the wage and salary schedules of all positions in the Plan, and be
  42|                       in a position to recommend to the Board of Selectmen any action deemed desirable to maintain a fair and equitable pay level.

   7|                       SECTION 8. SALARY RATES ABOVE MAXIMUM
   8|                        Any salary rate which is above the maximum rate for a job, as established by Salary Administration Plan, shall be deemed to
   9|                       be a personal rate, and apply only to the incumbent. When such incumbent leaves the employ of the Town or is transferred to
  10|                       another job with a higher maximum rate than their personal rate or higher maximum rates are established, the personal rate
  11|                       shall disappear. No other employee assigned to or hired for such job shall advance beyond the maximum of the job.
  12|                       SECTION 9. LAYOFF PROCEDURE - RECALL PROCEDURE
  13|                       Layoff Procedure - If the Town decides to lay off employees, it shall determine from which classification(s) or job
  14|                       function(s) layoffs shall be made. Layoffs shall then be made based on seniority within the classification.
  15|                       "Seniority" shall be the length of an employee's continuous service measured from the employee's most recent appointment
  16|                       date; provided an employee's seniority may be reduced for periods of unpaid leave, unless otherwise prohibited by law.
  17|                       If two or more employees in the same classification have the same seniority, the employee who has the shorter term of
  18|                       continuous employment with the Town shall be laid off. If there are two or more employees in the same classification with
  19|                       the same seniority and the same term of continuous employment with the Town, a part-time employee shall be laid off before
  20|                       a full-time employee. If there are still two or more employees in the same classification, the Town Manager shall determine
  21|                       which employee shall be laid off.
  22|                       An employee that is scheduled to be laid off shall have the right to bump other employees in lower classifications with less
  23|                       seniority, provided that the Town Manager, in his/her sole discretion, but with consultation of the Department Head, shall
  24|                       make a determination that the employee retained is qualified and able to perform the duties of the lower classification, subject
  25|                       to disability discrimination laws.
  26|                       Employees who have been laid off from employment are responsible for keeping the Town advised of their current address
  27|                       for the purpose of potential recall from layoff.
  28|                       Recall Policy - If the town conducts a recall from layoff, recall shall be offered in reverse order of layoff by classification
  29|                       provided the laid off employee is qualified and able to perform the duties of the classification to which the employee is being
  30|                       offered recall, subject to disability discrimination laws.
  31|                       Employees shall only be eligible for recall from layoff under the terms of this policy for one (1) year from the date of the
  32|                       employee's layoff.
  33|                       Any employee who is recalled from layoff under the terms of this policy shall retain his/her seniority, less the period of
  34|                       layoff.
  35|                       The Town will notify an employee of a recall opportunity by certified letter (or other verifiable method of delivery to the
  36|                       employee's last known address.             An employee who has been offered an opportunity to be recalled from layoff must advise
  37|                       the Town of his/her intention to accept recall within five (5) working days.

  41|                       He/she may also receive at the time one or more step rate increases, provided the maximum for the job is not exceeded, if the
  42|                       Department Head recommends that qualification and performance warrant it, and the Personnel Committee approves. If the
  43|                       Department Head should feel that there should be a trial period before recommending the promotional step rate increase or
  44|                       increases, on recommendation of the Department Head, the Personnel Committee may approve any such deferred
  45|                       promotional increase at the conclusion of the trial period.
  46|                       (b)If an employee is transferred to a job with a lower rate range, or rate of pay, he/she shall enter it at their own rate or at the
  47|                       maximum rate for the job, whichever is the lower.

  18|                       May 1 of that year.
  19|                       Employees who do not use any sick leave within a three month (3) month period (calculated quarterly), will be entitled to
  20|                       eight (8) additional personal hours for each such quarter; said personal hours are to be used in the same fiscal year in which
  21|                       they are earned. Personal leave shall be scheduled with the approval of the Department Head. Personal leave shall not be
  22|                       cumulative.
  23|                       SECTION 21. FAMILY AND MEDICAL LEAVE
  24|                       The Town agrees to abide by the provisions of the 1993 Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA). All eligible employees are
  25|                       entitled to up to twelve (12) weeks of unpaid leave for certain family and medical reasons in a one (1) year period.
  26|                       Employees are eligible for FMLA if they have worked for the Town for at least one (1) year and at least for one thousand two
  27|                       hundred and fifty (1,250) hours over the previous twelve (12) months.
  28|                        Unpaid FMLA leave must be granted for any of the following reasons:
  29|                       • To care for the employee's child after birth, or placement for adoption or foster care
  30|                       • To care for the employee's spouse, son, daughter, or parent, who has a serious health condition
  31|                       • For a serious health condition that makes the employee unable to perform the employee's job
  32|                       In accordance with the past practice, the Town allows, at the option of the employee, the use of paid leave time during the
  33|                       FMLA absence including accumulated Sick Leave, Vacation Leave, and Personal Leave. The Town requires all employees
  34|                       applying for an FMLA leave to notify their Department Head, in writing, thirty (30) days prior to the leave, if possible.
  35|                       SECTION 22. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
  36|                       In the event of death in an employee's family (wife, husband, mother (in-law), father (in-law), child, grandparents, brother, or
  37|                       sister) up to and including three days off duty may be granted, with pay, not to be charged against sick leave. Any time
  38|                       above the three days is to be charged against sick leave.
  39|                       SECTION 23. MILITARY LEAVE
  40|                        Town employees with a minimum of six consecutive month's employment with the Town who serve in the State or Federal
  41|                       military training forces under orders shall be allowed full pay for not more than one normal working week and shall be
  42|                       granted a military leave of absence for the period of their required service with said forces.
  43|                       Military leave of absence with pay shall be granted to employees of the Town on occasion of their appearance before local
  44|                       draft boards or draft appeal boards, or for physical examinations ordered by said boards; but no such leave shall be granted
  45|                       for a period of more than one day without the approval of the Personnel Committee. Such leave shall be deemed to be a
  46|                       military leave of absence.
  47|                        A military leave of absence without pay shall be granted to any Town Officer or employee called to active duty with the State
  48|                       or Federal Armed Forces for purposes other than the routine annual tour of duty for training purposes.

  16|                       SECTION 27. DISCIPLINE POLICY
  17|                       Items which constitute grounds for disciplinary action against an employee include: chronic absenteeism or tardiness,
  18|                       insubordination or refusal to work, infraction of work and safety rules, threat of physical violence against fellow employees,
  19|                       use of profane language to a supervisor or fellow employee, or offenses of comparable gravity or severity.
  20|                       Upon determination by the Department Head that disciplinary action is warranted, the following guidelines shall be
  21|                       implemented for all employees that have successfully completed a six (6) month probationary period:
  22|                       STEP 1. 1st Offense - Written warning.
  23|                       STEP 2. 2nd Offense - A formal written reprimand notifying the employee of a one day suspension and that one further
  24|                                              offense shall result in a three day suspension without pay.
  25|                       STEP 3. 3rd Offense - Employee will receive an immediate three day suspension without pay and a written notification that
  26|                                             any further offense shall be grounds for dismissal.
  27|                       STEP 4. 4th Offense - Immediate dismissal of the employee.
  28|                       Depending on the particular circumstances, one or more of the above steps may be skipped. The offense for which
  29|                       disciplinary action is taken may be any combination of the items described above. For example, offense number one could be
  30|                       tardiness, offense number two could be absenteeism, and offense number three could be refusal to work or insubordination.
  31|                       If an employee goes twenty-four (24) months without a further offense, the first offense beyond twenty-four (24) months
  32|                       shall be disciplined at the level of step one.
  33|                       The following infractions are grounds for the immediate dismissal of an employee for just cause by the Department Head.
  34|                       Just cause may include, but shall not be limited to, the following:
  35|                       • Willful neglect or non-performance of one or more assigned duties;
  36|                       • Demonstrated incompetence in the performance of one or more assigned duties;
  37|                       • Behavior that seriously interferes with the normal operation of the municipality, the department, or any member of the
  38|                             workforce:
  39|                       • Insubordination, which shall mean a refusal to carry out a direct order;
  40|                       • Dishonesty in the performance of assigned duties:
  41|                       • Chronic absenteeism or tardiness without reasonable excuse;
  42|                       • Unauthorized possession or use of alcohol or an unprescribed controlled substance during any period of assigned work;
  43|                       • Municipal theft.

  18|                        WHEREAS, the completion of the Project appears to create a connection between the proposed Fitchburg Lateral and the
