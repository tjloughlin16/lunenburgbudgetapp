  17|                       In the early 1990's, with the structure in need of repair/rebuilding The Lake Shirley Association turned
  18|                       over ownership of the dam to the Town. After completion of a total reconstruction, responsibilities for

  26|                       Winter Operations
  27|                                    Snow and ice present a major challenge to our Town. The safety of the general public depends on
  28|                       the ability of the DPW to maintain our roadways in a safe condition despite the severity or the duration of
  29|                       snow and ice events. To that end we develop a winter operations procedure based upon a detailed plan
  30|                       that must be flexible due to the unpredictability of each storm. A consistently high level of service is
  31|                       expected and must be delivered to every part of Town in a timely manner.
  32|                                    Departmental preparedness begins with successful implementation of the capital plan. Financial
  33|                       support is necessary to provide effective fleet rotation insuring minimal interruption of services due to
  34|                       breakdowns and repairs. Many of the front line vehicles are well beyond the optimum time for
  35|                       replacement. In the fall, salt and sand are delivered to the Chase Rd. facility. Lunenburg is in a
  36|                       consortium with 14 other communities in an effort to procure the lowest possible price for salt.
  37|                       Individually the Town also solicits bids for road sand. From late summer to early fall vehicle maintenance
  38|                       staff inspects and prepares the trucks, sanders and plows for winter operations. During the construction
  39|                       season road crews address right-of-way issues, overhanging branches, and pavement defects in order to
  40|                       eliminate obstacles to plowing. Each division within the DPW is involved in winter operations. Typically
  41|                       DPW staff combines with 18-20 subcontractors to provide service.
  42|                                    Generally it is not the amount of snow but the duration of the event that determines the overall
  43|                       cost. For example, a fast moving snow storm of 6-8 inches will cost significantly less than a long duration
  44|                       freezing rain event even though the accumulation is much less. Historically, the department operates in a
  45|                       deficit due to a yearly allocation that is below the cost of an average winter.
