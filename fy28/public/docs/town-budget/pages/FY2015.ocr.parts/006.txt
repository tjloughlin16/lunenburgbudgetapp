   1|                       4.17. VILLAGE CENTER DISTRICT
   2|                       PREAMBLE
   3|                       The Lunenburg Town Center region has tremendous importance in symbolizing the unique character and heritage of
   4|                       Lunenburg. As residents engage with the historic buildings and streetscape of this area in the course of their routine daily
   5|                       business, be it school related, recreational, commercial, or even just driving through, the experience greatly reinforces a
   6|                       profound bond with the town's present community and past heritage.
   7|                       There is an opportunity to significantly improve the quality of this already poignant civic experience and increase the number
   8|                       of people who will be drawn to share in it by expanding the businesses and other attractions in the area. There is also a key
   9|                       challenge to preserving this critical embodiment of our town. Appropriate new uses or remedies must be found to the
  10|                       increasing number of underutilized buildings located about the town center.
  11|                       Town planning experience and best practices, as exemplified in the state's model bylaw for Village Center Districts,
  12|                       demonstrates some key ingredients to sustaining and enriching this type of environment in small towns. One of these is
  13|                       careful stewardship through clear guidelines for development consistent with what the area means to the community.
  14|                        Another is encouraging a level of intensive mixed use development, so that the critical mass of social and economic activity
  15|                       can be reached that will naturally provide the resources and inspiration required to maintain and organically adapt the area
  16|                       landmarks, buildings, and infrastructure without excessive burden on the Town's public finances.
  17|                       4.17.1. Purpose
  18|                       In pursuit of this opportunity and to address this challenge, the Town implements this bylaw and designates this zoning
  19|                       district as Village Center District in order to encourage economic and residential growth that fits the character of the Town.
  20|                       The purposes of the Village Center District are to:
  21|                       a. Build upon the historic development patterns in the existing village centers to create attractive, walkable neighborhoods;
  22|                       b. Encourage adaptive reuse of abandoned, vacant or underutilized buildings or structures where appropriate;
  23|                       c. Allow for a mix of new land uses that are appropriate to both the needs of the community and the scale of surrounding
  24|                       neighborhoods;
  25|                       d. Provide incentives to develop larger parcels at higher densities and in a coordinated, planned approach;
  26|                       e. Maintain a consistently high level of design quality throughout the district;
  27|                       f. Encourage the development of mixed use buildings and campuses that are designed and constructed in a manner that is
  28|                       contextually sensitive to the existing structures and facilities.
  29|                       4.17.2. Establishment
  30|                       The Village Center District is hereby established as of 1 January 2016 and consists of the area shown as the Village Center
  31|                       District on Town of Lunenburg Zoning Map on file with the Town/City Clerk and dated 2 May 2015 as may be amended
  32|                       from time to time by Town Meeting.

  51|                              A.        It is the intent of this bylaw to serve and to protect the Town's compelling interest to promote, protect
  52|                                        and improve the health, safety and welfare of the citizens of the Town by creating areas around
  53|                                        locations where children, or the elderly regularly congregate in concentrated numbers wherein certain

  55|                                                                                                    175

===PAGE 176=== (ocr, 59 lines)
   1|                              A. After careful consideration, the Town finds that this bylaw is the most narrowly tailored means of
   2|                                        limiting, to the fullest extent possible, the opportunity for certain sex offenders to approach or otherwise
   3|                                        come in contact with children or the elderly in places where children or the elderly would naturally
   4|                                        congregate, and that the protection of the health and safety of our children and elderly is a compelling
   5|                                        governmental interest.
   6|                              B.        By the enactment of this or any other bylaw, the Town understands that it cannot remove the threat
   7|                                        posed to or guarantee the safety of children or the elderly or assure the public that sex offenders will
   8|                                        comply with the mandates of this bylaw. This legislation is intended to create a civil, non-punitive
   9|                                        regulatory scheme in order to protect children and the elderly to the extent possible under the
  10|                                        circumstances and not as a punitive measure of any kind.
  11|                              C.         Certain sex offenders pose a clear threat to the children and the elderly residing or visiting in the
  12|                                        community. Because certain sex offenders are more likely than any other type of offender to reoffend by
  13|                                        another sexual offense, the Town desires to impose safety precautions in furtherance of the goal of'
  14|                                        protecting the children and elderly. The purpose of this bylaw is to reduce the potential risk of harm to
  15|                                        children and the elderly in the community by limiting the ability of certain classified levels of sex

  17|                                        designed for use by or are primarily used by children and the elderly, the grounds of a public or private
  18|                                        school for children, a center of facility that provides day care or children's services, a park, other public

   1|                       Section 1. Purpose and Scope
   2|                       The purpose and scope of this bylaw is to protect the safety of the public, to prevent the destruction of aquatic life, waterfowl
   3|                       or other wildlife, the decay of water quality and damage to abutting private and public lands by the inappropriate use of
   4|                       motor vehicles, recreation vehicles or snow vehicles on the waters or frozen waters of Whalom Lake; to promote voluntary
   5|                       compliance with all applicable laws relating to the operation of motor vehicles and when necessary to deter noncompliance
   6|                       through fines and penalties.
   7|                       Section 2. Definitions
   8|                             a) Motor Vehicle: any motor vehicle as defined in M.G.L Chapter 90, § 1.
   9|                             b) All-terrain vehicle: means a motorized recreational vehicle designed or modified for travel on four low pressure
  10|                                   tires and having a seat designed to be straddled by the operator and handlebars for steering control, and recreational
  11|                                   vehicle or off-highway vehicle as defined in CMR 323, § 3.02 and governed by M.G.L Chapter 90B.
  12|                                 Snow vehicle: a motor vehicle designed to travel over ice or snow, having a curb weight of not more than 453
  13|                                   kilograms or 1000 pounds, driven by track or tracks in contact with the snow or ice and steered by a ski or skis in
  14|                                   contact with the snow or ice as defined in CMR 323, & 302 and governed by M.G.L Chapter 90B.
  15|                                   Operation: to ride in or on and control the operation of, or to maintain, suffer, supervise, or permit the operation of,
  16|                                   or to push a snow vehicle or an all- terrain vehicle, or in the case of a motor vehicle, any person who makes use of
  17|                                   any mechanical or electrical agency, which alone or in sequence will set in motion the motive power of the vehicle,
  18|                                   or if the engine is not running, any person who utilizes any combination of actions that would set the vehicle in
  19|                                   motion.
  20|                            e)  Public Access: shall mean the public access located in the City of Leominster or any other place where Whalom
  21|                                   Lake can be accessed by the public.
  22|                             f) Law Enforcement Officer: any police officer, constable or other officer authorized to make arrests or serve process
  23|                                   provided he is in uniform or displays his/her badge of office.
  24|                                 Board: Town of Lunenburg, Board of Selectmen
  25|                       Section 3. Operation of Snow Vehicles and All-Terrain Vehicles
  26|                             A. No person, or group of persons shall operate, or cause to be operated on the waters or frozen waters of Whalom
  27|                                   Lake, any snow vehicles that are not in compliance with Massachusetts CMR 323 § 2.01 to 3.09 & M.G.L. c. 90
  28|                                   § 21 to 35.
  29|                             B.    When the Town of Lunenburg or any other agency or state agency duly authorized by law has posted the waters or
  30|                                   frozen waters of Whalom Lake to prohibit access for or use of snow vehicles or all-terrain vehicles, no person, or
  31|                                   group of persons shall operate any snow vehicle or all-terrain vehicle upon such waters or frozen waters. In the
  32|                                  absence of such a posting, persons utilizing water or the frozen water of Whalom Lake do so at their own risk and
  33|                                  the Town of Lunenburg assumes no responsibility either implied or expressed for the safety of any person who
  34|                                   voluntarily assumes a known and obvious risk inherent in such activities. No person shall operate a snow vehicle or
  35|                                   all-terrain vehicle upon any frozen waters of Whalom Lake under any circumstance unless a minimum of five inches
  36|                                   of solid ice cover is present. The operator shall determine the thickness of the ice and assumes all responsibility in
  37|                                   undertaking an activity with an inherent and known risk.
  38|                       Section 4. Operation of Motor Vehicles Prohibited
  39|                             A. No person, group of persons or organizations shall operate, or cause to be operated, any motor vehicle as defined in
  40|                                   M.G.L. c. 90 § 1 on the waters or frozen waters of Whalom Lake.
  41|                       Section 5. Access
  42|                             B. No snow vehicle or all-terrain vehicle shall enter upon Whalom Lake except by public access or by written
  43|                                   permission by the landowner.
