   1|             SCOPE OF AUTHORITY
   2|                   The Water Supply Protection District is an overlay district superimposed on the zoning districts. This overlay dis-
   3|                   trict shall apply to all new construction, reconstruction, or expansion of existing buildings and new or expanded us-
   4|                  es. Applicable activities and uses in a portion of one of the underlying zoning districts that fall within the Water
   5|                  Supply Protection District must additionally comply with the requirements of this bylaw. Uses prohibited in the un-
   6|                  derlying zoning districts shall not be permitted in the Water Supply Protection District.

  12|                   CMR: Code of Massachusetts Regulations.

  14|                  nutrient content and which is designed for use, or claimed to have value in promoting plant growth, except un-
