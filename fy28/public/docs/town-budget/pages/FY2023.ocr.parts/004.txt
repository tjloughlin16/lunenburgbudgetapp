   3|             Lunenburg Middle School provides a rigorous academic curriculum as well as strong programs that support
   4|             students' social and emotional growth. These programs ensure that each child becomes a self-motivated, crea-
   5|             tive, lifelong learner in a safe, stimulating environment that encourages diversity and open honest communica-
   6|             tion. The dedicated and accomplished staff of professionals within the school embraces the philosophy of ed-
   7|             ucating the whole child academically, socially, and emotionally. This philosophy is a means by which all stu-
   8|             dents become positive community members that possess the skills to be successful in high school and beyond.
   9|              Lunenburg Middle School is a co-located middle - high school serving grades six through eight. Our effective,
  10|             student centered middle school embraces interdisciplinary teams of teachers that enable teachers to work with
  11|             smaller groups of students. Smaller groups of students allow teachers to learn and engage students in their pre-
  12|             ferred learning style, making them more confident and successful.
  13|             Our effective middle school model is student centered and provides opportunities to explore a variety of differ-
  14|             ent interests and talents through special areas and enrichment classes and extracurricular activities. One of our
  15|             goals is for students to discover something they are passionate about and want to continue exploring as they
  16|             move through high school and beyond. We will continue to seek out opportunities for students that foster criti-
  17|             cal thinking and problem solving skills that prepare them for high school and beyond or where their passion
  18|             may lead them to.
  19|             Our grade eight team of teachers continue to implement a schedule that mirrors the high school schedule. Our
  20|             philosophy is to ease the difficult transition from middle school to the first year of high school by developing
  21|             student's independent skills they will require to be successful in high school. Grade eight teachers function as
  22|             one inclusive team where students take classes with a variety of different teachers as they would in a high
  23|             school setting. This differs from our two interdisciplinary team of teachers that have been a successful model
  24|             in grades six and seven.
  25|             LMS continues to utilize the i-Ready learning platform to provide an additional learning component in addi-
  26|             tion to classroom instruction. i-Ready is being implemented during our intervention blocks and provides
  27|             teachers with individualized learning paths based upon the students individual learning needs. Parents will be
  28|             provided fall, winter and spring benchmark assessment reports created by I ready so they too can monitor stu-

  33|             need for these positions as during the pandemic, the number of students experiencing anxiety and depression
