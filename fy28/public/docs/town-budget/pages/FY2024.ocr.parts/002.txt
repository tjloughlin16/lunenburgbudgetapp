   2|             Under the umbrella of Massachusetts state and local town laws, Lunenburg's Assessing organization will work with the town opera-
   3|             tions and our citizens to meet our vision for delivering a fair and equitable taxation for our citizens. The Lunenburg Board of Asses-
   4|             sors will evolve and administer a series of "Best Practices" methodologies consistent our Vision. These practices will ensure equita-
   5|             ble treatment and compassion for all our citizens.
   6|             The Board of Assessors has empowered an Assessor organization that has the capability to evaluate and assess all property at its full
   7|             and fair market value. This is accomplished through a rigorous measurement of property, collection appropriate data, analysis and
   8|             maintenance of all property tax data records. Additionally, the Assessor department administers a motor vehicle excise tax, real es-
   9|             tate and motor vehicle tax abatements, and all statutory tax exemptions as allowed by law. The Assessor Organization will work co-
  10|             hesively with other town departments.

  12|             • We are fully staffed for the first time in over a year. Our new Principal Assessor was hired in March 2024. A Data Collector
  13|             was added in September and the Assessor's Admin role was filled in November.
  14|             • As a result, we have been able to resume a full calendar of reviews and inspections for new construction and building permits.
  15|             •  We actually have gone back to capture some of the permits for review and inspection that we were unable to review in CY2023
  16|             and CY2022.
  17|             • Even in the face of some delays from our consultants and technical difficulties with the financial system, FY2025 tax bills were
  18|             created and mailed on time.
  19|             We successfully lobbied the Town to improve several Exemption eligibility qualifications, including Age reduction for Seniors, As-
  20|             set limit increases, Reimbursement increases and automatic Cost of Living adjustments.
  21|             The Board of Assessors' efforts at restoring the trust of Lunenburg taxpayers include scheduling all our meetings during evenings
  22|             (with both PACC coverage and Zoom access), so everyone can participate more easily. The Board has also developed a Property
  23|             Tax program for use at the Senior Center. We created a guide for "How to Read Your Vision Property Card", that is available in

   2|              The Town of Lunenburg Conservation Commission is a seven-member commission, each appointed

   5|              MGL chapter 131 s 40 and Chapter 239 of the local bylaws for the protection of wetlands. The Com-

   9|              meetings for a wide range of projects from commercial development, subdivisions and small single

  14|             enough funds to offset a worst-case scenario with resident tax abatements. Without the use of ARPA funds, the proposed
