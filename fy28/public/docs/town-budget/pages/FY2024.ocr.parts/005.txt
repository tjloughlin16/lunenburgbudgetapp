  19|             250-4.9 WATER SUPPLY PROTECTION DISTRICT
  20|             A. PURPOSE OF DISTRICT
  21|             The purpose of this Water Supply Protection District is to:
  22|             1. promote the health, safety, and general welfare of the community by ensuring an adequate quality and
  23|                   quantity of drinking water for the Town of Lunenburg;
  24|             2. preserve and protect existing and potential sources of drinking water;
  25|             3. conserve natural resources in the Town of Lunenburg;
  26|             4. and prevent temporary and permanent contamination of the environment.
  27|             B. SCOPE OF AUTHORITY
  28|             1. The Water Supply Protection District is an overlay district superimposed on the zoning districts. This over-
  29|                  lay district shall apply to all new construction, reconstruction, or expansion of existing buildings and new
  30|                   or expanded uses. Applicable activities and uses in a portion of one of the underlying zoning districts that
  31|                  fall within the Water Supply Protection District must additionally comply with the requirements of this by-
  32|                  law. Uses prohibited in the underlying zoning districts shall not be permitted in the Water Supply Protec-
  33|                   tion District.
  34|             2. Nothing in 250-4.9 imposes regulation on parcels or portions of parcels located within Zone I.
