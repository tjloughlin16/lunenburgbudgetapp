First Day of School 
LMHS had a wonderful ﬁrst day back to school. LMHS administration met with all students 
to review what opportunities and supports LMHS has for students to succeed. 
THES - Great ﬁrst day back for staff and students!  Our third graders were greeted in 
cafeteria by their new teachers.   Grade level meetings were held to review expectations and 
get to know the staff at THES.
Primary - Building looks great! Easiest ﬁrst days we’ve had. Held grade level meetings to set 
expectations for students. Held staff meetings for safety processes.
