To: Lunenburg School Committee

Date: March 2, 2022 
Re: Addendum to LHS Program of Studies  
Please see the description of a new math course to be included in the course catalog for the 
2022-2023 school year with your approval. 
 
Algebra II Essentials    (New Course)                   1 credit 
Grades 11-12 
Prerequisite:   Algebra 1A,  Algebra 1B, and teacher recommendation 
This is the third course in a sequential program that is structured to meet the basic 
requirements of college entrance. It is designed to meet the common core standards for 
algebra 2. It continues to build on algebra 1 concepts. Topics to be studied include: linear and 
quadratic functions, polynomial functions, inverse functions, radical functions, and exponential 
functions.
