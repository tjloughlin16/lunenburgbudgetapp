Introduction to Law
We would like to add an Introduction to Law course to the Social Studies program of 
studies for the upcoming 2021-22 school year. The course description would read as 
follows:
Introduction to Law (SS) 0.5 credit
Grades 9-12
The Intro. to Law course includes learning about the civil and criminal legal process, case law and legal rulings as well as 
contract creation and interpretation. It is highly recommended that you join the Mock Trial team when taking this course as 
there will be a strong connection to the Mock Trial program. This course is particularly useful for those who will pursue 
careers in the legal field or law enforcement.
