===PAGE 5===
● Hands-on learning in music enhances learning throughout 
a child’s educational journey.
● Performing ensembles are where young musicians apply 
what they are learning in general music.
● Beginning band and chorus foster a child’s love of 
music-making.
● When children enjoy this experience in elementary school, 
they get hooked.  There is then a greater chance they’ll 
participate in music throughout middle and high school, 
and become lifelong music learners and performers.

Beginning Band
● This Spring, instrument demos will introduce students in 4th and 
5th grade to the instruments of the band and build excitement.
● We’re considering running a Summer Beginning Band Week, in 
coordination with David French Music Company, where students 
can try one instrument from each section (woodwind & brass) .
● Students can then sign up for band and select their instrument 
based on interest and success during the Summer Band Week.
