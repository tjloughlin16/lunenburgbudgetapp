Action Plan 
 Cultivating Culture & Climate 
Priority 1: Foster a climate of safety, caring and inclusivity for all in our district through secure facilities and safety training, evidence-based 
social emotional and mental health curricula, and building strong staff-student relationships,* student peer relationships, staff peer 
relationships, administrator-student/staff relationships, school-family relationships. 
Established/Completed 
❖ Piloted Kinship Caregivers program 
❖ Began a transition to a restorative justice model of discipline  
❖ Created a Behavioral Health Advisory Council to assess need and make recommendations for curriculum and screeners  
❖ Implemented universal screener for grades 9 -12 
❖ Maintain District Safety Team and execution of emergency drills: fire drills, evacuation drills, ALiCE drills  
❖ Established a Behavioral Health Support Team to review teacher referrals of students possibly in need of behavioral health in terventions/supports 
❖ Established a truancy protocol 
❖ Provided Positive Behavioral Interventions & Supports (PBIS) professional development at Lunenburg Primary School  
❖ Piloted EMOTE as a data collection and communication tool for teachers and administrators working with students  with behavioral health needs 
❖ Began a freshmen orientation program; offered school tours for rising students during COVID  
❖ Began using MyCAP to assist high school students in preparation for post -secondary options 
❖ Restructure of the grade 8 team model w/opp ortunity for high school algebra 1 class for grade 8 students  
❖ Guidance counselors no longer assigned to advisory classes so that they can be available to students during advisory  
❖ COVID Safety Protocol Review Team to monitor and review safety protocols  
❖ Reviewed and revised Emergency Operation Protocols  
❖ Continue to enhance safety drills 
❖ Piloted and adopted Tier 1 Social Emotional Learning (SEL) curriculum  
❖ Provide school community (staff and students and their families) with behavioral health supports (Social Worker  in each school, CareSolace referral 
service)
