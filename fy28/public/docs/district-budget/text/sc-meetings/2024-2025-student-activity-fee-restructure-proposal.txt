Student Activity Fee
 (as is defined currently in the High School Handbook)
Student activity fees support all extracurricular activities as well as field trips, 
assemblies, and funding for special events. We rely on these funds to maintain a 
wide variety of options that enrich our school community. Payment of the student 
activity fee is required of all students who wish to participate in clubs, 
organizations, and any school activity. Students who fail to pay this fee will not be 
allowed to participate in extracurricular activities.

Student Activity Fee
 (that incorporates base and special interest within the 
High School Handbook)
Student activity fees comprise a base fee and a special interest fee. The purpose of the 
base fee is to fund homecoming, float design, assemblies, and funding for special events that are 
driven by student council, class advisors, and/or honor society. The purpose of the special 
interest fee mirrors our athletic program in that it allows students to explore an interest 
and revisit that interest if they wish to throughout their high school experience. We rely on 
these both the base fee and special interest fees to maintain a wide variety of options that enrich 
our school community. Payment of the base student activity fee is required of all students who 
attend the high school. Students who fail to pay this fee will not be allowed to participate .  
Special interest fees are to support funding for extracurricular activities that allow students to 
explore and revisit that interest throughout their high school years. For a special interest group to 
be offered, enrollment must be at least 10 in number.
