How We Use DIBELS 8 (Grades K–5)
DIBELS 8 is an approved DESE early literacy screener that we use 
as part of our district-wide literacy assessment system.
● Grades K–2:
DIBELS 8 is used as a universal screener and progress-
monitoring tool to assess foundational reading skills such 
as phonemic awareness, phonics, accuracy, and fluency. 
This allows us to identify students who may need additional 
support early, when intervention is most effective.
● Grades 3–5:
DIBELS 8 is used strategically, primarily to:
○ Monitor oral reading fluency and accuracy
○ Support students who need continued foundational 
skills intervention
○ Inform instructional decisions alongside classroom 
assessments and curriculum-based measures
Across all grades, DIBELS data is reviewed by educators and 
specialists and is used in combination with other assessments, not 
in isolation.

Most Predictive Data
Why End-of-Year Kindergarten NWF & Composite Level Score (CLS) Matter
● NWF shows whether students have mastered the alphabetic principle and can decode unfamiliar 
words—skills essential for reading fluency in Grades 1 –2.
● CLS summarizes overall risk for future reading difficulty based on multiple foundational skills.
Why Oral Reading Fluency (ORF) Matters (Grades 1+)
● ORF measures how accurately and automatically students read connected text.
● Strong ORF frees cognitive energy for comprehension, while weak ORF signals ongoing decoding or 
automaticity gaps.
● ORF is a strong predictor of later reading comprehension and overall reading achievement.

How We Use USNS (Grades K–2)
● Universal screening tool for early number sense
● Identifies how students think about numbers
● Screens understanding of counting, quantity, and number relationships
● Identifies students who may need early math support
● Informs targeted small-group instruction
● Used for progress monitoring over time
What USNS Measures
● Place value understanding
● Counting and quantity
● Number magnitude and relationships
● Early addition and subtraction thinking
Why We Use USNS
● Early number sense predicts later math success
● Identifies gaps early when intervention is most effective
● Provides actionable, instruction-ready data
● Supports timely, targeted intervention
