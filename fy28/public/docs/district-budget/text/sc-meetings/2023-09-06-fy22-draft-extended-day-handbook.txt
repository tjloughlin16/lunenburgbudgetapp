Lunenburg Public Schools Extended Day Handbook 
The Lunenburg Public Schools offer Extended  Day services for families of students enrolled in 
the Lunenburg Public Schools and to children of school employees PK through grade 5. Children 
are required to be toilet trained to attend.  A summer program is also offered for students for PK 
through grade 5. The program is located at the Thomas C. Passios building. This handbook outlines 
the services to assist you in determining if the program meets the needs of your family. 
*Please note, changes in practice and procedure may be required due to c hanging COVID-19 
related guidance.

Rates and Fees 
 
The Extended Day Program is a self -sustaining program, and all fees collected are allocat ed to 
program costs.  The rate for extended day services is established by the School Committee on an 
annual basis.  Please check the web site or registration form for the annual rates. 
 
Call (978)582 -4106, X -315 or Cell  978-414-0246, C ell 978 -514-4202, email 
tperry@lunenburgonline.com, or visit our website at http://www.lunenburgschools.net/extended-
day-program.  
 
Families will be billed for services rendered at the end of each calendar month.  The bill is based 
upon a block billing rate. 
  
Statements are distributed on the first day of the following month and payments are due on the 10th 
of each month.  If the 10th falls on a weekend, payment will be due on the next school day.  Payment 
is expected on the due date unless prior arrangements are made.  Please speak to the p rogram 
Director if you are hav ing difficulty paying your bill on time.  Carrying an outstanding balance 
could result in your child being dropped from the program. If a child is picked up later than 5:30 
p.m., there will be an additional charge of $1.00 per minute . If a pattern of tardiness continues 
your child could be dropped from the program. 
Enrollment/Attendance 
Registrations will be available on February 1st. 
Parents will pay for set schedule, (even if not used) 
Registration forms and emergency contact information must be filled out every year in advance 
for each child attending the program. These are specific forms for the Extended Day Program 
and separate from any forms filled out for the school administration.   
An established cap for a.m. and p.m. sessions will be determined on a yearly basis, not to exceed 
125 per session. 
 
PLEASE NOTE 
We require a schedule of attendance be provided for each child so that we ensure appropriate levels 
of supervision.  Schedules will be provided February1st. It is the parents’ responsibility to notify 
the school and Extended Day of their child’s schedule.  No drop-ins and as needed registrations 
are no longer available. 
 
A schedule of registration notices and deadlines will be issued as needed through school 
publications and websites. 
 
Any child returning on a bus will be dropped off at the Extended Day program, and will be sent to 
the office until picked up.

Pre-Registration 
Pre-registration for early release, vacations, and the summer program will be made available 
throughout the school year. The se forms will be avai lable on line under our website, and at the 
front office.  It is extremely important to fully complete and indicate the time frame needed.  This 
is to ensure that we have appropriate staffing as well as adequate enrollment. 
 
 A non-refundable block billing rate fee will be applied if a child has been signed up on 
one of these days and does not attend. 
 If there is insufficient enrollment, services may be cancelled.  You will receive a 
minimum one-week notice if a decision to cancel services is made. 
 You can withdraw your child without penalty with one week advanced notice. 
Pick-up and Drop-off Procedures 
It is required that adults must sign-in/sign-out their child both in the morning and the afternoon. 
This is for your child’s safety.  Your child will only be released to those individuals listed on the 
registration form.  You must notify staff in writing  or call if someone not on the list is coming to 
pick up your child, or we will not release him/her. 
Please have an ID available when picking up your child. 
Medications/ Health 
Medication policies at Extended Day are the same as those set forth in the student handbook. No 
medications will  be dispensed at Extended Day other than those designated as emergency 
medications (i.e., Epi -pens, inhalers).  Staff are trained in first -aid, Epi -pen, CPR and AED 
Defibrillator.  
        
 The parent/guardian of any child on an inhaler or epi -pen should talk to the school 
nurse and Extended Day Director/Coordinator and establish a written health plan for 
the time the child is enrolled in the Extended Day program. 
 A nurse will be available  for the afternoons from 3:30 -5:30 only, in which if during 
that time medication needs be distributed, the nurse will tend to it. 
Rules, Conduct and Discipline 
Behavior and discipline standards at Extended Day are consistent with those of the school district.  
Students are expected to conduct themselves according to the sta ndards set forth in their student 
handbook.  Please refer to the current handbook for a fully detailed explanation.   
 All members of the Extended Day program are expected to treat each other with courtesy 
and respect at all times. 
 Students will show respect for school property and the property of others. 
 Bullying is absolutely not tolerated. 
 Toys and other personal items not required for the regular school day are prohibited from 
Extended Day.  Toys and other personal items may be permitted during vacations and early 
release days with the permission of program staff . and no sharing  between students 
allowed. 
===PAGE 4===
 Through both environmental and instructional structure, we strive to foster positive 
behavior and self -management in students, greatly reducing the need to ‘discipline’ or 
correct behaviors. 
 Children may be given a verbal reminder to correct inappropriate conduct.  If the behavior 
continues, the child will be asked to se parate from the situation for a brief period of time 
in order to collect themselves and may reenter the activity once they are able to choose 
appropriate behavior.   
 If a child continues to be unable to manage themselves, then a parent will be notified. 
 If behavior continues or is detrimental to the program or other students, then 
administrative intervention will be sought up to and including removal from the 
program.  
 
PLEASE NOTE:  Privacy issues limit the school administration from sharing behavior/education 
plans with Extended Day staff without the written consent of the parent.  Please do not assume that 
we have access to this kind of information.  If there is information that would be helpful in 
providing the best possible care for your child, please speak to the program director/coordinator to 
share any pertinent information. 
 
 
   Hours of Operation  
 
Extended Day PK-Grade 5 Before and After School: 
Standard hours of operation during the school year are 7:00 a.m. to 5:30 p.m., Monday through 
Friday.  The before school program starts at 7:00 a.m.; the after-school program starts at 3:30 p.m. 
for the elementary school and 3:30 p.m. for the primary school. 
 
Early Release Days 
The Extended Day after -school session will start after The Primary School and THE S dismissal 
time and operate 12:30-5:30 p.m. (Block billing rate) 
 
Full Day Professional Development :  The Extended Day program is CLOSED  on full day 
professional development days so that the staff may participate in PD offerings. 
  
Inclement Weather 
Extended Day services will be closed on weather emergency days.  For delayed openings, the 
Extended Day Program opens when the school opens. 
 
Vacations 
Services will be offered on April and February school vacation , with the exception of certain 
observed holidays, and will be provided based on enrollment levels. 
*Closed during the December break in accordance with the district. 
 
Summer 
 The Summer Program begins the first full week of July and will close two full weeks prior to 
school starting. 
Dates, Themes and Field trips will be posted February 1st for parents to plan accordingly.

Daily Activities  
Homework                      
The staff sets aside time for students to work on their homework each afternoon.  All children who 
have homework assignments will be required to work on them unless there is a note  or verbally 
stated from the parent stating that the child does not have to do their homework at extended day. 
Homework could be cancelled on certain days due to unforeseen circumstances. 
 
Indoor activities 
All children attending the extended day program will be allowed to choose from numerous games 
and activities.  
Game Roo m-pool table, air hockey, foos ball, p ing-pong and W ii sports and Just D ance for 
movement. 
Children can also choose to play in the ELC Room with developmental areas in the classroom.   
Art room-arts and crafts. 
The gymnasium can be used during inclement weather. 
 
Outdoor activities 
Students enrolled in the extended day program will go outside. Please be sure that children are 
properly dressed each day for outdoor recess and clothes are labeled. 
 
Snacks 
Children must provide their own snack /drink at Extended Day. This is in accordance with the 
school’s policy on snacks due to the number of children with food allergies.
