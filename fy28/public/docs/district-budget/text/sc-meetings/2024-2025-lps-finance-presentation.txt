FINANCIAL 
MANAGEMENT
 All administrators with budget 
authority manage their respective 
program’s, school’s, department’s 
budget.
 All purchases of goods and services 
are done using purchase orders.
 All contractual salaries are 
encumbered.  
 Purchase orders and the use of 
encumbrances are simple but very 
effective budgetary control 
measures.

FINANCIAL 
MANAGEMENT
 All purchases of goods and services 
should use a Requisition (Reqs)-to-
Purchase Order (POs) Process.
 All purchases of goods and services 
should have prior approval before funds 
are committed.
 Reqs and POs should have the proper 
required information included for 
processing (account number, correct 
amount, estimates for shipping, etc.)
 Reqs & POs are simple but very 
effective budgetary control measures.

FINANCIAL 
MANAGEMENT
 Personnel Control Ledger:  Most 
financial accounting systems have a 
system that has all budgetary 
components of personnel, commonly 
called the “Personnel Control Ledger 
(PCL).”
 A PCL assigns each position in the 
district a number.  Anytime a new 
position is created a new number is 
assigned.
 PCLs are effective to track changes in 
staffing.  They ensure proper approval 
of the new positions; the tracking of 
transfers more effectively; & vacancies.

FINANCIAL 
MANAGEMENT
 Periodically, there will be the need for 
administrators with budget authority to 
move funds from one account to another.
 TMS recommends the following procedure 
(the spirit of which could be adopted as 
policy).
 If a transfer is within DESE function codes, 
no school committee approval required.  
The transfer request does not alter the 
intent of the budget.
 If a transfer is between DESE function 
codes, between buildings or programs, or 
between salary and non-salary, school 
committee approval required.  The transfer 
request alters the original intent of the 
budget.
