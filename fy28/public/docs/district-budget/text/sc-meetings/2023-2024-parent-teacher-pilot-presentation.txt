THES Pilot for Parent/ Teacher Conferences 
2024-2025
For the upcoming year, Turkey Hill Elementary is proposing to hold parent/ teacher 
conferences on January 30th from 1-7pm.  By holding conferences at the mid-point in 
the year, teachers would be better able to describe students strengths and area for 
continued growth.  In addition, teachers would be able to describe the mid-year iReady 
scores.
This was presented to both our School Advisory Council and the Building 
Instructional Leadership Team as well as the  surveying the Turkey Hill Staff.

Lunenburg Primary School 
Parent Teacher Conferences Pilot 2024-2025
We are moving the Parent Teacher Conference date from the Fall to January 30th 2025 1-7 pm. 
We felt that a mid-year conference would better inform families and caregivers of student 
progress and provide the opportunity to develop plans that would address their concerns for 
continued growth for the remainder of the year. This coincides with our mid-year iReady math 
and reading assessments and the Term 2 report cards.
Historically we have not completed report cards in the 1st Term as we have used Parent Teacher 
Conferences as the “grading” for Term 1. With this Pilot we would be looking to providing grades 
in Term 1 and use the the Parent Teacher Conferences as the grading for Term 2. Terms 3 and 4 
would continue to have standardized report card grading.
This was presented to both the School Advisory Council and the Building Instructional 
Leadership Team as well as the  Primary School staff for discussion and feedback.
