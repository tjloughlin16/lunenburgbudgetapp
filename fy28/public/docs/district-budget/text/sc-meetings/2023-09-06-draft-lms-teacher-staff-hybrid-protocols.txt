Safety
 
●
All PPE protocols and requirements must be adhered to.
 
●
Masks/face coverings will be required for all staff and students in all
 
grades.  These face coverings should be worn at all times (outside of
 
mask breaks.)
 
 
●
Staff must wear a mask/face covering and maintain 6 feet of social
 
distancing between themselves and other staff and students.
 
●
There will be signs at entrances to the building reminding students of
 
the safety protocols that are in place to keep everyone safe. There will
 
be stickers placed in the hallways that indicate six feet of space in
 
between students in order to assist with distancing.
 
●
Addendum to LMS Student Handbook
 
●
Hand sanitizer will be available at each building entrance, and staff will
 
be monitoring each entrance upon student arrival and departure..
 
Students will use hand sanitizer upon entrance into the building.
 
●
Lockers will not be used by students in order to minimize passing
 
time.
 
●
Bathroom breaks for classrooms will be scheduled and times for use
 
will be up to the discretion of the teacher. No student will be denied
 
use of the bathroom.

Classrooms
 
●
Students will be assigned to a cohort of learners in the middle school.
 
The first cohort will be the traditional interdisciplinary teaming of
 
students (i.e, 7 Red, 7White) approximately 75 students per team.
 
During hybrid students will further be split into cohorts to keep
 
numbers of students in classrooms small. These will be Cohort A and
 
Cohort B. Cohorts should generally have 13 students.
 
●
Average class sizes across grade levels will remain consistent. In the
 
event that an imbalance occurs, students may be reassigned to
 
another class or team.
 
●
Classrooms will have been disinfected before student arrival. Students
 
will have an assigned seat depending on which cohort they are
 
assigned to in order to keep up to six feet of space between student
 
and teacher. Cohort A and Cohort B will have different student desk
 
assignments. Example:  25 desks in a classroom Cohort A is assigned to
 
12 desks 6 feet apart, Cohort B is assigned to a separate 12 desks six
 
feet apart.
 
●
When a cohort transitions within the team of classrooms, students will
 
sit at desks that were not occupied by the prior homeroom.  This
 
allows for transitioning students to always be seated at a disinfected
 
desk.  Upon exiting a classroom, students are dismissed by row, use
 
hand sanitizer, the teacher sprays the row that was occupied by the
 
cohort, row by row. The next cohort comes in and sits at desks that
 
were not occupied by students of the last class.
 
●
Every effort will be made to ensure that HR cohorts are not mixing in
 
the hallways when transitioning to and from all classes.
 
●
Students will travel to special areas and enrichment classes with their
 
homeroom assigned cohort. Students will attend that special area
 
class for approximately 6 weeks and then transition to a different
 
special area- Ex: PE first 6 weeks, Art six weeks, Tech 6 weeks. When
 
transitioning to special area and enrichment classes, we will attempt
 
===PAGE 3===
to transition as much as possible outdoors for an additional mask
 
break.
 
●
Physical education classes will have further guidelines in order to keep
 
students and teachers safe. Gym classes will happen outside when
 
weather allows, students will need to be 10 feet from each other if
 
participating in a strenuous activity. Equipment will be disinfected in
 
between cohorts of students. Classes may happen inside with
 
appropriate spacing and activity level for indoor gym.
 
 
●
Students will be supervised by an adult during transition times
 
●
Grade eight classes are a little different. Students are in
 
interdisciplinary teams, but also split to attend leveled math classes
 
and foreign language. Teams of students will be approximately 65
 
students. Students will also be split into cohort A and Cohort B.
 
Students might not necessarily travel as homerooms as the schedules
 
differ when attending math or foreign language classes. Students will
 
travel as homeroom cohorts to special areas classes.
 
Arrival &
 
Dismissal
 
Bus
 
●
When students arrive on the bus, they will be directed by an adult to
 
exit their bus two buses at a time.  For example, bus 1 and bus 10 will
 
exit the bus and enter the building. This staggered exit of the bus
 
keeps large amounts of students from forming.  Face coverings must
 
be worn at all times.
 
●
Once off the bus, students will be directed to enter the building
 
through three separate entrances. Grade 8 will enter through the main
 
entrance up the stairs and into their homeroom classrooms. Grade six
 
will enter the building through the middle school staff entrance
 
(located at the end of the middle school wing next to the staff parking
 
lot). Grade 7 will enter the building through the double doors next to
 
the grade six entrance, up the stairs and into their homeroom
 
classrooms. The two entrances will be separated by two double doors.
 
Dismissal
 
●
Bus students will be dismissed first, by homerooms in a staggered
 
fashion to allow for social distancing. After bus students are dismissed,
 
walkers and pickups will be dismissed in a staggered fashion.
 
Homeroom teachers will review arrival and dismissal protocols with
 
their homeroom students
 
 
Student Pick Up & Drop- off
 
 
●
Parent drop will continue in the drop off circle adjacent to the bus
 
circle with students from different families remaining 6 ft. apart. After

Teaching &
 
Learning
 
●
The full scope and sequence of the curriculum will continue to be
 
taught in all subjects.  Social-emotional learning components will also
 
be embedded.
 
●
Hybrid instruction will be rich and rigorous in order for students to
 
engage in the curriculum.  ​
New instruction must be included each
 
day.
 
●
Work/activities/assignments will be graded traditionally, and grades
 
should be recorded in PowerSchool.
 
●
Teachers will be responsible to plan in-person and remote lessons.
 
Best practices from remote and in-person teaching and learning will
 
be combined to create a blended experience for students.
 
 
●
It is strongly encouraged that work/activities/assignments be created
 
in concert with teachers’ grade level/department/subject area
 
colleagues in order to create common learning experiences for
 
students and enhance unity and support among teachers.
 
 
●
All educators will use Google Classroom as the platform through
 
which  to teach and connect with students.  Google Classrooms must
 
be created prior to the first day of school and students must be invited
 
to the Classrooms by the first day of school.
 
●
Administrators should be invited to be teaching members of Google
 
Classrooms in order for them to be able to visit Classrooms remotely,
 
provide support, and help maintain consistency across grade
 
levels/departments.
 
●
Google Meets with each class are required according to the building
 
schedule on Monday.
 
●
Hybrid learning model must provide students with access to at
 
least 35 hours of “live instruction” over a 10-school day period,
 
averaged across the grades.  Live instruction means the
 
combination of in-person and remote synchronous instruction.
 
●
Students must have an opportunity to interact with educators
 
each school day through live streaming. ,​
 including a required daily
 
“live” check-in between students and educators.

of what does and does not count:

Lunch
 
●
Staff members must remain at least 6 feet apart when they are eating
 
lunch. Staff must not be maskless in common areas such as teacher
 
planning rooms.
 
●
Staff should see administration if they are having difficulty finding a
 
safe place to eat lunch.
 
●
Students will be eating lunch in the cafeteria. We will utilize the
 
cafeteria to 25% of its occupancy (70 students). Tables have been
 
marked where students may sit six feet apart and facing in the same
 
direction. We will have homeroom assigned seating. Masks will be
 
removed and placed on a napkin, the outside facing down, until
 
finished eating when the mask is placed back on.
 
●
Transitions from lunch will mostly happen outside the school building,
 
weather permitting, with staff supervision back to academic or special
 
area classes.
 
●
Students will continue to be seated in the interdisciplinary groups and
 
cohorts in the cafeteria, but both grade level teams will be utilizing the
 
cafeteria at the same time. Seats and tables will be disinfected by staff
 
in between lunch periods.
 
 
During the hybrid model, teachers should wear clothing that enables
 
them to feel comfortable, while maintaining a professional culture.
 
Hospital pants or scrubs will be allowed.
