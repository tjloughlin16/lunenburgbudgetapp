Lunenburg  Public  Schools 
The  Mission  of  the  Lunenburg  Public  Schools  is  to  provide  all  students  with  the  skills,  confidence,  and  passion  for  life- long  learning  so  that  they  may  find  their  own  paths  to  successful  careers,  act  ive  citizenship,  and  rewarding  lives.

expansion
 
of
 
district
 
services.
 
A  substantial  portion  of  the  increase  reflects  the  rising  cost  of  maintaining  existing  operations,  including  
contractual

health

in
