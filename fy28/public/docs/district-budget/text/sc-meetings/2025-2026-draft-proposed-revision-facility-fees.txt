SUBJECT: FACILITIES RATES 
 
The memorandum outlines the proposed 50% rate increases of the facility rates to users of the Lunenburg 
Public Schools facilities. These rates were implemented starting July 01, 2025 and some labor rates revised 
on October 1, 2025. 
 
Invoicing 
Initial billing will be done upon approval of the event. Single event payment will be due  before 
the event. 
Seasonal user (i.e. LYSA, LYF & C and LJBBB) payment of fifty percen t (50%) will be 
required before the start of use and the final invoice will be completed at the end of the season 
and be due within ten (10) business days.
