Dear Lunenburg School Committee and Superintendent Dr. Kate Burnham, 
 
The LHS Class of 2015 would like to request that our funds be released to the class so 
that they can be deposited into our class bank account and be used for future class 
activities for the Class of 2015, most notably a yearly scholarship for an unsung hero of future 
Lunenburg High School graduates. 
 
During the time I served as advisor of LHS class of 2015, we fundraised and created a fund 
with more money than we needed or used. We decided to use the funds as a way to pay it 
forward and give away $500 of a scholarship each year. 
 
By working with the current class advisors, we are able to pick a student who is an unsung 
hero of the class, someone who is not in an elected position but plays an integral role in the 
success of their class’ events. Some examples include - building and decorating the float, 
planning the homecoming events, fundraising for the class, and helping to plan and execute a 
prom dance. 
 
By removing the funds from LHS, we will put it into a bank account to use for the scholarship each year. 
I look forward to helping students over the next decade or so, based on how much is in the account 
now. The current amount is $6692.68. 
 
 
Thank you, 
Hailee L. Attorri 
Former Class Advisor of 2015 
History Teacher 
Lunenburg Middle-High School
