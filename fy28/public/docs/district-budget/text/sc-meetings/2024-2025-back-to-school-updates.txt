First Day of School 
LMHS - A great start to the school year! All students met their teachers and followed their 
schedules. All LMS students met with LMS admin to welcome them and let them know they 
are supported. LHS students will meet with LHS admin on Wednesday with the same 
message. 
THES - Great ﬁrst day back for staff and students!  Our third graders were greeted in 
cafeteria by their new teachers.   Mr. Yvon met with each homeroom to introduce himself 
and  to review expectations.
Primary - Great ﬁrst week of school for students in grades 1 and 2, we look forward to 
welcoming Kindergarten and Preschool students on 9/3 for their ﬁrst day of school! Mrs. 
Squier and Mr. Smith met with all the homeroom teachers to introduce themselves.
