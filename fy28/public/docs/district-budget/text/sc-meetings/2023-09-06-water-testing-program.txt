Background
• In past years, Lunenburg Water District typically test 
two point annually in the district. Water District 
received authorization to reduce testing.
• LPS received a grant for MassDEP Lead and Copper 
Management Program in the Spring of 2018 under the 
Lead Contamination Control Act. 
• All water sources for drinking were identified, entered 
into the LCCA Management Program for LMHS, THES, 
Primary and Passios ES.
• Grant funded 100% testing of all sources in June 2018.
Commitment for LPS to test 1/3 of the water sources 
annually.
===PAGE 3===
Potential Sources of Lead in Schools 
Lead solder was banned in 1989. Buildings built before then will likely contain 
lead soldered pipes. Corrosion or wearing-away of lead-based materials can 
add lead to tap water, especially if water sits in the pipes for a long time 
before use. 
===PAGE 4===
Test Procedures
• Collect all water samples before the facility opens and before any water is 
used. Ideally, the water should sit in the pipes unused for at least 8 hours 
but not more than 18 hours before a sample is taken. Do not flush (known 
as pre-stagnation flushing) the tap prior to starting the minimum 8-hour 
stagnation time required for samples collected.
• Test sample in 2020 were done over multiple days (1 to 2 schools a day, 2 
PM flush followed by 8:00 AM draw the next day). Max stagnation time.
• Samples collected with chain of custody and tested by RI analytical.
• RI Analytical to forward results to LCCA Management.

Remediation
• Coordinate with Lunenburg Water District.
• Notifications to staff, parent, occupants
• Flush fixtures 30 seconds at the start of the 
day.
• Retest, if flush was uncertain.
• Replace selected fixtures and near in piping.
• Continue to monitor water conditions.
