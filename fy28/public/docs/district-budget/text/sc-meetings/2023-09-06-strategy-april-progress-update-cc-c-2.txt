Action Plan 
 Cultivating Culture & Climate 
Priority 2: Foster a culture of learning and data use where students, faculty, staff and administrators analyze, synthesize, and apply knowledge and 
skill to work collaboratively to solve problems, think critically, and adjust practice; learn collaboratively from each other and from mistakes; engage 
deeply in reflective practice; and justify allocation of resources needed to ensure a high-quality interactive deeper learning experience with 
appropriate levels of support and challenge for all students. 
Established/Completed 
❖ Professional Learning Communities (PLC) protocols incorporated into various types of meetings across the district.  
❖ Leadership Learning Walks are conducted with debrief. 
❖ Instructional Rounds (content) are conducted with debrief including administrators and teachers.  
❖ Joint Committee ( Admin/LEA reps) meets to review evaluation: power standards/indicators/elements, goals, timeline.  
❖ Central Office Leadership Team meets weekly to ensure the district is addressing needs and allocating resources in a timely manner.  
❖ District Leadership Team meets monthly to provide updates and discuss district -wide initiatives, challenges and successes. 
❖ Instructional Leadership Team established; meetings weekly focused on curriculum, instruction, assessment, goals, evaluation and supervision, and data.  
❖ Leadership Retreat held annually for professional development, reflection, review and revision of Strategy, and goal -setting. 
❖ Established math and literacy benchmark assessments K-5. 
❖ Educator Plans using district-wide goals. 
❖ Incorporating restorative justice in disciplinary practices. 
❖ Developed a Truancy Prevention Plan. 
❖ Created K-8/K-12 Curriculum Coordinators to lead content curriculum work.  
❖ Created a Title I Coordinator position to lead literacy work and math/literacy intervention K -5 
❖ Created a Director of Teaching & Learning position to lead curriculum, instruction, assessment, and professional development.  
❖ Conducted professional development in data use for Leadership. 
❖ Data meetings with teachers and administrators are lead by Title I Coordinator and Director of Teaching & Learning  
❖ Selected a tool to archive a myriad of data 
❖ Use data in Teacher Support Team (TST), 504, and IEP meetings  
❖ Use data to support budget development, resource requests, make adjustments to practice, identify needs  
❖ Established an elementary and secondary Education Research & Review Team  
❖ Curriculum structures include Curriculum Cabinet, Curriculum Department Heads a nd Coordinators, and curriculum teams 
❖ Regularly review benchmark data K-8 in grade-level teams 
❖ MultiTiered System of Supports (MTSS) -academic and SEL- established 
❖ Instructional rounds (content area sweeps by grade-level) are routinely conducted in all schools 
Project Manager: Director of Teaching & Learning
