 Grant Proposal Justification:  FY 2026 - FC 0213 Support  Implementing of Updated 
 Regulations Regarding Time-Out Practices (Federal/COMP) 
 The Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE) has issued 
 significant updates to regulations (603 CMR 46.00 and 603 CMR 18.05) governing the use of 
 time-out practices and designated cool-down or calming rooms. 
 The grant money (up to $50,000) will be used for professional development to ensure the district 
 understands and develops necessary procedures and practices to follow the new regulations as 
 well as to learn about recommended strategies to reduce the use of the Cool Down Room/Time 
 Outs. The grant can also cover costs to implement a robust data collection system to monitor 
 the effectiveness of the implemented strategies to reduce the use of time-out rooms, allowing for 
 ongoing evaluation and adjustment.
