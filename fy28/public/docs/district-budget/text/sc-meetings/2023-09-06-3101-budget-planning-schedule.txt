BUDGET PLANNING/SCHEDULE 3101 
 
The major portion of income for the operation of the public schools is derived from 
local property taxes, and the School Committee will attempt to protect the valid 
interest of the taxpayer. However, the first priority in the development of an annual 
budget will be the educational welfare of the children in our schools. 
 
The annual budget process will formally begin in late October with a general budget 
orientation session for all administrators. 
Coordinators, teachers, and other staff members are expected to submit their budget 
requests to Principals in sufficient time for Principals to review budget requests and 
submit their recommended budgets to the Superintendent before the second week in 
December. 
 
After his/her their review, the Superintendent will then present his/her their budget 
recommendation 
to the School Committee no later than the first scheduled meeting in February. 
 
 
Before completing their budget review and before voting a full budget amount, the 
School Committee will conduct a public hearing on its budget in accordance with 
Chapter 71, Section 38N of the General Laws. Such hearing shall be held not less 
than seven (7) days after publication of a notice thereof in a newspaper having 
general circulation in the town. 
 
It shall be the responsibility of the Superintendent  to coordinate the submission of 
the budget to the Town Manager, School Committee, Select Board and Finance 
Committee.Whatever dates are assigned, the final date for the submission of the 
budget to the Select Board will be arranged cooperatively with the Town Manager, 
School Committee and Finance Committee.
