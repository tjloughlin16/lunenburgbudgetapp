 FY26 Fund Code 0424 – Middle School Exploration Policy and MyCAP

Program Unit: 
 College, Career, and Technical Education 
Purpose and Alignment 
The purpose of this proposal is to support the integration of Lunenburg Public Schools’ Middle 
School Pathway Exploration Policy into a coherent and developmentally appropriate My Career 
and Academic Plan process that intentionally connects middle school exploration experiences to 
authentic high school pathways and postsecondary planning. 
This project aligns directly with the intent of FY26 Fund Code 0424 by strengthening the 
connection between middle school awareness activities and high school academic, career, and 
technical education options. Through intentional educator-led planning and collaboration, this 
work will ensure that middle school students gain early and meaningful exposure to the full 
range of learning opportunities available to them in high school so they are prepared to make 
informed, authentic choices for their secondary education and beyond. 
The proposed activities support the integration of the Middle School Pathway Exploration Policy 
into the district’s MyCAP scope and sequence and align to the Commonwealth’s Career 
Development Model and the state’s Middle School Scope and Sequence for MyCAP. This work 
supports the DESE Educational Vision and Vision of a Graduate by helping students identify 
their interests, strengths, and skills and connect those attributes to future learning and career 
opportunities. 
Project Description and Staff Responsibilities 
Lunenburg High School will convene a MyCAP Planning and Integration Team composed of 
high school educators, school counselors, administrators, and designated middle school partners. 
The team will be led by a designated MyCAP Lead who has direct responsibility for 
coordinating alignment between the Middle School Pathway Exploration Policy and the district’s 
MyCAP scope and sequence. 
Educators participating in this project will engage in structured planning and development 
activities that directly align to allowable uses under this RFP. Specifically, staff will: 
===PAGE 2===
● Collaboratively align the Middle School Pathway Exploration Policy to the district’s 
MyCAP scope and sequence 
 
● Identify and map middle school exploration activities to MyCAP benchmarks to ensure 
vertical coherence 
 
● Define how middle school students document exploration activities within individual 
MyCAPs 
 
● Develop consistent descriptions of high school academic, career, and technical education 
pathways to support student and family understanding 
 
● Participate in cross-grade collaboration between middle school and high school staff to 
strengthen communication and alignment 
 
● Review and refine the Middle School Pathway Exploration Policy to ensure clarity, 
accuracy, and alignment with current MyCAP expectations 
 
● Develop planning tools and guidance to support sustainable MyCAP implementation 
across grade levels 
 
All work will be conducted outside of contractual responsibilities and will focus exclusively on 
planning, development, and policy alignment activities allowable under FY26 Fund Code 0424. 
Staffing and Compensation Plan 
Grant funds will be used to compensate educators for planning, collaboration, and 
implementation work conducted outside of contractual responsibilities in support of integrating 
the Middle School Pathway Exploration Policy into the district’s MyCAP process. Compensating 
educators for this work ensures dedicated time for cross-grade collaboration and policy 
alignment that cannot be accomplished within existing contractual responsibilities. 
Educators will be compensated at an hourly wage rate of $40. Statutory payroll costs associated 
with this compensation will be charged to the grant, including FICA and Massachusetts 
Teachers’ Retirement System contributions. 
The total grant-funded direct cost per hour is approximately $46.67. 
The project will support up to approximately 428 total hours of educator work, collectively 
across participating staff. Total direct compensation costs will not exceed $20,000 and include 
all wages and required payroll costs. An additional 3.89% indirect cost rate will be applied in 
accordance with the district’s DESE-approved rate, bringing the total project cost to $20,778. 
Educator time will be documented in accordance with district procedures and grant requirements. 
Compensation will be limited strictly to allowable activities under this RFP, including MyCAP

Anticipated Outcomes and Deliverables 
As a result of this project, Lunenburg Public Schools will produce the following deliverables: 
● A MyCAP scope and sequence that explicitly reflects Middle School Pathway 
Exploration Policy activities 
 
● A refined Middle School Pathway Exploration Policy aligned to current MyCAP 
expectations 
 
● Planning tools and guidance documents to support consistent MyCAP implementation 
across grade levels 
 
===PAGE 4===
Anticipated outcomes include improved coherence between middle school exploration 
experiences and high school pathways, increased educator capacity to support informed student 
decision-making, and a sustainable planning structure that supports ongoing MyCAP 
implementation. 
These outcomes ensure that career exploration and academic planning are meaningful, 
intentional, and aligned across grade levels, preparing students for high school and beyond. 
Project Timeline 
Project activities will begin upon grant approval and conclude no later than June 30, 2026. All 
expenditures and activities will occur within the approved grant period.
