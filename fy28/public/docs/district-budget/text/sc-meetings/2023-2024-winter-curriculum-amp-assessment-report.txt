 Winter 2024 Assessment Data Report 
 Students in grades K-8 completed the winter diagnostic reading and math assessments in iReady during the first few weeks of January.  Teachers in all 
 grade levels in reading and math continue to work towards the two-year goal of getting 80% of students on or above grade level by the spring of 2024.  The 
 first two figures show some of the overall data from the winter assessments, including the percentage of students who are currently scoring on or above 
 grade level at the mid point in the year and the percent changes from the fall assessment and the spring assessment from 2023.  We are also comparing the 
 scores of each grade level cohort to their winter scores from last year’s winter assessment. 

===PAGE 2===
 HIGHLIGHTS 
 ●  70% of all percent changes are positive, and 6 of the negative percent changes are by 5% or less 
 ●  Five grade levels have 60% or more of students on or above grade level at the mid year (need to move 20% more to reach goal) 
 ●  All grade levels are at or above 50% average growth at the mid-year (50% growth would be typical for mid-year), and multiple grade levels are 
 already over 100% average growth (which is typical for the end of the school year).  We are working to have any students who are one or more 
 grade levels below move grow over 100% in a school year. 
 AREAS FOR CONCENTRATION 
 ●  Concern with larger than 5% percent of students placing two or more grade levels below at grades 4-8 in reading and math

 Updated K-5 Standards-Based Report Cards 
 We have updated the ELA and math standards on our K-5 standards-based report cards to more closely mirror the actual language of the standards.  We 
 combined any like standards (for example, RL (reading literature) and RI (reading informational text) standards.  If a particular standard in ELA or math 
 was not assessed in a particular quarter, that standard would not be graded.  Please see the following links to view our updated report card standards.
