MEMORANDUM
Lunenburg, Title I, IIA, and IVA Directors in FY 2024 Monitoring CohortTo:
Simone Lynch, Director of Federal Grant ProgramsFrom:
May 16, 2025Date:
Notice of FY 2024 ESSA (Titles I, IIA, and IVA) program monitoring completionSubject:
Thank you for submitting materials for your district’s FY 2024 federal grant program monitoring review. Our
federal grant program staff have reviewed the materials your district submitted and, in certain cases, have
requested and obtained additional documentation needed to show evidence of meeting program review
requirements. All district submissions, record of review, and the district’s monitoring outcomes report are located
in the Communication Hub and Monitoring Portal (CHAMP).
This letter is to inform you that your district has provided sufficient evidence to successfully complete the
requirements of the FY 2024 ESSA program monitoring process. There are no follow-up actions required by your
district, so the current review of your district’s federal grant programs (Title I, Title IIA, and Title IVA) is
considered complete.
Your time, effort, and cooperation with required program monitoring activities are greatly appreciated. If you have
any questions or concerns, please contact your ESSA grant monitoring reviewer.
