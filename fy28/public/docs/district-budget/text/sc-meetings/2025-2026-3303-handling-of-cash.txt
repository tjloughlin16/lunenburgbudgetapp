PROCEDURES FOR SAFE AND RESPONSIBLE HOLDING    3303 
AND HANDLING OF CASH RECEIPTS 
 
Whenever possible, receipts collected for school -associated programs should be in check or 
electronic deposit form.  All payments from school -associated accounts must be made according 
to established policy.   
 
Cash shall not be accepted on transactions of more than $50.  
 
When it is necessary to handle cash receipts, they must shall be counted and turned over to the 
appropriate supervisor or building principal on the same day as collected for subsequent deposit 
with the Treasurer on the day received.   
 
Cash receipts or petty cash accumulation, which needs to be temporarily held in a school building, 
must shall be placed in a locked area accessible only to the supervisor or principal.  Cash should 
be placed in a safe. 
 
Receipts collected at events held after school hours should be counted, recorded, and deposited on 
the next working day.
