1.  Decodable  Readers  (estimated  $10,000)  
Decodable  readers  are  texts  intentionally  aligned  to  the  phonics  patterns  students  are  taught,  
requiring

to

rather

or

Decades  of  peer-reviewed  research  demonstrate  that  systematic  phonics  instruction  paired  with  
decodable

in
 
early
 
reading
 
accuracy,
 
fluency,
 
and
 
word
 
recognition
 
(Foorman
 
et
 
al.,
 
2016;
 
Ehri,
 
2020;
 
National
 
Reading
 
Panel).
 
This  investment  is  expected  to  improve  accuracy  and  automaticity  in  early  reading,  strengthen  
the
 
foundation
 
for

and

and

the

It

As  appropriate  and  subject  to  availability  and  compliance  requirements,  this  allocation  may  also

(Ehri,
