Behavioral Health Multi-Tiered System of Support 
Just like academic support, social-emotional and behavioral health supports are 
designed based on level of needed support.
GOAL = provide ALL students foundational lifelong SEL skills.
Target at risk students through evidence based Tier 2 interventions.
Support our most vulnerable students via evidence based Tier 3 interventions.

Trails to Wellness: Tier 1 & 2
Tier 1: Universal Education & Awareness
Social and emotional learning (SEL) curriculum for the classroom to promote 
mental health and wellness and build resiliency among ALL students. 
Tier 2: Early Intervention
Skill-building sessions grounded in cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and 
mindfulness designed to equip students with tools to reduce symptoms of 
anxiety and depression.

SBIRT Screenings (Grade 8 & 10)
Screening, Brief Intervention, and Referral to Treatment (SBIRT) focuses on 
prevention, early detection, risk assessment, brief counseling, and referral for 
assessment in a school setting. 
Utilizes the CRAFFT + N Questionnaire 
March 2016 requires public schools to engage in substance use screening and 
education. 
2019-20 and 2020-21 school years was not required due to pandemic.
This year, Social Work/Adjustment Staff will take off nurse’s plates. Goal is return 
to a collaborative effort in coming years.

Care Solace v. INTERFACE 
Care Solace:
Can be initiated by self on website or phone call.
LPS behavioral health staff can assist with a 
“warm hand off”
Text, email, and calls within 1 hour.
24/7. 
NOT an Emergency Hotline.
Exclusive to school community; includes LPS 
staff
INTERFACE:
Must be initiated by self via phone call.
Staff can provide information, but can not call 
on the student/family behalf. 
Communication strictly via phone. 
Graduate students (clinicians in training) assist. 
Monday-Friday 9am to 5pm.
NOT an Emergency Hotline
Can be utilized by anyone living in Lunenburg

SNAP
School Nurse electronic health record (EHR) software system.
Secure, web-based software suite designed to save time and reduce district 
liability.

collateral contacts).
