1.  Overview 
 
As both the cost and manner of providing services have increased, municipalities and public school 
districts have consistently felt a strain to uphold countless and evolving standards in support of all 
stakeholders. Given the continual rise in these demands, municipalities and schools have been motivated 
to review internal operations, including staffing, in an effort to identify operational efficiencies, not only 
to achieve financial savings but also improved service delivery. 
 
The Town of Lunenburg, in partnership with Lunenburg Public Schools, is seeking opportunities to be more 
efficient in the delivery of finance -related operational activities . As the Town and District  continue to 
evolve through staffing transitions, implementation of technology, and increased financial transparency 
goals, an assessment is needed to identify areas to improve systems, processes, and workflows. This study 
will assess the practices and staffing in the finance functions within the Town and District. 
 
The Town and District seek assistance from the Edward J. Collins, Jr. Center for Public Management (Collins 
Center) to review the current roles and responsibilities of associated finance personnel. The Collins Center 
Project Team (Project Team) will inspect the organizational structure, staffing, and general workflow of 
the noted operational area  to identify potential efficiency improvements. The work may include 
interviewing key personnel, analyzing existing policies / procedures and job descriptions, contemplating 
current and future Town  and District goals and strategic plans, and/or employing an internal duties and 
responsibilities survey. Underlying this work, the Town  and District wish to foster a culture of employee 
staffing and workflow equity as well as excellent customer service.

Step 3: Conduct Interviews 
The Project Team will interview key staff as identified during Step 1, to understand the functions and 
relationships of their positions / departments and to solicit ideas and suggestions for improvements 
surrounding Finance / Accounting, Payroll and Benefits operations. These interviews could be one- on-
one, small group, or focus group compilations and span any number of departments. I nterviews are 
hosted by Project Team members and conducted virtually. 
Questions that may be asked and answered include, but are not limited to: 
General Questions  
1. From your perspective, in your role, what is the greatest strength of the Town  / District? What is 
the greatest challenge?  
2. Are there finance or accounting tasks, functions, and responsibilities not being addressed in a 
timely manner?  
3. Are there payroll and benefits tasks, functions, and responsibilities not being addressed in a timely 
manner? 
4. In your opinion, is the current complement of finance and accounting staff sufficient to meet your 
needs? Are additional personnel needed within the Town , District, or the Finance Department / 
Business Office? If yes, what positions/responsibilities are needed and why? If no, are there any 
redundancies?  
5. In your opinion, is the current complement of Payroll & Benefits staff sufficient to meet your 
needs? Are additional personnel needed within the Town, District, or the Payroll / Benefits areas? 
If yes, what positions/responsibilities are needed and why? If no, are there any redundancies? 
6. In general, are there any practices, tasks, and/or traditions that you or Town  / District staff 
perform that you believe are no longer necessary but continue to be done because “that’s the 
way we do it or always have done it” relating to Financ e/Accounting, Payroll or Benefits  
operations?  
7. Please describe your understanding and/or involvement with collaboration between the school 
district and the Town regarding services in the finance, accounting, payroll and benefits functions. 
8. What can be done in your area of responsibility to better serve all stakeholders?  
9. If you could make one immediate change, what would that be and why?

Step 4: Deploy Internal Online Survey 
The Project Team will develop and deploy an online survey designed to collect data surrounding the 
general roles and responsibilities of certain operational areas; finance, accounting, payroll and benefits  
being the primary focus. This information will be instrumental in identifying gaps in tasks as well as 
duplication of efforts. Each of the individuals identified for an interview will be asked to complete a survey 
to codify the understanding of their roles and responsibilities. The Project Team will then analyze the 
results and compare the data against trends that emerge during interview sessions.  
Step 5: Preparation of Preliminary Findings & Recommendations 
The Project Team will identify the trends and findings that emerge through the qualitative and 
quantitative methods discussed in Steps 2 through 4. A series of potential recommendations will be 
compiled to both frame and create actionable steps addressing Finance / Accounting, Payroll and Benefits 
operations. Potential actions might include, but are not limited to, a realignment of roles and 
responsibilities; the creation of new leadership and/or support positions; shared services modeling; and / 
or a more efficient workflow process. The draft report, summarizing the findings and alternatives, will be 
reviewed with the Town and District to gather feedback. Any comments from the Town and District will 
be incorporated into the report as appropriate. In the event the Project Team does not agree with changes 
suggested by the Town and District, and cannot come to a mutually agreeable solution, the Project Team 
reserves the right to discard the changes, include them with a disclaimer, or otherwise attribute such 
changes to the Town and District.   
Step 6: Develop and Submit Final Study Report  
The Project Team, in conjunction with Town and District leadership, will confirm the draft document and 
will transmit the final document in electronic format to the primary point of contact(s) identified in Step 
1.
