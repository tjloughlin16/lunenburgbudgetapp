Lunenburg Public Schools: Student Data 
Privacy and Security Policy 
Note: This is a working draft document currently under review and subject to change. 
At Lunenburg Public Schools (LPS), safeguarding student privacy is a fundamental 
responsibility. We thoughtfully collect and utilize student data to enhance academic 
programming, ensure student safety, and streamline district operations (such as food services 
and transportation). LPS is committed to adhering to industry best practices and all local, state, 
and federal privacy laws to ensure a secure educational and digital environment for every 
student. 
Protecting student data is an active, ongoing commitment. The district continuously reviews and 
tightens the protocols used to evaluate the digital tools, software, and resources deployed in our 
classrooms. This includes thoroughly vetting vendor terms of service, strict data privacy 
agreements, and requiring explicit permission before utilizing non-district resources that may 
require the exchange of personal student information. 
Categories of Data Collected by LPS 
To support our educational mission, the district collects specific types of information, including 
but not limited to: 
Enrollment & Demographic Data: 
● Biographical and household information 
● Demographics 
● Health, medical, and allergy information 
● Financial records (e.g., documentation for the Meal Benefits Program) 
Academic & Student Life Records: 
● State and District Student ID Numbers 
● Daily attendance and disciplinary records 
● Class schedules and academic grades 
===PAGE 2===
 
● Individualized Education Programs (IEP) and 504 Plans 
● Transportation and bus routing records 
Digital Footprint & Technology Records: 
● District-issued usernames, passwords, and email communications 
● Network traffic and internet browsing history on the district network and district owned 
devices 
● Educational software and application usage statistics 
How LPS Utilizes Student Data 
We leverage this information strictly for educational and operational enhancement, such as to: 
● Deliver high-quality academics, personalized learning experiences, and specialized 
programming. 
● Facilitate vital communication with parents and guardians regarding their child's progress 
and school events. 
● Manage and improve essential district services, including transportation, school nutrition, 
and athletics. 
● Determine eligibility for various local, state, and federal programs and accommodations. 
*Note: The examples provided above are intended to illustrate general data use and do not 
constitute an exhaustive list. For specific inquiries, please contact your school's administration. 
Data Access and Third-Party Sharing 
Internal Access: Student data is strictly provisioned on a "need-to-know" basis. Access is 
limited to school administrators, teachers, and contracted support staff who have a legitimate 
educational interest in the student. This includes personnel serving in academic, administrative, 
diagnostic, therapeutic, or guidance capacities. 
External Vendors: LPS occasionally partners with select third-party educational vendors. Data 
is only shared with these partners for the explicit purpose of supporting district curriculum, 
instruction, and daily operations. All third-party vendors are bound by strict data-sharing 
agreements that prohibit the unauthorized use or monetization of student information. 
LPS STRICTLY PROHIBITS THE SALE OF STUDENT DATA. 
Legal Framework & References: Data access is additionally governed by, and granted in 
accordance with, the following regulations and district policies: 
● Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) 
● Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

● Massachusetts Student Records Law - 603 CMR 23.00 
● Lunenburg Public Schools Student Handbooks 
● Lunenburg Public Schools Student Record Keeping Policy 
STUDENT DATA AND AI 
The District shall use AI in a manner consistent with the law and the needs of the District.  
However, student data covered by FERPA, COPPA, or this policy shall not be used, inputted, or 
otherwise shared with open AI systems.  Such information may be used, inputted, or otherwise 
shared with closed AI systems.  The District shall verify that all AI used with student data 
covered by this policy is a closed AI system.
