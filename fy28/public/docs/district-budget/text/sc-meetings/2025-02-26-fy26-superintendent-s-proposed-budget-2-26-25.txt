Mission
The Mission of the Lunenburg Public Schools is to provide all students with the skills, 
confidence, and passion for life-long learning so that they may find their own paths to 
successful careers, active citizenship, and rewarding lives.
Vision
The Lunenburg Public Schools is committed to becoming a leader in education by 
focusing on its values of attending to the personal well-being of its students, providing 
high-quality curricula and student-centered instruction for deeper learning at all levels, 
and promoting the development of attributes and skills necessary for continuous 
learning for all.

Class Size Research - Takeaways
A meta-analysis* of class size and student achievement found that (May 
2023)
➢ the claim that students learn faster and perform better in smaller classes is 
inconsistent with the bulk of empirical evidence. 
➢ the implied class size effect is close to zero (negligible) across methods, students, 
schools/jurisdictions, and also subjects
➢ there is no systematic evidence to suggest that disadvantaged students benefit from 
class size reductions
STAR (Student Teacher Achievement Ratio) Research (1985-1989)
➢ A 1999 independent investigation of the STAR experiment found important design and 
implementation issues; effects only in kindergarten and possibly first grade.
*sample of 1,767 estimates collected from 62 studies and for each estimate codify 42 factors reflecting 
estimation context

Reading Grade 3
Grade 3 is often cited in reading research because it is considered a critical juncture in a 
child's reading development. Slow reading acquisition has cognitive, behavioral, and 
motivational consequences that slow the development of other cognitive skills and inhibit 
performance on many academic tasks. In short, as reading develops, other cognitive 
processes linked to it track the level of reading skill. Knowledge bases that are in 
reciprocal relationships with reading also are inhibited from further development. The 
longer this developmental sequence is allowed to continue, the more generalized the 
deficits will become, seeping into more and more areas of cognition and behavior. 
Achieving reading proficiency by the end of third grade is crucial for future academic 
success.

Individuals with Disabilities Act (IDEA) and 
MTSS
IDEA is a law that designed to ensure students with 
disabilities receive appropriate educational support.
Traditional approach
Historically, schools often used a "wait to fail" model where students were only 
evaluated for special education needs after demonstrating significant 
academic difficulties, which can lead to delays in receiving necessary support.
Multi-Tiered System of Support
To address "wait to fail,” many schools now use MTSS, a system where students 
receive tiered interventions early on, allowing for earlier identification of learning 
difficulties and intervention before significant academic gaps develop.

instructional practice and significantly improve student learning
