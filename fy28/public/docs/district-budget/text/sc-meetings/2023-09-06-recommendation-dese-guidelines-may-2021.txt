Memorandum 
 
To: Lunenburg School Committee 
From: Dr. Kate Burnham, Superintendent of Schools 
Date: June 3, 2021 
Re: Recommendation- DESE updated guidelines 
 
DESE’s updated guidance released two weeks ago was:  
 stop outdoor masking; no social distance requirement when outdoors unmasked 
 high touch surface cleaning could be done just once per day 
 
We conducted the parent survey and consulted with the Board of Health. Based on that 
feedback, this is our recommendation for all schools and school programs: 
 
Drop the outdoor mask requirement (Parents in support; BOH in support) If 
approved, this change would begin Friday (6/4).  Any student may continue to mask 
outdoors if the family wishes them to do so. 
 
Maintain the cleaning protocols for high touch surfaces [at LMHS – disinfecting 
desks between classes] (Not as many parents comfortable with dropping this protocol; 
BOH in support of dropping this protocol.) With only 2 weeks remaining, I am 
recommending that we do not change this protocol until return in the fall.
