Educator Evaluation LinkStandard I: Curriculum, Planning, and Assessment
Key Data FindingsWe have completely revamped our History Department course selection options. Based on this year’s enrollment data we want to monitor over the next fewyears and compare to previous years. We have a 3.0 History credit for LHS graduation requirements. We are hoping to see in the data that more students enrollin 3 plus years of History classes with these new .5 course credit options. We hope to see students pursue more history classes designated to very specific timeperiods in history. For the ELA & Math changes we want to track student success in the class especially for Math.

Evaluate the English and Math curriculum and revamp/update those curriculum mapsand pedagogical directions. With a new English and Math curriculum we are hoping that we developed a high-quality, rigorous curriculum that demonstrateshigh levels of teaching and learning.
