GROWTH 
MEASURES
TYPICAL GROWTH
The average growth of students at each grade and initial 
placement level. Typical Growth allows you to see how a 
student is growing compared to average student growth at 
the same grade and initial placement level.
STRETCH GROWTH
The growth recommended to put students who are below 
grade level on a path toward proﬁciency and students who 
are on grade level on a path to advanced proﬁciency levels. 
Students who are further behind have larger growth 
benchmarks to catch them up, and it will likely take many 
students more than one year to achieve proﬁciency. Students 
who are already proﬁcient have aspirational stretch growth 
benchmarks to advance to or maintain above-grade level 
proﬁciency. Stretch Growth benchmarks are designed to be 
ambitious, yet achievable.

MTSS Intervention Model
● MTSS groupings are determined through a combination of iReady 
Diagnostic results as well as teacher observations and input
● iReady assigns instructional groupings which are used as the launch point 
for MTSS instructional groupings
● Small groups are created by grade level and need, typically between 2-6 
students per group based on need
Numbers and Operations
Algebraic Thinking
===PAGE 36===
Target Skills
● iReady provides target skills for students falling into an instructional group 2 or 
more grade levels below in Numbers & Operations, and Algebraic Thinking.
● Students receive two days of literacy and/or math instruction in pull-out 
Intervention blocks, each approximately 30 minutes in length.

Data Review and Communication
● Assessment data is reviewed by teachers, special education staff, and 
MTSS tutors, as well as the Math Instructional Specialist
● Student growth is expected to be 50% by midyear
● Data is analyzed after each diagnostic assessment, and every 8-10 
weeks when instructional groupings are completed in order to assess 
progress and make adjustments
● Letters are sent to parents when a student enters an MTSS group or 
exits the group.
● Principals share assessment data with families.

○ Progress in the iReady Personalized Learning Platform, or growth in the 
iReady diagnostic
○ Teacher and tutor feedback based on classroom observations and 
assessments
○ Progress to Annual Typical Growth
