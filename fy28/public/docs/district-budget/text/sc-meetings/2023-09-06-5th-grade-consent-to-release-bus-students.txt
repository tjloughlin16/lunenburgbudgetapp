PARENT CONSENT AND RELEASE  
FOR 5th GRADE STUDENT TO LEAVE BUS/SCHOOL WITHOUT ADULT PRESENT 
 
It is the current policy of the Lunenburg Public Schools not to let a student (PK -grade 5) off the 
bus or out of the school as a walker if there is no parent/adult there to receive them. Students not 
met at the bus stop  are brought back to T.C. Passios and the parents will be notified by the 
Superintendent’s Office. By signing this Consent and Release , you are asking the Lunenburg 
Public Schools to release your Fifth Grade student from the bus or the school building without a 
parent/adult present, and waiving any rights or claims of action you or your child may have as a 
result of this decision.

(“Student”), a student in Grade 5, do hereby CONSENT to his/her release from

without a parent or other adult present to supervise my child.   I understand that younger siblings

I acknowledge that this decision is voluntary , and that I have considered and understand the 
possible risks associated with allowing my child to be dropped-off from the school bus or allowed 
to leave school without adult supervision.  I give my consent freely with a complete understanding 
of these risks, which I assume on behalf of myself and Student.   
 
On behalf of myself and my minor child I hereby release, discharge, and covenant to hold harmless 
the Lunenburg Public Schools, the Town of Lunenburg, and their elected and appointed officials 
(including the Lunenburg School Committee) , officers, agents, employees, insurers, attorneys, 
servants, and affiliates, including their successors and assigns, from any and all manner of actions 
and causes of action, judgments, damages, claims, or demands whatsoever, known or unknown, at 
law or in equity, on account of, or in any way arising out of, my decision to allow Student to be 
released from school and/or from the bus without supervision. 
 
This is a legal document which, once signed, will have the effect of releasing legal claims that you 
might otherwise have against the Lunenburg Public Schools.  You are advised to consult an 
attorney before signing it to ensure that you fully understand your legal rights and obligations.
