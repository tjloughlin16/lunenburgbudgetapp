 Action Plan 
 Transforming Teaching & Learning 
Priority 1: Establish effective teams to lead the essential work of teaching and learning, including development of high-quality, rigorous, relevant 
and enriching curricula, with a priority focus on literacy and social-emotional learning; identification and communication of expectations for 
demonstration of high-quality teaching and learning competencies; and implementation of effective instructional practices, interventions, 
communication, and professional development. 
Established/Completed 
❖ Reviewed curriculum scope, sequence, pacing, and alignment by Curriculum Teams meeting once per month under the leadership of the Department Heads 
or Curriculum Lead Coordinators; overseen by Director of Teaching and Learning  
❖ Created a Curriculum webpage with DESE Curriculum Frameworks and district curriculum maps; some still under review  
❖ Established Professional Development Committee, now led by the Director of Teaching & Learning, which meets regularly to (1) plan and facilitate two full-
day PD days, (2) conduct an annual professional development (PD) needs assessment, (3) solicit teachers to teach workshops/book studies for teachers 
(developed a workshop proposal template and a book study facilitation template - which requires connection to Strategy or SIPs) 
❖ Implemented district benchmark assessments in literacy and math K -5 (iReady) 
❖ Professional Development provided by vendors for iReady Learning and Assessment platforms  
❖ Through Leadership Learning Walks, content Instructional Rounds, and review of DESE recommendations for evaluation (2020 -21; 2021-22,) identified 
highest priority Standards/Indicators/Elements for Educator Evaluation; proposed and accepted by Joint Evaluat ion Committee; created district-wide 
Professional Practice Goal and Student Learning Goal 
❖ Established small group intervention model using benchmark data and progress monitoring  
❖ Teachers/Leaders used data at Teacher Support Team (TST), Behavioral Health Su pport Team (BHST), 504 and Individualized Education Plan (IEP) 
meetings 
❖ Established District Leadership Team to ensure multiple perspectives and collective commitment to the Strategy  
❖ Established an Instructional Leadership Team to focus on instruction, cur riculum, assessment, data, supervision & evaluation 
❖ Established a Professional Development Committee to create a sustainable in -district and multi-district model of professional development 
❖ Established a Behavioral Health Advisory Council to lead diversity , equity and inclusion work in the district 
❖ Established an Equity Advisory Council to self-assess based on 11 Dimensions of Equity 
❖ Created Content Curriculum Coordinators to lead curriculum teams in the review and revision of curriculum  
❖ Examined schedules in each school and made changes to allow for more structured time on learning, common planning and intervention  
❖ Developed and implemented Assessment Schedule 
❖ Established an after school tutoring program 
❖ Adopted Social Emotional Learning curriculum (Trails to Wellness) 
❖ Established regular review of student data in common planning blocks  
❖ Provide coaching supports for teachers in math and ELA/literacy  
❖ Conduct curriculum walks 
❖ Established Curriculum Cabinet 
❖ Created an Education Research & Review Team 
❖ The LHS English department will use Common Lit benchmark assessments in the 22-23 school year. 
Project Manager: Director of Teaching & Learning
