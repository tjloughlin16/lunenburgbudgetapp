SUBJECT:  FY23 USE OF FACILITIES RATES 
 
The memorandum outlines the proposed rate increases to the labor and facility rates to users of the 
Lunenburg Public Schools facilities.  These rates are recommended to be implemented starting July 01, 
2022.  
 
Current Invoicing Remaining in Place 
Initial billing will be done upon approval of the event.  Payment will be due no later than seventy 
two (72) hours before the event, except for seasonal user (i.e. LYSA, LYF & C and LJBBB). 
Season user payment of fifty percent (50%) will be required seventy two (72) hours before the 
start of use, an additional twenty five percent (25%) is due forty five (45) days after the start of 
the season and the final invoice will be completed at the end of the season and be due within ten 
(10) work days. 
 
Recommended Labor Rates 
There are slight increases for the labor rates due to the new collective bargaining agreement 
being settled.  The new position in this group is the Concession Stand Monitor, who will be 
overseeing the compliance to local health and safety regulations.
