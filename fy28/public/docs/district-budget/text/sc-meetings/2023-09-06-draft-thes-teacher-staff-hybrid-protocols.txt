Safety
 
●
CHECK WITH KATIE MCGUIRE for any COVID related concerns.
 
●
All PPE protocols and requirements must be observed.
 
●
Masks/face coverings will be required for all staff and students in all
 
grades.  These face coverings should be worn at all times (outside of
 
mask breaks.)
 
 
●
Staff must wear a mask/face covering and maintain 6 feet of social
 
distancing between themselves and other staff and students.
 
●
There will be signs at entrances to the building reminding students of
 
the safety protocols that are in place to keep everyone safe. There will
 
be stickers placed in the hallways that indicate six feet of space in
 
between students in order to assist with distancing.
 
●
Hand sanitizer will be available at each building entrance, and staff will
 
be monitoring each entrance upon student arrival and departure..
 
Students will use hand sanitizer upon entrance into the classroom.
 
●
Lockers will be  used by students only for jackets and lunches.
 
Teachers will develop a schedule so only one cohort at a time accesses
 
lockers.
 
●
Bathrooms breaks for classrooms will be developed during common
 
planning first 10 days of school
 
 
 
Mask Breaks
 
●
Each homeroom has been assigned a mask break space outside the
 
school building. Teachers will design a schedule that offers masks
 
breaks approximately every hour.

●
If a student is attending class and suddenly is in need of an individual
 
mask break, they will be allowed to enter the hallway, stand on one of
 
the blue “six feet apart” stickers, remove their mask for a quick mask
 
break and then return to class with the mask back on the nose and
 
mouth. No mask breaks are allowed in the classroom.
 
●
Students are not allowed to take mask breaks in the classroom.
 
 
Water Breaks
 
●
Water breaks can be taken during scheduled mask breaks or can be
 
requested by a student on an individual basis in the hallway, on a six
 
foot dot away from peers.
 
Classrooms
 
●
Students will be assigned to a cohort of learners. For 4th and 5th
 
grade, the first level of  cohort will be the traditional interdisciplinary
 
teaming of students (i.e, 5 Red, 5White).  During hybrid students will
 
further be split into cohorts to keep numbers of students in
 
classrooms small. These will be Cohort A and Cohort B. Cohorts should
 
generally have no more than 13 students.
 
●
Classrooms will have been disinfected before student arrival. Students
 
will have an assigned seat to keep up to six feet of space between
 
student and teacher.
 
 
●
 Upon exiting a classroom, students are dismissed by row, use hand
 
sanitizer, the teacher sprays the row that was occupied by the cohort,
 
row by row. The mask break allows the disinfectant to dry on surfaces.
 
The next cohort comes in and sits at assigned seats.
 
●
No more than two educators should be assigned to a classroom
 
cohort.
 
●
Monitor classroom environment and immediately report to
 
administration if a student violates the mask or social distancing
 
guidelines.
 
●
Bins will be provided in the hallways to drop off micro fibers provided
 
for classroom cleaning and returned to the classroom.
 
 
●
Students will travel to special areas and enrichment classes with their
 
homeroom assigned cohort. Students will attend that special area
 
class for approximately 8 days and then transition to a different special
 
area- Ex: PE first 8 weeks, Art8 days, Tech 8 days. . When transitioning
 
to special area and enrichment classes, we will attempt to transition as
 
much as possible to include a mask break.
 
●
Physical education classes will have further guidelines in order to keep
 
students and teachers safe. Gym classes will happen outside when
 
weather allows, students will need to be 10 feet from each other if
 
participating in a strenuous activity. Equipment will be disinfected in
 
between cohorts of students. Classes may happen inside with
 
appropriate spacing and activity level for indoor gym.

Arrival &
 
Dismissal
 
Bus
 
●
When students arrive on the bus, they will be directed by an adult to
 
exit their bus two buses at a time.  For example, bus 1 and bus 10 will
 
exit the bus and enter the building. This staggered exit of the bus
 
keeps large amounts of students from forming.  Face coverings must
 
be worn at all times.
 
●
Once off the bus, students will be directed to enter the building
 
through two separate entrances. Grade 4 will enter through the main
 
entrance  on the left, out the gym door, across the quad, up the stairs
 
and into their homeroom classrooms. Grade 5 and 3 will enter
 
traditionally.
 
 
Dismissal
 
●
Bus students will be dismissed first, by bus numbers in a staggered
 
fashion to allow for social distancing. After bus students are dismissed,
 
pickups will be seated in the cafeteria in assigned seats and dismissed
 
individually..
 
 
Pick Up & Drop
 
●
Parent drop will continue at the street corner.  Students will enter the
 
side entrance (by the cafeteria) and, if it’s before first bell, will take an
 
assigned seat in the cafeteria.
 
Teaching &
 
Learning
 
●
The full scope and sequence of the curriculum will continue to be
 
taught in all subjects.  Social-emotional learning classes will also be
 
added to the specials rotation..
 
●
Hybrid instruction will be rich and rigorous in order for students to
 
engage in the curriculum.  New instruction must be included each
 
day.
 
●
Work/activities/assignments will be graded traditionally, and grades
 
should be recorded in PowerSchool.
 
●
Teachers will be responsible to plan in-person and remote lessons.
 
Best practices from remote and in-person teaching and learning will
 
be combined to create a blended experience for students.
 
 
●
It is strongly encouraged that work/activities/assignments be created
 
in concert with teachers’ grade level/department/subject area
 
colleagues in order to create common learning experiences for
 
students and enhance unity and support among teachers.
 
 
●
All educators will use Google Classroom as the platform through
 
which  to teach and connect with students.  Google Classrooms must

●
Communication with parents and students is part of a teachers

●
Students who have failed to engage during the remote days for the
 
hybrid model more than two times should be addressed by the
 
teacher in a communication home and a brief student
 
communication upon their return to school. Subsequent absences
 
after communication with home and the student will be brought to
 
guidance. Guidance will communicate with home and provide
 
strategies for engagement. If continued absences from remote
 
learning continue after the following steps have been taken,
 
administration and the Student Engagement Coordinator will develop
 
an engagement plan for the student and family.
 
 
●
Teachers are expected to reach out to homeroom students and
 
families between September 7-11  via email or telephone call.  This
 
initial outreach will be vital in establishing rapport, identifying
 
vulnerable students/families, and offering resources as necessary. It
 
will also serve as notifying families which team, homeroom and cohort
 
the student is assigned to.  For the remainder of the school year it is
 
encouraged that teachers reach out to students and families at a
 
minimum of twice a month for non-academic check-ins.

Lunch
 
●
Staff members must remain at least 6 feet apart when they are eating
 
lunch.
 
●
Students will be eating lunch in the cafeteria. We will utilize the
 
cafeteria to  50%of its occupancy (70 students). Tables have been
 
marked where students may sit six feet apart and facing in the same
 
direction. We will have homeroom assigned seating. Masks will be
 
removed and placed on a napkin, the outside facing down, until
 
finished eating when the mask is placed back on.
 
●
Recess will occur in the same area that has been identified as a mask
 
break area. All teachers will be responsible for supervising their cohort
 
during recess.
 
 
●
Transitions from recess will mostly happen outside the school building,
 
weather permitting, with staff supervision back to academic or special
 
area classes.
 
●
Seats and tables will be disinfected by staff in between lunch periods.
 
 
During the hybrid model, teachers should wear clothing that enables
 
them to feel comfortable, while maintaining a professional culture.
 
Hospital pants or scrubs will be allowed.
 
 
Synchronous
 
Learning
 
In December of 2020, teachers were provided with a variety of new
 
electronic equipment including:  updated laptops, docking stations,
 
microphone/speaker, tablets, and large monitors.  They had the
 
opportunity to attend several professional development classes as well as
 
receive  individual assistance to appropriately set up their classrooms
 
using the new equipment. They practiced with each other and with their
 
classes to prepare for a hard start date of 1/29/21.
 
 
 
 Each grade level created a live streaming schedule for their students,
 
except for the fully remote classes, for which nothing will change.  The
 
expectation is that the cohorts will essentially collapse, and students will
 
reunite into single homerooms.  Although students will still attend in
 
person either Tuesday and Thursday or Wednesday and Friday, their at
 
home days will be spent following the daily schedule of their in person
 
peers, receiving the same live instruction and assignments.
 
 
 
 There will no longer be back up videos or twice created lessons.  Students
 
in person and at home will hear and see the same lessons, participate
 
together in the same discussions, “gather” in small groups via the Google
