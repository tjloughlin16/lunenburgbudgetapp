Facilities Department Report
Mass Save Project - Phase 1
Lunenburg Schools has entered Phase 1 of a Mass Save project, in partnership with Unitil and Green Communities.
This initiative focuses on the control systems at LMHS to ensure that the RTUs and thermostats operate efficiently
together for maximum energy performance. The program may cover up to 100% of the project cost, including
repairs or replacements for faulty sensors, actuators, thermostats, and related components - which is our goal.
Generator Relocation Project
Facilities is obtaining pricing for the removal and installation of the generator located at 1025 Mass Ave (Passios
School). This project was originally included in the FY27 Capital Projects with plans for a brand-new generator.
However, after securing permission from the town to repurpose the existing unit, the generator will be relocated to
the Primary School. We expect to have updated pricing and projected cost savings available in the next few weeks.
Solar Array Repairs
Solect Solar Company continues its work on the LMHS solar array repairs. One array section is now back online,
with additional sections to follow. The Schools are coordinating with the Town Electrical Inspector to oversee and
approve each section before reactivation. We are maintaining high safety and performance standards and will only
accept work that meets those expectations to ensure the safety of students and staff.
Energy Efficiency and CBS Studies
Lunenburg Schools are collaborating with Unitil, National Grid, and Green Communities on Comprehensive
Building System (CBS) studies across all school buildings. These studies aim to identify areas for improving energy
efficiency and reducing annual operating costs. The process is in its early stages, and additional data and findings
will be presented as they become available.
