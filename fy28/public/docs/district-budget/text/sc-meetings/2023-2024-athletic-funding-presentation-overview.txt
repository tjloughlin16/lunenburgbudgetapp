===PAGE 15===
Facility Rentals
Facility rental fees are charged to users of our facilities (fields, gym, auditorium, etc) 
Fees paid by facility users (including Bengals, LYSA, Youth Basketball, etc) go into the 
Facility Revolving Account.
The fees are not used to fund the School Athletic Program.
These fees cover the maintenance of the facility with the added wear and tear from 
additional usage by non-school programs.
