Our Legal Mandate
LUNENBURG PUBLIC SCHOOLS  •  SCHOOL COMMITTEE 02
T he School Committee is the district’s governing and policy-making body. Massachusetts law 
establishes three core responsibilities.
01 Superintendent
Select, evaluate, and when 
necessary terminate the 
Superintendent.
02 Budget
Review and approve the district 
budget; oversee its alignment 
with priorities.
03 Goals & policy
Establish educational goals and 
district policies consistent with 
state law.
Statutory foundation: Education Reform Act of 1993; M.G.L. c. 71, §37.

Pillar 2 | Budget & Financial Oversight
LUNENBURG PUBLIC SCHOOLS  •  SCHOOL COMMITTEE 04
Review and approve
Consider the proposed budget, conduct required public budget process, and vote on district budget 
recommendations.
Advocate for the appropriation
Explain priorities and funding needs to municipal officials and Town Meeting.
Monitor fiscal performance
Receive regular financial reports, examine significant variances, and oversee fiscal controls and legally assigned warrant 
responsibilities.
Respect management authority
T he Superintendent administers expenditures and budget implementation within appropriations, law, contracts, and 
Committee policy.
Committee: sets and oversees the financial direction   |   Administration: manages execution

Collective B argaining & Legal Duties
LUNE NB UR G PUB LIC S CHOOLS   •  S CHOOL COMMIT TE E 06
Collective bargaining
The Committee is the public employer for school labor 
relations and may negotiate through designated 
representatives.
Open Meeting Law
Committee deliberations occur in properly noticed 
meetings. E xecutive session is limited to lawful 
purposes.
Conflict of interest
Members must comply with M.G.L. c. 268A, including 
required disclosures and ethics training.
Public accountability
Members must follow public records, confidentiality, and 
other applicable governance requirements.

A C T I N G  A S  O N E  G O V E R N I N G  B O D Y
How an Individual Member Operates
LUNENBURG PUBLIC SCHOOLS  •  SCHOOL COMMITTEE 08
Authority rests with the Committee
An individual member cannot direct district operations or act on behalf of the Committee without authorization.
Use appropriate channels
Refer individual complaints and operational matters to the Superintendent and established district processes.
Deliberate openly and respectfully
Come prepared, ask questions, consider evidence, and honor lawful decisions of the Committee.
Represent every student
Listen to the community while keeping the needs of the entire district at the center.
