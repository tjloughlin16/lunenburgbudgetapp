Parties agree that the Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE) advisory on
 
reopening released on June 25, 2020 requires Districts to develop three (3) plans to educate students,
 
including a plan for fully remote learning.  To that end, the District and the Union jointly agree to this
 
Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) for 
​
hybrid learning
​
.
 
 
1.
Unless explicitly outlined below, all provisions of the Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA)
 
shall remain in full force and effect.
 
 
2.
The District and the Union shall decide no later than August 10, 2020, which of the three (3)
 
models (in person, online, hybrid) it will adopt for the beginning of the 2020-2021 school year.
 
 
3.
The District and the Union will discuss all changes from one model to another, informed by
 
changing public health data or based upon the needs of the district and/or Union.  While
 
consideration shall be given to allow for educator preparation in changing from one model to
 
another, this shall not preclude the district from needing to immediately enter full remote
 
learning from a full in-person or hybrid model.
 
4.
Instructional Schedule:
 
 
●
Students in the Primary, Elementary and Middle schools shall be divided into two groups
 
of roughly equivalent numbers.  (Cohort A and Cohort B)
 
 
●
Students in the High School will follow their schedule of classes with students being split
 
into two groups of roughly equivalent numbers. (Cohort A and Cohort B)
 
●
Primary:  Classroom teachers shall be assigned to a classroom with a cohort of students
 
to allow for six (6) feet of social distancing.  Special area teachers will hold specials in
 
classrooms if possible.
 
●
Elementary:  Classroom teachers shall be assigned to a classroom or team of students
 
who, if needed, shall travel between classrooms.  The cohort of students in each room
 
will allow for six (6) feet of social distancing.
 
●
Middle School:  Classroom teachers shall be assigned to a team of students who shall
 
travel between classrooms.  The cohort of students in each room will allow for six (6)
 
feet of social distancing.
 
●
High School:  Classroom teachers shall be assigned classes with cohorts of students who
 
shall travel between classrooms to allow for six (6) feet of social distancing.
 
●
Special Education Services:  Special education services may be in or out of the
 
classrooms.  All students will be spaced six (6) feet from other students or staff
 
 
===PAGE 2===
whenever possible.  Students requiring closer contact must wear a mask, protective
 
shield and/or appropriate PPE whenever possible.
 
●
Each student cohort will physically attend school on alternating days (Tuesday/Thursday
 
or Wednesday/Friday).  On their remote learning days, students will engage in classes
 
virtually as they would in a physical classroom via Google Meets during a predetermined
 
and scheduled time.  These sessions will be mandatory for students.  All sessions will be
 
connected to the delivery of the scope and sequence of the curriculum.
 
 
●
Reassessment and/or creation of new building-based protocols for attendance, work
 
completion/quality, and remote responsibilities of our students gathering input from all
 
building educators and through discussion with Building Leadership Teams, Dept. Heads
 
and LEA Building Representatives.  Building-based protocols in building plans will be
 
reviewed by the district and the LEA by January 19th.
 
 
●
Mondays will be remote for all students.  Students will engage in both synchronous
 
lessons via Google Meets as well as asynchronous learning to complete posted
 
assignments or complete introductory lessons for the week.
 
●
Monday schedules for teachers will include approximately three (3) hours devoted to
 
teacher preparation and planning both individually and in small groups. Initial flexibility
 
will be needed to meet and maintain compliance with special services including testing
 
and meetings.  Educators will be working remotely on Mondays.
 
●
Attendance will be taken daily for both in-person and remote cohorts in accordance
 
with DESE guidance.
 
 
●
Educators will be expected to respond to parents/guardians within two (2) school days.
 
Questions and concerns from students should be attended to no later than the next
 
school day. Educators who are out of the classroom for sick/personal leave, will be
 
encouraged to post an away message on their email.  More specifics will be provided in
 
the building based plans.
 
 
●
Educators are expected to follow their building protocols for supporting students they
 
have concerns about.  These concerns may include attendance, work completion,
 
social-emotional/mental health, academic etc.
 
 
●
Until the live-streaming model is fully implemented in each school, teachers will
 
live-stream any students out of school for an extended period of time, including but not
 
limited to a COVID quarantine situation.  After teachers are notified that they will need
 
to live-stream for students on an extended absence from school, they will have 48 hours
 
to begin streaming.
 
 
●
Nursing staff will make the determination based on medical documentation or public
 
health quarantine requirements which hybrid students are eligible for a period of time
 
learning fully remotely via livestream.
 
●
In order to meet the needs of students on Individualized Education Plans (IEPs), some
 
students may physically attend school and receive in person instruction in accordance
 
with their IEP’s everyday that students are in the building for a hybrid model. If the
 
Governor declares schools shut, or if the community is advised to close by the local
 
Board of Health these services
​
 
​
would move to remote. High needs students and
 
 
===PAGE 3===
sub-groups (as outlined by DESE) will still be afforded in-person learning four (4) days a
 
week.
 
 
●
At this time, DESE has stated that we are only obligated to consider home based services
 
if no students can come into the school building.
 
 
●
If changes to a delivery model are needed with a transition to livestreaming, Special
 
educators can utilize the added preparation and planning time to make any necessary
 
changes to Special Education Service Learning Plans.  Additional preparation and
 
planning time will be provided if deemed necessary.
 
●
Educators who have both hybrid and fully remote students will adhere to the language
 
in the hybrid model.
 
5.
On or before August 31, the District shall begin the process to assign teachers to anticipated
 
remote teaching assignments for School Year 2020/2021, teachers for consideration will be:
 
●
Staff members who have certified to the Human Resource Director that they are at
 
increased risk of severe illness from COVID-19 or another illness that prevents them
 
from physically reporting to work,
 
●
Staff members who have documented to the Human Resource Director that their school
 
or place of care is closed (or child care provider is unavailable) for reasons related to
 
COVID-19,
 
●
Staff members who have documented to the Human Resource Director that their child’s
 
school instruction model varies from Lunenburg’s for reasons related to COVID-19 and
 
there are no daycare providers.
 
●
Appointments will be made on 1.) required licensure 2.) educators who qualify for a
 
remote position due to a documented health concern by a medical professional that
 
cannot be supported by a district accommodation 3.) seniority.  Special consideration
 
will be given to keep educators at their grade level and/or in their building if possible.
 
 
●
The District will provide a list of teachers and their specific remote assignments to the
 
Union as well as other impacted teachers.
 
 
●
 
​
If there are not enough teachers to fill the needed remote positions, the District shall
 
recruit volunteers from within the school location of the opening before making
 
involuntary transfers.  If there is more than one volunteer for a position, assuming that
 
licensure is sufficient for that position, first priority will be to award that position to a
 
member with the same teaching assignment, within that group, seniority will be the
 
determining factor.
 
●
If there are no volunteers to fill an available remote position, the member with the least
 
seniority in that teaching assignment would be chosen.
 
 
●
Teachers who have been appointed to a remote teaching position shall not be removed
 
from their remote assignment during school year 2020/2021.
 
●
On a case by case basis, reassignment of teaching positions could occur.
 
●
Refusal to apply for online teaching shall not waive an individual’s rights under any
 
Federal law, State law, or the terms of the collective bargaining agreement.
 
 
6.
In Person Safety Guidelines
 
 
===PAGE 4===
●
The maximum occupancy for a shared work area in a building will be determined by the
 
six (6) feet of distance guidelines.  The maximum occupancy will be posted outside each
 
shared space.
 
 
●
A minimum of six (6) feet distance shall be maintained at all times between individuals.
 
 
●
All staff will wear masks at all times while in the building, except for when they are
 
alone inside their own work space with the door closed.
 
 
●
Staff with a designated work space, not shared with other staff, should plan on using
 
this work space for their lunch period. For staff without a designated work space, their
 
building principal will provide an alternative space.
 
●
Educators who share a work or lunch space will follow all necessary safety guidelines.
 
●
Whenever possible, meetings will be held via Zoom or Google Meets.
 
●
Whenever possible, windows and classroom doors should be kept open to allow for
 
increased ventilation.
 
●
The District shall provide the following personal protection equipment (PPEs), safety
 
measures and training:
 
●
Disposable masks for students if needed.
 
●
Disposable gloves if needed.
 
●
Desktop/mobile shields or three side enclosed cubicles for work that require
 
closer contact than six feet or during provided services that warrant this type of
 
PPE.
 
●
Face shields, masks, and gowns/clothing coverings will be provided as needed
 
for required staff.
 
 
●
Classroom spray disinfectant and microfiber cloths to be cleaned by custodial
 
staff.
 
●
Hand sanitizer located in each classroom and hand sanitizing stations
 
throughout the building.
 
●
Training for staff and students on moving throughout the building and following
 
all safety guidelines.
 
●
Bathrooms will be checked regularly for adequate supplies.
 
●
All other high-touch surfaces such as door knobs, railings etc. will be wiped
 
down frequently.
 
●
Staff and students consequences, including students being removed from the
 
hybrid model, will be enforced for persons who willfully do not follow the safety
 
protocols.
 
●
Cleaning protocols will be available to all members of the building staff.
 
●
By the return of students, any required HVAC repairs will be completed, or timeline will
 
be provided.
 
 
●
Classrooms will be deep cleaned over the weekend and/or Monday remote day.
 
 
●
The district will provide HEPA filters for each classroom at Turkey Hill Elementary
 
School.
 
●
Indoor air quality will be monitored and an inspection schedule will be provided to LEA.
 
If the LEA identifies an air quality issue, they will report it; it will be investigated and
 
addressed appropriately in a timely manner.
 
 
===PAGE 5===
●
The Superintendent will cancel school or institute a remote school day in the case of
 
excessive heat and/or humidity using the heat index, or in the case of inclement
 
weather.
 
 
7.
Illness:
 
●
Aside from outlined here, all rules regarding leaves in the CBA shall apply.
 
 
●
The District will follow the Commissioner’s protocol for responding to COVID-19
 
scenarios in schools dated July 17, 2020 and any amendments thereafter.
 
●
 
​
This protocol will be used when any staff person who medically contracts COVID-19 or
 
has been in close contact of a COVID-19 case.
 
●
Any staff person who contracts COVID-19 that is traced to the school setting, as
 
identified by the Board of Health, shall use the “10 COVID sick days”provided by FFCRA
 
which expires on December 31, 2020 or if extended by Federal or State law.  If medical
 
documentation of an extended illness is provided for the employee to the Human
 
Resource Office, up to ten (10) additional days will be provided from the sick bank
 
before using any of their own accrued sick time.
 
●
Whenever possible, if an educator is required to quarantine or care for someone who is
 
quarantined, but is not symptomatic, that educator may, after consultation with his/her
 
administrator, be required to work remotely.
 
●
The decision to use or not use leave shall not waive an individual’s rights under any
 
Federal law, State law, or the terms of the collective bargaining agreement.
 
 
●
Aside from outlined here, all rules regarding leaves in the CBA shall apply.
 
 
8.
Calendar:
 
 
●
The District’s previously agreed upon calendar shall remain in effect except that the 10
 
days provided by the Commissioner of Education and DESE will take place up front
 
consecutively beginning on August 31st and ending on September 15th.  The first day for
 
students will be September 16th.  The first day for in-person cohorts will be September
 
17th and 18th.  PreK/Kindergarten screening for PreK/Kindergarten students can begin
 
on September 16th.  The first day of PreK/Kindergarten in-person cohorts will begin on
 
September 29th.
 
 
●
The 10 days provided by the Commissioner of Education and DESE shall consist of at
 
least 50% group and individual planning time with PD focused on technology
 
training/support, safety protocols, hybrid and remote teaching strategies,
 
social-emotional best practices, and standard school year trainings.
 
 
●
Early arrivals and late departures times for staff will be established by each building
 
principal after consultation with the Senior Custodian and the Director of Facilities and
 
Grounds.
 
●
All other professional development days shall be placed strategically throughout the
 
year to provide staff with professional development and planning.  The date of those
 
days are TBD and will be discussed with the union prior to placement.
 
9.
 Fully remote teaching assignment requirements:
 
 
●
The remote teaching day will follow a building-based school schedule for both teachers
 
and students.
 
 
===PAGE 6===
●
Student attendance will be taken daily or per class period consistent with the building
 
practice.
 
●
Office hours/extra help will be offered as needed per current contract language in the
 
CBA.
 
 
●
Educators will be expected to respond to parents by the next school day.  Questions and
 
concerns from students should be attended to as soon as possible.
 
 
●
Educator attendance will be required at all building based virtual meetings if necessary
 
as well as Monday planning sessions.
 
10.
Inclement Day:  If the Superintendent of Schools cancels a school day due to inclement weather,
 
then there will be no work and the day will be made at the end of the school year.  At the
 
discretion of the Superintendent, the day could be designated as a “remote learning day”
 
requiring the equivalent of three (3) hours of instruction per remote day.  Any work to be
 
completed as part of this day, students will be allowed two school days to submit the required
 
work; extension allowed by the classroom teacher.
 
 
 
11.
Evaluation:  Because of the unique nature of online learning, all evaluation and evaluation cycles
 
shall be supported through the offering of a common goal for the 2020/2021 school year.
 
 
●
An overarching goal will be created by the evaluation committee which consists of
 
district and union representation.  Each building may have a variation of the overarching
 
goal to best serve the needs of educators and students.
 
i.
This goal will cover both the student learning goal as well as the professional
 
practice goal.
 
ii.
Educators will be encouraged to work in groups to help support completion of
 
the goal and artifact collection/explanation.
 
 
iii.
Throughout the school year, support will be provided to attain this common
 
goal in individual buildings as well on the district level.
 
 
●
Educators will be able choose their own goal(s) if they prefer.
 
 
●
Educators in their first 3 years in the district will meet with their evaluator and if
 
desired, their mentor, at the mid-way point of the school year.  A targeted
 
conversation/sharing of work around power standards will take place at this meeting.
 
This meeting, its notes and observations will serve as the basis for the formative
 
assessment.  This will be a pilot program for the 2020/2021 school year.
 
 
12.
Technology:  All staff shall be provided by the District, at no cost to the employee, a laptop
 
containing sufficient hardware and software to conduct all requirements.  Staff who do not have
 
internet access in their home, or who have insufficient internet access, shall be provided with a
 
mobile hotspot.  The District shall determine the appropriate platforms for on-line instruction
 
with Google Classroom being the main platform.  All other supplemental platforms must be
 
approved and linked through Google Classroom.  A list of approved platforms will be made
 
available during the first 10 days of the school year and teachers shall be provided training or
 
training resources on these platforms.  Additional training will be offered during the school year.
 
Parent Engagement:
 
●
Open house/Back to School Nights:  Shall be virtual/videotaped for the 2020-2021
 
school year.
 
 
 
===PAGE 7===
●
Parent Teacher conferences shall be conducted remotely using Zoom.  Organizing a
 
schedule for PT Conferences will not be the responsibility of the educator.  Pick-a-Time
 
will be utilized for scheduling conferences.  The “waiting room” will be used with the
 
educators Zoom link.
 
 
●
Grading:  Each educator shall follow the traditional grading system used in their school
 
building.
 
 
 
13.
Unless modified as above, all the terms of the Collective Bargaining Agreement (CBA) between
 
the parties shall remain in full force and effect.  Any agreements/changes shall not be
 
considered past practice.
 
 
14.
The parties recognize that not all issues can be covered in this MOA, so the parties commit to
 
meeting on an as needed basis with a regular standing meeting scheduled every two weeks.
