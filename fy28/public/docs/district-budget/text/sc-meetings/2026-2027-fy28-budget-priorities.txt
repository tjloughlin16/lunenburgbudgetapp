Why begin with priorities?
Tonight is about direction—not line items, reductions, or a proposed spending plan.
Start with students
Name the experiences, supports, and 
outcomes we believe matter most.
Build shared direction
Use the Superintendent’s 
recommendations as a starting point for 
the Committee’s thinking.
Guide later decisions
Establish a lens for evaluating needs, 
trade offs, and proposals as the budget is 
developed.
We do not need to solve the budget tonight.
