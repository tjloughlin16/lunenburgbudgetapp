2022-2023 School Committee Goals 
 
1.  Equity  
 
It is the goal of the Lunenburg School Committee that all 
students, staff and families are welcomed and feel affirmed in 
out inclusive school environment. 
 
Action Steps: 
 
The School Committee will: 
    -  Review the outcome of the current Equity audit. 
    -  Collaborate with the Superintendent to incorporate any 
audit recommendations in resource allocation for future 
budget cycles. 
 
Benchmarks: 
 
-  Audit report 
-  Funding of any recommendations 
 
 
 
2.  Increase Communication with the Community 
 
It is the goal of the School Committee to establish a fluid, 
consistent communication system with the public.  The topics 
of our meetings should be readily available to all interested 
parties in the community.  We need to hear the opinions of the 
public regarding School Committee matters as well as 
communicate our actions in a timely manner.  
 
Action Steps: 
===PAGE 2===
 
The School Committee will: 
    - Use those modes of communication deemed most reliable 
by the survey results received in the Spring 0f 2022 (email, 
FaceBook, the School Department website, Town Website). 
    -Video presentations provided to the School Committee by 
the school staff will be shared on the website and emailed to 
parents. 
    - School Committee meetings will have a hybrid or Zoom 
format to facilitate community participation. 
     -Be conscious and careful that discussions in meetings using 
technical educational language and acronyms are defined for 
listeners. 
 
Benchmarks: 
 
-Survey parents in the Spring of 2023 regarding the 
communication of School Committee activity. 
- Gather a list of definitions to be posted on the School 
Committee website. 
-  Monitor the number of views of video presentations.
