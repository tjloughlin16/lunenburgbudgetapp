 SECTION  I  -  OVERVIEW   District  Profile  The  Wachusett  Regional  School  District  was  originally  formed  as  a  grade  9-12  High  School  district  in  
1950

1994.
 
The
 
member

Holden,
 
Paxton,
 
Princeton,
 
Rutland

Sterling.
 
 The  District  operates  thirteen  schools  in  pre-kindergarten  through  grade  twelve.  Paxton  and  Princeton  
have
 
one
 
K-8
 
elementary
 
school
 
in
 
each
 
town,
 
Holden
 
has

elementary
 
schools
 
and
 
one
 
middle
 
school,
 
Rutland
 
has
 
two
 
elementary
 
schools
 
and
 
one
 
middle
 
school,
 
and
 
Sterling
 
has
 
one
 
elementary
 
school
 
and
 
one
 
middle
 
school.
 
The

high
 
school
 
and

Early
 
Childhood

are

in
 
Holden.
 
The
 
Wachusett
 
Regional
 
School
 
District
 
is
 
the

regional
 
school
 
district
 
in
 
the
 
Commonwealth
 
of
 
Massachusetts.
  
Regional
 
School

 The  Wachusett  Regional  School  District  Committee  has  sixteen  elected  members.  Per  the  Regional  
Agreement
 
the

The

is
 
to

the
 
Superintendent,
 
establish

an
 
education

Committee

 The  annual  operating  budget  authorizes  the  District  to  hire  employees,  incur  obligations,  and  pay

  Policy  4100  Budget   This  policy  states  the  budget  is  built  following  M.G.L.  and  DESE  requirements.  The  District  will  involve

  Policy  4110  Budget  Planning  &  Adoption   This  policy  outlines  that  the  budget  reflects  the  educational  goals  of  the  District.  Presentation  of  the

  September  –  DESE  (Department  of  Elementary  and  Secondary  Education)  End  of  Year  Report

Committee.
  
Excess
 
&

(Department
 
of
 
Revenue
 
Division
 
of
 
Local
 
Services).
 
November  –  October  1st  enrollment  numbers  will  be  completed  which  will  have  an  impact  on  class

state

and
 
town
 
assessments.
 
District
 
and

will

staffing
 
and

November/December  –  Town  Manager  meetings  with  Superintendent  and  Director  of  Business  and

March/April  –  Superintendent  and  Director  of  Business  and  Finance  will  attend  joint  Finance
