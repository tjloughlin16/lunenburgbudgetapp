2. DESCRIPTION AND JUSTIFICATION
Description and scope: THES Roof Replacement   Age and deterioration: The existing roof has reached or is approaching the end of its expected service life, increasing the likelihood of failures and leaks. 
  Prevent water damage: An aging roof can allow water to penetrate the building envelope, potentially damaging ceilings, insulation, walls, electrical systems, and interior finishes. 
  Reduce ongoing maintenance costs: Replacing the roof can reduce the need for repeated patching, repairs, and emergency leak response. 
  Improve building reliability: A new roof provides a more dependable weather barrier and helps protect the school from rain, snow, ice, and freeze-thaw conditions. 
  Protect school operations: Roof leaks can disrupt classrooms, offices, and other occupied areas, potentially requiring temporary relocation of students or staff. 
  Protect school assets: Preventing moisture intrusion helps protect furniture, technology, instructional materials, and other equipment from water damage. 
  Improve energy efficiency: A new roofing system can improve insulation and reduce heat loss or heat gain, potentially lowering heating and cooling costs. 
  Address existing deficiencies: Replacement provides an opportunity to correct deteriorated flashing, seams, drainage issues, insulation deficiencies, and other components that may be contributing to roof problems. 
  Improve drainage: A new system can incorporate improvements to roof drainage and reduce standing water, which can contribute to premature deterioration. 
  Avoid escalating repair costs: As the roof continues to age, repairs can become more frequent and expensive, while underlying damage may become more extensive. 
  Improve safety: Preventing leaks helps reduce the risk of wet floors, falling ceiling materials, mold growth, and other building-related hazards. 
  Long-term asset protection: A new roof protects the school's underlying structure and represents a proactive investment in maintaining the facility.
