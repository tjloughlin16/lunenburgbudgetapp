II. Proposed Solution 
Lunenburg Public Schools has legacy switches in each of their schools that are up for a refresh. This 
budgetary proposal includes Cisco replacement switches for these end-of-life switches. 
III. Scope of Services – Network Infrastructure Upgrade
As part of this engagement, CCS will deliver a structured and comprehensive Scope of Work (detailed 
below) to ensure a smooth transition and minimize business disruption. Our approach includes careful 
planning, documentation, and execution across all key components of the Network Upgrade.
Scope of Work:
• Un-box and mount switches in its cabinet or rack
• Cross-connect switches
• Cross-connect the active devices terminating in the closet into available ports on the switches using 
the provided Category 6 copper UTP patch cables
• Cross-connect and activate all backbones to other closets
• Document the cross-connections
• Configure IP and name each switch with the appropriate IP address schema and name as provided 
by the school
• Configure the VLANs, Management and associated layer-3 routing on the core switches 
• Test connectivity
• Document
