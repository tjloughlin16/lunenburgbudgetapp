To: Lunenburg School CommitteeFrom: Dr. Jodi Fortuna, Superintendent of SchoolsRe: Homecoming Participation
I recommend that the School Committee limit participation in Homecoming float building and float meetings to students enrolled at Lunenburg High School. Students attending Monty Tech or other schools would continue to be welcome to watch the parade and attend the football game with the broader community.
I understand why students who grew up in Lunenburg want to share these traditions with their friends. Those relationships matter, and choosing another school does not diminish a student’s connection to our town.
At the same time, float building is a school activity that requires LHS class advisors and parent volunteers to supervise students throughout the process. Our staff are responsible for setting expectations, communicating with families, and responding when concerns arise. Students attending other schools are outside our regular school disciplinary process, which limits our ability to apply the same accountability to everyone participating.
Homecoming brings our community together, but it remains a Lunenburg Public Schools event. Its schedule, staffing, and activities are organized around our students and school responsibilities. That distinction also guided our response when the town’s 300th Anniversary Committee asked us to consider an earlier Homecoming date to incorporate the parade into the townwide celebration, a request we were unable to accommodate.
My recommendation is intended to establish a clear and consistent expectation that our staff and volunteers can support. It preserves opportunities for the community to enjoy Homecoming while keeping participation in float building with the LHS students for whom the activity is organized.
