2. DESCRIPTION AND JUSTIFICATION
Description and scope: Construct a new all-weather track and synthetic turf athletic field at the high school to provide a safe, durable, and multi-purpose facility for student athletics, physical education, and school/community activities. The project would include removal and replacement of the existing athletic surface, installation of a regulation track and synthetic turf field, drainage improvements, site preparation, appropriate field markings, and necessary related site improvements.The new facility will provide a reliable, year-round athletic venue that can accommodate multiple sports and activities while improving safety, accessibility, and overall usability of the high school athletic complex.

Consequences if not funded: Without funding for the new track and turf field, the existing athletic facilities will continue to experience wear, deterioration, and potential safety concerns. Limited or unreliable field conditions could result in canceled, relocated, or shortened athletic practices and competitions, reducing opportunities for students to participate in school sports and activities.The district may also face increasing maintenance and repair costs, as well as the potential need to rent or use off-site facilities. Delaying the project could allow existing infrastructure to deteriorate further, potentially increasing the eventual cost of replacement and limiting the school’s ability to provide a safe, accessible, and dependable athletic facility for students and the community.
