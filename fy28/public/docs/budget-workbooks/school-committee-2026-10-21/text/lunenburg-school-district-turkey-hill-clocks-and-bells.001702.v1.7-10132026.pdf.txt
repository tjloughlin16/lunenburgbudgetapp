Proposed Solution
Lunenburg Public Schools is modernizing life-safety communications at Turkey Hill School. CCS proposes a fully
IP-based Axis paging and mass-notification system that replaces reliance on legacy analog paging with 38 network-
addressable audio endpoints, centrally managed from a single Axis Audio Manager Pro appliance and operated
day-to-day from a dedicated desktop paging console in the main office.
Because every endpoint is a network device rather than a speaker on a 70-volt line, the district gains capabilities an
analog system cannot deliver: per-room zoning, synchronized clocks and on-screen text at the classroom level,
scheduled bells, pre-recorded and live emergency announcements, strobe alerting, and remote health monitoring of
every speaker from a single pane of glass.
The design basis for this proposal is the district-issued floor plan "Turkey Hill — Speakers," Rev. 1.0, which maps
every proposed speaker location and its modeled coverage footprint onto the building plan.

Scope of Services
As part of this engagement, CCS will deliver a structured and comprehensive Scope of Work (detailed below) to ensure 
a smooth transition and minimize business disruption. Our approach includes careful planning, documentation, and 
execution across all key components of the Lunenburg School District Turkey Hill Clocks and Bells
Scope of Work:
CCS’s Scope of Work will include the following: 
Discovery & Planning
 Project kickoff call with client stakeholders
 Site walkthrough to confirm mounting locations, cable paths, and rack space
 Confirm camera fields of view and coverage at each location
 Confirm door controller mounting, reader placement, and power locations
 Confirm IP address schema and naming conventions with client IT
 Review floorplans and finalize device placement map
 Confirm firmware and licensing requirements
 Audio Manager Pro Server
 Unbox and assemble recording server; update firmware
 Rack or place server in designated location; connect to network
 Assign static IP address per IP schema; apply base configuration
 Apply all VMS licenses and finalize camera associations
 Validate storage capacity, RAID configuration, and retention policy
 Confirm remote access and verify all cameras recording correctly
 IP Speaker Installation & Configuration
 Unbox and inventory all IP speakers; verify firmware and update as needed
 Mount and install speakers at designated locations
 Connect to PoE network and verify power delivery at each location
 Discover and import all speakers into audio management platform
 Assign IP addresses and device names per client naming convention
 Configure audio zones, page groups, and emergency broadcast settings
 Set audio levels and test all speakers for proper function at each location
 Validate scheduled announcements and emergency broadcast functionality
Cabling 
 Install and furnish Cat6/Cat6A cable runs to all speaker device locations per scope
 Terminate cables at patch panel(s) located in either MDF/IDF Location
 Leave 10 ft service loop at all new runs
 Label cables at endpoints and panel per client labeling scheme
 Test cables and provide results to IT Contact
Closeout Meeting & Project Documentation Review
 A close-out meeting to review pertinent project documentation including but not limited to:
o Overview of project deliverables
o Address any questions, or concerns
