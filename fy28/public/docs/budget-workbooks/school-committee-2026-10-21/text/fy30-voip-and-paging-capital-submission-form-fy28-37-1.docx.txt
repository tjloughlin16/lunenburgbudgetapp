2. DESCRIPTION AND JUSTIFICATION
Description and scope: This project aims to upgrade the voice and paging systems to a supported unified communications model for the entire district. This includes integrating the paging system at each school into the district voice system. Long-term, this system will also eventually be able to integrate with the school bells and clocks. Call processing and paging servers will be centralized at LMHS. The district’s current phone system will be end-of-support by the summer of 2029, making management and ability to source replacement parts increasingly challenging. The current phone system is also limited in its ability to integrate with ancillary systems such as paging, bells, and clocks. A centralized server that can integrate with various ancillary systems will improve ease of management and lessen the number of physical servers to maintain in district. Fewer servers will lessen maintenance costs and labor over time.
