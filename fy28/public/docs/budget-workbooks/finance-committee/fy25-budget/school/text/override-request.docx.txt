
March 11. 2024

Dear Selectboard, Finance Committee and Town Manager,

The intent of the School Committee is to continue funding the Lunenburg Public Schools at the current level of service minus the sunsetting federal ESSER grant funds at a cost of $24,850,044. With the Town Manager’s current 3.94% budget an override of $1,070,000 would be necessary. That is our request.

Lunenburg School Committee
Carol Archambault, Chair
