Preamble - Financial
The PAC revenue from Comcast is reserved for PAC Capital Improvements and Operations and does not affect Town budgets
The Enterprise Fund is reserved for fitting out and equipping our new studio
The franchise fee from Comcast is the principal source of PAC income 
In addition, PAC receives interest monthly
Some of the Enterprise Fund (under advisement from the Town Treasurer) has been invested in short term bonds, presently yielding about $30,000 annually
