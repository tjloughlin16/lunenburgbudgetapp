Why the increase in hours?• It is not possible for the Meal Site Manager to run an efficient meal site, Monday through Thursday, at 19.5 hours per week.• Not only do we deliver meals on wheels 4 days per week, we also have an active nutrition program 3 days per week that requires menu planning, shopping, cooking, and supervising 3 employees and 2 volunteers. • 4.5 hours would be added to Fridays in order to shop for following weeks menu items, inventory control and  assisting with monthly Veterans breakfast and Memory Café breakfast.
