FULLY UPDATED — STEP‑BY‑STEP TRUST FUND APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS 
 
PHASE 1 — PREPARATION BEFORE FILLING OUT THE APPLICATION 
1. Identify Which Trust Fund(s) You Are Applying For 
Different Lunenburg trust funds have donor‑specific eligibility requirements. 
 
2. Gather All Required Documentation (ALL APPLICANTS) 
Proof of U.S. Citizenship and Residency: 
- U.S. Birth Certificate with State‑issued ID 
- U.S. Passport 
Secondary documents accepted upon Board request. 
Persons who are legally qualified United States Citizens but are restricted from receiving 
government benefits (e.g., ineligible for SNAP, TANF, Medicaid, Medicare due to federal, 
state, or local rules) may still apply, even if they are restricted from these government 
programs because of misuse, suspension, or disqualification for cause. 
 
3. Additional Requirements — Veteran Applicants Only 
- DD‑214 (long form) 
- Additional service documentation if requested upon Board consultation with VSO 
Mandatory VSO verification required before hearing. 
 
4. Economic‑Impact Documentation 
Required only for funds where donor intent specifies financial hardship. 
 
PHASE 2 — COMPLETING THE APPLICATION 
Follow sections A–E of the 2026 Trust Fund Application.

PHASE 3 — ADDITIONAL DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS 
Low‑income funds require hardship materials; military funds require DD‑214 
documentation. 
 
PHASE 4 — SUBMISSION AND BOARD PROCESS 
Submit full packet → intake review → VSO verification (if veteran) → economic review (if 
donor‑required) → Board hearing. 
 
PHASE 5 — POST‑DECISION 
You will receive written notification with conditions and appeal rights.
