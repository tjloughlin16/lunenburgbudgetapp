Trust Fund Disbursement Eligibility Instruction Manual 
For the Trustees of the Lunenburg, Massachusetts Board of Trust Fund Commissioners 
Table of Contents 
 
Purpose & Scope 
This manual establishes uniform criteria and procedures for Trust Fund eligibility determinations including income, residency, FPL application, documentation, 
and appeals. 
Definitions 
%FPL: income divided by 100% FPL monthly 
Federal Poverty Level: HHS guideline 
Gross Monthly Income: income before taxes 
Household Size: all persons sharing income

Documentation & Recordkeeping 
Retain application, proofs, income docs, FPL worksheet, decision, notes for 6 years per MA law. 
Appeals & Exceptions 
Applicant, may appeal in 30 days; board reviews evidence and issues decision; exceptions for hardship. 
1. Commissioners’ Instructional Process — Determining Eligibility Across All Criteria 
1. Residency & Identity: Verify Massachusetts and Lunenburg and United States residency and identity for all adult household members. 
2. Household Size: Confirm all persons sharing income/expenses; document dependents. 
3. Income: Capture **gross monthly income** from all sources (wages, unemployment, Social Security, child support). 
4. Federal Poverty Level: Use **2026 HHS FPG** for the 48 states to compute **%FPL**; monthly = annual ÷ 12. (See Appendices B/C.) 
5. Assets/Resources (if program requires): Record liquid assets if relevant to a specific fund rule. 
6. Urgency/Use of Funds: Assess purpose (e.g., housing, utilities, medical, transportation) and whether other benefits are available. 
7. Duplication of Benefits: Ensure Trust funds do not duplicate aid already provided for the same need. 
8. Conflict Check: Commissioners with conflicts must recuse; note in minutes. 
9. Decision & Documentation: Apply the Sliding‑Scale schedule; set loan terms if applicable; record determination, amount, %FPL, and rationale.
