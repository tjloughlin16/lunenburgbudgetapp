Nathaniel Day - Donor Intent and Trust Summary
DONOR INTENT (Extracted):
"To each of the towns of Lunenburg and Shirley in the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, one
thousand dollars to be invested by the Selectmen or Overseers of the Poor of each of said
towns, the income derived therefrom to be used by them in improving the condition of the
worthy poor people living in and of the poor-houses in each of the said towns?
GOVERNANCE STATEMENT:
Nathaniel Day intended that the principal sum of $1,000, given to the Town of Lunenburg,
be permanently invested by the Selectmen or Overseers of the Poor. Only the income
generated from that investment is to be used, and solely to improve the condition and well-
being of the 'worthy poor, including those in the town's poorhouse.
BRIEF DESCRIPTION OF THE DOCUMENT:
This document is a historical record from approximately 1901 outlining a charitable trust
established through the will of Nathaniel Day. It directs that the towns of Lunenburg and
Shirley each receive $1,000, to be invested by local officials responsible for the care of the
poor. The income is to be used exclusively to improve conditions for the 'worthy poor;'
emphasizing donor intent and long-term stewardship.
