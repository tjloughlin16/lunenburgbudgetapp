Attorney General Administrative Pathway
Most funds under $75k and older than 20 years → eligible for AG review
Administrative equitable deviation or cy pres available
Exception: Post 283 Fund cannot use AG pathway (est. 2013)

Fiduciary &amp; Legal Risks
Town liable if distributing funds without verified donor intent
Misapplication violates MGL c. 206 §16 accounting standards
Inaction could trigger AG intervention
Failing to correct outdated purposes violates §805

Why the Appropriation Is Required
Trust funds cannot pay for their own modification
Legal memo confirms conflict of interest if trust assets pay
Professional legal research required to meet §807 delegation rules
Approximately 40 attorney hours needed for investigations

Conclusion
Funds require modernization to remain lawful and usable
Appropriation protects donor intent and compliance
Prevents financial, legal, and fiduciary risk to the Town
