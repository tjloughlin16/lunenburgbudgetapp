Disclaimer
: 
This Preliminary FY27 Capital Program is being transmitted to the Lunenburg Select Board and Finance Committee in accordance with Section 6-7 of the Town Charter, which requires the Town Manager to submit a
 capital improvement program at least 150 days before the start of the fiscal year to the Select Board and Finance Committee.
Under typical circumstances, the Town’s Free Cash would be certified prior to this submission.  However, due to transitions in the Finance Department, Fiscal Year 2025 has not been closed out and Free Cash has not yet been certified.  
In order to meet the intent and timing requirements of the Charter, this preliminary 
FY27 Capital Program has been formed using a conservative estimate of Free Cash.  Once Free Cash is certified, additional meetings of the Capital Planning Committee are anticipated.  
Accordingly, this FY27 Capital Program should be considered preliminary and subject to revision.

Benefit
Validity of Cost Estimate
Presently the bridge is closed to vehicular traffic per MassDOT's recommendation due to severe deterioration of the structure. The detour is an inconvenience for local residents.
The bid opening for the contract to replace the bridge was on March 27, 2024. There were 8 bids ranging from $835,230 to $1,498,000 with an average of $1,137,585.
The following funding is available: $600,000 MassDOT Small Bridge Grant and $300,000 FY22 Capital, total of $900,000. 
It is anticipated that $50,000 will be needed for Engineering and $1,200,000 for the construction of the bridge, totaling $1,250,000. $1,250,000-$900,000 = $350,000
Description of Need
Capital funds are required to completely fund the replacement of the Flat Hill Road over Catacoonamug Brook, Bridge L-17-003. In April 2024, the Town awarded a construction contract to Kenefick Corporation for the replacement of the bridge. The contract specified a Completion Date of May 31, 2025. Unfortunately, Kenefick Corporation failed to perform any of the required work within the agreed timeframe. Despite numerous attempts by the Town to engage in discussions and reach a resolution, Kenefick Corporation did not respond constructively or negotiate in good faith to complete the project. The following funding is available: $600,000 MassDOT Small Bridge Grant and $300,000 FY22 Capital, total of $900,000. It is anticipated that $50,000 will be needed for Engineering and $1,200,000 for the construction of the bridge, totaling $1,250,000.

Facilities
Benefit
Validity of Cost Estimate
Current roof is showing signs of failure and starting to leak. Replacing the roof will protect the entire building from water damage and mold caused by leaks.
Estimates based off of recent roofing projects on town properties.
Town Hall - Shingle Roof - 4,377 Sq/Ft @ $67,850 = $15.5 per Sq/Ft
DPW - Flat TPO Roof - 1,584 Sq/Ft @ $44,145 = $27.87 per Sq/Ft
PSB Roof = 15,163 Sq/Ft of Shingles + 7,694 of flat TPO = $449,458.28 + 10% Contingency = $494,404.11 
Description of Need
This request is for funding to replace the roof of the public safety building in its entirety. The roof is original to the building and over 20 years old.
We have had several leaks on the asphalt shingle section and the shingles are showing signs of serious deterioration.
The rubber roofing over the fire garage is also showing its age and is in the early stages of roof failure.
This capital project would be to remove all existing roofing and replace the shingled areas with new 25 year architectural shingles with 100% ice and water shield coverage. 
The rubber roof over the garage will be replaced with a white TPO roof which will help keep the building cooler in the summer months.

Operational Reliability: Proactive replacement prevents imminent hardware failures and avoids the high costs associated with emergency outages.
