The Department of Public Works is comprised of the Highway, Cemetery, Sewer (
Enterprise Fund
), and Vehicle Maintenance Divisions.  

The 
Highway Division 
provides daily maintenance and repairs of roads, drainage, stormwater, signs, trees, roadside vegetation maintenance, sewers, winter operations, and more. 
90 miles of roadways
1,471 catch basins
2,135 drainage pipes
527 outfalls
262 culverts
16 stormwater BMP’s
26 bridge/culverts

The 
Cemetery Division 
provides daily maintenance and burials in the 3 cemeteries.
North Cemetery (10 Acres)
South Cemetery (6 Acres)
Goodrich Family burial yard on Fish Street (0.12 Acres)
The
 Fleet Maintenance Division 
is responsible for the preventative maintenance and repair of all Town vehicles.  The vehicles include over 30+ pieces of highway construction equipment, high performance Police vehicles, Fire and Rescue first responder apparatus, mowers and more
.
