Town Manager Jennifer Warren-Dyment,
The Town of Lunenburg switched to utilizing the Nashoba Regional dispatch center some ten years ago. The Lunenburg Public Safety Building has been filling the front lobby and answering non-emergency calls with a full-time desk clerk between 7:00am-3:00pm Monday through Friday. During the hours of 3:00pm to 7:00pm Monday through Friday the front lobby and the department’s non-emergency phone lines have been manned with part-time officers, a desk clerk or full-time officers to keep the front lobby services open for the community until 7:00pm. 
The Salaries Radio Watch (lobby 3:00pm-7:00pm) funded FY’26 at $46,897.98, reducing that amount by $23,921.98 to FY’27 amount of $22,976.00.  This reduces the lobby being open from Monday-Friday 3:00pm-7:00pm to being open twice a week. This reduction in front lobby services would take effect on July 1, 2026
  Some of the usual daily requests are accident reports, crime reporting, phone call inquiries for non-emergency assistance, obtaining reports or requests, and prisoner monitoring during those hours. 
The impact of losing the funding the public will see a direct impact when a resident comes to the public safety building, they will encounter a locked door after 3:00pm and must speak with a dispatcher through a kiosk who will let them enter the building’s lobby and call an officer to handle the call in the lobby. Also, after 3:00pm when someone calls the non-emergency business lines of the Public Safety Building, they will either leave a voicemail or be directed to Nashoba dispatch center for assistance. This will require an officer at the station to return the phone calls during those hours when the front lobby is closed after 3:00pm. 
A direct impact on the dispatchers at NVRDD will be an increase in phone calls and lobby monitoring between the impacted hours.
 As a result of losing the lobby service this will be a possible increase in the lock-up budget. The lock-up budget for FY’26 and FY’27 is $15,000.00. Meaning when an individual gets arrested between 3:00-pm-7:00pm on Monday through Friday, this requires an officer to monitor the prisoner and will no longer be monitored by the lobby officer. This will create a residual effect on the lock-up budget line and the overtime line.
The loss of part-time Clerical staff funded for FY’26 at $21,185.92 with a reduction to $0. That position worked part-time sixteen hours a week processing accounts payable for all the police department’s receipts and detail accounts. This is required for billing companies for the detail accounts owned to the town payroll and processing receipts for the departments accounts payable. The impact of losing that position will be adding those responsibilities to administrative assistant.



Training Fy’27 $60,000.00, reducing the amount by $10,000.00 from FY’26 $70,000.00 the impact will be less officers going to specialized training offered for officers’ individual development in specialized fields.

Overtime FY’27 $160,000, reducing that amount by $5,000.00 from Fy’26 $165,000.00 that will impact filling open shifts approximately twelve over the year will reduce patrol staffing from three officers to two officers for those affected shifts.  That reduces the number of officers on patrol for those shifts.

Patrol Officers FY’27 $891,887.37 reducing that amount from $1,034,612.09 affects two officer vacancies which this reduces two SRO’s to one SRO in the school.
