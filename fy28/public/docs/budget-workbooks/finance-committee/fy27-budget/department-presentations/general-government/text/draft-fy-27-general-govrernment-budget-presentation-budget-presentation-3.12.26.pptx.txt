Assistant Town Manager
Current Assistant Town Manager/HR Director position created 
in 2019
Splitting the combined position 
has been discussed informally for several years
Performs mostly HR-related tasks 
Procurement
Many HR-related initiatives on going: implementing vacation accrual system, standing up Sick Leave Bank, implementing annual climate survey, improvement of the employee experience
Preliminary Budget Request of $60,000 to fund the position beginning January 1, 2027
Restructuring of 
Current Staffing
Recognizing the current challenges, the difficult decision was made to restructure staff in the Town Manager’s Office in order to fund this position on July 1
st
.  
Specifically, the position of Communications and IT Director has been eliminated.  The elimination of this position will be a service decrease as the level of our external communications will be diminished.  There will also be an operation impact to Departmental staff as it 
relates to the management of their webpages, IT, and graphic  needs. 
Du
e to the reconfiguration, the net increase to the Town in FY27 is $60,000.

Assessing Department 
Overview
The Assessing Department is responsible for accurately determining the value of all Real and Personal Property located within the Town of Lunenburg for the purpose of taxation. 
Assessors are obliged by the Massachusetts Department of 
Revenue (DOR) 
to assess all property at its full and fair market value as of January 1st of the calendar year for the Fiscal Year which runs from July 1st to June 30th of the following year.
The updated property assessments are submitted to the DOR each fiscal year for their review and approval before Lunenburg's tax rate can be set by the town’s Select Board.  
The Assessors are responsible for the maintenance of all Real and Personal property data records.

Town Clerk

Department 
Overview
Key Services
The Town Clerk's Office serves as the central information point for local residents and citizens. 
The Town Clerk is responsible for all elections, census, and voter registration, as well as overseeing the polling place and the conduct of all elections and elected-related activity, and records all actions of the Town Meeting. 
The Town Clerk records and certifies all official actions of the Town, including town meeting legislation and appropriations. The Town Clerk's Office administers the oaths of office and maintains appointments and resignations of all Town Officials.
The Town Clerk's Office issues licenses and permits, including marriage licenses, business licenses and renewals, dog licenses, and requests for vital records.
