Organizational Structure
Staffing
 
Overview
Land use is composed of 3 departments. All department heads report directly to the Town Manager.  The Conservation Agent staffs the Conservation Commission as well as supervising one  part-time administrative assistant. The Director of Planning and economic development supervises one full-time administrative assistant and  staffs the Planning Board. The Building Commissioner supervises one administrative assistant and four specialized code inspectors. The Building administrative assistant staffs and assists the Zoning Board of Appeals with administrative tasks including receiving applications and drafting decisions.

Service Trends by Fiscal Year
Conservation receives approximately 100 filings per year including requests for administrators' determination, NOIs, RDAs and requests for certificates of compliance. Many conservation applications, permits, and approvals directly relate to the volume of Building Permits issued.
The Planning Department receives and processes between 20 and 40 applications per year on behalf of the Planning Board. While the department receives applications for the Planning Board, this is only a small portion of the work of the department. Typically, the result of the Planning Department’s work can be directly shown by the volume and nature of the Building Permits issued later on.
The Building Department has maintained a steady volume in the last 5 years, the nature of the permits issued changes from year to year. The current trend has been focused on improving existing properties, although there is some sign of new residential development  coming as well as some level of commercial development interest. For the sake of this fiscal year, the figure shown represent a “best guess” based on permits issued to date, the red bar shows the actual as of 3/2/2026.
