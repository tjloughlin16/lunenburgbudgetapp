Medicare- $410,000
Medicare taxes are a 1.45% payroll deduction under FICA.  This budget line will be revisited once salaries are finalized.   

The Town and its employees negotiate health insurance through a Public Employee Committee under MGL c. 32B, s. 19.
The PEC is comprised of a representative of each bargaining unit, Town and School, and a retiree.  
The PEC and Town are in active negotiations re. the FY27 agreement and beyond.
If there are changes to the current benefit structure, these will need to be finalized no later than March 31, 2026.
Changes to the agreement will impact Town and School active and retiree plans.  
PEC Negotiations

The Town was 
bracing for a 20% increase to health insurance in FY27.  MIIA provided a renewal rate in February of 8.85%.  This marked reduction resulted largely from the elimination of coverage for GLP-1 drugs.
Health insurance will remain a significant cost driver for FY27 and beyond.
The PEC Agreement will 
affect the final FY27 health insurance budget.
