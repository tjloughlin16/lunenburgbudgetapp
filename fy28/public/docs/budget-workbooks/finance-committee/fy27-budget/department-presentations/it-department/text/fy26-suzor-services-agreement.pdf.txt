Local Gov Service Scope
Our mission is to help teachers teach, students learn, and municipal employees service their
community. The goal of this Service Plan and scope is to manage expectations in regards to IT
Service Management offered by Suzor IT.
Virtual O￾ce:
Included In: ✅Gov Plan 1 ✅Gov Plan 2 ✅Gov Plan 3
Our Virtual Office is a place where your team can go to get a Live Person over video to get their
IT issues resolved. The Virtual Office team is able to solve most problems remotely, if they
cannot solve a problem remotely they will dispatch the appropriate resources to come on-site
and solve the problem.
What to expect:
● Virtual Office is open M-F from 7:30 AM EST to 4:00 PM EST
● Customer service-focused support over video, live chat, or audio
● Available across laptops, desktops, and mobile devices
Ticketing & On-Demand In-Person Support:
Included In: ✅Gov Plan 1 ✅Gov Plan 2 ✅Gov Plan 3
Ticketing helps track and manage problems as they arise. However, we believe it is important
to leave the “ticketing” to the backend and not make it user-facing. We strive to offer a
ticketing solution that feels more natural and “conversation” like.
What to expect:
● Ticket channels include:
○ Virtual Office
○ Phone (text and calling)
○ Email
○ Ticket Form
○ Live Chat
● In-person support as needed during normal business hours
● Response time and resolution times defined in the SLC
