Service Trends &amp; Initiatives
IT Helpdesk Tickets submitted by Town employees, tracked by fiscal year. 
These trends illustrate workload/demand and average resolution time over the past few years. 
Tickets have consistently been between 800-1200 per fiscal year, for FY26 we have had over 630 tickets YTD. 
The current average resolution time for FY26 is about 18.7 hours. 
Large Equipment Upgrade in FY22, now maintaining a device replacement plan. 
Steadily increasing internal and external communications through routine website and social media updates, monthly employee newsletter, and implementation of the employee intranet. 
Reduced reliance on onsite IT Technician.
