Describe the difference between ALS response services and BLS response services.
BLS- Emergency response and Basic Life Support Care (Basic assessment, splinting, spinal immobilization, oxygen, BLS Medications); Generally stable, non-critical conditions

ALS- Emergency response with an ALS Assessment (Paramedic or Advanced EMT) and  one or more advanced level interventions.  (IV, monitor/EKG, Advanced/Paramedic level medications, Defibrillation/cardioversion/pacing, Intraossous fluids, Endotracheal Intubation, chest decompression); These are moderately ill to critical patients
