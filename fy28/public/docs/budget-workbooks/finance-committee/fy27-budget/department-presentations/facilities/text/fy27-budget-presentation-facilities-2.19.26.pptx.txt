The Facilities Department is responsible for the upkeep and maintenance of our eleven town buildings (Town Hall, Ritter Memorial, Library, Teen Center, Eagle House/ Adult Activity Center, Public Safety Building, DPW Building, Town Beach House, TC Passios and Brooks House, grounds (town commons), parks (Marshall Park, McNally Field, Fitzgerald Field, Wallis Park, and Veterans Park), and town beach.

Organizational Structure
Staffing
 
Overview
The Facilities and Parks 
Department is run by the Facilities Director and two dedicated full time staff members, the Facilities Superintendent and a Facilities Technician. Facilities administrative duties are covered by a shared Administrative Assistant with DPW. There is also a part time Recreation Director @ 19hrs per week who oversees recreation programming and the beach staff. The Recreation Director also handles parks billing.

Total FY27 Budget Request
TCP Revolving Fund
There is a TCP revolving fund that starts out each year with $0 and is funded by rental income from several sources. These funds are used to offset un-budgeted TCP Costs.
$
33,500

Funding Sources
The department’s funding sources primarily come from general fund. The facilities department also works with several town committees such as the parks commission and green communities to support revolving funds, grants, and fees, ensuring fiscal responsibility and transparency while maintaining essential services for our community.

The biggest changes to the facilities budget over last year is the proper budgeting of the TC Passios Building which has not been fully captured in any other budgets in recent years.
Even with the partial decommissioning of the buildings there is still a significant cost to operating that space.
