Overview of Veterans Services
Massachusetts General Law Chapter 115 requires municipalities to administer 
Chapter 115 
benefits
Each municipality must appoint a Veterans’ Service Officer (VSO)
Oversight by the Department of Veterans Services (DVS)

Chapter 115 Program
Means-tested financial assistance and medical support
Living expenses, medical costs, rental assistance, support of dependents, prevention of veteran homelessness, behavioral health
Eligibility based on income and assets; reviewed by the VSO
Funded jointly by the state and town (75/25)

Chapter 115 
Eligibility
Resident
Veteran or dependent
Income &amp; asset limits (FY25):
Individual: $2,609/month, $8,400 assets
Couple: $3,525/month, $16,600 assets
Honorable discharge or equivalent

Re
imbursements
Town must advance payments; State reimburses portion quarterly
Town pays 100%, State reimburses 75%
Only approved benefits are reimbursed
7 currently receiving benefits
Monthly benefits range from under $100 
to over $1,000

Role of VSO
	Assists with:
Chapter 115 applications and annual re-certifications
VA claims and appeals
Access to federal/state/local benefits
Memorial Day and Veterans Day
Lunenburg’s VSO serves in 3 other communities
Annual stipend of $11,000
Regular Office Hours- Thursdays 9-12

Budgetary
 Considerations
Benefit levels vary annually with caseload
- budget can fluctuate
Administrative costs remain a local responsibility
Town may want to consider joining or starting a Veterans Service District
