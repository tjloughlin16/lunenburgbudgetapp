STAFF GROWTH AND SALARY
The population of Lunenburg has grown significantly since the Library facility was built (2005). We continue to see increasing numbers of visitors, and we are actively engaging with underserved and non-library users to bridge that gap.
As the community continues to grow, and use of the library continues to grow, we will need additional staff. LPL is a beacon of excellence, and we regularly see visitors from the surrounding communities who come to use our Library.
In FY26, we face a number of staff changes. We will have a new Children’s Librarian, and supporting that newly configured staff arrangement will be our focus in FY27. The following slides outline our plans for the following years, as we try to expand to meet the need we see in our community.
