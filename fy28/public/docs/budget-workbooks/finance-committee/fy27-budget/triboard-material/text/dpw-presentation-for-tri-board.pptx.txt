Goals and Objectives Discussion
Goal # 3: Stormwater Utility
Establish a Stormwater Utility to fund stormwater work.
There are not enough Operational, Capital, and Chapter 90 funds available to sufficiently fund the labor and the equipment needed to perform the work required to fully comply with the MS4 Permit.
The MS4 permit refers to a regulatory authorization under the Municipal Separate Storm Sewer System (MS4) program, which is part of the National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) established by the Clean Water Act (CWA). The goal of the MS4 permit is to reduce pollution from stormwater runoff that is discharged into local water bodies through municipal storm sewer systems
Failure to comply can result in penalties that could exceed $50,000 per day.
