Justification for Interventionists
The Data: The Case for Support
Current Reality:
 Longitudinal data consistently show that approximately 
50% of our students
 are currently not meeting expected grade-level standards in ELA and Reading.
The Implication:
 These students require additional, targeted support to access the full curriculum and reach their potential.
The Research: The "Compounding Gap"
Skills Build Skills:
 Educational research demonstrates that early proficiency creates momentum. Conversely, small gaps in foundational skills tend to widen over time if not specifically addressed.
The Critical Window:
 Interventions implemented 
before
 Grade 3 are significantly more effective at closing these gaps than remediation attempts in later grades (Stanovich, 1986; Torgesen, 2004).
The Solution: Academic Interventionist
Role Function:
 Deliver high-frequency, small-group instruction to accelerate student progress.
Strategic Goal:
 Ensure students bridge the gap towards meeting grade level standards securing their long-term academic success.
