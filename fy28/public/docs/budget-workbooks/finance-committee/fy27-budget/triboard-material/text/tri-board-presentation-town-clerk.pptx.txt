Departmental Responsibilities
Keeper of vital statistics for the town; 
Custodian of the town seal; 
Administer the oath of office to all persons, elected or appointed to any town office; 
Issue such licenses and permits as are required by law to be issued by town clerks; 
Supervise and manage the conduct of all elections and all other matters relating to elections; 
Clerk of the town meeting, keep its records and, in the absence of the town moderator or deputy town moderator, preside over town meeting pending the election of a temporary town moderator. 
Other powers and duties as are given to town clerks by general law, charter, by-law or other vote of the town meeting.
