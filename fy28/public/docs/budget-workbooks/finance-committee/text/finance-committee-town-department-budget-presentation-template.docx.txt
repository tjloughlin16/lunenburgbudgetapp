Department Budget Presentation Template


Slide 1: Title Slide
Department Name
Presenter’s Name and Title
Presentation Date

Slide 2: Department Overview
Mission Statement: Summarize the Department's purpose and role in the community.
Key Services Provided: Briefly outline the essential services the Department offers.
Staffing Overview: Number of employees, breakdown of full-time vs. part-time staff.
Organizational Chart: If useful.

Slide 3-xx: Summary of Previous Year’s Budget
Previous Fiscal Year Total Budget: Highlight the total budget and significant categories (personnel, operations, etc.).
Actual Spending: Briefly discuss how funds were used.
Key Accomplishments: Mention notable achievements or services maintained under the prior budget.

Slide 4: Department Goals for the Coming Years
Service Goals: Outline any goals for maintaining or enhancing services, even within a level service budget.
Strategic Initiatives: Mention any initiatives to improve service delivery without increasing expenses (e.g., customer service improvements, technology upgrades).

Slides 5-xx: TM Target Budget for the Upcoming Year
TM Target Budget: Present the total proposed budget that fits within the Town Manager’s target increase/decrease. (High level)
Comparison to Last 3-5 Years: Show a percentage and dollar amount compared to the previous years.

Slides 6-xx: Above Target Requests, with Justification, for the Upcoming Year
Above Target Requests: Present funds requested above the TM Target with ample justification. 

Slide 8: Personnel Expenses
Salaries &amp; Wages: Overview of personnel-related expenses, including any increases due to contractual obligations, step increases, or collective bargaining agreements.
Benefits: Healthcare, pensions, and other benefits.
Employee Staffing: If any staffing changes are proposed, highlight them.

Slide 9: Operating Expenses
Summary of Operating Expenses: Include supplies, utilities, maintenance, etc.
Key Expense Drivers: Identify any factors causing increases/decreases in operational expenses (e.g., changing utility expenses, unanticipated repairs/expenses, efficiency measures, etc.).

Slide 10-xx: Status of Funded Capital Project(s) (if applicable)
Ongoing Capital Projects: Progress report on any existing Capital Projects funded from previous Fiscal Years.

Slide 11-xx: Requested Capital Projects (Current FY)
List requested Capital Projects: Benefits to the Department/Town, anticipated expenses reductions, and anticipated operational/administrative expenses (equipment replacement, building repairs, etc.).

Slide 12: Budget Efficiencies
Expense-Saving Measures: Highlight steps taken to reduce expenses while maintaining service levels (e.g., renegotiated contracts, energy efficiency improvements, etc.).
Operational Efficiencies: Discuss any process improvements or technological investments that are saving money.

Slide 13: Impact of Not Fully Funding the Above Target Requests (if applicable)
Service Reductions: Detail which services or programs would be cut or reduced if the above target requests are not fully funded.
Operational Impact: Explain the potential consequences on staffing, community services, and service quality.

Slide 14: Revenue Sources (if applicable)
Town Appropriations: Departmental percentage of Omnibus Budget. 
Other Revenues: Mention any external revenues (e.g., grants, user fees, or federal/state aid) that help fund the Department's services. 

Slide 15: Challenges in the Upcoming Years
External Factors: Discuss any external challenges (e.g., inflation, regulatory changes, economic uncertainty) that could affect the Department’s operations or budget needs.
Internal Pressures: Identify internal challenges (e.g., aging infrastructure, staffing shortages) that could impact service levels.

Slide 16: Conclusion and Summary
Summary of Key Points

Slide 17: Questions and Discussion
Invite Questions: Leave time for committee members to ask questions or raise concerns.
Contact Information: Provide your contact details for follow-up discussions or clarifications.



Additional Notes for the Presentation:
Keep Slides Simple: Focus on high-level summaries and key data points. Use visuals (charts, graphs) where appropriate.
Be Transparent: Provide clear explanations for any increases or changes in expenses from the previous year.
Prepare for Questions: Anticipate questions on justifications for expenses, potential savings, or service impacts.
Detailed Budget Documents: Please send line-item spreadsheets of the TM Target Budget and Above Target Requests to the Finance Committee at least 7 days before the scheduled presentation. Please include 5-7 years of detailed budget documents.
