	  Funding Proposal
Based on feedback from the Selectboard in Nov 2024, the Parks Commission is proposing that the $13,922,086 needed to cover the costs of the proposed project should come from debt excluded borrowing (outside the provisions of Prop 2 ½ (so-called).
Fallback Strategy: If it appears warranted, the Parks Commission reserves the right to amend the motion for borrowing at town meeting to reflect a reduced scope for the project to include only Phase 1 with the addition of the bathroom/concession building  at $5,824,518.

    Estimated Tax Impacts
We are working with the Town Manager, Finance Director and the Towns financial advisor to determine the best options for funding the full project as a debt excluded borrowing
We should have tax impact information available by the last week in March, and this will be provided directly to the Town Manager

	   Status of Grants 
We are committed to applying for as many grants as would be appropriate for the revised project. We have been urged by the grant administrator at the Div of Conservation Services to reapply for the grants previously awarded. 
Funds have been allocated to provide professional grant writing assistance to the Park Commission to develop and submit grants, and for proper administration of grants awarded.
