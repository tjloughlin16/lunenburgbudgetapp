Grants Providing Important Resources
Young Adults with Disabilities Grant - $132,900
The only school in Massachusetts to apply for and receive this 
award, Monty Tech takes special pride in this grant.  Capitalizing 
on the success in Year 1 of the program, Monty Tech 
successfully proposed doubling the size of the program, to 
serve as many as 25 young adults with disabilities, offering 
meaningful training in the vocation of their choosing.  Students 
between the ages of 18-35 who aspire to work in forward-facing 
jobs in the community will receive participation stipends, paid 
internship placements and job placement upon program 
completion.

Grants Providing Important Resources
Leveraging Resources for Large-Scale Projects
On occasion, Monty Tech is faced with an equipment repair or 
replacement that is too much for one grant to support. It is then 
that we rely on multiple sources and our ability to leverage a 
variety of programs to provide that needed funding.
For example, the Automotive Collision & Repair program has 
been in need of an updated refinishing booth for many years.  
The cost to replace this equipment, was significantly more than 
originally planned. Thus, by combining awards provided by the 
following agencies, Monty Tech is able to replace outdated 
training equipment, ensuring students are learning on industry-
standard equipment and technology:
• Anonymous Donor - $180,330
• Commonwealth Corporation, Donnelly Grant - $340,100
• FY25 Perkins Grant - $100,000 
• #1378 Instructional Equipment - $119,470

Grants Providing Important Resources
Targeted Gift to Support Simulation Training and Improved 
Engineering Technology Facilities - $440,000
To support the expansion of STEM instruction and workforce 
training activities, an anonymous donor has provided funding that 
will allow Monty Tech to purchase the school’s first-ever heavy 
equipment simulation devices, which will prepare students (both day 
and evening programs) for high skill, high-wage careers as heavy 
equipment operators.  
In addition, the donor provided funding that will enable the school’s talented 
Engineering instructors to update the program’s learning space, to reflect more 
student-friendly, collaborative environments that engage students, reflect real world 
work spaces, inspire creativity and collaboration, and develop talent.

Recent Edits to Monty Tech Admissions Policy &
             BESE CTE Admissions Policy Proposal
In August, – Monty Tech’s School Committee 
continued its effort to adjust our admissions policy 
in order to increase opportunity for all students 
In February, the Dept. of Elementary & 
Secondary Education proposed state-
wide changes to the vocational 
admissions policy. Currently, the new 
proposal would allow for CTE Districts 
to use criteria including attendance 
and discipline history as measures to 
determine eligibility for applicants to 
be included in a qualified lottery 
model. This model would honor long 
standing district agreements that call 
for the apportionment of seats for 
incoming freshmen. In addition, the 
proposal would mandate that all 
sending district middle schools would 
provide full access to the vocational 
schools in order to recruit students
and further require all sending districts 
to participate in the annual events 
such as Tour Day.
