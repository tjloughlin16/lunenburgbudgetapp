# Withheld: an email answering the Finance Committee's FY27 questions, 26 February 2026

**Written by us. The original is not published, and this note is why.**

## What it is

An email from a member of the School Committee to the chair of the Finance Committee and the
Superintendent, sent on the afternoon of 26 February 2026, ahead of that evening's Finance
Committee meeting. It answers, informally and from memory -- the writer says so -- the numbered
list of questions the Finance Committee had sent the Superintendent about the FY27 school
budget. It was in the committee's folder for the School Department's FY27 presentation.

Held privately, by sha256 `86208269645bfc0f61c1c5c2d21a48571dcae94180a5ef37f38ad9c055d742af`.

## Why it is withheld

One answer describes the writer's own family in personal detail, and another names a member of
staff as an example. Neither is about the budget. A redacted copy was published and then
withdrawn: the document is withheld whole rather than redacted, because what is left once the
personal material is removed is available elsewhere, officially.

## What it would tell a reader of the budget

Very little that the archive does not already hold in an official form. Most answers say the
Superintendent holds the information. The district's own written answers to the same questions
are in this delivery -- `FY2027 School Department Q&A` and `Fincom Questions 2.26.26`, the
second also published by the district -- and those are the documents to cite.

What the email adds is one member's account, and it is labelled here as that:

- **Health insurance.** The FY27 school budget assumed every insured employee at the highest
  coverage tier, made explicit that year, and the writer recalls the premium increase as
  about 9%. A budgeting assumption, as stated; the district's own Q&A is the source to cite.
- **Grants paying for staff.** The district has used grants to pay staff and, by the writer's
  account of the previous night's meeting, is moving away from that.
- **Outside service providers.** Tried before; no provider applied, which the writer
  attributes to the insurance the district requires of them.
- **Athletics.** The writer disputes the claim that half of Lunenburg's students play school
  sports, on the grounds that school sports are offered almost only at the high school. No
  count is given; the participation counts the district supplied are elsewhere in this
  delivery.
- **School choice.** Seats in upper grades go unfilled, and choice tuition does not cover the
  staff a fuller lower grade would need. An opinion, as the writer offers it.
- **Supplies** are put at about 0.6% of the budget, as recalled.

Nothing here is a figure the project should quote. Each point is either answered officially in
documents the archive holds or is one person's view, and rule 7 would require it to be labelled
as such wherever it was used.
