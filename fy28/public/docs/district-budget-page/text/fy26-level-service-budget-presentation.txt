===PAGE 17===
Context
Level Funded Budget maintains the bottom line; 
no increase to the total budget number from the 
previous fiscal year.
Level Services Budget assumes all programs, 
expenses and staffing levels remain unchanged from 
the prior fiscal year; increases for material and 
personnel costs are included. 
Needs-Based Budget maintains essential 
expenses and staffing to meet student needs and 
maintain operational services.
Target Budget sets clear total budget limit which 
is provided to the school department by the Town 
Manager.
