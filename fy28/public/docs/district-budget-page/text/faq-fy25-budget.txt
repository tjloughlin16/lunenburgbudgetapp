What is the budget process?
The typical process:
• The School Administration presents a preliminary budget proposal at 
the 2nd School Committee meeting in January.  
• A Q&A opportunity is on the agenda at the 1st School Committee 
meeting in February and March.  
• The School Committee holds a budget hearing at the 2nd School 
Committee meeting in March, and the Committee votes on the budget at 
the conclusion of that hearing.
• The School Administration presents the School Committee approved 
budget to the Finance Committee in March.
• The School Committee budget is included in the Town’s omnibus budget 
which is presented and voted at the Annual Town Meeting on the first 
Saturday in May.
• Residents can attend any of these meetings and make public comment.

What was the plan for the ESSER funding?
The plan from the very beginning was that we would use the ESSER money 
while we had it to provide as much support as possible to close academic 
learning gaps and provide mental/behavioral health supports to those in need.  
We have seen the benefits for kids as a result of that plan.  We will continue to 
do the best we can in those areas with reduced intervention staff (academic and 
behavioral health).

Why are we eliminating languages at the middle 
school?
Languages are not being eliminated at the middle school.  We said during 
the presentation that we looked at the impact of cutting one World 
Language teacher.  The Department Head felt that would mean that we 
would not be able to offer World Language in grade 8.  As we considered 
the longer term impact to that cut, we decided that cut was one that we 
would not recommend. 
We have a good number of students interested in Latin and French.  Most 
students take Spanish.  Students do have the opportunity to take AP 
French, AP Latin, and AP Spanish.  We have only one teacher certified to 
teach Latin, one certified to teach French.  We have four certified to teach 
Spanish.  

===PAGE 23===
I am concerned that high school students will not have 
electives and the impact to performance in science.
In the last 3 years we added staff in History, Math, and ELA.  We always had more 
teachers in Science than in other core content areas at LHS.  We can reduce 
positions and still offer electives, just not as many.  There are many classes at LHS 
with extremely low class sizes.  It is a nice thing to be able to offer so much to our 
students, but we simply do not have enough resources to continue to fund the "nice 
to haves".  In prioritizing achievement, we need to focus on meeting students' needs 
so they don't fall behind.  While we had available funding, we were able to do both - 
meet needs and offer extra.  We no longer have that option and must make difficult 
choices.
 
Our students in Middle School and High School outperform the state in 
Science/Technology.  Two years ago we added a STEM "special" at Primary and 
THES.  K-5 students take the added STEM class once in the 6-day specials rotation 
(art, music, library, gym, SEL, and STEM). We hold a K-5 STEM family night as 
well.
