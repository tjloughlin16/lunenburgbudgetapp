Outstanding “Unknown” factors: 
● COVID Grant Continuation: 
There are no known DESE COVID grants expected; however, the district is hopeful for additional 
school related grants will be announced.  There will be announcement from Department of 
Public Health on their entitlement grants.
● Circuit Breaker: 
Amount will not be released until end of FY23; expecting a value to cover 65% of costs as well as 
a planned increase for OOD transportation. (Governor’s budget has 15% increase, no details on 
allocation.)
● Federal & State Grants: 
Allocations are typically not announced for entitlement grants until late July; the district is 
expecting level funding.
