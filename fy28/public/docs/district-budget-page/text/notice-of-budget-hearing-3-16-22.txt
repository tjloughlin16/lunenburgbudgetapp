NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
The Lunenburg School Committee in compliance with M.G.L. and the Town of 
Lunenburg Charter advises all citizens the proposed FY23 Budget is available for review 
at the  Office of the Superintendent, 10 25 Massachusetts Avenue, Lune nburg, MA, 
between the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m., Monday  through Friday by appointment 
only.  Copies are also available on the website (www.lunenburgschools.net). 
 
A public hearing on the FY23  proposed budget will be  broadcast remotely  on 
Wednesday, March 16, 2022 at 6:00 p.m.  The proposed FY23 budget requests  
$  22,325,309  for personnel and non-personnel related expenses; a 
3.11% increase above the FY22 budget.
