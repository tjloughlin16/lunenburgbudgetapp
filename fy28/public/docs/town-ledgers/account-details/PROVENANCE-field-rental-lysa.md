# Records request, September 2026 — field rental receipts from Lunenburg Youth Soccer

## Where it came from

**Obtained from Lunenburg Public Schools** in response to a public records request under
M.G.L. c. 66 § 10. The district's business office prepared the workbook (its metadata names
the author and dates it 15 September 2026) and sent it by email to the requester, who
forwarded it to this project on 17 September 2026. There is no URL: the figures were not
published, and asking is the only route to them. The date the request was made is not
recorded here; the workbook's own creation date bounds it.

**The requester is deliberately not named**, for the reason the fund-1301 provenance file
beside this one gives: the address of a records request is the request and its date, and
the name of a private resident who asked a question is not part of it.

## What it is, and what it is not (rule 13a)

**A sheet somebody assembled, not a printout from the books.** Seven receipts — a date, an
amount and a payer name each — typed into a workbook titled "LYSA FIELD RENTAL FY24-FY26",
grouped by fiscal year. One row (FY25, 12 February 2025, $8,118) also carries a pasted
MUNIS cash-receipt record: journal `CRP`, reference `996437`, description
`THES RENTAL/FIELD RENTAL 2023/`, filled across roughly a thousand columns by accident.
That fragment is the only system mark on the document. Every figure here is therefore
`stated`: the district's own answer, carrying the authority of the person who typed it.

The 8 July 2024 receipt of $6,341 is listed under FY24 although the date is in FY25; the
district's own grouping is kept as printed, and the likeliest reading — a period-13
posting to FY24 — is not asserted.

## Where the money lands, as far as the archive can see

The town's FY26 special-revenue balances (`../fund-balances/special-revenue-fy2026-p09.xlsx`,
through 31 March 2026) carry **fund 1306, School Facilities Use Revolving** — revenue
$18,670.50, expenditure $12,353.50, balance $71,558.82 — and **fund 1545, Artificial Turf
Revolving** — revenue $45,000 in FY26 and $45,000 the year before, which is the 2016
funding plan as the Select Board described it on 3 May 2016 ($30,000 from the cell tower
and $15,000 from the association). Neither the athletics fund (1301) nor the general fund's
rental revenue lines carry any receipt from the association.

The FY26 receipts on this sheet through 4 September 2025 total $9,037.50, which sits inside
fund 1306's FY26 revenue. That is consistent, not proven: the archive holds the fund's
balance and not its journal.
