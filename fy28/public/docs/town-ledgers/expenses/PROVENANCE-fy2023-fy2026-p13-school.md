Covers every `-p13-gf-school` and `-p13-special-school` file in `expenses/`, and
`account-details/account-details-fy2026-trial-balance-fund1300.xlsx`.

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `glytdbud-expense-fy{2023,2024,2025,2026}-p13-gf-school.xlsx` | the school department's General Fund (0100), account by account, at period 13 |
| `glytdbud-expense-fy{2023,2024,2025,2026}-p13-special-school.xlsx` | the school department's special funds (revolving funds and grants, fund 1300 and up), account by account, at period 13; carries revenue lines (`TYPE` = `R`) as well as expense |

## What the documents say about themselves

Quoted from the printed copies delivered alongside the spreadsheets:

- **Program ID** `glytdbud`, `YEAR-TO-DATE BUDGET REPORT`, **`FOR <year> 13`** -- period 13,
  the year-end close, for each of FY2023 to FY2026
- **Report generated** `10/06/2026` -- all four years were run on the same day, so FY2023 to
  FY2025 are closed years as the system holds them today, not as they stood at close
- **Generated by** the Town Manager, whose name the report prints
- The trial balance: `Account Trial Balance`, `2026`, `Period 1 to 13`

## What is published, and what is not

Every MUNIS delivery is held at the gate until we have checked it ourselves (CLAUDE.md, 13e).
The check here read every distinct text cell -- account and org descriptions, grant and fund
names, the trial balance's journal references (vendors only) -- for a person, a login or a
per-person table, and found none.

- **The 9 spreadsheets are published** as delivered.
- **The 9 PDFs are not.** They are the same reports, but every page prints the MUNIS login
  of the user who ran them. They are held in the private bucket, `pending` in the register.
  The figures are all in the spreadsheets.
