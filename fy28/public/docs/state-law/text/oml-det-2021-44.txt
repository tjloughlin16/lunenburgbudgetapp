 RE:        Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Attorney Reich: 
 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on March 22, 2021, alleging that 
the Somerset Zoning Board of Appeals (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
30A, §§ 18-25.  The complaint was originally filed with the Board on March 2, 2021, and you 
responded, on behalf of the Board, by letter dated March 22, 2021.  The complaint alleges that 
the Board violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to announce the name of each member who 
participated remotely in the Board’s March 1, 2021, meeting. 
 
We resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), 
after reviewing the original complaint, the Board’s response to the complaint, the complaint filed 
with our office requesting further review, and the notice, minutes, and video recording of the 
Board meeting held on March 1, 2021.1  We find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting 
Law in the way alleged.     
 
On March 10, 2020, Governor Charles Baker declared a state of emergency in response 
to the public health emergency created by the outbreak of the 2019 novel Coronavirus (“COVID-
19”).  See Executive Order No. 591 (Mar. 10, 2020).  On March 12, 2020, Governor Baker 
issued an executive order temporarily suspending certain provisions of the Open Meeting Law 
during the state of emergency.  See Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open Meeting

Law, G.L. c. 30A, § 20 (Mar. 12, 2020) (the “Executive Order”).  The Executive Order 
temporarily suspends the requirements under the Open Meeting Law and the Attorney General’s 
Open Meeting Law regulations that a quorum of a public body, including the chair, be physically 
present at the meeting location and allows all members of a public body to participate in a 
meeting remotely.  See OML 2020-82.2  All other provisions of the law and regulations 
regarding remote participation remain in effect, including the requirement that “[a]t the start of 
the meeting, the chair shall announce the name of any member who will be participating 
remotely.  This information shall also be recorded in the meeting minutes.”  940 CMR 
29.10(7)(b). 
 
On March 1, 2021, the Board held a meeting in which all five members participated 
remotely via Zoom.  At the beginning of the meeting, Chair Steven Cadorette announced that the 
meeting was “a virtual meeting” held on Zoom and then asked for a roll call of the members who 
were present.  Each member then announced his own name and stated he was present.  Although 
Chair Cadorette did not initially announce his own name, he was reminded to do so by Board 
Member James Pimental and immediately announced his own name and stated that he was 
present.  We find that the combination of the Chair’s announcement that the meeting was being 
held virtually followed by a roll call in which each Board member stated his name and that he 
was present clearly and effectively conveyed to the public the identity of all Board members who 
were participating remotely, and fulfilled the transparency goals of the Open Meeting Law.  See 
OML 2020-168.  Therefore, we do not find a violation of the Open Meeting Law.   
 
Where this office previously explained in another determination on a complaint filed by 
the same complainant, and now holds again, that a public body is not in violation of the Open 
Meeting Law when it announces that the meeting is being held virtually and then completes a 
roll call, we discourage the complainant from filing Open Meeting Law complaints containing 
such allegations in the future.  Filing complaints containing frivolous allegations wastes both 
municipal and Commonwealth resources. 
 
For the reasons stated above, we find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting 
Law.  We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved.  This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Board.  Please feel free to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions 
regarding this letter.
