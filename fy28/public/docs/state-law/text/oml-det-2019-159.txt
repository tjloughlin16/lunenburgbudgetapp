RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint
Dear Attorney Powers:
This office received a complaint from John Pooley on November 12, 2019, alleging that 
the Massachusetts Automobile Damage Appraiser Licensing Board (the “Board”) violated the 
Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. Mr. Pooley alleges that the Board improperly 
allowed an attorney to speak on behalf of two appraisers during an executive session conducted 
under Open Meeting Law executive session Purpose 1.1
Following our review, we resolve these complaints by informal action in accordance 
with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a) and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. We 
directly addressed the issue raised in this complaint in a recent determination resolving a separate 
Open Meeting Law complaint against the Board. See OML 2019-50.2
There, we explained that, although the Open Meeting Law only requires the Board to 
allow a complaint subject’s attorney to attend a Purpose 1 executive session for the purpose of 
advising the individual, nothing in the Open Meeting Law prohibits the Board from opting to 
allow greater participation than what the law requires, such as by allowing the attorney to speak 
on the individual’s behalf. Id. For identical reasons, we find no violation of the Open Meeting 
Law here.
