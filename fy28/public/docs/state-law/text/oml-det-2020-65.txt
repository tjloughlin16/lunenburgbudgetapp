Miyares Harrington LLP 40 Grove Street, Suite 190 Wellesley, MA 02482 dbrewer@miyares-harrington.com   RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint Dear Attorney Brewer:  This office received three complaints from Jaden Crawford on February 5, 2020, alleging that the Wellesley Playing Fields Task Force (the “Task Force”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaints were originally filed with the Task Force on December 19, 2019, and you responded on behalf of the Task Force by letter dated January 14, 2020.1 In a separate letter, we explain that we decline to review the first two complaints alleging that the Task Force’s September 13  and October 11 meeting minutes were insufficiently detailed, as the complaints were not timely filed. This determination relates to the third complaint, in which Mr. Crawford alleges “there are to this day, absolutely no meeting minutes posted, or available to the public regarding [the October 25 Lights Subcommittee Task Force] meeting or any of the discussion that took place during the meeting.”  We resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law as alleged. The Open Meeting Law requires that a public body “create and maintain accurate minutes of all meetings, including executive sessions, setting forth the date, time and place, the members present or absent, a summary of the discussions on each subject, a list of documents and other exhibits used at the meeting, the decisions made and the actions taken at each meeting, including the record of all votes.” G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a). The meeting minutes must be reviewed and approved in a timely manner. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c). The Open Meeting Law itself does not define “timely manner.” However, the Attorney General’s Open Meeting Law regulations provide that “timely manner” means “within the next three public body meetings or within 30 days, whichever is later, unless the public body can show good cause for further delay.” 940 C.M.R. 29.11 (2).
