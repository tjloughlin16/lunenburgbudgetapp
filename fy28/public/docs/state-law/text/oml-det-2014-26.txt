RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Goldberg: 
This office received four complaints from Bernard Brahm on November 29, 2013, 
alleging that the Provincetown Board of Selectmen (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaints were originally filed with the Board on August 21, 
2013; August 22, 2013; August 23, 2013; and August 24, 2013. The Board responded by letter 
dated September 23, 2013, following an extension of time granted by this office. The complaints 
allege that 1) the Board failed to provide December 13, 2010 executive session minutes in 
response to an August 8, 2013 request; 2) the Board failed to provide April 9, 2012 executive 
session minutes in response to an August 9, 2013 request; 3) the Board failed to comply with this 
office's order in OML 2013-1011 by failing to amend its June 11, 2012 executive session 
minutes; and 4) the Board failed to comply with this office's order in OML 2013-65 by failing to 
amend its November 14, 2011 executive session minutes.2 
Following our review, we have decided to resolve this complaint by informal action, in 
accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting 
Law. 
In response to the first complaint, the Board states that it has no record of Mr. Brahm's 
August 8, 2013 request for the Board's December 13, 2010 executive session minutes. Mr. 
Brahm provided our office with a copy of an August 8, 2013 letter that he asserts was sent via

complaints. We remind the Board of the importance of responding to requests for executive 
session minutes within 10 days, as required by G.L. c. 30A, § 22(g)(2). 
Mr. Brahm's third complaint alleges that the minutes of the Board's June 11, 2012 
executive session fail to contain revisions ordered by this office in OML 2013-101, issued on 
July 22, 2013. We note that in OML 2013-101, we ordered no additional relief because the 
Board had already taken appropriate remedial action to amend its notice and minutes. The 
amended minutes included an attached summary of the specific litigation matters that the Board 
reviewed with Counsel during its June 11, 2012 executive session meeting. Therefore, we find 
that this complaint is without merit. 
Mr. Brahm's fourth complaint alleges that the Board failed to comply with this office's 
order, detailed in OML 2013-65 and issued on May 20, 2013, that the Board amend the minutes 
of its November 14, 2011 meeting to reflect all executive session purposes relied upon, and 
topics discussed by the Board during that meeting. Following our review, it appears that the 
Board complied with our order and amended those minutes at its June 24, 2013 meeting. 
Therefore, we find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law as alleged.3 
We now consider these complaints closed. This determination does not address any other 
complaints which may be pending with our office or the Board. Please feel free to contact the 
Division at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions regarding this letter.
