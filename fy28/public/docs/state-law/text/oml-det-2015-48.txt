RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Ms. Grieco: 
This office received a complaint filed by George Harris, dated March 24, 2015 alleging 
that the Wayland School Committee (the "Committee") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
3OA, §§ 18-25. The complaint alleges that: 1) the minutes of the Committee's July 30, 2014 
executive session are insufficiently detailed; and 2) that prior to entering into executive session 
on August 28, 2014, the Committee failed to publicly disclose a subject discussed during the 
executive session.1 The complaint was filed with the Committee on December 16, 2014. The 
Committee responded by letter dated February 13, 2015. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law in the ways 
alleged in the complaint. Mr. Harris requested that the Committee take three actions to resolve 
his complaint: 
1. The Committee acknowledge its errors and acknowledge its violations were 
intentional; 
2. The Committee engage an attorney to conduct an Open Meeting Law training for the 
Committee members to last not less than three hours; and 
3. The Committee shall henceforth comply with the Open Meeting Law. 
1 The Committee publicly released the relevant portions of the August 28, 2014 executive session minutes on 
November 3, 2014. 
===PAGE 2===
In response to the complaint, the Committee acknowledged its errors and amended its 
July 30, 2014 meeting minutes to provide the descriptive details sought by Mr. Harris. The 
Committee also agreed to the training proposed by Mr. Harris and pledged to comply with the 
law going forward. The Committee did not, however, acknowledge that any of its violations 
were intentional. 
The goal of this office in enforcing the Open Meeting Law is achieving compliance with 
that law. Where a body acknowledges it failed to fully comply, therefore, and takes steps to 
insure compliance going forward, we will generally find that to be sufficient remedial action in 
response to a complaint. Accordingly, we find that the action taken by the Committee was 
sufficient to address the violations that occurred, and we order no further relief in this matter. 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Committee. Please feci free to contact the Division at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions.
