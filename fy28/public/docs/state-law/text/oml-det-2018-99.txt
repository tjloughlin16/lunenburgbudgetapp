RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Riley: 
This office received a complaint from Mr. Timothy Toomey, dated May 10, 2018, 
alleging that the Rowley Board of Water Commissioners (the "Board") violated the Open 
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on 
March 29, 2018, and the Board responded by letter dated April 20, 2018. In his complaint, Mr. 
Toomey alleges that the Board failed to create and approve minutes from a meeting held in 
executive session on September 11, 2015.1 
Following our review, we resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a) and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. The complaint 
alleges that the Board failed to create and approve minutes of its September 11, 2015 meeting. 
The Board approved the minutes of its September 11, 2015 executive session on March 29, 2016 
and publicly released the minutes with some redactions based on the attorney-client privilege. 
The Board's legal counsel furnished us with a copy of the approved minutes.2 It is clear that the 
minutes were indeed created and approved. Therefore, we find no violation of the Open Meeting 
Law. 
During a telephone conversation with this office on June 1, 2018, Mr. Toomey explained 
that the substance of his complaint stems from his belief that the September 11, 2015 minutes are 
not an accurate representation of what occurred during that meeting. To the extent that the 
complaint alleges that the minutes which the Board approved were inaccurate, such an allegation 
is untimely and, accordingly, we decline to review it. Complaints regarding the sufficiency or
