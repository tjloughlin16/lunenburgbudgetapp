RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Chair Ahearn: 
This office received a complaint from Stanley Haberski, dated January 25, 2017, alleging 
that the Bolton Zoning Board of Appeals (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
3OA, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on December 5, 2016, and the 
Board responded by letter dated December 14, 2016. In his complaint, Mr. Haberski alleges that 
the Board failed to create and approve minutes of a meeting.1 
Following our review, we resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a). Although we find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged in 
the complaint, we find that the Board took appropriate remedial action in response to the 
complaint and we order no further action. 
On November 15, 2016, the complainant sent an e-mail to Town Planner Erica Uriarte to 
request the minutes of the Board's May 31, 2016 meeting. On this same date, Ms. Uriarte 
responded stating that the minutes did not exist. Indeed, the Board did not create minutes of its 
May 31,2016 meeting. After receiving the present complaint, the Board held a meeting on 
December 13, 2016, during which the Board created the missing minutes. The Open Meeting 
Law requires public bodies to create minutes of all meetings, and to approve them in a timely 
manner. G.L. c. 30A, §§ 22(a), (c). Here, the Board did not create minutes of the May 31, 2016 
meeting until December 13, 2016, after being prompted by the complaint. The Board 
acknowledged that it did not comply with the Open Meeting Law's requirements, but we credit
