RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Hewig: 
This office received a complaint dated April 6 from James Young Jr., on behalf of the 
Webster Police Union 1BPO Local #428, alleging that the Webster Board of Selectmen (the 
"Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.1 The complaint alleges that 
the Board discussed the reputation and character of two individuals during a February 27 open 
meeting without providing those individuals with notice. The complaint was originally filed 
with the Board on or about March 3, and you responded, on behalf of the Board, by letter 
dated March 24. 
Following our review, we resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. In 
reaching this determination, we reviewed the complaint filed with the Board, the Board's 
response, and the complaint filed with our office. We also spoke with Mr. Young by 
telephone on May 1. 
The Open Meeting Law does not require that a public body provide notice to 
individuals it may discuss during a meeting, unless its discussion will occur during executive 
session pursuant to G.L. c. 30A, § 21(a)(1). See OML 2017-18; OML 2013-41; OML 2011-
41.2 Here, the Board discussed two individuals during its February 27 open meeting. It did 
not discuss either individual in executive session. Therefore, the Board was not required to 
provide those individuals with notice of the anticipated discussion.

While we find no violation of the Open Meeting Law, we remind the Board that, when 
anticipated more than 48 hours before a meeting, a public body should list its review of an 
Open Meeting Law complaint as a topic for discussion in its meeting notice. G.L. c. 30A, § 
20(b). We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or 
the Board. Please feel free to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions 
regarding this letter.
