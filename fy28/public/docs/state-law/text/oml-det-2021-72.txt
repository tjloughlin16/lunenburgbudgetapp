 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
 
Dear Attorney Howayeck: 
 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on April 30, 20211, alleging that 
the Fall River Board of Health (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 
18-25. The complaint, dated March 18, was originally filed with the Board on March 19, and you 
responded on behalf of the Board by letter dated April 23. The complaint alleges that the Board 
violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to timely respond to a prior Open Meeting Law 
complaint that the Complainant had filed with the Board on February 26.2        
 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law.3 The 
Complainant filed an Open Meeting Law complaint with the Board on February 26 alleging that 
the Board had violated the Open Meeting Law on February 18. Although the March 18 
complaint states that the February 26 complaint was filed with the Board on February 18, it is 
clear from subsequent communications with both the Complainant and Board Counsel that the

complaint in question was not filed with the Board until February 26. The Board responded to

days, unless it secures an extension from the Director of the Division of Open Government. G.L.

the Board. Please feel free to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions.
