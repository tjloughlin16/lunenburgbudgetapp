Re: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Gay: 
This office received a complaint, dated November 16, 2011, from Thomas Medeiros, 
alleging a violation of the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25, by the Dighton Board of 
Selectmen (the "Board") and the Dighton Police Station Committee (the "Committee"). The 
complaint alleges that on September 22, 2011, both the Board and the Committee held a meeting 
at a location that was not compliant with the Americans with Disabilities Act (the "ADA"). 
Additionally, the complaint alleges that on September 28, 2011, the Committee held a meeting at 
a location that was not ADA compliant. The complaint was originally received by the Board and 
the Committee on October 19, 2011. We received your response on behalf of the Board and the 
Committee in a letter dated November 1, 2011. 
In reviewing this complaint, we reviewed the October 19, 2011 and November 16, 2011 
complaints; your November 1, 2011 response to the original complaint; the notice for the 
Board's September 22, 2011 meeting; the open invitation to attend the September 22, 2011 
meeting with Board Chair Erwin B. Whalon; the notice for the Committee's September 28, 2011 
meeting; the Committee's September 28, 2011 meeting minutes; an October 14, 2011 letter from 
Dighton Building Commissioner James D. Aguiar, Jr. to Thomas Medeiros; and we conducted a 
brief phone interview with the Chair of the Committee, James Howland on February 27, 2012. 
After reviewing this matter, we find that the Board and the Committee did not violate the 
Open Meeting Law on September 22, 2011 by holding a meeting in a location that was not ADA 
compliant because neither the Board nor the Committee convened a meeting, as defmed by the 
Open Meeting Law, on that date. Furthermore, while a meeting of the Committee did occur on 
September 28, 2011, we fmd no evidence that it was held in a location that was not ADA 
compliant.

FACTS 
On September 20, 2011, notice was posted for a Board meeting to be held on September 
22, 2011 at 220 Walker Street, North Dighton — the personal residence of one of the Committee's 
members. The meeting notice listed three topics: "Call to Order; Funding Efforts for a New 
Police Station; [and] Adjourn." In addition, an invitation was issued to employees of the Police 
Department and members of the public to attend a meeting with Board Chair Erwin B. Whalon 
on September 22, 2011 at the same location to discuss the new police station. According to 
Committee Chair James Howland, the purpose of this gathering was to provide general 
information about the police station project and to gather support for the project. 
We understand that Mr. Whalon was the only member of the Board to attend the event on 
September 22, 2011. We also understand that a quorum of the Committee was present, however 
notice was not posted for a meeting of the Committee. According to Mr. Howland, no meeting 
of the Committee was convened on that date, and the Committee's members did not even sit 
together at the event. Instead, members were dispersed throughout the home and there was no 
discussion between or among members of the Committee. Members of the public did approach 
and ask questions of the various Town officials in attendance, however, including Mr. Howland 
and Mr. Whalon. 
On September 26, 2011, notice was posted for a Committee meeting to be held on 
September 28, 2012 at the Dighton Nursery School, 1216 Williams Street, Dighton. The 
meeting convened at 6:00 P.M. and all Committee members were present during this meeting. 
According to the complainant, the Dighton Building Department conducted an inspection of this 
location on or around September 27, 2011, and the location failed inspection. We understand 
that the Building Department found three infractions on that date: the smoke detector needed 
cleaning; a back-up battery in an exit/emergency lighting unit was faulty; and materials were 
stored in the boiler room. According to Town Counse1,1  in addition to inspecting buildings for 
compliance with the State Building Code and the State Fire Code, the Building Inspector also 
inspects buildings for ADA compliance. Town Counsel's response to the complaint represented 
that no ADA violations were discovered during this inspection, and the infractions that were 
found were "minor in nature." Additionally, the Dighton Nursery School's website advertises 
that its 1216 Williams Street facility — located in the same building where the meeting was held — 
"was renovated during the summer of 2003, including handicap access and a handicap 
bathroom."2  
DISCUSSION 
The September 22, 2011 Event 
The Open Meeting Law defmes a "meeting" as "a deliberation by a public body with 
respect to any matter within the body's jurisdiction," but excludes the "attendance by a quorum 
of a public body at a public or private gathering, including a conference or training program or a 
media, social or other event, so long as the members do not deliberate." G.L. c. 30A, § 18.

"Deliberation" is defined, in relevant part, as "any oral or written communication through any 
medium, including electronic mail, between or among a quorum of a public body on any public 
business within its jurisdiction." Id. Finally, a "quorum" is defmed as "a simple majority of the 
members of the public body, unless otherwise provided in a general or special law, executive 
order or other authorizing provision." Id. 
When only one member of a public body attends an event, there is no deliberation 
between or among a quorum of the public body, and therefore no "meeting" for purposes of the 
Open Meeting Law. $ee OML 2011-41. Thus, because only one member of the Board attended 
the September 22, 2011 gathering, no meeting of the Board occurred on that date, despite the fact 
that notice was posted for a Board meeting that evening. 3  Furthermore, while a quorum of the 
Committee was present during the September 22, 2011 gathering, Mr. Howland indicated that no 
discussion took place between members of the Committee, and we have not been presented with 
evidence to the contrary. It therefore appears that a meeting of the Committee also did not take 
place on that date, because there was no deliberation by the Committee's members. 
The September 28, 2011 Meeting 
The Open Meeting Law requires that all meetings of a public body be open to the public. 
See G.L. c. 30A, § 20(a). The Open Meeting Law does not prohibit public bodies from meeting 
in locations other than publicly owned buildings however, in order for a meeting to be truly 
"open to the public," it must be held at a location that is accessible to persons with disabilities. 
See id. Furthermore, Title H of the ADA requires that municipal services, including public 
meetings, be made accessible to people with disabilities. 42 U.S.C. §§ 12131-12165 (1990). 
This means that public bodies must hold public meetings in a location with accessible parking, 
access to the building, access to the meeting room, and other amenities such as accessible 
bathrooms when bathrooms are provided to the public. See 28 C.F.R. § 35.150 (2011); Dep't of 
Justice, Americans with Disabilities Act: Title II Technical Assistance Manual, at 5.1000 (1993). 
We understand from Town Counsel that 1216 Williams Street was inspected prior to the 
September 28, 2011 Committee meeting, and the Building Inspector found no violations of the 
ADA. In addition, the website for the Dighton Nursery School advertises that its 12616 
Williams Street location was renovated in 2003 to include handicap access and handicap 
bathrooms. We credit Town Counsel's response and the School's claims, and find no evidence 
that the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law by holding a meeting on September 28, 2011 
at a location that was not ADA compliant. 
CONCLUSION 
We find that neither the Board nor the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law on 
September 22, 2011 by holding a meeting at a location that was not ADA compliant because 
neither body convened a meeting on that date, as defined by the Open Meeting Law. We also 
fmd no evidence that the Committee's September 28, 2011 meeting was held at a location that 
was not ADA compliant.
