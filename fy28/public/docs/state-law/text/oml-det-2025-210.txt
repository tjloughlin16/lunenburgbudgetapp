 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Ms. Putt: 
 
This office received a complaint from Mark Peterson on October 21, 2025, alleging that 
the Bridgewater Conservation Commission (the “Commission”) violated the Open Meeting Law, 
G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.  The complaint was originally filed with the Commission on August 5 
and you responded on behalf of the Commission by email dated August 18.
1  The complaint 
alleges that the Commission violated the Open Meeting Law when it approved minutes of 
meetings held on April 24, May 8, and May 22, which did not include lists of documents used 
during the meetings.  
  
Following review, we resolve this complaint by informal action, in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a).2  “A public body shall create and maintain accurate minutes of all meetings, 
including executive sessions, setting forth the date, time and place, the members present or 
absent, a summary of the discussions on each subject, a list of documents and other exhibits used 
at the meeting, the decisions made and the actions taken at each meeting, including the record of 
all votes.”  G.L c. 30A, § 22(a) (emphasis added).  A document is “used” at a meeting when, at a 
minimum, it is physically present, verbally identified, and its contents are discussed.  See OML 
2025-204; OML 2024-115; OML 2017-169.3

The Commission acknowledges, and we find, that the Commission violated the Open 
Meeting Law by failing to include within minutes of the meetings held on April 24, May 8, and 
May 22 lists of documents used during those meetings.  In response to the complaint, the 
Commission has revised minutes of those meetings to include lists of the documents used.  The 
Commission also has pledged that minutes of future meetings will include lists of documents that 
were used.  We commend the Commission for this commitment.   
 
We order the Commission’s immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting 
Law.  We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved.  This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Commission.  Please feel free to contact the Division at (617)963-2540 if you have any 
questions.
