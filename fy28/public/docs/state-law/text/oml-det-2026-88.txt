 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Chair Makepeace O’Neal: 
 
This office received a complaint from Amanda Alix on March 18, 2026, alleging that the 
Shutesbury Select Board (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.
1 
The complaint was originally filed with the Board on or about January 27, and you responded on 
behalf of the Board by letter dated March 3.
2  The complaint alleges that the Board improperly 
met in executive session during two meetings held on January 6. 
 
Following review, we resolve this complaint by informal action, in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a).3  The Board acknowledges, and we find, that the Board violated the Open 
Meeting Law by meeting in executive session for an improper purpose during each of two 
meetings held on January 6. 
The Board represents that its Chair now understands that a public body may not meet in 
executive session to consider or interview applicants for employment or appointment after they

We order the Board’s immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law.  We

of the Open Meeting Law complaint within 30 days of this letter if it has not done so already, 
and to certify to this office that it has done so.   
 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved.  This
