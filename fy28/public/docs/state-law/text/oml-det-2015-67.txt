Re: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Frank: 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins, dated April 6,1 alleging that the 
Swansea Town Administrator Search Committee (the Committee) violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on February 
27 and you responded, on behalf of the Committee, by letter dated March 12. In his complaint, 
Mr. Fliggins alleges that the Committee held a meeting on February 25 and discussed topics that 
were not listed on a meeting notice that was posted 48 hours prior to the meeting. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Committee did not violate the Open Meeting Law. The 
Committee initially posted notice for its February 25 evening meeting on February 23 at 11 ;59 
A.M. Among the discussion topics listed was an executive session. The notice included a 
verbatim recitation of the section of the Open Meeting Law that formed the basis for the 
executive session, but did not include the corresponding statutory citation (G.L. c. 30A, § 
21(a)(8)). The complainant subsequently raised concerns with the Committee about this topic 
and, in response, the Committee amended its notice one day before the meeting to add the 
corresponding statutory citation. 
The Committee's original meeting notice clearly stated the basis for its February 25 
executive session. The fact that the Committee recited the text of executive session Purpose 8 
rather than listing the statutory citation did not render the notice topic insufficient. OML 2015-
25.2 While we commend the Committee for its efforts to be responsive to a citizen's concerns, 
the information added in the amended noticed supplemented a topic that was already sufficiently
