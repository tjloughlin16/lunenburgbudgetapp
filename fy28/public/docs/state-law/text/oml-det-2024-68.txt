 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Mr. Barber: 
 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on March 13, 2024, alleging that 
the Dartmouth Board of Public Works (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
30A, §§ 18-25.  The complaint was originally filed with the Board on December 27, 2023, and 
you responded on behalf of the Board by letter dated January 18 and sent on January 22.
1  The 
complaint alleges that the Board (1) failed to conduct votes by roll call at its remote meeting on 
December 21, 2023 (“the December Meeting”); and (2) posted notice of the December Meeting 
without indicating the date and time that it was posted, in violation of the Open Meeting Law. 
 
Following review, we resolve this complaint by informal action, in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a).2  The Board acknowledges, and we find, that the Board violated the Open 
Meeting Law by failing to conduct votes by roll call at the December Meeting, as is required 
when any member of the public body participates in the meeting remotely.  See 940 CMR 
29.10(7)(c) (“All votes taken during any meeting in which a member participates remotely shall 
be by roll call vote.”).  The Board has committed to ensuring that votes will be done by roll call 
at future remote meetings.  Additionally, the chair voluntarily completed Open Meeting Law 
training on January 8.

We find that notice of the December Meeting complied with the requirements of the 
Open Meeting Law.  The Board’s website reflects the date and time of posting, December 13, 
2023 at 1:29 p.m.3 
 
We order the Board’s immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law.  We 
now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved.  This determination 
does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the Board.  Please 
feel free to contact the Division at (617)963-2540 if you have any questions.
