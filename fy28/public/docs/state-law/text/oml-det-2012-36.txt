RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Chair Parker: 
The Attorney General's Office received a complaint from Christine Bostek dated April 
11, 2012, alleging that the Manomet Village Steering Committee (the "Committee") violated the 
Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. Specifically, the complaint alleges that the 
Committee chair "intentionally and publicly distributed the Sands of White Horse Beach 
Association private email [to] the entire board of the Manoment Village Steering 
Committee...on February 8, 2012." The complaint was filed with the Committee on or about 
February 18, 2012, and the Committee responded by letter from the Chair' on March 29, 2012. 
After reviewing the complaint and the Committee's response, and speaking with both the 
Chair and the complainant by telephone on May 1, 2012, we have decided to resolve this 
complaint by informal action  in accordance with 940 CMR 29.017(2)(a). We find that the 
Committee violated the Open Meeting Law when the Chair forwarded an email on February 8, 
2012 to a quorum of the Committee and included his own opinion on a matter within the 
Committee's jurisdiction. This constituted an impermissible deliberation outside of an open 
meeting, in violation of G.L. c. 30A, § 18. See OML 2012-8; OML 2011-37; OML 2011-35. 2  
We understand from our May 1, 2012 telephone conversation with the Chair that the 
Committee will take steps to remedy this violation by acknowledging the violation at an 
upcoming meeting and publicly disclosing the February 8, 2012 email.
