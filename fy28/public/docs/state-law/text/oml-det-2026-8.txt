RE:  Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Attorney Cunningham, 
 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on December 1, 2025, alleging that 
the Arlington School Committee (the “Committee”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
30A, §§ 18-25.  The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on October 22, 2025, and 
you responded, on behalf of the Committee, by letter dated November 25, 2025, following an 
extension of time granted by this office.  The complaint alleges that the notice of the October 23, 
2025, Committee meeting was not properly posted to the municipal website as it required a 
username and password to access. 
 
Following our review, we find that the Committee did not violate the Open Meeting Law 
in the way alleged.  In reaching this determination, we reviewed the original complaint, the 
Committee’s response to the complaint, the complaint filed with our office requesting further 
review, and the notice and minutes of the Committee meeting held on October 23, 2025.   
 
The Open Meeting Law requires that public bodies post notice of every meeting “at least 
48 hours prior to such meeting, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays.”  G.L. c. 30A, 
§ 20(b).  Every notice must be printed in a legible, easily understandable format and must 
contain the date, time and place of the meeting, as well as a listing of topics that the chair 
reasonably anticipates will be discussed.  G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b).

For local public bodies, notice shall be filed with the municipal clerk and posted in a 
manner conspicuously visible to the public at all hours in or on the municipal building in which 
the clerk’s office is located.  G.L. c. 30A, § 20(c).  A municipality may designate a website as an 
alternative notice posting location for all public bodies within the municipality.  940 CMR 
29.03(2)(b).  The Town of Arlington has adopted the municipal website, 
http://www.arlingtonma.gov/boards, as the official method of posting notice.   
 
Here, the Committee posted notice of a meeting to be held on October 23, 2025, at 6:30 
P.M. in the School Committee Room of the Arlington Public Schools’ District Office located at 
14 Mill Brook Drive in Arlington, and also via Zoom.  The notice listed 15 main topics and was 
posted to the municipal website, the official notice posting method.  We find that the notice was 
properly posted and did not require a username or password to access.  Since the notice posted to 
the municipal website for the October 23, 2025, meeting was accessible and included the correct 
date, time and place of the meeting, as well as a list of reasonably anticipated topics, we find that 
the Committee complied with the Open Meeting Law’s notice posting requirements and did not 
violate the law.  See OML 2020-20; OML 2015-156.
1   
 
Although not raised in the complaint, we remind the Committee that the Open Meeting 
Law requires that “the date and time that the notice is posted shall be conspicuously recorded 
thereon or therewith.”  940 CMR 29.03(1)(d); see OML 2011-45.  Therefore, the date and time 
of posting may be listed either on the meeting notice itself or on the website next to the link to 
the meeting notice.  See OML 2019-102; OML 2018-53.  Notices that are revised within the 48 
hours before the meeting must conspicuously record both the date and time that it was posted 
originally and the date and time that the notice was revised.  See 940 CMR 29.03(1)(d); OML 
2017-183; OML 2013-44.  Recording the date and time enables the public to observe that public 
bodies are complying with the Open Meeting Law’s notice requirements and further provides 
this office with evidence of compliance with the law.  See OML 2013-44.   
 
For the reasons stated above, we find that the Committee did not violate the Open 
Meeting Law.  We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved.  
This determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or 
the Committee.  Please feel free to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any 
questions regarding this letter.
