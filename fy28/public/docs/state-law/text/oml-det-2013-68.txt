RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Chair Abbott: 
This office received a complaint from Lisa Danek Burke, dated August 13, 2012, 
alleging that the Rowe Board of Selectmen (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. 
c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint alleges that the Board deliberated on "public business outside 
of an open public meeting through oral communication at a Rowe School Committee meeting" 
on June 12, 2012. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on July 9, 2012. The 
Board responded to the complaint by letter dated July 13, 2012. 
In reaching a determination, we reviewed the July 9, 2012 complaint filed with the 
Board; the Board's July 13, 2012 response; and the August 13, 2012 complaint filed with our 
office. Additionally, we reviewed a partial transcript of the Rowe School Committee's June 12, 
2012 meeting, provided to our office by the complainant. Following our review, we have 
decided to resolve this complaint by informal action,  in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). 
We find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. 
The facts in this complaint are similar to those in three prior complaints filed with the 
Board by the complainant, and resolved in OML 2012-72, wherein we found that the Board did 
not violate the Open Meeting Law. Here, the complainant alleges that during the June 12, 2012 
School Committee meeting, Chair Abbott' addressed the School Committee and the complainant 
specifically regarding the earlier Open Meeting Law complaints against the Board. Although the 
other two members of the Board were present, Chair Abbott did not address them or 
communicate directly with them. He addressed the School Committee through open 
participation in the meeting. Because the Board Chair did not communicate with any other
