Re: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Goldberg: 
This office received a complaint, dated July 3, 2012, from Bernard Brahm, alleging a 
violation of the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25, by the Provincetown Historic District 
Commission (the "Commission"). Mr. Brahm alleges that the Commission failed to provide 
open session minutes of its March 21, 2012 meeting within 10 days of request. According to Mr. 
Brahm, this complaint was originally filed with the Commission on April 16, 2012. The 
Commission did not formally respond to the complainant, or to our office, as required by the 
Open Meeting Law. G.L. c. 30A, § 23(b); 940 CMR 29.05(5). 
On March 30, 2012, Mr. Brahm requested, by telephone, the open session minutes of the 
Commission's March 21, 2012 meeting. Mr. Brahm made a second request in-person on April 9, 
2012. Mr. Brahm then filed an Open Meeting Law complaint with the Commission on April 16, 
2012. In response to the complaint, the Commission provided Mr. Brahm with the March 21, 
2012 minutes by email on April 19, 2012. 
Following our review, we find that the Commission failed to make available the open 
session minutes of its March 21, 2012 meeting within 10 days of a request. See G.L. c. 30A, § 
22(c). However, we find that the Commission took appropriate remedial action by providing the 
complainant with the minutes shortly after receiving his complaint. We therefore order no 
additional remedy. Furthermore, where a public body takes corrective action in response to a 
complaint filed with that body, we strongly encourage complainants to consider whether further 
review by this office remains necessary.

As a final matter, we note that in correspondence to our office following the April 16, 
2012 complaint with the Commission, Mr. Brahm alleges that the Commission failed to make 
additional minutes available within 10 days of his request. Because these allegations were not 
raised in the original complaint filed with the Commission, we decline to review these 
allegations. See G.L. c. 30A, § 23(b); OML 2012-38 (noting that potential violations discovered 
after a complaint has been filed with a public body should be alleged in a subsequent complaint 
filed with that body. This allows the public body an opportunity to investigate and take 
appropriate remedial action with regard to the additional allegations). 1  
We now consider this matter closed. Please feel free to contact our office if you have any 
questions regarding this letter. Please be advised that closing this complaint does not resolve any 
other complaints that are currently pending with our office, the Town, or the Comnnssion.
