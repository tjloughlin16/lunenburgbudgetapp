RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. McAuliffe: 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on September 27, alleging that 
the Swansea Board of Selectmen (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, 
§§ 18-25.1 The complaint was originally filed with the Board on or about September 27, and 
you responded to the complaint, on behalf of the Board, by letter dated October 16. In his 
complaint, Mr. Higgins alleges that a topic listed on the Board's September 26 meeting notice 
lacked sufficient detail. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance 
with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. 
The complaint alleges that the topic, "Preliminary Review of Special Town Meeting 
Warrant," listed on the Board's September 26 meeting notice lacked sufficient detail. The 
Board responded that no presentable draft of the anticipated warrant articles existed at the 
time it posted notice of the meeting on September 22. Based on our review of the meeting 
minutes, the Board reviewed a draft list of the warrant articles that was created that same day. 
Some members made comments regarding specific articles. Where a public body anticipates 
discussing specific Town Meeting warrant articles, it must specifically list them in its meeting 
notice. See OML 2013-8; OML 2011-7.2 However, where a public body is reviewing all 
articles for a Town Meeting warrant, it is not required to identify all warrant articles on its 
meeting notice. See OML 2012-83. Because the Board planned to review the entire list of 
articles, rather than any specific articles, we find that the topic sufficiently informed the
