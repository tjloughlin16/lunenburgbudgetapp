RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Brown: 
This office received a complaint on September 29 from Christopher Murphy, alleging 
that the Somerset Board of Selectmen (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
30A, §§ 18-25.1 Specifically, the complaint alleges that during the Board's August 13 meeting, 
the Board's Chair refused to allow Mr. Murphy to address the Board during the citizen's input 
portion of the meeting. The complaint was filed with the Board on September 11. The Board 
responded by letter dated September 18. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. The Open 
Meeting Law states that "[n]o person shall address a meeting of a public body without 
permission of the chair, and all persons shall, at the request of the chair, be silent." G.L. c. 30A, 
§ 20(f). Thus, the Chair had discretion to decide whether to allow Mr. Murphy to address the 
Board during its August 13 meeting. OML 2013-67; OML 2012-23.2
