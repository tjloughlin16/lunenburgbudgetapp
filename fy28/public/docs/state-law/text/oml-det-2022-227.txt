 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Attorney Howayeck: 
 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on June 21, 2022,1 alleging that 
the Fall River Board of Assessors (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, 
§§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on June 8, and you responded on 
behalf of the Board by letter dated June 17. The complaint alleges that the Chair of the Board 
failed to announce that the Board’s May 26 meeting was being recorded.   
 
Following our review, we find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law as 
alleged. In reaching this determination we reviewed the original Open Meeting Law complaint, 
the Board’s response, the request for further review, a video recording of the Board’s May 26 
meeting,
2 and an affidavit from Attorney Matthew J. Thomas who serves as Special Counsel to 
the Board. We also communicated with Assistant Corporation Counsel by email.3  
 
The Open Meeting Law requires that, at the beginning of any meeting that is being audio 
or video recorded, “the chair shall inform other attendees of any recordings.” G.L. c. 30A, 
§ 20(f). The requirement that the chair inform attendees of any recordings being made includes 
any recordings being made by the public body itself, including those recordings made for public

The complaint alleges that the Chair of the Board did not announce at the beginning of 
the Board’s May 26 meeting that the meeting was being recorded. In support of this allegation, 
the complaint cites to a video recording of the May 26 meeting which does not include an 
announcement that the meeting was being recorded. The Board explains, through its response to 
the complaint and through an affidavit signed by Special Counsel to the Board, Attorney 
Matthew J. Thomas, who attended the May 26 meeting, that the Secretary for the Board read a 
statement at the beginning of the May 26 meeting notifying the public that the meeting was being 
recorded. The Board further explains that the recording of the May 26 meeting inadvertently 
failed to capture this statement. Absent evidence to the contrary, we credit the Board’s version of 
events and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law as alleged.  
  
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Board. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions.
