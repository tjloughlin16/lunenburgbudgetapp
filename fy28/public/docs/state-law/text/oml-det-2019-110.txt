RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint
Dear Attorney McNaught:
This office received a complaint from Brian DeLacey, on September 17, 2018, alleging 
that the Malden Hospital Site Development Committee (the “Committee”) violated the Open 
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on 
July 26, 2018, and the Committee responded by letter dated September 12, 2018. In his 
complaint, Mr. DeLacey alleges that the Committee has not maintained adequate minutes of its 
meetings. Upon receipt of the Open Meeting Law complaint, the Committee examined its 
practices, conducted an internal investigation and acknowledged that the Committee’s meeting 
minutes for two meetings violated the Open Meeting Law in several respects. The Committee 
promptly took remedial action by amending the deficient minutes and voting to approve the new 
minutes at its September 11, 2018, meeting.
We resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), 
and find that the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged. See OML 2019-84 
(minutes that failed to include items specified by the statute, such as the time and place of the 
meeting, violated the Open Meeting Law); OML 2014-105 (failure to include sufficient detail in 
the summary of certain discussions that took place during public body’s meetings violated the 
Open Meeting Law). However, the Committee has already taken corrective action by amending 
its deficient minutes for its January 16 and March 28, 2018, meetings and therefore we order no 
further remedial action. See OML 2019-60; OML 2019-109. We order the Committee’s 
immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and caution the Committee that a 
determination by our office of a similar violation in the future may be considered evidence of 
intent to violate the Open Meeting Law.
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Committee. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any 
questions.
