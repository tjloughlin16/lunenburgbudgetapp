RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Chair Roggeveen, 
This office received a complaint filed by Mary Wawro on March 8, 2011, alleging that 
the Nantucket Board of Selectmen (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, 
§§ 18-25. The complaint specifically alleges that the Board "took action on a matter that was not 
properly posted on its agenda 48 hours in advance of the meeting on January 5, 2011 as required 
by law." The complaint was originally filed with the Board on or about February 4, 2011, and 
the Board responded to the original complaint by letter dated February 17, 2011. We appreciate 
the parties' patience as we considered this matter. 
The complaint alleges that the Board failed to provide a sufficiently detailed notice 48 
hours before its January 5, 2011 meeting because a topic included in the notice stated only 
"Pending Contracts." The complainant alleges the Board should have provided more detail as to 
the specific contracts the Board anticipated discussing. The complainant proposed her own 
language for the topic in an email to the Board on December 30, 2010. The pending contracts 
the Board discussed during the January 5, 2011 meeting included discussion of the very contracts 
that the complainant suggested the Board would consider in her December 30, 2011 email. The 
Board considered these contracts from a spreadsheet labeled "Pending contracts for 5 January 
2011," which was prepared in advance of the meeting. 
We agree with the complainant that the meeting notice failed to include sufficient detail 
about this topic, as required by G.L. c. 30, § 20(b) and 940 CMR 29.03. See OML 2011 — 15 
(discussing the legal requirements for detail in a meeting notice); OML 2011 — 11 (finding that a 
===PAGE 2===
municipal soil board failed to provide sufficient detail in its meeting notice by not listing specific 
permits under consideration for renewal). 1  The Board should have stated the specific contracts, 
or category of contracts, under consideration. We do not express an opinion as to whether the 
Board should have adopted the complainant's proposed language. 
We order the Board's immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law. 
We now consider this matter closed. If you have any questions regarding this letter, 
please do not hesitate to contact me at the number below.
