 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Feodoroff: 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on August 24, 2022, alleging that 
the Rehoboth Board of Selectmen (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 
18-25. The complaint was filed with the Board August 8, 2022, and you responded to the 
complaint, on behalf of the Board, by letter dated August 23, 2022.
 The complaint alleges that 
the Board failed to comply with a prior order from our office. 
After review, we find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to notify 
this office that it had complied with an order that was issued in resolution of a prior complaint.  
 
On April 13, 2022, this office issued an Open Meeting Law determination, OML 2022-
83, finding that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to timely approve minutes 
of its meetings held on May 24, 2021, June 14, 2021, July 6, 2021, and September 7, 2021. We 
further found that additional sets of minutes between September and December 2021 had not 
been created or approved. “We order[ed] the Board to create and approve all outstanding 
meeting minutes within ninety (90) days of the Board’s receipt of [the determination], and to 
certify to our office in writing that it has done so.” OML 2022-83. The Open Meeting Law 
regulations also require that “[a] public body subject to an order of the Attorney General 
following a written determination issued pursuant to 940 CMR 29.07 shall notify the Attorney 
General in writing of its compliance with the order within 30 days of receipt of the order, unless 
otherwise indicated by the order itself.”

The Board did not notify the Attorney General, within 90 days of April 13, 2022, that it 
had complied with the order in OML 2022-83. On December 19, 2022, we contacted you 
regarding this matter. On that same day, you responded and notified us that the Board had 
created and approved minutes of meetings that took place between September and December 
2021. Specifically, the Board had approved meeting minutes from September 7, September 13, 
September 20, September 27, October 4, October 12, October 18, October 25, November 1, 
November 8, November 15, November 29, December 6, December 13, and December 20.
1 We 
reviewed the minutes of these meetings and find that the Board had approved them either before 
April 13, 2022, or within 90 days thereafter. 
 
We are satisfied that the Board has complied with our order to create and approve 
outstanding meeting minutes. However, because the Board did not notify the Attorney General 
that it had done so within 90 days of the date of the order, we find the Board violated the Open 
Meeting Law as alleged. We order the Board’s immediate and future compliance with the Open 
Meeting Law. 
 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the  
Board. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any questions.
