RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Chair Manning: 
This office received a complaint from Karen Summit, dated October 2, 2015, alleging 
that the Rowley Board of Water Commissioners (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, 
G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on September 18, 
2015, and the Board responded by letter dated October 2, 2015. In her complaint, Ms. Summit 
alleges that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law by entering executive session without a 
valid statutory purpose. 
This complaint concerns a Board meeting that took place on September 11, 2015. On 
February 16, 2016, our office issued a determination resolving identical issues concerning this 
same meeting. See OML 2016-21. We resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance 
with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). For the reasons stated in that determination, we find that the Board 
violated the Open Meeting Law. We refer the complainant to OML 2016-21 for further 
discussion of this matter. 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Board. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any questions.
