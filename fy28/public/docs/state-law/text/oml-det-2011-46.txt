Dear Mr. Beckley: 
This office received a complaint dated August 5, 2011 from David Gardner (the 
"Complainant"), alleging a violation of the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25, by the 
Easthampton Economic Development and Industrial Commission (the "Commission"). The 
complaint alleges that the Commission failed to provide meeting minutes within ten days of a 
request by the Complainant. The complaint was originally filed with the Commission on June 
13, 2011. We received a response to the complaint in a letter from you dated June 27, 2011. 
The Complainant states that, on May 3, 2011, he requested copies of all meeting minutes 
from the Commission for the years 2009, 2010, and 2011. In your response on behalf of the 
Commission, you indicated that you had provided the Complainant with all the minutes that were 
available. However, the Complainant alleges in his complaint with our office that there were 
additional meeting minutes and that certain meeting minutes "were never kept or are not publicly 
available for other reasons." 
The Open Meeting Law requires public bodies to "create and maintain accurate minutes 
of all meetings." See G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a). The Open Meeting Law states, "[m]inutes of all 
open sessions shall be created and approved in a timely manner. The minutes of an open session, 
if they exist and whether approved or in draft form, shall be made available upon request by any 
person within 10 days." See G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c). 
In a letter to our office dated October 20, 2011, you indicated that you had sent the 
Complainant copies of meeting minutes that were not previously provided. We spoke with the 
Complainant on November 1, 2011 and he confirmed that he received the missing meeting 
minutes and was now satisfied with the Commission's response.
