 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Clerk Livingstone: 
 
This office received a complaint from Stephen Morgan on April 5, 2024,
1 alleging that 
the Ashland Sustainability Committee (the “Committee”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. 
c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on or about March 12, 
and you responded on behalf of the Committee by letter dated March 29. The complaint alleges 
that the Committee failed to timely approve minutes for meetings held on December 5, 2023, and 
January 11, January 17, January 30, and February 1, 2024.
2 
 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action, in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a).3 After reviewing Committee meeting minutes available online, we find 
that the Committee failed to timely approve the minutes of its meetings held on January 11 and 
30. The Committee timely approved all other minutes at issue in the complaint. A few days prior

For the reasons stated above, we find that the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law 
by failing to timely approve meeting minutes. We order the Committee’s immediate and future 
compliance, and caution that a similar violation in the future may be considered evidence of an 
intent to violate the Law and may result in a civil penalty of up to $1,000 per violation. 
 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with the Committee or 
with our office. Please feel free to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions
