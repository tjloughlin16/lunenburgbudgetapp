Re: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Abbott: 
This office received a complaint from Lisa Danek Burke on July 5, 2012, alleging that 
the Rowe Board of Selectmen (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 
18-25. The complaint alleges that the Board failed to provide Ms. Danek Burke open session 
minutes within 10 days of her request. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on 
May 15, 2012. We received the Board's response in a letter dated June 22, 2012. Following our 
review, we find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to provide certain open 
session meeting minutes within 10 days of a request. 
On April 19, 2012, Ms. Danek Burke sent an email to Board Chair Noel Abbott,1  
requesting "the Board of Selectmen minutes from all meetings since January 18, 2012." Chair 
Abbott responded by email on April 19, 2012, writing "Ask [Town Coordinator] Ellen [Miller] 
for these." According to Ms. Danek Burke, a follow up email request was sent to Ms. Miller and 
Chah Abbott on April 23, 2012, that went unanswered. Ms. Danek Burke subsequently filed an 
Open Meeting Law complaint with the Board on May 15, 2012. 
Between January 18, 2012 and April 23, 2012, the Board held 16 meetings. We 
understand that at the time of Ms. Danek Burke's April 19, 2012 and April 23, 2012 requests, 
only seven sets of minutes had been created. The Open Meeting Law requires that draft meeting 
minutes, if they exist, be provided within 10 days of a request. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c). Here, the 
Board should have provided Ms. Danek Burke with the existing open session meeting minutes

within 10 days of her original request. The Board should have also responded to Ms. Danek 
Burke, notifying her within that time frame that the remaining minutes did not exist in either 
draft or fmal form.2  We understand that the Board has since created the outstanding meeting 
minutes, and that Ms. Danek Burke received the requested meeting minutes during the third 
week of July 2012.3  
We order the Board's immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and 
caution that future similar violations may be considered evidence of intent to violate the Law. 
Because the Board has provided Ms. Danek Burke with all of the existing meeting minutes that 
she requested, we order no additional remedy. 
We now consider this matter, closed. Please be advised that closing this complaint does 
not resolve any other complaints that are currently pending with our office, the Board, or the 
Town.
