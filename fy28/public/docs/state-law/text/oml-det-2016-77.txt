This office received a complaint from Karen Vilandry, dated June 2 alleging that the 
Fairhaven Historical Commission (the "Commission") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
30A, §§ 18-25.1 The complaint was originally filed with the Commission on April 19, and the 
Commission responded by letter dated April 20. In her complaint, Ms. Vilandry alleges that the 
Commission failed to provide sufficiently specific meeting notice. Furthermore, Ms. Vilandry 
alleges that this was an intentional violation. 
We resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). 
This complaint concerns the notice of the Commission's March 30 meeting, which failed to list 
the street address of the meeting location. On March 21, 2016, our office issued a determination 
resolving identical issues concerning a meeting held on December 2, 2015. See OML 2016-38. 2 
For the reasons stated in that determination, we find that the Commission violated the Open 
Meeting Law. However, because the complaint does not allege that any member of the public 
was confused by the meeting notice at issue, we find that this error was harmless. Furthermore, 
because the Commission had not yet received our office's determination at the time of the March 
30 meeting notice posting, and thus was unaware of the relief ordered at that time, we find that 
this violation was not intentional. For these reasons, we order no relief. We refer the 
complainant to OML 2016-38 for further discussion of this matter, and take this opportunity to 
remind the Commission to list the address of its meeting locations on its meeting notices.
