RE: Open Meeting Law Complaints 
Dear Attorney Zick: 
This office received five complaints from Jason Grow alleging that the Gloucester 
Community Arts Charter School ("GCACS") Board of Trustees (the "Board") and Governance 
Committee (the "Committee") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. Mr. 
Grow filed two complaints with this office on October 26, 2011; two complaints on July 5, 2012; 
and the final complaint on August 29, 2012. 
We understand that on December 18, 2012, the state Board of Elementary and Secondary 
Education accepted a decision by the GCACS Board to surrender its charter and close the school 
at the end of academic year. We further understand that the School actually closed operations on 
January 11, 2013. Following this event, we spoke with Mr. Grow regarding the status of his 
complaints. Mr. Grow offered to withdraw his complaints, subject to our determination of 
allegations in his July 5, 2012 complaint regarding the public release of certain records. 
In that complaint, Mr. Grow seeks release of the Board's March 24, 2012 and March 31, 
2012 executive session meeting minutes. The Board did not respond to this complaint. Despite 
the closure decision, the Board has a continuing obligation as it winds down its affairs to comply 
with the Open Meeting Law and make meeting minutes available to the public. We therefore 
order the Board to release the executive session minutes of its March 24, 2012 and March 31, 
2012 meetings, unless the attorney-client privilege or one or more of the exemptions to the 
Public Records Law applies to withhold the minutes, or any portion thereof, from disclosure. 
G.L. c. 30A, § 22(f). If Mr. Grow wishes to appeal the denial of requests for documents other 
than meeting minutes, such as written communications between Board members, he may do so 
by contacting the Supervisor of Public Records within the Secretary of the Commonwealth's 
Public Records Division.
