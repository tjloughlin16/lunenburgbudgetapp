RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint - Wenham Audit Committee 
Dear Mr. Andrews: 
This office received an Open Meeting Law complaint from Ms. Michelle Bailey, dated 
April 1, 2013, alleging that the Wenham Audit Committee (the "Committee") violated the Open 
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. Specifically, the complaint alleges that the Committee 
failed to provide Ms. Bailey with the minutes of its November 27, 2012 meeting within 10 days 
of her request. The complaint was filed with the Committee on or about January 29, 2013. 
Town Administrator Mark Andrews' responded to the complaint on behalf of the Committee in a 
letter dated February 15, 2013. 
Following our review, we have decided to resolve this complaint by informal action, in 
accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the Committee violated the Open Meeting 
Law by failing to provide minutes within 10 days of a request. Flowever, the Committee has 
since provided the minutes at issue, thus we order no additional relief' 
We remind the Committee that the minutes of all open sessions shall be created and 
approved in a timely manner. G.L c. 30A, § 22(c). The minutes of an open session, if they exist 
and whether approved or in draft form, shall be made available upon request by any person 
within 10 days. Id. Although the Open Meeting Law does not define "timely manner," we 
recommend that minutes of a meeting be approved at the next meeting, whenever possible. See
