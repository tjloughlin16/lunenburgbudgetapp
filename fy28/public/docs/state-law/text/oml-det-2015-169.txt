RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Galvin: 
This office received a complaint from Donald A. Mauch, Sr., dated September 8, 2015, 
alleging that the Norwell Pathways Committee (the "Committee") violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. Specifically, the complaint alleges that the Committee failed to 
consistently create and approve minutes of its meetings held between August 2011 and July 
2015. The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on August 5, 2015. The 
Committee responded by letter dated August 14, 2015. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged. 
The Committee is a public body subject to the Open Meeting Law. G.L. c. 3OA, § 18. A public 
body must create and approve meeting minutes in a timely manner. G.L. c. 3OA, §§ 22(a), (c). 
The Open Meeting Law does not provide a definition of "timely manner," but we recommend 
that minutes be approved at the next meeting whenever possible. See OML 2013-173; OML 
2013-37; OML 2012-91.1 While the Committee appears to have created draft minutes for 
meetings held between August 2011 and July 2015, it had not approved those minutes as 
required by G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c) at the time the complaint was filed. 
In response to the complaint, the Committee acknowledged its violation, approved the 
outstanding meeting minutes during its August 26, 2015 meeting, and pledged to create and 
approve minutes of all future meetings within two months. We find this was appropriate 
remedial action in response to the complaint. In addition to the action already taken, we order 
the Committee's immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and caution the
