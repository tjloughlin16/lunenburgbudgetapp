Re: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Wagner: 
This office received a complaint from Mark Brooks, dated April 23, 2015, alleging that 
the Worthington Selectboard (the Board) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. 
The complaint was originally filed with the Board on March 10 and you responded, on behalf of 
the Board, by letter dated March 17, 2015. In his complaint, Mr. Brooks alleges that the Board 
failed to approve minutes from its November 25, 2014 meeting in a timely manner.1 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. The Board 
meets weekly between Labor Day and Memorial Day and bi-weekly during the summer months. 
The Board generally approves its meeting minutes two weeks after each meeting. In this 
instance, however, the November 25, 2014 meeting minutes were not approved until February 
10, 2015 due to a Board member's absence from meetings and several meeting cancelations due 
to winter weather. 
Minutes of all open sessions shall be approved in a timely manner. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c). 
The Open Meeting Law does not provide a definition of "timely manner," but we have 
recommended that minutes be approved at the next meeting whenever possible. See OML 2013-
173; OML 2013-37; OML 2012-91.2 Although here the Board took nearly three months to 
approve the minutes at issue, we nevertheless find this was the earliest possible date on which 
the Board was able to do so. Furthermore, we are satisfied that the Board understands its 
obligations regarding the approval of minutes, and that the circumstances leading to the delayed
