RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Hendricks: 
This office received an Open Meeting Law complaint from Gerald Baker on December 4, 
2013, alleging that the Uxbridge Planning Board (the Board) violated the Open Meeting Law, 
G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint specifically alleges that the Board convened an executive 
session without giving a proper purpose and utilizing an improper procedure. The complaint was 
originally filed with the Board on October 29, 2013. The Board responded by letter, through the 
Town Manager, on November 21, 2013. 
After reviewing the complaint and the Board's response, we have decided to resolve this 
complaint by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find the Board 
violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to vote by roll-call to enter into executive session. 
G.L. c. 30A, § 21(b)(2); OML 2012-39.1 We also find that the Board violated the Open Meeting 
Law by failing to state a purpose for the executive session prior to meeting behind closed doors. 
G.L. c. 3OA, § 21(b)(3); OML 2011-44. The Board acknowledged its error in its November 21, 
2013 response to the complaint, and we do not find that the violation was intentional. 
Furthermore, immediately upon entering executive session, the issue of its propriety was raised 
and the meeting was concluded before any substantive discussion could occur. We therefore 
find that the violation was de minimis. 
The Town Manager has provided remedial training to the Board on the proper procedures 
and purposes for entering into executive session. The Board independently reviewed the sufficiency 
of the specificity of its agenda items and has pledged to improve these as well. We are satisfied

that the Board has taken the appropriate action in response to the complaint. Accordingly, we 
order the Board's immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and caution 
that future similar violations may be considered evidence of intent to violate the Law. 
As a final matter, we remind the Board that "within 2 weeks of qualification for office, all 
persons serving on a public body" must review the Open Meeting Law as well as the Attorney 
General's Open Meeting Law regulations and Open Meeting Law Guide, and must sign a 
Certificate of Receipt of OML Materials.2 G.L. c. 30A, § 20(g). This requirement applies to 
both volunteer and compensated public body members. 
We now consider this matter closed. Please be advised that this letter does not resolve 
any other complaints that may be pending with this office, the Town, or the Board. Please 
contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions regarding this letter.
