Dear Chair Leonardo; 
This office received a complaint from Mr. Gerald Baker, Jr., dated April 13, 2015, 
alleging that the Uxbridge Planning Board (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on February 24, 2015, and the 
Board responded to the original complaint by letter dated February 25, 2015. In his complaint, 
Mr. Baker alleges that the Board held a meeting on October 22, 2014 without creating meeting 
minutes. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2), and find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. In reaching 
this determination, we reviewed the complaint filed with the Board; the Board's response; and 
the request for further review filed with our office. 
The Open Meeting Law requires that a public body "create and maintain accurate 
minutes of all meetings, including executive sessions, setting forth the date, time and place, the 
members present or absent, a summary of the discussions on each subject, a list of documents 
and other exhibits used at the meeting, the decisions made and the actions taken at each meeting, 
including the record of all votes." G.L. c. BOA, § 22(a). Here, the meeting at issue was held on 
October 22, 2014. The minutes of the October 22, 2014 meeting were approved during a 
meeting held on December 10, 2014. The minutes contain brief summaries of the topics 
discussed by the Board. A copy of the approved minutes of the October 22, 2014 meeting was 
stamped as received by the Uxbridge Town Clerk on December 11, 2014. Because the Board 
complied with the requirements of the Open Meeting Law, we find no violation.1 
1 We note that the Board's meeting notice refers to the October 22, 2014 meeting as "a workshop meeting." The 
complaint states that the Board treats so-called "workshop meetings" as distinct from regular meetings of the Board.
