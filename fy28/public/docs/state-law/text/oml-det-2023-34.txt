 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Braun: 
This office received a complaint from Steven Ballard on January 27, 2022, alleging that 
the Boxborough Finance Committee (the “Committee”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on November 7, 2022, and you 
responded on behalf of the Board by letter dated November 23, 2022. The Complainant alleges 
that the Committee failed to timely respond to a previous Open Meeting Law complaint filed 
with the Board on October 13, 2022.
 1 
Following our review, we find that the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law as 
alleged. A public body must respond to an Open Meeting Law complaint within 14 business 
days, unless it secures an extension from the Director of the Division of Open Government. G.L. 
c. 30A, § 23(b); 940 CMR 29.05(5). The Complainant previously filed a complaint with the 
Committee on October 13. The Committee responded to that complaint by letter on November 
23 – 21 days past the 14-business day deadline for responding. The Committee acknowledges 
that it failed to timely respond to the complaint due to an oversight, as the staff member charged 
with monitoring the Committee’s inbox missed that particular complaint and failed to forward it 
to the Committee’s attention. Because the Committee failed to respond to an Open Meeting Law 
complaint within 14 business days, we find that it violated the Open Meeting Law. 
We order the Committee’s immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law 
and caution the Committee that a determination by our office of a similar violation in the future 
may be considered evidence of intent to violate the Open Meeting Law.
