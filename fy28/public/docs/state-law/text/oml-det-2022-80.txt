 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaints 
 
Dear Ms. Blackmer: 
 
This office received a complaint from Jennifer Barbeau on October 13, 2021, alleging 
that the North Adams City Council (the “Council”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, 
§§ 18-25.  The complaint was originally filed with the Council on or about July 30, 2021, and 
you responded to the complaint, on behalf of the Council, by letter on August 20, 2021.1  The 
complaint alleges that certain Council members failed to comply with the certification 
requirements of the Open Meeting Law.   
 
We resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), 
after reviewing the original complaint, the Council’s response to the complaint, and the 
complaint filed with our office requesting further review.  In addition, we communicated with 
the complainant on February 14, 2022, and with City Clerk Marcus Lyon on April 4, 2022.  We 
find that the Council violated the Open Meeting Law in the way alleged.   
 
The Open Meeting Law requires that “within 2 weeks of qualification for office, all 
persons serving on a public body shall certify, on a form prescribed by the attorney general, the 
receipt of a copy of the open meeting law, regulations promulgated pursuant to section 25 and a 
copy of the educational materials prepared by the attorney general explaining the open meeting 
law and its application pursuant to section 19 ….  The certification shall be evidence that the

member of a public body has read and understands the requirements of the open meeting law and 
the consequences of violating it.”  See G.L. c. 30A § 20; OML 2021-97; OML 2011-39.2   
 
The complaint alleges that seven of nine Councilors - Lisa Blackmer, Keith Bona, 
Benjamin Lamb, Peter Oleskiewicz, Bryan Sapienza, Jessica Sweeney, and Wayne Wilkinson - 
did not complete the Open Meeting Law certification form.  The Council acknowledges, and we 
agree, that these Councilors failed to timely sign the certification form.  As such, we find that 
these Councilors violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to complete the required certification 
within two weeks of either their election or appointment.3  See OML 2021-48.  Where the 
Councilors took prompt remedial action in response to the complaint to ensure that the required 
certificates were completed, we order no further remedial action.  
 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved.  This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Council.  Please feel free to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions 
regarding this letter.
