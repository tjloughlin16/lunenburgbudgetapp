RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Troy: 
This office received a complaint from Valian Norris on March 22, 2015, alleging that the 
Massachusetts State Retirement Board (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint specifically alleges that the Board failed to provide Ms. Norris 
with open session meeting minutes within 10 days of her request.1 This complaint was originally 
filed with the Board on February 24, 2015. The Board responded by letter dated March 16, 
2015. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to 
provide meeting minutes within 10 days of a request. In reaching this determination, we 
reviewed the complaint filed with the Board, the Board's response, and the complaint filed with 
our office. Additionally, we spoke with Ms. Norris by telephone on March 23, 2015. 
The Open Meeting Law requires that a public body "create and maintain accurate 
minutes of all meetings." G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a). The minutes of an open session meeting, if they 
exist and whether approved or in draft form, must be made available upon request by any person 
within 10 days. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a). On January 5, 2015, Ms. Norris' attorney wrote a letter to 
the Board requesting "copies of the minutes of any meetings of the Board in October 2014." The 
1 Ms. Norris' complaint also alleges that the Board failed to provide her with the "information and documents

Board did not provide Mr. Norris with the responsive meeting minutes until it responded to her 
Open Meeting Law complaint on March 16, 2015, more than two months after her request. 
Clearly, the Board failed to provide meeting minutes within the timeframe required by the Law. 
See OML 2014-59; OML 2013-18.2 
We order the Board's immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and 
caution the Board that a similar future violation may be considered an intentional violation of the 
Law. Because the minutes have already been provided to the complainant, we order no 
additional relief. We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. 
This determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or 
the Board. Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions.
