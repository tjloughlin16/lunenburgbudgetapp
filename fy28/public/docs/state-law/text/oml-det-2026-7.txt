RE:  Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Mr. Ullucci, 
 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on September 26, 2025, alleging 
that the Somerset Board of Health (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, 
§§ 18-25.  The complaint was originally filed with the Board on or about August 27, 2025, and 
you responded, on behalf of the Board, by email dated September 10, 2025.  The complaint 
alleges that the Board failed to post notice of a joint meeting held on August 27, 2025, with the 
Board of Selectmen. 
 
Following our review, we find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law in the way 
alleged.  In reaching this determination, we reviewed the original complaint, the Board’s 
response to the complaint, and the complaint filed with our office requesting further review.  In 
addition, we reviewed the notice and open session minutes of the Board of Selectmen meetings 
held on August 27, 2025, and September 10, 2025.   
 
FACTS 
 
We find the facts as follows.  Both the Board and Board of Selectmen are three-member 
public bodies, and the same three individuals—Allen Smith, Todd Costa and Jamison Souza—
serve on both public bodies.   
 
The Board of Selectmen duly posted notice of a meeting to be held on August 27, 2025, 
at 5:30 P.M.  One of the topics listed on the notice was “Board of Health Hearing – Lit Vape

DISCUSSION 
 
The Open Meeting Law requires that public bodies post notice of every meeting “at least 
48 hours prior to such meeting, excluding Saturdays, Sundays and legal holidays.”  G.L. c. 30A, 
§ 20(b).  Every notice must include “the date, time and place of such meeting and a listing of 
topics that the chair reasonably anticipates will be discussed at the meeting.”  G.L. c. 30A,          
§ 20(b).   
 
The complaint alleges that the Board failed to post notice of a joint meeting held on 
August 27, 2025, with the Board of Selectmen.  Two or more public bodies may hold a joint 
meeting.  When two public bodies contemplate a joint meeting, they may either post a single 
joint notice that clearly indicates that both public bodies will meet together, or they may each 
post their own notice for the joint meeting.  See G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b); OML 2021-69; OML 
2020-9; OML 2016- 117.1  For purposes of clarity, all bodies involved should note in their 
meeting notices that the meeting will be a joint meeting with the other public body.  See OML 
2012-6; OML 2011-16.  Any notice of a joint meeting must meet all the requirements of the

Here, the header 
 of the notice for the August 27, 2025, meeting indicated that only a 
Board of Selectmen meeting would be held on that date.  However, during the meeting, the three 
Board of Selectmen members convened a Board of Health meeting to conduct a public hearing to 
consider a violation of the Lit Vape Smoke Shop for offering the sale of flavored tobacco 
products.  We find, here, that the notice of the August 27, 2025, meeting posted by the Board of 
Selectmen did not clearly indicate that the meeting would also include a meeting of the Board.  
Moreover, the Board did not post any notice of the August 27, 2025, meeting.  As such, we find 
that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law.  Although we find that the Board violated the 
Open Meeting Law, we order no further remedial action where the Board did not engage in any 
substantive discussion but simply postponed the public hearing until September 10, 2025, and 
then adjourned the meeting.  See OML 2024-63.  We commend both the Board and Board of 
Selectmen for posting notices that clearly indicated that both public bodies would meet jointly on 
September 10, 2025. 
 
CONCLUSION 
 
For the reasons stated above, we find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law.  We 
order the Board’s immediate and future compliance with the law’s requirements, and we caution 
that similar future violations could be considered evidence of intent to violate the law.   
 
We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved.  This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Board.  Please feel free to contact our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions
