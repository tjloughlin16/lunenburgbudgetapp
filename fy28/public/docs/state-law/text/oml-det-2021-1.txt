 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Ms. Aronstein: 
This Office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on September 25, 2020, alleging 
that the Swansea Board of Selectmen (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 
30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on August 11, and Acting 
Town Administrator James Purcell responded on behalf of the Board by letter dated August 31.1  
In his complaint, Mr. Higgins alleges that the posted notice for the Board’s August 11 meeting 
failed to include the date and time the notice was posted, and that the notice failed to identify the 
specific trash abatement applications to be discussed. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to 
include the list of trash abatements on the meeting notice, but that the Board did not violate the 
law in the other way alleged.   
First, we find that the Board satisfied the requirement in the Open Meeting Law 
regulations that “[t]he date and time that a meeting notice is posted shall be conspicuously 
recorded thereon or therewith.”  940 CMR 29.03(1)(d).  The Board’s official method of posting 
notice is by posting to a glass-enclosed bulletin board outside the municipal building.  The 
complainant attached to his complaint photos of the notice, apparently taken in dark conditions, 
purportedly showing that the date and timestamp was not visible on the posted notice.  We

acknowledge that due to a reflection in the glass and glare from surrounding light, it is difficult 
to read the date and time in the upper right corner of the notice; nonetheless, it is evident from at 
least one of the complainant’s photos that the date and time were stamped on the notice.  We 
therefore find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law in this regard. 
Next, we find, as the Board acknowledges, that the Board violated the Open Meeting 
Law by failing to include in its meeting notice a list of the trash abatement matters to be 
discussed at the August 11 meeting.  The notice stated “ITEM 9 Trash Abatements – (attached)”; 
however, due a clerical error, the attachment was not posted as part of the meeting notice.  Our 
office has previously advised the Board that a notice should include “specific details about each 
... abatement applicant.”  See OML 2016-167; see also OML 2016-2016-90; OML 2011-11.  We 
find that the failure to post the list of trash abatement applicants was an unintentional clerical 
oversight and therefore do not find an intentional violation of the law, although we caution the 
Board that a similar future violation could be considered evidence of an intent to violate the law.   
 We now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved. This 
determination does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the 
Board.  Please feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963 - 2540 if you have any questions.
