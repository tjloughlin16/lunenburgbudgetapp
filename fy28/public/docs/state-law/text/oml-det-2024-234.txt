RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint  
 
Dear Attorney Mello:  
 
This office received a complaint from Sean McNamara on November 8, 2024, alleging 
that the Attleboro Planning Board (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 
18-25.  The original complaint was filed with the Board on or about October 28, 2024, and you 
responded, on behalf of the Board, by letter dated November 7, 2024.  The complaint alleges that 
the Board has not made public nor posted minutes of meetings since July 2024. 
 
Following our review, we find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law in 
the ways alleged.  In reaching this determination, we reviewed the original complaint, the 
Board’s response to the complaint, the complaint filed with our office requesting further review, 
and relevant notices and minutes of Board meetings held between on July 15, 2024, and 
November 19, 2024.   
 
The Open Meeting Law requires that a public body “create and maintain accurate 
minut
es of all meetings, including executive sessions, setting forth the date, time and place, the 
members present or absent, a summary of the discussions on each subject, a list of documents 
and other exhibits used at the meeting, the decisions made, and the actions taken at each meeting, 
including the record of all votes.”  G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a).  Minutes of all open sessions must be 
created and approved in a timely manner.  G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c).  “Timely manner” means 
“within the next three public body meetings or within 30 days, whichever is later, unless the 
public body can show good cause for further delay.”  940 CMR 29.11.  Whenever possible, we

recommend that minutes of a meeting be approved at the next meeting.  See OML  2024-114; 
OML 2018-67; OML 2017-133.1   
 
To the extent the complaint alleges that minutes of Board meetings were not approved in 
a timely manner, we find that the Board created and approved minutes of meetings held between 
August 19, 2024, and October 7, 2024, within the timeframe required by the Open Meeting Law, 
that is, within the later of the next three meetings or 30 days.  See OML 2024-143.  To the extent 
the complainant argues that the meeting minutes should have been posted to a municipal website, 
we note that the Open Meeting Law does not require public bodies to post meeting minutes.  See 
OML 2023-179; OML 2015-173; OML 2013-117.  Rather, the Law requires that “[t]he minutes 
of an open session, if they exist and whether approved or in draft form, shall be made available 
upon request by any person within 10 days.”  G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c).  We note that minutes of 
Board meetings held between August 19, 2024, and October 7, 2024, are posted to the municipal 
website.  
 
To the extent the complainant separately asserts that he requested, and was not provided 
with,
 minutes, we find that this allegation was not clearly raised in the complaint and thus we do 
not review its merits.  Here, the complainant states that he reached out to the Board “to inquire 
when the meeting notes would be made public.”  It is not clear that the complainant requested the 
minutes from the Board; rather, the complaint implies that the complainant merely asked when 
the minutes would become available.  Complainants must allege violations with a degree of 
specificity, as our office will not conduct broad audits of public bodies based on generalized 
allegations.  See OML 2014-119; OML 2012-106.  Nonetheless, we remind the Board that a 
request for meeting minutes that is directed to a public body will fall under the Open Meeting 
Law and the body must make minutes of an open session available within 10 days of a request, if 
they exist, whether approved or in draft form.  See G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c).  However, the Open 
Meeting Law does not specify in what format minutes must be made available.  Therefore, 
directing a requester to a website where the requested minutes are posted and accessible 
complies with the requirement to make the minutes “available.”  See OML 2024-24; OML 2022-
205.  As noted, the Board has posted meeting minutes to the municipal website.
