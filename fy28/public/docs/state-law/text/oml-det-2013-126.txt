RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Ms. Sabourin: 
This office received an Open Meeting Law complaint from Terry Kinnas, dated February 
7, 2013, alleging that the Pittsfield Reid Middle School's School Council Community Outreach 
Subcommittee (the "Subcommittee") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. 
Specifically, the complaint alleges during the Subcommittee's November 14, 2012 meeting, the 
"door to the building was locked and no information was posted on the door as to how to get to 
the room where the meeting was taking place." The complaint was filed with the Committee on 
or about November 16, 2012. The Committee responded by letter dated December 3, 2012. 
After reviewing the complaint and the Committee's response, we have decided to resolve 
this complaint by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). Following our 
review, we find that the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law by meeting in a location that 
was inaccessible to the public. See OML 2013-8; OML 2012-46; OML 2011-52.1 Flowever, we 
find that the Subcommittee took appropriate remedial action in response to the complaint. 
Mr. Kinnas attended the Subcommittee's November 14, 2012 meeting, and found the 
"door to the building was locked and no information was posted on the door as to how to get to 
the room where the meeting was taking place." After about 5 to 7 minutes, Mr. Kinnas was let in 
the building and eventually found the meeting. In response to the complaint, the Subcommittee 
took the following actions: 
MARTHA COAKLEY 
ATTORNEY GENERAL

1. Reposted the agenda for the November 14, 2012 meeting and reconvened the 
Subcommittee on November 21, 2012 to replicate all activities and actions as Mr. Kinnas 
requested. 
2. Placed signage for meeting of the School Council and its subcommittees in the mail 
office of the middle school. 
3. Ensured that both the school secretary and head custodian are included in all future 
meeting notifications to ensure that signage is placed and the front doors are unlocked 
during all committee and subcommittee meetings. 
We find that the actions taken by the Subcommittee in response to the complaint are 
appropriate and order no further remedial action. We now consider the complaint addressed by 
this determination to be resolved. Please feel free to contact our office (617) 963-2540 if you 
have any questions or believe any facts in this letter to be inaccurate.
