RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Chair Bolduc: 
This office received two complaints from David Kokindo (the complainant) dated July 7, 
2014 alleging that the Hinsdale Police Chief Search Committee (the Committee) violated the 
Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaints specifically allege that the 
Committee posted the incorrect time of its April 16, 2014 and April 18, 2014 meetings on its 
meeting notices and agendas. The complaints were originally filed with the Committee on April 
23. 2014. You responded to the complaints, on behalf of the Committee, by letter dated May 12, 
2014.1 
Following our review, we have decided to resolve these complaints by informal action 
in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the notices sufficiently alerted the public 
of the Committee's 6:00 PM meetings. In reaching a determination, we reviewed the April 23, 
2014 complaints filed with the Committee; the chair's May 12, 2014 response; and the notices 
and minutes from the Committee's April 16, 2014 and April 18, 2014 meetings. 
The notice for the Committee's April 16, 2014 meeting stated, "The Police Chief Search 
Committee will be meeting on Wednesday, April 16, 2014, at 6:00 PM, at the Fire Station 
meeting room." Similarly, the notice for the Committee's April 18, 2014 meeting stated "The 
Police Chief Search Committee will be meeting on Friday, April 18, 2014, at 6:00 PM, at the 
Fire Station meeting room." On both notices, below this statement, the agenda, so marked, listed 
the topics the Chair reasonably anticipated discussing at each meeting. The first item of each 
agenda read "1. 7:00 PM, open the meeting, turn off all phone[s]." The complainant alleges that 
the different start times in the header and in the agenda created confusion in potential attendees. 
For the purpose of clarity in this letter, we will refer to you in third person hereafter. 
===PAGE 2===
The chair asserts that the 7:00 P.M. time for opening the meetings was a typographical error. 
Both meetings actually began at 6:00 P.M. 
"Meeting notices . . . shall contain the time, date, and place of [a] meeting." G.L. c. 30A, 
§ 20(b). Although the inclusion of an agenda item on the two notices indicating a different 
opening time was confusing to anyone viewing those documents, we have no reason to believe 
the time inconsistency was anything more than a clerical error. Because the correct date and 
time were included on the meeting notice, we do not find a violation of the Open Meeting Law. 
See OML 2013-46.2 
We note that, as of June 4, 2014, this Committee has been disbanded. We encourage the 
Committee, should it ever be reconstituted, to be more attentive to prevent errors that may cause 
confusion as to meeting times. 
We now consider this matter closed. This determination does not address any other 
complaints that may be pending before this office or the Committee. Please feel free to contract 
our office at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions regarding this letter. 
cc: David Kokindo
