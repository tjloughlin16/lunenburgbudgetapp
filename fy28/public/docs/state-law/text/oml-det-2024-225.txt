 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Gibbons: 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on August 9, 2024, alleging that 
the Clinton Select Board (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.1  
The complaint was originally filed with the Board on July 12, and the Board’s Chair, Julie 
Perusse, responded on behalf of the Board by letter dated August 9, following an extension of 
time granted by our office.  The complaint alleges that the Board did not list on the notice for its 
July 10 meeting the names of candidates to be considered for reappointment. 
Following review, we resolve this complaint by informal action, in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged.2  We have 
consistently explained that for a meeting notice to be sufficiently specific, the names of 
individuals who are being considered for employment or appointment must be included on the 
meeting notice if known 48 hours in advance of the meeting.  See, e.g., OML 2022-217; OML 
2020-80; OML 2016-167; OML 2011-23.3  The Board acknowledges, and we find, that the 
Board failed to include a list of candidates to be considered for appointment, whose identifies 
were known more than 48 hours in advance of the meeting, in violation of the Open Meeting 
Law.

The Board explained that it now understands the requirement, as demonstrated by the 
notice for its August 7 meeting which included as part of the notice document a list of each 
public body for which an appointment would be made, along with an indication of the number of 
corresponding appointments for each and the names of the candidates for consideration.4  We 
commend the Board for acknowledging the violation when the complainant brought the Open 
Meeting Law requirement to the Board’s attention and by bringing its practices into compliance 
with the Law.   
We order the Board’s immediate and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law.  We 
now consider the complaint addressed by this determination to be resolved.  This determination 
does not address any other complaints that may be pending with our office or the Board.  Please 
feel free to contact the Division at (617) 963-2540 if you have any questions.
