This office received a complaint from Owen Smith on January 20, 2023, alleging that the 
Newburyport Traffic Safety Advisory Committee (the “Committee”) violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.  The complaint was originally filed with the Committee on or about 
December 1, 2022, and you
1 responded, on behalf of the Committee, by letter dated December 
20, 2022.  The complaint alleges that the Committee failed to timely respond to a request for 
meeting minutes.   
 
We resolve this matte
 r by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), 
after reviewing the original complaint, the Committee’s response to the complaint, the complaint 
filed with our office requesting further review, and the notice and minutes of the Committee 
meeting held on December 8, 2022, as well as a video recording of that meeting.
2  We find that 
the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law in the way alleged.

minutes of all meetings, including executive sessions.”  G.L. c. 30A, § 22(a).  The minutes of an

On November 9, 2022, the complainant sent an email to Committee Co-Chairs Andrew 
Levine and Jamie Tuccolo requesting copies of minutes of Committee meetings held in August, 
September, and October.  Mr. Levine responded 45 minutes later stating that he would “look into 
why [the minutes] haven’t been posted and should be able to have that corrected in the next few 
days.”  The complainant received no further response from the Committee, and thereafter filed 
the Open Meeting Law complaint at issue here regarding the Committee’s failure to provide 
meeting minutes.  During a meeting held on December 8, 2022, the Committee reviewed the 
Open Meeting Law complaint, approved the minutes of meetings held in August, September and 
October, and subsequently posted them to the municipal website.
4  While we acknowledge that 
the Committee initially responded to the complainant’s request within 10 days, Mr. Levine’s 
response was ambiguous.  An unequivocal explanation of whether the minutes did or did not 
exist in either approved or draft form should have been provided to the complainant within 10 
days of his request.  See OML 2018-98; OML 2017-50; OML 2016-71; OML 2015-173.  
Because the Committee failed to provide such a response, we find that it violated the Open 
Meeting Law by not properly responding to a request for minutes.  We order no further remedial 
action as the Committee has since posted all requested minutes to its website.   
 
With respect to the complainant’s concern that the Committee does not post minutes to 
the mu
nicipal website, the Open Law Meeting does not require that public bodies post their 
minutes to a website or elsewhere, but only that minutes be created, maintained, and approved in 
a timely manner, and made available within 10 days of request. G.L. c. 30A, §§22(a), (c); OML 
2020-78; OML 2017-98.  We remind the Committee that the Open Meeting Law requires that a 
public body “create and maintain accurate minutes of all meetings, including executive sessions, 
setting forth the date, time and place, the members present or absent, a summary of the 
discussions on each subject, a list of documents and other exhibits used at the meeting, the 
decisions made and the actions taken at each meeting, including the record of all votes.”  G.L. c. 
30A, § 22(a).  The meeting minutes must be reviewed and approved in a timely manner.  G.L. c. 
30A, § 22(c).  “Timely manner” means “within the next three public body meetings or within 30 
days, whichever is later, unless the public body can show good cause for further delay.”  See 940 
CMR 29.11; OML 2018-48.  Whenever possible, we recommend that minutes of a meeting be 
approved at the next meeting.  See OML 2014-15; OML 2012-91.  
 
For the reasons stated above, we find that the Committee violated the Open Meeting Law 
by failing to timely respond to a request for meeting minutes.  We order immediate and future 
compliance with the law’s requirements, and we caution that similar future violations could be 
considered evidence of intent to violate the law.
