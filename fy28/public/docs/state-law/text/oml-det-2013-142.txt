RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Frank: 
This office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins, dated July 28, 2013, 
alleging that the Swansea Recreation Commission (the "Commission") violated the Open 
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the 
Commission on or about April 30, 2013, and you responded on behalf of the Commission 
to the original complaint by letter dated July 12, 2013. In his complaint, Mr. Higgins 
alleges that the Commission's April 25, 2013 meeting notice failed to conspicuously 
record the date and time that the notice was posted.1 
Following our review, we have decided to resolve this complaint by informal 
action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the Commission violated 
the Open Meeting Law by failing to conspicuously record thereon or therewith the date and 
time that its April 25, 2013 meeting notice was posted. 940 CMR 29.03(l)(b); OML 2013-
9; OML 2011-45. Flowever, we are satisfied that the Commission and the Town Clerk are 
now aware of their obligations in this regard and have committed to compliance with this 
requirement going forward. 
Additionally, while it was not raised in the original complaint, we find that the 
Commission violated the Open Meeting Law for failing to respond to the complaint within 
fourteen (14) business days. The Commission did not respond until almost two and a half 
months after the complaint was filed. We take this opportunity to remind the Commission 
that unless our office grants an extension, a public body must respond within 14 business 
' Mr. Higgins further alleges that Town Clerk Susan Taveria also violated the Open Meeting Law. Ms. 
Taveria is an individual public employee who is not a member of a public body, thus she was not required to 
comply with the Open Meeting Law and could not have violated it. We therefore decline to review the 
allegations against her and only consider her actions to the extent they relate to the complaint against the 
Commission.
