RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Turner: 
This office received a complaint dated November 14, 2013 filed by William Weigle (the 
complainant) alleging that the Egremont Board of Selectmen (the Board) violated the Open 
Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint specifically alleges that the Board failed to 
provide reasonable accommodation to the public to attend its August 12, 2013 meeting. The 
complaint also alleges that Board members failed to speak up so that all persons present on that 
date could hear the proceedings. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on or about 
September 9, 2013. The Board responded by letter dated September 23, 2013. 
After reviewing the complaint and the Board's response we have decided to resolve this 
complaint by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the 
Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law, for the same reasons discussed in a prior decision 
resolving a complaint against the Board, OML 2014-3.1 The facts and allegations in this 
complaint substantially mirror those raised in OML 2014-3, but concern a subsequent meeting. 
In response to the prior complaints, the Board stated its intent to move future meetings to a larger 
space when a municipal construction project is completed. We credit the Board's assurances, and 
note that the present complaint concerns a meeting that occurred prior to the date on which they were 
made.
