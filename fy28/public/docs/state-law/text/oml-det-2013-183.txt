RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Reed; 
This office received a complaint from Steve Dondley on July 18, 2013, alleging that the 
Westfield City Council (the "Council") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 3OA, §§ 18-25. 
Specifically, the complaint alleges that a Councilor sent an email to a quorum of the Council 
expressing his opinion on the Mayor's proposed budget and the Council's proposed budget. The 
complaint was filed with the Council on or about June 18, 2013. The Board responded by letter 
dated July 15, 2012, following an extension of time granted by this office. 
Following our review, we have decided to resolve this complaint by informal action, in 
accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that a member of the Council violated the Open 
Meeting Law by sending a June 17, 2013 email to a quorum of the Council regarding public 
business within the Council's jurisdiction; however the Council took appropriate remedial action 
in response to this complaint. See G.L. c. 30A, § 18; OML 2013-29; OML 2012-67.1 We 
understand that during the Council's September 5, 2013 meeting, the Council voted to accept a 
recommendation from the Assistant City Solicitor acknowledging a violation of the Open 
Meeting Law and adopting procedures regarding email communications to ensure future 
compliance. We find this was appropriate remedial action. We additionally order the Council to 
publicly disclose the June 17, 2013 email, if it has not done so already.
