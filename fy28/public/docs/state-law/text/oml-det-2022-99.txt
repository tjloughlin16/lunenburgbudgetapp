Dear Mr. Parker: 
 
This determination addresses a complaint from Steven Fitzgerald alleging that the Everett 
School Committee (the “Committee”) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.1  
The complaint is dated September 16, 2021, and the Committee responded by email on October 
5, 2021.2  The complaint alleges that the Committee held a “hybrid” meeting on August 30 and 
failed to take votes by roll call.3   
 
We resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), 
after reviewing the original complaint, the Committee’s response to the complaint, and the 
complaint filed with our office requesting further review.  We also reviewed the minutes of the 
August 30 meeting as well as a video of the August 30 meeting.4  We find that the Committee 
did not violate the Open Meeting Law.   
 
The Attorney General's Open Meeting Law regulations require that “all votes taken 
during any meeting in which a member participates remotely must be by roll call vote.”  940 
CMR 29.10(7)(c); see also OML 2020-82; OML 2015-131; OML 2013-195.  The Committee 
represents, and the video of the August 30 meeting confirms, that no Committee member 
participated in the August 30 meeting remotely.  Although the Committee allowed other
