 RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
 
Dear Mr. Sutherland: 
This Office received a complaint from Patrick Higgins on August 5, 2019, alleging that 
the Swansea Water District Board of Commissioners (the “Board”) violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint was originally filed with the Board on July 5, and the 
Board responded by letter dated July 24.1  In his complaint, Mr. Higgins alleges that the Board 
improperly met in executive session during its meeting on June 4, despite the notice for that 
meeting not including an executive session 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law as alleged.  The 
topic that the Board discussed in executive session on June 4 – “District Employees: Fiscal Year 
2020 Compensation and Benefit Package” – was included on the meeting notice, but as an open 
session topic.  A public body may discuss a topic not listed on a meeting notice if the topic was 
not anticipated by the chair 48 hours in advance of the meeting.  See OML 2019-167; OML 
2017-51.  Here, however, the topic was not unanticipated; in fact, it was included on the meeting 
notice, albeit for open session.  The meeting notice should have specifically identified that the
