RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Ms. Baily: 
This office received a complaint from Ronald Beaty on November 27, 2017, alleging 
that the Cannabis Control Commission (the "Commission") violated the Open Meeting Law, 
G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.1 The complaint was originally filed with the Commission on or about 
October 19, and Commissioner Britte McBride responded to the complaint, on behalf of the 
Commission, by letter dated January 11, 2018.2 In his complaint, Mr. Beaty alleges that the 
notice for the Commission's October 17 meeting failed to conspicuously record the date and 
time that the notice had been posted. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance 
with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Commission violated the Open Meeting Law. 
In its response, the Commission conceded that its October 17 meeting notice failed to 
record the date and time that it had been posted. The Attorney General's Open Meeting Law 
regulations require that the date and time that a meeting notice is posted be conspicuously 
recorded on or with the notice. See 940 CMR 29.03(l)(d); OML 2015-43.3 Because its 
October 17 meeting notice failed to include this detail, we find that the Commission violated 
the Open Meeting Law. We acknowledge that the Commission is a newly created public
