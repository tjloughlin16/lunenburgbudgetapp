RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Sarkisian: 
This office received a complaint from George Harris, dated September 3, alleging that the 
Wayland Planning Board (the "Board") violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.1 
Specifically, the complaint alleges that the Board failed to timely approve meeting minutes for 
four meetings.2 The complaint was originally filed with the Board on July 22. The Board 
responded by letter dated September 9. 
Following our review, we resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a), and find that the Board violated the Open Meeting Law. In response to 
the complaint, the Board took appropriate remedial action by reviewing and approving minutes 
for the meetings identified in Mr. Hams' complaint. 
Minutes of all open session meetings must be approved in a timely manner. G.L. c. BOA, 
§ 22(c). The Open Meeting Law does not provide a definition of "timely manner," but we have 
recommended that minutes be approved at the next meeting whenever possible. See OML 2015-
43; OML 2013- 173; OML 2013-37.4 In response to the complaint, the Board approved the 
outstanding minutes during its July 28 meeting. This was two to four months after the meetings 
at issue occurred. The Board meets at least twice a month, thus we find that it was not timely for
