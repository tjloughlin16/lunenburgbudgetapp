Our office received a complaint from Barbara Trainor-Tessier dated May 14, 2011,
alleging a violation ofthe Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25 by the Cotuit Board of Fire
Commissioners (the "Board"). The complaint alleges that the Board's meeting notices and
agenda contained a disclaimer stating "persons interested are advised that in the event any matter
taken up at the meeting remains unnished at the close of the meeting, it may be put off to a
continued session of this meeting, without fuher notice." The complaint was originally
submitted to the Board on April 19, 201 i. We also received the Board's response on May 12,
2011.
The Open Meeting Law requires that public bodies "post notice of every meeting at least
. 48 hours prior to such meeting, excluding Satudays, Sundays and legal holidays." Each notice
"shall contain the date, time and place of such meeting and a listing of topics that the chair
reasonably anticipate wil be discussed at the meeting." G.L. c. 30A, § 20(b). If a public body
chooses to continue the discussion of a topic to a future date, the public body must post the topic
on a meeting notice at least 48 hours in advance of the next meeting.!
