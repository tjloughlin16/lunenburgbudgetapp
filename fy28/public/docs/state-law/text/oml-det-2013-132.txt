RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Attorney Thody: 
This office received an Open Meeting Law complaint from Doreen Reilly, dated 
February 12, 2013, alleging that the Kingston Green Energy Committee (the "Committee") 
violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25. The complaint specifically alleges that 
the Committee failed to provide meeting minutes after Ms. Reilly requested copies of or access 
to all minutes of all Committee meetings ever held. The complaint was originally filed with the 
Committee on or about January 31, 2012. The Committee responded by letter, through counsel, 
dated February 4, 2013. 
After reviewing the complaint and the Board's response, we find that the Committee 
violated the Open Meeting Law by failing to make open session meeting minutes available 
within 10 days of a request. See G.L. c. 3OA, § 22(c); OML 2013-18; OML 2012-18.' The 
complainant requested the meeting minutes on January 3, 2013, and the Committee did not 
provide them until March 7, 2013. The Committee responded appropriately to the complaint by 
providing the requested minutes shortly after it was filed. We order the Committee's immediate 
and future compliance with the Open Meeting Law, and caution that a future similar violation 
may be considered evidence of an intentional violation. 
' Open Meeting Law determinations may be found at the Attorney General's website,
