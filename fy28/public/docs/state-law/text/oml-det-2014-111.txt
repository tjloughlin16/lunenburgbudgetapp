RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Mr. Checklick: 
This office received a complaint from Eric Bibler, dated July 30, alleging that the Board 
of Directors of the Cape & Vineyard Electric Cooperative ("CVEC") violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.1 Specifically, the complaint alleges that CVEC failed to include in 
its meeting minutes a list of the documents and other exhibits used at its March 27 meeting. The 
complaint was filed with CVEC on April 15. CVEC responded by letter dated June 23, 
following an extension of time granted by our office. 
We have decided to resolve this complaint by informal action in accordance with 940 
CMR 29.07(2)(a). Following our review, we find that CVEC failed to include in the minutes for 
its March 27 meeting a list of the documents and exhibits that CVEC used during the meeting as 
required by G.L c. 30A, § 22(a); OML 2013-144; OML 2011-44.2 In response to the complaint, 
CVEC amended its minutes to include that list.3 We find this to be an appropriate response to 
the complaint, and order no further remedial action. We are encouraged by CVEC's adoption of 
a minute-taking policy and commitment to use the Attorney General's minutes checklist when 
creating and approving minutes.4
