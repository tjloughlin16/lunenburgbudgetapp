Re: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
This office received a complaint from Don Warner Saklad dated June 25, alleging that 
the Cambridge Public Library Board (the Board) violated the Open Meeting Law, G.L. c. 30A, 
§§ 18-25.1 The complainant specifically alleges that minutes for the Board's March 13 and April 
9 meetings were not made available to him upon request.2 The complaint was originally filed 
with the Board on April 24, and the Board responded on June 19, after our office granted the 
Board's request for an extension pursuant to 940 CMR 29.05(5)(b). 
Following our review, we have decided to resolve this complaint by informal action, in 
accordance with 940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting 
Law by responding as it did to the complainant's request for meeting minutes. 
A public body must create and maintain accurate minutes of all meetings. G.L. c. 30A, 
§ 22(a). The minutes of an open session, if they exist, must be made available upon request by 
any person within 10 days. G.L. c. 30A, § 22(c). The complainant submitted his request for 
meeting minutes in the early morning hours on April 10, just hours after the Board's April 9
