RE: Open Meeting Law Complaint 
Dear Ms. Dearborn; 
This office received a complaint from Dr. Michael Roberts on May 24, 2017 alleging 
that the Andover Spring Grove Cemetery Trustees (the "Trustees") violated the Open Meeting 
Law, G.L. c. 30A, §§ 18-25.1 The complaint was originally filed with the Trustees on or 
about April 13, 2017 and you responded, on behalf of the Trustees, in an undated letter, which 
the complainant provided to us.2 In his complaint. Dr. Roberts alleges that the Trustees failed 
to post notice of its meetings held between January and November 2016. 
Following our review, we resolve this matter by informal action, in accordance with 
940 CMR 29.07(2)(a). We find that the Board did not violate the Open Meeting Law. In 
reaching this determination, we reviewed the complaint filed with the Board, the Board's 
response, and the complaint filed with our office. We also spoke with the town clerk's office 
by telephone on June 7, 2017. 
The complaint alleges that the Trustees failed to post notice of its meetings held 
between January and November 2016. Since 2011, the Town website has been the official 
notice posting location for the Town. On December 15, 2016, the Town migrated to a new 
computer system. Notices of the Trustees' meetings held in prior years were not migrated 
over to the new system. Accordingly, the website now indicates that there are no agendas for 
meetings held prior to 2017. However, according to the Trustees and the town clerk's office, 
the notices for those prior meetings were posted to the website during the relevant time. 
While we agree with the complainant that the new website creates uncertainty about whether
