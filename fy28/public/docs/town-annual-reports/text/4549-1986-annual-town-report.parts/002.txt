No School Signals - 1986-87
The decision to close schools is made by the Superintendent of Schools after consulting with
the Highway Superintendent and the Police Department to determine the condition of the roads.
The practice is to close schools only in case of extreme weather conditions or unforeseen emer-
gency; therefore, when schools are in session on stormy days, parents are urged to exercise
their personal judgment as to the wisdom of sending their children to school.

educational experience.

some observations and perceptions of a number of
positive aspects of the Lunenburg Schools based on

5B INVOLVED IN THE STOCK MARKET
The students in Mr. Romano's math class were heavily involved in The Boston Globe Stock Market
Game this year. The game is part of The Globe's Newspaper in the Classroom Program. The game is
participated in the junior high/middle school division.
Mr. Romano's class was divided into teams of five students. Each team started with $100,000.00
(on paper) and bought and sold short sold, and short covered any common stocks listed on the New York
and American Stock Exchanges. The winning team had the highest valued portfolio at the end of the ten-
week trading period.
Before actually buying or selling stocks (through The Globe's computers), the students learned
about the origin and nature of the Stock Market. The young capitalists also learned about different ways
