OR RECORDS WHICH WERE FORWARDED TO THIS TOWN FROM OTHER MUNICIPALITIES IN THE STATE. LUNENBURG
RESIDENTS ARE URGED TO NOTIFY THE TOWN CLERK IF IN-STATE RECORDS HAVE NOT BEEN RECEIVED AND
RECORDED. IF A BIRTH, MARRIAGE, OR DEATH OCCURRED OUTSIDE THE STATE, THE SAME MAY BE RECORDED IN
THE LUNENBURG TOWN CLERK'S OFFICE IF CERTIFIED RECORDS ARE PRESENTED. IN SOME CASES THE TOWN
CLERK'S RECORDS ARE SUPPLEMENTED BY RECORDS OF BURIALS'WHICH APPEAR ELSEWHERE IN THIS REPORT. IN

EXCERPTS FROM ANNUAL AND SPECIAL TOWN MEETING MAY 10, 1997
The Meeting was called to order at 9:00 A.M. by Moderator Richard J. Bishop, a quorum having been
declared present.
The Town Clerk read the opening and closing of the Town Meeting Warrant.
The Moderator requested that a moment of silence be observed for those who had died in the past year. The
Meeting pledged allegiance to the United States Flag.
Selectman Michael Sauvageau read proclamations for Chester J. Pliska and Thomas W. Lawson.
David W. Rodgers, 82 Highland Street was recognized and asked that the Town recognize the years of
service of the following persons who have retired from employment with the Town: Walter Schuster 10 years
Highway Department; Philip Crouse 34 years, Ronald Lamothe 29 years, Frank Massak 24 years School
Department; and Nancy Bigelow 23 years Ritter Library.
Helen Brockelman, 56 Cross Street, was recognized and gave a brief history of Lunenburg High School in
observance of its 100th commencement this year.
Katherine Lowe, 99 Fairview Road, was recognized and moved that this meeting NOT require that a count be
taken where a 2/3rds vote is required. The motion was seconded and passed unanimously.
The Annual Town Meeting was adjourned at 9:25 A.M. in order to take up the Special Town Meeting Warrant.
