- RETIREMENTS DURING 1979 -

ANITA E. FOREST - Anita served as a cafeteria substitute worker from May 1964 until November 1964
when she was hired as a permanent general worker to replace someone who was out ill. In
September 1965, she became a member of the high school cafeteria staff. when the Middle
School was opened and the Elementary School cafeteria reactivated, she trans ferred in March
of 1969 to the Elementary School to serve as its Baker. She was appointed as the Cook/Manager
at the Middle School in January 1974 and remained in this position until she resigned on
July 13, 1979 with retirement effective November 8, 1979. Our congratulations go to Anita for
the dedicated service she displayed over her fourteen years as a member of our food service
department. We wish her many happy and healthy years of retirement.
DOROTHY A. HILLMAN - Dorothy began as a part-time teacher aide in the learning disabilities pro-
gram at the Elementary School on January 4, 1971, During her time with us she assumed the
responsibility for teaching all kindergarten physical education classes as well as working
with individuals and small groups as a motor training aide. Her knowledge in this area plus
her outstanding way with children produced an excellent program. She gave eight years of
dedicated and conscientious service to Lunenburg students who were privileged to be in her
program. Dorothy retired as of June 18, 1979.
ISABELLE L. KARL - In September 1966, Isabelle was appointed as a grade 4 teacher in the Elemen-
tary Annex. The following year she trans ferred to teach a grade 3 class. After 13 years of
dedicated service to her students, Isabelle submitted her intention to retire as of June 18,
1979. May she enjoy her retirement years to the utmost.
ALMA M. KILPATRICK - On January 1, 1963, Alma began as a teacher of grade 2 in the Elementary
School to replace a teacher who had resigned. She remained on the staff until June 1964 when
she resigned for family reasons. However, she returned to teaching again in September 1965
until September 1976 when she requested a leave-of-absence. In September 1977 she returned
as a 2/7ths reading teacher at the Middle School. Her service spanned fifteen years and was
one of distinction and dedication. On February 1, 1980, she retired to move to the coast of
Maine. Our best wishes go to Alma for many enjoyable and healthy years of retirement.
BARBARA H. PATCH + Barbara began in the Elementary School as a teacher of grade 5 in September
1962. In all, Barbara contributed 17 years of outstanding service to our schools. Effective
June 18, 1979, she retired so that she and her husband could do some traveling. Our sincere
thanks to Barbara and our best wishes for the years ahead.

GEORGE W. SHOSEY, JR. - George served as a custodian in the Middle School from April 7, 1975 until
January 8, 1980 when he retired. He performed his duties cooperatively and with dependability.

ANNE M. STYRNA - Anne served as a part-time Senior Clerk/Typist in the Middle School from May 17,
1971 until June 29, 1979. A warm, friendly person and enthusiasm for life, Anne will be
missed by all who had the pleasure of knowing and working with her over her eight years of
service. May she enjoy many happy and relaxing years ahead. Don't ever lose that smile,
Anne!
WILLIAM B. WELTON - On June 28, 1971, Bill came to work as a custodian in the High School. He was
appointed as Groundsman for the Lunenburg Public Schools on April 17, 1972 and served in this
capacity until his retirement on June 8, 1979. Over the eight years Bill was with us he
always greeted everyone with a smile, a pleasant word and had boundless energy for the tasks
assigned. All of us at the Brooks House will miss Bill who looked after our building as well.
The children at the Elementary and Primary Schools will also miss "Santa" Bill as well. We
wish him the very best in the years ahead.
