Shepherd ahead in Lunenburg but the district tally had Ms.Scarsdale ahead by 17 votes – this triggered a 
recount.  On December 10, 2022 Lunenburg conducted the recount in the Passios Building Cafeteria.  
Townsend Town Clerk, Kathleen Spofford and Pepperell Town Clerk, Brynn Durno, who had already 
completed their own recount for this election showed up and said we’re helping! They came in and took over 
the preparation of the precinct blocks. Their expertise and that of Town Counsel, Adam Costa made the entire 
process much less stressful for all.  The Registrars and I would like to thank all those who volunteered their 
time on a Saturday to get this job done. The total difference between the two candidates ended up being seven 
votes district wide.
