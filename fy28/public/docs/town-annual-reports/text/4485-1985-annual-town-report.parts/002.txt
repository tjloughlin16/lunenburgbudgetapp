Annual Report of Superintendent of Schools
It is a privilege to submit my eighth annual
report as Superintendent of Schools in Lunen-
burg. This annual report covers the year end-
ing December 31, 1985.
As you read the reports of the other admin-
istrators, you will receive a more detailed
accounting of the events that have taken place
in the school at each level. The purpose of
this report is to summarize the total aspect of
the Lunenburg Public Schools.
Lunenburg's Pride in its School System.
All of us are aware of what it takes to pro-
vide a community with a quality school system.
For eight years, my entire tenure as Superinten-
dent, I have written, spoken on and dialogued
about the quality of this school system.
At this time, I want to step back and thank
this community for demonstrating its faith and
support for its schools. All of you as citizens
have contributed to its success. Since the pas-
sage of Proposition 21, Lunenburg has supported
its schools with increase of 8.2% in FY-83; 4.3% in FY-84; 10.4% in FY-85 and 8.5% increase in FY-
86. What these increases have provided for you is a school system which has been able over
those years to recoup many of the losses it suffered. As citizens, we have benefited by increased
value to our homes, some of which is attributed to providing the seller with an excellent school
system for his children.
Pride is evidenced in many ways. If one has pride in their appearance, they will work very
hard to look good and be neat. Being neat and well appearing requires work, effort and time.
It does not just happen because one wishes it. If a team in sports has pride, it will work with
every fiber it possesses to appear disciplined, well ordered and in control. That pride must be
contributed to by every member of the team. Consequently, every member of the team
contributes, every member gains in stature and worth.
We find the same effects happening when an entire community shows pride in its school
system. Aside from the initial self-satisfaction of helping young people grow and develop into
well-educated and law-abiding citizens, there is the feeling that as an individual, you contributed
to a community effort. If the entire community seeks better schools, better roads, better recre-
ational facilities, better town management, etc., we all gain by everyone contributing to the
process.
The increases that this town has provided its schools has resulted in allowing us to properly
staff our classrooms, maintain our facilities and provide the resources to implement an excellent
curriculum. We have been able to provide our teaching staff with competitive salaries and proper
supplies. Our custodial staff has had the resources to provide a clean, safe and inviting envi-
ronment in which to work and learn in. Everyone who works in this school system feels the
pride that this community has in its schools.
