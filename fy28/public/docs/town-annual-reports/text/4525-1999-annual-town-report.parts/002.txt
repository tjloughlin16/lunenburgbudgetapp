FOLLOWING ARE THOSE RECORDS OF BIRTHS, MARRIAGES, AND DEATHS THAT EITHER OCCURRED IN OUR TOWN OR RECORDS WHICH WERE
FORWARDED TO THIS TOWN FROM OTHER MUNICIPALITIES IN THE STATE LUNENBURG RESIDENTS ARE URGED TO NOTIFY THE TOWN CLERK IF
IN-STATE RECORDS HAVE NOT BEEN RECEIVED AND RECORDED. IF A BIRTH, MARRIAGE, OR DEATH OCCURRED OUTSIDE THE STATE, THE SAME
MAY BE RECORDED IN THE LUNENBURG TOWN CLERKS OFFICE IF CERTIFIED RECORDS ARE PRESENTED. IN SOME CASES THE TOWN CLERK'S
RECORDS ARE SUPPLEMENTED BY RECORDS OF BURIALS WHICH APPEAR ELSEWHERE IN THIS REPORT. IN ORDER FOR THESE RECORDS TO
ALSO BE INCLUDED IN THE OFFICIAL VITAL STATISTICS RECORDS, THE FAMILY OF THE DECEASED SHOULD PRESENT A CERTIFIED COPY OF THE
DEATH CERTIFICATE TO THE TOWN CLERK. IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE GENERAL LAWS, MARRIAGES WHICH OCCUR HERE BUT RELATE TO
LICENSES WHICH WERE OBTAINED ELSEWHERE IN THE STATE ARE NO LONGER INCLUDED IN OUR OFFICIAL TOWN RECORDS.
