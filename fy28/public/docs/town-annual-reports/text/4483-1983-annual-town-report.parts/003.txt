STUDENT SUPPORT GROUP:
In the fall of 1983, students of Lunenburg High
School organized a local chapter of the state-wide
group known as Students Against Driving Drunk (SADD).
This organization seeks the cooperative efforts of
students to prevent drunk driving. One hundred and
fifty students in the school signed the statement
of agreement to provide alternatives in real situa-
tions where the possibility exists of students
driving under the influence of alcohol. A total of
fifty students are actively engaged in the leader-
ship activities of the organization.

PARENT-COMMUNITY SUPPORT GROUP:
Also in the fall of 1983, under the leadership of
health teacher Barbara Jezak, and school nurse Delores
Brown, a large group of parents and members of the
community founded the Lunenburg Community Action Net-
work (L-CAN). This organization has as its objective
the prevention of drug and alcohol abuse in the
community, which includes the high school. More than
four hundred members of the Lunenburg community con-
vened for the initial meeting on this effort in the
fall. After being guided in its initial deliberations
by a distinguished panel of Lunenburg citizens, and
under the very able chairmanship of Mr. Fritz Foerster,
the organization has continued meetings and dis-
cussions aimed at designing action programs, to pro-
vide alternatives in prevention of alcohol and drug
abuse in the community.
ADMINISTRATIVE REORGANIZATION AT THE HIGH SCHOOL:
Over the last two years an administrative reorgan-
ization has been put into effect at the high school.
The objective of the reorganization has been to pro-
vide better support services for teaching and learn-
ing, which is the main business of the school.
Within the faculty, the decline in enrollment and
the budgetary restrictions of Proposition 21 required
the elimination of department heads supervising each
subject. To partly restore the necessary coordination
and support for teaching, Program Coordinators have
been put in place over the several departments com-
prising distinctive teaching areas. Three program
coordinators are now in place, coordinating and facil-

- RETIREMENTS DURING 1983 -
MIRIAM W. CHEESMAN
May 2, 1966 - June 30, 1983
Miriam Cheesman began as school librarian during the year
1965-66. She had served as assistant librarian at the Ritter
Memorial Library (Lunenburg) for a period of five years and had
also worked at public libraries in Arlington, Mass. and Fort
Wayne, Indiana. She received her B. A. degree from Swarthmore
College in Pennsylvania and her B. S. in Library Science from
Simmons School of Library Science in Boston. She also served
with the United States Navy in Communications from 1943 - 1950
and at the time of her discharge on October 26, had a commission
of Lieutenant Junior Grade.
In her last year of service with the Lunenburg Public
Schools, she served as the single librarian in the school system.
The quality of her work with students and staff during her tenure
were certainly recognized by everybody. We wish her retirement
years to be filled with memories of her past experiences and the
Miriam W. Cheesman
excitement of new adventures in the future.
ROBERT C. COLEMAN
May 10, 1976 - June 27, 1983
Bob Coleman began his employment with us under the CETA program when he was hired for the position of
utility man in the Lunenburg Public Schools by the Board of Selectmen. On May 9, 1977, when his employment
under the CETA program terminated, the school department hired him as a custodian at the high school. On
April 30, 1979, he transferred to the elementary school as senior custodian until April 27, 1981 when he
returned to the high school as senior custodian. On August 27, 1981, he transferred once again--this time to
the primary school to serve in a dual role as primary school custodian and town hall custodian. It was from
this position on June 27, 1983 that Bob Coleman retired after seven years of service. We wish him many happy
years of retirement.
SHIRLIE W. RUGGLES
May 4, 1966 - March 2, 1984
Shirlie Ruggles was hired as a part-time clerk/typist in the elementary school on May 4, 1966 and the
following September assumed the duties as secretary for the Title III, ESEA program. She held this position
until July 1, 1968 when she was elected as secretary to the elementary principal. This position she held until
her retirement on March 2, 1984 after eighteen years of outstanding service as a member of the secretarial staff
of the Lunenburg Public Schools. (See also the Report of the Elementary School Principal)
We all appreciate Shirlie's efforts on behalf of the students of this school system and wish her only the
best in her retirement years ahead.
HELEN L. WALKER
August 25, 1969 - July 15, 1983
Helen Walker began her employment with the Lunenburg Public Schools
by her appointment as payroll clerk in the Central Office. She assumed
and carried out this responsibility until she applied for the position
of guidance secretary in the high school in 1973. She transferred to
the high school on August 27, 1973. In 1981, due to a reorganization of
the main office at the high school, Helen was appointed as Registrar.
Helen retired on July 15, 1983 after serving the school system with
distinction and outstanding merit for a period of fourteen years. We
all miss her but wish her many happy retirement years.
