ARTICLE 6. To see if the Town will vote to amend Section VI of its Protective By-law by adding thereto procedural and de-
sign requirements for driveways in order to provide moximum protection to the public through orderly control of traffic moving onto
and from a street, to provide uniformity in the design and construction of driveways and to provide adequate drainage where required,
by adding a new sub-section to be known as sub-section D, to read as presented to the Town Meeting in the text of the handout
supplied, or act anything thereon. VOTED THAT THE TOWN AMEND ITS PROTECTIVE BY-LAW BY ADDING AFTER SECTION
VI-C, A NEW SECTION TO BE KNOWN AS SECTION VI-D, AS FOLLOWS:
D. Driveways and Entrances
1. Purpose
a. To provide maximum protection to the public through the orderly control of traffic moving onto and from
a street.
b. To provide a uniform practice in the design and layout of driveways and entrances.
C. To provide for adequate drainage where required.
2. Procedure
Prior to any construction of a driveway, an owner shall make written application for approval to the Building
Inspector acting through the Selectmen. Before approval is granted, the application shall be referred to the
Highway Superintendent and if necessary the Conservation Commission.

3. Design Requirements
a. Entrances shall be located to the best advantage with regard to street alignment, profile, sight distance
and safety conditions as determined by the Highway Superintendent.
b. Entrances to a given tract of land must be made from an accepted or approved Town way or from those
ways in existence prior to October 2, 1951.
C. Driveway grades and locations shall be such to provide for an access for vehicles, including fire and police.
d. Not more than two dwellings may maintain one common entrance and/or driveway.
