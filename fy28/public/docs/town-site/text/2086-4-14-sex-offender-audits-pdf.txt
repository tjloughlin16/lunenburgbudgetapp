I. CONSIDERATIONS:   
 
The Sex Offender Registry Board (SORB) is tasked with classifying and registering convicted sex 
offenders.  Once a sex offender is registered within a municipality it is the responsibility of the 
Police Department to confirm the information that the offender has provided.  In order to 
ensure that the registry information is accurate and up to date the Department will conduct 
audits of registered sex offenders living and working in the Town of Lunenburg.    
  
II. POLICY:   
 
It is the policy of the Lunenburg Police Department to conduct audits of Level 2 and Level 3 
registered sex offenders living and working in the Town of Lunenburg, twice per year.  The 
Administrative Lieutenant, or their designee, will conduct these audits no more than six months 
apart in a calendar year.    
 
III. PROCEDURE:  
 
The audit will consist of reviewing the list of registered sex offenders in the Town of Lunenburg 
through the SORB website.  The Administrative Lieutenant will visit each Lunenburg address 
listed for Level 2 and Level 3 sex offenders.  The audit will confirm the residential and 
employment addresses within the Town.  A record of the audit will be kept on file.  
 
Appropriate action will be taken when a violation(s) is found, which may include criminal 
charges.  The SORB will also be notified of the violation(s).     
 
The Administrative Lieutenant will provide a written report of the audit on an annual basis.
