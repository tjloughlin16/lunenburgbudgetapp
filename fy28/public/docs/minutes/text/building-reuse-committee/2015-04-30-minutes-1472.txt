Building Re-Use Committee
Meeting Minutes 4/6/2015
Members Present: Jamie Toale, Ron Albert, Damon McQuaid, Dave MacDonald, Mike Mackin,
Rebecca Lantry, Mark Erickson
Members Absent: Deb Seeley
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order April 30, 2015 @ 6:35PN
Announcements:
None
Public Comment:
Dave Rodgers expressed his opinion that the Passios building should be torn down and retained
Current Business:
Workshop with School Committee
School Committee has supplied a new plan by the landscape architect showing a proposed
location for expansion parking for the school project.
Dr. Berthiaume asked if a potential developer could determine a boundary line based on their
needs. Ron Albert explained that typically, for an effective RFP process, and clear boundary and
set of parameters is ideal.
It was explained that in order to make changes to the parking plan, approval would be needed
from the MSBA
The option was also presented that the Passios building could be demolished using left over fund
from the school project contingency if there are adequate funds remaining.
Dr. Berthiaume asked how we (the town) could be sure that a commercial project would be
successful when other businesses in the town center have struggled. His concern being that we
would end up with another vacant building in the town center. The Committee response was that
adding competition and diversity to the business community would be beneficial to both new and
existing businesses.
Dave MacDonald suggested that the School Committee make the decision if they want / need the
Passios property. Mike Mackin responded that it has always been the plan that the school
committee would turn over the parcel to the town.
The school Building Committee will check with MSBA about the limit of work line for the school
project and how that relates to this discussion.
It was decided that this conversation will be continued after the results of the town meeting are
known.
