Construction Activity Update
Jonathan Hirst (SDC) presented an overview of a construction progress. JLA
presented progress photos which are also available on the School Building Project
Website.
General:
• Procurement / submittal process is ongoing.
• Snow removal is ongoing.
• Underground plumbing and electrical work is ongoing.
• Steel erection, bolting and details work at Area A is complete.
• Area A sign off for access is executed.
• The following work is underway in Areas B & C:
• Steel erection.
• Bolting and detail work.
• Second floor / roof decking work.
• Moment welds and inspections.
• Steel erection at Area D is will commence beginning of March, 2015.

Change Order Process
Change Order process was reviewed by the Committee. It was decided to follow
up on the process after first change order is issued.
School Storage Garage
Currently the school uses a storage garage which will be demolished during
construction. The school may need at least a two bay storage garage built. This
topic will be reviewed at a later date once it is determined if funds may be
available for such a construction.
Topping Off Ceremony
Topping Off Ceremony was tentatively scheduled for April 27 & 28, time still to be
decided. Two (2) Elevator beams painted white will be available on the
Middle/High School premises where students can access them for signature. Next
scheduled Fly-Over previously scheduled for July, 2015 will be coordinated with
the April Topping Off ceremony.
Other Business
The Committee was made aware about the interest voiced by the students to
install a Time Capsule in the new building. Tappe Architects provided several
ideas to the LSBC for consideration. Since the decision is not schedule driven the
LSBC will take the matter under review.
Nathan Lockwood informed the LSBC he would not be seeking reelection to the
Planning Board and they should seek form the Planning Board another Building
committee representative
