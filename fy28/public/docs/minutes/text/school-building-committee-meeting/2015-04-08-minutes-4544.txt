Finish Board Presentation
Tappe presented proposed interior finish selections. The Committee was in favor
of the presented finishes. Concerns were voiced in regards to the classroom wing
accent color selections (blue & green schemes). Following group review and
deliberations, the Committee agreed that Tappe will make minor modifications to
the colors of concern. The scheme was otherwise approved as presented. The
final scheme summary will be presented at the School Building Committee
Meeting next month.
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve proposed
finishes as presented by Tappe with understanding that minor adjustments will
be made to the accent colors of concern in the classroom wings.
