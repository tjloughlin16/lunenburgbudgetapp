Monthly Invoices and Commitments
Joslin Lesser (JLA-NV5) presented monthly progress invoices for work completed
through August, 2015 for Joslin Lesser - NV5 (OPM), Tappe Architects (Designer),

invoices as presented by Joslin, Lesser (JLA-NV5). The motion was unanimously
