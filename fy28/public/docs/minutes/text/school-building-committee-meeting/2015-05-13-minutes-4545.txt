Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the meeting
minutes from April 8,2015 with revisions made and presented at the meeting.
Monthly Invoices
Joslin Lesser & Associates (JLA) presented monthly progress invoices for work
completed through April, 2015 for Joslin, Lesser & Associates (OPM), Tappe Architects
(Designer), Shawmut Design & Construction (Payment Requisition No.9), UTS Testing
Services (April 2015) & Gaw Photography invoice #3.
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the April 2015
invoices as presented by Joslin, Lesser & Associates. The motion was unanimously
approved.
Monthly Approvals - Change Order #1
Joslin Lesser & Associates (JLA) presented Change Order No.1 for the amount of
$51,378 to the Committee. The Change Order was previously issued for review two
weeks prior to the meeting. This Change Order Package consists of six change requests
which were vetted through by JLA, Tappe and Shawmut prior to presenting to the
Committee.
Despite a concern in regards to the presence of a change order in this project, Tappe,
JLA & Shawmut clarified that change orders are a standard process for a project this
size due to required coordination of details in field. Funds for change orders are
allocated thru construction contingency which is pre-determined during Guaranteed
Maximum Price (GMP) review.
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the Change
Order No.1 as presented by Joslin, Lesser & Associates. The motion was unanimously
approved.
