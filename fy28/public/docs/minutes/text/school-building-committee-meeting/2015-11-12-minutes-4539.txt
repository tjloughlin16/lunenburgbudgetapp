Approval of School Building Committee Minutes
The Lunenburg School Building Committee reviewed minutes from the following
meetings:
• October 14, 2015 LSBC Meeting
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the meeting
minutes from October 14, 2015. The motion was unanimously approved.
Monthly Invoices and Commitments
Joslin Lesser (JLA-NV5) presented monthly progress invoices for work completed
through October, 2015 for Joslin Lesser - NV5 (OPM), Tappe Architects (Designer),
Shawmut Design & Construction (Payment Requisition No.15), UTS Testing Services
(October 2015).
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the October,
2015 invoices as presented by Joslin, Lesser (JLA-NV5). The motion was unanimously
approved.
Monthly Approvals
Shawmut (SDC), Tappe Architects(TA) and Joslin Lesser (JLA-NV5) presented Change
Order #5 Package as well as a log with pending potential change orders.
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve Change Order #5
as presented by Shawmut, Tappe Architects and Joslin Lesser (JLA-NV5). The motion
was unanimously approved.
Construction Activity Update
Inga Knox (JLA-NV5) & Jonathan Hirst (SC) presented a construction update to the
Committee.
Note: Weekly Construction Meetings are on Wednesdays at 10:00am in the Shawmut
trailer. Everyone is welcome to attend and walk the site, Rob Alger is available to set
up time for site visit.
Public Comment
No Public Comment.
