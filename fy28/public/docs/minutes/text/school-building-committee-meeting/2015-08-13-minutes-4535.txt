Approval of School Building Committee Minutes
The Lunenburg School Building Committee reviewed minutes from the following
meetings:
• July 21, 2015 LSBC Meeting
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the meeting
minutes from July 21, 2015. The motion was unanimously approved.
Monthly Invoices and Commitments
Joslin Lesser & Associates (JLA) presented monthly progress invoices for work
completed through July, 2015 for Joslin, Lesser & Associates (OPM), Tappe Architects
(Designer), Shawmut Design & Construction (Payment Requisition No.12), UTS Testing
Services (July 2015), Gaw photography (Areal Photographs).
Motion: The Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to approve the July, 2015
invoices as presented by Joslin, Lesser & Associates. The motion was unanimously
approved.
PFA Amendment
Loxi Calmes (LPS) presented a summary of a PFA Amendment. The PFA Amendment
was voted on and approved by the Town. JLA and LPS worked with MSBA to review the
PFA Amendment prior to Town's approval. The amendment is going through the
process of finalization on the MSBA side. It was also noted that JLA will present a
financial update during on of the Committee meetings this Fall 2015.
Motion: The
Lunenburg School Building Committee voted to authorize for the PFA Amendment to
be signed and processed according to standard MSBA protocol. The motion was
unanimously approved.
Grounds Garage
It was brought up to the Committee's attention that the existing grounds garage will be
demolished and a new storage garage will be built on school premises. This is going to
be a change order that will be presented in detail once pricing is available.
