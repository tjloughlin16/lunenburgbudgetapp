Minutes
Executive Session
May 24, 2010
Time: 8:40 PM
Chair called for Executive Session for purpose of addressing minutes of a previous executive session and not to return to
general session. Roll call vote, Mr. Bodkin Jr., aye; Ms. Bilotta, aye; Mr. Lockwood, aye; Mr. Bakaysa aye; Mr. Saiia, aye.
Discussion: Members read minutes of said meeting prepared by Laura Williams for the Board of Selectmen for the joint meeting
called by the Board of Selectman with Town Counsel. Subject was the O' Brien lawsuit against the Town. Members' consensus
was to accept Select Board minutes as an addendum to the Planning Board minutes. Motion, Mr. Bakaysa, Second, MS. Bilotta,
Motion passed. Board signed the original Planning Board minutes with the addition.
Director informed the Board that Lynne Klaft will not be working for the Planning Department after June 30th. Lynne has the
opportunity to apply for the Assistant Town Clerk position. Marjorie Boggio will be offered the opportunity to increase her work
week to forty hours. These changes have been instituted by the Town Manager as part of the budget process. This information
is being presented in Executive Session due to it being a personnel matter and not yet released for public information.
Chair called for adjournment of the Executive Session. Motion to adjourn, Mr. Bakaysa, Second, Ms. Bilotta, Roll call vote, Mr.
Bodkin, Jr., aye; Ms. Bilotta, aye; Mr. Lockwood, aye; Mr. Bakaysa, aye; Mr. Saiia, aye.
