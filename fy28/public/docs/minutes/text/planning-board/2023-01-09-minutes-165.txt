Minutes 
Public Hearing 
475 Leominster Shirley Road 
January 9, 2023 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes 
Place:  Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA   
Time:  6:26 p.m. 
 
Present:  Matthew Allison, Matthew Brenner, Timothy Willsmer, Adam Burney 
Amanda Reid participated remotely under the OML Simple Extension Legislation.  
Absent:  Erik Torgersen 
 
The Hearing  was to hear and review  a modification to a site plan approval under Chapter 250 of the Code of the Town of 
Lunenburg for site signage at 475 Leominster Shirley Road .  Owner/applicant BP Lunenburg Fee Owner LLC, 100 Grandview 
Road, Suite 203, Braintree, MA. 
 
The owner/applicant was represented by Bill Hannigan, Hannigan Engineering, Inc., 8 Monument Square, Leominster, MA.  Also 
present were John Brennan of BP Lunenburg Fee Owner LLC and Jack Floyd from ViewPoint Sign and Awning.   
 
During the site plan review process the on -site traffic signage was roughly laid out.  Final signage was not laid out relative to 
tenant space, docking, and the mo nument sign for the entrance.  The Board was in possession of plans which showed the 
entrance monument sign, visitor entrance signs, truck deliv ery, docks and exit signs.  The monument sign, located at the  front 
right of the building, was ninety ( 90’) feet from the side property line and twenty-nine and a half (29 ½’) feet  from the front 
property line.    
 
M. Brenner made Motion to close the publi c hearing, T. Willsmer Seconded, Roll Call Vote - A. Reid, aye; T. Willsmer, 
aye; M. Brenner, aye; M. Allison, aye.  Passed unanimously. 
 
Hearing closed 6:36 p.m.
