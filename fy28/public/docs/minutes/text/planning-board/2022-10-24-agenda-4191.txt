PUBLIC HEARING 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on October 24, 2022, 6:05 p.m., Town 
Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to  hear a citizen’s petition to amend the Zoning Map by 
rezoning a portion of the land located at 199 Massachusetts Avenue (Parcel 074 -0051-0000) 
from Residence A to Commercial District so the entire lot is in the Commercial Dist rict.  Materials 
are available for viewing at the Planning Office, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.  
This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, on FacebookLive on the Public 
Access Facebook page ( https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-
157427270975305/) and will be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel 
within 24 hours after the meeting.  To participate remote ly: from a computer, please use the link 
below to join the public hearing webinar:  https://us02web.zoom.us/j/83904066060 or from a
