Public Hearing 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on September 12, 2022, 6:05 
p.m., Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to hear and review an application for site 
plan approval under Chapter 250, Section 8.4. of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg for a 
change of use from restaurant to office space at 3 Lancaster Avenue  (Parcel 071-0001).  
Applicant/owner is One Family Insurance Agency LLC, 1 Main Street, Suite 15, Lunenburg, 
MA 01462.  Materials are available for viewing at the Planning Office, 960 Massachusetts 
Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.  This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, 
on FacebookLive on the Public Access Facebook page 
(https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-157427270975305/) and will 
be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel within 24 hours after the 
meeting.   To participate remotely: from a computer, please use the link below to join 
the public meeting webinar:  https://us02web.zoom.us/j/87960783227 or from a 
telephone, dial the toll free number (888) 475-4499 and enter meeting ID: 87960783227
