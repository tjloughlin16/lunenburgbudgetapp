PUBLIC COMMENT: T. Cole noted a conversation he had with a n individual in the health and human services field regarding 
permitting of child care facilities.  There is a vacant building on the Lunenburg/Fitchburg line, but a child care facility is unable to

ANR PLAN:  425 West Townsend Road & 100 Howard Street -  T. Cole recused himself.  Propose d Parcel A of 240 SF at 100 
Howard Street will be combined with 425 West Townsend Road.  The addition of the 240 SF will create a buildable lot of 30,000 
SF, which is required for installation of a septic system to serve a three bedroom house.  P. Bertram made Motion to approve 
the ANR Plan for 425 West Townsend Road and 100 Howard Street, M. Thompson Seconded, all aye.    
 
PUBLIC HEARING:  1325 Massachusetts Avenue- see separate minutes.    
 
EARTH REMOVAL BYLAW:   There was discussion as to how the new bylaw will be presented at town meeting.  P. Bertram 
inquired if the existing bylaw has an abutter notification requirement.  If not, it should be included.  Dra ft provided to the Board 
this evening has 1500 cubic yards triggering a permit application.  Other changes were made to the bylaw c overing requirements 
if a permit is not required.  Draft Rules and Regulations were also reviewed with particular attention paid to soil testing.   
 
Katey Adams, Select Board .  She is questioning the Planning Board working on these details even though the Select  Board is 
the permitting authority.  She feels there is no purpose to the ongoing planning.  She suggested that the permitting authority for 
earth removal be moved from the Select Board to the Planning Board at town meeting.  A. Burney needs to confer with  Town 
Counsel.   
 
The draft will be supplied to both the Select Board and current permit holders for comments.  A. Burney suggested public 
discussion be scheduled for the first Board meeting in March.  There was discussion on standards for screening.  Initial permits 
will be issued for one year, with renewal possible up to two years.  Public hearings shall be required both for the initial permit and 
permit renewals.   
 
A. Burney will make noted changes to the bylaw in preparation for  public discussion at th e Board’s March 9 th meeting.  Board 
discussion of the rules and regulations will continue at the Board’s February 24 th meeting.
