WORKSHOP: 
Chapter 325, Subdivision Regulations, Procedure for Submission and Review of Plans - All the necessary information was 
contained within the draft, but in a much more readable/understandable format than the current 325 - 4.0.  M. Brenner suggested 
lead-in text be changed to provide clarity.  Section 4.3.3.32- He questioned why the landscaping plan would include the center of 
a proposed cul-de-sac.  A. Burney suggested that cul -de-sacs be not only pavement, but also contain a wooded/landscaped 
area.  Discussion would continue at a future workshop. 
Chapter 250, Zoning, Section 250 -6.1, Off Street Parking and Loading Areas -  Board discussion ensued on Section 250 -6.1 
and the Table handout.  Board members had concerns regarding adequate number of parking spaces fo r private club, restaurant 
and auction house  category.  The current bylaw also d id not account for employee parking or takeout spaces .  For other 
permitted uses not contained in the Table, the draft specified authority for determination of number of spaces  would be changed 
from the Building Commissioner to the Zoning Board of Appeals ; Board mem bers did not agree .  There was discussion of 
combining 6.1 and 6.4.  A. Burney would work on revising the draft.
