The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on August 24 , 2020, 
6:35 PM, via Zoom video conference  to hear and review a request under M.G.L. 
Ch. 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road Designations and under M.G.L. Ch. 87, 
Section 3, Cutting of Public Shade Trees.  Application is to remove six small 
trees, one large tree leaning towards Northfield  Road, and create two or three 
new driveway entrances off Northfield Road at 300 Holman Street West  (Map
