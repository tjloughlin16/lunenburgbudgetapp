Present:  Tanner Cole, Matthew Allison, Paula Bertram, Matthew Brenner, Erik Torgersen, Adam R. Burney

325-3.4. Definitive Plan of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg  for a six-lot subdivision consisting of six duplex buildings at 101
