COMMITTEE REPORTS: 
School Reconfiguration Committee -   Televised meeting to be held  at Town Hall, April 10, 2013 to discuss preferred Options.  
The Committee-preferred three Options have been sent to MSBA (Massachusetts School Building Authority) ; they are 1)  9-12 
new build, 2) 6-12 new build, and 3) 6-12 addition/renovation.  The “educators” prefer a 6-12 new build.   
Capital Planning Committee - Ms. Benson, as Capital Planning Committee Chair, noted some changes made by the Town 
Manager, which have been discussed at Finance Committee meetings.   
 
PLANNING DIRECTOR’S REPORTS/NEW BUSINESS/DEVELOPMENT STATUS REPORTS:  
Agricultural Commission - Board of Selectmen approved development of a commission as a Warrant Article for May 2013 
Town Meeting.  The Right To Farm Bylaw will not be presented at May 2013 Town Meeting.  Commission will be under the Town 
Bylaws and the Right To Farm will be under the Zoning Bylaws.   
MRPC- Board has received MRPC contract approval for economic section of the Master Plan.  Once Board reviews, Contract will 
then go to Board of Selectmen for signature.   
Highfield Village, Northfield Road - Planning Director noted Sewer Commission letter (on file in Planning Office) to EOEA 
(Executive Office of Energy and Environmental Affairs) dated April 5, 2013, which discusses the Draft Environmental Impact 
Report submitted by the Applicant.  Ms. Bilotta-Simeone noted a workshop  will be held with the Board of Selectmen and Sewer 
Commission on April 9, 2013 regarding a new Fitchburg IMA (Intermunicipal Agreement) to secure up to an additional 70,000  
gpd (gallons per day) which is intended to include this project’s needs.  Ms. Bilotta -Simeone made Motion to extend Highfield 
Village’s Planning Board extension to July 1, 2013, pending outcome of workshop noted above and the Fitchburg IMA, Second, 
Mr. Lockwood, Motion passed.   
Aro Estates, Howard Street- Hearing Continuation to be held April 10, 2013, Ritter Memorial Building, 6:30 PM   
New England Farms, Leominster-Shirley Road- Plan being revised per Conservation Commission input.  Once revised, will be 
reviewed with Jesse Johnson, David Ross Associates.   
White Tail Crossing- There are issues that need resolution prior to road acceptance at a town meeting.  Tabled at Ms. Benson’s 
request.
