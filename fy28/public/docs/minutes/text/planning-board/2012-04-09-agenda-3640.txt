PUBLIC HEARING 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on  April 9, 201 2, 7:15 PM, 
Joseph Bilotta Meeting Room, Town Hall, 17 Main Street , Lunenburg, MA  0 1462, to  
amend the Zoning Byl aw by deleting from the current Section 6.6., Subsection 6.6.7. 
entitled Solar Energy Systems and add to the current Section 4.0. Use Regulations a new 
Section entitled Section 4.15. Solar Energy Systems.  Copies are on file in  the Planning 
office. Notice of said hearing will be duly posted in the usual posting places.
