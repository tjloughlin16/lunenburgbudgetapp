Minutes 
Public Hearing 
8 Massachusetts Avenue 
September 12, 2016 
Meeting Posted: Yes 
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  6:40 PM 
Present:  Matthew Allison, Damon McQuaid, Gregory Bittner, Kenneth Chenis, David Prokowiew, Adam R. Burney 
 
The Clerk opened the Hearing with the reading of the statutory notice published in the Sentinel and Enterprise on August 25 and 
September 1, 2016.  The Hearing is to hear and discuss an application for site plan approval under Section 8.4. of the Protective 
Bylaw of the Town of Lunenburg for an auto detailing shop at 8 Massachusetts Avenue, Map 76, Parcel 28.   
 
Applicant Douglas Mata gave a brief description of his proposed business, which usually  entails picking up vehicles from auto 
dealerships, bringing them back to 8 Massachusetts Avenue for detailing, and then returning the vehicle to the dealer ship.  
Occasionally a p rivate citizen may come by with a vehicle.   The Applicant was issued a Specia l Permit by the Zoning Board of 
Appeals in May.   
 
A. Burney noted that the previous tenant neglected to strip five parking spots  in front of the “unit” the Applicant  currently 
occupies.    
 
K. Chenis questioned the Applicant as to the delay in appearing b efore the Planning Board.  A. Burney noted that he had 
suggested to the Applicant that he wait as there was the possibility of an additional business coming to the site and both 
businesses could apply at the same time.  That did not occur as the other business did not require site plan approval.    
 
K. Chenis made Motion to close the Hearing, D. McQuaid, Second, all aye.   
 
Hearing closed at 6:50 PM.
