WAIVER REQUESTS: 
119 Massachusetts Avenue -  Application received for awning sign to be attached to the building at 119 
Massachusetts Avenue.  As the sign is >36 sq. ft., site plan approval is required.  The Applicant is requesting waiver 
from the site plan approval process.  P. Bertram recused herself due to a professional relationship.  K. Chenis made 
Motion to accept the requested sign waiver as documented, T. Cole Seconded, all aye. 
 
554 Lancaster Avenue-  Applicant Dick’s Market Garden represented by Stephen Violette.  Applicant seeking waiver 
from site plan approval for barn construction for washing /prepping vegetables for storage, which is currently being 
done outdoors.  John Maura informed the Board that barn construction will be partially state -funded through a grant 
from the Agricultural Preservation and Restriction Improv ement Program (AIP).  The proposed barn will be 40 feet x 
80 feet, off the road, and will be us ed for rinsing and cooling  of vegetables and bringing vegetables that are in the 
open into a building.  Board members were provided with a packet showing proposed barn location.  The site already 
has Town water.  The Applicants are trying to become more re gimented due to upcoming new food safety 
regulations.  Th e Applicants’ process of vegetable washing and prepping has been ongoing for ~12 years, so the 
Applicants do not expect any increase in traffic or parking    T. Cole made Motion to approve requested waiver for 
Dick’s Market Garden, K. Chenis, Seconded, all aye. 
 
PUBLIC HEARING: 190 Electric Avenue-  see separate minutes 
 
RECREATIONAL MARIJUANA-  A. Burney presented a first draft of a zoning bylaw for non-medical marijuana.  The 
Use Table did not includ e Craft Cultivator as the Board is still awaiting response from the Cannabis Control 
Commission (CCC).  A. Burney’s understanding of Craft Cultivator was that the Board wanted Town residents to be 
able to participate in the industry on a small scale appropriate to their individual location.  D. Prokowiew noted that he 
intends to attend the next Agricultural Commission meeting for any suggestion.  There was lengthy discussion about 
Craft Cultivator and licensing.  It is still not clear to the Board if each i ndividual under a coop needs to be licensed  or 
just one license for the coop .  P. Bertram felt it was important to iden tify on a map churches, schools, daycare and 
recreational facilities that would be within 500 feet of a facility.  This will give a better idea of wh ere licensing would 
be prohibited in Lunenburg.  T. Cole questioned if the Board had the ability to do any enforcing as it is not the
