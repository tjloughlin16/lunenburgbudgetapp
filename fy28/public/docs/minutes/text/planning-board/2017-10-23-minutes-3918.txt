Minutes
October 23, 2017
Meeting Posted: Yes
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA
Time: 6:30 PM
Present: Gregory Bittner, Matthew Allison, Kenneth Chenis, David Prokowiew, Michael-Ray Jeffreys, Adam R. Burney
PUBLIC HEARINGS:
994 Northfield Road- see separate minutes
Board deliberation: M. Jeffreys felt that the graphical depictions were more in line with the profile view, yet due to the grades, the
array would still be too visible from some of the neighboring houses. He continued to have fire concerns and opined that the
project will have an adverse impact on public safety. He felt that the surety proposal was out of compliance with the Bylaw. He
anticipates that the project will result in a reduction in wildlife habitat in addition to the amount of clear cutting presenting a
detriment to both the environment and neighborhood. To summarize, he felt the project did not comply with the Bylaw.
D. Prokowiew made the analogy of a "fat hand shoved into a tiny glove". He went on to note that the Plans have come a long
way, but there are still many concerns.
K. Chenis stated that his primary concern with screening had been addressed. The PILOT (Payment in Lieu of Taxes) will
benefit the Town.
M. Allison appreciated B. Gustus'
approach and offer (conservation restriction) along with the outreach to the neighbors,
nevertheless, he did not feel this was an appropriate location and also felt that the Chapter land should have been brought forth
earlier.
G. Bittner felt that the project narrative was not in line with what was stated during the Hearing. He also echoed the concerns of
his fellow Board members.
Motion made, M. Jeffreys, to deny the proposal, Prokowiew, Second for Discussion. D. Prokowiew was not prepared to
vote either way at this time. M. Allison also stated he wanted more time. A. Burney noted to the Board that the Motion could be
rescinded and the topic placed on the next meeting agenda for discussion. M. Jeffreys rescinded his Motion, Second, D.
Prokowiew. M. Allison made Motion to continue deliberation to the November 13%h Board meeting, D. Prokowiew,
Second, Discussion; K. Chenis wanted to make it clear that Board members cannot go out and seek additional information.
They will be in deliberation time, M. Jeffreys, nay, D. Prokowiew, aye; K. Chenis, aye; M. Allison, aye; G. Bittner, aye.
Motion passed 4-1.
5 & 9 Electric Avenue- see separate minutes
All remaining agenda items were tabled.
