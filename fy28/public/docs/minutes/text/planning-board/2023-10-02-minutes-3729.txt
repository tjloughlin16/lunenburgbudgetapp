III. ANR PLAN - 274 Prospect Street  
Presented Anthony Cleaves from Haley Ward 510 Mechanic St Leominster, MA on behalf of A&M construction. 
Application requests 3 lots by Pond street, Kimball street, and prospect street. Driveways for Lots 1 and 2 off of 
Pond St and lot 3 off of Kimball St. frontage and area requirements have been met. M. Allison made Motion, A. 
Reid Seconded. All, aye.  
  
IV. REQUEST FOR DE MINIMIS CHANGE Special Permit and Site Plan Approval – 43 Summer Street  
43 Summer Street is a soon to be Cannabis Dispensary – made decision to change name from Uma Flowers to Bliss 
Cannabis. When applying for Sign Permits, application named business as Bliss Cannabis, special permit s included 
name of business was Uma Flowers and requesting De Minimis Change to change name of business on special 
permit to Bliss Cannabis. M. Allison deemed change De Minimis, Seconded by A. Reid. All Aye. T. Willsmer 
made Motion to approve De Minimis Change, A. Reid Seconded. All, aye.
