Public Hearing 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on January 9, 2023, 6:05 p.m., 
Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to hear and review applications for 
amendments to a site plan approval and stormwater permit under Chapter 250, Section 
8.4. and Chapter 204 respectively of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg for a contractor 
yard, personal improvement building, and office building at 1325 Massachusetts Avenue 
(Map 062, Parcel 000 3), owner- 1325 Mass Ave Realty Trust Daniel C & Jean  M. Proctor, 
Trustees, 1325 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA; applicants- Daniel & Jean Proctor, 
1529 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.  Materials are available for viewing at the 
Planning Office, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.  This meeting will be 
broadcast live through local access cable, on FacebookLive on the Public Access Facebook 
page ( https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-157427270975305/) 
and will be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel within 24 hours
