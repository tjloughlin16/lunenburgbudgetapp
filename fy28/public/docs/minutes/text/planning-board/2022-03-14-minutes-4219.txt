Public Hearing 
1255 Lancaster Avenue 
March 14, 2022 
 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes 
Place:  Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA   
Time:  7:30 p.m. 
 
Present:  Tanner Cole, Matthew Allison, Adam Burney 
Paula Bertram and Erik Torgersen participated remotely under the Act relative to extending certain COVID -19 measures adopted 
during the state of emergency.   
Absent:  Matthew Brenner 
 
The Hearing was to hear and review a request under M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road Designations and under M.G.L. 
Ch. 87, Section 3, Cutting of Public Shade Trees to relocate a driveway by removing one tree and 40 feet of stone wall  at 1255 
Lancaster Avenue (Map 139, Parcel 00 01), Lunenburg, MA .  Owner/applicants were James and Allison Lattanzi, 331 Marshall 
Road, Fitchburg, MA  01420.    
 
J. Lattanzi recently purchased this property after leasing it for the past 10 years for a haying o peration.  Four to five acres at the 
southernmost portion of the property are used for haying.  The current ingress/egress is opposite an intersection and on a 
dangerous curve in Lancaster Avenue.  The proposal was to move the ingress/eg ress further south.   The one tree J. Lattanzi 
wished to remove was located in the center of the proposed drive.  He would also remove 40 feet of stone wall which would be 
relocated to close off the existing drive.   
 
M. Allison made Motion to close the hearing, E. Torgersen Seconded, Roll Call Vote -  P. Bertram, aye; E. Torgersen, 
aye; M. Allison, aye; T. Cole, aye.  Passed unanimously.   
 
Hearing closed 7:37 p.m.
