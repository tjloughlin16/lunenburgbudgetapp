Minutes 
Public Hearing 
AT&T, 2005 Massachusetts Avenue 
February 25, 2013 
Meeting Posted: Yes 
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA 01462 
Time:  6:45 PM 
 
Present:  Emerick R. Bakaysa, Joanna L. Bilotta-Simeone, Thomas W. Bodkin Jr., Nathan J. Lockwood, Marion M. Benson 
 
Chair opened the Public Hearing with the reading of the legal notice published in the Sentinel and Enterprise o n February 11 and 
February 18, 2013.  The Hearing is to hea r and discuss a modification to a special permit  under Section 4.6., Subsection 4.6.4.2. 
of the Protective Bylaw of the Town of Lunenburg.  The modification is to add three antennas and place equipm ent in the existing 
telecommunications shelter at 2005 Massachusetts Avenue .  Property owner is Peter Knox, Tru stee; applicant is New Cingular 
Wireless/ATT Mobility. 
 
The Applicant was represented by Brian Allen, KJK Wireless, Site Acquisition Consultant.  AT&T currently has six antennas 
mounted on a 190 foot monopole tower.  The antennas are centered at 181 feet (the second grouping of antennas down from the 
top of the tower).  AT&T wants to add three additional antennas to provide LTE (Long Term Evolution ) to the community. 
 
There are four different providers on this tower.  Verizon antennas are placed slightly higher than the AT&T antennas, and Nextel 
and T Mobile are slightly lower.   
 
No proponents or opponents. 
 
Motion, Ms. Bilotta-Simeone to close the Public Hearing for 9 Chase Road, Second, Mr. Bodkin Jr. All aye. 
 
Closed 6:50 PM.
