DIRECTOR’S ITEMS:  
§250- 4.15. Solar Energy Systems- no update 
Recreational Marijuana Regulations -A. Burney noted that of the 150 ± responses so far, few favor prohibition.  Approximately 
1/3 of respondents wanted to prohibit retail, but more than 2/3 wanted no prohibition.  He wa s surprised at locations where 
respondents indicated it should be allowed and felt that maybe some of them didn’t understand the definition of various zoni ng 
districts.  The majority of respondents are looking for restriction, but no prohibition , with it being allowed in the Commercial and 
Office Park/Industrial Districts.  K. Chenis noted the Board has to carefully construct the bylaw to meet the Town’s nee ds and to 
also pass at town meeting.  A. Burney will work with Town Counsel on the bylaw language .  Some Board members felt there 
should be five (5) ballot questions, which would cover each use; if they are done all together, they may not pass  at town meeting.  
The Town should have the ability to prohibit based upon the use.   A. Burney was of the opinion that the zoning bylaw should be 
written to cover all of the uses.  He will provide some bylaw examples to the Board  and hopes to have a draft near completion by 
September.
