Roll Call conducted by Adam Burney, Land Use Director:  Matthew Allison, present; Tanner Cole, present; Paula Bertram, 
present; Matthew Brenner, present. 
Absent:  Erik Torgersen 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Time:  8:01 p.m. 
 
The Hearing , via Zoom video conference, was to hear and review applications for site plan app roval and stormwater 
management permit under Ch. 250, Section 8.4. and Chapter 204 respectively of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg  for 
construction of four 2 -unit townhouse buildings totaling eight (8) units at 711 Massachusetts Avenue Lt 4  (Map 0 59, Par cel 
0030), Lunenburg, MA.  Owner/applicant was EF Realty, LLC, 654 Elm Street, Leominster, MA.    
 
The owner/applicant was represented by Wesley Flis, Whitman & Bingham Associates, hereinafter “W&B” and Eric Fowler, EF 
Realty, LLC.   
 
W&B was designing the project around environmental issues  as there was a perennial stream on the western side of the 
property with associated wetlands.   It was noted that the March 10, 2021 Zoning Board of Appeals approval  was for two 4 -unit 
townhouse buildings totaling eight (8) units.  The application filed with the Planning Board was for four 2 -unit townhouses totaling 
eight (8) units.  The change was made to accommodate parking between the buildings instead of at the front of the site.  There 
would be two parking spaces per unit.  W&B would consult with the Building Commissioner/Zoning Official as to the  change in 
layout from the Zoning Board of Appeals approval.  There would be six three -bedroom and two two -bedroom units.  The site 
would be serviced by municipal water and s ewer and there would be a private sub-surface drainage system.  A stormwater basin 
would be located on the westerly side  of the site and there would be swales throughout the site.  The Board viewed unit 
architecturals.   
 
M. Allison brought up that the per ennial stream was a cold water fishery .  He also had concerns with the project location due to 
Massachusetts Avenue being heavily travelled, the site entrance located on a curve with a nearby road intersection, and also 
located across the street from a day care center.   
 
Board members mentioned various stormwater items; the proposed gate valve  in the detention basin , whether or not the 
applicant planned on having monitoring wells, and the proposed wick drain.  It was anticipated that the forthcoming peer r eview 
would address those items.
