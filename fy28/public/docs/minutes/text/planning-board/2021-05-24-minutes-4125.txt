Minutes 
Public Hearing 
150 Holman Street 
May 24, 2021 
 
Roll Call conducted by Adam Burney, Land Use Director:  Tanner Cole, present; Matthew Allison , present; Paula Bertram, 
present; Matthew Brenner, present. 
Absent:  Erik Torgersen 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Time:  7:30 p.m. 
 
The Hearing , via Zoom video conference, was to hear and review an application for a stormwater manage ment permit under 
Chapter 204 of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg  for property located at 150 Holman Street (M ap 0 39, Parcel 00 02), 
Lunenburg, MA.  Owner/applicant was PSG Realty 225-259 LLC, 133 Leominster Shirley Road, Lunenburg, MA.    
 
The owner/applicant was represented by Brian Marchetti, McCarty Engineering, Inc.  The property consisted of ~18 acres, which 
would be divided into seven (7) ANR (Approval Not Required) lots  ranging from 40,000 square feet to ~3.5 acres.  Lots 1 and 2 
would have a common  drive off Holman Street as would Lots 6 and 7 off Northfield Road.  All driveways would be routed to 
either roadside vegetated swales or rain gardens and rooftops would be routed to dry wells on each lot.   
 
The Board was in receipt of Graves Engineering,  Inc. review letter dated May 24, 2021; which B. Marchetti proceeded to 
address.  Items addressed were in the categories of Section 204 – Stormwater Management: NPDES Phase II Permits, 
hydrology computations & MassDEP Stormwater Management, and general eng ineering comments.  The Plans would be 
revised to address the review comments.   
 
P. Bertram was concerned with  enforcement of the maintenance and inspections required for the drywells, rain gardens and 
swales.  Pea stone diaphragms (stone trench) would fu nction as sediment forebays.  B. Marchetti responded that  the 
Conservation Commission would require a  notice of intent and order of conditions for each lo t.  The Drainage Report included an 
O&M (Operations & Maintenance)  Plan containing an inspection form to be filled out annually by individual property owners and 
submitted to the Conservation Commission.  P. Bertram requested the applicant provide the Board with a SWPPP (Stormwater 
Pollution Prevention Plan) and stressed to the Board that it should begin r equiring maintenance reports.  M. Allison inquired as to 
the process since the applicant was requesting a stormwater permit for seven (7) lots that had not yet been created.  A. Burney 
responded that the Board would be unable to approve the stormwater permit until the ANR Plan was presented.   
 
P. Bertram made Motion to continue the hearing for 150 Holman Street to June 14, 2021, 6:35 p.m., M. Brenner 
Seconded, Roll Call Vote-  M. Allison, aye; P. Bertram, aye; M. Brenner, aye; T. Cole, aye.  Vote passed 4 -0. 
Hearing recessed 7:44 p.m.
