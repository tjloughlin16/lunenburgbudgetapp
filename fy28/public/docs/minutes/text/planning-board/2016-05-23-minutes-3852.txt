Minutes 
Public Hearing 
175 Northfield Road 
May 23, 2016 
Meeting Posted: Yes 
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  6:35 PM 
Present:  Matthew Allison, Damon McQuaid, Gregory Bittner, Kenneth Chenis, David Prokowiew, Adam R. Burney 
 
The Clerk opened the Hearing with the reading of the statutory notice published in the Sentinel and Enterprise on May 9 and May 
16, 2016.  The Hearing is to hear and discuss a stormwater management special permit for the development of nine single family 
home lots and associated site work at 175 Northfield Road, Map 051, Parcel 0037, under Section 6.7. of the Protective Bylaw of 
the Town of Lunenburg.   
 
The applicant Matthew Olson/Ashoryn, LLC, was represented by Peter Blaisdell, Williams & Sparges, LLC.  The nine lots were 
created by an ANR Plan endorsed by the Board in October 2015.  All lots will be accessed off existing roads.  Lots 1, 2, 3, and 4 
will be serviced by septic and lots 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9 wil l be  on Town sewer.  The applicant is utilizing  LID (Low Impact 
Development) to manage stormwater runoff.  There will be no catch basins.  Stormwater runoff will be collected by a series of 
shallow infiltration basins and trenches.  Each driveway will have its own stone trench.  The drives on Northfield Road will have a 
little crossing onto the property  with twin twelve (12”) inch culverts .  An ANRAD (Abbreviated Notice of Resource Area  
Delineation) has been filed.  The individual homeowner will be responsible for system maintenance.  A Board Condition will 
specify that in conjunction with the registry recording of the Board’s Decision, the Operations & Maintenance Plan (OMP) shall 
also be recorded which notes that per the OMP, the homeowner is responsible for maintenance of swale and pond.   
 
John Brush, 100 Royal Fern Drive-  Any increase to runoff rate?  No. 
 
D, McQuaid, Motion to approve stormwater permit, Second, D. Prokowiew; conditions - natural and/or faux stone finish 
on culverts, inclusion of O&M recordi ng with Board’s Decision, and individual homeowners be provided with copy of 
Board’s Decision so they are aware of their responsibility for maintenance.  All aye.  
 
Hearing closed 8:20 PM
