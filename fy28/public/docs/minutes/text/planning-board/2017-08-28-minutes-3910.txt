ANR PLAN:   961 & 994 Northfield Road, Saliba & 994 Northfield Rd LLC - The Applicants, John and David Saliba, were 
represented by Neil Gorman, David Ross Associates.  The site is located on the south side of Northfield Road and abuts Settler’s 
Golf Course.  Two new lots will be created on 961 Northfield Road and two new parcels will be created  which will be a swap 
between 994 Northfield and 961 Northfield.  The property line run s through the 9 th hole on Settler’s Crossing Golf Course.  The 
parcel swap will put the golf course completely on 994 Northfield Road property.  The Plan was endorsed by the Board.  
 
APPOINTMENTS:   
Request for Waiver - Walker Orthodontics, 119 Massachusetts Avenue - The Applicant, Walker Orthodontics, was 
represented by Matthew Fournier, President, Elite Construction and Design, Inc.  On February 27, 2017, the Board approved a 
site plan approval application for the creation of a sixteen (16) space parking lot.  On August 9, 2017, the Applicant petitioned to 
the Zoning Board of Appeals for use of the lower level in the building.  There will be no changes to the plan that was submit ted to 
the Board in February.  The lower space is currently being used for storage.  Motion, K. Chenis, to grant waiver from site p lan 
approval, Second, D. Prokowiew, all aye. 
 
Request for Waiver - 84 Lakefront Avenue - The Applicant, Kenneth Ricker owns Embers Pizzeria on the Lakefront.  He was 
requesting a waiver from the site plan approval process to increase the current patio around  the side of the building by 
approximately sixteen (16) by twenty -three (23) square feet.  This will add approximately 16 -20 more seats.  The Applicant will 
still remain within the seating capacity .  There was discussion regarding two parking spaces in the  front of the restaurant that the 
Applicant has marked no parking.  These spaces are public parking and do not belong to the Applicant.  G. Bittner noted he had 
spoken with the Conservation Administrator who indicated that the Applicant may want to consult  with the Administrator 
regarding an RDA from the Conservation Commission.  Motion, M. Jeffreys, to grant waiver from site plan approval, 
Second, D. Prokowiew, all aye.   
 
PUBLIC HEARINGS: 
40 Pioneer Drive & 98 Leominster Shirley Road - See separate minutes.  The Board will send a letter to the Zoning Board of 
Appeals recommending approval for the third drive.  The Board discussed Conditions for its Decision: 
 The existing and the proposed fire department connections on the building will be combined regardles s of two or three

 A hydrant will be located at the fire department connection.

Motion, K. Chenis, to approve the above Conditions, Second, D. Prokowiew, all aye.
