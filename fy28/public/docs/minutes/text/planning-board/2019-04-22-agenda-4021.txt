PUBLIC HEARING 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on April 22 , 2019 , 6:35 PM,  
Joseph Bilotta Meeting Room, Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA 01462 to make 
alterations to Code of the Town of Lunenburg , MA, Chapter 250 Zoning: (i) Article V, 5.6. 
Cluster Development by adding a new Subsection F , (ii) by citizen petition, Article IV, 
4.13. Solar Energy Systems, to change the special permit granting authority, (iii) by 
citizen petition, Article IV, 4.1. Permitted and Prohibited Uses, 4.1.J. Recreation Uses, to 
add (7) Exhibition and Workshops, (iv) by citizen petition, Article IV, 4.1. Permitted and 
Prohibited Uses, 4.1.G Use Table , to add  the corresponding use, and (v) by citizen 
petition, to amend the Zoning Map by rezoning portions of land located at 131  (Map 146, 
Parcel 0003), 133 (Map 146, Parcel 0004), 151 (Map 146, Parcel 0027) and 181 (Map 146, 
Parcel 0008)  Leominster Shirley Road.  Copies are on file in the Planning Office, 960 
Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.
