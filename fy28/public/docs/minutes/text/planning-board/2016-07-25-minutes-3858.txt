Minutes 
Public Hearing 
1026 Northfield Road 
July 25, 2016 
Meeting Posted: Yes 
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  6:35 PM 
Present:  Matthew Allison, Damon McQuaid, Gregory Bittner, Kenneth Chenis, David Prokowiew, Adam R. Burney 
 
Board Clerk G. Bittner opened the Hearing with the reading of the statutory notice published in the Sentinel and Enterprise o n 
July 22 and July 18, 2016.  The Hearing is to hear and discuss a request to e nlarge the existing driveway at 1026 Northfield 
Road, Map 037, Parcel 0012, under M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road Designations and under M.G.L. Ch. 87, Section 3, 
Cutting of Public Shade Trees. 
 
The owner/applicant, Ronald Smith noted that the exis ting driveway is a semi -circle, and he desires to make it a larger radius to 
protect the roots of 100+ year maple tree s that line the street.  These trees have created broken concrete in the existing drive.  
That concrete will be removed and the westerly p ortion of the driveway will be enlarged and relocated west of the maple trees.  
Photographs were presented annotating smaller trees that he wishes to remove  to relocate the drive .  The DPW Director has 
given permission for R. Smith to trim back the foliage  in the right -of-way.  Motion, D. McQuaid, to approve enlarging the 
existing driveway at 1026 Northfield Road, Second, K. Chenis, all aye.   
 
Decision will be prepared for Board endorsement. 
 
Hearing closed 7:08 PM.
