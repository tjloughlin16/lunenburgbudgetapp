The Hearing continuation was to hear and review an application under Chapter 325 Subdivision Regulations, Article III, Section 
325-3.4. Definitive Plan of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg  for a six-lot subdivision consisting of six du plex buildings at 101 
Pleasant Street (Map 095, Parcel 00 03), Lunenburg, MA .  Owner/applicant was MA Pleasant LLC, P O Box 903, Ashburnham, 
MA.     
 
There was no change to the plan.  As the retaining wall was within the right -of-way (ROW), Paul Grasewicz, GRAZ Engineering, 
LLC had submitted a waiver request on the right-of-way width.  The request was to reduce the width from 50 feet down to 35.  No 
utilities would be affected.  Waiver request letter dated August 5, 2021, stated, “The purpose of the right-of-way (ROW) reduction 
is to ensure that the proposed retaining walls are owned by (and the responsibility) of the lot owners versus the town… This 
request is justified as the town has expressed a desire to have the walls outside of the ROW.  Granting the wai ver is therefore in 
the public’s best interest.  Furthermore, the sole function of the walls is to reduce the w etlands loss, which is also in the best 
interest of the public.  P. Bertram inquired how the property owners would  be made aware of any lot restrictions.  A. Burney 
noted those would be addressed in the condominium documents attached to the individual deeds.  Board members were not 
inclined to grant the waiver request.  GRAZ Engineering noted that if the waiver was denied, he would have to move the  walls 
outside the right -of-way which would increase the wetlands loss and double the replication area.  He would have to see if a 
resolution could be met with the Conservation Commission. 
 
Andrea Adams- Sympathized with the applicant, but did not feel reducing the right-of-way was an acceptable solution. 
 
M. Allison made Motion to not approve right -of-way waiver request, M. Brenner Seconded, Roll Call Vote - M. Allison, 
aye; M. Brenner, aye; P. Bertram, aye; T. Cole, aye.  Vote passed 4-0.  The waiver request was denied.
