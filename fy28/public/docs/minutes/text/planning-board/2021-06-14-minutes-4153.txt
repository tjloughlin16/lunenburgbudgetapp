The Hearing  continuation, via Zoom video conference, was to hear and review an application for a sto rmwater management 
permit under Chapter 204 of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg  for property located at 150 Holman Street (M ap 039, Parcel 
0002), Lunenburg, MA.  Owner/applicant was PSG Realty 225-259 LLC, 133 Leominster Shirley Road, Lunenburg, MA.    
 
The owner/applicant was represented by Brian Marchetti, McCarty Engineering, Inc.   
 
In response to Jeff Walsh, P.E., Graves Engineering, Inc. hereinafter “GEI” , review letter dated May 24, 2021, the following 
changes were made to Lots 5 and 6.  Lot 6 was revised to accommodate a four foot retaining wall, which would allow ~15-17 feet 
of clear width along the property line to the retaining wall.  Grading along the northern edge of Lot 5 was revised to 
accommodate a small swale for runoff from the north.  The se changes would prevent stormwater runoff originating on abutting 
properties from running onto Lots 5 and 6.   
 
As these lots would eventually all be under separate ownership,  P. Bertram was concerned with  stormwater maintenance on the 
individual lots.  B. Marchetti responded that all lots had been approved by the Conservation Commission on June 7, 2021 with 
conditions that each individual deed contain ed a  provision into perpetuity for the homeowner’s annual completion of a 
maintenance inspection form .  Th is form was contained in the Operation and Maintenance Plan  (O&M Plan)  and would be 
submitted annually to the Conservation Commission.  P. Bertram would like a condition placed on this approval for the completed 
maintenance logs to also be provided to the Planning Board.   
 
P. Bertram made Motion to close the hearing, M. Allison Seconded, Roll Call Vote - M. Allison, aye; P. Bertram, aye; E. 
Torgersen, aye; T. Cole, aye.  Vote passed 4-0. 
 
Hearing closed 7:25 p.m.
