PUBLIC HEARINGS: 
Zoning Warrant Articles-  
 Amend Zoning Map , 199 Massachusetts Avenue - See separate minutes.   M. Brenner made Motion to 
recommend in favor of the citizen petition  for zoning change at 199 Massachusetts Avenue, A. Reid Seconded, 
passed unanimously.   
 Use Table- See separate minutes.  M. Brenner made Motion to approve the Article, A. Reid Seconded, passed 
unanimously. 
 
88 Massachusetts Avenue- See separate minutes.   
M. Brenner made Motion to accept all waivers listed in the submitted Project Narrative, Section 4.4, A. Reid Seconded, 
passed unanimously.   
M. Brenner made Motion to approve the Site Plan Approval for 88 Massachusetts Avenue with Standard Conditions to 
include construction hours of Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Saturday 8:00 a.m. to 3 p.m., no Sundays 
or holidays, plus a Special Condition specifying no snow storage higher than three feet within a thirty foot triangle on 
either side of the ingress/egress, A. Reid Seconded, passed unanimously.   
 
260 Falulah Road- See separate minutes. 
 
NOTICE OF INTENT TO SELL:  Right of First Refusal, Parcel 3, Sunny Hill Road - Both Parcel 3, containing 1.34 acres, and 
Parcel 2, containing 24.25 acres, were classified recreational land.  The purchaser of the land intended to combine Parcel 3 with 
Parcel 1, which was not chapter land,  for the purpose of constructing  three single family homes.  M. Brenner made Motion to 
recommend the Select Board pass over its Right of First Refusal, A. Reid Seconded, passed unanimously.    
 
DECISION:  245 Holman Street, Review and Endorsement-  Decision endorsed by the Board. 
 
COMPREHENSIVE PERMIT APPLICATION:  Pond View Commons, LLC, Howard Street -  The Zoning Board of Appeals was 
in the hearing process.  Planning Board comments had been provided.  At the request of the Chair, Zoning Board of Appeals, A.  
Burney was exploring potential review consultants.

BOARD COMMENT/CONCERNS:  M. Allison brought up the Zoning Board of Appeals hearing for Pond View Commons.  The 
applicant had stated that he would cover the cost of stubs  for every property that was along the three mile extension .  M. Allison 
stressed that the Board should be having on-going discussions concerning potential zoning changes  for more control over 
development within Lunenburg .  He was of the opinion that the Board could be prepared with some articles for annual town 
meeting.
