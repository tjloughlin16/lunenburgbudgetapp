The Hearing continuation was to hear and review applications for site plan approval and stormwater management perm it under 
Ch. 250, Section 8.4. and Chapter 204 respectively of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg  for construction of a 372,000 square 
foot warehouse and distribution building off Leominster -Shirley Road at 1263 Reservoir Road (Map 142, Parcel 0006) , owner 
Paul Keating (Heirs) c/o Mary Coburn, et. al., 998 Reservoir Road, Lunenburg, MA  01462 and 435 Leominster -Shirley Road  
(Map 143, Parcel 0007), Lunenburg, MA, owner J&A Realty Trust, Daniel L. McNiff Trustee, 280 Great Road, Shirley MA 01464.  
Applicant is JMC/TBG Lunenburg, LLC, 100 Grandview Road, Suite 203, Braintree, MA  02184.   
 
In attendance w ere project engineer  Bill Hannigan, Hannigan Engineering  and representatives from The Brennan Group , Inc. 
and John M. Corcoran & Co. 
 
B. Hannigan addressed items in the September 23, 2021 review letter of Jeffrey Walsh, Graves Engineering, Inc. (GEI) . GEI had 
concern with the sightline looking west out of the site egress.  B. Hannigan noted vegetation that obstructed the sightline in the 
right-of-way would be clea red.  GEI also requested the stabilized construction entrance(s) be shown on the site development 
plans as they exist.  B. Hannigan presented sketches showing construction entrance location s on both the east and west sides.  
A g ravel drive would be placed at the entrance during construction.  The  crushed stone would go in about 50 feet with the 
purpose of retaining mud before it got to the street.    
 
An agreement had been reached with P.J. Keating Company and the property owners (Keating family) for  the cutting of brush 
and trees to grade 20 feet from the edge of pavement for a distance of ~650 feet across from the entrance to Reservoir Road.

Michael Laning, Environmental Engi neer, Tech Environmental, Inc.  performed a review of the site  to determine how to contain 
the sounds on the site; it would be done through  berms and walls twelve (12) feet from grade.  He asserted this was an excellent 
site for the warehouse due to existing topography.  There was already an existing berm along the upper northwesterly side of the 
site, industrial sites located on both sides of the proposed project, and also across the street from the site.  Primary areas of 
concern were to the northeast and northwest of the site.  A berm was proposed at the line of construction on the west side of the 
site closest to Leominster-Shirley Road and also at the north end of the site.  A sound deadening wall would be located towards 
the rear of the east side of the  site running along the railway spur.   The berms would reflect the sound back onto the site.  
Collectively, the berms would create a U -shaped barrier reflecting sound back onto the site.   The berms would be vegetated, 
likely with loam, seeded, and a wildfl ower mix.  The applicant would supply a sample of the wall composition.  Revisions would 
be made to the site plan based on M. Laning’s recommendations.   
 
GEI noted the majority of the review comments had been met.  He had concerns with the line of sight l ooking west.  Vegetation 
clearing would need to be performed and maintained so that the sight line was not blocked.  He noted hydrology and stormwater 
management was in place.   
 
The Board had requested an outside peer traffic review of the study submitted  by Greenman-Pedersen, Inc. (GPI).  A. Burney 
approved direct communication between GPI and the peer reviewer, MDM Transportation Consultants, Inc. to ensure they were 
in receipt of any required documentation.   
 
The tree and brush clearing would commence once the building permit was issued and would be completed prior to occupancy.  
M. Allison inquired if that could commence immediate, thereby allowing MDM to view the results.  B. Hannigan thought that 
would not be possible due to the financial expenditure by an entity other than the property owner.   
 
The applicant was waiting for the peer traffic review and anticipated having a final set of plans for the Board’s October 25 th 
meeting.   
 
P. Bertram made Motion to continue the Hearing to October, 25, 2021, 6:35 p.m., M. Allison Secon ded, all aye.  Vote 
passed-4-0.   
 
Hearing recessed 7:40 p.m.
