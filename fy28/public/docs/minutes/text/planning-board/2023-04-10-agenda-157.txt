TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
PUBLIC HEARING 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on April 10, 2023, 6:05 
p.m., Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  to make alterations to Code of 
the Town of Lunenburg, MA, Chapter 250, Zoning, Article IV Use Regulations, 
Section 250-4.1 by inse rting 250-4.1K(20) Self-Storage.  Materials are available 
for viewing at the Planning Office, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.  
This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, FacebookLive on 
the Public Access Facebook page ( https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-
Public-Access-Cable-157427270975305/).  To participate remotely: from a 
computer, please use the link below to join the public hearing webinar:  
https://us02web.zoom.us/j/88068452023 or from a telephone, dial the toll free 
number (888) 475-4499 and enter meeting ID: 88068452023
