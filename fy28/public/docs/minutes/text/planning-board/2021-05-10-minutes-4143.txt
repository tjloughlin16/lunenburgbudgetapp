Minutes 
Public Hearing 
North End Subaru, 757 Chase Road 
May 10, 2021 
 
 
Roll Call conducted by Adam Burney, Land Use Di rector:  Matthew Allison, present; Tanner Cole, present; Paula Bertram, 
present; Matthew Brenner, present. 
Absent:  Erik Torgersen 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Time:  7:44 p.m. 
 
The Hearing continuation, via Zoom video conference, was to hear and review applicati ons for site plan approval and stormwater 
management permit under Ch. 250, Section 8.4. and Chapter 204 respectively of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg  for 
construction of a new Subaru auto dealership at the same location as the existing dealership at 75 7 Chase Road ( Map 0 14, 
Parcel 0003), Lunenburg, MA.  Owner/applicant was Empire Management Corporation, 171 Great Road, Action, MA.     
 
The owner/applicant was represented by James T. Almonte, RLA, Principal  and Mike Scott , Land Design Collaborative , 
hereinafter “LDC” and Brian Lafferty, Empire Management Corporation .  Plans had been  revised in response to Graves 
Engineering, Inc. review letter dated March 17, 2021.  The revised plans and response letter were not received by e ither Graves 
Engineering, Inc. or in the Planning Office in enough time for the Board to review prior to this evening’s hearing.   
 
At a Zoning Board of Appeals hearing on April 14, 2021, a special permit was granted to continue the pre -existing nonconforming 
use.   
 
The Board impresse d upon the applicant that delivery vehicles needed to be able to navigate the site without using parking on 
the side of the public way or backing in off Chase Road. 
 
Soil testing had been performed and the septic system would be located in the same area as  the current system , behind the 
Mazda dealership in the left hand corner of the property.  P. Bertram wanted to ensure  the system  would not be  negatively 
affected by topography or stormwater flow.   
 
P. Bertram made Motion to continue the hearing to June 14, 6:35 p.m., M. Brenner Seconded, Roll Call Vote - M. Brenner, 
aye; P. Bertram, aye; T. Cole, aye; M. Allison, aye.  Vote passed 4-0. 
 
Hearing recessed 8:00 p.m.
