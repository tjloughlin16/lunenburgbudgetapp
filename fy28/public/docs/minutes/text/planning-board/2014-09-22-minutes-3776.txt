Public Hearing was to hear and discuss a Modification to a Development Plan Review and Special Permit under Section 5.5., 
Planned Residential Area, of the Protective Bylaw of the Town of Lunenburg,  for 221 White Street (Map 75, Parcel 53).  
Owner/Applicant is RIW Realty Trust, John I. White, Trustee, 13 Jacklyn Drive, Lunenburg, MA.     
 
Larry Sabean, Hannigan Engineering Inc., representing applicant John (Jack) White.  A P lanned Residential Area (P RA) was 
approved under a Special Permit in 2006 ; then modified  in 2013 to put the remaining four units into duplexes.  Building 6 has 
been constructed too close to the property line .  The bylaw specifies a 40 foot setback; building is 39 .2 feet.  Requesting a 
modification of the front setback to 38 feet.  Building is also 64.2 feet from the centerline of the right -of-way which is less than the 
approved 65 feet under the Approval; therefore requesting modification to allow building 6 to be  63 feet from the centerline of the 
right-of-way. 
 
Motion D. McQuaid to close hearing, Second, M. Allison, all aye. 
 
Hearing closed 7:31 PM.
