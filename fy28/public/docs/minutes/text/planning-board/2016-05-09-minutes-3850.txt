Minutes 
Public Hearing 
233 Lancaster Avenue 
May 9, 2016 
Meeting Posted: Yes 
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  6:35 PM 
Present:  Damon McQuaid, Matthew Allison, Kenneth Chenis, Gregory Bittner, David Prokowiew, Adam R. Burney 
 
Chair D. McQuaid opened the Hearing  with the reading of the statutory notice published in the Sentinel and Enterprise on April 
25 and May 2, 2016.  The Hearing is to hear and discuss a request to construct  a driveway at 233 Lancaster Avenue, Map 081, 
Parcel 00 05, under M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road Designations and under M.G.L. Ch . 87, Section 3, Cutting of 
Public Shade Trees.   
 
The applicant  and property owner  Joseph White , 334 Sunny Hill Road , was r epresented by Daniel Proctor of 1529 
Massachusetts Avenue.  The proposal is to build a driveway for a single family home, thirteen (13’) feet wide (width incorrect on 
application), twelve (12”) inch gravel base, three (3”) inch top and binder asphalt.  The  Driveway Design Plan shows five 
retention basins.  Approximately six (6) trees of six  (6”) inches or greater within forty  (40’) feet of the drive are proposed to be 
removed.  There are also ten to twelve smaller saplings that would be removed.  The drive will be located at the southerly -most 
point of the site to increase the sight distance to the north.  The existing scenic stone wall at the property line will be removed 
during the work and then upon work completion, those stones will be used to create kne e walls on either side of the drive  to 
maintain aesthetics. 
 
Motion, K. Chenis to close Hearing, Second, G. Bittner, all aye. 
 
Hearing closed 6:45 PM
