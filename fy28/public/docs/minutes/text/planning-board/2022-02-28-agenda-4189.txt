PUBLIC HEARING 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a public hearing on February 28, 2022 , 6: 05 p.m.,  via 
Zoom video conference to hear and review an application to relocate a driveway at 1255 
Lancaster Avenue (Map 139, Parcel 1), under M.G.L. Ch, 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road 
Designations and under M.G.L. Ch. 87, Section 3, Cutting of Public Shade Trees, by removing 
one tree and 40 feet  of stone wall.  The removed stones will be used to close off the existing 
driveway.  Owner/applicant- James & Allison Lattanzi, 331 Marshall Road, Fitchburg, MA  01420.

Lunenburg, MA .  This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable and on
