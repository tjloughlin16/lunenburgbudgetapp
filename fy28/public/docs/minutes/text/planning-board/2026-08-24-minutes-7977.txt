3. CHAIR / DIRECTOR’S REPORT  
 S. Wallace reported that Town Counsel is reviewing the zoning amendments planned for 
May 2027 Annual Town Meeting.  The Board plans to post the proposed amendments 
online in September, meet with relevant boards and committees during the fall, hold a 
public forum, conduct the required public hearing in March and bring the amendments to 
Town Meeting in May, 2027.  Town Counsel has reviewed the minor zoning amendments 
planned for the November Special Town Meeting.  The Board discussed a possible 
additional amendment to the shared driveway provision.  S. Wallace reported that he and 
the Town Administrator met with Town Counsel regarding the creation of an affordable 
housing trust.  Town Counsel will prepare a step by step guide. 
4. APPROVAL NOT REQUIRE (ANR) PLANS 
50 Page Street (ANR-26-6) 
Greg Roy of Dillis & Roy represented the owners of 50 and 52 Page Street.  The application 
proposes an equal exchange of land between the two parcels.  The Board discussed the 
resulting frontage configuration and determined that the proposed configuration was not 
precluded by the bylaw. 
A Burney moved to endorse the ANR for 50 Page Street.  A. Reid Second 
All In favor. 
5. PUBLIC HEARINGS 
 881 Flat Hill Road, Special Permit, Flood Plain  (PMSP-26-5) 

===PAGE 2===
Matt Marro of Matthew. S. Marro Environmental Consulting represented Nathan Jones.  
The application proposes reconstruction of a boathouse and deck area on an island in 
Lake Shirley within the flood plain.  The proposed boat house is approximately 10 feet 
by 12 feet and would use the existing support system.  No filing, grading or changes to 
drainage patterns were proposed.  The applicant reported that the Conservation 
Commission had issued an Order of Conditions.  The Board discussed the existing 
supports and the limited nature of the work.  No public comment was received. 
A. Burney moved to close the public hearing.  A. Reid Second 
All In favor. 
A Burney moved to approve the proposed project at 881 Flat Hill Road with standard 
administrative conditions and construction hours of 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, Monday 
through Friday and 9:00 am to 2:00 pm Saturdays and no Sundays.  A. Reid Second. 
All in favor.   
 18 Oak Ridge Road  Special Permit Flood Plain, (PBSP-26-9)  
Dan Wolf of David Ross Assoc. represented Melissa McCarthy and Steven Vittone.  The 
proposal is to remove and reconstruct an existing two-bedroom single family dwelling 
farther from Lake Shirley and outside the flood plain.  The applicant reported that the 
septic system will be replaced and that the Conservation Commission had issued an 
Order of Conditions.  A Zoning Board of Appeals hearing is scheduled regarding 
reconstruction of the pre-existing non-conforming dwelling.  There was no public 
comment. 
A Burney moved to continue to close the public hearing.  A. Reid Seconded.  
All in favor 
A Burney moved to approve the proposed project at 18 Oak Ridge Road with standard 
administrative conditions and construction hours of 8:00 am to 5:00 pm, Monday 
through Friday and 9:00 am to 2:00 pm Saturdays.  No Sundays.  A. Reid Seconded.  
All in favor 
 27 Fire Road 15A Special Permit Two Dwellings on one lot,  (PBSP-26-8),

through Friday and 9:00 am to 2:00 pm Saturdays.  No Sundays.  A. Reid Seconded.  
All in favor
