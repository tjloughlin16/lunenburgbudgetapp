Recreational Marijuana Regulations- Retail- There was limited discussion, due to the late hour.   
 
Kim Cole, 563 Massachusetts Avenue - Wants prohibition.  She realizes she may be in the minority, so regulations 
need to be in place if prohibition fails.  State law requires a 500 foot buffer, but municipalities are allowed to decrease 
that distance.  Whatever the distance may be, she inquired how that distance would be measured.  She has viewed 
the regulations in various communities  and notes what some other towns are putt ing in place.  E.g., S ecurity plan, 
operation and maintenance plan, emergency response plan, no drive-up, no walk-up, security lighting, signage.  She 
would like to see it prohibited in all districts as a home occupation.   
 
D. Prokowiew inquired as to Boa rd of Health jurisdiction.  A. Costa respon ded that adult use marijuana is heavily 
regulated at the State lev el, which makes it difficult for local communities to set their regulations.  The Board of 
Health can only control issues within its jurisdiction.   
 
9:57 PM - G. Bittner requested a Motion to continue the meeting past the allotted time, D. Prokowiew moved 
the Motion, M. Allison Seconded, all aye.   
 
Both David Rodgers, 82 Highland Street  and Katey Adams, Williams Drive would like to see it prohibite d in 
Lunenburg. 
 
The Board emphasized that a regulation needs to be in place in the event prohibition does not pass a town meeting 
vote. 
 
The retail marijuana discussion will continue at the next Board meeting.
