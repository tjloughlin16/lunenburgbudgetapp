MPO Update- At the last Board meeting, P. Bertram inquired if there should be a Planning Board representative to the MPO.  A. 
Burney inquired and learned that sub -regional delegates are elected.  There is no Planning Board representative on MPO unless 
elected through the regional process.   
LID (Low Impact Development) Bylaw -  Neighboring community drafts supplied to Board members.  As the Board looks ahead 
to crafting an LID Bylaw, members will provide comments to A. Burney.   
Rules & Regulations Governing the Subdivision of Land - Board members previously communicated that they wished to work 
on the Master Plan before working on th e Rules & Regulations.  A. Burney believed it was important for the Board to start 
working on the Rules & Regulations, expressing that they are not affected by the Master Plan.  The Rules & Regulations regulate 
subdivision construction and if they are put on hold for another  2-3 years, the Town could see land subdivisions coming into 
Town that are working with regulations that are twenty years old.  He also felt this would coincide with working on a n LID Bylaw.

ONGOING ITEMS:  Chapter 204, Stormwater and Storm Sewers - M. Brenner noted the re are references to setting up a 
system to deal with a preset amount of runoff; e.g., equivalent to, or greater than, 1.0 inch.  He inquired if that number was based 
on historic trends or future predictions.  A. Burney responded usually historic trends.  A lot of the numbers found in the bylaw 
come from the EPA National Pollution Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) Phase II Permit for the Town’s municipal separate 
storm sewer system (MS4).   He had a similar question concerning the percentages for Total Suspended Solids (TSS) and Total 
Phosphorus (TP).  Those percentages also came from the NPDES Phase II Permit.
