Minutes 
Public Hearing (Continuation) 
Aro Estates, Howard Street 
April 3, 2013 
Meeting Posted: Yes 
Place: Ritter Memorial Building, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA 01462 
Time:  6:30 PM 
 
Present:  Emerick R. Bakaysa, Joanna L. Bilotta-Simeone, Thomas W. Bodkin Jr., Nathan J. Lockwood, Marion M. Benson 
Also present:  Town Counsel Attorney Joel Bard and Robert Oliva of David Ross Associates 
 
Chair Bakaysa opened the hearing continuation for Aro Estates.  The initial hearing was held on January 30, 2013, continuatio ns 
held on February 13th, February 27th; March 4th, March 16th, March 23rd, and continued to this date.   
 
Wesley Flis, Whitman & Bingham Associates, represent ing the Applicant, presented a waiver letter  for Aro Estates 8 from Rules 
and Regulations Governing the Subdivision of Land,  Section 4.10.4.6. “At least three (3) feet of cover will be required over all 
drains unless otherwise specified or approved by the Board.”.  Motion made, Ms. Bilotta-Simeone to accept Waiver, Second, Mr. 
Bodkin Jr., Roll Call Vote, Mr. Lockwood, aye; Mr. Bodkin Jr., aye; Ms. Bilotta -Simeone, aye; Mr. Bakaysa, aye.  Waiver 
accepted. 
 
An in house mee ting was held this date with the Applicant, Project Engineer, Reviewing Engineer and Planning Director.  As a 
result of that meeting and J. Johnson’s review letter dated March 29, 2013, some changes will be made to the Plans.  Changes 
to drainage calculations will be made.  (Review letter on file in the Planning Office.)   
 
Rennie and Anne Shattuck, 396 Howard Street - Concerned with stormwater drainage onto their property.  Mr. Flis noted there 
will be a decrease to runoff into their property.  Mr. Oliva will note concern to J. Johnson and get input.  Applicant’s attorney Mark 
Johnson noted meetings taking place (this is a civil matter) between Mr. O’Brien’s attorney and the Shattuck’s attorney.  
 
Hearing will continue to Wednesday, April 10, 2013 , Ritter Memorial Building, 6:30 PM.   Motion made, Mr. Bodkin Jr. , Second, 
Ms. Bilotta-Simeone, Motion passed.  (Town Counsel Attorney Brian Winner to attend.)  After the 10th, Town Counsel anticipates 
working on preparing a draft Decision.   
 
Applicant and Board agreed on an extension for final action to April 30, 2013.  Extension will be filed with the Town Clerk. 
 
Board meetings will be scheduled for Saturday, April 13, 2013, 9:00 AM, Town Hall and Monday, April 22, 2013, 6:30 PM, Town 
Hall.  Only agenda item will be Aro Estates Subdivisions 1 – 10.  (Town Counsel Attorney Joel Bard to attend.)   
 
Motion to adjourn, Mr. Bodkin Jr., Second, Ms. Bilotta-Simeone, Adjourned 7:25 PM.
