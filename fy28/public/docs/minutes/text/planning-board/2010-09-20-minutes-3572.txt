Director presented data notebooks to the members.  Notebooks consisted of the following: 
MGL (Massachusetts General Law), Ch. 41, Section 81 D, Master or Study Plan 
CLURPA (Comprehensive Land Use Reform and Partnersh ip Act) 
Master Plan 2002 
Envision 2007 
Master Plan Review 2008 
Thomas Planning Assessment 
Update (draft) to Land Use section of 2002 Master P lan 
 
Chair led a discussion session on the organization of a Master Plan.  The topics were Contents, Goals,  Land Use Plan, Housing, 
Economic Development, Natural Resources, Open Space , Circulation, Implementation, Amendments, Economic  Development 
Supplement and a new Energy Component.    
 
Several topics arose from the discussions on the in tent of each of the Elements.  The proposed law tha t has been designed to 
replace MGL Ch. 41 does not demand all present Elem ents in a Master Plan.  Some of these Elements will  be optional.  Other 
ideas proposed were an Economic Development Ad Hoc Committee, reviewing present and future commercial and industrial 
expansion, regionalization, conservation ownership,  and housing. The schools will be a major part of t he Facilities Element. Data 
and discussion will be held with the School Superin tendent/School Committees.  
  
Following discussion on each topic, it was noted th at involvement of all Boards, Commissions and Depar tments, as well as 
representation from the general public, would be br ought together for input.  Past procedure was that meetings were held in the 
school libraries. There were groups of 8-10 persons  at each table who reviewed, discussed and came to consensus.  All was put 
in report form for final presentation to the public  as a whole.   
 
The conclusion of the meeting was that the Board me mbers review all the data in their notebooks.  The next workshop meeting 
will be held on October 18, 2010. Discussion will e nsue on the Goals, and the Land Use Plan. 
 
Motion to adjourn, Mr. Lockwood, Second, Mr. Saiia,  Motion passed. Adjournment at 8:50 PM.
