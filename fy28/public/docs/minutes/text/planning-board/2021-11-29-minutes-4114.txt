APPOINTMENT:  234 Summer Street:  At the Board’s November 15 th meeting, Stanley Miller, 234 Summer Street, submitted a 
request for De Minimis change to remove a fence between 234  and 236 Summer Street and reinstall it along the frontage of 240.  
At that meeting, the Board questioned the ownership of the subject address as the Assessor Maps did not reflect the Millers as 
the property owners.  A. Burney had confirmed with the Assessor’s Office that the Millers were the current owners.  P. Bertram 
made Motion to approve the De Minimis change for fence removal  at 234 Summer Street, M. Allison Seconded, passed 
unanimously.
