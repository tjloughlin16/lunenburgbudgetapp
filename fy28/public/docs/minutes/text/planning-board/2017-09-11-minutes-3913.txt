Open Space Ad Hoc Committee- No report.
Charter Review Committee- The Committee had its first meeting and elected officers- Chair, Bob Ebersole; Vice Chair, Jamie
Toale; Clerk, Michael-Ray Jeffreys. M. Jeffreys has recommended the Committee adopt a four-phase process; i.e., appoint a
liaison for under-represented committees, establish work groups, draft legislation, public outreach. The goal of the Committee is
to have an updated charter available for vote at May 2018 Town Meeting. The next meeting will be September 27th.

draft. A. Burney will solicit input from both the fire and police departments as to lighting under #8. Street Furniture.
