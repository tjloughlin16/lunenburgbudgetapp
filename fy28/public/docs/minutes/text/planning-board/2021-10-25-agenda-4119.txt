TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
PUBLIC HEARING 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on October 25 , 2021, 6:35  p.m., Town Hall, 17 
Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to  make alterations to Code of the Town of Lunenburg, MA, Chapter 250, 
Zoning, Article IV Use Regulations, Section 250-4.9. Water Supply Protection District, B.(1), by updating the 
Lunenburg Water District Aquifer Protection Zone map date, Article V, Dimensional Regulations , Section 
250-5.1. Lot Area, B., by deleting the Retail Commercial and defining the required lot area, required lot 
width through building and required lot width and frontage for Limited Business/Residential,  Article III, 
Establishment of Dis tricts, 250-3.1. by replacing G. Retail Commercial District with Village Center District, 
250-3.2. Location of Districts by amending the zoning map date to November 16, 2021 , and to amend the 
current Zoning Map to reflect the change in the Water Supply Protection District.   Materials are available 
for viewing at the Planning Office, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.  This meeting will be 
broadcast live through local access cable, on FacebookLive on the Public Access Face book page 
(https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-157427270975305/) and will be able to be 
found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel within 24 hours  after the meeting.   To participate 
remotely: from a computer, please use the link below to join the public hearing webinar:  
https://us02web.zoom.us/j/82275299684 or from a telephone, dial the toll fre e number (888) 475 -4499 and 
enter meeting ID: 82275299684.
