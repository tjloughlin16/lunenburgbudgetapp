Public Hearing
1054 Northfield Road
June 11, 2018
Meeting Posted: Yes
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA
Time: 6:35 PM
Present: Gregory Bittner, Matthew Allison, David Prokowiew, Tanner Cole, Adam R. Burney
Absent: Kenneth Chenis
The Hearing was to hear and discuss a request for the cutting of trees and construction of a driveway at 1054 Northfield Road
(Map 036, Parcel 0032) under M.G.L. Ch. 40, Section 15C, Scenic Road Designations and under M.G.L. Ch. 87, Section 3,
Cutting of Public Shade Trees.
Board member T. Cole disclosed that he owns property at 1083 Northfield Road.
Owners/Applicants Brian and Erica Burns made the presentation. Trees and a portion of an existing stone wall need to be
removed to allow access for delivery of two fourteen (14) by fifty-four (54') foot boxes containing the portions of a modular home.
A total of seventeen (17) trees would be removed, but the Applicants need Board approval for only eleven (11) as six (6) are less
than six (6) inches in diameter and do not require approval under the Scenic Road Act. The largest tree to be removed is sixty-
nine (69") inches circumference (twenty-two (22") in diameter). Up to thirty-two (32) feet of the existing stonewall will need to be
removed for construction access and delivery of the modular sections. Thirty (30') feet of the stone wall will be rebuilt with the
remaining foot on each side allowing for a twenty (20) foot driveway.
