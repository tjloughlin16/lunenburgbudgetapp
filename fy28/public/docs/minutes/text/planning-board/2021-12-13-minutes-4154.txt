Minutes 
Public Hearing – Stormwater Permit 
324 Flat Hill Road 
December 13, 2021 
 
 
Meeting Posted: Yes  
Place: Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA  
Time:  7:16 p.m. 
 
Present:  Tanner Cole, Matthew Allison, Paula Bertram, Adam Burney 
Absent:  Matthew Brenner, Erik Torgersen 
 
The Hearing continuation was to hear and review a stormwater management permit under Chapter 2 04, Stormwater and Storm 
Sewers, for 324 Flat Hill Road (Map 087, Parcel 26.1), Lunenburg, MA.  Owner/applicant was Matson Homes II Inc., 129 Mason 
Road, New Ipswich, NH  03071.    
 
Representing the owner/applicant were Mark Elbag, M.A. Elbag Engineering a nd Ken Matson, Matson Homes II, Inc.  A. Burney 
summarized Board concerns from the November 29 th hearing, mainly the location of the reserve area.  The review letter from 
Graves Engineering, Inc. did not indicate any issues.  The Board of Health Agent provided A. Burney with correspondence noting 
that the location of the stormwater facilities in proximity to the reserve area did not pose issues.  The Agent also noted he did not 
have concerns with the stormwater/drain features as shown on the Plan.  If the redesign of the system changed anything 
significantly, he would notify the Board.  P. Bertram made Motion to close the hearing for 324 Flat Hill Road, M. Allison 
Seconded, passed unanimously.   
 
Hearing closed 7:20 p.m.
