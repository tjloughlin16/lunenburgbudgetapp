Public Hearing was to hear and discuss a Special Permit under Section 4.4.3. and a Development Plan Review under Section 
8.4. of the Protective Bylaw of the Town of Lunenburg  for an in -home antique and consignment shop at 909 Massachusetts 
Avenue (Map 60, Parcel 0003).  Owner is Mary L. Padula, 909 Mass Ave, Lunenburg, MA.  Applicant is DJ Layhe, 100 Lancaster 
Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.   
 
Presentation by DJ Layhe.  Currently owns  antique business at 3 Lancaster Avenue.  Has moved to 909 Massachusetts Avenue 
and plans to relocate business there.  Antique business is a slow flow business – asking for operation hours of Wednesday - 
Saturday 10-5 (maybe Wednesday nights ‘till 8 PM).  Sunday 10-5, closed Monday and Tuesday.   No staff other than family that 
is residing in the house.  Having shop in home would reduce expenses.  Would request same type sign as currently at 3 
Lancaster Avenue.  Business will be named Harley House Décor.  Wants to maintain current traffic flow of separate entrance and 
exit, but due to poor sight lines would like to have “entrance” and “exit” stenciled on a four foot post at both locations.  Would also 
like a 3 by 5 foot  flag attached to each post .  One flag would say “consignment” and the other “furniture”.  Sample flag was 
shown.  The shop will be at the rear of the house.  Would like to place  an eighteen by eighteen inch “ open” or “entrance” sign at 
door as there are numerous doors to the building .  Would like to have one free standing sign  of forty-eight by thirty-two inches at 
front of the property.   
 
M. Allison suggested applicant consider a white arrow painted on the paved portion of the drive indicating direction of trave l.  He 
also suggested a “do not enter” sign placed at the exit. 
 
Lois Filiau, 919 Mass Ave - supports the antique business but was concerned with the two large proposed flags .  Suggested a 
wooden colonial sign. 
 
Donna Cameron, 919 Mass Ave- concerned with flags obstructing view of traffi c if the day is windy.  Asked if a site plan review 
was done.  No site plan review being proposed as it is being changed to a lesser use and only change is signage.  Applicant 
hopes to purchase the property at some point in the future.
