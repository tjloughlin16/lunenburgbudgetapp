The Hearing continuation was to hear and discuss a  stormwater management special permit under Ch. 204 of the Code of the 
Town of Lunenburg  for an earth removal project at 1325 Massachusetts Avenue, Map 062, Parcel 0003.   Owners/Applicants 
were Daniel and Jean Proctor, 1529 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.   
 
Owners/applicants w ere represented by Frank McPartlan , Ducharme & Dillis Civil Design Gr oup, Inc. (‘ DDCDG’).  DDCDG 
presented a revised Plan to addre ss issues brought forth at the February 10 th Hearing continuation.  Revised plans show ed an 
increase in buffer from 20 to 50 feet on the north and east sides of the property.  As a result of the increased buffer, the yield of 
earth removal f rom the parcel  would be reduced.  The Applicant now anticipate d removing 25,000 cubic yards instead of the 
original 50,000.  There w ould also be additional screening along the north property line.  At the abutt er’s request, s creening on 
the east side ha d been changed f rom arborvitae to white pine.  DDCDG summarized its February 28, 2020  letter (on file in the 
Planning Office), which addressed comments f rom the Planning Board, Conservation Commission and M assDEP.  Above the 
soil erosion fencing at the top of the basin , temporary sediment basins would be constructed on an as needed basis.  P. Bertram 
raised concerns she wanted addressed; e.g., the infiltration rate , the depth of the borings , the location of the sep tic system for 
the nearest abutter in proximity to the end point of the infiltration system.  She wanted to see the test pits overlaid on the basin.  
As stated in previous meetings, DDCDG stressed that the stormwater system ha d been over-designed, even mor e so now in 
light of the reduction in yield of earth removal.   
 
DDCDG requested the Hearing be continued to the next Board meeting on March 23, 2020.  P. Bertram made Motion to 
continue the Stormwater Hearing for 1325 Massachusetts Avenue to March 23, 202 0, 6:35 PM, T. Cole Seconded, all aye. 
 
Hearing recessed 7:28 PM.
