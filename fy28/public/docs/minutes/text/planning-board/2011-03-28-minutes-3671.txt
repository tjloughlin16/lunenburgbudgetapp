Minutes
Executive Session
March 28, 2011
Chair called for Motion to enter Executive Session at 7:45 PM for purpose of litigation discussion. Motion, Ms. Bilotta, Second,
Mr. Bodkin Jr., roll call vote, Mr. Bakaysa, aye; Ms. Bilotta, aye; Mr. Lockwood, aye; Mr. Saiia, aye; Mr. Bodkin Jr., aye.
Chair reviewed correspondence from Town Counsel Attorney Joel Bard. Noted was an electronic mail dated March 24, 2011
concerning status. Attorney Bard was to file a Motion to enter Judgment. The entry for Judgment ends the trial portion of the
case. Two new stages will now begin; the appeal portion and the filing of a new definitive plan.
The Board discussed the status and the procedure leading to filing for Judgment. Board members expressed dissatisfaction with
the procedure. Town Counsel was given several directions to enter Judgment and kept coming back to the Board with a different
request in regard to timing and presentation of a conceptual plan.
Board member Attorney Bodkin suggested that the Board might want to entertain using the services of a different attorney and
that the Board should seek the record of spending on this case. Further, an estimate should be sought for the cost of the service
for the remaining preparation for this litigation.
Chair called for adjournment of Executive Session. Motion, Ms. Bilotta, Second, Mr. Lockwood, roll call vote, Mr. Bakaysa, aye;
Ms. Bilotta, aye; Mr. Lockwood, aye; Mr. Saiia, aye; Mr. Bodkin Jr., aye. Adjourned 8:30 PM.
