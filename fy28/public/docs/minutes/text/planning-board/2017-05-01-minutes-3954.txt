Minutes
Public Hearing
Town Meeting Warrant Article
May 1, 2017
Posted: Yes
Place: Ritter Memorial Building, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA
Time: 6:30 PM
Present: Matthew Allison, Damon McQuaid, Gregory Bittner, David Prokowiew, Adam R. Burney
Absent: Kenneth Chenis
The Hearing opened with the reading of the notice published in the Sentinel and Enterprise on April 17, 2017 and
April 24, 2017. The Hearing was to enact certain changes to the text of the Protective Bylaw as noted by deletions
and additions as set forth in the document entitled FINAL DRAFT (RED LINE VERSION) February 21, 2017", on file
in the office of the Town Clerk. Notices were sent to abutting towns, cities, agencies and Town departments.
A. Burney noted there are no substantive changes to the zoning bylaws in the recodification. He did note that the
earth removal bylaw, currently in the general bylaws, will be moved to the zoning bylaws. If the Article passes at
Town Meeting, the Planning Board will need to hold a public hearing for the Rules and Regulations Governing the
Subdivision of Land to place them into the same numbering scheme.
i. Bittner Motion to recommend favorably the changes to the text of the Protective Bylaw by additions and additions as
set forth in the document entitled Final Draft (Red Line Version), February 21, 2017, Second, D. Prokowiew, all aye
Hearing closed 6:40 PM.
