PACC Meeting Minutes
April 4, 2011
L.H.S. Rm. 228
Members Present: Rhonda Lisio, Martha Cheesman, Lance May, Fred Crellin
Also Present: John Gugarty, Jo McLaughlin
Call to Order: 7:06pm
Minutes: Read and approved
Progress Report - John Gugarty:
John reported on a series of interviews with the language dept. and a 3 part series on
bullying. He also attempted to explain why the mock trial team from LHS was not
filmed (dead battery, overslept). Members expressed displeasure and asked that he
convey his apologies to those affected. There was discussion of a planned training
program for Tuesday. Additional ideas/recommendations were discussed. (the musical,
interview with Pete McCauliff regarding possible sports programming, graduation)
Facility Update -
With regard to the Brooks House, Jo will meet with Nancy Strom regarding internet
service and will also check on phone service.
Progress Report - Jo McLaughlin: See "Progress Report March 2011-April 2011"
Old Business -
The recommended By-law Changes were not accepted at Town Hall because they
were submitted one day late. They will therefore be submitted for the fall Town Meeting.
Recruitment of new members: It was suggested that a request for new members
of PACC be placed on the scroll on the town's cable channels.
There was a discussion concerning a possible restructuring of the duties of our
coordinator positions with the production coordinator reporting directly to Jo McLaughlin
for greater coordination.
Meeting Adjourned: 9:44pm
