Meeting Minutes 
AGRICULTURAL COMMISSION 
September 19, 2019 
Ritter Memorial Building 
 960 Massachusetts Avenue 
 
Present: M. Allison, R. Stober, G. Burns, Y. Fusaro, M. Fontaine 
Chair called the meeting to order at 7:04pm.   
 
General discussion focused on R. Stober relating a Parks Commission meeting where 
candidates for a new Recreation Director position.  Also that Matt ‘Woody’ Woodward donated 
use of a crane at Cherry Hill Farm. 
 
M. Allison announced the Grange pumpkin carving event on October 18 at the Eagle House.  R. 
Stober had been contacted about a conflict regarding proximity of a rooster to the neighbors 
home, but neither party appeared at the meeting.  Hollis Hill Farm is mostly back up and running 
after suffering an electrical fire in the main store/barn.   
 
LCFM update:  total of 37 applications received to date, 16-22 vendors and average about 300 
attendees to the market every Sunday.  One vendor, Tully Farm, received the 2019 “Green 
Pasture Award” for Massachusetts’ dairy farm of the year.  Managers submitted request for 
reimbursement of expenses.  R. Stober moved to reimburse $725, G. Burns 2 
nd , all aye. 
 
A short discussion was had about AgCom and market hats, tees, bags and other marketing. 
 
Next meeting set for October 17. 
 
R. Stober moved to adjourn, G. Burns second, adjourned at 8:28pm.
