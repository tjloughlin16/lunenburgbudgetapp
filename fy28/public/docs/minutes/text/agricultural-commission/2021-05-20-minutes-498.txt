Meeting Minutes 
AGRICULTURAL COMMISSION 
May 20, 2021 
Zoom Meeting 
 
Present: M. Allison, R. Stober, G. Burns, J. Marzuca, M. Holman, K. McClellan 
Chair called the meeting to order at 7:03pm.   
 
General discussion: M. Allison stated that he had finally sent the letter on farm complaints out.  
Discussion was had about existing regulations affecting farms and farming in Lunenburg.  Our 
agendas now have general topics specific to farm complaints and Farmers Market Updates. 
M. Holman read the Market Update.  Reorganization was tabled as appointments had not been 
made by the Select Board.  A new MGL was discussed that requires BOH to discuss any new 
regulations affecting agriculture with the AgCom.  M. Holman gave a detailed presentation on 
her progress with the flyer to advertise farming in Lunenburg.  J. Marzuca asked about setting 
up a composting program. 
 
Next meeting set for June 17, 2021. 
 
R. Stober moved to adjourn, M. Holman 2nd.  Rollcall, all aye.  Adjourned at 8:05pm.
