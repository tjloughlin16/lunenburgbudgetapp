APR -  
 Can apply to put land into APR with minimum of 
5 acres  
 Must be actively farmed for at least past two years 
 Soil testing is performed 
 If approved, the agreement is with the state for 
ROFR if you sell, but landowner is paid difference 
in market value and ag land value 
 Lower taxes 
 Land cannot be developed even after sale 
 Buyer needs to be approved by MDAR. Buyer 
needs to have a business plan 
 Land sold in two lots, one for the house and one 
for the ag land. The house and land do not need to 
be sold to the same buyer, but they can be. 
Difficult to buy just the house unless the state is 
buying the land.  
 When approved for APR, the lot is divided into 
two lots, one for the house and one for the APR 
land. 
 APR has to make money $500/ first 5 acres, and 
$5 additional per additional acre
