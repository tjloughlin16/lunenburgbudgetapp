MVP - Municipal Vulnerability Preparedness Program 
Created in 2017 to identify climate hazards and develop 
action plans to create resiliency again climate change 
MVP Communities must have workshops to identify 
vulnerabilities and threats and create and action plan to 
increase a towns resiliency (withstand, respond to, and 
take actions to mitigate in this case climate change). Ex: 
changes to processes such as change school times to 
avoid high heat in summer, changes to structure such as 
to add a new sea wall, new culvert to adapt to frequent 
flooding.  
MVP Communities are able to apply for Action Grants 
which reflect actions in action plan 
MRPC works with 22 communities from Groton to 
Hubbardston to Athol areas 
Regional approach has more support and potential for 
more success
