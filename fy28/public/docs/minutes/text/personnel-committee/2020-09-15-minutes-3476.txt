Town of Lunenburg 
Personnel Committee 
Minutes:  September 15, 2020 - Zoom Meeting 
___________________________________________________________________________________ 
Attendees: 
Dave MacDonald, Eric Arnold, Sue Andrews, Donna Fortune, Joanna Bilotta  
Also Present: 
Heather Lemieux, Town Manager, Julie Belliveau, Asst. Town Manager/HR Director 
Meeting Began at 5:00pm with all attendee's online; Eric Arnold presiding as Chairman 
Approval of Minutes: 
Minutes of the July 15, 2020 meeting reviewed 
Motion by D. MacDonald to accept the minutes as presented; Second E. Arnold 
Roll Call Vote:   All in Favor 
New Business: 
New Members: 
E. Arnold introduced two new members to the Personnel Committee, Joanna Bilotta and Donna 
Fortune.  Both bring broad experience in managing Human Resource programs and projects and are a 
welcome addition to this group. 
Reorganization of committee: 
With the recent changes in the committee membership, the group discussed how it would be organized 
during the next appointment period.  
Motion by D. MacDonald to nominate E. Arnold as the committee Chairman; Second: J. Bilotta 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
Motion by D. MacDonald to nominate J. Bilotta as Vice Chairman; Second: E. Arnold 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
Motion by D. MacDonald to nominate S. Andrews as Clerk; Second:  D. Fortune 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
Article for Special Town Meeting: 
H. Lemieux presented the proposed article to the Special Town Meeting that would recommend the 
updating of the Salary Administration Plan to include two new positions: Principal Assessor and 
Intermittent Heavy Equipment Operator.  Both positions had been reviewed by this committee.  The 
Principal Assessor's job description and grade level assignment at Grade 15, were reviewed and 
===PAGE 2===
approved at that time.  The job description for the Intermittent Heavy Equipment Operator was 
presented and a general discussion covered the role and recommended grading at Grade 9 at this 
meeting. It was determined that the job description allowed for flexibility in the direction of various 
public works assignments by the Director of Public Works. 
Motion by J. Bilotta to approve the recommendation with amendment to clarify tree work for the 
Intermittent Heavy Equipment Operator, to be placed as a Grade 9 in the SAP. It will be submitted to the 
Special Town Meeting for final approval by the Board of Selectman.   Second:  E. Arnold 
Roll Call Vote:  All in Favor 
Motion:  J. Bilotta:  Adjourn meeting at 5:35pm    Second:  S. Andrews    Roll Call Vote to Adjourn: All in 
Favor
