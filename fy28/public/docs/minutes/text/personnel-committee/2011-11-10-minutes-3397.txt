November 10, 2011
PERSONNEL COMMITTEE MEETING MINUTES
At 6:00 p.m. the Chair called the meeting to order. Present were Chair Robert Rand, William Murray,
and Deborah Christen. Barbara Reynolds was absent.
On-going Business:
1. Public Comment: None.
Current Business:
1. Town Meeting Article: Discussion about placing an article on the warrant of the upcoming
Special Town Meeting to amend Article XV of the Town's By-law entitled "Salary
Administration Plan of the Town of Lunenburg" (copy on file in the Selectmen's and Towr
Clerk's office), by modifying the section Alphabetical Listing of Class Titles to add new job
titles and remove obsolete titles, or take any other action relative thereto. LPC voted
unanimously to submit the above proposed article for the Special Town Meeting ((18t- William,
2nd - Deb).
2. Next Meeting Agenda: Standard On-going (Public Comment, Personnel Actions/Approvals,
Personnel Change/Hiring Notifications, and Review of Previous Minutes) and Current
Business to include Job Description Format Discussion, Discussion on, Review of Progress
and Assignments of LPC Initiatives for 2011, Town Report, Articles for Annual Town Meeting,
and Next Meeting Agenda.
The next scheduled meeting will be on November 15, 2011 at 6:00 p.m. at the Lunenburg Public
Library. The meeting adjourned (Deb 1s1, William 21ª) at 6:06 p.m.
Respectfully submitted,
Deborah Christen
Deborah Christen, Secretary
