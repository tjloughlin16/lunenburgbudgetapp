THE LUNENBURG CEMETERY COMMISSION MET ON SEPTEMBER 21, 2015 AT THE LUNENBURG
PUBLIC LIBRARY.
MEMBERS PRESENT WERE LANCE MAY, LORRAINE MENDOZA AND STEVE SCHAFF WITH GUEST,
HISTORIAN, RICHARD McGRATH.
NO MINUTES WERE AVAILABLE TO REVIEW.
NO PUBLIC DISCUSSION TOOK PLACE.
THE MEETING CAME TO ORDER WITH CHAIR MAY RECOGNIZING HISTORIAN RICHARD MCGRATH,
WHO APPEARED BEFORE THE COMMISSION TO DISCUSS HIS PROPOSAL TO RESTORE THE NORTH
STREET CEMETERY TOMB.
RICHARD DURING HIS PRESENTATION OFFERED TO RESTORE THE BRICK WORK, RE-CALK AND RE-
POINT THE BRICK AND GRANITE FOUNDATION, SCRAPE, PAINT, AND CALK THE WOODEN FASCIA
AND BRACKETS, TRIM THE HEDGE 15' FROM THE BUILDING AND REMOVE POISON IVY.
HE STATED ALL OF THE MATERIALS AND WORK WOULD BE DONATED AND PAID FOR THRU
GRANTS AND DONATIONS. THE TOTAL ESTIMATED COST TO BE $15,000.00.
THE COMMISSION REQUESTED FROM RICHARD, THE FOLLOWING:
A FUNDING MECHANISM, WHEN WILL THE DONATIONS+ GRANTS BE APPLIED FOR.?
WHEN WOULD A REASONABLE TIME TABLE BE ESTABLISHED TO HAVE THESE FUNDS AVAILABLE
FOR USE?
WHO WILL BE DOING THE PROPOSED WORK?
CHAIR MAY INQUIRED ABOUT SECURING INFO ON A BOND OR LIABILITY COVERAGE THAT MIGHT
BE REQUIRED.
A QUESTION ON ADDT'L BIDS WAS RAISED WITH THE DETERMINATION TO BE MADE AT A LATER
DATE.
A QUESTION ON WHO WILL WRITE THE GRANT AND SEEK PRIVATE DONATIONS WAS ASKED -AS
WELL-AS WHAT WOULD BE THE TIME-TABLE TO SECURE THE GRANT AND DONATED S.
RICHARD WOULD COLLECT THIS INFORMATION AND GET BACK TO THE COMMISSION
NO FURTHER BUSINESS CAME BEFORE THE COMMISSSION AND THERE WAS NO PUBLIC
COMMENT, A MOTION WAS MADE BY STEVE SCHAFF TO CLOSE THE MEETING AND SECONDED BY
LORRAINE MENDOZA TO ADJOURN. THE VOTE WAS UNANIMOUS.
RESPECTFULLY SUBMITTED,
LORRAINE MENDOZA
