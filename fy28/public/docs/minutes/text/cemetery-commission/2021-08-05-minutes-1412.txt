Minutes 
Cemetery Commission Meeting 
Thursday August 5, 2021 – 3:30pm 
Conference Room DPW Bldg. 
 
 
Meeting was called to order at 3:30pm by Chairman Tyler.  Present were 
Commissioners Yasko, Dorren and DPW Director Oliva. 
 
No one was present from the public  
 
 
Meeting started with Chairman Tyler advising the commission that the Garage at the 
North Cemetery will be painted on Friday August 6th or Monday August 9th by All Phases 
Contracting. DPW Director Oliva stated he would have the container and bricks put in 
the back of the garage before the Lance May dedication in September.  
 
Discussions were also centered on trimming the Hedges at the North Cemetery along 
Holman Street.  DPW Director Oliva will get quotes.  Commissioner Dorren mentioned 
how difficult it was to exit the roads from the cemetery due to the hedges hindering the 
site path on Holman Street.  Chairman Tyler mentioned that depending on the costs 
received we may want to start the trimming of the hedges around the exit roadways first 
and then due the rest at a later time.  
 
 
DPW Oliva said that one of the part time positions has been filled yet at the cemetery. 
That person will start next week.

The topic was brought up again about the new parking lot at the North Cemetery being 
opened up this Winter.  Chairman Tyler mentioned after considerable review it is his 
opinion that we should not open the parking lot during the winter months.  There may 
be a lot of conflict and confusion by the public on why one section is open at the 
Cemetery and not the rest of the Cemetery.  A 3-0 vote was taken to keep the Cemetery 
closed during the winter. 
 
 
Corrected minutes of the June 24,2021 board meeting were read and approved by 
Commissioners Yasko, Dorren and Chairman Tyler.  
 
 
 
In new business, Chairman Tyler brought up about the Security at the North 
Cemetery. Commissioner Dorren stated that there was not enough Solar to install the 
cameras and the distance the cameras provide is not long enough to help us secure the 
North Cemetery.  Chairman Tyler suggested a camera be put on the Cemetery Garage 
facing the last entrance on Holman Street.  Chairman Tyler and Commissioners Yasko 
and Dorren approved. 
 
 
Commissioner Yasko mentioned the stone walls at North Cemetery having fallen in a few 
places. Commissioner Tyler will contact a local stone mason to see if he would help with 
rebuilding.  
   
 
Commissioner Tyler asked Director Oliva why the Commission was paying for rubbish 
removal at the cemeteries. After a short discussion Director Oliva stated that going 
forward, this cost will be absorbed within the DPW budget.  
   
 
Commissioner Tyler asked Director Oliva if he was familiar with surveying, pertaining to 
the laying out of new plots that are needed at South Cemetery. He stated he would look 
into it.
