1. Call to Order
• Terri Burchfield, Chair called the meeting to order at 6:30pm.
2. Public Comment
• Dave Passios commented that he and Michelle Walton attended the MMA
Association of Town Finance Committees meeting on 10/21. Between
them they approximately 10 sessions.
Mr. Passios reported on a presentation from the town of Arlington, which
appropriates their capital planning as 5% of the overall operating budget,
rather than a function of free cash as we do.
The state auditor is still working on the effects of new educational funding
formulas.
3. Informal Discussion with Sue Doherty, Eagle House Director
• Ms. Doherty reported on a number of programs and issues that are
concern to the Council on Aging and Eagle House.
• Current documents indicate that the current number of Lunenburg
residents age 60 or over exceeds 3000, up from approximately 2100 in the
2010 census.
The 55+ development at Tri-Town landing will further effect those #'s.
There is growing need for another van and driver due to increasing
requests for services.
===PAGE 2===
4. Town Manager Report
• Selectman have signed a Memorandum of Agreement with the Police
Officers Union. Annual increases will be 2.5%, 2%, and 2% respectively
over the 3-year contract. Additional cost to fund the contract over previous
cost will be $139,000 over the 3-year term of the contract. There were also
changes to COLA, overtime, educational benefits, and longevity bonus
payments.
• New growth was certified today for $88,222. Town manager recommends
to follow-through with provisions of Article 2 on the Special Town Meeting
warrant, appropriating those funds to the Special Purpose Stabilization
Fund.
5. Informal Discussion with Dr. Kate Burnham, Superintendent of
Schools, and Michael Cassidy, Finance and HR Director
• There may be a need to expand behaviorial services programs at Turkey
Hill Elementary School
• Projected need for an additional kindergarten class due to increasing
birthrates, which would require 1 teacher and 1 paraprofessional.
Rationale for a data specialist was discussed.
Discussion of budget-building process. This year's budget will likely bring
more focus on deeper-learning and emotional well-being. Probably a social
worker at lower grade-levels.
Discussion of a budget process that includes both public comment and
time for deliberation.
6. Public Comment
• School Committee Member Carol Archambault thanked everyone for
organizing, presenting, and attending the symposium.
• Resident Rhonda Lisio commented that she believes the Finance
Committee is overreaching.
7. Next Meetings:
November 12, 2019 Special Town Meeting
