1. Mark E. called the Finance Committee meeting of March 31st to order at 7:00 pm. He announced that this
meeting is being recorded and broadcast over the Lunenburg Public Access Cable Channel. The video
recording is posted on YouTube at https://www.youtube.com/user/LunenburgAccess
2. Comments:
Public: None
• New Communications:
• Mark read a notice of a Planning Board Informational meeting at Town Hall on April 25" to hear and
discuss two planning issues.
• Denise Baker of the MMA informed the Finance Committee that the MMA will have three workshops on
Town Finance issues. The topics are managing debt, building a capital plan, and planning for and
presenting at town meeting. Mark will forward the email to all committee members.
• Mark announced that we are a committee of seven members. This committee currently has five
members-only one member more than the required minimum for a quorum. If two or more members
cannot be present for a meeting, the committee cannot meet. He invites any interested citizens to get a
talent bank form from the Town Clerk's office, complete it, and hand it in.
