4. Public Access Cable Committee (PACC) FY16 Budget Presentation by Steve Walker
Note: Click for slides of the PACC presentation that provide more detail than is included in these minutes.
Purpose:
i. PACC provides local programming, public service announcements, Board of Selectmen
meetings and, occasionally, other meetings of other boards and committees on channels 8 and
9.
ii. Viewers can also view videotaped programs on LunenburgAccess
B. Goals:
i. Expand programming and increase production quality.
ii. Recruit and train volunteers.
C. Resources and budget:
i.
The sole source of revenues for Lunenburg Public Access is a fee paid by Comcast for the
privilege of providing cable to residents of the Town. This fee totals about $92,000 per year
and is paid quarterly.
•
No taxpayer funds are provided for the operation or equipping of Lunenburg Public
Access.
ii. The projected balance of accumulated Comcast payments at the end of FY15 is $301,292.
These funds are for paying operating expenses and capital investments.
ili. FY16 projected operating budget is $147,500. The largest expense--payroll and consulting
services--is $75,000 of that total.
D. Investments being planned or under consideration:
i. Planned in FY16 budget: Update and upgrade existing and aging equipment.
ii. Under consideration: Purchasing a mobile unit.

5. Sewer Commission and Enterprise Fund FY16 Budget Presentation by Carl Luck
Note: Click for slides of the Sewer Commission presentation that provide more detail than is included in
these minutes.
A. Purpose: The Sewer Commission provides sewer services to neighborhoods of need identified by the
Town's Comprehensive Wastewater Management Plan (CWMP). This includes:
i. Assuring capacity is adequate to meet demands on the system.
Maintaining a capacity bank.
• Reducing Inflow/Infiltration (1/1).
• Upgrading and maintaining pump stations.
Improving user compliance with grease trap policy through education and
enforcement.
il. Planning, implementing and managing extensions of sewers to areas of need.
B. Revenue note: The sole sources of revenues are user fees and betterments; thus no taxpayer funds are
provided for the operation or capital equipment of the Sewer Commission.
C. Revenues and Expenses-FY15 and FY16:
i. In FY15 the budget is that revenues equal expenses-retained earnings do not change.
ii. The FY16 budget is for revenues to increase by about $104.000—12.8%, and expenses
increase by $34,000-4.2%.
Retained earnings would increase by nearly $70,000.
D. Enterprise Fund Tracking and Projections: Revenues, Expenses, Retained Earnings
Note: Tracking and projecting the enterprise fund balance is a process that is being refined by
the Sewer Commission. As it is refined, this projection will likely change.
Based on the current projections of revenue from betterments and associated revenues and
fees, the Sewer Enterprise Fund would decline from $1,200,000 in FY15 to about $100,000 in
•
Projected revenue is a baseline that does not include any new betterment revenues
It is likely there will be revenues from betterments in the next decade.
ii. Tracking and projecting the Enterprise Fund will be done annually. So the Town will be
updated on any issues that need to be addressed.
