What Does Loss of Certification Mean?
• No inter-library loans!
• This means that there would be no resource sharing
for Lunenburg residents. Lunenburg citizens would
not be able to borrow items from other libraries in
the Commonwealth, nor could other communities
borrow items from Lunenburg.
• The Library would Tose State Aid monies for FY 2012:
this is over $12,000. This aid funds our software
and partially funds our network fees. These fees
would have to be funded elsewhere.
• A decertified library must meet state aid
requirements for an entire fiscal year, before it may
re-apply for certification. This is not a 1 year cut to
services!
