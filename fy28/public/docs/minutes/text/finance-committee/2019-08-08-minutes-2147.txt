6. Committee/Department updates/assignments
Peter Beardmore reported on the school Committee meeting on 31 July,
which was convened for the purpose of addressing the elementary school
busing / drop-off procedure, requiring that elementary-age students must
have a parent or older sibling meeting them at drop-off (5th grade is
waiver-able). Walkers policy was also changed, effectively ending
unaccompanied students walking home from school (except for grade 5).
Unclear as to if there will be any spending ramifications of these policy
changes. Also, there have been no enrollment spikes as of July 24th, Also, 9
students have been accepted into school-choice during the summer. Out-
going school-choice numbers have not been discussed. Also, there have
been findings of lead and copper during water testing this summer. There
will be communication to parents upcoming. Also, school starts on August
27h, Also, after a food-services audit, lunch costs have been set at $3
across the system. Also, there have been 2 new secretaries hired, there has
been one additional retirement announced, and two resignations. Peter
Beardmore has asked if there will be a budget update on the effects off
changes that occurred during the summer.
