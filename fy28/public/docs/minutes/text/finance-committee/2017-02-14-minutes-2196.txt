2. CPC presentation:
The Facilities Maintenance had requested $248,050 to fund five projects. Three of the projects were
rated a priority one by the CPC. The CPC recommended $216,050 for three priority one projects.

rated as a priority one. The CPC recommend $65,767 for the two priority one items.

recommended $145K for the one priority project.

rated a priority. The CPC recommended $25,000 for the Fingerprint system.

for trade offs.
Allot any available cash over Capital recommendation to a "reserve" fund to fund large expense items in
the future.

3. Town Manager Report: Budget recommendations FY18: The Town manager is new to Lunenburg. She just
started in her new position on January 17th of this year.
The TM started the budget presentation with some disclaimers: There are a number of assumptions
that are include with this preliminary budget. Some of these numbers will firm up as we get closer to
Town Meeting. State assessments, Insurance renewals, State aid, free cash estimate, Monty Tech
assessment, budgets that have not been received to date and the snow and ice deficit.
The DPW Director reported as of today, the snow and ice deficit is at $86,647. This balanced budget
recommendation reflects a minimum of level service, but contains a certain above level service
expenditures and those reflect the Town's need increased public safety services. Free cash to be used
to fund capital items, with the balance of free cash to go to a special stabilization fund to fund large
expense capital items. The budget meets the Towns financial policy on keeping the minimum five
percent in the stabilization fund. This balanced budget proposal is based on use of reoccurring
revenues for operating cost. No free cash or stabilization funds are used for the operating budget.
FY2018 Revenue Estimates are:
Property Tax: $25,639,730. No new overrides or Exclusions. New Growth estimated at $300K.
State Aid: $8,877,879. There is significant increase State aid which includes $883K in Chapter 70
funds, a 13.9% increase. $39K in General Government Aid, a 3.8% increase.
Local Receipts: $2,913,168. Estimated 2.5% increase in Motor Vehicle Excise tax and $140K in Solar
revenues.
Available Funds $2,419,818. MSBA Reimbursement of$534,198. Free Cash estimated at $900K.
Expenditures: $36,891,930. Expenditures reflect the cost of providing services to the Town. There
were above level service request from Police, Fire, DPW, COA, Library, and Lunenburg Public Schools.
Not all request will be funded.
Expenditures include $633,317 to fund the capital budget, and the Debt Service at $4,796,858.
Unclassified Expenditures includes estimated 6% increase in Health Insurance. 10% increase in
Police/Fire Injured on Duty (IOD) and Workers Comp.
