3. Accounting and acceptable use of the Federal Disaster Relief Funds from FEMA
Background:
i. FEMA will reimburse the Town $38,064.43 in Federal Disaster Relief Funds to defray
some of the snow and ice removal expenses the Town had from the January, 2015
storm.
ii. To make these funds available to spend, the state will permit the Town to set up a
special account with a debit balance of $38,064.43.
This would allow the Town to prospectively reduce the snow and ice account
deficit by $38,064.43 in anticipation of receiving these funds.
•
On receipt of the reimbursement, the Town will credit the special account,
zeroing it out.
(NB: At this point the committee joined the Board of Selectmen, which was meeting
elsewhere at the school, and we continued our discussion.)
B. The issue is whether the Town could apply these funds to the Pavement Management Account
(PMP).
i. These funds are non-recurring and the PMP is treated as an operating expense in the
Town budget.
ii. Normally we would not use non-recurring revenues to fund an operating expense;
However as there is a degree of discretion regarding funding the PMP-as was
demonstrated during the FY16 budget process-the general sense was that funding
the PMP would be an acceptable use of the FEMA re-imbursement.
C. Motion: John made a motion to recommend approval to use $38,064.43 from free cash to
fund the PMP. Karin seconded the motion. The committee approved the motion 4-0.
i. Note: The Board of Selectmen approved a similarly worded motion 5-0.
4. The committee went into recess at 8:35.
