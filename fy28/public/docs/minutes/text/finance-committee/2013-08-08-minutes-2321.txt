Committee Reports:
SR reported that Capital Planning is set for the beginning of the budget process. The building reuse committee will be
reviewing 4 different scenarios and plans to be ready with recommendations based on the progress of the school building
committee project.
ME reported on the School Building Committee and schematic design. The Committee, with the OPM and Architect, has
been having informational meetings with town committees. They will be before the Board of Selectmen 8/14/2013. They
will come before Finance on 9/26. The plan is to have all meetings televised so people have several opportunities to be
informed on the project. There are also several avenues through the internet for staying updated including through the
school website.
JH reported that Jack Rodriguenz will have a DPW report to the Board of Selectmen on 9/10.
