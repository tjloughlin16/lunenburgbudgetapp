5. Town Manager / Finance Director Report 
 Ms. Lemieux said that since the June 13 they have had some invitations for bids that came in. 
Guardrail repair services were awarded to Premier Fence LLC in the amount of $80,886 and that will be 
paid out of the Mass DOT Winter Recovery Assistance program funds. Invitation for bids for Page 
Street Culvert and the town received a bid from Powell Construction in the amount of $86,913 to be 
paid out of the fiscal 23 Capital Plan appropriation. The five year reassessing valuation came in and the 
town received one proposal from Vision and that can in much higher than we estimated at $112,000 so 
the Select Board voted to appropriate additional funds out of ARPA to cover that difference. Three bids 
were received on LED Street light conversation and those are still being evaluated to determine the low 
bidder. Ms. Lemieux also said there were Roadway Maintenance Fog Ceiling bid that was due this 
week and the Assistant Town Manager is evaluating that as well as working on the IFB for the generator 
project at the Adult Activity Center and the two dump trucks which are being put out to BID since the 
town hasn’t had any luck with the State bid contract.

Update on the tax assessment  - Municipal Finance Services has fulfilled their contract and the total was 
$82,400 and that was all paid out of ARPS funding that the select board allocated. The total abatements 
issued from the assessor side is $1,351,147 and that does not include the tax collector issuing interest.  
925 Mass Ave project is substantially complete and the EPA has finalized their work. There is material 
that needs to be removed once the DEP number is issued and the underground storage and the canopy 
has been removed. ms. Lemieux said she applied for a grant through Mass Development for the Real 
Estate Technical Assistance program that would allow Mass Development to provide technical support 
for a feasibility study, conceptual plans for what we could use that property for, Market Analysis and to 
develop an RFP to solicit proposals for reuse of that property and the notice of that award is in the 
October/November time Fram  but she believes the town has a really competitive application. 
  
6. New Business  
Investment options for better returns - Mr. Watters spoke with Ms. Brochu via email  briefly 
and she was going to send over a report of all the funds they currently have and that this may be a 
conversation to be had with the treasurer. Mr. Watters said he would invite the treasurer to a future 
meeting. 
Mr. Gray said he didn’t now if there were regulations on how much risk we can take but would like to 
know their limitations. Mr. Gray also said interest rates mater and looking at cash flow and what cash 
flow looks like for next year needs to be known. 
Mr. Balish said to build on Mr. Gray’s point that in a federal money market fund today the yields are 
well above 4% so with minimal risk and in the short term that would be his first stop for a safe 
investment but was unsure of the rules and hopes the town is looking into those types of things. 
7. Old Busines 
Tax Assessment/Abatement Discussion - Mr. Simeone said the Board of Assessors has been reviewing 
abatements and voting on them and that notices have been going out.  
 Primary School air conditioning project 
Mr. Watters heard from the School Department and the window for the summer to have this job done 
has already closed so whatever the discussion is this evening won’t change things for this summer. Mr. 
Gray asked if it would be coming up at the Special Town Meeting and Mr. Watters said he expected it to 
and they’d find out once the warrant comes out. 
Mr. Watters said that although it wasn’t the outcome he would have preferred, he thanked the committee 
for coming to their own conclusions and standing by them.
