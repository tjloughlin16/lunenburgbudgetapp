1. Meeting called to order at 7:00 pm.
2. Meeting minutes
TB motioned to approve minutes of 7/11/2013 as written. KM seconded, motion carried unanimously.
3. Committee Reports:
BL reported that the Library Trustees are off for the summer. The Friends of the Library have volunteered to replace the
door system, so there will not be a capital request for that. The DPW has issued specs for painting.
ME reported on the School Building Committee and site plan draft. They will be before the MSBA on 7/31 to approve
moving into schematic design.
4.
Town Manager Updates
Ms. Speidel brought forward several Chapter 44 Section 33B transfers. Most of the transfers moved available funds from
several sources to Legal Expenses to cover the costs associated with codification of the Town's bylaws. BL motioned to
approve the transfers in the amounts presented (attachment). JH seconded, motion carried unanimously.
FY13 encumbrances were reviewed.
FY 13 revenues were reviewed. |
FY14 state budget has been approved. The cherry sheet and changes to the cherry sheet were reviewed.
The Sewer Committee has signed a contract for maintenance.
5. Fiscal policies
The policies from Littleton were shared. There was consensus that the 4 categories addressed; stabilization fund, free cash,
debt management and capital planning were the topics important to include in Lunenburg policies. The next steps will be to
take the topics one at a time, beginning with the stabilization fund, review samples from other municipalities on the topic,
and then assign the writing of each section among members.
6. Future Items
The Committee will schedule a visit to the Regional Dispatch Center.
7. Next meeting
August 8t is subject to confirmation, August 22"d would be next meeting if we are off on the 8th.
Fiscal Policies will be on the agenda.
TB motioned to adjourn. KM seconded, meeting adjourned at 8:56 pm.
