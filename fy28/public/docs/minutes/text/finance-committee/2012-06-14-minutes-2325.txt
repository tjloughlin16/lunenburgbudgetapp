5) Committee Reports
• Schools - last night voted on reconfiguration of school buildings from 4 buildings to 3; TCP will not be used next
school year for academic programs
• Library - Committee met and they will reduce overall library hours from 44 to 40 hours rather than reducing
children's library hours
• School Building Committee - enrollment projections from state are considerably lower than projected for grades 6-
12; 780 count vs. 900; looking to hire a project manager
6) Review election results - general discussion stating there was not enough information to successfully inform voters
7) Town Manager Updates
• To close out the fiscal year, will not accept P.O.'s after June 15 in order to close the books by July 15
• Will be submitting a reserve fund transfer next meeting to close out expenses from October 2011 storm; $32K
overage funded from reserve
• Reissued some bonds at a lower rate; will be saving a net of $15k per year and state saves $25k per year; retained our
AA credit rating
• Town Manager met with Mayor Wong yesterday on a piece of land partially in Lunenburg; land is accessible from
Fitchburg so they will provide police, fire, etc. but Lunenburg will receive some tax revenues when developed
