4. Policy Discussion 
 
School Committee provided information on contract and increase in salaries.   
 
Terri  - typically schools look at and compare salaries with at least 5 other similar 
districts.  This gives an idea of why a district may be losing personnel.   
 

===PAGE 2===
 
 
Chris – maybe some discussion about procedures regarding salary schedule with 
the personnel committee.  What would be expected that the Finance Committee 
should vote on – a matrix with Committee expectations.   
 
Chris believes that Boards and Commissions that have oversight and are part of a 
department budget should review their budget prior to Finance Committee 
presentation Floor;  for example DPW, Conservation Commission, Cemetery.  
They all play relevant parts and yet they have no say in the budget they are to use 
for the upcoming fiscal year.    
  
Chris also believes there has to be a better way to go about the budget than to 
have money figures at Town Meeting.  These are all things the Finance 
Committee should look to clean up in policy.   
 
There is some discussion around Debt Exclusion, the TCP/THES and belief that 
the Finance Committee has to come up with a long-term plan.  There is no Town 
Consensus on what to do with Town Buildings and the Vertex Plan.  Chris would 
like more information on Chapter 90 Funds, Pavement Management.  He 
believes one department does a lot and that it should be broken up.   
 
Heather L. would like a short meeting next week 4/29  prior to Town Meeting.  
She will have the Figures for the Collective Bargaining Articles.
