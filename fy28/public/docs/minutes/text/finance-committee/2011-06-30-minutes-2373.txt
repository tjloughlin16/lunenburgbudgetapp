7) Town Manager Updates
• Eagle Heights Senior Housing: application submitted; hope to hear by Sep on status of Sec 202 grant.
• Summer Street analysis underway, to be complete end of Aug. Next steps are BOS review, then public hearings.
• Jones House approved for sale at minimum bid of $80,000. No bids received. Will rebid this fall with same
restrictions but no minimum.
• MART Bus subsidy caused confusion. MART will replace the existing van with a new, larger van and operate as now.

• Vacant Main Street Gas Station: DEP wants to clean the property but needs property owner permission; he refuses.
Legislation being proposed to bypass property owner approval.
• Warrant for STM: DPW facility renovation over budget. Plans modified and rebid but still $200k short. Impact of
financing this would be roughly $15,000 over 15 years.
