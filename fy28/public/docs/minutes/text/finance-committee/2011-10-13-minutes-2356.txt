October 13, 2011
Location:
Town Hall
Present:
Mark Erickson, Scott Gile, Brian Laffond, John Male
Absent:
Barbara Reynolds, Martha McDonald
Also Present: Kerry Speidel
1) Meeting called to order by the Chairman at 7:02 PM
2) Review Green Energy Task Force (GETF)- Guests David Blatt, Chairman and John Londa, Advisor, explained that the GETF
was established in 2009 to review Lunenburg's Green Community Status for the State. (see Handouts)
• John Londa discussed 4 projects that they have worked on:
1. A Federal grant for $150,000 has paid for a Solar Array at Turkey Hill Middle School. The Town will own the
array after 15 years and it has an expected life of 25 years. For the first 15 years, the savings should be about
$10,000 per year. After that the savings will increase substantially.
2.. Installation of $48,000 worth of improved lighting at TC Passios was funded largely by Unitil ($34,000). Cost
to the Town was $14,000 and will result in savings of $5,500 per year.
3. Installation of $48,000 worth of improved lighting at Turkey Hill Middle School was also funded largely by
Unitil Cost to the Town was $17,000 and will result in savings of $5,000 per year.
4. An RFP for a proposed 1.5 Megawatt Solar Farm at the Youngs Road was issued. 8 firms investigated, but
only 1 responded. Details of a contract have not been finalized, but potential savings would be $112.000-
$127,00 in the first year of operation and should be effective for at least 20 years.
• There is also a private developer who is proposing a 6 Megawatt Solar Farm on 25 acres of a 108 acre parcel
on Chase Road. The GETF hopes to work with him at the appropriate time.
• David Blatt discussed the criteria for a Green Community.
1. As-of-Right Siting for RE/AE - Lunenburg has appropriate bylaws on the books.
2. Expedited Permitting - Lunenburg has appropriate procedures.
3. Energy Reduction Plan -Baseline is done to support a 20% reduction.
4.
Fuel-Efficient Vehicles - Lunenburg needs to adopt guidelines.
5. Minimize Life-Cycle Costs - (Stretch Code) will require Town Meeting Approval.
Qualifying as a Green Community would result in a first year grant of $175,000
This information will be presented to the BOS on November 1, 2011.
There will be a workshop on November 17, 2011.
This is expected to be presented at Special Town Meeting on December 5, 2011.
3) Minutes
• Reviewed minutes from September 8, 2011 meeting; JM moves we accept minutes, BL seconds, all approved
4) Committee Reports
• Public Safety - Scott Gile has agreed to be the new liaison.
• Service Level Team - Mark Erickson reported that they met on September 27, 2011. Primary discussion on re-
focusing on the Service Level versus budget. They will reconvene on October 25, 2011.

Criteria 5 - Minimize Life Cycle Costs
Require all new residential construction over 3,000
square feet and all new commercial and industrial real
estate construction to minimize, to the extent feasible, the
life-cycle cost of the facility by utilizing energy efficiency,
water conservation and other renewable or alternative
energy technologies.
The DOER recommended way for cities and
towns to meet this requirement is by adopting the
BBRS Stretch Code (780 CMR 120.AA) an
appendix to the MA State Building Code.
• In a town, the Stretch Code must be
adopted as a general bylaw by its Town
Meeting.
• In a city, the Stretch Code must be adopted
by the City Council, preferably by general
ordinance.
