5. Committee discussion on the FY2017 budget process:
a. Projected revenues constrain the level of services the Town can fund. Thus this
committee needs to quantify the shortfall of revenues to needs, recommend to the
Town which services to fund, and how much to fund those services.
i. One way to categorize expenditures is as non-discretionary, partially
discretionary, and discretionary. Discretionary does not imply un-necessary.
ii. Is there a way to determine which services the Townsfolk want to fund?
