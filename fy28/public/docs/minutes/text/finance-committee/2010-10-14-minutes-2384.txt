1) Meeting called to order by the Chairman at 7 PM.
Minutes - reviewed minutes from 9/9/10; BR made motion to approve as amended, MM seconded, all approved.
3) Committee Reports
• Capital Planning - met 10/12; all capital requests received and starting to compile; many are returning requests
from prior year; totals $2.3M.
• DPW - DPW Building Committee received 5 bids from architects for design phase; have selected 3 for interviews
and will meet with HKA Associates of Groton first. Holman Street Bridge has been closed since last March and
requires $500K to repair; residents are requesting that repairs not be completed so the road is not used as a cut-
through for trucks; other issues exist with road width and sight problems; more information will be needed from
the Chiefs on any public safety concerns. Pleasantview Street residents have been outspoken about road
deterioration, sewer problems and water problems; all three issues involve different boards and City of
Leominster.
• School Regionalization - 9/13 meeting; committee has found there would be no significant savings in a merger
but could offer expanded services; cost sharing based on enrollment, not income; Lunenburg share estimated at
29.1%; some discussion about inviting members of regionalization board to speak to FinComm.
4) New Business
• BOS has settled contracts for both Fire and Police Chief; HANDOUT: Contract for Police Chief, Contract for Fire Chief;
request for transfer from Salary Reserve to settle Chief's contracts for fire for $2,330 and police for $2,987 ($746
Quinn and $2,241 salary); MM made motion to transfer funds, ME seconded, all approved.
5) Town Manager Updates
• New meals tax of .75% started out slow with revenues of $1700 in first 2 months, next three months averaged $4900
per month; expect estimate of $6OK annually to be reached.
• Two grants awarded to Lunenburg Police - $9,450 for bullet-proof vests; $1,000 for advanced training for Domestic
Violence for 2 officers.
• Cardboard recycling dumpster will no longer be available at DPW; use weekly recycling.
6) Town Manager Updates - HANDOUT: Revenues/Expenditures Year-to-Date; YTD Budget Report
7) Next Meetings - Special Town Meeting 11/30; Public Hearing for Special Town Meeting will be held before the 5 year
Financial Review on 11/4 at 7 pm; no meeting 11/11; next meetings 10/28, 11/4, 11/18.
8) Adjournment - ME made motion to adjourn at 8:43 PM and MM seconded.
