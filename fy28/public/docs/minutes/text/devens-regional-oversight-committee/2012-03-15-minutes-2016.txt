Finance sub-committee report
The committee reviewed financial modeling and determined:
• Roof should not be pursued until operating costs are balanced
• Roof fund reserves should be used to buffer operating costs
• An extension of DEP grant which has an end date of June 30 should be requested to maintain fund
balance of $7877.
• Lower price points ($10 for up to 2.5 gallons/pound and $20 for up to 5 gallons/pounds) should be
promoted to encourage participation.
• MOTION: Rich made a motion to promote a minimum load of $20 for up to 5 gallons/pounds, Don
Lowe seconded the motion and it passed unanimously.
• Participation and revenue should be reviewed after first quarter of second year to determine if fees
should be incréased and if roof is feasible. If roof is not deemed feasible an alternative expenditures for
the DEP grant fund balance should be identified.
The committee also discussed that the goal is to meet quarterly and have subcommittees meeting in between.
