CURRENT BUSINESS -
RFP status-Station Operations & Maintenance- The Commission discussed the comments noted on the O&M RFP draft
by the Town Counsel and Town Manager. They also discussed how to incorporate the changes into the draft and whether
to have another conversation with Town Counsel to ask for help in clarifying language. The Commission agreed to have
Barb and Jack talk to Town Counsel. Jack added that it is more important to get it right and if it takes a couple extra
weeks, Al Packard (Empey) is able to stay on.
RFP for Generator Maintenance Discussion- The Commission discussed the Generator RFP and whether it is
necessary to do a full RFP since they just need a vendor to do 2 scheduled maintenance per year plus provide emergency
service and the proposed cost will not be great enough to require a formal bid process. Commission agreed to solicit 3
quotes and to try and keep it around the budgeted amount of $11k with repairs. They also agreed to put it under the DPW
with an agreed upon scope and agreed upon level of maintenance and frequency of once per year.
SWSS termination of Contract update -The Commission had received a draft of the notice of default for SWSS from
Town Counsel, who had noted that litigation must be authorized by the Board of Selectmen. Dave said he would want to
discuss it in an Executive Session, but Barb noted that Town Counsel did not believe that it qualified for Executive Session
at this time. Commission agreed that they had no issue with the draft notice. Barb will talk to Counsel again during counsel
hours and advise that it can be sent. The Commission discussed recovering losses for costs incurred by SWSS's breach
and will consult Counsel regarding next steps.
FY21 Budget - Barb noted that the budget was presented to FinCom. The 10% rate increase will be effective 7/1/20,
reflected in the October billing. The current rate is $115 per 1000 cubic foot(cf) and will go to $126.50/1000cf, and the
minimum charge will increase from $97.20 to $107. Barb added that the warrant article for the Capital Reserve Transfer
that was originally voted on by the Commission was changed after speaking with the Town Manager, due to the
Commission's most recent approval of the budget. With the Commission's approval of the FY21 budget as presented to
the FinCom, the Capital Reserve amount, originally $29,000, now shows as $30,000 on the current budget, and that is the
amount that will appear on the warrant. Carl thanked Dave and Barb for their good job in handling the budget presentation.
