Barb had provided handouts of the proposed FY14 Budget. For the sake of discussion of possible costs, the
proposed transfer to Retained Earnings was at $204,943.66. It was the task of the meeting to review the budget
and reduce that amount.
Barb started with the revenues, stating that we are currently at 673 customers, and we are proposing an
additional 40 in FY14. 2 would be Fitchburg flow, 8 would be Leominster flow plus an estimated 30 additional
Leominster flow customers that come from Emerald Place. Emerald Place is broken out since they have already
paid their connection fees and cannot be figured into the estimated revenues for connection.
Outsourcing line item used a general assumption of $100,000 cost. Discussion took place regarding the savings
from personnel and Vehicle maintenance, and the proposed cost of Outsourcing, including the 'outside coverage
hours' period, not included in the proposals. Fringe benefits line may be adjusted. Barb will talk with Kerry. It has
been low for a while and due for an increase, but by taking Corey out of the budget, there may be a reduction.
Engineering services include the l/l project of $65,000 for the home inspections to discover illegal connections.
Also to be considered are the replacement of clay pipes of the old Leominster sewer that could exist between the
home and the PVC stub which was installed from the main to the property line when sewers were laid. These
factors could contribute to the I/l issue in the Whalom area for which Weston & Sampson proposed the $65k
project. Jack pointed out that we are overdue to commence paying Leominster based on flow meter readings,
and this project cannot be put off. Luckily Leominster hasn't pushed us on it, because it will bankrupt the Sewer
Enterprise. Roger Brooks (Leominster DPW) is retiring in 3 years. Dave stated that since they hadn't yet made
the switch over, we could put off the project for FY14.
Capital cost for Fitchburg was discussed, and John Male suggested that, even if Fitchburg commences the work
on John Fitch Highway in the summer, we would probably not be billed for a year - outside of current budget
period. It should come out of Retained Earnings anyway by vote at Town Meeting, and not included as part of
operating costs. The capital estimate of $66K was removed. Carl pointed out that that cost will not go away, and
we must keep it in mind.
Commission discussed raising connection fees to $2250 since Fitchburg is proposing a connection fee of $2000
and it was determined that it would be applied to all customers, not just Fitchburg connections. $250 should
cover Lunenburg's costs of inspection and administration. The Commission also discussed raising Drain Layers
License fee. Corey had stated at one time that some towns add in for I/l remediation $150-200. Commission
agreed we could double the fee to $100. Connection fees will increase 7/1/13 - and we could advertise the
increase, encouraging people to sign up before then. Carl reviewed for Jack another motivator discussed
previously on illegally connected customers and we could combine them all in a newspaper article to encourage
connections prior to these rate increases.
