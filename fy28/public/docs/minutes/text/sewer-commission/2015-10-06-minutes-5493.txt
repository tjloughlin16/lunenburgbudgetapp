Appointments
Fats, Oil, Grease (FOG) Working Session with Deb Trumbull, SWSS
The goals for the workshop were outlined by the Commission. There was discussion on defining terminology and what the
current regulations are. The Commission also discussed the importance of getting restaurants to comply and the repercussions
for non-compliance. Educating restaurants on the regulations and applying for waivers was reviewed. The Commission went on
to define what the expectations of the contractor are. It was determined that the current regulations need to be revisited and
updated. It also needs to be confirmed that log postings are located by each grease trap. The Nashoba Board of Health will be
contacted regarding what happens when a permit to discharge FOG into the system is suspended.
