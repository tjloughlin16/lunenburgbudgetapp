E-permitting system policy- Adam Burney presented information on the new e-permit system that the town has implemented for
applying and collecting permit fees online. The fees can be collected digitally or in person and annual renewals would be sent by email
each year. A 5% technology fee would be added to all permits to offset costs. Adam is requesting that the 5% fee be added by the
Sewer Commission to their permits. Joan asked where the fee revenues would go and Barb said into the general fund. Commissioners

time with the annual permit process. The Commission further discussed concerns about the fees added on top of the permit costs and
