Chair: Richard McGrath

Meeting Minutes: Tuesday March 29th, 2016 
 
 
meeting open and called to order at 7:15 PM 
 
Minutes from 3-8-16 reviewed, voted on and approved. 
 
Previously unapproved minutes from February 9th were read and also approved 
The board reviewed the draft of the design guidelines, sections 1 and 2, submitted by Jim 
LaVeck.  The draft was well-received with several edits and comments. 
 
The board revisited the section of the APDC bylaw dealing with Demolition by delay and 
discussed how policies and procedures might be included in the guidelines to address the goals 
explained in the bylaw. 
 
The board also reviewed the need to define temporary structures that would fall under APDC 
jurisdiction, and those that will not. 
 
The board divided responsibility for writing rough draughts of the sections of the guidelines 
dealing with specific treatment of parts of the building: 
McGrath: sheathing and trim 
Corcoran: cellars and foundations 
LaVeck: Roofs 
Dwyer: windows and doors 
 
Next meeting set for 4-29-16 
 
Adjourned 8:15 PM 
 
Respectfully Submitted, 
Cullen Dwyer, recording clerk
