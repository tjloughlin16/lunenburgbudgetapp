 was presented, reviewed and accepted into the 
guidelines.

its next meeting on Tuesday, October 11th, 2016.  Once that is done, the guidelines will be 
available for review and acceptance by the Board of Selectmen. 
 
Next meeting set for the second Tuesday of October, the 11th.
