TOWN OF LUNENBURG  
PUBLIC HEARING  
 
The Lunenburg Architectural Preservation District Commission will hold a public hearing on May 9, 2024, 
7:30 p.m., Ritter Memorial Building, Basement Conference Room, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, 
MA. The hearing will also be available via Zoom. For those who want to particpate remotely please use the 
link to join the public meeting webinar,https://us02web.zoom.us/j/7927370353?omn=83450516998 
Meeting ID: 792 737 0353. The hearing will review and adopt changes to existing design guidelines as 
required Architectural Preservation District, S 113-5, Paragraph A. The revised guidelines are available for 
viewing at the Architectural Preservation District webpage on the Town of Lunenburg  website.
