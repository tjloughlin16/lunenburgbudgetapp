Town of Lunenburg 
Architectural Preservation District Commission  
         
Richard McGrath, Chair 
Cullen Dwyer, Vice Chair 
Tony Nicastro, member     
                           
Meeting Minutes 
Tuesday August 5th, 2021 
Ritter Building 
Call to Order: At 7:30PM with McGrath, Dwyer and Nicastro in attendance. 
Correspondence: 
A letter was read from Ronda Lisio recommending soliciting funds and publicizing the preservation of the Old Primary School. 
Richard wrote to Keith Cheneaux- chairman of the Fitchburg Historical Society, and architecture teacher at Fitchburg State, re-
questing guidance on how to preserve the OPS. 
Richard also shared content from New View Communities webpage, explaining how schools of the same age were renovated in 
Athol.  New View Communities, a non-profit organization in Fitchburg.  Redeveloped high school in Leominster and other 
projects. 
Lisio was available to comment on the lack of rational thinking at the last town meeting. 
Discussion of the Old Primary School Building: 
Nicastro hoped to get the developer (O’Brian) on Howard Street to develop the OPS as part of his required affordable housing. 
Dwyer stated his opinion that the APD bylaw and regulations allowed the commission to grant a CTA for the conversion of the 
OPS into an open-air pavilion.  He furthermore presented his design concept for the same. 
Other Business: 
Leandra White-Walker’s house at Lancaster and Whiting is still in demo by neglect.  Dwyer proposed the APDC offer to buy the 
property, renovate and sell.  Discussion followed. 
Next Meeting: Sept 9th,7:30 
Adjourned: at 8:50PM
