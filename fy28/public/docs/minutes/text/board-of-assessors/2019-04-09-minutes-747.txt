Senior Tax-Work-Off: Senior Tax Work-Off President Robert Hamel, presented information regarding
the Senior Work-Off Program. Mr. Hamel was seeking the Boards' support in increasing the opportunity
for eligible senior citizens to receive up to $1,500.00 in salary per year up from the current $1,000.00. He
explained that currently for each hour worked an eligible senior age 60+ is paid $10.00 per hour and can
work up to 100 hours between Jan. 1 to Nov. 1 of the same calendar year. He is proposing that the salary
be increased to $12.00 per hour and the worker then would have the ability to earn the maximum amount
of $1,500.00 which would represent 125 hours per year. He explained that the income guidelines also
would be increased for a one person household from $40,000 to $50,000. After some discussion, the
Board endorsed the changes as proposed by Mr. Hamel. Mr. Hamel explained that he will now take this
proposal to the BOS for their endorsement / approval..
