• Neighborhood Review Vote from Town Meeting
The Department of Revenue requires there to be a full re-evaluation of
neighborhoods every 5 years. Neighborhoods are recommended to have
similar characteristics within those codes called site indexes. The site
indexes are what affects valuation based on sales.
The Board of Assessors is looking to hire a temporary assessing assistant
who specializes in this field to complete the reviews. This position has
been approved in both the Salary Administration Plan and the Town Budget.
