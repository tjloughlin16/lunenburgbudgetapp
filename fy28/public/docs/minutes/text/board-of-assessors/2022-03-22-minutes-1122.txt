Minutes from the BOA (Board of Assessors) meeting held: March 22, 2022
Members present: Board Members, Christine Higdon and Kevin Fish. Also present Principal Assessor
CJ Carroll, Rena Swezey (Interim Assessor) and Administrative Assessing Assistant, Sheila Craigen
Also present: Kevin O'Brien, of The Homes at Aro Estates, LLC
Christine opened the meeting at 10:04 am
Open Meeting:
• Kevin made a motion to approve both the February 24, 2022 and March 3, 2022 minutes. Christine
seconded. Minutes approved
• Rena introduced Kevin O'Brien to the BOA (Board of Assessors). Mr. O'Brien filed abatements on his
real estate tax bills and did a presentation to the BOA to support his filings. After much discussion, the
BOA thanked Mr. O'Brien and advised decisions would be made in executive session. Mr. O'Brien left the
meeting at 10:55 am.
• In executive session, the BOA reviewed real estate abatement applications. Rena also
presented several abatement applications on Personal Property tax bills.
Kevin made a motion to exit executive session at 12:00.
• BOA reviewed and approved expense vouchers, abatements, warrants and commitments.
Adjournment:
• Meeting adjourned at 12:21 pm
Sheila Craigen
