The meeting opened at 5:45 pm. Chairman, Richard Letarte, Board Members, Louis Franco, Rena
Swezey, Regional Assessor, Harald Scheid and Louise Paquette, Administrative Assessor.
Board reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, abatements, warrants, commitments, payroll
and August 14, 2018 minutes.

granted: $48,050.00.
DOR DLS Signature Authorization: Louise stated that she has been notified of new requirements this
year in the tax rate setting process in the reporting in Gateway to the MA Department of Revenue's
Division of Local Assessment. For FY19, a majority of the board members are required to sign both the
LA-5 and page 1 of the Tax Recapitulation Sheet. They are requiring that all board members have a
username and pass word for the DLS Gateway application. Louise stated that she will be in touch with
Karen who will assist with account set-up.
Signature Authorization for DLS Gateway Entries: BOA authorized Joshua & Harald to sign Sales
Report & Tax Rate Forms in DLS Gateway on behalf of the Board.

regarding the issues they have with the property land lines. He reiterated what Josh spoke on last month.

working on the conversion that will create a code that will enable this adjustment without going into each
individual parcel. (See Residential Land Chart attachment)
