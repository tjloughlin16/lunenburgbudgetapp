that an administrative error had been made regarding the property. The office discovered that the same
residential structure know as 282-284 Chase Road had been doubled assessed. The colonial home was
rightfully listed and taxed on an abutting parcel identified as Map 53, Lot 12; said property having been
subdivided from the parent parcel Map 38, Parcel 4 for assessment in FY 10. The same house had not

One additional exemption application for a V1, Clause 22 was reviewed and approved in the amount of
$400.00. Louise reported that 153 statutory exemptions have been granted totally $89,428.63.
Louise reported that the total overlay for FY11 is presently $119,987.37. The opening balance from recap
is $124,378.48. A Memorandum from Karen Brochu, Town Accountant regarding the transfer of Overlay
surpluses was received and reviewed. The Board reviewed and approved the following:

Harald spoke with the Board regarding the ABC Charitable applications. (Applications to be received by
March 15) Louise reported that presently there are 5 applications and there has been 5 ABC exemptions
approved over the last several years. There was some discussion regarding an application. Harald
reviewed with the Board what the qualifications are to be considered charitable organizations. Louise
presented to the Board The Taxpayers Guide to Local Property Exemptions for Religious & Charitable
Organizations. The Board requested that they would like some time to review this and voted to table the
approvals of the applications.
Louise spoke about the upcoming billing calendar. The office is working to keep all the incoming deeds
up to date and entered into CAMA. She stated she hopes that the billing file will be completed as soon as
possible to prepare for the preliminary billing. She mentioned that she has spoken with the Tax Collector
regarding the billing process with Munis and he stated that at this time the move to change software from
Munis to Vadar is now on hold. The Accountant did not want to make any changes at this time.
Harald did not share any updates on new development for FY12 tonight however he stated that it is a full
revaluation certification year in real estate. The DOR has requested some PRC and will be out to begin
some inspections.
Next meeting is slated for Tuesday, May 3d at 6:00 pm. Meeting adjourned at 7:05pm.
