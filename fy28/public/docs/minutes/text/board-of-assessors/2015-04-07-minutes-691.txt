Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held April 7, 2015
The meeting opened at 5:45 pm. Present: Board Members, Matt Papini, Sr., Christopher Comeau,
Regional Assessor, Harald Scheid and Louise Paquette, Administrative Assistant. Chairman, Louis
Franco was not present.
Board reviewed and approved current expense vouchers, abatements, warrants, commitments, payroll
and March 3, 2015 minutes.
The Board reviewed and signatures were notarized to approve the filing of a Chapter 61A (Classified
Agricultural Land) Release for property previously owned by Richard W. Brown of 90 West Townsend
Road. Louise reported that RE taxes in the amount of $4,712.38 was collected on February 15, 2015 for
Roll-Back taxes for years 2011 through 2015.
Harald reported that George Bourgault did receive a call (after the last meeting) to inspect the property
located at 131 Leominster-Shirley Rd. (RE abatement application was filed on 1/20/15 by Michael
Padula, Trustee of The Rental Equipment CO Realty Trust). Harald requested that the Board review their
"denial" decision from the prior meeting and review the findings gathered by George after the inspection.
After reviewing the findings of the inspection, the BOA voted to deny again the application for abatement.
The value the Applicant was seeking did not include the site prep, concrete or concrete finishing. The
applicant will be sent notification of their decision.
BOA is in receipt of 7 ABC applications (Religious & Charitable Organizations) seeking FY16 property tax
exemptions. Harald presented all the applications for review and approval. They are as follows:
Lunenburg Historical Society (History Museum) - 10 School St. - Granted
Twin City Baptist Temple - (Religious Education) - 194 Electric Ave - Granted
Twin City Baptist Temple - (Vacant Land) - 190 Electric Ave. - Denied

Louise reminded the Board that The Commonwealth of Massachusetts requires Boards of Assessors to
immediately tax new structures at full valuation upon issuance of a Certificate of Occupancy or Temporary
Certificate of Occupancy. Properties undergoing significant improvements in which the resulting building
value increases by 50 percent or more are also subject to immediate taxation upon completion. Prior to
this statutory enactment, the extent of taxation for the upcoming fiscal year was the building's
construction status on June 30th
She informed the Board that FY 15 Supplemental Bill notification letters
will be sent to approximately 29 taxpayers with new construction and Certificate of Occupancy issued by
the Building Department.

Louise reported on FY15 Overlay. The opening balance (from recap) is $207,330.39. The overall total
abatements processed are $133,262.09 with ending balance of $74,068.30.
Harald reported in a Memorandum to the Town Manager an estimated Fiscal Year 2016 New Growth
Estimate. He stated based on an analysis of active building projects and projected qualifying
developments to date, he estimates that Lunenburg will realize new taxable valuation of approximately
$17,500.00 generating new growth of $320,000.00.
Harald stated that he will be working on lot splits, maps and creating a FY16 test file for Real Estate
billing. He also stated that he has been contacted by the property owner of 400 Holman St. regarding
Roll-Back taxes on this property.
Harald presented a letter to Chris regarding his outstanding 20 plus years of service to the Town and the
Board of Assessors. (See attached)
Next meeting will be held on Tuesday, May 5, 2015 at 5:45 pm.
