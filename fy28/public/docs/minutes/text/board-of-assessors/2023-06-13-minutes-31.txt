Location: In person at the Lunenburg Town Hall and broadcast over the Lunenburg Public Access
Channel.
The meeting was opened at 3:00 PM.
Members Present: Principal Access CJ Carroll, Administrative Assessing Assistant Sheila Craigen,
Board Member Kevin Fish, Chairman Richard Letarte, Board Member John Rabbitt, Interim
Principal Assessor Rena Swezey.
Abatement Application Update
300 applications have been completed.
On May 4*h, the Board of Assessors voted to accept the findings of MFS regarding property
value. MFS members consisted of 3 qualified assessors that made site visits to each property. If
there is a disagreement with the findings and the homeowner can find an error in the
calculation of the values, then please contact the Board of Assessors. If there is no error, and
this is just an opinion, the suggestion is to file ATB (Appellate Tax Board) within that 90-day
window of the date the abatement was granted or denied.
John Rabbitt confirms that if anyone is having any concerns come and meet with the board.
Those concerns will be run by Nate Cramer, but his decision will be the final.
Public comment
Dave McDonald - raised concern that there are many residents who haven't received
notification. The board confirms that a batch consisting of 100 applications were mailed
yesterday and today. At this point, everything has been mailed.
Upcoming Meetings
Tentative remote scheduled meetings are June 14th at 2:00 PM and June 15th at 3:00 PM. The
hope is to get everything completed in order to get the tax bills in the mail on time.
Adjournment
Motion to adjourn and enter into executive session and not return to the open meeting by John
Rabbitt, Seconded by Richard Letarte. All in favor.
The meeting adjourned at 3:10 PM.
