STATUS REPORT FROM PRINCIPAL ASSESSOR.
• Update on Tax classification hearing, tax billing Munis update. Other items as
needed.

with MUNIS.

approval from the state. The Select Board approved a residential single tax
rate that is estimated to be around $14.39 per thousand. The median single family
home value for FY 2026 was $517,296 and the average tax was $7,443.89.
In the past month:
- The office has received 9 abutters list requests.
- 35 motor vehicle excise tax abatements were received.
- Robin Nano has processed commitment number 6 of 2025. There
were 442 excise tax bills that totaled $44,864.32.
- Lynda Cajka sent the forms of list to businesses in town. The forms
need to be returned by March 1, 2026.
Ongoing tasks:
- Continued research of Clause 18.
- Finalize billing to get to printer by December 14t.
- Awaiting approval from the state regarding tax recap.
