Minutes from the Board of Assessors meeting held: February 3, 2015
The meeting opened at 5:45 pm. Present: Chairman, Louis Franco, Board Member, Matt Papini, Sr.,
Regional Assessor, Harald Scheid and Louise Paquette, Administrative Assistant.
Board Member,
Christopher Comeau was not present.
Board reviewed and approved January 13, 2014 minutes, current expense vouchers, abatements,
warrants, commitments & payroll.
Harald presented five RE abatement applications. The Board reviewed applications and voted to grant
four abatements totaling $5,553.63. and one application submitted by Centro GA Lunenburg Crossing LLC
of 301 Massachusetts Ave. was denied. Harald reported that there are a total of eighteen (18)
applications received as of this date. He explained that applications received with a Feb. 2n°
made by the USPS dated on or before the filing deadline will also be acceptable. He reminded the Board
that all applications not timely filed, lose all rights to abatement and the Assessors cannot by law grant
one. These deadlines cannot be extended or waived for any reason.
