Location: In person at the Lunenburg Town Hall and broadcast over the Lunenburg Public Access

Members Present: Principal Access CJ Carroll, Administrative Assessing Assistant Sheila Craigen,
Board Member Kevin Fish, Chairman Richard Letarte, Board Member John Rabbitt, Interim
Principal Assessor Rena Swezey.
Abatement Application Update
Currently, the board is wrapping up the final batch of 250 applications.
Upcoming Meetings
Tentative remote meetings are scheduled for June 5 at 2:00 PM, June 6 at 3:00 PM, June 8 at
3:00 PM, June 13 at 3:00 PM, and June 15 at 3:00 PM. The goal is to have all abatements
complete in order to have tax bills in the mail on time.
Public Comment
Mary Perkins - 62 Ruth Street - submitted additional information at 11:30 AM at the Assessors'
Office. The original assessment did not include the information that Fire Road 15 runs through
her property with rights of way. Additionally, she shares a driveway with number 66 Ruth Street.
She inquired if those factors were taken into consideration with the categorization of her
property received.
J. Senja Morgan - 22 May Street - wanted to add that their home is used as a seasonal cottage
and the land and building value has increased significantly over the past 3 years. She wants to
know if these factors were taken into consideration. Kevin Fish states that each property is going
to be individually reviewed.
Adjournment
Motion to adjourn and enter into executive session and not return to the open meeting by
Kevin Fish, Seconded by Richard Letarte. All in favor.
The meeting adjourned at 3:08 PM.
