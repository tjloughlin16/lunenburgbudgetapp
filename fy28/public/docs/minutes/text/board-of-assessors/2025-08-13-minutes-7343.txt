STATUS REPORT FROM PRINCIPAL ASSESSOR.
• FY2026 Growth, Accomplishments of Assessing Department, Addition of tax billing
responsibilities in office. Other items as needed.
In the past month the office has received 12 abutters list requests, 17 motor
vehicle excise tax exemptions, and 1 motor vehicle commitment. We also
received 2 Motor Vehicle commitments for 2024 that were resolved by Robin
Nano.
The 4 properties that received chapter land roll back tax that didn't have liens
were sent a letter. 2 of the properties reached out to the office while the other 2
did not. Lynda Cajka will provide an update at the next meeting. The chapter land
applications were previously mailed by Robin Nano on August 1" (deadline of
December 1, 2025, for 2026).
Denise McCue and Lynda Cajka continued inspections on chapter land properties.
There were some functional and economic obsolescence discounts on properties,
some of which have documented justification, but many don't. The office sent
out letters with a due date of August 15"". If a response has been received, then an
inspection has been completed. Discounts with no explanation will be removed.
This will be added to the next agenda to continue to review.
The Fiscal 2026 real estate exemption applications will be mailed on September
1, 2025.
Lynda Cajka and Robin Nano have been going through the registry of deeds to
find parcels that were split that include plans that haven't yet been submitted to
the Assessor's office.
