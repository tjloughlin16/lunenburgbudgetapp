FY17 RE Supplemental Bills: Becky reported that a letter was sent to each property owner today that
explained the supplemental tax bill as follows:
"This letter is to explain your supplemental tax bill which you will soon be receiving in the mail. In
compliance with General Law Chapter 59 Section 2D, cities and towns must make supplemental pro-rate
tax assessments on the value of certain improvements to real estate made after the June 30 assessment
date for new construction. Properties undergoing significant real estate improvements in which the
building value increases by at least 50% are subject to immediate taxation upon completion as indicated
by a Certificate of Occupancy or Temporary Certificate of Occupancy. Your supplemental property tax bill
is calculated from the date of your occupancy permit to the end of the fiscal year and is in addition to your
actual tax bill for FY2017".

Louise reported that the Town Meeting will be held on May 6* and Town Election on May 20t.
Becky reported that the periodic / cyclical inspections are underway and that the DOR audit will take
place in FY19. Louise reported that the Assessors' budget line item for the Measure & List program for
the periodic inspections was initiated in Fiscal 14 for three years and it is still ongoing. The Board had
much discussion regarding the inspections and requested the number of properties that fall into the nine/
ten yr. program and wanted to know how many properties have been inspected since the Measure and
List Program began in FY2014. They also requested a report from Harald regarding how many more
properties still need to be inspected for the FY19 audit. Louise will inform Harald of this request and
suggest that he provide this info at the next meeting.
