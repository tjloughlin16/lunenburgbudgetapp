Minutes from the BOA (Board of Assessors) meeting held: July 13, 2022
Members present: Chairman, Richard H, Letarte, Board Member, Kevin Fish. Also present Principal
Assessor CJ Carroll and Administrative Assessing Assistant, Sheila Craigen
Rich opened the meeting at 2:32 pm
Open Meeting:
• Approval of June 14, 2022 minutes motioned, as amended, by Rich and seconded by Kevin.
• CJ stated he had a virtual meeting with Nugen Capital Management. CJ felt the meeting went well and
is hoping to come to an agreement before ATB (Appellate Tax Board) is filed. The BOA agreed a simple
pilot, depreciation plus an escalation clause to match Proposition 2 1/2 would be best.
• CJ stated Julie Belliveau is drafting and RFP (Request for Proposal) for cyclical inspections in FY23
(Fiscal Year 2023). Approximately 1,500 (one thousand five hundred) inspections are needed to be ready
for our upcoming Revaluation & Recertification by the State. CJ stated all commercial and industrial
properties are done.
• CJ presented a real estate application received July 7, 2022. The BOA reviewed and voted to deny due
to application not timely filed
• CJ stated preliminary real estate bills went out smoothly. Supplemental bills also issued for new
construction.
• Some discussion ensued regarding Elderly Exemptions and the new program for Senior Tax Relief
being considered by the town. Kevin made a motion to approve current exemption amounts.
Rich seconded.
• BOA reviewed and approved expense vouchers, abatements, warrants and commitments.
Adjournment:
• Meeting adjourned at 3:30 pm.
