STATUS REPORT FROM PRINCIPAL ASSESSOR.
• FY2025 Abatement Update, Chapter Land Application/Lien Issues. Other items
as needed.
14 abutters list requests and 30 motor vehicle excise tax abatements have
been received.
There were 132 real estate exemptions for FY2026.
Denise McCue is continuing to collect data and follow up with building
permits.
Cyclical inspections started back up on May 12th. The Assessor's website and the
Lunenburg Facebook page have been updated with anticipated streets for each
week. All postcards for this fiscal year have been mailed, thereby providing
notices to residents. If there is an inspection of the property, but the resident
doesn't want an interior inspection they are able to decline.
There were a few chapter land applications that weren't received nor a response to
the 2 letters sent. Those properties have been returned to full value. There are 3
additional chapter land properties that do not have liens nor applications.

Next month's goals include:
- Continue building inspections.
- Continue to work on FY2026 growth.
Currently the growth is $333,842.29 which is an assessed
value of $23,248,070. It was estimated to be $400,000 for
FY 2026.
- Start working on Scope of Work for the Neighborhood Review.
Expect to take ~ 3 years to complete.
