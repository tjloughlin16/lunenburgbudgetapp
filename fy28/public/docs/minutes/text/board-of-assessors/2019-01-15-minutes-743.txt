FY19 Chapter Account Completion of Approvals:
"New" for FY20 - Low Places Ranch, LLC - Cassidy - Powell, Jeffrey - 285 E. Goodrich St., Map
129, Parcel 1. BOA reviewed the application seeking 103 acres for chapter designation. The BOA
granted 81.66 to be classified as Chapter 61A as Agricultural Land and the remaining 21.34 acres to be
classified as Recreational Land under Chapter 61B. Mr. Powell will need to get in touch with Forest
Manager if he wishes to maintain the Forest Management Plan which is presently in place. A new lien will
be available to the Board for review at the next meeting.
Stillman, Garth & Colleen - BOA reviewed the Chapter 61A application which consists of land located
at 991, 992 & 1168 Lancaster Ave with a total acreage of 180 acres. (285 E Goodrich St was previously
under their ownership). Income documentation was not provided with application. After much discussion,
the BOA voted to grant the continuation of the Chapter Land designation after recognizing that the
property is also identified as Agricultural Preservation Restriction Land; thus receipts of income are not
required by the Board. Board recommended that a letter be sent to the Stillmans' to notify them of the
decision and the following: the application needs to be filed timely; contact the State Forester to
remove 285E Goodrich St., parcel 129-01 from the current Forest Management Plan and an amended
partial lien release must be recorded at the Registry of Deeds.
