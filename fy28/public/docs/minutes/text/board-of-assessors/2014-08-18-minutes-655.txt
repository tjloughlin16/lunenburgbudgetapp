684 FLAT HILL ROAD
Paul Bertrand met with the Board to discuss the private well at the above property. Mr. Bertrand has
owned this property for 12 years. He stated that there has always been limited water at the property but it
was always adequate. Tenant advised that there was no water. Mr. Bertrand stated that the well is not
easily accessible so deepening the well is costly and beyond his resources. He further stated that he has
found a company that can "frack" the well remotely.
Chairman George Emond stated that Housing Regulations require water be provided to the home. If
fracking does not provided the necessary water, then a new well will have to be drilled and if there is no
water than there can be no occupancy of this home.
....
Mr. Bertrand' acknowledged that he was aware of the Regulations and would report back to the Board as
to the status of the well.
174 NORTHFIELD ROAD
Chris MacKenzie met with the Board on behalf of the owner. The septic system on this property is in
failure. Mr. MacKenzie is requesting a variance for an offset to groundwater from 4 feet to 3 feet. In all
other respects, this is a compliant design.
