The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on MONDAY, October 27th, 2025 
at 6:05 p.m., Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to make a recommendation to 
Annual Town Meeting 2025 to the Code of the Town of Lunenburg, MA to conform with 
the MBTA Communities Compliance Zoning Amendment § 250-4.19 Multi-Family 
Overlay District M.G.L c. 40A.§ 3A. , and to make an ammendment to the Code of the 
Town of Lunenburg, MA,  § 250-6.4C(7)(c)(6)  by “Removing Bituminous Concrete” 
Requirement from Common Driveways.
