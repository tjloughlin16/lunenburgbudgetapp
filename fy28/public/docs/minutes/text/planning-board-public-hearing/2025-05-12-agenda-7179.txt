PUBLIC HEARING
Monday 2025-05-12 – 6:05 P.M | 17 Main St, Lunenburg MA
63 Lancaster Ave (Scenic Road Alteration)
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a joint Public Hearing with the Tree Warden on 2025-05-12 at 
6:05 p.m., Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to hear and review:
An application for Scenic Road Alteration, pursuant to M.G.L. Ch. 40 § 15C  by Dave Clapham (on behalf of
Unitil), for removal of a large dying Sugar Maple tree (including stump grinding and replanting with a 
new tree Sugar Maple tree) near Unitil pole 8.
