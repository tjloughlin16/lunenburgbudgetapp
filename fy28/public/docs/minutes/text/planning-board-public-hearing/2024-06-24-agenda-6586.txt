Public Hearing 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on Monday June 24, 2024 6:05 p.m., 
Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to hear and review a Special Permit Application by 
Steven A. Borodawka/Alpha Signs for a a free-standing sign in the Village Center District under 
Section 250-4.17 H(3)(j)of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg (as amended at the November 2023 
Special Town Meeting). The subject property is located at 3 Lancaster Avenue (Parcel Number 71-
0001) and is owned by One Family Insurance Agency, 3 Lancaster Road, Lancaster, MA 01434. 
Materials are available for viewing at the Planning Office, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, 
MA. This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, on FacebookLive on the Public 
Access Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-
157427270975305/) and will be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel 
within 24 hours after the meeting. To participate remotely: from a computer, please use the link 
below to join the public meeting webinar: https://us02web.zoom.us/j/88251881562 or from a 
telephone, dial the toll free number (888) 475-4499 and enter meeting ID: 882 5188 1562. Please 
note that while an option for remote attendance and/or participation is being provided as a courtesy 
to the public, the hearing will not be suspended or terminated if technological problems interrupt 
the virtual broadcast, unless otherwise required by law. Members of the public with particular 
interest in the subject matter of this public hearing should make plans for in-person vs. virtual 
attendance accordingly.
