PUBLIC HEARING 
2024-10-28 – 6:05 P.M | 17 Main St, Lunenburg MA  
1260 Reservoir Rd (Site Plan Approval)  
 
   
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on 2024-10-28 at 6:05 p.m., Town Hall, 17 Main 
Street, Lunenburg, MA to hear and review:  
  
An application for Site Plan Approval pursuant to §250-8.4(B) of the Code of the Town of Lunenburg 
(as amended at the 2016-08-22 Special Town Meeting) by P.J. Keating Company for construction of 
a new quality control building. 
  
Subject Property Information: 
Location: 1260 Reservoir Rd (Assessor’s Map 142 Parcel Number 7-0). 
Owner: Heirs to Paul J. Keating, 998 Reservoir Rd, Lunenburg, MA 01462 
  
Materials are viewable online at https://lunenburgma.portal.opengov.com/records/11422 , or in-person at the 
Planning Office (960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA)
