Town of Lunenburg  
Public Hearing 
 
The Lunenburg Planning Board will hold a Public Hearing on October 30, 2023, 6:05 p.m., 
Town Hall, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA to make alterations to Code of the Town of 
Lunenburg, MA, Chapter 250, Zoning, Article II Definitions Section 250-2.1 Terms Defined; 
word usage; Article IV Use Regulations, Sections 250-4.1C(2); 250-4.5A Commercial District 
Accessory Uses; 250 -4.6A Office Park/Industrial District Accessory Uses; 250-4.9 Water 
Supply Protection District; 250 -4.13 Solar Energy Systems; Article V  Dimensional 
Regulations Section 250-5.1B(5); Article VI General Provisions Affecting All Districts Section 
250-6.4 Driveways and Entrances; and updates to the Official Zoning Map .  Materials are 
available for viewing at the Planning Office, 960 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA.  
This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, on FacebookLive on the 
Public Access Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg-Public-Access-Cable-
157427270975305/) and will be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel 
within 24 hours after the meeting.   To participate remotely: from a computer, please use 
the link below to join the public meeting webinar:  https://us02web.zoom.us/j/7663167607 
or from a telephone, dial the toll free number (888) 475 -4499 and enter meeting ID: 766
