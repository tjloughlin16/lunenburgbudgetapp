NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING  
  
The Lunenburg School Committee in compliance with M.G.L. and the Town of Lunenburg Charter 
advises all citizens the proposed FY24 Budget is available for review at the Office of the 
Superintendent, 1025 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA, between the hour s of 8:00 a.m. 
and 4:00 p.m., Monday through Friday by appointment only .  Copies are also available on the 
website (www.lunenburgschools.net). 
  
A public hearing on the FY24 proposed budget will be broadcast remotely on Wednesday, March 
22, 2023 at 6:30 p.m.   The proposed FY24 budget requests  
$ 22,883,442 for personnel and non-personnel related expenses; a 2.50% increase above the FY23 
budget.
