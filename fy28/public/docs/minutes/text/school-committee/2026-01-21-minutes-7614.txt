10. New Business 
 
Action 
a. Grant Amendment Proposal Dr. Sarfde reports that because we had the unexpected 
resignation, we would like to reallocate the funds from the Student Opportunity Act (SOA).  We 
want to use these funds in a way that maintains the fidelity to the grants purpose.  We would be 
focused on building the capacity of the people in the buildings now.  For example, helping to 
plan and facilitate professional learning communities or helping design lesson plans that teachers 
would use in small groups, this would happen outside normal school hours.  I am also looking 
for two positions to do some work over the summer, we’d like to offer some professional 
development, and more planning and digging into data that would help build the capacity of our 
teachers.  Ms. Young makes the motion to approve the amendment for the grant, Ms. 
Gilman seconds the motion, all approve 
 
b. Preschool Tuition Increase Ms. Squier presents a new model for 2 day, 3 day, 4 day, and a 5 
day option.  We would like our afternoon to be an all-inclusive program.  Mornings will stay the 
same, our afternoon would change to a 5 day option, along with the 2 and 3 day option.   Tuition 
increase would be 4% Ms. Gilman makes a motion to approve the addition of 5 days and 
tuition increase, Ms. Young seconds the motion, all approve
