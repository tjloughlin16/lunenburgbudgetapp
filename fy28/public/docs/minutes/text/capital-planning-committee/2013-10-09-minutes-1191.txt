Meeting Posted:  Yes 
Place: Ritter Memorial Building, 960 Massachusetts Avenue

Present:  Chair, Marion M. Benson, Jamie Toale, Steve Raboin, Colleen Shapiro, Dennis Mannone, Jr.  
 
9-25-13 minutes approved. 
 
Discussion on department returns.  It was noted there was not a vehicle inventory in the Police Dept. returns.   
 
Police Department:   
 
Technology:  Chief Marino noted that as he has been busy with technology issues relating to the move to Regional Dispatch  
(RD), he and the IT Director  have not had much opportunity to confer.  Items needed in relation to the move to RD are two IP 
(Internet Protocol) security cameras on the perimeter o f the PSFB (Public Safety Facility Building) which can be remotely 
monitored.  Currently have analog cameras which require someone to manually move them .  He is requesting a Crime Scene 
Camera.  Tablets to go in the cruisers instead of laptops to create mo re room.  Toughbooks need to be replaced.  Fax Machine 
needs replacing.  These four items were agreed upon between Chief Marino and Steve Malandrinos (IT Director).  Chief Marino 
would prioritize as , 1)  IP Cameras, 2) Tablets, 3) Fax Machine and 4) Crime Scene Camera .  Mr. Raboin inquired if funds 
available through Regional Dispatch for IP Cameras.  None that Chief Marino is aware of.  
 
Facilities:  DPW request (for FY 17) to develop unused space above Police Department for record storage.  Chief Marino wou ld 
like for FY 16.  Also requested epoxy cell floors for sanitation and safety (FY 15).   
 
Vehicles:  Requesting one marked patrol vehicle for FY 15.  He wanted to bring to the Committee’s attention that there will be 
years when the purchase of three vehicles will be necessary.  Replacing vehicles in the future will become problematic if the plan 
is not adhered to; the purchase of police vehicles is a priority.  Mr. Raboin inquired how to avoid purchasing three vehicles in one 
year.  The regula r patrol vehicles get a lot of miles on them and they can keep them aside as spare vehicles.  Find a way to 
recycle to get more out of them?  Chief Marino noted that usually by the time the Police Dept. is ready to get rid of a v ehicle, it 
has body and frame decay.  The Committee noted that i f the vehicle is good , but for operational reasons no longer needed  
anymore, the Committee could possibly find another home for it.  Chief Marino wanted to note that prices may change as prices 
he currently obtained are for 2014.  Come spring, no more 2014’s will be manufactured and then will have to use the price for a 
2015 model.  Consequently, he ends up with less money for outfitting the vehicle as the figures he submits includes that cost .
