• Presentation of Capital Planning Requests: Town Facilities and Parks 
Presentation by Facilities Director Chris Ruth and Parks and Recreation Anna Lockwood 
 
FY 2025 requests: 
- Town wide keying and card access for all town buildings except for schools: $185,000 
o This will be cloud based. The lifespan for the hardware is 20 years and the alarm.  
- Engineering cost to fit out office space and file storage at the public safety building on 
the 2nd floor:  $35,000  
- Partial roof replacement at the DPW: $65,000 
o Specifically, over the office portion. Currently there are water issues, but it has 
not started to leak into the building.   
- Floor repair and coating in DPW garage floor: $60,000 
- TCP asbestos testing: 50,000  
o This will need to be completed even if the building is torn down. 
- Floor replacement at the Ritter Building: $60,000

- Patch and paint interior of the Ritter Building: $60,000 
- Stair cover at the rear access at the Ritter Building: $35,000 
- Senior center parking: $150,000  
o Currently there are only 9 designated parking spots. 
- Marshall Pond weed treatment- partial: $50,000 
o Includes removal of weeds under the surface with a hydro rake. After weed

      - Resurface basketball and tennis courts at Wallace Park: $70,000
