5. Fire Department Capital Requests from FY2017 to FY2020 total $1,860,000.
• As a general rule, the Town spends around $500,000 on capital projects each year.
• The Town borrowed to buy the Fire Truck this fiscal year and will be paying about
$100,000 per year through FY2019.
6. Capital Planning Committee Web site:
• To address a potential issue where the link to the calendar on the Capital Planning Site
either does not work or links to a different version of the CPC meeting agenda, the
committee agreed remove the link. Rather, this link on the Town's web site goes to the
CPC agendas.
• The CPC agreed to post the list of funded capital projects for FY2015 as approved at the
Annual Town Meeting early May, 2014.
• Mission Statement: Dennis M. and Joanna had submitted mission statements for the
committee to consider for the web site. Dennis' submission laid out the committee's
responsibilities, while Joanna's described committee procedures.
• Heather moved that the web site include Dennis statement of goals, followed by
Joanna's description of procedures. Dennis seconded her motion, and the
committee approved the motion 5-0.
• Jamie will ask Steve Malandrinos to make these additions to the Capital
Planning Committee web site.
7. Agendas through December 16. The CPC approved the suggestion that Jamie and John craft
agendas for the meetings of November 18, December 2, and December 16.
• Jamie and John will draft agendas for these meetings and send them to Dennis,
Heather, and Joanna to look over prior to the November 18 meeting.
8. Adjournment: Joanna moved to adjourn the meeting; Heather seconded her motion, and the
committee voted 5-0 to adjourn the meeting at 6:20 pm.
