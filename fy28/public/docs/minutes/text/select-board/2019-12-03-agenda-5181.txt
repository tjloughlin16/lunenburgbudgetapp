VI.  WORKSHOP ON OLD PRIMARY SCHOOL BUILDING 
1.   Selectmen Objective 2: 
To identify, define and propose at least one project to the 2020 Annual Town Meeting regarding the use and/or re -use of the 
Old Primary School building, and/or the land on which it sits. 
i. Action:  Review the various paid studies that were performed that included that building with special attention being paid 
to its structural analysis, potential suggested uses and all applicable costs.  
ii. Action:  Overlay the results of the previous action with the short- and long-term needs of the Town, viewing those through 
the lens of an established list of prioritized criteria through which to evaluate them (as determined in the previous Objecti ve).  
Establish options (more than one but no more than four) to be examined more closely.  
iii. Action:  Have open sessions with other boards, committees and commissions as well as with the general public in 
discussing the options defined from Action iii. Evaluate the pros, cons and costs for each option, potential variations as we ll 
as possible timelines. 
iv. Action:  Determine one (or two, if applicable) option(s) to be fully developed into a proposed project for the building 
and/or site to be presented to the 2020 Annual Town Meeting for vote.
