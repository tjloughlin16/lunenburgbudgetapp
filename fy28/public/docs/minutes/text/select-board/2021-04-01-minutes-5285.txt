Members present: Tom Alonzo, Chair; Katey Adams, Clerk; Todd Dwyer, Jim Marino
Not present: Michael-Ray Jeffreys, Vice Chairman; Heather Lemieux, Town Manager
Chairman Alonzo called the meeting to order at 6:00 p.m. and announced in accordance with
the requirements of the Open Meeting Law, please be advised that this meeting is being
recorded and broadcast over the Lunenburg public access channel. In accordance with the
Governor's Order Suspending Certain Provisions of the Open Meeting Law, G. L. c. 30A, §
20, all public meetings are being conducted remotely. Mr. Alonzo read the remote meeting
information for anyone who would like to join the meeting. He asked if everyone was present
by roll call.
The meeting opened with the Pledge of Allegiance that was recited by Ms. Adams.
PUBLIC COMMENT
There was none this evening.
Mr. Alonzo stated the Select Board was holding this emergency meeting to act on a time
sensitive item. The position of Town Moderator was left off the Annual Town election
warrant that had been voted on by the Board at the March 30, 2021 meeting. The Board needs
to revote the amended warrant in order for it to be placed into the Annual Town Meeting
Warrant that is being mailed to voters and for the position to be on the election warrant.
Motion: J. Marino
2nd: K. Adams
To sign the revised Annual Town Election Warrant
Vote: Mr. Marino, Ms. Adams-aye, Mr. Dwyer- aye, Mr. Alonzo -aye
Mr. Alonzo advised the members to sign the warrant in person at Town Hall on Monday.
Motion: T. Dwyer
To adjourn the meeting
Vote: Mr. Marino, Ms. Adams-aye, Mr. Dwyer-aye, Mr. Alonzo-aye
2nd: J. Marino
The meeting adjourned at 6:10 p.m.
