II. Joint Meeting with the School Committee 
     Topics of Discussion: 
 The future of Turkey Hill Elementary School (long term plan) and 10 year projected costs; 
 Their input on the conceptual plan the Selectmen received from the architect hired to provide 
three different conceptual designs to consolidate town offices currently located in the Town 
Hall and Ritter Building, School central offices (including the ACE program), the Cable 
Department, and community use spaces such as the gym and kitchen area; 
 Inclusion of a town representative to participate in union negotiations;  
 School population projections and what that means for future building needs; 
 The role of liaisons (i.e. the Selectmen have been discussing the role of their liaisons to 
various boards/committees); 
 Joint planning opportunities such as events, outreach, and communication.
