Quarter FY23 Financial Report from Finance Director
Ms. Brochu presented her report. Summarized as below:
Revenues:
Total collections to date are $14,799,680.70, or 31.13% of FY23 estimates through 9/30/22.
Below is a summary of the individual categories:
Local Receipts: The local receipt estimate for FY23 is $3,168,990.50. We have collected
$695,049.26 or 22% of that estimate through 9/30/2022.
Taxes: Based on votes from the 5/7/22 ATM, the total tax levy for FY23, net of the overlay,
is $30,548,749.95. At this time we have collected $7,854,265.95 (26%) of the levy. We have
also received $33,225.29 in tax lien revenue.
State Aid: The total state aid (Cherry Sheet) estimate based on the Governor's proposed
budget for FY2023 is $10,258,395.00, net of school choice and library state aid. We have
received $2,682,222.63 (26%) of our state aid estimate.
Expenditures: The total budgeted expenditures for FY23 are $47,544,278.31. We have
expended $12,789,627.75 (26.9%) of budgeted expenditures as of 9/30/22. All expenditures
appear to be at the level they should be at this point in the fiscal year.
