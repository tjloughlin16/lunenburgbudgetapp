Select Board Meeting Minutes 
November 13, 2024 
Town of Lunenburg 
Select Board Meeting Minutes 
Ritter Memorial Conference Room, 960 Mass Ave and via Zoom 
Wednesday November 13, 2024 
 
Members Present: Caesar Nuzzolo, Michael-Ray Jeffreys, Amanda Moore, Tom Alonzo 
Also Present: Carter Terenzini, Interim Town Manager  
Public Comment Participants are listed in the minutes  
 
CALL TO ORDER  
The meeting was called to order at 6:05 PM by Chair Nuzzolo
. Chair Nuzzolo noted that it was 
being recorded and broadcast live on Lunenburg Public Access Channel, Facebook Live, and 
would be uploaded to the Lunenburg Access YouTube channel. the agenda listed all topics which 
may be discussed, but not all items listed may be discussed, and other items not listed may be 
brought up for discussion to the extent permitted by the open meeting law. (Chair did not recite 
pledge due to Flag not being present in meeting room).  
 
PUBLIC COMMENT 
 
Glenn McLeod, 436 Reservoir Road:, a resident, addressed the board regarding the previous 
night's town meeting. He encouraged the board to bring up two issues at the next town meeting: 
pay and vacation time for new employees. He emphasized the importance of these issues for 
recruitment. McLeod also suggested obtaining a report on why there was a mass resignation 
from the Personnel Committee. 
 
Action Re: ARPA Reprogramming 
Chair Nuzzolo began by discussing the reallocation of ARPA funds. He went through the current 
requests that have money allocated, starting with the municipal building design committee, 
which came in under $40,000. Member Jeffreys provided an update, stating that interviews were 
likely to be pushed back to November 20th, with the intention of bringing them under contract 
by the end of November. 
other projects: 
• Water district and Sewer Commission were noted as being all set. 
• Public exterior envelope had $27,000 remaining. 
• Procter Memorial Garden was all set with $1 remaining. 
• Teen room came in at $107,000 according to estimate. 
• Parks Commission was returning approximately $800,000 out of the $1,000,000 
allocated. 
 
Mr. Terenzini explained that the Parks Commission project had been broken into multiple phases, 
and the current allocation would cover 60% design for phases 1 and 2, including cost estimates 
and public presentations. 
The board then discussed the Munis web deployment, for which they were waiting on a quote for 
a two-year contract.

Chair Nuzzolo went through several other projects, including IV pumps, furniture for public 
safety, historical society tree removal, DPW garage floor replacements, and partial roof 
replacement, DPW Tree Removal, and Council on Aging kiosk and Adirondack chairs. 
The board then discussed how to reallocate surpluses to cover capital overruns, particularly for 
the town hall and senior center roofs. Mr. Terenzini explained the need for contingencies in these 
projects. 
 
A motion was made to reallocate surpluses to cover capital overruns: 
Member Jeffreys moved to reallocate surpluses to cover capital overruns as presented. Tom 
Alonzo seconded the motion. The motion passed unanimously. (5-0-0) 
 
The board then discussed a list of potential projects to fund with the remaining ARPA money.  
 
Member Jeffreys proposed a motion to allocate funds for specific items: 
Member Jeffreys moved for the select board to commit $387,565 of ARPA funds for the 
purpose of purchasing body camera upgrades for the police department ($45,000), power 
stretcher and loader ($74,000), replacement of 15 portable radios ($50,000), a stair chair 
($23,000), gas powered cement mixer ($4,720), mini excavator ($72,500), trailer for the mini 
excavator ($14,700), a pickup truck with plow for the school department ($64,780.05), 
Middle High School window safety film ($26,865), and a fence for the council on aging 
generator ($12,000). Member Moore Seconded. The motion passed unanimously (5-0-0). 
 
The board agreed to revisit the remaining requests and get updated information for their next 
meeting on Tuesday.
