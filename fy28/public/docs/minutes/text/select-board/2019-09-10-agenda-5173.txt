VI.  WORKSHOP:  TOWN FACILITIES WITH FOCUS ON OLD PRIMARY SCHOOL, 30 SCHOOL STREET 
1. Discussion re: letters from APDC re: old Primary School and cost estimate from Facilities Director 
2. Discussion re: Selectmen’s Objective #1 and #2 for Fiscal Year 2020: 
Objective 1:  To identify, define, discuss and finalize the criteria to be used in determining the use and/or re -use of the 
following buildings:  Town Hall, Ritter Building, Old Primary School, Brooks House, T.C. P assios building. 
i. Action:   Schedule a variety of meetings and discussion sessions (at Selectboard meetings, public charrettes, etc.) to get 
input from the widest variety of voices possible and practicable.  
ii. Action: Write a final report at the conclusion of those meetings and discussions to be voted upon by the Selectboard. That 
report will list AND prioritize the criteria to be used by the Selectboard and other municipal bodies in evaluating any/all 
projects to be done with the above named buildings and the land on which they lie. 
Objective 2: 
To identify, define and propose at least one project to the 2020 Annual Town Meeting regarding the use and/or re -use of the 
Old Primary School building, and/or the land on which it sits. 
i. Action:  Review the various paid studies that were performed that included that building with special attention being paid 
to its structural analysis, potential suggested uses and all applicable costs.  
ii. Action:  Overlay the results of the previous action with the short- and long-term needs of the Town, viewing those through 
the lens of an established list of prioritized criteria through which to evaluate them (as determined in the previous Objecti ve).  
Establish options (more than one but no more than four) to be examined more closely. 
iii. Action:  Have open sessions with other boards, committees and commissions as well as with the general public in 
discussing the options defined from Action iii. Evaluate the pros, cons and costs for each option, potential varia tions as well 
as possible timelines.
