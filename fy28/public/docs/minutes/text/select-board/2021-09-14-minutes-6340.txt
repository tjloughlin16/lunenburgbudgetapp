Also present: Town Manager Heather Lemieux
The meeting commenced at 9:25 p.m.
Ms. Lemieux reported on the property located at 665 Massachusetts Avenue. Resident Dave MacDonald had
spoken with her and offered to purchase this property and sell it to the Town for $400,000. She was not sure
what the advertised purchase price is. The assessed value is $265,000. She reported that Mr. Marino, before
retiring as Police Chief, had researched purchasing another lot directly in front of the Public Safety Building
(PSB) for additional egress from the PSB lot. It would also have been an ideal location for an Animal Control
facility.
Ms. Lemieux noted the 665 Mass Ave. property would not provide alternative egress to Beal Street in the event
of an accident on Mass Ave.
Question asked was what would be Mr. MacDonald's profit margin. Discussion ensued about the soil
conditions on the property and a lot of unique factors dealing with the sale. Ms. Lemieux noted Mr. MacDonald
has also offered to build an animal control facility on the lot.
Mr. Alonzo asked if anyone has been in contact of the property owner of the lot directly located in front of the
PSB. No one had. It was suggested to approach that property owner to revisit purchasing it.
Discussion resumed about Mr. MacDonald's offer and the Board's consensus was that Mr. MacDonald's offer
did not fit what the town needed. Mr. Marino will reach out to the other property owner discussed above.
Motion: J. Marino
To adjourn from Executive Session
Vote: Mr. Marino-aye, Mr. Franco-aye, Mr. Jeffreys-aye, Mr. Alonzo-aye
2nd: L. Franco
The meeting adjourned at 9:45 p.m.
