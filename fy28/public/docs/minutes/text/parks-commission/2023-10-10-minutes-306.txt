Meeting Minutes 
October 10, 2023 
1. A. Lockwood called the meeting to order at 7:18pm. 
Present: K. Menard, C. Sullivan, A. Lockwood, SelectBoard Members Michael-Ray Jeffreys, Lou Franco, Caesar 
Nuzzollo, Renee Emkey 
Absent: C. Shapiro 
2. Joint meeting with Select Board to interview/appoint to Parks Commission Vacancy until the May 2024 
Election: Anita Schiepers, 63 Chestnut Street 
Schiepers introduced herself and why she was interested in joining the Park Commission. 
M. Jeffreys asked if A. Schiepers had seen the plans for Marshall Park and what parts she liked. A. Schiepers said 
she had and that it was improved parking and the improvements that would benefit all age groups. She also said 
that what is being proposed is such multi level and multi generation improvements that the project was worth 
working towards. 
Park Commission and Selectboard thanked her for her willingness to volunteer.  
K. Menard motioned and C. Sullivan seconded to appoint A. Schiepers to the Park Commission through May 
2024. Passed unanimously.
