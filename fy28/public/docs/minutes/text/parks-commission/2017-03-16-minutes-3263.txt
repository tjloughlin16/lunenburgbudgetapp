1) Called to order by Dennis at 7:03pm. Attendance: Karin Menard, Dennis Mannone, Pete McCauliff (late ar-
rival @ 7:40pm)
2) Review Minutes of February 16, 2017 - Dennis Mannone motioned to accept minutes and Karin Menard sec-
onded. Approval passed 2-0
3) DPW Director / Town Manager Updates - none
4) Ongoing Business
a. Leominster Lacrosse Turf Request - Karin let the commission know that Lacrosse had provided in-
surance certificates that she forwarded to the Town Manager for approval. Heather Lemieux forwarded it to the
towns insurance carrier who said the Col is sufficient. She also said that Lacrosse had not yet submitted a
schedule but she expects that to happen soon.
b. Turf Complex Update and Discussion - Dennis informed the commission that the school is looking to
use the turf for their upcoming summer programs 6/26-6/30, 7/3-7/7 and 7/10 - 7/14. Pete explained that the
program was running as if it was a regular school day so no additional insurance is required. There was some
discussion on whether or not there needed to be a fee to use the field since it is outside of the normal school
year but the commission thought we should tackle and address this type of usage in the MoA with the school
district.
c. Memorandum of Agreement with Lunenburg Public Schools discussion - tabled due to time con-
straints and DPW not in attendance.
