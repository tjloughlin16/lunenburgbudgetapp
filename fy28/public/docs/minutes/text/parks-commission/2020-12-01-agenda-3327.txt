In accordance with the requirements of the Open Meeting Law, please be advised that this meeting is being recorded and broadcast

of Health, Massachusetts Department of Public Health and the CDC regarding the virus and steps communities can take to prevent the

recorded and be able to be found on the LunenburgAccess YouTube channel within 24 hours after the meeting.
