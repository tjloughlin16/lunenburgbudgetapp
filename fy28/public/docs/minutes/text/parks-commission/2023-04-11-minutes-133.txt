MINUTES 
April 11, 2023 
Joint meeting with Select Board 
Ana Lockwood called the Parks Commission meeting to order at 7:38 p.m. Present - A. Lockwood, K. Menard, D. Passios 
A.Lockwood presented the attached power point presentation to the Select Board. 
The board thanked Ms. Lockwood for the presentation. Mr. Alonzo asked what amount of ARPA funding is uncommitted. 
Ms. Lemieux stated $2.3 million. ARPA funds must be committed by December 31, 2024 and spent by December 31, 
2026. Ms. Lockwood stated her best case scenario would be breaking ground next summer after baseball season. 
Construction to completion would take 18 months; breaking ground by 2024 and completion by 2026. 
Discussion about the grant application process and deadlines for applying. K. Menard noted that their April 19th meeting 
is a community meeting with WOLA via ZOOM and we are starting the schematic phase. This will be the first of multiple 
community meetings. 
The Parks Commission also reported they are continuing on with their Earth Day projects and it's been very successful so 
far. Last week the Cub Scouts helped work on Veterans Park and two groups working at the Lane Property (Holman 
Street) removing invasive plants with more projects the upcoming weekend. More information is on the town website, 
Town Facebook page and also the Parks and Recreation page. 
K. Menard motioned and D. Passios seconded to adjourn at 8:14pm. Passed 3-0.
