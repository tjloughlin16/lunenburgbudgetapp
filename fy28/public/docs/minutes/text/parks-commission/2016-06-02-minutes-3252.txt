2. Marshall Park (Chestnut Street Field) - A residential home sustained damage to two windows
due to a foul ball during a scrimmage and a game last weekend. This has been an on going
problem with some foul balls causing damage to this home. An additional backstop had been
installed before the start of the season but that has clearly failed and an alternative needs to be
found and a temporary solution put in place. Jack has done some research and reached out to a
local company for a design and quote to construct a netted barrier that would contain most foul
balls. He will forward information as it becomes available.
Motion made by Dennis Mannone / Seconded by Bob Robuccio - Baseball games
temporarily halted and practice will be allowed with use of batting tunnel. Passed 3-0.
3. Wallace Courts (unanticipated) - Jack Rodriquenz and subsequently Dennis Mannone were
approached by a local group wanting to play summer basketball at Wallace Park. Discussion was
had regarding instituting a $10 per Lunenburg player per season for use of the courts since that is
what is currently being charged to Lunenburg Little League for use of the baseball fields.
Motion made by Dennis Mannone / Seconded by Bob Robuccio- $10 per Lunenburg
Player Fee for use of Wallace Park. Schedule to be submitted by organization and a
Certificate of Insurance must be provided. Passed 3-0.
4. Motion for Adjournment at 8:45 by Dennis Mannone / Seconded by Karin Menard. Passedrel
3-0.
