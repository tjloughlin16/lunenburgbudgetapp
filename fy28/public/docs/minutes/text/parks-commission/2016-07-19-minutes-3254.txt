Meeting Minutes
Tuesday July 19, 2016
Town Hall - Bilotta Meeting Room
Lunenburg, MA 01462
1) Call to Order - 7:10pm
2) Mr. Pete McCauliff was the single applicant for the open Parks Commission seat. Mr. McCau-
liff spoke briefly on the reasons why he is a good candidate for the open position. Ms. Luck
asked Mr. McCauliff if he would recuse himself from voting on items involving the school since
he is employed by the school as the Athletic Director. Mr. McCauliff replied that he has had dis-
cussions with the Massachusetts Ethics Commission and will recuse himself where necessary
and would also contact the Ethics Commission if he needed further clarification. Mr. Toale asked
Mr. McCauliff if he would be willing to run for the position in the next town election cycle and Mr.
McCauliff said he would consider it as long as there doesn't end up being too many conflict of
interest issues.
Both Dennis Mannone and Karin Menard supported the appointment of Mr. McCauliffe.
Motion made by Karin Menard / Seconded by Robert Ebersole - to appoint Pete McCau-
liffe to the Parks Commission until the next election cycle.
Vote: Mir. Toale - yes, Mr. Ebersole - yes, Ms. Luck - yes, Mr. Mannone - yes, Ms.
Menard - yes.
3) Adjournment - 7:20
