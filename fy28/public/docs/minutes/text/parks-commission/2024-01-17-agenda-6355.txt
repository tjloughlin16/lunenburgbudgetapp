In accordance with the requirements of the Open Meeting Law, please be advised that this meeting is being recorded and it will be 
broadcasted over Lunenburg Public Access channel and uploaded to the LunenburgAccess YouTube channel.

meeting. Please note that while an option for remote attendance and/or participation is being provided as a courtesy to the public, the

law. Members of the public with particular interest in any specific item on this agenda should make plans for in-person vs. virtual 
attendance accordingly.
