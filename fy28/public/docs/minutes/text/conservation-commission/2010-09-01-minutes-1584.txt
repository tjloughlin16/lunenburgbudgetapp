Lunenburg Conservation Commission
Minutes
September 1, 2010
Rob Verge, JJ Cote, Ken Jones, Mark Murphy, Sharon Jordan, Jan Carrier
7:30 PM ~ Miscellaneous ~ Ken Jones Resignation: The Commission accepted Ken's resignation
with regret, CoC for 410 Lancaster Avenue was signed (1999)
7:40 PM - 64 Spring Street Extension ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of
Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent
filed by Sandra Perko (Chris MacKenzie of Whitman & Bingham Associates, LLC). The proposed project is
for a Septic system tight tank installation, conversion of deck to habitable space, conversion of second
existing camp building into storage shed.
Chris presented. JJ/Ken approved tight tank installation.
Unanimous
7:50 PM ~ 72 Hemlock Drive ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg
Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Request for Determination of
Applicability filed by Richard A/Patricia B Kullman. The proposed project is for removal of four trees
approximately 40' and 70' from the lake. Both trees closest to the lake are oaks, leaning 20 degrees.
About 20" and 24" in diameter. Dick Kullman presented. Ken/Mark Negative 3-Unanimous
8:00 PM ~ 18 Cove Terrace ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg
Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Request for Determination of
Applicability tiled by Kenneth L. Franklin (Rick Haggar Surfside Pool Company). The proposed project is
for Installation of an inground pool 12 × 28. Ken presented. JJ/Ken Negative 3-Unanimous
8:10 PM ~ 24 Hemlock Terrace ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg
Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent filed by
Patricia Michaud (Steven Marsden of Marsden Engineering Inc). The proposed project is for replacement
of a failed sewage disposal system. Steve presented. JJ/Mark-Unanimous
8:20 PM ~ 20 Fire Road 19 ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg
Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent filed by
Norman/Pamela J Vincent. The proposed project is for demolition of the existing house and replace with a
new one on same footprint. Pam/Norm presented. Ken/Mark-Unanimous.
