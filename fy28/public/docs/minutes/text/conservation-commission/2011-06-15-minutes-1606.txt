Town of Lunenburg
Conservation Commission
MINUTES
June 15, 2011
7:00 PM
Ritter Memorial Building
Attendees: Rob Verge, JJ Cote, John Lattanzi, Mark Murphy, Sharon Jordan, Jan Carrier, Matt Marro
7:00 PM ~ Janet Morrison, Executive Director of North County Land Trust met with the
commission to explain and recommend the Conservation Restriction on the former
Millington property on Northfield Road. The property consists of 21.68 acres and abuts
Mulpus Brook and the Lane property off of Holman Street. The property would stay in
the ownership of Mr. Millington and his wife. All details are explained fully in the
paperwork presented. The commission members unanimously voted to approve the
restriction and signed the necessary document.
