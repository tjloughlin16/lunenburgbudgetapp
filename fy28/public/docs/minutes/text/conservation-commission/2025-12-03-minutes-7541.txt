NEW PUBLIC HEARINGS
a. 199 Mass Ave - NOI-25-20
A wetland and wetland buffer zone restoration project due to historic disturbance activities at the site. An open
wetlands violation was issued on April 1, 2025, by the Lunenburg Conservation Commission. The 0-30'; 30'-50',
and 50'-100' buffer zones will be restored using wetland seed mixes and a line of boulders and new maple trees
spaced 50' on center along the limit of the 0-30' 'No Touch Zone'.
Ryan Proctor (Dillis & Roy), Mike Chapdelaine and Mark Chapdelaine present. R. Proctor reviewed the project.
The wetland disturbance was about remediation of the property. Trees were removed, but stumps remain. Most of
the cleanup is now complete, but further restoration is planned.

b. 13 Birch Island Way- RDA-25-17
Construction of conventional wood with vinyl siding and asphalt roofing 3 season porch off the main entrance of
the existing home. The addition will be attached to the center of the home facing the lake with portions of the new
footprint replacing the existing deck. The room will be supported with a Helical Pier style foundation to avoid
excavation or concrete work and provide minimal ground disturbance. The addition will not extend towards the
lake any further than the existing deck.
Paul and Caroline Fortin and their contractor (Michael McPhee) attended. Teh proposed deck is almost entirely in
the 50-foot no-build zone. Portions of the deck would be a 3-season room.
