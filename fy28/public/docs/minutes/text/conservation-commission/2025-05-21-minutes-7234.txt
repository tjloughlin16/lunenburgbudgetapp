3. APPOINTMENTS
a. Hickory Hills Landowners, Inc - Yearly Flumioxazin spot treatments in Hickory Hills Lake to combat
larger Fanwort areas.
i. Solitude Lake Management explained that where the fan wart is dense, hand pulling is inappropriate,
The use of an EPA and state registered aquatic herbicide is recommended. Solitude and members of
the lake group conducted a survey on May 1 and found 10 areas totaling 32 acres. Treatment will be
done on June 11 using a GPS-controlled system to ensure only approved locations are treated.
ii. A second invasive aquatic plant (brittle niad najas minor) was found in Little Hickory Hills Lake and
will be treated later in the summer.
iii. The lake group will do monthly water testing through the summer
iv. Some adjustments to the plan were made due to restrictions on how often this product can be used
in the same location. We are also well under the maximum of one quarter of the lake surface.
