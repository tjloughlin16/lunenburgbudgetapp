Notice of Intent by Daniel & Rachel Brackett for a common driveway to provide access to four residential lots.

snow, site walks were not completed. DEP's comments were received today, but no responses have been generated yet. G.
Roy explained that this is a complex project. Four lots each with a five-unit townhouse for a total of 20 residential housing

should be allowed to regrow.
• The clearing was marked as long-term forest management.
• The property should be modeled as "good condition" woods.
• Adequate free treatment is required for infiltrating BMPPS to achieve the listed credit percentage.
