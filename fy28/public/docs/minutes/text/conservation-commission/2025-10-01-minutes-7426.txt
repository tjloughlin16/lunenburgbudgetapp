NEW PUBLIC HEARINGS
a. 100 Howard St- RDA-25-12 Video link
A Request for Determination of Applicability for the reclassification of Mulpus Brook as an intermittent stream.
Mary Ann DiPinto (Three Oaks Environmental) represented the applicant. The applicant attended as well. M.
DiPinto shared photographs and noted that what is being considered is a tributary to Mulpus Brook, not what is
documented in the paperwork. Stream stats in OpenGov are for 'a portion of Mulpus Brook.' J. Pewtherer noted
her maps show it as Mulpus Brook. M. Wilson noted that Mass Mapper shows it as Mulpus Brook up to the pond
at 390 Howard.
J. Pewtherer ready the DEP comments relating to drought declarations, including this location. DEP requested the
application be withdrawn.
Tanner Cole (856 Mass Ave) stated he owned the property since 2017 and that the stream dries out every summer.
R. Bursch wondered if a peer review was appropriate.
M. DiPinto suggested they can take the 4 picture in May and then refile. T. Cole decided to withdraw the
application based on this information.
