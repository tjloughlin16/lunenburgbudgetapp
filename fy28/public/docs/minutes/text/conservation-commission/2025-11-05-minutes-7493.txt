b. 34 Pine Grove Rd - RDA-25-13
A REQUEST FOR DETERMINATION OF APPLICABILITY by Marc and Linda Chase for emergency repairs to
a 50+ year old retaining wall on the shore of Lake Whalom. The wall measures 27'-0 long and is within
10'-0 of the lake shore. The wall is seriously damaged, and our consulting engineer found that it was in
danger of collapsing. The repairs will consist of placing concrete retaining wall blocks against the
existing wall. The work will include preparing a concrete base for the blocks and grading the yard
behind the wall.
Ryan Proctor (Dillis & Roy) represented the applicant and reviewed the project. The plan is to build a
new block wall in front of the existing block wall with gravel fill in between. Project should only take
two days. It approved, it will need a building permit.
C. St Germain wanted to confirm the new wall will be strong enough to retain the existing wall. R.
Proctor explained that is the reason the new wall leans inward. T. Doucette confirmed that the soil will
be restored. M. Larouche confirmed that the two walls will be free-standing and not connected.
