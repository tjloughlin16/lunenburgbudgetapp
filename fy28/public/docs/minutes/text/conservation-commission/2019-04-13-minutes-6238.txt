   NOTICE OF MEETING  
 123 Hollis Road, Hollis Road Conservation Land 
Saturday, April 13 2019 at 9:00 AM 
  
 
MINUTES 
 
 
1. Call to Order Chariman Bursch called to order at 9m with all present except BP and CC  
2. Site Walk of Conservation Land:  Review of proposed Forest Stewardship Plan submitted by 
New England Forestry Consultants. Gary Gouldrup toured the commission and the public through 
each area specified in the stewardship plan. This was in preparation for public disc ussion at the meeting 
scheduled for April 17, 2019 in the Billotta meeting room 2 nd floor at 17 Main Street.  Ginni and Norbert 
Albertson raised concerns relevant to the continued protection of the pollinator habitat. Norbert Albertson 
examined any potential revenue generation with the commission. KJ noted that revenue generation is part of 
the equation to ensure the work towards bird protection can be affordable to execute but was not the primary 
driving factor. Jon Jones spoke of other conservation parcels that have bird habitat cultivated and how well 
they work. One of the biggest concerns of the participants continues to be the impact of skidder trails to 
access interior foresting. Several ideas were suggested to minimize this impact including access thr ough the 
field from the south of the house as well as access via Rt. 2A through the Lunenburg Water District site.  
3. Adjourn – The Commission adjourned at 11 am with a motion by TD and a second by KJ with a unanimous 
vote.
