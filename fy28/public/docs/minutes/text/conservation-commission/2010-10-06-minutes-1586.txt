7:50 PM ~ 23 Valley Road ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg
Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent filed by
Carolyn McCarthy (James Rheault of Whitman & Bingham Associates LLC). The proposed project is for
selective cutting of trees within a wetland and bordering vegetative wetlands and their respective buffer
zones to allow for construction of solar panels on the roof of a single family home. Jamie Rheault from
Whitman & Bingham presented new plans which would include NO stump removal. Stated there
will be 22 solar panels. The commission suggested that they have a selective cutting plan and a
plan for crane cutting. The gas tank excavation will require a separate NOI. Rich/Mark 6 Yes 1 No
8:00 PM ~ Wilder Beach Association ~ Phil Porter, Steve DiPace, Russell Hume re: Whalom Spillway
The group met with the Commission to inform them that the Wilder Beach Association has been
formed and now owns the beach and spillway at Wilder Road. Wanted to know who took care of
the spillway and who is responsible for it. They own it and are responsible for it. The Town, by
the state, was authorized to_control the spillway. They will schedule to meet with the Commission
again.
