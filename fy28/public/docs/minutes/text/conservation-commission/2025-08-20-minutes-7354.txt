d. O Whalom Ra- NOI-25-17
A Notice of Intent by the Town of Lunenburg for the use of hand harvesting/diver assisted suction
harvesting, herbicides, and algaecides to manage the growth of non-native aquatic plants and
prevent the growth of algae blooms in Lake Whalom. The project will address eutrophication,
improve aquatic habitats that support fish and wildlife species, and restore the natural capacity
of the resource area to protect the interests identified in the Wetlands Protection Act (M.G. L. c.
131940)
Matt Ladewig (TRC) represented the Town. Also present were Christopher Ruth (Facilities,
Ground, & Recreation) and Karen Menard (Park Commission). M. Ladewig presented the project.
The focus is on Town Beach and the non-native species with high biomass. The State has
designated the lake category 4C. The proposal is a combination of hand, diver and DASH
