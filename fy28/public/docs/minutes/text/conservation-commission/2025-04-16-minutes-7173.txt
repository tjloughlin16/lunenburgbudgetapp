NOTICE OF INTENT by Shawn Farrow for the construction of two landscape replacement walls,
placement of two removable docks & replenishment of beach sand within the 100 ft, 50 ft & 30 ft buffer
zone of a wetland resource area on Lake Shirley.
Matt Marrow represented the applicant. No trees are proposed to be removed. No flood plain filling is
proposed. Part of the request is to remove grass that has established itself on the sand beach and to
replace some sand. Request also includes an 'after the fact' wood dock. A 15-foot section of retaining
wall needs repair and would have a defined erosion blanket. DEP had no comments. Since the
removable docks are not actually taken out in winter, this suggests the work that is within the wetlands.
They plan to complete the project during the draw down. Requested details regarding the type of wall
to be built. Requested confirmation that the outer edge of the block will not extend beyond the current
excursion. There was general agreement that removing the grass to recover the sand beach was
acceptable.
