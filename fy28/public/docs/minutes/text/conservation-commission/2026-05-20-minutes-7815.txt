b. 934 Massachusetts Avenue: NOI-26-17 ‹Video link>
Notice of Intent by Unitil for the construction of a new substation and replacement of utility poles
Ting Chang and Nathan Sherwood (Unitil) represented the applicant. T. Chang provided a quick history of the
site. Proposal is to reduce some of the abandoned substation and expand 38,000 square feet for the new
substation. Existing asphalt pavement will be replaced with gravel. Property will be enclosed with an 8-foot chain
fence with barbed wire on top. The only impervious area is the 211x29 foot control building. Two replication
options were reviewed. Since the work will impact both the 30-foot buffer and the 50-foot buffer, Unitil is
requesting a waiver from the local bylaws to allow the project to go forward.
M. Larouche asked about abandoning the substation and was in formed that the equipment would be removed. R.
Bursch asked about the height of the substation and that he would prefer to see a replication area equal to the size
of wetlands that will be affected. T. Doucette agreed. M. Wilson wondered what growth period this was intended
to cover. N. Sherwood suggested it would be good enough for 50 years. Construction could begin in January or
February, but the replication area would be done first. Construction would take 4-6 months. R. Bursch questioned
the tree varieties listed for the replication area. T. Chang explained the trees were recommended by their wetlands
scientist but are willing to adjust. J. Pewtherer noted that the DEP number has not been received, so a vote cannot
be taken.
