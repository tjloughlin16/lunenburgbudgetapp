4. NEW PUBLIC HEARINGS 
All hearings are pursuant to MGL Ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and Municipal Bylaws: 
a. 199 Mass Ave – NOI-25-20 
A public hearing on a wetland and wetland buffer zone restoration project due to historic disturbance activities at the site. An open 
wetlands violation was issued on April 1, 2025 by the Lunenburg Conservation Commission. The 0-30'; 30'-50', and 50'-100' buffer zones 
will be restored using wetland seed mixes and a line of boulders and new maple trees spaced 50' on center along the limit of the 0-30' 'No 
Touch Zone'. 
b. 13 Birch Island Way- RDA-25-17
 
A public hearing on the construction of conventional wood with vinyl siding and asphalt roofing 3 season porch off the main entrance of 
the existing home.  The addition will be attached to the center of the home facing the lake with portions of the new footprint replacing the 
existing deck.  The room will be supported with a Helical Pier style foundation to avoid excavation or concrete work and provide minimal 
ground disturbance.  The addition will not extend towards the lake any further than the existing deck. 
5. CONTINUED PUBLIC HEARINGS 
a. 480 Mulpus Rd – RDA-25-15 (Continued since October 1) 
A public hearing on a Request for Determination of Applicability by Kenneth McNamara for a project that involves construction of a shed 
(16’-0” x 14’-0”) approximately 75 to 100 feet from the edge of the stream. The shed will be set on concrete blocks that are 8 x 8 x 4. 
b. 30 Summer St- NOI-25-22 (Continued from November 19) 
A public hearing on a Notice of Intent by SafStor Real Estate Co.,LLC for a project that involves floodplain fill and associated 
compensatory floodplain compensation. 
c. 763 Northfield Rd- NOI-25-19 (Continued from October 15 and November 5) 
A public hearing on a Notice of Intent by the Town of Lunenburg-DPW for the replacement of the deteriorated culvert at the Northfield 
Road crossing over Mulpus Brook in order to mitigate further deterioration, prevent potential flooding caused by the existing undersized 
culvert, and meet the Massachusetts River and Stream Crossing Standards to the maximum extent practicable. The proposed culvert 
replacement is a 38-foot, 4-foot diameter precast reinforced concrete piped culvert with precast headwalls at both pipe ends. The proposed 
culvert will match the existing culvert’s alignment. (asked to continue to 12/3/2025)
