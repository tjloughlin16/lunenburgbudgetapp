4. NEW PUBLIC HEARINGS
a. 260 Falulah Road- CAOC-25-2
A Request for Amended Order of Conditions by Fitchburg Land Property Owner, LLC for the reduction of
scope and size of originally approved 367,000 sf warehouse building to 183,500sf warehouse structure.
Bill Hannigan from Hannigan Engineering represented the applicant. The project is half in Fitchburg and
half in Lunenburg. Soil borings identified buried material not conductive to structurally supporting the
building, so they eliminated the building in that area. Drainage plans have been adjusted accordingly.
The new plans also include an improved 2:1 slope for runoff in an area that was previously a 1.5:1
slope.
M. Wilson noted that the NOI number was not listed on the agenda, making it more difficult to see
what was originally approved. J. Pewtherer found it in OpenGov as record 1283 (prior to using
workflow naming). R. Bursch asked if there was a change to the riverfront disturbance. B. Hannigan
indicated it had been significantly reduced and discussed the technical reasons that resulted in
dropping the building by 2-2.5 feet and then shared the updated table. A comparison of the 2022
version and the 2025 version indicates no substantial change. Considering the building changes are all
outside of the riverfront area, this seemed reasonable.
Motion:
M. Larouche, to approve on the condition the new documents showing the grading
changes are uploaded to OpenGov.

A Determination of Applicability by Melissa Cannavino for the renovation of a patio, stairs, and
walkway within the 50-foot buffer zone of Hickory Hills Lake.
Ryan Proctor (Dillis &Roy) represented the applicant. R. Proctor reminded the Commission that this is
an after-the-fact filing and that they were coming back with a revised remediation plan as requested at
the last meeting. He reviewed the new plan. The new plan takes the landscape ratio from 1.8:1 to
2.75:1.

for them to approve and suggested a deadline of the Friday before a meeting to have all items
uploaded to OpenGov.

suggested 90 days.

c. 31 Round Rd - NOI-25-8 (Cont. From 07-16)
A Notice of Intent by Shawn Farrow for the construction of two landscape replacement walls, placement
of two removable docks & replenishment of beach sand within the 100 ft, 50 ft & 30 ft buffer zone of a
wetland resource area on Lake Shirley.
Matt Marro represented the applicant. Christopher Stoddard (Royalston) is the new engineer on the
project and re-evaluated all the plans.
R. Bursch wondered why the wall will not stay at the 30-foot? M. Marrow explained it will be from the
base of the slope up to three feet, essentially an exchange of surfaces. The existing wall will be
replaced because the applicant is concerned the stones will start coming out. The new wall will be of
the same composition.
M. Marro confirmed the intent is to do work during a drawdown and expects that to be part of the
conditions. He explained that the proposed new dock would be a 40x4 wheeled removable dock.
R. Bursch stated he was not in favor of building a new wall inside the 30-foot zone.
