7:45 PM ~ 155 Isiand Road ~ Pursuant to MGL ch 131, s 40 as amended and the Town of Lunenburg Wetlands Protection and
Municipal Bylaws there will be a public hearing on a Notice of Intent filed by David V/Betty A MacDonald. The proposed project
is for the installation of an in ground pool, fence, extend retaining wall, remove 4 trees, replace 4 decorative trees, shrubs &
plantings.
The MacDonald's and Gary Shepherd from David Ross presented. A septic system was added to the NOI as
requested. The 30' and 100' buffers were outlined and the only work in the buffer will be the pump chamber. Betty
outlined the rain garden and how it will catch runoff from the pool, retaining wall extension. Four trees to be removed,
two are already damaged and will be replaced in another spot. Pool will use an ozone filter, uv filter and cartridge filter.
There is also the fact the pool is gunnite and has very little chance of leakage. JJ/Jack Closed and approved
Unanimous

8:15 PM ~ Hickory Hills Lake Association ~ Update ~ Tom Bertram came in from the HH Lake Association and spoke of
the lake management plan and updated the commission on the progress of the plans. He noted that the strategy of
using the coir logs has worked well. Plantings of dogwood and blueberry on islands. Fanwort is continued to be
removed. Lake is being watched and any discoveries are documented and the suction harvester is used to remove the
offending weeds. The association is also pursuing grants for a rain garden near Mulpus to handle the storm water from
that area. Snow Riders: replaced bridges..now widened. The trails opened up. Beaver dam to be breached as trails
are being overrun with water. They will inform Jan as to when trappers are out there.
Tom notified Jan on 12/8 that they were installing a beaver deceiver the next day.
