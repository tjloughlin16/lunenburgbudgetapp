40 FIRE ROAD 12 - TRAVIS MOORE
Mr. Moore met with the Board on behalf of the owner, Susan Robbins. A large addition
as made to the property and utilized as a great room. This property currently has a tv
edroom septic permit. The proposal is to modify the exiting structure to have tw
bedrooms upstairs along with a kitchen and living room. Currently the basement (ground
level) has two finishes spaces. Mi. Moore provided the Board with a sketch of the
property after renovations.
After discussion, and on Motion by Rabbitt and second by Sund, the Board voted
unanimously to allow the renovation of the home conditioned upon the execution of a
two bedroom deed restriction.
175 PLEASANT STREET -
John Mederios met with the Board to discuss this property. This is a voluntary upgrade
with no Title 5 having been performed. Vice Chairman George Emond advised that in
order to apply for and for the Board to approve a Title 5 variance, a Title 5 Inspection
Report must be performed. No Title 5 Inspection Report was performed. Mr. Mederios
advised that the owner feels that this system is in failure and would like to voluntarily
upgrade the system.

DISCUSSION
HINI UPDATE
Jim Garreffi, our NABH Agent provide the Board with the following H1N1 Update:
"This office has been monitoring the guidance form the Massachusetts Department of Public
Health (MDPH) and Centers for Disease Control (CDC) relative to the H1N1 flu and vaccine
production.
I have been working with the Lunenburg School Department (Sue Glenny) to produce a plan for a
HINI vaccination clinic for Lunenburg. In an attempt to target children, the clinic is planned for
TC Passios.
Since the exact delivery date for vaccine is not yet known, the date of the clinic has not been
finalized. I will continue to work with the school and once the final date and plan is ready, I will
forward it to the Board."
PROTOCOL FOR REVIEW AND REVISIONS OF DOCUMENTS

RECENT EPA RECOMMENDATIONS CONCERNING PC'S IN BUILDINGS
CONSTRUCTED 1950 - 1978
DEP has advised that there is the possibility of PCB's in the calking of windows that
were constructed between 1950 and 1978. It is suggested that we contact CAFO Kerry
Speidel and Superintendent of School, Loxi Calms.
