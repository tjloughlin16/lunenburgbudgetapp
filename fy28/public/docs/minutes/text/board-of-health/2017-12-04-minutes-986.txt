181 HOLLIS ROAD
Dan Proctor met with the board on behalf of the owner. This system is in failure. Soil testing
indicated a shallow depth of ledge throughout the site. A minimum of two feet is allowed on
upgrades with a variance. Variances requested are a reduction in depth of soil to two feet, offset
to groundwater and a reduction to less than 12 inches for inlet and outlet tees. Agent Garreffi
recommended the used of monolithic tanks with rubber boots on the outlets.
After discussion and on Motion by Jewell and second by Fortin, the variances were granted with
the condition that monolithic tanks be used with rubber boots on the outlets. All in favor.

serviced by public water. When soil testing was conducted a shallow depth of ledge and noted
throughout the lot. Title 5 allows a minimum of two feet of soil for upgrades using a Presby
system (as is being proposed) without a variance but a variance to groundwater offset is
requested from five feet to three feet.
After discussion and on Motion by Fortin and second by Jewell. All in favor.
