21 SANDY COVE ROAD  
 
Bob Proctor met with the board of behalf of the owner.  This discussion continued from the 
October 7, 2013 meeting.  At that time, the abutter requested that the plan be re-evaluated to see 
of the system could be placed further back on the property, thereby place the proposed well 100 
feet from Mr. Foskett (abutter) well.  Further testing was completed and a site was found 
allowing the proposed well to be great than 100 feet from abutting wells.  A Presby system is 
being proposed requiring a reduction in groundwater offset [310 CMR 15.284 11.5(b) and 
Lunenburg Regulations Article IV, Section 4(a)]. 
 
After discussion and on Motion by Shea and second by Jewell, the variance was granted.  All in 
favor.

331 MULPUS ROAD 
 
Chris Mossman met with the board on behalf of the owner.  The owner of this property may 
want to add a bedroom in the future and is requesting an increase in the number of bedrooms in 
the house.  This system is in failure.  Mr. Mossman is requesting a variance to design the system 
with the reserve system between the system (trenches between the trenches), a variance to 
Lunenburg Board of Health Regulations.  No other variances are requires. 
 
After discussion and on Motion by Jewell and second by Shea, the variance for was approved.  
All in favor. 
 
Chairman Emond requested that a discussion for the Woodlands be placed on the agenda for the 
next Board of Health meeting. 
 
Having no other business before the board, meeting adjourned at 7:45 pm.
