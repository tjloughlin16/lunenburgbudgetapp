The Board of Health met at 7:00 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building

George Emond, Perry Jewell, and Paul Fortin

Chris Mossman and owner Ron Boulay met with the Board. This system is in failure due to high ground
water. The property is connected to public water.
This site is small, has a pool and out buildings. The engineer has proposed a design that best sites the
system on the lot. A variance is requested, offset to wetland from 100 feet to 41 feet. Access to the lot
for the installation of the new system will be through the neighboring property. An agreement for same
was available to the board to review.
After discussion and on Motion by Jewell and second by Fortin, the variance was approved. All in favor.
Having no further business before the board, meeting adjourned at 8:00 pm.
