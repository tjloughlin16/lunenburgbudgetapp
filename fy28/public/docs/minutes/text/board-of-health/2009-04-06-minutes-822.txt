Agent Jim Garreffi has reviewed the plan as submitted and has stated that based on the
Title5 inspection done in 2003, the Board deemed the system in failure in 2005.
This site is restricted by its size, high water table and large perc rate. The designer has fit
a system in the back of the property but it does need Local Upgrade Approvals for the
offset to groundwater 4' to 3' and from the offset to the cellar wall from the tanks and
field.
A small wall will be installed to minimize the proximity of the fill to the lot lines.
A system in full compliance with Title 5 would not be possible on this site. Agent
Garreffi does not have a problem with the requested Local Upgrade Approvals.
After discussion and on Motion my Shea and second by Rabbit, the request for variances
was unanimously approved.
