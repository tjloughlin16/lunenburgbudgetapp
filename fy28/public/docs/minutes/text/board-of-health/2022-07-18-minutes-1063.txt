LUNENBURG BOARD OF HEALTH 
MEETING MINUTES 
Monday, July 18, 2022 
 
The Board of Health met at 7:00 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building  
 
Members Present:  George Emond, Perry Jewell and Paul Fortin  
 
MINUTES APPROVED 
 
June 6, 2022 
 
On motion by Fortin and second by Jewell, the minutes were approved. 
 
441 NW TOWNSEND ROAD 
 
Nick Eaton, owner, met with the board.  This house was hit by a tree and re-built.  A new septic system 
was designed.  In order not to disrupt the location of the public water line going into  the house, Mr. 
Easton is requesting a variance of less than 10 feet from the tank to the water line.  The sleeved water 
will be at least 7 feet. 
 
It is Agent Jim Garreffi’s opinion that not disturbing the water line is in the best interest of all parties 
and has no issue with this variance.   
 
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Fortin , the variance was approved.  All in 
favor. 
 
Having no further matters before the board and on  motion Fortin and seconded by Jewell, the meeting 
was adjourned at 7:20 pm, all in favor.
