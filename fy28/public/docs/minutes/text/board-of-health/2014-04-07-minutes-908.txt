129 PENINSULA DRIVE
Owners, met with the board to discuss the expansion of their home. Currently the home has three
bedrooms and the plans for the proposed house expansion indicate no change in the number of bedrooms
but two new rooms will be on the first floor of the addition. The bedroom and bathroom the existing
second level will be converted to an open space which is accessible by a spiral staircase. Based on the
Lunenburg Board of Health Regulations, this additional space could meet the definition of a bedroom.
After discussion and on Motion by Jewell and second by Rabbitt, the request to have a building permit
signed to expand this home was approved provided a three (3) bedroom deed restriction is placed on the
property prior to the permit being signed.
All in favor.
711 WEST STREET
Steve Negy, owner of the property, met with the Board. Mr. Negy and his wife purchased this property
in February of 2014. Mr. Negy is proposing an addition to this residence. The house currently has two
bedrooms with a three bedroom SDS.
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Rabbitt, the request was approved based on the
plan as proposed. All in Favor.
