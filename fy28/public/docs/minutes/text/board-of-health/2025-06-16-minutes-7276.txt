LUNENBURG BOARD OF HEALTH 
MEETING MINUTES 
Monday, June 16, 2025 
 
The Board of Health met at 4:30 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building  
 
Members Present:  Perry Jewell, Paul Fortin, Matt Proctor and Lorraine Rudd, Nick Pirro 
 
MINUTES APPROVED 
 
June 2, 2025 on motion by Jewell and second by Fortin, minutes were approved.  All in favor. 
 
314 BURRAGE STREET 
 
Trevor Fletcher of Ganz Engineering met with the board on behalf of the owner.  They are requesting a 
variance to less than 50 feet from a sewer line to an existing well.  This request is made to allow the 
plumbing in the accessary building to flowing into the existing septic tank. 
 
Variance requested are offset well from 50 feet to 30.9 feet.  
 
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Rudd, the variance were approved.  All in favor. 
 
Having no further business and on motion by Rudd and second by Proctor the meeting was adjourned at 
5:40 pm.  All in favor.
