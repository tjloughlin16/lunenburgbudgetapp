FLOOR DRAIN REGULATION
Correspondence was received from Francis McNamara, Superintendent of the Lunenburg
Tater District, requesting that the Board review implementing a Floor Drain Regulation
fter discussion, it was agreed that Mr. McNamara would be invited to the Board
Health Meeting to be held on December 7, 2009 at 7:00 pm.
HINI REGION 2 GRANT
Administrative Assistant Andrea Schnepf advised that the Town of Lunenburg has been
awarded grant funds through Region 2, Emergency Planning. As more information
becomes available, Ms. Schnepf will advise the board.
Ms. Schnepf also update the board of the available of H1N1 innoculant. To date NABH
has only received limited doses. Once an adequate supply has been received, we will
schedule our H1NI clinic.
Having no further business before the Board, the meeting was adjourned at 7:50 pm.
