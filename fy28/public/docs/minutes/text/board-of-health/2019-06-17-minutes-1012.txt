32 PEARL STREET 
 
Scott Kirouac, owner, met with the Board.  Mr. Kirouac is proposing an addition to his house at the 
above address.  Since there are no Board of Health records, it is the Board’s practice to establish the 
septic system capacity (number of bedrooms) based on the Assessor’s records.  The Assessor’s records 
indicate the house has six rooms and two bedrooms.  Based on the proposal submitted, Mr. Kirouac 
would like to add a “seating room” and family room.  Because of the number of rooms currently in the 
home, including an office, this requires Board approval. 
 
Mr. Kirouac has a good size lot with good soil conditions.  There is a passing Title 5 from 2013. 
 
After discussion, and on motion by Fortin and second by Passios, the board voted to allow the addit ion 
to the house with the condition that both the “s eating room” and family room have at least six foot 
cased openings, which removes privacy.  All in favor. 
 
Meeting adjourned at 8:15 pm
