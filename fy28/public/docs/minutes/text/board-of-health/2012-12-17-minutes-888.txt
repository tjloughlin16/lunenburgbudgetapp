155 ISLAND ROAD
Neil Gorman, Dave MacDonald and Betty MacDonald met with the Board. This system is in
failure. This is a small lot on Hickory Hills. The lot has high ground water and the house sits in
the middle of the lots. The house has five bedrooms which requires a fairly large system. The
house and the surrounding houses are served by public water.
The applicant is requesting a reduction in groundwater offset from five feet to three feet as well
as a reduction in requirement of a twelve inch separation between the inlet and out let tees. A
Presby System is being proposed.
After discussion and on motion by Shea and second by Jewell the board voted to approve the
variances. All in favor.

1417 MASSACHUSETTS AVENUE
Dan Proctor met with the board of behalf of the owner. This system is in failure and is served by
town water. The water table on this site is high. The owner is requesting a reduction in
groundwater offset from 4 feet to 3 feet.
On motion by Jewell and second by Shea, the board voted to approve the variances. All in favor.
MAPLEWOOD GOLF COURSE
Laura Gustus met with the Board representing Settlers Crossing Golf Course, previously
Maplewood Golf Course. Ms. Gustus provided the board with a Food Service Permit
Application, proposed floor plan and proposed menu. The Board advised that they would
forward the information provided to Nashoba food service for their review.
