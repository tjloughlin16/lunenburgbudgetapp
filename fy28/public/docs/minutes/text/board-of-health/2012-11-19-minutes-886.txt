SEAN QUINN - OUTDOOR WOODFIRED BOILER
Owner Sean Quinn met with the board to discuss the installation of an outdoor wood fired boiler.
Mr. Quinn provided the Board with specification sheets and information on the boiler he is
proposing.
Chairman George Emond advised Mr. Quinn that the boiler he had selected is not on the
approved DEP list for wood fired boilers. Mr. Quinn stated that this "stove" is also approved for
indoor use.
After discussion, Chairman Emond advised that if Mr. Quinn installed the "stove" inside his
home, it would no longer be considered an outdoor wood fired boiler and would not come under
the current DEP/Board of Health regulations but rather those of the Building Department and
Building Regulations.

BRUCE ST. JEAN - ENFORCABLE AGREEMENT
Bruce St. Jean came before the Board to present an alternative Release for Enforceable
