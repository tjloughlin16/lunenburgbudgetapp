322 FLAT HILL ROAD 
 
John Boardman, Engineer, met with the board on behalf of the owner.  This matter was continued from 
December 16, 2019 at the request of Mr. Boardman.  As a summary:  The owner is requesting a well 
variance from 50 feet to 14 feet.  The owner has a large parcel of land and is proposing dividing it into 
three lots.  One will contain the existing house and the two remaining lots will be to the rear of the 
property off of a common dr iveway.  The lot which has the original house on it is served by a private 
well.  Once subdivided, this well does not meet the offset requirement from the newly created common 
drive. 
 
George Emond stated that he does not believe that this is a variance tha t the board can grant.  The 
owner has created this situation by subdividing the property and believes that a new well will have to 
be drilled. 
 
Dave Passios stated that he agrees, the owner has created this hardship.  The board does grant 
variances in cases where there is no other alternative, but in this case it is possible to drill a new well 
and feels that a new well should be drilled. 
 
After discussion, and on motion by Passios and second by Emond the variance was not approved.  
Votes as follows: 
 
Mayhew: Approved 
Passios: Denied 
Emond: Denied 
Fortin:  Denied 
Jewell:  Denied
