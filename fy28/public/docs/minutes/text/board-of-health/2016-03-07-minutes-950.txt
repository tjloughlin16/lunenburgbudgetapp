55 COVE ROAD
Kevin Richie met with the board of behalf of the owner. Chris Kimball, owner of the property was also
present. This system is in failure due to high ground water. This property is currently served by a well
but is to be connected to public water. The existing well is to be destroyed. A reduction in groundwater
from five feet to four feet is being requested. This offset will allow the system to be a gravity system.
After discussion and on Motion by Auge and second by Jewell, the requested variance was approved. All
in favor.
STORMWATER TASK FORCE

SEPTIC REPAIR LOAN PROGRAM
Administrative Assistant advised that there is one applicant to date for the loan program. The application
was available for review. Chairman advised that the application will be reviewed at the next meeting
together with any others that apply.
Dave Passios voiced concern as to the lack of advertising with regard to the availability of the program.
Administrative Assistant reported that the information was provided to both the Lunenburg Ledger as
well as the Sentinel and Enterprise. In addition, the information is on the "News and Announcement"
page of the Town's Website. Contact will be made to the Lunenburg Senior Center for addition to their
newsletter.
Mr. Passios will provide updated information for the newspaper.
