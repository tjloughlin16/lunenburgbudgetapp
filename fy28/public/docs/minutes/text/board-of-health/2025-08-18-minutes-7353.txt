MINUTES APPROVED 
 
July 21, 2025 on motion by Pirro and second by Rudd, minutes were approved.  All in favor. 
 
110 BURRAGE STREET 
 
Matt O’Toole and Dan Dobeck Jr. met with the board to discuss well drilling permits.  They had applied 
to Nashoba Associated Board of Health to drill two water wells on the property.  Nashoba is unwilling 
to approve the permits and had requested that the Board discuss and approve. 
 
The applicants stated that they would like to use these well as test wells and also believe that the property 
has been cleaned up. 
 
The board advised that according to DEP the property has been cleaned up to a point that it can remain 
as open land.  In order to be developed it would require more oversight by DEP. 
 
After discussion it was decided that monitoring wells could be utilized without obtaining a well permit 
from anyone.  They are withdrawing the well permit applications.
