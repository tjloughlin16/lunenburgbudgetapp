LUNENBURG BOARD OF HEALTH 
MEETING MINUTES 
Thursday, June 20, 2024 
 
The Board of Health met at 7:00 pm at the Ritter Memorial Building  
 
Members Present:  George Emond, Perry Jewell, Paul Fortin and Matthew Proctor 
 
MINUTES APPROVED 
 
None 
 
607 RESERVOIR ROAD  
 
Fiona King, RE Agent and owners Todd Cole and Ashley Brawn were present. 
 
A Title 5 on the property has been submitted as a conditional pass.  Surrounding well s needed to be 
tested because of the proximity of the wells to the septic system at 607 Reservoir Road.  The well was 
tested at the instant property as well as 605 Reservoir Road, with passing results.  The owner of 609-611 
Reservoir Road declined testing  after con versation with the owner and Cara Olegarz of Nashoba 
Associated Boards of Health. 
 
After discussion and on motion by Jewell and second by Proctor, the board voted to accept and approve 
the Title 5 for 607 Reservoir Road as submitted based on all the currently available information.  All in 
favor. 
 
Having no further matters before the board and on motion by Emond and seconded by Fortin, the meeting 
was adjourned at 7:30 pm, all in favor.
