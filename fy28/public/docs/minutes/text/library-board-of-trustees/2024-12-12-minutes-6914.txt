LUNENBURG PUBLIC LIBRARY DIRECTOR’S REPORT 
   FY25 Budget Update 
 
   Staff Update 
We submitted our Letter of Intent for LSTA grants this year. We plan to apply for a civics hub grant, 
with a revised and distilled version of last year’s grant. 
Building on her long experience with cataloging and technical services, we will be making Marabeth 
a “Records Manager” for our Evergreen catalog. This status will allow her to create original 
cataloging records for items not already in our network. 
Our Angel Tree was very successful this year. Several dozen children will receive gifts because of the 
connections made at the library. Our Food Pantry dropbox has been busy, too. Many donations to 
the pantry were made by vendors at the Artisan Fair, too. 
Most of the staff and their partners gather for our first holiday party in a number of years. It was a 
festive night and nice to see our professional and dedicated staff in a more relaxed environment. I 
am planning to bring a staff-wide training session to the library in the next month or so to refresh 
and support developing our performance in high stress situations and when dealing with unhoused 
populations.  
Lydia and I continue to strategize staff development and cross-training. 
   Programs 
The Holiday Artisan Fair was a smashing success. The library transformed into what seemed like an

HIGHLIGHTS FOR NOVEMBER: 
Homeschoolers Meet-Up and Craft Hour: 
Both of these events were well attended and much appreciated by kids and families of our local 
homeschooling groups.  The meet-up was held in the Wallace Room- we had tables set up for board 
games and coloring.  We had Lego out for their use as well.  The kids had a great time hanging out 
with each other in our warm, inviting library space.  The grown-ups were so appreciative that we had 
set this up for them!   They explained how important this type of social activity, and meeting space, 
was to them as a group, especially with the upcoming colder weather.  The craft activity was to have 
the kids make homemade cranberry sauce.  They measured ingredients by following a recipe, peeled 
apples, and stirred and measured out the final product.  The sauce was delicious, and the kids had a 
great time doing this together.

Trustee Report 
Adult Services Department 
December 2024 
 
A few outstanding programs we hosted this November include: 
➢ On Thursday November 7th we ran a successful Sewing 101: Sewing Machines Demystified 
workshop for 8 attendees. Each person brought their own machine and learned about how to use it. 
Because of the interest in this program, we will be hosting a monthly open sewing program for novice 
sewers to gain confidence in a community setting. These will start in January 2025. 
➢ Our fall puzzle swap was another success on Saturday November 9th. We had close to 600 puzzles 
and 45 people visit the library for new to them puzzles. 
➢ Leominster native Ronny LeBlanc returned for a paranormal hotspots program on Tuesday 
November 12th to a smaller than normal crowd of 12 people, but they were engaged in the material 
and made some good connections. 
➢ Editing and writing experts led a memoir writing workshop on Saturday November 16th where 5 
people spent two hours learning about the best ways to start writing down their stories.  
➢ The November installment of the monthly music series was held on Wednesday November 20th with 
a visit from Misty Blues frontwoman Gina Coleman and a keyboard accompanist. We had 26 people 
join us for the performance which was of a stellar caliber.  
➢ We ended the month with our afterhours Craft & Chill program, run with Kaitee, where we taught 9 
people how to make veggie sushi rolls on Friday November 22nd 
 
Looking ahead, here are the December events at the library: 
➢ We are restarting the Saturday morning music clinics on December 7th with Lunenburg music teacher 
Jason Graham. These will meet once a month through May. 
➢ The winter artisan showcase and December concert will both be taking place on Sunday December 
8th throughout the library. We will have over 30 vendors selling their crafts inside from 9 am to 1 pm, 
Santa will be taking pictures from 9 am to noon, and Chris and Katie Laford will entertain us with a 
Celtic Christmas concert from 2 – 3:15 pm. It should be a busy day at the library! 
➢ A special holiday puzzle race will be taking place on December 17th from 6 – 7 pm.  
➢ Craft and Chill for December will be cookie decorating and recipe sharing on December 20th after the 
library closes at 6 pm. 
➢ I will be running an all-ages holiday music bingo program the day before Christmas Eve in the 
afternoon. It should be a fun break from holiday craziness for families. 
➢ On December 30th we will have an introductory “Sew Simple” meeting for anyone interested in 
learning to sew to meet, discuss potential projects, materials that may be needed, and other details 
for our monthly meetings going forward.  
 
Respectfully submitted, 
Lydia Paolilli
