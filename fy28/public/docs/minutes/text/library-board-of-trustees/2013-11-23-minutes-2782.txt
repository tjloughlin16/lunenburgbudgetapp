 Lunenburg Public Library 
 
 
 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Policy Subcommittee Meeting minutes 
November 23, 2013 
 
Attending: Lisa Krowitz, Robin Venezia, Lars Widstrand and Martha Moore, LPL 
Director 
 
Meeting called to order at 11:03 a.m. in the Wallace Community Room by Robin 
Venezia. 
 
 
•  Participants discussed changes to the existing Appropriate Library Use Policy 
- Add “wearing” before cleats 
- Rephrase creating disruptive noises…. to “Creating disturbance, such as loud 
noise or excessive movement” 
- Several changes will be made to refer to other library policies that address 
certain situations, such as the Food and Beverage Policy, Meeting Room 
Policy, and Child Safety Policy. 
- Add requirement addressing the practice of leaving personal items unattended. 
- Robin will make proposed changes to be reviewed at the next subcommittee 
meeting. 
 
 
•  Participants discussed revisions to the draft Promotional Materials Policy 
- Change subtitle from Posting Policy to Posting Requirements 
- Remove the Trustees from the prior approval requirement 
- Add “unless authorized by the library director” aft er the discussion of 
permanent installations. 
- Change subtitle from Collection Boxes to Collection Requirements 
- Change subtitles from Sales to Soliciting, Petitions and Sales Requirements 
- Reorganize to address:  
1.  The library endorses only the sale of tickets and items that support library 
services and programs and the community. 
2.  Unauthorized soliciting is forbidden. 
3.  Petitions for campaigns and ballot initiatives are the only exceptions if 
they are official forms from the Secretary of State’s office. 
- Martha will make the proposed changes to be reviewed at the next 
subcommittee meeting. 
 
 
•  Participants discussed restructuring of the Art Exhibition Policy to separate the 
policy from the contract between the library and exhibiting artists. Lisa will make 
these changes to be reviewed at the next subcommittee meeting.
