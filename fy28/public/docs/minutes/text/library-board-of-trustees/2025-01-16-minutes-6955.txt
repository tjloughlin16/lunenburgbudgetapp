   FY25 Budget Update 
 
   Staff Update 
I will meet with Deb Goodsell, the new Director for the COA, on Thursday afternoon. We plan to 
discuss coordinating and collaborating on programming and a potential joint grant opportunity. She 
and I have already discussed regular collaborative meetings.  
I trained Kaitee on book common book repairs, and she will help us moving forward to keep the 
volume of books trapped in “Damaged” status to a minimum. 
We have been working on coordinating the renovation project with staff to minimize disruption. 
Construction should start January 24th with the two new offices, then progress to the demolition and 
changes to the Teen Room space. At this point, we are estimating two months total for the work. 
Although there will not be dedicated room space for teens, the majority of the collection should still 
be accessible once it is setup in the Main Hall, and the rest will be in storage. Teen visitors will have a 
designated computer bank and area during afterschool hours, where Kaitee will be on duty to assist. 
We have asked the schools, and Boys and Girls Club to include informational details in their 
newsletters. We will send out a special newsletter message with details of the renovation plan, too. 
Announcements will also be made on social media, and requested to be made by the Select Board. 
Our Budget requests are due to the Town Manager by January 21, and I have prepared our list of 
requests. 
I will have School Resource Officer Munroe offer an informal staff training at our February staff 
meeting on de-escalating interactions and approaching patron interactions with respect. I am also

Our holiday donation drives were also successful: we hosted the Salvation Army Angel Tree that is

Summary of Last Month’s Programs & Events 
➢ We restarted the Saturday morning music clinics on December 7th with Lunenburg music teacher 
Jason Graham. And it was a great success for the first session in the series with 12 attendees. 
➢ The winter artisan showcase and December concert took place on Sunday December 8th throughout 
the library. We had over 30 vendors selling their crafts inside from 9 am to 1 pm, Santa took pictures 
from 9 am to noon, and Chris and Katie Laford entertained an audience of 62 people with their Celtic 
Christmas concert from 2 – 3:15 pm. It was a smashing success for the library and despite the 
amount of effort it took to coordinate, I think this is something we will definitely consider doing as a 
new annual tradition! 
➢ 24 puzzle enthusiasts joined us for a special holiday puzzle race on December 17th from 6 – 7 pm. It 
was a very challenging 500-piece puzzle, but the teams had a wonderful time working together to 
finish what they could in the allotted time. We will host another puzzle race during the February 
school vacation week. 
➢ Craft and Chill took place on a snowy evening on December 20th after the library closed at 6 pm. 
There were 13 people who braved the elements for cookie decorating, recipe sharing, and hot cocoa 
sipping. Of note were four women who each brought a teen son with them for a group mother-son 
outing. They all had a blast and appreciated that the library offers programs that support a multi-
generational experience. 
➢ I ran a totally new in-house created program for the first time: an all-ages holiday music bingo 
program the day before Christmas Eve. We had huge registration numbers, with over 40 people 
expressing interest in attending, so we prepared accordingly. However, only 23 people came for 
holiday music bingo that day, which ended up working out for the best as the rounds went by much 
quicker than anticipated. We gave out mini trophies to winners and were able to do a few extra 
rounds. Ages of attendees varied from toddlers to seniors, but all enjoyed the experience. We may 
attempt another music bingo program in the future.  
➢ On December 30th the library held the introductory “Sew Simple” meeting for anyone interested in 
learning to sew to meet, discuss potential projects, materials that may be needed, and other details 
for our monthly meetings going forward. Only three people were in attendance, but I am optimistic we 
will have better success at the Sew Simple sessions this year. 
 
This Month’s Programs & Events 
➢ The next Saturday morning music clinic with Jason Graham will be on January 4th. 
➢ We are hosting a new monthly Saturday program: board game brunch on January 11th for patrons to 
stop by for coffee and snacks while making new friends over the library’s stash of board games. 
➢ I will be running a DIY workshop for household cleaners on Wednesday January 15th at 6:00 pm. 
➢ The January Craft & Chill program will be held on Friday January 17th. Participants will make New 
Year wish bracelets with a design, color, or word to help them in their new year resolution.  
➢ The January music concert will be on Saturday January 18th with a return performance from Marc 
Berger. He will play a mix of folk and Americana music for patrons that Saturday afternoon.  
➢ Melanie will be leading a workshop to teach patrons about hoopla on the morning of Wednesday 
January 22nd.  This is the first in a series of digital services workshops run with Melanie’s help.

Recurring Program Update 
➢ Book clubs are going well with attendance continuing to grow. 
o 100 Books Bucket List book club meets every first Tuesday morning. We averaged 9 
attendees at each meeting last year, and we continue to get new members each month.  
o The Brighter Days book club meets every second Wednesday evening. There are 8 
consistent members each month and we are building bonds of friendship after each book 
discussion. The books may miss the mark for “uplifting reads” but they generate deep and 
meaningful conversations among our readers.  
➢ The Community Chat & Color program was initially somewhat successful but has fallen off the last 
two months. We will pull this program from the offerings in February to free up space for other ideas. 
➢ Stitchcraft knitting and crochet group attendance has bloomed in recent months with some weekly 
meetings having nearly a dozen crafters! There are consistently at least 4-5 people here every 
Tuesday, but most weeks we will have 6-7 people with us for the drop-in program. Quite a hit!
