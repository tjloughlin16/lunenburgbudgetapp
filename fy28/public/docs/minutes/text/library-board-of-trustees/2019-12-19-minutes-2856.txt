Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes  
December 19, 2019 Sudolnik Meeting Room, Lunenburg Public Library  
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Lars Widstrand, Maia 
Wentrup 
 
Absent:  Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:48 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
● Harry inquired about a snow day when the library was closed and the walkways had not been cleared. Muir said that 
either the DPW or Kit will normally clear the walkways. Sometimes the staff will assist if they are here.  
 
Approval of Minutes 
o The minutes for November 2019 were reviewed. Motion to approve minutes was unanimously approved with 
a number of edits. 
 
  
Agenda Items 
● Director’s Report 
o Muir has received the target budget from the town manager. It is down .19% from last year. The library ’s 
budget requests are due to the Town Manager on 1/2/2020. 
o Muir will meet with FinCom on 2/20/2020. 
o Muir will do some research on energy projections for next year. 
o The Hannaford grant for $3500 has been submitted. 
o Donna asked about cleaning keyboards and computer mice. 
 
● Fine-Free Discussion  
o Deferred to our January 16, 2020 meeting. 
 
● Trust Funds – Next Steps 
o The BOT has been given sample language for a potential town meeting warrant article and subsequent 
special legislation. 
o The town meeting article must be submitted by the middle of March to be included in the upcoming May town 
meeting. 
o The legislative change should be submitted through our local state representative. (Jen Benson has resigned 
so this must be determined.) 
 
● Friends of LPL Update - 
o Deferred. 
 
● Director Evaluation 
o Harry has scheduled a special BOT meeting for December 29 th to compile the Director annual review from 
comments submitted by BOT members. 
 
● Board Comments 
o Kate asked about the BOT contact list. Harry will send out.
