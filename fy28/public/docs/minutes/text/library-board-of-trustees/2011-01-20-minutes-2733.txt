Trustees of the Lunenburg Public Library

Meeting was called to order at 6:30 p.m. 
Attending: Noelle Bodkin, Lisa Krowitz, Dick Mailloux, John Mara, Kathy Murray, Jeanne Raboin, Amy Sadkin, Len Smetana 
 
 
1. There was no public comment. 
2. New Business 
 December 16, 2010 minutes approved unanimously. 
 Director’s Report (please see report for more detail) 
o Budget: FY12 budget submitted to Town Manager; FY11 budget on track, salary variances for 
December, YTD and Total Year are under; as of December 2010, FY2011total budget under 
$63, 907. 
o Circulation: a bit under for December 2010 (FY11); Meeting Room usage also down for 
December 2010. Reasons could be due to weather, holiday and School groups using their 
rooms.

1/14/11, discussion was about eReaders; LPL Kindle lending program and policy was noticed by 
a Sydney, Australia librarian. 
o Facilities: Clean Carpets did free spot cleaning in December when library was cl osed; snow/ice 
removal has been adequately taken care of and the HVAC is being carefully watched.  
o Technology Update: Jen continues to work on the main hall computer monitor and it will soon be 
ready for use. 
o Programs: ALL of the children, YA and adult programs are very well-attended and successful. 
Herbalist Kenyon Keily will be speaking the last week in January 2011.  
o Friends/Endowment: $5,000 dontatin from the estate of Vida Waller was placed into the 
Endowment Fund; Friends have written a media release describing the mission of the Friends 
and Endowment; fundraisers and programs being discussed for 2012: LPL mini -golf, wine and 
beer tasting, new Cultural Horizons series, Book & Bake sales. 
o Old Mutual Fund account has shown an increase, total earnings for last calendar year: $784.09
