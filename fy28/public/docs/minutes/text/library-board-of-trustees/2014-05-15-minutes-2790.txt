2. Salary Recommendation 
 a. RM compiled a list of MA library director’s salaries with similar town 
populations (from MBLC web site)  
 b. Recommendation: $63,192. This reflects a 3% increase ($1,840.00/year). This 
recommendation reflects the following reasons based on the Director’s evaluation from 
Trustees, LPL staff and external evaluations: 
 Excellent staff (internal) and external evaluation comments 
 Trustees value her expertise 
 Acknowledgment Director’s hard-work and achievements in FY13 
 Development and implementation of policies and procedures in library 
that resulted in more efficient and effective services 
 Director reduced LPL dependence on town IT support/resources

 c. Motion made by Richard Mailloux and seconded by Robin Venezia to 
recommend a 3% salary increase ($1,840.00), resulting in $63,192 for FY14. Unanimous.
