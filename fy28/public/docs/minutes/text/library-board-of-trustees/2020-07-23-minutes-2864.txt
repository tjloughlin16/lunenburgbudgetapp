Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes July 23, 2020  
Remote meeting via conference call and video link. All votes were done by roll call.  Meeting was recorded. 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, Maia Wentrup 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:47 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
o Harry did a roll call for attendance. 
o Harry read the requirements for remote meeting. 
  
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o The June 21st minutes were reviewed and accepted as presented. Dick made a motion to accept, seconded 
by Kate. Passed unanimously. 
• Director’s Report 
o FY20 is very close to being 100% expended on all budget lines. 
o Some funds were transferred from salary and a few other places into library materials.  
o Some book carts were purchased to replace older ones. These are needed in order to reopen for browsing 
and will help in quarantining books. 
o Kate asked about office supplies which are expended at only 47%. Muir said that the Amazon statement is 
not out yet and that some invoices have not been received 
o The FY21 preliminary budget was approved at Town Meeting. Muir prese nted a preliminary FY21 budget 
report. So far only salary has been expended. 
o A posting will go out soon for the YA opening. State aid should be known by mid-August. 
o The summer reading program is ongoing and has generated a good response in Beanstack.  
o Plexiglass dividers have been installed. Hand sanitizer units will be installed. 
o Interlibrary loan deliveries have started up again which is increasing volume.  
o Some discussion was held about the best ways to handle the library reopening to patrons. Muir said the goal 
is to prioritize safety while serving the greatest number of people. Maia pointed out that the safety of the staff 
needs to be considered also. 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o The Friends annual meeting is this Saturday, July 25 th. Will be held outside at the library. 
• Board Comments 
o Harry spoke to Michael-Ray about what we need to do to fill the 7th Trustee spot. We should have 7 
according to the town charter. We need to post the open position. The Trustees can then nominate 
candidates. A joint meeting will be held between the BOS and the BOT to select the person. The term would 
be until the next election. Kate and Muir know some people who might be interested. 
o Muir noted that the trust fund article passed Town Meeting and the BOS sent a letter to our local 
representatives. 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick, seconded by Donna. Meeting was adjourned at 7:45 p.m.
