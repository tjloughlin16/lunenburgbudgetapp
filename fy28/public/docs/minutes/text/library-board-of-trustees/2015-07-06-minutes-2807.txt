Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes  
July 06, 2015; MaryAnn Sudolnik conference room, Lunenburg Public Library 
 
Attending: Harry Kubetz (via FaceTime in NYC), Dick Mailloux, Gare Thompson, Susan Visser, Lars Widstrand 
Bob Ebersole, (selectman representative) @ 7:36 p.m. 
Absent: none 
 
The meeting was called to order at 6:45 p.m. by Chairperson Dick Mailloux. 
 
 Public Comment 
o No public comment 
 Trustee Discussion of Applicant Questions 
o General discussion about applicants, recent trustee [population] history, and events leading up to current meeting. 
 
 Interviews of Applicants for Positions on the Lunenburg Public Library Board of Trustees  
o Introductions and background by Dick.  Trustees asked candidate about background and interest in library, availability 
on the Thursday meeting time slot for meetings; outlook on future path of library; library future role as community 
center. 
o Donna Saiia @ 7 p.m. 
o Kiirja Paananen @ 7:18 p.m. 
o Joy Ruth Cohen @ 7:38 p.m. 
 
 Board Discussion of Applicant Interviews 
o The three applicants’ qualifications were discussed by the trustees.  All three candidates are highly qualified; they bring 
interesting aspects as well as diverse backgrounds that would benefit the trustee board composition.  
 
 Board selection of Applicants to Go Forward to Joint Meeting with BOS 
o Dick made a motion that the trustees will bring forward all three candidates to the joint BOS/Trustee meeting for further 
interviews, selection and appointment.  So moved by Gare, second by Lars; voted all in favor. 
 
 Adjourn 
o Gare asked for a motion to adjourn the meeting, so moved by Susan; second by Dick; voted all in favor at 8:21 p.m. 
 
 
 
 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
Lars Widstrand 
Secretary, Trustee of Lunenburg Public Library
