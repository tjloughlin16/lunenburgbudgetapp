Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes October 11, 2023  
Attending: Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz (Via Zoom), Dick Mailloux, Donna Saiia, 
Muir Haman – Director LPL, Laura Boussom, Maia Wentrup, Jana-Lee Balish, Heather Lemieux – Town Manager 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order to enter into executive session at 6:58 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
 
Agenda Items 
• Executive Session to discuss alleged policy violation by a staff member 
o Heather presented the facts of an alleged policy violation, attaching a personal computer to the town 
network. 
o The board members asked questions and discussed the alleged violation. 
o The library employee admitted to the facts as stated by the Town Manager. 
o Harry explained the scope of possible actions: dismissal, administrative leave, paying for town resources 
used i.e., electricity. 
o Dick presented a summation stating that while there was a breach of town policy, there was no lasting or 
drastic impact on the operation of the library or damage to the town.  He thought it should be considered a 
serious lack of judgement. 
o Dick made a motion to craft a letter of reprimand regarding the breach of town policy, including 
reimbursement for resources used, seconded by Donna. 
o The motion passed unanimously. 
 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn executive session by Dick. Seconded by Bob. Executive session was adjourned at 7:24 p.m. 
o Motion to adjourn meeting by Dick. Seconded by Donna. Meeting was adjourned at 7:25 p.m.
