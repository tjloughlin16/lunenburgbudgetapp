Lunenburg Public Library 
Trustees of Lunenburg Public Library, Meeting minutes  
June 18, 2020  
Remote meeting via conference call and video link. All votes were done by roll call.  Meeting was recorded. 
  
Attending: Kate McCarron, Muir Haman – Director LPL, Bob Conroy, Harry Kubetz, Dick Mailloux, , Donna Saiia, Lars Widstrand, 
Maia Wentrup, Michael-Ray Jeffreys 
 
The LPLBT meeting was called to order at 6:55 p.m. by Chairperson Harry Kubetz.  
 
 
Public Comment 
• No public comment. 
o Harry did a roll call for attendance. 
o Harry read the requirements for remote meeting. 
  
  
Agenda Items 
• Approval of Minutes 
o The May 21st minutes were reviewed and amended. Dick made a motion to accept amended minutes , 
seconded by Kate. Passed unanimously. 
 
• Exceptional Meetings in July and August 
o Discussion was held on whether to hold BOT meetings during July and August due to the recent 
circumstances of the pandemic and the library closing. It was decided that we should hold meetings to keep 
up to date with any changes in the library.  
 
• Board of Trustees Reorganization 
o The BOT nominated and selected a new slate of officers for the next year. Dick suggested we nominate and 
vote on a group slate. Bob nominated Harry as chair, Dick as vice-chai and Bob as Secretary. Kate 
seconded. Harry polled the group and it was passed unanimously. 
o Vote was also taken on subcommittees as follows: Personnel: Harry, Kate, Maia. Friends Endowment: Harry, 
Bob. Budget: Dick, Donna. Policy: Dick, Maia, Kate, Bob. Trust Fund: Donna, Harry, Dick. Donna made a 
motion to vote and Dick seconded. This was passed unanimously. 
 
• Director’s Report 
o 83% of the budget has been expended. Some expenses left for the year include electricity and library 
materials. Harry asked if any unspent funds will be reappropriated. Michael-Ray suggested that Muir spend 
the library material line item to 100% and asked about hitting the max on office suppl ies. Also suggested that 
Muir draft the next year’s budget based on what was spent this past year.  
o The FY21 omnibus budget passed at town meeting. This is the town manager’s preliminary budget. The 
library is budgeted at $433,197.08. This includes money for the strategic plan, increases for staff, and the 
16% requirement for library materials. These are the big priorities. 
o This budget will be up again at special town meeting in the fall when the info for state aid is known.  
o Staff has been back at the library for two weeks and are doing curbside pickups. All holds have been sent 
out. Book returns are currently being accepted. Books are quarantined for 72 hours before check -in and 
shelving. 
o Lydia is implementing Beanstack for summer reading. Beanstack website should be live this week and she 
will begin pre-registering for summer reading.

o Summer reading is the big program coming up. The program has been scaled back a bit. The focus is on 
activities and reading challenges, trying to give people an incentive to ge t away from screens. The program 
will have an adult component also. 
o Staff workstations have been moved around so staff can work without masks.  
o Plexiglass dividers have not been installed yet. Hand washing stations are in place. There is a town -wide 
control plan but Muir created a library specific checklist. 
o Muir presented the newsletter, children’s area, and facebook engagement stats.  Instagram stats are up for 
adult services with 195 followers and 117 posts. 
o Hannaford sponsored half height shelf has been installed in the children’s area. It opens up the room and lets 
in more light. 
o Kate asked about how the library is paying for the cleaning and PE supplies. A lot of supplies come from the 
DPW.  Library budget doesn’t cover the increased cost of those supplies.  
 
• Annual Town Meeting Update 
o The article for the library trust funds passed. It was included with many other articles that were voted on as a 
block. 
 
• Friends of LPL Update  
o No Friends update.  
 
• Goodbye and Thank you to Lars 
o The BOT gave a heartfelt thank you and goodbye to Lars for his years of service. 
 
• Board Comments 
o Dick: Thank you to Hannaford for the shelving in the Children’s area. 
o Bob: Thank you to Michael-Ray for his interest and work on behalf of the library. 
o Kate: Thank you to the library staff for all that they are doing. 
• Adjourn 
o Motion to adjourn by Dick, seconded by Maia. Meeting was adjourned at 8:18 p.m.
