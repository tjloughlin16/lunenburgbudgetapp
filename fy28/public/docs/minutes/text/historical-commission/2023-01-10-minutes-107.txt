LUNENBURG HISTORICAL COMMISSION 
MINUTES 
JANUARY 10, 2023 
 
Meeting commenced at 7 pm with a quorum present.  
PUBLIC HEARING   re: 1325 Mass. Ave.  Jean and Dan Proctor have applied for demolition of 
1325 Mass. Ave. Very little of the house is original, and would not be economically feasible to 
restore. Bill Lakso read the site visit report, and a vote was taken. The Commission unanimously 
approved the demolition with no delay. 
PUBLIC HEARING  re: 44 Pierce Ave. Mr. Colamita has applied for the demolition of 44 Pierce 
Ave. Unanimous vote taken for demolition without delay. 
Minutes from December’s meeting were read by Jennifer Sanderson and accepted. 
NO OLD BUSINESS 
68 ELM ST. Chair Richard McGrath has received a response from the Mass. Preservation 
Society. 
The Commission still has money in the budget, which could potentially be used for 
signage/banners.  Jennifer Sanderson is going to look into the Hometown Hero Program. 
The APDC meeting regarding the Ritter Building was televised. The architect hired to replace 
the windows has retired, and an incorrect count of the windows will require new plans, and a 
new architect. 
Meeting adjourned at 7:30. Next meeting to be held February 7, 2023. 
 
Jennifer Sanderson 
Acting Secretary
