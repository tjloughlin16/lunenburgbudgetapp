TOWN OF LUNENBURG
Lunenburg Historical Commission
Meeting Minutes:
January 25, 2016
Members Present:
Becky Lantry, Amory Phelps and Richard McGrath
Members Absent:
Cullen Dwyer and Lois Lewis
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order at 6:40 p.m.
Present Business/New Business:
This special meeting was called to consider whether the Demolition Delay Bylaw Section
22 (the six month delay provision) was applicable to the property at 37 Burrage Street,
339 Electric Avenue and 165 Townsend Harbor Road.
Voted: Demolition Delay Plan Review is applicable to the property at 37 Burrage Street
(shown on the 1833 Cyrus Kilburn map).
It was determined 339 Electric Avenue and 165 Townsend Harbor Road did not qualify
for this demolition delay review.
Adjournment:
Meeting adjourned at 7:30 p.m.
The next monthly meeting will be February 2, 2016 at 6:30 p.m. at the Ritter Building
