TOWN OF LUNENBURG
Lunenburg Historical Commission
Meeting Minutes:
May, 3 2016
Members Present:
Becky Lantry, Amory Phelps, Cullen Dwyer and Lois Lewis.
Members Absent:
Richard McGrath
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order at 6:35 p.m.
Minutes Approval:
April, 5 2016 minutes were approved,
Correspondence:
Notice was received of A Conference for Massachusetts History Organizations scheduled
June 13, 2016 at the College of the Holy Cross in Worcester. Further information is
available at masshumanties.org.
New Business/Present Business:
There has been no demolition delay requests received.
The Planning Board will consider at least two requests at the May 9, 2016 meeting: 1) the
request to install a Satelite, at 39 Massachusetts Avenue, and, 2) a petition requesting
permission to cut trees down for a driveway at 233 Lancaster Avenue. Although neither
issue is particularly a Historical Commission matter, the safety of motorists exiting from
or passing by this proposed driveway on Lancaster Avenue was discussed. Cullen Dwyer
expressed his intention to attend this Planning Board meeting.
Becky Lantry is developing a plan to have bronze markers for historical residences
available for distribution to those interested.
Amory Phelps reiterated David Grossman, the Editor of the Lunenburg Ledger, needs the
Historically Speaking articles to be received by the first Tuesday of the month in which
the article is to appear.
Becky Lantry presented two newspaper articles concerning the Community Preservation
Act for discussion: one a town's favorable implementation of the Act and the other a
more unfavorable critique of this Act.
