TOWN OF LUNENBURG
Lunenburg Historical Commission
Meeting Minutes
January 6, 2015
Members Present:
RL Becky Lantry, Amory Phelps, Lois Lewis Richard McGrath and Cullen Dwyer.
Members Absent:
None.
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order at 6:40 p.m.
Minutes Approval:
December 2, 2014 minutes were approved.
Correspondence and Communications:
There was no correspondence.
New Business/ Present Business
Welcome was extended to Cullen Dwyer as a new member of the Historical Commission.
At the start of the meeting Town Selectman, Bob Ebersole, was present to express
interest in the proposed Architectural Preservation District Bylaw.
Becky reported the Building Reuse Committee has considered various alternative plans
for the Town Hall and the Ritter Buildings.
After an initial review of the primary school, the old high school, Becky was pleased to
report the building was in good condition.
Richard advised the Commission the request by Powell Stone and Gravel was not within
the Commission's jurisdiction, since the buildings were not older than seventy five years
and dated only to 1986.
An Architectural Preservation District Bylaw for submission to Adam Burney, the Land
Use Director, and the Planning Board was drafted.
Adjournment:
Meeting adjourned at 8:55 p.m.
Next meeting scheduled for February 3, 2015 @ 6:30 p.m. in the Ritter Building.
