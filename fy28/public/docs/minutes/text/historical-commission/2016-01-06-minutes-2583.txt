TOWN OF LUNENBURG
Lunenburg Historical Commission
Meeting Minutes:
January 6, 2016
Members Present:
Becky Lantry, Amory Phelps, Lois Lewis, Cullen Dwyer and Richard McGrath
Members Absent:
None
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order at 6:40 p.m.
Minutes Approval:
Minutes were approved for December 1, 2015
Correspondence and Communications:
None
New Business/ Present Business:
The Community Preservation Act, whereby residents would contribute to a fund based on
a percentage of the assessed value of their property, was discussed. This fund, with some
state support, would then be able to finance various approved town projects. Amory
Phelps will obtain further information about this Act and its implications.
Cullen Dwyer has been sworn in as a member of the Architectural Preservation District
Commission by the Board of Selectmen. A meeting of this Commission was held
January 4, 2016. Three members, as a quorum, started to consider the guidelines for this
new Commission.
Richard McGrath has received only one demolition delay request, 37 Burrage Street, that
requires a hearing by the Historical Commission. A special meeting was scheduled for
January 25, 2016 at the Ritter Building at 6:30 p.m.
The Building Resuse Committee has scheduled a meeting for January 15, 2016 to discuss
the plans for moving and consolidating all the town offices to the Passios School
Building. Lois Lewis has agreed to be present at this meeting for Becky Lantry.
