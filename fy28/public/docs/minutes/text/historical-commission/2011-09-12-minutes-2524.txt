Minutes of HISTORICAL COMMISSION MEETING September 12, 2011
Meeting Called to order @6:30 Pm at the Lunenburg Public Library
RECEIVED & FILED
Members Present Rebecca Lantry, Richard McGrath, Pat Slattery
No old meeting notes from May 2, 2011 available at this time
M: 02 2012
LUNENBURG TOWN
TERK OFFICE
2. CORRESPONDENCES:
Antiques Homes - directory received- No Jones House Listing
Terra Firma - a publication of Massachusetts Dept of Conservation and Recreation's Historic
Landscapes Preservation Initiative
Email: From the National Trust for Historic Preservation: announcing OPEN HOUSE Sept 17,
2011.
Email: Chris Skelly from the Mass Historical Commission, announcing new round of survey and
planning grants
3. Old Business:
Jones House: New request for bids packet reviewed and discussed
Vacant seats on the Commission: Damon McQuaid, is in attendance and is requesting to join
the Historic Commission. Talent Bank form reviewed. Discussion about what the Historical
Commission does and has done in the past was held with all members participating. A vote
was taken to accept Damon McQuaid as a new member. All members were in agreement.
Daman will go before the SELECTBOARD -Tuesday Sept 13, 2011 for confirmation.
4. New business: Richard McGrath needs digital photograph for the North Cemetery Project:
Becky Lantry will take one for him.
5. Next meeting October 3, 2011
6. Adjournment at 8:30 pm
Rebecca Lantry - Vice Chairperson and recording secretary for this meeting
RL.
