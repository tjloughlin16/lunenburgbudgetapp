Meeting Minutes
April 28, 2015
Members Present:

Members Absent:
None.
New Business/Present Business
This meeting was called to prepare the presentation of the Architectural Preservation
Bylaw for the Town Meeting, May 2, 2015.
Two proposed amendments were discussed:
(1) Section 7: Alterations for which Advisory Review is Recommended
The APDC shall provide its finding in writing and within 20 days (change to 45
days) of receiving a request for advisory review.
(2) Section 8: Alterations which Require APDC Review
Paragraph (c) The APDC's written determination shall be submitted to the
property owner within 20 days (change to 45 days) of the close of the public hearing....
These amendments will be submitted at the Town Meeting, May 2, 2015.
