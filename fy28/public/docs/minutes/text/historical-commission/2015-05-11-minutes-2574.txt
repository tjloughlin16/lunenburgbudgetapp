TOWN OF LUNENBURG
Lunenburg Historical Commission
Meeting Minutes:
May 11, 2015
Members Present:
Becky Lantry, Amory Phelps, Lois Lewis, Richard McGrath and Cullen Dwyer.
Members Absent:
None.
Call to Order:
Meeting called to order at 7:00 p.m. after an acknowledgement of the adoption as
interdependent the Village Center District Bylaw and the Architectural Preservation
District bylaw at the Town Meeting, May 2, 2015.
Minutes Approval:
April 7, 2015 minutes and April 28, 2015 minutes were approved.
Correspondence and Communications:
None.
New Business/ Present Business:
Richard reported there were no demolition delay requests.
Richard advised the members of the necessary repair work for the receiving tomb at the
North Cemetery.
Becky has obtained information for the various sizes and materials for plaques to mark
historic homes.
Becky will provide the Massachusetts Historical Society an electronic copy of the survey
of the Village Center District prepared by Damon McQuaid.
Becky will submit an article for the Lunenburg Ledger, Historically Speaking, for May.
A possible Historic Homes tour for sometime in 2016 was discussed.
An article in the Lunenburg Ledger reported that in the summer of 2014 the Park and
Recreation Department to expand a parking lot cut down trees and took down a historical
stone wall at the southwest end of the Chestnut Street entrance to Marshall Park. Cullen
will look into this matter and report back to the Commission.
The meeting adjourned at 7:55 p.m.
The next meeting will be June 2, 2015 at 6:30 p.m. at the Ritter Building.
