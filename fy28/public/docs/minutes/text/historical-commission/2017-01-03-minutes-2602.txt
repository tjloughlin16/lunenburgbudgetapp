TOWN OF LUNENBURG 
 
Lunenburg Historical Commission 
 
Meeting Minutes: 
January 3, 2017 
 
Members Present 
: 
Becky Lantry, Lois Lewis, Richard McGrath and Cullen Dwyer 
 
Members Absent : 
None. 
 
Call to Order :  
Meeting called to order at 6:39 p. m. 
 
Minutes Approval: 
Minutes were approved for December 12, 2016. 
 
Correspondence and Communications : 
There was no correspondence received. 
 
New Business/ Present Business 
Richard McGrath received a demolition delay application for 420 Flat Hill Road.  The 
plan does not apply since this split level residence was constructed in 1967, and has no 
historical value. 
 
Becky Lantry is completing the public notice for the demolition delay application and 
process. 
E forms of these documents will be made available as well. 
 
Richard McGrath and Cullen Dwyer, members of the Architectural Preservation District 
Committee, reported the Committee’s proposed rules and regulation were submitted to 
Adam Burney, the Land Use Director, for review.  Further meetings have been scheduled 
to consider the suggested changes and corrections.   
 
Adjournment: 
The meeting adjourned at 7:40 p.m. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
The next monthly meeting will be February 7, 2017 at 6:30 p.m. in the Ritter Building.
