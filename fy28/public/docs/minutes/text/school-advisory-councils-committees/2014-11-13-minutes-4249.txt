*          Update Regarding Meeting with School Calendar Committee: Loxi brought our proposed calendar to 
Internal Calendar Committee: 
 
Internal Calendar Committee was not at all interested in having day off before Thanksgiving, they want to keep 
to the tradition of 1/2 day. 
 
Internal Calendar Committee would agree to starting Aug 28/29th just this year, does not want to go 5 days first 
week.    
 
Internal Calendar Committee was not supportive of shortened Feb break. 
 
Internal Calendar Committee was in favor of rolling out both calendars at same time.  2015/2016 and 2016/2017 
 
Teachers contracts has language around start date of school year, but not around vacation weeks. 
 
Also found out that building orientations could occur before school got out in 2016 school year. 
 
Internal Calendar Committee presents calendar to school committee in January.  School committee decides the 
calendar based on the best interest for the students education. 
 
Higher grades were in favor of full vacation weeks 
Lower grades would be willing to modify vacation weeks. 
It was suggested that we come up with guidelines to be consistent on the calendars going forward.  would be 
easier to do calendar with guidelines to adhere by.   Brandon Kibbe nominated Louis Bettencourt to come up 
with a standard set of guidelines.   
 
*      Begin Draft of Final Report: Group decided to present calendar as we had set it on 10/30 meeting to school 
committee as originally discussed.   Also discussed that we should be able to talk to and defend each change, 
based upon our established guidelines.
