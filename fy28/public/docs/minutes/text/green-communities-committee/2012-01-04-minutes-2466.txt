Londa 1 .Meeting called to order at 7:09 P.M. The minutes from 11/2/11 were approved.  2. Solar Landfill Project: Borrego has the contract back from Town Counsel and should hear     from Unitil by 1/14/12 regarding the interconnect. The Development Plan Review needs to proceeds through the    Planning Board to the Building Inspector.  The site includes both Commercial and Residential      zones.  3. The THMS solar project has produced 56 kW of power at a savings of $10,000.00.  4. Pilgrim Baptist Church is going forward with their proposed solar project to the Planning      Board on 1/9/12 and will hold a Public Hearing on 1/13/12.     3. M.Benson submitted town warrants from Lancaster and Townsend regarding         the Stretch Code for our review.  5. Green Community Act: Our directive from the Board of Selectmen is to make additional       public presentations to address unanswered questions regarding the pros and cons of adopting      the Stretch Code, the availability of HERS raters, necessity and cost. The material needs to be     covered in more depth prior to the vote and appeal to a great global vested interest.      D. Blatt will write a series of articles for the Ledger addressing “What is in it for you, the      taxpayer”.  They will start in late March. J. Londa stressed that there is indeed a financial      impact to the Town through savings in energy reduction and grants to achieve that goal.     An informational sheet on energy produced and savings realized from the THMS solar project         would reinforce that point.  6.
