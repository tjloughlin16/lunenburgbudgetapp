Green Community Task Force Minutes 
November 2, 2011 
 
 
 Present:    Members    D. Blatt, J. McQuaid, N.Sullivan 
                  Advisors     J. Londa 
 
 
 
1.Meeting called to order at 7:09 P.M. The minutes from 9/7/11 and 10/5/11 were approved. 
 
2.Solar Landfill Project: Borrego is moving forward with the interconnect process    
even though the contract’s financial language has not been approved. There are still questions 
about a  third party involvement.  Unitil requested $15,000. for an impact study which should 
be completed in 55 days. The current offer for cost of electricity  (.078/kW) would be about a 
25% reduction ($113,00) per year. 
 
   3.MASS PV1 (EOSOLAMERICAS): They are moving through the procedural channels. 
 
4.Green Community Act- There will be a televised workshop on 11/17/11 with someone from    
    the State office to present the Green CommunityAct/ Stretch Code to the Town prior to the    
    12/5/11 Special Town Meeting. It has been advertised on the Town webpage, and local papers. 
   
   D.Blatt cited information that the average cost of to implement the Stretch Code is $900.00 to   
   $2,000.00;  for a 2,500 sq.ft. home the cost would be about $1,200.00. 
 
 5.Ongoing topics- J. McQuaid noted that Ayer is hosting a ‘Recycle your Reusables’ event on   
  11/5/11 which is open to Lunenburg residents. She will look into what resources are available at    
  the Devens Eco Efficiency Center for the next meeting. J.Londa noted that the school is still  
  paying more to recycle but less for trash removal. D. Blatt will look at the paper retriever idea  
  again.  The THMS solar project is function well. 
 
 6. No new meeting date was set. Meeting adjourned at 7:48 P.M.
