PUBLIC HEARING NOTICE 
The Lunenburg Select Board will hold a public hearing on Tuesday June 4, 2024 at 7:30 PM in 
Town Hall, 17 Main St., 2nd Floor, Lunenburg, MA, to consider possible revocation of Class II 
Auto Dealer License #745, issued to Jose Iris De Oliviera Jr., d/b/a Maxi Auto Inc., and 
Lunenburg Auto Center, for premises located at 36 & 40 Summer Street, Lunenburg, pursuant to 
G.L. c. 140, §§ 57-59, for non-compliance with and violation(s) of said statute(s) and/or the rules 
and regulations of the Massachusetts Registrar of Motor Vehicles, as applicable.  Interested 
parties are welcome to attend and comment; persons wishing to be heard should appear in person 
or via Zoom on the date and at the time designated above. 
 
The following information is also provided for members of the public that would like to 
participate in the meeting remotely:  
To Participate Remotely from a computer, please use the link below to join the public meeting 
webinar: https://zoom.us/j/9091740347 or from a telephone, dial the toll free number (888) 475-
4499 and enter Webinar ID: 909-174-0347 
If you have not registered with Zoom you will be asked for your name and an e-mail address. 
Note: No participant ID is needed, just press # when asked. Telephone users may dial *9 to 
request to speak after joining the meeting. Computer and app users may use the “raise hand” 
feature to request to speak.
