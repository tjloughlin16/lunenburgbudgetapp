SELECT BOARD 
PUBLIC HEARING NOTICE 
 
The Lunenburg Select Board will hold a public hearing on Tuesday June 18, 2024 at 7:15 PM 
in Town Hall, 17 Main St., 2nd Floor, Lunenburg, MA, on the application submitted by Pals Golf 
Lounge LLC d/b/a Blue Sky Golf for an All-Alcoholic Beverages, General On-Premises License 
and an Automatic Amusement Device License, located at 5 Summer St, Unit 1, Lunenburg, MA. 
The premises described as a 2,141sq ft unit with basement with one entrance and exit containing 
a bar and seating area, three golf simulator areas, and storage. 
 
The following information is also provided for members of the public that would like to 
participate in the meeting remotely:  
To Participate Remotely from a computer, please use the link below to join the public meeting 
webinar: https://zoom.us/j/9091740347
 or from a telephone, dial the toll free number (888) 475-
4499 and enter Webinar ID: 909-174-0347 
If you have not registered with Zoom you will be asked for your name and an e-mail address. 
Note: No participant ID is needed, just press # when asked. Telephone users may dial *9 to 
request to speak after joining the meeting. Computer and app users may use the “raise hand” 
feature to request to speak.
