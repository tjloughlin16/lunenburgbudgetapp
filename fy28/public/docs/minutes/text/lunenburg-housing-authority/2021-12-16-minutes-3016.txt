b. State-Aided Housing Report – Ben Gold 
We received seven standard applications in November; four elderly/young disabled and three family. 
We have 1,318 elderly/young disabled and 3,292 families on the waiting list.  There were no lease-ups 
and one vacancy in November.  We are at 98.11% occupancy with one vacancy coming in December, 
staff working to fill. 
5.  Unfinished Business – None 
6.  New Business  
a.   Resolution 2021-19 Approval of FY2021 Budget for Submittal to DHCD.   
Sue Honeycutt presented the FY2022 budget.  This year there will be a 4% increase in our ANUEL 
(subsidy).  No change to rental income budgeted.   Solar credits – we are now allowed to keep only 
50%; in the  past we were given 100%.  Materials/Contract Costs  were raised up.  Non -routine 
maintenance allowing $25,000 for appliances and $8,000 for the smoking gazebo.   Projected profit of 
$2,846 and reserves will be at 8.93%. 
Linda McDonald moved to approve the FY2022 Budget as presented for submittal to DHCD.  Catherine 
Clark seconded  and upon roll call vote of Linda McDonald -aye, Catherine Clark -aye, Deborah 
Christen-aye; motion passed with 3 in favor; 0 opposed.
