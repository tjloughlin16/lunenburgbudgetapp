MINUTES OF THE REGULAR MEETING 
 
August 17, 2017 
 
Members Present:   Elizabeth Murphy, Chair 
     Paul Doherty, Vice Chair 
     Caroline Fortin, Member 
Linda McDonald, Member 
 
Members Absent:   Deborah A.H. Christen, Member 
 
Also Present:    Peter Proulx Executive Director/Recording Secretary 
      
 
Residents were present with comments.        
   
1. The Chair declared a quorum present and opened the meeting at 6:40 p.m.   
 
2. The minutes of the May 18, 2017 meeting were presented.  Elizabeth Murphy made a motion 
to accept the minutes ; Linda McDonald  seconded.  All  were in favor and the minutes were 
accepted. 
 
3. The Executive Director Report: 
 The E.D. went over the Community Building Upgrade projects. 
 
4.  Financial Report: 
 Three months of financials for review (May/June/July). 
 July statement – page F2 – Reserves at 71.29%. 
 We are in very good condition. 
 
Paul Doherty  made a motion to accept the Feb/Mar/Apr 2017 financials ; Caroline Fortin  
seconded and all were in favor. 
 
5.  Modernization Report:    
 Building Envelope Repair – construction completed, we are awaiting close -out 
documents. 
 
6.  State-Aided Housing Report: 
 We received 29 standard applications in July; 7 elderly/young disables; 22 family. 
 Overall occupancy is at 100%; there were no new vacancies for July. 
 19 of the 22 work orders for July were completed within 24 hours. 
 
7.  Old Business:  None

The following resolution was presented: 
RESOLUTION TO WRITE-OFF UNRECOVERABLE DEBTS 
RESOLUTION 201709 
Whereas: There were no vacated tenant balances older than ninety (90) days for the 
Lunenburg Housing Authority as of June 30, 2017. 
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, 
That the Lunenburg Housing Authority verified there are no unrecoverable 
debts for vacated tenant balances older than ninety (90) days as of June 30, 
2017. 
RESOLVED, Motion made by Elizabeth Murphy to accept verification that there are no 
vacated tenant balances older than ninety (90) days as of June 30, 2017 
and therefore no unrecoverable debts to write-off. 
SECONDED, Caroline Fortin seconded the motion, and the motion was passed by a 
unanimous vote. 
RESOLUTION TO ALLOW A PROPRIETARY BID SPECIFICATION FOR THE WINDOW 
SASH REPLACEMENT AT THE 705-1 WHITE STREET DEVELOPMENT 
 
DHCD PROJECT #162026 
RESOLUTION 201710 
Whereas: The Lunenburg Housing Authority needs to replace the single pane        windows 
at the 705-1 White Street development, and 
Whereas: The Lunenburg Housing Authority seeks to minimize its costs in providing 
window replacement at its 705-1 White Street development, and 
Whereas: It will reduce costs to use the same Anderson Narrowline Sash Conversion Kits 
for the window sash replacement. 
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED: 
That the Lunenburg Housing Authori ty Board of Commissioners  hereby votes to 
allow for a Proprietary Bid utilizing Anderson Narrowline Sash Conversion Kits 
for the window sash replacement at the 705 -1 White Street development for all 
windows within the specification. 
Resolved, Motion made by Paul Doherty to allow for a Proprietary Bid utilizing Anderson 
Narrowline Sash Conversion Kits for the window sash replacement. 
Seconded, Linda McDonald seconded the Motion, and the Motion was passed by a 
unanimous vote.
