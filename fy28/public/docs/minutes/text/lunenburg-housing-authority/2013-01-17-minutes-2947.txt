LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY
MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
January 17, 2013
Members Present:
Elizabeth Murphy, Chairperson
Deborah A.H. 'Christen, Vice Chair
Paul Doherty, Member
Amaryllis Leet, Member
Members Absent:
Sheila Lumi, Member
Also Present:
Eugene Capoccia, Secretary
There were no tenants present at this meeting.
At 6:30 p.m. the Chair declared the meeting opened. Minutes of the
October 18, 2012 meeting were presented. On a Motion by Paul Doherty, seconded by
Deborah A.H. Christen; the minutes were approved as presented. All were in favor.
The Executive Director then provided his report. He informed that the yearly rent re-
certifications had been completed. He then brought the board up to date on the parking lot
project.
Mr. Capoccia then moved to the Financial Report. He pointed out that the financial report
being reviewed was for the eleventh month, November 2012. The December 2012 and
year-end financial would not be ready until the end of January 2013. Mr. Capoccia noted
that the Housing Authority was doing very well. He noted that if it wasn't due to the high
utility cost the Housing Authority would probably not need subsidy from DHCD.
After reviewing the financial report in detail, on a motion by Deborah A.H Christen;
seconded by Paul Doherty, financial was approved as presented.
Mr. Capoccia then presented the following resolution:
RESOLUTION FOR CERTIFICATION OF 2010 COMPLIANCE
WITH NOTIFICATION PROCEDURES FOR FEDERAL
AND STATE LEAD PAINT LAWS
Resolution 201304

Whereas:
The Lunenburg Housing Authority has the need to be in
compliance with notification procedures for Federal and State lead
paint laws as follows:
1. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) regulations at 40
CFR 745 Subpart F "Disclosure of Known Lead-Based Paint
and/or Lead-Based Paint Hazards Upon Sale of Lease of
Residential Property",
Massachusetts Department of Public Health Lead Poisoning
Prevention and Control regulations at 105 CMR 460.725,
3. EPA regulations at 40 CFR 745 Subpart E "Residential
Property Renovation", and
4.
Massachusetts Division of Occupational Safety De-leading and
Lead-Safe Renovation Regulations at 454 CMR 22.00.
Whereas:
the Lunenburg Housing Authority is maintaining proper record-
keeping related to such requirements.
Whereas:
DHCD has mandated that housing authority boards certify the
compliance.
NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED,
That members of the Lunenburg Housing Authority Board of
keeping related to such requirements for year 2010.
RESOLVED,
Motion made by Elizabeth Murphy for certification of compliance
with the notification procedures for Federal and State Lead Paint
Laws.
SECONDED,
Deborah A.H. Christen seconded the motion, and the motion was
passed by a unanimous vote.
Mr. Capoccia then informed the board of DHCD's new requirement that all Board
members make their names and contact information public. After much discussion, it was
decided to create e-mail addresses for all members.
With no other business, on a motion by Deborah A.H. Christen, seconded by Sheila
Lumi, the Board voted to adjourn the meeting at 8:02 PM. All were in favor.
