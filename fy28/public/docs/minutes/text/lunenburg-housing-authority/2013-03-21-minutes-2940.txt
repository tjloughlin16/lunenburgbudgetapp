LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY
MINUTES OF REGULAR MEETING
Members Present:
March 21, 2013
Elizabeth Murphy, Chairperson
Deborah A.H. Christen, Vice Chair
Paul Doherty, Member
Amaryllis Leet, Member
Members Absent:
Also Present:
Sheila Lumi, Member
Eugene Capoccia, Secretary
Sue Honeycut, Fenton & Ewald Accountant
Evelyn Perez, Off-Site Housing Coordinator
There were several tenants present; several questions and concerns were addressed by the
board and Mr. Capoccia.
At 6:30 p.m. the Chair declared the meeting opened. Minutes of the February 21, 2013
meeting were presented. On a Motion by Paul Doherty, seconded by Elizabeth Murphy;
the minutes were approved as presented. All were in favor.
The Executive Director then presented his report. He informed Sunshine Paving, the low
paving contractor, will be at a tenant meeting in April to explain the process of the
parking lot work and answer any questions.
Mr. Capoccia then moved to the Financial Report. He noted the year started a bit in the
negative. Utilities seemed high. Maintenance labor is up due to the snow storms and this
is not including March. As we move into the Summer season it should come along. A
clearer financial status will be sighted in the budget months of August or October.
Sue Honeycut, accountant representative of Fenton & Ewald was present at the meeting
and reviewed the year-end Financial Statement, along with the monthly statement also.
She addressed questions and reviewed the Lunenburg Housing Authorities financial
status with the board. The financial report including a review of accounts payable was
accepted, on a motion by Amaryllis Leet; seconded by Deborah A.H. Christen, financials
were approved as presented.
Mr. Capoccia then presented the following resolution:
RESOLUTION FOR ACCOUNTING SERVICES
Resolution 201309

Whereas:
The Lunenburg Housing Authority requires a fee accountant to

Federal financial requirements and,
Whereas:
