LUNENBURG HOUSING AUTHORITY 
 
MINUTES OF JOINT MEETING WITH THE BOARD OF SELECTMEN 
TO MUTUALLY APPOINT TO HOUSING AUTHORITY BOARD 
VACANCY DUE TO FAILURE TO ELECT 
 
Town Hall, Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, 2nd Floor 
 
October 1, 2019 
 
Members Present:   Linda McDonald, Vice Chair 
Deborah A.H. Christen, Member 
Karin Menard, Member 
      
Others Present:   Lunenburg Board of Selectmen 
      
The Vice Chair declared a quorum present and called the meeting to order at 7:20 p.m.   
 
Mr. Ronald Dionne, 131 White Street,  spoke before both boards on his background.  The 
Lunenburg Housing Authority Board and the Board of Selectman voted to appoint Mr. Dionne to 
the Lunenburg Housing Authority Board until the next annual town election in May 2020. 
Deborah Christen made a motion to adjourn the meeting at  7:30 p.m.  Karin Menard seconded, 
all were in favor, and the meeting was adjourned.   
 
Respectfully submitted:   
________________________ 
Linda McDonald, Vice Chair
