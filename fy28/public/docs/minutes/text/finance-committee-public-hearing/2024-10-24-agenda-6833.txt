PUBLIC HEARING NOTICE 
In accordance with the Town Charter (Section 6-6), the Lunenburg Finance Committee will hold 
a public hearing at their Thursday, October 24, 2024 meeting which begins at 7:00 P.M. at 
Lunenburg Town Hall, 17 Main Street, 2nd Floor, Lunenburg, MA., on the November 12, 2024  
Fall Special Town Meeting warrant articles at their regularly scheduled meeting.  
If you would like to participate remotely via ZOOM, please check the Finance Committee 
agenda posted on the Town website calendar at https://www.lunenburgma.gov/calendar for sign- 
in instructions. Agendas are posted 48 hours in advance of the meeting.
