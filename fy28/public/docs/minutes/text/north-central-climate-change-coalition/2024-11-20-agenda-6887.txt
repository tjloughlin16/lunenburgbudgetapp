NC4 is composed of municipal and civic leaders across Massachusetts Congressional District 3 working on the frontlines of climate 
change resistance, resilience, and adaptation. We see first-hand climate change’s impacts to the health and well-being of our 
communities and to our natural and built environment. Our hopes are to harness the power of people and communities working 
together to build awareness and attract funding for regional efforts to address already baked in climate impacts and opportunities to 
create a healthier and safer future.  The mission of The North Central Climate Change Collaborative (NC4) is to foster and 
strengthen regional municipal collaborative efforts to combat climate change through:  
 
    • shared projects, educational efforts, and best practices, and 
    • informing state and federal legislative actions. 
Since convening on an informal basis in the Summer of 2021, people from communities in the 3rd Congressional District have met 
regularly to share knowledge on climate initiatives in their communities and ideas for how a regional group of communities could 
more effectively work together.
