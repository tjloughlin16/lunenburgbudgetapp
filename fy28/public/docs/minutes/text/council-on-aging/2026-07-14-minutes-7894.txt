OLD BUSINESS
1. There has been no spending during the new fiscal year. Any remaining funds from the previous fiscal
year reverted to the Town. The Council on Aging budget primarily consists of salaries and
miscellaneous supplies.
2. The status of the Bylaw Committee remains unclear and will require follow-up.
3. The Outreach Coordinator candidate is pending ratification by the Select Board.
4. No update.
5. The Reading Buddy volunteer program continues, Volunteer training is scheduled for September 9.
6. An Honorary Memory Luncheon is planned for September. The luncheon date has not yet been
determined.
