Lunenburg Council on Aging 
Advocacy Subcommittee Minutes 
December 13, 2011 
 
The COA Advocacy Subcommittee met on December 13, 2011, at 9:03AM, at the  
Eagle House Senior Center. 
 
Present: Jane Doyle, Mary Lynn Conrad, Joyce Wass and Elisabeth Montuori. 
 
We discussed seeking approval from the COA to move forward and stress the urgency of 
moving the septic mound for extended parking. 
 
We also discussed tax assessment levels and the use of the Eagle House Senior Center for 
an emergency shelter. 
 
Meeting adjourned at 9:20AM. 
 
Respectfully Submitted, 
Elisabeth Montuori, Chairperson 
COA Advocacy Subcommittee 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Minutes approved at the 2/14/12 COA Meeting
