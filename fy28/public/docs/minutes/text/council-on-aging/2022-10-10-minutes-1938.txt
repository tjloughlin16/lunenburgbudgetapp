Lunenburg Council on Aging 
Meeting Minutes 
October 11, 2022 
 
CALL TO ORDER: 9:30 AM 
 
Present:  Director Susan Doherty, Chairperson Deb Lincoln, Barbara Brown, Jim McGuigan, Susan 
O’Sullivan, Diane Nowd, Peter Lincoln, Betty DiGiacomo, Jane Rabbitt, Vice Chairperson. 
 
Guests:  Lou Franco Select Board member, Marilyn Talley Eagle House Supporters. 
 
Absent: Judy Tarbell, Cheryl Moisan, Jacquelyn Dwyer. 
 
Announcements:  No announcements. 
 
Minutes Approval: Mr. Lincoln motioned to accept minutes from the September 13, 2022 meeting. Ms. 
O’Sullivan seconded. On vote motion carried unanimously. 
 
Current Business:  
1. The Lunenburg Business Association will assist the COA with developing a new logo for the Eagle 
House Senior Community Center. 
2. Members of the COA were asked to do a final review of the new COA Board Member 
Orientation packet. We will vote on accepting the packet at the next COA meeting. 
3. Boston Post Cane recipient. Ms. Doherty has not yet been able the reach the next recipient on 
the list. 
4. The Volunteer/ Legislative Breakfast will be held on Monday October 31, 2022 @ 9 am. The 
breakfast will be co-sponsored by the Eagle House Supporters and Senator Cronin and the 
$100,000.00 check for the new patio and recreation area will be presented there by Senator 
Cronin. Discussed ways to increase participation in the Volunteer Appreciation event. Catering 
the breakfast will be tried this year.  Also giving small gift certificates to volunteers to Dunkin 
Donuts was suggested. 
5. A Veteran’s Expo is being planned by the Lunenburg Veteran’s Officer and hopefully will take 
place in April, 2023.  This event provides information on services available to Veterans. 
6. There are still tickets available for the Annual High Tea and Mercantile. 
7. Continued discussion of how to get younger people to participate in events in the evening and 
possibly once a month for entertainment on Saturdays. 
 
Director’s Report: 
1.  Council on Aging Finance Update: Ms. Doherty presented the Council on Aging Finance Update. 
This will also be presented at the next Board of Select Persons meeting. The FY23 State Formula 
Grant Funds including FY22 Surplus is $42,306.31. 
2. Capital Plan: The new roof on the new addition should be complete by the end of November or 
early December. The Eagle House has been painted. The current outdoor space is not ADA 
compatible. The plan for the new patio was presented. Representative Cronin secured 
$100,000.00 from the state for construction of this patio.  $100,000.00 has also been included in 
the Capital Plan for this project. 
3. Upcoming Events: The Volunteer/Legislator Appreciation Breakfast is scheduled for October 31, 
2022 at 9 AM. Also scheduled is the High Tea and Mercantile on Monday October 24, 2022 at 
Unanimously approved at November 8, 2022 COA meeting.
