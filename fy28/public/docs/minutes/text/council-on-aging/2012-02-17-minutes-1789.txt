Minutes Approved at 9/11/12 COA Meeting
Minutes
Town of Lunenburg
Council on Aging Director's Livaluation sub-Committee
A meeting of the Council on Aging (CUA) Director's Livaluation Sub-Committee was held on Friday,
February 17, 2012 at 9:00 a.m at the Eagle House Senior Center. Present were committee members
Deb Seeley, Fred Crellin, Jane Doyle, Jim Hays and invited guest, Doreen Noble, Council on Aging
Director.. The nurnose of the meeting was to begin the process for completing the COA Director's job
description and evaluation based on the information provided by Town Manager Kerry Speidel at the
last meeting
The Committee reviewed dratt copies of a job description and job evaluation provided by Deb based on
documents used in the past. The Committee agreed that the job evaluation be based on the job
description and made changes to both documents. Fred suggested that the job evaluation tool be
broken down into major categories to include: Fiscal Planning and Management, Compliance and Rish
Management, Supervision and Leadership, Communication, Program Development and Personal
Characteristios and Development. The Committee agreed and thanked Fred for his input.
Deb will make the changes to the job evaluation tool and bring the revised documents to the next
meeting for approval.
The next meeting of the Director's Evaluation Sub-Committee will be held on Friday, February 24th at
9:00 a.m at the Eagle House. The meeting adjourned at 10:20 a.m.
