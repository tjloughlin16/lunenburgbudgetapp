Minutes Approved at March 12, 2013 COA Meeting
COA Finance Committee Meeting Minutes
January 15, 2013
Present: Jim Hays, Fred Crellin
Absent: Deb Seeley, Jane Doyle
The meeting was called to order at 1:00 PM
Doreen Noble presented the committee with a copy of the FY14 Budget Instructions.
She has been asked to provide two budgets: (1) a Target budget (level funded plus
contractual salary increases) and (2) an above Target budget (level service). We
discussed the two budgets. It was suggested that the Target budget add $100 to the
line item for office equipment maintenance, and that $100 be subtracted from the sewer
line item. It was suggested that the Above Target budget add $250 for programs, $400
for training and $1800 for office equipment maintenance.
The meeting adjourned at 2:02PM.
