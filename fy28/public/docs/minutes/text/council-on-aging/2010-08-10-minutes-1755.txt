Lunenburg Council on Aging Meeting Minutes    Minutes Approved at 9/14/10 COA  Meeting 
August 10, 2010 
 
Welcome :  Pete Lincoln, Chairperson, called the meeting to order at 9:31 a.m.   
 
Present 
: Pete Lincoln (Chairperson), Mary Lynn Conrad (Vice Chairperson), Jane Doyle, Jim Hays,  
Joyce Wass, Fred Crellin, and Sue Doherty (Administrative Assistant) 
 
Excused: 
  Barbara Brown, Deborah Seeley, Sis Montuori, & Doreen Noble 
 
Absent: 
  Sandra Flanagan 
 
Minutes: 
  A motion was made and seconded to accept the June  8, 2010 COA annual meeting minutes as 
presented.  All approved, motion carried.  
 
Director’s Report: 
  
 
Finance/Informational Report : Doreen Noble is on vacation therefore there is no  Director’s Report for the 
August meeting. 
 
Old Business: 
 
 
Parking Lot:  Pete Lincoln informed the board that since the last COA meeting in June, Lloyd Carlson has been 
added to the Eagle House parking lot committee.  The committee had met with three architect firms and the one 
that was selected to do the engineering for the parking lot was Hudson HEG.  The committee will be meeting 
again on August 30, 2010. 
 
New Business: 
  No new business was discussed at this meeting. 
 
Committee Reports: 
  It was decided that at the September meeting COA members will be assigned to the COA 
subcommittees for the upcoming year. 
 
Correspondence: 
  A thank you note from former COA board member Emily Foster was read. 
 
General Discussion: 
  The open meeting law was discussed.  Some COA mem bers were concerned about the 
possibility of having to “sign off” on this issue. 
 
Upcoming Events 
:  See attached agenda. 
 
Adjournment: 
 A motion was made and seconded to adjourn the meet ing.  All approved, and the motion was 
carried.   The meeting ended at 10:10 a.m. 
 
 
 
Respectfully submitted, 
Susan Doherty, Administrative Assistant
