Minutes Approved at 9/11/12 COA Meeting
Vilnutes
Town of Lunenburg
Council on Aging Director's Evaluation Sub-Committee
A meeting of the Council on Aging (CUA) Director's Evaluation Sub-Committee was held on Friday,
March 2, 2012 at 9:00 a.m at the Eagle House Senior Center. Present were committee members Deb
Seeley, Jane Doyle, Fred Crellin and Jim Hays. The purpose of the meeting was to review the final
changes to the job description and job evaluation discussed at the previous meetings.
Minor changes were made to the documents and the Committee voted to accept the final job description
and job evaluation tools. Discussion was held regarding the rating scale and there was concern that the
"outstanding" category could be overused and contused with "exceeds expectations". Intormation will
be given at the COA meeting on how the documents were developed, the relationship between the job
description and the evaluation tool and how to use the rating scale.
The process of conducting the actual evaluation will be determined by the COA so the Sub-Committee
agreed that their assignment was complete and the results would be reported at the next COA meeting.
The meeting adjourned at 10:20 a.m.
Respectfully submitted,
been cal peel
