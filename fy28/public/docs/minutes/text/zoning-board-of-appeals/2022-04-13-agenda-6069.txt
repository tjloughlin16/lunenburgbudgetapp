LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING CONTINUANCE 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, April 13, 2022 at 7:00 P.M. at the Town Hall, Joseph F. Bilotta 
Meeting Room, 2nd Floor, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of  Michael Magane d/b/a Dan’s 
Paving, 691 Chase Road, Lunenburg, MA 01462.   The property which is the subject of 
this case is located at 691 Chase Road, Lunenburg, MA  01462, 
 
The petitioner  is seeking a Special Permit to operate a  contractor’s yard for a paving 
company.   
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit.  The basis for 
this Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Code of Lunenburg, §250-4.1 L (6). If you 
wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by 
agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be viewed in the Town Clerk’s office 
or the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
