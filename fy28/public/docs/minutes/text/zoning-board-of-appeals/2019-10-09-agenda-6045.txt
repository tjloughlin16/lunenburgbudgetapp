Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, October 9, 2019 at 7:30 P.M. at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd F loor, 
Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of  NJLJ Properties, LLC, 280 Ayer Road, 
Harvard, MA  01451 .    The property which is the subject of this case  is located at 400 Electric 
Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462, Assessors Map 98-168. 
 
The petitioners are seeking a Special Permit for dimensional variation to install a pylon sign and 
canopy that do not meet the size requirements outlined in §250-6.5 of the Code of Lunenburg.    
 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by 
agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or 
the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
