NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public hearing 
on Wednesday, July 26, 2023 at 7:00 P.M . at the Town Hall, 2nd Floor, Joseph F. Bilotta 
Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, on Facebook Live on the Public 
Access Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg -Public-Access-Cable-
157427270975305/) and will be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube chann el 
within 24 hours after the meeting.  
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of EF Realty, LLC, 654 Elm Street, 
Leominster, MA  01453 .    The property which is the subject of this case is located at 711 
Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg MA  01462.  Assessors Map 59-30. 
 
The petitioner hereby makes application as follows: A Special Permit is being sought for the 
construction of townhouses at 711 Massachusetts Avenue per Section 250 -4.1.H (6) of the Code 
of Lunenburg.  The project site is in the Residence A and Limited Business/Residentia l Zoning 
districts.  A Special Permit was issued by the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals March 10, 
2021 for two 4-unit townhouse buildings.  The Special Permit has since lapsed.
