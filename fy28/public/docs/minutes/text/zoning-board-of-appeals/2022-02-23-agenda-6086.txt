LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, February 23, 2022  at 7:00 P.M. at the Town Hall, Joseph F. 
Bilotta Meeting Room, 2nd Floor, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of D&A Lunenburg, LLC, 494 Ele 
cctric Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462 d/b/a Il Forno Restaurant.   The property, 
which is the subject of this case is located at 494 Electric Avenue , Lunenburg, MA  
01462.    
 
The petitioner makes application as follows:  Seeking relief under §250-7.3A and §250 
7.4A for the construction of a 30 ’ x 50’ Pullman (prefabricated) type build ing with 
seasonal opening garage doors on a grade of concrete slab in the area shown on a 
submitted plan.  The proposed structure will be a detached and permanent.   
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit  for 
dimensional variation.  The basis for this Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Code 
of Lunenburg , §250-7.3A and §250 7. 4A   If you wish to appear in support for or in 
opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by agent or by attorney.  Copies of 
the application may be viewed in the Town Clerk’s office or the Zon ing Board office 
during normal business hours. 
 
Alfred Gravelle, Chairman
