Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, June 14 @ 7:30 P.M. at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor, 
Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of  Schwowens Properties LLC, 30 
Main Street, Ayer, MA  01432.   The property which is the subject of this case is located 
at 68 Laurel Lane, Lunenburg, MA 01462.   
 
The applicant is seeking a Special Permit to reconstruct a structure on the same footprint 
of a pre-existing non-conforming building.   
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit for dimensional 
variation.  The basis for the Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Code of Lunenburg, 
§250-7.4 B.  
 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in 
person, by agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town 
Clerk’s office or the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
