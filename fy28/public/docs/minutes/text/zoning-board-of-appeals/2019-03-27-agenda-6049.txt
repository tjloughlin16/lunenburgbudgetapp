Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
CONTINUANCE 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public hearing on 
Wednesday, March 27, 2019 at 7:30 P.M. at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 17 Main Street, 2nd floor Bilotta 
Meeting Room, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of William Tapply, 146 Lancaster Avenue, 
Lunenburg, MA  01462.  The property which is the subject of this case is owned by William C. & Bevin 
M. Tapply, 146 Lancaster Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462. Assessors Map 71/29. 
 
The applicant is seeking a Special Permit for Dimensional Variation to build an addition on the existing 
home.  The proposal will require relief from both the side yard setback and height regulations as outlined 
in the Code of Lunenburg Sections §250-5.2E (1a) and §250-5.3A (2) respectively.   
 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by agent  or 
by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or the Zoning Board 
office during regular business hours.
