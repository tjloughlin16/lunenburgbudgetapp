ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS
MINUTES
February 9, 2011
The Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals held a public hearing on Wednesday February 9, 201! at 7:30
PM. The hearing was held at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor Conference Room, 17 Main Street,
Lunenburg, MA.
A Special Permit application was before the Board with regard to the Protective Zoning By-Law of the
Town of Lunenburg, Section 4.6.3.1.j. for the outdoor storage and display of ten motor vehicles for sale.
Board Members present: Donald F. Bowen, Chairman, Alfred Gravelle, James Besarkarski, Sheila Luti
and Hans Wentrup. Present but not voting were David Blatt and Paul Doherty
The petitioner Mr. Edward Alleva asked the Board to allow him to withdraw his petition without prejudice
1o allow him to continue to clean up the site that was hindered by recent snowfall.
After due delibcration James Besarkarski made a motion to allow the petitioner to withdraw his application
withoul prejudice. Sheila Lumi seconded the motion.
All members voted in favor of allowing the petitioner to withdraw his application without prejudice and to
refile at a convient time to be determined by the applicant,
Hearing Adjourned al 7:40 P.M.
Minutes submitted by Lisa A. Normandin, Board Secretary
