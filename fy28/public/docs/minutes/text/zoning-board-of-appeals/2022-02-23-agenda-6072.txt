Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, February 23, 2022 @ 7:00 P.M. at the Lunenbur g Town Hall, 
2nd Floor, Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 
01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of  Randy J. G. & Jacqueline M. 
Leblanc, 1390 Lancaster Avenue , Lunenburg, MA 01462 . The property which is the 
subject of this case  is located at 1390 Lancaster Avenue , Lunenburg, MA  01462 .  
Assessors Map 140-11. 
 
The applicants are seeking Special Permit approval to vary the dimensional requirements 
to construct a garage 28’ x 46’.  
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals wi ll consider authorizing the Spe cial Permit  for 
dimensional regulation.  The basis for the Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Code 
of Lunenburg, §250-5.2. E. 2. (a).  
 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in 
person, by agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town 
Clerk’s office or the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
