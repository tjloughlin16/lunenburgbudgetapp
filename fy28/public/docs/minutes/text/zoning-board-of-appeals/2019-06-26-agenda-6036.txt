LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, June 26, 2019 at 7 :00 p.m. at the Town Hall, Joseph F. Bilotta 
Meeting Room, 2nd Floor, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Wal-Mart Store #2118, 301 
Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462.  The property, which is the subject of 
this case is located at 301 Massachusetts Avenue, Lunenburg, MA  01462 and owned by  
Centro GA Lunenburg Crossing, LLC, P.O. Box 4900, Dept. 124, Scottsdale, AZ  85261-
4900  
 
The petitioners are seeking a Special Permit to provide new seasonal outdoor 
display/sales area to occupy eight (8) existing parking spaces adjacent to the garden 
center on the west side of the Walmart store.  
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Per mit.  The basis for 
this Appeal for a Special Permit is found in the Code of Lunenburg §250-4.5.A. If you 
wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by 
agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be viewed in the Town Clerk’s office 
or the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
