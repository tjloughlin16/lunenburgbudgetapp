LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING

The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of S&R Construction Concepts, 10 Rustic 
Trail, Groton, MA  01450.    The property which is the subject of this case is located at 41 
Country Road, Lunenburg, MA 01462.   
 
The petitioner is seeking a Special Permit for a dimensional variation for lot frontage to create a 
buildable lot.  The subject property is located at 41 Country Road, Lunenburg, MA  01462.  
This action was previously granted on December 9, 2020 and has expired. 
 
 The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit.  The basis for this 
Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Lunenburg Protective By -law, Section 8.3.2.1.b) a.  If 
you wish to appear in  support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by 
agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be viewed in the Town Clerk’s office or the 
Zoning Board office during normal business hours.
