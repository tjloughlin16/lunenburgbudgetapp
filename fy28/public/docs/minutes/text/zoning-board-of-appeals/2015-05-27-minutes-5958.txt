ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS
MINUTES
May 27, 2015
The Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals held a continuance of public hearing on Wednesday,
May 27, 2015 at 8:00 PM. The hearing was held at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 2nd Floor
Conference Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, MA.
The applicant Richard A. Bursch, Trustee, LTN Realty Trust, 308 Electric Avenue, Lunenburg,
MA was seeking a Special Permit for the construction and operation of a "Self Storage Facility"
for his property which is located at 270 Electric Avenue, Lunenburg, MA 01462.
Board Members present: Raymond E, Beal Chairman, Donald F. Bowen James Besarkarski,
Alfred Gravelle and Paul Doherty. Hans Wentrup was present but not voting.
Others Present: Richard A. Bursch, Trustee, LTN Realty Trust, 308 Electric Avenue and
Michelle Harvey.
8:00 PM Chairman Raymond Beal reopened the hearing. There was no public discussion as it
was only the applicant present.
The vote was delayed until the May 27, 2015 meeting and Mr. Beal acknowledged a glitch in the
abutter mailing.
James Besarkarski made a motion to grant the Special Permit for a Self-Storage Facility as
requested, Paul Doherty seconded the motion. The submitted plans are part of the decision and
subsequently on file with the Zoning Board of Appeals.
All members voted in favor of approving the project.
Meeting was adjourned @ 8:08 p.m.
Minutes submitted by Lisa A: Normandin, Board Secretary
Approved by Chairman of the Board
