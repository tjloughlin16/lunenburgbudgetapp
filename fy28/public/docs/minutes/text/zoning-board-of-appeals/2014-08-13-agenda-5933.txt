LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, August 13, 2014 at 7:30 P.M.  at the Town Hall, 2nd Floor 
Conference Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition o f Kenneth J. Ricker & Joshua 
Hillman, d/b/a Embers Pizzeria, 84 Lakefront Avenue , Lunenburg, MA  01462.  The 
property which is the subject of this case is locat ed at 84 Lakefront Avenue, 
Lunenburg, MA  01462.    
 
The petitioners are seeking a Special Permit for an  outdoor dining area 20x30 that will be 
enclosed by a retaining wall as detailed on the sub mitted plan.   The Zoning Board of 
Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permi t.  The basis for this Appeal for 
Special Permit is found in Lunenburg Protective By-law Section 4.6.3.1.j).  If you wish to 
appear in support for or in opposition to the petit ion, you may do so in person, by agent 
or by attorney.  Copies of the petition are availab le for viewing in the Town Clerk’s
