LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, April 23, 2014 at 7:30 P.M. at the Town Hall, 2nd Floor Joseph 
F. Bilotta Conference Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition o f Nathan DiPerri, David & 
Sandra A. DiPerri, 115 Fairview Road, Lunenburg, MA .    The property, which is the
