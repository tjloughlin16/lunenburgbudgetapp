The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Barros Family Irrevocable Trust, 
Valzencleber & Brenda Barros, Trustees, 615 Townsend Street, Fitchburg, MA  01420 .  
The property, which is the subject of this case, is located at 615 Townsend Street , 
Fitchburg, MA 01420.  Assessors Map 56/7. Property is located in both Lunenburg and 
Fitchburg. 
 
The applicants are seeking a Special Permit for relief from §250 -4.1H 1 (K) for the 
required one year waiting period for occupancy of an accessory dwelling unit on the 
property after occupancy of the primary dwelling.   
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit.  If you wish to 
appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by agent or 
by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or the 
Zoning Board office during normal business hours.
