LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
  
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, January 25, 2023 at 7:00  P.M. at the Town Hall, 2nd Floor 
Conference Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of J&E Realty Enterprises, Inc .  
(Jacques Nassif, owner) 436 Chandler Street, Worcester, MA  01602.  The property, 
which is the subject of this case,  is located at 383 Whalom Road, Lunenburg, MA  
01462.    
 
The applicant is seeking Special Permit approval to operate an auto repair center and to 
display thirty-five (35) automobiles for sale.  
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit authorization. The 
basis for the Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Code of Lunenburg, §250-4.1.L (1). 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in 
person, by agent or by attorney. 
 
Alfred Gravelle, Chairman
