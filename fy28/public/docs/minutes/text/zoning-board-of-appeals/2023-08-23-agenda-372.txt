The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Damon McQuaid, 49 Brookview 
Terrace, Lunenburg, MA 01462. The property which is the subject of this case is located 
at 49 Brookview Terrace, Lunenburg, MA  01462.  Assessors Map 30-17. 
 
The petitioner is proposing to construct a new dwelling on an existing non-conforming lot 
and convert the existing home to an accessory dwel ling unit.  Seeking relief from §250-
5.1, Table B for lot area and lot width through building.  In addition, the applicant is seeking 
relief from §250 -4.1H 1 (K) for the required one year waiting period to occupy the 
accessory dwelling unit.
