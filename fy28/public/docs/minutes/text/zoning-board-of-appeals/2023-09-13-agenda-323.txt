Zoning Board of Appeals 
 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, September 13, 2023 at 7:00 P.M. at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 
2nd Floor, Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 
01462.  Public hearing also available by ZOOM remote i n accordance with the 
requirements of the Open Meeting Law. 
 
This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, on Facebook Live on the 
Public Access Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg -Public-Access-
Cable-157427270975305/) and will be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube 
channel within 24 hours after the meeting.  
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Christopher & Tiffany Hurlbut, 469 
Sunnyhill Road, Lunenburg, MA  01462   The property which is the subject of this case is 
located at 3 Lancaster Avenue, Lunenburg MA  01462.  Assessors Map 71-1. 
 
The petitioners are seeking a  Special Permit to operate within the Village Center District  
at 3 Lancaster Avenue.  Christophe r Hurlbut, President of Bright Eyed, Inc. is seeking 
approval to rent 700 square feet of office space for the primary purpose to provide weekend 
food kits to students in need.  The space will act as headquarters for administrative needs, 
storage for food to be utilized within the kits and provide space for volunteers to pack kits 
on a weekly basis.  The basis for the Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Code of 
Lunenburg, §250-4.1.
