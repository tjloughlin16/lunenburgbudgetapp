The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of PSG Realty 393 -435 LLC & 
Raudonat Family Revocable Trust, 155 Arbor Street , Lunenburg, MA  014 62.  The 
property, which is the subject of this case, is located at 155 Arbor Street, Lunenburg, MA 
01462.  Assessors Map 68-20 & 21. 
 
The petitioners are seeking a Special Permit for dimensional variation for the reduction of 
the lot width through the building at 155 Arbor Street.  The permit is being filed as part of 
the ongoing permitting process of 171 Arbor Street to accommodate three proposed lots to 
be served by one common driveway. 
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit.  If you wish to 
appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by agent or 
by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or the 
Zoning Board office during normal business hours.
