display thirty-five (35) automobiles for sale.  
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit au thorization.

4.1.L (1) & 250-4.1L (2). 
 
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in
