Members present: Chairman Donald F. Bowen, Alfred Gravelle, Hans Wentrup, Sheila Lumi and James
The Board came to order at 5:00 p.m. Chairman Donald F. Bowen proposed going into executive session
to discuss pending litigation.
Chairman Bowen declared that the reason for executive session would be to discuss litigation strategy in
Hollis Hills LLC vs. Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals, HAC No. 07-13 and that having the discussion
in open session may be detrimental to the Town of Lunenburg's litigation position. Chairman Bowen made
a motion to go into executive session under MGL Chapter 30A, Section 21a (9) to discuss litigation
regarding Hollis Hills, LLC vs. Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals. Housing Appeals Court No. 07-13,
he asked also that the Board not return to open session. The roll call vote was as follows: Donald Bowen
voted yes, Alfred Gravelle voted yes, Hans Wentrup voted yes Sheila Lumi voted yes and James
Besarkarski voted yes.
In executive session, the Board discussed matters related to sewerage and betterment assessments with
regard to the Hollis Hills 40B development. After due deliberation a Hans Wentrup motion was made to
authorize Town Counsel to initiate an appeal from the HAC ruling on March 25, 2013 known as Hollis
Hills, LLC vs. Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals. Housing Appeals Court No. 07-13, James Besakarski
seconded the motion
The roll call vote was as follows: Donald Bowen voted yes, Alfred Gravelle voted yes, Hans Wentrup
voted yes Sheila Lumi voted yes and James Besarkarski voted yes.
The Board by roll call vote, voted to exit the executive session as follows: Donald Bowen voted yes,
Alfred Gravelle voted yes, Hans Wentrup voted yes Sheila Lumi voted yes and James Besarkarski voted
yes.
The meeting was adjourned at 5:20 P.M.
Respectfully submitted,
