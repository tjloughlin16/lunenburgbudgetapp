The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Justin Tousignant, 12 Sunset Lane, 
Lunenburg, MA  014 62.  The property, which is the subject of this case, is located at 12 
Sunset Lane, Lunenburg, MA 01462.  Assessors Map 123-22. 
 
The petitioner is seeking a Special Permit for dimensional variation to construct a new 
single family home.   This action requires a relief from the building height requirement in 
the Code of Lunenburg Chapter 250, Section 5.3. A previous request for Special Permit for 
Dimensional Variation was approved April 14, 2021.  The house has been demolished and 
applicant is seeking approval to rebuild. 
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit.  If you wish to 
appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in person, by agent or 
by attorney.  Copies of the application may be inspected in the Town Clerk’s office or the 
Zoning Board office during normal business hours.
