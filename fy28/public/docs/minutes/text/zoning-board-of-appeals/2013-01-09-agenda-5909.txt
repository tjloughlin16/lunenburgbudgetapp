LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, January 9, 2013 at 7:00 P.M. at the Town Hall, 2nd Floor 
Joseph F. Bilotta Conference Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Dayton Semerjian & Nanette 
Moss, 21 White Oak Lane, Sudbury, MA  01776.    The property, which is the subject 
of this case, is located at 192 Peninsula Drive, Lunenburg, MA  01462.   
 
The petitioners are seeking a Special Permit to demolish the existing house and replace 
with a new home on a parcel containing .33 acres.  The subject property is located at 192 
Peninsula Drive, Lunenburg, MA  01462.   
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit.  The basis for 
this Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Lunenburg Protective By-law, Section 7.4.  
If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may do so in 
person, by agent or by attorney.  Copies of the application may be viewed in the Town 
Clerk’s office or the Zoning Board office during no rmal business hours. 
 
 
Donald F. Bowen, Chairman
