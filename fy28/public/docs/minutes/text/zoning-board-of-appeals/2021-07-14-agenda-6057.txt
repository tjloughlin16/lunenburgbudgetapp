LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, July 14, 2021  at 7:00 P.M . at the Town Hall, 2nd Floor 
Conference Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Paul M. & Leslie K. Gosselin, 21 
Brookview Terrace, Lunenburg, MA  01462.  The property which is the subject of this 
case is located at 21 Brookview Terrace, Lunenburg, MA  01462.    
 
The petitioner s are  seeking a Special Permit for Dimensional Variation to construct a 
deck.   This action requires a relief from the front setback requirement of forty feet (40’) 
found in The Code of Lunenburg Chapter 250, Section 5.2 A (1).  
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit, Finding and 
or Variance.  If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you may 
do so in person, by agent or by attorney.
