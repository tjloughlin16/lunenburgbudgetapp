LUNENBURG ZONING BOARD OF APPEALS 
NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING CONTINUANCE 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, May 11, 2022 at 7:00 P.M . at the Town Hall, Joseph F. Bilotta 
Meeting Room, 2nd Floor, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 01462. 
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of CD Reality Trust, Donald R. 
Dobeck, Trustee, 65 Parker St. Clinton, MA 01510. The property which is the subject of 
this case is located at 410 Hollis Road, Lunenburg, MA 01462, 
 
The petitioner is seeking a Special Permit Dimensional Variation for a lot that does not 
meet the minimum required lot size. 
 
The Zoning Board of Appeals will consider authorizing the Special Permit. The basis for 
this Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Code of Lunenburg, §250 -7.1 Non -
Conforming Lots. If you wish to appear in support for or in opposition to the petition, you 
may do so in person, by agent or by attorney. Copies of the application may be viewed in 
the Town Clerk’s office or the Zoning Board office during regular business hours.
