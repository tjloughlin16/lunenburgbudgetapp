NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
 
Notice is hereby given that the Lunenburg Zoning Board of Appeals will conduct a public 
hearing on Wednesday, September 13, 2023 at 7:15 P.M. at the Lunenburg Town Hall, 
2nd Floor, Joseph F. Bilotta Meeting Room, 17 Main Street, Lunenburg, Massachusetts 
01462.  Public hearing also available by ZOOM remote i n accordance with the 
requirements of the Open Meeting Law. 
 
This meeting will be broadcast live through local access cable, on Facebook Live on the 
Public Access Facebook page (https://www.facebook.com/Lunenburg -Public-Access-
Cable-157427270975305/) and will be able to be found on the Lunenburg Access YouTube 
channel within 24 hours after the meeting.  
 
The Lunenburg Zoning Board will hear the petition of Paula M. Valente, 32 St. Peter Street, 
Fitchburg, MA  01420 and Jason M. Valente, 1  County Road, Westminster, MA  01473.   
The property which is the subject of this case is located at 30 Johnson Street, Lunenburg 
MA  01462.  Assessors Map 137-33. 
 
The petitioners are seeking a  Special Permit to construct a home on a pre -existing non-
conforming parcel.  The previous dwelling was removed in December of 2022.  The basis 
for the Appeal for Special Permit is found in the Code of Lunenburg, §250-7.4.B(2).
