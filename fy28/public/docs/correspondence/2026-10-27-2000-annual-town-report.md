## What it is

The cover reads `LUNENBURG TOWN REPORT 2000` over a town map, with a handwritten first name
in the top corner: this is somebody's personal copy, scanned, not a file the town produced.
The index on PDF page 3 lists the financial sections at printed pages 62-87 (assessors,
balance sheet, receipts, summary of accounts, capital project fund, bonded indebtedness,
collection of taxes, trust funds, treasurer's cash) and wages at 120-132.

**Both, by section.** The FINANCIAL STATEMENTS are fiscal year 2000: PDF page 67 heads the
combined balance sheet `FOR THE YEAR ENDED JUNE 30, 2000`, and the assessors' tables are headed
`(FY2000)`. The DEPARTMENT NARRATIVES are calendar 2000: PDF page 21, *"my annual report for the
year ending December 31, 2000"*. Filed as **FY2000** for its figures.

**PDF pages run three ahead of the printed page numbers** in the financial section (printed 64,
the balance sheet, is PDF 67). PDF page 4 is blank: no dark pixel, and neither reader found text.

**Text:** every page read by Amazon Textract, 27 October 2026, $2.01 for 134 pages;
`text/2026-10-27-2000-annual-town-report-select-board-chair.pdf.txt`, page-marked.

## Why it was held, and the decision

It prints **Births (printed pp. 88-90), Marriages (91-93) and Deaths (94)** -- private
individuals by name, children included. The screen cannot read a scan for names, so the gate
held it in the private bucket as `pending`. TJ decided, 27 October 2026: *"the births
marriages and deaths should be captured. its all public info"* -- the town printed them in its
own annual report, and is publishing the same report in its archive. Published whole.

## When the town's own copy arrives

The town is expected to publish this report in its archive. If the file it publishes is
**byte for byte this one** (same sha256), it is the same document by a second route: the town's
address is added to this record and nothing is processed twice. If it is a different scan,
it is a different document, and each is kept under its own route. That is to be built in
`notes/PLAN-ANNUAL-REPORT-PIPELINE.md`, P1.
