## What it does and does not settle

**Observed:**

- The two offices disagreed on 1 September 2026 about whether the 6/29 entry was right.
- The school's written position was that it should be reversed.
- The Town Manager questioned that and withheld year-end figures pending an answer.
- The entry stood in the 6 October reports.

**Not established:**

- Why the school called the entry incorrect, given that the Superintendent signed the form
  authorising it.
- Why the school named S5511062 *Sp. Ed Transportation* when the ledger names it collaborative
  tuition.
- Whether it was reversed after 6 October.

**What a reversal would do to the FY26 figure (arithmetic, not a forecast).** It would move
$333,494.89 of expense back onto the school general fund. The school general fund's
unexpended balance at period 13, $482,118.07, would become $148,623.18. Nothing here says it
happened.

**The paraprofessional statement is evidence of a decision, not of an outcome** (rule 7). It
says what the district decided to charge where. It does not say how many posts moved or what
they cost. Gap: *Whether a rise in the paraprofessional line is more posts or a grant
unwinding* (`sources/data/money-gaps.csv`).
