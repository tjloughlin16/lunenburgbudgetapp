# Three documents from the Select Board meeting of 20 October 2026, by email

None of these came off a website. Each was attached to an email to the site owner, so the
email is the address (rule 12): who sent it, to whom, when, and in reply to what.

## The request

TJ Loughlin (this project) to Renee Emkey and Jennifer, Wednesday 21 October 2026, 4:06 PM:
*"Could you please send me the presentation decks that were used during the recent select
board meeting? … the stormwater presentation and the track and field one from the
superintendent. I don't THINK I need to mark this as an official request, but I do mean it
that way!"*

**Who answered.** Renee Emkey is a member of the Select Board, and its Chair at the time:
the Board's own agenda for 20 October 2026 announces a chat session *"with Chair Renee
Emkey"* (`meetings/select-board/2026-10-20-agenda-8080.pdf`, page 1). She is not the Town
Clerk. An earlier note here called her that. *Clerk* is an officer's title she has held on
boards: Clerk of the Select Board in earlier minutes (*"Chair Amanda Moore, Clerk Renee
Emkey"*), and *"Clerk- Renee Emkey, Select Board"* on Budget Task Force agendas.

**1. The Superintendent's deck.** *A Timely Next Step for the LMHS Track and Field
Facility*, Dr. Jodi Fortuna, Superintendent, a presentation to the Select Board asking for a
$195,830 professional-services article at the 17 November 2026 Special Town Meeting. Twenty
slides, digital (`pdf_kind`: 19 text, 1 text+image). Attached to Renee Emkey's reply of
21 October 2026, 4:26 PM: *"Please find attached the SWTF presentation and the
superintendent's presentation. I also included Stephen Wallace's presentation on the Brooks
House."* Its figures are **as presented, not voted**.

**2. The Brooks House deck.** *Selling the Brooks House: Issues to Resolve*, Town of
Lunenburg, dated November 17, 2026 on its cover, by Stephen Wallace, Director of Planning and
Economic Development (agenda item *"Presentation of Brooks House Report by Stephen
Wallace"*). Twenty-two slides, digital (14 text, 8 text+image). **The filename marks it
`Draft`.** It is the version attached to that email, and it may not be the version shown at
Town Meeting. Same email as the deck above.

**3. The Track and Field complex history.** Ten slides of capital-plan requests and Town
Meeting articles. Attached to Renee Emkey's second reply, 21 October 2026, 5:59 PM, verbatim:

> *"Attached is the information I collected for last night's meeting. I did it manually so
> do not assume it is complete."*

**A compilation one person put together by hand, and by her own account incomplete.** Under
rule 13a it is `stated`: an argument somebody assembled, not a record the accounting system
printed. Its use here is as a checklist against our own turf history
(`notes/findings/TURF-HISTORY-COMPARISON.md`), not as an authority over it. Every figure it
quotes rests on a capital-plan request or a Town Meeting vote, and that underlying document
is what settles a figure.

**It is held in the private bucket, `pending`.** `pdf_kind` reads it as `mixed` (3 text,
6 text+image, 1 image). Page 10 is a picture, and the screen holds any PDF with a page it
cannot read (rule 13d). Page 10 was rendered and read by eye on 23 October 2026. It shows two
slides from the 18 November 2021 Special Town Meeting, Article 8 (the free cash history,
FY15-FY22, and *"What will a YES vote do?"*), under the heading *"Slides presented by MRJ at
2021 STM"*. The only people it names are public officials in their roles. Making it public
needs a `publish` decision in `sources/data/redactions.csv`, which waits for TJ.
