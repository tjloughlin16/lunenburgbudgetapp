Lunenburg spends less on each student than almost any school district in Massachusetts.

In FY2025 the town spent $18,027 per student, counting every source of money. The state median was $23,520. Ranked from the most spent per student to the least, Lunenburg placed 310 of 318 districts, and it has been in the state's bottom quarter in 17 of 17 years the state publishes.

For comparison: Monty Tech, the regional vocational school 97 Lunenburg students attend, spent $25,827 per student the same year, by the same state measure: $7,800 more than Lunenburg's own schools. A vocational school is a different kind of school, and the state's own formula sets a larger budget for a vocational student.

Two more you may not have heard:

🏠 The average single-family tax bill rose 47.7% in ten years, while the tax rate fell 26.6%. The bill went up because the average home's assessed value went from $256,969 to $517,296.
