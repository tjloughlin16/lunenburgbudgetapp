$85 or $109 a year: that's what the stormwater program would cost a typical single-family home in Lunenburg under the two options, as presented. On Tuesday, November 17, Town Meeting gets an advisory vote on which way to go: a new stormwater fee, or the regular budget paid through property taxes.

The program costs about $480,000 a year either way. The warrant itself says the tax route "will likely require a Proposition 2 1/2 override in the future." Select Board: No position (4-1-0).
