Five days out, here is where Lunenburg's boards stand on the biggest questions at Special Town Meeting, Tuesday, November 17, 7:00 PM, Lunenburg Middle/High School Auditorium. On the ones that cost money or change what you pay, the Select Board has backed none of them. The room decides.
👉 Every article, on one page: lburg.org/threads/stm-2026-11-17

🎒 $300,000 from free cash for the special education reserve. Select Board: Recommend Disapproval (4-1-0). Then on Wednesday, November 4, the School Committee, which put it on the warrant, voted to pass it over at Town Meeting. The chair voted yes, calling it "a very sad aye."

🏟️ $195,830 to engineer the Middle/High School track and field, design and bid documents only, toward a project estimated at about $2 million. The Select Board has deferred it until the School Committee takes a position. The Capital Planning Committee says it will make no recommendation because the project did not go through its process.

🏚️ Tear down TC Passios? Advisory only: no money and no demolition is voted. A yes asks the Select Board to come back with costs, funding and a timeline; a no asks it to keep looking at reuse. A building committee member put demolition at $2–3 million. Select Board: No position (4-1-0).

💧 Stormwater: a new fee, or property taxes? Also advisory. As presented, the program costs about $480,000 a year: about $85 a year for a typical home under a fee, about $109 through taxes. The warrant itself says the tax route "will likely require a Proposition 2 1/2 override in the future." Select Board: No position (4-1-0).

…plus budget adjustments, a prior-year bill, corrective zoning, and letting the Planning Board set stormwater permit fees.

📣 The Finance Committee takes up the articles Thursday, November 12 at 7:00 PM, on Zoom. Its recommendations are still blank on the warrant.
