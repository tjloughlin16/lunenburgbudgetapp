Welcome to the Lunenburg Budget Project 👋

This is our first post, so let's start with the thing I use most: the board pages.

Every town board and committee has its own page. Open one and you get:

📅 The next meeting: when it is, and what's on the agenda
🗳️ What the board recently voted on
📄 Past meetings, with the agenda, minutes and recording wherever they were posted
🔔 For many boards, a feed you can follow, so you hear when something new is posted

For example, the Select Board meets Tuesday, October 6. On the agenda: Opioid-settlement money for AEDs, the Special Town Meeting warrant, and the future of the Tri-board.
👉 https://lunenburgbudgetproject.org/boards/select-board

Every board, in one list:
👉 https://lunenburgbudgetproject.org/boards
