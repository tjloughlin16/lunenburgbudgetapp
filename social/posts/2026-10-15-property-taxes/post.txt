Put the same $500,000 home in Lunenburg and the towns around it, and Lunenburg's tax on it is $7,195 a year: 5th-highest of 15 nearby.

That is Lunenburg's FY2026 rate, $1,439 for every $100,000 a home is worth, applied to one home for every town so the comparison is like for like. The average single-family home here is worth $517,296 and its average bill was $7,444. We compared the towns Lunenburg borders, this project's peer towns, and the towns whose schools Lunenburg children attend instead.

The same $500,000 home: $6,830 in Leominster, where 18 Lunenburg children go to school; $7,935 in Harvard, home of The Bromfield School. Lunenburg's average home is worth less than in 9 of the 14 other towns, so its rate is higher on lower-value homes.
👉 lburg.org/homes-and-taxes/home-value-and-bill

One more you may not have heard: Lunenburg pays Monty Tech an assessment, like each of its 18 member towns, and it comes out of the same town budget as everything else. In FY2026 it was 3.80% of the town's tax levy. In proportion, that is about $283 of the average bill. That figure is our estimate: state aid pays into the same budget, and nothing published says which money pays which bill.
👉 lburg.org/homes-and-taxes/regional-school-share

These are averages from the state's Division of Local Services: the average single-family value at the town's rate. No one household's bill, and a lower bill is not a cheaper town, because towns pay for different things.
