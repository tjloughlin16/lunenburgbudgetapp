Lunenburg's average home is worth less than in most of the towns around it. Its tax bill takes a bigger share of that home's value than in most of them.

In FY2026 the average single-family home in Lunenburg was assessed at $517,296 and its average bill was $7,444: 1.44% of the value. We set it beside the towns Lunenburg borders, the peer towns this project compares it with, and the towns whose schools Lunenburg children attend instead. On home value Lunenburg ranks 10th-highest. On the bill as a share of value it ranks 5th-highest of 15.

For comparison: in Leominster, where 18 Lunenburg children go to school, the average bill was $6,489 on a $475,021 home (1.37%). In Harvard, home of The Bromfield School, it was $14,562 on a $917,594 home, the highest of these towns.
👉 lburg.org/homes-and-taxes/home-value-and-bill

One more you may not have heard: Lunenburg pays Monty Tech an assessment, like each of its 18 member towns, and it comes out of the same town budget as everything else. In FY2026 it was 3.80% of the town's tax levy. In proportion, that is about $283 of the average bill. That figure is our estimate: state aid pays into the same budget, and nothing published says which money pays which bill.
👉 lburg.org/homes-and-taxes/regional-school-share

These are averages from the state's Division of Local Services: the average single-family value at the town's rate. No one household's bill, and a lower bill is not a cheaper town, because towns pay for different things.
