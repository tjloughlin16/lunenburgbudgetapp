Lunenburg has written itself a report every single year since 1959, and we've now gone through every one of them. 📚

67 annual town reports, 1959 to 2025: 8,997 pages in all. Each one is the town's own account of its year: the tax rate, the Assessors' counts, the Treasurer's books, the school rosters, the election results. Town officers, boards and committees wrote them, and the town printed them, year after year, for more than six decades. It is a gift to anyone who wants to know how we got here. Thank you to everyone who wrote one.

Of those 8,997 pages, 2,332 hold financial tables. We read every one of them, and 2,158 check out against totals the town itself printed on the page.

A few things we found:

💸 The 1969 report prints a tax rate of $133.00 per $1,000. Then: "In 1970 Lunenburg went to full and fair valuation." The 1970 report prints $33.00. Same town, new assessment. A tax rate only compares within one assessment, so neither number tells you what a household paid.

🏕️ In 1960 the Assessors counted 452 camps beside 1,734 dwelling houses: seasonal places, not lived in year-round, 21% of all the homes they counted. In 1910 there were none.

🥪 The 1959 report counts 126,016 school lunches sold at 25 cents each.

🏛️ A study committee recommended a town manager in 1966. The charter passed in 1999, 422 to 150: 33 years later.

👩‍🏫 One name appears on the school rosters for 38 years, from 1963 to 2001. Can you guess whose? It's on the page.

🏡 The town's fact pages count 5,282 residents in 1955 and 10,280 in 2010.
