Harvard's public schools, home of The Bromfield School, spend $7,538 more on each student than Lunenburg's.

In FY2025 Harvard spent $25,565 per student, counting every source of money. Lunenburg spent $18,027. The state median was $23,520. Ranked from the most spent per student to the least, Harvard placed 108 of 318 districts and Lunenburg 310 of 318.

In FY2009 the gap was $2,265.
👉 lburg.org/bromfield/harvard-per-pupil

Where the difference goes: teachers are the biggest piece, $1,951 per student more. And grants and revolving funds, money outside the town's own appropriation, paid 24.0% of Harvard's spending, against 10.1% of Lunenburg's.
👉 lburg.org/bromfield/harvard-line-by-line

The caveat that matters: the two districts serve different students. 10.8% of Harvard's students are low-income, against 23.3% of Lunenburg's. Nothing here shows that spending more causes better results, or that spending less has changed them; comparing two districts cannot separate spending from everything else that differs.
