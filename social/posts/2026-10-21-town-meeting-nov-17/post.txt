Millions of dollars, and places people use every week, are on the line at Lunenburg's Special Town Meeting on Tuesday, November 17. 9 questions are on the warrant so far:
👉 Every detail, on one page: lburg.org/threads/stm-2026-11-17

🏚️ Tear down TC Passios? A non-binding question to voters. The building loses heat this winter and the food pantry is planning to move out. A building committee member put demolition at $2–3 million.

🏟️ $195,830 to engineer the Middle/High School track and field, the first step toward a project estimated at about $2 million. The Select Board held it off the warrant at first, then put it on unanimously two weeks later. What changed?

🎒 $300,000 from free cash for the special education reserve.

💧 Stormwater: a new fee, or the town budget? A non-binding vote. As the Stormwater Task Force presented it to the Select Board, the program costs about $480,000 a year. Paid through property taxes, a typical home would pay about $109 a year; under a separate stormwater fee, $85, with businesses and big parking lots paying more. Nothing has been voted. A second article lets the Planning Board set stormwater permit fees.

…plus the FY27 budget, corrective zoning and a bill from a prior year. The Select Board kept 2 other articles off.
