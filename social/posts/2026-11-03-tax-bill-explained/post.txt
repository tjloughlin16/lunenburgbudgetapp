Prop 2½ is usually described as a 2.5% cap on property taxes. It caps the town's total, not anyone's bill. Lunenburg's average single-family tax bill went from $1,637 in FY1991 to $7,444 in FY2026, about 4.4% a year. Grown 2.5% a year, it would be $3,885.

So where did the rest come from? We split the rise using the state's own levy files:
• The 2.5% a year, compounding: 57% of the rise. It needs no vote.
• New growth: 31%. New homes and additions don't raise anyone else's bill, but they raise the average: our reading is that newer and bigger homes join it.
• Overrides and debt exclusions, everything voters approved: 9%.
• Homes carrying more of the total as businesses became a smaller share (86.5% of the levy, now 91.3%): 4%.

So if Lunenburg's taxes feel high, overrides and exclusions are about a tenth of the story.
