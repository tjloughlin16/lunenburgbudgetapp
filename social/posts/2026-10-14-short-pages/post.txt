The site is easier to read now 📚

Since our last post we've rebuilt the Lunenburg Budget Project around one idea: you shouldn't have to read a long report to get one answer.

Every subject now has its own page, and under it a set of short pages, each answering one question. There are 186 of them across 16 subjects: the schools, the town, and the options for closing the budget gap.

Take Athletics. Instead of one long report, each question gets its own page:

▪️ Athletic fee: The high school fee for a first child, a season, went from $250 in FY2025 to $325 in FY2026.
▪️ Participation: The district counted 649 sport participations in FY2026, against 675 in FY2024.
▪️ Athletic bus funding: From FY2025, athletic buses moved from player fees to the town budget, mostly taxes.
👉 lburg.org/athletics

Finding things is easier too:

📋 lburg.org/reports opens with a contents list of every subject
☰ The Menu at the top of every page reaches every section
🔎 Search covers the pages, the documents and the meeting minutes

Every page still shows where its figures come from, and says what the numbers don't prove.

Try it: pick a subject, open one short page, and tell us in the comments what question you'd want answered that isn't there yet.
